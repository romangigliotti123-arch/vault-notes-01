# Trust or Escalate: LLM Judges with Provable Guarantees for Human Agreement
## Key claims
- Judges should abstain when low-confidence and escalate: a cascade of cheap judges that escalates to stronger ones or humans only when needed can guarantee a user-specified human-agreement level.
- Example: on a Chatbot Arena subset where GPT-4 rarely reaches 80% human agreement, the method guarantees over 80% agreement with almost 80% coverage, even using cheap models like Mistral-7B first.
