# Debating with More Persuasive LLMs Leads to More Truthful Answers
## Key claims
- Weaker non-expert models or humans judging two expert LLMs debating reach 76% (models) and 88% (humans) accuracy vs naive baselines 48% and 60%; optimising debaters for persuasiveness improves judge accuracy.
- Setting has an information asymmetry (experts see the passage, judge does not).
