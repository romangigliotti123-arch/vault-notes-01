# Claude Cowork
## Key claims
- Interaction model Anthropic chose for a non-coding delegated-work assistant: "Give it a goal, and it works across your files and tools. You come back to polished work for your review." "Say what, not how." "Claude shows each step: the files it opens, tools it uses, and choices it makes. Follow along from anywhere, and redirect it if you need to." "Start at your desk, pick it up on your phone." "Close your laptop, it keeps going. Schedule a task for any cadence, and it runs unattended."
- Sandboxed by folder: works "directly in folders and tools you choose"; separate built-in browser with its own logins instead of the user's browser; takes over the screen "only when it has to" (computer use as last resort).
- Big jobs are split into chunks that run together; plugins bundle skills + connectors + sub-agents ("specialist from day one").
- Search-snippet context (not opened): launch coverage (Jan 2026) says Anthropic warned of file deletion and prompt-injection risk and recommended non-sensitive files initially; a later Claude-in-Chrome GA post reportedly said an initial Cowork injection eval was saturated (no attacks succeeded on newer models) - unverified.
