# Judging the Judges: A Systematic Study of Position Bias in LLM-as-a-Judge
## Key claims
- 15 LLM judges, MTBench + DevBench (22 tasks, ~40 solution models), 150,000+ evaluation instances. Metrics: repetition stability, position consistency, preference fairness.
- Position bias is not random chance; varies by judge, candidate and task. Weakly affected by prompt length, strongly affected by the quality gap between solutions (small gap = more bias).
