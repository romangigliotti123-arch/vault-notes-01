# ExpeL: LLM Agents Are Experiential Learners
## Key claims
- No weight updates: gather experiences on training tasks, distil natural-language insights, recall insights and past experiences at inference.
- Performance improves with accumulated experience; shows transfer across tasks.
- Template for a manager lessons log that is distilled and recalled, not just appended.
