# Self-Consistency Improves Chain of Thought Reasoning
## Key claims
- Sample many reasoning paths and take the most consistent final answer by marginalising paths: GSM8K +17.9%, SVAMP +11.0%, AQuA +12.2%, StrategyQA +6.4%, ARC-challenge +3.9% over greedy CoT.
- Works only where answers can be compared/voted (a discrete final answer); disagreement between samples is itself a signal of low confidence.
