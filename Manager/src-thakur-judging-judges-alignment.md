# Judging the Judges: Evaluating Alignment and Vulnerabilities in LLMs-as-Judges
## Key claims
- 13 judge models judging 9 exam-taker models in a clean setting with high inter-human agreement: only the best, largest judges reach reasonable alignment with humans and still fall well short of inter-human agreement; scores can differ from human scores by up to 5 points.
- Judge vulnerabilities: sensitivity to prompt complexity and length, and a tendency toward leniency.
- Judges with high percent agreement can still assign vastly different scores: use alignment metrics beyond percent agreement.
