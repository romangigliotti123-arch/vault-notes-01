# On scalable oversight with weak LLMs judging strong LLMs
## Key claims
- Debate beats consultancy (single AI persuades judge) across tasks; vs direct judge QA results depend on task: debate wins on extractive QA with information asymmetry, mixed elsewhere.
- When debaters choose their side, judges are less often convinced by the wrong answer in debate than in consultancy. Stronger debaters raise judge accuracy only modestly.
- Manager lesson: a single worker arguing "I am done and correct" is a consultancy; an adversarial second view is better than one-sided persuasion.
