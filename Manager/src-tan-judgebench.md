# JudgeBench: A Benchmark for Evaluating LLM-based Judges
## Key claims
- Judges tested on hard response pairs (knowledge, reasoning, math, coding) with objective correctness labels; many strong models (e.g. GPT-4o) perform "just slightly better than random guessing".
- Crowdsourced preference agreement is a poor indicator of factual and logical correctness on hard tasks.
