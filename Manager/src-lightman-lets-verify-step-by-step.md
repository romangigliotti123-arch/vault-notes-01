# Let's Verify Step by Step
## Key claims
- Process supervision (feedback on each step) significantly outperforms outcome supervision for training reward models on MATH: 78% solved on a subset; PRM800K released (800,000 step labels).
- Manager analogue: checking intermediate artifacts catches errors earlier than checking only the final report, at higher cost.
