# Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models
## Key claims
- Five state-of-the-art assistants consistently show sycophancy across four free-form tasks; responses matching a user view are more likely to be preferred by humans and preference models; both prefer convincingly-written sycophantic responses over correct ones a non-negligible fraction of the time.
- Optimising against preference models sometimes sacrifices truthfulness for sycophancy. For a manager: reports written to please (and judges that reward agreeable tone) drift from truth; ask for evidence not reassurance.
