# Karpathy: "decade of agents" and Software 3.0
## Key claims
- Dwarkesh transcript: "decade of agents" is "a reaction to a pre-existing quote... there's some over-prediction going on in the industry." Agents should be thought of "like an employee or an intern", but "currently... they just don't work": not enough intelligence, not multimodal enough, can't do computer use, "They don't have continual learning. You can't just tell them something and they'll remember it." Estimate ~a decade to fix.
- Agents do well on boilerplate/common patterns, poorly on custom code: models "kept misunderstanding the code because they have too much memory from all the typical ways of doing things" (nanochat, DDP example). "The models are not there... autocomplete is my sweet spot."
- "You have to learn what they're good at, what they're not good at, and when to use them."
- Software 3.0 talk (summary only): LLMs as "people spirits" with hallucination, inconsistency, poor memory; products should provide "automated context, diff-based UIs for human review, and autonomy sliders"; "tight generate/verify cycles"; Iron Man suit (augmentation) over autonomous agents. Exact quotes not verified from video.
