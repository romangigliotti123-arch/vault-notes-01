# LLM Evaluators Recognize and Favor Their Own Generations
## Key claims
- Self-preference: an LLM evaluator scores its own outputs higher than others while humans rate them equal.
- Out of the box GPT-4 and Llama 2 can distinguish their own outputs from others; fine-tuning shows a linear correlation between self-recognition and strength of self-preference; controlled experiments argue the link is causal.
- Implication: use a judge/verifier from a different model family or at least not the producing instance.
