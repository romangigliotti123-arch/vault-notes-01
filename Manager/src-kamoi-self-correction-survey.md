# When Can LLMs Actually Correct Their Own Mistakes? A Critical Survey
## Key claims
- (1) No prior work demonstrates successful self-correction with feedback from prompted LLMs except in tasks exceptionally suited for it; (2) self-correction works well when reliable external feedback is available; (3) large-scale fine-tuning enables self-correction.
- Many positive results come from unfair evaluations or impractical frameworks.
