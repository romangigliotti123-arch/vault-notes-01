# ToolRet: Retrieval Models Aren't Tool-Savvy
## Key claims
- Benchmark with 43k-tool corpus and 7.6k retrieval tasks, six model types.
- Strong IR models (good on conventional benchmarks) perform poorly on tool retrieval; poor retrieval degrades end-to-end pass rate of tool-use LLMs.
- Releases >200k training instances that improve IR models on tool retrieval.
- Implication: generic embedding quality on web QA does not transfer; evaluate on YOUR job queries.
