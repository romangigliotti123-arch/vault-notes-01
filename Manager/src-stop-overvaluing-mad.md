# Stop Overvaluing Multi-Agent Debate
## Key claims
- Across 5 MAD methods, 9 benchmarks and 4 models, MAD often fails to beat single-agent CoT and Self-Consistency despite far more compute.
- Model heterogeneity (different models in the debate) is "a universal antidote" that consistently improves MAD.
