# MAS-ProVe: Understanding the Process Verification of Multi-Agent Systems
## Key claims
- Empirical study: 5 verifiers (LLM-as-a-judge, reward models, process reward models), 4 context strategies, 6 MAS frameworks.
- "process-level verification does not consistently improve performance" and often shows high variance; LLM-as-judge generally beats reward-based methods; judge-vs-single-agent gap is small; context length trades off against verification performance.
- "effective and robust process verification for MAS remains an open challenge".
