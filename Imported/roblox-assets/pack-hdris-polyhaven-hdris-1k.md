## Import into Roblox Studio
Roblox cannot import HDRI directly: convert the .hdr to six cube faces with a free cubemap tool, upload the faces and assign them to a Sky object (SkyboxBk/Dn/Ft/Lf/Rt/Up). Also useful as lighting reference.
