# Psychonauts 2: image credits

All Psychonauts 2 screenshots and art are © Double Fine Productions and Xbox Game Studios. Used here under fair use as transformative critical commentary on game design, always alongside substantive analysis and never as a decorative gallery.

Sources below are the **hosts the files were downloaded from**, taken from browser download history. Most are outlets and reference sites republishing publisher-supplied or self-captured screenshots rather than the original rights holder, so they are recorded as the retrieval host, not as a claim of authorship. If a publisher requests removal of any image, comply immediately and replace it with a textual description.

All files were re-encoded to progressive JPEG at quality 88 and downscaled to a maximum width of 1920 px. `emotional-baggage-lineup.png` is kept as PNG because it is a cut-out with an alpha channel.
