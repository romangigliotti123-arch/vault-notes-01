# Mastering Monetization: Turning Creativity into Revenue | RDC 2025

## Summary
RDC 2025 panel on monetization with Roblox staff (Sophia Randawa, Ben Razai) and devs Zach Letter (Wonderwork, Spongebob Tower Defense) and Jesse (Adopt Me). Covers personalized offers, price optimization, regional and managed pricing, rewarded video ads, developer subscriptions, e-commerce, and monetization-engagement balance.

## Tips
- [3:29](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=209) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Tailor offers by player segment: cheap starter pack for new players, mid-tier upgrade for casuals, limited high-price offer for high spenders.
- [4:16](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=256) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Write clear titles and descriptions for every item; Roblox vision-language models read them to rank and recommend items to users.
- [5:06](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=306) **[[pricing]]** Use price optimization and regional pricing rather than guessing; Roblox says recommended prices come with no engagement loss.
- [22:36](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1356) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Treat monetization as engagement: 10 users spending 100 Robux beats one user spending 1,000 Robux for long-term retention and discovery.
- [23:17](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1397) **[[monetisation-other]]** Include monetization from the start; retrofitting paid offers later is harder and players may call it pay-to-win.
- [23:49](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1429) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Avoid popup spam of stacked offers after first purchase; aim to convert new players to a first purchase quickly.
- [25:13](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1513) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Monetize speed or convenience in the core loop, not basic movement; Spongebob TD gave 2x free to group members, converting a $5 3x-speed product.
- [25:54](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1554) **[[pricing]]** Constrain supply through pricing in trading games so monetized items hold value; Adopt Me considers future resale value, not just the purchase price.
- [28:36](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1716) **[[monetisation-other]]** Monetize each mechanic from launch rather than adding paid tiers later, so free players never feel something was taken away.
- [9:14](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=554) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Place rewarded video ads in high-traffic areas like lobbies or shops, and tie them to natural breaks such as level changes, not constant interruptions.
- [10:35](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=635) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Give rewarded-ad viewers a meaningful benefit and prompt them clearly, since the reward is their reason to watch.
- [16:29](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=989) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Spongebob TD gives a tradable cosmetic mount after 10 ad watches to engage free players without cannibalizing spenders.
- [17:09](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1029) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Let free players earn older paid perks through ads once a paid perk loses appeal, such as 3x and 5x speed in Spongebob TD.
- [18:30](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1110) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ad fill is no longer a problem for Spongebob TD developers in 2025; Roblox sales team and Google back-channel gave near-full fill.
- [19:12](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1152) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Combine sales data, broad social sentiment, and one-to-one conversations with engaged players to understand why products do or do not sell.
- [21:14](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1274) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Build low-entry starter bundles, scale them for repeat buyers, then unlock elite bundles and private offers as spending thresholds are reached.
- [25:13](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1513) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Let players taste a premium benefit early, e.g. free 2x speed for group members, to drive first conversion.
- [26:35](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1595) **[[pricing]]** Set entry-level prices so spenders with small budgets still get value, leaving high-value tiers for scarcity-driven trading items.
- [29:17](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1757) **[[pricing]]** Estimate average revenue per paying user by genre before greenlighting; hyper-casual may be about $2 ARPPU while tower defense may be near $20.
- [29:58](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1798) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid exclusive units in hyper-casual games; they can hurt longevity and engagement, while sellable items in trading games add perceived value.
- [30:38](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1838) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Plan monetization six weeks before each update and track how it trends across events and content to avoid a money-hungry reputation.
- [31:58](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1918) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Use fixed-value game passes for clear pricing and trade-economy items for perceived value; players can plan toward a known price.
- [27:16](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1636) **[[monetisation-other]]** Adopt Me ran a pet-to-plush preorder tied to Jazwares; players responded positively, but commerce tooling and international shipping remain challenges.
