# Deep Systems, Deep Engagement: Building Complex RPG Mechanics | RDC 2026

## Summary
RDC 2026 talk by Josh Kurtz (ex-WoW/Rift/FF designer, Roblox dev as trace amaranth) and Alex Hicks (abstract Alex, Red Manta/Twin Atlas) on building deep RPG systems: scaling content costs, content runway vs. build time, time-based balancing and front-loaded progression for free vs paying players, class design by purpose, emergent behavior, and community-driven retention.

## Tips
- [0:08](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=8) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Ask 'what went wrong' on past games; Sword Burst 3 failed by going linear and predictable while trying to be different from a beloved predecessor. Keep what made the original work.
- [4:51](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=291) **[[common-mistakes]]** Watch scaling costs: a skill tied to 3 dungeons becomes a per-update burden at 16+ dungeons. Each new class/dungeon multiplies future maintenance, so add content sparingly.
- [6:53](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=413) **[[common-mistakes]]** Adding classes early seems cheap but level-cap increases force new weapons, skills and armor per class. Model maintenance cost of each system before expanding it.
- [5:31](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=331) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Space content releases over weeks (world, then one dungeon a week) rather than one big DLC drop, to avoid long gaps without updates.
- [8:17](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=497) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check first-10-minute drop-off for simple blockers (missing button, loading screen), then drop-off per world; a late big drop-off signals a content problem.
- [10:24](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=624) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Use randomness to multiply content runway. World Zero gave cosmetics random colors (~16 billion combos) so repeat earns differ and drive trading and grinding.
- [13:11](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=791) **[[pricing]]** Balance free and paying players by time: estimate how long something takes to earn and price it at what an hour of the player's time is worth.
- [14:32](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=872) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Front-load XP and progression into the first hour so light players get most of the daily gain; heavy players progress slowly unless they buy a booster.
- [15:14](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=914) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Use daily quests on login that clear quickly, then taper rewards, as battle passes do, so players return daily without being stalled.
- [15:55](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=955) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build level curves from time: mob kill time times mobs per level, plus travel, shopping and social time. Put estimates in a spreadsheet, then playtest to compare.
- [18:15](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=1095) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Run closed tests with real critics, not friends. Use community polls to learn what players want before building content for launch.
- [19:46](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=1186) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build a Discord community early (World Zero's has ~10 years of members). Engaged players return on each update, so you are not beholden to algorithm swings.
- [20:28](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=1228) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** RPGs are niche. Judge success by players who stay hours, not those who bounce in 3 minutes from ads, and find your core audience there.
- [21:46](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=1306) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Define each class by a one-to-two sentence purpose, and compare purposes side by side in a document before building to avoid homogeneous classes.
- [22:28](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=1348) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Place your game on a spectrum from hyperrealistic to superpowers to decide how far you can stylise (cinematic realism like John Wick).
- [23:52](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=1432) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make players explore a new area once, then give fast travel (menu or waystones). Do not make players sit through long travel every time.
- [25:10](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=1510) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Design cozy, comfort-style RPG experiences as an escape. Comfort-game players return after work, which helps retention.
- [26:32](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=1592) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** For end-game players, stretch power gains with timed dungeon entries (e.g. 3 per day or hourly unlocks). Give small incremental power, but never double power.
- [28:31](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0?t=1711) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Lean into emergent behavior: allow item dropping and PvP so players form gangs and clans. Turn blocking and tolls into social hooks.

## Games
- **World Zero**: Open-world MMO RPG; beaten in 5 hours at launch, so they added staggered world and dungeon releases and random cosmetic colors. Discord community supports updates.
- **Sword Burst 3**: Failed by going linear and predictable, with too-easy content delivery. Sword Burst 2's open exploration was the fix.
- **Sword Burst 2**: Popular for 10+ years; open-world PvP led players to form gangs charging tolls, which was fun emergent behavior.
