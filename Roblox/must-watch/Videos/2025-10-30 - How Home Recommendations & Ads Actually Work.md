# How Home Recommendations & Ads Actually Work

[Watch](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU) | MustWatch | 5,820 views | 2025-10-30

## Summary
Roblox discovery webinar by Peter Yang (Creator Analytics), Sandeep Rajagopal (Discovery) and Ethan Binder (Ads). Explains Home "Recommended For You" ranking signals, why impressions drop, using retention-by-acquisition-source analytics, and how ads Manager campaign types work alongside organic distribution, plus AMA answers.

## Tips
- [8:37](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU?t=517) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Only plays from Recommended For You count toward home ranking signals; ads, search and direct link plays are excluded from ranking.
- [5:54](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU?t=354) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Track six RFY signals: qualified playthrough rate, 7-day playtime, 7-day play days, 7-day Robux spend, 7-day Robux spend days, 7-day intentional co-play days.
- [8:37](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU?t=517) **[[analytics-metrics]]** When impressions drop, check four factors: your own updates, Roblox algorithm changes, overall platform user volume/seasonality, and competitors improving their signals.
- [13:21](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU?t=801) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Break retention down by acquisition source; a drop in overall D1 may come from a traffic-mix shift to low-retention external links, not your game.
- [22:29](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU?t=1349) **[[common-mistakes]]** Myth: games are quarantined after weak early days. Algorithm never permanently removes a game; revived games with new plays can re-enter home recommendations.
- [25:57](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU?t=1557) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Running ads should not harm organic distribution; ad users are excluded from RFY ranking signals, so ads add incremental players on top.
- [27:20](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU?t=1640) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Start ad budget at a comfortable level, scale up only if results are good; check ad-user retention before spending more.
- [16:24](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU?t=984) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ads Manager offers four goals: maximize plays (launch), acquire new users, drive retention (0-30 day players), reactivate lapsed users.
- [19:08](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU?t=1148) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Upload up to 10 custom thumbnails per ad campaign; use purpose-built sets for retention versus acquisition campaigns.
- [28:45](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU?t=1725) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ads are useful across the lifecycle, not only launch: use them to bring back lapsed users and grow established games.
- [28:45](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU?t=1725) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** No paid promotion is required for home recommendations; a small engaged friend/follower group can suffice if retention is strong.
- [29:26](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU?t=1766) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Niche games can win: the algorithm finds the segment that loves a game; forcing it on everyone can hurt signals.
- [32:07](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU?t=1927) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Expect a spike in new RFY users after high retention can lower KPIs temporarily, since new cohorts may be less engaged.
- [34:08](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU?t=2048) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** If a user first plays from RFY then returns via search, the later sessions still count toward the 7-day RFY signals.
- [11:32](https://youtu.be/tUz_OekIIiU?t=692) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use the Creator Analytics comparison benchmarks (similar experiences or same genre) but note benchmarks are not used in ranking.
