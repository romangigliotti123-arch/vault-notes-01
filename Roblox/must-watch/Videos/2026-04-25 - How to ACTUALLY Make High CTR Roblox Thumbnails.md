## Summary
Walkthrough for making Roblox thumbnails and icons with a high click-through rate. Covers recreating a reference thumbnail in Photopea (free) at 1920x1080, separating sections with gradients and color overlays, adding stroked icons, and using the RoVis AI tool with reference images. Claims about a ~3% CTR come from the creator's own game.

- [1:22](https://youtu.be/SjjPGduApZU?t=82) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Create thumbnails at 1920x1080 pixels, the Roblox thumbnail size.
- [1:22](https://youtu.be/SjjPGduApZU?t=82) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Start from a reference: right-click a well-performing game's thumbnail, import it into Photopea, and recreate its layout.
- [2:04](https://youtu.be/SjjPGduApZU?t=124) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use a separate gradient image as the background layer, placed behind everything, then split the thumbnail into colored sections with lasso-selected copies and layer color overlays.
- [2:44](https://youtu.be/SjjPGduApZU?t=164) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make icons from in-game objects by snipping them, removing the background with an online tool, then adding a stroke via Layer > Layer Style > Stroke.
- [4:06](https://youtu.be/SjjPGduApZU?t=246) **[[ai-and-tools]]** With RoVis, give a reference image plus a prompt; the creator says it works best with references, and icon prompts work fine without one.

- [5:29](https://youtu.be/SjjPGduApZU?t=329) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't generate thumbnails without a reference image; the creator says that was their original mistake.
