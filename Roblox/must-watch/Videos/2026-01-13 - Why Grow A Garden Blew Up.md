# Why Grow A Garden Blew Up

[Watch](https://youtu.be/BjyJlN1npdw) | MustWatch | 10,782 views | 2026-01-13

## Summary
Speaker argues Grow a Garden is one of Roblox's best games of the last decade. Credits its simple stud-based visuals for mobile/broad accessibility, a relatable core loop (buy seeds, watch plants grow), a 5-minute scarce seed-shop restock hook, offline growth that levels the playing field and drives return visits, and a non-competitive, helpful community.

- [0:58](https://youtu.be/BjyJlN1npdw?t=58) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Simple, low-poly visuals with stud-style design run well on old phones and tablets, widening your potential audience.
- [2:51](https://youtu.be/BjyJlN1npdw?t=171) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build the core loop around a broadly relatable activity (e.g. gardening) that is satisfying even for players who don't do it in real life.
- [3:56](https://youtu.be/BjyJlN1npdw?t=236) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Use a timed, scarce shop restock (~5 minutes, limited stock) to create fear of missing out and bring players back.
