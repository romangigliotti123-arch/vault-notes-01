# LiveOps Essentials

[Watch](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg) | MustWatch | 2,580 views | 2023-08-18

## Summary
Dan's LiveOps overview: post-launch support as a mix of regular content cadence (weekly/biweekly), major expansions (every 4-6 months), live events, quality-of-life fixes, and bug fixes. Covers seasonal and community content, expansion types (social, competitive, collections, live events), and release tactics like Friday launches, themed updates, and measuring impact.

## Tips
- [0:07](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=7) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Plan a roadmap mixing content cadence, major updates, live events, quality-of-life fixes, and bug-fix weeks. Players leave when they run out of content.
- [2:00](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=120) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use the Creator Hub dashboard to spot problems and compare against peers. Low D1 retention suggests dedicating updates to onboarding.
- [3:16](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=196) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Design every update around the core loop. If new content doesn't fit into a part of the core loop, cut it.
- [3:16](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=196) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Integrate updates into the game world rather than adding separate side windows, so the world feels alive with weekly new things.
- [3:57](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=237) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Put new content on time limits. Urgency to get it before it disappears drives engagement, and seasonal content should be time-appropriate.
- [4:34](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=274) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build content cadence on existing systems instead of new ones. New systems add bugs and create an unsustainable treadmill.
- [5:56](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=356) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Healthy cadence shows as a wave on the dashboard: each new release pulls in players, then activity dips as they finish the content.
- [6:54](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=414) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Make seasonal content internationally appealing. Pick themes like summer barbecue over US-only holidays such as July 4th.
- [7:22](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=442) **[[community-and-discord]]** Run community contests and feature player-made assets, always asking permission and crediting creators. This builds goodwill and helps lean teams keep up.
- [8:02](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=482) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build goodwill with players through feedback and transparency. Communities forgive mistakes from teams they know and trust.
- [9:06](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=546) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Build major expansions as sustainable, evergreen systems that can be updated later, not one-off content.
- [10:29](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=629) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Plan major expansions on a 4-6 month cycle. That is long enough to shake up the game and bring players back.
- [11:51](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=711) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use major expansions for team morale too. They give the whole team a creative challenge beyond routine content.
- [13:01](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=781) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add social systems like guilds, groups, and trading. Players return for friends they made in the game, which makes it stickier.
- [14:06](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=846) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add competitive systems such as PvP, leaderboards, and tournaments to give players a reason to compete.
- [14:45](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=885) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Create short, mid, and long-term player goals through collections and achievements, so players always know what to do next.
- [14:55](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=895) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Run live events at set dates and times to pull a dense player count at once. Influencers talking about them can generate more buzz.
- [15:37](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=937) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Release on Fridays, with development cut off around Wednesday night or Thursday midday. Launch when players are off work and school, not on Tuesdays.
- [15:37](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=937) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Theme each update tightly, such as a domestic, fantasy, or polar pet set. This avoids retreading old ground and constrains creative scope.
- [16:17](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=977) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Update the game icon or listing with a small 'new update' marker, keeping the general iconography so players notice the change.
- [16:59](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=1019) **[[community-and-discord]]** Announce every update on social media and your Discord or Guilded community. Don't keep releases secret from the community.
- [17:40](https://youtu.be/kNE757v3Bwg?t=1060) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Measure every release in analytics: player engagement and revenue impact. Use results to iterate or change course.
