# How To Build a Business On Roblox

[Watch](https://youtu.be/ysqeKxPqBXA) | MustWatch | 5,284 views | 2025-09-09

## Summary
Business-strategy talk on building a Roblox business via leverage. Covers progressing from low-leverage commissions to games, hiring first contractors (GFX artist, builder), managing a team, using written agreements, weekly payments and fast replacement of slow devs, and building a systemized studio that ships without the owner doing studio work. No specific games, numbers on revenue, or platform tools beyond rough hiring costs.

## Tips
- [1:05](https://youtu.be/ysqeKxPqBXA?t=65) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Treat commissions as a low-leverage starting income, then move to games and teams as capital allows; your time is the bottleneck.
- [2:13](https://youtu.be/ysqeKxPqBXA?t=133) **[[team-and-budget]]** Hire small first (e.g. a GFX artist for an icon) to learn hiring, delegating and communicating requirements before hiring for bigger work.
- [3:35](https://youtu.be/ysqeKxPqBXA?t=215) **[[team-and-budget]]** Outsource non-core pieces like base modeling so you can focus on scripting the core systems of your game.
- [4:36](https://youtu.be/ysqeKxPqBXA?t=276) **[[team-and-budget]]** Build a team of scripter, builder and modeler once you have capital; reportedly a map costs roughly 20k-40k Robux to hire.
- [5:17](https://youtu.be/ysqeKxPqBXA?t=317) **[[team-and-budget]]** Write a contractor agreement in Google Docs stating independent-contractor terms and that you can remove a dev at short notice for missed standards.
- [5:17](https://youtu.be/ysqeKxPqBXA?t=317) **[[team-and-budget]]** Pay contractors weekly so you can realistically remove slow devs without losing a large upfront payment.
- [5:57](https://youtu.be/ysqeKxPqBXA?t=357) **[[team-and-budget]]** Give slow contractors one warning, then replace them if deadlines keep slipping; cycle through people quickly to find good ones.
- [5:57](https://youtu.be/ysqeKxPqBXA?t=357) **[[team-and-budget]]** Reward contractors who deliver good work and build long-term relationships with them.
- [6:31](https://youtu.be/ysqeKxPqBXA?t=391) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build a repeatable system of funding, project management, accountability and hiring so shipping no longer depends on your own studio work.
- [7:13](https://youtu.be/ysqeKxPqBXA?t=433) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Even if wealthy, start small with hires to build project-management and leadership skills before funding a full team.
- [7:53](https://youtu.be/ysqeKxPqBXA?t=473) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Plan the progression deliberately rather than expecting a large Robux balance to produce a studio overnight.
- [2:13](https://youtu.be/ysqeKxPqBXA?t=133) **[[team-and-budget]]** Expect slow devs as a common problem; a simple rebirth system should not take three weeks, so enforce deadlines.
