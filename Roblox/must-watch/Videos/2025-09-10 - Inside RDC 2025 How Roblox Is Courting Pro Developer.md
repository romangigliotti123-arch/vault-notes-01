# Inside RDC 2025: How Roblox Is Courting Pro Developer

[Watch](https://youtu.be/8pruyZlxMI4) | MustWatch | 555 views | 2025-09-10

## Summary
Podcast recap of Roblox Developer Conference 2025 with two industry guests (GameFam, Max Power Gaming). Covers platform growth, the 8.5% DevEx payout increase, rewarded video ads in beta with Google, brand integrations vs standalone brand games, the self-service IP licensing program, Roblox Moments, and safety measures.

## Tips
- [11:11](https://youtu.be/8pruyZlxMI4?t=671) **[[roblox-platform-changes]]** DevEx rate rising 8.5%; Roblox is shifting payouts away from engagement toward monetizing new players, so monetization-driven games gain most.
- [14:33](https://youtu.be/8pruyZlxMI4?t=873) **[[monetisation-other]]** Be careful that monetization doesn't feel predatory to your community; one dev said they had to temper aggressive monetization for this reason.
- [18:21](https://youtu.be/8pruyZlxMI4?t=1101) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ad setup reduced from about 40 steps to 3; new objectives include retention, not just visits; cost per visit reportedly under 1 cent.
- [21:05](https://youtu.be/8pruyZlxMI4?t=1265) **[[monetisation-other]]** Rewarded video requires about 100,000 average DAU over the past month and a mild or minimal content maturity rating; it is still in beta with Google ad tech.
- [23:51](https://youtu.be/8pruyZlxMI4?t=1431) **[[monetisation-other]]** Test rewarded video placement; a dev who saw few impressions had not optimized where the ad appears, so low fill may be your setup.
- [26:32](https://youtu.be/8pruyZlxMI4?t=1592) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Brands pay for in-game integrations (quests with rewards inside existing popular games) rather than video ads; this is a route for developers to earn brand money.
- [28:34](https://youtu.be/8pruyZlxMI4?t=1714) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Standalone brand games usually get almost no players (over 99% have essentially no users per Row Monitor); only build one if you can expect tens of thousands of DAU.
- [33:02](https://youtu.be/8pruyZlxMI4?t=1982) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Hubs with minigames attached do not drive engagement; Roblox discovery runs on recognized game genres, so build a genre-based game instead.
- [38:31](https://youtu.be/8pruyZlxMI4?t=2311) **[[monetisation-other]]** IP licensing: anyone can now submit proposals to IP owners; expect IP owners to demand approval processes and oversight before approving.
- [49:39](https://youtu.be/8pruyZlxMI4?t=2979) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** User acquisition on Roblox is algorithm-driven; without engagement metrics, marketing spend will not gain meaningful traction.
- [43:36](https://youtu.be/8pruyZlxMI4?t=2616) **[[team-and-budget]]** Pair with a publishing partner once a game gains traction to scale it over years; a 3D modeler or programmer gap is a common pain point.
- [46:11](https://youtu.be/8pruyZlxMI4?t=2771) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Roblox Moments (opt-in short clips with a join button) may help discovery; it is early, with low video quality and few users at launch.
