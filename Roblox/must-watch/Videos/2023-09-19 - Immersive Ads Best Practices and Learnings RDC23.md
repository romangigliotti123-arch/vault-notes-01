# Immersive Ads: Best Practices and Learnings | RDC23

[Watch](https://youtu.be/XJLW1G1Z-8A) | MustWatch | 24,147 views | 2023-09-19

## Summary
Roblox economy team lead Richard Sim explains Immersive Ads (portal and image ads) in beta: how the supply/demand marketplace prices impressions, early publisher earnings, engagement-neutral holdout results, placement and viewability best practices, and the 2023-2024 roadmap.

## Tips
- [25:36](https://youtu.be/XJLW1G1Z-8A?t=1536) **[[monetisation-other]]** Place portals where you expect player traffic (heat maps), not at spawn or in dead zones; spawn-adjacent portals drive very high teleports, hidden ones earn little.
- [28:35](https://youtu.be/XJLW1G1Z-8A?t=1715) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use custom backfill images in ad slots that have no advertiser fill; unfilled slots earn nothing, so make the backfill look native to your environment.
- [28:51](https://youtu.be/XJLW1G1Z-8A?t=1731) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Make sure image ads are actually viewable: Roblox uses IAB viewability standards, so ads placed off-camera or far away earn zero impressions pay.
- [31:16](https://youtu.be/XJLW1G1Z-8A?t=1876) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Don't bury ads behind walls or at bad angles; a viewable ad needs most of its area visible for about a second.
- [30:36](https://youtu.be/XJLW1G1Z-8A?t=1836) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't force players through portals in gameplay (e.g. racing game where a teleport blocks the racer); Roblox monitors implementations and underpays gamed placement.
- [30:46](https://youtu.be/XJLW1G1Z-8A?t=1846) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't mislead users into accidentally teleporting; Roblox is building intentional-teleport confirmation.
- [22:56](https://youtu.be/XJLW1G1Z-8A?t=1376) **[[monetisation-other]]** Roblox holdout tests found immersive ads neutral on engagement, monetisation and search/discovery; most teleporting users return within 24 hours thanks to the back button.
- [24:20](https://youtu.be/XJLW1G1Z-8A?t=1460) **[[monetisation-other]]** Per Roblox, earnings per teleporting user from ads exceed what that user would have generated in-experience, so ads are net positive if placed well.
- [33:01](https://youtu.be/XJLW1G1Z-8A?t=1981) **[[monetisation-other]]** Upcoming publisher controls: set a minimum payout per teleport and choose which advertiser categories may show in your experience.
- [25:12](https://youtu.be/XJLW1G1Z-8A?t=1512) **[[monetisation-other]]** To start: open Studio, find Immersive Ads templates, drag into experience; instant validation runs on publish and you start earning.
- [15:34](https://youtu.be/XJLW1G1Z-8A?t=934) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Until brand suitability controls ship, only all-ages rated experiences can host immersive ads; wait for those controls if rated higher.
- [32:20](https://youtu.be/XJLW1G1Z-8A?t=1940) **[[monetisation-other]]** Adopt immersive ads early: supply of publishers is small versus millions of experiences, and demand is expected to rise and raise prices.
- [20:54](https://youtu.be/XJLW1G1Z-8A?t=1254) **[[monetisation-other]]** Portal ads earn far more than image ads; portal ad dollars are about 10x image ad dollars, so image-only publishers may earn little.
