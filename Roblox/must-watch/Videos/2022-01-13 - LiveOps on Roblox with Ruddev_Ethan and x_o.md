# LiveOps on Roblox with Ruddev_Ethan and x_o

[Watch](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs) | MustWatch | 2,393 views | 2022-01-13

## Summary
Roblox DevRel round table with Bad Business devs (Rudev Ethan, Exo) on live operations: bi-weekly content updates, quarterly major updates, content backlog, holiday strategy, team growth, and community/feedback practices.

## Tips
- [4:02](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=242) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Release 2-3 playable content items per update (weapons, maps, grind levels, features) so players always have something new to play or buy to skip grind.
- [2:20](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=140) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build a content backlog before launch so new content can ship at any time and absorb contractor delays; Bad Business lacked one early and regretted it.
- [5:25](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=325) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Use a cadence: daily/weekly rotating shop items plus bi-weekly Friday updates. Bad Business moved from weekly to bi-weekly because weekly was unsustainable.
- [7:22](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=442) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Aim for a major update about once a quarter, alternating types (new modes, big content drops, systems) to re-engage lapsed players.
- [10:16](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=616) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Expect added systems to multiply asset work: the attachment system turned one gun model into ~30 models, slowing each gun's implementation.
- [9:34](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=574) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid releasing variations of the same content every week; it caused community drop-off and team burnout. Vary content types between updates.
- [12:08](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=728) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Focus forward-facing updates on real content rather than bug fixes; keep bug fixes invisible in larger patches unless they are necessary.
- [16:40](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=1000) **[[team-and-budget]]** Avoid crunch: give contractors breathing room, take the day off after Friday deploys, and never force others to work weekends to fix a release.
- [18:32](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=1112) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Track the content log in Notion: chronological checklist, tagged by cadence vs major, content type and team member; plan months ahead and update constantly.
- [21:22](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=1282) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track weapon and attachment usage, map voting rates and spending in PlayFab; avoid letting analytics drive every decision, use them as one input alongside intuition.
- [23:05](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=1385) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Run holiday updates around the bi-weekly cycle: release a week before the holiday, reuse prior content, and sell past holiday items in a holiday shop.
- [25:08](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=1508) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Create your own in-game holiday via anniversary or lore events to stand out from the many generic holiday updates competing for attention.
- [26:30](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=1590) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Judge live ops success by baseline earnings (weekday average plus weekend), engagement/average playtime per update, D1, W1 and M1 retention, and new users.
- [34:46](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=2086) **[[common-mistakes]]** Re-engage lapsed players through cross-promotion events; players with a bad first impression will not return until you prove the game changed.
- [41:05](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=2465) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Announce updates on Twitter and ping everyone on Discord at release to create a surge of new players; silent releases rely on community discovery.
- [41:45](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=2505) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Change the game icon with every content update, a variation of the template highlighting new content; they say it works for them but not necessarily for all games.
- [28:34](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=1714) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** For FPS hit detection, client-side hit detection with server checks (ammo, state, position, geometry) favors feel; check known exploits server-side and monitor exploit hubs monthly.
- [30:40](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=1840) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Establish a regular update pattern rather than hard dates so players trust content will arrive, and keep a backlog to cover delays.
- [31:48](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=1908) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Update frequently early on to build community trust; once established, bi-weekly can outperform weekly. Do not assume more frequent is better.
- [36:49](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=2209) **[[community-and-discord]]** Use Discord announcement and feedback channels for detailed info (Twitter's word limit is restrictive) and tag feedback so developers keep final say.
- [43:19](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=2599) **[[team-and-budget]]** Keep a stable core team and build a flexible contractor base around it; hire for vision and culture alignment, then grow skills, rather than hiring purely on technical skill.
- [52:24](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=3144) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Write planned items into an internal schedule so ideas are not forgotten days later.
- [58:15](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=3495) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Invest in tooling early for team expansion; good tools make onboarding new contractors far easier than teaching custom workflows.
- [58:56](https://youtu.be/oV8DKuAjQQs?t=3536) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Treat power creep as manageable: design weapons into distinct niches, use the existing content as a framework, and keep feature-complete systems untouched.
