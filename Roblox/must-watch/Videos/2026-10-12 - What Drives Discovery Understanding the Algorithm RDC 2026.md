# What Drives Discovery: Understanding the Algorithm | RDC 2026

[Watch](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw) | MustWatch | 23,125 views | 2026-10-12

## Summary
Roblox RDC 2026 talk on the discovery algorithm. Roblox says it rewards long-term value (retention plus monetisation) using 28-day windows, the signals are unchanged, and play-through rate gates recommendations. Turning Tables and Voldex (Brookhaven) share launch and retention lessons.

## Tips
- [9:06](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=546) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Prioritise retention first, then monetisation; discovery rewards long-term retention and spend over 28 days.
- [9:06](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=546) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check your weakest top-group signal in Creator Dashboard home recommendations tab and fix it first; benchmarks compare to similar games.
- [5:32](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=332) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Expect the algorithm to measure day 1, days 2-7 and days 8-28 separately; short-term spikes alone won't earn recommendations.
- [6:12](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=372) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Make thumbnails match gameplay; users who click then leave quickly are measured separately and hurt recommendations.
- [11:01](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=661) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build meaningful progression with a goal to chase and new unlocks, not a repetitive loop.
- [11:01](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=661) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give reasons to return tied to new content unlocks, not just timed coin or potion offers.
- [11:01](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=661) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Make the game social by design: require playing together (e.g. hard dungeon co-op) rather than invite-for-reward pop-ups.
- [11:01](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=661) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Plan live-ops updates before building the game.
- [11:01](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=661) **[[monetisation-other]]** Design for monetisation from the start; unmonetised games struggle to fund updates and the algorithm favours earning games.
- [13:07](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=787) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Treat icon, title and thumbnail as packaging; play-through rate gates every other signal, so weak packaging means no impressions.
- [13:49](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=829) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build MVPs fast, launch, and iterate on real player data instead of spending a year building in the dark.
- [14:29](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=869) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track funnels, economy events and custom events, not just D1 retention and playtime, to find where onboarding loses players.
- [15:10](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=910) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Run launch ads to gather enough users for funnel and economy data; small samples are not useful.
- [15:10](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=910) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship fixes in the first days after launch; e.g. if only 40% finish onboarding, find the cause and fix it fast.
- [15:51](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=951) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Use Roblox experiments to A/B test onboarding.
- [16:33](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=993) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't rely only on home recommendations; top games get many players from search, friends and community, so build toward being less algorithm-dependent.
- [31:38](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=1898) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Test names with multiple game copies and ads that teleport players into the main game; Time Bomb Arena was renamed to Time Bomb Tag because 'tag' suited search better.
- [22:04](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=1324) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Keep metrics holding as volume grows; scaling reach dilutes metrics, and standout featuring spikes often show declines.
- [24:45](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=1485) **[[monetisation-other]]** Strong long-term retention drives most spend; Brookhaven's revenue mostly comes from deeply retained players.
