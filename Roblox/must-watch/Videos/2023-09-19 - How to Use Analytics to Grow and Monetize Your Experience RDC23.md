# How to Use Analytics to Grow and Monetize Your Experience | RDC23

[Watch](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo) | MustWatch | 9,774 views | 2023-09-19

## Summary
RDC23 panel with Roblox product lead Peter Yang and creators of Jailbreak, Piggy, and Creatures of Sonaria on using analytics to grow and monetize. Covers engagement/retention, monetization (free-to-paid conversion, ARPPU), and acquisition (on- and off-platform), plus Q&A on update cadence, lagging metrics, and UGC.

## Tips
- [2:27](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=147) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Prioritise engagement and retention first: make the game fun enough to return to before optimising monetization or acquisition.
- [3:07](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=187) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Get players into the core loop fast by avoiding launch tutorials; Jailbreak uses contextual hints like footstep markers instead.
- [4:28](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=268) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Jailbreak tested guided UI tutorials and saw no lift in progression metrics, so it dropped them in favour of watch-and-learn hints.
- [5:09](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=309) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Track anti-objectives (e.g. getting caught) as a risk signal, but check whether players return later before cutting them.
- [5:51](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=351) **[[pricing]]** Use measured earnings per hour to price developer products, e.g. price a bundle to skip roughly 3 to 6 hours of in-game earning.
- [6:32](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=392) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make purchases frictionless: Jailbreak moved vehicle buying into the garage UI, which now drives about 40% of purchases.
- [7:13](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=433) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Use multi-week season passes so content cannot be cleared in days; Jailbreak reported good Day 7 and Day 30 retention impact.
- [8:22](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=502) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Story-driven games need constant big updates; Piggy found weekly chapter drops left players replaying little, so add replay hooks.
- [9:02](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=542) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Serve two player types: story enjoyers and gameplay gamers. Piggy added collectibles, multiple endings and unlockable abilities for both.
- [11:01](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=661) **[[monetisation-other]]** Improve free-to-paid conversion from the start: shop visibility, starter packs, and new-purchaser discounts.
- [11:43](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=703) **[[monetisation-other]]** Raise revenue per paying user with a diverse item set, seasonal items, and repeat purchases such as refills.
- [13:46](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=826) **[[monetisation-other]]** If selling random purchases, add pity systems and fair rates to keep players happy and avoid backlash.
- [15:11](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=911) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** On-platform recommendations weigh engagement, retention, monetization and relevance; test icons and metadata to lift impressions-to-play.
- [15:51](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=951) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Update title, description and icon to surface updates to a scattered audience, and link social, group and developer profiles together.
- [17:53](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=1073) **[[community-and-discord]]** Use short social posts (Instagram, X), Discord and Roblox groups for updates; teasing small leaks sparks community theories.
- [20:33](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=1233) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Pick an update cadence you can sustain; Jailbreak ran weekly for two years, then moved to monthly Friday releases to avoid burnout.
- [22:53](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=1373) **[[analytics-metrics]]** For lagging monetization metrics, log each update and review the trend retroactively; review weekly with a monthly oversight.
- [24:56](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=1496) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use a real-time player-location map to spread players across the map so monetized vehicles are needed to reach earning spots.
- [25:38](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=1538) **[[monetisation-other]]** Watch community posts to find which cosmetics players engage with, then ship targeted sales (Sonaria's skin overlays).
- [27:42](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=1662) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Free UGC items spike traffic briefly; tie them to core mechanics so players stay, and tune unlock difficulty since free items draw fast grinds.
- [29:06](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=1746) **[[community-and-discord]]** Run monthly community design contests for vehicles and cosmetics; Jailbreak sources many assets this way and players learn to model.
- [29:49](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=1789) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Changing a game's name, icon or description did not remove an active game from recommendations per one creator; changing the title reportedly may.
- [31:11](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=1871) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Let players buy Game Passes or season passes for friends; a creator said this had a huge impact.
- [20:09](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=1209) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use third-party analytics such as GameAnalytics plus webhooks to track custom events, since Roblox funnels were not yet supported.

## Games
- **Jailbreak** [3:48](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=228): Contextual hints replaced tutorials; garage purchases, season passes and monthly fan contests drive progression and retention.
- **Piggy** [7:42](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=462): Story chapters need replay hooks: multiple endings, collectible pages and blueprints serve story and gameplay players.
- **Creatures of Sonaria** [25:38](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=1538): Watch community posts to find what players want; targeted skin overlay sales performed well.
- **Dragon Adventures** [12:23](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=743): Shop UI should be bright and premium with clear currency icons to invite exploration without click friction.
