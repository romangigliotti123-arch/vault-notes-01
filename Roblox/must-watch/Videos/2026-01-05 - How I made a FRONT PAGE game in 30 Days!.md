# How I made a FRONT PAGE game in 30 Days!

[Watch](https://youtu.be/4-ge-E9WvM4) | MustWatch | 202,240 views | 2026-01-05

## Summary
Solo dev's account of making a cops-and-thieves Roblox game (Manhunt-inspired) that went from flop to ~2,000 CCU. Covers playtesting with Discord, bug-fixing, ad spend (~$700 total on sponsors) that stalled after ads stopped, slow organic growth, and the marathon mindset. No formal tutorial; mostly devlog and motivation.

## Tips
- [4:33](https://youtu.be/4-ge-E9WvM4?t=273) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Start with a smaller ad budget than the ~$300/week the creator spent; scale up after making improvements, since the game wasn't retained by ads alone.
- [5:43](https://youtu.be/4-ge-E9WvM4?t=343) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Don't judge a game by CCU dropping when paid ads stop; organic growth may lag. Keep shipping updates during the dip, since growth appeared days later.
- [6:53](https://youtu.be/4-ge-E9WvM4?t=413) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Treat Roblox development as a marathon: keep updating a fun game consistently rather than abandoning it after early numbers disappoint.
- [2:03](https://youtu.be/4-ge-E9WvM4?t=123) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Playtest early with a community server (e.g. Discord fans) on bare-bones prototypes to validate the core fun before polishing.
- [3:25](https://youtu.be/4-ge-E9WvM4?t=205) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Expect solo development to take about twice as long as planned; build in buffer before release dates.
- [2:43](https://youtu.be/4-ge-E9WvM4?t=163) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add progression and unlocks (tools, upgrades, levels, daily chest, leaderboards) to give players depth and reasons to return.
- [5:13](https://youtu.be/4-ge-E9WvM4?t=313) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship metric-focused updates (daily challenge, mini-game in lobby, bug fixes) to improve engagement during a launch push.
- [4:33](https://youtu.be/4-ge-E9WvM4?t=273) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Add a few gamepasses before release once the core game is in a stable state.
