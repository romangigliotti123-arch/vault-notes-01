## Games
- **Liftopia**: Triggers captures and makes sharing off-platform easy, driving awareness and linking friends back to the same experience.
- **Dress to Impress**: Chaotic, unexpected content and lore (secret room, backstory) drive viral posts and content creation.
- **On Tap**: Uses audio API for zone-based muffling through walls; plans parties for collaborative secret areas and puzzles.
- **Neighbors**: Fast matchmaking with friends in large parties; annoying voice tools (helium balloon) create funny shareable moments.
- **Lethal Company**: Cited as example of game where spatial voice removal increases fear; party voice must not break gameplay.
