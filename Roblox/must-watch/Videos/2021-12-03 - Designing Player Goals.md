# Designing Player Goals

[Watch](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE) | MustWatch | 4,949 views | 2021-12-03

## Summary
Roblox Game Insights designer Kale (dinosmore) walks through player goals for retention: long/mid/short-term goal structure feeding into each other, the Bartle player types (killers, achievers, socializers, explorers) for choosing a primary and secondary audience, example games, and a worked design exercise for a fictional farming game.

## Tips
- [0:38](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=38) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Map goals in three tiers: long-term (weeks/months), mid-term (days to a week), short-term (achievable in one session, usually the core loop). Each tier should feed the next.
- [5:16](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=316) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Put something at stake in core gameplay. Goals that tie each session to a larger outcome make the core loop more interesting than repeated actions with no consequence.
- [6:37](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=397) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Start goal design from the long-term goal, since it is the main driving force. Then derive mid and short-term goals from it.
- [7:19](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=439) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Without mid-term goals, players feel no progress while chasing long-term goals and lose motivation. Add goals completable in days to a week.
- [8:01](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=481) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** If short-term goals are not your core loop, either the core loop needs work or the short-term goals need reframing.
- [10:05](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=605) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Pace unlocks of new features across areas to avoid bombarding new players. Focus them on the core loop first, then unlock features deeper in the game.
- [14:55](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=895) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Pick one primary Bartle player type to build main goals around, then a secondary type. Ask of every new feature how it affects the primary type.
- [18:47](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=1127) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Do not try to appeal to all player types. Catering to all groups makes the experience less engaging overall. Accept that some players won't like the game.
- [20:11](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=1211) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Explorers and killers conflict. If mixing them, limit PvP to specific areas so explorers can play without being killed.
- [21:47](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=1307) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Trading systems suit both achievers and socializers: achievers maximise collections, socializers must communicate to trade.
- [25:14](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=1514) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Role-playing games should not add PvP or stat-maxing as secondary goals. Explorer-style hidden secrets and interactable objects fit better.
- [41:01](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=2461) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** In competitive games where progression must not grant advantage, use cosmetic-focused season pass progression, or rank players by skill and match them with similar-skill players.
- [43:46](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=2626) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Define player goals early in design to keep scope focused. It is still useful to revisit them on a live game to focus future updates.

## Numbers
- [3:11](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=191) Diminishing marginal utility: **Core gameplay gets less fun the more it is played; goals promise more fun later** (Kale's ice cream analogy applied to game time)
- [6:37](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=397) Long-term goal timeframe: **weeks to months** (Kale's suggested duration for long-term goals)
- [7:19](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=439) Mid-term goal timeframe: **days to a week** (Kale's suggested duration for mid-term goals)
- [8:01](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=481) Short-term goal timeframe: **single session** (Kale's suggested duration for short-term goals)

## Games
- **Pet Simulator X** [8:43](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=523): Clear long-term goal (collect all pets) shown from spawn, with area walls and egg unlocks as mid-term goals and coin-earning as core loop.
- **Bloxburg** [11:16](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=676): Long-term dream house goal broken into room upgrades and job promotions, with going to work as the short-term loop.
- **Jailbreak** [12:09](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=729): Season pass top prize as long-term goal, tier unlocks as mid-term, and contracts/XP from core robbery loop as short-term.
- **Arsenal** [21:47](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=1307): PvP shooter: primary killers, secondary achievers via levels, badges and collection systems.
- **Adopt Me** [23:10](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=1390): Primary achievers via pet collecting; secondary socializers via a robust trading system that serves both.
- **Lumion Legacy** [24:33](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=1473): Primary explorers via deep story and world; secondary achievers via creature collection and leveling.
- **Brookhaven** [25:14](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=1514): Primary socializers for roleplay; secondary explorers via hidden secrets and interactable objects, not killers or achievers.
- **World Zero** [13:47](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=827): PvP arena unlocked at level 40 gives a goal for competitive players.
- **Bed Wars** [14:29](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=869): Battle pass ranks show skill and social status as a goal.
