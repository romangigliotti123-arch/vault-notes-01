# Here's Why Roblox Games Blow Up

[Watch](https://youtu.be/Y93DRfB-Deo) | MustWatch | 283,382 views | 2025-06-07

## Summary
Argues Roblox games blow up because of a strong idea: easy to explain, easy to understand, naturally fun, mass appeal, and built-in retention (FOMO). Explains that Roblox's algorithm promotes games that hold playtime, retention, and non-spammy monetisation, and suggests small Robux ad spend over 2-4 weeks to seed data. Mentions collaboration and commissions.

## Tips
- [1:15](https://youtu.be/Y93DRfB-Deo?t=75) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Make the game explainable in one sentence; if it takes more than 10 seconds to understand from the thumbnail, most players leave.
- [2:19](https://youtu.be/Y93DRfB-Deo?t=139) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Let players start playing within seconds of loading in; avoid long tutorials, and use big buttons, arrows, animation and sound to guide them.
- [3:21](https://youtu.be/Y93DRfB-Deo?t=201) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make every interaction give immediate feedback or reward (explosion, dopamine hit); don't gate fun behind grinding or pay-to-win.
- [4:02](https://youtu.be/Y93DRfB-Deo?t=242) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** To find what is fun, play games you enjoy and identify what specifically makes them fun.
- [5:06](https://youtu.be/Y93DRfB-Deo?t=306) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Target mass appeal; avoid niche themes or gender-targeted designs that isolate large demographics.
- [6:09](https://youtu.be/Y93DRfB-Deo?t=369) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Design FOMO hooks such as timed rare drops, leaderboards, and limited-time events so players feel pulled back.
- [7:46](https://youtu.be/Y93DRfB-Deo?t=466) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Earn algorithmic recommendations by optimising for average playtime, retention, and non-spammy monetisation; Roblox promotes games that keep players on platform.
- [8:28](https://youtu.be/Y93DRfB-Deo?t=508) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Seed the algorithm with small Robux ad spend spread over 2-4 weeks to generate play and retention data, rather than one big spend.
- [9:08](https://youtu.be/Y93DRfB-Deo?t=548) **[[community-and-discord]]** Share the game with influencers and dev communities for feedback; partner with developers and offer value through commissions.
- [9:48](https://youtu.be/Y93DRfB-Deo?t=588) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** If unknown with no budget, avoid going all-in on one project; build passion projects and commissions to grow skills and connections.
