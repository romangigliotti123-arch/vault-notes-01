# What are retention metrics on Roblox?

[Watch](https://youtu.be/LpAU6TheAZ4) | MustWatch | 34,403 views | 2023-09-08

## Summary
Defines Roblox retention metrics (day 1, day 7, day 30) and what low values likely indicate. Gives a checklist for improving retention: onboarding, core loop introduction, common drop-off points, progression, motivation, and end-game reward systems. Uses a hypothetical "Cat Police" case study to illustrate.

## Tips
- [0:13](https://youtu.be/LpAU6TheAZ4?t=13) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track day 1, day 7 and day 30 retention. Low D1 suggests onboarding problems, low D7 suggests weak progression, low D30 suggests lacking end-game content.
- [0:44](https://youtu.be/LpAU6TheAZ4?t=44) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Review the first few minutes of gameplay for onboarding improvements and check whether players reach the core loop fun quickly.
- [0:44](https://youtu.be/LpAU6TheAZ4?t=44) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Look for common denominators in actions, accomplishments or defeats right before players quit, to find where they drop off.
- [1:04](https://youtu.be/LpAU6TheAZ4?t=64) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Check progression: how much content players consume before leaving, and clearly communicate reasons to return daily and reward commitment.
- [1:33](https://youtu.be/LpAU6TheAZ4?t=93) **[[updates-live-ops]]** After day 30, make sure the core loop is repeatable and add an end-game reward system (permanent items, status, lofty goals) so players aren't out of content.
- [2:14](https://youtu.be/LpAU6TheAZ4?t=134) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Tell new players what to expect from the experience, with a glimpse of later progression to give them something to aspire to, which can lift D7 to D30 retention.
- [2:35](https://youtu.be/LpAU6TheAZ4?t=155) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Scale challenges to match player progression (e.g. enemies grow with upgraded gear) so long-term play has a reason to continue.
