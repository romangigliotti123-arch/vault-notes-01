# Promoting Your Experience With Content Creators | RDC23

## Summary
RDC23 panel on working with content creators. Roblox influencer marketing lead gives a platform update, then developers (Adopt Me, Doors, RB Battles-adjacent) and creators (Lana Ray, Sabrina) discuss how creators choose games, how devs engage them, feedback sources, content formats, and metrics.

## Tips
- [3:26](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=206) **[[monetisation-other]]** Offer creators value even without budget: early access, behind-the-curtain previews, Discord access, or exclusive limited-time items that only they can get.
- [0:41](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=41) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Give influencers features that ease content creation, such as private server kickouts and streamer-friendly presence settings that stop stream sniping.
- [1:35](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=95) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Involve creators from the start of planning. Seed content and countdowns before a live event so creators can react to it at launch.
- [19:00](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=1140) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship consistent small updates plus periodic big ones. Creators return to games that keep updating; Rainbow Friends' players and creators dropped off after Chapter 1 until Chapter 2.
- [20:25](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=1225) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Showcase something never seen before, such as a secret pet, so creators have a reason to cover the update.
- [21:46](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=1306) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Pitch creators persistently and personally early on. Doors' dev kept reaching out to YouTubers during Ragdoll Universe, which made later coverage easy.
- [23:07](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=1387) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Engage creators beyond giving them content: reply to, share and react to their videos, and run back-and-forth reaction content to make them feel part of the team.
- [27:48](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=1668) **[[community-and-discord]]** Run a private creator section in your Discord where creators get support and can collaborate with each other, with info shared publicly to all creators.
- [28:12](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=1692) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Make creator-inspired features available to everyone (Doors' unofficial rule), so creators with zero subscribers can also make videos.
- [16:16](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=976) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Weight feedback by source: YouTube comments with timestamps and long-form viewers tend to be more useful than rapid short-form Tick Tok feedback from non-fans.
- [14:12](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=852) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Identify a few trusted taste-maker players and watch their reactions; if they don't enjoy an update, the target audience likely won't either.
- [34:41](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=2081) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track engagement rate per platform weekly; RB-style benchmark was at least 1 like or reply per 10 views, and a drop signals losing the audience.
- [32:38](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=1958) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Judge creator videos by views over likes; post consistently, since consistency helped view counts and streams drive followers and recommended views.
- [18:19](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=1099) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan creator collaborations far ahead for big events; one championship tie-in was planned 12-14 months in advance.
- [25:47](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=1547) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Creators' best-performing formats: green screen, split-screen with gameplay, collection/pet content and multi-creator videos. Use them as a guide for what to showcase.
- [31:38](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=1898) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add hard, aspirational achievements (e.g., Doors' 1000 secret rooms) that creators will grind and film for content.
- [36:04](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=2164) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Long-form games can release periodic large updates rather than weekly small ones; Adopt Me's cadence works for them, so match the cadence to your game's format.

## Games
- **Adopt Me** [5:35](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=335): Relaunched influencer program with Discord access; uses taste-maker players for feedback and staggered small and big updates to sustain creator coverage.
- **Doors** [21:46](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=1306): Built creator relationships on earlier game Ragdoll Universe first; later added modifiers console inspired by YouTuber challenge content, offered to all players.
