# How to make an ad campaign with Roblox Ads Manager

## Summary
Official Roblox Ads Manager walkthrough by the Ads Manager team: prerequisites, goal (maximize plays only for now), audience selection (new, recent, lapsed), budget and targeting tradeoffs, up to 10 custom thumbnails, and the ~24-hour learning period before judging metrics.

## Tips
- [0:04](https://youtu.be/5HcH-9E7USc?t=4) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ads Manager requires users to be 13+, an ad account, and a payment method (card or Robux converted to ad credits).
- [1:04](https://youtu.be/5HcH-9E7USc?t=64) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** For a new experience, leave audiences unselected and let Roblox find users; for an established one, target new users to grow the base.
- [1:04](https://youtu.be/5HcH-9E7USc?t=64) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** After a major update, target lapsed users (played 30-180 days ago, not returned) to win them back.
- [1:43](https://youtu.be/5HcH-9E7USc?t=103) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Narrow targeting raises cost; start with a lower daily budget for about a week to test performance before expanding audience.
- [2:17](https://youtu.be/5HcH-9E7USc?t=137) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Upload up to 10 custom ad thumbnails and let the campaign find which creatives perform best, rather than using only the default thumbnail.
- [2:36](https://youtu.be/5HcH-9E7USc?t=156) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** With stacked advanced targeting filters, lower the daily budget and run longer so the system has time to find specific users.
- [2:56](https://youtu.be/5HcH-9E7USc?t=176) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Do not judge a new campaign immediately; wait about 24 hours for the learning/calibration period before reading metrics.
