## Tips
- [4:28](https://youtu.be/vumAuOqSmNA?t=268) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Verified 18+ US players earn creators a 42% DevEx rate, so age-verified adult audiences pay better than standard rates.
- [5:09](https://youtu.be/vumAuOqSmNA?t=309) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Design for long-term retention: the algorithm now weighs day-7 and day-28 retention over short-term virality and quick monetisation.
- [16:29](https://youtu.be/vumAuOqSmNA?t=989) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build an immediate, satisfying moment-to-moment mechanic first, then wrap it in long-term progression and narrative that can be extended with content.
- [19:13](https://youtu.be/vumAuOqSmNA?t=1153) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make entry feel like a red carpet: players should know where to go and feel good doing it, without being put on rails.
- [19:54](https://youtu.be/vumAuOqSmNA?t=1194) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Avoid tiny fonts and tiny UI text; check readability on mobile, which carries about 60% of minutes.
- [20:35](https://youtu.be/vumAuOqSmNA?t=1235) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Avoid fine manual-dexterity mechanics like clicking moving targets; they alienate older players while kids tolerate them.
- [22:13](https://youtu.be/vumAuOqSmNA?t=1333) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Let veteran and new players play together: allow item trading and revive so strong players can carry weaker ones.
- [23:34](https://youtu.be/vumAuOqSmNA?t=1414) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Test risky choices early with A/B tests and segment results by age; a camera choice that hurt overall numbers was neutral for 18+.
- [24:53](https://youtu.be/vumAuOqSmNA?t=1493) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Prioritise consistent, legible art over photorealism; cohesive style and sound matter more than budget.
- [28:32](https://youtu.be/vumAuOqSmNA?t=1712) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Iterate in Studio on feel-critical values like enemy hit reaction and attack wind-up; instant iteration speeds this up.
- [30:39](https://youtu.be/vumAuOqSmNA?t=1839) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Ship a prototype early and get player feedback; a competitor launched a similar multiplayer idea first while the team sat on theirs.
- [31:20](https://youtu.be/vumAuOqSmNA?t=1880) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't copy the process of successful studios because they succeeded; their methods are not what made them successful.
