# Designing Player Progression

[Watch](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc) | MustWatch | 3,080 views | 2021-12-09

## Summary
Roblox DevRel "Level Up" workshop part 2 on player progression. Covers what progression systems are (XP, seasonal, level, character, collection/random drop, economy), how they tie to the core loop, staging difficulty, pacing content consumption, showing progress, enabling decisions, replayability, meaning in rewards, not locking initial fun, and Q&A on metrics, power creep, adding progression to live games, and end-of-progression content. Uses a road-trip analogy.

## Tips
- [1:52](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=112) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Pick progression type to fit core loop and audience; XP is most flexible, economy suits loops that earn coins to buy items.
- [7:00](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=420) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build progression on top of the core loop; a progression system competing with core gameplay splits player attention.
- [8:23](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=503) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Shape difficulty as a curve: ramp up, peak at boss fights, then dip so players recover and feel powerful.
- [11:08](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=668) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Gate advanced features behind progression so new players learn the core mechanic first, then introduce trading or merging later.
- [12:30](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=750) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Gate lower-tier items behind progression so they gain prestige and players learn their mechanics instead of skipping them.
- [14:35](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=875) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Pace content with progression: estimate average daily earnings and price content so it lasts a target number of days before the next update.
- [15:59](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=959) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Plan each content drop's lifespan up front (e.g., 5 days) so the team has time to build the next update without burnout.
- [17:23](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=1043) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Show progress clearly (e.g., level 6 of 10); visible invested time raises the cost of leaving.
- [19:16](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=1156) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Offer meaningful choices in progression (e.g., skill tree picks) so players develop strategies and talk about them.
- [20:54](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=1254) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give progression rewards that matter; a level-up that does nothing loses engagement and players stop caring.
- [23:37](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=1417) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Do not lock initial fun behind progression; provide enough content upfront (e.g., many decorations) that players see the fun before grinding.
- [26:09](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=1569) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use retention by day to locate progression problems: D1/D7 for early grind, D30 for the late progression tail.
- [28:10](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=1690) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Add progression to a live game by targeting new content only; locking existing items behind XP alienates current and new players.
- [31:35](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=1895) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** At progression end, use competitive systems like leaderboards and social features so players become content for each other.
- [31:35](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=1895) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Extend end-game with prestige cycles that get harder each loop, not easier, for simulator-style games.
- [27:30](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=1650) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Avoid power creep by rebalancing overpowered items and taking player feedback; seasonal resets and trading also help.

## Games
- **Jailbreak** [1:19](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=79): Level-based season progression (10 levels) shows players their position and lets them compare with others.
- **Anime Fighting Simulator** [3:56](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=236): Character progression: upgrading character stats makes the character able to take on bigger enemies.
- **Adopt Me** [4:37](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=277): Random pet drops keep pet collection engaging by making desired pets take time to obtain.
- **Welcome to Bloxburg** [5:19](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=319): Economy progression: buying house items costs money earned through work, gating content by time.
- **Bedwars** [15:18](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=918): Battle pass XP balanced so the average player finishes the season in a set number of days.
- **Survive the Killer** [18:45](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=1125): Visible levels act as a badge of honor and status among players.
- **Star Scape** [19:16](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=1156): Upcoming skill tree lets players choose upgrades, enabling different playstyles.
