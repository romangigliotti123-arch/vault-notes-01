# How to grow your experience with Roblox Ads Manager

[Watch](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw) | MustWatch | 8,873 views | 2025-06-09

## Summary
Roblox webinar (Product Marketing and Product team for ads) on the new Ads Manager: three-step setup, objectives (Maximize Plays, Drive Retention, Reactivate Users, New Users coming), run-continuously campaigns, 1-2 week flights, automated targeting, reporting, and FAQs on organic discovery impact.

## Tips
- [10:48](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=648) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Start with the new Ads Manager flow: three steps (goal, budget, creative) instead of the old ~40 clicks; accept recommended budget and duration first.
- [14:40](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=880) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Run campaigns at least one to two weeks; one- to two-day campaigns don't give the system enough time to optimize for the right players.
- [14:40](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=880) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use daily budget with recommendations rather than lifetime budget; run-continuously works as an always-on traffic stream until ad credits run out.
- [28:42](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=1722) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Split budget across objectives in a funnel, e.g. ~60% Maximize Plays, ~20% Drive Retention, ~20% Reactivate Users, as one example split.
- [30:04](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=1804) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use Maximize Plays for new launches, mid-life games, or updates; use Drive Retention for players who played in the last 0-30 days.
- [31:26](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=1886) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** For a major update on an old game, run Maximize Plays and Reactivate Users together in a one-to-two-week flight to reach both new and lapsed players.
- [36:18](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=2178) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Avoid stacking manual targeting (gender, age, country); it shrinks audience size. Keep targeting open at first and test geo or device guard rails separately.
- [38:19](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=2299) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Run an A/B test of no-targeting in the new flow against targeted campaigns in the classic flow, comparing cost per play, before fully committing.
- [22:31](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=1351) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Upload up to five 16:9 custom creatives per campaign; traffic splits evenly, and per-creative plays, impressions and clicks show which performs best.
- [21:49](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=1309) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make different thumbnails for new players versus recently-played or lapsed players, since each stage of the player journey responds differently.
- [25:15](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=1515) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Use Drive Retention or Reactivate campaigns with custom thumbnails to get a second homepage tile alongside the fixed Continue tile.
- [23:53](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=1433) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Homepage sponsored row is now dynamic (row 2 to row 10, previously fixed at row 5); engaging games rank higher, so retention and engagement matter.
- [30:45](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=1845) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check D1, D7 and D30 retention in Creator Analytics after running Drive Retention campaigns to measure their effect.
- [50:08](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=3008) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Ads Manager Robux earnings attributed to a campaign keep updating for 30 days; check ROI at day 30, not day 1.
- [50:50](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=3050) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Treat Ads Manager reporting as source of truth for ad impact; Creator Analytics blends all sources, so it understates ads.
- [41:10](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=2470) **[[pricing]]** Start with a budget you're comfortable with; increase only if cost per play matches your goals, since there is no max bid in the new flow.
- [46:13](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=2773) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Per Roblox, ads do not hurt organic discovery; they can help cold-start games and high-monetization, low-retention games that score lower on Recommended.
- [56:35](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=3395) **[[roblox-platform-changes]]** Roblox improved pacing and bidding in the last one and a half months to remove CCU and spend spikes; delivery should be smoother through the day.
- [57:57](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw?t=3477) **[[analytics-metrics]]** If players join and leave immediately, the quality fix should show up in rising 7-day and 30-day retention and Robux spend; watch those rather than plays alone.
