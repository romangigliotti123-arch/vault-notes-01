# Future of the Roblox Creator Economy | RDC23

[Watch](https://youtu.be/8R136Y_T5G4) | MustWatch | 17,161 views | 2023-09-19

## Summary
RDC23 economy session by Roblox economy team (Enrico D'Angelo, Anthony Chowdhury, Schumann, Jehan Damji). Covers economy stats, the per-dollar cost split and developer payouts, experience subscriptions (testing Sept 2023, launch Nov 2023, broad rollout Q1 2024), Creator Marketplace 100% net proceeds with USD pricing, and UGC limiteds with fixed and flexible quantities.

## Tips
- [9:13](https://youtu.be/8R136Y_T5G4?t=553) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Roblox says about 29% of an average dollar goes to developers via payouts; 25% covers experience hosting, storage, moderation and localization costs.
- [21:37](https://youtu.be/8R136Y_T5G4?t=1297) **[[monetisation-other]]** Experience subscriptions auto-renew and are paid in real-world currency; retention incentives give creators a larger payout share for long-retained subscribers.
- [22:40](https://youtu.be/8R136Y_T5G4?t=1360) **[[monetisation-other]]** Subscriptions are created in Creator Hub and linked to benefits in Studio; you can activate multiple subscriptions per experience and cancel or delete weak ones.
- [16:13](https://youtu.be/8R136Y_T5G4?t=973) **[[monetisation-other]]** Speaker claims a retained subscriber can be worth 2x the lifetime value; creators had been hacking subscriptions with Dev products before this.
- [27:08](https://youtu.be/8R136Y_T5G4?t=1628) **[[monetisation-other]]** Creator Marketplace plugins (and soon models) keep 100% of net proceeds after payment processing fees and taxes; prices are in USD.
- [38:44](https://youtu.be/8R136Y_T5G4?t=2324) **[[monetisation-other]]** UGC limiteds with fixed supply and a per-unit fee: 77% sell out and 80% resell above original price, with a 3x transaction price versus non-limiteds.
- [40:48](https://youtu.be/8R136Y_T5G4?t=2448) **[[monetisation-other]]** Flexible-quantity limiteds (early 2024) let creators re-batch and replenish supply at a per-unit cost, so creators can restock sold-out items.
- [35:02](https://youtu.be/8R136Y_T5G4?t=2102) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Giving a brand item (Monstercat plushie) as an access card for members-only content and lounges doubled engagement per active user.
- [44:11](https://youtu.be/8R136Y_T5G4?t=2651) **[[common-mistakes]]** New UGC creators will only be allowed to make limiteds at launch; ID verification and a strike system are required for Marketplace creation.
- [12:04](https://youtu.be/8R136Y_T5G4?t=724) **[[roblox-platform-changes]]** Roblox rolls new features out to a small group first and expands them to everyone over time, per the economy team.
