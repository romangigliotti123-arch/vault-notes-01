# Unlocking Fandoms: Evolution of Intellectual Property on Roblox | RDC 2025

[Watch](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A) | MustWatch | 11,153 views | 2025-09-26

## Summary
Roblox RDC 2025 talk on IP (intellectual property) basics and the new License Manager/License Catalog system. Covers copyright vs trademark, registration benefits, DMCA takedown risks, licensing terms (15-30% revenue share typical, 7-DAU eligibility minimum), and the IP scanner that detects experiences using licensed IP. Includes a Kodansha case study.

## Tips
- [2:57](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=177) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't use third-party IP without permission: violations can get your whole experience removed via DMCA takedown, not just the infringing part.
- [9:37](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=577) **[[common-mistakes]]** Repeat IP infringement reports can lead to permanent loss of your entire Roblox account.
- [11:00](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=660) **[[common-mistakes]]** IP enforcement depends on rights holders reporting; similar content staying up does not mean it is allowed.
- [4:22](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=262) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Register copyrights and trademarks you created (USCO and USPTO guides); registration deters infringers and enables statutory damages and fee recovery in US federal court.
- [6:25](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=385) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Trademarks only protect when used to sell a product; generic descriptive titles like 'Really Tall Obby' are weakest, fanciful names are strongest.
- [4:22](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=262) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Assets bought from the creator marketplace can't be protected individually; only your original arrangement of them is protectable.
- [5:03](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=303) **[[ai-and-tools]]** AI-generated output likely can't be copyrighted; modifications and arrangements by a human author can be.
- [14:24](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=864) **[[common-mistakes]]** File a counter notice only if a takedown was truly mistaken; content restores after 10-14 business days if the reporter shows no lawsuit.
- [20:54](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=1254) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Apply for an IP license via License Catalog in Creator Hub; write a clear pitch describing how you use the IP, since IP holders are often not game makers.
- [23:17](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=1397) **[[monetisation-other]]** You can defer monetisation when applying for a license if you are not yet using the IP; the IP holder decides when monetisation starts.
- [24:40](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=1480) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Licensed IP revenue share typically 15-30% and covers in-game purchases, ads, and a share of creator rewards.
- [24:40](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=1480) **[[common-mistakes]]** If the IP scanner flags your experience, you have 7 days to dispute or remove the IP, or the license activates automatically.
- [21:54](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=1314) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Read IP holder content standards carefully; common restrictions include no mixing with other third-party IP and no use of actor likenesses. Violations get you rejected.
- [26:02](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=1562) **[[common-mistakes]]** Fix content standards deviations within 10 business days or the IP holder may terminate the license and take down the experience.

## Numbers
- [21:54](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=1314) Licensor revenue share: **Typically 15-30%, some higher** (Applies to IP holder share of in-game purchases, ads, creator rewards)
- [21:11](https://youtu.be/2HotST5mF3A?t=1271) Minimum eligibility for licensed IP: **7 DAU minimum (IP holder can set)** (IP holders may set max maturity rating and minimum DAU; lifetime DAU criterion planned)
