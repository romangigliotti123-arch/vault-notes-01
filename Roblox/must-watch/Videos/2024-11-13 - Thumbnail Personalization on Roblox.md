# Thumbnail Personalization on Roblox

[Watch](https://youtu.be/I65eJ_uUsY8) | MustWatch | 5,223 views | 2024-11-13

## Summary
Roblox Discovery team explains Thumbnail Personalization: multiple thumbnails (2-5) are active at once and an algorithm routes traffic to whichever wins on qualified playthrough rate, updated hourly. Shows creator Hub setup, how to read stats, and thumbnail best practices (reflect real gameplay, avoid clickbait, keep consistent theme, test new perspectives).
