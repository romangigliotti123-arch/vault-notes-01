# Scale Your Business on Roblox | RDC 2024

[Watch](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ) | MustWatch | 5,882 views | 2024-09-23

## Summary
RDC 2024 talk by Roblox's ads team on scaling a Roblox business: developer earnings, sponsored and search ads, upcoming Ads Manager features, rewarded and immersive video ads, subscription changes, and Price Optimization. A panel with Adopt Me and Vex (Ultimate Football, Driving Empire) shares results.

## Tips
- [5:22](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ?t=322) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Moving sponsored experiences higher on home drove 5x impressions with engagement constant, so more bid inventory means cheaper acquisition.
- [6:43](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ?t=403) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Try search ads: users acquired via search often beat sponsored-experience users on engagement and monetization. Bid on unique title keywords.
- [8:05](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ?t=485) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Check keyword volume with Audience Estimator before bidding; favour high-volume keywords few other creators bid on, since ads are auctioned.
- [23:15](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ?t=1395) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use ads at key moments like Halloween to re-engage old users and drive day-5 and day-7 return, not just day-1 installs.
- [30:03](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ?t=1803) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Split-test immersive ads before full rollout; Vex saw revenue uptick with neutral or improved player metrics.
- [30:03](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ?t=1803) **[[monetisation-other]]** Rewarded video lets non-payers earn in-game currency; Driving Empire got about 7% of monthly revenue from it.
- [25:59](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ?t=1559) **[[monetisation-other]]** Subscriptions can add meaningful revenue even when buried: Ultimate Football gets 5-7% of monthly revenue with about 1% of audience exposed.
- [26:39](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ?t=1599) **[[pricing]]** Pick a subscription price you can hold long-term (Adopt Me chose $2.99 and won't raise it) and make it the best offer on the platform.
- [26:39](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ?t=1599) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Sell a core perk as a dev product (Adopt Me: 250 Robux for two equipped pets) and put extra exclusives, like a monthly pet, behind the subscription.
- [13:45](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ?t=825) **[[monetisation-other]]** Subscription earnings are based on the price paid regardless of platform, with no platform fee on renewals; compare earnings by price in Studio.
- [31:23](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ?t=1883) **[[pricing]]** Use Price Optimization to test dev product and gamepass prices over about 2 weeks and apply suggested prices with one click; Adopt Me saw +3.5% revenue over 12 weeks.
- [16:58](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ?t=1018) **[[pricing]]** Do not copy competitor prices; test prices on your own audience to maximise volume times price.
- [18:21](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ?t=1101) **[[pricing]]** Set item prices per region to reflect purchasing power; Roblox will offer regional recommendations, with you keeping final say.
- [4:06](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ?t=246) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Run the flywheel: build, promote (word of mouth, social, influencers, sponsored and search ads), earn, then iterate on engagement and monetization.
- [32:44](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ?t=1964) **[[pricing]]** Manual price testing is a lot of work (setup, run, analysis); automated optimization saves that time, though it may find no uplift if you are already at equilibrium.

## Games
- **Adopt Me**: Seven-year game uses timed events and ads to re-engage old users; subscription at $2.99 with Robux-only core perk; Price Optimization gave +3.5%.
- **Ultimate Football**: Subscriptions add 5-7% of monthly revenue even when buried; built for console and being renamed NFL Universal Football.
- **Driving Empire**: Rewarded video ads give about 7% of revenue and let non-payers access the economy; manual pricing already at equilibrium, so no Price Optimization uplift.
- **Dungeon Quest**: Vex title using teleports for monetization; one of the original dungeon crawlers.
