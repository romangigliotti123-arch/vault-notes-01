# "Jailbreak" Creator, Asimo, Talks the State of the Platform | Creators on the Blox

[Watch](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY) | MustWatch | 55,589 views | 2026-06-05

## Summary
Podcast interview with Asimo, co-creator of Jailbreak (with Bad CC), covering his early Roblox history, Jailbreak's 2016-2017 origins and record launch, staying motivated, handling criticism, the 2025 "Aura Improvement Project" overhaul, live events, and future plans. Mostly anecdotal; little concrete growth advice.

## Tips
- [24:17](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=1457) **[[team-and-budget]]** Partner with someone whose skills complement yours (builder + scripter); Asimo and Bad CC paired this way and found it a near-perfect working match.
- [47:15](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=2835) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Periodically play your own game as a brand-new player for a sustained stretch (Asimo did two weeks) to find onboarding and balance problems you're blind to.
- [47:56](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=2876) **[[updates-live-ops]]** When a game has accumulated problems, batch many fixes into one large changelog update (Asimo pushed 200+ changes at once) rather than dribbling them out.
- [55:46](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=3346) **[[common-mistakes]]** Check the profile of harsh critics; fans often write negative feedback out of passion. Treat even harshly-worded feedback as applicable to the game.
- [55:04](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=3304) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Limit time on social media; it makes criticism feel personal. Separate the critique from the person giving it.
- [33:12](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=1992) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Build every day, even if only 30 minutes. Asimo says daily practice is what makes you improve quickly.
- [1:01:31](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=3691) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Revisit finished work a day later with fresh eyes; returning to polish finished assets repeatedly made his Avatar Closet better.
- [17:39](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=1059) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Jailbreak went from idea (Dec 2016) to April 2017 release in about four months, built as a passion project alongside full-time jobs.
- [16:57](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=1017) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Find the gap after a genre's core goal: prison games ended once escape was done, so Jailbreak added a world outside the prison with team-vs-team risk and getting caught sends you back.
- [36:34](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=2194) **[[common-mistakes]]** Making a puzzle too hard risks it going unsolved; Asimo worried about this with Ready Player One style hidden events. Balance difficulty and discoverability.
- [52:43](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=3163) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Live events that change the map without kicking players out (volcano/city sinking, 2018) keep players engaged; Asimo learned from watching Fortnite live events.
- [46:33](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=2793) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Make a player-perspective pass: list what confuses or overpowers new players, then ship a focused improvement phase rather than a schedule.
- [28:25](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=1705) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Make games that fit Roblox's kitschy, campy style; polished mobile-industry-style games (e.g. Subway Surfers clones) don't land with the Roblox audience.
- [58:31](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=3511) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Don't resent popular low-effort genres; the audience decides, and there is room for every type of game to find success.
- [59:11](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=3551) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Top-10 placement was needed to earn a living in 2016; now a top-1000 game can be financially viable, so it's a good time to make a quality game.

## Numbers
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=81) Jailbreak lifetime visits: **~7.8 billion (approaching 8B)** (Stated during interview; milestone expected this year)
- [21:07](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=1267) Jailbreak launch peak players: **40-50k night one; ~70k Saturday (Roblox record at the time)** (Launched April 2017 on a Friday)
- [17:39](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=1059) Jailbreak development time: **~4 months (Dec 2016 to April 2017)** (Passion project, both creators with full-time jobs or school)
- [47:56](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=2876) Changes in one Jailbreak update: **200+** (Epic changelog update during Aura Improvement Project)
- [1:14:00](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=4440) Jailbreak update count: **~120+ updates before the overhaul** (Asimo's 2025 description of the game's history)
- [1:06:22](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=3982) Bloxy Awards: **12 or 13 awards in 2018, then disqualified the next year** (Roblox said they had won too many awards)
- [1:10:33](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=4233) Jailbreak live event timing: **Launched around 7am Pacific after a near-2am player-count crunch** (Team stayed up checking player counts, 2018 volcano/sinking city event)
- [1:13:19](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=4399) Top-game income threshold: **Top-10 needed to earn meaningful money in 2016; now top-1000 can be viable** (Asimo's view on the current platform economy)
- [17:39](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=1059) Jailbreak live Roblox app icon: **Used as Roblox mobile app icon for 2-3 years** (Roblox chose it, not the team)

## Games
- **Jailbreak** [0:01](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=1): Prison escape extended into outside-world team-vs-team gameplay; ~7.8B visits; live events and frequent secret updates keep it alive.
- **Redwood Prison / Prison Life** [16:57](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=1017): Prison games ended once escape was done; Jailbreak solved the boredom with a larger world and risk of getting caught.
- **Ultimate Marble Rider** [11:29](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=689): Earlier work from Asimo's portfolio, discussed without specific lessons.
- **The Wind** [12:10](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=730): 2012 showcase that won recognition and was used by Roblox graphics engineers to test updates.
- **Cliffside** [44:45](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=2685): 2025 showcase with ~12,000 wood pieces; Asimo checked every piece for good wood grain.
- **Grow a Garden** [29:07](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=1747): Top game that stays true to Roblox's retro, charming aesthetic.
- **Steal a Brainrot** [29:07](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=1747): Top game that captures Roblox's retro aesthetic and performs well.
- **Front Lines** [29:47](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=1787): Shown as an example of high-polish Roblox game quality; praised but not a tip.
- **Mad City** [40:39](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=2439): Copied Jailbreak's prison layout and E-circle system early; Asimo felt it was not original, but respected it later.
