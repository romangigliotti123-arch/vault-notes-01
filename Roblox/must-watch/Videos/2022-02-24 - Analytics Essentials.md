# Analytics Essentials

[Watch](https://youtu.be/RfpMZZFy8Zw) | MustWatch | 3,326 views | 2022-02-24

## Summary
Level Up short explaining Roblox analytics KPIs: engagement (DAU, MAU, average session length), retention (D1, D7/week 1, D30/month 1), and monetization (ARPDAU, conversion rate, ARPPU). Explains what each signals and where to look when it is low.

## Tips
- [0:52](https://youtu.be/RfpMZZFy8Zw?t=52) **[[analytics-metrics]]** If Day 1 retention is below benchmark, check onboarding and first-time experience first; players are likely confused or frustrated.
- [4:56](https://youtu.be/RfpMZZFy8Zw?t=296) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Low Day 7 retention suggests progression systems lack a clear mid-term goal players can see for the next week.
- [7:10](https://youtu.be/RfpMZZFy8Zw?t=430) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Low Day 30 retention points to long-term goals: endgame content, social bonds, and ongoing challenges for high-level players.
- [9:20](https://youtu.be/RfpMZZFy8Zw?t=560) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Treat conversion rate as the key monetization metric; compare it to competitor benchmarks and review whether offers feel worth buying.
- [10:00](https://youtu.be/RfpMZZFy8Zw?t=600) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Low ARPPU often means a purchase was either so powerful (e.g. infinite money pass) that buyers never need to spend again, or it felt unrewarding.
- [4:03](https://youtu.be/RfpMZZFy8Zw?t=243) **[[analytics-metrics]]** After a major feature update, compare average session length before and after to judge whether the feature helped or hurt.
- [3:21](https://youtu.be/RfpMZZFy8Zw?t=201) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Compare monthly active users year over year, same month, to separate seasonal dips (e.g. October, December) from real decline.
