# Navigating the Difference Between Roblox and Mobile Free-to-Play | RDC23

[Watch](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44) | MustWatch | 7,715 views | 2023-09-19

## Summary
Voldex (Roblox acquisition studio owning Driving Empire, Zoo Samurai, Base Battles, Encounters) contrasts Roblox with mobile F2P: cheaper acquisition, faster iteration, co-development with players, retention drives discovery, teaching how to play and giving reasons to return, and budget-conscious monetization where veterans drive most revenue.

## Tips
- [9:31](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=571) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Improve retention before growing spend: Roblox's algorithm gives more impressions to retentive games, so better retention brings more free traffic.
- [11:07](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=667) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Telling players where to go is not enough; teach them how to play (e.g. a tutorial teaching core actions), which lifted retention over just dropping them in.
- [12:27](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=747) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Give new players an easier starter option (Encounters' 'Noob' champion); A/B test showed new users retained better with it.
- [13:49](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=829) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add goals and feedback: a lightweight XP/level system with a leaderboard gave a 7% lift in day-3 retention on Zoo Samurai.
- [14:32](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=872) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Customize event goals and rewards for new players; Base Battles' new-player event lifted day-7 retention by 10%.
- [7:38](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=458) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Release risky updates first to your most engaged players, collect feedback, iterate, then ship to everyone (Driving Empire car handling).
- [3:22](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=202) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Mobile paid UA is costly (~$2.20 per install); on Roblox, organic traffic lets small creators compete if retention, engagement and monetization are strong.
- [16:16](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=976) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Existing players can boost spending (new users with veterans spent 92% more) but can hurt retention; consider new-player servers or bots for first matches.
- [16:57](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=1017) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Don't throw new players into matches against veterans; a bot opponent for first match retained 5% better in Encounters.
- [19:14](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=1154) **[[pricing]]** Only show offers and dev products/game passes when they're good value; a poor-value starter pack reduced total spend in Base Battles.
- [18:34](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=1114) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Offer larger cash packs with better Robux-to-cash value; Driving Empire saw an 8% sustained revenue-per-user increase.
- [19:56](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=1196) **[[monetisation-other]]** Focus monetization on veteran players (90+ days played) who drive most revenue; keep releasing new things for them to buy.
- [22:02](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=1322) **[[pricing]]** Test price points: Encounters' 650 Robux product converted worse than 400/300/250, but revenue per user rose at lower prices; find the sweet spot.
- [24:44](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=1484) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Be transparent with players when A/B testing so they don't feel their experience is unfair; test big high-risk changes, prototype small ones via Discord.
- [25:25](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=1525) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Build or use A/B tooling to split players into experiences and compare metrics per cohort; hard to test everything in social/teleport games.
- [32:16](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=1936) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Discovery depends on D1-D7 retention, time spent, monetization, and icon click-through relative to other games.
- [3:22](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=202) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Roblox updates are instant and feedback fast, so plan development in days rather than months as on mobile.
- [4:43](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=283) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Roblox handles authentication, permissions and friends graph upfront, so players reach gameplay faster than on mobile.
