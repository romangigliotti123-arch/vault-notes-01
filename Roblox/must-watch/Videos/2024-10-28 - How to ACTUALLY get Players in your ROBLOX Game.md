## Tips
- [0:22](https://youtu.be/3BHo5a_OF5U?t=22) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make the game icon and thumbnail look good so people actually want to click and play.
- [0:22](https://youtu.be/3BHo5a_OF5U?t=22) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Put keywords in the game description and add emojis; don't exaggerate claims.
- [0:45](https://youtu.be/3BHo5a_OF5U?t=45) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check Creator Hub > your game > Overview; aim for green or yellow bars and fix each red one individually.
- [1:25](https://youtu.be/3BHo5a_OF5U?t=85) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Raise session time: obbies need plenty of stages; simulators/incrementals must not have slow progression.
- [1:25](https://youtu.be/3BHo5a_OF5U?t=85) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Make the game fun enough that players return after a day; D1 retention is the share who come back.
- [2:06](https://youtu.be/3BHo5a_OF5U?t=126) **[[monetisation-other]]** Don't spam rainbow buttons on the side of the screen; use a subtle notification for purchases.
- [2:06](https://youtu.be/3BHo5a_OF5U?t=126) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Don't add unnecessary bloat to the game title.
- [2:34](https://youtu.be/3BHo5a_OF5U?t=154) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add a button inside the game that invites friends; speaker calls this the most important tip.
- [2:34](https://youtu.be/3BHo5a_OF5U?t=154) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add like goals in the description or in-game if your like ratio is low, since a ~30% ratio makes players click away.
- [3:14](https://youtu.be/3BHo5a_OF5U?t=194) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Prompt players to favorite the game; speaker says this surfaces the game in favorites and lifts D1/D7 retention.
- [3:21](https://youtu.be/3BHo5a_OF5U?t=201) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use a lifetime budget rather than daily; speaker says lifetime delivers waves of players and beats daily.
- [3:21](https://youtu.be/3BHo5a_OF5U?t=201) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Set the ad to run exactly one day; the speaker has not tested other durations.
- [4:02](https://youtu.be/3BHo5a_OF5U?t=242) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** In the ad set, pick Sponsored Experience, all regions and ages (unless blood/alcohol), and enable only devices your game truly supports, such as console.
- [4:02](https://youtu.be/3BHo5a_OF5U?t=242) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Bid around 0.01 to 0.02 per the speaker; he says most people bid wrong here.
- [4:44](https://youtu.be/3BHo5a_OF5U?t=284) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ad settings change over time; test budgets, bids and durations yourself rather than copying a guide.
