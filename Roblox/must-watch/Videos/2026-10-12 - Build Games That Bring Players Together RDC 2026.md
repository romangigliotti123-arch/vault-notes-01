# Build Games That Bring Players Together | RDC 2026

[Watch](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc) | MustWatch | 1,713 views | 2026-10-12

## Summary
Roblox social team (Ryan and Ria) presents at RDC 2026 on designing games around social features: why friends drive joins and retention, communication tools (chat rephrases, global chat, Quick Words customization, friends chat, voice), persistent friend/global leaderboards, community tools (announcements, polls, analytics, player support beta). Nick from Fish shares how the game went from ~10K to 1.3M+ CCU via grouped map design, visible goals, titles, leaderboards, voice, and new-player servers.

## Tips
- [2:53](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc?t=173) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Design for meaningful play sessions with friends; Roblox data says this is the top predictor of users returning.
- [4:49](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc?t=289) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Keep players physically close in small shared spaces so they see and chat with each other; Fisch's Moosewood dock and Steal a Brainrot bases follow this.
- [16:08](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc?t=968) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Set goals that feel distant but achievable: make the next island or goal visible from the start area so players are motivated to go for it.
- [17:32](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc?t=1052) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Broadcast server-wide events and notable catches in chat so players rush to join and feel FOMO.
- [18:53](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc?t=1133) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Show players far stronger gear from the start (e.g., a high-tier rod next to a basic one) so they want to chase upgrades.
- [19:34](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc?t=1174) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add a visible invite-friends UI that gives a bonus (XP in Fisch) for having friends in the server; include the API call snippet.
- [19:34](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc?t=1174) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add leaderboards for coins or levels; they show progression against other players and trigger competitive behaviour quickly.
- [20:14](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc?t=1214) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Enable voice chat; personal connections made by voice tie players more closely to the game.
- [21:36](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc?t=1296) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Group new players into servers with other new players, not with high-level veterans, to avoid intimidation. Finding the level band takes tuning.
- [5:29](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc?t=329) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Register an ordered data store and enable native leaderboards in Creator Hub to get persistent friend and global leaderboards with no backend and no new storage cost.
- [9:25](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc?t=565) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Use persistent friend leaderboards to create async competition that brings friends back after the player leaves the server.
- [6:11](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc?t=371) **[[community-and-discord]]** Use Roblox native community page announcements with polls; members are notified instantly without manual subscribing, and community analytics show membership trends.
- [12:14](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc?t=734) **[[community-and-discord]]** Listen to players directly through in-game announcements and feedback; Fisch's biggest turnaround came from removing unpopular content and talking to the community.
- [12:48](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc?t=768) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Quick Words customization is coming within a month; pre-add phrases relevant to your game. Publishing runs policy checks (about a few seconds) before they go live.
- [12:14](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc?t=734) **[[common-mistakes]]** Blocking chat messages frustrates players; Roblox's AI rephrase approach suggests an appropriate alternative instead, which players said kept chat more positive and fun.
