# Why "Steal A" Games and "Cashgrabs" Are So Successful: Game Design Secrets Revealed

[Watch](https://youtu.be/vBllyv2fnWw) | MustWatch | 5,976 views | 2025-07-09

## Summary
Breakdown of why "Steal a Brainrot"-style cash grab simulators succeed on Roblox: a simple primary loop (buy and steal cars), a secondary loop (sell and upgrade gear, protect base), and a long-term rebirth system. Argues for a strategy-first approach to making simple trend games rather than passion projects.

## Tips
- [1:01](https://youtu.be/vBllyv2fnWw?t=61) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build a simple primary loop of one repeated action (buy, steal) that players can repeat endlessly.
- [1:36](https://youtu.be/vBllyv2fnWw?t=96) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add a secondary loop where players sell lower-tier items to buy better ones, creating an upgrade cycle.
- [2:20](https://youtu.be/vBllyv2fnWw?t=140) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Vary the loop with many item tiers and visual differences so players feel progression, not endless repetition.
- [3:00](https://youtu.be/vBllyv2fnWw?t=180) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add a rebirth system as a long-term goal that multiplies cash and upgrades core actions, giving players a reason to keep playing.
- [4:06](https://youtu.be/vBllyv2fnWw?t=246) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Make simple games first, especially within trends; they are easier to produce and appeal to the young mobile-heavy Roblox audience.
- [4:47](https://youtu.be/vBllyv2fnWw?t=287) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Play trending games objectively and take notes on what works instead of dismissing them as low quality.
- [5:07](https://youtu.be/vBllyv2fnWw?t=307) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Start a new game by building a reusable template first.
- [5:48](https://youtu.be/vBllyv2fnWw?t=348) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Avoid spending years on a complex dream game before shipping; a smaller, realistic project teaches more.
