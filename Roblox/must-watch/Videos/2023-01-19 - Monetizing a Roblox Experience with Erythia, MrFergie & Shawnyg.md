# Monetizing a Roblox Experience with Erythia, MrFergie & Shawnyg

[Watch](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc) | MustWatch | 3,646 views | 2023-01-19

## Summary
Level Up roundtable (part 1) with Emergency Response: Liberty County (Shawnyg, Tripp), Erythia (Twin Atlas/Sonar Studios, Griffin's Destiny), and Roblox's Dan (Dspav) on monetization: goals, ethics, pay-to-win, paid access vs free-to-play, in-game currencies, event currencies, limited-time items, and sales.

## Tips
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[monetisation-other]]** Design the fun feature first; add monetization afterward only where it fits naturally. Don't make a fun feature worse to force a sale.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[pricing]]** Price items by time-to-earn: compare what a free player grinds for (e.g. 8 hours) against the Robux price, then price higher if you want grinding preferred.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Ensure free players can access all core content; paywalls should sell cosmetics or convenience, not gate progression or required skill-based performance.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[monetisation-other]]** Sell early access to unlocks instead of XP boosts, so rank still reflects playtime and paid players don't skip progression.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[monetisation-other]]** Offer a tiered range of prices so both 15-Robux and 1000-Robux spenders have something valuable to buy.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Use in-game currency as a free-to-play path and as a sample: let players trial paid items with earned currency to convert hesitant buyers.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Create event-specific currency for limited events so rich hoarders cannot buy out event content on day one and participation is required.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[pricing]]** Highlight the best-value bundle and show per-Robux value or bonus percentages so players don't have to do the math.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Avoid random loot boxes as your only revenue; add pity systems or guaranteed-rarity chests so players aren't stuck spinning for rare items.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[monetisation-other]]** Keep limited-time items that are not consumables permanently owned; removing earned cosmetics after an event caused backlash.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Run sales only a handful of times per year at a consistent but infrequent cadence; frequent sales train players to wait and buy only on sale.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Check monetization graphs are healthy before running a sale; Emergency Response saw 2-4x revenue spikes from 25-30% sales.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Consider a paid access period only if the game offers unique value; Emergency Response charged 300 then 50, then 25 Robux during early access before going free.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Before going free-to-play, build matchmaking and private servers so high-stat players are separated from new players and keep their experience.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[monetisation-other]]** Limit gamepass count; showing dozens of gamepasses on a page can overwhelm players and hurt perceived value.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't let pay-to-win creep in; free players leaving causes paid players to churn too, leaving a dead game with zero concurrency.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[common-mistakes]]** If a monetization bug cheats paying players, detect affected purchases in data and notify players with an apology and fix.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[common-mistakes]]** Tune prices and balance after launch: lower overpriced items and add free content to recover trust after over-monetizing.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) **[[community-and-discord]]** Be transparent about what players receive, show odds and numbers, and provide a support channel for failed transactions.

## Numbers
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) Paid access price: **~300 Robux at early access launch, lowered to 50, then 25, then 15-25 before going free** (Emergency Response, paid access period before free-to-play)
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) Currency bundle rate: **Top bundle ~571 in-game currency per Robux vs ~214 for smallest** (Emergency Response currency shop; per-Robux rate shown)
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) Revenue spike from sales: **2-4x revenue increase at 25-30% off** (Erythia's experience running sales)
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) Currency ratio: **~1 in-game currency to 0.15 Robux** (Erythia's Creatures of Sonaria currency pricing, derived from per-minute earnings models)
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) Rotating shop cycle: **Every 6 or 12 hours; price rises with each purchase** (Creatures of Sonaria rarity-cycling shop used to sink excess currency)
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0) Matchmaking gate: **Certain XP and playtime required for matchmaking servers** (Emergency Response matchmaking and private server system)

## Games
- **Emergency Response: Liberty County** [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0): Launched paid access, then lowered price and went free after private/matchmaking servers were ready; sold unlocks early instead of XP.
- **Griffin's Destiny** [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0): Free-to-play animal roleplay; guaranteed legendary chests and a regular shop to avoid pure RNG frustration.
- **Creatures of Sonaria** [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0): Free in-game currency lets players trial Robux creatures; developer-creature shop items; rotating shop to sink economy.
- **Dragon Adventures** [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0): Launched without monetization; added it after the game was playable and enjoyable.
- **Pet Simulator X** [0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0): Pay-to-win-adjacent pet progression works because no single pet is overpowered (about 50% of combined pets).
