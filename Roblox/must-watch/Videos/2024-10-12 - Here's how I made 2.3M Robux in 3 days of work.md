# Here's how I made 2.3M Robux in 3 days of work

[Watch](https://youtu.be/zW0VdnOqxMk) | MustWatch | 1,466,993 views | 2024-10-12

## Summary
Solo dev shows two ways to earn Robux: taking commissions (building, scripting, art) and making a gimmick game. Compares his friend's gimmick game (1M visits, 1.5M Robux, about a week to build) with his own The Final Countdown (1.6M visits, 2.3M Robux, 3 days). Advises learning by reading open-source game code.

## Tips
- [0:16](https://youtu.be/zW0VdnOqxMk?t=16) **[[team-and-budget]]** Beginners without a full game can earn Robux by taking commissions (building, scripting, thumbnails) found on Roblox Talent Hub or the hidden devs Discord.
- [1:06](https://youtu.be/zW0VdnOqxMk?t=66) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Build a gimmick game built around one hook (e.g. a global countdown timer) to drive curiosity and repeat visits, rather than a standard genre game.
- [1:19](https://youtu.be/zW0VdnOqxMk?t=79) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Use a shared global timer that players can shorten by waiting, spending Robux, or completing activities, creating a reason to keep playing.
- [1:59](https://youtu.be/zW0VdnOqxMk?t=119) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Schedule a spike moment (timer nearing zero) to pull in a surge of players; the spikes faded once the game's population declined.
- [2:54](https://youtu.be/zW0VdnOqxMk?t=174) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Learn scripting by reading open-source Roblox games and working out what each script and function does; check the code is well written first.
- [2:54](https://youtu.be/zW0VdnOqxMk?t=174) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect roughly a week or two of Studio practice before doing commissions, and around two months before building a gimmick game (his estimate).
