# How I made 23M ROBUX this year as a small roblox developer (5M Visits)

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Watch](https://youtu.be/wKI3PBO3bB0) | MustWatch | 140,346 views | 2025-12-24

## Summary
Small Roblox incremental-game developer reviews his 2025 earnings (~23M Robux from owned games, all built in about a month each). Covers Omega Rune Incremental 2, Energy Incremental (underperformed on analytics), and Infinite Mining Incremental (biggest hit, sold 50% for 3.5M Robux). Mostly devlog/entertainment with few actionable steps.

## Tips
- [4:51](https://youtu.be/wKI3PBO3bB0?t=291) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Keep the number of gamepasses small (he uses about six); many gamepasses overwhelm players and reduce purchases.
- [5:36](https://youtu.be/wKI3PBO3bB0?t=336) **[[monetisation-other]]** Sell a gamepass bundle covering all current and future passes at a small 10-15% discount; it was his top earner.
- [6:19](https://youtu.be/wKI3PBO3bB0?t=379) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Sell limited-stat developer products like 'global runes' that give a stat to every online player; these sell well.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/wKI3PBO3bB0?t=0) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Make incremental games where numbers go very high; they attract an older audience and are highly profitable.

## Numbers
- [0:45](https://youtu.be/wKI3PBO3bB0?t=45) Omega Rune Incremental 2 revenue: **~2M Robux in first month (1.955M total), ~200k visits, ~800 peak CCU**
- [3:29](https://youtu.be/wKI3PBO3bB0?t=209) Omega Rune Incremental 2 ARPDAU and payer conversion: **ARPDAU above 10 Robux; payer conversion 2-5%**
- [4:51](https://youtu.be/wKI3PBO3bB0?t=291) Energy Incremental revenue: **~5.5M Robux total; ~5M from developer products, ~700k from passes**
- [7:44](https://youtu.be/wKI3PBO3bB0?t=464) Energy Incremental retention: **Day-1 retention over 15% (about 20-30% cited); day-7 retention solid**

## Games
- **Omega Rune Incremental 2** [0:45](https://youtu.be/wKI3PBO3bB0?t=45): Sequel to first big hit; ~2M Robux in a month from a small game built in about a month.
- **Energy Incremental** [4:08](https://youtu.be/wKI3PBO3bB0?t=248): Strong retention and analytics but limited monetisation and Discord-inflated CCU; underperformed versus expectations.
- **Infinite Mining Incremental** [10:13](https://youtu.be/wKI3PBO3bB0?t=613): Mining mechanic mixed with incremental genre; biggest hit, 12.6M Robux plus 50% sale for 3.5M Robux.
