# How To Make A Popular Roblox Game

[Watch](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk) | MustWatch | 491,646 views | 2025-04-28

## Summary
Generic advice video on making a popular Roblox game: build a satisfying core loop, make games replayable rather than quickly completed, play your own game, don't rely on ads to fix a weak game, track session length, retention and monetisation, add social hooks, innovate moderately, and ship fast and iterate.

## Tips
- [0:42](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=42) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build a satisfying core loop: the repeated action players do over and over, which gives them a reason to return tomorrow.
- [1:25](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=85) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Avoid games completable in one session; add replayability via leaderboards, daily challenges, modes, or varied opponents so each join feels different.
- [2:31](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=151) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Only build a game you enjoy playing yourself; you will play it thousands of times and must maintain it with updates.
- [3:45](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=225) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't spend Robux on ads to fix a weak game; ad spikes without retention burn the budget and players drop back to zero.
- [3:45](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=225) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Test a basic core loop with friends, watch what they do, and refine before adding features or spending on marketing.
- [4:50](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=290) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track session length, retention and monetisation; the speaker says Roblox's algorithm judges games on these three.
- [5:56](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=356) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** If players leave after 5 minutes, survey them on difficulty and fun to diagnose progression and core loop problems.
- [6:46](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=406) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Sell convenience, not pay-to-win: speed-ups, timed boosts, or a teleporter pass that removes repetitive actions.
- [6:46](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=406) **[[monetisation-other]]** Offer cosmetics so players can express themselves, since devoted fans will want to spend on them.
- [7:24](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=444) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make purchases feel satisfying; the first purchase is pivotal and should make players want to buy again.
- [7:49](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=469) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add social hooks such as multiplayer, trading, gifting, or chat so players bring friends, driving viral growth.
- [8:27](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=507) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Keep about 80-90% of a game familiar and change only 10-20% for novelty; avoid risks new players can't understand.
- [9:33](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=573) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Ship a testable MVP quickly, gather feedback, then update; don't spend six months perfecting an idea before release.
- [2:31](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=151) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't build purely because you think it will make money; games made for love of play perform better.
- [3:45](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=225) **[[community-and-discord]]** Grow slowly from about 10 to 50 to 100 players, building a Discord server along the way, before scaling marketing.
