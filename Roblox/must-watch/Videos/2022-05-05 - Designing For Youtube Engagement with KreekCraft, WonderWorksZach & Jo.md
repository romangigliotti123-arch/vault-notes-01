# Designing For Youtube Engagement with KreekCraft, WonderWorksZach & JoshWWS1

[Watch](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo) | MustWatch | 4,851 views | 2022-05-05

## Summary
Level Up roundtable with Creek (live streamer), WonderWorks (Zach, Josh) and Roblox's Dan on working with YouTubers and streamers. Covers how streamers pick games, what makes games watchable, live events, streamer-friendly tools, pitching influencers, Twitter/Discord hype, holiday update pacing, and collectibility for content.

## Tips
- [4:24](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=264) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Pick streamable content: check it's fun to play live and that your audience enjoys it; horror games were hit-or-miss for Creek's audience.
- [5:47](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=347) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Build streamer-friendly tools: hide HUD, free cam, streamer mode anonymity, and kick/whitelist controls for private servers so streamers can run smooth sessions.
- [35:17](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=2117) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build a Twitter presence with a hashtag like #robloxdev, tag Roblox, and move engaged followers to Discord for a 24/7 community.
- [35:57](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=2157) **[[common-mistakes]]** Ask partners to link the game in descriptions or titles; reward influencers who give credit with in-game items or early access.
- [48:16](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=2896) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** A single YouTuber pickup is often the biggest boost; one case went from 200 to 4,000 players overnight after a 100k-view Spanish video.
- [49:39](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=2979) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Run a tournament with a prize to get creators to make videos; one tournament (Traitor, made in 3 weeks) drew 5M YouTube views and 6M visits.
- [52:21](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=3141) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Use streamers as QA: their chat reports bugs in real time at scale.
- [56:26](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=3386) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Target creators whose audience matches your game's genre and demographics before reaching out.
- [1:01:35](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=3695) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Set a realistic target audience early and scope the game to it; don't expect 100k CCU from a niche audience.
- [1:02:57](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=3777) **[[common-mistakes]]** Release a product you're proud of, then iterate on feedback; avoid feature creep and endless polish before launch.
- [1:04:45](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=3885) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Contact creators about 3 months before release; give early access and let them post topic videos weeks before launch.
- [1:05:26](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=3926) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Don't pitch creators an unfinished idea; show a playable build first.
- [1:08:41](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=4121) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Smaller creators with loyal, active audiences are often better value; pay in in-game items, ranks or skins when cash budgets are tight.
- [1:14:44](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=4484) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Hype with tea over time: a few months before release, drop small teasers, escalating toward launch, to avoid overhyping and lost interest.
- [39:17](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=2357) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Add collectibles across multiple asset classes (hats, pets) so small weekly drops are easy and give creators content.
- [40:39](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=2439) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Use Easter egg hunts and battle passes between big updates to keep players and creators engaged.
- [46:51](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=2811) **[[updates-live-ops]]** For holiday events, release about 60% of the update in week one and the rest over the following weeks.
- [43:22](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=2602) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Give a creator early access for months before launch; Overlook Bay sold 1.2M paid access copies in two weeks and reached 90k CCU at free release.
- [44:43](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=2683) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Run coded/cipher puzzles on Twitter to get communities decoding for hours, tying story or skin rewards to the solve.
- [36:26](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=2186) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build an influencer program that rewards creators who link and credit your game.
- [1:16:07](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=4567) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Leaks and secret reveals create buzz; treat early leaks as viral marketing rather than fighting them.
- [25:22](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=1522) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give hostile players upgradeable abilities and give all players health (three strikes) so a single mistake isn't instant elimination.
- [26:02](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=1562) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Expect players to glitch and find unintended strategies; playtest widely before launch, as one elevator glitch let players finish in 2 minutes.
- [1:15:27](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=4527) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid over-promoting 6-8 months out; viewers lose interest and expectations rise too high.
