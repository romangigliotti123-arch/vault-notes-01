## Summary
Roblox RDC23 talk on discovery: Roblox's home-page recommendation funnel (candidate generation, ranking, final balancing), pre-warming servers for thundering-herd joins, player invite prompts, and upcoming notification APIs and search improvements. Mostly platform-side explanation with some developer-relevant signals.

## Tips
- [24:33](https://youtu.be/V1uFFUUCLpo?t=1473) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Home ranking weighs engagement, social co-play, retention, variety, monetization and international signals; design for these, not just clicks.
- [21:05](https://youtu.be/V1uFFUUCLpo?t=1265) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Home impression swings are meant to track engagement; judge changes by whether engagement stays up, not impressions alone.
- [14:17](https://youtu.be/V1uFFUUCLpo?t=857) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add player invite prompts: about a third of top 1,000 developers adopted them in the last year, and they lift engagement and retention.
- [14:58](https://youtu.be/V1uFFUUCLpo?t=898) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Build natural in-world invite moments, like a boxing game's step-on-square invite, which the speaker says improved the experience.
- [28:26](https://youtu.be/V1uFFUUCLpo?t=1706) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Enable experience-to-place access for friends if you want players to follow each other between places rather than losing them at spawn.
- [9:08](https://youtu.be/V1uFFUUCLpo?t=548) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Treat the discovery tile as a storefront: use highly visual, immersive art, since a bigger tile canvas is coming and will be tested.
- [10:38](https://youtu.be/V1uFFUUCLpo?t=638) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Expect possibly different tile creatives for new vs returning users; returning players need less info, new players need more.
- [9:08](https://youtu.be/V1uFFUUCLpo?t=548) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Supply creative metadata and descriptive info about your experience; the discovery system uses it, and semantic search infers intent from free text even without structured metadata.
- [5:28](https://youtu.be/V1uFFUUCLpo?t=328) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Analyze other games for what makes them succeed or fail before building your own, per the Animal Life Forest Roleplay creator.
- [4:47](https://youtu.be/V1uFFUUCLpo?t=287) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Quality matters: the creator got no traction with no ads, then Roblox began recommending the game after steady visits and good like ratio.
- [25:54](https://youtu.be/V1uFFUUCLpo?t=1554) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Roblox pre-warms servers for anticipated join spikes; plan for crowd joins, and note a join queue now lets players wait for space.
