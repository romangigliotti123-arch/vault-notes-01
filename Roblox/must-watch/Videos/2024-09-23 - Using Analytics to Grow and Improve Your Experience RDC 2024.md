# Using Analytics to Grow and Improve Your Experience | RDC 2024

[Watch](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM) | MustWatch | 6,960 views | 2024-09-23

## Summary
RDC 2024 panel from Roblox analytics team and three developers (Vexx/Dungeon Quest, Twin Atlas/Swordburst 3, Sorse Interactive/Walmart Discovered, Ultimate Football) on using analytics to grow engagement, monetization and acquisition. Covers funnels, benchmarks, A/B tests, personalized offers, store redesigns, thumbnails and qualified play rate.

## Tips
- [1:22](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=82) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use the Experience Overview benchmarks; focus on metrics below benchmark (session time, D1 retention, payer conversion, QPTR) first.
- [2:03](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=123) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Build an onboarding funnel and find the step with the biggest drop-off; fixing it likely lifts retention and engagement.
- [3:42](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=222) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Qualified play rate (joining and staying a few minutes or engaging) drives discovery; improve below-benchmark input metrics.
- [5:56](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=356) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Fix load times by removing unneeded images/content from the first session; Dungeon Quest cut 25% loss at join-to-play step.
- [6:37](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=397) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Add a guided tutorial for starting the core loop; Dungeon Quest had 60% drop-off before starting a dungeon and 70% failing first dungeon.
- [7:59](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=479) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Test onboarding variants: teleporting new players straight into the dungeon beat both no-onboarding and full tutorial; yielded +40% D1 retention.
- [9:42](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=582) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Follow the user journey from join to drop-off to find what to fix; Walmart Discovered found confusion causing 3.8-min session time versus benchmarks.
- [11:08](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=668) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make the spawn route guide players to core loop mechanics and make minigame entry obvious; this drove ~110% session time increase.
- [13:17](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=797) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Let players collect rewards inside the progression area; Swordburst 3 floor-reward staircase drop-off smoothed after rewards became collectable in-tower.
- [14:45](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=885) **[[analytics-metrics]]** If payer conversion or ARPPU is below benchmark, check the economy: if sources exceed sinks and wallets keep rising, add items or improve shop UX.
- [15:26](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=926) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Launch themed items and first-time purchase bonuses with major events and updates to ride the hype.
- [16:08](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=968) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Use personalized, time-limited offers shown before the first, second and third dungeon; Dungeon Quest got +180% payer conversion on day one.
- [17:08](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=1028) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Redesign the store: scrolling layout, quick-access navigation, rotating offers, daily reward; Dungeon Quest store revamp gave +20% ARPU.
- [18:08](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=1088) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** For events, make them accessible to all levels and add one-time first-purchase bonus per Premium currency tier; drove purchases and 50% lift for two weeks.
- [19:30](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=1170) **[[monetisation-other]]** Track player wallets during events; avoid oversaturating players with currency, and aim for healthy sink-back of generated currency.
- [21:01](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=1261) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Test thumbnails and icons separately; a strong thumbnail with a weak icon loses returning players, so optimize both.
- [22:23](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=1343) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Simplify thumbnails with a single clear focal point; Swordburst 3 used popular pop-culture subject (giant worms) and saw big CTR and recommendation uplift.
- [22:45](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=1365) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Test creatives continuously; Ultimate Football's best of six thumbnails converted 2.5x the weakest, which brought more impressions.
- [25:05](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=1505) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Pair a big update with a large ad spend to boost early algorithm pool prioritization and recommendations.
- [26:44](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=1604) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Prioritize engagement, then monetization, then acquisition; retention is the base since acquisition without it is a leaky bucket.
- [30:11](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=1811) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Push below-benchmark KPIs above the 50th percentile; that move drives the most extra surfacing.
- [30:53](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=1853) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Prioritize CTR; poor icons or thumbnails can cost up to 4-5x recommended players even with a good game.
- [34:20](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=2060) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Change thumbnails with updates, monitor closely, revert if they dip, and don't update more than once a week.
- [35:42](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM?t=2142) **[[monetisation-other]]** For low-paying regions, localize the game for target demographics and consider premium payouts instead of only changing prices.

## Games
- **Dungeon Quest (Vexx)**: Fixed load times, added guided tutorial, teleport-in onboarding, personalized offers and store revamp; major D1 retention and conversion gains.
- **Walmart Discovered (Sorse Interactive)**: Opened spawn area and guided players to core loop and minigames after following user journey; session time up ~110%.
- **Swordburst 3 (Twin Atlas)**: Let players collect Void Tower rewards inside tower to fix staircase drop-off; 75% of DAU at max level shaped content priorities.
- **Ultimate Football (Vexx)**: Testing six thumbnails found a 2.5x conversion gap; better thumbnails increased impressions.
- **World Zero (Twin Atlas)**: Dragon boss event with first-purchase bonuses per Premium tier and cosmetics boosted revenue; wallets monitored.
- **Driving Empire (Vexx)**: Store revamp produced a strong ARPU uplift about a year earlier.
