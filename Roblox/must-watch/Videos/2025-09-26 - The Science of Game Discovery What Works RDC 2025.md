# The Science of Game Discovery: What Works | RDC 2025

[Watch](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU) | MustWatch | 7,969 views | 2025-09-26

## Summary
RDC 2025 talk by Roblox's Seb Barios and data scientist Sway on how Roblox discovery works: six ranking signals (qualified playthrough rate, 7-day playtime, Robux spend, play days, co-play days, spend days), ad-driven growth, thumbnail personalization, video previews, and case studies of 99 Nights in the Forest and Ninja Challenge, plus Q&A.

## Tips
- [5:03](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=303) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Track the six discovery signals in creator analytics: qualified playthrough rate, 7-day playtime, Robux spend, play days, spend days, and co-play days; these drive home recommendations.
- [9:49](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=589) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Add video previews to your game page; Roblox reported higher playthrough rate, playtime and spend in experiments because players see the game loop before clicking.
- [6:49](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=409) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Start with a small ad budget to get first players, fix UX and bugs from their behaviour, then scale spend once stats rise and retention holds.
- [6:49](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=409) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use ads to find players who will actually stay and enjoy the game, not just click; retention of ad-acquired users is what triggers algorithmic recommendation.
- [13:54](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=834) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Upload several thumbnails with thumbnail personalization on; the system tests them per segment and picks winners in about 48 hours. Make thumbnails that authentically show gameplay.
- [15:17](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=917) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship regular meaningful updates and events; 99 Nights' D7 retention almost tripled after events, a strong signal to the algorithm.
- [16:08](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=968) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build a genuinely fun game first; Roblox says the top reason players return is that the game is fun, and discovery amplifies fun rather than replacing it.
- [18:15](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=1095) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Run monthly updates with new challenges and a fresh thumbnail each time to sustain daily active users after launch buzz fades.
- [19:37](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=1177) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Target ad campaigns at former players to remind them of the fun; this brought Ninja Challenge back into the home recommendation consideration set.
- [24:34](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=1474) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Running ads does not block organic discovery per Roblox; they cite 370 advertised experiences where 99% saw total impression lift.
- [30:05](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=1805) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Do not chase monetisation as a ranking shortcut; Roblox says spend is one signal among several and fun, long-term retention matters most.
- [32:10](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=1930) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build an in-game feedback channel and read it constantly to find pain points like difficulty or wait times, then target fixes there.
- [32:51](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=1971) **[[team-and-budget]]** Run updates with a small group chat and fixed deadlines per task; commission art such as thumbnails early enough to finish before the update.
- [35:36](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=2136) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Do not optimise the six metrics individually; build a fun game and monitor them. Qualified playthrough rate was the best growth metric for Ninja Challenge.
- [32:10](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=1930) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Skip paying YouTubers or TikTok influencers early; Ninja Challenge did almost no social marketing and a fun game attracted creators organically.
- [9:49](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=589) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make the thumbnail, title and description convey what is fun about the game; players decide mostly from these before clicking.

## Games
- **99 Nights in the Forest** [10:28](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=628): Launched June 2025; ads found engaged players, thumbnail personalization and frequent events drove recommendations and D7 retention.
- **Ninja Challenge** [18:15](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=1095): Monthly challenge updates with new thumbnails; ads to former players revived home recommendations, and it grew without influencer marketing.
