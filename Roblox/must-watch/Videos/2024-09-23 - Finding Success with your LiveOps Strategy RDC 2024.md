# Finding Success with your LiveOps Strategy | RDC 2024

[Watch](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I) | MustWatch | 11,633 views | 2024-09-23

## Summary
Tower Defense Simulator team (Paradox Games) at RDC 2024 explains live operations: feedback loops, KPI/analytics use (native and third-party), designing content/feature/patch updates, seasonal events with timed missions, roadmap rules, testing, teasers, and retrospectives.

## Tips
- [5:25](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=325) **[[community-and-discord]]** Set up feedback channels (Discord, Reddit, YouTube, Guilded) and actively monitor them for positive and negative patterns, not just create them.
- [5:25](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=325) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use a project management board (Asana, Monday, Trello, Jira) to prioritise and track player feedback so live-ops doesn't get chaotic.
- [7:40](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=460) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use Roblox's native analytics: retention, engagement, acquisition, revenue, plus economy events (top sinks, average wallet balance) and funnel events for onboarding drop-off.
- [8:22](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=502) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Build tutorial funnels to find drop-off steps; TDS saw 5% drop at tutorial entry, 24% at the 'Demo Man selected' step, and 72% completion.
- [10:25](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=625) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track win/loss ratio per wave/difficulty: keep it low for hard modes and around 3-4 for easy modes to tune difficulty.
- [11:06](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=666) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Generate heat maps from in-game placement events to see where players build and rebalance or redesign maps accordingly.
- [12:47](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=767) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Start updates from data: pick a problem (TDS's new-player retention was low), prioritise it, then set KPI objectives before designing.
- [14:51](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=891) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Classify updates as content (uses existing systems), feature (changes core loop), or patch (fixes feedback/bugs); split big fixes across types.
- [16:14](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=974) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Show a discounted starter pack bundle right after the tutorial completes to lift paying-user conversion.
- [16:56](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=1016) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Seasonal live events give a large temporary retention and revenue boost and can reactivate lapsed players; story-driven events with limited items worked well.
- [18:05](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=1085) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Give limited-time UGC rewards to the first N players who finish an event to drive urgency and community buzz.
- [19:50](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=1190) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan the roadmap in quarter-to-6-month chunks, not a full year, and embrace iteration as player data changes.
- [20:30](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=1230) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Space content, feature and patch releases apart so users keep returning and small risky features don't collide with big launches.
- [21:12](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=1272) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Balance player feedback with the original vision; it's fine to say no to feedback to avoid a flood of formulaic copies.
- [22:31](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=1351) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Test every update, even with friends or colleagues, and use community betas or paid testing groups for larger updates.
- [23:14](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=1394) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use per-developer staging environments, merge features into a release branch, and deploy to a replica test server before launch.
- [24:36](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=1476) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Post small teasers (renders, short clips) about 3-7 days before launch; avoid revealing too much.
- [25:17](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=1517) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Hold a retrospective after every update covering what went well, what went wrong, blockers and fixes, then monitor the KPI goals set for the feature.
- [29:23](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=1763) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Lock event content behind in-game timers (e.g., statues teleporting players into the event server) so updates ship without shutting down servers.
- [34:55](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I?t=2095) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Stretch event value by releasing content in timed chunks (Mission every 42 hours) and pairing it with a month-long battle pass.
