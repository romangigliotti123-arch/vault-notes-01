## Summary
RDC 2024 session on expanding Roblox experiences internationally. Covers where non-US/Canada growth comes from, four under-served genres (open world action, sports, driving/racing, social co-op), Japan and Brazil profiles, and platform tools (experience price optimization, regional pricing, automatic translation, text chat service, localized Studio). Panel of Clipit and Carta shares localization anecdotes.

## Tips
- [1:41](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=101) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Target unmet international demand: open world action, localized sports, driving/racing, and social co-op genres all show search demand outside US/Canada.
- [4:52](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=292) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Localize popular genres with local teams, events, or culture (e.g. local football clubs) to drive growth that can go viral on and off Roblox.
- [8:50](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=530) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** For Japan, localize heavily; English is less prevalent, and local content and culture shape top charts. Consider train/train-simulation and arcade sports like volleyball and soccer.
- [10:52](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=652) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Optimize for low-end Android devices: Android dominates most markets outside Japan. Keep a budget Android device to test updates.
- [11:45](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=705) **[[pricing]]** Prepare for experience price optimization and future regional pricing; set different virtual item price points per market rather than one global price.
- [13:07](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=787) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Use the text chat service API for real-time automatic translation of dynamically loaded text that scraping-based translation cannot handle.
- [14:29](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=869) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Check your users' languages, geographies and device types first, then decide which markets to expand into.
- [14:29](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=869) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Partner with brands, influencers or IPs that resonate with a target region once you see traction there.
- [15:11](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=911) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Insert local flavor (art, music, regional references, offline events) so the experience feels native to each market, not just translated.
- [22:33](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=1353) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Translating text is the baseline; cultural details (landscapes, references, festivals) are what make players feel the game was made for them.
- [27:19](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=1639) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Give the team a budget for low-end Android and iPhones and test on-device; hide models instead of reparenting them for load performance (Clipit tip).
- [31:00](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=1860) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Regional targeting is limited to country level in ads; for sub-country targeting, partner with creators whose audience matches the region.
- [33:03](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=1983) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Out-of-region players can still create word of mouth that feeds back into the target region through social graphs.
- [34:27](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=2067) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Console users tend to be more retentive, monetize better and are older; optimize for console and desktop to reach a complementary cohort.
- [35:49](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=2149) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Keep global appeal by staying authentic to the IP rather than pandering; humor tends to translate across cultures.
- [16:20](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=980) **[[community-and-discord]]** Local flavor for Brand and regional experiences can be gated by access codes (e.g. a 4-digit code) to restrict audiences.
