## Summary
Roblox docs page on regional content availability. Explains that content may be restricted in specific countries due to local legal requirements: games become undiscoverable and unplayable there, and avatar items become undiscoverable and taken off-sale there. Status elsewhere is unchanged, and restrictions can change over time.
