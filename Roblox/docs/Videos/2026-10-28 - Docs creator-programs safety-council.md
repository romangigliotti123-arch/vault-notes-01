## Summary
Roblox Creator Programs page describing the Community Safety Council: a 6-month, invite-based group of trusted creators who meet monthly with the Safety Group to raise community safety concerns. Lists eligibility (18+, 2 years on platform, 1 hour monthly, 6-month commitment) and FAQ. Applications currently closed.
