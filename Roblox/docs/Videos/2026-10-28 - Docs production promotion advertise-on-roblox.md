# Docs: production / promotion / advertise-on-roblox

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise-on-roblox.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official docs on advertising. Lists Roblox-served ad products (Immersive ads, Search ads, Sponsored games, Sponsored items) with what each promotes and where it displays. Explains independent advertising, questions to decide whether content is advertising, and that violations can lead to game/account suspension.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise-on-roblox) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Pick the ad product by content type: Immersive ads for games/videos/images, Search ads for game tiles by keyword, Sponsored games for 9+/13+/all-ages games, Sponsored items for UGC.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise-on-roblox) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Roblox-served ads let you choose audience, schedule duration, and set a daily budget.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise-on-roblox) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** If your in-game content promotes an off-Roblox product or service, treat it as independent advertising and follow the Independent Advertising Policy.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise-on-roblox) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Independent ads must be hidden from users ineligible to receive ads and clearly disclosed as advertising.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise-on-roblox) **[[common-mistakes]]** Violating advertising standards can get your game or account suspended; check content against the questions in the docs before publishing.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise-on-roblox) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** For brand or sponsor deals, consult regulatory guidance and seek legal counsel when unsure whether content counts as advertising.
