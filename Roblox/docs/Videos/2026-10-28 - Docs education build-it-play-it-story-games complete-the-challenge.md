## Summary
Roblox Creator Challenge wrap-up page. Explains how to make a private game public (Experience Settings > Permissions > Public), share via Copy Link, and suggests extending the story by adding characters (new variables like name2/name3) and new lines by replacing objects.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-story-games/complete-the-challenge) **[[community-and-discord]]** Make your game Public (File > Experience Settings > Permissions > Public, then Save) so friends and others can play it; private games are only visible to you.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-story-games/complete-the-challenge) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Share via Studio's Share button > Copy Link to send the game to friends.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-story-games/complete-the-challenge) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Extend a finished prototype incrementally by adding content, such as new characters or lines, to keep learning.
