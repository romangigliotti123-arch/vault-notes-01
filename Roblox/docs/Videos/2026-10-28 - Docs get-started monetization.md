# Docs: get-started / monetization

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official docs overview of monetization options: DevEx cash-out threshold (30,000 earned Robux), Dev Products vs Game Passes, 70% Robux revenue share, subscriptions, Creator Rewards, Creator Store and UGC avatar item earnings, immersive ads, brand integrations, physical merchandising, and automated/regional pricing tools.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Use Dev Products for repeatable purchases (boosts, consumables) since they can be bought many times and generally monetize better than one-time passes.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Use Game Passes for one-time purchases such as VIP passes, content bundles, or battle passes; they can only be bought once.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** For brand integrations, choose partners matching your game's vibe, aesthetics and goals; limited activations usually run 2-4 weeks.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Weave brand content into the core loop so it feels like a natural extension of gameplay, enhancing progression.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization) **[[pricing]]** Use Price Optimization to auto-test price points on user subsets and find the revenue-maximizing price.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization) **[[pricing]]** Use Regional Pricing to auto-set local item prices; early tests showed spending gains in Mexico, Brazil and the Philippines.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Reach the 30,000 earned Robux minimum before submitting a DevEx cash-out request.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization) **[[monetisation-other]]** Sell UGC avatar items in your own game to keep 70% of Robux spent (30% creator + 40% seller) versus 30% when sold on the Avatar Marketplace.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization) DevEx minimum: **30,000 earned Robux** (Required before submitting a cash-out request)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization) In-app purchase revenue share: **70% to creator, 30% marketplace fee** (Applies to Robux spent on Dev Products and Game Passes)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization) Daily engagement payout: **5 Robux** (Per qualifying user (spent $9.99+ in last 60 days) playing 10+ minutes; first 3 games/day)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization) Audience expansion revenue share: **35% on first $100 Robux purchases** (New/returning users arriving via link or search who play 10+ minutes)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization) UGC avatar item split: **30% creator, 40% seller, 30% market fee** (Avatar Marketplace sales; 70% when sold in your own game)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization) Brand activation length: **2-4 weeks** (Typical limited-time brand activation duration)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization) Subscription pricing: **Monthly recurring, local currency, auto-renew** (Players get in-game benefits for recurring monthly fee)
