# Docs: marketplace / marketplace-policy

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs on Marketplace policy for avatar assets. Covers creator requirements (ID verification, Roblox Plus/Premium 1000/2200 membership, upload and publishing fees), general creation guidelines (community standards, no Roblox IP, no overly similar or limited re-publishes, no obscuring avatars/UI), categorization rules, and avatar body rules (required parts, caged heads, FACS controls, modesty layers, no accessories/tattoos/makeup on bodies).

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Verify government ID (or link a verified parental account) before uploading or publishing; 2-step verification is also required to publish.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Keep Roblox Plus or Premium 1000/2200 active; required to publish and to keep existing items on-sale.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Budget 80 Robux per upload from Studio or Creator Dashboard, and 10-2500 Robux publishing advance when publishing to Marketplace.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not re-publish an existing item as Limited, or re-publish a Limited item; overly similar items violate policy.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not sell items that obscure the avatar, in-game UI, other users' avatars or view, or make avatar parts disappear.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not miscategorize items (e.g. pants listed as Shirt); complete hairstyles go in Hair, hats in Hat, Tops/Bottoms must match garment type.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Ensure accessory attachment points sit on the visible surface of the head mesh; floating attachments violate policy.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Heads must be caged, with one mouth region, one or two eye regions, and at least the 17 minimum FACS controls including happiness and sadness deformation.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Avatar bodies cannot include accessories, clothing, tattoos or makeup; upload and sell these separately.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Human-like avatars with smooth skin-like textures in chest or groin need opaque modesty layers in a color different from skin tone; upper torso layer must cover breasts.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Textures (repeating patterns covering at least 50% of the avatar) are allowed; tattoos (distinct drawings differing in color) are not on bodies.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Face makeup beyond shading (facial painting, multicolor eyeliner or lips) must be sold as separate items, not baked into the head.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Offer customizable skin tones for human-like avatars; recommended though optional.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Avoid sexual, political, religious, gory, violent, drug or self-harm themed items, and items likely used for such roleplay; avoid excessive text.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Only sell items you have rights to; get IP owner permission for licensed or inspired content, and never use Roblox branding or assets.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not create items that depend on Roblox platform glitches or issues.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) Upload fee: **80 Robux** (Upload from Studio or Creator Dashboard)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) Publishing advance: **10-2500 Robux** (Required to publish content to the Marketplace)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) Membership requirement: **Roblox Plus or Premium 1000/2200** (Needed to publish and keep existing content on-sale)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) Minimum FACS controls: **17** (Required on all avatar heads; more poses recommended)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) Body parts: **1 each: left/right arm, torso, left/right leg** (Avatar bodies allow no additional appendages)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy) Texture coverage threshold: **at least 50% of avatar** (Repeating pattern needed to count as texture, not tattoo)
