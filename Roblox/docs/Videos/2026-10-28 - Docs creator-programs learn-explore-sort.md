# Docs: creator-programs / learn-explore-sort

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/learn-explore-sort.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox creator-programs page describing the Learn & Explore sort: an education-focused discovery recognition for games that teach subjects like science, history, or math. Lists eligibility criteria (educational value, non-aggressive monetization, classroom-appropriate, like ratio above 50%, at least 15 CCU) and states applications are reviewed every 1-2 months by the Education team.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/learn-explore-sort) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Apply for the Learn & Explore sort to get recognition as an educational developer and increased discovery as an educational game.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/learn-explore-sort) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Avoid aggressive monetization in the first-time user experience; monetization must not block large portions of content or grant unfair gameplay advantages.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/learn-explore-sort) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Keep content appropriate for a classroom of students of all ages, even when subject matter is advanced.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/learn-explore-sort) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Maintain a like ratio above 50% and at least 15 concurrent users to be eligible.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/learn-explore-sort) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Make the educational value clear to an educator, since eligibility depends on it being evident without explanation.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/learn-explore-sort) **[[community-and-discord]]** Expect applications to be reviewed every 1-2 months; only applicants considered for the sort will be contacted, so do not expect follow-up otherwise.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/learn-explore-sort) Like ratio: **above 50%** (Eligibility threshold for Learn & Explore sort)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/learn-explore-sort) Concurrent users: **at least 15** (Minimum CCU for Learn & Explore sort eligibility)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/learn-explore-sort) Review cadence: **every 1-2 months** (Education team reviews Learn & Explore applications)
