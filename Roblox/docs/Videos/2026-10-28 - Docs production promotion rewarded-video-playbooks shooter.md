# Docs: production / promotion / rewarded-video-playbooks / shooter

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/shooter.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official playbook on using Rewarded Video ads in shooter games: where to place them (natural breaks, never mid-fight), four strategies (pre-match boost, armory currency, weapon trial, post-match payout boost), and key metrics (opt-in rate, daily frequency).

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/shooter) **[[monetisation-other]]** Place rewarded video only at natural breaks: lobby, death screen, results screen. Never prompt mid-fight; if unavoidable, pause damage or keep prompt in a safe zone.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/shooter) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use pre-match boosts (2X XP, credits, keys) that speed progression without touching damage, health, or aim so lobbies stay fair.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/shooter) **[[monetisation-other]]** Offer a free currency option in the armory/shop alongside paid bundles; ads earn small amounts toward weapons and skins.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/shooter) **[[monetisation-other]]** Offer timed weapon/skin trials for one match via ad; trial expires before it can replace a purchase.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/shooter) **[[monetisation-other]]** Offer post-match option to double coins or XP just earned by watching a short ad; players are invested win or lose.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/shooter) **[[monetisation-other]]** Keep rewards fixed and fair: valuable enough to motivate watching, but not tied to your highest-revenue item, so ads supplement rather than replace monetization.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/shooter) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make the prompt prominent, reachable in one or two clicks, and state exactly what the player gets.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/shooter) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Tag each rewarded video surface with a Placement ID to see which placement drives the most views.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/shooter) Opt-in rate target: **10%+ of total eligible users engage with the ad**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/shooter) Daily frequency target: **3+ views per user per day**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/shooter) Ad length: **6-30 seconds, full-screen horizontal**

## Games
- **Rivals**: Uses Weapon Trial rewarded video strategy as a best-in-class example.
- **Hypershot**: Uses Pre-Match Boost rewarded video strategy as a best-in-class example.
