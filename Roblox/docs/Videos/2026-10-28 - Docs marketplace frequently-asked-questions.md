# Docs: marketplace / frequently-asked-questions

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs FAQ on Marketplace selling eligibility for 3D avatar items: ID verification plus Roblox Plus/Premium, publishing advance, per-unit fees for free Limited items, 30% minimum creator earnings, and IP/DMCA/DSA reporting channels.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions) **[[monetisation-other]]** To sell in Marketplace you need ID verification, Roblox Plus or Premium 1000/2200, and compliance with Community Standards and Marketplace Policies.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Creators earn at least 30% of gross item sales; the rest is shared with the platform and the game developer if sold in a game.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Publishing any non-free item requires a rebatable publishing advance paid upfront per item; free Limited items are exempt but charged per unit.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** You can publish any number of non-Limited items but only 1 Limited item per day; there is no upload cap from Studio.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Restock free Limited items from the configure page; restocking is only allowed if the item has never been made resellable.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Roblox may temporarily or permanently revoke creation access for policy violations; appeals go through the appeals page.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Use Rights Manager to file IP infringement removal requests; DMCA requests only get processed if you are the rights holder or authorized representative.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** For non-IP violations of Community Standards on avatar items, use Report Abuse on the item's catalog page or submit a support ticket; EU residents use the Illegal Content Reporting Form (DSA).
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** You can request archiving of your own uploaded items to take down infringing material yourself.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions) Roblox Premium tiers required: **Roblox Plus or Premium 1000 or 2200** (Eligibility to upload and sell in Marketplace)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions) Minimum creator share of gross item sales: **30%** (Remainder shared with platform and game developer if sold in a game)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions) Per-unit fee for free Limited items: **100 Robux** (Charged per unit created; dynamic and may change)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions) Limited item publish rate: **1 per day** (Non-Limited items have no publish limit)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/frequently-asked-questions) Publishing advance changes frequency: **2-3 times per quarter at most** (Roblox expects advance to change at most this often)
