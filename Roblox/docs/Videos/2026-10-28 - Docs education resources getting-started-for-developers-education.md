# Docs: education / resources / getting-started-for-developers-education

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/getting-started-for-developers-education.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Official Roblox docs page for developers interested in educational games. Covers education on Roblox with example games, Roblox Studio as the dev engine, design guides for educational games, funding and opportunity programs (Community Fund, Open Office Hours, Learn and Explore sort), and FAQs on contracting and monetization in schools.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/getting-started-for-developers-education) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Look at the Inspiration Guide and live educational games for topics and example mechanics before designing an educational game.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/getting-started-for-developers-education) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Integrate gameplay with learning objectives rather than bolting quizzes onto a game; see the Designing Educational Games article.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/getting-started-for-developers-education) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan for classroom use: review classroom structures, user needs, and student progress assessment in the Planning for Educational Settings article.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/getting-started-for-developers-education) **[[monetisation-other]]** Monetization is prohibited during school hours; for schools, consider offering teacher training and lesson resources outside Roblox instead.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/getting-started-for-developers-education) **[[monetisation-other]]** Edutainment games for informal learning can use traditional Roblox monetization.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/getting-started-for-developers-education) **[[studio-business-funding-and-publishing]]** Apply to the Roblox Community Fund for investments, grants, or advances if building curriculum or educational games that leverage the platform.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/getting-started-for-developers-education) **[[studio-business-funding-and-publishing]]** Book free one-on-one Education Open Office Hours with the Roblox Education team to assess educational value and development issues.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/getting-started-for-developers-education) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Apply for the curated Learn and Explore sort to get more traffic and visibility for educational games.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/getting-started-for-developers-education) **[[team-and-budget]]** If you need developers, coders, or creators, find them via the Talent Hub; Roblox does not develop games itself.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/getting-started-for-developers-education) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Build custom Studio templates for lessons such as computer science or building if teaching Roblox Studio.

## Games
- **Mission: Mars**: Rover engineering challenge on Martian terrain designed by Boston Museum of Science; fosters STEM and design thinking.
- **Lua Learning**: Interactive Luau and computational thinking courses, plus user-created lessons on advanced topics like network security.
- **Untamed Animals**: Park ranger and animal roleplay in an ecosystem that builds empathy for wildlife.
