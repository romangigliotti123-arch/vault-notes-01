# Docs: production / analytics / acquisition

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox Creator Docs page on the Acquisition analytics dashboard: where new/returning users come from (Home, Continue Play, Search, Charts, sponsored/search/portal ads, teleport, share links), attribution metrics (D7 retention, 7D playtime, 30D payer conversion, 30D revenue per user), and how to improve acquisition via retention, engagement, monetization and metadata.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Open Creator Dashboard > your game > Analytics > Acquisition to see new vs returning users by source and the cumulative new-user funnel.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Home is where most users find games; raise session time and Day 1 retention to match or beat similar-game benchmarks, since the algorithm weighs them.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Games with high Day 1 retention and high average session time are more likely to be featured in Recommended Experiences.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) **[[monetisation-other]]** Get payer conversion rate and ARPPU comparable to or above similar-game benchmarks; the algorithm reads these as investment signals.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Enable nothing that excludes you from recommendations: if Exclude from Recommendations is on, Home recommendation traffic drops to zero.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Use a title, description and icon that are true and unique to your game to raise play-through rate from Roblox sources.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Add images and videos to your game detail page to engage users and improve conversion from impressions to plays.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Create social accounts (Twitter, YouTube, Instagram), post regular updates and teasers, and link them on your Game Detail Page.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build a user community and engage it by hosting events and replying to feedback, suggestions and bug reports.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Partner with creators making similar games and reach out to YouTube/Twitch influencers for use or review.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Create share links per channel and compare Users with qualified plays, 7D playtime, D7 retention and 30D revenue per user in the New users funnel table.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Only run acquisition campaigns or event announcements once retention and engagement are good, since paid traffic is wasted on weak retention.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Run an acquisition funnel to find sources that drive the most new users and sources with the best end-to-end conversion rate.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) D7 retention definition: **Share of new players acquired in range who played again on D8 after first session ended**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) 7D playtime per user: **Cumulative first-7-day playtime of new players divided by new players acquired**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) 30D payer conversion: **Paying users among new players acquired, within first 30 days**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition) 30D revenue per user: **Revenue excl. subscriptions, engagement payouts, immersive ads, per new player over first 30 days**
