## Summary
Roblox docs page on the Roblox Brand Link creator program: an invite-only, Roblox-managed program connecting creators with brands for paid in-game integrations. Lists eligibility signals (500k+ MAU game, responsiveness, organisation, willingness to partner) and post-selection duties (custom deck, brand conversations, co-produced integrations).

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/managed-brand-integrations) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Brand Link is invite-only; ideally own a game with 500k+ MAU, respond promptly, stay organised, and be willing to partner closely with brands.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/managed-brand-integrations) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** If selected, work with program staff to build a custom deck describing your game for brand integration opportunities.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/managed-brand-integrations) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Expect to evaluate brand opportunities, hold exploration conversations with brands, and co-produce integration projects.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/managed-brand-integrations) Suggested MAU for Brand Link consideration: **500k+ MAU game** (Ideal eligibility guideline for invitation to Roblox Brand Link)
