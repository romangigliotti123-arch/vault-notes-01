## Summary
Roblox Education docs page on creating engaging experiences: stresses UX for first impressions, then sets up a starter Boardwalk template with ProximityPrompt ticket booths and walks through building a guided tutorial path using Beams and Attachments (PlayerAttachment, GoalAttachment, TutorialBeam).

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-mansion-of-wonder/creating-engaging-experiences) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Design the first session so new players can figure out where to go and how to reach goals without frustration; overwhelming first impressions cause players to quit.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-mansion-of-wonder/creating-engaging-experiences) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Avoid dumping too much information up front; players struggle to remember large amounts of info presented at game start.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-mansion-of-wonder/creating-engaging-experiences) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Guide new players with in-world cues, such as a visible beam path leading to the first objective, rather than text-heavy instructions.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-mansion-of-wonder/creating-engaging-experiences) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** When using your own project, base the tutorial on your game's main player actions (e.g. collecting wheat and selling at a shop booth in a farming game).
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-mansion-of-wonder/creating-engaging-experiences) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use Beams with Attachments on the player and goal objects to draw a visible guiding path; Beams also suit lasers or force fields.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-mansion-of-wonder/creating-engaging-experiences) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Import the starter objects into your own project and then replace premade models with your own, renaming script variable references as needed.
