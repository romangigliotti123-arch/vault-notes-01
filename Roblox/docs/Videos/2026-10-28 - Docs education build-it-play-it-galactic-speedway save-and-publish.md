## Summary
Roblox Education tutorial page (Build It, Play It: Galactic Speedway) on saving and publishing a place in Studio. Covers publishing via File > Publish, naming the place, creating it as private, and re-saving with Alt+P or Cmd+P. Recommends publishing every ten minutes.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/save-and-publish) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Publish your game every ten minutes while working, or after any big change, so progress is saved and reachable from any computer.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/save-and-publish) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Keep a new place private until it is ready, then switch it to public for players.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/save-and-publish) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** After the first publish, re-save with File > Publish or the hotkey Alt+P (Windows) or Cmd+P (Mac).

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/save-and-publish) Recommended publish interval: **every 10 minutes** (Official tutorial advice for saving work while building in Studio)
