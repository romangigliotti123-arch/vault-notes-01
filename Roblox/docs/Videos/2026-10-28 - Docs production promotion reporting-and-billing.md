## Summary
Roblox Creator Docs page on Ads Manager reporting and billing (beta). Defines campaign, ad set and ad reporting columns, impression criteria, delivery statuses, card vs ad-credit billing thresholds and limits, and ad fraud refund timing.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/reporting-and-billing) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Read ad performance at three levels: campaign for budget allocation, ad set for targeting and budget effectiveness, individual ad for creative impact.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/reporting-and-billing) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track CPP (total cost divided by plays) alongside CTR and play rate; play rate is plays divided by impressions.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/reporting-and-billing) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use 7D playtime and 30D Robux earnings per converted player to judge ad traffic quality beyond raw clicks.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/reporting-and-billing) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Card payments are charged when the $100 threshold is reached, otherwise at month end; daily ad spend cap on cards is $300.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/reporting-and-billing) Card payment threshold: **$100 USD**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/reporting-and-billing) Card ad spend daily limit: **$300 USD per day**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/reporting-and-billing) Image impression criteria: **viewed at least 1 second, occupies 1.5% of viewport, angle up to 55 degrees, at least 50% pixels visible**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/reporting-and-billing) Video impression criteria: **viewed at least 0.5 seconds, occupies 1.5% of viewport, angle up to 55 degrees, at least 50% pixels visible**
