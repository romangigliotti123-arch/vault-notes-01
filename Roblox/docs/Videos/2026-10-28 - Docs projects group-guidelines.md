# Docs: projects / group-guidelines

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/group-guidelines.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs on group guidelines: content in groups must follow Community Standards and Terms of Use; owners must moderate and use audit logs; violations lead to enforcement, with ownership restrictions after repeated failures or permanent removals (90-day wait after one removal, loss of ownership after multiple).

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/group-guidelines) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Ensure all group-uploaded assets, avatars and games follow Roblox Community Standards and Terms of Use; these apply to groups too.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/group-guidelines) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** As group owner, make reasonable effort to address harmful behavior and proactively moderate using group audit logs.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/group-guidelines) **[[community-and-discord]]** Monitor group member activity and address member violations promptly; repeated failure to act can restrict future group ownership.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/group-guidelines) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Do not use your group to promote or upload violative content; this can lead to enforcement on your account and permanent group deletion.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/group-guidelines) Ownership block after permanent group removal: **90 days**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/group-guidelines) Ownership privileges after multiple permanent removals: **Complete loss**

## Tools
