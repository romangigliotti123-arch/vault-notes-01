## Summary
Roblox Education docs page on the "Build it, Play it, Create and Destroy" city-building experience. Covers placing medium buildings (10 points each on smash) evenly around each SpawnLocation, adding roads as needed, and finishing with non-scoring props like trees and cars for realism.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-create-and-destroy/buildings-and-props) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Place medium buildings evenly around each SpawnLocation so each spawn has a similar amount of scoring targets nearby.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-create-and-destroy/buildings-and-props) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Place 8 to 10 medium buildings, adding roads as needed to connect them.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-create-and-destroy/buildings-and-props) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Build roads first, then buildings, then props last; props add realism and don't affect scoring.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-create-and-destroy/buildings-and-props) points per medium building smashed: **10** (Points awarded to players for smashing a medium building in the tutorial city)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-create-and-destroy/buildings-and-props) medium buildings to place: **8-10** (Recommended count for the city layout in this tutorial step)
