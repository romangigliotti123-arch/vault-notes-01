## Summary
Roblox education landing page for the Story Games project (Hour of Code). Walks learners through installing Roblox Studio and completing three coding lessons (variables, player answers, telling the story), each followed by a quiz game for prizes. Includes account safety tips.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-story-games/landing) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Never share your Roblox password, even with friends. Make it hard to guess; don't base it on your username.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-story-games/landing) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Roblox employees will never ask for your password. Report anyone who asks using Report Abuse.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-story-games/landing) **[[monetisation-other]]** Ignore anyone offering free Robux or secret methods to get it. Such offers are scams.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-story-games/landing) Lessons in project: **3** (Creating Variables, Getting Player Answers, Telling the Story)

## Games
- **Roblox Story Games (education project)**: Learners fill in missing story words by coding a story game; each lesson unlocks a quiz-game prize.
