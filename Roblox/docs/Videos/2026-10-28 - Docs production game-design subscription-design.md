# Docs: production / game-design / subscription-design

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/subscription-design.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs page on subscription design: core principles (value, trust), KPIs (engagement, retention, monetization), best practices, and use cases (bundles incl. currency and item packs, membership/VIP benefits, season passes), with a currency-pack pricing procedure.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/subscription-design) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make subscription value clear and obvious: highlight exclusivity, savings, or unique benefits so players recognize worth immediately on subscribing.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/subscription-design) **[[monetisation-other]]** Deliver promised subscription content on time and consistently; reliability builds trust, which is a prerequisite for long subscriber retention.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/subscription-design) **[[monetisation-other]]** Design subscription content to be informative, diverse, progressive and engaging, matching each stage of player progression from beginner to veteran.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/subscription-design) **[[monetisation-other]]** Fill item packs with intentionally designed items players will anticipate each month, not random items; use a content cadence to keep interest.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/subscription-design) **[[monetisation-other]]** Be intentional with durable items in subscription packs; overrelying on them forces constant design of new permanent items.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/subscription-design) **[[pricing]]** When pricing currency packs, compare the new pack's contents to existing single-purchase Robux offerings to keep relative value consistent.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/subscription-design) **[[monetisation-other]]** Choose subscription structure by economy: few items means steady demand; many items means monthly curation can surface lesser-known beneficial items.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/subscription-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Only offer a monthly trickle of items if core loop and content are deep enough to track players from new to veteran status.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/subscription-design) **[[monetisation-other]]** Tie subscription exclusives to in-experience events and systems to make them feel exciting and special.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/subscription-design) **[[monetisation-other]]** Season pass subscribers unlock a premium track; players subscribing mid-season can claim premium rewards already earned because progress tracks across both tracks.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/subscription-design) Subscription price tiers used in currency pack calculation: **$2.99, $4.99, $7.99, $14.99 per month**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/subscription-design) Currency pack calculation base: **Roughly $1 USD per Robux exchange rate; soft currency set to X = 1 R**
