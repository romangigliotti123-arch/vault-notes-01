# Docs: art / characters / creating / head-objects

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/characters/creating/head-objects.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox art docs on the avatar character body template head structure. Lists extra head mesh objects (Head_Geo, teeth, tongue, lashes, eyes) and head bone children that exist for editing. Warns that these must be joined and removed during cleanup before export, or they cause Marketplace validation errors.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/characters/creating/head-objects) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Join and remove the extra head objects (Head_Geo, UpperTeeth_Geo, LowerTeeth_Geo, Tongue_Geo, RightLash_Geo, RightEye_Geo, LeftLash_Geo, LeftEye_Geo) during cleanup before exporting to avoid validation errors.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/characters/creating/head-objects) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Remove extra head-related bones (Tongue, LowerTeeth, UpperTeeth, LeftEye, RightEye) before export; bone children may differ between templates, so check each template.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/characters/creating/head-objects) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Do not leave helper head objects in the final export; they exist only to aid modification and cause validation issues on Marketplace upload.
