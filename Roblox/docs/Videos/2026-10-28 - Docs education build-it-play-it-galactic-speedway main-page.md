## Summary
Official Roblox education page for the Galactic Speedway lesson series. Covers installing Roblox Studio (free, PC/Mac), recommends a 3-button mouse with scroll wheel, gives account safety basics, and lists three step-by-step tutorials (Get Off the Ground, Adding Wings, Customize and Share) with quiz-game rewards. Teaches combining parts to build spaceships.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/main-page) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use a 3-button mouse with a scroll wheel when building in Roblox Studio, as the page strongly recommends.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/main-page) **[[common-mistakes]]** Never share your Roblox password, make it hard to guess, and never trust anyone claiming free Robux or a secret method to get it.
