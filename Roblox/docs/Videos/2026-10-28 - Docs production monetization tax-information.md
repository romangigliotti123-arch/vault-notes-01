# Docs: production / monetization / tax-information

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs on DevEx tax information. Tax info moves to the Taxes page in Creator Hub (July 15, 2026). Terms of Use update on Nov 1, 2026 classifies DevEx payments as royalties; US withholding applies based on tax info on file by Oct 31, 2026. Covers W-9/W-8 forms, 24% backup withholding, treaty claims, and year-end forms.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Submit or validate tax information in Creator Hub Taxes page before Oct 31, 2026 to determine withholding rate on Nov 1 payouts.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information) **[[business-and-payouts]]** US creators with valid W-9 on file get no US withholding; invalid or missing TIN triggers 24% backup withholding.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information) **[[business-and-payouts]]** International creators without valid tax info by Oct 31, 2026 may face 24% backup withholding on full payout; with valid info, 0%-30% on US-sourced portion.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information) **[[business-and-payouts]]** To claim a treaty rate, Form W-8 must be valid, include a TIN, and list citizenship/incorporation country matching permanent residence address.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Request DevEx payouts by Oct 15, 2026 (recommended) to avoid the Terms of Use transition.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information) **[[common-mistakes]]** Payouts processed on or after Nov 1, 2026 are subject to withholding even if the request was submitted earlier.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information) **[[common-mistakes]]** Never send tax forms or sensitive info by email; official Roblox emails come from [email] or [email].

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information) US backup withholding rate: **24%** (Applied if W-9 invalid, TIN missing, or IRS B-Notice received)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information) International US withholding range: **0% to 30%** (On US-sourced DevEx earnings, depending on country and treaty)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information) 1099-MISC reporting threshold: **$10 or more** (Royalty payments reported in Box 2 for US creators)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information) Historical tax form lookback: **7 years** (Prior-year 1099-NEC/1042-S requestable via support)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information) Key deadline: **Oct 31, 2026** (Last day under current Terms; tax info submitted by then sets rate for Nov 1 payouts)
