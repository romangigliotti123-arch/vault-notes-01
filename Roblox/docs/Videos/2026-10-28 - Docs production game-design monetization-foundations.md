# Docs: production / game-design / monetization-foundations

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Official Roblox docs on monetization foundations: the three components (items, shop, season passes), item categories (durable vs consumable; enhancement vs expression), good shop design (integrated, contextual, inviting), and season pass design (free + premium tiers, attractive rewards, manageable timeframes). Includes a glossary of monetization terms.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Before choosing items, know your players: whether they are competitive, collaborative, casual or experienced, and how easy the experience is to learn.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make every sold item serve a clear purpose and be instantly relevant to the experience, e.g. Doors' flashlight helps players explore dark rooms.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations) **[[monetisation-other]]** Describe purchasable items accurately and truthfully so players know exactly what they are buying and the value it gives.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations) **[[monetisation-other]]** Sell durable items (cosmetic skins) and consumables (temporary boosts); categorise each as enhancement (gameplay benefit) or expression (personalisation).
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations) **[[monetisation-other]]** Design the shop as a deliberate part of the experience, easy to reach, with a consistent icon and quick open/close so players are not disrupted.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations) **[[monetisation-other]]** Give shop items context: show how each item relates to gameplay and to other options, so new players understand its value.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Rotate shop stock and introduce new items regularly so the shop feels fresh and invites players back.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations) **[[monetisation-other]]** Treat the shop as a place to linger: show options, atmosphere and reasons to browse, especially in lobby areas.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use a season pass as a limited-time, quest-based progression system with a clearly stated end date so players do not miss rewards.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Offer both a free and a premium season pass: free rewards let non-paying players progress, while premium is a superset with extra bonuses.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Space season pass rewards relative to average session length, and mix short-term and long-term missions for day-to-day engagement and retention.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make season pass rewards tied to the core loop, preview them in the shop, and clearly show what is in the free versus premium tier.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Show the XP needed per level clearly so players know how far they are from the next reward.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Place a starter pack (initial offer) where new users see it within the first few minutes after joining.

## Games
- **Doors**: Flashlight purchase is instantly understood as useful for exploring dark rooms; shop explains Revives as a limited core mechanic.
- **Tower Defense Simulator**: Cosmetic skins as an example of durable items.
- **Evade**: Temporary boosts as an example of consumable items.
- **Bot Clash Simulator**: Season pass shown as distinct from the shop, with stated time remaining and descriptive rewards.
- **Dragon Adventures**: Shop with rotating stock; daily and weekly missions as season pass progression.
- **Jailbreak**: Free and premium season pass tiers with a clearly previewed final reward.
