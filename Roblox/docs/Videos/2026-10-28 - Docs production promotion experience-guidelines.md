# Docs: production / promotion / experience-guidelines

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs on Experience Guidelines: the age recommendation and content descriptor system shown on experience pages, how to complete the Experience Questionnaire, category definitions and thresholds (violence, blood, crude humor, fear, etc.), 17+ verification requirements, and moderation consequences for inaccurate answers.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Complete the Experience Questionnaire for every experience so it reaches the largest appropriate audience; experiences without guidelines are treated as Ages 13+.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not add 17+ content before generating a 17+ age recommendation; the questionnaire must be completed first or the experience risks moderation.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines) **[[common-mistakes]]** Base questionnaire answers on the most mature or extreme content players can encounter, not the typical content.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Resubmit the questionnaire whenever an update changes any answer; failing to do so breaks compliance with Community Standards.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines) **[[common-mistakes]]** Any implied violence (e.g. off-screen sounds) automatically counts as mild; any realistic consequence of violence, even once, counts as moderate or strong.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines) **[[common-mistakes]]** Any depicted blood, even from a distance, counts as light; pools, gushing, organs, dismemberment or up-close spatter count as heavy (17+ only).
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines) **[[common-mistakes]]** Experience guidelines don't cover user-generated avatar items players bring in; only content you create counts.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** If content is too mature for the target audience, modify the experience to fit, then resubmit; use Class.PolicyService to adjust content per audience.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines) **[[common-mistakes]]** Inaccurate guidelines can lead to guideline removal, restriction to 13+ visibility, and moderation actions; misrepresentation may bring consequences.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines) **[[common-mistakes]]** Free-form user creation and social hangouts are restricted to players 13+ regardless of parental controls.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines) Account age required for questionnaire: **30 days** (Roblox account must be at least 30 days old to generate guidelines)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines) Experiences without guidelines: **Treated as Ages 13+** (Roblox doesn't recommend unrated experiences to younger players)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines) Minimum age for 17+ content: **17 (verified)** (17+ experiences only playable by age-verified 17+ players)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines) Minimum age for social hangouts and free-form user creation: **13** (Restricted to players over 13 regardless of parental controls)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-guidelines) Age recommendation tiers: **All ages, 9+, 13+, 17+** (Assigned from questionnaire answers with content descriptors)
