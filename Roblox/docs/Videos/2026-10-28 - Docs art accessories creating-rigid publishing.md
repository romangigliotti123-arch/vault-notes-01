# Docs: art / accessories / creating-rigid / publishing

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/accessories/creating-rigid/publishing.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Docs page on publishing a rigid accessory to the Marketplace. Two steps: Upload (Save to Roblox, Content Type set to Avatar Item, fields, technical validation, fee) and Publish (moderation up to 24 hours, then sale options edited in Creator Hub). Creators must meet eligibility requirements and pay an upload fee and payment advance. Publishing is optional, only for creators who sell.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/accessories/creating-rigid/publishing) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Create a custom thumbnail before uploading a rigid accessory; it cannot be added after upload.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/accessories/creating-rigid/publishing) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Set the asset Category to match the Accessory Type chosen during fitting and conversion, or validation will not pass.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/accessories/creating-rigid/publishing) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Confirm you meet Marketplace creator requirements before attempting to sell; eligibility is needed to sell assets.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/accessories/creating-rigid/publishing) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Budget for the upload fee and a payment advance required during upload and publishing.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/accessories/creating-rigid/publishing) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan for up to 24 hours of moderation after upload before the item can be edited and enabled for sale.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/accessories/creating-rigid/publishing) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Write a clear title and short description at upload; both can be adjusted later.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/accessories/creating-rigid/publishing) Moderation queue time: **up to 24 hours** (Uploaded accessory waits in moderation before sale options unlock)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/accessories/creating-rigid/publishing) Upload costs: **Upload fee plus payment advance** (Required during upload and publishing; amounts in Marketplace fees docs)
