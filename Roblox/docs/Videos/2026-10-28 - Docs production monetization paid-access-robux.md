# Docs: production / monetization / paid-access-robux

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-robux.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs page on paid access: charging a one-time Robux fee (25 to 1000) to enter a game. Covers eligibility (verified email, 30-day-old account), 7-day escrow payouts, no refunds, setup in Creator Hub or Studio, and policies such as no private servers and no game copying.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-robux) **[[monetisation-other]]** Paid access can shrink your player count because some users won't pay an entry fee. Weigh this before enabling it.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-robux) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Use temporary paid access as a closed beta for your most engaged users. Tell buyers they are purchasing a beta version.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-robux) **[[pricing]]** The price you set directly determines how many Robux you earn per sale. Choose it within the 25 to 1000 Robux range.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-robux) **[[common-mistakes]]** Refunds are not available for paid access purchases. Launch the price carefully, since buyers cannot get their Robux back.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-robux) **[[monetisation-other]]** Buyers keep access even if you later raise the price or disable paid access. Lowering the price later will not affect existing buyers.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-robux) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Paid access earnings sit in escrow for up to 7 days before they are deposited to your linked bank. Plan cash flow around this delay.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-robux) **[[common-mistakes]]** If many users report the game as broken, it can be quarantined and escrowed earnings refunded to users. Make sure the game runs properly before charging.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-robux) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Paid access requires the game to be public, with no private servers, and copying is disabled automatically. Plan your access model around these constraints.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-robux) Paid access price range: **25 to 1000 Robux** (One-time fee to access the game)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-robux) Escrow hold period: **Up to 7 days** (Paid access payouts held before deposit to bank)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-robux) Minimum account age: **30 days** (Required to charge Robux for paid access)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-robux) Xbox availability: **Not available** (Games with paid access are not on Xbox)
