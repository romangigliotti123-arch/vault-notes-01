## Summary
Roblox docs page on the Creator Events program: organizers host virtual events (webinars, live streams, in-game) via a voluntary application. Lists participant benefits and requirements (age 16+, public speaking, complete Talent Hub profile, good moderation history) and the application timeline.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-events) **[[community-and-discord]]** Requirements for Creator Events organizers: be 16+, comfortable with public speaking, keep a complete Talent Hub profile, and have good moderation history.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-events) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** Creator Events organizers get potential highlight opportunities across the site and Roblox social media, plus 1:1 connections and office hours.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-events) Application response time: **1–2 weeks** (Expected update on Creator Events application; delays possible with high volume)
