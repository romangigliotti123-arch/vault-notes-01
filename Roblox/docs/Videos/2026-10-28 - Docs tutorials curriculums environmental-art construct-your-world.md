# Docs: tutorials / curriculums / environmental-art / construct-your-world

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox Creator Docs tutorial on constructing a laser tag environment: applying asset library to greybox geometry, distinct floor materials for indoor and outdoor areas, team color-coding, set dressing, and perimeter hallways. About 90 minutes to recreate.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Use distinct Material/MaterialVariant on indoor vs outdoor floors so players always know whether they are inside or outside the building.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Color-code each team's spawn zone and nearby combat pocket walls with a unique hue so players can orient by team color.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Give interior walls of each combat pocket a different hue from exterior walls so players can tell when they are inside looking out.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world) **[[common-mistakes]]** Limit combat pockets to at most three entrance/exit points to avoid giving players choice overload while navigating.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Use set dressing props (signage, foliage, planters as cover) to imply a living world and give indirect information about the environment.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Expect iteration between greybox and final art; keep greybox intent (sight lines, engagement distances) while changing geometry as needed.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Place spawn zones at opposite ends of the map, far from enemy fire, so players start safely.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/construct-your-world) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Contain playable areas with walls or boundaries so players stay in combat and do not fall off the island.
