# Docs: production / analytics / monetization

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Official Roblox docs on the Creator Dashboard Monetization analytics page: where to find revenue, conversion rate, paying users, ARPPU and ARPDAU, plus high-level guidance on improving each metric.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Open Creator Dashboard > Creations > your game > Monetization > Overview to see revenue, conversion, paying users, ARPPU and ARPDAU.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization) **[[monetisation-other]]** Plan monetization before launch; it is much harder to build a monetization strategy after the game is live.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Offer a mix of consumable and durable items so both short-term and long-term players have purchase options.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization) **[[pricing]]** Sell items across a wide range of Robux price points to fit users with different spending habits.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Expand offerings of your top-selling items, since limited catalogs leave players with nothing new to buy.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Add seasonal items tied to holidays or real-world events to create excitement around them.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization) **[[pricing]]** Offer welcome offers and discounts for first-time purchasers to lower the barrier to trying paid content.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization) **[[common-mistakes]]** Diagnose low conversion: check for expensive items, poor shop visibility, unappealing content, and confusing purchase flow steps.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Improve retention and engagement; engaged users generate more monetization opportunities and loyalty.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization) **[[monetisation-other]]** A high ARPPU with low ARPDAU means revenue comes from few users; broaden the audience to improve both metrics.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make the storefront easy to find and understand; invest creativity in the shop as much as the rest of the game.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization) **[[monetisation-other]]** Balance in-game economy with discounts, limited-time offers and free items.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization) Conversion rate: **Percent of daily active users who are also paying users** (Official docs definition)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization) ARPDAU: **Average revenue per daily active user** (Equals conversion rate times ARPPU)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization) ARPPU: **Average revenue per paying user; measures repeat purchaser spend** (Official docs definition)
