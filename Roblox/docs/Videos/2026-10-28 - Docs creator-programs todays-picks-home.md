## Summary
Roblox creator-programs page for Today's Picks on Home, a daily curation feature highlighting games with notable updates, new releases, events, and seasonal or cultural moments. Creators nominate their game via a survey; the editorial team reviews on an ongoing basis and only selected games are notified.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/todays-picks-home) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Submit a nomination through the Today's Picks survey each time your game has a notable update, since unselected nominations get no notification.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/todays-picks-home) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Time notable updates, new content, events, and seasonal or cultural tie-ins, as these are the criteria Today's Picks curates for Home placement.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/todays-picks-home) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Align major content drops with seasonal or cultural moments to improve chances of being highlighted on Home.
