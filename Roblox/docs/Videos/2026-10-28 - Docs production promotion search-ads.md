# Docs: production / promotion / search-ads

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/search-ads.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official docs on Search ads: keyword-matched ads shown at top of search results, Visits campaign objective in Ads Manager, up to 10 keywords per Ad Set, relevance-weighted second-price auction, and reporting limits.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/search-ads) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Bid only on keywords most relevant to your game; the auction weights relevance, so irrelevant keywords (e.g. horror game bidding on 'obby') get little sustained traffic.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/search-ads) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Group related keywords within each Ad Set (max 10 per Ad Set) for clearer performance reporting.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/search-ads) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Create single-keyword Ad Sets to compare how an individual keyword performs, since keyword filtering isn't available in reports.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/search-ads) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Search ads must follow Community Standards; Roblox suppresses games identified as clickbait or spam.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/search-ads) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Search ads use the Visits campaign objective; you cannot modify brand suitability or audience targeting for them.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/search-ads) Max keywords per Ad Set: **10**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/search-ads) Minimum age reached by search ads: **13+**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/search-ads) Auction pricing example: **Winner with $12 bid pays $10.01 (second-highest bid + $0.01)**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/search-ads) Auction tie-break increment: **$0.01 above second-highest price**
