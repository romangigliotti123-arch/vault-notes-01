## Summary
Official Roblox docs page on how the platform differs: frictionless cloud play (no downloads, automatic regional matchmaking, cross-platform publishing), community involvement in development, fast updates for direct feedback, and creator reach onto YouTube/TikTok. Mostly positioning, little numeric data.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/how-is-roblox-different) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Ship updates quickly and use the resulting direct player feedback to iterate toward a more focused experience for your audience.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/how-is-roblox-different) **[[community-and-discord]]** Cultivate a healthy community and involve users in development; this is described as key to success on Roblox.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/how-is-roblox-different) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Build a creator presence on YouTube and TikTok; creators with loyal followings can extend reach to new players.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/how-is-roblox-different) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Publish across all major devices so players can join on whichever platform they use, widening your addressable audience.
