# Docs: creator-programs / jumpstart

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/jumpstart.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox Jumpstart program page: an invest-and-support program for ambitious creators building novel 18+ games. Covers target genres (RPG, strategy, shooter underrepresented), gameplay and visual criteria, eligibility (at least one 18+ member, English proficiency), and in-kind support rather than cash or salary.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/jumpstart) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Target underrepresented 18+ genres (RPG, strategy, shooter) where Roblox says demand from older players is strong but supply is low.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/jumpstart) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build deep systems: metagame systems, skillful challenges and meaningful progression are what keep players returning, per Roblox's criteria.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/jumpstart) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Pursue genre mash-ups or traditional mechanics blended with avatars, social features and cross-platform support to stand out.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/jumpstart) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Pick a distinctive visual style (hyper-realistic 3D, stylized 2.5D sprites, high-fidelity avatars) so players think 'Wait, that's Roblox?'
