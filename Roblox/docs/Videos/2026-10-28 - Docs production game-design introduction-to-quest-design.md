# Docs: production / game-design / introduction-to-quest-design

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/introduction-to-quest-design.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs page explaining quest design: quest structure (objective, quantity, reward), achievements/badges vs dailies, and the roles quests play (goals, feature surfacing, tutorials, session variety, resource drip, engagement via limited-time content, progression, new gameplay, narrative).

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/introduction-to-quest-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Structure quests as objective + quantity + reward; larger quantities raise difficulty, and harder quests should offer more valuable rewards.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/introduction-to-quest-design) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Use quest chains as tutorials: break complex features into discrete objectives, one quest per feature, spread over time; quest tracking doubles as drop-off analytics.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/introduction-to-quest-design) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Mix short-, mid- and long-term goals so players feel frequent progress; dailies should contain only short-term, single-session goals.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/introduction-to-quest-design) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Use dailies with small, reliable rewards to drip-feed soft currency and incentivize daily logins.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/introduction-to-quest-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Tie quests to the core loop so players naturally earn and spend resources; scarce or effort-heavy sources hurt economy participation and retention.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/introduction-to-quest-design) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Use limited-time quests during seasonal events (e.g., Halloween, anniversaries) to create urgency and drive regular logins.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/introduction-to-quest-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Use quest-based season passes with reward tiers tied to core-loop activities, showing progress UI against the season deadline.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/introduction-to-quest-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Use achievements/badges for challenging alternate goals (e.g., unusual gameplay combos) to re-engage experienced players between updates.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/introduction-to-quest-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Use quests to surface under-discovered features in large worlds or complex games; a quest can be the first time players learn a location exists.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/introduction-to-quest-design) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid scarce or overly effort-intensive reward sources; they discourage economy participation and create perceptions of unfairness.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/introduction-to-quest-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add optional flavor text to quests to deliver narrative and tie rewards thematically to the story.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/introduction-to-quest-design) Daily quest availability window: **24 hours** (Dailies must be completed within 24 hours of being available)

## Games
- **Jailbreak**: Badge requiring shooting a helicopter while riding a vehicle, rated 'impossible', shows quests encouraging new gameplay.
- **Squishmallows**: Quest surfaces a campsite location, incentivizing exploration of the map.
- **RoCitizens**: Tutorial implemented as a quest to teach features through active play.
- **The Wild West**: Quests add session variety by directing players to underused areas and systems.
- **Pet Simulator X!**: Daily quests used as a reliable drip of currency.
- **Dragon Adventures**: Season pass with quest-based missions tied to hatching and raising dragons; exclusive final dragon reward.
- **Spellbound**: NPC quest with humorous flavor text; reward hat thematically tied to story.
- **World // Zero**: Example of daily quests with simple objectives completable in one session.
