# Docs: production / analytics / insights

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/insights.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox Creator Docs page on Analytics insights: overview-page insights (metric changes vs similar games, updated daily, 100+ DAU), achievement panel for six-month highs, and AI-generated weekly/monthly analytics reports and player feedback reports with their DAU and comment thresholds.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/insights) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use the 'Explore this metric' button on an overview insight to slice revenue or other metrics by platform, country, and more.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/insights) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Compare your metrics against similar-game benchmarks, not just your own history; a metric can improve yet still lag benchmarks and needs continued work.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/insights) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check the weekly and monthly AI analytics reports to find which monetization product or acquisition channel drives revenue and DAU changes.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/insights) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use automatic outlier detection in reports to find segments (platform, age, country) behind a metric decline instead of filtering manually.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/insights) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Read the player feedback report for top requests and takeaways, then prioritize the next work from them; verify AI summaries against your own judgment.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/insights) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** When revenue drops relative to similar games, investigate monetization by platform and country before changing other parts of the game.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/insights) Insight eligibility: **100+ DAU, insights updated daily** (Overview-page insights require at least 100 daily active users)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/insights) AI analytics report eligibility: **1000+ DAU** (Weekly and monthly AI-generated analytics reports require this DAU level)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/insights) Player feedback report eligibility: **20+ feedback comments** (Player feedback reports require at least 20 player feedback comments)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/insights) Achievement window: **6 months** (Achievement panel highlights metrics at six-month highs)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/insights) Example revenue insight: **-21% vs similar games +15%** (Doc example: own revenue declined 21% while similar games rose 15%)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/insights) Report timing: **Monthly on first week; weekly at start of each week** (Report availability schedule)
