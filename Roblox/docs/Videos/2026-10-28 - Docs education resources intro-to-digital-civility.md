## Summary
Roblox Education docs page on digital civility: defines digital citizenship, online identity accountability, building positive community culture, and being an upstander against cyberbullying and griefing. Includes classroom discussion prompts and activity suggestions. Not game-development advice.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/intro-to-digital-civility) **[[community-and-discord]]** Set clear community rules (e.g. 'no put-downs', 'stay relaxed - it's only a game') in new online spaces, or create your own with friends if none exist.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/intro-to-digital-civility) **[[community-and-discord]]** Be proactive in-game: ask new players if they need help, share tips, or start chat conversations to build a positive culture.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/intro-to-digital-civility) **[[community-and-discord]]** Don't engage with trolls; most negative online behavior is done for fun, so withholding reactions removes the payoff.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/intro-to-digital-civility) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Learn how to report and block users on the platform; reporting is quick, simple, and anonymous.
