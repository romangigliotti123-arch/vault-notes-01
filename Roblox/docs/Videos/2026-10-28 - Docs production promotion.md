# Docs: production / promotion

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official docs page on promotion methods: search ads, sponsored games, immersive portal ad campaigns, time-based events, player invite prompts, friend invite rewards, experience notifications (13+ users), sponsored avatar items, social media links, share links, and group community promotion. Mostly a feature catalogue with little quantitative guidance.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion) **[[community-and-discord]]** Name your community group after your studio or company so the group page reflects your brand.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use thumbnails and descriptions on your community group page to communicate brand and identity.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion) **[[community-and-discord]]** Add social links such as YouTube, Discord, or X to the community page so players can follow you off-platform.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add player invite prompts inside your game to encourage friends to join and boost co-experience gameplay.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Use a friend invite reward system to reward existing players for bringing in new users.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Use time-based events shown on your game's details page to invite users back into the game.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Set a per-day budget and schedule and choose your audience when running sponsored game or item campaigns; search ads can target keywords.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Use experience notifications, configured for in-game activities you consider important, to bring 13+ users back; notification content is developer-defined.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Use share links on social media to promote games and track off-platform acquisition, unlike social links which point to your channels.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion) **[[common-mistakes]]** All promotional content must follow Community Standards and Terms of Use; advertising must also follow Advertising Standards.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion) Experience notification eligibility: **13+ users only** (Notifications are available only to users aged 13 and older)
