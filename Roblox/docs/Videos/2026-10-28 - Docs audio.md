# Docs: audio

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official docs page on audio: lists audio object classes (AudioPlayer, AudioEmitter, AudioListener, device I/O, text-to-speech, speech-to-text, Wires), notes Creator Store offers 100,000+ free-to-use sound effects and music tracks, and describes audio effects (AudioEqualizer, AudioCompressor, AudioReverb) for modifying AudioPlayer streams.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Only import audio you have permission to use, such as audio you made yourself; otherwise use Creator Store assets.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use the Creator Store for free-to-use sound effects and music (100,000+ professionally produced tracks) instead of sourcing audio from scratch.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio) **[[sound-and-music]]** Mix positional (AudioEmitter/AudioListener) and non-positional audio to immerse players and give feedback on actions.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio) **[[sound-and-music]]** Use AudioEqualizer to muffle sounds such as rain, AudioCompressor to cap a sound's maximum volume, and AudioReverb for realistic interior reflections.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio) Creator Store free audio assets: **more than 100,000 sound effects and music tracks** (Professionally-produced, free-to-use, from audio and music partners)

## Tools
AudioPlayer, AudioEmitter, AudioListener, AudioEqualizer
