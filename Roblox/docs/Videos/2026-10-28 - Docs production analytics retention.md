# Docs: production / analytics / retention

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox Creator Docs page on the Retention analytics dashboard: how D1/D7/D30 cohorts are defined, where to find them, and suggested levers to improve day 1, day 7 and day 30 retention (core loop, FTUE, performance, progression, ending system).

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Read retention by cohort: x-axis is each user's first play date, so changes made on a given day show up in that day's new-user cohort.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Expect D7 and D30 data to be empty for recent dates; D7 appears after 7 days and D30 after 30 days.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use daily and weekly cohort tables to compare users acquired during events or campaigns against earlier users, including 7D playtime, 7D conversion, and 7D/30D revenue per user.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Improve D1 retention by focusing on the core loop, first-time user experience, and performance, in that order of the docs' emphasis.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make core loop steps clear and fun, balance challenge to avoid frustration and boredom, and reward loop completion with feedback, currency, items or achievements.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Keep the tutorial brief or use tooltips; avoid long tutorials. Aim to get new users to the fun in 5 minutes or less.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Deliver a joyful moment after the first core-loop completion and preview the progress available on repeat completions.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Tag each FTUE step with in-game items to track completion rates, track both wins and losses, and fix the biggest drop-off step first.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Test on different devices and platforms, and monitor crash rates, frame rates and errors on every update.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) **[[community-and-discord]]** Set up a user community that can help identify bugs and crashes.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Improve D7 retention through the progression system: set short-term and long-term goals, add content variety, and balance difficulty so players neither run out of content nor stall.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Measure progression by granting a special item at each level to see how long players take to reach the next level, then adjust task difficulty.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship smaller updates to existing mechanics every 2-4 weeks and bigger new-feature updates every 2-3 months to keep users returning.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add social mechanics such as trading, parties, guilds, PvP, tournaments and leaderboards to keep players engaged past the ending.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) D1 retention: **Users who first played on a date and returned the next day**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) D7 retention: **Users who first played on a date and returned after 1 week (data appears after 7 days)**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) D30 retention: **Users who first played on a date and returned after 1 month (data appears after 30 days)**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) Time to fun: **5 minutes or less after entering the game**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) Small update cadence: **Every 2-4 weeks on existing mechanics**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) Big update cadence: **Every 2-3 months with new features**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) Daily cohort window: **First 10 days of retention**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention) Weekly cohort window: **10 weeks, Monday-Sunday grouping**
