# Docs: production / monetization / paid-access-local-currency

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs on paid access in local currency: one-time fee in local currency (USD fallback), eligibility (verified email/ID, 13+, Tipalti account, supported country), revenue share tiers, 48-hour refunds, escrow/monthly payouts, demo mode with PlayerOwnsAssetAsync, and policy rules.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) **[[monetisation-other]]** Paid access purchasers keep access even if you raise the price later or disable paid access, so early buyers are protected.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) **[[pricing]]** Desktop local-currency paid access revenue share tiers: $9.99 gives 50%, $29.99 gives 60%, $49.99 gives 70%, before taxes/VAT.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use paid access for a closed beta for engaged users, and clearly tell buyers they are purchasing a beta version.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) **[[common-mistakes]]** Paid access can shrink your player count since some users won't pay a fee to join; weigh this before enabling it.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Local-currency earnings are escrowed for at least 60 days and paid monthly; you cannot withdraw outside the payout schedule.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Set up Tipalti early: account verification can take 5 to 8 business days after submitting payment info.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) **[[monetisation-other]]** Paid access games must give buyers the entire game; you cannot paywall parts of the game behind Robux. Passes and dev products inside the game are allowed.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use demo mode plus PlayerOwnsAssetAsync to limit non-owners to a preview area or features, and prompt them to purchase.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) **[[common-mistakes]]** Games reported broken by many users may be quarantined and escrowed earnings refunded; make sure the game runs properly.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) **[[common-mistakes]]** Paid access games cannot have private servers, be copied, or repeat existing games; violations can suspend your account.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) Desktop revenue share at $9.99: **50%**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) Desktop revenue share at $29.99: **60%**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) Desktop revenue share at $49.99: **70%**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) Refund window after purchase: **48 hours**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) Minimum escrow hold for local-currency earnings: **60 days**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) Tipalti setup email timing after submitting payment info: **5 to 8 business days**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-access-local-currency) Minimum age to charge for paid access: **13 years with verified ID**
