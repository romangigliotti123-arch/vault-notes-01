## Summary
Official Roblox Studio docs tour of the core UI: mezzanine, toolbar tabs, 3D viewport camera controls, Toolbox tabs, Explorer (data model per place) and Properties windows. Orientation only, no monetisation or growth advice.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/studio/explore-ui) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use a 2-button mouse with a scroll wheel; Roblox strongly recommends it for navigating 3D space in Studio.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/studio/explore-ui) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Think of each place as a level with its own environment, objects and scripts; Explorer shows only the active place's data model.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/studio/explore-ui) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Select a part and press F to focus the camera on it; use , and . to orbit around the focused object.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/studio/explore-ui) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Check Toolbox Inventory and Creations for your own assets; Creations excludes Creator Store items, Recent shows latest used.
