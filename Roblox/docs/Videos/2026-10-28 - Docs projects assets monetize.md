## Summary
Roblox docs page on monetizing creator assets. Covers creating and selling Studio plugins via the Creator Store: free or paid at a minimum of $4.99 USD, with only taxes and payment processing fees deducted from sales.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/assets/monetize) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Plugins can be sold on the Creator Store for a minimum of $4.99 USD, or offered free; only taxes and payment processing fees are deducted.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/assets/monetize) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Selling Studio plugins is a way to earn income from development tooling; Roblox states its revenue share is market-leading.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/assets/monetize) Minimum plugin price: **$4.99 USD**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/assets/monetize) Plugin sale deductions: **Only taxes and payment processing fees**
