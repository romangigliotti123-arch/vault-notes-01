# Docs: production / analytics / analytics-dashboard

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox Creator Docs page on the Analytics dashboard: KPI categories (retention, engagement, acquisition, demographics, feedback, monetization), eligibility (more than 10 DAU and 10 play hours for 7 consecutive days), enrollment steps, filters, Explore page, benchmarking tiers, breakdowns, and sales data CSV export.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Dashboard KPIs unlock only after the game has more than 10 DAU and 10 play hours for 7 consecutive days.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use Filter By cohorts (in-experience activity status, user engagement) to separate Early, Active, Lapsed and Reactivated players when reading charts.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use period-over-period overlays (e.g. 28-day offset) and 7-day moving averages to remove weekly seasonality before judging trends.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Compare KPIs against benchmarks; read the 50th-90th percentile range as the spread of similar games, not a single target.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Create calculated metrics (e.g. RoundWins / RoundsPlayed, CurrencySources - CurrencySinks) to track difficulty, inflation and feature adoption.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Download monthly sales CSV (Sales of Goods) to track pending vs released Robux and plan DevEx cash-outs; link expires after 48 hours.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Set up an Authenticator App 2-Step verification and verified email to access sales data downloads.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) **[[common-mistakes]]** Group game analytics are visible only to the owner and members with sufficient role permissions, since some metrics are sensitive.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) Dashboard eligibility: **>10 DAU and 10 play hours for 7 consecutive days** (Required to access all dashboard KPIs)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) Benchmark pool minimum: **100 DAU** (Games needed for similar-player benchmarks)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) Overall benchmark tiers: **Top 200 / Top 500 / Top 1000 by 30-day playtime** (Pool excludes games under 30 days old)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) Early status window: **First 30 days after first play** (In-experience activity status cohort)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) Lapsed definition: **No sessions in last 30 days** (In-experience activity status cohort)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) User engagement buckets: **Inactive 0d, low 1d, medium 2-3d, high 4-7d** (Days played in last 7 days)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) Sales data refresh: **Every 48 hours** (Auto-generated CSV of sales)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) Sales data range: **Any month up to two years back** (Selectable in transactions page)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard) Sales CSV Revenue vs Price: **Revenue excludes marketplace/affiliate fees and recurring group payouts** (Price is Robux paid by buyer)
