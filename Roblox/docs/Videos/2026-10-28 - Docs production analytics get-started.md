# Docs: production / analytics / get-started

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox Creator Docs overview of analytics: how to use DAU/MAU, session length, D1/D7/D30 retention, ARPDAU, conversion rate, ARPPU, and segmentation (payer status, cohort, platform spender) to answer player questions, then iterate with experience configs and experiments.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Low D1 retention usually signals poor onboarding: players are confused, frustrated, or not reaching the fun fast enough.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Low D7 retention often means weak progression; players lack a tangible reason to return a week later.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Low D30 retention often indicates missing end-game content or end-game goals for seasoned players.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Conversion rate (share of users who ever spend) is one of the most important monetization metrics, since initial purchasers tend to buy again.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Low ARPPU often means purchasable item value is not optimized; top payers typically spend several times more than other segments.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Compare average session length against your content length after updates or events to gauge how new content was received.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Segment by Active Payer Status: if the top 15% of payers contribute much less than ~50% of revenue, improve retention/monetization for top spenders.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Segment by When user first played: in mature games, older cohorts should make up a growing share of DAU; declining old cohorts call for new content or events.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** For Daily Engagement Rewards, active spenders must play at least 10 minutes; check average playtime against this threshold.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Use experience configs to tune values live without publishing, and experiments to test feature versions on separate player groups.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Follow the loop: analyze metrics, make a targeted change via configs or experiments, monitor results, then repeat; change incrementally.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started) Revenue concentration: **Top 15% of active payers account for roughly 50% of revenue on average** (Roblox docs, average across many games)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started) Daily Engagement Rewards threshold: **10 minutes** (Active spender must play at least this long to qualify)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started) DAU example: **1,000,000 falling to 500,000** (Illustrative downtrend that warrants troubleshooting)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/get-started) New-game DAU source: **Most DAU from players who joined within first 30 days** (Typical for newly launched games)
