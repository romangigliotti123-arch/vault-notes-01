# Docs: education / resources / intro-to-digital-safety

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.

## Summary
Roblox education docs page on digital safety for students. Covers keeping personal information private, filing specific in-game reports, spotting scams (free Robux, credential requests, unofficial links, Rule of 3 source checking), and enabling 2-Step Verification. Mostly classroom and safety guidance, not game-building advice.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/intro-to-digital-safety) **[[community-and-discord]]** Never share personal info like email, password, or date of birth, even with friends; it can enable identity theft or account takeover.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/intro-to-digital-safety) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Enable 2-Step Verification on your Roblox account so a password alone cannot log in; a unique code is emailed at each login.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/intro-to-digital-safety) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Treat free Robux offers and requests for account info as scams; Roblox staff never ask for passwords or personal identifying information.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/intro-to-digital-safety) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Do not follow links outside Roblox, even from friends; lookalike domains (rublux.com, r0blox.com) and texture-upload tutorials have been used to steal accounts.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/intro-to-digital-safety) Rule of 3: **3 trusted sources to verify a claim** (Recommended verification threshold for unsure information)
