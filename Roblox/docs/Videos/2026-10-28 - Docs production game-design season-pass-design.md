# Docs: production / game-design / season-pass-design

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs page on season pass design: time-bound quest-based progression with XP and tiers. Covers goals (core loop, motivation, comprehension, anticipation), parameters (length ~1 month, 10 tiers, one-week rest), tier and final rewards, free vs premium passes, daily mission design, mission categories and difficulty, surfacing, and asset/tech requests.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Tie season pass missions to every system and content type in the experience so players learn systems and have varied things to do.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make missions manageable so they don't consume a player's entire daily playtime; offer varied tasks for different playstyles.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Let season length be set by team bandwidth, with one month as a starting point, and allow at least a one-week rest period between seasons to prevent burnout.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Start with about ten season tiers; keep the count short enough that the season isn't daunting at the start.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Scale tier rewards to the XP effort required; include wanted but not overly valuable items like consumables, small currency, temporary buffs, or small cosmetics.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make the final reward worthwhile and ideally a brand-new asset retired after the season to create exclusivity and anticipation.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Require players to claim rewards via a button in the season pass UI rather than auto-granting, and show an icon badge when rewards are uncollected.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Keep free and premium tracks separate: free players cannot complete premium missions or earn premium rewards without upgrading.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** When a free player upgrades to premium mid-season, grant premium rewards for all tiers already completed so they aren't penalized for waiting.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Never require hard currency for missions; this excludes free players and forces premium players to spend more.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Reset missions daily and unlock new missions daily to encourage daily engagement and prevent fast consumption of season content.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Vary mission difficulty from easy to hard, and keep categories broad with at least three across the whole experience.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Keep daily missions achievable; if players regularly fail them, interest in the season pass wanes.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make the season pass highly visible: large colorful buttons, a visible timer, themed HUD graphics, and first-login messages after changes.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Add new missions whenever a new feature ships, and consider weekly high-reward missions and double XP in the final week as catch-up.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Write asset requests and tech requests before building to scope work, separating evergreen assets from those needed each season.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) Season length: **~1 month starting point** (Length depends on team bandwidth)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) Rest period between seasons: **At least 1 week** (Buffer against burnout and gives dev time)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) Season tiers: **10 to start** (Short enough not to feel daunting)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) Mission categories: **At least 3 across experience** (Broad categories encompassing multiple activities)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/season-pass-design) Daily missions: **Free players get a set number daily; premium gets bonus missions** (Premium bonus missions visible to all, completable only by premium)
