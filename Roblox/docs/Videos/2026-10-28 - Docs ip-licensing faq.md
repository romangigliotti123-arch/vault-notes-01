# Docs: ip-licensing / faq

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/faq.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox documentation FAQ for the IP Licensing program. Covers copyright vs trademark, what data rights holders see, license eligibility, creative freedom under content standards, conditional offers, the 14-day dispute window, termination notice, and communication rules.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/faq) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Only use an IP in your game with explicit permission from the rights holder; otherwise remove content that uses it. Pre-existing agreements remain valid.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/faq) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** You can request a license with a partially finished game or a fork of an existing game; it serves to showcase your creator abilities to rights holders.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/faq) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** IP must be central to and present throughout the full game; licenses cannot cover a single level or portion of a game.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/faq) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Respond to conditional offers within 14 days by marking changes done or disputing; conditional offers do not auto-activate after 14 days.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/faq) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Disputes to inaccurate offers must be filed within 14 days; you can dispute a standard or conditional offer, but not a conditional approval of your own request.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/faq) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Keep to the license content standards; you may use your own assets without templates as long as you follow the license terms.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/faq) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** If a license is terminated for reasons like repeated standard violations, you get at least 30 days notice to remove the IP before terms and revenue share end.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/faq) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Do not negotiate licensing terms, request financial or personal information, use harassing language, or ask to connect outside Roblox in license communications, or messages get moderated.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/faq) Dispute window for inaccurate offers: **14 days** (Days to dispute a license offer you believe is inaccurate)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/faq) Conditional offer response window: **14 days** (Days creator has to make requested changes or dispute)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/faq) Dispute attempts: **2 each** (Times rights holder can attempt to license and creator can dispute an offer)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/faq) Termination notice: **at least 30 days** (Advance notice before license termination takes effect)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/faq) Rights holder metrics shared: **visits, 7-day average active users (7DAU), anonymized transactions** (Metrics given to rights holder after agreement: visits, 7DAU, transactions if revenue share set)
