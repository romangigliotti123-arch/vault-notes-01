## Summary
Roblox Education docs page (Build It, Play It: Galactic Speedway). Explains duplicating parts (Ctrl+D / Cmd+D) to reuse parts from the scrapyard, positioning duplicates with Move/Rotate/Scale, toggling rotation snapping for free rotation, and adding decorations and extensions to build bigger ships.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/duplicate-and-design) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Duplicate parts with Ctrl+D (Cmd+D on Mac) to reuse existing parts instead of rebuilding them from scratch.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/duplicate-and-design) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Turn off rotation snapping in the toolbar next to Rotate for free rotation; remember to turn it back on afterward.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/duplicate-and-design) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Use parts to extend the build away from the body so you have more places to attach objects when making larger builds.
