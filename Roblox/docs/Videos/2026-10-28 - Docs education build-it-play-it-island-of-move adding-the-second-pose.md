# Docs: education / build-it-play-it-island-of-move / adding-the-second-pose

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.

## Summary
Roblox Studio education tutorial (Build It, Play It: Island of Move) on adding the second pose in the animation timeline. Covers setting the time to 0:15, rotating body parts from torso outward, resetting a pose, and enabling Loop to preview the animation with Space.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/adding-the-second-pose) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Pose body parts connected to the torso first (upper arm, thigh), then work outward to hands and feet to pose faster.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/adding-the-second-pose) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Move the camera around while posing so the pose reads correctly from every angle.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/adding-the-second-pose) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** To restart a pose, right-click its top white diamond and select Reset Selected to return it to the default.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/adding-the-second-pose) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Turn on Loop before previewing so the animation repeats instead of stopping, giving a better idea of in-game look.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/adding-the-second-pose) second pose timeline position: **0:15** (Time at which the second pose is placed in the middle of the timeline)

## Tools
Roblox Studio Animation Editor, Rotate tool, Loop toggle, Space bar (play/stop preview)
