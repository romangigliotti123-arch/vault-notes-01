## Summary
Roblox Education docs page "Go beyond the challenge" for the Build It, Play It: Galactic Speedway tutorial. Suggests next steps after building a custom driftspeeder model and engine-speed code: sharing speeders, particles, importing new models, colors/textures, and painting snow or water to customize the world.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/go-beyond-the-challenge) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Import new models to add variety to vehicles or props, and customize colors and textures to give a vehicle a distinct style.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/go-beyond-the-challenge) **[[vfx-and-particles]]** Add particle effects such as thruster fire or sparks to make a vehicle feel more dynamic.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/go-beyond-the-challenge) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Customize the environment with terrain painting (e.g., snow or water) and build your own levels and decorative props.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/go-beyond-the-challenge) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Let players share their creations with friends so they can use or send each other custom vehicles.
