# Docs: production / promotion / rewarded-video-playbooks / rpg

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/rpg.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official playbook on rewarded video ads for RPG games. Covers key metrics (opt-in rate, daily frequency), four placement strategies (convenience, loss aversion, scarcity, grind), and economy guardrails (daily caps, deterministic rewards only, account-bound non-tradable rewards).

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/rpg) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Frame rewarded video as paying with time: reward time saved (e.g., bypass crafting cooldown), not power.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/rpg) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Trigger rewarded ads at loss-aversion moments, e.g., watch a 15s video to revive at checkpoint instead of starting village.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/rpg) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use scarcity placements: offer hard-to-farm items like a Luck Potion boosting rare drops for 10 minutes.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/rpg) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use grind placements such as timed 2x EXP boosts to speed progression and lift session length and retention.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/rpg) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not reward random outcomes; state the exact reward, e.g., 'Watch to receive [Specific Item]'. RNG rewards are not allowed under 2026 standards.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/rpg) **[[common-mistakes]]** Make ad rewards account-bound. Tradable currency from ads enables alt-account farming that destroys the player marketplace.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/rpg) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Set a daily reward cap based on economy value to prevent hyper-inflation; test to find the right cap.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/rpg) Opt-in rate: **15%+ of eligible users engage with ad** (Key benchmark from Roblox rewarded video playbook for RPG)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/rpg) Daily frequency: **3+ views per user per day** (Target daily ad views per user)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/rpg) Second Wind ad length: **15 seconds** (Example revive-at-checkpoint rewarded video)
