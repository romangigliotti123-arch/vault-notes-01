# Docs: production / promotion / rewarded-video-playbooks / tycoon

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/tycoon.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official playbook on Rewarded Video for Tycoon games. Covers three strategies (time skip doubling offline earnings, progressive rewards, progression accelerators), key metrics (opt-in rate, daily frequency), example games, and placement/testing pro tips.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/tycoon) **[[monetisation-other]]** For tycoons, offer a rewarded ad to double offline earnings when players return; it hooks the wealth-accumulation motivation at session start.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/tycoon) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use progressive rewards: a sequence of consecutive ad views where payout rises in value or rarity after each view.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/tycoon) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use progression accelerators: rewarded cash or build/booster injections that help players progress now, giving a reason to keep playing instead of logging off.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/tycoon) **[[monetisation-other]]** Place rewarded video prompts prominently in high-traffic areas (home screen, store, post-match screens); buried prompts cause engagement to plummet.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/tycoon) **[[pricing]]** Balance reward value: enough that 30 seconds of watch time feels worth it, but not so generous it cannibalises in-game purchases or breaks the economy.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/tycoon) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Continuously A/B test placement, button design, reward type and reward amount to find the highest-converting combination.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/tycoon) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Refresh the rewarded ad setup regularly with new reward items or updates to maintain interest over time.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/tycoon) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Focus on two metrics: opt-in rate (target 15%+ of eligible users engaging) and daily frequency (target 3+ views per user per day).

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/tycoon) Opt-in rate: **15%+** (Share of total eligible users who engage with the ad)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/tycoon) Daily frequency: **3+** (Times each user views the ad per day)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/tycoon) Rewarded video length: **6-30 seconds** (Full-screen horizontal ad placements that users watch for a reward)
