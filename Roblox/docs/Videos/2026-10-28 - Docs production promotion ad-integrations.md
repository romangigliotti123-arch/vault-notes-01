# Docs: production / promotion / ad-integrations

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs on ad integrations: what counts as an ad, requirements (disclosure, no misleading offers, rewarded video 13+), 4-step submission (registration, asset creation, deployment with eligibility check and ad labels, moderation), campaign/asset statuses, and the 2027 revenue share based on qualified visits with CPTV tiers and caps.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Register campaigns early and submit at least 5 days before the campaign start date.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Submit all ad assets to Roblox for review before exposing them to users; moderation typically completes within 48 hours.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Clearly and prominently disclose ads in simple language; use standardized Ad labels within 500 studs and frequent, large labels if players 12 or under are targeted.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Do not use misleading content to drive engagement, such as offering free Robux. Violations risk account suspension or loss of ad eligibility.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Show rewarded video ads only to users 13 and older.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Add new assets to a separate campaign with fixed dates instead of an active campaign; during review the whole campaign serves only 18+ users.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Check eligibility with AdService:GetCampaignEligibilityAsync; a rejected asset makes it return false for all users.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Self-promotional assets, such as merchandise or off-platform products with calls to action, also count as ads and need registration.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) **[[pricing]]** From 2027, cap campaign cost by shortening duration or toggling the campaign off once impression targets are reached; deals are capped at $400,000.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Invoices are issued quarterly; payment is due within 90 days, so budget for brand payment timing. Owner invoicing info must be complete or the campaign stays Pending.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not leave ad campaigns Pending: the owner's invoicing info must be complete before the campaign can run.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) Submit campaign before start: **at least 5 days ahead**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) Asset moderation time: **typically within 48 hours** (Roblox review of submitted assets)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) Ad label legibility distance: **500 studs** (Label must be legible within this range of ad assets)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) Average CPTV charged by creators globally: **$4 to $5** (Typical rate creators charge brands)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) CPTV US (first 28 days): **$1.50** (Cost per 1,000 qualified visits)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) CPTV Tier 1 (first 28 days): **$0.75** (UK, Canada, Australia, NZ, Nordics etc.)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) CPTV Tier 2 (first 28 days): **$0.20** (Western Europe, Japan, South Korea)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) CPTV Tier 3 (first 28 days): **$0.05** (Global floor)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) CPTV after first 28 days: **$0.10** (Flat rate for rest of campaign, max 12 months)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) Campaign hard cap 2027: **$400,000** (Maximum cost for deals)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) Revenue share start: **January 1, 2027** (Applies to campaigns beginning on or after this date)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ad-integrations) Payment terms: **90 days** (Invoices issued quarterly; payment due after invoice)
