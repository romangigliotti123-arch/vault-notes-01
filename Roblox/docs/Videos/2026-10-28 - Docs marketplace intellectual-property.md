# Docs: marketplace / intellectual-property

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/intellectual-property.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official docs on intellectual property for avatar items. Explains copyright basics, that originality (not general concepts like baseball caps or top hats) determines protection, how to strengthen your own IP, Roblox's DMCA takedown process and consequences, and how Roblox-created items are treated.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/intellectual-property) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Make designs unique; copyright protects only original, creative elements, so the more distinctive your design, the stronger your takedown position against copies.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/intellectual-property) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Get permission from the original creator before replicating real-world or other-platform designs; crediting the owner does not grant usage rights.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/intellectual-property) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Add a unique trademark or branding to items to build your own brand on Roblox and visually differentiate them from copies.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/intellectual-property) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Only upload content you own or have the owner's permission to use; infringement violates both Roblox Terms of Use and the law.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/intellectual-property) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Infringing items are removed and repeat infringers terminated; creators lose Limited per-unit fees and Robux earned from sales of the removed item.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/intellectual-property) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not assume publishing a design found elsewhere gives you ownership of it on Roblox; others may still make similar designs.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/intellectual-property) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Only the rights holder or their authorized representative can file a takedown report; reports from third parties cannot be acted on.
