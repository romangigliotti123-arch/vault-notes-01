# Docs: production / publishing / publish-games-and-places

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Official Roblox docs on creating, publishing and releasing games and places: start place vs additional places, metadata best practices for name and description, early testers, target audience settings, release requirements for age groups, and optional publishing and expedited review fees.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Keep the game name consistent; renaming often reduces players' ability to find it under the previous name.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Avoid repeating words in the name (e.g. 'OBBY OBBY OBBY'); frequent repetition may cause demotion.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Use at most one or two well-placed emojis in the name; excessive decoration confuses players.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Summarize the genre and content in the first sentence of the description.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Include relevant keywords naturally in the description, but avoid keyword lists or irrelevant terms, which may cause demotion.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Use early testers (up to 10 similar-aged friends) for feedback before public release; the watermark marks them as preview.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Publish a place to the cloud early; it stores the data model and lets you work from any computer.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use File > Publish As with 'Add as a new place' to add gameplay areas; split large worlds into places for streaming and teleportation.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) **[[common-mistakes]]** When changing start place, use Publish As and choose Overwrite; do not use Publish, which overwrites the place itself.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) **[[common-mistakes]]** Keep places under 100 MB (104,857,600 bytes); larger places may fail to save or publish, usually due to inefficiencies.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) **[[roblox-platform-changes]]** Expect release requirements to apply to every update, new version, Publish As, and audience change, not just first release.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) **[[roblox-platform-changes]]** Release to all ages requires 2FA, age verification, and either 2 consecutive months of Premium or a one-time fee, plus Kids/Select review.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) **[[business-and-payouts]]** The 1,000 Robux publishing fee is refundable if the game reaches 25 highly engaged players for 60 days without moderation.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) **[[business-and-payouts]]** The 50,000 Robux expedited review fee is refundable after 90 days if the game stays in good standing with 25 highly engaged players.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Set 'Exclude from recommendations' under Audience > Advanced Settings to limit reach if you want fewer players.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) Max place size: **100 MB (104,857,600 bytes)** (Beyond this, Save/Publish to Roblox may fail)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) Early testers max: **10** (similar-aged friends invited per preview)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) Publishing fee: **1,000 Robux per game** (refundable; needs 25 highly engaged players for 60 days)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) Expedited review fee: **50,000 Robux per game** (refundable after 90 days if in good standing)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) Account age to release to 16+: **at least 2 days old** (also needs good standing and age check)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) Free game releases per day: **5 new games** (never-before-released games; resets daily)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) Premium subscription for all-ages: **2 consecutive months** (Roblox Plus or Premium, alternative to fees)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/publish-games-and-places) Expedited review duration: **48 hours** (minor violations restart the clock on resubmission)
