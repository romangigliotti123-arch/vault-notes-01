## Summary
Official Roblox Education tutorial step for the Mansion of Wonder carnival ride. It explains how to test the ride in Studio: press Play, join the ride via the Play It character with E, shoot enemies with left click, and stop testing with the Stop button or Shift+F5. Mostly a usage walkthrough, not game-design advice.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-mansion-of-wonder/test-and-play) **[[testing-debugging-and-qa]]** Press 1 during a test to free the mouse from the game; mouse clicks can't reach Studio while the ride is active.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-mansion-of-wonder/test-and-play) **[[testing-debugging-and-qa]]** Stop a playtest with the Stop button or Shift+F5 instead of closing the window.
