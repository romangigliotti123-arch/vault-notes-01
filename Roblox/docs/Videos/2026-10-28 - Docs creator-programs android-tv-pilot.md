## Summary
Roblox docs page for the Android TV Pilot Program: a 90-day, 25-creator program offering Roblox engineer support for D-pad/remote-friendly experiences on Android TV, with a possible "Play With Your Remote" featuring on completion.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/android-tv-pilot) **[[cross-platform-and-console]]** Android TV pilot favors experiences with straightforward controls that adapt to a D-pad: 2D games, simple movement, or menu-driven mechanics.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/android-tv-pilot) **[[cross-platform-and-console]]** Use TV Remote simulation in Studio plus hardware testing to validate input mapping before publishing for TV remote play.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/android-tv-pilot) **[[cross-platform-and-console]]** Plan input mapping and UI optimization for D-pad navigation; Roblox offers hands-on technical help for this in the pilot.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/android-tv-pilot) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Dedicate a team member to hit technical milestones and attend milestone reviews within the 90-day window.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/android-tv-pilot) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Actively maintain the experience and have capacity to test and publish updates when applying to the pilot.
