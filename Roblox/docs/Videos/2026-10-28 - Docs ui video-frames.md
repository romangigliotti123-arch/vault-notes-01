# Docs: ui / video-frames

## Summary
Roblox docs page on Video frames: uploading video assets (beta feature, 13+ ID verified, requirements, costs) and playing them via VideoFrame in ScreenGui/SurfaceGui/BillboardGui, with a code sample.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/video-frames) **[[roblox-platform-changes]]** Enable File > Beta Features > Video Uploads before uploading videos to Roblox.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/video-frames) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Only upload videos you have legal rights to use and that meet Roblox Community Standards and Terms of Use; non-compliant uploads are rejected.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/video-frames) **[[pricing]]** Each video upload costs 2,000 Robux, so budget for upload costs before adding video content.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/video-frames) **[[optimization-and-streaming]]** Keep videos at 5 minutes or less, 4096×2160 max resolution, under 3.75 GB; alpha channels are ignored, so use opaque footage.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/video-frames) Video upload cost: **2,000 Robux per upload**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/video-frames) Max video length: **5 minutes**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/video-frames) Max video resolution: **4096×2160**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/video-frames) Max video file size: **3.75 GB**
