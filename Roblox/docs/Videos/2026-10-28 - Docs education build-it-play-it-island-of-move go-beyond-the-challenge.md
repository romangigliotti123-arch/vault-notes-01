## Summary
Roblox documentation page closing out the "Build It, Play It: Island of Move" education lesson on animating a character and importing that motion into a simulator game. It points to further options: sharing animations, learning Roblox coding through traps and power-ups, designing new worlds, and adding new models.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/go-beyond-the-challenge) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Add new models from the asset library to change the scenery and keep the simulator world visually fresh.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/go-beyond-the-challenge) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Customize your game and create new environments to differentiate the simulator's world.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/go-beyond-the-challenge) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Share animations with friends or use animations created by others to speed up character motion work.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/go-beyond-the-challenge) **[[luau-language-and-types]]** Learn basic Luau coding by building simple traps and power-ups to extend gameplay.
