# Docs: production / publishing / ip-guidelines

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/ip-guidelines.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official IP Guidelines doc: how to file copyright and trademark notices, what to include, how removed-content users counter-notice or appeal, FAQs on AI-altered copies, off-platform infringement, and abuse of IP processes.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/ip-guidelines) **[[common-mistakes]]** Copyright infringement still applies even if you don't monetize, credit the creator, downloaded it free, bought a physical copy, or believed it was fair use.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/ip-guidelines) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't submit knowingly false copyright or counter notices; this is illegal under DMCA 512(f) and violates Roblox policy.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/ip-guidelines) **[[common-mistakes]]** Using AI to modify someone's work to avoid an exact copy still counts as infringement if it reproduces their protected expression.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/ip-guidelines) **[[team-and-budget]]** Link a verified email to your account so you receive copyright and trademark removal notifications promptly.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/ip-guidelines) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use only assets you own or are licensed to use in your game, since removed content can lead to account suspension for repeat infringement.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/ip-guidelines) **[[common-mistakes]]** Off-platform infringement (other sites) is generally not actioned by Roblox; report it through that platform's own process.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/ip-guidelines) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** If a copyright dispute leads to harassment or threats, report immediately; safety issues are handled separately under Community Standards.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/ip-guidelines) **[[common-mistakes]]** If your content is removed by mistake, submit a counter notice (copyright) or appeal by email to [email] (trademark), or contact the other party to withdraw the notice.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/ip-guidelines) Counter-notice restoration window: **Content generally restored within 14 business days unless reporter files lawsuit**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/ip-guidelines) Age requirement for self-submitting notices via IP Platform: **Must be over 18; under 13 requires parent/guardian**
