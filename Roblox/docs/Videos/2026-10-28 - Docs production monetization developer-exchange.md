# Docs: production / monetization / developer-exchange

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official DevEx docs: what counts as Earned Robux, exchange rates (0.0038 standard, 0.0054 for eligible U.S. 18+ verified players, legacy 0.0035 before Sept 5 2025), eligibility, cash-out steps, review timelines, and rejection/FAQ rules.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Cash out only once per calendar month; a completed DevEx request is limited to one per month.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Use your exact legal name in the DevEx form; nicknames or mismatched names can cause ineligibility or delays, and name discrepancies require Support contact, not resubmission.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Complete DevEx Portal registration (payment method and tax form) within one week of the invite, or the request is auto-declined and Robux refunded.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Keep DevEx Portal info accurate and up to date; inaccuracies affect payments.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Spending Robux does not reduce old-rate (pre-Sept 5 2025) balances; those must be cashed out first at 0.0035 before current-rate Earned Robux.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Do not submit another request after a name-discrepancy rejection; contact Roblox Support first, as ID verification may be required.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) **[[common-mistakes]]** Direct Robux purchases, subscription monthly grants, traded items, transfer-received Robux, gift cards, and template-game passes with no real visits do not count as Earned Robux.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) **[[monetisation-other]]** Earned Robux sources include developer products, passes, in-game subscriptions, paid private servers, paid access, ads within games, Creator Store sales, avatar items, and Creator Rewards.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Reaching eligibility requires at least 30,000 Earned Robux, a verified email, W-9 (US) or W-8 (non-US) on file, and age 13+.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Cash-out requests cannot be cancelled once submitted; the Robux is held during review and refunded only if rejected.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) Standard DevEx exchange rate: **0.0038 USD per Earned Robux (30,000 Robux = $114)**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) Higher rate for eligible U.S. 18+ verified players' purchases: **0.0054 USD per Earned Robux**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) Legacy rate for balances before Sept 5 2025 10am PT: **0.0035 USD per Earned Robux (30,000 = $105)**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) Minimum Earned Robux to cash out: **30,000**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) Minimum age: **13 years**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) Portal registration deadline after invite: **1 week**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) Review time, first-time participants: **~10 business days (estimate)**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) Review time, returning participants: **~5 business days (estimate)**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) Delay between sales and Earned Robux balance update: **up to 24 hours**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange) Max cash-out frequency: **once per calendar month**
