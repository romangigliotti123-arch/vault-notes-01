## Summary
Roblox docs page for the Notable Translators Program (Pilot), now deprecated. Lists eligibility (18+, Age ID verified, 2+ month old account, native-level language skill), the translation test process for English to German, Japanese, and Thai, and a disclaimer that Roblox does not employ or endorse translators or Job Creators.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/notable-translators) **[[localization-and-international]]** Program is deprecated; don't plan around Notable Translators as a hiring source. Hire translators through your own vetting and contracts.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/notable-translators) **[[localization-and-international]]** Vet translators directly: Roblox does not guarantee translation quality, payment, or completion of work. Protect yourself contractually.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/notable-translators) Minimum age: **18 years** (Eligibility requirement for Notable Translators candidates)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/notable-translators) Minimum account age: **2 months** (Roblox account age required for eligibility)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/notable-translators) Test submissions: **1 per candidate** (Only one translation test submission allowed per candidate)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/notable-translators) Test result timing: **within a few weeks** (Results emailed after test submission)
