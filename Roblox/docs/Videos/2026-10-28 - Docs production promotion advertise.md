# Docs: production / promotion / advertise

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official docs page on Ads Manager, the self-service advertising platform. Covers Sponsored Ads on Home and Search, audience targeting (new, recent, lapsed players), and in-game ad formats (Rewarded Video, Billboards, Portals) for ad revenue. Includes a Hypershot case study on creative testing.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Test many ad creatives and scale winners. Hypershot went from 10 to 25 unique creatives to find high-CTR visuals, then paused weaker ones.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use Ads Manager 'Plays' campaigns targeting All Players and Recent Players to drive plays; Hypershot says sponsored ads reached up to 10% of its plays.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Use lapsed and recent player audiences to re-engage players who already showed interest and give them a reason to return.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ramp ad traffic up or down to test features, support live updates, or scale; run always-on campaigns to optimise the user funnel.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise) **[[monetisation-other]]** Deploy in-game ads to earn from non-paying as well as paying players; Roblox handles delivery and earnings.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise) **[[monetisation-other]]** Choose in-game ad formats: Rewarded Videos (offer in-game rewards for watching), Billboards (image or video), or Portals (teleport to an advertised game).

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise) Impression lift: **150%** (Lift in impressions for games that run ads (Roblox-cited))
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise) Plays increase: **24%** (Increase in plays for games that run ads (Roblox-cited))
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise) Playtime increase: **16%** (Increase in playtime for games that run ads (Roblox-cited))
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise) Share of plays from sponsored ads: **up to 10%** (Hypershot, top-100 game, per Roblox case study)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/advertise) Creatives tested and scaled: **10 to 25** (Hypershot increased unique ad creatives to find high-CTR visuals)

## Games
