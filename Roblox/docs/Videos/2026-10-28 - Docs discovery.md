# Docs: discovery

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official Discovery docs: two-stage Recommended for You algorithm (retrieval, ranking), ranked engagement signals, Home Recommendations analytics, and discovery best practices and exposure limits.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Focus on retention and engagement of players who arrive via Recommended for You; only their behavior counts in the ranking stage.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Watch first-play bounce rate; a rise signals players aren't finding enough to stay engaged early.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check play-through rate, play days and playtime first in Analytics > Acquisition > Home Recommendations, then secondary signals.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery) **[[common-mistakes]]** Use a custom thumbnail; default-thumbnail games are ineligible for Home recommendation impressions.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Keep metadata accurate and don't lead titles with monetary rewards; mismatched or giveaway-led metadata reduces exposure.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid metadata and visuals that closely copy existing games; non-unique games rank lower.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery) Playtime cap for ranking: **60 minutes per user, per game, per day maximum**

## Tools
Creator Analytics dashboard, Home Recommendations analytics tab, Creator Dashboard
