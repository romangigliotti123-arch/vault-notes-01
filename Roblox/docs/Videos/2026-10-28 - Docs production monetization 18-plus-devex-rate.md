# Docs: production / monetization / 18-plus-devex-rate

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs for the U.S. 18+ DevEx rate (0.0054 per Earned Robux), effective June 8, 2026. Covers eligibility: verified 18+ U.S. players, R15-only or qualifying custom human/nonhuman characters that spend 100% of active playtime, R15 animation packs, group payout handling, and ongoing compliance checks.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate) **[[monetisation-other]]** To qualify, remove R6 entirely: if a player can spawn into or switch to R6 during active playtime, the game is ineligible for the 18+ DevEx rate.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate) **[[monetisation-other]]** Set Studio Avatar Settings to 'R15 Only' (Avatar tab > Settings > ellipsis > R15 Only) when using the platform avatar system.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate) **[[monetisation-other]]** Custom human-form characters need about 1 head, 2 legs, 2 arms, 12 limb parts or joints equally split among limbs, a torso with 2+ parts/joints, full bipedal range of motion, and each joint articulating separately.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate) **[[monetisation-other]]** Animation packs applied to player characters must be R15, not R6.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate) **[[monetisation-other]]** Custom avatar systems qualify automatically if they meet requirements and intent, but Roblox reviews games ongoing and removes eligibility if they stop qualifying.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate) **[[monetisation-other]]** Fix eligibility issues flagged by Roblox's background compliance check promptly; the game stays ineligible until resolved.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate) **[[monetisation-other]]** NPCs are not evaluated; only player characters count. Games without a visible player character meet the custom nonhuman-form criteria.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Group-owned games: one-time payouts and recurring splits respect the 18+ rate, and you can view the split between 18+ and standard rate amounts when cashing out.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate) DevEx exchange rate: **0.0054 USD per Earned Robux (U.S. 18+ players)** (Applies to eligible developer products, passes, subscriptions, private servers)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate) Effective date: **June 8, 2026** (Start of U.S. 18+ DevEx rate)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate) Limb parts/joints required: **12 equally distributed among limbs** (Custom human-form characters)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate) Torso parts/joints minimum: **2** (Custom human-form characters)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate) Parts or joints threshold: **15+ parts or 15+ joints (either suffices)** (Custom human-form character FAQ)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate) Active playtime character share: **100%** (Player characters must be compliant for all active playtime)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate) R15 advanced rig joints: **up to 37 additional joints** (Advanced R15 rigs; standard R15 has 15 joints)
