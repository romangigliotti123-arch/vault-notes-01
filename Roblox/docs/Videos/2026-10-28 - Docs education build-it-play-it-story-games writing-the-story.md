## Summary
Roblox Education docs page on writing the story for a "Build it, play it" story game. Covers brainstorming a 2-3 sentence opening paragraph and turning key words into numbered placeholders that players fill in. Beginner-level, no monetisation or growth advice.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-story-games/writing-the-story) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Keep the story idea simple: write 2-3 sentences for the opening paragraph before coding anything.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-story-games/writing-the-story) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Pick a single word in the first sentence (name, action, or noun) to become a player input placeholder.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-story-games/writing-the-story) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Number each placeholder (e.g. name1, name2) so multiple player inputs can be told apart when building the story.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-story-games/writing-the-story) Opening paragraph length: **2-3 sentences**

## Tools
Roblox Studio, text editor or paper for drafting
