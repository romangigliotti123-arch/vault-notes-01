# Docs: tutorials / curriculums / building / work-with-parts

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/building/work-with-parts.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox Creator Docs tutorial on inserting parts (block, sphere, cylinder, wedge, corner wedge), moving/scaling/rotating them with Move, Scale and Rotate tools, and anchoring them so they don't fall under gravity when playtesting an obby.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/building/work-with-parts) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Select the start platform and press F to focus the camera there so new parts insert near the start of the obby.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/building/work-with-parts) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Zoom and center the view where you want a part before inserting, for more control over placement; parts spawn at the exact center of the camera view.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/building/work-with-parts) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Keep jump distance between platforms in mind; if the early jumps are too hard, players may quit instead of continuing.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/building/work-with-parts) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Check each platform's position and orientation from multiple camera angles to confirm players can actually jump to it.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/building/work-with-parts) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Anchor platforms (Shift-select, then toggle Anchor) so they don't fall under gravity at playtest and stay put when players bump them.

## Tools
Roblox Studio, Part inserter, Move tool, Scale tool, Rotate tool, Anchor tool, AI Assistant
