## Summary
Roblox docs page on adding wings to a driftspeeder in the Galactic Speedway tutorial. Covers rotate/move snapping, rotating and scaling wing parts, mirroring the second wing, and keeping parts inside the blue canyon limits. Only one body part is allowed for racing; wings and other parts are unlimited.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/adding-wings) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Driftspeeder must have only one body, but can have as many wings and other parts as needed for race vehicles.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/adding-wings) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Enable Rotate snapping at 45° before positioning wings to align them quickly.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/adding-wings) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Press Ctrl+R (or ⌘+R) to rotate a selected part 90 degrees.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/adding-wings) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Keep all vehicle parts within the blue lines so the vehicle fits through the canyon while racing.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/adding-wings) Rotate snapping increment: **45°**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/adding-wings) Hotkey rotation amount: **90 degrees**
