# Docs: production / publishing / thumbnails

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs on thumbnails: specs (16:9, 1920×1080, up to 10 images/videos per game), video rules (authentic gameplay, 3 uploads/month quota), and thumbnail personalization for Home page (2–5 active thumbnails, qualified play-through rate metrics, keep multiple active, test new thumbnails per update).

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Upload 16:9 thumbnails at 1920×1080; other ratios get stretched. Keep essential text and elements off the bottom edge, where player count metadata covers them.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use authentic gameplay in video thumbnails. Misrepresented gameplay, artificial visuals, voiceovers, and promo claims like '50% off' get rejected and still count against the quota.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Activate 2–5 thumbnails to start Home page personalization. With only one active, every user sees the same thumbnail.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Keep multiple thumbnails active rather than picking one winner early; removing them too soon loses later impressions from changing user trends.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Test several new thumbnails with each major update, then leave them unchanged until the next update.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Judge thumbnails by Qualified Play Through Rate (qualified plays ÷ home page impressions), not impressions alone, and check Average Playtime per qualified play.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use camera work, minor post-processing, logo overlays and curated highlight sequences in videos, provided they show real mechanics.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid ambiguous or unclear imagery in thumbnails; it causes player confusion and fewer clicks.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) Thumbnails per game detail page: **up to 10 images or videos**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) Recommended thumbnail resolution: **1920×1080 (16:9), under 3 MB**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) Monthly video thumbnail upload quota: **3 uploads**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) Personalization active thumbnails: **2–5 (minimum 2 to start)**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails) Average qualified play-through rate lift from personalization: **+8.5% average; some games +50%**
