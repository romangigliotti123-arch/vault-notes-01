## Summary
Roblox docs page for the Galactic Speedway education tutorial. Covers painting terrain with the Terrain Paint tool, changing terrain material colors via Workspace.Terrain MaterialColors in Properties, and a closing quiz/badge and avatar-item reward. Aimed at beginners learning terrain editing.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/design-the-world) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use Terrain Paint to change a level's look (snow, water, lava) without altering terrain shape; paint changes appearance only.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/design-the-world) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Recolor terrain materials via Workspace.Terrain Properties > MaterialColors to create unique palettes from default materials like Snow.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/design-the-world) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Vary a map's season or mood by repainting existing landscape instead of rebuilding geometry; experiment with materials and brush sizes.

## Tools
Terrain Editor, Terrain Paint tool, Explorer, Properties panel
