# Docs: audio / assets

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs page on audio assets: finding free audio in the Creator Store, import requirements and limits for custom audio via Asset Manager, Creator Dashboard, and Open Cloud, and how songs vs. sound effects are classified and shown on game details pages.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Only import audio you have legal rights to; it must meet Community Rules and Terms of Use.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use Right-click > Copy Asset ID on Creator Store audio and paste into an AudioPlayer, not a Sound, for dynamic playback.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Keep imports to a single track in mp3, ogg, wav or flac, under 20 MB and 7 minutes, sample rate at most 48 kHz.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** If Studio rejects an import, update your audio tool (older tools write invalid headers) and re-check the requirements.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Imported audio stays private until you grant usage permissions to specific friends or experiences.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** To get a song shown on your game details page, meet moderation, duration, play count, age, title, ID verification and Audio Terms of Use requirements.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Give songs a meaningful, appropriate title and set Song Artist field to credit composers; blank defaults to your name.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets) Creator Store audio library size: **100,000+ sound effects and music tracks** (Free-to-use, made by Roblox and community, from partners)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets) Max audio file size: **20 MB** (Import requirement)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets) Max audio duration: **7 minutes** (Import requirement)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets) Max sample rate: **48 kHz** (Import requirement)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets) Free audio imports, ID verified: **2,000 per 30 days** (Replaces older daily limit)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets) Free audio imports, unverified: **100 per 30 days** (Replaces older daily limit)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets) Song preview length on song details page: **15 seconds** (Players hear this preview on the track's details page)
