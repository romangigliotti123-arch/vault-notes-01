# Docs: physics / constraints / rigid

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/physics/constraints/rigid.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs page on RigidConstraint: connects two Attachments or Bones to hold a fixed relative position/orientation, enabling accessory attachment on character rigs. Explains Studio Constraint-tool click behaviour and cancellation rules. Points to WeldConstraint for BasePart-to-BasePart joins.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/physics/constraints/rigid) **[[physics-pathfinding-and-npc-ai]]** Use RigidConstraint (not WeldConstraint) when connecting Attachments or Bones, e.g. attaching accessories to attachments on a character rig.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/physics/constraints/rigid) **[[building-and-level-design]]** With the Constraint tool, click existing Attachments or Bones to reuse them; clicking a BasePart creates a new Attachment on it. Any click off a BasePart, Attachment or Bone cancels the workflow.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/physics/constraints/rigid) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** To join two BaseParts together, use WeldConstraint instead of RigidConstraint.

## Tools
Roblox Studio Constraint tool, RigidConstraint, WeldConstraint
