## Summary
Official Roblox education landing page for the "Build It, Play It!" Mansion of Wonder template. Covers installing Roblox Studio, creating an account, basic account safety tips, and a three-part series where learners customize a magic blast weapon, with redeemable codes for avatar items after each lesson.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-mansion-of-wonder/beginner-landing-page) **[[common-mistakes]]** Never share your Roblox password, even with real-life friends; choose a password that isn't a trivial variant of your username.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-mansion-of-wonder/beginner-landing-page) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Report anyone asking for your password using the Report Abuse feature; Roblox employees never ask for passwords.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-mansion-of-wonder/beginner-landing-page) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Distrust anyone offering free Robux; there is no legitimate free Robux source.
