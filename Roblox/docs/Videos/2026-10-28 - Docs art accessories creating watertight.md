# Docs: art / accessories / creating / watertight

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/accessories/creating/watertight.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs tutorial on making a clothing mesh watertight by sealing open holes at neck, waist, and wrist in Blender-style Edit mode. Explains why non-watertight meshes expose backfaces that hurt rendering and performance, then lists the extrude-and-merge steps and suggests further clothing techniques to experiment with.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/accessories/creating/watertight) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Make accessory meshes watertight so only the visible outer surface is exposed; open holes expose backfaces that affect rendering and performance when equipped.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/accessories/creating/watertight) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** To seal a hole: Alt-click the last edge, press E to extrude, click once to add a small extrusion, right-click > Merge Vertices > At Center, then G to reposition the vertex.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/accessories/creating/watertight) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Repeat the seal process until every interior-exposing hole (neck, waist, wrist on a t-shirt) is closed before testing the asset on a character.
