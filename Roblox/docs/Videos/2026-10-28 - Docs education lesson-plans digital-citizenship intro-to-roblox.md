# Docs: education / lesson-plans / digital-citizenship / intro-to-roblox

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.

## Summary
Roblox Education lesson plan for an intro course: teaches private vs personal information and spotting unreliable info via a "Beat the Scammers" scavenger hunt, then a 25-minute guided Roblox Studio tutorial building a simple obstacle course (Explorer, camera rotation, anchoring).

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/intro-to-roblox) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Start new learners with a finished template (Obby) to show what they'll build before teaching tools.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/intro-to-roblox) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Rotate the camera view often to judge part placement accurately in 3D.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/intro-to-roblox) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use the Explorer panel as a core foundation for organizing and selecting objects in Studio.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/intro-to-roblox) **[[common-mistakes]]** If parts fall or disappear during playtest, turn on Anchoring for those parts.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/intro-to-roblox) Scavenger hunt exploration time: **about 5 minutes** (Students explore Beat the Scammers and collect clues on handout)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/intro-to-roblox) Studio tutorial length: **25 minutes** (Guided Intro to Roblox Studio obstacle course build)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/intro-to-roblox) Total lesson length: **60 minutes** (Sum of intro, digital safety, scavenger hunt, Studio, wrap-up)

## Games
- **Obby (template)**: Used as an opening demo of Roblox Studio and the kind of obstacle course students will build.
- **Beat the Scammers**: Played in a private server for a scavenger hunt identifying unreliable information and scam cues.

## Tools
Roblox Studio, Explorer, Roblox Studio Cheatsheet handout, Digital Safety Scavenger Hunt handout
