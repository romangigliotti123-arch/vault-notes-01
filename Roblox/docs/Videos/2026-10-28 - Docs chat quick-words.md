# Docs: chat / quick-words

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/chat/quick-words.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs for Quick Words, the built-in preset chat system. Covers setup under Creator Hub > Configure > Chat, category and word limits, the policy review before publishing, and the four approval states (Draft, Pending, Not approved, Published).

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/chat/quick-words) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Use Quick Words to let players coordinate gameplay (e.g. 'Need help', 'Go left') instead of open chat; phrases must not be open-ended questions.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/chat/quick-words) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't write Quick Words that are social chat or open-ended questions; Roblox review will reject them and you must revise and republish.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/chat/quick-words) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Save changes as a draft if you need more time; publish only when ready, since publishing triggers the policy review.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/chat/quick-words) **[[localization-and-international]]** Write Quick Words in English only; Roblox auto-translates them into each user's chosen chat language.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/chat/quick-words) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Keep each Quick Word under 25 characters so it fits the chat UI; use 3-10 words per category.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/chat/quick-words) Custom categories: **Up to 3** (Custom categories replace default Roblox categories; General remains available)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/chat/quick-words) Quick Words per category: **3 to 10** (Each custom category needs between three and ten words)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/chat/quick-words) Max characters per Quick Word: **Fewer than 25** (Applies to each individual Quick Word)
