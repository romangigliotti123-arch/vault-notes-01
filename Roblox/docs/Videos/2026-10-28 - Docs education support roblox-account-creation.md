# Docs: education / support / roblox-account-creation

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.

## Summary
Educator docs on creating a Roblox account and logging into Roblox Studio. Covers account safety basics: keep passwords private, avoid guessable passwords, ignore staff impersonators, and distrust free Robux claims. Includes Studio install and login steps. Not game-development advice beyond setup.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/support/roblox-account-creation) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Install Roblox Studio and confirm it runs on your computer before starting any build work.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/support/roblox-account-creation) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Never share your Roblox password, even with real-life friends.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/support/roblox-account-creation) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Make passwords hard to guess; do not just append digits to your username (e.g. bloxcool123).
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/support/roblox-account-creation) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Roblox employees never ask for passwords; report anyone who does using Report Abuse.
