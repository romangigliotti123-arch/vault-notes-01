## Summary
Official Roblox Education doc (Build It, Play It: Island of Move) on creating the first pose of a character animation. Explains rotating body parts/joints via handles, troubleshooting handle visibility and click-blocking, undoing changes, and reviewing the pose from multiple camera angles.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/create-the-first-pose) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** To rotate a body part, click it to show handles, then drag a handle; each handle rotates in a different direction.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/create-the-first-pose) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** If you can't click a part's rotation handles, rotate the camera until a blue box appears on hover; another part may be blocking the click.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/create-the-first-pose) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** If you see arrows instead of circular handles, enable the Rotate tool by pressing R.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/create-the-first-pose) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Undo any pose change with Ctrl+Z (or Cmd+Z on Mac) while posing.
