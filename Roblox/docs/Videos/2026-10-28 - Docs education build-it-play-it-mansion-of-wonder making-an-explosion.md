## Summary
Roblox Creator Docs tutorial (Mansion of Wonder, "Build it, play it" education path) on making an explosion: change ExplosionParticles color and Texture asset ID, then playtest to fine-tune the look. Mostly a beginner VFX walkthrough.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-mansion-of-wonder/making-an-explosion) **[[vfx-and-particles]]** Edit the ExplosionParticles object's Color and Texture properties to restyle explosions; pick a color that matches or contrasts with the blast.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-mansion-of-wonder/making-an-explosion) **[[vfx-and-particles]]** Explosion texture can match or differ from the magic blast texture; reuse the same skill (color, size, texture) across effects.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-mansion-of-wonder/making-an-explosion) **[[testing-debugging-and-qa]]** Particle colors and textures only show accurately in play mode, so test the game after every visual tweak.
