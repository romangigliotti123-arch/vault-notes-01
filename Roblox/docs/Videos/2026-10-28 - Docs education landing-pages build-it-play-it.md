# Docs: education / landing-pages / build-it-play-it

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.

## Summary
Roblox docs page listing five "Build It, Play It" beginner workshop series (effects, animation, 3D modeling, level design, scripting). Each challenge takes under an hour with step-by-step tutorials and a game to customize. Classroom lesson plans are provided for ages 10 and up. Contains no monetisation, growth, or publishing advice.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/landing-pages/build-it-play-it) Time per challenge: **Often less than 1 hour** (Beginner Build It, Play It workshop, per challenge, per docs)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/landing-pages/build-it-play-it) Target age for classroom lesson plans: **Ages 10 and up** (Lesson plans provided for classrooms; self-directed also possible)

## Games
- **Mansion of Wonder**: Special effects: magical blasts in an arcade game, and arrow beams that guide new players around a world.
- **Island of Move**: Animation: build a simulator where players move to gain points, e.g. swimming through air or dancing along a track.
- **Galactic Speedway**: 3D modeling: build a spaceship to race in a multiplayer game with friends.
