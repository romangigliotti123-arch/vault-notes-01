# Docs: marketplace / marketplace-fees-and-commissions

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official docs on Marketplace fees and commissions: upload fees, publishing advances (refundable, rebated from platform commission), creator/game-owner/Roblox revenue splits, progressive revenue share by price floor, 30-day escrow, Limited per-unit fees, reselling splits, rate limits, timed options, and price ranges.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Expect a 30-day escrow hold on every sale's commission; rebates are the exception and are not escrowed.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) **[[pricing]]** Price avatar items above the asset type's floor to earn more: at 2x the floor creators get 50% instead of 30%. Marketplace only; in-game sales stay at 30%.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) **[[monetisation-other]]** Sell avatar items in games you own to earn the 40% game-owner commission on in-game purchases via Avatar Inspect Menu, Avatar Editor Service, or MarketplaceService.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) **[[common-mistakes]]** Upload fees are not refunded if moderation rejects the item, so check guidelines before paying the 80 Robux (500 for emissive) fee.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) **[[monetisation-other]]** Budget for the publishing advance: refundable upfront fee recouped via platform commission rebates, paid back after the item's listed amount is recovered.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) **[[common-mistakes]]** Free Limited items cost a per-unit fee that Roblox keeps entirely; listing 200 hats at 100 Robux per unit costs 20,000 Robux.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) **[[monetisation-other]]** Enable timed options (3, 7, or 14 day rentals at lower cost) on 3D shirts, pants, and sweaters to add cheaper purchase tiers.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) Upload fee: **80 Robux per submission; 500 Robux for emissive mask items** (Required after ID verification before moderation review)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) Creator commission (Marketplace / in-game): **30% / 30% (game owner gets 40% in-game)** (Standard split; Roblox takes remainder)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) Progressive creator share: **1x floor 30%; 2x 50%; 3x 62%; 5x 69%; 6x+ capped 70%** (Marketplace purchases only; regional pricing does not affect share)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) Body publishing advance: **2500 non-limited; 20000 paid limited** (Hats 1500 non-limited; Shirts/pants 600; Makeup 1000)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) Emissive non-limited publishing advance: **5x standard non-limited rate** (Limited advances unchanged)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) Escrow hold: **30 days from sale date** (Does not apply to publishing advance rebates)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) Reselling Limited split (community-created): **50% reseller, 10% creator, 10% seller/affiliate, 30% Roblox** (Original creator's own resales: 10% creator commission per resale)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) Limited purchase rate limits: **9 purchases/min/user; 1 request per 2s per item; max 3 per user per item** (Anti-bot limits for Limited items)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) In-game Limited purchase requirement: **60 seconds in game before purchase completes** (Anti-automation)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions) Reselling holding period: **Up to 30 days after purchase** (Limited items cannot be resold during hold)
