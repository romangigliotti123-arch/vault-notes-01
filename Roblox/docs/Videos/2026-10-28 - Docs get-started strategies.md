# Docs: get-started / strategies

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Official Roblox docs "Recommended strategies" for developers: assemble a team, pick proven genres, study similar games and players, define KPIs (D1, D7, D30 retention, playtime, conversion, ARPPU), design for mobile and low-end devices, build an MVP and playtest early, use community Discord, plan updates and events, and use sponsored ads with earned Robux.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[team-and-budget]]** Recruit developers with expertise in game design, 3D modeling, scripting, and optimization from the Roblox community.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Choose a proven genre (RPGs, simulators, obbies, survival) to raise the likelihood of attracting and retaining players.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Define KPIs (engagement, retention, monetization) before development begins so they guide design from the start.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Let users answer what makes the game fun, different, and replayable within the first few minutes of play.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Avoid long tutorials; if needed, keep them short, contextual, and interactive.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Don't restrict socialization with individual server instances; let friends play together.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build a core loop of satisfying, repeatable, progressively challenging action-reward cycles.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build reusable systems (quest, collection, event frameworks) to cut future update cost and time.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give players a mix of short-, mid-, and long-term goals to suit different playstyles and reduce churn.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Ensure enough content is available upfront so new users can have fun in their first session when gating progression.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Offer virtual items at multiple price points and a starter pack aimed at new players.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Favor repeatable purchases (consumables) over one-time purchases since spending is not capped.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make the shop transparent and frictionless; clearly communicate item value and availability.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build a minimal MVP that demonstrates the core loop, then get it to players early to iterate and avoid late pivots.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Use small group playtests to check players understand the game and find it fun.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Run a limited-time public test (weekend/week) to get organic D1 retention and session length; then remove the game.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[community-and-discord]]** Set up a moderated community chat server linked to your game for dedicated members and feedback.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Compare analytics to target KPIs, find causes via community, change, retest; repeat until KPIs hit targets.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Optimize performance; many users are on low-end devices and non-performant games struggle to grow.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Design UI that works across PC, mobile, and console, and make it touch-friendly even for PC-leaning genres.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Strong packaging (icon, thumbnail, media, description) can improve D1 retention and play-through rate.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Spend earned Robux on ad campaigns rather than purchased Robux, since the exchange rate is higher for earned Robux.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[updates-live-ops]]** If sustainable, ship minor content updates every 1–2 weeks and major system updates every 2–3 months.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Plan a 6–12 month roadmap of content and systems, scoped to team capacity.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) Early organic KPI: **D1 retention and average playtime per user (avg sessions/day × session length)** (Organic social growth KPIs listed by docs)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) Sustained KPI: **D7 and D30 retention, conversion (% of users spending Robux), ARPPU** (Sustained growth KPIs listed by docs)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) Minor content update cadence: **every 1–2 weeks** (If sustainable for the team)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) Major system update cadence: **every 2–3 months** (If sustainable for the team)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) Roadmap horizon: **6 to 12 months** (Planned content roadmap)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) Limited-time public test length: **a weekend, week, etc.** (Then the test game is removed)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies) Robux exchange rate: **higher for earned Robux than purchased** (Use earned Robux for ad campaigns)
