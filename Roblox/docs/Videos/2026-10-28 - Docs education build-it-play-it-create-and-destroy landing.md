## Summary
Roblox Education lesson landing page for "Create and Destroy": a beginner course teaching a multiplayer map where players destroy cities with laser beams for points. Covers installing Roblox Studio, account safety, and three quiz-rewarded lessons (Getting Started, Build and Test, Polish and Publish).

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-create-and-destroy/landing) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use a 3-button mouse with scroll wheel for navigating 3D space in Roblox Studio.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-create-and-destroy/landing) **[[common-mistakes]]** Never share your Roblox password, even with real-life friends; choose a password that isn't a trivial variant of your username.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-create-and-destroy/landing) **[[common-mistakes]]** Roblox employees never ask for your password; report anyone who does via Report Abuse. There is no such thing as free Robux.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-create-and-destroy/landing) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Practise by building a multiplayer combat map first; the skills transfer to building battle royales or other combat games.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-create-and-destroy/landing) Lessons in course: **3** (Getting Started, Build and Test, Polish and Publish; quiz after each for prizes)

## Games
- **Create and Destroy Test Map**: Finished reference version of the tutorial game; players destroy cities with laser beams for points.
