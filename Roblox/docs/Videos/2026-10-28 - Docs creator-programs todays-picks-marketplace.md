## Summary
Roblox creator-programs docs page on Today's Picks on Marketplace, a curated avatar-item sort chosen daily by in-house curators plus the recommendation system. Lists eligibility (17+, ID-verified, meets Community Standards and Marketplace Policy) and states that submissions are reviewed weekly via a nomination survey, with only selected creators contacted.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/todays-picks-marketplace) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** To get Today's Picks on Marketplace, you must be 17+, ID-verified, and meet Community Standards and Marketplace Policy before nominating an item.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/todays-picks-marketplace) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Nominate avatar items via the survey; submissions are reviewed weekly. If not contacted, reapply when you have new items to submit.

## Numbers
