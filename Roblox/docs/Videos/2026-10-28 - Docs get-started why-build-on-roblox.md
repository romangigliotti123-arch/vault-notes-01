## Summary
Roblox docs page "Why should I develop on Roblox?" summarising platform value: free tools, platform handles hosting/payments/fees, built-in multiplayer, servers up to 100 players, expanded free storage with paid scaling, no publishing approvals with automatic moderation, built-in free analytics, and paid UA system for traffic insights.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/why-build-on-roblox) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Start immediately with Roblox Studio and built-in services; no upfront tooling costs are required to begin building.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/why-build-on-roblox) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Lean on built-in multiplayer: every game supports multiplayer by default, so no custom backend or self-hosted servers are needed.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/why-build-on-roblox) **[[team-and-budget]]** You don't need a big team to make a big game; the platform handles hosting, storage, and support so a small team can focus on gameplay.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/why-build-on-roblox) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Publish updates without a publishing approval queue; assets are auto-moderated so you can ship to console, desktop and mobile immediately.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/why-build-on-roblox) Max players per server: **100** (Servers support up to 100 players per server)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/why-build-on-roblox) Upfront costs: **None** (Tools like Roblox Studio are free; hosting and fees covered by platform)

## Tools
Roblox Studio, Built-in Roblox analytics, Paid UA system
