# Docs: production / game-design / design-for-roblox

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Official Roblox docs page on designing for Roblox across three lenses: behavior (tourists vs locals, FTUE, social play), audience (younger vs older users, influencers), and engine (mobile-first performance, UI/UX, genre examples table).

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Get players into the fun quickly; lengthy, detailed tutorials bore users and cause them to bounce, since joining and leaving games is easy.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make tutorials as visual as possible; this helps less experienced readers and simplifies translation.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Match the design patterns and UX of the most popular games in your genre so seasoned users need less explanation upfront.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build social interaction into gameplay (parties, trading) so players invite friends; single-player games often struggle to build and retain an audience.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Social gameplay aids retention because players become evergreen content for each other; spontaneous interactions bring users back.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not change the shared Roblox avatar identity without a good reason; users invest heavily in avatars and want them seen by friends.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Do not price private servers prohibitively; expensive private servers discourage groups of friends from playing together.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[monetisation-other]]** Design monetization for tourists as well as locals; many locals start as tourists and convert later.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Strange and innovative games can succeed on Roblox more than elsewhere, though iterating on proven designs and themes remains relevant.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Prioritise fun over visual fidelity; younger users are less sensitive to graphics and stay if they enjoy the game.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not just port a game from another platform; adjust for Roblox by slimming upfront tutorials and emphasising social gameplay.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Design with streamers in mind: make it easy for them to involve friends, and consider features like hiding UI for recording (Brookhaven's Creator Cam).
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Watch performance on low-end devices; most users are on mobile and are sensitive to friction and load times.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use consistent icons and keep UI visual rather than text-heavy; many younger users are reading-averse.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use solid modeling and the Toolbox to prototype quickly for testing and iteration.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Design UI and UX mobile-first; bind common actions to gamepad buttons and keyboard keys if targeting console or PC.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Replicate common Roblox UI patterns (e.g. inventory slots along the bottom) so experienced users understand the interface intuitively.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox) **[[common-mistakes]]** Test UI regularly so it does not conflict with core Roblox UI like chat and player list, or disable what you do not need.

## Games
- **Brookhaven**: Roleplay that mirrors playing house; social interaction drives spontaneous content and retention; includes Creator Cam for streamers.
- **Islands**: Building set similar to Lego; appeals to younger exploration-driven users.
- **Jailbreak**: Deeper version of cops and robbers; childhood-experience framing.
- **Phantom Forces**: Realistic multiplayer FPS; example of a game appealing to older users.
- **Arsenal**: Fast-paced arcade shooter with rotating weapons; appeals to older users.
- **Doors**: Procedural survival horror; example of older-audience success.
- **Fashion Famous**: Example of the customization genre, centred on dressing up and shopping for clothes.
- **Piggy**: Example of hide and seek genre, combined with puzzle elements.
- **Tower of Hell**: Example of obby genre (obstacle course).
- **Bee Swarm Simulator**: Example of simulator genre: repetitive actions to upgrade items.
- **Natural Disaster Survival**: Example of survival genre: escaping environmental hazards.
- **Lumber Tycoon 2**: Example of tycoon genre: droppers produce currency to unlock builds.
