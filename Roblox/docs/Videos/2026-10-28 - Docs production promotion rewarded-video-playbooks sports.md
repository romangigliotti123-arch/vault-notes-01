# Docs: production / promotion / rewarded-video-playbooks / sports

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/sports.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs playbook on Rewarded Video ads for sports games. Covers what rewarded video is, key metrics (opt-in rate, daily frequency), and four strategies: post-match boost, currency reward, rematch rescue, pre-game edge. Includes placement and session-only boost guidance.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/sports) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Place rewarded video prompts on high-traffic surfaces: HUD, pre-match lobby, and loss screen. Buried placements kill engagement rates.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/sports) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Time ad triggers to emotional moments: before a match (anticipation), after a loss (frustration), after a win (reward). Contextual timing beats a static button.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/sports) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Offer post-match ad option to double coins or XP just earned. Players are invested in their result, win or lose.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/sports) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Add a free-currency-via-ad option in the store/HUD next to paid coin bundles so ads feel like earning, not interruption.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/sports) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** On the loss screen, offer an ad to instantly re-enter a match instead of waiting. Targets loss aversion.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/sports) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Make ad-bought boosts last one match or one session only. Permanent rewards create pay-to-win perception and hurt competitive balance.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/sports) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Offer a short pre-match boost (speed, stamina, or power) activated by watching an ad; keep it session-scoped to preserve balance.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/sports) Opt-in rate target: **15%+ of eligible users engage with the ad** (Share of total eligible users who engage with rewarded video)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/sports) Daily frequency target: **3+ views per user per day** (How many times each user views the ad daily)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/sports) Ad length: **6-30 second full-screen horizontal video** (Rewarded Video placement format, user-chosen for a reward)

## Games
- **NFL Universe Football**: Uses the currency-reward strategy for rewarded video ads.
- **Blue Lock Rivals**: Combines currency reward with progressive unlock for rewarded video.
