## Summary
Roblox docs page for NoCollisionConstraint. It disables collisions between two specific parts while they still collide with the rest of the world. Contrasted with collision groups; example is a car wheel not scraping its body.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/physics/constraints/no-collision) **[[physics-pathfinding-and-npc-ai]]** Use NoCollisionConstraint to disable collision between two specific parts, e.g. a vehicle wheel and its body, instead of a broad collision group.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/physics/constraints/no-collision) **[[physics-pathfinding-and-npc-ai]]** NoCollisionConstraint only affects the two parts it connects; they still collide with all other world parts.
