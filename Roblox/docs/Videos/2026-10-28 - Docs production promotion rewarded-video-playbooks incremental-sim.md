# Docs: production / promotion / rewarded-video-playbooks / incremental-sim

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/incremental-sim.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs playbook for Rewarded Video in incremental sims. Defines rewarded video (6-30s full-screen ads users opt into for a developer-set reward), key metrics (opt-in rate 13%+, frequency 2.8/day), and four reward strategies: velocity boost, welcome-back doubling, resource refill, timed automation. Cites Sol's Fast Roll, Fisch Luck Boost, Gym Star Simulator as examples.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/incremental-sim) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Track opt-in rate and daily frequency as the two key metrics driving rewarded video earnings.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/incremental-sim) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use rewarded video to bridge friction (waiting time between upgrades) for non-paying players while keeping whales on premium options.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/incremental-sim) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Offer a velocity reward (temporary speed/multiplier boost) to shorten grind in incremental games.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/incremental-sim) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Offer a welcome-back reward that doubles offline earnings on re-entry.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/incremental-sim) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Offer an instant refill of energy or stamina meters that limit grinding.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/incremental-sim) **[[monetisation-other]]** Let players watch ads to temporarily unlock automation (e.g. Auto-Click or Auto-Sell for 10 minutes); this also tests premium features before purchase.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/incremental-sim) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use multiple rewarded video placements; multi-placement strategies work best.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/incremental-sim) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Keep the rewarded video UI clean with a clear value proposition.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/incremental-sim) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Balance rewards for player value against economic impact on your game economy.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/incremental-sim) Opt-in rate: **13%+ of eligible users engage with the ad** (Benchmark stated in Roblox docs for rewarded video)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/incremental-sim) Frequency: **2.8 views per user per day** (Average times each user views the ad daily)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/incremental-sim) Ad length: **6-30 seconds, full-screen, horizontal** (Rewarded video format definition)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/incremental-sim) Automation unlock duration: **10 minutes** (Example duration for Auto-Click/Auto-Sell reward)

## Games
- **Sol's Fast Roll**: Uses automation as its rewarded video strategy.
- **Fisch Luck Boost**: Uses velocity reward (luck boost) as its rewarded video strategy.
- **Gym Star Simulator**: Uses progression jump as its rewarded video strategy.
