# Docs: education / developer / beta-testing-experiences

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox Education docs on beta testing. Compares four beta methods (closed access, open paid access, limited time test, open access beta), with benefits, considerations, and criteria for when to launch a beta. Covers feedback channels (in-game forms, Discord, think-aloud), analytics to track, and FAQ on beta length and community building.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Launch a beta only when the game is playable end to end, performs on all target platforms, and you have people dedicated to handling feedback.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** If unsure which beta type to use, start with closed access testing; it is the safest and still surfaces bugs and gameplay issues.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Use closed access betas via a Roblox group with role-based access to test unpolished features with a trusted, engaged group before going wider.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Use a limited time test window (e.g. a weekend) to get short-term D1 retention, playtime and session length without a month-long beta commitment.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** For a limited time test, consider spending roughly $100-250 USD on Roblox ad space to drive traffic to the test window.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** In an open access beta, tag the game title with Beta and use in-game pop-ups to set expectations about bugs and unfinished features.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Enable Exclude from Recommendations during an open beta if you do not want it shown under Recommended For You.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build a community (Discord, Twitter) before beta testing; screenshots, videos or livestreams are enough to start, and it makes sourcing testers easier.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Design in-game feedback surveys carefully: leading questions skew data, and someone must clean responses before use.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[community-and-discord]]** Study large community servers such as Adopt Me's Discord to decide channel structure and expected engagement patterns.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Run think-aloud sessions with participants from your target age group and use a script to avoid biasing them.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track onboarding and tutorial completion, drop-off points, D1/D7 retention, session length, and age demographics during a beta.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check that the beta's actual age mix matches your intended target age; a mismatch signals a design or marketing problem.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Aim for at least an 80% like ratio after a beta closes; likes strongly influence whether new players try the game.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan a beta for 7 to 30 days, long enough to measure D1 and D30 retention.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) **[[community-and-discord]]** Consider rewarding players with badges for meeting the developer in-game, a common Roblox culture practice.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) Ad spend for limited time test: **~$100-250 USD** (Roblox ad space to generate traffic during a limited-time test window)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) Like ratio after beta: **at least 80%** (Recommended target after closing an open beta program)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) Beta duration: **7 to 30 days** (General timeframe needed to get D1 and D30 retention data)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/developer/beta-testing-experiences) Limited test retention metrics: **D1 retention, playtime, session length** (Short-term data gathered over a weekend limited time test)
