# Docs: art

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox documentation hub for 3D art: Studio art tools, third-party asset import (Blender/Maya FBX), avatar item creation and Marketplace, environmental art tutorials, staff articles on terrain, skyboxes, alpha-mask PBR textures, seamless audio loops, and ZBrush symmetry, plus reference specs and rigging resources.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use alpha masks in PBR textures to customize colors instead of duplicating textures; staff article says this saves time and optimizes performance.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Check General mesh specifications and Blender/Maya FBX export settings before importing third-party models to avoid import problems.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Follow Texturing requirements and PBR texture guidance so materials render correctly in Studio.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Assemble modular environments and light with props, following the environmental art curriculum, to build high-quality levels.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Use Studio Terrain Editor and terrain material tools to build landscapes such as buttes and mesas.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art) **[[sound-and-music]]** Make seamless audio loops with the staff technique for ambience, music and sound effects.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art) **[[sound-and-music]]** Add 3D audio or 2D audio depending on whether the sound should be spatial.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Follow the Avatar character specifications, Rigid and Layered accessory specifications, and Marketplace policy before publishing avatar items.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Use the Animation Editor and Animation Capture tools for animation; export Maya animations and use inverse kinematics where needed.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Review Marketplace fees and commissions before publishing avatar items to the Marketplace.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Use the avatar item templates and tutorials (accessories, clothes, characters) as a starting point for sellable items.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Rig basic meshes and skin facial bones with the provided rigging overview and auto skin transfer guidance.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use the Importer, Accessory Fitting Tool, Material Generator and Texture Generator Studio tools to speed up asset pipelines.
