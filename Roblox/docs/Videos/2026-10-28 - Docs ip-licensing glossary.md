# Docs: ip-licensing / glossary

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/glossary.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox Creator Hub glossary for the IP licensing system: defines IP families, licenses (public/private), revenue share rate and timing, standard vs conditional offers, conditional approval, and license showcases.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/glossary) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Create only one IP family per IP; the primary keyword should be the IP's official name, with secondary keywords for characters, places and objects.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/glossary) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Offer several licenses per IP family (e.g. full license, revenue share, maturity-limited) so creators can pick terms that fit their game.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/glossary) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Choose revenue share timing deliberately: 'Monetize on activation' applies share immediately, 'Monetize later' lets the rights holder switch it on at a chosen date.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/glossary) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Use a conditional offer when a game needs content changes first; creators get 14 days to make changes and mark them done, and offers do not auto-activate.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/glossary) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Standard offers auto-activate after 14 days if the creator does not dispute them; creators can accept earlier.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/glossary) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Set private licenses for creators you reach out to directly; public licenses are visible to all creators on the Licenses page.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/glossary) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Add a license showcase of up to 10 games with existing agreements to highlight them on a license's details page.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/glossary) Conditional/standard offer response window: **14 days**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/glossary) Standard offer auto-activation: **after 14 days if not disputed**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/glossary) License showcase max games: **10**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ip-licensing/glossary) Revenue share base: **percentage of gross Robux earned by the game after platform fees, from agreement activation**
