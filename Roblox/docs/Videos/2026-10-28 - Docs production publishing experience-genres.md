# Docs: production / publishing / experience-genres

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/experience-genres.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox Creator Hub reference on experience genres and subgenres: where genres appear, the once-per-three-months change limit, how to change genre in the Creator Dashboard, Roblox's genre review and appeal process, best-practice selection rules, and descriptions of each genre and subgenre.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/experience-genres) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Only pick a genre once you are confident what your game is; genre changes are limited to once every three months.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/experience-genres) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** When multiple genres fit, pick the one matching the game's primary purpose or core gameplay loop.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/experience-genres) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Select a subgenre when one applies to describe core mechanics more precisely; a genre without subgenre is acceptable if none fits.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/experience-genres) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Genre affects placement in genre-specific top and trending sorts on the Charts page; allow a few days for genre changes to reflect there.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/experience-genres) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not submit a new genre selection while a genre appeal is pending, as it cancels the original appeal review.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/experience-genres) Genre change limit: **once every three months** (Creator Dashboard genre changes are restricted to this interval)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/experience-genres) Genre change propagation: **a few days** (Time for genre change to reflect in Charts genre sorts)
