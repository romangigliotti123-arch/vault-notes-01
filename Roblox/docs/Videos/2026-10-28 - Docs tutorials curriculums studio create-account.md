## Summary
Official Roblox docs page on creating a Roblox account: sign-up steps and account safety tips (password hygiene, no staff asking for passwords, no free Robux). Points to installing Roblox Studio next.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/studio/create-account) **[[common-mistakes]]** Never share your password, even with real-life friends; avoid passwords that just append digits to your username.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/studio/create-account) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Roblox employees never ask for your password; report anyone who does using Report Abuse.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/studio/create-account) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Ignore anyone or any site claiming free Robux; treat such offers as scams.
