## Summary
Roblox Studio education challenge page "Island of Move" for building a simple movement-based simulator game in three tutorial lessons. Covers installing Studio, creating an account, and redeeming a code after each lesson for avatar prizes. Includes account safety tips.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/beginner-landing-page) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Download Roblox Studio (free, PC or Mac) to build any Roblox game; a 2-button mouse with scroll wheel is strongly recommended.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/beginner-landing-page) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Beginner project idea: a simulator where players gain points by moving, with the movement style defined by the creator (swimming, dancing, etc.).
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/beginner-landing-page) **[[common-mistakes]]** Never share your Roblox password, even with real-life friends; use a password that is not guessable from your username.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/beginner-landing-page) **[[common-mistakes]]** Roblox employees never ask for passwords; report anyone who does via Report Abuse. There is no free Robux; ignore sites or players claiming otherwise.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/beginner-landing-page) Tutorial lessons: **3 step-by-step lessons, each yielding a redeemable code**
