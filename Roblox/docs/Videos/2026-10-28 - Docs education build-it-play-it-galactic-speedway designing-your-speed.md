## Summary
Roblox Education docs page for the Galactic Speedway lesson on designing a driftspeeder. Covers the three build steps (body, wings, decorations), salvaging parts from the scrapyard sections, turning off collisions and move snapping, and camera controls.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/designing-your-speeder) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Turn off Collisions in the Geometric toolbar and turn off Move snapping before positioning parts so they pass through each other and move freely.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/designing-your-speeder) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use F to focus the camera on a selected part; use WASD, right-drag, and scroll to move, rotate, and zoom while building.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-galactic-speedway/designing-your-speeder) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Click inside the game world if the camera stops responding to WASD.

## Games
- **Galactic Speedway**: Education tutorial game; players build a driftspeeder in three steps: body, wings, decorations.
