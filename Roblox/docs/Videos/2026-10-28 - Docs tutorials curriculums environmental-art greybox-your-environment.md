# Docs: tutorials / curriculums / environmental-art / greybox-your-environment

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/greybox-your-environment.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Official Roblox docs section on greyboxing (massing out) a three-lane, team-based FPS laser tag map: layout components, sizing rules (10-stud minimum widths/wall heights), placeholder color coding for orientation, and playtesting checklist and simulation modes.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/greybox-your-environment) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Greybox with simple block and wedge parts first to find layout issues (hard traversal, unfair vantage points, bad scale) before polishing art.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/greybox-your-environment) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Set transform snapping to 5 studs and 90° so all greybox geometry aligns on 5-stud increments.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/greybox-your-environment) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Keep doorways and hallways at least 10 studs wide and walls at least 10 studs tall so two users pass concurrently, default 5-stud jump can't clear walls, and the camera doesn't clip.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/greybox-your-environment) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Use elevation changes (rises and drops) to control sight lines and engagement distances beyond horizontal movement.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/greybox-your-environment) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Give spawn zones two exit points; one causes bottlenecks, three give too little cover after match start.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/greybox-your-environment) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Limit most combat pockets to at most three entrance/exit points to avoid choice overload.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/greybox-your-environment) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Place cross lanes with minimal cover so they act as transitory spaces that discourage lingering.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/greybox-your-environment) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Apply distinct placeholder colors to key walls and floors so players can deduce their location and orientation from any visible color.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/greybox-your-environment) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Playtest the layout at nearly every development step to catch issues before they grow costly; check team advantage, orientation, choice overload, and bypassable areas.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/environmental-art/greybox-your-environment) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not run a single flat floor with no elevation change; it leaves players little strategy beyond hiding from line of sight.
