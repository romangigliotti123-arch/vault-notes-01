# Docs: creator-programs / creator-affiliate

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-affiliate.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs page on the Creator Affiliate Pilot Program. Creators earn up to 50% of spend from new users who sign up via affiliate links and buy Robux, capped at $100 per user over six months. Page notes the program is deprecated as of July 24, 2025 and replaced by Creator Rewards; application status is closed.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-affiliate) **[[monetisation-other]]** Creator Affiliate earnings were capped at $100 USD per new user, based on their first six months of Robux purchases. Program is deprecated; use Creator Rewards instead.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-affiliate) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** Use hyperlinked anchor text (e.g. 'Join Roblox for free') rather than raw links; if sharing a bare link, use a custom URL shortener.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-affiliate) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** Include an FTC disclosure stating you may earn a commission from the affiliate link wherever you share it.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-affiliate) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** Share affiliate links on YouTube videos/descriptions/about page, social posts, website, newsletters and blogs.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-affiliate) Affiliate revenue share: **up to 50% of spend** (New users' Robux spend via affiliate links, Creator Affiliate pilot)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-affiliate) Earnings cap per new user: **$100 USD** (Based on first six months of Robux purchases)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-affiliate) Minimum age: **13 (parental consent under 18)** (Creator Affiliate pilot requirement)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-affiliate) Payout currency: **Robux, exchangeable via DevEx** (Affiliate earnings paid in Robux)
