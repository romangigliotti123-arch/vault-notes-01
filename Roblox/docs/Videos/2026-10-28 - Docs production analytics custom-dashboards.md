# Docs: production / analytics / custom-dashboards

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.

## Summary
Roblox Creator Docs page on Custom dashboards in Analytics. Covers creating up to 20 dashboards per experience, adding charts from custom dashboards, Explore, or other dashboards, summary card aggregation options, and pinning dashboards to the sidebar. Publish is required to save.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/custom-dashboards) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Group dashboards by purpose, e.g. Economy Health, Live-Ops, or Retention, so related charts sit in one view.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/custom-dashboards) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Put top KPIs such as daily revenue, D1 retention, or peak CCU in summary cards at the top of each dashboard.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/custom-dashboards) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Click Publish after editing a dashboard; unpublished changes are lost when you leave the page.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/custom-dashboards) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Pin daily-check dashboards to the left navigation with Pin to sidebar for one-click access.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/custom-dashboards) Dashboards per experience: **up to 20** (Maximum custom dashboards allowed per experience)
