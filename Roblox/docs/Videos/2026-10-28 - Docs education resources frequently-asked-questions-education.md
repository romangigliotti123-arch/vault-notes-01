## Summary
Roblox Education FAQ: explains Roblox as a platform of user-made experiences, age recommendations (Studio ~10+), hardware needs for play vs. creation, free cost model, Robux basics, Studio vs Client, Luau as the scripting language, installation/network notes, classroom chat blocking, and marketing/logo guidelines.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/frequently-asked-questions-education) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not alter the Roblox logo (color, transparency, drop-shadow); type Roblox, not ROBLOX, in external communications.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/frequently-asked-questions-education) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Creating games in Roblox is free and requires no paid licenses; Roblox Studio is free to use.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/frequently-asked-questions-education) **[[pricing]]** Private servers are set by individual developers, often around $1 USD per month, not by Roblox.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/frequently-asked-questions-education) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Roblox Studio requires a PC or Mac; it cannot run on Chromebooks or phones. Use a mouse with a scroll wheel for easier building.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/frequently-asked-questions-education) Recommended Studio age: **10 and up** (Roblox team recommendation for Roblox Studio; younger students may start earlier)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/frequently-asked-questions-education) Recommended PC age for best play performance: **Under 5 years old with dedicated GPU** (Or laptop under 3 years old with integrated video card)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/frequently-asked-questions-education) Typical paid private server price: **$1 USD per month** (Set by individual developers, described as a minor cost)

## Tools
Roblox Studio, Luau, Roblox Client
