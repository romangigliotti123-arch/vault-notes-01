# Docs: education / lesson-plans / digital-citizenship / finishing-projects

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/finishing-projects.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox Education lesson plan for finishing a student project: a 60-minute structure for self-evaluating a game against three goals (fun, challenging, error-free), making small improvements, then a class discussion and showcase of student games. Targets ages with digital citizenship framing.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/finishing-projects) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Before getting feedback, evaluate the game against three goals: fun, challenging, error-free. Use these to decide what to fix.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/finishing-projects) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Keep pre-feedback improvements small, such as tweaking one obstacle rather than adding new parts.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/finishing-projects) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Ask testers to play only after a self-check pass; the lesson treats self-evaluation as the step before feedback.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/finishing-projects) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Make the game challenging but fair: players should be challenged without being frustrated or punished.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/finishing-projects) **[[testing-debugging-and-qa]]** Check for script errors and unusual-looking parts or visuals before release.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/finishing-projects) **[[community-and-discord]]** Make the game public and share links so peers can play it; pick 2-3 projects to play as a group.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/finishing-projects) Lesson duration: **60 min total: 5 intro, 40 guided work, 15 wrap-up and showcase** (Roblox Education lesson plan, finishing projects)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/lesson-plans/digital-citizenship/finishing-projects) Showcase selection: **2-3 student projects played as a class** (Showcase step of the lesson)
