# Docs: production / monetization

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official docs page on monetization: revenue options (subscriptions, Robux/local-currency access fees, passes, developer products, private servers, Creator Store sales), best practices for tourists vs locals, live ops cadence, and guidelines for genuine discounts and policy-compliant presentation.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Sell passes for one-time benefits like restricted areas, avatar items, or permanent power-ups; locals favor long-term benefits such as battle passes.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Use developer products for repeatable purchases such as currency, ammo, or potions; record purchases yourself with DataStoreService to avoid data loss.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[monetisation-other]]** Tourists prefer items with immediate, visible effects that make them stand out; locals buy long-term value.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[monetisation-other]]** Avoid appointment mechanics and timers that can be removed or brought forward; Roblox users dislike games whose fun ends prematurely.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[common-mistakes]]** Downvotes follow monetization users disapprove of; be transparent about chance-based monetization and use it responsibly.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Combine limited-time items or events with a trading system; the two are particularly effective together.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Aim for a weekly update cadence; at minimum, update monthly, since regaining lost user interest is harder than maintaining it.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Give each update a single theme so users can see what is new and promotional assets stay coordinated.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Make users earn access to new content via monetized update content or prerequisite conditions, so they feel achievement.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[pricing]]** Discounts must be genuine and fair: an item permanently 'on sale' at the same price, or a discount lasting only briefly, is not allowed.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not show fake urgency: no inaccurate stock claims, and no countdown timers that are inaccurate or automatically restart.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[monetisation-other]]** For Creator Store plugins and models, you can sell at a minimum of $4.99 for plugins and $2.99 for models; only taxes and processing fees are deducted.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Expect a 30-day escrow hold on Creator Store sales; your share is held for 30 days from the sale date.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Use Price optimization or Managed pricing to find best price points for passes and developer products across regions.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid pushy language with minors: use 'View Item', 'See Price', 'Open Shop' instead of 'GET IT NOW', 'LAST CHANCE', 'BUY BEFORE IT'S GONE'.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[monetisation-other]]** If paid random items can be bought with Robux, use PolicyService to block them for users not permitted to use random items.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) **[[monetisation-other]]** Offer private servers as a monthly Robux subscription; they can be free or charged to monetize.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) Creator Store plugin minimum price: **$4.99 USD** (Minimum price for selling plugins on Creator Store)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) Creator Store model minimum price: **$2.99 USD** (Minimum price for selling models on Creator Store)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) Escrow hold on purchases: **30 days** (Roblox holds developer's share from date of sale)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) Recommended update cadence: **Weekly; minimum monthly** (Ideal cadence is weekly; bare minimum is monthly)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization) Private server pricing: **Monthly Robux fee** (Private servers can be free or charged monthly)
