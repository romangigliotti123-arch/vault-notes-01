# Docs: production / promotion / rewarded-video-playbooks / obby

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox official playbook on Rewarded Video ads in obbies: four-ish placement points in the player journey (first view, mid-session invest/progress, post-failure second chance), the two earnings metrics (opt-in rate and daily frequency) with genre averages and best-in-class benchmarks, and three top-performing obby examples.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Offer timed buffs or extra daily-login bonuses via Rewarded Video on first spawn to monetize new players early.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use Rewarded Video mid-session to give free currency or trials of high-value items during play.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** After failure, offer a second chance that preserves progress (e.g., respawn at previous checkpoint or claim a one-life item).
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Show the rewarded video prompt when the game starts or when a round ends so it is visible.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** State the reward clearly in the prompt; do not label it just 'ad'so players know what they get.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Stack rewards or offer multiple engagement points to raise daily views per user.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track opt-in rate (eligible users who engage with the ad, DUV/DAU) and daily frequency (views per user) as the two key earnings metrics.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby) Ad format length: **6-30 second full-screen horizontal video** (Rewarded Video placement, user opts in for reward)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby) Opt-in rate (DUV/DAU) genre average: **15%-25%** (Obby genre average per Roblox docs)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby) Opt-in rate best-in-class: **30%+** (Top Rewarded Video performers)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby) Daily frequency per user genre average: **3** (Views per user per day, obby genre)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby) Daily frequency per user best-in-class: **4+** (Views per user per day, top performers)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby) Escape the Carnival of Terror Obby opt-in rate: **27%** (Frequency 3.9; random one-life item on failure)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby) Glide Tower opt-in rate: **47%** (Frequency 2.3; ad to respawn at previous checkpoint)

## Games
- **Escape the Carnival of Terror Obby**: On failure, watch video for random one-life item; 27% opt-in, 3.9 frequency.
- **Glide Tower**: After failed attempt, watch ad to respawn at previous checkpoint; 47% opt-in, 2.3 frequency.
- **Free UGC Obby**: Example of first-view rewards: timed buffs or daily login bonus offered at spawn.
- **Escape Papa Pizzas Pizzeria**: Example of mid-session rewarded video for free currency or high-value item trials.
- **Ninja Challenge**: Example of post-failure second chance preserving current progress.
