# Docs: production / promotion / rewarded-video-playbooks / survival

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox docs playbook on Rewarded Video for survival games. Covers key metrics (opt-in rate 13%+, frequency 2.5+ per day), four placement strategies (Second Chance, Pre-Round Power-Up, Streak Saver, Daily Survival Kit), and economy protection rules: deterministic rewards only, consumables only, daily caps. Cites three best-in-class examples.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Track opt-in rate and daily frequency as the two key rewarded video metrics; target 13%+ opt-in and 2.5+ views per user per day.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Offer a revive or respawn near the death spot on the death screen, when loss aversion peaks.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Place a pre-round power-up offer (shield, speed boost, tool) in the lobby; it lasts one round and drives repeat views each session.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Offer to preserve an active win or survival streak via ad when a player dies; this targets your most engaged users.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Offer a daily consumable (booster, temporary morph, resource pack) behind one ad view that resets every 24 hours to build check-in habit.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make rewards deterministic: state exactly the item received. Random rewards are not allowed under Roblox 2026 standards.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival) **[[monetisation-other]]** Reward only consumables that are used up and reset, not permanent gear, tools, or stat upgrades, to protect scarcity.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Set a daily engagement cap on rewarded video to prevent farming and keep rewards feeling special.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival) Rewarded video ad length: **6-30 seconds, full-screen, horizontal**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival) Opt-in rate target: **13%+ of eligible users engage with ad**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival) Daily frequency target: **2.5+ views per user per day**

## Games
- **Evade**: Uses Second Chance and Streak Saver rewarded video placements.
- **Natural Disaster Survival**: Uses Pre-Round Power-Up rewarded video placement in the lobby.
- **Scary Shawarma Kiosk**: Uses Pre-Round Power-Up rewarded video placement in the lobby.

## Quotes
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival) "Per Roblox 2026 standards, no RNG. You cannot reward a "random item." State exactly what the player receives: "Watch to receive [Specific Item].""
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival) "Rewards should be items that are used up and reset, not permanent gear, tools, or stat upgrades. Scarcity is the core loop of survival games."
