# Docs: production / creator-store

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Official Creator Store docs: how to find and insert assets, distribute free or paid (USD) models and plugins, asset requirements and monthly distribution limits, seller account (Stripe) setup and eligibility, pricing ranges, escrow and payout schedule, reimbursements, and FAQ on Robux, permissions and AI data sharing.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Seller accounts must be set up through Stripe, one Stripe account per Roblox account. Applications can take up to 7 days to review.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Do not edit banking or address info right after approval. Edits return the account to Pending and freeze payouts; update it just after your next payout instead.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Only sell assets owned by an individual account. Group-owned assets are ineligible and must be re-uploaded under individual ownership.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) **[[common-mistakes]]** Robux cannot be used to price or buy Creator Store models; sales are USD only.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not sell composites containing Restricted assets you did not create; Roblox will not list them on the Creator Store.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) **[[common-mistakes]]** Enable Asset Privacy before importing meshes, images and decals you plan to sell, so child assets default to Restricted.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid obfuscated code, loadstring/require of remote assets, and bloated scripts in assets, as they break Creator Store rules and risk moderation.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Earnings sit in a 30-day escrow, then pay out monthly on the 1st to the linked bank account. Earnings cannot be accessed outside the payout schedule.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Refunds and chargebacks are deducted from the seller balance. Moderated assets must have appeals resolved within 5 days or reimbursements apply.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) **[[pricing]]** Set model prices between $2.99 and $49.99 and plugin prices between $4.99 and $249.99 in USD.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) **[[team-and-budget]]** Sellers should verify with government ID or age check; phone verification does not count and limits distribution volume.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Asset listings allow one video and up to 5 thumbnail images, available to ID or phone verified creators.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Selling models or plugins yields 100% of net proceeds minus taxes and payment processing fees, with no DevEx or platform fee.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) Model price range (USD): **$2.99 to $49.99**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) Plugin price range (USD): **$4.99 to $249.99**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) Seller revenue share: **100% of net proceeds (only taxes and processing fees deducted)**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) Escrow period: **30 days**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) Payout schedule: **Monthly, on the 1st**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) Stripe seller application review time: **Up to 7 days**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) Verified account distribution limit per 30 days (mesh/image/model/audio/plugins): **200 / 200 / 200 / 100 / 10**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) Unverified account distribution limit per 30 days (mesh/image/model/audio/plugins): **10 / 10 / 10 / 10 / 2**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) Distributor account minimum age: **2 days old**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) Asset listing media limit: **1 video and up to 5 images**
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/creator-store) Asset AI data sharing removal after opt-out: **Removed from dataset within 30 days; trained models updated within 365 days**
