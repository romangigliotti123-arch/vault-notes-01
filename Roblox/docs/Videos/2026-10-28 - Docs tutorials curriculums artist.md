## Summary
Roblox official tutorial introducing VFX art in Studio using particle emitters, built on the Mansion of Wonder sample. Covers a laser blast and explosion for a carnival game, across four chapters: workspace setup, particle emitter appearance/behavior, testing and publishing, and next steps.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/artist) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Start from the Mansion of Wonder sample template to learn VFX in Studio; it gives a working workspace to modify rather than building from scratch.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/artist) **[[vfx-and-particles]]** Build VFX with particle emitters: adjust their appearance and behavior properties, then playtest to see how the effect actually looks in motion.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/tutorials/curriculums/artist) **[[testing-debugging-and-qa]]** Playtest your VFX in Studio before publishing so you can judge timing and look in a live run.
