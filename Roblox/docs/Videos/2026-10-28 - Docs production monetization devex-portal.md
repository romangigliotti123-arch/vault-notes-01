# Docs: production / monetization / devex-portal

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Official Roblox DevEx Portal docs: how to create a Tipalti account after DevEx approval, choose payout methods with processing times and fees, and submit W-9/W-8 tax forms. Notes the November 1, 2026 royalty classification change affecting US withholding.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Select payment method and tax form within one week of receiving the Tipalti registration link, or the setup window lapses.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Enter payment method, tax form and personal info accurately and keep them current; inaccuracies can delay or impact payments.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) **[[business-and-payouts]]** If paying via bank account, make the name in the DevEx Portal exactly match the name on the bank account.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Review and update tax information before November 1, 2026; DevEx payments become royalties then, which may change US withholding.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Verify tax-form emails come from [email] to avoid phishing; ignore lookalikes.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Factor in Tipalti transaction fees and 1.9%-3% FX fees when choosing payout method and currency.

## Numbers
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) Setup window after registration link: **1 week** (Select payment method and tax form within this period)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) Form 1099-NEC threshold (US): **$2,000** (Prior-year cash earnings above this get reported to IRS)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) 1099-NEC issue date: **on or before January 31** (Roblox generally issues annually)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) Foreign exchange fee: **1.9% to 3%** (Applies when converting payout to local currency)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) ACH processing time: **3 to 5 business days** (Estimate for common payment method)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) eCheck processing time: **3 to 5 business days** (Estimate for common payment method)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) PayPal processing time: **2 to 3 business days** (Estimate for common payment method)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) Wire transfer processing time: **1 to 5 business days** (Estimate for common payment method)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) Check processing time: **7 to 14 business days** (Estimate for common payment method)
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/devex-portal) Royalty classification date: **November 1, 2026** (DevEx payments classified as royalties for US withholding)
