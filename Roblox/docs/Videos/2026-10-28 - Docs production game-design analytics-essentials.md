## Summary
Roblox docs overview of analytics for growing a game. Covers three headline KPIs (engagement, retention, monetization) to spot improvement areas, then using Experiments (A/B tests to measure causal impact of changes) and Configs (adjust in-game values live without restarting servers) to iterate.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/analytics-essentials) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Start with the three KPIs (engagement, retention, monetization) to find the highest-level areas for improvement in your game's health.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/analytics-essentials) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Use Experiments to A/B test a specific change so you can measure its causal impact on your metrics rather than guessing.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/analytics-essentials) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Use Configs to change in-game values in real time, optimizing without restarting servers or shipping a new version.

## Tools
