# Docs: production / roblox-user-base

[Open](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/roblox-user-base.md) | Roblox Creator Docs | 0 views | 2026-10-28

## Summary
Roblox developer docs on the platform's user base: demographics (more users 13+ than under 13, growing outside US/UK/Canada), behavior (players sample several games per session, YouTube-like), social/word-of-mouth traffic, and core platform culture and glossary terms.

## Tips
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/roblox-user-base) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Design your game around three platform strengths: content variety, low friction (play in seconds on almost any device), and social connection (friends playing together).
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/roblox-user-base) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Study and emulate common platform conventions and mechanics so players learn your game quickly and are less likely to bounce in frustration.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/roblox-user-base) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Expect players to sample several games per session; design for short visits and switching, not only long single-game sessions.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/roblox-user-base) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Build a strong social component (friends joining, invites); it can massively accelerate growth and engagement for many games.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/roblox-user-base) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Make the game fun enough for word-of-mouth: players invite friends who invite more friends, the main source of organic traffic.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/roblox-user-base) **[[community-and-discord]]** Communicate with users actively and iterate quickly on real-time feedback; this is critical given word-of-mouth dependence and community-generated culture.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/roblox-user-base) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Strong organic traffic and low friction keep user acquisition (UA) costs low; prioritise organic growth before paid UA.
- [page](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/roblox-user-base) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not assume Roblox audiences match other platforms; Roblox is closer to YouTube than the App Store in how users browse and switch.
