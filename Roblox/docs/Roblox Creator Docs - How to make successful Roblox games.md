# Roblox Creator Docs - How to make successful Roblox games

Roblox's official docs frame a successful game as a loop: pick a proven genre and audience, define a core loop, prototype it cheaply, then build onboarding, retention hooks and config-driven tuning before launch. Instrument analytics early, price with Managed Pricing or tested price points, and use accurate metadata and authentic thumbnails for discovery. After launch, grow through ads, events and share links, and sustain the game with a steady content cadence, experiments and metric-driven updates. Treat platform limits (badges, events, analytics quotas, announcement frequency) as design constraints from day one.

**Style:** Official Roblox Creator Docs: reference-style and rule-heavy, with exact quotas, compliance rules and feature catalogues rather than creative or genre-specific tactics. Best for developers who need precise limits and a structured path from concept to live ops.

## Playbook
1. **IDEA: Choose a genre and audience** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Know whether players are competitive, casual or experienced, and pick the genre matching your core loop (pg-cdb8a8dc9). Copy proven design patterns from popular genre games (pg-ea0014cc5). Licensed IP can give instant familiarity (pg-727bfc84a). Genre changes are limited to once per three months.
2. **IDEA: Define the core loop** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: A core loop is minute-to-minute interaction, the most-repeated actions, and a progression engine (pg-3793c21c6). Prototype and tune this first. Write rules in detail, such as respawn timers and locations. Put tunable values like boss health, XP and prices in configs, not constants (pg-00002a495).
3. **BUILD: Prototype cheaply, then playtest in Studio** [[development-process-and-speed]]: Use paper prototypes for broad system and UI exploration; use Studio prototypes to playtest reusable mechanics (pg-0b9dc6906). Keep scope narrow and test the key aspect, not the whole feature. Stage config changes in Studio before publishing; SetTestingValue affects one server only (pg-00002a495).
4. **BUILD: Design first-session onboarding** [[first-session-onboarding]]: Teach essentials, reach fun fast, and leave players wanting more (pg-bb3ac75d8). Combine visual cues, just-in-time tutorials and timed hints (pg-6b1230c51). Set hint delays from playtest completion times. Measure onboarding with the New User First Session Retention chart (pg-4133ccd7b).
5. **BUILD: Layer goals, quests and season passes** [[retention-d1-d7]]: Mix short, mid and long-term goals; dailies must be completed within 24 hours and stay single-session (pg-2b0e8a88b). Quests are objective + quantity + reward. A season pass runs about one month with ten tiers and a one-week rest (pg-451e794d2). Tie quests to core-loop actions.
6. **BUILD: Hit performance budgets and accessibility** [[scripting-and-performance]]: Target 60 FPS, a 16.67 ms frame budget, and watch heartbeat, memory and join time (pg-73c149260). VR targets 72 FPS on Quest (pg-403e92388). Design for mouse, touch and gamepad (pg-d417f045f) and check text size and colour contrast (pg-eb92a32d5). Wrap ConfigService calls in pcall (pg-00002a495).
7. **BUILD: Instrument analytics before launch** [[analytics-metrics]]: Log economy, funnel and custom events server-side, only after the operation succeeds (pg-d4a60b651, pg-4fb48240b). Use up to 3 custom fields with 8,000 combined unique values, and cap custom event names at 100 (pg-42a3daf13, pg-4acf4147b). Verify in View Events; charts take about 24 hours.
8. **BUILD: Design passes and developer products** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: Use Game Passes for one-time perks and developer products for repeatable consumables (pg-382ad1370, pg-dc09eb1ed). Implement ProcessReceipt so purchases grant immediately and listed products qualify for Shop surfacing (pg-61306c8d3). Grant pass privileges on PlayerAdded for purchases made outside the game. Offer both consumable and durable items (pg-ade33a20f).
9. **BUILD: Price with tests and regional rules** [[pricing]]: Managed Pricing bounds regional prices at 30–100% of default, and price optimization applies only when incremental revenue is positive (pg-c50ae28d8, pg-bc3872c4f). Replace hard-coded prices so Dynamic Price Check passes (pg-8e53deb55). Read prices with GetProductInfoAsync so Plus discounts display correctly (pg-1a3ee8cba).
10. **LAUNCH: Make a clear icon and thumbnails** [[thumbnails-icons]]: Use a 512×512 square icon with relevant art and strong contrast (pg-6a0212207). Thumbnails are 16:9 at 1920×1080 under 3 MB, up to 10 per game, with authentic gameplay only and 3 video uploads a month (pg-0a0a26b39). Keep 2–5 thumbnails active for Home personalization (average +8.5% qualified play-through lift).
11. **LAUNCH: Write true, unique titles and descriptions** [[titles-descriptions]]: Use a title, description and icon that are true and unique to the game (pg-5f520152e). Keep the name consistent, since renaming hurts findability. Avoid repeated words and more than one or two emojis. For external SEO, use a ~160-character hook, updates, content and info section; skip tags and hashtags (pg-d955cfac3).
12. **LAUNCH: Check discovery settings and eligibility** [[discovery-algorithm]]: Recommended for You ranks on engagement signals such as session time and Day 1 retention (pg-fdd98e376, pg-a3c4bcacf). Leave Exclude from Recommendations off unless you want less reach. Publish across devices. Complete the Experience Questionnaire (account 30+ days old) so age guidelines apply; unrated games are treated as 13+ (pg-23a10d275).
13. **GROWTH: Acquire with ads, share links and invites** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Match ad product to goal: Search ads take up to 10 keywords (pg-aa65badef); Immersive ads need a 13+ owner and 2,000 unique monthly visitors (pg-13d63bba4). Rewarded video gives 3–10 Robux-equivalent rewards, never randomised (pg-ce4b224f8). Add share links, invite prompts and referral rewards (pg-2e3dbba56, pg-839b47e39, pg-446690d00).
14. **LIVE OPS: Run events and a steady update cadence** [[updates-live-ops]]: Events need 1,000 RSVPs and must start within 7 days to trend; max 10 ongoing or upcoming (pg-0daad24c5). Announcements: 60 characters, once every three days. Off-platform featuring: submit 7+ days ahead, run 7–30 days. Ship small drops kept under three weeks of effort (pg-bc791b5fe), announce in advance, and track hourly (pg-4044db182).

## Key numbers
- Active configs per experience: up to 1,000; publish propagates in about 15 seconds to 1 minute (pg-00002a495)
- Config string/JSON value limit 100,000 characters; 100 conditions per game and 20 per key (pg-00002a495)
- Badges: 5 free per game per 24h GMT, then 100 Robux each; reorder up to 50 at once (pg-0a3b962c5)
- Thumbnails: 1920×1080 16:9, up to 10 per game, 3 video uploads/month, 2–5 active for personalization (pg-0a0a26b39)
- Frame budget: 60 FPS = 16.67 ms per frame; Quest auto quality targets 72 FPS (pg-73c149260, pg-403e92388)
- Analytics: 3 custom fields per event; 8,000 combined unique values; 100 custom event names; 90-day rolloff (pg-42a3daf13, pg-4acf4147b, pg-12d3540c9)
- Analytics dashboard eligibility: more than 10 DAU and 10 play hours for 7 consecutive days (pg-2e7060431)
- Events: 1,000 RSVPs to trend; max 10 ongoing or upcoming; off-platform submission 7+ days ahead; 7–30 day runs (pg-0daad24c5)
- Update announcements: 60-character limit, once every 3 days (pg-0daad24c5)
- Experience notifications: 100+ visits; 1 per user per experience per day; 99-character strings (pg-2d3a7ea4c)
- Managed Pricing regional prices bounded 30–100% of default (max 70% discount) (pg-c50ae28d8, pg-8e53deb55)
- Creator share: 70% on Robux items (100 Robux item = 70 Robux to creator) (pg-34177a379)
- DevEx rate: 0.0038 standard; 0.0054 for eligible US 18+ verified players from June 8, 2026; legacy 0.0035 before Sept 5, 2025 (pg-95c08415d, pg-d45e13dc9)
- DevEx cash-out threshold: 30,000 earned Robux (pg-34177a379)
- Rewarded video benchmarks: opt-in rate 13%+; incremental sim frequency 2.8/day; survival 2.5+/day (pg-ae6d9d38f, pg-b6f35f0f0)

## Common mistakes
- Hard-coding boss health, XP or prices, which forces a new version for every tweak instead of using configs (pg-00002a495)
- Expecting configs to work without code calling GetValue, or setting conditional values whose type differs from the default (pg-00002a495)
- Using GetConfigAsync for targeted values; targeting requires GetConfigForPlayerAsync (pg-00002a495)
- Calling ConfigService from the client, or leaving config calls unwrapped without fallback values (pg-00002a495)
- Logging economy events when a purchase is attempted rather than after it succeeds, which creates misleading data (pg-d4a60b651)
- Leaving Exclude from Recommendations on, which drops Home recommendation traffic to zero (pg-fdd98e376)
- Submitting thumbnails with misrepresented gameplay, voiceovers or promo claims; they get rejected and still use quota (pg-0a0a26b39)
- Keeping only one active thumbnail, so every user sees the same image, or removing thumbnails too early (pg-0a0a26b39)
- Hard-coding prices that fail Dynamic Price Check, or changing a private server's price, which cancels all active subscriptions (pg-8e53deb55, pg-60551fb67)
- Building revenue strategy on Engagement-Based Payouts, which was deprecated July 24, 2025 (pg-d0ea3df77)

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Start place and metadata: true, unique name and description; keep the game name consistent (pg-5f520152e, pg-d955cfac3)
- [ ] Icon at 512×512 square with clear, relevant art (pg-6a0212207)
- [ ] Thumbnails at 1920×1080 16:9 showing authentic gameplay; 2–5 active (pg-0a0a26b39)
- [ ] Experience Questionnaire completed (account 30+ days old) so age guidelines apply (pg-23a10d275)
- [ ] Genre matches core loop; remember the once-per-three-months change limit (pg-cdb8a8dc9)
- [ ] Exclude from Recommendations is off unless you want reduced reach (pg-fdd98e376)
- [ ] Identity verification and 2FA completed if targeting all ages (pg-e4e4f0dfe, pg-95c78b502)
- [ ] Configs staged in Studio; condition order and data types checked; SetTestingValue tested on one server (pg-00002a495)
- [ ] ConfigService and BadgeService calls wrapped in pcall with fallbacks (pg-00002a495, pg-0a3b962c5)
- [ ] Badges confirmed Enabled before players can earn them; creation kept within 5 free per 24h (pg-0a3b962c5)
- [ ] ProcessReceipt implemented before selling developer products (pg-61306c8d3)
- [ ] Passes and products have custom icons, are listed in Shop, and use no hard-coded prices (pg-382ad1370, pg-8e53deb55)
- [ ] Managed Pricing enrolled or prices checked with Dynamic Price Check (pg-c50ae28d8)
- [ ] Analytics events verified in View Events; no more than 3 custom fields (pg-12d3540c9)
- [ ] Performance tested against the 60 FPS budget on the lowest target device (pg-73c149260)
- [ ] Adaptive input and accessibility checked: gamepad, touch, text size, no colour-only cues (pg-d417f045f, pg-eb92a32d5)
- [ ] Tax form and payout setup: Tipalti within one week; tax info current before Oct 31, 2026 (pg-16126ed7e, pg-6e7726467)
- [ ] If using ads: disclosure in place and PolicyService AreAdsAllowed checked (pg-e54cf5d76)

## How the advice changed over time
- Engagement-Based Payouts were deprecated July 24, 2025 and replaced by Creator Rewards; older guidance on them no longer applies (pg-d0ea3df77).
- DevEx rate changed: legacy 0.0035 before Sept 5, 2025, then 0.0038 standard, and from June 8, 2026 0.0054 for eligible US 18+ players. The most recent rate applies (pg-95c08415d, pg-d45e13dc9).
- Tax information moves to the Taxes page on July 15, 2026; DevEx payments are classed as royalties from Nov 1, 2026, so US withholding depends on tax info on file by Oct 31, 2026 (pg-6e7726467).
- Deep links are deprecated in favour of share links for public tracked links (pg-16dc71a07, pg-2e3dbba56).
- Ad integration revenue share based on qualified visits with CPTV tiers starts in 2027 and is forward-looking; current payout plans should not assume it (pg-7142c76fb).
