# "Rigging and animation for Roblox"

Back to [[blender-README]] | [[blender-playbook]]

Sources establish a Blender-to-Roblox rigging pipeline: prepare the mesh (apply transforms, weld, remove loose parts), choose rig density by vertex count (Rigify human only above ~3,200 verts), skin with automatic weights plus cleanup, and respect Roblox limits (max 4 influences per vertex, none on Root/HumanoidRootNode, frozen transforms, root at origin). Standard R15 hierarchy and the Roblox template are recommended; advanced R15 adds up to 37 optional bones. Export FBX with Add Leaf Bones off and Bake Animation only for animated content, one animation track per export. Animation moves via FBX import, the Animation Clip Editor importer, or a Roblox plugin round trip. The Avatar Joint Upgrade (Phase 2, May 2026) replaces Motor6D with AnimationConstraint. Blender-MCP rigging tools exist but do not cover Roblox specifics.

## Key points
- **[CORROBORATED]** [CORROBORATED] Before rigging: apply scale and rotation, place the mesh at the origin facing -Y, weld duplicate vertices and remove loose parts. Disconnected islands make automatic weights fail; auto weights skipped 48 vertices on a villager. *(multiple-sources, high)* [[sources/gh-ahujasid-mcp-for-blender-f-src-blender_mcp-guides-rigging.md|gh-ahujasid-mcp-for-blender-f-src-blender_mcp-guides-rigging.md]] [[sources/gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-references-characters.md|gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-references-characters.md]]
- **[OUTDATED/UNSURE]** [OUTDATED/UNSURE] Pick rig density by vertex count. Under 700 verts use a hand-built rig of 12-20 bones; 700-3000 use basic_human (35 bones); above that use full human (160 bones). A Rigify human on a 370-vertex mesh left 107 of 160 deform bones dead and detached the head. Aim for 20 or more verts per bone (auto weights need at least ~5). *(multiple-sources, high)* [[sources/gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-SKILL.md|gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-SKILL.md]] [[sources/gh-elithril-blender-kiln-rel-v1.1.0|gh-elithril-blender-kiln-rel-v1.1.0]] [[sources/gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-references-characters.md|gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-references-characters.md]]
- **[CORROBORATED]** [CORROBORATED] Rigify generation pitfalls: addon_utils.enable('rigify') is insufficient, so use bpy.ops.preferences.addon_enable. Generated limbs ship with IK_FK=0.0, so FK rotations silently do nothing. Skin to the generated rig, not the metarig. *(multiple-sources, medium)* [[sources/gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-references-characters.md|gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-references-characters.md]] [[sources/gh-ahujasid-mcp-for-blender-f-src-blender_mcp-guides-rigging.md|gh-ahujasid-mcp-for-blender-f-src-blender_mcp-guides-rigging.md]]
- **[OUTDATED/UNSURE]** [OUTDATED/UNSURE] Bone Heat failure fixes: merge by distance and remove interior faces; or skin a voxel-remeshed copy and transfer weights with a Data Transfer modifier; or scale up 10x, skin, then scale back. Mechanical objects can be parented to bones with parent_type BONE instead of skinning. *(single-source, high)* [[sources/gh-ahujasid-mcp-for-blender-f-src-blender_mcp-guides-rigging.md|gh-ahujasid-mcp-for-blender-f-src-blender_mcp-guides-rigging.md]]
- **[OFFICIAL]** [OFFICIAL] Roblox skinning limits: max 4 bone influences per vertex, no influences on Root or HumanoidRootNode (dropped on import), and all bones frozen at scale 1 and rotation 0 with the root at 0,0,0. Zero-influence bones are removed on import unless Keep Zero Influence Bones is enabled. *(multiple-sources, high)* [[sources/off-art_modeling_skin-a-humanoid-model|off-art_modeling_skin-a-humanoid-model]] [[sources/off-art_modeling_specifications|off-art_modeling_specifications]] [[sources/off-studio_importer|off-studio_importer]] [[sources/off-avatar_character-bodies_specifications|off-avatar_character-bodies_specifications]]
- **[OFFICIAL]** [OFFICIAL] Standard R15 hierarchy is Root > HumanoidRootNode > LowerTorso > UpperTorso > Head/arms and legs. Root and LowerTorso are at 0,0,0, and all 15 meshes must be parented to one armature. Roblox supplies Rig_and_Attachments_Template.blend. Parent with Empty Groups and assign weight 1 per segment for rigid R15, or Automatic Weights for clothing. *(multiple-sources, high)* [[sources/off-avatar_character-bodies_specifications|off-avatar_character-bodies_specifications]] [[sources/web-2a50b96cb8|web-2a50b96cb8]] [[sources/off-art_modeling_rig-a-humanoid-model|off-art_modeling_rig-a-humanoid-model]] [[sources/web-0edc91a2bc|web-0edc91a2bc]]
- **[OFFICIAL]** [OFFICIAL] Advanced R15 allows up to 37 optional bones. After import, HumanoidRigDescription and/or DigitsRigDescription must be inserted to sell on Marketplace, and they detect bones by name. Optional bones must follow naming conventions and have skinned meshes. *(multiple-sources, high)* [[sources/off-avatar_character-bodies_specifications|off-avatar_character-bodies_specifications]] [[sources/web-2a50b96cb8|web-2a50b96cb8]]
- **[OFFICIAL]** [OFFICIAL] Weight-painting workflow: set the armature to display as Sticks, enable Auto Normalize so mistakes remove the original influence, and paint per limb at brush strength 1 (0.25 for shoulder/upper torso). Use X-Axis Mirror for symmetry. Test in Pose mode and reset with Alt+R. *(multiple-sources, high)* [[sources/web-1cc5c97477|web-1cc5c97477]] [[sources/off-art_modeling_skin-a-humanoid-model|off-art_modeling_skin-a-humanoid-model]] [[sources/off-art_modeling_rig-a-humanoid-model|off-art_modeling_rig-a-humanoid-model]]
- **[OUTDATED/UNSURE]** [OUTDATED/UNSURE] The glTF exporter binds unweighted vertices to a synthetic neutral_bone, so a defective rig still validates. Check weights before export. Pass export_def_bones=True (865 joints became 319) and do not manually re-parent deform bones. *(multiple-sources, high)* [[sources/gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-bench-README.md|gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-bench-README.md]] [[sources/gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-references-characters.md|gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-references-characters.md]]
- **[OFFICIAL]** [OFFICIAL] FBX export settings: disable Add Leaf Bones and disable Bake Animation unless exporting keyframed content. Only a single animation track per mesh export, so export multiple animations as separate files. Facial animation characters need a timeline of 0-330. *(multiple-sources, high)* [[sources/web-3a1e35acf3|web-3a1e35acf3]] [[sources/off-art_modeling_specifications|off-art_modeling_specifications]] [[sources/web-7195087017|web-7195087017]] [[sources/web-19fbb4beda|web-19fbb4beda]]
- **[OFFICIAL]** [OFFICIAL] Animation import into Studio: the Animation Clip Editor importer has Rig Type (R15/Custom), Rig Scale, Rest Pose Source and track selection. Multiple clips in one glTF/FBX are all saved on import. A Roblox plugin round trip (Rebuild rig, then Load large animation) is an alternative but does not support skinned meshes. *(multiple-sources, high)* [[sources/off-dev_4775593|off-dev_4775593]] [[sources/off-dev_4260501|off-dev_4260501]] [[sources/web-af064b2633|web-af064b2633]]
- **[OFFICIAL]** [OFFICIAL] Avatar Joint Upgrade Phase 2 (May 2026) replaces Motor6D with AnimationConstraint and BallSocketConstraint by default. Opt out temporarily with StarterPlayer.AvatarJointUpgrade=Disabled. For procedural animation multiply AnimationConstraint.Transform in PreSimulation, and do not edit RigAttachment CFrames. Scripts using IsA('Motor6D') will miss the new constraints. *(multiple-sources, high)* [[sources/off-dev_4541689|off-dev_4541689]] [[sources/off-dev_4656414|off-dev_4656414]]
- **[CORROBORATED]** [CORROBORATED] Blender animation practice: loop a cycle of P frames by keying 0..P-1, not P, or use a CYCLES modifier with identical first and last keys. Push actions into NLA strips and bake before export. Measure rotation signs and the IK pole from the rest pose rather than assuming them. *(multiple-sources, medium)* [[sources/gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-references-animation.md|gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-references-animation.md]] [[sources/gh-ahujasid-mcp-for-blender-f-src-blender_mcp-guides-animation.md|gh-ahujasid-mcp-for-blender-f-src-blender_mcp-guides-animation.md]] [[sources/gh-sandraschi-blender-mcp-f-docs-blender-TOOL_REFERENCE.md|gh-sandraschi-blender-mcp-f-docs-blender-TOOL_REFERENCE.md]]
- **[OFFICIAL]** [OFFICIAL] Reimport (Dec 2025) supports rigs, bones, Motor6Ds and cages, and treats the skeleton as one unit, so bones missing from the file are deleted. Avoid it while the Animation Clip Editor is open. Mesh Streaming does not support skinned meshes. *(multiple-sources, high)* [[sources/off-dev_4112031|off-dev_4112031]] [[sources/off-dev_4525340|off-dev_4525340]]

## Techniques
- **[CORROBORATED]** **[CORROBORATED] Rigify humanoid generation**: Enable rigify via preferences.addon_enable, add human metarig, fit to mesh, run rigify_generate, skin to the generated rig. [[sources/gh-ahujasid-mcp-for-blender-f-src-blender_mcp-guides-rigging.md|gh-ahujasid-mcp-for-blender-f-src-blender_mcp-guides-rigging.md]] [[sources/gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-references-characters.md|gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-references-characters.md]]
- **[OUTDATED/UNSURE]** **[OUTDATED/UNSURE] Auto-weights with fallback**: parent_set ARMATURE_AUTO; on failure clean the mesh, use a voxel-remeshed proxy with Data Transfer, or scale 10x. [[sources/gh-ahujasid-mcp-for-blender-f-src-blender_mcp-guides-rigging.md|gh-ahujasid-mcp-for-blender-f-src-blender_mcp-guides-rigging.md]]
- **[OFFICIAL]** **[OFFICIAL] Rigid R15 skinning with empty groups**: Parent with Empty Groups, assign each segment to its bone's vertex group at weight 1, then test in Pose mode. [[sources/web-ec54006323|web-ec54006323]] [[sources/off-art_modeling_rig-a-humanoid-model|off-art_modeling_rig-a-humanoid-model]]
- **[OUTDATED/UNSURE]** **[OUTDATED/UNSURE] Dead deform bone detection**: Count vertex groups with no vertex above 0.01 weight; if any, drop to a lower rig tier. [[sources/gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-SKILL.md|gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-plugin-SKILL.md]]
- **[OUTDATED/UNSURE]** **[OUTDATED/UNSURE] Deformation probe**: Bend each deform bone 25° and measure edge stretch p99 and max. [[sources/gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-bench-README.md|gh-elithril-blender-kiln-f-bench-README.md]]
- **[OFFICIAL]** **[OFFICIAL] Blender-Roblox animation round trip**: Export rig OBJ, Rebuild rig in Blender, key with LocRot keying set, export to clipboard, Load large animation in Studio. [[sources/web-af064b2633|web-af064b2633]]
- **[OFFICIAL]** **[OFFICIAL] Mixamo retarget**: Export rig FBX, upload to Mixamo, download the animation, then use Import FBX or map rig. [[sources/web-af064b2633|web-af064b2633]]
- **[OFFICIAL]** **[OFFICIAL] Procedural animation with AnimationConstraint**: Multiply AnimationConstraint.Transform in RunService.PreSimulation on the client. [[sources/off-dev_4656414|off-dev_4656414]]

## Disagreements
- **Whether glTF/FBX skinned animation or the plugin route is preferred for skinned meshes**: Plugin round trip does not support skinned meshes or bones; use Roblox's built-in FBX animation importer. [[sources/web-af064b2633|web-af064b2633]] | The Animation Clip Editor importer and Reimport handle rigs, bones and multiple clips directly. [[sources/off-dev_4260501|off-dev_4260501]] [[sources/off-dev_4112031|off-dev_4112031]] [[sources/off-dev_4775593|off-dev_4775593]] Note: The ACE importer sources are dated 2025-2026 and the plugin source is undated. Prefer the ACE importer for skinned rigs.
- **Reliability of FBX/animation export**: Animations are not exported by the tool, and some FACS rigs export incorrectly (known bug). [[sources/off-dev_3905928|off-dev_3905928]] | Animation can be baked into FBX export, one track per file. [[sources/off-art_modeling_specifications|off-art_modeling_specifications]] [[sources/web-3a1e35acf3|web-3a1e35acf3]] Note: These likely refer to different tools, so this is not a genuine conflict. S247 is dated 2025-08.

## Gaps
- No source documents blender-mcp Claude prompts that produce Roblox-compliant R15 rigs. The MCP rigging tools (S178, S184, S196) do not mention R15 or Motor6D.
- Little detail on how to author AnimationConstraint-compatible rigs from Blender after the AJU change.
- No hard numbers on animation import fidelity such as frame rate or scale factor values.
- Dynamic head and FACS rigging from Blender is barely covered, and the S247 FACS bug is unresolved.
- Several R15 doc sources are undated, so version currency is unclear.
- Evidence for the vertex-count tiers comes from one author's tests (S55, S65, S72), which share a repository.
