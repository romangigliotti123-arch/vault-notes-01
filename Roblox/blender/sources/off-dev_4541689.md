# off-dev_4541689

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Roblox staff notice (2026-03-27) on Avatar Joint Upgrade (AJU) Phase 2 (May 2026 opt-out) and Humanoid Rig Description. Covers Motor6D to AnimationConstraint change, Welds to RigidConstraints for rigid accessories, optional Bone instances, and a Studio Importer "Constraint Type" option for R15 and custom rigs. Little on Blender, bpy, or mesh export; mostly relevant to rig import and script migration.

## Claims
- c1: AJU Phase 2 launches May 2026 and changes from opt-in to opt-out for existing experiences; Phase 2 of a 3-phase rollout. *(doc, high; rigging-animation)*
- c2: With AJU, Motor6Ds are replaced by AnimationConstraints, which can connect Parts to chains of Bones; Motor6Ds do not support Bones. *(doc, high; rigging-animation)*
- c3: Studio Importer 'Rig General' tab gets a 'Constraint Type' option: Joint Upgrade (AnimationConstraints) or Legacy (Motor6Ds); default is Joint Upgrade. *(doc, high; blender-roblox-export-import)*
- c4: Rigs with more than one bone connecting body parts, or multiple bones on the same part, should use Joint Upgrade; importing with Motor6Ds raises an importer warning and animations will not play back correctly. *(doc, high; blender-roblox-export-import)*
- c5: Optional extra bones (clavicle, spine, neck, wrists, fingers, toes) layer on top of R15; the 15 physical parts stay unchanged. Platform avatars with >15 articulations get an HRD under the character model. *(doc, high; rigging-animation)*
- c6: Humanoid Rig Description (HRD) maps core 15 joints plus optional Bone instances and enables Adaptive Animation, so existing animations work across avatars. Advanced avatars require both AJU and HRD. *(doc, medium; rigging-animation)*
- c7: With AJU, rigid accessories connect via RigidConstraints to Attachments instead of Welds; Neck and Shoulder RigAttachments move under spine Bones instead of UpperTorso. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c8: Scripts should use FindFirstChild(name, true) or QueryDescendants('#Name') rather than direct child indexing, because optional bones and accessories make direct paths fragile. *(doc, high; automation-and-creator-workflows)*
- c9: AJU is enabled via the Beta Features tab in Studio; templates ship with AJU on and BreakJointsOnDeath set to False. Classic death is restored by enabling StarterPlayer.CharacterBreakJointsOnDeath. *(doc, high; automation-and-creator-workflows)*
- c10: Changes apply to spawned avatars and imported rigs including custom rigs; existing saved models such as NPCs are not affected. *(doc, medium; rigging-animation)*

## Numbers
- n1: **Rollout date**: May 2026 (AJU Phase 2, opt-out) 
- n2: **R15 physical parts**: 15 (no additional parts added) 
- n3: **Articulation threshold for HRD**: >15 articulations 
- n4: **Rollout phases**: 3 

## Techniques
- t1: **Migrate avatar script lookups to recursive search**: Replace head:FindFirstChild(name) with FindFirstChild(name, true) or head:QueryDescendants('#Name') and check for zero results, since Attachments may sit under optional Bones.
- t2: **Import custom rigs with Joint Upgrade**: In Studio Importer, Rig General tab, keep Constraint Type = Joint Upgrade for rigs with multiple bones per part or multiple bones connecting parts; avoid Legacy (Motor6D) to prevent broken animation playback.
