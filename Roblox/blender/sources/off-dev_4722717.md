# off-dev_4722717

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Roblox staff post on unifying 2D and 3D avatar item upload and publishing rules from July 14, 2026. Covers verification, 2-step, Plus/Premium requirement, upload and publish costs, and revenue split changes. It has no Blender, bpy, MCP, FBX/glTF, or mesh pipeline content. Off-topic for the Blender-to-Roblox pipeline.

## Claims
- c1: From July 14, 2026, upload requires ID verification or a linked parent account; publishing also requires 2-step verification and active Plus or Premium. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c2: Per the July 13 update, uploading a 2D or 3D avatar item costs 80 Robux; publishing 2D items stays at 10 Robux; 3D publishing advances unchanged. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c3: Unsubscribed creators' items will be taken off sale starting August 1, 2026; grace window for 2D subscription requirement ran through July 31. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c4: Marketplace purchase revenue split is 30% creator for both 2D and 3D; in-game split is 30% creator and 40% game owner. Previously 2D was 70% marketplace and 60/10 in-game. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c5: Previously purchased 2D items remain wearable and unaffected by the change. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c6: The source names no Blender, bpy, blender-mcp, FBX/glTF, or Roblox Blender plugin tooling; it contains no pipeline steps. *(doc, high; automation-and-creator-workflows)*

## Numbers
- n1: **Upload cost, 2D and 3D**: 80 Robux per item (July 13 update) 
- n2: **Publish cost, 2D**: 10 Robux 
- n3: **Publishing advance start (July 7 post)**: 600 Robux, varies by category 
- n4: **Marketplace creator share**: 30% 
- n5: **In-game creator / game owner share**: 30% / 40% 
- n6: **Effective date**: July 14, 2026; off-sale for non-compliant accounts August 1, 2026 

## Quotes (brief, for attribution)
> Starting July 14, 2026, we're unifying upload, publishing, and economic requirements for 2D and 3D avatar items on the Marketplace.  (devforum.roblox.com)

## Contradictions flagged
- July 7 post: upload costs 200 Robux per item (reduced from 300 for 3D); publishing advance starts at 600 Robux for 2D. (vs July 13 update in the same thread: upload costs 80 Robux for 2D and 3D; 2D publishing stays at 10 Robux. The later update supersedes the earlier figures.)
