## Summary
arXiv abstract page for MeshGPT (Nov 2023), a research method generating compact triangle meshes autoregressively with a decoder-only transformer over learned quantized triangle embeddings. It has no content on Blender, Claude, bpy, or Roblox pipelines. Only tangentially relevant to mesh triangle compactness.

## Claims
- c1: MeshGPT generates triangle meshes as sequences of triangles, producing compact artist-like meshes rather than dense iso-surfaced meshes from neural fields. *(doc, high; other)*
- c2: Method: learn a vocabulary of latent quantized embeddings using graph convolutions, decode sequences into triangles, then train a transformer to predict the next embedding index. *(doc, high; other)*
- c3: Reported gains over prior mesh generation methods: 9% increase in shape coverage and 30-point FID improvement across categories. *(data, medium; other)*

## Numbers
- n1: **shape coverage improvement**: 9% vs state of the art mesh generation methods
- n2: **FID improvement**: 30 points across various categories
