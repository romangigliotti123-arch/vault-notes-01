Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Roblox creator doc page on making a clothing mesh watertight in Blender edit mode (LongSleeve t-shirt example): seal neck/waist/wrist holes so only the outer surface is visible, avoiding exposed backfaces that hurt rendering and performance. Nothing on Claude, blender-mcp, bpy, or import/export settings.

## Claims
- c1: Watertight mesh means only the top visible surface is exposed at any angle; non-watertight assets may expose single-sided backfaces affecting rendering and character performance. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c2: Holes at neck, waist and wrist of a clothing mesh must be sealed; repeat until all interior-exposing holes are closed. *(doc, high; common-mistakes-fixes)*
- c3: Sealing method: Alt-click last edge loop, E to extrude, Merge Vertices > At Center, G to reposition the new vertex. *(doc, high; creator-workflows)*

## Techniques
- t1: **Watertight sealing by extrude and merge**: Alt-click last edge of hole, press E and click to extrude, right-click Merge Vertices > At Center, press G to move the vertex inside the mesh. Repeat for every open hole.

## Quotes (brief, for attribution)
> A watertight shape ensures that only the top visible surface of the mesh is exposed at any angle.  (create.roblox.com)
