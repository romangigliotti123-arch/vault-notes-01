Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
arXiv abstract page for Hunyuan3D-Omni (Sep 2025), a controllable image-to-3D generator built on Hunyuan3D 2.1 that accepts point clouds, voxels, bounding boxes and skeletal pose as extra conditions. Only the abstract is available; nothing on Blender, Claude, MCP, bpy or Roblox.

## Claims
- c1: Hunyuan3D-Omni is built on Hunyuan3D 2.1 and accepts images plus point clouds, voxels, bounding boxes and skeletal pose priors as conditioning, for control over geometry, topology and pose. *(doc, high; procedural-generation)*
- c2: It uses a single cross-modal architecture rather than separate heads per modality. Training picks one control modality per example and samples harder signals (skeletal pose) more often than easier ones (point clouds). *(doc, high; procedural-generation)*
- c3: The authors say the extra controls improve generation accuracy, enable geometry-aware transformations and increase robustness for production workflows. The abstract gives no metrics. *(doc, medium; procedural-generation)*

## Techniques
- t1: **Difficulty-aware modality sampling**: Select one control modality per training example. Bias sampling toward harder signals such as skeletal pose and downweight easier ones such as point clouds. This is meant to give robust fusion and graceful handling of missing inputs.
