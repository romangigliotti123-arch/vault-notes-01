# web-917659c9e0

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Roblox doc on in-experience makeup creation: AvatarCreationService prompts (single vs multi-layer), creation tokens, painting on player heads via WrapTextureTransfer projection, cage/target UV conversion with WrapTarget, Decal wrapping, submission code, and moderation events. Runtime Luau only; nothing on Blender or Claude.

## Claims
- c1: Makeup is supported only on R15 characters with MeshPart heads. Face, lip and eye makeup are painted textures; eyebrows and eyelashes are layered mesh accessories. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c2: PromptCreateAvatarAssetAsync creates a single makeup asset (FaceMakeup, LipMakeup, EyeMakeup, EyebrowAccessory, EyelashAccessory). PromptCreateMakeupAsync composites two or more layers into one outfit with a shared head color and requires at least two entries. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c3: Each makeup asset type needs its own avatar creation token whose creation type matches the AvatarAssetType passed to the prompt. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c4: The player's head EditableMesh generally can't be read directly. Call WrapTextureTransfer:PrepareProjectionMeshDataAsync(), then pass the transfer to EditableImage:DrawImageProjected or SampleImageProjected. Re-prepare it if the head mesh changes. *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c5: Projection painting works in the shared cage UV layout, so the result is already a cage-space texture. WrapTarget CreateTextureInTargetSpaceAsync and CreateTextureInCageSpaceAsync are only needed for authoring heads or experience-owned meshes painted in native UV space. *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c6: For makeup, pass MakeupRules.WrapTextureTransferUVBounds (MinBound/MaxBound from GetValidationRules) as MinUVBounds/MaxUVBounds to CreateTextureInCageSpaceAsync. Without them, bounds come from the whole cage. Bounds must be finite, with max greater than min on both axes. *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c7: A finished texture is wrapped with AssetService:CreateDecalAsync into a Decal, and the WrapTextureTransfer used to paint it is parented to that Decal. Eyebrow and eyelash layers pass the authored Accessory directly. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c8: The experience owner earns Marketplace commissions as publisher and the player is the creator. In-experience makeup isn't listed on the Marketplace and can't be resold or traded. Purchased assets save to the player's inventory. *(doc, high; automation-and-creator-workflows)*
- c9: Moderation: AvatarAssetModerationCompleted fires per asset. For outfits, AvatarOutfitModerationCompleted also fires once for the whole outfit. Listen to both as needed. *(doc, high; automation-and-creator-workflows)*

## Numbers
- n1: **supported makeup AvatarAssetTypes**: 5 Face, Lip, Eye makeup plus Eyebrow and Eyelash accessories
- n2: **minimum layers for PromptCreateMakeupAsync**: 2 Fewer than two means use PromptCreateAvatarAssetAsync

## Techniques
- t1: **Projected painting on player head**: Call PrepareProjectionMeshDataAsync on the WrapTextureTransfer in a pcall. Create an EditableImage with AssetService:CreateEditableImage. Call DrawImageProjected(textureTransfer, projectionParams, brushConfig).
- t2: **Submit makeup entries**: Build entries with TokenId, Instance, AvatarAssetType and SortOrder. Use PromptCreateAvatarAssetAsync for one entry, or PromptCreateMakeupAsync(player, entries, headColor) for several. Check the result enum.

### Code: Limit cage-space conversion to the makeup UV region
```lua
local validationRules = AvatarCreationService:GetValidationRules()
local makeupBounds = validationRules.MakeupRules.WrapTextureTransferUVBounds

local cageSpaceTexture = wrapTarget:CreateTextureInCageSpaceAsync(targetSpaceTexture, {
	MinUVBounds = makeupBounds.MinBound,
	MaxUVBounds = makeupBounds.MaxBound,
})
```
