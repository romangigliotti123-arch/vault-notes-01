## Summary
arXiv abstract page for Text2Room (ICCV 2023), which generates room-scale textured 3D meshes from text using 2D text-to-image models, monocular depth estimation and inpainting. It says nothing about Blender, Claude, Roblox, bpy or asset pipelines.

## Claims
- c1: Text2Room generates room-scale textured 3D meshes from a text prompt by combining pretrained text-to-image models, monocular depth estimation and text-conditioned inpainting, with viewpoint selection and continuous alignment to fuse frames into one seamless mesh. *(doc, high; other)*
