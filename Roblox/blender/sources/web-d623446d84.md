## Summary
Andrew Price's BlenderGuru post (undated, covers Blender 2.64 to 2.71) benchmarks Cycles render speed across versions on BMW, Tears of Steel quadbot, hair/fur and SSS scenes. Claims Cycles is about twice as fast as two years earlier. Does not cover Claude, blender-mcp, bpy scripting, Roblox export, or any asset pipeline.

## Claims
- c1: Cycles render speed in Blender 2.71 is roughly twice as fast as two years earlier, per the author's tests on BMW and Tears of Steel scenes. *(data, medium; other)*
- c2: Author reports 2.71 was fastest in every test, with CPU and GPU both tested on one computer. *(data, medium; other)*

## Numbers
- n1: **Cycles speedup vs two years earlier**: ~2x Author's claim across BMW and Tears of Steel tests, 2.71 vs older versions
- n2: **SSS render time change**: nearly halved Subsurface scattering, introduced May 2013, CPU only, over just over a year
