Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
arXiv abstract for TextMesh (2023): text-to-3D method that uses an SDF-backbone NeRF for better mesh extraction and a texture finetuning step to reduce over-saturation. No content on Blender, Claude, bpy, or Roblox pipelines.

## Claims
- c1: TextMesh extends NeRF with an SDF backbone to improve 3D mesh extraction from text-prompt-driven generation. *(doc, high; other)*
- c2: It proposes a texture finetuning step that removes high saturation and improves detail, addressing the cartoonish look of prior NeRF-based text-to-3D methods. *(doc, high; other)*
- c3: Prior diffusion-supervised text-to-3D methods output NeRFs rather than meshes, which the authors say makes them impractical for most real applications. *(doc, medium; other)*
