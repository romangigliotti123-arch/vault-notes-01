## Summary
Theory post on Conditional Random Fields (CRFs) for sequence labelling such as POS tagging. Explains why modelling p(Y|X) avoids the cost of modelling p(X), and why first-order label dependence (a linear-chain CRF) beats naive independence. It has no Blender, bpy, blender-mcp, Roblox, or asset-pipeline content.
