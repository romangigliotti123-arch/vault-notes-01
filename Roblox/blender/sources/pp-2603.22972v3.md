## Summary
arXiv abstract page for WorldMesh (ECCV 2026), a research method generating multi-room 3D scenes by building a mesh scaffold from text, populating it with objects, and rendering it to condition image diffusion. Contains no content on Blender, Claude, blender-mcp, bpy, or Roblox pipelines.

## Claims
- c1: WorldMesh splits 3D scene generation into a mesh scaffold (walls, floors) and appearance synthesis by image diffusion conditioned on renders of that scaffold, for cross-view consistency. *(doc, high; other)*
- c2: Objects are added to the mesh scaffold via image synthesis, segmentation and object reconstruction. This is a research pipeline, not tied to Blender or Roblox. *(doc, high; other)*
