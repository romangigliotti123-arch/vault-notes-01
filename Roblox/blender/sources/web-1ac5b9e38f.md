## Summary
Roblox docs page on optional PBR textures for custom characters. Explains PBR uses multiple texture maps for lighting response, typically made in tools like Substance 3D or ZBrush. Says the Blender texturing tutorial in this section skips PBR, pointing to the surface-appearance docs. Nearly no Blender, bpy, or export-pipeline content.

## Claims
- c1: PBR textures use multiple texture maps to define how a surface reacts under different lighting and environmental conditions. *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c2: PBR textures are typically created in texturing tools such as Substance 3D or ZBrush, not in Blender. *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c3: The Roblox character Blender texturing tutorial does not cover the PBR texturing process; advanced PBR is deferred to the surface-appearance docs. *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
