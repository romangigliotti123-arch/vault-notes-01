## Summary
Roblox DevForum staff post (2026-09-23) on Studio's built-in generative tools: texture generation (ribbon button, right-click > Generate > Texture, /generate_texture), /segment_mesh for imported meshes (up to five part names per run), and image previews (four options) for /generate_mesh and /generate_procedural_model. Does not cover Blender, bpy, blender-mcp, or Blender-to-Roblox export.

## Claims
- c1: Post covers Roblox Studio's built-in Assistant generation features, not Blender or external DCC tools. *(doc, high; other)*
- c2: /segment_mesh accepts up to five part names per run; more parts require running it again on a resulting part. *(doc, high; other)*
- c3: Generated textures are described as a starting point; the post recommends exporting them to external texturing software for further editing. *(doc, medium; other)*
- c4: Image previews offer four generated image options before committing to a generated mesh or procedural model. *(doc, high; other)*

## Numbers
- n1: **segment_mesh part names per run**: 5 
- n2: **image preview options**: 4
