# web-4cfbea9fa4

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Promotional post announcing Poliigon, a texture library by Andrew Price (BlenderGuru). Describes PBR material sets with pre-generated maps, 6K-24K seamless textures, overlays, repeating patterns and design elements, plus a free 30-day trial and subscription pricing. Nothing on Claude, blender-mcp, bpy, Roblox export or Roblox asset rules.

## Claims
- c1: Poliigon provides PBR material sets with all maps (e.g. normal, roughness) pre-generated, downloadable in one click, saving roughly 20-60 minutes per material versus generating maps in CrazyBump or Knald. *(opinion, medium; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c2: Poliigon textures are captured mostly at 6K and some up to 24K resolution, and almost all are seamless (tileable). *(doc, medium; mesh-texture-limits)*

## Numbers
- n1: **texture resolution**: 6K (most), up to 24K 
- n2: **library size**: 1,000+ PBR materials, 192 patterns, 600+ design elements (at publication) 
- n3: **pricing**: free 30-day trial; $17/month or $170/year
