# pp-2411.02293v5

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
arXiv abstract page for Hunyuan3D 1.0, a two-stage text/image-to-3D generator (multi-view diffusion, then feed-forward reconstruction). Only abstract-level info; nothing about Blender, Claude, bpy, or Roblox pipelines.

## Claims
- c1: Hunyuan3D 1.0 is a two-stage approach (lite and standard versions) supporting text- and image-conditioned 3D generation; stage 1 is multi-view diffusion, stage 2 is feed-forward reconstruction. *(doc, high; other)*
- c2: Multi-view RGB generation takes about 4 seconds; feed-forward reconstruction of the 3D asset takes about 7 seconds. *(doc, high; other)*
- c3: Text-conditioned generation uses the Hunyuan-DiT text-to-image model first. The standard version has 3x more parameters than the lite version and other existing models. *(doc, medium; other)*

## Numbers
- n1: **multi-view diffusion time**: ~4 s generating multi-view RGB images
- n2: **reconstruction time**: ~7 s feed-forward 3D reconstruction from multi-view images
- n3: **standard vs lite parameters**: 3x standard version has more parameters than lite and other existing models
