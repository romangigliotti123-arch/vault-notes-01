# off-dev_3550013

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Roblox staff preview (2025-03-14) of a Layered Clothing fitting algorithm update, planned rollout to all users by about 3/31. Addresses flaring, bulkiness, and inner-layer show-through. Replaces cage alignment with a linear deformer, samples inner and outer cages, and deprecates three WrapLayer/WrapTarget properties. Not about Blender, bpy, or the Blender-to-Roblox pipeline.

## Claims
- c1: Layered Clothing fitting algorithm update targeted for rollout to all users by about 2025-03-31; preview posted 2025-03-14. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c2: Update addresses three fit defect classes: flaring/bellbottoming, bulkiness, and inner-layer show-through. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c3: WrapLayer.Puffiness, WrapLayer.ShrinkFactor, and WrapTarget.Stiffness are deprecated; they are ignored on existing LC assets and body parts with non-default values. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c4: Cage alignment step switches from Radial Basis Function to a linear deformer; RBF is kept for clothing fit. Source says this fixes overshoot. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c5: New algorithm samples both inner and outer cages to reduce clothing bulk and layer penetration. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c6: Improved compression computes initial cage stack, then computes a combined outer silhouette, then compresses the stack with the linear deformer to match it, reducing lumpiness from underlying layers. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c7: Roblox states performance will be the same or better in all cases. *(doc, medium; avatar-ugc-accessories)*

## Numbers
- n1: **Rollout target**: ~3/31 (2025) 
- n2: **Fit defect classes addressed**: 3 
- n3: **Deprecated WrapLayer/WrapTarget properties**: 3 

## Techniques
- t1: **Linear deformer cage offset**: Offset each cage point directly to position the next layer's cage, replacing RBF-based cage alignment; keep RBF for clothing fit to retain smoothness.
- t2: **Inner+outer cage sampling**: Sample both inner and outer avatar cages when fitting clothing to reduce bulk and layer penetration.
