Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Video showcases "Action Lightrooms," a Blender add-on for lighting Roblox GFX scenes with presets. Covers installing from a Gumroad zip, the N-panel UI, adding and deleting preset lights, and Eevee vs Cycles results. Free demo has 10 presets; full version has 20 and costs at least £10. Not about blender-mcp, bpy scripting, or the Roblox import/export pipeline.

## Claims
- c1: [0:02] Action Lightrooms adds up to 20 lighting presets for Roblox GFX scenes in Blender; free demo includes 10 presets. *(doc, high; creator-workflows)*
