# web-1e6940581a

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Roblox creator doc describing the extra head objects in the avatar body template. Lists 8 head meshes (Head_Geo, UpperTeeth_Geo, LowerTeeth_Geo, Tongue_Geo, RightLash_Geo, RightEye_Geo, LeftLash_Geo, LeftEye_Geo) and 5 head bones that may differ between templates. Says these must be joined/removed during cleanup before export or Marketplace validation fails. Not about Blender MCP or bpy scripting.

## Claims
- c1: Avatar body template includes 8 extra head mesh objects: Head_Geo, UpperTeeth_Geo, LowerTeeth_Geo, Tongue_Geo, RightLash_Geo, RightEye_Geo, LeftLash_Geo, LeftEye_Geo. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c2: Leaving extra head meshes and face bones in the project at final export causes validation issues on Marketplace upload. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c3: Extra head bone children: Tongue, LowerTeeth, UpperTeeth, LeftEye, RightEye; these may differ between templates. *(doc, high; rigging-animation)*
- c4: Cleanup before export requires joining head geometry and removing extra bones, per linked Roblox docs pages. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*

## Numbers
- n1: **extra head mesh objects**: 8 
- n2: **extra head bone children**: 5 

## Techniques
- t1: **Join head geometry and remove extra bones before export**: In Blender cleanup, join the separate head meshes (see combine-head-geometry doc) and remove the extra face bones (see remove-extra-bones doc) prior to exporting to avoid validation errors.

## Quotes (brief, for attribution)
> To avoid validation errors, you must join and remove these extra objects during the cleanup process prior to exporting.  (create.roblox.com)
