## Summary
Darkfall video (2020) demoing v1.03 of a Blender text-tool addon: Radial Curve tool (curved text along Bezier, top/bottom position) and Radial Border tool (presets or custom characters, dot/letter spacing via modifier). Covers select-both-before-transform and apply-scale tips. No Roblox, Claude, blender-mcp, bpy, or export content.

## Claims
- c1: [0:00] Video is about a Blender text-tool addon update (v1.03 per title, 'Version 1.3' in narration) adding Radial Curve and Radial Border tools; not about Roblox, Claude, blender-mcp or bpy. *(doc, high; other)*
- c2: [0:12] Radial Curve tool: opened from sidebar (N) in the text tool addon, prompts for text, creates curved text around a Bezier curve; only option is position (top or bottom). *(demo, high; other)*

- c7: [5:18] Author says the result is 'not perfect' and overlaps may remain; presented as a quick way to make custom borders. *(opinion, medium; other)*

## Techniques
- t1: **Apply scale before radial border fill**: Scale border objects, then Ctrl+A apply scale so the pattern closes; then scale the Bezier curve to fix remaining overlap.
- t2: **Move radial text with its curve**: Select text and Bezier curve together, then G or S to move/scale; adjust offset and spacing in the text tool options.
