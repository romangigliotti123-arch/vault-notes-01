Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Roblox official tutorial for creating a layered accessory (t-shirt) in Blender from provided mannequin/cage/armature templates, texturing, rigging to Roblox templates, exporting, and importing into Studio to convert to a Class.Accessory. Manual Blender workflow; no Claude, blender-mcp, or bpy content.

## Claims
- c1: Layered accessories (layered clothing) stretch and fit on avatar via rigging armature and cage meshes so they animate and layer over existing clothing. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c2: Tutorial workflow: model shirt on mannequin template, texture, cage with Roblox template cages, rig with Roblox armature templates, export from Blender, import to Studio and convert to Class.Accessory. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c3: Tutorial is aimed at creators with moderate Blender experience; the same concepts apply to other third-party modeling apps. *(doc, high; creator-workflows)*
- c4: Marketplace listing requires Roblox Plus or Premium account, an upload fee, and a publishing advance. *(doc, high; common-mistakes-fixes)*

## Techniques
- t1: **Layered clothing setup from templates**: Start from provided Blender mannequin template, model clothing over it, apply Roblox template cage and armature, then export and convert in Studio to Class.Accessory.
