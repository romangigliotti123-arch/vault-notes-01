# web-10a771fa93

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Roblox docs index of downloadable layered-accessory reference files: blank Classic/Rthro mannequins (with and without body cages), and Blender, Maya, and general FBX rig and cage templates with R15 armature and attachment points. Mostly a file list; no bpy, MCP, or export-setting guidance. Only marginally relevant to the Blender-to-Roblox pipeline.

## Claims
- c1: Roblox provides blank mannequin FBX files for Classic, Rthro Normal, and Rthro Slender bodies; *_With-Cages variants contain body cages for layered accessory design. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c2: Cage-variant mannequin FBX files may not import correctly into Studio until cage objects are configured/modified. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c3: Rig_and_Attachments_Template (.blend/.ma/.fbx) contains a standard R15 rig with correct naming conventions and attachment points for rigging bodies and layered accessories. *(doc, high; rigging-animation)*
- c4: Clothing_Cage_Template (.blend/.ma/.fbx) includes full-body inner and outer cage meshes for caging layered accessories. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c5: Combined-Template.blend/.ma bundles the rig armature, body cages, and attachment points for rigging and caging bodies and accessories together. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c6: Doc says Blender and Maya users should use the program-specific template files; the general FBX templates were exported from Maya and are for other modeling apps. *(doc, high; blender-roblox-export-import)*
- c7: Source contains no blender-mcp, bpy scripting, or Claude-specific guidance; it only lists downloadable reference files. *(doc, high; claude-blender-ai)*
- c8: Source lists no FBX export settings, scale, axis, or transform-apply steps; it only names template files. *(doc, medium; blender-roblox-export-import)*
- c9: Caging example zip is linked to a YouTube guide titled 'How to cage Roblox 3D clothing'; the link label in the source mislabels it as Additional-FBX-assets.zip. *(doc, medium; avatar-ugc-accessories)*

## Techniques
- t1: **Start from cage template**: Download Clothing_Cage_Template.blend, cage the accessory around the inner/outer body cage meshes, then export.
- t2: **Start from R15 armature template**: Open Rig_and_Attachments_Template.blend to get R15 armature with correct naming and attachment points for rigging a body or accessory.
