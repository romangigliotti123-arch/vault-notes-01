# off-dev_4820200

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Staff announcement (dated 2026-08-19) that Roblox removed 3D Marketplace items whose mesh, texture, or animation data were overly similar to previously published items. Covers no strikes for existing items, Robux refunds within seven business days, appeal and Creator Dashboard notices, and exceptions for same-user or same-Community items. Not about Blender, Claude, bpy, or the export pipeline; only tangentially relevant.

## Claims
- c1: Roblox removed 3D Marketplace items whose meshes, textures, or animation data were overly similar copies of previously uploaded content. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c2: Detection looks at the combination of mesh plus texture; a face mask template with a unique face is not flagged, but same mesh+texture with minimal change is. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c3: Existing removed items get no account strikes or penalties; future publishing of overly similar items may lead to moderation or suspension. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c4: Removed items are flagged as greyed out in Creator Dashboard and creators get a system message notification. *(doc, high; automation-and-creator-workflows)*
- c5: Upload and publishing fees are not refunded for items removed for policy violation. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c6: Buyers of removed items get full Robux refunds automatically within seven business days, plus an Inbox message. *(doc, high; other)*
- c7: Overlap is not flagged when both items come from the same user, the same Community, or a user and a Community where that user can manage avatar items; Limited versus non-Limited pairs are still violations. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c8: In-experience creation items may look identical; the copy-protection logic accounts for this, but using IEC to bypass Marketplace policy is still moderated. *(doc, medium; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c9: Wrongly removed items are contested via the appeal process; removals across alt accounts are reported via DevForum bug report. *(doc, high; automation-and-creator-workflows)*
- c10: Templates or simple designs should include clear, distinctive changes from the source design to avoid detection. *(doc, medium; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c11: Roblox plans to extend these similarity detection models to additional Marketplace item types over time. *(doc, medium; avatar-ugc-accessories)*

## Numbers
- n1: **Refund window**: 7 business days Full Robux refunds for buyers of removed items, issued automatically

## Techniques
- t1: **Distinctive variant of template**: Start from a shared template mesh or texture, then make clear, distinctive changes to mesh and texture together so the combination is not flagged as overly similar.
- t2: **Single-account or single-Community uploads for variants**: Publish item variants under one account or within one Community, which is exempt from overly-similar flagging, to avoid publishing issues.
