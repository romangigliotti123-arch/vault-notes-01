# pp-2601.16148v2

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
arXiv abstract for ActionMesh (CVPR 2026), a feed-forward temporal 3D diffusion model producing rig-free, topology-consistent animated meshes from video, text, or mesh+text. No content on Blender, Claude, bpy, or Roblox; only marginally related to animation/retargeting.

## Claims
- c1: ActionMesh generates animated 3D meshes feed-forward from monocular video, text, or a 3D mesh plus animation text prompt; outputs are rig-free and topology consistent. *(doc, medium; rigging-animation)*
- c2: Method adds a temporal axis to 3D diffusion and uses a temporal 3D autoencoder converting independent per-frame shapes into deformations of a reference shape. *(doc, medium; rigging-animation)*
- c3: Authors report state-of-the-art geometric accuracy and temporal consistency on Consistent4D and Objaverse; v2 adds the ActionBench benchmark and more baselines. *(doc, medium; other)*
- c4: Consistent topology is said to enable texturing and retargeting; the abstract gives no Blender or Roblox export details. *(doc, low; other)*
