# yt-7hJ0wi2nEEA

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Video about 3D-printing a modular playable D&D city (1.5 x 2.5 m table). Uses ChatGPT to plan layouts, split AI-generated models into printable parts, build assembly instructions and print batches, and cut boolean holes for stacking walls. Mentions 3D AI Studio credit costs. No Blender, bpy, Roblox, or MCP content.

## Claims
- c1: [4:05] Tripo and Hunyuan built-in segmentation tools were judged too slow for splitting buildings into printable parts; the speaker used ChatGPT instead. *(experience, medium; other)*
- c2: [14:18] One model in Hunyuan 3D cost about 100 credits and Tripo 3.0.1 about 35 credits on 3D AI Studio; a Studio plan was insufficient, so Pro was used for months. *(experience, medium; other)*
- c3: [8:28] Asking ChatGPT to generate per-building assembly instructions and batch print files for Bambu Lab and Elegoo Saturn 4K printers worked well, though some parts were lost or misprinted. *(experience, low; other)*

## Numbers
- n1: **3D generation credits**: Hunyuan 3D ~100 credits; Tripo 3.0.1 ~35 credits per model 
- n2: **Table size**: 1.5 x 2.5 m 
- n3: **Resin spend**: at most ~$400 

## Techniques
- t1: **LLM-assisted mesh segmentation for printing**: Generate a building with an image-to-3D tool, then prompt ChatGPT to split it into color-marked parts, add interior walls and windows, and cut matching holes so towers and walls stack.
- t2: **Hybrid resin and filament printing**: Print fine details (windows, banners, small items) in resin and large structural parts in filament to avoid warping heavy roofs.
