## Summary
Roblox creator docs index page for avatar tutorials. Lists three Blender-based tutorial tracks: rigid accessories (Model, Texture, Import into Studio, Fit and convert with Accessory Fitting Tool, Publish), clothing (Model, Texture, Rig to R15, Cage, Export, Import as Accessory), and bodies (template pick, Model, Texture, Caging, Cleanup, Export). Prerequisite: Blender 3.0+. Contains no bpy, MCP, or Claude-specific content.

## Claims
- c1: Roblox's avatar tutorials use Blender 3.0+ as the modeling tool. *(doc, high; blender-roblox-export-import)*
- c2: Rigid accessory workflow: Model, Texture, Import into Studio, Fit and convert via Accessory Fitting Tool, then Publish to Marketplace. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c3: Clothing workflow: Model, Texture, Rig to standard R15, Cage (inner/outer surfaces), Export from Blender, Import into Studio and convert to Accessory. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c4: Body workflow starts from Roblox provided avatar templates with pre-baked components: pick template, model, texture, caging, cleanup, export. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c5: Clothing items are attached to a standard R15 rig during the Rig step, before caging. *(doc, medium; rigging-animation)*
- c6: Studio's Importer is used to bring custom rigid accessory meshes into Studio. *(doc, medium; blender-roblox-export-import)*
- c7: Source does not mention blender-mcp, bpy scripting, or Claude; no coverage of AI-driven Blender workflows. *(doc, high; claude-blender-ai)*
- c8: No triangle limits, texture sizes, or UV rules are stated on this page; they are deferred to linked subpages. *(doc, medium; mesh-texture-limits)*
