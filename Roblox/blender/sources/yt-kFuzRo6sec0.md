## Techniques
- t1: **Untextured Blender base render**: Build scene and camera move in Blender without materials or lights; render solid mode; feed the clip to Seedance as the video reference.
- t2: **Skill-file prompt generation**: Upload readme.md and skill.md to Claude Code, answer its interview questions (lighting, hero subject), take the generated main and negative prompts.
- t3: **@video tagging**: Click the at-video token in the prompt to tag the uploaded video; convert every video reference to a tag before generating.
