# off-dev_4358444

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Roblox staff post (2026-02-12) announcing higher max texture resolution for platform avatar heads, bodies, layered clothing and rigid accessories: up to 2048x2048 for albedo, metal, roughness and bump maps. Delivered via texture streaming. Devices under 3.5GB RAM stay capped at 1k. Source does not cover Blender, bpy, blender-mcp, or the FBX/glTF export pipeline; only texture-limit facts are relevant.

## Claims
- c1: Max uploadable texture resolution for avatar heads, bodies, layered clothing and rigid accessories is now 2048x2048 (2k), for albedo, metal, roughness and bump maps. *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c2: Platform avatars receive higher-resolution textures via texture streaming when appropriate, stated to not affect framerate. *(doc, medium; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c3: Devices with under 3.5GB RAM (lower-end mobile) keep a 1k max texture resolution even if the uploaded texture is 2k. *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c4: Player-visible 2k results vary by device type, configuration and usage; the texture streaming post's platform status section is the reference for where 2k is visible. *(doc, medium; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c5: Staff say performance was tested across low, medium and high end devices and scenarios, with no negative performance impact found. This is the vendor's claim, not independently verified. *(doc, low; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c6: Updated limits are listed in the Avatar Setup documentation; this post does not itself state the full limit table. *(doc, medium; other)*

## Numbers
- n1: **max avatar texture resolution**: 2048x2048 (2k) albedo, metal, roughness, bump maps; heads, bodies, layered clothing, rigid accessories
- n2: **low-end device cap**: 1k, devices under 3.5GB RAM applies even when uploaded texture is 2k
- n3: **example 2k texture size**: 1280x1280 (2k example images listed at 1280x1280, 181-300 KB) example images in post; body/jeans 2k 278 KB, skin 2k 300 KB
