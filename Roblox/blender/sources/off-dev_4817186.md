# off-dev_4817186

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Roblox staff update on avatar body validation. Bounding-box minimums lowered and maximums slightly raised, standardized across Normal, Slender and Classic body scales. New checks block geometric workarounds on newly published avatars. Custom Height defaults of 3.6-6.5 studs apply to some templates. Autofix for existing small avatars is planned. Nothing here covers Blender, Claude, bpy, or export pipelines.

## Claims
- c1: Bounding-box minimum dimensions lowered and maximum limits slightly raised, standardized across Normal, Slender and Classic body scales. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c2: Part dimensions are measured after rotating each part to a standard orientation, so any pose (A, T, I) is measured the same way; length is the rotated part's height, width and depth come from its cross-section. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c3: Newly published avatars now fail validation if they use geometric workarounds that distort the bounding box. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c4: Custom Height in Avatar Settings defaults to 3.6-6.5 studs for some templates, upscaling avatars below the prior bounding-box range. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c5: Creators are advised to enable Custom Height and Consistent Gameplay in competitive games that rely on consistent hitboxes and body sizes. *(doc, medium; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c6: Existing small avatars will get an autofix that removes problematic mesh pieces and updates bounding boxes; creators are prompted to accept or reject within coming months. *(doc, medium; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c7: Small avatars are not planned for removal; avatars that cannot be made compliant by removing workaround geometry may be taken down later. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c8: Small avatars do not rescale in experiences that require uniform heights, which is the stated reason for the change. *(doc, medium; other)*
