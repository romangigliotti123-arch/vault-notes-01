# web-6f9b567670

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
DevForum thread on Roblox's automatic RenderFidelity (LOD) for MeshParts. A 2020 user reports it fails for very large distant parts and tiny nearby parts, which can keep more triangles than they should. A 2023 user reports seam gaps between multi-MeshPart assets when performance LODs swap in, and asks for custom LOD naming. Staff replies (2020, 2024) give no timeline. Not about Blender, bpy or Claude.

## Claims
- c1: Automatic RenderFidelity is unusable for parts that are not normal-sized; large far parts keep high triangle counts while tiny nearby parts get more triangles than a massive distant part. *(experience, medium; roblox-mesh-limits)*
- c2: Automatic RenderFidelity/LOD generation and object selection were being reworked by Roblox staff as of April 2020, with no firm ETA. *(doc, medium; roblox-mesh-limits)*
- c3: Automatic LODs on multi-MeshPart assets that must line up can show gaps/seams when performance LODs are swapped in. *(experience, medium; roblox-mesh-limits)*
- c4: Automatic LOD can over-crush meshes and lose important shading detail, per a 2023 user report. *(experience, medium; roblox-mesh-limits)*
- c5: A 2023 user aligned multi-part modular meshes by adding a zero-area triangle to match bounding boxes, and proposed name-suffix custom LODs (e.g. '[Lod:2]') as a workaround. *(experience, low; common-mistakes-fixes)*
- c6: As of February 2024, Roblox staff stated no specific timeline for fixing the automatic RenderFidelity/LOD issues. *(doc, high; roblox-mesh-limits)*

## Numbers
- n1: **Thread date**: 2020-03-06 (first post); staff replies 2020-04-17 and 2024-02-02 Dates of the posts in the thread

## Techniques
- t1: **Zero-area triangle bounding-box alignment**: Add a zero-area triangle to each modular MeshPart so bounding boxes match, keeping parts aligned when LODs swap. Reported by a user as a workaround for their own export tooling.
