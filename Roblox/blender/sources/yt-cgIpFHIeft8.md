## Summary
Off-topic for Claude/blender-mcp/bpy/Roblox pipeline. A personal vlog about building a basic bone rig in Blender 2.79 (missing Symmetrize, no multi-object edit, bone shapes need manual edit-mode work, no weighted normals modifier) and sharing it on Gumroad. Mentions the rig is for Roblox-style blocky avatars but gives no export or import steps.

- c5: [6:13] The 2.79 rig is distributed free-ish as a Gumroad download as a passion project; the creator's GPU does not work with 2.79, so renders are CPU-only. *(experience, low; creator-workflows)*
