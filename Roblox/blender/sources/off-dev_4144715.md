## Summary
Staff announcement (2025-12-11) that Roblox removed partially or fully invisible heads from the Marketplace and inventories. Buyers get automatic Robux refunds within seven business days; equipped heads revert to default; published creators get no strikes. Relevant only to head/accessory compliance, not Blender, bpy, or the Blender-to-Roblox pipeline.

## Claims
- c1: Roblox removed partially or fully invisible heads from the Avatar Marketplace and user inventories, effective immediately, citing the Marketplace Body Parts Requirements policy. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c2: Creators who published invisible heads get no moderation strike for previously uploaded assets; they are notified by email. Buyers are refunded automatically in Robux within seven business days. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c3: Equipped non-compliant heads revert to the default head; users can re-equip any other head in inventory. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c4: New uploads that circumvent policy or bypass validation rules for Marketplace items face standard moderation consequences, effective immediately. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c5: Users still seeing invisible heads should report them via the Marketplace report system; the post gives no timeline for full removal. *(doc, medium; automation-and-creator-workflows)*
