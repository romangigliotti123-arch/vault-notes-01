# off-dev_4137281

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Roblox staff update (2025-12-08) on improved auto-skinning for eyebrows and eyelashes. Adds leader/follower vertex designation to WrapLayer.AutoSkin (requires EnabledOverride), enables eyebrows/eyelashes in Dynamic Head bundles under Makeup Studio Beta, and describes DoubleSided/Transparency setup for eyelash MeshParts with Blender alpha-export auto-fix and a Maya FBX known issue. Little on Blender scripting or MCP.

## Claims
- c1: WrapLayer.AutoSkin gained leader/follower vertex designation: leaders deform with body mesh, followers follow nearest leader vertices. Fixes unnatural stretching of eyelashes connecting to eyeball. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c2: Leader/follower skinning works automatically only when WrapLayer.AutoSkin is set to EnabledOverride. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c3: Eyebrows and eyelashes can be created as part of a Dynamic Head bundle; future plans allow selling on Marketplace and swapping in Avatar Editor. Requires Makeup Studio Beta enabled. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c4: Eyebrow/eyelash MeshParts are copied into the avatar's Accessories folder during Play to equip them. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c5: Sample eyelashes require MeshPart DoubleSided = true and SurfaceAppearance AlphaMode = Transparency for the intended look. *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c6: Importer auto-sets DoubleSided and Transparency if, in Blender, the color map is connected to alpha in the PBR material and exported as GLTF/FBX. *(doc, high; blender-roblox-export-import)*
- c7: Known issue: FBX exported from Maya does not get Transparency/DoubleSided auto-set; must change manually in Studio after import. *(doc, high; blender-roblox-export-import)*
- c8: Preview in Play: Play/Pause and Reset buttons (top-right) drive an eye open/close animation to test skinning across avatars. *(doc, medium; avatar-ugc-accessories)*

## Techniques
- t1: **Leader/follower vertex skinning**: Mark accessory vertices as leaders (anchor to body mesh) or followers (move with nearest leaders); set WrapLayer.AutoSkin to EnabledOverride; test with Play/Reset eye animation.
- t2: **Alpha-from-color export**: In Blender, connect color map to alpha in PBR material, export GLTF/FBX so importer sets Transparency and DoubleSided.
