## Summary
arXiv abstract page for FA-QEM, a quadric-error-metric mesh simplification method for dense, noisy, non-manifold generated and scanned meshes. It combines geometric deviation, boundary curvature and normal consistency terms. It claims lower error and faster runtime than existing methods. It says nothing about Blender, Claude, MCP, bpy or Roblox. Only the abstract is available, with no numbers or code.

## Claims
- c1: Generative 3D pipelines (neural rendering, large-scale 3D asset generation) often produce dense, noisy, non-manifold meshes that need simplification before downstream use. *(doc, medium; procedural-generation)*
- c2: FA-QEM is a multi-term quadric error metric (geometric deviation, boundary curvature, normal consistency) intended to preserve sharp features under aggressive simplification. The abstract claims lower geometric error and faster runtime than existing methods, with no figures given. *(doc, medium; roblox-mesh-limits)*
- c3: The authors claim better geometric simplification improves downstream appearance transfer, using successive mapping techniques for texture mapping. The abstract gives no detail. *(doc, low; texturing-baking-uv)*
- c4: Evaluation covered AI-generated meshes, Thingi10K and the Real-World Textured Things dataset. Accepted at the CVPR 2026 3D4S workshop (Best Paper Award Runner-up). *(doc, high; procedural-generation)*

## Techniques
- t1: **Feature-Aware QEM decimation (FA-QEM)**: Add boundary-curvature and normal-consistency terms to the standard quadric error. Use the combined quadric to place each collapsed vertex optimally and keep sharp features. Only the abstract is available, so implementation details are not given.
