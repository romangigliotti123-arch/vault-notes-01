Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
arXiv abstract page for Stream3D, a training-free streaming wrapper for frozen view-conditioned 3D generators (SAM 3D, TRELLIS, Hunyuan3D) using an evidence-scored fixed-size frame memory. It contains nothing about Blender, Claude, bpy, or Roblox pipelines. Off-topic for this subject.

## Claims
- c1: Stream3D is a training-free mechanism that turns a frozen view-conditioned 3D generator into a streaming generator with constant cross-chunk memory, using an evidence score to cache the most informative historical frames. *(doc, high; other)*
- c2: Applying SAM 3D, TRELLIS or Hunyuan3D to each streaming frame independently causes severe temporal inconsistency; Stream3D reportedly beats KV-cache reuse and flow-based feature editing baselines on photometric and geometric metrics. *(doc, medium; other)*
