# web-502fe911a1

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
2018 DevForum thread defining "low poly" for Roblox builders. Replies split on whether low poly means a cartoony art style (fewer bricks, F3X builds) or just a low triangle count. Mentions Blender for mesh-making and an unverified Roblox mesh triangle limit. Little on Blender, Claude, bpy, or the Roblox import pipeline.

## Claims
- c1: Poster says Roblox uploaded meshes are limited to 5,000 triangles, then says it may be 3,000. Unverified and dated 2022 reply. *(opinion, low; roblox-mesh-limits)*
- c2: Low poly is a style meaning minimum polygons to show the form; it looks cartoony. Counter-view: low poly is only a triangle count and not a style. *(opinion, medium; creator-workflows)*
- c3: Practice method: recreate concept art by breaking the subject into larger, simpler geometric shapes, then add only slightly more detail. *(opinion, medium; creator-workflows)*
- c4: Ukendio, who says they build low poly in Blender, defines low poly as objects under 8k (presumably triangles), with no source. *(opinion, low; other)*
- c5: Low-poly props reused many times, with instancing, reduce memory; low-poly terrain is also practical. Opinion from a 2022 reply. *(opinion, low; creator-workflows)*

## Numbers
- n1: **Roblox mesh triangle limit (as claimed)**: 5,000 (or possibly 3,000) 
- n2: **Low-poly threshold (Ukendio, unsourced)**: under 8k 
- n3: **F3X minimum move increment (JukeRblx, F3X not Blender)**: 0.25 studs 
- n4: **F3X minimum rotation increment (JukeRblx)**: 10 degrees 

## Techniques
- t1: **Block-out with large simple shapes**: Keep move/resize increments large (not below 0.25) and rotation at 10 degrees or more. Break reference art into big simple shapes first. Add only a little detail after the layout.
