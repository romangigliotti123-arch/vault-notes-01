## Summary
arXiv abstract page for MeshAnything (2024): a VQ-VAE plus shape-conditioned decoder-only transformer that converts any 3D representation into low-face-count artist-style meshes. Claims hundreds of times fewer faces than current mesh extraction methods, with comparable precision. No Blender, Claude, or Roblox content; only tangential relevance to low-poly mesh generation for game assets.

## Claims
- c1: MeshAnything generates Artist-Created Meshes with hundreds of times fewer faces than current mesh extraction methods, improving storage, rendering and simulation efficiency, with precision comparable to previous methods (abstract's claim). *(doc, medium; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c2: Architecture: a VQ-VAE learns a mesh vocabulary, then a shape-conditioned decoder-only transformer generates meshes autoregressively on that vocabulary. *(doc, high; other)*
- c3: Paper says current mesh extraction methods use dense faces and ignore geometric features, which causes inefficiency and complicated post-processing. *(doc, medium; other)*
