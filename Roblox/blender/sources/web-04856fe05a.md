Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Roblox doc on texturing a character face in Blender Texture Paint: raise resolution to 2048/4096, paint facial features (radius 5px, strength 0.3, X-mirror) with an opaque texture, then return to 1024x1024. Covers face only; no bpy, MCP, or export steps.

## Claims
- c1: Face texturing step: set texture resolution to 2048 or 4096 in Texture Paint tab before painting facial details, then resize back to 1024x1024 when done. *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c2: Brush settings for facial detail: Radius 5px, Strength 0.300, with X-Axis Mirroring enabled under Symmetry. *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c3: Face texture should be opaque only so the custom skin tone remains visible; uses a neutral dark shade for shadows. *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c4: Painted detail (nostrils, wrinkles, ears, chin) looks softer once texture resolution is resized down, so verify appearance after resizing. *(doc, medium; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c5: Verify face textures across several skin tones to confirm they look acceptable with various skin types. *(doc, medium; avatar-ugc-accessories)*

## Numbers
- n1: **Working texture resolution for face painting**: 2048 or 4096 px 
- n2: **Final texture resolution**: 1024 x 1024 
- n3: **Brush Radius**: 5px 
- n4: **Brush Strength**: 0.300 

## Techniques
- t1: **Resolution swap for detail painting**: Raise texture resolution to 2048/4096, paint fine facial detail with small brush and X-mirror, then drop back to 1024x1024 for the final texture.
