## Summary
Roblox Avatar Team announcement (dated 2026-10-22) of UGC Validation Visualization in Studio. Lets creators run validation on bodies, heads, layered clothing and rigid accessories and highlight errors (red) and warnings (yellow) on the mesh. Adds Asset Quality checks for bodies: attachment placement, mesh structure, geometry integrity, vertex similarity. Says nothing about Blender, bpy, blender-mcp, or export settings; only tangentially relevant to a Blender-to-Roblox pipeline.

## Claims
- c1: UGC Validation Visualization is available in Studio under the Avatar tab; it supports validation for Bodies, Heads, Layered Clothing and Rigid Accessories. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c2: Workflow: Avatar tab > UGC Validation Visualization, select avatar/asset in Explorer, click Run Validation, select messages to visualize issues. Errors shown red, warnings yellow. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c3: Asset Quality checks for UGC bodies cover four areas: attachment placement (neck, hip, grip), mesh structure (disconnected mesh parts limits), geometry integrity (degenerate triangles, triangle intersections), vertex similarity (clusters of closely spaced vertices). *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c4: Recommended: avatar bodies manifold (each edge shared by exactly two faces) and watertight with no holes or missing faces; heads excluded from watertight requirement. *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c5: Asset Quality measures technical soundness, not realism. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c6: Planned future: auto-fix options for common validation errors, Make Up and Animation validation visualization, and validation error surfacing through MCP. *(doc, medium; automation-and-creator-workflows)*
- c7: Source does not mention Blender, bpy, blender-mcp, FBX/glTF export settings, or scale/axis handling; it is only relevant indirectly as a pre-publish check for meshes exported into Studio. *(doc, high; blender-roblox-export-import)*
