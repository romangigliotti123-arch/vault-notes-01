Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Roblox creator doc page on modifying the outer cage of a clothing accessory in Blender sculpt/edit mode so it fits tightly over the mesh. Covers X-axis symmetry, Inflate brush, and wireframe edit passes. Explicit warning against deleting cage vertices or faces. Mostly manual Blender steps; no bpy, MCP, or Claude content.

## Claims
- c1: Outer cage must be fitted as close as possible to the clothing (t-shirt) mesh; always covering it, to ensure best deformation and layering. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c2: Do not delete any vertices or faces of provided cages; destructive edits can cause import issues and stop clothing importing/displaying correctly. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c3: For small angular areas such as the armpit, switch to Edit Mode and move individual vertices to avoid crowding cage vertices. *(doc, medium; common-mistakes-fixes)*
- c4: Caging workflow: Sculpt mode, X-Axis symmetry, Inflate brush (F to resize) to expand cage over majority of mesh, then disable symmetry and refine individual spots. *(doc, medium; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c5: Final tightening passes done in Edit mode with wireframe enabled. *(doc, medium; avatar-ugc-accessories)*

## Techniques
- t1: **Symmetric inflate caging**: Sculpt mode, enable X-Axis symmetry, Inflate brush, press F to set radius, expand cage one side over most of the mesh, then disable symmetry.
- t2: **Vertex refinement in Edit mode**: Enable wireframe in Edit mode, grab individual cage vertices in tight areas (armpit), repeat passes for close fit without deleting geometry.

## Quotes (brief, for attribution)
> Do not delete any vertices or faces of the provided cages. Destructive modification of the cages can cause import issues  (create.roblox.com)
