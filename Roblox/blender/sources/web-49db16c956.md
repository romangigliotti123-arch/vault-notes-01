## Summary
Part 1 of a Flutter mini-game engine log by hydroxide.dev (2024-05-03). Covers flutter_filament's Filament renderer, and why AABB collision fails for animated and rotated models. The author's workarounds: hidden Blender collision cubes and a camera node exported as a child. Nothing on Blender MCP, bpy, Claude, or the Roblox pipeline.

## Claims
- c1: Source is a 2024 blog post about flutter_filament (Filament PBR 3D rendering in Flutter) and a mini 3D game; contains no Blender, bpy, blender-mcp, Claude, or Roblox content. *(doc, high; other)*
- c2: Author found Filament/glTF AABBs unreliable for collision: rest-pose bounds ignore skinned animation, and AABBs rotate and overlap without visual collision. *(experience, high; other)*
- c3: Filament computes model bounding boxes from the rest pose at import, so animated limbs (e.g., swipe animation) fall outside the box. *(experience, high; rigging-animation)*
- c4: Author worked around collision by attaching small hidden cubes in Blender to collidable parts instead of relying on mesh/AABB collision. *(experience, medium; other)*
- c5: Author exported the canoe/character model from Blender with a camera node as a child to get an over-the-shoulder camera without engine work. *(experience, medium; other)*

## Techniques
- t1: **Hidden proxy collision cubes authored in Blender**: Add small hidden cubes to riverbanks, canoe, and scoop front in Blender; use them as collision volumes instead of model AABBs.
- t2: **Camera node exported as child of model**: Parent a camera node to the character/canoe in Blender before export so the engine-side camera follows the model.
