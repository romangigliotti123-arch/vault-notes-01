# off-dev_4301330

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Roblox staff update log for the Marketplace avatar Head policy (Feb to Oct 2026). Covers new head requirements (caged model, eye/mouth regions, 17 minimum FACS controls), Avatar Auto-Setup as the update path, and repeated deadline changes. Not about Blender, bpy, or Claude workflows; no Blender export settings, scale, or MeshPart guidance.

## Claims
- c1: All new Head submissions must include a Caged Model, defined eye and mouth regions that respond to animation, and minimum 17 FACS controls expressing Joy and Sadness. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c2: Policy applies to all Heads, both sold individually and inside Body Bundles. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c3: Updated heads keep the same Asset ID; updates are not charged to creators who already paid to upload. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c4: Heads that were not updated move off sale rather than being removed from player inventories; owners can keep and equip them. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c5: Oct 2026 update: Asset Versioning does not yet support head bundles; Q1 2027 planned. Affected creators then get one free version per off-sale head. *(doc, medium; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c6: Auto-generated 3D head versions only apply to heads with detectable front-view facial landmarks, enough resolution for auto-rig, and that pass validation. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c7: Deadlines moved several times: off-sale from Mar 3 to Mar 27 (top 5 heads) then Sept 1, 2026; removal from inventories from Jun 15 to Oct 27, 2026. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c8: Avatar Auto-Setup in Studio generates cages and FACS data; the 2D-to-3D decal-like face conversion mode shipped May 2026, about one month later than planned. *(doc, high; automation-and-creator-workflows)*
- c9: Auto-Setup may not solve heads whose faces are already posed with expressions too far from neutral (staff acknowledged). *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c10: Staff opinion: most heads should get valid FACS and caging from Auto-Setup; some need edits in glTF export via Blender or Maya. *(opinion, medium; avatar-ugc-accessories)*

## Numbers
- n1: **minimum FACS controls**: 17 
- n2: **off-sale date for non-compliant heads (initial)**: Mar 3, 2026 
- n3: **off-sale date (revised)**: Sept 1, 2026 
- n4: **inventory removal date (revised)**: Oct 27, 2026 
- n5: **top equipped heads deadline (removed)**: Mar 27, 2026 
- n6: **free versions per off-sale head after bundle support**: 1 
- n7: **Q1 2027 target for head bundle versioning**: Q1 2027 

## Techniques
- t1: **Avatar Auto-Setup head update**: In Studio, run Auto-Setup on head/body bundle to generate cage and FACS data. Preview eye/mouth animation. Adjust cage edges if needed. Resubmit via the email instructions.
- t2: **DCC fallback for heads**: If the auto-result needs model changes, export head to glTF, edit in Blender or Maya, then re-run Auto-Setup and re-submit.
