# web-7195087017

Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Roblox creator-docs general modeling specs for custom meshes from Blender/Maya: 20,000-triangle cap per mesh, watertight geometry, quads preferred, frozen transforms and max 4 bone influences per vertex, single animation track per export, and _InnerCage/_OuterCage naming. Does not cover Claude/blender-mcp, bpy, or the Roblox Blender plugin; export settings are on linked pages.

## Claims
- c1: Individual meshes cannot exceed 20,000 triangles; avatar items have separate budgets for character bodies, rigid, and layered accessories. *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c2: All geometry must be watertight with no exposed holes or backfaces; meshes must have volume (no zero thickness). *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c3: Use quads where possible; N-gons should be avoided. *(doc, high; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c4: All Blender bones (or Maya joints) must be frozen with scale 1,1,1 and rotation 0,0,0; root joint at 0,0,0. *(doc, high; rigging-animation)*
- c5: A vertex can be influenced by at most 4 bones; do not apply influences to the Root bone/joint. *(doc, high; rigging-animation)*
- c6: Only a single animation track can be exported with a mesh or model; multiple animations require separate exports. *(doc, high; rigging-animation)*
- c7: Inner and outer cage meshes must be named after the primary mesh with _InnerCage and _OuterCage suffixes. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c8: Character models must include an outer cage to equip layered accessories; do not delete cage vertices or alter cage UVs. *(doc, high; avatar-ugc-accessories)*
- c9: Roblox supports basic color textures and modern PBR textures; texture images assigned in modeling tools can import natively with the mesh. *(doc, medium; mesh-texture-limits)*
- c10: Source does not mention blender-mcp, bpy scripting, or Claude; no Claude-Blender workflow information is present. *(doc, high; claude-blender-ai)*
- c11: Source points to separate export settings pages for Blender and Maya; it does not give the FBX/scale/axis values itself. *(doc, high; blender-roblox-export-import)*

## Numbers
- n1: **max triangles per mesh**: 20000 Individual meshes; avatar items have their own budgets
- n2: **max bone influences per vertex**: 4 Vertex cannot be influenced by more than 4 bones or joints
- n3: **animation tracks per export**: 1 Single track only; separate exports for each animation
- n4: **bone scale and rotation**: scale 1,1,1; rotation 0,0,0 Frozen transforms; root joint at 0,0,0

## Techniques
- t1: **Pre-export geometry check**: Verify watertight mesh, no backfaces or holes, nonzero thickness, quads where possible, and under 20,000 triangles before exporting.
- t2: **Freeze rig transforms**: Freeze all bones or joints so scale is 1,1,1 and rotation 0,0,0; keep root at 0,0,0; cap vertex influences at 4 and skip root influences.
- t3: **Cage naming**: Name cage meshes after the primary mesh with _InnerCage and _OuterCage suffixes; keep cage vertices and UVs unaltered.
