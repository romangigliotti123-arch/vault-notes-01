Personal-use research note. Brief quotes only, with attribution; see the original at the URL above.

## Summary
Darkfall video announcing Shader Library add-on v1.6 for Blender with three new procedural materials (Brick, Sci-fi Gold, Clouds). Covers basic usage: Rendered view, Cube Projection UVs, Shade Smooth, Ctrl+A scale apply. Not related to Roblox export or Claude/MCP workflows.

## Claims
- c1: [0:00] Shader Library Blender add-on v1.6 adds Brick, Sci-fi Gold and Clouds materials; author says it now works on future Blender versions and anything below 2.9; author develops on 2.8. *(demo, high; other)*
- c2: [0:40] Brick material shows nothing in Solid view; author switches viewport to Rendered (Cycles, also works in Eevee), then Tab into Edit Mode, U, Cube Projection to fix UVs. *(demo, high; other)*
- c3: [2:03] Sci-fi gold material applied to a UV sphere after Shade Smooth; tunable via noise texture scale, detail and distortion; color set in RGB node. *(demo, high; other)*
- c4: [2:46] Clouds material applied to a cube; scaling object then requires Ctrl+A apply scale or material scaling looks wrong. *(demo, high; other)*
- c5: [3:31] Cloud look improved by adding lights; noise factor can drive Emission Strength through a Color Ramp for overblown clouds. *(demo, medium; other)*
- c6: [4:41] Author says future version will add UI options (color, size, scale) on the material buttons instead of fixed values. *(opinion, medium; other)*
