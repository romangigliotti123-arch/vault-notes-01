## Summary
arXiv abstract for MeshFlow, a research method generating artist-like meshes using a VAE and rectified-flow transformer, claimed 18x faster than the fastest autoregressive generator. No Blender, Claude, or Roblox content, and no export, limits, rigging or UGC details.

## Claims
- c1: MeshFlow generates artist-like 3D meshes using a contrastively supervised VAE and a Rectified Flow transformer that generates all vertices and edges in parallel. *(doc, high; other)*
- c2: Autoregressive mesh generators scale poorly because inference cost is quadratic in mesh size, and they need vertex quantization, which introduces errors. *(doc, high; other)*
- c3: MeshFlow reports meshes generated 18x faster than the fastest AR generator, with excellent accuracy on standard mesh-generation metrics. *(doc, medium; other)*
