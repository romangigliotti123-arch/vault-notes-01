## Summary
Video on Studs.gg, an AI tool that generates a complete Roblox game (scripts placed in ServerScriptService, assets in ReplicatedStorage) from a text prompt, with publish or download. Free tier: 25 credits/day, one credit per game. No Blender, bpy, blender-mcp, FBX/glTF, or asset-pipeline content.

## Claims
- c1: [0:11] Studs.gg generates a playable Roblox game from a text prompt and places scripts inside the Roblox project, unlike ChatGPT which only gives scripts. *(demo, medium; other)*
- c2: [2:53] Studs.gg free tier gives 25 credits per day, one credit per generated game, resetting daily. *(doc, medium; other)*
- c3: [2:53] Re-pasting the same prompt sometimes fixes a generated game that does not work, according to the presenter. *(experience, low; other)*
