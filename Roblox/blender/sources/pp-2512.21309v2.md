## Summary
Off-topic arXiv abstract on AgentReuse, a plan reuse mechanism for LLM-driven agents (personal assistants). It uses intent classification to detect similar requests and reuse plans, cutting latency. Nothing on Blender, Roblox, blender-mcp, bpy, or asset pipelines.

## Claims
- c1: About 30% of requests received by LLM-driven agents are identical or similar (real-world dataset), enabling reuse of previously generated plans. Not related to Blender or Roblox. *(data, medium; other)*
- c2: AgentReuse uses intent classification to evaluate request similarity and reuse plans; it reports 93% effective reuse rate, F1 0.9718, accuracy 0.9459, and 93.12% latency reduction versus no reuse. *(data, medium; other)*

## Numbers
