## Summary
Talk on SciExplorer, a prototype Blender add-on for microscopy data. It filters segmented 3D objects by CSV measurements (percent, count, comparison operators), colors them, exports selections as CSV/meshes, and plots measurements in the viewport. It uses microscopy-nodes and CSV importers. Nothing on Claude, blender-mcp, Roblox, or the Roblox export pipeline.
