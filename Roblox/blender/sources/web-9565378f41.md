## Summary
Andrew Price's BlenderGuru post on realistic texturing: create texture variations of a single texture in Photoshop/GIMP and apply them in Blender Cycles nodes to avoid flat-looking materials. Covers general Blender shading only; no Roblox, Claude/MCP, bpy, or FBX/glTF pipeline content. Mostly promotional for Poliigon.
