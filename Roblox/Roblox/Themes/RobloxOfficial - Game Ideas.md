# RobloxOfficial: Game Ideas

All RobloxOfficial tips on this theme, grouped by topic, oldest first. Cross-creator synthesis: see [[README]] in roblox-kb.

## [[game-ideas-and-genres]]
- 2018-07-24 [12:20](https://youtu.be/a2DaoXERzhE?t=740) Choose an age demographic deliberately; content for under-9s rarely appeals to 17-year-olds, but owning one segment can beat a diluted broad audience. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Building Games for Reach|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Building Games ]])
- 2018-07-24 [29:12](https://youtu.be/f8cSSzVnh9s?t=1752) Intense, in-depth melee combat is an underserved Roblox niche compared with FPS; being first with a deep melee game gives easier market entry. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat Design and Tech|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat: D]])
- 2018-07-24 [45:44](https://youtu.be/f8cSSzVnh9s?t=2744) Target older Roblox players seeking depth when designing complex combat; don't force that complexity on younger audiences. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat Design and Tech|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat: D]])
- 2018-07-24 [5:00](https://youtu.be/u0SxwDxFQB8?t=300) Define the game's tone (safe, frenetic, mysterious, playful) early; tone drives colors, lighting and asset treatment. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds You Envision|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds]])
- 2020-11-20 [5:11](https://youtu.be/GqleMaqFhPE?t=311) Let players create their own characters (e.g. custom superheroes) instead of only playing others' licensed heroes; this drove Superhero Life's success. ([[2020-11-20 - A Roblox Story CJ Oyer|A Roblox Story: CJ Oyer]])
- 2022-09-20 [0:24](https://youtu.be/ImH6XYqNJUs?t=24) Find ideas by asking what people want to feel and what they can't do yet; build items that solve that problem. ([[2022-09-20 - A Roblox Story - Samuel Jordan|A Roblox Story - Samuel Jordan]])
- 2022-09-20 [2:24](https://youtu.be/ImH6XYqNJUs?t=144) Make things you have played and loved yourself; if you love it, other people likely will too. ([[2022-09-20 - A Roblox Story - Samuel Jordan|A Roblox Story - Samuel Jordan]])
- 2023-09-19 [4:21](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=261) For educational games, go beyond gamified drilling (points for math problems) toward immersive game-based learning where learning happens through play. ([[2023-09-19 - Grow Q&A RDC23|Grow Q&A | RDC23]])
- 2024-09-23 [7:32](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=452) Consider shooters, sports, and open-world action genres for console; shooters make up half the top 10 most played Xbox titles. ([[2024-09-23 - Building a Business on Console RDC 2024|Building a Business on Console | RDC 2024]])
- 2024-09-23 [7:26](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=446) Make one item with multiple colors: non-limited for common colors, limited for unique ones, a strategy fashion brands use. ([[2024-09-23 - Monetization for Avatar Creators RDC 2024|Monetization for Avatar Creators | RDC 2024]])
- 2024-09-23 [20:26](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=1226) Find a niche and a personal style; there is a lot of content, so stand out rather than copy trends. ([[2024-09-23 - Monetization for Avatar Creators RDC 2024|Monetization for Avatar Creators | RDC 2024]])
- 2025-09-26 [7:02](https://youtu.be/Tese1QLD3nU?t=422) Look for underserved item categories: creators had been publishing shoes as pants because no shoes category existed; shoes and emotes are now separate UGC categories. ([[2025-09-26 - Making Awesome Avatars Trends, Tools, and Opportunities RDC 2025|Making Awesome Avatars: Trends, Tools, and Opportu]])
- 2026-04-02 [0:44](https://youtu.be/IuJWzpvpIa8?t=44) Pick an idea the team finds genuinely fun to play; Rivals pivoted to PvP duels because the team enjoyed playing them together. ([[2026-04-02 - Game Changers Nosniy and the Making of a Hit Game|Game Changers: Nosniy and the Making of a Hit Game]])
- 2026-05-27 [0:04](https://youtu.be/-14vhiLL4xE?t=4) Combine several things you personally love into one concept; Dead Rails mixed history, trains and werewolves and then blew up overnight. ([[2026-05-27 - Game Changers Evan and the Goofy Vision Behind Dead Rails|Game Changers: Evan and the Goofy Vision Behind De]])

## [[core-loops-and-game-design]]
- 2018-07-24 [14:42](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=882) Consider social buffs: small XP, health or item bonuses for inviting or playing with friends, to reward bringing friends and staying longer. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Game More Social|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Gam]])
- 2018-07-24 [19:04](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=1144) In competitive shooters, separate competitive zones from adjacent social or safe zones where players can chat instead of fighting. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Game More Social|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Gam]])
- 2018-07-24 [23:51](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=1431) For two-player mechanics, require dexterity, speed or timing so one person cannot do both roles, which discourages alt-account farming. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Game More Social|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Gam]])
- 2018-07-24 [21:17](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=1277) Build rewarding content for non-paying players too; they make the game's paying players' spending possible and visible. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Pa]])
- 2018-07-24 [8:14](https://youtu.be/f8cSSzVnh9s?t=494) Build stagger into every hit so the hit player gets feedback to stop spamming; stagger communicates mistakes better than a health bar. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat Design and Tech|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat: D]])
- 2018-07-24 [12:33](https://youtu.be/f8cSSzVnh9s?t=753) Give parries a risky, mastery-rewarding payoff; players who parry consistently become feared yet learnable, adding depth over time. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat Design and Tech|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat: D]])
- 2018-07-24 [11:54](https://youtu.be/f8cSSzVnh9s?t=714) Balance offense and defense so neither extreme dominates; design feedback that discourages spamming, passive blocking, and rewards calculated risk. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat Design and Tech|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat: D]])
- 2018-07-24 [15:27](https://youtu.be/f8cSSzVnh9s?t=927) Add a stamina system to prevent attack spam, and use directional attacks and blocks so defense is an active choice, not a passive hold. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat Design and Tech|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat: D]])
- 2018-07-24 [21:18](https://youtu.be/f8cSSzVnh9s?t=1278) Make parry a timed window at the end of an attack that costs your block for that period; this creates a read-and-counter risk. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat Design and Tech|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat: D]])
- 2018-07-24 [33:18](https://youtu.be/f8cSSzVnh9s?t=1998) Add a guard break mechanic so blocking cannot be spammed; avoid having two options with identical results and weaknesses. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat Design and Tech|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat: D]])
- 2018-07-24 [33:59](https://youtu.be/f8cSSzVnh9s?t=2039) Avoid solved, tic-tac-toe-like combat where one strategy always wins; add layers of depth so mastery remains possible. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat Design and Tech|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat: D]])
- 2018-07-24 [40:09](https://youtu.be/f8cSSzVnh9s?t=2409) Use combos to gate powerful attacks behind setup steps, making high-reward moves predictable and fair to defend. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat Design and Tech|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat: D]])
- 2018-07-24 [9:40](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=580) Design gameplay with constant action and a clear hook so it is easy for YouTubers to make exciting videos. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Marketing Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Market]])
- 2018-07-24 [5:23](https://youtu.be/NmyzbBbrBS4?t=323) Pick ideas for what other people will want to play, not just what you find fun; this correlates with success level. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Leveraging Your Skills to Make Game|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Leveraging Your]])
- 2018-07-24 [2:57](https://youtu.be/u0SxwDxFQB8?t=177) Answer global questions first (game type, tone, player camera perspective) before placing any art; they change UI, environment, and camera together. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds You Envision|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds]])
- 2018-07-24 [8:29](https://youtu.be/u0SxwDxFQB8?t=509) Camera perspective changes layout: first-person needs close, narrowed focus; third-person needs multiple points of interest around the player; isometric needs clear foreground, midground, background. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds You Envision|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds]])
- 2018-07-24 [17:54](https://youtu.be/u0SxwDxFQB8?t=1074) Use color to tell players what enemies are and how dangerous they are, so threats are readable without text or tutorial. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds You Envision|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds]])
- 2018-07-24 [30:03](https://youtu.be/u0SxwDxFQB8?t=1803) Dim the space right around the player and make the key landmark the brightest object, so the player naturally heads toward it. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds You Envision|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds]])
- 2018-07-24 [6:40](https://youtu.be/u0SxwDxFQB8?t=400) In open worlds, reuse assets heavily and use obstructions and partial blockers to steer players; Roblox instancing will reduce load for reused objects. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds You Envision|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds]])
- 2018-07-24 [7:22](https://youtu.be/u0SxwDxFQB8?t=442) In obstacle courses, keep the play area clear: subdue and desaturate set pieces, and make collision match what players see, or they stop trusting the game. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds You Envision|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds]])
- 2018-07-24 [39:34](https://youtu.be/u0SxwDxFQB8?t=2374) Limit play areas with deterrence: hills, dense trees, dim lighting and distant landmarks discourage going elsewhere; use invisible colliders as backup. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds You Envision|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds]])
- 2018-07-24 [41:38](https://youtu.be/u0SxwDxFQB8?t=2498) For endless-looking worlds, use hills that rise and fall, fog, and far-off simplified objects; for edges, use cliffs or insurmountable water with visible limits. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds You Envision|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds]])
- 2023-09-19 [6:36](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=396) Build high fidelity around four pillars: immersive world, responsive character feel, real-world-like details, and a sense of always something new. ([[2023-09-19 - Creating Experiences to Reach New Audiences RDC23|Creating Experiences to Reach New Audiences | RDC2]])
- 2024-09-23 [11:38](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=698) Design console gameplay for longer sessions: console users spend 50% more time per session than mobile, so reward depth rather than quick re-engagement. ([[2024-09-23 - Building a Business on Console RDC 2024|Building a Business on Console | RDC 2024]])
- 2024-09-23 [12:19](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=739) Add deeper content and secondary activities (e.g. player customization) so players stay in the game during breaks from the main loop. ([[2024-09-23 - Building a Business on Console RDC 2024|Building a Business on Console | RDC 2024]])
- 2024-09-23 [14:23](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=863) Mix short and long progression goals: short goals (~10 min) suit mobile, long goals (1-2+ hours) give console players a strong sense of accomplishment. ([[2024-09-23 - Building a Business on Console RDC 2024|Building a Business on Console | RDC 2024]])
- 2024-09-23 [27:11](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=1631) Copy familiar control schemes from existing games for the genre rather than reinventing them; players want to use what they already know. ([[2024-09-23 - Building a Business on Console RDC 2024|Building a Business on Console | RDC 2024]])
- 2024-09-23 [24:28](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=1468) Bridge skill gaps across platforms: gunfight Arena uses a weaker assist on console and stronger on mobile, and matches console players in console lobbies. ([[2024-09-23 - Building a Business on Console RDC 2024|Building a Business on Console | RDC 2024]])
- 2024-09-23 [12:13](https://youtu.be/vWx8UTxsi0A?t=733) Identify main gameplay areas (landmarks) and check travel time between them so players don't lose interest or get lost. ([[2024-09-23 - Finding Your Art Direction RDC 2024|Finding Your Art Direction | RDC 2024]])
- 2024-09-23 [15:58](https://youtu.be/vWx8UTxsi0A?t=958) Treat each location as a movie scene with its own lighting mood so the player's journey progresses from beginning to end. ([[2024-09-23 - Finding Your Art Direction RDC 2024|Finding Your Art Direction | RDC 2024]])
- 2024-09-23 [26:50](https://youtu.be/vWx8UTxsi0A?t=1610) Avoid saturated colors that conflict with game signals; players read red as lava and green as safe, so test colors in playtests. ([[2024-09-23 - Finding Your Art Direction RDC 2024|Finding Your Art Direction | RDC 2024]])
- 2025-09-05 [4:46](https://youtu.be/6iB5__RCQDc?t=286) Let progress accrue while players are offline (e.g., plants grow while away) so returning after hours or days feels rewarding. ([[2025-09-05 - Tech Talks EP29 Creator|Tech Talks EP29: Creator]])
- 2025-09-05 [5:27](https://youtu.be/6iB5__RCQDc?t=327) Keep chase goals reachable for most players so missing a week doesn't leave someone behind; they can rejoin the next update. ([[2025-09-05 - Tech Talks EP29 Creator|Tech Talks EP29: Creator]])
- 2026-05-27 [1:26](https://youtu.be/-14vhiLL4xE?t=86) Let players explore the mechanics to find their own fun; he cites drag-and-weld of any object to the train as an example. ([[2026-05-27 - Game Changers Evan and the Goofy Vision Behind Dead Rails|Game Changers: Evan and the Goofy Vision Behind De]])
