# RobloxOfficial: Retention

All RobloxOfficial tips on this theme, grouped by topic, oldest first. Cross-creator synthesis: see [[README]] in roblox-kb.

## [[first-session-onboarding]]
- 2018-07-24 [8:38](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=518) Give a generous free currency amount in the tutorial and have players buy their first item, so the first purchase is low-friction. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Pa]])
- 2018-07-24 [10:02](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=602) Make the first sale small in price and big in reward so players feel the value before entering payment details. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Pa]])
- 2018-07-24 [43:25](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=2605) Get players into the shop early, for example with a tutorial that gives free currency and asks them to buy one item. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Pa]])
- 2018-07-24 [45:04](https://youtu.be/f8cSSzVnh9s?t=2704) For younger audiences, keep systems simple but discoverable; accidental parries that feel good let kids learn the mechanic by playing. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat Design and Tech|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Melee Combat: D]])
- 2018-07-24 [20:19](https://youtu.be/u0SxwDxFQB8?t=1219) Make the play path brighter and more saturated than the surrounding set pieces, which are dimmed, so players instantly see where they can walk. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds You Envision|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds]])
- 2018-07-24 [30:44](https://youtu.be/u0SxwDxFQB8?t=1844) Guide players by environment and asset placement rather than telling them where to go; players then feel they discovered the route themselves. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds You Envision|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds]])
- 2024-09-23 [32:52](https://youtu.be/cWDNVvOh4o0?t=1972) Keep load time down: about 10,000 instances replicate per second on join, so 150k instances is ~15 s. Use StreamingEnabled and a content-aware loading bar. ([[2024-09-23 - Using Roblox Tools to Improve Performance RDC 2024|Using Roblox Tools to Improve Performance | RDC 20]])
- 2024-09-23 [33:34](https://youtu.be/cWDNVvOh4o0?t=2014) Remember textures, audio and other players' character appearances must finish loading before a player enters; new joiners push load back up. ([[2024-09-23 - Using Roblox Tools to Improve Performance RDC 2024|Using Roblox Tools to Improve Performance | RDC 20]])
- 2026-04-02 [1:27](https://youtu.be/IuJWzpvpIa8?t=87) Make sure new players get the best experience right off the bat, since first impressions drive whether they stay. ([[2026-04-02 - Game Changers Nosniy and the Making of a Hit Game|Game Changers: Nosniy and the Making of a Hit Game]])

## [[retention-d1-d7]]
- 2018-07-24 [21:49](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=1309) Reward players who invite friends. You can see when an invited friend joins the inviter's game, so you can reward both players for playing together. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Game More Social|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Gam]])
- 2018-07-24 [7:56](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=476) Reward login streaks with premium currency and boxes so new players open items and become likelier to buy later. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Pa]])
- 2023-09-19 [9:27](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=567) Re-engagement is moving into recommendations; social hooks like friends who played together can pull players back into experiences. ([[2023-09-19 - Grow Q&A RDC23|Grow Q&A | RDC23]])
- 2024-09-23 [3:17](https://youtu.be/cWDNVvOh4o0?t=197) Slow loads and overheating phones end sessions early; optimized games get played longer, and more than half of players are on mobile. ([[2024-09-23 - Using Roblox Tools to Improve Performance RDC 2024|Using Roblox Tools to Improve Performance | RDC 20]])
- 2025-09-05 [32:12](https://youtu.be/6iB5__RCQDc?t=1932) Avoid pressure to log in daily; a game that is fun to return to after two weeks retains players without manipulative hooks. ([[2025-09-05 - Tech Talks EP29 Creator|Tech Talks EP29: Creator]])

## [[social-features-and-virality]]
- 2018-07-24 [9:02](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=542) Any game can be social. Add mechanics like friend lists, join-friend and invite features, not just chat. Not every game needs to be a chat-centric game. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Game More Social|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Gam]])
- 2018-07-24 [9:42](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=582) Add an in-game friends list showing who is online and last played, with notifications and click-to-teleport to a friend (Meep City pattern). ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Game More Social|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Gam]])
- 2018-07-24 [10:24](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=624) In round-based games, let players invite online friends into their team from the lobby before the round starts (Island Royale pattern). ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Game More Social|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Gam]])
- 2018-07-24 [11:07](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=667) Reserve a social buffer of slots so friends can join full servers. Her data: buffered games had better retention than those without it. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Game More Social|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Gam]])
- 2018-07-24 [12:39](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=759) Add spectate mode so late joiners or eliminated players can watch a friend and still feel like they are playing together. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Game More Social|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Gam]])
- 2018-07-24 [14:00](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=840) Give players tradeable items. Trading is a low-risk icebreaker that gives strangers something to talk about. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Game More Social|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Gam]])
- 2018-07-24 [20:26](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=1226) Ease strangers into friendship: repeated encounters, low-risk actions like trading, then team tasks with shared bonuses, before expecting chat. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Game More Social|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Gam]])
- 2018-07-24 [6:50](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=410) Contact YouTubers early via email, Twitter, Facebook; most won't reply, but one or two can trigger a domino effect of other creators. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Marketing Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Market]])
- 2018-07-24 [7:33](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=453) Pitch new games to YouTubers as new content; start with smaller creators and send links to existing videos of your game. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Marketing Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Market]])
- 2018-07-24 [8:16](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=496) Give each YouTuber a unique promo code granting in-game cash; split codes across creators to measure which performs best. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Marketing Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Market]])
- 2018-07-24 [14:16](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=856) Write tweets that match the game theme, include the game link, and offer an incentive such as a promo code. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Marketing Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Market]])
- 2023-09-19 [31:19](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=1879) Stay authentic and consistent with content updates; that sustains influencer coverage and word of mouth across social platforms. ([[2023-09-19 - Grow Q&A RDC23|Grow Q&A | RDC23]])
- 2025-09-05 [32:12](https://youtu.be/6iB5__RCQDc?t=1932) Build altruistic social mechanics, such as letting players place sprinklers on others' plots or gift expensive items to newcomers, to drive delight and retention. ([[2025-09-05 - Tech Talks EP29 Creator|Tech Talks EP29: Creator]])
- 2025-09-05 [19:21](https://youtu.be/6iB5__RCQDc?t=1161) Use live events and global chat/messaging features; Jandelle expected live events to scale strongly when retention is good. ([[2025-09-05 - Tech Talks EP29 Creator|Tech Talks EP29: Creator]])
- 2025-09-26 [2:44](https://youtu.be/lUc3Xj8Ojd8?t=164) Design for co-play: players who play with friends stay longer, return more often, and spend about twice as much, per the speaker. - 2025-09-26 [6:32](https://youtu.be/lUc3Xj8Ojd8?t=392) Add voice using the new voice chat APIs; team voice can be set up in a few lines of code, and custom distance attenuation curves tune falloff. ([[2025-09-26 - Building Connected Experiences with Roblox’s APIs RDC 2025|Building Connected Experiences with Roblox’s APIs ]])
- 2025-09-26 [9:36](https://youtu.be/lUc3Xj8Ojd8?t=576) Use the Party API to show where party members are, keep parties on the same team, and give shared rewards to bring groups back. ([[2025-09-26 - Building Connected Experiences with Roblox’s APIs RDC 2025|Building Connected Experiences with Roblox’s APIs ]])
- 2025-09-26 [16:04](https://youtu.be/lUc3Xj8Ojd8?t=964) Trigger captures at key moments (challenge complete, rare item, race finish) via CaptureService, but a player must consent to save or share.
