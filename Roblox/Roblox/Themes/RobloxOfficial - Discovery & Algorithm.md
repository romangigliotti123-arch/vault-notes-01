# RobloxOfficial: Discovery & Algorithm

All RobloxOfficial tips on this theme, grouped by topic, oldest first. Cross-creator synthesis: see [[README]] in roblox-kb.

## [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]
- 2018-07-24 [5:11](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=311) Run ads consistently for a week at a fixed daily budget (~25,000 Robux/day); a test showed ~100-150% more revenue than the no-ad week. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Marketing Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Market]])
- 2018-07-24 [3:48](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=228) Optimise both ad CTR (ad click) and the second CTR (game page click to Play); clickbait ads may get high first CTR but low Play rate. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Marketing Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Market]])
- 2018-07-24 [11:59](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=719) Sponsored games place your game on the front page; sponsored listings hide the like/dislike ratio, useful after a rough beta. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Marketing Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Market]])
- 2018-07-24 [25:37](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=1537) Start with small ad budgets (~500 Robux) and reinvest returns to scale up gradually. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Marketing Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Market]])
- 2018-07-24 [36:39](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=2199) Combine ads, sponsored placements, YouTube and Twitter together; small channels add up to a big impact. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Marketing Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Market]])
- 2023-09-19 [13:16](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=796) TikTok can drive large reach even without a creator following; short clips of the game got millions of views despite ~300 views per post. ([[2023-09-19 - Creating Experiences to Reach New Audiences RDC23|Creating Experiences to Reach New Audiences | RDC2]])
- 2023-09-19 [14:12](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=852) Put portals in your experience: one publisher reported up to 27% of monetization coming from portals. ([[2023-09-19 - Grow Q&A RDC23|Grow Q&A | RDC23]])
- 2023-09-19 [12:36](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=756) A lightweight branded trailer-style experience can be built in a week or two and is a viable path for brand campaigns like movie or product launches. ([[2023-09-19 - Grow Q&A RDC23|Grow Q&A | RDC23]])
- 2025-09-26 [19:52](https://youtu.be/Tese1QLD3nU?t=1192) Ads Manager integration is planned so you can target ads at audiences for individual items and for whole Shops. ([[2025-09-26 - Making Awesome Avatars Trends, Tools, and Opportunities RDC 2025|Making Awesome Avatars: Trends, Tools, and Opportu]])

## [[discovery-algorithm]]
- 2020-11-20 [5:52](https://youtu.be/GqleMaqFhPE?t=352) Once quality improved and players arrived, the game reached Roblox's front page, which drove further growth. ([[2020-11-20 - A Roblox Story CJ Oyer|A Roblox Story: CJ Oyer]])
- 2023-07-18 [26:01](https://youtu.be/RNwPOSY8NjI?t=1561) As user-generated content explodes, invest in recommendation systems so players can find relevant experiences; more content makes discovery harder (Roblox's stated view). ([[2023-07-18 - Gaming Content Creation in 2023 (Collision 2023)|Gaming Content Creation in 2023 (Collision 2023)]])
- 2023-09-19 [5:43](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=343) Engagement carries the most weight in discovery: time played, session count, and retention over days, weeks and months, not just one long session. ([[2023-09-19 - Grow Q&A RDC23|Grow Q&A | RDC23]])
- 2025-09-26 [17:04](https://youtu.be/Tese1QLD3nU?t=1024) Build a Shop to showcase your full catalog in one place instead of relying on users finding items one by one; you can also link to it from outside Roblox. ([[2025-09-26 - Making Awesome Avatars Trends, Tools, and Opportunities RDC 2025|Making Awesome Avatars: Trends, Tools, and Opportu]])
- 2025-09-26 [19:11](https://youtu.be/Tese1QLD3nU?t=1151) Premium items priced higher will be eligible for Marketplace Select, a curated space for top-quality items; qualification criteria will be published on the dev forum. ([[2025-09-26 - Making Awesome Avatars Trends, Tools, and Opportunities RDC 2025|Making Awesome Avatars: Trends, Tools, and Opportu]])
