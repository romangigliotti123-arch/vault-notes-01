## [[pricing]]
- 2018-07-24 [3:14](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=194) Test prices with live data: Alex changed coin-to-Robux price points over three to six months and doubled revenue. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Pa]])
- 2018-07-24 [15:12](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=912) Tie big sales to national holidays, pair them with free limited events for non-payers, and keep sales rare so players don't wait for discounts. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Pa]])
- 2018-07-24 [26:51](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=1611) There is no formula for pricing; start at a price, lower it if few buy, and raise it on the next version. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Pa]])
- 2024-09-23 [7:26](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=446) Set price relative to dynamic price floor: price = floor + your offset. Optionally set a minimum sales price so the floor cannot drag your item below it. ([[2024-09-23 - Monetization for Avatar Creators RDC 2024|Monetization for Avatar Creators | RDC 2024]])
- 2024-09-23 [28:00](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=1680) Price by market research: compare comparables and your own catalog, test new styles first, and price higher for items that took long to make. ([[2024-09-23 - Monetization for Avatar Creators RDC 2024|Monetization for Avatar Creators | RDC 2024]])
- 2024-09-23 [30:04](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=1804) Keep most items affordable for typical players and make only a few expensive exclusives. ([[2024-09-23 - Monetization for Avatar Creators RDC 2024|Monetization for Avatar Creators | RDC 2024]])
- 2025-09-26 [18:30](https://youtu.be/Tese1QLD3nU?t=1110) Price avatar items above the price floor to qualify for higher revenue share: up to 70% instead of the standard 30%, visible on the creator dashboard. ([[2025-09-26 - Making Awesome Avatars Trends, Tools, and Opportunities RDC 2025|Making Awesome Avatars: Trends, Tools, and Opportu]])
- 2026-10-12 [18:23](https://youtu.be/gCp4zzjjJ14?t=1103) Emissive UGC has a higher price floor by design, to protect the novelty of the new tech from being washed out. ([[2026-10-12 - Avatars at the Center Driving Gameplay, Identity, and Commerce RDC 202|Avatars at the Center: Driving Gameplay, Identity,]])
