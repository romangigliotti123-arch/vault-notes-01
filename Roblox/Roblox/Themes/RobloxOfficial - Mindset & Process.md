## [[analytics-metrics]]
- 2018-07-24 [5:56](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=356) Track every purchase and every currency source and sink; compare currency bought versus earned, especially during sales. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Pa]])
- 2018-07-24 [7:19](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=439) Compare sales where players spend from balance with sales that trigger new Robux deposits; the latter signals stronger demand. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Pa]])
- 2018-07-24 [37:16](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=2236) Use GameAnalytics (free, about an hour to set up) for retention, new versus returning users, and play times. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Pa]])
- 2018-07-24 [23:34](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=1414) Use a game analytics module to tie promo code redemptions to players and measure campaign effectiveness. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Marketing Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Market]])
- 2018-07-24 [3:14](https://youtu.be/rBrILsiVdtQ?t=194) Target join time: median around 5 seconds is good; over 10 seconds is not great. Measure up to the interactive state, including loading screens. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Designing for Performance|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Designing for P]])
- 2018-07-24 [6:03](https://youtu.be/rBrILsiVdtQ?t=363) Check the Developer Console client and server memory breakdown per device type; memory categories differ between platforms, so measure on the target device. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Designing for Performance|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Designing for P]])
- 2018-07-24 [7:29](https://youtu.be/rBrILsiVdtQ?t=449) Use Microprofiler (Ctrl+Alt+F6 on desktop, enabled in settings on mobile) to find why frames are slow, including your own script timings. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Designing for Performance|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Designing for P]])
- 2023-09-19 [18:24](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1104) Iterate on small changes using analytics rather than building big unvalidated features; avoid feature creep. ([[2023-09-19 - Partnering for Success Tips for Creators and Brands RDC23|Partnering for Success: Tips for Creators and Bran]])
- 2023-09-19 [20:16](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1216) Measure partner-specific KPIs: interactions with brand NPCs and time spent in game, compared against the partner's other channels. ([[2023-09-19 - Partnering for Success Tips for Creators and Brands RDC23|Partnering for Success: Tips for Creators and Bran]])
- 2023-09-19 [21:39](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1299) Without platform benchmarks, benchmark against your own store or e-commerce engagement and social media time to see where audiences engage. ([[2023-09-19 - Partnering for Success Tips for Creators and Brands RDC23|Partnering for Success: Tips for Creators and Bran]])
- 2025-09-05 [46:33](https://youtu.be/6iB5__RCQDc?t=2793) A/B testing breaks at scale: at about 0.5-1M players, analytics queries became too expensive and data unreliable; Grow a Garden switched to player-feedback-driven decisions. ([[2025-09-05 - Tech Talks EP29 Creator|Tech Talks EP29: Creator]])
- 2025-09-05 [43:33](https://youtu.be/6iB5__RCQDc?t=2613) Use built-in experiments in Creator Hub and Studio to run A/B tests that control for confounding variables when changing things serially. ([[2025-09-05 - Tech Talks EP29 Creator|Tech Talks EP29: Creator]])

## [[playtesting-and-feedback]]

- 2018-07-24 [16:18](https://youtu.be/B3oqg1il2tI?t=978) Give visual feedback for every state: enabled/disabled, pressed, hover on desktop, current selection on console, win/lose and loading states. - 2018-07-24 [17:51](https://youtu.be/B3oqg1il2tI?t=1071) Playtest, iterate, and test on real devices; screen emulation in Studio is approximate. - 2018-07-24 [24:16](https://youtu.be/B3oqg1il2tI?t=1456) Use the iPhone 5 as the minimum test case for mobile UI; once you have an established audience, run A/B tests on UI layouts. - 2018-07-24 [13:34](https://youtu.be/NmyzbBbrBS4?t=814) After a bad soft launch (dislikes about 2x likes), step back, take the feedback, and return with a fresh mind before iterating. - 2023-09-19 [30:49](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1849) Run constant QA, playtesting and community testing to confirm players will resonate with the experience, not just that it works. - 2023-09-19 [21:30](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=1290) Start by watching real users play; a Roblox dev said nothing replaces seeing what works and what doesn't in person. ([[2023-09-19 - Grow Q&A RDC23|Grow Q&A | RDC23]])
- 2024-09-23 [25:49](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=1549) Find console-native players in your community to test; jailbreak gets spreadsheets of ~100 bugs per update from a QA team of about a dozen. ([[2024-09-23 - Building a Business on Console RDC 2024|Building a Business on Console | RDC 2024]])
- 2024-09-23 [27:31](https://youtu.be/vWx8UTxsi0A?t=1651) Run gameplay tests early: players avoided bushes and ran into hazards because the color cues misled them, prompting a redesign. ([[2024-09-23 - Finding Your Art Direction RDC 2024|Finding Your Art Direction | RDC 2024]])
- 2025-09-05 [46:33](https://youtu.be/6iB5__RCQDc?t=2793) Gather structured player feedback and build tooling for it when A/B testing is no longer viable. ([[2025-09-05 - Tech Talks EP29 Creator|Tech Talks EP29: Creator]])

## [[common-mistakes]]
- 2018-07-24 [3:59](https://youtu.be/a2DaoXERzhE?t=239) Avoid forking control scripts per platform unless intentional; a PC-only fork cuts off console/tablet/mobile players from day one. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Building Games for Reach|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Building Games ]])
- 2018-07-24 [10:03](https://youtu.be/B3oqg1il2tI?t=603) Don't put persistent panels (e.g. a task list) where they overlap restricted areas; players then can't move or act while it's open. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - User Experience Design|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - User Experience]])
- 2018-07-24 [10:43](https://youtu.be/B3oqg1il2tI?t=643) Keep button positions consistent across screens. Swapping Delete and Save between screens causes muscle-memory mis-clicks and bad surprises. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - User Experience Design|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - User Experience]])
- 2018-07-24 [11:51](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=711) Premium currency hoards build up and top players spend stored balance instead of buying; plan sinks before big content drops. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Pa]])
- 2018-07-24 [24:44](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=1484) Don't rush a trend game with heavy monetisation at the expense of fun; one speaker's rushed monetised game and fun unmonetised game both underperformed. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Pa]])
- 2018-07-24 [27:33](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=1653) Check where players spend time before adding shop items; items placed in areas players ignore won't sell. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Pa]])
- 2018-07-24 [22:51](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=1371) Avoid clickbait ads; they may boost early numbers but damage trust in your studio brand. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Marketing Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Market]])
- 2018-07-24 [39:23](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=2363) Don't spam advertisements in groups or comments; it risks moderator bans. Read the Terms of Service. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Marketing Panel|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Market]])
- 2018-07-24 [9:15](https://youtu.be/qoWMP-AFT08?t=555) Don't build kill feeds or messages from concatenated sentences with parameters; grammar in Spanish etc. changes verb forms and gender, forcing sentence rewrites. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Accessing a Global Market|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Accessing a Glo]])
- 2018-07-24 [15:18](https://youtu.be/qoWMP-AFT08?t=918) Machine translation loses idioms, puns and memes; add translator notes explaining intent and maintain two-way communication with translators. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Accessing a Global Market|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Accessing a Glo]])
- 2018-07-24 [31:25](https://youtu.be/u0SxwDxFQB8?t=1885) Don't ignore environment quality: visual polish affects how outsiders judge a game's popularity, so unpolished art lowers results. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds You Envision|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Creating Worlds]])
- 2022-06-28 [35:12](https://youtu.be/SOoe0E1Ql5M?t=2112) Assume generic systems like data storage will be abused or spammed; design rate limits and abuse protection in from the start. ([[2022-06-28 - Scaling Innovation at Roblox I Tech Innovators Panel|Scaling Innovation at Roblox I Tech Innovators Pan]])
- 2022-06-28 [23:28](https://youtu.be/SOoe0E1Ql5M?t=1408) Don't break existing experiences or avatars with surprise changes; treat APIs as permanent and plan changes years ahead. ([[2022-06-28 - Scaling Innovation at Roblox I Tech Innovators Panel|Scaling Innovation at Roblox I Tech Innovators Pan]])
