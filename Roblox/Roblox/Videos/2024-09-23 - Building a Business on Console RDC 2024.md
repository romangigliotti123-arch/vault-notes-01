# Building a Business on Console | RDC 2024

[Watch](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U) | RobloxOfficial | 10,329 views | 2024-09-23

## Summary
RDC 2024 panel on building for console (PlayStation/Xbox). Covers why console users are valuable (more engagement, higher spend), genres that do well (shooters, sports, action), deeper gameplay and longer goals, UI scaling and controller design, and panelist experiences from gunfight Arena, jailbreak, Ultimate Football.

## Tips
- [7:32](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=452) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Consider shooters, sports, and open-world action genres for console; shooters make up half the top 10 most played Xbox titles.
- [11:38](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=698) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Design console gameplay for longer sessions: console users spend 50% more time per session than mobile, so reward depth rather than quick re-engagement.
- [12:19](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=739) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add deeper content and secondary activities (e.g. player customization) so players stay in the game during breaks from the main loop.
- [14:23](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=863) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Mix short and long progression goals: short goals (~10 min) suit mobile, long goals (1-2+ hours) give console players a strong sense of accomplishment.
- [15:04](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=904) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Scale UI about 1.5x for anything players interact with (fonts, icons, buttons) since players sit 10+ feet from the TV; simplify the UI.
- [16:27](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=987) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Support d-pad up/down/left/right, select and back navigation; use the simplified UI so controller navigation is easy.
- [17:07](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=1027) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Use the get image for key code API to show the correct PlayStation or Xbox button icons; use haptic feedback APIs for immersion.
- [25:49](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=1549) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Find console-native players in your community to test; jailbreak gets spreadsheets of ~100 bugs per update from a QA team of about a dozen.
- [27:11](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=1631) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Copy familiar control schemes from existing games for the genre rather than reinventing them; players want to use what they already know.
- [24:28](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=1468) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Bridge skill gaps across platforms: gunfight Arena uses a weaker assist on console and stronger on mobile, and matches console players in console lobbies.
- [29:14](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=1754) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use context-sensitive ('live') controls HUD that updates to the current action instead of static on-screen control lists, as Ultimate Football does.
- [30:36](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=1836) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Map buttons consistently with player expectations (A jump, B secondary, X main action) and make controls customizable.
- [35:24](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=2124) **[[monetisation-other]]** Get mobile and PC players to try a controller; they often already use one, which makes them more likely to play on console.
- [31:19](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=1879) **[[community-and-discord]]** Console players socialize via native party chat, Discord, or Guilded; design radial/emote-style quick-communication for console.

## Games
- **gunfight Arena** [19:39](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=1179): Play your game on console; use a reduced assist on console versus mobile; teleport console players to console-only lobbies for fairness.
- **jailbreak** [25:49](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=1549): Recruit console-native testers from the community; simplify UI to two central buttons; deep map and two modes add depth.
