# Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Leveraging Your Skills to Make Games & Revenue

## Summary
Developer-relations intern Brandon (cowboy r16) shares 10 years of Roblox development: finding your role, recognizing opportunity via willing customers, failing fast, diversifying side projects, asking for help, and persisting. Mostly personal career narrative with light practical advice.

## Tips
- [3:27](https://youtu.be/NmyzbBbrBS4?t=207) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Decide your role (solo, team member, freelancer) but know you can change it; try one, and if it fails, learn and repeat.
- [5:23](https://youtu.be/NmyzbBbrBS4?t=323) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Pick ideas for what other people will want to play, not just what you find fun; this correlates with success level.
- [6:46](https://youtu.be/NmyzbBbrBS4?t=406) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Start by joining a team if you're a weaker developer, then move to freelancing or solo work as your skills grow.
- [8:58](https://youtu.be/NmyzbBbrBS4?t=538) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Don't put all eggs in one basket; being absorbed in one project causes you to miss other opportunities.
