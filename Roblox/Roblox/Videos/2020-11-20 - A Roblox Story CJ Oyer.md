## Summary
CJ Oyer's personal story of becoming a Roblox developer at age 10, building with his dad, and the Superhero Life series. Mostly biographical. Key business point: the first Superhero Life flopped until he fixed its customization system, after which players arrived and it reached the front page.

## Tips
- [5:11](https://youtu.be/GqleMaqFhPE?t=311) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Let players create their own characters (e.g. custom superheroes) instead of only playing others' licensed heroes; this drove Superhero Life's success.
- [5:11](https://youtu.be/GqleMaqFhPE?t=311) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Fix core systems like character customization before expecting players; the first Superhero Life got almost no players because the customization was poorly built.
