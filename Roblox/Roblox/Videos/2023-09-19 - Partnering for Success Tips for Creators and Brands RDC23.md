# Partnering for Success: Tips for Creators and Brands | RDC23

[Watch](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4) | RobloxOfficial | 4,868 views | 2023-09-19

## Summary
Roblox RDC23 panel with brand and developer studios (Tommy Hilfiger, iHeartMedia, Livetopia, The Gang, Super Social, Century Games). Covers why brands use Roblox, how to partner with brands, staffing for brand projects, and KPIs. Mostly experience-sharing, with some practical process advice.

## Tips
- [9:12](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=552) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Brand experiences need a long-term presence: keep updating seasonally and give players reasons to return, or it is just an ad campaign.
- [10:32](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=632) **[[business-and-payouts]]** For brand integrations into an existing game, keep them organic and role-play based; Livetopia said players embraced known-brand integrations like a Lego car.
- [13:57](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=837) **[[team-and-budget]]** When choosing a developer partner, look for someone who can present a brand tastefully and still build a great game, not just a logo placement.
- [16:59](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1019) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan launches and platform timing in advance; brand calendars are finite, and development slips push campaigns into conflict.
- [18:24](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1104) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Iterate on small changes using analytics rather than building big unvalidated features; avoid feature creep.
- [26:44](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1604) **[[team-and-budget]]** A small team of about 4-7 (artist, engineer, designer, producer) can build a premium multiplayer world in 4-6 months.
- [27:26](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1646) **[[team-and-budget]]** Brands need a dedicated owner and weekly check-ins; part-time marketing contacts (e.g. 5 hours a week) will not work for Roblox projects.
- [28:06](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1686) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Ship rapidly with simple builds; Roblox cycles are closer to TikTok than mobile or AAA, so set expectations with partners accordingly.
- [29:27](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1767) **[[team-and-budget]]** Adding many layers of staff (designers, producers, PMs) was needed to scale a large Roblox game; one designer/dev/artist setup stopped scaling.
- [30:08](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1808) **[[common-mistakes]]** Decline brands that come for the wrong reason or do not fit the community; a logo slap or opportunistic moment does not work.
- [30:49](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1849) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Run constant QA, playtesting and community testing to confirm players will resonate with the experience, not just that it works.
- [30:49](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1849) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Have a cohesive go-to-market plan for live ops, including influencer activation on YouTube and other channels.
- [20:16](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1216) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Measure partner-specific KPIs: interactions with brand NPCs and time spent in game, compared against the partner's other channels.
- [21:39](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1299) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Without platform benchmarks, benchmark against your own store or e-commerce engagement and social media time to see where audiences engage.
- [22:19](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1339) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Let players earn items through gameplay as well as buy them, so non-paying players still get rewards.
