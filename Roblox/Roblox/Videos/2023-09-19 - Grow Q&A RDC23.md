# Grow Q&A | RDC23

## Summary
RDC23 Grow Q&A with Roblox leaders on discovery, portals and branded ads, influencers, education, civility and the Avatar affiliate program. Mostly platform philosophy, with a few concrete points on what the algorithm weighs and how to iterate.

## Tips
- [5:43](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=343) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Engagement carries the most weight in discovery: time played, session count, and retention over days, weeks and months, not just one long session.
- [7:44](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=464) **[[monetisation-other]]** Player spending is read as a value signal, since purchases in Roblox experiences are meant to improve the experience rather than sit outside it.
- [21:30](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=1290) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Start by watching real users play; a Roblox dev said nothing replaces seeing what works and what doesn't in person.
- [22:44](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=1364) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Iterate fast: Roblox streams content and handles backend, so you can test hypotheses and get feedback almost instantly, then double down on what works.
- [4:21](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=261) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** For educational games, go beyond gamified drilling (points for math problems) toward immersive game-based learning where learning happens through play.
- [14:12](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=852) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Put portals in your experience: one publisher reported up to 27% of monetization coming from portals.
- [12:36](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=756) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** A lightweight branded trailer-style experience can be built in a week or two and is a viable path for brand campaigns like movie or product launches.
- [31:19](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=1879) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Stay authentic and consistent with content updates; that sustains influencer coverage and word of mouth across social platforms.
- [32:40](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=1960) **[[monetisation-other]]** Avatar creators can merchandise items through affiliate shops or malls; the 40% affiliate share is there to be used, and affiliates can scale businesses.
- [28:32](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=1712) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Design for global audiences: check device types, support languages, and use machine translation to cover most of the way to other languages.
- [25:47](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=1547) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Focus on your experience rather than marketing to platform staff; Roblox says it aims for democratized distribution through recommendations and ads.
- [32:40](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=1960) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Build for longevity, not short-term stats; think years ahead rather than only the next release.
- [34:02](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=2042) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Ask whether your experience could be more civil and connect people more safely; give Roblox feedback and ask them to test things.
- [9:27](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=567) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Re-engagement is moving into recommendations; social hooks like friends who played together can pull players back into experiences.
- [20:53](https://youtu.be/yUDvI8mGnn8?t=1253) **[[roblox-platform-changes]]** Instagram and other off-site links are not supported on the experience Details page; Roblox says it is on the roadmap to revisit next year.
