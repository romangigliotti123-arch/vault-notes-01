# Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Making Your Game More Social

[Watch](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA) | RobloxOfficial | 3,863 views | 2018-07-24

## Summary
Roblox social product director Kelly (sister alligator) presents data from about 1,000 top games showing players who play with friends stay longer and spend more. She covers platform social features (chat game cards, invites, friend-join), and design patterns: friend notifications and teleport, adding friends to teams in lobbies, reserved server slots, spectate mode, group integration, trading, and social buffs. Q&A covers competitive balance and anti-alt design.

## Tips
- [9:02](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=542) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Any game can be social. Add mechanics like friend lists, join-friend and invite features, not just chat. Not every game needs to be a chat-centric game.
- [9:42](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=582) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add an in-game friends list showing who is online and last played, with notifications and click-to-teleport to a friend (Meep City pattern).
- [10:24](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=624) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** In round-based games, let players invite online friends into their team from the lobby before the round starts (Island Royale pattern).
- [11:07](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=667) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Reserve a social buffer of slots so friends can join full servers. Her data: buffered games had better retention than those without it.
- [12:39](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=759) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add spectate mode so late joiners or eliminated players can watch a friend and still feel like they are playing together.
- [13:20](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=800) **[[community-and-discord]]** Promote your Roblox group in-game, e.g. a group-only item that tells players which group to join. Integrate user-created groups too, not just fan groups.
- [14:00](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=840) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Give players tradeable items. Trading is a low-risk icebreaker that gives strangers something to talk about.
- [14:42](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=882) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Consider social buffs: small XP, health or item bonuses for inviting or playing with friends, to reward bringing friends and staying longer.
- [19:04](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=1144) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** In competitive shooters, separate competitive zones from adjacent social or safe zones where players can chat instead of fighting.
- [20:26](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=1226) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Ease strangers into friendship: repeated encounters, low-risk actions like trading, then team tasks with shared bonuses, before expecting chat.
- [21:49](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=1309) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Reward players who invite friends. You can see when an invited friend joins the inviter's game, so you can reward both players for playing together.
- [23:51](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=1431) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** For two-player mechanics, require dexterity, speed or timing so one person cannot do both roles, which discourages alt-account farming.

## Numbers
- [4:27](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=267) Session length: **~30% longer when playing with friends** (Data from about 1,000 top games, friends vs alone)
- [4:27](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=267) Day-0 to day-1 return: **60% more likely for players with friends** (Same 1,000-game dataset)
- [5:09](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=309) Long-term retention: **Gap persists to week 4 and grows to week 12 (no exact figures given)** (Players with friends stay active longer)
- [5:09](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=309) Robux spend per session: **~20% more when playing with friends** (Same dataset)
- [12:28](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=748) Retention with reserved social-buffer slots: **Day 1 ~20% better, ~30% better at longer window, month 1 ~60% better** (Games using max/preferred players buffer vs none, top games)
- [11:48](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=708) Social buffer size example: **~20-25 reserved slots on a 100-200 player server; 1 slot on a very small server** (Automatic buffer scaling described for upcoming server fill setting)

## Games
- **Meep City** [7:21](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=441): Friends list with online status and click-to-join teleport; friend-online notifications drive joins.
- **Island Royale** [10:24](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=624): Lobby friend invites add friends to your team before the round starts.
- **Mining Simulator** [13:20](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=800): Group-only item tells players to join the fan group, building early connections.
- **Tales from the Valley** [13:20](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=800): Lets players input user-generated groups (e.g. guilds) to play together through the game.
- **Restaurant Tycoon** [20:26](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=1226): Recently released a team-building feature; speaker suggests team-building alone may not be enough to keep players engaged.
- **Jailbreak** [23:51](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=1431): Museum robbery requires two players, raising alt-farming concerns; fix by requiring dexterity or timing.
