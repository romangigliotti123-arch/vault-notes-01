# Navigating Acquisitions | RDC 2025

[Watch](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A) | RobloxOfficial | 6,539 views | 2025-09-26

## Summary
Panel on Roblox game acquisitions (Voldex CEO Alex Singer, Rogue Unit's Filipe Lucas, Roblox corp dev lead Kyle Price). Covers motivations to sell (liquidity, freeing time, adding expertise), trade-offs (capped upside, loss of control, earnout risk), prep (legal hygiene, timing, aligning expectations, due diligence on buyer), and Q&A on valuation, escrow, and contracts.

## Tips
- [1:43](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=103) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Be clear on why you want to sell (liquidity, freeing time, needing expertise) so you can pick the right deal structure and buyer.
- [12:55](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=775) **[[common-mistakes]]** Get contracts with all collaborators (IP ownership, contractor vs. revshare vs. owner status) before seeking a sale; messy arrangements hurt deals.
- [13:38](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=818) **[[common-mistakes]]** Sell while a game is established and stable; brand-new games are valued much lower and declining games attract few buyers.
- [15:23](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=923) **[[team-and-budget]]** Get your own legal advisors into every conversation and get your own valuation rather than accepting the first number offered.
- [15:23](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=923) **[[team-and-budget]]** Make sure the acquirer understands Roblox as well as you do, or the community and platform issues may cause later problems.
- [16:03](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=963) **[[team-and-budget]]** Run a worst-case analysis on every contract clause to protect against future disputes.
- [10:50](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=650) **[[team-and-budget]]** List your team's needs and pains, then check whether a specific investor actually meets them before negotiating.
- [11:34](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=694) **[[team-and-budget]]** Align non-negotiables with all partners and test whether the team accepts a more corporate structure before signing.
- [6:40](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=400) **[[team-and-budget]]** Do due diligence on the acquirer: talk to creators they bought from and research the company, since your earnout depends on them.
- [7:21](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=441) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Reflect on whether you are comfortable with deadlines, budgets and consistency, since investors expect commitment and may limit frequent direction changes.
- [28:19](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=1699) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Use an escrow with a bank so the seller transfers the game only after funds are confirmed.
- [28:59](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=1739) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Require sign-off from all shareholders before proceeding with a sale.
- [26:56](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=1616) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Ask the buyer for proof of funds and check references from past sellers before signing.
- [20:01](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=1201) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Disclose third-party assets such as licensed music or freelancer IP in the contract disclosure schedule.
- [25:34](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=1534) **[[community-and-discord]]** Network early with other developers and studios; relationships formed now can matter for future acquisitions or partnerships.
- [17:24](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=1044) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Spend time brainstorming future joint work with a potential partner to test how you'd collaborate.
