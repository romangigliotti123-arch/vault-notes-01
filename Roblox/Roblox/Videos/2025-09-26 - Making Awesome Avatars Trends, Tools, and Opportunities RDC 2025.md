## Summary
Roblox Developer Conference 2025 talk on avatar marketplace growth and upcoming features: PBR items, shoes and emotes categories, a makeup system demo (studio beta, UGC next year), Shops storefronts, Marketplace Select premium curation, and revenue share up to 70% above price floor. Includes Q&A.

## Tips
- [18:30](https://youtu.be/Tese1QLD3nU?t=1110) **[[pricing]]** Price avatar items above the price floor to qualify for higher revenue share: up to 70% instead of the standard 30%, visible on the creator dashboard.
- [7:02](https://youtu.be/Tese1QLD3nU?t=422) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Look for underserved item categories: creators had been publishing shoes as pants because no shoes category existed; shoes and emotes are now separate UGC categories.
- [6:20](https://youtu.be/Tese1QLD3nU?t=380) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use PBR (roughness and metal properties) for avatar accessories to make them look realistic; PBR items can be published alongside non-PBR ones.
- [17:04](https://youtu.be/Tese1QLD3nU?t=1024) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Build a Shop to showcase your full catalog in one place instead of relying on users finding items one by one; you can also link to it from outside Roblox.
- [27:25](https://youtu.be/Tese1QLD3nU?t=1645) **[[monetisation-other]]** Sell full head-to-toe looks as bundles, not only single items; the speaker confirmed full avatars can be showcased in Shops from day one.
- [19:11](https://youtu.be/Tese1QLD3nU?t=1151) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Premium items priced higher will be eligible for Marketplace Select, a curated space for top-quality items; qualification criteria will be published on the dev forum.
- [19:52](https://youtu.be/Tese1QLD3nU?t=1192) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ads Manager integration is planned so you can target ads at audiences for individual items and for whole Shops.
- [31:28](https://youtu.be/Tese1QLD3nU?t=1888) **[[monetisation-other]]** Makeup will likely be sold as bundles whose layers buyers can mix and match across bundles, so you can sell single layers or full looks.
- [23:55](https://youtu.be/Tese1QLD3nU?t=1435) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Makeup UGC (planned early next year) will use Maya/Blender templates with paintable regions (face, eye, lip); max texture size 1024x1024.
- [28:47](https://youtu.be/Tese1QLD3nU?t=1727) **[[roblox-platform-changes]]** Trading of UGC Limiteds is under discussion for 2026 but not committed; originality and trade safety are still being worked out.
