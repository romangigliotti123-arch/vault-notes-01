## Summary
Roblox panel on scaling innovation, hosted by Alex Krotoski with CTO Dan Sturman and engineering directors for avatars, economy, and developer platform. Covers platform scale, avatar face tech, ad formats, open cloud and primitives-over-bespoke-services, backward compatibility, hiring for systems thinking, and child safety. Little direct advice for individual game makers.

## Tips
- [35:12](https://youtu.be/SOoe0E1Ql5M?t=2112) **[[common-mistakes]]** Assume generic systems like data storage will be abused or spammed; design rate limits and abuse protection in from the start.
- [39:18](https://youtu.be/SOoe0E1Ql5M?t=2358) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Abstract low-level complexity out of exposed APIs; exposing internals let the community misuse them, forcing a relaunch of the facial API.
- [17:43](https://youtu.be/SOoe0E1Ql5M?t=1063) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Build generic primitives (e.g. sorted map, priority queue) rather than bespoke services; developers then build matchmaking and other systems on top.
- [23:28](https://youtu.be/SOoe0E1Ql5M?t=1408) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't break existing experiences or avatars with surprise changes; treat APIs as permanent and plan changes years ahead.
