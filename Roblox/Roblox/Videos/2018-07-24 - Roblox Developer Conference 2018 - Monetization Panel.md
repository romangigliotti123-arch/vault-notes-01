# Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Monetization Panel

[Watch](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk) | RobloxOfficial | 8,411 views | 2018-07-24

## Summary
RDC 2018 monetization panel with Andrew Brezza (Miners Haven), Alex Piniella (Mega City, Dodgeball) and Jeff Mayer (Roblox analytics, ex-Kabam). Covers virtual currency, mystery boxes, limited-edition items, sales timing, first-purchase tactics, analytics, pricing and paid access.

## Tips
- [3:14](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=194) **[[monetisation-other]]** Decide which items you sell before picking a price or audience; mystery boxes are only the delivery mechanism for the items inside.
- [3:14](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=194) **[[pricing]]** Test prices with live data: Alex changed coin-to-Robux price points over three to six months and doubled revenue.
- [4:36](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=276) **[[community-and-discord]]** Tie monetisation to in-game events (e.g. a race for cars on sale) so non-paying players take part too.
- [5:56](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=356) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track every purchase and every currency source and sink; compare currency bought versus earned, especially during sales.
- [7:19](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=439) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Compare sales where players spend from balance with sales that trigger new Robux deposits; the latter signals stronger demand.
- [7:56](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=476) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Reward login streaks with premium currency and boxes so new players open items and become likelier to buy later.
- [8:38](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=518) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Give a generous free currency amount in the tutorial and have players buy their first item, so the first purchase is low-friction.
- [10:02](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=602) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make the first sale small in price and big in reward so players feel the value before entering payment details.
- [11:51](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=711) **[[common-mistakes]]** Premium currency hoards build up and top players spend stored balance instead of buying; plan sinks before big content drops.
- [13:52](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=832) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Check top spenders' balances: high balances mean they've run out of things to buy, so build more shop content.
- [15:12](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=912) **[[pricing]]** Tie big sales to national holidays, pair them with free limited events for non-payers, and keep sales rare so players don't wait for discounts.
- [18:14](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=1094) **[[monetisation-other]]** Offer limited-edition seasonal items that can't be bought after the event; they create strong demand and pride in ownership.
- [19:35](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=1175) **[[monetisation-other]]** Give away some rare items early; a player who gets a rare item is more likely to spend later.
- [21:17](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=1277) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build rewarding content for non-paying players too; they make the game's paying players' spending possible and visible.
- [21:58](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=1318) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Get retention, playtime and optimisation right before focusing on revenue; monetisation built into gameplay works better than bolted on.
- [24:44](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=1484) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't rush a trend game with heavy monetisation at the expense of fun; one speaker's rushed monetised game and fun unmonetised game both underperformed.
- [26:51](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=1611) **[[pricing]]** There is no formula for pricing; start at a price, lower it if few buy, and raise it on the next version.
- [27:33](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=1653) **[[common-mistakes]]** Check where players spend time before adding shop items; items placed in areas players ignore won't sell.
- [29:54](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=1794) **[[monetisation-other]]** Sell items players want but wouldn't be upset not having; avoid paywalling core content.
- [31:14](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=1874) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan monetisation during design and iterate; start with a store in the game, collect data and adjust prices.
- [37:16](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=2236) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use GameAnalytics (free, about an hour to set up) for retention, new versus returning users, and play times.
- [38:40](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=2320) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Don't sell passes that block core progress; sell optional extras, and let players earn content needed to play.
- [43:25](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=2605) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Get players into the shop early, for example with a tutorial that gives free currency and asks them to buy one item.
- [44:54](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=2694) **[[monetisation-other]]** Paid access can work: Welcome to Bloxburg charged 2500 Robux and stayed on the front page, but free access shows your real audience.
