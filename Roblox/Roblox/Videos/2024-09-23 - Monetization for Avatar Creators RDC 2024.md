# Monetization for Avatar Creators | RDC 2024

[Watch](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8) | RobloxOfficial | 4,897 views | 2024-09-23

## Summary
Roblox RDC 2024 panel on monetizing Avatar (UGC) creations. Covers Marketplace growth stats, new avatar creation tools (glTF import, Blender live link, Avatar Auto Setup, accessory fitting), publishing decisions (limited vs non-limited, price floor pegging, minimum sales price, distribution), and a creator panel on branding, pricing, community and brand deals.

## Tips
- [0:45](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=45) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Marketplace data: 18M daily visitors shop avatars; 2 of 3 spenders buy avatar content. Speaker says now is a good time to invest in avatar content.
- [7:26](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=446) **[[pricing]]** Set price relative to dynamic price floor: price = floor + your offset. Optionally set a minimum sales price so the floor cannot drag your item below it.
- [7:26](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=446) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't assume a sale is final: items can have zero sales at launch and trend weeks later; keep listings up and promote them.
- [7:26](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=446) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Make one item with multiple colors: non-limited for common colors, limited for unique ones, a strategy fashion brands use.
- [8:46](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=526) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Choose distribution deliberately: sell in Marketplace for reach and Today's Picks eligibility, or only in your experience to drive engagement with your game.
- [14:15](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=855) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Creators in the panel said on-platform publishing mechanics (upload, publishing) must be learned; technical skills matter even for non-technical brand managers.
- [16:17](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=977) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build a community around your personal brand early, using Twitter and a Discord server; the community then helps decide which styles to make next.
- [18:22](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=1102) **[[community-and-discord]]** Let community input drive design: community suggestions helped creators find their style and gave players ownership.
- [20:26](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=1226) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Find a niche and a personal style; there is a lot of content, so stand out rather than copy trends.
- [21:49](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=1309) **[[community-and-discord]]** Monster Cat ran a Twitter poll-style engagement campaign where people's engagement set the release quantity; over 100,000 interactions. Plan launches rather than winging them.
- [28:00](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=1680) **[[pricing]]** Price by market research: compare comparables and your own catalog, test new styles first, and price higher for items that took long to make.
- [28:41](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=1721) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use scarcity and collector mechanics: unlimited items as entry pieces, limited items for collectors; gating items behind a collector score drove fomo.
- [30:04](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=1804) **[[pricing]]** Keep most items affordable for typical players and make only a few expensive exclusives.
- [32:26](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=1946) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Beginners: start with free 3D modeling software and basic tutorials, then find other UGC creators for plugins and tips.
- [35:10](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=2110) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Pursue brand deals by reaching out to brands that already worked on the platform and approaching them with specific value; the Partnership Brand Program helps.
- [4:19](https://youtu.be/NEK7jq1LFu8?t=259) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use Blender live link for live-updating avatar content in Studio, glTF import, and the Avatar Preview tool to check bodies and animations before publishing.
