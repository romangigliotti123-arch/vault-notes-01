# Creating Experiences to Reach New Audiences | RDC23

## Summary
Maximal Studios (Front Lines, Operation Scorpion) explains how they built a high-fidelity Roblox FPS that drew off-platform players. Covers their four fidelity pillars, animation, environment, sound design, UI/VFX polish, performance, Twitter/TikTok/Discord marketing, and community moderation. Previews their upcoming racing game.

- [5:56](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=356) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Prioritise performance for all audiences, including high-FPS hardcore players and mobile; understand every code segment's performance.
- [6:36](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=396) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build high fidelity around four pillars: immersive world, responsive character feel, real-world-like details, and a sense of always something new.
- [8:45](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=525) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Realistic characters and animation, not just graphics, drew older audiences; avoid overly linear or robotic animation.
- [10:30](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=630) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Fine-tune lighting per map, add props and wear-and-tear textures, and use PBR so surfaces catch light realistically.
- [11:11](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=671) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use sound groups for automated mixing, e.g. dampen gameplay audio when health is low; dampen enemy audio behind walls.

- [12:35](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=755) **[[titles-descriptions]]** For marketing, repost individual developers' posts on a brand Twitter account to showcase team talent alongside the game.
- [13:16](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=796) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** TikTok can drive large reach even without a creator following; short clips of the game got millions of views despite ~300 views per post.
- [13:57](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=837) **[[community-and-discord]]** Use a Discord server for announcements, feedback and community; add a stats bot and channels for engagement like music sessions and show-off channels.
- [14:20](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=860) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Show players you care: act on feedback, add attention to detail, Easter eggs and polish in every aspect of the experience.
- [22:23](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=1343) **[[community-and-discord]]** Scale Discord moderation with written guidelines, separate game and Discord moderators, and defined ban-length rules.
- [24:02](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=1442) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** For mobile, use proper techniques and clean topology, and plan a mobile-friendly version alongside a custom LOD system for large maps.
- [29:16](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=1756) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** For racing, use a server-authoritative networking model with full control of physics and car positions for smoothness.

- [19:20](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=1160) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Prototype sound systems in a standalone concept first, prove them, then integrate into the game; account for Roblox sound-trigger delay.
