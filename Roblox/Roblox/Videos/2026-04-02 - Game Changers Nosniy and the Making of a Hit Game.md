## Summary
Short interview with Nasney, co-creator of Rivals, on its turnaround. Rivals launched with only a few hundred players and wasn't fun, so the team pivoted to PvP duels, a genre rare on Roblox at the time. They stress experimenting, not giving up, and that a small team can ship using Roblox's built-in player base and engine.

## Tips
- [1:27](https://youtu.be/IuJWzpvpIa8?t=87) **[[team-and-budget]]** Roblox lets a small team ship: the engine and existing player base are already there, so you don't need a huge team.
- [0:44](https://youtu.be/IuJWzpvpIa8?t=44) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Pick an idea the team finds genuinely fun to play; Rivals pivoted to PvP duels because the team enjoyed playing them together.
- [0:44](https://youtu.be/IuJWzpvpIa8?t=44) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** When early numbers and fun are poor, change the game rather than iterating on a failing version; Rivals was reworked after launch with only a few hundred players.
- [1:27](https://youtu.be/IuJWzpvpIa8?t=87) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make sure new players get the best experience right off the bat, since first impressions drive whether they stay.
