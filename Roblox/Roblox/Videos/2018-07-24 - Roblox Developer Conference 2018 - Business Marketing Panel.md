# Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Business Marketing Panel

[Watch](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk) | RobloxOfficial | 4,622 views | 2018-07-24

## Summary
Panel at RDC 2018 on marketing Roblox games. Covers banner ad experiments and CTR, paying YouTubers with promo codes and domino effects, sponsored games, Twitter posting structure, timing marketing pushes to launch together, and community/Studio groups for retention of audience.

## Tips
- [5:11](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=311) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Run ads consistently for a week at a fixed daily budget (~25,000 Robux/day); a test showed ~100-150% more revenue than the no-ad week.
- [3:48](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=228) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Optimise both ad CTR (ad click) and the second CTR (game page click to Play); clickbait ads may get high first CTR but low Play rate.
- [6:50](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=410) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Contact YouTubers early via email, Twitter, Facebook; most won't reply, but one or two can trigger a domino effect of other creators.
- [7:33](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=453) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Pitch new games to YouTubers as new content; start with smaller creators and send links to existing videos of your game.
- [8:16](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=496) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ask YouTubers for multi-part series; pair an update with a new episode to boost view counts.
- [8:16](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=496) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Give each YouTuber a unique promo code granting in-game cash; split codes across creators to measure which performs best.
- [9:40](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=580) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Design gameplay with constant action and a clear hook so it is easy for YouTubers to make exciting videos.
- [11:59](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=719) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Sponsored games place your game on the front page; sponsored listings hide the like/dislike ratio, useful after a rough beta.
- [12:40](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=760) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** On the game page, use bold text, a quality background image and emojis in the title to stand out; keep the icon consistent with the game theme.
- [14:16](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=856) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Write tweets that match the game theme, include the game link, and offer an incentive such as a promo code.
- [17:28](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=1048) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Line up YouTube videos, Twitter posts, group shouts, and image updates to land together; this drove Zoo Tycoon up to #2 on PC within 5-6 hours.
- [18:51](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=1131) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Launch or build hype on Wednesday so you have momentum entering Friday; weekends are hard to climb due to ad-heavy competition.
- [22:51](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=1371) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid clickbait ads; they may boost early numbers but damage trust in your studio brand.
- [23:34](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=1414) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use a game analytics module to tie promo code redemptions to players and measure campaign effectiveness.
- [24:16](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=1456) **[[titles-descriptions]]** To relaunch a shut-down game, rebrand with new GFX, thumbnail, icon, and add 'relaunch' to the title.
- [25:37](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=1537) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Start with small ad budgets (~500 Robux) and reinvest returns to scale up gradually.
- [28:23](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=1703) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Keep icons simple; video trailers gave little lift versus thumbnails, so wait before investing in them.
- [31:06](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=1866) **[[community-and-discord]]** Maintain your community, reply to fan questions, and use a studio group so players see future releases.
- [32:27](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=1947) **[[community-and-discord]]** Reach out to other developers on Twitter; cross-promotion via retweets and plays brings new players from their audiences.
- [33:51](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=2031) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Tease features and show work-in-progress via social media to build hype before launch and updates.
- [36:39](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=2199) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Combine ads, sponsored placements, YouTube and Twitter together; small channels add up to a big impact.
- [38:01](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=2281) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Measure ROI on marketing: reinvest returns (e.g. 1,000 spend returning 5,000) and scale spend only once profitable.
- [39:23](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=2363) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't spam advertisements in groups or comments; it risks moderator bans. Read the Terms of Service.
- [40:04](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=2404) **[[monetisation-other]]** Pay YouTubers in group funds that they spend in your game, so the payment returns to you while they demo it.
