All leads tips on this theme, grouped by topic, oldest first. Cross-creator synthesis: see [[README]] in roblox-kb.

## [[analytics-metrics]]
- 2018-07-24 [11:30](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=690) Set decision razors before launch: retention (daily actives, return rate) and engagement (minutes per active player). Kill features that don't move them. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote Kristian Segerstrale|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote: Kristi]])
- 2018-07-24 [12:10](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=730) Treat time spent as the truest signal of love; purchases can mislead because players may regret buys they didn't value. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote Kristian Segerstrale|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote: Kristi]])
- 2022-02-01 [17:22](https://youtu.be/WGRpglQ87XE?t=1042) Tag each onboarding step as an event to build a funnel; the funnel pinpoints the exact churn points to fix. ([[2022-02-01 - Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D1 to D100|Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D]])
- 2022-02-01 [25:16](https://youtu.be/WGRpglQ87XE?t=1516) Pair qualitative findings with telemetry. Small samples explain why something happens; telemetry shows how many players are affected. ([[2022-02-01 - Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D1 to D100|Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D]])
- 2022-06-11 [37:44](https://youtu.be/Mep4_w8qxeE?t=2264) Skip metrics during prototyping. Use qualitative feedback early and add analytics close to launch, when most systems exist. ([[2022-06-11 - (ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Level Up Connect 2022|(ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Lev]])
- 2025-06-26 [1:43:15](https://youtu.be/krMTcTvaj_8?t=6195) Set a realistic CCU target for niche games; the streamer targets roughly 300-500 players for a niche motorcycle game. ([[2025-06-26 - Dream Game Stream (World Building!) Roblox Development|Dream Game Stream (World Building!) | Roblox Devel]])
- 2026-10-17 [7:18](https://youtu.be/ltbZzXRrDF8?t=438) Tune upgrade prices and income rates so progression feels rewarding, not too easy or too slow. ([[2026-10-17 - How To Make An ENTIRE Roblox Game With AI - Full Guide|How To Make An ENTIRE Roblox Game With AI - Full G]])
- 2026-10-23 [14:28](https://youtu.be/OfObWnrucRY?t=868) Track visits against a daily goal (speaker used 500 visits/day) and active players; the speaker cited 250 active players as the threshold for targeting under-16 audiences. ([[2026-10-23 - Building a Viral Roblox Game with AI FULL PROCESS|Building a Viral Roblox Game with AI [FULL PROCESS]])

## [[playtesting-and-feedback]]
- 2022-02-01 [30:44](https://youtu.be/WGRpglQ87XE?t=1844) Run diary studies of about 40-50 players for several months. They reveal how the game unfolds over time and why players drop, which one-off playtests miss. ([[2022-02-01 - Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D1 to D100|Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D]])
- 2022-02-01 [50:01](https://youtu.be/WGRpglQ87XE?t=3001) Use open daily diary surveys and rate motivation each day on a 1-7 scale. Plot the scores to find dips in the experience. ([[2022-02-01 - Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D1 to D100|Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D]])
- 2022-02-01 [51:33](https://youtu.be/WGRpglQ87XE?t=3093) Layer what players say with telemetry. Cooking Dash saw that players loved prize wheels despite misunderstanding them, which led to clearer design. ([[2022-02-01 - Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D1 to D100|Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D]])
- 2022-02-01 [43:29](https://youtu.be/WGRpglQ87XE?t=2609) Keep longitudinal studies cheap: most play is unmoderated, so reuse short broad daily surveys and spend moderator time on a follow-up interview. ([[2022-02-01 - Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D1 to D100|Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D]])
- 2022-02-01 [58:14](https://youtu.be/WGRpglQ87XE?t=3494) Use a small sample to understand why something happens. Use telemetry to measure how widespread it is. ([[2022-02-01 - Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D1 to D100|Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D]])
- 2022-04-25 [15:07](https://youtu.be/Zrf1cou_yVo?t=907) Watch higher-level players for exploit discovery after patches; they find economy breaks that early testers miss. ([[2022-04-25 - How Video Game Economies are Designed|How Video Game Economies are Designed]])
- 2022-06-11 [32:20](https://youtu.be/Mep4_w8qxeE?t=1940) Playtest with friends, family and followers who haven't seen the game. First-time players reveal gaps the team can't see. ([[2022-06-11 - (ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Level Up Connect 2022|(ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Lev]])
- 2026-10-23 [6:50](https://youtu.be/OfObWnrucRY?t=410) Playtest every iteration; the speaker found issues like boss move sets, stairs not connecting, and character customization that looked off-brand only by playing. ([[2026-10-23 - Building a Viral Roblox Game with AI FULL PROCESS|Building a Viral Roblox Game with AI [FULL PROCESS]])

## [[team-and-budget]]
- 2018-07-24 [7:27](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=447) Prioritise talent density and culture over headcount; three excellent people beat twelve okay ones. Supercell's Clash games were made by teams of about 10-11. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote Kristian Segerstrale|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote: Kristi]])
- 2025-06-26 [1:58:41](https://youtu.be/krMTcTvaj_8?t=7121) Identify the bottleneck roles early (bike system scripter, terrain artist, custom vector art) and hire for them rather than waiting on one person. ([[2025-06-26 - Dream Game Stream (World Building!) Roblox Development|Dream Game Stream (World Building!) | Roblox Devel]])

## [[development-process-and-speed]]
- 2018-07-24 [12:10](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=730) Ship ideas as quickly as possible so you can fail fast on metrics rather than polishing untested concepts. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote Kristian Segerstrale|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote: Kristi]])
- 2022-06-11 [19:42](https://youtu.be/Mep4_w8qxeE?t=1182) Prototype early (first 2-3 weeks) to find flaws, fun and technical roadblocks before building maps, art and features on top. ([[2022-06-11 - (ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Level Up Connect 2022|(ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Lev]])
- 2022-06-11 [22:43](https://youtu.be/Mep4_w8qxeE?t=1363) Use paper prototypes for fast design iteration and UI/mobile layout testing; use studio prototypes for physics, AI and technical risk. ([[2022-06-11 - (ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Level Up Connect 2022|(ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Lev]])
- 2022-06-11 [28:14](https://youtu.be/Mep4_w8qxeE?t=1694) Build studio prototypes with reusable code, not throwaway hacks, though temporary placeholders (cylinders, auto-hit towers) are fine. ([[2022-06-11 - (ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Level Up Connect 2022|(ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Lev]])
- 2022-06-11 [30:16](https://youtu.be/Mep4_w8qxeE?t=1816) Prototype the core loop first. It aligns the team and tells artists and programmers what to build next. ([[2022-06-11 - (ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Level Up Connect 2022|(ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Lev]])
- 2022-06-11 [35:13](https://youtu.be/Mep4_w8qxeE?t=2113) Milestones: prototype (core loop), then vertical slice (one login-to-logout session), then MVP (all features needed to ship). ([[2022-06-11 - (ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Level Up Connect 2022|(ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Lev]])
- 2022-06-11 [55:10](https://youtu.be/Mep4_w8qxeE?t=3310) If a feature would take more than 2-3 weeks, use a cheap intermediate solution to get an early feel for the game. ([[2022-06-11 - (ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Level Up Connect 2022|(ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Lev]])
- 2025-05-23 [4:43](https://youtu.be/Gw2FwndwnOg?t=283) Dev 2 spent about 2 hours on core money and save scripts; scope each session to one system so progress is testable. ([[2025-05-23 - Can 6 DEVS Make GROW A GARDEN Without Communicating|Can 6 DEVS Make GROW A GARDEN Without Communicatin]])
- 2025-05-23 [15:29](https://youtu.be/Gw2FwndwnOg?t=929) Use a clear hand-off list between devs; the video shows the cost of unplanned changes to an existing codebase. ([[2025-05-23 - Can 6 DEVS Make GROW A GARDEN Without Communicating|Can 6 DEVS Make GROW A GARDEN Without Communicatin]])
- 2026-10-23 [3:53](https://youtu.be/OfObWnrucRY?t=233) Build in iterative passes: one large prompt, playtest, then send a numbered fix list. Each pass took roughly 2-4 hours in the speaker's run. ([[2026-10-23 - Building a Viral Roblox Game with AI FULL PROCESS|Building a Viral Roblox Game with AI [FULL PROCESS]])

## [[common-mistakes]]
- 2022-02-01 [42:40](https://youtu.be/WGRpglQ87XE?t=2560) Do not judge a game by a single session. Many important issues only appear over days or weeks, so use longitudinal tests. ([[2022-02-01 - Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D1 to D100|Research Methods to Improve Live Game Retention, D]])
- 2022-04-25 [0:01](https://youtu.be/Zrf1cou_yVo?t=1) Test for infinite-resource exploits before launch; a repeatable tap (e.g. respawning cows) can let players farm unlimited wealth early. ([[2022-04-25 - How Video Game Economies are Designed|How Video Game Economies are Designed]])
- 2022-04-25 [7:30](https://youtu.be/Zrf1cou_yVo?t=450) Patching an exploit with a punishing spawn can create a new exploit; CD Projekt Red's fix (level 27 monster spawn) was itself farmed for heads, requiring a second patch. ([[2022-04-25 - How Video Game Economies are Designed|How Video Game Economies are Designed]])
- 2022-06-11 [15:01](https://youtu.be/Mep4_w8qxeE?t=901) Avoid 'tumors': systems that compete with the core loop for attention. Players split badly, one system dies, and some players quit. ([[2022-06-11 - (ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Level Up Connect 2022|(ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Lev]])
- 2022-06-11 [52:31](https://youtu.be/Mep4_w8qxeE?t=3151) Don't spend months on maps, characters or tech without showing core gameplay. Teams that did this often threw away large amounts of work. ([[2022-06-11 - (ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Level Up Connect 2022|(ENGLISH) Core Loops and Prototyping by Roblox Lev]])
- 2026-10-23 [13:25](https://youtu.be/OfObWnrucRY?t=805) Test multiplayer and servers before promoting; the speaker found bugs only after publishing and said he had not tested multiplayer until late. ([[2026-10-23 - Building a Viral Roblox Game with AI FULL PROCESS|Building a Viral Roblox Game with AI [FULL PROCESS]])

## [[mindset-and-strategy]]
- 2018-07-24 [10:49](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=649) Expect most ideas to fail; assume roughly 70% or more of ideas won't work, even ones you feel sure about. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote Kristian Segerstrale|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote: Kristi]])
- 2018-07-24 [13:32](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=812) Build lasting value beyond one game: invest in team, reusable tools/analytics pipelines, and IP/characters/community that outlive any single title. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote Kristian Segerstrale|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote: Kristi]])
- 2018-07-24 [14:13](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=853) Plan for a 30-40% hit ratio at best; a successful game doesn't guarantee the next one, so keep iterating. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote Kristian Segerstrale|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote: Kristi]])
- 2018-07-24 [16:18](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=978) Don't assume success transfers to unfamiliar genres just because you analysed them; you'll miss the small details players care about. ([[2018-07-24 - Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote Kristian Segerstrale|Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote: Kristi]])
