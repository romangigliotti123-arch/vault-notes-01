## Summary
Beginner leveling guide for Project Slayers 2 (Roblox). Covers the customization/clan screen and storage bag (VIP), starter-village quests that must be done, the skill tree priorities (double jump, wall climb, cutlass grind), titles stacking, map-based progression path, the level-65 dungeon unlock quest, selling materials at Mist Village, horse taming, and public-server quest EXP scaling.
