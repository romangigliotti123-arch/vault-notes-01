# Roblox Developer Conference 2018 - Keynote: Kristian Segerstrale

[Watch](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE) | leads | 3,732 views | 2018-07-24

## Summary
Roblox Developer Conference 2018 keynote by Kristian Segerstrale (ex-Glu Mobile, Supercell investor). Covers team and culture over ideas, killing ideas with retention and engagement metrics, building lasting value (team, tools, IP), building games you love, thinking globally, and developer community. Mostly inspirational, not Roblox-specific tactics.

## Tips
- [7:27](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=447) **[[team-and-budget]]** Prioritise talent density and culture over headcount; three excellent people beat twelve okay ones. Supercell's Clash games were made by teams of about 10-11.
- [10:49](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=649) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect most ideas to fail; assume roughly 70% or more of ideas won't work, even ones you feel sure about.
- [11:30](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=690) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Set decision razors before launch: retention (daily actives, return rate) and engagement (minutes per active player). Kill features that don't move them.
- [11:30](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=690) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Retention curve drives compound growth; if players don't want to come back, the experience isn't working regardless of how good it seems.
- [12:10](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=730) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Treat time spent as the truest signal of love; purchases can mislead because players may regret buys they didn't value.
- [12:10](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=730) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Ship ideas as quickly as possible so you can fail fast on metrics rather than polishing untested concepts.
- [13:32](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=812) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Build lasting value beyond one game: invest in team, reusable tools/analytics pipelines, and IP/characters/community that outlive any single title.
- [14:13](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=853) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Plan for a 30-40% hit ratio at best; a successful game doesn't guarantee the next one, so keep iterating.
- [15:01](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=901) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Make games you personally love playing; small-detail execution depends on genuine enthusiasm for the genre.
- [16:18](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=978) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Don't assume success transfers to unfamiliar genres just because you analysed them; you'll miss the small details players care about.
- [17:00](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=1020) **[[localization-and-international]]** Look beyond English: US is under 5% of the world's population and English is spoken by under 20%; the fastest-growing game markets are non-English.
- [17:40](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=1060) **[[community-and-discord]]** Recruit community managers in non-English markets and spend regular time listening to those players to shape where the game goes.
- [19:01](https://youtu.be/EDY-8V943IE?t=1141) **[[community-and-discord]]** Share ideas with other developers rather than treating peers as pure competitors; collaboration helps the platform and your own growth.
