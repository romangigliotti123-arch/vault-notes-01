## Summary
Animation tutorial for My Movie (Roblox animation tool) covering exponential in/out easing, perspective, effects layering, building impact frames from decals/shapes, and uploading custom decals (800x800 transparent PNG via Creations > Development Items).

## Tips
- [0:29](https://youtu.be/k3id70yCMRY?t=29) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Mix exponential-in and exponential-out easing: move object halfway with exponential in, then copy the frame and finish with exponential out for smoother motion.
- [1:20](https://youtu.be/k3id70yCMRY?t=80) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Move the far-side arm back on the lowest layer to make a character look like it's turning to punch; perspective sells the motion.
- [2:04](https://youtu.be/k3id70yCMRY?t=124) **[[vfx-and-particles]]** Add a short (~0.5s) effect like a wind layer under the punching limb, fading out with exponential-out easing, to add polish.
- [2:45](https://youtu.be/k3id70yCMRY?t=165) **[[vfx-and-particles]]** Build impact frames: add a white circle at the hit point, animated wavy lines crossing the frame, and a full-screen white square that fades out (exponential or smooth out).
- [4:53](https://youtu.be/k3id70yCMRY?t=293) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Custom decals must be 800x800 px with transparent background; upload via Creations > Development Items > Decals, then paste the asset ID into My Movie's search.
