# Increasing Premium Payouts - Starting Today!

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/increasing-premium-payouts-starting-today/1072865) | Roblox DevForum | 1,966 views | 2021-02-26

## Summary
Roblox staff announcement (Feb 2021) that Premium Payouts rate increases again, >75% more Robux per engagement score. Notes prior increases: tripled April 2020, raised again September 2020, roughly 9x total since launch. Page warns info may be outdated.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/increasing-premium-payouts-starting-today/1072865) **[[pricing]]** Premium Payouts pay developers based on engagement scores of Premium users; the rate per Robux changed multiple times, so check current DevHub docs rather than old figures.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/increasing-premium-payouts-starting-today/1072865) **[[monetisation-other]]** Premium Payouts is a revenue stream independent of purchases; engagement (time spent by Premium users) drives payouts, so retention-focused games benefit most.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/increasing-premium-payouts-starting-today/1072865) Premium Payouts rate increase (Feb 2021): **>75% more Robux earned per engagement**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/increasing-premium-payouts-starting-today/1072865) Rate tripled: **April 2020**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/increasing-premium-payouts-starting-today/1072865) Total increase since launch: **roughly 9x (nearly 5x by Sept 2020)**
