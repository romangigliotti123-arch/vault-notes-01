# Guide: Effectively QA Testing Your Roblox Experience

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-effectively-qa-testing-your-roblox-experience/2199838) | Roblox DevForum | 142 views | 2023-02-26

## Summary
Guide by QA tester Abcreator on running QA tests for Roblox experiences: misconceptions, free vs dedicated testers, team structure, compensation factors, tester selection, running sessions, collecting reports, and stress tests.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-effectively-qa-testing-your-roblox-experience/2199838) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Treat QA testing as a paid job, not casual play; don't downplay testers as regular players. Testing should happen well before release, not as the last step.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-effectively-qa-testing-your-roblox-experience/2199838) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Ask testers for game-design/UX feedback and feature ideas, not just bugs. Testers may reveal confusing UX that requires redesign.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-effectively-qa-testing-your-roblox-experience/2199838) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Don't release until you pass a full test with no critical issues, since fixes can introduce new bugs.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-effectively-qa-testing-your-roblox-experience/2199838) **[[community-and-discord]]** Set up a feedback channel before testing (Discord threads, Google Forms, or Slack) that all testers can access and that fits your budget.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-effectively-qa-testing-your-roblox-experience/2199838) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Collect device info, tester identity, and screenshots/dev-console logs at report time; these can't be recovered later.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-effectively-qa-testing-your-roblox-experience/2199838) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Let testers self-assess bug severity, then correct it yourself later. Make reporting easy and avoid discouraging duplicate reports.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-effectively-qa-testing-your-roblox-experience/2199838) **[[team-and-budget]]** Free QA groups suit small games but have high tester turnover and weak control over report quality and secrecy; dedicated teams give better communication and control.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-effectively-qa-testing-your-roblox-experience/2199838) **[[team-and-budget]]** Decide tester structure first: who they talk to, live sessions vs multi-day, access method (friend, group, whitelist), compensation, and whether to require an NDA.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-effectively-qa-testing-your-roblox-experience/2199838) **[[team-and-budget]]** Don't pay more experienced testers more within the same QA program. Scale pay by your revenue, bug volume, and whether testers are fans or professionals.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-effectively-qa-testing-your-roblox-experience/2199838) **[[team-and-budget]]** Advertise tester openings publicly rather than targeting specific individuals. Let practice-testing on a public version assess inexperienced testers.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-effectively-qa-testing-your-roblox-experience/2199838) **[[ab-testing-and-experiments]]** Use legal caution: avoid under-13 testers when using text platforms like Discord or when an NDA applies; consult a lawyer for contracts.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-effectively-qa-testing-your-roblox-experience/2199838) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Watch testers during sessions as a note-taking exercise, not a break. If blind testers need your help to progress, revisit the game design.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-effectively-qa-testing-your-roblox-experience/2199838) **[[optimization-and-streaming]]** Run stress tests with many invited players before launch (or post-launch), since Roblox rate limits and RAM limits can break games under load; judge stress-test feedback less than bug reports.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-effectively-qa-testing-your-roblox-experience/2199838) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Ask testers to communicate clearly; vague or non-reporting testers are of little value, so weigh communication skill when selecting.
