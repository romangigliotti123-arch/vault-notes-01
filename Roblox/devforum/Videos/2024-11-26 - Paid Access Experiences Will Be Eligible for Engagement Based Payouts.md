# Paid Access Experiences Will Be Eligible for Engagement Based Payouts

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/paid-access-experiences-will-be-eligible-for-engagement-based-payouts/3275498) | Roblox DevForum | 689 views | 2024-11-26

## Summary
Roblox staff announcement: starting Dec 3, 2024, paid access experiences (including local-currency paid access) become eligible for engagement-based payouts, calculated from the share of time Premium subscribers spend in the experience. Payouts are automatic, add to other revenue, and projected earnings appear in December with distribution starting January. Staff recommend adding the Premium Purchase Modal to grow these payouts.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/paid-access-experiences-will-be-eligible-for-engagement-based-payouts/3275498) **[[monetisation-other]]** Add the Premium Purchase Modal to your paid access experience to encourage Premium subscribers to spend more time, potentially increasing engagement-based payouts.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/paid-access-experiences-will-be-eligible-for-engagement-based-payouts/3275498) **[[monetisation-other]]** Engagement-based payouts require no setup; they are calculated automatically from Premium subscribers' share of time in your experience.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/paid-access-experiences-will-be-eligible-for-engagement-based-payouts/3275498) Engagement-based payouts eligibility start: **Tuesday, December 3, 2024** (Paid access experiences become eligible)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/paid-access-experiences-will-be-eligible-for-engagement-based-payouts/3275498) Projected earnings visible: **December 2024** (Creators see projected engagement-based payout earnings)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/paid-access-experiences-will-be-eligible-for-engagement-based-payouts/3275498) Payout distribution start: **January 2025** (Engagement-based payouts begin distributing)
