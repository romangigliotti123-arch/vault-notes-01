# Expanding How Commerce Can Help You Earn and Grow Your Communities

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/expanding-how-commerce-can-help-you-earn-and-grow-your-communities/3651181) | Roblox DevForum | 266 views | 2025-05-15

## Summary
Roblox announcement that Commerce APIs leave closed beta, letting eligible creators sell physical Shopify merchandise inside experiences, optionally bundled with digital avatar items. Covers eligibility thresholds, bundling fees, placement guidance, beta results from Twin Atlas, and the Approved Merchandiser Program linking retail purchases to digital items.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/expanding-how-commerce-can-help-you-earn-and-grow-your-communities/3651181) **[[monetisation-other]]** Pair physical merch with an exclusive digital avatar item or developer product; bundling increases the reason to buy.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/expanding-how-commerce-can-help-you-earn-and-grow-your-communities/3651181) **[[community-and-discord]]** Check community feedback on desired merchandise first; requests from your community signal which products to start with.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/expanding-how-commerce-can-help-you-earn-and-grow-your-communities/3651181) **[[monetisation-other]]** Place commerce prominently and natively, such as lobby billboards, menus or in-game stores, where players already make virtual purchases.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/expanding-how-commerce-can-help-you-earn-and-grow-your-communities/3651181) **[[common-mistakes]]** Ensure all listed products comply with Roblox Commerce Standards, Community Standards and Advertising Standards to avoid moderation issues.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/expanding-how-commerce-can-help-you-earn-and-grow-your-communities/3651181) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use IsEligibleToPurchaseCommerceProduct to hide the Commerce entry point from ineligible users.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/expanding-how-commerce-can-help-you-earn-and-grow-your-communities/3651181) Eligibility to pair physical with digital benefit: **100K average DAUs and 1M Robux earnings in last 3 months** (Alternative: $50K USD advertising spend agreed with Roblox)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/expanding-how-commerce-can-help-you-earn-and-grow-your-communities/3651181) Minimum age for commerce users: **13+** (Unless local regulations differ)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/expanding-how-commerce-can-help-you-earn-and-grow-your-communities/3651181) Twin Atlas beta revenue: **Over six figures, thousands of items sold** (Across Creatures of Sonaria, Dragon Adventures, World // Zero)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/expanding-how-commerce-can-help-you-earn-and-grow-your-communities/3651181) Share of Twin Atlas orders via Roblox commerce: **~90%** (Remaining sales via their own website)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/expanding-how-commerce-can-help-you-earn-and-grow-your-communities/3651181) Twin Atlas repeat-customer share of orders: **~50%** (Indicates players want to engage more and buy again)

## Games
- **Creatures of Sonaria**: Beta experience for Twin Atlas physical merch integration; strong community drove plushie and sticker demand.
- **Dragon Adventures**: Beta experience used by Twin Atlas for Commerce integration and items.
- **World // Zero**: Beta experience used by Twin Atlas for Commerce integration and items.
- **Deddy Bears Tower Defense**: Innov8 Creative Academy paired Deddy Bears plush with digital wings, hoods and crowns via AMP.
