# Creator Spotlight: Meet Ahlvie, the Environmental Artist Behind Fantastical Worlds

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) | Roblox DevForum | 183 views | 2024-05-10

## Summary
Roblox Creator Spotlight on environmental artist Ahlvie (Twin Atlas). Covers her art style, a world-building workflow (PureRef mood boards, greyboxing, Part to Terrain, Blender props, placement plugins), and her freelance path from Kingdom Life 2 to Twin Atlas.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Greybox the map with simple parts first to plan paths, spawns and endpoints before sculpting or detailing.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) **[[building-and-level-design]]** In open worlds, use curves and multiple directions instead of straight paths to encourage exploration.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use the Part to Terrain plugin to convert placed parts into terrain, then sculpt the landscape.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Gather a mood board of many reference images (e.g. in PureRef) before building to define look and feel.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Sketch unusual fantastical objects by hand when reference images fail to provide inspiration.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Model stylized props in Blender and texture them in Substance Painter when needed.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Speed up environment dressing with plugins: Brushtool for bulk prop placement, TerrainPainter for color and material variety, MeshVox for instant hills and mountains (sculpt down after).
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) **[[building-and-level-design]]** When redesigning an existing game, keep the map layout unchanged to avoid confusing veteran players; upgrade only the visuals.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Add lived-in details (fences, parks, gardens) to make an environment feel alive.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Draw on real-life surroundings and personal hobbies as inspiration to give a world a distinctive identity.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Add small unique details, such as a different flower per floor, to reward players who explore.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) **[[team-and-budget]]** Stay organized when juggling several art styles and multiple feedback sources across parallel projects; she often works on up to four experiences at once.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) Mood board time: **A couple of hours** (Ahlvie's reference gathering phase per new world concept)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) Concurrent experiences: **Up to 4** (Projects Ahlvie can work on at once at Twin Atlas)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648) Team size on first freelance project: **2 people** (Kingdom Life 2 redesign: Ahlvie and one programmer)

## Games
- **Kingdom Life 2**: Redesigned visuals while keeping the original map layout so veteran players aren't confused.
- **Swordburst 3**: Multi-floor open world; a unique flower on each floor rewards exploration and delights players.
- **Griffin's Destiny**: Example of her mystical, stylized habitat environment work.
- **Horse Life**: Designed flowers for the beta; example of stylized nature art.
- **Winds of Fortune**: Her first Twin Atlas role was 3D modeling here; she also designed crocodiles for it.
- **Dragon Adventures**: Worked on it while at Twin Atlas after Swordburst 3.

## Tools
Studio, Blender, Substance Painter, PureRef, Part to Terrain plugin, Brushtool plugin, TerrainPainter plugin, MeshVox plugin
