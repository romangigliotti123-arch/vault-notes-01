# Taking Action on Copies of Roblox-Created Limiteds

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/taking-action-on-copies-of-roblox-created-limiteds/2226390) | Roblox DevForum | 1,107 views | 2023-03-22

## Summary
Roblox staff post (March 2023) on the policy against copying Roblox-created Limiteds in the UGC Program. Copies violate Community Standards and Marketplace Requirements; violators are removed from the program with all their items taken off-sale. Refunds and pending-payment cancellation remove incentives, and a 30-day escrow forfeits earnings. Roblox promised better pre-publish copy detection.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/taking-action-on-copies-of-roblox-created-limiteds/2226390) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Do not create items overly similar to existing Roblox-created Limiteds; copies violate Community Standards, Terms of Use and Marketplace Requirements.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/taking-action-on-copies-of-roblox-created-limiteds/2226390) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Copying Roblox items can get you removed from the UGC Program, with all items created during membership taken off-sale.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/taking-action-on-copies-of-roblox-created-limiteds/2226390) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Earnings from a sale are held in 30-day escrow; if an item is removed for copying, you forfeit the escrowed earnings.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/taking-action-on-copies-of-roblox-created-limiteds/2226390) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Reported copies that are removed are refunded to buyers and pending payments cancelled, so copied items earn nothing durable.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/taking-action-on-copies-of-roblox-created-limiteds/2226390) Share of Marketplace items that are community-created: **over 90%**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/taking-action-on-copies-of-roblox-created-limiteds/2226390) Commission escrow hold period per purchase: **30 days**
