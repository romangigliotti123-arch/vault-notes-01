# Alternate Publishing Requirements for Roblox Kids and Select

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944) | Roblox DevForum | 4,286 views | 2026-05-11

## Summary
Roblox staff update on the Kids and Select account rollout and new publishing requirements. Covers the 1,000 Robux refundable publishing fee as an alternative to Plus/Premium, highly engaged player thresholds (500 to qualify, under 100 to lose eligibility), playtester and Private audience rules, refund timing, and uncopylocked games.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Regularly update the game and address user feedback; this speeds up Kids/Select evaluation.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Keep the game actively maintained; games with fewer than 100 highly engaged players in 60 days can lose Kids/Select eligibility.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Promote your game to users over 16; engagement from age-checked 16+ users speeds up Kids/Select evaluation.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build an active community and creator presence; games without them are more likely to face safety review.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not rely on Private for playtesters; Private is now limited to Edit-permission creators. Use Limited>Playtesters instead, which is hidden from search and home page.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Publishing fee is refunded 90 days after Kids/Select eligibility, or 90 days after payment if never eligible, unless the game is permanently removed.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944) One-time refundable publishing fee: **1,000 Robux per game** (Alternative to two consecutive months of Plus/Premium for Kids/Select)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944) Highly engaged players to qualify existing games: **500 in last 60 days** (Auto-eligible for Kids/Select without fee during initial rollout)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944) Highly engaged players below which eligibility is lost: **fewer than 100 in 60 days** (Re-qualifying requires 500 highly engaged players in 60 days)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944) Publishing fee refund timing: **90 days after eligibility** (Later update changed from 3 months to 90 days)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944) Free Plus subscription granted: **6 months to 100,000 creators** (Covers publishing fees while subscription is active)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944) Plus/Premium alternative to fee: **2 consecutive months** (Subscription must be held to skip the publishing fee)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944) Highly engaged player definition: **Minimum purchase anywhere on Roblox in last 60 days** (Spend in your own game not required)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944) US 18+ audience growth: **over 40% year over year (Q1 2026)** (Roblox-reported; 18+ users monetise about 50% higher than under-18)
