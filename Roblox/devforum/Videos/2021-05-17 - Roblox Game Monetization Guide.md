# Roblox Game Monetization Guide

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) | Roblox DevForum | 181 views | 2021-05-17

## Summary
Community DevForum guide on Roblox monetization: the three revenue paths (Gamepasses, Dev Products, Premium Payouts), what to sell in each, pricing tiers by player type (entry/gameplay/whale), and how to integrate products by theme, marketing and selling moments. Mostly opinion, with Robux/DevEx figures from the time of writing.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Sell exclusive items, group ranks, or exclusive levels/titles as gamepasses; avoid selling XP/coins or stat boosters as gamepasses since they are one-time purchases.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Optimise gamepasses since only 15 slots exist; donation gamepasses waste a slot unless the game is a showcase or learning project.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Never gate the game behind a paywall; purchases should add to the experience, not block play.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Use Dev Products for consumables and economy currency, and for instant-satisfaction boosts that pay off within the first minute.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Sell currency in uneven tiers (e.g. 100, 2000, 7600, 10200) rather than round numbers, and discount larger bundles; highlight a 'best seller'.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Create recurring Dev Products (e.g. a bail-out purchase) players naturally repeat, since repeat purchases feel reliable.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Avoid overpriced Dev Products that give only brief satisfaction; reserve high Robux prices for currency.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make some content exclusive to Premium subscribers and use the Premium Upsell modal to earn Premium Payouts even from non-spending players; the game must be free to access.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Treat players as First Purchasers, Repeat Purchasers and Whales; the first purchase is the hardest, so offer a cheap entry item first.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) **[[pricing]]** Price entry items around 50-400 Robux, gameplay items 400-6000, and whale items above 6000; increase price per milestone offer.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) **[[pricing]]** Price escalating tiers as: 450, 1000, 2200 by doubling the previous price and adding 100 (or 200 for the last one). Check competitor prices and put slow sellers on limited sale.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make monetisation match the game's theme and show the gamepass benefit with one or two clear highlights; do not overpromise.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) **[[monetisation-other]]** Trigger offers at the moment of need, such as when a player is blocked from a serve or killed in PvP; do not show sales on join.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Keep updating even minor content, since updates bring players back and increase time played, which nudges players toward whale spending.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) Robux marketplace fee: **30%** (Roblox takes 30% off any purchase as marketplace fee)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) DevEx minimum: **100,000 earned Robux** (Minimum earned Robux to use Developer Exchange; also needs Premium and verified email)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) DevEx rate (as stated): **100,000 Robux = 350 USD** (Rate stated in the guide at time of writing)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) Robux purchase rate: **1 USD = 100 Robux (approx.)** (Approximate Robux purchase rate given in guide)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) Gamepass slots: **15** (Maximum gamepass slots per game)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) Group ranks use case: **Clanlabs or custom service** (Service for selling higher group ranks)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) Entry item price range: **50-400 Robux** (Cheap first-purchase item; 400 is highest logical entry price)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) Gameplay item price range: **400-6000 Robux** (Items normal users buy to add to the game)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) Whale item threshold: **above 6000 Robux** (Items aimed at very small majority of players)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) Gun tier pricing example: **450 / 1000 / 2200 Robux** (Doubling previous price plus 100, last tier plus 200)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) Revenue concentration: **75% from 1-5% of players** (Typical share of monetised game revenue from whales (per cited source))
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) Robux repeat purchasing: **95% of Robux buyers buy more than once** (Cited from 2018 RDC talk)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207) Example bail-out revenue: **2 million sales could exceed 40,000 USD** (Liberty County bail-out Dev Product example, author's estimate)

## Games
- **Liberty County**: Recurring bail-out Dev Product for ~15 Robux is a repeat-purchase pattern because players are often arrested.
- **Star Wars themed gamepass store (unnamed in guide)**: Gamepasses that all follow one theme and format look cohesive and support the game's identity.
