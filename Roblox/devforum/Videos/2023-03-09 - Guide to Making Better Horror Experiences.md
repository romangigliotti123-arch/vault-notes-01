# Guide to Making Better Horror Experiences

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) | Roblox DevForum | 120 views | 2023-03-09

## Summary
Web post by FROGMIRE (TEARAPARTJAMES) on making better Roblox horror games across story, visuals, lighting, audio, gameplay (uncertainty, pacing, enemy design, jumpscares) and quality-of-life features. Mostly opinion and design principles, few metrics.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Let gameplay carry the game over story; learn pacing, plot and characters before writing one.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Keep model quality consistent: a low-detail dagger next to a high-detail sword breaks immersion.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Use environmental storytelling (notes, corpses, decals, enemy placement) to convey backstory without dialogue.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Build tension with buildup and release; give players recovery time, e.g. safe rooms.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Break your own established rules late in the game to create unease, since uncertainty scares more than known threats.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) **[[sound-and-music]]** Invest in subtle ambient sound (creaks, humming, pipes, breathing, heartbeat); Roblox games are often lacking here.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) **[[sound-and-music]]** Custom soundtracks help identity, but Roblox APM music or free music is an acceptable substitute.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Treat horror as a theme or style rather than a genre, and design mechanics around it.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Avoid excessive darkness where it doesn't belong; use lighting to guide players or deliberately mislead them.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Avoid DepthOfField and Blur post-processing, which the author finds detrimental.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) **[[physics-pathfinding-and-npc-ai]]** Give enemies behaviour patterns beyond wandering; avoid enemies that look like standard Roblox avatars.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) **[[combat-and-gameplay-systems]]** Avoid instant-kill enemies unless the player has tools to prevent it; otherwise let them hurt the player so near-misses are felt.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Use jumpscares sparingly; make them expected but not predictable, and avoid repeating the same jumpscare every time.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Add quality-of-life options: progress saving, subtitles, objective/puzzle hints, skippable cutscenes, brightness sliders, toggleable crouch/sprint.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/guide-to-making-better-horror-experiences/2212571) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Provide objective and puzzle hints so new players are not stuck.

## Games
- **BUNKER**: Strong environmental storytelling: empty bunker with no corpses or blood keeps the mystery of where people went.
- **Amnesia: The Bunker**: Breaks established rules late in the game (monster hole appears in safe room) and gives tools against instant-kill enemies.
- **Total Miner: Forge**: Spider enemy with a loud screaming sound was memorably frightening.
