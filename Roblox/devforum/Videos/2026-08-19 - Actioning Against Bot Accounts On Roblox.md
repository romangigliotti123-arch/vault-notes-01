# Actioning Against Bot Accounts On Roblox

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/actioning-against-bot-accounts-on-roblox/4820656) | Roblox DevForum | 548 views | 2026-08-19

## Summary
Roblox staff announcement on a platform-wide enforcement wave against bot accounts. Explains expected drops in visits, concurrent players, follows, friend requests, votes and group joins; RFY and Discovery ranking unaffected; no creator action required; metrics may fluctuate over coming weeks.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/actioning-against-bot-accounts-on-roblox/4820656) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Expect Creator Hub > Analytics visits and players to drop for affected periods as bots are removed; this is an accuracy correction, not a real engagement loss.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/actioning-against-bot-accounts-on-roblox/4820656) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Judge engagement against real-user trends over weeks, since concurrent player counts may fluctuate for several weeks during the cleanup.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/actioning-against-bot-accounts-on-roblox/4820656) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Do not expect Discovery ranking changes from this enforcement; Roblox says known bot engagement is excluded from Recommended For You.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/actioning-against-bot-accounts-on-roblox/4820656) **[[community-and-discord]]** Expect follows, pending friend requests, votes and group joins from bot accounts to be rolled back; do not treat the drop as a community loss.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/actioning-against-bot-accounts-on-roblox/4820656) Example visit drop from bot removal: **100 visits with 20 bots: visits drop to ~90, then ~80 as more removed** (Roblox illustrative example; games with high bot share see bigger shifts)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/actioning-against-bot-accounts-on-roblox/4820656) Enforcement timeline: **Bot removals continue over coming weeks** (Fluctuations expected during this period; ongoing legitimacy checks)
