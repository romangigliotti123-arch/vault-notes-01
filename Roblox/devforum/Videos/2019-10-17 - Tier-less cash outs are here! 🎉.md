# Tier-less cash outs are here! 🎉

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tier-less-cash-outs-are-here-%F0%9F%8E%89/370137) | Roblox DevForum | 1,361 views | 2019-10-17

## Summary
Roblox Developer Relations announces tier-less DevEx cash outs: any amount above a 100,000 Robux minimum, still one cash out per month, no cap, and a simplified DevEx submission form requiring only name, email and password verification. The change is live.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tier-less-cash-outs-are-here-%F0%9F%8E%89/370137) **[[business-and-payouts]]** DevEx cash outs are no longer tiered: you can cash out any exact amount you have, as long as it is at least 100,000 earned Robux.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tier-less-cash-outs-are-here-%F0%9F%8E%89/370137) **[[business-and-payouts]]** You are still limited to one DevEx cash out per month, so plan the amount rather than waiting for a tier threshold.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tier-less-cash-outs-are-here-%F0%9F%8E%89/370137) **[[business-and-payouts]]** The DevEx submission form now only asks for name, email and password verification; address and phone number are no longer required.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tier-less-cash-outs-are-here-%F0%9F%8E%89/370137) Minimum DevEx cash out: **100,000 earned Robux** (Minimum earned Robux required to cash out; no longer tier-based)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tier-less-cash-outs-are-here-%F0%9F%8E%89/370137) Maximum DevEx cash out: **No cap (above R$600,000,000 allowed)** (Cash out amounts are unrestricted at any size)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tier-less-cash-outs-are-here-%F0%9F%8E%89/370137) Cash out frequency: **One per month** (Limit unchanged by the tier-less update)
