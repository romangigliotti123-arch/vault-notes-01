# Recommended For You Algorithm Improvements That Better Value Long-Term Retention

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575) | Roblox DevForum | 430 views | 2026-06-15

## Summary
Roblox announcement that Recommended For You algorithm signals expand from 7 to 28 days (Day 1, Day 2–7, Day 8–28 windows), QPTR removed in favor of PTR and first play bounce rate, and relative signal importance is shared in Creator Analytics > Acquisition > Home Recommendations. Benchmarks do not affect the algorithm; thumbnail personalization will move to PTR.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Build for retention over 28 days: the Home recommendation algorithm now measures Day 1, Day 2–7, and Day 8–28 engagement, so games that bring players back are rewarded.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check Creator Analytics > Acquisition > Home Recommendations to see the updated signals and their relative importance, and find where your game is weak.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Watch PTR and first play bounce rate: the algorithm now measures what happens right after a click, so a high bounce rate on first play hurts distribution.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use benchmark games only as a comparison point; they do not affect the recommendation algorithm, but low signals against them show opportunities.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Expect distribution fluctuations after the rollout; if the game keeps players returning, the update is designed to reward that more clearly.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Expect distribution to drop if competing games engage players significantly better, even when your own signals stay steady.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Thumbnail personalization is being tested with PTR instead of QPTR, so improve click-through to play-through quality.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Expect weekly seasonality: Home impressions usually peak on Saturday and dip on weekdays; summer, back-to-school and holiday periods also shift totals.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575) Signal lookback window: **7 days changed to 28 days** (Recommended For You signals now measured over 28 days)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575) Retention windows measured: **Day 1, Day 2–7, Day 8–28** (Three windows used by the algorithm for longer-term retention)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575) Signals listed: **playtime, play days, qualified play sessions, intentional co-play days, spend days, Robux spent** (Familiar signals now measured across the three windows)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575) Removed signal: **QPTR (qualified play-through rate) replaced by PTR and first play bounce rate** (Measures what happens right after a player clicks a game)
