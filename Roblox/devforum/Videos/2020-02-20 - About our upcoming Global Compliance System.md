# About our upcoming Global Compliance System

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/about-our-upcoming-global-compliance-system/461447) | Roblox DevForum | 1,949 views | 2020-02-20

## Summary
Roblox staff announcement (Feb 2020) of the upcoming Global Compliance System, which aims to let developers control where games are available by country and platform. It starts with China, with the GetPolicyInfoForPlayerAsync API documented and a compliance guide for major markets promised. Developers keep the choice of which countries publish their games.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/about-our-upcoming-global-compliance-system/461447) **[[localization-and-international]]** Choose which countries your game is published in; Roblox says developers keep this choice and local compliance is enforced only in those markets.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/about-our-upcoming-global-compliance-system/461447) **[[localization-and-international]]** Before entering a new market, use the content checklist of requirements to confirm your game's content fits that country's criteria.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/about-our-upcoming-global-compliance-system/461447) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Use GetPolicyInfoForPlayerAsync to check a player's region policy info so game features can comply with local rules.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/about-our-upcoming-global-compliance-system/461447) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Review local regulations on privacy, safety and content before launching in a market with different rules than your home market.
