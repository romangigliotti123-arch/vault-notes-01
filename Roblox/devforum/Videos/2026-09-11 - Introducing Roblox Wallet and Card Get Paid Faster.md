# Introducing Roblox Wallet and Card: Get Paid Faster

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-roblox-wallet-and-card-get-paid-faster/4865505) | Roblox DevForum | 855 views | 2026-09-11

## Summary
Official Roblox announcement of Roblox Wallet, a Creator Hub financial account. US-based 18+ sole proprietors can opt in from December 2026 to receive USD payouts every business day instead of DevEx monthly cooldowns. Wallet Robux repurchase at $0.0038/Robux. DevEx ends mid-2027 in US; Roblox Card arrives 2027.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-roblox-wallet-and-card-get-paid-faster/4865505) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Once Wallet is open, future Creator Payments arrive in USD every business day after hold periods, instead of waiting on the monthly DevEx cooldown.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-roblox-wallet-and-card-get-paid-faster/4865505) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Enable the strongest account security settings; 2FA is required before any earnings can be paid out to Wallet.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-roblox-wallet-and-card-get-paid-faster/4865505) **[[monetisation-other]]** Wallet earnings can be used to repurchase Robux at $0.0038/Robux, so you can keep testing and publishing without buying back at retail Robux rates.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-roblox-wallet-and-card-get-paid-faster/4865505) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Use the Wallet transaction logs, filters and export to reconcile sales with accounting; per-purchase records include buyer details for player support.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-roblox-wallet-and-card-get-paid-faster/4865505) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Wallet funds can be spent directly on buying ads, in addition to Robux.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-roblox-wallet-and-card-get-paid-faster/4865505) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Earnings are subject to a hold period of up to 30 days that varies by transaction; plan cash flow around it.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-roblox-wallet-and-card-get-paid-faster/4865505) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Keep your account in good standing with the Terms of Use and Community Standards; violations can suspend earnings, extend holds, or forfeit earnings.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-roblox-wallet-and-card-get-paid-faster/4865505) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Creators who will move to Wallet should check their tax documentation in the central Creator Hub tax pages, where Wallet tax forms also appear.
