# An Update on Automated Action Against Modified Clients

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/an-update-on-automated-action-against-modified-clients/3640609) | Roblox DevForum | 2,716 views | 2025-05-06

## Summary
Roblox staff announce automated action against modified clients (third-party or runtime-modified Roblox binaries) starting May 2025, with accounts subject to warning/termination. Edit-permission creators are exempt when testing anti-cheat in their own place as of May 30, 2025. FAQ covers Linux, Bloxstrap, multi-client tools, and OAuth tools.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/an-update-on-automated-action-against-modified-clients/3640609) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Download Roblox only from official sources (roblox.com, App Store, Google Play). Don't run software claiming to modify Roblox, or risk automated account action.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/an-update-on-automated-action-against-modified-clients/3640609) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Creators with edit permissions can test anti-cheat against exploits in their own place without being actioned (effective May 30, 2025). Exploits in others' experiences still trigger consequences.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/an-update-on-automated-action-against-modified-clients/3640609) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Server Authority (early access application) is Roblox's planned tool to reduce the exploit surface in experiences.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/an-update-on-automated-action-against-modified-clients/3640609) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Expect fewer exploiters in your game over time as automated detection rolls out; Roblox says exploit use should decrease for experiences that get exploited.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/an-update-on-automated-action-against-modified-clients/3640609) Rollout date of automated modified-client action: **May 8, 2025 (full-time, all users and experiences)**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/an-update-on-automated-action-against-modified-clients/3640609) Exemption for creators testing in own place: **Effective May 30, 2025; edit-permission holders only, own place only**

## Games
- **The Hunt: Mega Edition**: Used Roblox's modified-client detection to ban exploiting accounts, proving the system's effectiveness before full rollout.
