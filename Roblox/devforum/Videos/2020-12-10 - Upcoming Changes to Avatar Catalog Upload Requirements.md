# Upcoming Changes to Avatar Catalog Upload Requirements

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/upcoming-changes-to-avatar-catalog-upload-requirements/915265) | Roblox DevForum | 11,875 views | 2020-12-10

## Summary
Announcement (Dec 2020) that Avatar Shop clothing uploads will drop the Premium requirement in favor of a Robux upload fee. Jan 2021 partial rollout and Mar 2021 full rollout details: 2D items 50 R$ then 10 R$, creators earn 70% of sales, existing items exempt.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/upcoming-changes-to-avatar-catalog-upload-requirements/915265) **[[studio-business-funding-and-publishing]]** Test clothing designs in Studio before uploading to the Avatar Shop, since upload now costs Robux per item.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/upcoming-changes-to-avatar-catalog-upload-requirements/915265) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Upload fees now apply to clothing; plan item count and pricing knowing each new shirt or pants costs Robux to create.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/upcoming-changes-to-avatar-catalog-upload-requirements/915265) Creator earnings share: **70% of item sales for all users, regardless of Premium (Jan 2021)** (Applies to items created after the fee feature rollout)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/upcoming-changes-to-avatar-catalog-upload-requirements/915265) Upload fee (initial, partial rollout): **50 R$ per 2D shirt or pants; t-shirts free to upload, 50 R$ one-time fee at first sale** (Jan 20, 2021 partial rollout)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/upcoming-changes-to-avatar-catalog-upload-requirements/915265) Upload fee (full rollout): **10 R$ to create shirts/pants or to sell t-shirts** (March 25, 2021 full rollout for all users)
