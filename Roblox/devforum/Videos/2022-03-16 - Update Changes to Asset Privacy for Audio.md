# [Update] Changes to Asset Privacy for Audio

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-changes-to-asset-privacy-for-audio/1715717) | Roblox DevForum | 6,628 views | 2022-03-16

## Summary
Roblox DevForum update (March 2022) on audio asset privacy changes. Music audio assets becoming private by a March 22 deadline; SFX work to keep many public; Pro Sound Effects SFX library added; new per-experience audio permissions; upload limits (10/month default, 100 with ID verification).

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-changes-to-asset-privacy-for-audio/1715717) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Use the Audio Discovery plugin in Studio to review ownership of all audio assets in your experience before the March 22 deadline.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-changes-to-asset-privacy-for-audio/1715717) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Audio uploaded under personal accounts can be kept working by granting experience permissions in the asset's Configure Item page instead of re-uploading to a Group.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-changes-to-asset-privacy-for-audio/1715717) **[[sound-and-music]]** Replace privately-restricted music with public or Pro Sound Effects library assets; search '[name] (SFX)' to find them.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-changes-to-asset-privacy-for-audio/1715717) **[[sound-and-music]]** Plan audio uploads around monthly limits: 10 uploads per month by default, 100 with ID verification.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-changes-to-asset-privacy-for-audio/1715717) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Expect abrupt platform policy changes; audit third-party dependencies (audio) early since deadlines may be short.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-changes-to-asset-privacy-for-audio/1715717) Audio upload limit (default): **10 files per month**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-changes-to-asset-privacy-for-audio/1715717) Audio upload limit (ID verified): **100 files per month**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-changes-to-asset-privacy-for-audio/1715717) Pro Sound Effects SFX added to catalog: **over 100,000**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-changes-to-asset-privacy-for-audio/1715717) Music privacy deadline: **March 22 (2022)**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-changes-to-asset-privacy-for-audio/1715717) Length threshold for music being made private: **longer than 6 seconds**

## Tools
Audio Discovery plugin, Configure Item page (audio asset permissions), Pro Sound Effects library
