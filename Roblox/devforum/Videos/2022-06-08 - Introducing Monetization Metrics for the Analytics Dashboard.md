# Introducing Monetization Metrics for the Analytics Dashboard

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-monetization-metrics-for-the-analytics-dashboard/1826688) | Roblox DevForum | 567 views | 2022-06-08

## Summary
Roblox Creator Services announcement introducing a Monetization subpage in the standard analytics dashboard. Defines KPIs (Daily Revenue, Revenue Source, Paying Users, ARPDAU, ARPPU, Conversion Rate), explains how to read them, breakdowns by age/country/platform, notes on net revenue and latency, deprecates some Developer Stats charts, and lowers the DAU threshold from 500 to 300 for engagement/retention KPIs.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-monetization-metrics-for-the-analytics-dashboard/1826688) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use ARPPU alongside conversion rate: high conversion with low ARPPU means players buy once but don't spend again; focus on post-first-purchase content.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-monetization-metrics-for-the-analytics-dashboard/1826688) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Compare monetization KPIs across Age Group, Country, Language, Platform and OS to find which segments monetize best, then tailor content to them.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-monetization-metrics-for-the-analytics-dashboard/1826688) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Don't rely on monetization KPIs for financial reporting; refunds aren't tracked. Check My Transactions for actual income.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-monetization-metrics-for-the-analytics-dashboard/1826688) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Expect a two-day latency on monetization KPIs due to system limitations.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-monetization-metrics-for-the-analytics-dashboard/1826688) **[[monetisation-other]]** Track Revenue Source breakdown (shop items, game passes, private servers, paid access, affiliate fees) to pick which revenue area to optimise.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-monetization-metrics-for-the-analytics-dashboard/1826688) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Don't focus on monetization below ~10 DAUs; prioritise D1 retention and average playtime by iterating on design and onboarding first.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-monetization-metrics-for-the-analytics-dashboard/1826688) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Design progression and economy so players make a first purchase, since first-purchase conversion drives longer-term paying users.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-monetization-metrics-for-the-analytics-dashboard/1826688) DAU threshold for Engagement and Retention KPIs: **300 (lowered from 500)** (Standard dashboard engagement/retention KPIs require this many DAUs)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-monetization-metrics-for-the-analytics-dashboard/1826688) DAU threshold for monetization KPIs: **10** (Monetization KPIs available from 10 DAUs per FAQ)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-monetization-metrics-for-the-analytics-dashboard/1826688) Monetization KPI latency: **2 days** (Due to system limitations)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-monetization-metrics-for-the-analytics-dashboard/1826688) ARPDAU formula: **Daily revenue / DAU** (Revenue measured in Robux)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-monetization-metrics-for-the-analytics-dashboard/1826688) ARPPU formula: **Daily revenue / Paying users** (Average revenue per paying user)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-monetization-metrics-for-the-analytics-dashboard/1826688) Conversion rate formula: **Paying users / DAU** (Percentage of DAUs who are paying users)
