# Introducing Creator Rewards: Earn More by Growing the Community

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) | Roblox DevForum | 5,092 views | 2025-06-24

## Summary
Roblox official announcement of Creator Rewards, replacing Engagement Based Payouts and Creator Affiliate from July 24, 2025. Daily Engagement Reward pays 5 Robux per Active Spender who plays 10+ minutes across the first three experiences they launch each day. Audience Expansion Reward pays 35% on the first $100 of new/reactivated users' Robux purchases. Payouts held 60 days.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Optimise for fun, repeatable loops that bring users back every day; Roblox says this is the path to maximising Creator Rewards earnings.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build a deep in-experience economy; Creator Rewards only counts the first 10 minutes, and Roblox says top earners will still earn mostly from well-designed loops and microtransactions.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Aim for 10+ minutes of play per Active Spender per day; time can be split across sessions, and extra time beyond 10 minutes earns nothing more.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Get launched within the first three experiences a user opens each day; only those first three qualify for the Daily Engagement reward.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Use Share Links to drive traffic; Creator Analytics breaks down Share Link visits against resulting engagement and earnings.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Target new or returning users (inactive 60+ days) reached by direct link or name search; they must play 10+ minutes to qualify for Audience Expansion.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not encourage existing players to create alt accounts, and avoid bots, automation, or manipulated teleports; these lead to forfeited rewards or account termination.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Register with Tipalti and keep account in good standing to be eligible for Audience Expansion Rewards; Robux convert to cash via DevEx.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Expect a 60-day holding period before earned Robux reach your balance; track estimates daily in Creator Hub.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Keep average DAU at 100 or more during the 60-day holding period to earn Audience Expansion Rewards.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track Daily Engagement, Audience Expansion and Share Link Analytics in Creator Analytics to see which actions drive earnings.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not rely on long, passive or AFK sessions; under EBP these were rewarded but Creator Rewards removes that advantage, so experiences with unusually long sessions may earn less.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) Daily Engagement Reward: **5 Robux per qualifying Active Spender per day, first 3 experiences, 10+ min** (At launch; may change in future)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) Audience Expansion Reward: **35% revenue share on first $100 of new/returning user Robux purchases** (During users' first two months; users inactive 60+ days)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) Active Spender threshold: **$9.99 spent in last 60 days (Robux, Premium, subscriptions)** (Excludes users who signed up or reactivated in same window)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) Daily Engagement time threshold: **10 minutes total per user per day** (Can span multiple sessions)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) Average DAU requirement: **100 average DAU over 60-day holding period** (Needed for Audience Expansion Rewards)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) Holding period: **60 days** (Before earned Robux credited to balance)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) Creator earnings 2024 DevEx: **over $922 million** (Roblox-stated figure)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628) Creator earnings Q1 2025 DevEx: **$281.6 million, up 39% year over year** (Roblox-stated figure)
