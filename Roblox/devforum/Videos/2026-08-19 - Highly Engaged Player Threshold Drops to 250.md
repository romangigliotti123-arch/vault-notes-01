# Highly Engaged Player Threshold Drops to 250

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/highly-engaged-player-threshold-drops-to-250/4820164) | Roblox DevForum | 746 views | 2026-08-19

## Summary
Roblox staff announce the Highly Engaged Player (HEP) entry threshold for Roblox Kids and Select catalog drops from 500 to 250, effective Aug 19 (2026). Maintenance bar remains 25 HEP (set July 16). Track progress on Audience Reach tab in Creator Hub.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/highly-engaged-player-threshold-drops-to-250/4820164) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Check your Highly Engaged Player count on the Audience Reach tab in Creator Hub against the new 250 entry threshold to see if you qualify for Roblox Kids and Select.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/highly-engaged-player-threshold-drops-to-250/4820164) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Content safety bar is unchanged; the lower threshold does not relax moderation standards for Kids and Select catalog entry.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/highly-engaged-player-threshold-drops-to-250/4820164) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Qualified games only stay in the catalog with 25 HEP maintenance; entry needs 250 HEP, so aim to exceed entry bar and keep a buffer above 25.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/highly-engaged-player-threshold-drops-to-250/4820164) Highly Engaged Player entry threshold (Kids and Select): **250 (down from 500, Aug 19)**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/highly-engaged-player-threshold-drops-to-250/4820164) Highly Engaged Player maintenance threshold: **25 (lowered from 100 on July 16)**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/highly-engaged-player-threshold-drops-to-250/4820164) New games entering catalog after July 6 HEP definition relaxation: **+33%**
