# Create Experiences for People 17 and Older on Roblox

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) | Roblox DevForum | 3,078 views | 2023-06-20

## Summary
Roblox official announcement on opening 17+ experiences: age verification (government ID + selfie) required for owners and creators, questionnaire sets Ages 17+ recommendation, no 17+ assets yet (combine under-17 assets), simulated gambling banned from Sept 18 2023, ads unavailable for 17+ experiences, unavailable in Korea, Saudi Arabia, Turkey.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** 17+ experiences cannot use Immersive Ads, User Ads or Sponsored Experiences at launch; plan monetisation without ad-driven acquisition for 17+ titles.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Complete the Experience Guidelines questionnaire honestly, basing answers on the most mature content users could encounter; unanswered questionnaires default to 13+ and may not include 17+ content.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Ensure descriptions, thumbnails and videos are all-ages appropriate even for 17+ experiences.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Both experience owner and questionnaire filler must be verified 17+ before adding 17+ content; collaborators must also be age-verified 17+.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** 17+ assets are not supported yet; depict mature content by combining under-17 assets, e.g. weapons with animations and audio for violence, multiple realistic blood assets for gore, red cups in animations for alcohol.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Do not use prohibited assets: alcohol assets explicitly labeled beer/wine/alcohol, graphic violence, severe injury or real-world events.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Simulated gambling (virtual chips, simulated betting) is banned; unplayable casino content, bingo, arcade-style luck games and paid random item generators/lootboxes remain allowed.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Sexual content, nudity, and depictions of drugs or tobacco remain prohibited for all age recommendations; strong language was not permitted at launch.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Converting an existing experience to 17+ removes all Team Create members; only owner can playtest via Play button on public page, verified collaborators playtest in Studio.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** 17+ experiences are recommended only to eligible verified 17+ users, so audience is limited to verified users.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) Share of DAU aged 17+: **38% in 2022** (people over 17 made up 38% of daily active users in 2022)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) Ad audience age floor: **over 13** (only people over 13 see ads)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) Simulated gambling ban deadline: **September 18, 2023 (90 days)** (experiences with simulated gambling must comply or face moderation)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) Unverified questionnaire default: **13+** (experiences without questionnaire treated as 13+)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-experiences-for-people-17-and-older-on-roblox/2432184) Restricted regions: **South Korea, Saudi Arabia, Turkey** (17+ experiences unavailable in these countries)

## Tools
Experience Guidelines questionnaire, Age Verification (government ID + selfie)
