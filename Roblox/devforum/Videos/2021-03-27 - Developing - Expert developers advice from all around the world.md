# Developing - Expert developers advice from all around the world

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) | Roblox DevForum | 187 views | 2021-03-27

## Summary
DevForum compilation by CrashFacts of advice from developers: starting-out tips, motivation, recommended plugins, admin systems, commission pricing by hourly rate plus premium, Toolbox malware risks and checks, team communication, Studio autosave/recovery, paid-access trade-offs, and coping with low self-esteem.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Start with simple, original projects slightly above your skill level; avoid making a complex game as your first creation.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't use many free Toolbox models; if you do, check them for malicious scripts first and prefer Roblox-approved or DevForum Resources models.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Search scripts for fenv, RoSync, require, and loadstring via View > Find Results to spot common virus code; not every hit is malicious.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Enable Team Create so Studio saves every change; otherwise recover from File > Advanced > Open Autosaves.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Use plugins such as Developer Tools, Auto Anchor, AutoScale Lite, and Stravant's Model Reflect and ResizeAlign to speed building and GUI scaling.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Use a proven admin system (Cmdr, HD Admin, Adonis, etc.) unless you want full control; scripting your own gives a unique, customizable result.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[pricing]]** Price commissions by hourly rate plus a complexity premium, e.g. $15/hr x 6 hrs with a 30% premium for moderate detail.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[team-and-budget]]** Keep team communication open, make tasks clear so nobody is idle, and avoid destructive leadership that pushes developers out.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[monetisation-other]]** Paid access can add revenue, filter scam bots, and build hype during alpha/beta, but shrinks the playerbase and blocks Premium payouts; charge only if the game is worth it.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Compare current builds to your older builds rather than to other developers to measure real improvement.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[community-and-discord]]** Get regular feedback by posting work on Twitter, YouTube, Discord, or the DevForum, and act on genuine suggestions.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Start with a small goal and take smaller steps; if a game is beyond your current time or skill, build something smaller first.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't optimise for performance or good practices too early as a beginner; premature optimisation is unnecessary at the start.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Don't start with a front-page-style game; simple originals avoid the demotivation of comparing against multi-year projects.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Get inspired by others' work but do not copy games; put your own spin on ideas.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developing-expert-developers-advice-from-all-around-the-world/1134437) **[[community-and-discord]]** Get involved in DevForum discussions and share work on social media to gain feedback and visibility.
