# Roblox Premium is here!

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-premium-is-here/355721) | Roblox DevForum | 2,316 views | 2019-09-23

## Summary
Developer Relations announcement (Sept 2019) on Roblox Premium rollout: all Premium tiers get identical perks and DevEx eligibility, Premium users can join/create 100 groups, multi-month memberships convert to monthly renewing, Builder's Club lifetime members get equivalent lifetime Premium, and Robux allowances are granted upfront at purchase/renewal. Post is marked outdated.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-premium-is-here/355721) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Any Premium tier (450, 1000, or 2200) qualifies you for the Developer Exchange program; you do not need Premium 2200 to cash out Robux.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-premium-is-here/355721) **[[studio-business-funding-and-publishing]]** Premium users can create and be in up to 100 groups, which lets developers run many separate development studios.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-premium-is-here/355721) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Plan for membership changes: multi-month memberships convert to monthly renewing Premium at renewal, and tier changes are only possible when the membership expires.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-premium-is-here/355721) Premium tiers: **450, 1000, 2200 Robux monthly allowance; identical perks across tiers** (Premium allowance granted upfront at purchase or renewal)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-premium-is-here/355721) Group limit for Premium users: **100 groups** (Applies to all Premium levels)
