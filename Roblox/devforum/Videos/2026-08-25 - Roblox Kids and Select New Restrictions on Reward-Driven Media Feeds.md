# Roblox Kids and Select: New Restrictions on Reward-Driven Media Feeds

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-new-restrictions-on-reward-driven-media-feeds/4829188) | Roblox DevForum | 1,257 views | 2026-08-25

## Summary
Roblox policy update: games in Roblox Kids and Select that combine a media feed, autoplay/loop/infinite scroll/autoscroll, and rewards (or implied rewards) for continued viewing are no longer allowed. Non-compliant games lose Kids and Select access unless redesigned. Rewarded Video Ads are not affected.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-new-restrictions-on-reward-driven-media-feeds/4829188) **[[monetisation-other]]** Rewarded Video Ads are exempt: player must actively start the ad and Roblox rate-limits views. Use these instead of feed-style reward loops.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-new-restrictions-on-reward-driven-media-feeds/4829188) **[[ab-testing-and-experiments]]** Redesign mechanics rather than delete them; redesigned games are re-reviewed for Kids and Select acceptance without resetting Engaged Player criteria.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-new-restrictions-on-reward-driven-media-feeds/4829188) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Review your game in Creator Hub > Creations for media feed plus reward mechanics. Non-compliant games are removed from Kids and Select until changed.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-new-restrictions-on-reward-driven-media-feeds/4829188) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Test whether the average player believes watching the feed is required for rewards; if so, the policy applies even without explicit wording.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-new-restrictions-on-reward-driven-media-feeds/4829188) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not assume a feed is fine if rewards are only implied; implied requirement of ongoing viewing counts as a violation.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-new-restrictions-on-reward-driven-media-feeds/4829188) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Build in natural stopping points rather than endless loops, as Roblox considers continuous feeds without breaks inappropriate for younger users.
