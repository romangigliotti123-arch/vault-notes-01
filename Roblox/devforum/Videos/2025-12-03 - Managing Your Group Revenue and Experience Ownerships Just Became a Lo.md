# Managing Your Group Revenue and Experience Ownerships Just Became a Lot Easier

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/managing-your-group-revenue-and-experience-ownerships-just-became-a-lot-easier/4114133) | Roblox DevForum | 263 views | 2025-12-03

## Summary
Creator Hub update (Dec 2025) on group revenue and ownership tools: CSV bulk collaborator payouts (max 20), recent-payee warnings, new 'Monetize Experience' permission for monetization features, expanded Activity Tracker events, and transferring experiences between groups. Mostly feature announcement.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/managing-your-group-revenue-and-experience-ownerships-just-became-a-lot-easier/4114133) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Use CSV upload (userId + Robux amount, max 20 collaborators per batch) for one-time group payouts to reduce manual entry errors.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/managing-your-group-revenue-and-experience-ownerships-just-became-a-lot-easier/4114133) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Check the recent-payee history and first-time payee warning before confirming payouts; Robux payouts cannot be undone once sent.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/managing-your-group-revenue-and-experience-ownerships-just-became-a-lot-easier/4114133) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Grant the 'Monetize Experience' permission only to roles that need to manage gamepasses, subscriptions and other monetization; it is separate from 'Edit and Publish Experience'.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/managing-your-group-revenue-and-experience-ownerships-just-became-a-lot-easier/4114133) **[[team-and-budget]]** Use the Activity Tracker (now incl. payouts configured, spend group funds, asset/place moderated, member joined/left, ad campaign created) to monitor collaborator actions.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/managing-your-group-revenue-and-experience-ownerships-just-became-a-lot-easier/4114133) CSV payout collaborator limit: **20 collaborators per batch** (Limit per CSV one-time payout; Roblox says it hopes to increase it)
