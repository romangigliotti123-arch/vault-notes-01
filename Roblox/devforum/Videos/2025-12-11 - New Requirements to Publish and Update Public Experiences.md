# New Requirements to Publish and Update Public Experiences

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-requirements-to-publish-and-update-public-experiences/4143953) | Roblox DevForum | 9,078 views | 2025-12-11

## Summary
Roblox staff announcement of new eligibility rules to publish or update public experiences, effective Dec 17, 2025. Creators need one of: completed ID Verification, a real-currency or gift-card purchase since Jan 1, 2025, edit access to an experience with 100+ playtime hours (Nov 9 to Dec 10, 2025), or a completed DevEx request in the 12 months ending Dec 10, 2025. Covers group, collaborator, Open Cloud and private-experience FAQs.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-requirements-to-publish-and-update-public-experiences/4143953) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Before publishing publicly, confirm eligibility via ID Verification, a real-currency or gift-card purchase since Jan 1, 2025, or a completed DevEx request in the 12 months ending Dec 10, 2025.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-requirements-to-publish-and-update-public-experiences/4143953) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Experiences also need a content maturity label to be public and playable; complete the Content & Maturity questionnaire.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-requirements-to-publish-and-update-public-experiences/4143953) **[[team-and-budget]]** Only the person publishing must meet eligibility; collaborators with edit access can keep editing public experiences without meeting criteria.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-requirements-to-publish-and-update-public-experiences/4143953) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build and playtest privately with teammates; private experiences have no eligibility requirement for playtesters or editors.
