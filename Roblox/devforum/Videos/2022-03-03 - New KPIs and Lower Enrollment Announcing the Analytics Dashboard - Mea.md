# [New KPIs and Lower Enrollment] Announcing the Analytics Dashboard - Measure, Track, & Make Data-Driven Decisions

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) | Roblox DevForum | 391 views | 2022-03-03

## Summary
Roblox DevForum announcement of the Analytics Dashboard: standard KPIs (engagement and retention) for experiences meeting enrollment thresholds, plus May 2022 update adding MAU, Average Session Time, W1 Retention, lowered threshold to 500 DAU/500 hours, and a group permission for viewing analytics.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use Day 1 and Day 7 retention against benchmarks before spending money promoting a newly launched experience; fix onboarding and retention first.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use average session time together with D1 retention as an early indicator of long-term retention.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Calculate DAU/MAU ratio to measure how often an average player returns within a month; a high ratio means frequent engagement.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** If building a casual on-and-off experience, track W1 retention; a low or declining W1 suggests first-time player experience or early progression problems.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Compare KPIs against the benchmark shown for the next tier up to set realistic improvement goals; benchmarks update quarterly.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Segment engagement KPIs by age group, country, language, platform, and OS to find where to iterate content.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Export chart data as CSV for offline analysis beyond the dashboard.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Restrict the group permission 'View group experience analytics' to trusted roles, since KPIs like revenue are sensitive; it is independent of game edit permission.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Measure average session time per session by using total playtime divided by number of sessions; teleports between places count as one session.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) Initial enrollment threshold: **600+ DAU and 600 play hours in past 7 days** (Original eligibility for standard dashboard, March 2022)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) Lowered enrollment threshold: **500 DAU and 500 hours total playtime consecutively over past 7 days** (Updated May 2022 eligibility)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) Private beta testers: **5000+ experiences** (Dashboard tested before March 2022 rollout)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) Benchmark tiers: **6 tiers, benchmarks updated quarterly** (Benchmarks shown for the tier above the experience)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) Default date range: **28 days** (Changed from 7 days in May 2022 update)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) MAU definition: **Unique players joining at least once in rolling past 30 days** (Updated daily; calendar-month MAU read on 1st of month)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-kpis-and-lower-enrollment-announcing-the-analytics-dashboard-measure-track-make-data-driven-decisions/1688794) W1 retention definition: **% of Week0 (D0-D6) new players who played in Week1 (D7-D13)** (New KPI May 2022)
