# UGC Catalog is Now Live!

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ugc-catalog-is-now-live/331405) | Roblox DevForum | 10,946 views | 2019-08-15

## Summary
Roblox DevRel announcement of the UGC Catalog live rollout (Aug 2019). Only a select group of developers may upload UGC Hat Accessories; access expands in waves. Covers revenue share, price floors (Hats 120 R$), copying protection, Limited restrictions, and future Groups/other accessory support.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ugc-catalog-is-now-live/331405) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** UGC Catalog uploads were initially restricted to a small invited group of developers with 3D modeling experience; the program was expanding in waves through 2019-2020.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ugc-catalog-is-now-live/331405) **[[pricing]]** Initial UGC Hat price floor was 120 R$; floors and ceilings were set from price ranges Roblox saw the most success with and could change.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ugc-catalog-is-now-live/331405) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Creators earn a revenue cut on UGC Accessories, but Roblox fees and revenue share were expected to be adjusted over time.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ugc-catalog-is-now-live/331405) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Creators cannot release Limited, Limited Unique, or resellable UGC items; UGC items cannot currently be free, so giving them as game rewards is not yet supported.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ugc-catalog-is-now-live/331405) UGC Hat price floor: **120 R$** (Initial floor for Hats at UGC Catalog launch; ceiling not stated)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ugc-catalog-is-now-live/331405) Developer access rollout: **Waves through rest of 2019 and 2020** (Incremental creator access expansion, initial group small)
