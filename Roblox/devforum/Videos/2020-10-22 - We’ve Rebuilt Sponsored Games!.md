# We’ve Rebuilt Sponsored Games!

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/we%E2%80%99ve-rebuilt-sponsored-games/832597) | Roblox DevForum | 2,262 views | 2020-10-22

## Summary
Roblox DevForum announcement (Oct 2020) of rebuilt Sponsored Games: redesigned ad creation flow with a Sponsor button on Creations, gender/basic age targeting, ads running up to 28 days, detailed performance reporting, and attributed play stats. Includes FAQ on access, bidding, attribution windows, and refunds for stopped ads.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/we%E2%80%99ve-rebuilt-sponsored-games/832597) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use the new scheduled ads: one ad can run up to 28 days, so you no longer need to set a bid for every day it runs.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/we%E2%80%99ve-rebuilt-sponsored-games/832597) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use the Sponsor button next to your game on the Creations page to create new sponsored ads in the rebuilt system.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/we%E2%80%99ve-rebuilt-sponsored-games/832597) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use gender and basic age targeting to aim ads at the audience most likely to play your game.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/we%E2%80%99ve-rebuilt-sponsored-games/832597) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check attributed play stats in the new reporting to measure how many plays each ad drives; attribution is 1-day view, 28-day click-through.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/we%E2%80%99ve-rebuilt-sponsored-games/832597) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** If you stop an ad early, Roblox refunds each full day the ad did not run, so stop promptly to recover unused Robux.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/we%E2%80%99ve-rebuilt-sponsored-games/832597) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Export or save old sponsored reports before the old reporting is deprecated (after 12/31/2020, later the week of Jan 13th).

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/we%E2%80%99ve-rebuilt-sponsored-games/832597) Maximum ad run length: **28 days** (New Sponsored Games schedule; no daily bid needed)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/we%E2%80%99ve-rebuilt-sponsored-games/832597) Attribution window: **1-day view, 28-day click-through** (Standard industry convention for attributed plays)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/we%E2%80%99ve-rebuilt-sponsored-games/832597) Max attributed plays per ad per user: **1** (Repeat plays from same clicker not counted again for that ad)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/we%E2%80%99ve-rebuilt-sponsored-games/832597) Refund on early stop: **Full refund for each complete unused day** (Example: 3-day ad stopped on day 1 refunds 2 days)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/we%E2%80%99ve-rebuilt-sponsored-games/832597) Old reporting deprecation: **On or after 12/31/2020** (Later moved up to week of Jan 13th)
