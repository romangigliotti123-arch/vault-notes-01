# Updates and Changes to the UGC Catalog Application

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/updates-and-changes-to-the-ugc-catalog-application/1974990) | Roblox DevForum | 7,166 views | 2022-09-07

## Summary
Roblox staff announcement that the UGC Catalog Application is now permanently open year-round, with guidelines on who fits the program (community conduct, passion, compliance with Terms of Use, DMCA and program guidelines), the application process, and rejection rules for invalid emails and unfollowed directions.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/updates-and-changes-to-the-ugc-catalog-application/1974990) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Follow the application directions exactly; applications that don't follow them are rejected, and the format differs from previous waves.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/updates-and-changes-to-the-ugc-catalog-application/1974990) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Submit a valid email address on the application; an invalid email causes automatic rejection, and onboarding info is sent there.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/updates-and-changes-to-the-ugc-catalog-application/1974990) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Be an active, rule-following community member; participants have a highly visible position in the program.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/updates-and-changes-to-the-ugc-catalog-application/1974990) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Show genuine passion for creating avatar content; the program accepts a limited number of creators per wave.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/updates-and-changes-to-the-ugc-catalog-application/1974990) Creators welcomed this wave: **over 250**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/updates-and-changes-to-the-ugc-catalog-application/1974990) Review wait time: **could take multiple weeks**
