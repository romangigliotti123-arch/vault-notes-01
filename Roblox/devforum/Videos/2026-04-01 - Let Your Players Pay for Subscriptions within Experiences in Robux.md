# Let Your Players Pay for Subscriptions within Experiences in Robux

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/let-your-players-pay-for-subscriptions-within-experiences-in-robux/4552995) | Roblox DevForum | 391 views | 2026-04-01

## Summary
Roblox DevForum/docs announcement: subscriptions inside experiences can now be priced in Robux as well as local currency. Covers global availability, 49 Robux minimum price, regional pricing, price changes every 60 days with 30-day notice for increases, 70% developer share, and resources including a design guide and Livetopia example.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/let-your-players-pay-for-subscriptions-within-experiences-in-robux/4552995) **[[monetisation-other]]** Offer subscriptions in Robux to reach players who prefer spending Robux balances; local-currency-only subscriptions were a barrier for them.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/let-your-players-pay-for-subscriptions-within-experiences-in-robux/4552995) **[[pricing]]** Robux subscriptions can start at 49 Robux, lowering the price floor to reach more players.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/let-your-players-pay-for-subscriptions-within-experiences-in-robux/4552995) **[[pricing]]** Price changes are allowed every 60 days. Price increases give subscribers 30 days' notice; decreases apply at next renewal.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/let-your-players-pay-for-subscriptions-within-experiences-in-robux/4552995) **[[monetisation-other]]** Subscription design guide and Livetopia's implementation guide are available for structuring offerings and integrating subscriptions into an economy.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/let-your-players-pay-for-subscriptions-within-experiences-in-robux/4552995) **[[monetisation-other]]** To offer both Robux and local-currency subscriptions, create two separate subscriptions.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/let-your-players-pay-for-subscriptions-within-experiences-in-robux/4552995) Minimum Robux subscription price: **49 Robux** (Lowest allowed price floor for subscriptions priced in Robux)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/let-your-players-pay-for-subscriptions-within-experiences-in-robux/4552995) Price change interval: **Every 60 days** (How often a subscription price can be changed)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/let-your-players-pay-for-subscriptions-within-experiences-in-robux/4552995) Advance notice for price increases: **At least 30 days** (Roblox notifies users before increases take effect)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/let-your-players-pay-for-subscriptions-within-experiences-in-robux/4552995) Developer revenue share on Robux subscriptions: **70%** (Developers keep 70% for first month and all renewals)
