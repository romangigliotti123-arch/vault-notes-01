# How to Successfully Grow Your Game (Ft. Sensei_Developer & Vikinglaw) | Promoting and Advertising your Game

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) | Roblox DevForum | 264 views | 2023-08-18

## Summary
Article on promoting Roblox games, drawing on interviews with Sensei_Developer (Dummies vs Noobs), Vikinglaw, Crazedbrick1 and Jevangelise. Covers networking, free social/YouTuber promotion, core loops and engagement, invite mechanics, the recommendation algorithm and keyword search, and ad CTR testing with colour and font choices.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build a satisfying, simple core loop first; Sensei_Developer says graphics don't make a good game, core gameplay does.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Make unique games rather than copying existing ones; Crazedbrick1 advises avoiding fad-driven games that only follow trends.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Keep players always doing something; constant objectives drive engagement and return play.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[community-and-discord]]** Add rewards for inviting friends and an invite button; friend-join waves drove Dummies vs Noobs growth.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Put keywords related to your game in the description so search surfaces it for related queries; playtime is the key algorithm signal.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Make the game engaging to increase playtime, since Roblox recommendations are mostly driven by playtime.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Target ads to players aged 13+, since Roblox only shows ads to that age group; match the game's audience.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Aim for ad CTR of at least 1.0%, ideally above 3.0%; stop underperforming ads quickly and try new ones.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Split ad budget across variants, measure CTR, then shift most spend to the best performer while keeping some testing.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use bold, large fonts and bright saturated colours (red, green, blue, gold, yellow, orange, purple, pink) for ads; test readability on light and dark themes.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[community-and-discord]]** Use Discord to communicate with players and announce updates, but expect it to take real effort to attract members.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** DM small YouTubers (1-10k subscribers) to play for free; offer in-game codes as rewards to encourage their audience to join.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Network with developers and influencers to partner on cross-promotion; ask friends to playtest and give feedback.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Playtest a lot and observe how players behave so you can model the game accordingly.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Expect the first 3-4 games to be near-complete flops; stay consistent, learn why they fail, and keep building.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[monetisation-other]]** Do not make games for money; monetisation should not feel predatory or cash-grab, per Sensei_Developer.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not assume spending Robux alone guarantees success; ads cannot replace an engaging, original game.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Adopt good coding practices early; avoid hard-coded logic to reduce bugs and post-launch patching.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[team-and-budget]]** Only hire when absolutely necessary; Vikinglaw recommends a like-minded partner to ease development.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Build personal discipline to work regardless of motivation; consistency matters more than inspiration.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Reinvest sustainable revenue into advertising your game, per Vikinglaw.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) Ad CTR minimum target: **1.0%, ideally above 3.0%** (Stated benchmark for Roblox ad click-through rate)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) Example A/B budget test: **R$40,000 total: R$5k sponsored (0.5% CTR), R$5k normal (1.2%), R$5k normal (2.0%)** (Shift R$10k to the 2.0% ad; keep R$15k for further tests)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) Ad age target: **13+** (Roblox only shows ads to users aged 13 and older)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) Small YouTuber size: **1-10k subscribers** (Easiest to get free playthroughs from)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) Dummies vs Noobs squad size: **8 players** (Co-op wave shooter; example of viral friend-invite growth)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) Failed early games: **First 3-4 games near-complete flops** (Vikinglaw's expectation for new developers)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) GRP grind time: **Up to 12 hours a day** (Vikinglaw grinding Group Recruitment Plaza with no capital)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279) Development start: **Around 5 years ago (Sensei_Developer)** (Sensei_Developer's career length at interview)
