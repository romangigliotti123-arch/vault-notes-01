# How to Rig a Car

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-rig-a-car/523855) | Roblox DevForum | 723 views | 2020-04-14

## Summary
Four-part DevForum/docs-style series on rigging a 3D-modeled car in Roblox: setup and assembling the model in Studio, configuring attachments and constraints, server-side vehicle scripting, and client-side driver control scripting.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-rig-a-car/523855) **[[physics-pathfinding-and-npc-ai]]** Pick up a car model first, then export and assemble it in Studio before rigging; the series assumes a 3D-modeled car as the starting point.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-rig-a-car/523855) **[[physics-pathfinding-and-npc-ai]]** Set up attachments and constraints (e.g. hinges/springs on wheels) before writing any vehicle scripts, so physics behaviour is established first.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-rig-a-car/523855) **[[client-server-and-remotes]]** Split vehicle logic: handle the car physics on the server and let a client script read player input and send control to it.
