# How we are improving Home this year

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571) | Roblox DevForum | 848 views | 2026-03-09

## Summary
Roblox staff post on 2026 Home changes: Today's Picks becomes Standout Games (curated novel games), new Deep play through rate signal for Recommended For You, 7-day qualified play sessions signal, gameplay video previews on Home, ads blended into RFY, and a mobile flyout menu for resuming games.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Upload a real gameplay video to your place's Videos tab in Creator Hub; approved videos can auto-play on Home. Videos help players grasp the game and draw more committed players.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Gameplay videos must be real, current in-game footage: no cinematic trailers, dramatized or over-enhanced graphics, real-life footage, voiceovers, narration, lyric music, promo text, or removed content.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Keep gameplay videos simple: straightforward recordings of the core loop with minimal editing; curated highlight reels are fine if every shown part exists in the game. In-game UI is acceptable.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Video playback on RFY does not change ranking; position is still set by the same algorithm. Video just gives players more context.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check Creator Analytics > Acquisition > Home recommendations for the new Deep play through rate and 7-day qualified play session signals; check Overview tab for 'not interested' counts.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build for the signal relevant to your genre: deep-play games may have fewer repeat sessions. Focus on making the game fun rather than optimizing against unpublished weights.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** The 7-day qualified play sessions per user signal rewards games that bring players back after their first session within the next 7 days.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Use clear, intuitive onboarding so players of different backgrounds and experience levels can start playing quickly, and use high-quality thumbnails, videos and metadata that accurately represent the game.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Novel games (new mechanics, distinctive visuals, underrepresented genres) can be nominated for Standout Games via the Curation team's nomination form; not required for organic Home discovery.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ads Manager targets players likely to play the advertised game. A featured or high-performing ad moves users to playing, so RFY impressions may dip as those users move to the Continue sort; this is expected.
