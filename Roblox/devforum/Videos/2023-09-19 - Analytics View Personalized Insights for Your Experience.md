# Analytics: View Personalized Insights for Your Experience

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-personalized-insights-for-your-experience/2600369) | Roblox DevForum | 108 views | 2023-09-19

## Summary
Roblox announces "Insights" on the experience overview page (Sept 2023). Insights flag metrics below the 50th percentile of similar experiences, week-over-week changes of x%, and 6-month highs. Insights appear only after 100 DAU; stats shown include DAU, new users, session time, D1 retention, daily revenue.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-personalized-insights-for-your-experience/2600369) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Prioritise average session time and day 1 retention first; they show whether the experience is fun enough for users to return.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-personalized-insights-for-your-experience/2600369) **[[monetisation-other]]** Track payer conversion rate and ARPPU to gauge how much users want to invest in your experience.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-personalized-insights-for-your-experience/2600369) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Work on D7 and D30 retention to confirm users make progress and return long-term.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-personalized-insights-for-your-experience/2600369) **[[analytics-metrics]]** When a metric drops or spikes week over week, use chart breakdowns (e.g. by platform) to find the cause.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-personalized-insights-for-your-experience/2600369) Insight threshold: metric below percentile: **50th percentile of similar experiences** (Benchmark comparison for insight type 1)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-personalized-insights-for-your-experience/2600369) Daily active users needed for insights: **100 DAU** (Below this, getting-started steps replace insights)
