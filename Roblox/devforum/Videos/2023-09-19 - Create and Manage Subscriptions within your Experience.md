# Create and Manage Subscriptions within your Experience

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-and-manage-subscriptions-within-your-experience/2600664) | Roblox DevForum | 558 views | 2023-09-19

## Summary
Roblox DevForum/docs announcement on Subscriptions for experiences: rollout timeline, eligibility (ID/phone verification, experience created and in Creator Hub before Sept 1 2023), creation in Creator Hub Associated Items with monthly price tiers, new MarketplaceService APIs, user-side safety, refund and analytics rules, and Robux payouts based on base web price.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-and-manage-subscriptions-within-your-experience/2600664) **[[monetisation-other]]** Subscriptions are monthly recurring, chosen from set price tiers; test purchase flows and benefit granting in Studio before users can buy them.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-and-manage-subscriptions-within-your-experience/2600664) **[[monetisation-other]]** Do not publish an experience with subscription APIs before the user launch; purchases will show an error message to users.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-and-manage-subscriptions-within-your-experience/2600664) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Subscriptions auto-renew unless canceled, so they create ongoing revenue and help retain subscribers; reward tenure or retention with consistent published benefits across devices.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-and-manage-subscriptions-within-your-experience/2600664) **[[common-mistakes]]** Ensure subscription descriptions clearly state benefits; benefits not delivered due to unclear descriptions or removing the subscription may be refunded.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-and-manage-subscriptions-within-your-experience/2600664) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Creators are paid in Robux based on the base web price of the subscription, with the same cut as experience products; retained subscribers may earn more.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-and-manage-subscriptions-within-your-experience/2600664) **[[monetisation-other]]** Renewing subscribers bought on desktop or web get a lower platform fee incentive; this is not available on the mobile app.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-and-manage-subscriptions-within-your-experience/2600664) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use subscription analytics to track sales, revenue, cancellation trends, and breakdowns by subscriber type (new, renewing, previously canceled) and platform.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-and-manage-subscriptions-within-your-experience/2600664) **[[common-mistakes]]** Subscriptions are not a fit for every experience; cancel or delete subscriptions that are not gaining traction.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-and-manage-subscriptions-within-your-experience/2600664) Eligibility deadline: **Experience created in Studio and available in Creator Hub before September 1 (2023); account verified via ID or phone**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-and-manage-subscriptions-within-your-experience/2600664) Subscription pricing: **Monthly recurring price tiers selected in Creator Hub**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-and-manage-subscriptions-within-your-experience/2600664) Early benefit deadline: **Adding subscriptions before December 15, 2023 brings new benefits**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-and-manage-subscriptions-within-your-experience/2600664) User purchase availability: **November 2023 user launch**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/create-and-manage-subscriptions-within-your-experience/2600664) Payout basis: **Robux paid on base web price of subscription, same cut as experience products**
