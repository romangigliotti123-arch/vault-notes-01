# Improve Lighting (STEP BY STEP)

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improve-lighting-step-by-step/1387033) | Roblox DevForum | 52 views | 2021-08-02

## Summary
Step-by-step guide to tuning Roblox Lighting for a showcase: Atmosphere and fog, water settings with a foil strip for sun reflections, ExposureCompensation with higher Brightness for crisp lighting, ColorCorrection, Bloom, and SunRays. Each effect is shown with before/after screenshots and starting values.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improve-lighting-step-by-step/1387033) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Tweak every lighting setting until it fits your own game; the provided values are starting points, not universal settings.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improve-lighting-step-by-step/1387033) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Add a thin foil-material block under the water surface to create extra sun reflections on water.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improve-lighting-step-by-step/1387033) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Set ExposureCompensation to about -3 and raise Lighting Brightness to about 65 for crisper daytime lighting; lower exposure needs higher brightness.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improve-lighting-step-by-step/1387033) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Exposure compensation trick requires brighter spot/point lights and does not work well for night scenes.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improve-lighting-step-by-step/1387033) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Exposure compensation also removes the glare from neon parts.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improve-lighting-step-by-step/1387033) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Bloom has little visible effect once the exposure compensation trick is applied.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improve-lighting-step-by-step/1387033) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use SunRays for any time of day, but keep them subtle for realism since real sun rays are not very intense.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improve-lighting-step-by-step/1387033) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** If sun rays make the sun look too big, change SunAngularSize in the Skybox instead of only changing the ray spread.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improve-lighting-step-by-step/1387033) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Atmosphere requires a skybox; fog is a simpler alternative found in Lighting.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improve-lighting-step-by-step/1387033) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use ColorCorrection to set a cinematic look or a black-and-white effect across the whole screen.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improve-lighting-step-by-step/1387033) ExposureCompensation: **-3** (Suggested starting value for crisp lighting; pair with Brightness 65)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improve-lighting-step-by-step/1387033) Brightness: **65** (Lighting Brightness paired with ExposureCompensation -3)
