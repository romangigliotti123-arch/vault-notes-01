# Announcing Free Badge Creations!

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/announcing-free-badge-creations/1680515) | Roblox DevForum | 2,160 views | 2022-02-25

## Summary
Roblox Creator Services announcement (Feb 2022): badge creation becomes free for the first five badges per experience every 24 hours (GMT), with additional badges still costing 100 Robux each. Available on web and in Studio.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/announcing-free-badge-creations/1680515) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use the free badge allowance (5 per experience per 24h GMT) to set up achievements early without paying 100 Robux per badge.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/announcing-free-badge-creations/1680515) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Badges are a low-cost way to add achievement hooks that encourage players to return; the change lowers the barrier to adding them.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/announcing-free-badge-creations/1680515) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Groups no longer need an initial Robux fund before creating badges, removing a barrier to getting started with group-owned experiences.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/announcing-free-badge-creations/1680515) Free badge creations: **5 per experience per 24 hours (GMT)** (Badges beyond the fifth in a 24h period still cost 100 Robux each)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/announcing-free-badge-creations/1680515) Previous badge creation cost: **100 Robux each** (Original price before the February 2022 change)

## Tools
Roblox Studio (Game Settings / Monetization / Badges / Create), Roblox.com creator dashboard
