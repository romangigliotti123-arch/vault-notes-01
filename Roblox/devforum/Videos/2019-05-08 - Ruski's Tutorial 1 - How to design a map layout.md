# Ruski's Tutorial #1 - How to design a map layout

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) | Roblox DevForum | 795 views | 2019-05-08

## Summary
Map design basics for Roblox: decide genre, theme, player count, art style and shape before building; sketch on paper; design around Q&A (purpose, spawns, theme); place doors/windows to balance entry points; use boundary bricks; group Workspace as you go. Includes a worked "infection" map example.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Decide genre, theme, player count, art style and map shape before opening Studio; FPS maps should be fluid with few dead ends, open-world maps need draw-in landmarks.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Sketch map layouts on paper first and collect a few dozen reference photos; it saves time even if drawings are not used exactly.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Keep players looking at something interesting; never leave empty space or bare walls as the main view.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Limit enterable buildings to about two or three entry points each to balance attackers and defenders; keep some dead ends as risk/reward spots.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Use curves and jogs rather than boxy shapes so the map feels fluid.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Mark off-limits rooftops and areas with clearly coloured boundary bricks instead of leaving players to wander into them.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Scale buildings to the camera: in first person, keep buildings 2-3 levels since players cannot zoom out to view the map.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Add cover in open areas and alleys, plus second floors, roofs, holes in walls and balconies so players have multiple routes.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Test the simple blockout early and adjust layout based on play before detailing textures and props.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Group and name parts in Workspace as you build; it makes organising the finished map much easier.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) Player count for infection map spawn area: **12-20 players**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) Zombie spawn delay in infection example: **30 seconds after players spawn**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) Reference player counts cited: **6v6 (CoD-style compact) vs 32v32 (Battlefield-style large)**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) Building height for first-person city map: **about 2-3 levels**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ruskis-tutorial-1-how-to-design-a-map-layout/277853) Entry points per building: **about 2-3**
