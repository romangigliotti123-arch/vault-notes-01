# How to make your game have Replayability

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) | Roblox DevForum | 48 views | 2021-03-03

## Summary
Argues Roblox simulators/tycoons need flexible, non-rigid outcomes for replayability, not daily rewards. Uses Uncharted 4, Team Fortress 2 weapon combinations, Lumber Tycoon 2 exploration, and rebirth ideas (scale-up across planets/universe) as examples.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Avoid rigid outcomes; let players change results by choices so a second playthrough differs noticeably from the first.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Don't rely on cheap daily rewards and free coins to bring players back; give a mystery or long goal they want to finish.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Mutate a simple click loop into varied outcomes (e.g. mining with unknown finds, clicks that trigger combat) instead of pure clicking.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** In tycoons, let independent player decisions pay off later so players are motivated to experiment.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Design for easy learning but long mastery; depth of skill keeps players playing for hours.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give tools that change playstyle rather than straight stat upgrades; avoid flat +health per level.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make rebirth/prestige change the context (new scale or setting), not repeat the same loop with bigger numbers.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Mix a familiar core (traditional) with a novel twist (innovative), Pixar-style, so the game feels known yet new.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give players a clear game philosophy about how to play (e.g. safe play vs risk) and keep the game consistent with it.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make the game easy to learn at first; reserve difficulty for mastery.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid unfair bugs and cheap server-side exploits (e.g. being crashed remotely) that frustrate players and drive them away.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Place map mechanisms and hidden areas that unlock new activities so the loop is not just clicking and jumping.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Let players feel they have a reason to return by showing upcoming content and unresolved mysteries.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) Team Fortress 2 weapon combinations: **about 1200 per class** (Combinations of weapons create varied matches; cited by author)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) Time to master one TF2 weapon: **about 200 hours** (Author's estimate for learning a single weapon; plus 50 hours per new primary)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) Typical Roblox player session expectation: **around 300 hours for a long goal** (Author suggests a goal players spend about this long on, on average)

## Games
- **Uncharted 4**: Rigid single outcome means replay offers little; use it as a contrast for flexible outcomes.
- **Team Fortress 2**: Many weapon combinations and adaptive enemies create different matches; players stay for thousands of hours.
- **Lumber Tycoon 2**: Map exploration, hidden mysteries, and upgrades that vary per tree and tool drive fun and replayability.
- **Car Crushers 2**: Author criticises server-side crash exploits that frustrate players; ensure interactions can't be abused remotely.

## He points to
- Pixar films (Toy Story, Monsters Inc) as example of tradition plus innovation

## Quotes
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) "Replayability comes from the idea that the value of the second playthrough won't be the same as the previous playthrough."
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) "Give the players a feeling to come back. Don't make it daily rewards cheap, give them a mystery they want to solve."
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-game-have-replayability/1082534) "Allow the players to improve if they do something different."
