# Introducing Premium Payouts!

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-premium-payouts/455347) | Roblox DevForum | 3,668 views | 2020-02-19

## Summary
Announcement of Premium Payouts (Feb 2020): developers earn Robux from Premium subscribers' playtime in their games, paid per Premium time unit regardless of game size. Explains Premium Playtime Score and Robux Earned charts, a 28-day payout lag, and that paid-access games are excluded.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-premium-payouts/455347) **[[monetisation-other]]** Premium Payouts pay on top of game passes and developer products; they are additive revenue, not a replacement.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-premium-payouts/455347) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make the game engaging to Premium subscribers; payouts scale with Premium time spent, paid equally per unit regardless of game size.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-premium-payouts/455347) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use the Premium Playtime Score to get daily feedback on new features; a sustained rise usually precedes payout increases.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-premium-payouts/455347) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Judge feature impact over months, not days; the Robux Earned figure aggregates 28 days of Premium behaviour.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-premium-payouts/455347) **[[monetisation-other]]** Offer Premium benefits using the Premium Purchase Modal so players can buy Premium without leaving the game.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-premium-payouts/455347) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Paid-access games are not eligible for Premium Payouts; use free access if you want this revenue.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-premium-payouts/455347) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Expect a 28-day lag plus a 3-day pending period between a day's engagement and its payout; group payouts follow the group's distribution settings.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-premium-payouts/455347) Payout lag: **28 days after measured day, plus 3-day pending period** (Premium Payouts timing per FAQ and community clarification)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-premium-payouts/455347) Premium Playtime Robux Earned window: **Aggregates past 28 days of each Premium user's behaviour** (Per-user aggregation; not daily)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-premium-payouts/455347) Eligibility: **All games except paid access** (Paid access games show score but no payout)
