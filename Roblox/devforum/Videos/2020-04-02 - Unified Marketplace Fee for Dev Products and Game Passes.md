# Unified Marketplace Fee for Dev Products and Game Passes

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/unified-marketplace-fee-for-dev-products-and-game-passes/507109) | Roblox DevForum | 2,640 views | 2020-04-02

## Summary
Roblox announcement (April 2020): the marketplace fee on Robux from developer products and game passes is now a flat 30% for all creators, whether or not they have Premium. Previously 90% without Premium and 30% with Premium.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/unified-marketplace-fee-for-dev-products-and-game-passes/507109) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Expect a flat 30% marketplace fee on developer product and game pass sales regardless of Premium status; no need to buy Premium to get the lower fee.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/unified-marketplace-fee-for-dev-products-and-game-passes/507109) Marketplace fee on game passes and developer products (no Premium): **90% before, 30% after (April 2020)**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/unified-marketplace-fee-for-dev-products-and-game-passes/507109) Marketplace fee on game passes and developer products (with Premium): **30% before and after**
