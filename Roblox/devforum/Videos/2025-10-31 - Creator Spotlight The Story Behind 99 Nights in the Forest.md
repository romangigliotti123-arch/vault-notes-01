# Creator Spotlight: The Story Behind 99 Nights in the Forest

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) | Roblox DevForum | 199 views | 2025-10-31

## Summary
Roblox staff spotlight on 99 Nights in the Forest by its three co-creators (Cracky4, ForyxeV, Viridial). Covers the game-jam origin, a 3-month build from March to June 2025, a client-heavy "snappy" combat approach, asset workflow for mobile performance, weekly "Update Party" community sessions, the Halloween update, and five tips on scope, playtesting and moving on.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) **[[common-mistakes]]** Keep scope small and work quickly; no developer has a 100% hit rate, so stay flexible and follow what is fun.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Make something you would genuinely play; imagine friends and favourite YouTubers playing it.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Playtest repeatedly. Filter feedback, but act when many players independently give the same feedback.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Watch players when they do not know they are observed to see where they truly get stuck and confused.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Recognise when an idea isn't working and move on quickly rather than holding onto it.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** For responsiveness, run actions on the client immediately (hit, damage, visuals), send the result to the server, and roll back if the server rejects it. Expect some rollback edge cases.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) **[[combat-and-gameplay-systems]]** Build rollback systems alongside client-predicted actions, since rollbacks are tricky; the team reports the approach working smoothly about 99% of the time.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Build structures in stages: visual references, then blockout, then shapes, then details. Keep part, vertex and instance counts low for mobile performance.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Designer and programmer scale systems together: build a small piece, then push it to thousands of entities until something breaks, then iterate.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) **[[community-and-discord]]** Run a weekly sandbox session before each update (the team uses a 45-minute 'Update Party') to talk to players, hand out items and find bugs.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Balance event content for both casual and grinding players; limited-time special items drive dedicated grinding.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Thematic and atmospheric event updates with a wider range of event-store items are the team's stated direction for future events.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use a fast build process: the team's 3-month build was far faster than their previous 6-month projects, and speed allowed iteration.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use a repeatable grind dungeon with a reward currency (diamonds) to give players a long-term reason to return.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Combine a proven trend (survive N days) with a clear theme and reference games (Dead Rails, cozy forest) for a fresh take.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) Peak CCU: **14 million** (99 Nights in the Forest peak since June 2025 launch)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) Build time: **3 months (March to June 2025)** (Team's fastest build; previous projects took about 6 months)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) Client-prediction success rate: **~99%** (Share of time client-first hit/damage works smoothly, per ForyxeV)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) Cultist Stronghold build time: **about 2 days** (Viridial's asset workflow for a dungeon structure)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) Update Party length: **45 minutes weekly** (Sandbox mode run before each weekly update)
