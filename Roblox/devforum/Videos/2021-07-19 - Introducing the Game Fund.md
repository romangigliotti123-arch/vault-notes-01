# Introducing the Game Fund

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477) | Roblox DevForum | 2,356 views | 2021-07-19

## Summary
Roblox DevRel announcement of the Game Fund, a 2021 partnership program funding developers (minimum $500,000 per project) for ambitious, multiplayer, cross-platform, social experiences. Lists priority idea traits, application requirements (18+, pitch deck, milestone schedule, budget), and differences from the Accelerator program.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Game Fund prioritises multiplayer focus, cross-platform with mobile optimisation, social mechanics as core, and avatar/Metaverse item integration; design pitches around these.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477) **[[team-and-budget]]** Applicants may be solo devs, small teams or studios; all applicants must be 18+. Under-18 team members are allowed if the developer complies with applicable laws.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477) **[[studio-business-funding-and-publishing]]** Application requires team details, a short project description, a pitch deck (high-level strategy, design specs), and a project milestone schedule and budget.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477) **[[studio-business-funding-and-publishing]]** Funding is milestone-based: Roblox agrees deliverables with accepted teams and releases funds according to progress toward those milestones.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477) **[[studio-business-funding-and-publishing]]** Prior Roblox experience is not required; new and indie developers can apply. Rejected applicants need not reapply; applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477) **[[studio-business-funding-and-publishing]]** Choose Game Fund for ambitious, next-gen projects with large budgets; choose Accelerator for smaller, fixed per-person funding in a 3-month hands-on program.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477) Minimum funding per project: **$500,000** (Game Fund minimum funding for approved participants, stated in 2021 announcement)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477) Minimum applicant age: **18+** (Required for all applicants, including solo devs, teams and studios)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477) Accelerator program length: **3 months** (Defined hands-on program with lower, per-individual fixed funding)
