# How to Cooperate with a Development Team

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-cooperate-with-a-development-team/122601) | Roblox DevForum | 511 views | 2018-05-01

## Summary
DevForum post by DerpyMcDerpell on cooperating with development teams: treat teammates as people, assign work they can do, give constructive criticism, understand differing views, critique work not persons, and collaborate on game ideas. Mostly opinion/humor; few concrete numbers.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-cooperate-with-a-development-team/122601) **[[community-and-discord]]** Recognize teammates as people: reward effort and praise their work; people are more motivated when treated like humans.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-cooperate-with-a-development-team/122601) **[[team-and-budget]]** Confirm a teammate can do a task before assigning it; don't give a scripter building work or an animator scripting work.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-cooperate-with-a-development-team/122601) **[[team-and-budget]]** Give realistic deadlines; don't set deadlines one hour after assignment.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-cooperate-with-a-development-team/122601) **[[team-and-budget]]** Give constructive criticism, not insults; let people improve rather than firing them unless quality is clearly below standard.
