# How to make a successful Roblox Game

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) | Roblox DevForum | 381 views | 2020-07-26

## Summary
Roblox 101 guide by a game designer/producer covering core mechanic design, easy-to-learn gameplay, reward systems, project management basics (initial meeting, daily/twice-weekly planning), and advertising via CTR thresholds and a staged launch budget test.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Ask whether you'd keep playing after about four rounds; if parts feel boring or unengaging, restart the core mechanic design from scratch.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build a core mechanic that is engaging, diverse, stimulating, and easy to update; an obby works by adding harder new sections as players progress.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make the game easy to learn so players spend time having fun rather than learning; let complexity reveal itself gradually over time.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Ensure enough depth so players don't get bored after five minutes; easy to learn, but fun to play.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give players rewards such as scoreboard ranks, in-game economies, cosmetics, or login rewards, since players are less likely to act without a reward.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) **[[team-and-budget]]** Hold an initial team meeting (e.g., via Discord call) to cover payment, expectations, and sign a formal agreement before contracting developers.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Have the dev team meet daily to review completed and planned tasks; at minimum meet twice a week.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Do not run any ad that falls below 1.0% CTR; aim for about 3.0% CTR.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Decide first whether you want a small dedicated community (40-90 players) or a wider discovery audience (100-1,000+) before choosing ad types.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Launch test: split budget into three equal ads (clickbait, community, mobile sponsor), then put daily spend on the highest-CTR one for three days; archive the second-best.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Simple, eye-catching ad creatives made quickly in Photoshop or Paint.NET can outperform expensive art on CTR, per the author.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** After launch, keep running smaller ads on the archived second-best channel, funded by game revenue, to maintain a playerbase of 500+.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) **[[analytics-metrics]]** During launch research, track average session time and which gamepasses or developer products sell best.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) Boredom check: **~4 plays before boredom signals flawed mechanic** (Author's self-test for whether core mechanic is working)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) Minimum CTR: **1.0%; target ~3.0%** (Stop running ads below 1.0% CTR; clickbait banners typically 1%-3%)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) Community size targets: **40-90 consecutive players (community) vs 100-1,000+ (wide discovery)** (Choose target audience before running ads)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) Sustained playerbase goal: **500+ players** (Most games need constant ads to maintain this after launch)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) Launch budget split: **R$100,000 example: R$10,000 each on 3 ad types, then R$10,000/day for 3 days** (Example allocation; author says it scales down proportionally)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-successful-roblox-game/690805) Team meeting cadence: **Daily ideally; at least twice a week** (Dev team check-ins during project planning)

## Games
- **Doomspire Brickbattle**: Single clear objective (destroy the opposing tower) needs no rulebook or tutorial; players grasp it on joining.
- **Obby (generic)**: Repetitive core task stays fun when new sections with rising difficulty appear as players progress.
