# Increasing DevEx — Creators Will Now Earn 8.5% More

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/increasing-devex-%E2%80%94-creators-will-now-earn-85-more/3920159) | Roblox DevForum | 1,602 views | 2025-09-05

## Summary
Roblox staff announcement (Sept 5, 2025) that the DevEx exchange rate rises from $0.0035 to $0.0038 per Earned Robux (~8.5% more) for Robux earned from 10 AM PT Sept 5, 2025. Pre-change balances keep the old rate; group owners get a split breakdown for paying collaborators.
