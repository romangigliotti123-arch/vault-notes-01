# Analytics: Understand the success of your experience events

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-understand-the-success-of-your-experience-events/4019126) | Roblox DevForum | 171 views | 2025-10-21

## Summary
Roblox DevForum announcement of new Event analytics under Engagement > Event & Updates. Covers comparing up to three past events on one chart with demographic filters, pre/post event analysis windows, short-term vs strong success definitions, and a summary table of RSVPs, notification joins, playtime and revenue per DAU. Metrics available from June 15, 2025.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-understand-the-success-of-your-experience-events/4019126) **[[analytics-metrics]]** For pre-post event analysis, compare 7 days before, the event period, and 7 or 14 days after the event.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-understand-the-success-of-your-experience-events/4019126) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Judge events by goal: track ARPDAU and revenue for monetization; DAU and returning users for engagement and reactivation.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-understand-the-success-of-your-experience-events/4019126) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Expect short-term success (KPIs return to baseline after event) versus strong success (sustained higher baseline); major content and feature updates more often achieve the latter.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-understand-the-success-of-your-experience-events/4019126) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Instrument funnels and custom events to track how users progress through an event.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-understand-the-success-of-your-experience-events/4019126) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Use RSVP counts and joins-from-notifications to explain early-event engagement spikes.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-understand-the-success-of-your-experience-events/4019126) Events compared on one chart: **up to 3 past events**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-understand-the-success-of-your-experience-events/4019126) Recommended pre-post windows (week-long event): **7 days before, event period, 7 or 14 days after**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-understand-the-success-of-your-experience-events/4019126) Metrics data availability: **from June 15, 2025**

## Games
- **Winter Event (Roblox example experience)**: Higher early-day user acquisition and most RSVPs and notification joins; better days 1-2, fell behind later.
- **New Dragon (Roblox example experience)**: Stronger engagement days 3-7 and highest average DAU and daily revenue despite fewer RSVPs.
