# Analytics: View Home Recommendations Conversion Rate and Recent Icon Updates

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-home-recommendations-conversion-rate-and-recent-icon-updates/2731683) | Roblox DevForum | 232 views | 2023-12-06

## Summary
Roblox Creator Docs/DevForum update (Dec 2023) on the Analytics Acquisition page: adds Home recommendations conversion rate chart, icon-change annotations (data since Oct 9, 2023), new-user-only acquisition charts and funnel table, and returning-user plays/impressions per source. Lists planned future metrics.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-home-recommendations-conversion-rate-and-recent-icon-updates/2731683) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Monitor Home recommendations conversion rate (percent of users who play after viewing on Home) on the Acquisition page, filterable by platform.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-home-recommendations-conversion-rate-and-recent-icon-updates/2731683) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check icon-change annotations on acquisition charts to see whether an icon update affected impressions or conversion; icon data only exists since Oct 9, 2023.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-home-recommendations-conversion-rate-and-recent-icon-updates/2731683) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Treat Home recommendations conversion rate as one signal only; also monitor Engagement, Retention and Monetization in Insights and other analytics pages.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-home-recommendations-conversion-rate-and-recent-icon-updates/2731683) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use the new users funnel (cumulative) table to find which source drives the most new users and which has the highest new-user conversion rate.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-home-recommendations-conversion-rate-and-recent-icon-updates/2731683) Icon update data availability: **Since October 9, 2023** (Earliest date for icon-change annotations on acquisition charts)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-home-recommendations-conversion-rate-and-recent-icon-updates/2731683) Home recommendations conversion rate: **Percent of users who play after viewing on Home recommendations** (Definition; no benchmark given yet, benchmarks planned for next year)
