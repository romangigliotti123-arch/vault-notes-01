# Map Design and Scene Building: Fantasy, Medieval and Nature maps

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) | Roblox DevForum | 248 views | 2020-08-06

## Summary
Ahlvie (Kingdom Life II builder/map designer) walks through map design for a fantasy medieval nature town: stylization, layout, terrain sculpting, scene building, atmosphere lighting via Atmosphere objects and TweenService, and final detail polish.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Decide map layout (buildings, paths, landmarks) before making assets; use scaled placeholder blocks for houses, lanterns and trees to avoid resizing later.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Test in test mode often to view the map from player perspective and adjust scale, enclosure (mountains in distance) and tree/building heights.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Place tall buildings on downward slopes so they feel less imposing relative to the player.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Mark water locations (rivers, lakes, waterfalls, oceans) early in layout, before placing assets.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Use curved, varied slopes and subtle elevation changes rather than straight slopes for natural-looking terrain.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Keep cobblestone paths not too wide, and mix terrain materials (cobblestone, grass, leafy grass, rock) to avoid unnatural looks.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Build rivers/lakes by subtracting a deep curved gap with the square brush, then adding water with the add tool, and paint sides rock.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Build walls by painting the cobblestone path first, keep stone walls lower than the player, fill interior with grass, then erode excess terrain.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Use Terrain Grow tool with rock, with base size and strength turned down, to create natural rocks; dot them in empty areas.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Vary repeated props (flowers, etc.) by colour, size and angle; fill empty space with barrels, crates, rocks and plants at varied sizes.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Use bright accent colours (flags, flowers) against a muted palette for contrast; keep palette limited with unified shades.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Animate ambient life (swaying trees, falling leaves, NPCs, birds, butterflies, day/night script) to bring a scene to life.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[vfx-and-particles]]** Add leaf particles near trees for a falling-leaf effect as a low-cost final detail.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[vfx-and-particles]]** For day/night: create multiple hand-tuned Atmosphere objects per time of day, then TweenService between them, tuning Haze, Glare, Color and Decay.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[vfx-and-particles]]** Animate Lighting ColorShift_Top during the day to make sunlight feel warmer.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Pick a stylization first: low poly uses bright flat colours for cheerful mood; high detail uses muted, darker tones for realism or danger.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Keep vibrant colours rare in realistic builds so that intentional accents like flowers or mushrooms stand out.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/map-design-and-scene-building-fantasy-medieval-and-nature-maps/709866) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Check and fix clipping issues at the end by testing from the player's perspective.

## Games
- **Kingdom Life II**: Case study: revamped from a near 10-year-old build to a high-detail realistic map; muted unified palette, layout-first design.

## Tools
Roblox Studio Terrain tools (Grow, add, subtract, erode, paint), Atmosphere object, TweenService
