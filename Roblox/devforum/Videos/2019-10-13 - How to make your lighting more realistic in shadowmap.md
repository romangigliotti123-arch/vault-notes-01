# How to make your lighting more realistic in shadowmap

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-lighting-more-realistic-in-shadowmap/367805) | Roblox DevForum | 942 views | 2019-10-13

## Summary
Tutorial on realistic lighting in Roblox's Shadowmap technology: switch Lighting to Shadowmap, set ShadowSoftness to 0, pick a morning or late-afternoon time, raise Brightness, tint ColorShift_Top, and add a matching skybox. Provides a downloadable template place. No baking or scripting.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-lighting-more-realistic-in-shadowmap/367805) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Set Lighting Technology to Shadowmap to get dynamic shadows without baking or scripting.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-lighting-more-realistic-in-shadowmap/367805) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Set ShadowSoftness to 0 for crisper shadows.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-lighting-more-realistic-in-shadowmap/367805) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use a morning or late-afternoon time of day (e.g. 09:00:00) for warmer, more atmospheric light.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-lighting-more-realistic-in-shadowmap/367805) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Raise Lighting Brightness from the default 2 to 3-5 for most builds; use 6-7 for nature builds.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-lighting-more-realistic-in-shadowmap/367805) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Set ColorShift_Top to a warm yellow-orange (e.g. 255,138,35) for a morning glow.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-lighting-more-realistic-in-shadowmap/367805) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use a cool blue ColorShift_Top (e.g. 44,128,255) for a moon-glow night look; adjust brightness to taste.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-lighting-more-realistic-in-shadowmap/367805) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Pair lighting with a skybox that matches the time of day; the author says it noticeably improves realism.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-lighting-more-realistic-in-shadowmap/367805) Lighting Brightness: **default 2; 3-5 typical, 6-7 for nature builds** (Brightness guidance in Shadowmap lighting tutorial)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-lighting-more-realistic-in-shadowmap/367805) ShadowSoftness: **0** (Setting for crisp shadows)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-lighting-more-realistic-in-shadowmap/367805) Time of day: **09:00:00** (Morning example time)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-lighting-more-realistic-in-shadowmap/367805) ColorShift_Top morning glow RGB: **255,138,35** (Warm yellow-orange morning tone)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-your-lighting-more-realistic-in-shadowmap/367805) ColorShift_Top moon glow RGB: **44,128,255** (Blue night tone)
