# Game Design Theory: Becoming a Leader for Project Management and Team Building

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) | Roblox DevForum | 159 views | 2020-07-26

## Summary
Guide by a Roblox studio Operations Manager (Cloud Entertainment, 77 staff) on project management: what managers do, writing job posts, interviewing, when to hire, team culture, leads and delegation, firing, payment models (royalty vs rate vs per-assignment), Agile/Scrum/Waterfall, and PM tools.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[team-and-budget]]** Avoid royalty splits for hires; they don't scale and renegotiating percentages to add staff causes conflict. Reserve royalty for 2-3 owners on a passion project.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[team-and-budget]]** Pay contracted team members hourly by tracked time, paid on the 1st of the month, rather than royalties or per-assignment rough estimates.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[team-and-budget]]** Budget over 12 months, not one month, and assume you may need to hire 2-3 more staff during that time.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[team-and-budget]]** Before hiring, check whether a cheap asset pack (about $15-20) can unblock programmers, and whether a task really needs a new hire.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[team-and-budget]]** Hire for a specific struggling area (e.g. cross-platform UI) rather than adding people generically, once the team is visibly overloaded or behind.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[team-and-budget]]** Write job posts with About the Company, Responsibilities, Requirements, Payment and Contact; never hide pay behind 'contact me'.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[team-and-budget]]** State required responsibilities explicitly (e.g. a UI designer must also implement UI in Studio) to avoid mismatched hires and timeline slips.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[team-and-budget]]** Separate required skills from nice-to-have skills; list only what is needed to engage in the project from day one.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[team-and-budget]]** Use a form (Google Forms or ClickUp Forms) for applications to organise candidates and keep application process transparent.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[team-and-budget]]** In interviews, ask questions tied to a goal, such as how they handle problem solving in a team project, and hire team players.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[team-and-budget]]** Assign a lead for each discipline (senior programmer, builder director) to keep code and art style consistent as the team grows.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[team-and-budget]]** When delegating, confirm with the developer how long a task will take and its difficulty; cut or delay scope accordingly.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[team-and-budget]]** Fire team members who repeatedly miss promised deadlines and communication; waiting for a perfect replacement lets problems snowball.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[team-and-budget]]** Do not give investors more than you can afford to lose; keep 100% ownership where possible and never put ownership on the line.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Run Agile sprints of consistent length (2-3 weeks); never delay a sprint, drop unfinished items from it instead, and ensure visible progress each sprint.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Avoid Waterfall for games; late testing misses design issues that Agile/Scrum review cycles would catch early.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Write user stories in a Who / When / Why format so programmers and artists understand the goal.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build a Discord community and use Patreon supporter roles as a way to fund development early on.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Strong developers do not automatically make strong managers; choose or train managers for communication, coordination and organisation.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Save a budget and build income before funding development; few professionals work without a budget.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not hire a friend to avoid conflict or keep someone on who is falling behind; it damages the whole team.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid cutting royalty percentages later, which causes disputes; decide compensation structure before the project starts.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) Studio headcount: **77 total staff, 14+ developers/testers/translators/moderators** (Cloud Entertainment, author's studio at time of writing)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) Asset pack price: **$15-20** (Cheap alternative to hiring a modeler for swords/armor)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) Monthly pay budget example: **$75-100 per team member per month from a $1000 budget** (Illustrative rate-based budgeting example)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) Royalty share for scripters: **25-50%** (Typical requested share that locks in on contract)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) Sprint length: **2 or 3 weeks** (Recommended consistent Agile sprint duration)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) Per-assignment milestone payment example: **$200 for 50% of project** (Illustrative per-assignment pay example)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475) Budget horizon: **12 months, plus 2-3 possible new hires** (Cost evaluation period advised)
