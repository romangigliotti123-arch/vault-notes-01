# Account Security PSA

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/account-security-psa/792056) | Roblox DevForum | 595 views | 2020-09-26

## Summary
Developer Relations PSA about phishing links sent via private messages to developers. Urges use of Report Abuse on suspicious off-platform link messages and lists basic account security habits: strong passwords, 2-step verification, not trusting unknown links, not sharing personal info.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/account-security-psa/792056) **[[common-mistakes]]** Never click links you do not recognize, even from friends; phishing DMs can come from compromised accounts that look friendly.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/account-security-psa/792056) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Enable 2-step verification in account settings to protect your account, which matters for developers who control games and Robux.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/account-security-psa/792056) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Use strong passwords mixing letters, numbers and symbols; never give personal information to anyone.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/account-security-psa/792056) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Use the Report Abuse button on private messages asking you to visit an off-platform link.
