# Monetisation 101: How to effectively generate revenue in your Roblox Game

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) | Roblox DevForum | 478 views | 2019-03-01

## Summary
Roblox staff-hosted DevForum guide by Jed (peraldon) on monetisation: key terms, player types (first-purchase, repeat, whale), monetisation tools (pay-to-join, subscriptions/battle passes, hard currency, loot boxes), placement (cosmetic vs functional), a three-stage conversion plan, analytics questions, and case studies (Brawl Stars, Apex, Runescape).

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Show a personal, time-limited bundle about 30-60 minutes in, right after a milestone, when the player is engaged enough to buy.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Price first offers like a cup of coffee (a couple of dollars); first purchases should be small.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Escalate bundle prices gradually at each new milestone, a couple of hours apart, without frustrating players.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Make the store easy to reach and route players to it for free rewards so they see every sale and event.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Keep the hard currency scarce; let players earn small amounts slowly so they buy meaningful items with real money.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Prefer cosmetic monetisation in competitive games; functional items that give power cause players to feel cheated and quit.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Never sell items that fix a loss or remove challenge; offer purchases with a positive outset, not a negative one.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[monetisation-other]]** Consider battle passes: free and paid reward tracks, paid tier unlocks many more milestone rewards and exclusives, proven in Fortnite.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use subscriptions alongside a free option to tease locked content; note Roblox lacks auto-renewal billing, reducing their benefit.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[monetisation-other]]** If using loot boxes, show all possible rewards and drop chances up front, or limit them to cosmetics with strong exclusivity.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track time to first purchase, time to repeat purchase, and the percentage of users converting between player stages.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Build custom monetisation tracking; Roblox base analytics do not show how your specific purchase systems perform.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid pay-to-win: if players think you over-monetised, they call the game P2W and leave.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not rely on paid entry as a growth lever; thumbs-up gains do not reliably correlate with success.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Plan for whales: about 75% of revenue typically comes from 1-5% of players, so retaining them decides a game's survival.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Study successful games' monetisation (Brawl Stars, Apex Legends, Runescape, Clash of Clans) and adapt the lessons.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship regular updates; live content lets you repeatedly offer medium and large purchases to engaged players.

## Games
- **Brawl Stars**: Gem hard currency buys cosmetics and loot boxes; targeted bundles convert users; rare characters add perceived worth without power.
- **Apex Legends**: Cosmetic-only loot boxes in a competitive game avoid P2W; timed exclusives and a battle pass drive spending.
- **Runescape**: Free base game with monthly premium subscription; bond trading teases premium time; trial hooks users.
- **Bloxburg**: Pay-to-join example on Roblox; cited as notable exception to free-to-play.
- **Vesteria**: Pay-to-join example on Roblox; cited as notable exception to free-to-play.
- **Clash of Clans**: Players slowly accrue gems through play, making small infrequent purchases attractive; scarcity keeps gems valuable.
- **Fortnite**: Battle pass model with free and paid reward tracks and exclusive paid rewards is a powerful monetisation tool.
