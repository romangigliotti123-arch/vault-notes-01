# Mass Uncopylocked | 35 free games and projects

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/mass-uncopylocked-35-free-games-and-projects/2880269) | Roblox DevForum | 955 views | 2024-03-18

## Summary
GamerKreep's post open-sources about 35 of his past Roblox games and projects (uncopylocked, editable in Studio or downloadable as .rbxl). Covers war clanning, tower defense, tycoon, obby, vehicle and simulator prototypes. Warns that sounds and animations must be re-uploaded, many scripts are broken, and some older files may contain viruses or backdoors.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/mass-uncopylocked-35-free-games-and-projects/2880269) **[[common-mistakes]]** Sounds and animations do not carry over from uncopylocked places; re-upload them under your own account before expecting them to work.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/mass-uncopylocked-35-free-games-and-projects/2880269) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Scan older community place files and assets for viruses or backdoors before use; check scripts for hidden require() or loadstring calls.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/mass-uncopylocked-35-free-games-and-projects/2880269) **[[testing-debugging-and-qa]]** Many of these projects have broken scripts; verify each system (weapons, terminals, inventory) works in Studio before building on it.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/mass-uncopylocked-35-free-games-and-projects/2880269) **[[physics-pathfinding-and-npc-ai]]** Constraints (e.g. train in Vehicle Testing) may not replicate from server to client; test vehicle constraints in multiplayer.

- **Robbing Simulator**: Open world stealing tycoon with police AI; mostly free models, custom bank and store robberies.
