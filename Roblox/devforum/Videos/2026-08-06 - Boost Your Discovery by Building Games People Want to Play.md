# Boost Your Discovery by Building Games People Want to Play

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042) | Roblox DevForum | 1,210 views | 2026-08-06

## Summary
Roblox creator update on Home/Recommended For You discovery. Home recommendations now weigh long-term retention and monetization. Low retention: fix core gameplay, first session, loops, return reasons. High retention: add sustainable, fair monetization. Strong in both gets broader distribution; single-area strength still gets impressions.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** If retention is low, fix core gameplay first: great first session, satisfying core loop, reasons to return, and iterate on feedback and analytics.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** If retention is high, add optional monetization (items, passes, dev products, subscriptions, rewarded ads) that is clear, useful, and tied to how players play.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042) **[[monetisation-other]]** Integrate purchase offers into progression and player goals rather than interrupting gameplay or gating enjoyment behind a paywall.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Do not chase a single metric; Home considers a range of signals measuring value over time.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check Creator Analytics > Acquisition > Home Recommendations and the Overview page; use Benchmark signals to spot low signals as opportunities.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Keep shipping content and improvements so the community sees ongoing value in staying engaged.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042) **[[monetisation-other]]** Design monetization to feel fair and transparent; lasting value comes from players who feel good about returning and spending.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Expect metric fluctuations for weeks after the algorithm change; most games see minimal impact on Home impressions.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Games strong in both retention and monetization get broader Home distribution; single-area strength still earns impressions.
