# Discovery on Roblox: Past, Present, and Future Vision

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) | Roblox DevForum | 742 views | 2024-02-28

## Summary
Roblox staff post explaining the Discovery system (Home, Matchmaking, Search, Notifications, Discover), its three principles and optimisation goals, year-over-year discovery stats, and a six-step path for new experiences to earn recommendations.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Build with high-quality metadata and media (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, videos); Roblox uses these inputs to match experiences to the right audience.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Expect an initial impression burst for assessment, then normalisation; buying a small set of ads can supply the signal the algorithm needs to start ranking you.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship thoughtful content updates, events and automatic translations; Roblox rewards engagement spikes from updates with increased impressions.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use Creator Analytics to track Home recommendation conversion rate, engagement, progression and monetisation trends to find improvements.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Introduce new products and price points via dev products, gamepasses or subscriptions to improve monetisation and retention.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use on-platform ads, community, friends and off-platform influencers to grow the initial audience before the algorithm assesses you.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Prioritise craftsmanship, quality, performance, device compatibility, localisation and good game design, since Discovery optimises for engagement and retention.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not rely on tags alone; Discovery weighs engagement (play-through, session length, retention, friend invites) and monetisation, not just metadata.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Keep join times low: server pre-warming makes ~70% of joins that would have waited instant, improving conversion from recommendations.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect discovery to be merit-based: invest in updates and events, since they correlate with audience reach.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) Daily Discovery impressions: **7.5B per day** (Total impressions served by Discovery across Roblox)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) Home share of traffic: **>90%** (Home's share of Roblox Discovery traffic)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) Discover share of traffic: **<5%** (Discover's share of Roblox traffic, being reworked)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) Recommendation eligibility threshold: **Top 1,000 in 2019 vs nearly every experience now** (Experience needed top 1,000 popularity to be recommended on Home in 2019)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) Unique experiences recommended YoY: **+56%** (Q4 2023 vs Q4 2022)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) New experiences in top 150 within 90 days: **+61%** (Q4 2023 vs Q4 2022)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) Time spent from recommended sessions YoY: **+9%** (Q4 2023 vs Q4 2022)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) Instant joins after pre-warming: **~70%** (Joins that would otherwise wait for server start, after late-October rollout)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) Peak concurrents experiences reached: **1m+ CCU** (Experiences reaching this over the last year, friends still able to play together)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111) Platform size in 2019: **1.5B experiences, 70M MAU** (Community scale when the top-1,000 threshold applied)

## Games
- **LifeTogether**: Small August 2023 ads buy gave initial signal; 11th update and Christmas TikTok/YouTube spike then up to 14 updates led to breakout growth.
- **Rainbow Friends**: Chapter 2 release on June 2, 2023 triggered a sharp increase in recommendation impressions.
