# Understand the Roblox algorithm

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) | Roblox DevForum | 127 views | 2023-12-31

## Summary
DevForum post by AtreyuDev on why some Roblox games go viral. Argues concept/packaging (icon and title) matters most, then retention (tutorial testing, short/mid/long-term goals, game feel, social play, daily rewards), then monetization (24hr starter packs, recurring purchases, whale-driven revenue). Largely opinion with a few anecdotes.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Find a hole in the market: an original concept that fills a gap (e.g. no good shooter after Arsenal died) can outperform trend clones.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make the icon simple, colorful and readable at a glance; it has only milliseconds to convince someone to click. Make it easy to swap for seasonal updates.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Time launches to trends: a Piggy-like clone launched while Piggy-likes were viral kept new players after the trend faded when it had real quality.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Show the game to friends and note whether mechanics are confusing, goals unclear, UI/map unpleasant, or instructions hard to follow.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Keep mechanics intuitive enough to skip a tutorial; if not possible, add a tutorial or simplify controls. Scrap projects that stay confusing.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give players goals at three horizons: short-term (next game/kill), mid-term (new weapon/skin/challenge via quests), long-term (season pass, best item).
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make the game feel reactive with sound effects and UI animations; without feedback (enemy damage, building progress) players may leave confused.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Build social play: friends keep players longer and make sessions more fun, and cosmetics sell better when players interact.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add daily rewards with prizes that are worth returning for, backed by enough content to keep players engaged.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Use a cheap starter pack that expires in 24 hours to create urgency and break new players' no-spend mindset.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[pricing]]** Keep early offers cheap: new players rarely spend much Robux in their first 10-30 minutes.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[monetisation-other]]** Design recurring purchases specific to your genre after the first offer; generic deals fail across genres.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[monetisation-other]]** Build a whale-friendly spending system (cosmetics, faster progress, loot boxes, cash) since about 2% of players may generate ~90% of revenue, per the author.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Roblox promotes games that earn the most Robux, so monetization strongly affects how much the algorithm pushes your game.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect luck to matter: optimize concept, retention and monetization so you are ready when a trend or market gap appears.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Be willing to scrap projects that do not work, as large studios like Supercell do.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) Revenue concentration: **~2% of players may make up ~90% of revenue (author's claim)**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) Starter pack expiry: **24 hours**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) Early spend window: **new players rarely spend much in first 10-30 minutes**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) Author's paid-access game earnings: **~70k Robux, built in a few weeks**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921) Adopt Me vs Brookhaven 2020: **similar player counts; Adopt Me earned almost 6x more**

## Games
- **Arsenal-like shooter (unnamed)**: Filled a shooter gap after Arsenal declined; original concept plus clear icon.
- **Arachnophobia-themed clicker (unnamed)**: Simple, instantly readable icon; launched during Piggy-like trend and kept players after trend faded.
- **Breaking Point, Murder Mystery 2, Gunfight Arena**: Player-vs-player interaction drives social retention and cosmetic sales.
- **Bedwars**: Competitive matches where players rarely leave before winning or losing.
- **Adopt Me**: Similar player count to Brookhaven but far higher earnings, showing monetization depth matters.
- **Murder Mystery 2**: Uses loot boxes to monetize high-spending players.
- **Welcome to Bloxburg**: Balanced economy where Robux buys cash for building; no gambling or pay-to-win.
- **Blox Fruits**: Monetizes via pay-to-fast mechanics, earning highly with ~15k players.
- **Brookhaven**: Comparison benchmark for player count versus revenue.
