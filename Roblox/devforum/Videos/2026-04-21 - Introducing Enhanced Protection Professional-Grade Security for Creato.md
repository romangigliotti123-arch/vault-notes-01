# Introducing Enhanced Protection: Professional-Grade Security for Creators

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-enhanced-protection-professional-grade-security-for-creators/4589347) | Roblox DevForum | 353 views | 2026-04-21

## Summary
Roblox DevForum/docs announcement of Enhanced Protection, an optional-but-recommended stricter 2-Step Verification mode requiring phishing-resistant methods (passkeys or hardware keys) for sign-in and 2SV challenges, to protect assets and group payouts. Explains prerequisites and recovery requirements.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-enhanced-protection-professional-grade-security-for-creators/4589347) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Enable Enhanced Protection in Settings > Security if you are a top creator or hold high Robux balances, to protect assets and group payouts.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-enhanced-protection-professional-grade-security-for-creators/4589347) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Use passkeys or hardware security keys instead of email OTP or authenticator codes; those are phishable and are disabled for 2SV under Enhanced Protection.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-enhanced-protection-professional-grade-security-for-creators/4589347) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Before enabling, add at least one passkey (device-bound or hardware key) and a verified email.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-enhanced-protection-professional-grade-security-for-creators/4589347) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Add a verified phone number or save backup codes so you can recover the account if passkeys are lost.
