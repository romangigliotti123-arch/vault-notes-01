# Boost Your Discovery with the Improved Recommended For You Algorithm and Analytics for Creators

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) | Roblox DevForum | 635 views | 2025-03-31

## Summary
Roblox DevForum announcement on the improved Recommended For You (RFY) discovery algorithm and the Home Recommendations analytics tab. Covers the RFY signals (qPTR, 7-day playtime/play days/spend days/Robux per user, intentional co-play), how to read the new dashboard, and FAQ on why impressions drop. December 2025 update changes co-play counting in reserved servers and attribution fixes.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Improve qPTR by focusing on thumbnail personalization: show new content or gameplay, keep multiple thumbnail variations active, avoid overly similar thumbnails.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Design gameplay that motivates players to play together; add invite prompts and private server options to drive intentional co-play signal.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Optimize all RFY signals together (retention, engagement, monetization); focus on a high-quality core experience rather than gaming individual signals.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Recommendation benefit is capped at the first 60 minutes of daily playtime per user per experience; don't expect extra reach from longer sessions.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check Analytics > Acquisition > Home Recommendations tab; signal changes usually show in impressions a few days later, so compare signal dips to impression drops with a lag.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Treat signals below benchmark as opportunities; if 7-day playtime and play days are low, improve the onboarding funnel to bring players back.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Target long-term retention: RFY is designed to optimize for players who keep returning, so retention matters more than one-off plays.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Expect a temporary QPTR drop when impressions surge during initial discovery; it should stabilise as traffic normalises. Monitor after Home surges.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Keep updates consistent and keep experience details accurate; these support discovery and loyal community building.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Most signals update within 24 hours in analytics and the algorithm, so you can measure the effect of changes quickly.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Account for seasonality (weekly peaks on Saturdays, summer/back-to-school, holidays) before blaming your game for an impression drop.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't assume larger experiences win: signals are per-user averages, so small experiences with highly engaged users are not disadvantaged.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) **[[roblox-platform-changes]]** December 2025: play sessions in reserved servers now count as intentional co-play; clicks and plays are attributed only from RFY for all signals.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) Home share of traffic: **over 90% of traffic begins on Home** (Roblox statement on Home as discovery source)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) Daily recommendation playtime cap: **first 60 min per user per experience per day** (Playtime beyond this gives no recommendation benefit)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) 7 day playtime per user cap: **max 60 min per user per experience per day** (Counted in the 7 day playtime signal)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) Signal update latency: **within 24 hours** (Most signals in analytics and algorithm)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) Signal-to-impression lag: **a few days** (Example: QPTR dip Feb 28 led to impression drop Mar 3)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-with-the-improved-recommended-for-you-algorithm-and-analytics-for-creators/3587441) RFY signals list: **qPTR, 7d playtime/user, 7d play days/user, 7d spend days/user, 7d Robux/user, 7d intentional co-play days/user** (Six signals in the RFY algorithm)
