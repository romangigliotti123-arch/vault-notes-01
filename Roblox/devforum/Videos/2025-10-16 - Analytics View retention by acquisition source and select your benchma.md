# Analytics: View retention by acquisition source and select your benchmark set

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-retention-by-acquisition-source-and-select-your-benchmark-set/4010157) | Roblox DevForum | 233 views | 2025-10-16

## Summary
Roblox Analytics update (Oct 16, 2025): D1/D7/D30 retention can be broken down by acquisition source on Experience Overview and Retention pages, to separate traffic-mix shifts from real retention changes. Also adds switchable benchmark sets (similar experiences vs genre) and clarifies that ads don't harm organic Home recommendation distribution, which depends on organic players' engagement.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-retention-by-acquisition-source-and-select-your-benchmark-set/4010157) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Break D1/D7/D30 retention down by acquisition source to check whether an overall drop is caused by a shift in traffic mix (e.g. more 'Other' external-link users).
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-retention-by-acquisition-source-and-select-your-benchmark-set/4010157) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use the Home recommendation signals analytics to diagnose organic distribution, not ad performance, since ads don't affect Home recommendation distribution.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-retention-by-acquisition-source-and-select-your-benchmark-set/4010157) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ads can accelerate consideration for organic distribution and help solve the cold-start problem, but won't fix declining organic engagement.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-retention-by-acquisition-source-and-select-your-benchmark-set/4010157) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Organic distribution depends on engagement of players who arrive via Home Recommendations; focus on engaging gameplay, fresh content, player return, and accurate metadata.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-retention-by-acquisition-source-and-select-your-benchmark-set/4010157) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** After distribution rises, expect QPTR, 7-day playtime, play days, Robux spend and co-play to fluctuate before settling at a new baseline; this is normal.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-retention-by-acquisition-source-and-select-your-benchmark-set/4010157) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Benchmarks (similar experiences vs genre) are for comparison only and don't change recommendation impressions; the scorecard will move to 7-day averages to smooth daily noise.
