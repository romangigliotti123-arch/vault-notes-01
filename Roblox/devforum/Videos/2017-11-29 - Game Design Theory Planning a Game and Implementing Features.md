# Game Design Theory: Planning a Game and Implementing Features

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) | Roblox DevForum | 267 views | 2017-11-29

## Summary
A 2017 DevForum guide (TechSpectrum/DieSoft) giving a staged game design process for Roblox: planning questions, rough-draft concept building, review of playtest feedback, refurbishing and pitching, beta testing, then maintenance in the final stage.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Before building, answer: target audience, desired learning curve, what differentiates your game, player goals and rewards, and the main anchoring feature.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't focus on monetisation early; spend that time designing the anchor features of the game instead.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Don't plan every feature upfront; build only what the pitch needs and add features once the concept shows potential.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build the concept as a rough draft: plain boxes are fine, and aim for a working idea over clean code since it is a prototype.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** After about a week on a feature, check whether it is still fun or has become more trouble than it is worth.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Get friends to play early, then ask how hard it was to learn, their favourite part, and whether they would play again.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** If players don't engage, diagnose the cause: a steep learning curve may need a tutorial, and too many stats may need trimming.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Reward the behaviour you want rather than punishing other play styles, e.g. reward stealth rather than penalising aggression.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Keep players longer by playing with friends and rewarding goals; find what drives the most activity and lean into it.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[community-and-discord]]** Give players a feedback channel such as a Discord webhook so they can report issues directly.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Add an analytics tool such as GameAnalytics to see where players spend time and money.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[business-and-payouts]]** When pitching to developers, list project name, short summary, pitch, team, open roles, payment type, contact info, and visuals; avoid 'contact me privately'.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[studio-business-funding-and-publishing]]** Do not hire paid builders or scripters unless the concept truly requires professionals; free resources and friends can cover basics.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Share images and videos of the concept early; avoid secret projects because visibility helps attract collaborators.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Avoid misleading 'Alpha' or 'Beta' labels that stay for months; once the game is stable, focus on maintenance and updates without wiping player data.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Don't rush; a rushed project leads to unforeseen problems and a trend alone cannot keep a disorganised game alive.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) Pitch summary length: **No more than about 5 to 7 sentences**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) Feature check interval: **About one week after finishing a feature**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838) Builder/scripter hiring example: **50k Robux mentioned as an example to avoid**
