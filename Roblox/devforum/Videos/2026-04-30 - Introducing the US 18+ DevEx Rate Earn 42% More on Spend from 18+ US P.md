# Introducing the US 18+ DevEx Rate: Earn 42% More on Spend from 18+ US Players

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) | Roblox DevForum | 4,534 views | 2026-04-30

## Summary
Roblox staff announcement of the US 18+ DevEx rate: $0.54 per 100 Earned Robux (up from $0.38, +42%) for age-checked 18+ US players' spend in eligible games, starting June 8, 2026. Covers eligibility (R15 or qualifying custom avatars, or no avatars), the Age Check API, Paid Access inclusion, and Roblox Plus interaction.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) **[[monetisation-other]]** Check eligibility in Creator Dashboard; eligible games get the 18+ US rate automatically with no application needed.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) **[[monetisation-other]]** Eligible spend includes game passes, developer products, Robux subscriptions, private servers and Paid Access. Avatar and Marketplace items do not qualify.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Avatar items do not count toward the 18+ rate; do not rely on them for this earning boost.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Eligible avatar setups: R15 only, or always-custom human rig with 15+ joints distributed human-like, or always-custom non-human rig, or no visible player characters.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Use the Age Check API: Player:PromptAgeCheck() on a deliberate player action, not on spawn; check Player.AgeChecked.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Prompt age check only once per player per 15 minutes per session; extra calls are silently dropped.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not gate core gameplay behind AgeChecked; console and VR cannot complete age check, so gate optional rewards or zones instead.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) **[[cross-platform-and-console]]** Console and VR do not currently support the age check flow; plan fallback experiences for those players.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) **[[monetisation-other]]** Treat Unchecked as a normal, sometimes permanent state; ensure the game still works fully for those players.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track 18+ US earnings as a separate line item in Creator Analytics to see what drives payouts.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Target the 18-34 cohort with genres, mechanics and high-fidelity visual styles; this audience is growing 50%+ YoY in the US.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Standout Games sort gives prime Home placement to novel games; genre-focused and first-time-player sorts are being tested.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Roblox is exploring longer-horizon engagement signals, so games that retain players long-term may be favoured in discovery.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) **[[monetisation-other]]** Plus incentive and the US 18+ rate are not additive; you receive whichever is higher, which is the 18+ rate.
