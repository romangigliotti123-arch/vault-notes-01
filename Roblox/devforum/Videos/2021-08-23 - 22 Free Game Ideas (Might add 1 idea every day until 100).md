## Summary
A community post listing 22 original Roblox game concepts (simulators, tycoons, FPS, racing, RPG, card trading, etc.), each with a short gameplay pitch and genre tags. It is a brainstorm list rather than a how-to; the author invites feedback on any idea used.

## Games
- **Motor Manic/Mania**: Idea: driving simulator where destroying structures and NPCs earns currency for vehicle upgrades.
- **CRASH!**: Idea: vehicle ramming PvP with upgrades and attachments; modes 1v1, 2v2, 4v4, FFA.
- **Rocket Rumble/Blast Off!**: Idea: gather varied planet materials via different methods (fishing, mining, combat) to fuel rocket travel.
