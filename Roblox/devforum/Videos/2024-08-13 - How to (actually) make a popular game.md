# How to (actually) make a popular game

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) | Roblox DevForum | 104 views | 2024-08-13

## Summary
Argues Roblox's algorithm weighs engagement (playtime, retention), CTR (icon/thumbnail/title), and marketing. Gives playtime benchmarks, advises advertising over social media/YouTubers for launch, and outlines ad settings and a 5-7 day abandon checkpoint.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Only trust playtime and CTR data once you have at least a few thousand daily visits; smaller samples are unreliable.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Focus on why players leave and what feels boring; the author says this is the best way to raise playtime.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Make the game replayable (achievements, badges, speedrun timers, difficulties, rebirths); a strong core loop drives retention on its own.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add daily rewards as a retention aid, though the author calls them a small trick compared with core loop replayability.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Start from an interesting game idea so the icon and thumbnail are easy to make eye-catching; generic ideas are hard to market.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Prefer Roblox ad campaigns over social media or YouTuber outreach for release; the author says influencer spikes fade within a day or two.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Spend about 10,000 R$ on sponsorship spread slowly over 10-15 days so the algorithm can pick the game up.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Set a low max bid (author cites 0.02 at time of writing); Roblox refunds unspent campaign budget.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Advertise heavily in the first three days to learn which device and age group engages, then target that audience.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Sponsor in 2-4 day intervals; if metrics are still bad by day 5-7, consider abandoning the project.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Collect good playtime and CTR data early, since Roblox decides how much to promote a game in its first 10-25 days.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) Average session playtime: **<5 min really bad; 5-7 avg; 7-9 good; 9-15 excellent; >15 crazy good** (Author's rough benchmarks; not hard data)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) Minimum daily visits for reliable metrics: **a few thousand** (Needed before trusting playtime or CTR)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) Recommended sponsorship budget: **10,000 R$ over 10-15 days** (Distributed slowly to let algorithm pick up game)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) Max bid: **0.02** (Low bid cited as best at time of writing (2024))
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) Sponsor interval: **every 2-4 days** (Abandon if day 5-7 metrics still bad)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791) Post-release promotion window: **first 10-25 days** (Roblox decides promotion level in this window)
