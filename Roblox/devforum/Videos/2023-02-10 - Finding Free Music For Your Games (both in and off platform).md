# Finding Free Music For Your Games (both in and off platform)

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) | Roblox DevForum | 50 views | 2023-02-10

## Summary
Guide to finding free, legally usable music and sound effects for Roblox and off-platform games. Covers filtering the Toolbox (with a warning that its search and settings are unreliable and AI/DistroKid tracks pollute results), license basics (avoid NC, CC0 is true public domain, royalty-free does not mean free to use), free music sources, and artists with CC-licensed tracks.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Search the Toolbox audio by mood words rather than exact track names; exact names return few results or the same track everyone uses.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Filter Toolbox audio to tracks over 1 minute long to exclude sound effects, since audio search cannot exclude SFX by name.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Filter Toolbox results by creator to avoid unlabeled AI-generated DistroKid tracks; Monstercat and APM were considered safer at the time.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use the web audio dashboard (create.roblox.com/store/audio) instead of the Toolbox, since the Toolbox settings filters were reported as broken.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not assume 'royalty free' or 'copyright free' labels grant usage rights; always check the actual license, since these phrases are not legally binding alone.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not use Creative Commons NC (non-commercial) licensed tracks on Roblox, because any game with premium payouts or paid content counts as commercial.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Free games on Roblox are still commercial if premium payouts are enabled, since premium payouts cannot be turned off, so NC licenses are unsafe even in free games.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Give proper credit to creators even when the license (such as CC0) does not require attribution, to encourage future permission to reuse their work.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use TLDRLegal to get a brief summary of license conditions before using a track.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** For off-platform music, check OpenGameArt, itch.io (music tag), Incompetech (with attribution), and Freesound (filter by license) for usable tracks and SFX.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** On SoundCloud, search, select tracks, then choose 'to use commercially' under the listen-to options to limit results to commercially usable tracks; still verify the license.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** When you like a track, look up its album on an external site and browse the album, since the Toolbox album feature returned random results.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not rely on unauthorized uploaded MP3s; the author notes most user-uploaded audio is stolen or made private, and stolen music risks DMCA takedowns.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use RbxMusic or AudioScape plugins to search the Roblox audio library by genre, album, artist, and length, and to see sound licenses.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) Toolbox audio length filter: **over 1 minute to mostly exclude sound effects**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/finding-free-music-for-your-games-both-in-and-off-platform/2179490) Commercial use threshold: **any revenue (paid access, microtransactions, paid DLC) makes a game commercial**

## Tools
Roblox Toolbox audio search, create.roblox.com/store/audio, RbxMusic plugin, AudioScape, TLDRLegal, Incompetech, OpenGameArt, itch.io
