# New Benchmarking & Insights [Analytics Dashboards]

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-benchmarking-insights-analytics-dashboards/2025790) | Roblox DevForum | 195 views | 2022-10-17

## Summary
Announcement of updated Creator Analytics Dashboards benchmarks: KPI benchmarks ranked into 5 tiers from top 10K experiences by playtime, updated daily. Also covers new ToS acceptance requirement and retirement of the Monthly Game Stats Data report (no reports after September 2022; downloads available until November 30, 2022).

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-benchmarking-insights-analytics-dashboards/2025790) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use the Engagement, Retention and Monetization dashboard benchmarks to compare your experience's KPIs against others; benchmarks update daily.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-benchmarking-insights-analytics-dashboards/2025790) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Improving a KPI can move your benchmark tier higher, so track each KPI's tier as a progress signal.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-benchmarking-insights-analytics-dashboards/2025790) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Switch from the Monthly Game Stats Data report to the new dashboards, which have more timely data and better benchmarks.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-benchmarking-insights-analytics-dashboards/2025790) Benchmark tiers: **5 tiers** (KPI values ranked against top 10K experiences by playtime)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-benchmarking-insights-analytics-dashboards/2025790) Benchmark update frequency: **Daily** (Benchmarks refresh daily to show trends)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-benchmarking-insights-analytics-dashboards/2025790) Monthly Stats report retirement: **No reports from September 2022 onward** (Previous reports downloadable until November 30, 2022)
