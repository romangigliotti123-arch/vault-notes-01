# The Art of Robux Getting

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) | Roblox DevForum | 255 views | 2022-08-14

## Summary
Essay on earning Robux as a Roblox developer: Robux economy (devex ~0.35 cents/Robux at $350 per 100k), inflation favoring spending over saving, picking a vocation and niche, networking and mentors, starting by joining established teams before solo projects, keeping budgets tight, finishing simple games, and a 50:50 dev/ads budget split.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Avoid DevEx cashing out when possible; spend earned Robux on ads and services instead, since DevEx converts at a loss versus buying Robux.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Keep the current DevEx rate in mind (author cites 100k Robux = $350) and compare your Roblox earnings against local minimum wage.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Invest Robux into games rather than saving or hoarding them, since Robux held long-term loses value as developer-side Robux inflates.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Pick a vocation you are genuinely good at and enjoy; your location (genre/community) can change later, your vocation cannot.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Choose a genre where your skill is scarce and useful; the author was the only scripter in showcase and roleplay communities and got constant work.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Avoid pioneering genres with no established community; it is riskier than entering an existing one.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid building for a niche that excludes most of its own community; do not purposely limit scalability of the audience.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Aim for a finished, polished simple core loop first rather than a feature-heavy game; complexity can come after you have a successful title.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[team-and-budget]]** Get quotes from every developer you plan to hire before starting, so you do not run out of funding midway.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[team-and-budget]]** Identify your game's unique selling point and put most of the budget into it (e.g. thumbnails/icon for simulators, building for roleplay, scripting for RPGs).
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[team-and-budget]]** Split the budget roughly 50:50 between development and ads when starting out, and keep a large buffer for emergencies.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[team-and-budget]]** Hire developers who have shipped a finished game, not only developers who show systems they have built.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Offer a cheap gateway gamepass or developer product so players become invested and are more likely to pay more later.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Prefer long-term team roles and passive revenue streams (own games, revenue shares) over short-term low-budget commissions, which rarely ship and give little credit.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Research Roblox ad systems for your genre and run analytics on every ad campaign before launching a game, clothing group, or UGC.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[team-and-budget]]** Avoid debt entirely and only spend what you can afford; keep a fixed percentage of earnings for personal spending.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not raise gamepass prices expecting player-side inflation; the author says gamepass prices have hardly moved because only developers, not players, get richer.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[community-and-discord]]** Be polite and professional with everyone and avoid public arguments, since reputation travels and drives future work referrals.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Open multiple communication and portfolio channels (Discord, Twitter, Reddit, etc.) with a clear, concise public portfolio; avoid flashy presentation.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Read up on NDAs and employment contracts; international contracts are rarely enforceable in practice, so weigh that risk before working cross-border.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) DevEx rate: **100k Robux = $350 (0.35 cents per Robux), as of writing** (Author's stated rate; says to keep it updated)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) Ad cost to reach players: **1000 Robux can get hundreds of people to see a game** (Author's claim about Roblox ad efficiency)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) Earnings milestone difficulty: **First 100k Robux hardest; next hundreds of thousands and millions easier** (Author's claim about compounding earnings)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) Dev vs ad budget split: **50:50 when starting out** (Recommended split of project budget between development and ads)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578) Author's track record: **A few million Robux earned; ~15 million joins contributed to** (Author's own stated experience)
