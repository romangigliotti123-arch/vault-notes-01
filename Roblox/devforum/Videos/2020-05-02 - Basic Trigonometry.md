# Basic Trigonometry

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/basic-trigonometry/554329) | Roblox DevForum | 273 views | 2020-05-02

## Summary
Beginner trigonometry tutorial for Roblox developers. Covers right-triangle side naming, labeling conventions, SOH CAH TOA, inverse trig, special triangles (45-45-90, 30-60-90), the Pythagorean identity, Law of Sines and Cosines (including SAS handling), csc/sec/cot, and Roblox Luau math function equivalents (math.rad/deg, Vector3 Dot).

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/basic-trigonometry/554329) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Roblox math.sin/cos/tan take radians; convert degrees with math.rad(). Inverse functions return radians; convert with math.deg().
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/basic-trigonometry/554329) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Use Vector3:Dot(other) divided by magnitudes to get the cosine of the angle between two vectors (|a||b|cos(Θ)).
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/basic-trigonometry/554329) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Law of Cosines only applies when the known angle sits between the two known sides; for SAS, compute the missing side first, then Law of Sines.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/basic-trigonometry/554329) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** In SSA/Law of Sines cases, solve for the angle opposite the smaller known side first to avoid picking the wrong angle.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/basic-trigonometry/554329) Angle mode: **Calculators must be set to degrees, not radians, for these answers** (Practice example: cos(25°) gives h = 5.5 when in deg mode)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/basic-trigonometry/554329) Triangle angle sum: **180 degrees** (Used to find the third angle in a triangle)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/basic-trigonometry/554329) Special triangle ratios: **45-45-90: hyp = x√2; 30-60-90: sides x, x√3, 2x** (Side ratios for special right triangles)
