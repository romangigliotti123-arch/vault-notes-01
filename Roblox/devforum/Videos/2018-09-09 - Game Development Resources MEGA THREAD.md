## Summary
A DevForum mega-thread by RuizuKun_Dev listing plugins, YouTube channels, Roblox-related channels, and DevForum threads/guides for scripting, UI, game design and monetisation resources. Mostly a link directory; few actionable lessons beyond saving offline before testing and publishing.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-resources-mega-thread/177780) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Always save your game offline before playtesting and publishing; Studio crashes or stops responding often, so do not trust it to keep your work.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-resources-mega-thread/177780) **[[code-architecture-and-frameworks]]** Learn OOP and uni-directional data flow in Lua via the Kurdiez Scripting series (#3-#5) to structure larger code.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-resources-mega-thread/177780) **[[optimization-and-streaming]]** Read 'The Basics of Basic Optimization' and the efficiency thread; compress PNGs with Save for Roblox before uploading to reduce size.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-resources-mega-thread/177780) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Remove malicious scripts from free models using Ro-Defender before publishing; free models often contain virus-like scripts.
