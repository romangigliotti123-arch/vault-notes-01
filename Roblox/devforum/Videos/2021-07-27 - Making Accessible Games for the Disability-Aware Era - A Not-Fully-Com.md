# Making Accessible Games for the Disability-Aware Era - A Not-Fully-Comprehensive Guide by an Actual Disabled Developer

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) | Roblox DevForum | 392 views | 2021-07-27

## Summary
A disabled Roblox developer's compiled guide to making games accessible: visual (contrast, brightness, colorblind palettes, animation/camera-shake toggles, size scaling), auditory (per-category volume, mute, captions, unique sounds), motor (keybinds, toggle vs held buttons), and other rules (anti-ableism, triggers, tutorials, calm areas, logs). Explicitly not a scripting tutorial.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Build contrast/saturation sliders with normal in the centre, lowest on the far left and highest on the far right, so lower contrast is available as well as higher.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Provide brightness sliders; dark scenes hide content for low-vision players while bright ones cause eyestrain or seizures. Don't rely on a single default light source.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Use Adobe's Color Accessibility tool to pick UI palettes safe for every colour-blindness type, so colourblind players don't need a special mode.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Offer a colour-lock style option that limits team/ink colours to maximally contrasting pairs, as Splatoon does, for colourblind players to tell allies from enemies.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Add toggles to disable camera shake, view bobbing, particles (minimal/decreased/all) and animation; reduce overstimulation, motion sickness and seizure risk, and lag.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Add a health/epilepsy warning before play if you cannot remove strobing or camera-shake effects, and avoid strobing lights where possible.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Provide size adjustment for cursor, text, chat box and GUI elements (scale, width, height) so low-vision and dyslexic players can read and track them.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[sound-and-music]]** Offer individual volume sliders per sound category (ambience, music, weapons, footsteps, enemies) rather than only a master volume.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[sound-and-music]]** Add mute buttons for specific sounds, since players with noise sensitivity or trauma need to fully silence triggers, not just lower them.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[sound-and-music]]** Make sounds distinct per object (e.g. straw vs glass drinks) so players can identify items by audio, which also helps blind players.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[sound-and-music]]** Consider directional audio captions that indicate what sound is playing and whether it comes from left or right, since captions are mostly prewritten in games.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Support customisable keybinds; ergonomic and RSI players often need non-default layouts, and Roblox does not yet provide this out of the box.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Offer toggle alternatives to held buttons (toggle sprint, crouch, fly, emotes) and avoid requiring holding multiple keys at once or repeated presses.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't use bright, flashing or excessive light sources as the only visibility solution; too-dark scenes with no light source are as harmful as too-bright ones.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[community-and-discord]]** Test your game's controls with disabled players and ask for feedback; the author reports tested controls with no complaints when designed for reduced mobility.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[common-mistakes]]** Write and enforce anti-ableism rules in your game; ableist language is a bannable offence under Roblox ToS, but minor ableist jokes should be stopped too.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Keep calm areas and quest/activity logs so overstimulated or distracted players can rest and recover track of objectives.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make tutorials available and skippable/re-readable, since players with memory, attention or processing disabilities need to revisit instructions.

- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) Cursor size: **about 2x default for the author** (Author's own cursor setting due to poor vision)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) Colour types of colourblindness: **4 primary types** (Deuteranopia, protanopia, tritanopia, achromatopsia)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919) Tetrachromacy colour gain: **about 1.5x more colours (author); up to 100x claimed** (Author's own partial tetrachromacy; claimed upper figure in general)

## Games
- **Minecraft**: Benchmark: separate view bobbing, particles, FOV and distortion sliders; per-sound volume; chat scale/width/height; toggle sneak/sprint.
- **Splatoon 1 & 2**: Colour Lock setting limits ink colours to maximally contrasting palettes for colourblind players.
- **Restaurant Tycoon 2**: Has a day/night cycle toggle and volume changer, but NPC animations can't be disabled (poor example).
- **Gem Galaxies**: Great: ambient light changer and toggle run, fly and emotes; earlier GUI caused eye strain.
- **3008**: Good: disables camera shake and has a keybind changer; toggle crouch but no toggle sprint.
- **Wings of Fire (Early Access & Seven Throne)**: Night vision for some species makes other species hard to navigate in the dark; toggle fly and emotes.
- **Minus Decendance / Breaking Point**: Poor brightness design: flashlight or player light sources are the only visibility option.
- **Pet Zoo (Tycoon) / Dreamcore | Weirdcore Home**: Good: day/night cycle toggle and a lantern light source respectively.
- **Rogue Lineage / Horrific Housing**: Camera shake with no way to disable it (cited as a problem).
