# Analytics [Similar Experience Benchmarks & Broader Access]

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-similar-experience-benchmarks-broader-access/2210285) | Roblox DevForum | 330 views | 2023-03-06

## Summary
Roblox analytics update (March 2023): adds similar-experience (genre) benchmarks in the 50th–90th percentile range for per-user metrics (D1/D7/D30 retention, average session time, payer conversion, ARPPU, ARPDAU), and extends analytics access to all experiences with 10+ DAU over the past 7 days.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-similar-experience-benchmarks-broader-access/2210285) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Compare D1, D7, D30 retention, session time, payer conversion, ARPPU and ARPDAU against similar-genre benchmarks, not all-Roblox averages, to judge performance.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-similar-experience-benchmarks-broader-access/2210285) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Read benchmarks as percentiles: 50th–90th range shows where your experience sits among similar experiences.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-similar-experience-benchmarks-broader-access/2210285) Example D1 retention benchmark range (50th–90th percentile): **12.4% – 24.1%** (Similar experiences, per Roblox example)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-similar-experience-benchmarks-broader-access/2210285) Analytics access threshold: **10+ DAU** (Daily active users over past 7 days)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-similar-experience-benchmarks-broader-access/2210285) Benchmark percentile range shown: **50th – 90th percentile** (Similar experience benchmarks for per-user metrics)
