# Jespone's guide to animations

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/jespones-guide-to-animations/225752) | Roblox DevForum | 847 views | 2019-01-15

## Summary
Jespone's guide to Roblox animation. Covers the 12 Disney animation principles applied to Roblox rigs (squash & stretch, anticipation, staging, pose-to-pose, follow-through, slow in/out, arcs, secondary action, timing, exaggeration, solid drawing/avoiding twinning, appeal), easing styles and directions, and practice tips. Recommends Roblox's default animator, the old animator, Blender, and Moon Animator.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/jespones-guide-to-animations/225752) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Start with Roblox's default or old animator, then try Moon Animator or Blender once basic animations like a wave are comfortable.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/jespones-guide-to-animations/225752) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Add anticipation before every action (e.g. squash before a jump, wind-up before a punch) so players read what is about to happen.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/jespones-guide-to-animations/225752) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Apply follow-through and overlapping action to secondary parts like capes, hair and limbs, but not the root, and avoid making it too flowy on a rig.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/jespones-guide-to-animations/225752) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Use easing (Sine, Quad etc.) rather than linear keyframes; linear motion looks robotic. Use In/Out/InOut/OutIn directions to set speed through the motion.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/jespones-guide-to-animations/225752) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Avoid twinning: keyframes in straight lines with no inbetweens. Vary timing and add secondary actions to break up uniform motion.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/jespones-guide-to-animations/225752) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Move character through key poses first (pose-to-pose), then refine the transitions between them.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/jespones-guide-to-animations/225752) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Exaggerate key poses for game feel; realistic motion often reads as weak in games.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/jespones-guide-to-animations/225752) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Add arcs to limb and body movement; motion without arcs looks robotic.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/jespones-guide-to-animations/225752) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Improve by repeating old animations, studying films and other games, copying references, polishing every animation, and trying varied styles.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/jespones-guide-to-animations/225752) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Practice with cinematics and long scenes (fights, running, flying) as a strong skill-building exercise.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/jespones-guide-to-animations/225752) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Use the Ease Visualizer (GreenSock) to understand easing curves before animating.
