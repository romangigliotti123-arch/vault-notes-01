# [Pilot] Create and Sell Animation Packs and Emotes on Marketplace

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-animation-packs-and-emotes-on-marketplace/4684642) | Roblox DevForum | 1,344 views | 2026-06-15

## Summary
Roblox pilot announcement opening Animation Packs (7 locomotion clips replacing default movement) and Emotes/Animation Packs with additional joints to creators via a first-come pilot application. Covers eligibility, hold period, FAQs on Studio use, reused animations, game compatibility, takedowns, version locking, and R15/R6 support.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-animation-packs-and-emotes-on-marketplace/4684642) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Applications for the animation pilot were first-come, first-served and closed once capacity was reached; existing active UGC Creators are auto-accepted.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-animation-packs-and-emotes-on-marketplace/4684642) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Author animation clips in external tools (Blender, Maya, Moon Animator), but final upload and bundle assembly must be done in Roblox Studio.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-animation-packs-and-emotes-on-marketplace/4684642) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** All 7 clips in an Animation Pack must be originally authored; packs reusing default Roblox animations will fail upload.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-animation-packs-and-emotes-on-marketplace/4684642) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Animation packs apply only in games using the default 'Player Choice' animation setting; forced standard animation games will not play the pack.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-animation-packs-and-emotes-on-marketplace/4684642) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Published pack clips are version-locked; plan to get animations right before publishing since they cannot be updated later.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-animation-packs-and-emotes-on-marketplace/4684642) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Taken-down packs are removed from Marketplace and all buyer inventories with full refunds; appeals go through the Appeals Portal.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-animation-packs-and-emotes-on-marketplace/4684642) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** R15 emotes with additional joints work on R15 avatars without those joints in games with Retargeting enabled; R6 support is not yet available.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-animation-packs-and-emotes-on-marketplace/4684642) **[[cross-platform-and-console]]** Animation Packs (R15) require 7 clips: Idle, Walk, Run, Jump, Fall, Climb, Swim.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-animation-packs-and-emotes-on-marketplace/4684642) Animation Pack clip count: **7 locomotion clips (Idle, Walk, Run, Jump, Fall, Climb, Swim)**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-animation-packs-and-emotes-on-marketplace/4684642) Pilot application deadline: **Monday, June 29 (2026)**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-animation-packs-and-emotes-on-marketplace/4684642) Eligibility hold period: **Listing/selling eligible by end of June for opted-in creators**
