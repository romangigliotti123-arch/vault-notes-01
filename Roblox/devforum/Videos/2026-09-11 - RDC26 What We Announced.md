# RDC26: What We Announced

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880) | Roblox DevForum | 958 views | 2026-09-11

## Summary
Roblox staff post recapping RDC26 announcements: platform stats, publishing changes (Highly Engaged threshold 250 to 100, fewer bans, simpler age questionnaire), testing/support/observability tools, pre-roll ads, Wallet/Card payouts, web play, Studio tools, avatar and engine upgrades, with availability timelines.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Highly Engaged Player threshold drops to 100 (from 250) in November; Roblox expects bans for well-intentioned creators to fall by nearly 40%.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Early testing (late 2026) lets you invite up to 10 age-checked friends of similar ages to test before publishing.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Developer push notifications (late 2026) for opted-in players enable async turn-based play, e.g. alerting when it's their turn.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Pre-roll video ads (coming soon) need no code; Roblox optimizes delivery and shares revenue. Roblox Plus subscribers won't see them.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Roblox Wallet (late 2026) pays earnings automatically every business day in USD, with per-purchase transaction records.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880) **[[cross-platform-and-console]]** Share the game's Details Page URL on YouTube, X or Discord; players can start on web in Chrome with no install (late 2026).
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880) **[[testing-debugging-and-qa]]** Asset Moderation feedback (late 2026) gives instant detailed issues during development so you can fix them before publishing.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880) **[[version-control-and-tooling]]** Branch and Merge (early 2027) lets isolated Place branches be merged with property and script-line conflict resolution.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880) **[[optimization-and-streaming]]** Minimum draw distance rises to 500 studs (from 200) even on low-end Android at quality level 1 (late 2026).
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880) **[[testing-debugging-and-qa]]** Observability Platform (late 2026) traces client sessions and logs with troubleshooting insights inside Studio and Creator Hub.
