# Update on Verified Badge Requirements for Experiences

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-on-verified-badge-requirements-for-experiences/4070915) | Roblox DevForum | 1,643 views | 2025-11-14

## Summary
Roblox DevForum update (Nov 2025) changing Verified Badge eligibility for experience contributors. Eligibility now covers experience owners and holders of a 10%+ automatic per-experience group payout, for experiences with over 1M playtime hours in the last 90 days. Covers FAQs on payout type, co-owners, DevEx, and display-name rules.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-on-verified-badge-requirements-for-experiences/4070915) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Verified Badge for experience contributors requires owner status or a 10%+ automatic per-experience group payout; manual/direct payouts do not qualify.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-on-verified-badge-requirements-for-experiences/4070915) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Selling the Verified badge violates the rules and can lead to Roblox revoking status and moderation action on your account.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-on-verified-badge-requirements-for-experiences/4070915) **[[common-mistakes]]** Keep your display name identical to the one you had when first granted the badge; changing it removes eligibility until reverted and Support is contacted.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-on-verified-badge-requirements-for-experiences/4070915) **[[business-and-payouts]]** DevEx process and destination bank account do not affect Verified Badge eligibility; only group payout percentages count.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-on-verified-badge-requirements-for-experiences/4070915) Verified Badge playtime threshold: **>1M playtime hours in last 90 days** (Experience must exceed this to qualify for contributors)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-on-verified-badge-requirements-for-experiences/4070915) Minimum automatic group payout for eligibility: **10%+ per-experience automatic payout** (Recurring per-experience payout within owning group)
