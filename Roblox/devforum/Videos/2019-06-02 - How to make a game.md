# How to make a game

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-game/287493) | Roblox DevForum | 232 views | 2019-06-02

## Summary
Fly's 2019 handbook on starting a Roblox game: why make one (Robux-to-USD income math), game genre survey with named examples, storytelling and conceptual art, assembling a friend-based team, and the argument to publish early and accept criticism rather than stay in planning.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-game/287493) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Publish early, even if incomplete. Players give feedback and motivation; the author's team stalled for years until they launched a rough version.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-game/287493) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Expect your idea to already exist. Study the competitor that succeeded or failed and understand why, rather than abandoning the idea.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-game/287493) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Present an old concept in a new way rather than chasing wholly original ideas; creativity means good execution of familiar structures.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-game/287493) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Recruit collaborators from friends first, with each member specialising in a different area (building, scripting, etc.) to avoid overlap and discussion.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-game/287493) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Give builds a written story or lore first; storytelling-driven builds (e.g. After The Flash) were cited as captivating players.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-game/287493) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Create conceptual art summarising the game's ideas, context, style and art direction; reuse it across design stages and marketing.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-game/287493) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Model income as avg price x exchange rate x gamepass count x (paying-player rate x player count) to estimate revenue before building.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-game/287493) Robux exchange rate: **100K R$ = $350; 1M R$ = $3,500; 10M R$ = $32,000; 150M R$ = $525,000 (as of May 1, 2019)**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-game/287493) Example revenue model: **5 gamepasses at 100 R$, 10,000 monthly players, 5% buy: 35,000 R$/month (~$350 at 0.3 rate)**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-game/287493) Typical gamepass price: **R$300-500 average (author's note)**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-game/287493) Builders Club exchange factor: **0.3 used as rate for Builders Club users in author's calculation**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-game/287493) District 45 group growth: **Over 2000 group members in under one month; ~1000 visits; 85% rating (10% of map released)**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-game/287493) Swordburst scale (MMORPG leader): **772,920 favorites and 72M+ visits as of May 1, 2019**

## Games
- **Swordburst**: Largest Roblox MMORPG in 2019; MMO success relies on good mechanics and a vast world rather than graphics.
- **Vesteria**: Recent MMO example by berezaa; same lesson: mechanics and world size matter more than graphics.
- **After The Flash**: Roleplay game built on pre-written lore with constant additions; simple building style carried by story.
- **Downtown RP**: Roleplay hangout that many groups use for members; shows group-hangout demand in RP.
- **Jailbreak**: Virtual life example where the goal is fostering interpersonal relations between players.
- **Phantom Forces**: Prime example cited for the shooter genre.
- **Vehicle Simulator**: Cited as racing-adjacent because it centres on vehicles and shares similar features.
- **District 45**: Group-built map that reached 2000+ members quickly after partial public release.
