# New Publishing Requirements & Evaluation Process for Games

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-publishing-requirements-evaluation-process-for-games/4573166) | Roblox DevForum | 40,833 views | 2026-04-13

## Summary
Roblox staff announcement of new publishing requirements effective May 19 (2026): age check, 2FA, account age of 2+ days, and an active Plus or Premium subscription for all-ages publishing, plus a 1,000 Robux refundable publishing fee as an alternative. Games reaching all ages must first pass an evaluation trial with age-checked 16+ users. Also covers free Plus for eligible creators and the IARC rating transition.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-publishing-requirements-evaluation-process-for-games/4573166) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Complete age check, enable 2FA, and keep account in good standing before publishing to 16+ and Trusted Friends audiences; eligibility is rechecked on every publish.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-publishing-requirements-evaluation-process-for-games/4573166) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** To reach all ages, also be ID verified and hold an active Plus or Premium subscription, or pay the 1,000 Robux refundable publishing fee instead.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-publishing-requirements-evaluation-process-for-games/4573166) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** To speed the all-ages evaluation, regularly update the game, address user feedback, and promote it to users over 16, since those players trigger the trial.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-publishing-requirements-evaluation-process-for-games/4573166) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** For group games, the group owner must meet the Plus requirement; authorized members only need 2FA to publish.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-publishing-requirements-evaluation-process-for-games/4573166) **[[monetisation-other]]** Check eligibility for the free 6-month Plus grant: one owned game needs 100+ hours playtime in the last 30 days and no major Community Standards violations.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-publishing-requirements-evaluation-process-for-games/4573166) Publishing fee: **1,000 Robux per game, one-time, refundable (alternative to Plus/Premium for all-ages publishing)**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-publishing-requirements-evaluation-process-for-games/4573166) Account age for 16+ publishing: **at least 2 days on platform**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-publishing-requirements-evaluation-process-for-games/4573166) Free Plus grant for existing creators: **about 100,000 creators, 6 months, starting May 19**
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-publishing-requirements-evaluation-process-for-games/4573166) Free Plus eligibility threshold: **100 hours total playtime over last 30 days on an owned game**
