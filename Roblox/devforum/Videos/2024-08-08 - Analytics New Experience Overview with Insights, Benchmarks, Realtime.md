# Analytics: New Experience Overview with Insights, Benchmarks, Realtime

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-new-experience-overview-with-insights-benchmarks-realtime/3108840) | Roblox DevForum | 250 views | 2024-08-08

## Summary
Roblox DevForum announcement of the upgraded Experience Overview analytics page: week-over-week Insights (daily, 100+ DAU), a benchmark scorecard against similar experiences (engagement, monetization, acquisition metrics), a realtime panel, and achievements for milestones over the past 6 months.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-new-experience-overview-with-insights-benchmarks-realtime/3108840) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Focus on the key metric groups: engagement (average session time, D1, D7 retention), monetization (payer conversion rate, ARPPU), and acquisition (qualified play-through rate).
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-new-experience-overview-with-insights-benchmarks-realtime/3108840) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Compare your metrics to similar experiences via the benchmark scorecard to find which metric is lagging, rather than optimizing everything at once.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-new-experience-overview-with-insights-benchmarks-realtime/3108840) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** If engagement metrics like average session time or D1 retention lag benchmarks, use funnel analytics to improve the onboarding funnel.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-new-experience-overview-with-insights-benchmarks-realtime/3108840) **[[monetisation-other]]** If revenue drops while similar experiences grow, drill into monetization by platform, country and other dimensions to diagnose the issue.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-new-experience-overview-with-insights-benchmarks-realtime/3108840) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Benchmarks are not a direct discovery-algorithm signal; use them only as a comparison point for improving engagement, monetization and acquisition.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-new-experience-overview-with-insights-benchmarks-realtime/3108840) Insights availability: **Experiences with 100+ DAU; updated daily** (Week-over-week insight panel requires minimum daily active users)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-new-experience-overview-with-insights-benchmarks-realtime/3108840) Example revenue insight (illustrative): **Own revenue -21% vs similar experiences +15%** (Illustrative numbers from announcement, not real benchmarks)
