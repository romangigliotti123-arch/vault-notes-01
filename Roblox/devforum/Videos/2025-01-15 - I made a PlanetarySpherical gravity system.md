# I made a Planetary/Spherical gravity system

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-made-a-planetaryspherical-gravity-system/3383865) | Roblox DevForum | 104 views | 2025-01-15

## Summary
Devforum showcase by anirudh851 of a Roblox planetary/spherical gravity game: walking on spheres with per-planet gravity, custom swimming and underwater camera effects, rover and spaceship vehicles, combat with aliens, latitude-based sun alignment and distance-based brightness. Mostly a showcase; little actionable advice.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-made-a-planetaryspherical-gravity-system/3383865) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Space/planetary games are niche on Roblox because the base idea is complicated for a young audience; the author argues polish and playability are needed to bring the genre mainstream.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-made-a-planetaryspherical-gravity-system/3383865) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Make the sun track the player: change the lighting's geographic latitude so the skybox sun aligns with the 3D sun, and scale brightness with distance for a sense of scale.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-made-a-planetaryspherical-gravity-system/3383865) **[[common-mistakes]]** Prior spherical-gravity attempts looked janky because they were never properly polished; polish the feel (movement, lighting, visuals) rather than just shipping the mechanic.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-made-a-planetaryspherical-gravity-system/3383865) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Mimic the default Roblox humanoid controller's feel when building custom character controllers; the author says this made the movement satisfying.

## Games
- **Solar System Exploration 2**: Has planetary gravity and 3D sunlight but is primitive; limits the sun to one hemisphere, which the author's system improves on by allowing planets around the sun.
