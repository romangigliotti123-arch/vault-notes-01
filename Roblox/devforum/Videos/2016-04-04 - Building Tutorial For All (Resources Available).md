# Building Tutorial For All (Resources Available)

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919) | Roblox DevForum | 236 views | 2016-04-04

## Summary
Crykee's DevForum beginner/intermediate building guide: practice and patience, essential building plugins (Build v4, F3X, qCmd, Resize Align, Gapfill), grid-based building to avoid Z-fighting, using varied shapes, triangle terrain tool choice, decals with transparency, and adding atmosphere via ParticleEmitters, lighting and fog.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Be patient: the author's popular game took over a year to become popular. Don't rush builds; invest time and effort.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Learn building plugins and their keyboard shortcuts (Build v4, F3X, qCmd Ult); the author says most builders only got good after using plugins.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Study other showcases closely for detail rather than skimming for two minutes; use Google images for inspiration.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Build on a fixed grid (e.g. 0.2 or 0.25 studs) and never switch increments mid-build to avoid Z-fighting and messy flow.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** When adding detail on a 0.2 grid, offset parts by 0.04 studs (0.05 on a 0.25 grid) instead of leaving gaps.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Combine wedges, corner wedges, cylinders, spheres and blocks instead of only blocks and triangles to make curves, bevels and ramps without overlap.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use a triangle terrain tool that does not leave gaps between triangles; Build v4 has one built in.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use Resize Align and Gapfill (by stravant) to fix alignment and gaps between parts.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Add transparency to scorched or ground decals so they blend into the background instead of looking pasted on.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919) **[[vfx-and-particles]]** Add atmosphere with ParticleEmitters, PointLights and Lighting properties; for a desert map use sandy fog, orange ambient, dark red ambience and a matching skybox.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Revisit old builds that look weak and upgrade them, while keeping the map's building style consistent.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919) grid offset for added detail: **0.04 studs on 0.2 grid; 0.05 studs on 0.25 grid** (Offset parts to stick out without z-fighting)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-tutorial-for-all-resources-available/23919) time to popularity: **over a year** (Author's own game, per the guide)
