# New Analytics Dimensions: Get insight into your players’ spending behaviors

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-analytics-dimensions-get-insight-into-your-players%E2%80%99-spending-behaviors/4438963) | Roblox DevForum | 211 views | 2026-02-25

## Summary
Roblox Creator Hub announcement adding three analytics dimensions (Active payer status, When user first played, Platform spender status) to Retention, Engagement, Acquisition, Demographics and Monetization pages. Explains segment definitions and how to use them for monetization, retention/DAU cohort analysis, and Creator Rewards bonus optimization. Previews mid-year and year-end segmentation features.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-analytics-dimensions-get-insight-into-your-players%E2%80%99-spending-behaviors/4438963) **[[analytics-metrics]]** In Monetization > Overview, break the Revenue Chart down by active payer status to see which segments drive revenue.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-analytics-dimensions-get-insight-into-your-players%E2%80%99-spending-behaviors/4438963) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Review ARPPU, payer conversion, playtime and Retention charts broken down by active payer status to check whether spending is holding steady over time.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-analytics-dimensions-get-insight-into-your-players%E2%80%99-spending-behaviors/4438963) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** If top payers are not contributing about half of revenue, consider a season pass with frequent tiered rewards and progressively challenging missions to keep payers engaged.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-analytics-dimensions-get-insight-into-your-players%E2%80%99-spending-behaviors/4438963) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** For new experiences (0-30 days), focus on acquisition and retention; add daily rewards or similar mechanics to bring new players back.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-analytics-dimensions-get-insight-into-your-players%E2%80%99-spending-behaviors/4438963) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** For established experiences, use Engagement > Breakdown by When user first played to see when players stop playing and where to focus.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-analytics-dimensions-get-insight-into-your-players%E2%80%99-spending-behaviors/4438963) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** If the 181-365 day cohort declines while 0-30 days is flat, add fresh content, events or gameplay updates to give long-term players reasons to return.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-analytics-dimensions-get-insight-into-your-players%E2%80%99-spending-behaviors/4438963) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Active Spenders must play at least 10 minutes per day to earn the Daily Engagement reward; if average playtime is near or below 10 minutes, increase engagement.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-analytics-dimensions-get-insight-into-your-players%E2%80%99-spending-behaviors/4438963) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Use Acquisition > Overview filtered by Active Spender to identify which traffic channels bring in potential spenders.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-analytics-dimensions-get-insight-into-your-players%E2%80%99-spending-behaviors/4438963) Top 15% payers share of revenue: **~50% on average** (Average across experiences per Roblox; benchmark for payer concentration)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-analytics-dimensions-get-insight-into-your-players%E2%80%99-spending-behaviors/4438963) Active payer percentile bands: **Top 15% (85-100th), Intermediate 35% (50-84th), Casual 50% (0-49th)** (Based on spend in last 30 days; plus Lapsed and Never segments)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-analytics-dimensions-get-insight-into-your-players%E2%80%99-spending-behaviors/4438963) Active Spender threshold: **$9.99 USD qualifying purchases in 60 days** (Anywhere on Roblox; platform spender status)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-analytics-dimensions-get-insight-into-your-players%E2%80%99-spending-behaviors/4438963) Daily Engagement reward minimum playtime: **10 minutes per day** (Active Spender must play this long daily to earn bonus)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-analytics-dimensions-get-insight-into-your-players%E2%80%99-spending-behaviors/4438963) First-played cohort buckets: **0-30, 31-90, 91-180, 181-365, 365+ days** (Time since user first entered experience)
