# How to Make a Terrifying Horror Game

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) | Roblox DevForum | 257 views | 2021-12-10

## Summary
Part 1 of izNatt's DevForum guide on writing and planning a narrative horror Roblox game: originality, finishing scripts before building, easter eggs. Part 2 gives 16 horror design tips (layered fear/shock/disgust, delayed horror start, phobias, uncanny valley, difficulty tiers, limiting light, vulnerability, flavor text) plus mindset notes.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Make the idea original; if inspired by another game, add something of your own, since players will compare and a downgrade is punished.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Finish writing the full script before starting development; avoid putting the script on hiatus, as returning after weeks makes creativity harder.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Never build overly complex projects at once; start with small steps and progress at your own pace.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't compare your game to other games; judge your own project on its own terms.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Include a little of each horror element: fear, shock, and disgust, even if Roblox limits some of them.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Don't start horror immediately on join; let players get familiar with environment, lighting, controls, and atmosphere first.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Turn happy, fun things scary (the FNAF-style cover-up approach) to mess with player expectations.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Use a range of common phobias (nyctophobia, arachnophobia, astraphobia, agoraphobia, mysophobia), not just darkness.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Use beings in distress (crying, screaming, panting) only when tied to the game's lore; placement matters.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Lock the camera to first person only at the end of development; test in third person during building for convenience.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Avoid overusing jumpscares; place them where players least expect them and only when they enhance the story.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Use uncanny-valley visuals (human-like but off) for monsters and environments to unsettle players.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Offer Normal, Hard, Extreme difficulties rather than Easy/Normal/Hard, since easy options feel less intimidating.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Make players feel weak and vulnerable: limit movement speed, disorient them, darken the screen as fear rises, make combat risky.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Minimise player and NPC interactions to isolate the player, unless it damages the story.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Make characters relatable and likable so players feel bonded and care about their impact on the story.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Mix emotions beyond fear, such as sadness, by revealing the villain's backstory through clues.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** If flashlight is the main light, make its use cost something (battery drain, noise, creature approaches); scale battery systems to difficulty.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Place batteries in drawers, cabinets, or interactable devices, not scattered on floors and tables for no reason.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Add flavor text to items and make them more interactable, but keep monologue short so it does not become annoying.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Punish players for ignoring the environment (e.g., an unfed pet dies) to keep them attentive, as a subtle horror touch.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Avoid overly dark or poorly lit scenes; lamps should give sufficient light, or progression becomes painful rather than scary.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Create a false sense of safety before scares only when you know what you are doing; it can backfire.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-terrifying-horror-game/1583917) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Add easter eggs throughout the game and reward discovery with badges, hinting at them in the badges tab.

## Games

- **TRAVERSAL**: Cited as an example of unreliable, double-edged combat that puts the player at risk.
