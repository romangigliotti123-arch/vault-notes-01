# Game Development Theory 101

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-theory-101/457156) | Roblox DevForum | 259 views | 2020-02-14

## Summary
Art-theory guide for Roblox builders covering scale/proportion, contrast, colour (hue, saturation, lightness, palettes), style selection, a basic-shapes-to-details workflow, recommended plugins, and light colour and direction. Mostly conceptual; few hard numbers.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-theory-101/457156) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Define scale, proportion and contrast before building; use contrast to direct the viewer's eye to the element you want to emphasise.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-theory-101/457156) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Build in passes: basic shapes first, then minimal details for semi-realistic perspective, then nuance, then fine details last.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-theory-101/457156) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Pick a colour scheme (monochromatic, analogous, complementary or triadic) and use palette tools like coolors.co, colorhunt.co or colormind.io.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-theory-101/457156) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Set OutdoorAmbient and lighting to establish atmosphere; showcase places are usually dominated by one colour or a small set of colours.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-theory-101/457156) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Research the cultural meaning of colours in the region you're emulating instead of copying online colour-meaning charts.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-theory-101/457156) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Gather references from ArtStation, DeviantArt, Dribbble and Behance, then derive something original from them rather than copying.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-theory-101/457156) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Choose a style (e.g. Gothic, Brutalist, Steampunk) and research it deeply before building; style determines the whole look.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-theory-101/457156) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Block building and low-poly are both valid; choose based on whether you're willing to learn a new 3D tool like Blender.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-theory-101/457156) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use Light Editor plugin to view and tweak all lights in a map; use Atmos to browse and insert skyboxes quickly.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-theory-101/457156) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use Stravant ResizeAlign and GapFill plugins to align faces and fill gaps between parts faster than manual resizing.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-development-theory-101/457156) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Lighting strongly changes a scene's feel; choose natural or artificial light sources deliberately to match the environment.
