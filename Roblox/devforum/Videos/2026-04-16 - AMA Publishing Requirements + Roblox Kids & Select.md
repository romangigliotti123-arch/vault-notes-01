# [AMA] Publishing Requirements + Roblox Kids & Select

[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ama-publishing-requirements-roblox-kids-select/4580953) | Roblox DevForum | 2,684 views | 2026-04-16

## Summary
Roblox staff AMA on the new publishing requirements and Roblox Kids/Select age-based access. Covers the Plus/Premium subscription and ID-verification requirement for all-ages publishing, the 500 highly engaged user threshold for Select eligibility, evaluation and appeals, age checks, Vietnam/VNG restrictions, and Creator Store policy.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ama-publishing-requirements-roblox-kids-select/4580953) **[[updates-live-ops]]** To speed up Roblox Select evaluation, regularly update the game, address user feedback, and promote it to users over 16.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ama-publishing-requirements-roblox-kids-select/4580953) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track the Select Eligibility dashboard on Creator Hub; you get notified when the game nears falling below the highly engaged user threshold.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ama-publishing-requirements-roblox-kids-select/4580953) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Complete the Content Maturity Questionnaire accurately; a mismatch can fail evaluation, but you can appeal via Creator Hub if it was a misunderstanding.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ama-publishing-requirements-roblox-kids-select/4580953) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Plus is not required to publish to 16+ audiences; it is only needed for all-ages publishing or publishing updates to all-ages games.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ama-publishing-requirements-roblox-kids-select/4580953) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Complete an age check and enable 2-step verification; creators on Open Cloud must have each contributing creator age-checked with 2FA enabled.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ama-publishing-requirements-roblox-kids-select/4580953) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Before Premium stops being sold on April 30, 2026, note that Plus is a separate subscription; Premium holders keep their Premium and get a 1-month Plus trial.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ama-publishing-requirements-roblox-kids-select/4580953) Highly engaged users needed for Select/Kids eligibility: **500** (Games below this threshold cannot be appealed for lack of data)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ama-publishing-requirements-roblox-kids-select/4580953) Creators whose games qualify who earn more than Plus cost: **99%** (Per Roblox, as of announcement)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ama-publishing-requirements-roblox-kids-select/4580953) Monthly Plus start date: **April 30, 2026** (Plus launches monthly; annual planned within three months)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ama-publishing-requirements-roblox-kids-select/4580953) Proposed one-time refundable publishing fee per game: **1,000 Robux** (Alternative to Plus/Premium for all-ages publishing (May 11 update))
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ama-publishing-requirements-roblox-kids-select/4580953) US daily active users completed age check: **65%** (Global figure is over 50%)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ama-publishing-requirements-roblox-kids-select/4580953) Biometric data retention after age/ID check: **30 days** (Selfie images deleted immediately after processing)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ama-publishing-requirements-roblox-kids-select/4580953) Creator share of Creator Store model/plugin sales: **100% of net proceeds** (Creator Store sales policy)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ama-publishing-requirements-roblox-kids-select/4580953) US 18-34 audience growth year over year: **over 50%** (Monetises about 40% higher than young users in US)
