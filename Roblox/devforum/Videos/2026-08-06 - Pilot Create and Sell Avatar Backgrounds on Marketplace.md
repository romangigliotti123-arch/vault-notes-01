# [Pilot] Create and Sell Avatar Backgrounds on Marketplace

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Open](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-avatar-backgrounds-on-marketplace/4778993) | Roblox DevForum | 1,142 views | 2026-08-06

## Summary
Roblox pilot announcement for creating and selling Avatar Backgrounds on the Marketplace. Covers eligibility (IDV, Roblox Plus, clean moderation/DMCA history, a Marketplace item in past 30 days, NDA), first-come-first-served application by August 13, upload-now/publish-later staging, and a plan to open publishing to all creators in coming months.

## Tips
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-avatar-backgrounds-on-marketplace/4778993) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Pilot requires an item published on the Marketplace in the past 30 days plus clean moderation (no actions in 12 months) and clean DMCA history.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-avatar-backgrounds-on-marketplace/4778993) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Accepted pilot creators can upload Avatar Backgrounds immediately but cannot publish for sale until Roblox notifies them publishing is enabled.
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-avatar-backgrounds-on-marketplace/4778993) **[[common-mistakes]]** A non-response to a pilot application does not mean decline; reviews are rolling and accepted creators are contacted via DevForum in waves.

## Numbers
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-avatar-backgrounds-on-marketplace/4778993) Application deadline: **August 13 (2026)** (Pilot applications for Avatar Backgrounds creators)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-avatar-backgrounds-on-marketplace/4778993) Past moderation actions window: **12 months clean** (Required clean moderation history for eligibility)
- [page](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/pilot-create-and-sell-avatar-backgrounds-on-marketplace/4778993) Recent Marketplace publish requirement: **At least 1 item in past 30 days** (Required for pilot eligibility)
