# Roblox DevForum - How to make successful Roblox games

Build a simple, satisfying core loop that players repeat, and check it with retention and playtime data before spending on ads or monetisation. Price offers with cheap entry items, cosmetic or non-blocking purchases, and whale-friendly content. Launch with tested ads (keep CTR at or above 1.0%, aim for 3.0%). Keep updating live content. Since 2026, Home recommendations weigh long-term retention and fair monetisation more than raw launch spikes (pg-17b78fef2, pg-b899aedd9). Expect early flops and learn from each.

**Style:** Practical, numbers-driven Roblox guides mixing staff announcements with veteran developer opinion. Strongest on monetisation price tiers, ad CTR testing and analytics definitions. Suits developers who want concrete thresholds. Much 2019-2021 advice on payouts, ad budgets and discovery is dated.

## Playbook
1. **1. Idea: pick a niche and genre where your skills are scarce** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Choose a genre where your skill is scarce and useful; avoid pioneering empty genres. Find a gap in the market, and avoid fad clones. Prefer culturally neutral, short-session fantasies that work across ages (pg-7938ab2fb, pg-bcfa105bd).
2. **2. Idea: plan packaging before building** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Concept and packaging (icon and title) matter most for virality, per AtreyuDev (pg-bcfa105bd). Start from an interesting idea that makes an eye-catching icon and thumbnail easy to make.
3. **3. Build: design a core loop that holds attention** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: Ask whether you would keep playing after about four rounds; if not, restart the mechanic. Make the loop easy to explain in one sentence and rewarding through immediate feedback (pg-82a3079ab, pg-a43159501). Graphics do not carry a weak core loop (pg-a8c8cd33d).
4. **4. Build: make onboarding fast with no long tutorial** [[first-session-onboarding]]: Players should be playing within seconds. Teach through visual affordances, big UI and motion cues; reduce cognitive load at entry (pg-a43159501). If D1 retention or session time lags benchmarks, fix the onboarding funnel first.
5. **5. Build: instrument analytics before launch** [[analytics-metrics]]: Track time to first and repeat purchase and conversion between player stages. Use custom events (up to 100, three custom fields) for UI, preference and economy data (pg-ef27c13f7, pg-d1395da13). Do not monetise below about 10 DAU; fix retention first.
6. **6. Build: design monetisation around player types** [[monetisation-gamepasses]]: Treat players as first-purchase, repeat and whale segments. Sell cosmetics in competitive games, and exclusives or titles as gamepasses. Use Dev Products for consumables. Avoid functional power sales (pg-5fe08d603, pg-ae9e9d6ac).
7. **7. Pricing: set tiers and an entry offer** [[pricing]]: Entry items 50-400 Robux, gameplay 400-6000, whales above 6000. Escalate tiers like 450 / 1000 / 2200 (pg-5fe08d603). Offer the first bundle 30-60 minutes in (pg-ae9e9d6ac). Starter packs expire after 24 hours (pg-bcfa105bd).
8. **8. Launch: make icon and thumbnail readable at a glance** [[thumbnails-icons]]: Icons have milliseconds to win a click. Use bold, large fonts and saturated colours; test on light and dark themes. Upload real, current gameplay footage with no trailers or heavy edits (pg-a8c8cd33d).
9. **9. Launch: run a tiered ad test** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Split budget into three equal ads, then put daily spend on the highest-CTR one for three days. Stop anything below 1.0% CTR and aim for about 3.0%. Target 13+ audiences (pg-82a3079ab, pg-a8c8cd33d). Decide whether you want a 40-90 player community or 100-1,000+ discovery audience first.
10. **10. Growth: optimise for playtime and retention signals** [[discovery-algorithm]]: Recommended For You now uses 28-day windows (Day 1, Day 2-7, Day 8-28), drops QPTR for PTR and first-play bounce rate (pg-b899aedd9). Home weighs long-term retention and fair monetisation (pg-17b78fef2). Older claims that Robux earned drives ranking are superseded.
11. **11. Growth: use social and creator outreach** [[social-features-and-virality]]: DM small YouTubers with 1-10k subscribers for free playthroughs and in-game codes. Add invite rewards and friend-join incentives, which drove Dummies vs Noobs growth (pg-a8c8cd33d). Build social play, since friends extend session length (pg-7938ab2fb).
12. **12. Live ops: ship regular updates and events** [[updates-live-ops]]: Frequent updates bring players back and give engaged players reasons for medium and large purchases. Use Event analytics to compare up to three past events on pre/post windows (pg-722eb5355). Configs and Experiments allow real-time toggles and A/B tests (pg-49525655f).
13. **13. Live ops: review benchmarks and cohorts** [[analytics-metrics]]: Compare D1, D7 and D30 retention against genre benchmarks at the 50th-90th percentile (pg-1792c0a9a). Check new-user cohorts (pg-3109cab8d) and retention by acquisition source (pg-a488be6e6) to separate traffic-mix shifts from real changes.
14. **14. Live ops: choose payouts and ads carefully** [[business-and-payouts]]: Rewarded Video is open to all ads-eligible creators (2,000 monthly unique visitors) (pg-818024a03). Creator Rewards replaced Engagement Based Payouts in July 2025 (pg-0a421c903). Premium Payouts guidance from 2020-2021 is historical (pg-78b6b76ab).

## Key numbers
- Ad CTR: minimum 1.0%, target about 3.0% (pg-82a3079ab, pg-a8c8cd33d)
- Boredom test: about 4 rounds before asking whether mechanic is flawed (pg-82a3079ab)
- Ad audience sizing: 40-90 consecutive players (community) vs 100-1,000+ (wide discovery) (pg-82a3079ab)
- Launch budget test: three ads, then top CTR gets daily spend for three days (pg-82a3079ab)
- Dev vs ad budget split: 50:50 when starting out (pg-7938ab2fb)
- Robux price bands: entry 50-400, gameplay 400-6000, whale above 6000 (pg-5fe08d603)
- Tier pricing example: 450 / 1000 / 2200 Robux (pg-5fe08d603)
- Revenue concentration: about 75% from 1-5% of players (pg-ae9e9d6ac, pg-5fe08d603)
- Repeat Robux buying: 95% of buyers buy more than once (pg-ae9e9d6ac)
- First offer timing: 30-60 minutes in (pg-ae9e9d6ac)
- Starter pack expiry: 24 hours (pg-bcfa105bd)
- Monetisation KPIs visible from 10 DAU; Engagement/retention KPIs at 300 DAU (pg-9df421889)
- Insights need 100 DAU; analytics access from 10+ DAU over 7 days (pg-6f21eb795, pg-1792c0a9a)
- Subscriptions in Robux: minimum 49 Robux; price changes every 60 days with 30-day notice for increases (pg-e64d68c10)
- Creator Rewards: 5 Robux per Active Spender playing 10+ minutes; 35% on first $100 of new users' purchases; 60-day hold (pg-0a421c903)

## Common mistakes
- Exposing GameKey or SecretKey in shared or distributed code (pg-d1395da13)
- Calling ServerInit more than once, or ClientInit more than once per client, which breaks event tracking (pg-d1395da13)
- Forgetting to enable HTTP service, so GameAnalytics events never send (pg-d1395da13)
- Putting spaces in plain-text analytics string arguments, which breaks event formatting (pg-d1395da13)
- Selling power, fixes for losses, or paywalls in competitive games, which causes pay-to-win complaints (pg-a43159501, pg-5fe08d603)
- Selling XP, coins or stat boosters as one-time gamepasses (pg-5fe08d603)
- Monetising at very low DAU before retention and playtime are fixed
- Running ads below 1.0% CTR (pg-82a3079ab)
- Treating GameAnalytics acquisition data as reliable for Roblox games (pg-d1395da13)
- Hoarding Robux or cashing out DevEx early instead of reinvesting in ads and services (pg-7938ab2fb)

## How the advice changed over time
- Analytics: 2017 and 2019 advice relied on third-party GameAnalytics and custom monetisation tracking. From 2022 onward, Roblox's native Monetization, Benchmarks, Insights, Custom Events and Retention cohorts (pg-9df421889, pg-1792c0a9a, pg-6f21eb795, pg-ef27c13f7, pg-3109cab8d) cover most of this, so older custom-tracking advice is partly superseded.
- Enrollment thresholds: 600 DAU and 600 hours (March 2022), then 500 DAU and 500 hours (May 2022), then 300 DAU for engagement and retention KPIs (June 2022), then 10+ DAU analytics access (March 2023). Latest figures are 300 and 10 DAU.
- Premium Payouts: 2020 advice said all non-paid-access games qualify. Paid-access games became eligible from Dec 3, 2024 (pg-2850e26ce). Creator Rewards replaced Engagement Based Payouts from July 24, 2025 (pg-0a421c903). Use the newer scheme.
- Rewarded Video: July 2025 limited it to 100K+ average DAU creators (pg-bd719df22). November 2025 opened it to all ads-eligible creators (pg-818024a03). Use the newer rule.
- Discovery: earlier advice said playtime dominates and that top-Robux earners get promoted. 2025 RFY used 7-day signals. 2026 changes use 28-day windows, drop QPTR for PTR, and weigh long-term retention and fair monetisation (pg-b899aedd9, pg-17b78fef2).
- Subscriptions: 2023 launched in Robux or local currency. From April 2026, they can be priced in Robux from 49 Robux (pg-e64d68c10). Subscriptions are now a fuller revenue option than the 2019 advice implied.
- Home: Today's Picks became Standout Games in March 2026 (pg-23d3ba02d). Ads are blended into RFY.
- Gamepass 15-slot limit (2021) and the 30% marketplace fee are stated as current in the 2021 guide. Recheck before relying on them.
