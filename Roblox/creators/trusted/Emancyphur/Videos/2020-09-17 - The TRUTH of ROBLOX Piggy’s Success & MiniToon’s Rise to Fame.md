# The TRUTH of ROBLOX Piggy’s Success & MiniToon’s Rise to Fame

[Watch](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk) | Emancyphur | 3,609 views | 2020-09-17

## Summary
Breakdown of how MiniToon's Piggy rose to become a top Roblox game: a one-week prototype riding a Peppa Pig free-model idea, early creator video exposure (Flamingo), diverse game modes and accessibility, no pay-to-win, a serialized story with teaser marketing, sub-communities (speedrunning, Build Mode, roleplay), and strong developer-community relations.

## Tips
- [1:29](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=89) **[[common-mistakes]]** Skill compounding matters: MiniToon spent ~3 years on free models and smaller projects before Piggy; his scripting and building skill made a one-week prototype viable.
- [2:09](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=129) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build a barebones prototype fast (MiniToon made Piggy's first version in about one week) by reusing parts of older projects, then iterate on feedback.
- [2:50](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=170) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Take inspiration from proven hits (Granny) and reframe them into a new genre twist (escape-room style with a chasing enemy).
- [2:50](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=170) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Getting popular Roblox YouTubers to cover the game early drove large player growth without paid advertising.
- [3:34](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=214) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship frequent fixes, new maps, skins and features, mixing community suggestions with your own roadmap, to sustain growth across platforms.
- [4:16](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=256) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Add accessibility options such as colorblind mode for puzzle games where players must distinguish similar colored items.
- [5:02](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=302) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Offer multiple modes that keep the same objective but change who the enemy is (bot, player-controlled, traitor, infection) to add replayability without new content.
- [5:42](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=342) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Balance the chaser with escape options (vents, doors, freeze tools, NPC help) so the game stays co-operative and not too hard for average players.
- [5:42](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=342) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make all progression earnable through play, with Robux purchases being cosmetic only, so paying players don't gain unfair advantages.
- [6:25](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=385) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Keep paid items purely cosmetic (skins) in a shop, and let a soft currency earned by playing unlock traps and other items.
- [6:25](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=385) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Use an open-ended story with anonymous notes and indirect character backstories to give players reasons to return and theorize.
- [7:47](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=467) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Use custom music composed for the game to set tone in cutscenes and chapters.
- [8:29](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=509) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Tease upcoming updates with sneak peeks and story-relevant quotes plus a countdown date to build hype before each release.
- [9:10](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=550) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Schedule major updates with a set launch time so the whole community arrives together; this produced a #1 Games page spike to 500k+ concurrent players.
- [9:54](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=594) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Design hidden endings and secret requirements (True Ending needing a picture carried across chapters) that streamers will investigate publicly.
- [11:17](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=677) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Support content creators, since streamers like KreekCraft (100k+ viewers) drove discovery by livestreaming mysteries.
- [11:59](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=719) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Design modes that spawn sub-communities such as speedrunning, sandbox Build Mode and roleplay to widen the audience.
- [12:39](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=759) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Add a sandbox or build mode so players can create their own maps without scripting, extending the game's lifespan.
- [13:22](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=802) **[[community-and-discord]]** Engage directly with fans by answering questions, responding to fan art and offering support; this builds lasting loyalty.

## Games
- **Piggy** [0:00](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=0): Horror escape-room with chasing enemy; multiple modes, cosmetic-only shop, serialized story and teaser-driven launches drove huge growth.
- **Granny** [2:09](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=129): Inspiration for Piggy's chase-and-escape concept, reframed into an escape-room style.
- **Five Nights at Chuck E. Cheese** [0:46](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=46): MiniToon's first game to pass 100k visits in 2014, which led him to learn from other developers.
- **Site-35 Foundation Facility** [1:29](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=89): Early SCP-themed project from MiniToon's shift away from free models in 2016.
- **Containment Breach** [1:29](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=89): SCP-genre game where MiniToon met IK3As, later hired for polished building work.
- **Accurate Piggy RolePlay** [12:39](https://youtu.be/C0ybJ5ghSHk?t=759): Fan-made roleplay spinoff by TenuousFlea letting players play as Piggy characters on original maps.
