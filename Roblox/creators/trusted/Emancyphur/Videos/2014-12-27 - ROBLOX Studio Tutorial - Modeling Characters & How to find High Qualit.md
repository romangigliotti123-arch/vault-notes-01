# ROBLOX Studio Tutorial - Modeling Characters & How to find High Quality Models

## Summary
Beginner Roblox Studio tutorial on posing and modeling characters using the Explorer and rotate/move tools, plus finding Roblox-verified high-quality models in the Toolbox. Shows enabling the Explorer, Properties, Toolbox, and other panels, and testing a place in play mode.

## Tips
- [0:59](https://youtu.be/AFY6Yfm-oaw?t=59) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Enable Explorer, Properties, Toolbox, Game, Advanced and Command panels via right-click on empty space in Studio's top bar.
- [2:21](https://youtu.be/AFY6Yfm-oaw?t=141) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Select a model part in Explorer, then use the Rotate tool (blue, yellow, red rings) to pose it; use Move to reposition.
- [5:07](https://youtu.be/AFY6Yfm-oaw?t=307) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** In Toolbox > Models, look for the high-quality badge (circle, triangle, square icon); Roblox hand-picks these as reliable assets.
- [5:47](https://youtu.be/AFY6Yfm-oaw?t=347) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Test high-quality models in play mode before saving; anchor parts first or they may fall over.
