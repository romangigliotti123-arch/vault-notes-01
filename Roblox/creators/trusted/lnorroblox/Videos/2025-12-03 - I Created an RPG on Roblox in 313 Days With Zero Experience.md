# I Created an RPG on Roblox in 313 Days With Zero Experience

[Watch](https://youtu.be/Nywo2NwQPNo) | lnorroblox | 17,354 views | 2025-12-03

## Summary
Developer recounts building Battle Quest, a Roblox RPG, over ~10 months starting Jan 2025 with a friend. Covers early prototype bugs, Discord playtest feedback, a costly mid-project rewrite of the item system, a 7-month major update, post-release bugs, and a launch with about three players and no marketing.

## Tips
- [1:31](https://youtu.be/Nywo2NwQPNo?t=91) **[[common-mistakes]]** Scanning all world objects every second to find players burned ~30% of server resources. Use event-driven detection or cached lists instead.
- [1:31](https://youtu.be/Nywo2NwQPNo?t=91) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Too many enemies using PathfindingService made the prototype very laggy. Limit active pathfinding agents and enemy counts.
- [2:39](https://youtu.be/Nywo2NwQPNo?t=159) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Gather suggestions from a small Discord playtest group. Only about 3 of 5 suggestions were implemented, and one (party system) was added to fix a PvP-loss problem.
- [3:19](https://youtu.be/Nywo2NwQPNo?t=199) **[[community-and-discord]]** Let one highly engaged early player drive demand for updates; his constant requests justified continuing development.
- [3:19](https://youtu.be/Nywo2NwQPNo?t=199) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Start promoting on social media (Shorts/TikTok-style clips) early, even when the game looks rough, to build awareness.
- [4:40](https://youtu.be/Nywo2NwQPNo?t=280) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan the big update as a set of systems before coding. Rewriting the item system took months, so design data structures for the whole game up front.
- [5:32](https://youtu.be/Nywo2NwQPNo?t=332) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect burnout during long solo or duo builds. Scheduled days off restored focus and productivity for the team.
- [6:11](https://youtu.be/Nywo2NwQPNo?t=371) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Expect the final stretch to slip. Estimates of 'next week' stretched by months, with a month spent on one exploit (pickaxes mining ores).
- [6:11](https://youtu.be/Nywo2NwQPNo?t=371) **[[testing-debugging-and-qa]]** Test edge-case tool use before launch. Pickaxes could be used to cut trees, a bug that cost about a month to fix.
- [6:46](https://youtu.be/Nywo2NwQPNo?t=406) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** Do marketing before launch. The big update launched with only about three players because no marketing had been done.
- [7:27](https://youtu.be/Nywo2NwQPNo?t=447) **[[monetisation-other]]** Consider an affiliate system that pays creators a cut of Robux spent in the game by players they refer.
