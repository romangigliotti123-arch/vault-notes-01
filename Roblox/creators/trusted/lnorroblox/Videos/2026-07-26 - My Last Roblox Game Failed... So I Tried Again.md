# My Last Roblox Game Failed... So I Tried Again

[Watch](https://youtu.be/U6gv03QGcDs) | lnorroblox | 22,357 views | 2026-07-26

## Summary
A solo developer and roommate built a cute farming/dumpling-rolling Roblox game in 45 days after a failed previous game. Covers team split with a modeler paid by revenue share, early onboarding fixes, retention issues from slow first crop, and ad targeting to verified 18-24 players that lifted CTR from 1.5% to 8%. Results: about $117 spent on ads, 80 engaged players, roughly 1,800 Robux earned.

## Tips
- [0:33](https://youtu.be/U6gv03QGcDs?t=33) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Only build ideas you can explain in 15 seconds; the creator's rule after a failed RPG was simple, clear concepts.
- [1:11](https://youtu.be/U6gv03QGcDs?t=71) **[[team-and-budget]]** Offer artists a revenue percentage instead of upfront pay when budget is tight; a modeler accepted this arrangement.
- [2:04](https://youtu.be/U6gv03QGcDs?t=124) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Give art feedback via references rather than blunt criticism; the modeler agreed to redesign the UI in the wanted style.
- [2:46](https://youtu.be/U6gv03QGcDs?t=166) **[[optimization-and-streaming]]** Recycle models with an object pool instead of constantly creating and deleting them to protect FPS.
- [4:17](https://youtu.be/U6gv03QGcDs?t=257) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make crops grow through multiple visible stages instead of instant harvest to keep the watering loop engaging.
- [5:37](https://youtu.be/U6gv03QGcDs?t=337) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Don't give tutorial rewards that skip the core mechanic; players never learned rolling when given a free dumpling.
- [5:37](https://youtu.be/U6gv03QGcDs?t=337) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Cut the first crop growth time to ~5 seconds; 30 seconds made players quit while staring at dirt and retention improved.
- [4:55](https://youtu.be/U6gv03QGcDs?t=295) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Check that ad clicks can become plays: unverified users were clicking ads they could not enter, wasting spend.
- [6:17](https://youtu.be/U6gv03QGcDs?t=377) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Target verified players aged 18-24 on PC and mobile; the same thumbnails rose from 1.5% to over 8% CTR in a day.
- [4:55](https://youtu.be/U6gv03QGcDs?t=295) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Account for age-gated audiences when launching; the game was made for kids, so unlocking all ages cost too much to advertise broadly.
- [4:55](https://youtu.be/U6gv03QGcDs?t=295) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Don't assume AI-generated flashy thumbnails will convert; this one reached only ~1.5% CTR before targeting was fixed.
- [7:02](https://youtu.be/U6gv03QGcDs?t=422) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track engaged players per ad dollar and average session length; here ~$117 yielded 80 engaged players with ~5 minute average play.
- [7:02](https://youtu.be/U6gv03QGcDs?t=422) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Expect average play time to be the key test; the creator planned an update specifically to lift the ~5 minute session length.
