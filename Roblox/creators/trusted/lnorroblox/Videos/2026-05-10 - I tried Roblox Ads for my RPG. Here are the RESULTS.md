# I tried Roblox Ads for my RPG. Here are the RESULTS

[Watch](https://youtu.be/QOS7dQowyRk) | lnorroblox | 1,092 views | 2026-05-10

## Summary
Creator's RPG (pet/achievement-heavy) ran a ~6-day Roblox ad campaign (~8,000 Robux, 5 credits/day). CTR rose slightly after AI-made thumbnails, playtime rose ~10→50 min, but D1 retention dropped. Campaign earned ~500 Robux plus one 5,000 Robux donation and ~450 Robux creator rewards. Shifting to organic promotion.

## Tips
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/QOS7dQowyRk?t=0) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add daily and session quests that reset on join, plus progression loops, so players always have something to do. Creator says low retention forced weekly updates.
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/QOS7dQowyRk?t=40) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Turn enemy kills into collectible, levelable pets with rollable stats to make combat feed a long-term build loop.
- [1:22](https://youtu.be/QOS7dQowyRk?t=82) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Redo thumbnails with AI; creator saw a slight CTR gain versus raw in-game screenshots.
- [2:02](https://youtu.be/QOS7dQowyRk?t=122) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Don't spend the whole budget at once. Creator spent ~8,000 Robux with no testing and earned ~500 back. Test with small budgets first.
- [2:02](https://youtu.be/QOS7dQowyRk?t=122) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Check that the game matches what the ad promises. Higher playtime but worse D1 retention from ads suggests audience or messaging mismatch.
- [2:02](https://youtu.be/QOS7dQowyRk?t=122) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Search-driven players stayed longer than sponsored-ad players, so invest in search-discoverable traffic.
