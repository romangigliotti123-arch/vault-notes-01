## [[playtesting-and-feedback]]
- 2026-05-30 [4:46](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=286) Join players as they load in, watch them go through the tutorial, and note confusion and logoff points; data alone misses this. ([[2026-05-30 - How I Advertise My Games on Roblox|How I Advertise My Games on Roblox]])
- 2026-06-19 [6:03](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=363) Beware developer blindness: periodically view your game through a new player's eyes, since familiar mechanics may be unintuitive to others. ([[2026-06-19 - My Approach To Making Roblox Games|My Approach To Making Roblox Games]])
- 2026-06-19 [7:38](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=458) Run real playtests with outside testers and try to break the game: many players per server, long-lived servers, bad network, rejoins, varied screen sizes and inputs, free vs paid play. ([[2026-06-19 - My Approach To Making Roblox Games|My Approach To Making Roblox Games]])
- 2026-06-19 [8:19](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=499) Use playtest sessions to guide balancing; expect extra development days after each test before you publish. ([[2026-06-19 - My Approach To Making Roblox Games|My Approach To Making Roblox Games]])

## [[development-process-and-speed]]
- 2026-05-12 [0:00](https://youtu.be/dbNA3K8lt2w?t=0) Build small, proven-loop games fast: the creator made Grow Bamboo in about one week by copying a working core loop. - 2026-05-30 [7:30](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=450) From day 5, stop ads if benchmarks and core loop are weak; keep running them if benchmarks are good until home recommendations appear. ([[2026-05-30 - How I Advertise My Games on Roblox|How I Advertise My Games on Roblox]])
- 2026-06-19 [2:44](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=164) Scope the project with a clear core loop and a release checklist; decide team, budget, timeline and paid assets upfront. ([[2026-06-19 - My Approach To Making Roblox Games|My Approach To Making Roblox Games]])
- 2026-06-19 [3:25](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=205) Assume you will take longer than you think; most developers underestimate how long tasks take. ([[2026-06-19 - My Approach To Making Roblox Games|My Approach To Making Roblox Games]])
- 2026-06-19 [3:39](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=219) Build a template place file with your standard structure, modules, configs, lighting and services, and reuse it for every new project. ([[2026-06-19 - My Approach To Making Roblox Games|My Approach To Making Roblox Games]])
- 2026-06-19 [5:22](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=322) When stuck or overwhelmed, switch to a different system or task that sounds fun, then return later. ([[2026-06-19 - My Approach To Making Roblox Games|My Approach To Making Roblox Games]])
- 2026-06-19 [6:38](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=398) Reach a functional MVP first, then add polish: VFX, SFX, particles and animations replace placeholders. ([[2026-06-19 - My Approach To Making Roblox Games|My Approach To Making Roblox Games]])
- 2026-07-23 [6:22](https://youtu.be/Bw2ty2T46z4?t=382) Building a simulator solo in about 3 weeks was feasible, but the creator said the result was not original enough, so scope should include a unique hook.
