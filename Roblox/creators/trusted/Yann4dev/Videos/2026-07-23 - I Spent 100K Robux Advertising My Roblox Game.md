# I Spent 100K Robux Advertising My Roblox Game

[Watch](https://youtu.be/Bw2ty2T46z4) | Yann4dev | 93,802 views | 2026-07-23

## Summary
Creator spent 100,000 Robux (380 ad credits) advertising Hatch a Dragon, a solo-built simulator made in 3 weeks. Covers ad CTR/CPP, retention decline, monetisation by product, algorithm signals, and reasons the project may fail (originality, client FPS on low-end devices).

## Tips
- [0:57](https://youtu.be/Bw2ty2T46z4?t=57) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't copy a popular game's loop too closely; the creator thinks originality was the main risk. Add a meaningful mechanic that differentiates the game.
- [2:11](https://youtu.be/Bw2ty2T46z4?t=131) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Pay for ads only while engaged players are arriving; the creator ran ads 19 days at $20/day and said they should have stopped sooner.
- [1:40](https://youtu.be/Bw2ty2T46z4?t=100) **[[optimization-and-streaming]]** Test on low-end devices if your audience is from lower-income countries; a powerful PC barely ran the game, so FPS issues hit many players.
- [4:12](https://youtu.be/Bw2ty2T46z4?t=252) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Watch play-through rate and bounce rate; the algorithm gave home-rec impressions only after these signals improved following tutorial and thumbnail changes.
- [2:53](https://youtu.be/Bw2ty2T46z4?t=173) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Put a high-value consumable on the wheel spin/shop (e.g. spins giving best dragons); it was the top earner, and rebirth-skip products also sold well.
- [3:34](https://youtu.be/Bw2ty2T46z4?t=214) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Cheap 2x cash training-speed passes were the best-selling gamepasses in this simulator; cheap passes suit grinding players.
- [3:34](https://youtu.be/Bw2ty2T46z4?t=214) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Push a low-price starter pack on screen; the creator noted it was pushed heavily but at only 9 Robux it still needs testing.
- [1:40](https://youtu.be/Bw2ty2T46z4?t=100) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check retention against region: a low-spending, lower-income player base limits conversion and ARPPU, so set benchmarks by geography.
- [0:57](https://youtu.be/Bw2ty2T46z4?t=57) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Day-1 retention fell from about 13% to 5-8%; track it daily because a spike from players of a similar game can mask weak core retention.
- [6:22](https://youtu.be/Bw2ty2T46z4?t=382) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Building a simulator solo in about 3 weeks was feasible, but the creator said the result was not original enough, so scope should include a unique hook.
