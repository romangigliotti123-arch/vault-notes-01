# How I Advertise My Games on Roblox

[Watch](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ) | Yann4dev | 107,626 views | 2026-05-30

## Summary
Beginner's guide to advertising a Roblox game: get feedback from other devs, use Roblox Ads Manager as the core promotion tool, set budget by trend/multiplayer/genre, and manage ad phases by day using CTR, CPP and benchmarks until home recommendations take over.

## Tips
- [0:50](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=50) **[[community-and-discord]]** Ask other developers to playtest your game, and give serious feedback on theirs; it earns respect and surfaces bugs early.
- [2:02](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=122) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use Roblox Ads Manager as the core promotion tool; use daily budget, maximize plays, all regions and all devices.
- [2:43](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=163) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Set ad budget by three factors: trend urgency, whether the game needs multiplayer, and genre supply versus demand.
- [3:25](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=205) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Simulators need less ad spend than multiplayer games like tag or knockout, and niche genres like racing need more.
- [4:46](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=286) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Day 1 of ads is a learning phase: do not change creatives or stop ads; watch error logs, funnel drop-offs and live players.
- [4:46](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=286) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Join players as they load in, watch them go through the tutorial, and note confusion and logoff points; data alone misses this.
- [5:26](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=326) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Days 2-4: swap out underperforming thumbnails and remove the worst ones once winners emerge; target CTR of at least 2.5% and cost per play of 0.008 or better.
- [6:48](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=408) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Read the six overview benchmark stats; if all are above the 50th percentile on ads you are in good shape, low stats mean fix the game.
- [6:48](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=408) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Low day-7 retention is a core loop problem; adding daily rewards will not fix it.
- [6:48](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=408) **[[pricing]]** In the first week, sell gamepasses and products at roughly half price to get a realistic payer conversion rate and ARPPU from ad traffic.
- [7:30](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=450) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** From day 5, stop ads if benchmarks and core loop are weak; keep running them if benchmarks are good until home recommendations appear.
- [8:10](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=490) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Stop ads once home recommendations bring enough players that the lowest-CCU time still fills at least two full lobbies.
- [8:52](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=532) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Budget $50-100 for advertising a finished game; the speaker considers it the cheapest scalable investment available.
