# How Much I Made In 1 Year Developing on Roblox... as a Small Roblox Developer

[Watch](https://youtu.be/dbNA3K8lt2w) | Yann4dev | 152,088 views | 2026-05-12

## Summary
Solo/small Roblox developer shares revenue and metrics for three games made in about a year: Painter or Die (flop, ~20K visits), Brain Rot Tag (~1.5M visits, 920K Robux), and Grow Bamboo for Pandas (~575K visits, 331K Robux). Total earnings about $6,500 USD. Emphasises developer products as main revenue and cheap pricing.

## Tips
- [2:44](https://youtu.be/dbNA3K8lt2w?t=164) **[[pricing]]** Price cheap early to boost payer conversion; the creator says at peak they could have doubled prices and sales would still have held.
- [3:25](https://youtu.be/dbNA3K8lt2w?t=205) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Bundle gamepass-style perks (e.g. exclusive packs) into one developer product; their 500-coin product was their best seller.
- [3:25](https://youtu.be/dbNA3K8lt2w?t=205) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Gamepasses like unlock-all can sell well, but many items sold as passes should have been developer products.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/dbNA3K8lt2w?t=0) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build small, proven-loop games fast: the creator made Grow Bamboo in about one week by copying a working core loop.
- [0:41](https://youtu.be/dbNA3K8lt2w?t=41) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not assume your first game will succeed; the first game took 3 months and lost money as a learning experience.
- [5:29](https://youtu.be/dbNA3K8lt2w?t=329) **[[common-mistakes]]** Copying a core loop without understanding why it works leads to low-quality products; add meaningful monetisation rather than filler.
- [4:48](https://youtu.be/dbNA3K8lt2w?t=288) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Offer a starter pack aimed at new players; it was a strong seller in Grow Bamboo for Pandas.
- [4:48](https://youtu.be/dbNA3K8lt2w?t=288) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Random gambling-style products (like lucky-block-style random secrets) sell well in the creator's experience.
- [0:41](https://youtu.be/dbNA3K8lt2w?t=41) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Day-7 retention is weak in games with little longevity; add content depth to keep players returning.
- [1:23](https://youtu.be/dbNA3K8lt2w?t=83) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Creator Rewards (Premium payouts) can add significant income; one game earned 351K Robux from them at peak.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/dbNA3K8lt2w?t=0) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Understand the platform deeply before expecting success; the creator says it takes skill and platform knowledge.
