# My Approach To Making Roblox Games

[Watch](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM) | Yann4dev | 51,514 views | 2026-06-19

## Summary
Outline of the developer's pre-development and development process for Roblox games: clarify personal and per-project goals, pick and scope a validated idea, set up a reusable template, prepare assets, build core systems first, iterate with a new-player lens, polish, optimize, and playtest broadly.

## Tips
- [0:14](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=14) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Define your personal reasons for making Roblox games and set per-project goals; goals change over time and should match your skill and experience.
- [2:03](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=123) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Validate an idea: check target audience, whether it exists on other platforms or on Roblox already, whether it's proven, saturated, and easy to make content for.
- [2:44](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=164) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Before building, know your marketing: draft the title, icon, and a couple of thumbnails for the game.
- [2:44](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=164) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Scope the project with a clear core loop and a release checklist; decide team, budget, timeline and paid assets upfront.
- [3:25](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=205) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Assume you will take longer than you think; most developers underestimate how long tasks take.
- [3:39](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=219) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build a template place file with your standard structure, modules, configs, lighting and services, and reuse it for every new project.
- [4:31](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=271) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Gather most assets before building systems; placeholders are fine if they resemble the final look.
- [4:54](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=294) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build core systems first and make them feel right; dependent systems (shops, inventory) only make sense after the systems they rely on exist.
- [5:22](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=322) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** When stuck or overwhelmed, switch to a different system or task that sounds fun, then return later.
- [6:03](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=363) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Cut systems that take too long, are unaffordable, or don't serve the core loop; iterate without drifting far from the original scope.
- [6:03](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=363) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Beware developer blindness: periodically view your game through a new player's eyes, since familiar mechanics may be unintuitive to others.
- [6:38](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=398) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Reach a functional MVP first, then add polish: VFX, SFX, particles and animations replace placeholders.
- [7:04](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=424) **[[optimization-and-streaming]]** Do a final optimization pass before release so low-end devices aren't excluded and you don't lose players.
- [7:38](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=458) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Run real playtests with outside testers and try to break the game: many players per server, long-lived servers, bad network, rejoins, varied screen sizes and inputs, free vs paid play.
- [8:19](https://youtu.be/zWeDpmrjhUM?t=499) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Use playtest sessions to guide balancing; expect extra development days after each test before you publish.
