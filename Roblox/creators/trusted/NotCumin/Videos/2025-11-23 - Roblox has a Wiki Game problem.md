# Roblox has a "Wiki Game" problem...

[Watch](https://youtu.be/cbOZKwPmmko) | NotCumin | 89,176 views | 2025-11-23

## Summary
Rant video on Roblox RPGs that rely on wikis instead of in-game onboarding. Contrasts Deepwoken/Pilgrim/Attack on Titan Revolution with Block Tails (Tutorial Terry). Criticises Fandom ad overload, code-gated grinds, and devs deprioritising tutorials in favour of new content.

## Tips
- [5:20](https://youtu.be/cbOZKwPmmko?t=320) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Teach core controls and mechanics in-game with a clear tutorial and breadcrumb hints; don't rely on an external wiki to explain basic gameplay.
- [8:18](https://youtu.be/cbOZKwPmmko?t=498) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Avoid tutorials that just darken the screen and show arrows to click; let players act and learn, not blindly click mystery buttons.
- [8:18](https://youtu.be/cbOZKwPmmko?t=498) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** For deep games, build an in-game library/codex with all needed info where it's convenient to access.
- [5:20](https://youtu.be/cbOZKwPmmko?t=320) **[[monetisation-other]]** Don't gate progression behind codes from off-platform Discord/Twitter; codes expire and it forces players off-platform before they follow on-platform.
- [6:42](https://youtu.be/cbOZKwPmmko?t=402) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Keep wiki content maintained yourself; fan wikis can go outdated, unfinished or inaccurate, so essential info belongs in the game.
- [3:38](https://youtu.be/cbOZKwPmmko?t=218) **[[community-and-discord]]** If you need a wiki, host it on wiki.gg rather than Fandom, which has heavy intrusive ads.
- [6:55](https://youtu.be/cbOZKwPmmko?t=415) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Balance new content against improving onboarding for new players; devs usually favour new content, which leaves newcomers lost.
- [8:58](https://youtu.be/cbOZKwPmmko?t=538) **[[common-mistakes]]** Assuming fans will explain your game via wikis is a mistake; poor communication, not complexity, is the problem.
