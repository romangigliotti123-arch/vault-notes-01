## Summary
Commentary video arguing Roblox monetization is exploitative toward children. Covers Robux/DevEx opacity, social pressure, cosmetic pricing, FOMO tactics (limited deals, micro-events like Steal a Brain Rot), premium payout padding, and subliminal monetization. Concludes it is unethical; suggests legal regulation and supporting small developers. Little actionable development advice.
