# This dev sent me their Roblox game, but...

[Watch](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok) | NotCumin | 23,309 views | 2026-02-07

## Summary
Creator reviews The Horde Comes, a first-person dungeon-quest alpha inspired by Ultrakill, and calls it severely underbaked. Covers empty hub, bugged ground slam, RNG-only progression, broken cash shop, misleading objectives, and enemy spawn bugs. Also gives a rant on how devs should pitch games to YouTubers.

## Tips
- [0:41](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=41) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** When pitching YouTubers, include screenshots, a trailer and a direct playable link in the first message. Don't send a bare 'play my game' DM.
- [13:17](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=797) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't publish with non-functional monetisation. Shop buttons that do nothing make an 'alpha' look abandoned and kill first impressions.
- [13:17](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=797) **[[common-mistakes]]** Make a public release presentable. First impressions decide whether players stay for the full release.
- [5:21](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=321) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't ship a game with only one or two repeated mission templates. The reviewer finished all four maps, which look identical, in one session.
- [6:03](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=363) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give players a reason to fight enemies, e.g. enemies blocking the path. Don't let trivial grunts endlessly spawn while objectives can be skipped.
- [9:29](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=569) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Avoid enemies spawning behind players in areas already passed. It reads as a bug and makes the objective feel pointless.
- [10:09](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=609) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Mark objective locations clearly on screen, such as a marker for the keep. Players should know where to go.
- [10:09](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=609) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make objective failure logic match the displayed objective. Here the win fired even after failing, so the objective text and the logic disagreed.
- [8:06](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=486) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Use consistent, discoverable access for menus. Mixing glowing circles, keybinds and randomly placed stands with no signage hides the upgrade menu.
- [8:47](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=527) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Show crafting materials in the inventory. Players expect to check their resources there and will be confused without them.
- [8:06](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=486) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Don't fill the hub with decorative props that do nothing. Empty big hubs and non-functional training dummies feel unfinished.
- [11:30](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=690) **[[combat-and-gameplay-systems]]** Tune resource systems so they matter. Near-infinite stamina regen and rare ammo make dash, push and ranged combat feel pointless.
- [12:52](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=772) **[[combat-and-gameplay-systems]]** Don't make optional objectives consume slots needed for core survival items. Carrying them blocked healing and made the secondary objective impossible solo.
- [6:44](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=404) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Make higher difficulty add meaningful variation, not just higher enemy damage. Otherwise replaying it gives no engagement.
- [6:44](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=404) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make level-ups meaningful. If XP levels give no stat or unlock benefit, players see progression as fake.
- [12:11](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=731) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make sure every ability works and has real impact. A bugged ability wastes a slot and players notice quickly.
