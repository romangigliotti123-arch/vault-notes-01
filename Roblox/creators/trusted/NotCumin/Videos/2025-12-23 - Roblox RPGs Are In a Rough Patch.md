# Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch...

[Watch](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU) | NotCumin | 163,217 views | 2025-12-23

## Summary
Critic review of Roblox RPGs: Souls RNG (boss raid combat, telegraphing), Pilgrim (tutorial and bestiary praised; fog, empty open world, no quest direction, no fast travel criticized), Pixel Blade (dash i-frames and roguelike perks praised; lingering hitboxes, party/multiplayer flow, art style criticized), Critical Fantasy (no quests, hidden items in chests, bad NPCs). Core message: RPGs need direction, telegraphing, fast travel, and playtesting.

## Tips
- [1:54](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=114) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give RPGs a clear goal from the start: a main quest or objective marker. Without one, players quit; the speaker quit Pilgrim and Critical Fantasy for this reason.
- [5:16](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=316) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Telegraph enemy attacks visibly so players can dodge or parry. Unreadable boss attacks make combat feel unfair and unsatisfying.
- [12:38](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=758) **[[combat-and-gameplay-systems]]** Add input buffering so a block or action held during an attack executes once the animation ends. Without it, precise timing feels awful.
- [14:01](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=841) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Place fast-travel points before every major boss and across large maps. Making players walk back after death is a major frustration.
- [14:43](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=883) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Avoid excessive fog that hides the map. Players lose their sense of direction, especially without objective markers.
- [11:57](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=717) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Check whether the first boss can be cheesed by simply running. If so, players never learn the core mechanics the boss should teach.
- [15:24](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=924) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Include a real tutorial that covers movement, combat and builds. Pilgrim's tutorial was called a major upside; many RPGs skip it.
- [13:20](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=800) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Make the world denser rather than sprawling. Large empty spaces with few NPCs or useful content feel like wasted time.
- [16:45](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=1005) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Hook players within the first 5 hours. The speaker lost interest in games that failed to hook him in that time.
- [19:23](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=1163) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Use randomized per-run perks on level-up in dungeon games; Pixel Blade's three-choice perks felt meaningful and roguelike.
- [20:05](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=1205) **[[combat-and-gameplay-systems]]** Give dodge dashes i-frames tied to stamina. Pixel Blade's dash was praised as more useful than parries.
- [20:45](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=1245) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Ensure enemy attack hitboxes end when the animation ends. Lingering hitboxes causing damage after attacks stop feel amateur and unfair.
- [21:27](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=1287) **[[client-server-and-remotes]]** Account for server-side timing windows for parries and dodges so valid inputs register. Otherwise players see parries fail with no reason.
- [22:08](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=1328) **[[matchmaking-and-teleport]]** Let players create a dungeon room with a chosen dungeon and difficulty that others can join. Requiring invites makes multiplayer hard to start.
- [28:30](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=1710) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Show a Coming Soon sign for unfinished features instead of an NPC error. Players who skip patch notes otherwise get confused.
- [29:52](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=1792) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Don't let players travel to zones they cannot return from. A one-way fare that strands under-levelled players is punishing.
- [10:36](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=636) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Don't make death punishment exceed losing some gold. Losing half the gold per death plus a long walk back frustrates players.
- [23:29](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=1409) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Playtest combat with real players before release. Several flaws (hitboxes, multiplayer, broken parries) suggest the games were not tested.
- [4:36](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=276) **[[combat-and-gameplay-systems]]** Design enemies that give players a sense of power. Bosses that kill quickly while the player deals tickle damage kill engagement.
- [5:57](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=357) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Use a built-in bestiary showing defeated enemies and loot drop chances. The speaker called it a good resource that should be common.
- [9:56](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=596) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Let players reset skill points for free rather than gating them behind Robux. This was praised as a quality-of-life win.
- [27:10](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=1630) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Have NPCs and quest-givers do useful things. Filler NPCs with no reason to exist make the world feel empty.
- [26:29](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=1589) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Guide players toward key progression items rather than hiding them in random chests. Leaving them undiscoverable makes progression feel arbitrary.
- [6:33](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=393) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Avoid one-button progression designs; a game with a single attack and no depth gives players nothing to master.
