# Simulators are a PLAGUE on Roblox

## Summary
Critique of Roblox simulator games (Pet Simulator 9, Sonic Speed Simulator, Bee Swarm Simulator) framed as gambling-like, numbers-driven, shallow loops. Covers why simulators feel hollow: static loop, numeric-only progression, no fail state, predatory monetisation. Mostly entertainment; little actionable advice.

## Tips
- [12:38](https://youtu.be/YmAwFbqUA0U?t=758) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give simulator-style loops real mechanical depth; reviewer argues a static loop with only a rising number feels hollow, unlike RPGs that add interacting systems and non-numeric challenges.
- [13:19](https://youtu.be/YmAwFbqUA0U?t=799) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add a fail state or challenges that can't be beaten by raw numerical strength; without them, players only have patience as the challenge.
- [4:31](https://youtu.be/YmAwFbqUA0U?t=271) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Beware one-dimensional collect-then-rebirth formulas; they are easy to clone, so differentiation needs a distinct mechanic or gimmick.
- [5:53](https://youtu.be/YmAwFbqUA0U?t=353) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Licensed IP plus faithful movement mechanics (Sonic Speed Sim emulating slopes, loops, homing attacks) can make a speed game stand out from generic clones.
