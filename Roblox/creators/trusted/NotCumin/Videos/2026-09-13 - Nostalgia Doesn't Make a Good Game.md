# Nostalgia Doesn't Make a Good Game.

[Watch](https://youtu.be/kjLtxvB_iyk) | NotCumin | 191,211 views | 2026-09-13

## Summary
Critique of Dungeon Quest Reborn, a Roblox dungeon-crawler RPG re-release. Covers what works (group dungeon loop, hotbar abilities, physical/magic build split) and what doesn't (archaic group finder, level-locked difficulty padding, RNG-gated abilities, vague ability stats, repetitive enemy design). Proposes level-based lobbies, a quick-queue teleport, and cross-server matchmaking as fixes.

## Tips
- [4:47](https://youtu.be/kjLtxvB_iyk?t=287) **[[matchmaking-and-teleport]]** Add level-bracketed lobbies (e.g. level 1+, 30+, 60+) so players find teammates of similar skill instead of one mixed server pool.
- [5:28](https://youtu.be/kjLtxvB_iyk?t=328) **[[matchmaking-and-teleport]]** Offer a quick-queue: send players to a bare queue server and teleport the group to the dungeon once enough players are gathered.
- [5:28](https://youtu.be/kjLtxvB_iyk?t=328) **[[matchmaking-and-teleport]]** For robust matchmaking, let servers message each other across servers (e.g. via MessagingService or a library like Muster) to search for groups; this is significant work.
- [6:09](https://youtu.be/kjLtxvB_iyk?t=369) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Avoid long repeated grinds on one dungeon's difficulties; stagger level locks so players rotate into fresh content more often.
- [7:30](https://youtu.be/kjLtxvB_iyk?t=450) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Don't gate core build-defining abilities purely behind random rolls; let players earn or buy them with currency or XP.
- [7:30](https://youtu.be/kjLtxvB_iyk?t=450) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Show full ability stats in descriptions: hit count, cooldown, and exact scaling ratio, not vague phrases like 'scales with spell power'.
- [8:11](https://youtu.be/kjLtxvB_iyk?t=491) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Let players upgrade equipped gear without unequipping it first; forced unequips feel lazy and add friction.
- [8:11](https://youtu.be/kjLtxvB_iyk?t=491) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Give enemies varied behaviours beyond walking straight at the player; single-note enemies lead to kiting into an AoE clump and nothing else.
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/kjLtxvB_iyk?t=40) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't rely on nostalgia for a re-release; modernise systems like matchmaking and progression, because players judge it against current games.
- [2:34](https://youtu.be/kjLtxvB_iyk?t=154) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Group-up-in-lobby, instanced dungeon loops with a small hotbar of abilities give a solid base for an RPG, though variety in build choices matters.
