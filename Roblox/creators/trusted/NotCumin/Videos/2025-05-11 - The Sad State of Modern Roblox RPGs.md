## Summary
Critique-style review of Roblox RPGs: Blox Fruits (repetitive quests, level-locked grind, merch/gamepass monetisation), BlockTales (turn-based Paper Mario-style combat, weak telegraphing, short bosses, weak story), World Zero (engaging dungeons, but a paywall update locking tower chests), and bandit beater RPGs (samey, easy to make). Mostly opinion; few actionable lessons.

## Tips
- [5:47](https://youtu.be/2auVezIcNAE?t=347) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid level-locked progression with huge gaps and repeating the same quest template; the speaker says it makes players grind one dungeon for hours.
- [5:47](https://youtu.be/2auVezIcNAE?t=347) **[[combat-and-gameplay-systems]]** Give enemies varied behaviour beyond walk-in-a-straight-line-and-attack; the speaker criticises bare-bones enemy AI in bandit-beater RPGs.
- [9:38](https://youtu.be/2auVezIcNAE?t=578) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Telegraph enemy attacks clearly (e.g. slow approach then pounce) so players can learn dodge timing quickly, per the BlockTales critique.
- [10:20](https://youtu.be/2auVezIcNAE?t=620) **[[combat-and-gameplay-systems]]** Make boss fights last longer than a minute or two and make boss attacks match their fiction, as the speaker says BlockTales bosses were too short.

## Games
- **Blox Fruits** [3:45](https://youtu.be/2auVezIcNAE?t=225): Repetitive template quests and heavy level locks make the game feel like grinding the same quest; players get bored despite high popularity.
- **BlockTales** [8:17](https://youtu.be/2auVezIcNAE?t=497): Turn-based Paper Mario-style combat needs more personality, clearer enemy telegraphs, longer bosses and a hooky story to stand out.
- **World Zero** [12:47](https://youtu.be/2auVezIcNAE?t=767): Engaging, varied dungeons are a strength, but locking previously free tower rewards behind a gamepass caused backlash.
