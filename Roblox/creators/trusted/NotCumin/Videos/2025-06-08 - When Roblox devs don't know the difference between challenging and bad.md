# When Roblox devs don't know the difference between challenging and bad game design...

[Watch](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg) | NotCumin | 186,615 views | 2025-06-08

## Summary
A rant-style review of Attack on Titan: Freedom War, a multiplayer AOT Roblox game. Criticises permadeath with long waits, sparse servers, interconnected stages with dead travel time, ghost doors, slow walking, locked impulse ability behind skill points, unintuitive blade loading/reloading, and weak onboarding. Contrasts with Titan Warfare.

## Tips
- [2:35](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=155) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Avoid permadeath with long respawn waits; players quit when they spend most of a session spectating instead of playing.
- [3:15](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=195) **[[matchmaking-and-teleport]]** Keep server population healthy; with only two servers per region, high ping and empty lobbies drive players away.
- [3:55](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=235) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Make practice mode viable; a private server with clumped, unkillable AI titans gives no real practice.
- [4:21](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=261) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Don't pad levels with long empty travel between objectives; a straight walk with nothing to do kills engagement.
- [7:50](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=470) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Make doors match collision; ghost doors you phase through confuse players and break immersion in modern high-fidelity games.
- [9:22](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=562) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Make it clear where vehicles/mounts are kept and add a visible pickup prompt; hidden horses cost players minutes.
- [10:04](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=604) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make the core movement fun from the start; don't gut traversal (slow walk, stamina sprint) in a movement-based game.
- [11:53](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=713) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give players a default impulse/boost move; locking core movement behind skill points makes early play feel broken.
- [12:34](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=754) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Don't rely on a 'it gets better later' promise; early movement must already feel good enough to keep players.
- [13:15](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=795) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Auto-reload or single-button reload for consumable weapons; manual multi-step loading is frustrating and easy to forget.
- [13:56](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=836) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Show clear HUD warnings for durability or ammo running out, with sound cues, so players aren't surprised mid-fight.
- [14:14](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=854) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Simplify durability tracking; a single durability bar (as in Titan Warfare) keeps combat momentum better than hidden counters.
- [14:53](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=893) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Tutorials must prepare new players for real gameplay; basic controls alone plus 'get good' drives new players away.
- [15:33](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=933) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't mistake challenge for cheap, unfair design; hard games need fair, readable mechanics and clear failure causes.
