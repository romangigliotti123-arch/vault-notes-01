- 2025-04-06 [4:31](https://youtu.be/YmAwFbqUA0U?t=271) Beware one-dimensional collect-then-rebirth formulas; they are easy to clone, so differentiation needs a distinct mechanic or gimmick. ([[2025-04-06 - Simulators are a PLAGUE on Roblox|Simulators are a PLAGUE on Roblox]])

- 2025-12-23 [6:33](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=393) Avoid one-button progression designs; a game with a single attack and no depth gives players nothing to master. ([[2025-12-23 - Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch|Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch...]])

## [[core-loops-and-game-design]]
- 2025-04-06 [12:38](https://youtu.be/YmAwFbqUA0U?t=758) Give simulator-style loops real mechanical depth; reviewer argues a static loop with only a rising number feels hollow, unlike RPGs that add interacting systems and non-numeric challenges. ([[2025-04-06 - Simulators are a PLAGUE on Roblox|Simulators are a PLAGUE on Roblox]])
- 2025-04-06 [13:19](https://youtu.be/YmAwFbqUA0U?t=799) Add a fail state or challenges that can't be beaten by raw numerical strength; without them, players only have patience as the challenge. ([[2025-04-06 - Simulators are a PLAGUE on Roblox|Simulators are a PLAGUE on Roblox]])
- 2025-05-11 [9:38](https://youtu.be/2auVezIcNAE?t=578) Telegraph enemy attacks clearly (e.g. slow approach then pounce) so players can learn dodge timing quickly, per the BlockTales critique. ([[2025-05-11 - The Sad State of Modern Roblox RPGs|The Sad State of Modern Roblox RPGs...]])
- 2025-05-11 [18:55](https://youtu.be/2auVezIcNAE?t=1135) Let players roam the world regardless of level and add collectibles that boost progression; the speaker praised Shadowvis for this. ([[2025-05-11 - The Sad State of Modern Roblox RPGs|The Sad State of Modern Roblox RPGs...]])
- 2025-06-08 [10:04](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=604) Make the core movement fun from the start; don't gut traversal (slow walk, stamina sprint) in a movement-based game. ([[2025-06-08 - When Roblox devs don't know the difference between challenging and bad|When Roblox devs don't know the difference between]])
- 2025-06-08 [11:53](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=713) Give players a default impulse/boost move; locking core movement behind skill points makes early play feel broken. ([[2025-06-08 - When Roblox devs don't know the difference between challenging and bad|When Roblox devs don't know the difference between]])
- 2025-06-08 [14:14](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=854) Simplify durability tracking; a single durability bar (as in Titan Warfare) keeps combat momentum better than hidden counters. ([[2025-06-08 - When Roblox devs don't know the difference between challenging and bad|When Roblox devs don't know the difference between]])
- 2025-07-31 [9:39](https://youtu.be/jXogdTgtlXE?t=579) Never reset all of a player's progress on death in a non-roguelike; it makes the progression loop feel pointless. ([[2025-07-31 - No, Get Good doesn't excuse bad Roblox games|No, "Get Good" doesn't excuse bad Roblox games.]])
- 2025-07-31 [13:17](https://youtu.be/jXogdTgtlXE?t=797) Check that the repeated tasks players do for the whole session lead to a satisfying experience before building around them. ([[2025-07-31 - No, Get Good doesn't excuse bad Roblox games|No, "Get Good" doesn't excuse bad Roblox games.]])
- 2025-07-31 [3:36](https://youtu.be/jXogdTgtlXE?t=216) Only use a campaign structure if it has real progression, story presentation and changing content; otherwise drop it. ([[2025-07-31 - No, Get Good doesn't excuse bad Roblox games|No, "Get Good" doesn't excuse bad Roblox games.]])
- 2025-09-06 [1:42](https://youtu.be/Jf2RvsWMPes?t=102) Speaker argues designing for long-term play (not just quick monetisation) is what keeps a game alive, though he says Roblox's front page currently rewards the opposite. ([[2025-09-06 - The Roblox Front Page is a Joke|The Roblox Front Page is a Joke.]])
- 2025-12-23 [1:54](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=114) Give RPGs a clear goal from the start: a main quest or objective marker. Without one, players quit; the speaker quit Pilgrim and Critical Fantasy for this reason. ([[2025-12-23 - Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch|Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch...]])
- 2025-12-23 [19:23](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=1163) Use randomized per-run perks on level-up in dungeon games; Pixel Blade's three-choice perks felt meaningful and roguelike. ([[2025-12-23 - Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch|Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch...]])
- 2025-12-23 [29:52](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=1792) Don't let players travel to zones they cannot return from. A one-way fare that strands under-levelled players is punishing. ([[2025-12-23 - Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch|Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch...]])
- 2026-02-07 [6:03](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=363) Give players a reason to fight enemies, e.g. enemies blocking the path. Don't let trivial grunts endlessly spawn while objectives can be skipped. ([[2026-02-07 - This dev sent me their Roblox game, but|This dev sent me their Roblox game, but...]])
- 2026-02-07 [9:29](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=569) Avoid enemies spawning behind players in areas already passed. It reads as a bug and makes the objective feel pointless. ([[2026-02-07 - This dev sent me their Roblox game, but|This dev sent me their Roblox game, but...]])
- 2026-02-07 [10:09](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=609) Make objective failure logic match the displayed objective. Here the win fired even after failing, so the objective text and the logic disagreed. ([[2026-02-07 - This dev sent me their Roblox game, but|This dev sent me their Roblox game, but...]])
- 2026-02-07 [6:44](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=404) Make level-ups meaningful. If XP levels give no stat or unlock benefit, players see progression as fake. ([[2026-02-07 - This dev sent me their Roblox game, but|This dev sent me their Roblox game, but...]])
- 2026-02-07 [12:11](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=731) Make sure every ability works and has real impact. A bugged ability wastes a slot and players notice quickly. ([[2026-02-07 - This dev sent me their Roblox game, but|This dev sent me their Roblox game, but...]])
- 2026-09-13 [6:09](https://youtu.be/kjLtxvB_iyk?t=369) Avoid long repeated grinds on one dungeon's difficulties; stagger level locks so players rotate into fresh content more often. ([[2026-09-13 - Nostalgia Doesn't Make a Good Game|Nostalgia Doesn't Make a Good Game.]])
- 2026-09-13 [7:30](https://youtu.be/kjLtxvB_iyk?t=450) Don't gate core build-defining abilities purely behind random rolls; let players earn or buy them with currency or XP. ([[2026-09-13 - Nostalgia Doesn't Make a Good Game|Nostalgia Doesn't Make a Good Game.]])
- 2026-09-13 [2:34](https://youtu.be/kjLtxvB_iyk?t=154) Group-up-in-lobby, instanced dungeon loops with a small hotbar of abilities give a solid base for an RPG, though variety in build choices matters. ([[2026-09-13 - Nostalgia Doesn't Make a Good Game|Nostalgia Doesn't Make a Good Game.]])

## [[genre-mechanics]]
- 2025-05-11 [14:49](https://youtu.be/2auVezIcNAE?t=889) Vary dungeon designs so each feels unique; the speaker cites World Zero's dungeons as the most engaging part of Roblox RPGs. ([[2025-05-11 - The Sad State of Modern Roblox RPGs|The Sad State of Modern Roblox RPGs...]])
- 2025-06-08 [3:55](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=235) Make practice mode viable; a private server with clumped, unkillable AI titans gives no real practice. ([[2025-06-08 - When Roblox devs don't know the difference between challenging and bad|When Roblox devs don't know the difference between]])
- 2025-07-31 [5:35](https://youtu.be/jXogdTgtlXE?t=335) In a movement-based game, design maps around the core movement; tall, reachable structures matter more than realism. ([[2025-07-31 - No, Get Good doesn't excuse bad Roblox games|No, "Get Good" doesn't excuse bad Roblox games.]])
- 2025-07-31 [11:43](https://youtu.be/jXogdTgtlXE?t=703) Make PvE difficulty scale up across campaign stages; a flat difficulty with player power growth makes the late game trivial. ([[2025-07-31 - No, Get Good doesn't excuse bad Roblox games|No, "Get Good" doesn't excuse bad Roblox games.]])
- 2025-07-31 [15:21](https://youtu.be/jXogdTgtlXE?t=921) Consider separating PvP from PvE modes so PvP players don't block campaign progress. ([[2025-07-31 - No, Get Good doesn't excuse bad Roblox games|No, "Get Good" doesn't excuse bad Roblox games.]])
- 2025-09-06 [5:05](https://youtu.be/Jf2RvsWMPes?t=305) Shooters are harder to turn into pay-to-win cash grabs than simulators; gunplay must feel cohesive, which takes real effort to build. ([[2025-09-06 - The Roblox Front Page is a Joke|The Roblox Front Page is a Joke.]])
- 2025-12-23 [11:57](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=717) Check whether the first boss can be cheesed by simply running. If so, players never learn the core mechanics the boss should teach. ([[2025-12-23 - Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch|Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch...]])
- 2025-12-23 [5:57](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=357) Use a built-in bestiary showing defeated enemies and loot drop chances. The speaker called it a good resource that should be common. ([[2025-12-23 - Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch|Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch...]])
- 2026-09-13 [8:11](https://youtu.be/kjLtxvB_iyk?t=491) Give enemies varied behaviours beyond walking straight at the player; single-note enemies lead to kiting into an AoE clump and nothing else. ([[2026-09-13 - Nostalgia Doesn't Make a Good Game|Nostalgia Doesn't Make a Good Game.]])
