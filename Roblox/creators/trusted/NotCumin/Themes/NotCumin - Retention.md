## [[first-session-onboarding]]
- 2025-06-08 [14:53](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=893) Tutorials must prepare new players for real gameplay; basic controls alone plus 'get good' drives new players away. ([[2025-06-08 - When Roblox devs don't know the difference between challenging and bad|When Roblox devs don't know the difference between]])
- 2025-07-31 [7:04](https://youtu.be/jXogdTgtlXE?t=424) Build an interactive tutorial that makes players practise each control and mechanic, not just a text list of controls. ([[2025-07-31 - No, Get Good doesn't excuse bad Roblox games|No, "Get Good" doesn't excuse bad Roblox games.]])
- 2025-07-31 [8:28](https://youtu.be/jXogdTgtlXE?t=508) Explain every in-game signal or UI color (e.g. flare colors) so players know what each means. ([[2025-07-31 - No, Get Good doesn't excuse bad Roblox games|No, "Get Good" doesn't excuse bad Roblox games.]])
- 2025-11-23 [5:20](https://youtu.be/cbOZKwPmmko?t=320) Teach core controls and mechanics in-game with a clear tutorial and breadcrumb hints; don't rely on an external wiki to explain basic gameplay. ([[2025-11-23 - Roblox has a Wiki Game problem|Roblox has a "Wiki Game" problem...]])
- 2025-11-23 [8:18](https://youtu.be/cbOZKwPmmko?t=498) Avoid tutorials that just darken the screen and show arrows to click; let players act and learn, not blindly click mystery buttons. ([[2025-11-23 - Roblox has a Wiki Game problem|Roblox has a "Wiki Game" problem...]])
- 2025-11-23 [8:18](https://youtu.be/cbOZKwPmmko?t=498) For deep games, build an in-game library/codex with all needed info where it's convenient to access. ([[2025-11-23 - Roblox has a Wiki Game problem|Roblox has a "Wiki Game" problem...]])
- 2025-12-23 [15:24](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=924) Include a real tutorial that covers movement, combat and builds. Pilgrim's tutorial was called a major upside; many RPGs skip it. ([[2025-12-23 - Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch|Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch...]])

## [[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]
- 2025-04-06 [10:36](https://youtu.be/YmAwFbqUA0U?t=636) Let players feed or upgrade companions and keep them relevant across zones; reviewer says Bee Swarm's bees feel more like companions than disposable Pet Simulator pets. ([[2025-04-06 - Simulators are a PLAGUE on Roblox|Simulators are a PLAGUE on Roblox]])
- 2025-04-06 [11:58](https://youtu.be/YmAwFbqUA0U?t=718) Pace reward delivery deliberately rather than flooding the screen with numbers and loot-box style openings; reviewer contrasts Bee Swarm's slower pacing favourably. ([[2025-04-06 - Simulators are a PLAGUE on Roblox|Simulators are a PLAGUE on Roblox]])
- 2025-05-24 [7:43](https://youtu.be/E7bedfC8A10?t=463) Early progression that forces long waits for seeds and crops made the creator lose interest fast; the novelty of the core activity wore off within minutes. - 2025-06-08 [12:34](https://youtu.be/Z1tj1OPOEZg?t=754) Don't rely on a 'it gets better later' promise; early movement must already feel good enough to keep players. - 2025-07-31 [4:57](https://youtu.be/jXogdTgtlXE?t=297) Keep respawn timers short (a few seconds in shooters, about a minute max in MOBAs); long waits make a game boring and cut learning time. - 2025-12-23 [5:16](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=316) Telegraph enemy attacks visibly so players can dodge or parry. Unreadable boss attacks make combat feel unfair and unsatisfying. ([[2025-12-23 - Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch|Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch...]])
- 2025-12-23 [10:36](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=636) Don't make death punishment exceed losing some gold. Losing half the gold per death plus a long walk back frustrates players. ([[2025-12-23 - Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch|Roblox RPGs Are In a Rough Patch...]])
- 2026-02-07 [6:44](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=404) Make higher difficulty add meaningful variation, not just higher enemy damage. Otherwise replaying it gives no engagement. ([[2026-02-07 - This dev sent me their Roblox game, but|This dev sent me their Roblox game, but...]])
