# I built 3 Roblox Games, they all failed (Here's why)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4) | TheRoyalDev | 187 views | 2026-08-07

## Summary
Creator reviews three failed Roblox games (Panic Button, Blindfolded Tortilla Slap, Different Word Wins) with stats: day-1 retention ~17-27%, low 5-min retention. Causes: no onboarding, niche/difficult concepts, two-player requirements, poor ad strategy, ignoring analytics.

## Tips
- [1:43](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=103) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track day-1 retention, average session time and percent still playing at 5 minutes; ignoring these analytics let the failures go unnoticed.
- [2:45](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=165) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Join your own game and watch new players; the creator saw players walk around and leave, which the stats alone didn't explain.
- [4:07](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=247) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Add a tutorial and a how-to-play flow; dropping new players in cold with no guidance made them leave immediately.
- [4:47](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=287) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give players a reason to return, such as leaderboards, rewards or progression, rather than a one-off round.
- [5:28](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=328) **[[common-mistakes]]** Assume players know nothing; creators underestimate how much of their obvious mechanics new players need explained.
- [5:28](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=328) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Match the concept to the majority audience (largely children on mobile) rather than a very niche or hard audience.
- [6:09](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=369) **[[testing-debugging-and-qa]]** Verify game logic thoroughly; a coding bug where a button registered twice frustrated players and drove them away.
- [8:10](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=490) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Don't require two players to start the core loop; if you must, give the waiting player something to do, or add an AI mode.
- [8:51](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=531) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Test whether the core concept itself appeals before building; one game's concept was described by many players as flat-out stupid.
- [9:32](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=572) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Show players what to do through immediate visible consequences instead of text, since players skip reading instructions.
- [12:56](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=776) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Explain roles and mechanics in-game clearly; players didn't realise they were the picker and did not write on the whiteboard.
- [13:36](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=816) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't assume obvious mechanics are obvious; a whiteboard instruction the developer thought obvious confused players.
- [14:17](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=857) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Run ads consistently rather than repeatedly switching them on and off; the creator's stop-start ad pattern hurt results.
- [8:10](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=490) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Promote social and friend features with clips and social media so players invite friends; solo players who find an empty server leave.
- [15:38](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=938) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give solo players something to do in multiplayer games, e.g. a practice area similar to the core loop, as Knockout does.

## Games
- **Panic Button** [0:01](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=1): Too difficult and niche for kids on mobile; no tutorial or reward, players walked around and left.
- **Blindfolded Tortilla Slap** [6:09](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=369): Required two players to start, leaving solo players idle; text onboarding was skipped and concept felt dumb to many.
- **Different Word Wins** [10:52](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=652): Needed two players and confused players about roles and whiteboard mechanics; ads were stopped and restarted repeatedly.
- **Knockout** [14:57](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=897): Offers an aim practice area similar to the core loop so solo players have something to do while waiting.
- **Grow a Garden** [9:32](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=572): Shows immediate visible consequences of player actions (planting, growth) instead of explaining them in text.
- **The Industrialist** [3:25](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=205): Cited as a hard-mechanics game that succeeds despite being on a kids' platform; not a model for a broad-audience game.
