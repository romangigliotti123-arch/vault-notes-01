# How I'm trying to revive my REALLY dead Roblox game

[Watch](https://youtu.be/LpqqLed1dBU) | TheRoyalDev | 51 views | 2026-08-12

## Summary
Creator revives a dead Roblox puzzle/escape game "The Panic Button" (inspired by Press the Button Under Pressure). Diagnoses failures: too difficult, unclear onboarding, no reason to return, unpolished UI/code, D1 of 0.72%. Plans a UI-based rebuild with clearer buttons, humor, and fun-but-fair challenge.

## Tips
- [1:24](https://youtu.be/LpqqLed1dBU?t=84) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Separate challenge from impossibility; the creator's game had too many simultaneous inputs (physical map, NPCs, button commands) and players quit.
- [2:04](https://youtu.be/LpqqLed1dBU?t=124) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make the first session explicit: players left within minutes because they didn't know what to do. Tell players clearly what to do immediately.
- [2:04](https://youtu.be/LpqqLed1dBU?t=124) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give players a reason to come back after first session; the previous game had no return incentive and players left.
- [2:45](https://youtu.be/LpqqLed1dBU?t=165) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Replace a physical map with UI-based navigation: buttons with clear labels (Play, Select Mode, Shop) so players can't get lost and spend effort on the core loop.
- [2:45](https://youtu.be/LpqqLed1dBU?t=165) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Add humor and a more human, reactive button character to make the interaction engaging while keeping it challenging but not impossible.
- [3:25](https://youtu.be/LpqqLed1dBU?t=205) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't ship rough polish: placeholder UI and hastily written scripts hurt the game; polish is needed to stick to the algorithm.
- [3:25](https://youtu.be/LpqqLed1dBU?t=205) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track D1 retention and the share of players still playing after 5 minutes; target over 18% past 5 minutes.
- [6:09](https://youtu.be/LpqqLed1dBU?t=369) **[[common-mistakes]]** A good idea can still fail if poorly executed; execution determines how the game performs with the Roblox algorithm.
- [6:51](https://youtu.be/LpqqLed1dBU?t=411) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Ship and iterate publicly rather than endlessly planning; document the process, failures included.
