# Stop Overcomplicating Roblox Ads

[Watch](https://youtu.be/mLMXeMWmTwU) | TheRoyalDev | 19,723 views | 2026-08-15

## Summary
Creator argues Roblox ads need no secret settings: pick experience, choose engagement/audience, set budget, then let ads run untouched for the learning period. Pulling and reposting ads resets Roblox's learning, which hurt his last two games. Game quality is the real variable.

## Tips
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/mLMXeMWmTwU?t=81) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Budget, daily vs lifetime, and start time barely matter; pick the experience, choose engagement or audience targeting, and start.
- [2:43](https://youtu.be/mLMXeMWmTwU?t=163) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Let ads run continuously. Roblox spends about 24 hours learning after publish; pulling and reposting resets that learning.
- [2:43](https://youtu.be/mLMXeMWmTwU?t=163) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Don't judge ads after 2-3 days with on/off cycling; this created the worst results for the speaker's last two games.
- [3:25](https://youtu.be/mLMXeMWmTwU?t=205) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** If ads bring players who leave quickly, fix the game itself; ads push to the most likely players, so weak retention reflects game quality.
- [2:03](https://youtu.be/mLMXeMWmTwU?t=123) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't blame ads for poor results. Strong ads deliver the right players, but a game that isn't good won't keep them.
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/mLMXeMWmTwU?t=40) **[[common-mistakes]]** Ignore popular 'secret' ad setting recipes (specific budgets, start days, days of week); the speaker says none are proven.
