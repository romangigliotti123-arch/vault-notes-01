# 3 BIGGEST MYTHS about roblox development

[Watch](https://youtu.be/6CefFrCFqcI) | TheRoyalDev | 282 views | 2026-08-25

## Summary
Myth-busting video on three Roblox development beliefs: ads can't rescue a weak game, high-end graphics aren't required (simple loops and memes win), and solo devs are not outdated. Mostly opinion with examples of solo-developed hits.

## Tips
- [1:22](https://youtu.be/6CefFrCFqcI?t=82) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Don't pour money into ads before retention and session length are good; ads only scale what already works and burn cash on weak games.
- [1:22](https://youtu.be/6CefFrCFqcI?t=82) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check day-one retention, analytics and session length before spending on ads; poor numbers mean the game loop needs fixing first.
- [3:26](https://youtu.be/6CefFrCFqcI?t=206) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Prioritise a strong core loop over high-poly models and custom lighting; stylised, simple visuals outperform graphically heavy games with weak loops.
- [4:48](https://youtu.be/6CefFrCFqcI?t=288) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Test whether the game is clippable: could a short clip be shared online and get a 'that's cool' or meme reaction.
- [5:29](https://youtu.be/6CefFrCFqcI?t=329) **[[team-and-budget]]** Solo devs can be more cost-efficient and sometimes faster; big teams can become disastrous, so a solo build is a valid path.
