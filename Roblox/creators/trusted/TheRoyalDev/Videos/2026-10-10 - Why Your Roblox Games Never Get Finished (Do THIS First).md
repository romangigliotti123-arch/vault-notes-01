# Why Your Roblox Games Never Get Finished (Do THIS First)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/DLqlgL_7XrY) | TheRoyalDev | 719 views | 2026-10-10

## Summary
Designer explains his pre-production routine for finishing Roblox games: a plain-document game design doc (name, references, genre, core loop, progression, onboarding, monetization, script/system lists, UI/VFX/build needs, misc tab) and Trello boards with role labels, checklists, and deadlines. Says he has made 5-6 games, none successful yet.

## Tips
- [0:41](https://youtu.be/DLqlgL_7XrY?t=41) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Write a one-page design doc before coding: name, reference games, genre, core loop, progression, onboarding, monetization. Prevents forgetting design decisions later.
- [2:03](https://youtu.be/DLqlgL_7XrY?t=123) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Spend the most design time on onboarding; he calls it the most crucial part of any game and checks whether players actually want to progress.
- [2:03](https://youtu.be/DLqlgL_7XrY?t=123) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Include a monetization strategy in the design doc; his Hidden Anomaly game uses a cheap starter pack and small power purchases with some larger bat purchases.
- [2:44](https://youtu.be/DLqlgL_7XrY?t=164) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** List every script system needed (e.g. map generation, ragdoll physics, UI) and every build, UI, VFX and model need, so the team sees what remains.
- [2:44](https://youtu.be/DLqlgL_7XrY?t=164) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Attach reference screenshots to each UI item in the design doc so developers know the target look.
- [3:26](https://youtu.be/DLqlgL_7XrY?t=206) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Write down ideas immediately; good ideas get forgotten within a day or two before development starts.
- [3:57](https://youtu.be/DLqlgL_7XrY?t=237) **[[team-and-budget]]** Use Trello with role labels (builder, GUI, animation, scripter, GFX) and priority/bug labels to organize a team's work.
- [4:38](https://youtu.be/DLqlgL_7XrY?t=278) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Break each task into a Trello checklist and tick items off; he says the checking-off gave motivation while hand-coding.
- [5:19](https://youtu.be/DLqlgL_7XrY?t=319) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Set fixed deadlines on Trello cards and enable notifications; fixed dates make you more likely to complete work.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/DLqlgL_7XrY?t=0) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Finish every game you start, even if it fails; he claims this habit is what keeps him shipping.
