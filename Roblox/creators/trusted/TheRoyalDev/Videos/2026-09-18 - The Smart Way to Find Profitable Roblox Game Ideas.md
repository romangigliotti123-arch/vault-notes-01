# The Smart Way to Find Profitable Roblox Game Ideas

## Summary
Creator explains his process for finding profitable Roblox game ideas: study the Up and Coming and Top Playing charts, pick a proven niche and add a twist, mine TikTok/YouTube trends, and check non-Roblox web games and Steam games with existing audiences. He admits his own backrooms and panic-button experiments underperformed.

## Tips
- [0:42](https://youtu.be/nLuxSFg5viw?t=42) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Check the Up and Coming charts first to see what is newly gaining traction, rather than only the established Top Playing games.
- [0:42](https://youtu.be/nLuxSFg5viw?t=42) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Don't build something wholly new; pick a proven niche (e.g., lift games, plus-one, anime, base-steal) and add your own spin.
- [1:23](https://youtu.be/nLuxSFg5viw?t=83) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Copying proven genres is viable: games that look like clones still reach thousands of players, so the market rewards what already works.
- [2:04](https://youtu.be/nLuxSFg5viw?t=124) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Monitor TikTok and YouTube for trending formats (e.g., word/party games like Wavelength or Find the Impostor) and adapt them to Roblox.
- [2:48](https://youtu.be/nLuxSFg5viw?t=168) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Browse web game portals for games with proven audiences (1-7M plays) that are not on Roblox, then port the best mechanics.
- [3:29](https://youtu.be/nLuxSFg5viw?t=209) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Look at paid Steam games and make a free-to-play Roblox version, since the audience already exists; the speaker's attempt (Panic Button) did not succeed, so treat it as a test.
- [4:11](https://youtu.be/nLuxSFg5viw?t=251) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Idea selection takes substantial research time; don't pick a game just because it looks cool.
- [0:01](https://youtu.be/nLuxSFg5viw?t=1) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't assume a niche will work just because it trends; the speaker's Locus-in-backrooms and word-game experiments underperformed, so validate with small tests.
