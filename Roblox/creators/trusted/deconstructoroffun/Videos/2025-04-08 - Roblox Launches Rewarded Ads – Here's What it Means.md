## Summary
Commentary on Roblox launching rewarded video ads via a Google advertising partnership, announced around GDC and IAB. Covers reported completion rates, ad eligibility rules (developers 18+, no ads for under-13s), regional DAU and bookings gaps, and comparisons to King's Candy Crush ad revenue projections.
