# Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox?

[Watch](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns) | deconstructoroffun | 1,002 views | 2026-04-06

## Summary
Roblox executives (Nick Turno, Roblox) and Zach Letter (Wonder Works, SpongeBob Tower Defense) discuss platform discovery, the six analytics benchmarks, rapid MVP-then-live-ops development, small team sizes, influencer and Discord growth, and the immature state of Roblox ads.

## Tips
- [8:43](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=523) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track the six Roblox benchmarks: avg playtime per user per day, D1 and D7 retention, payer conversion, ARPPU. Zach aims for the 90th percentile on all.
- [14:00](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=840) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Get players into the core loop within the first minutes. Short-form traffic gives only seconds of attention, so show what is special immediately.
- [14:40](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=880) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Ship a bite-size MVP in 5-6 weeks instead of 3-4 months, then live-op weekly with the community based on player feedback.
- [16:43](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=1003) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Run a weekly update cadence: sprint Monday, ship Friday. Wonder Works shipped SpongeBob TD's 52-week live ops with new modes and content.
- [18:03](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=1083) **[[team-and-budget]]** Keep teams small: never more than about 4 per project, roughly 3 projects at once. Survive Bikini Bottom took 5 weeks with 2 people.
- [21:02](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=1262) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Iterate many times a day. Survive Bikini Bottom shipped 10 builds in 3 days with no app-store review step.
- [40:02](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=2402) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Design for co-play without punishing solo players. Reward friend invites, e.g., milestone rewards for inviting friends in week one, and bonus currency for group play.
- [22:40](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=1360) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not skip console at launch. Wonder Works regretted dropping console support at release because players expect cross-device friend connection.
- [31:35](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=1895) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Focus on platform SEO and in-platform discoverability first; influencer traction, Discord growth and live-op attention follow from it.
- [31:35](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=1895) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Run an influencer program: early update access, gamepass resources and codes so creators publish videos the day an update drops.
- [34:28](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=2068) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use paid spend to seed the algorithm: buy traffic to find your audience, let engagement data feed the algorithm, then advertising can re-kick a stale game after big updates.
- [10:47](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=647) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Roblox does not prioritise playtime beyond a threshold. Design for healthy sessions, not addictive time sinks, since the algorithm rewards retained, monetising, co-played experiences.
- [26:55](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=1615) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Do not chase only high-fidelity visuals. Breakthrough hits can be simple if the core loop, gameplay and social hook are strong.
- [42:22](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=2542) **[[common-mistakes]]** Expect failure: Zach says for every success there are a couple dozen failures. Release often and learn from each project.
- [42:22](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=2542) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Treat Roblox as a new platform: approach it as a beginner regardless of past experience. Past success does not guarantee the next hit.
- [53:58](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=3238) **[[team-and-budget]]** Aim for a small studio of about 10-20 people with a couple of top-100 experiences rather than scaling to hundreds.
