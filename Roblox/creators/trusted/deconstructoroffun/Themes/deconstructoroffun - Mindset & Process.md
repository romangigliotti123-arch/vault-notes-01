## [[analytics-metrics]]
- 2026-04-06 [8:43](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=523) Track the six Roblox benchmarks: avg playtime per user per day, D1 and D7 retention, payer conversion, ARPPU. Zach aims for the 90th percentile on all. ([[2026-04-06 - Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox|Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox?]])

## [[team-and-budget]]
- 2026-04-06 [18:03](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=1083) Keep teams small: never more than about 4 per project, roughly 3 projects at once. Survive Bikini Bottom took 5 weeks with 2 people. ([[2026-04-06 - Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox|Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox?]])
- 2026-04-06 [53:58](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=3238) Aim for a small studio of about 10-20 people with a couple of top-100 experiences rather than scaling to hundreds. ([[2026-04-06 - Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox|Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox?]])

## [[development-process-and-speed]]
- 2026-04-06 [14:40](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=880) Ship a bite-size MVP in 5-6 weeks instead of 3-4 months, then live-op weekly with the community based on player feedback. ([[2026-04-06 - Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox|Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox?]])
- 2026-04-06 [21:02](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=1262) Iterate many times a day. Survive Bikini Bottom shipped 10 builds in 3 days with no app-store review step. ([[2026-04-06 - Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox|Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox?]])

## [[common-mistakes]]
- 2026-04-06 [22:40](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=1360) Do not skip console at launch. Wonder Works regretted dropping console support at release because players expect cross-device friend connection. ([[2026-04-06 - Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox|Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox?]])
- 2026-04-06 [42:22](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=2542) Expect failure: Zach says for every success there are a couple dozen failures. Release often and learn from each project. ([[2026-04-06 - Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox|Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox?]])

## [[mindset-and-strategy]]
- 2026-04-06 [42:22](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=2542) Treat Roblox as a new platform: approach it as a beginner regardless of past experience. Past success does not guarantee the next hit. ([[2026-04-06 - Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox|Can You Build a Real Business on Roblox?]])
