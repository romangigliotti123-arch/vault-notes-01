# alexbuilds_games - How to make successful Roblox games

Make a game that fits a niche players already engage with, then match monetization to its genre instead of stacking every method. Launch with a strong title, icon and thumbnail, targeting about 5% CTR. Use a roughly 7-day Roblox ad test to gather audience data, then stop paying and let home recommendations take over. Scale in CCU stages while improving the weakest stat, whether CTR, stay time, or D1/D7 retention. Keep live updates aimed at that weak stat. Keep the monetization set small: a few strong sellers, with cheap extras kept secondary.

**Style:** Practical, data-driven, and blunt about what sells. He ranks monetization tiers from experience across 15+ Roblox games and treats the algorithm as a stat-optimization problem. Suits developers who want concrete numbers and genre-specific monetization advice.

## Playbook
1. **Pick a niche trend with existing demand** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Build on demand players already show instead of inventing new tastes. Choose a trend with active engagement and study the top games in that niche as references.
2. **Fit monetization to genre before building** [[monetisation-gamepasses]]: Build monetization in from the start. Sell exclusive pay-to-win packs in competitive games (combat, tower defense), expression items only where players already invest in expression, and bundle VIP with real perks (x_SflcuSi1w).
3. **Add cheap situational offers, not many** [[monetisation-other]]: Add troll or jump-scare buys only to social multiplayer games (two-player obbies, story, horror). Use RNG crates or chests in nearly any game, priced higher in high-value games. Avoid stacking many methods, since extra cheap offers distract from best sellers (x_SflcuSi1w).
4. **Set D1 and D7 retention targets** [[retention-d1-d7]]: Set explicit D1 and D7 targets. Mild trend games may accept lower retention, but evergreen games need strong D1 and D7 to keep being recommended (wb8XBVFI2i4).
5. **Deprioritize currency packs** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: Speaker found currency packs weakest in most genres (D tier). Buyers prefer direct item purchases, so if you keep them, place them at the bottom of the shop UI (x_SflcuSi1w).
6. **Treat the game as a brand on the home page** [[thumbnails-icons]]: Titles, icons and copy form the first impression on the home page. Aim for about 5% CTR; 0.5% is poor and implies roughly 200 impressions per click with weak demand (wb8XBVFI2i4).
7. **Run a ~7-day Roblox ad test** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Use ads early for fast data instead of slow SEO. Run about 7 days at about $16/day (~$112 total). Roblox's learning phase is about 2 days, then it optimizes over 7 to find engaged players (wb8XBVFI2i4).
8. **Stop ads and let recommendations take over** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: After the ad run, stop spending. The home feed then targets players similar to those who engaged with your ad traffic. Judge the result on play time, CTR and D1/D7 (wb8XBVFI2i4).
9. **Diagnose the weakest post-ad stat** [[analytics-metrics]]: Example weak startup: 11 average play time, ~4% CTR, poor D1/D7. Identify which stat is weakest and fix that before scaling further.
10. **Scale CCU in stages** [[discovery-algorithm]]: Roblox tests a small audience (about 5 CCU), then about 100 CCU. If stats don't stabilize it scales back to about 30 CCU. Keep improving weak stats through each stage (wb8XBVFI2i4).
11. **Keep convenience and rerolls cheap** [[pricing]]: Price pay-for-convenience items (auto collect at $2.99, trade slots, teleports) very low as secondary sellers. Price crate rerolls around 4-5 Robux so players keep rerolling (x_SflcuSi1w).
12. **Price progress boosts by game value** [[pricing]]: In brain-rot games keep progress boosts under 60 Robux. In combat games, where progress gives real advantage, price them high (x_SflcuSi1w).
13. **Judge conversion by region** [[analytics-metrics]]: Conversion varies by region, for example China is far below the US. Not all CCU earn the same, so read monetization results by region.
14. **Ship updates against the weakest stat** [[updates-live-ops]]: Roll out updates targeting whichever stat is weak, such as retention items when D1 or D7 is poor. Roblox rewards improved stats (wb8XBVFI2i4).

## Common mistakes
- Stacking many monetization methods; extra cheap offers distract from your best sellers.
- Putting currency packs prominently in the shop; buyers rarely choose them over direct purchases.
- Selling expression items (auras, trails) in games where players aren't invested in self-expression.
- Adding jump-scare or troll items to open-world or high-value games where they don't fit.
- Pricing a plain overhead VIP badge ($3.99) with no real perks; it won't sell.
- Relying on slow SEO as the main early traffic source instead of short ad tests for fast data.
- Ignoring weak stats (CTR, play time, D1/D7) and not iterating on them.
- Accepting low retention in evergreen games; they need strong D1/D7 to keep getting recommended.
- Expecting uniform conversion across regions; China converts far below the US.
- Overpricing progress boosts in brain-rot games; keep them under 60 Robux.
