# the roblox monetization tierlist nobody is honest about

[Watch](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w) | alexbuilds_games | 3,926 views | 2026-06-16

## Summary
Speaker ranks Roblox monetization methods into tiers based on his experience across 15+ games. Covers pay-to-win packs (top), troll/jump scare (top for social/horror), crates/chests (strong across genres), revive (high), and weaker options like currency packs, VIP, and pay-for-convenience. Stresses that fit to game type matters and that too many monetization methods dilute the best ones.

## Tips
- [1:57](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=117) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Sell exclusive pay-to-win packs in competitive games (combat, tower defense); speaker calls them his S-tier method, bundling items into one gamepass.
- [1:09](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=69) **[[monetisation-other]]** Price progress boosts by value: cheap (under 60 Robux) for brain-rot games, high for combat games where progress gives real advantage.
- [4:24](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=264) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Don't rely on currency packs; speaker found them weakest (D tier) in most genres, with buyers preferring direct item purchases.
- [5:40](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=340) **[[monetisation-other]]** Add cheap impulse troll or jump-scare purchases to social multiplayer games (two-player obbies, story, horror) where friends play together.
- [6:20](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=380) **[[monetisation-other]]** Only add jump-scare or troll items where they fit the game; don't put them in open-world or high-value games.
- [7:22](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=442) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid stacking too many monetization methods; extra cheap offers distract from your best sellers.
- [7:22](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=442) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Pay-to-express items (auras, trails) only sell in games where players are already invested in expression, like anime or Adopt Me-style games.
- [8:43](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=523) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Bundle VIP with real perks such as exclusive areas or secret levels; a plain overhead badge at $3.99 won't sell.
- [10:19](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=619) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Keep pay-for-convenience items (auto collect, trade slots, teleports) very cheap and use as a secondary seller, not the main income.
- [11:20](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=680) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Sell revive as a cheap impulse purchase; speaker says it was a top earner in story, horror and combat games.
- [12:06](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=726) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use chests, crates or lucky blocks with gambling-style RNG in nearly any game; price higher in high-value games (he cites up to 1,900 Robux).
- [14:48](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=888) **[[pricing]]** Offer cheaper rerolls (about 4-5 Robux) on crates so players keep rerolling; speaker says this was a main earner in a past game.
- [15:30](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=930) **[[monetisation-other]]** Put currency packs at the bottom of the shop UI; buyers rarely pick them over direct purchases.
