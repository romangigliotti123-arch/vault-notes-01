# how to get your Roblox game recommended by the algorithm (explained simply)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/wb8XBVFI2i4) | alexbuilds_games | 4,762 views | 2026-05-16

## Summary
Explains the Roblox recommendation algorithm as optimizing for click-through, stay time, and D1/D7 retention. Recommends running short ads (about 7 days, around $16/day) to gather audience data, then shutting ads off so Roblox's home feed takes over, and iterating on whatever stat is weak.

## Tips
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/wb8XBVFI2i4?t=0) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Roblox's algorithm aims to keep people on Roblox longer; it pushes games that get clicks, keep players staying, and bring them back (D1/D7).
- [2:05](https://youtu.be/wb8XBVFI2i4?t=125) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Treat your game as a brand: titles, icons and copy form the first impression on the home page. Study top games in your niche and adapt their creatives.
- [3:27](https://youtu.be/wb8XBVFI2i4?t=207) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Aim for a CTR around 5%; a 0.5% CTR is poor, meaning it takes ~200 impressions per click and signals weak demand.
- [4:08](https://youtu.be/wb8XBVFI2i4?t=248) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Build on existing demand in a niche rather than inventing new tastes; pick a trend players already engage with.
- [6:11](https://youtu.be/wb8XBVFI2i4?t=371) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Set D1 and D7 retention targets for evergreen games; mild trend games may accept lower retention, but evergreen games need it.
- [8:15](https://youtu.be/wb8XBVFI2i4?t=495) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use Roblox ads to gather fast data early rather than relying on SEO, which the speaker calls a slow, weaker main traffic driver.
- [8:55](https://youtu.be/wb8XBVFI2i4?t=535) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Run ads for about 7 days so Roblox's learning phase (about 2 days, then optimizing over 7) finds your most engaged audience, then stop them.
- [10:18](https://youtu.be/wb8XBVFI2i4?t=618) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** After ads, stop spending and let home recommendations fill the game; the algorithm targets players similar to those who engaged with your ad traffic.
- [12:21](https://youtu.be/wb8XBVFI2i4?t=741) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Roblox scales games in stages: test a small audience (e.g. 5 CCU), then 100 CCU; if stats don't stabilize it scales back, so keep improving weak stats.
- [13:43](https://youtu.be/wb8XBVFI2i4?t=823) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Roll out updates targeting whichever stat is weak, such as retention items when D1 or D7 is poor, since Roblox rewards improved stats.
- [14:24](https://youtu.be/wb8XBVFI2i4?t=864) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Build monetization in from the start; conversion varies by region (e.g. China far below the US), so not all CCU earn the same.
