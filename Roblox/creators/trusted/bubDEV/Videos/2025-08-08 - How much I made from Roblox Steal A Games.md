# How much I made from Roblox Steal A Games

[Watch](https://youtu.be/Iv80MDXZBXU) | bubDEV | 81,730 views | 2025-08-08

## Summary
Creator shares revenue from Steal-a-Brainrot-style "steal" games on Roblox: his first game (Steal Anime Characters, ~900K visits) was abandoned due to clones, so he made Steal Sprunkkey in about a day from a reusable template, which now earns ~20-40K Robux/day. He also sells the template itself.

## Tips
- [2:27](https://youtu.be/Iv80MDXZBXU?t=147) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build the game as a reusable template so a new game can be re-skinned and released in under a day.
- [2:53](https://youtu.be/Iv80MDXZBXU?t=173) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Make the template drag-and-drop so theme swaps (characters, shop, index) need minimal code changes.
- [2:02](https://youtu.be/Iv80MDXZBXU?t=122) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Catch a viral trend early (under 5K CCU) and move fast; the creator says he and his partner did this with Steal a Brainrot.
- [1:00](https://youtu.be/Iv80MDXZBXU?t=60) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid crowded clone themes; the creator abandoned Steal Anime Characters because many identical anime-stealing games appeared.
- [4:53](https://youtu.be/Iv80MDXZBXU?t=293) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Selling your template to other creators can earn more than making games yourself, per the creator's own estimate.
- [6:51](https://youtu.be/Iv80MDXZBXU?t=411) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Don't assume you need 10K concurrent players; a game with ~200 active players can still earn meaningful Robux.
