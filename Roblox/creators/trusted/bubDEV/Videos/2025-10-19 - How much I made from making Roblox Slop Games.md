# How much I made from making Roblox "Slop" Games

[Watch](https://youtu.be/N-u2gI4DUyw) | bubDEV | 217,642 views | 2025-10-19

## Summary
Creator shows revenue and CCU stats for several template-based "slop" Roblox games (Don't Wake Up series, Don't Steal the Brain Rods) and argues the templates are lucrative, citing D7 retention and limited-time items as drivers. Templates are sold via Discord and sell out within 24 hours.

## Tips
- [5:51](https://youtu.be/N-u2gI4DUyw?t=351) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Prioritise D7 (and D30) retention over D1 or average playtime; the speaker says weak D7/D30 is the main reason games fall off after a week to a month.
- [7:14](https://youtu.be/N-u2gI4DUyw?t=434) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Add a limited-time purchasable item to the game; the speaker says the template lacked one and he expects it to lift ARPDU.
- [7:14](https://youtu.be/N-u2gI4DUyw?t=434) **[[monetisation-other]]** Ship with a currency and starter pack at minimum; the template only came with currency and a starter pack, which he calls minimal monetisation.
- [4:20](https://youtu.be/N-u2gI4DUyw?t=260) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Reskin a proven template with a trending brand or theme while changing the name (e.g. Don't Wake to Don't Steal) so it is not an identical copy.
- [2:51](https://youtu.be/N-u2gI4DUyw?t=171) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Reuse a proven core mechanic (driving away from police) with a new theme such as cars or soccer players to ride existing player interest.
- [10:39](https://youtu.be/N-u2gI4DUyw?t=639) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Treat cash-grab templates as a reliable short-term earner, but original games are the speaker's view of the top-earning long-term titles.
