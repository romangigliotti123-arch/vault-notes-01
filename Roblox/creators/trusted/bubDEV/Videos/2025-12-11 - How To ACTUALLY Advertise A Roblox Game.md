# How To ACTUALLY Advertise A Roblox Game

[Watch](https://youtu.be/7z8rfg_jBuk) | bubDEV | 90,663 views | 2025-12-11

## Summary
Creator explains three ways to get a Roblox game into the algorithm: sponsored ads (Roblox's own ad system, ~250 Robux-style lifetime budget over ~5-7 days), organic search ranking via title/description keywords (template games ranking high in search), and external promotion (YouTube/TikTok/Discord hype) for a loyal player base. Heavy promotion of his own Discord/templates.

## Tips
- [1:40](https://youtu.be/7z8rfg_jBuk?t=100) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Roblox's algorithm needs initial player data; you must give the game an initial push before it will recommend it further.
- [3:29](https://youtu.be/7z8rfg_jBuk?t=209) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** For a new game, set ad goal to Maximize Plays, lifetime budget around 250 (range 150-400), running 5-7 days; longer runs are unnecessary.
- [3:29](https://youtu.be/7z8rfg_jBuk?t=209) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Keep ad device and location targeting at defaults (all devices); narrowing to computer/mobile/tablet cut reach significantly in his tests.
- [4:51](https://youtu.be/7z8rfg_jBuk?t=291) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make ad thumbnails clear and clickable so the game's type is understood at a glance; players spend very little time on thumbnails.
- [7:12](https://youtu.be/7z8rfg_jBuk?t=432) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Use a keyword in the title or description that ranks in search; a clickable, keyword-rich name can drive early search traffic that triggers the algorithm.
- [7:12](https://youtu.be/7z8rfg_jBuk?t=432) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Watch whether a new game ranks high in search against games with far higher CCU; this is a strong early signal it will take off.
- [10:17](https://youtu.be/7z8rfg_jBuk?t=617) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** Pre-release hype via YouTube, TikTok or Instagram brings a ready, loyal player base that gives the algorithm early engagement.
- [11:38](https://youtu.be/7z8rfg_jBuk?t=698) **[[community-and-discord]]** Funnel creators and players into a Discord server or community to keep an engaged audience in one place.
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/7z8rfg_jBuk?t=40) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Game quality still does most of the work; a good game by Roblox's standards with a strong loop is what the algorithm rewards.
