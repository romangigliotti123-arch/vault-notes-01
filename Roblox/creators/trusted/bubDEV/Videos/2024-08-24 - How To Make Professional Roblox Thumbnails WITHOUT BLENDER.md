# How To Make Professional Roblox Thumbnails WITHOUT BLENDER

[Watch](https://youtu.be/th2DJ7-5MnU) | bubDEV | 118,279 views | 2024-08-24

## Summary
Shows how the creator builds a Roblox YouTube thumbnail without Blender: pose noob avatars in Studio, capture a render, cut out and stroke them in Photopea, add yellow effects, a sky, an explosion, and a Pressure horror-game cutout, then add vignettes. Mostly a process walkthrough with no growth data.

## Tips
- [1:22](https://youtu.be/th2DJ7-5MnU?t=82) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Remove the Noob's name tag (or set Humanoid DisplayName to none) to clean up the character before capturing it.
- [2:03](https://youtu.be/th2DJ7-5MnU?t=123) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Pose avatars by moving limbs with Studio's move tools rather than the animation editor; offset shoulders so they are never in line.
- [2:44](https://youtu.be/th2DJ7-5MnU?t=164) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Vary duplicated characters slightly in pose so a crowd of copies does not look copy-pasted.
- [3:24](https://youtu.be/th2DJ7-5MnU?t=204) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Place a backdrop part behind the characters and set the wall colour to a contrasting shade so the subjects stand out.
- [4:36](https://youtu.be/th2DJ7-5MnU?t=276) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Hide the output and toolbox panels and capture at a larger resolution to get more pixels to work with.
- [5:09](https://youtu.be/th2DJ7-5MnU?t=309) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Create a 1280x720 canvas in Photopea for YouTube thumbnails and use the magic wand on a white background to select and cut out characters.
- [7:15](https://youtu.be/th2DJ7-5MnU?t=435) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Add a thick black outer stroke plus a thinner white stroke to make cutouts pop; copy and paste layer styles between characters for consistency.
- [9:22](https://youtu.be/th2DJ7-5MnU?t=562) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Boost brightness and contrast on the character layer to give a drawn, illustrated look that stands out from a plain Roblox render.
- [12:19](https://youtu.be/th2DJ7-5MnU?t=739) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Enlarge the Roblox face asset, outline it in black, and place it over the head for a more readable expression.
- [14:22](https://youtu.be/th2DJ7-5MnU?t=862) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Add a shadow to the leader character and emphasise key elements so the focal point is clear.
- [16:31](https://youtu.be/th2DJ7-5MnU?t=991) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Pair two contrasting subjects on each side of the thumbnail, linked by an arrow, to communicate the video's premise at a glance.
- [17:55](https://youtu.be/th2DJ7-5MnU?t=1075) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Finish with vignettes and light effects to unify the composition.
- [17:14](https://youtu.be/th2DJ7-5MnU?t=1034) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use a referenced game's recognisable character to attract clicks even when the thumbnail meaning is loose; the creator admits it is brainrot-style clickbait.
