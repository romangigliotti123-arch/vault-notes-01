# bubDEV - How to make successful Roblox games

bubDEV says a Roblox game succeeds when it earns four green analytics signals: long sessions, returning players, spending, and click appeal. These usually come from a strong, proven core loop. His newest advice (Oct 2025 to Jan 2026) is to build a reusable drag-and-drop template, re-skin it with a trending theme and keyword-rich title in about a day, then launch with sponsored ads (lifetime budget ~250, 5-7 days) and creator or Discord hype. His earlier advice (Aug-Sep 2025) favoured originality. Revenue figures are self-reported.

**Style:** Numbers-driven, template-first operator who shares revenue screenshots and sells his own templates via Discord, so promotion is built in. Suits solo developers who want fast re-skin-and-ship projects built on proven loops. Less suited to developers building original, long-term IP. Verify all revenue figures independently.

## Playbook
1. **Idea: reuse a proven core loop** [[genre-mechanics]]: Pick a mechanic already proven on Roblox and re-theme it (e.g. police-chase driving reskinned with cars or soccer players) to ride existing player interest. Catch trends early, ideally under 5K CCU, and move fast. Cited: Iv80MDXZBXU, 2lPsxPtj2mM.
2. **Idea: design for the four engagement signals** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Before building, design the concept around long session time, players returning, players spending, and click appeal. The creator argues a strong core concept carries a game further than polish. Cited: kg_h09_x7no.
3. **Build: make a reusable template** [[development-process-and-speed]]: Build the game as a drag-and-drop template so characters, shop and index can be swapped with minimal code changes, letting a new game be re-skinned and released in under a day. Cited: Iv80MDXZBXU, N-u2gI4DUyw.
4. **Build: minimum monetisation plus a limited-time item** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: Ship with a currency and starter pack at minimum. The creator says his template lacked a limited-time purchasable item and expects one to lift ARPDU. Cited: N-u2gI4DUyw.
5. **Build: thumbnails and icons** [[thumbnails-icons]]: Create a 1280x720 canvas, remove the Noob name tags, vary duplicated poses, use a contrasting backdrop, hide UI, cut out characters in Photopea and add black and white strokes. Cited: th2DJ7-5MnU.
6. **Launch: keyword-rich title and description** [[titles-descriptions]]: Re-skin a proven template with a trending theme and change its name (e.g. Don't Wake Up to Don't Steal) so it is not an identical copy. Use search keywords in title and description to drive early search traffic. Cited: 7z8rfg_jBuk.
7. **Launch: pre-release hype and community** [[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]: Build hype on YouTube, TikTok or Instagram to bring a loyal player base that gives the algorithm early engagement. Funnel players into a Discord server to keep the audience in one place. Cited: 7z8rfg_jBuk, N-u2gI4DUyw.
8. **Launch: sponsored ads, first week only** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Set ad goal to Maximize Plays with a lifetime budget around 250 (range 150-400), run 5-7 days, and keep device and location targeting at defaults. Narrowing targeting cut reach significantly in his tests. Cited: 7z8rfg_jBuk.
9. **Launch: read the four signals about two days in** [[analytics-metrics]]: Check analytics a couple of days after release and mark each of the four signals green or red. Aim for all green to judge viral potential. Cited: kg_h09_x7no.
10. **Growth: give the algorithm an initial push** [[discovery-algorithm]]: The algorithm needs initial player data before it recommends a game further. Watch whether a new game ranks high in search against games with far higher CCU. Earnings may only appear after about a week. Cited: 7z8rfg_jBuk, 2lPsxPtj2mM.
11. **Growth: prioritise D7 and D30 retention** [[retention-d1-d7]]: Weak D7 and D30 retention is, per the creator, the main reason games fall off after a week to a month, so prioritise these over D1 or average playtime. Cited: N-u2gI4DUyw.
12. **Live ops: update days and limited-time items** [[updates-live-ops]]: Plan updates as player spikes. Don't Steal the Brain Rods reached 5,800 active on update day versus ~1,000-2,000 normally. Add limited-time items to drive spending. Cited: N-u2gI4DUyw.
13. **Optional: sell the template** [[business-and-payouts]]: The creator sells his template via Discord in stock batches of 25 that sell out within 24 hours, and estimates template sales can out-earn making games yourself. This is his own estimate and a promotion. Cited: Iv80MDXZBXU, N-u2gI4DUyw.

## Common mistakes
- Cloning a crowded theme after rivals appear; Steal Anime Characters was abandoned after many identical clones (Iv80MDXZBXU)
- Treating self-reported Robux-to-USD conversions as verified income; they are the creator's DevEx estimates (kg_h09_x7no, 2lPsxPtj2mM)
- Judging a game by D1 or average playtime while ignoring weak D7 and D30 retention (N-u2gI4DUyw)
- Shipping a template without a limited-time purchasable item, which the creator says left monetisation minimal (N-u2gI4DUyw)
- Keeping the original name when re-skinning a template, producing an identical copy (7z8rfg_jBuk)
- Narrowing ad device or location targeting, which cut reach significantly in tests (7z8rfg_jBuk)
- Running ads longer than 5-7 days; the creator says longer runs are unnecessary (7z8rfg_jBuk)
- Expecting earnings or algorithm reach immediately; it can take about a week after release (7z8rfg_jBuk, 2lPsxPtj2mM)
- Posing duplicated characters identically in thumbnails, so the crowd looks copy-pasted (th2DJ7-5MnU)
- Assuming you need 10K concurrent players; a game with ~200 active players can still earn meaningful Robux (Iv80MDXZBXU)

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Confirm the core loop reuses a proven mechanic and the trend is still early (under 5K CCU) (Iv80MDXZBXU, 2lPsxPtj2mM)
- [ ] Write four-signal targets (session length, returns, spending, click appeal) before building (kg_h09_x7no)
- [ ] Build as a drag-and-drop template so re-skin takes under a day (Iv80MDXZBXU)
- [ ] Ship with currency and starter pack, plus at least one limited-time purchasable item (N-u2gI4DUyw)
- [ ] Rename the re-skin with a trending theme and a search-keyword title and description (7z8rfg_jBuk)
- [ ] Make a 1280x720 thumbnail with varied poses and a contrasting backdrop (th2DJ7-5MnU)
- [ ] Line up pre-release hype on YouTube, TikTok or Instagram and a Discord server (7z8rfg_jBuk)
- [ ] Set ad goal to Maximize Plays, lifetime budget ~250, 5-7 days, default targeting (7z8rfg_jBuk)
- [ ] Check analytics about two days after release and colour each signal green or red (kg_h09_x7no)
- [ ] Compare search rank against games with far higher CCU as an early signal (7z8rfg_jBuk)
- [ ] Track D7 and D30 retention, not just D1 (N-u2gI4DUyw)
- [ ] Schedule the first update to create a player spike (N-u2gI4DUyw)

## How the advice changed over time
- Aug 2025 (Iv80MDXZBXU): abandoned original Steal Anime Characters after clones and moved to a reusable template, Steal Sprunkkey, built in about a day.
- Sep 2025 (kg_h09_x7no): argued originality beats copying winning games. This is superseded by later template-first advice; the newer stance is preferred for tactics.
- Oct 2025 onward (N-u2gI4DUyw): promoted template-based games and sold templates via Discord, reversing the originality stance above.
- Dec 2025 (7z8rfg_jBuk): added search keywords and sponsored ads as growth levers, with a specific ad budget.
- Jan 2026 (2lPsxPtj2mM): main thesis became reusing proven core loops; Escape Tsunami template is the newest case study.
- Thumbnail advice (th2DJ7-5MnU, Aug 2024) predates the template strategy and has not been updated.
