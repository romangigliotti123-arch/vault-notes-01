## Summary
Creator spends 100,000 Robux on Roblox ads over ~2 months for his obby/building game, tracking ad credits, concurrency, favorites, likes and monetization. Result: 731 Robux revenue, a clear loss. Shows ad spend without retention/monetisation fixes fails.

## Tips
- [0:41](https://youtu.be/B2vC4-QArWg?t=41) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Split large ad budgets across multiple days rather than spending it all in one day; spread spend to test performance over time.
- [1:22](https://youtu.be/B2vC4-QArWg?t=82) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Fix broken core functionality (queueing, bugs) before spending on ads; ads sent to a broken game were wasted.
- [2:04](https://youtu.be/B2vC4-QArWg?t=124) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Monetisation was too subtle; make monetisation more prominent in-game. Lowering a gamepass price did not fix low earnings.
- [9:35](https://youtu.be/B2vC4-QArWg?t=575) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Paid ads alone did not convert: 100k Robux spend yielded 731 Robux revenue. Validate monetisation and retention before scaling ad spend.
