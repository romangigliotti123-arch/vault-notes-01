## Summary
Creator Mulch shows a Dragon Ball avatar try-on experience with 800 visits and no gamepasses that earned about 7,000 Robux in a year via Roblox UGC commission (30% of item sales made in his game, including purchases shortly after leaving). Explains how to build it with product-ID prompts and free models.

## Tips
- [2:06](https://youtu.be/An_5Mu0aR5I?t=126) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Display UGC items for sale in your experience to earn the 30% Roblox commission on sales made through your game, even if you don't create the items.
- [3:28](https://youtu.be/An_5Mu0aR5I?t=208) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Commission also pays on purchases made just after a player leaves your game, not only during play, so prompt purchases in the game.
- [5:31](https://youtu.be/An_5Mu0aR5I?t=331) **[[combat-and-gameplay-systems]]** Use a ClickDetector that fires a short script to prompt purchase of a product ID; Roblox handles the purchase popup for you.
- [6:11](https://youtu.be/An_5Mu0aR5I?t=371) **[[building-and-level-design]]** A base plate with simple union parts and free models is enough for a try-on showroom; visuals don't need to be polished.
- [6:53](https://youtu.be/An_5Mu0aR5I?t=413) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Pick characters you like, make outfits, and use an avatar loader plugin to build a try-on experience.
