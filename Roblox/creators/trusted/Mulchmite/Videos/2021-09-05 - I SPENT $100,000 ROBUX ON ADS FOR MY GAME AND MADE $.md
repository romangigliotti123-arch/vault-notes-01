# I SPENT $100,000 ROBUX ON ADS FOR MY GAME AND MADE $...

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Watch](https://youtu.be/yG44d_Xnbwg) | Mulchmite | 468,180 views | 2021-09-05

## Summary
Creator spent ~100k Robux on a sponsored experience ad for his first Roblox game (Store Simulator) over three days, then reports the ad's reach, click-through, cost per click, and game-pass and dev-product revenue. Mostly a results breakdown with a few cost observations.

## Tips
- [1:34](https://youtu.be/yG44d_Xnbwg?t=94) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Sponsored experiences ran about 33,333 Robux/day; a broad all-platform, all-ages ad was seen by 280.3M people but got only ~0.019% CTR.
- [2:55](https://youtu.be/yG44d_Xnbwg?t=175) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Sponsored ad cost about 5 Robux per play (cost per click ~1.86 Robux, ~0.008% post-view conversion). Check whether these benchmarks fit your goal before spending.
- [3:56](https://youtu.be/yG44d_Xnbwg?t=236) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Sell game passes and dev products together; in this game gamepasses earned ~12.4k Robux and dev products ~8k Robux over three days.
- [5:22](https://youtu.be/yG44d_Xnbwg?t=322) **[[pricing]]** Game pass prices varied from 4.99 to 399 Robux; the 75 Robux pass sold most (38), while the 399 Robux pass sold only 6.

## Numbers
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/yG44d_Xnbwg?t=40) Sponsored ad spend: **~99,999 Robux over 3 days (~33,333/day)** (Store Simulator, all platforms, all ages)
- [2:14](https://youtu.be/yG44d_Xnbwg?t=134) Ad impressions: **280.3 million** (Sponsored experience ad reach)
- [2:14](https://youtu.be/yG44d_Xnbwg?t=134) Ad click-through rate: **~0.019% (53,000 clicks)** (Clicks out of 280.3M impressions)
- [2:55](https://youtu.be/yG44d_Xnbwg?t=175) Cost per click: **~1.86 Robux** (Spend divided by clicks)

## Games
- **Store Simulator** [0:54](https://youtu.be/yG44d_Xnbwg?t=54): Creator's first game, sponsored for 100k Robux; ~14.4k Robux net back in 3 days, showing ads rarely pay back quickly.
