## Summary
Creator revisits Store Simulator, a Roblox game launched with 100,000 Robux of ads 19 months earlier. Tallies about 34,064 Robux earned after Roblox's 30% cut, a net loss of about 66,000 Robux. Attributes the decline to a three-day ad run, leaderboard scaling problems and a rushed release.

## Tips
- [8:58](https://youtu.be/6perjdMg3T4?t=538) **[[common-mistakes]]** Ads returned about 34k net on 100k Robux spend here. Prove retention and monetisation before spending on ads.
- [8:17](https://youtu.be/6perjdMg3T4?t=497) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Multiply gross Robux by 0.7 to get real earnings before comparing against ad spend.
- [9:38](https://youtu.be/6perjdMg3T4?t=578) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Leaderboard scores grew too large and crashed players' scores; cap or rescale currency rewards early.
- [8:17](https://youtu.be/6perjdMg3T4?t=497) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** A rushed release hurt retention; polish the core experience before sending paid traffic.
