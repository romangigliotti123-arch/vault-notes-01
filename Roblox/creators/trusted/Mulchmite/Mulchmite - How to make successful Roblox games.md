# Mulchmite - How to make successful Roblox games

Mulchmite's core claim: revenue comes from a game people keep playing, not from buying visibility. His 100k-Robux ad experiments lost money every time: about 34k Robux net after 19 months in 2023 (6perjdMg3T4), and only 731 Robux revenue in 2025 (B2vC4-QArWg). Build simple, novel ideas fast from free models, then monetise with private servers, cosmetic gamepasses at 59-99 Robux, and UGC commissions. Fix bugs and polish the core before any paid traffic, and keep live ops going with fresh-themed maps.

**Style:** Blunt, numbers-first results reporter who builds fast, low-art games (free models, one line of code) and publishes losses as well as wins. Suits solo developers who want realistic expectations on ads, private servers, gamepasses and UGC commissions. Less useful for polished-game craft or large-team production advice.

## Playbook
1. **Pick a simple, novel idea** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Choose a multiplayer twist on a popular genre that you can build in minutes, such as a 3-player obby. He built one in about 5 minutes with free models and one line of code (pqCXX06vlDw). Keep scope tiny so you can ship and measure quickly.
2. **Consider a UGC try-on showroom as a low-effort option** [[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]: A try-on needs only a base plate, simple union parts and free models; visuals need not be polished. Pick characters you like, make outfits, and use an avatar loader plugin. Display UGC items for sale to earn the 30% commission (An_5Mu0aR5I).
3. **Test core mechanics in-game early** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: Check whether blocks or items need rotation or directional placement before building many variants. In race obbies, start all paths from the same front line so the race is fair. Validate the core loop with real playtests before layout work.
4. **Build maps with distinct themes** [[building-art-and-assets]]: Theme each map differently (he cites a stud/Lego look and a cloud-start theme) so the game feels fresh; he found this the most fun to build (y9Ii0VEFaEU). Use a floor texture strip for a checkered finish line and neon kill bricks for hazards that stand out.
5. **Reuse templates and outsource what you cannot finish** [[development-process-and-speed]]: Reuse start/finish area templates from earlier games to speed up new obby maps. If your own queue system attempt fails, commission a working one rather than sinking time into it. Speed matters more than custom polish at this stage.
6. **Add a level-select menu** [[matchmaking-and-teleport]]: Add a level-select menu on join that teleports players to other maps, with a close option so they can stay put (y9Ii0VEFaEU). Test teleports on the published game, because TeleportService does not work inside Studio.
7. **Fix core bugs before launch** [[first-session-onboarding]]: Make sure core functionality such as queueing and the respawn/reset popup works. A respawn popup that reappears after each death can make the game unplayable. Rushed releases hurt retention, so polish the core experience before sending any paid traffic.
8. **Sell gamepasses and private servers** [[monetisation-gamepasses]]: Sell cosmetic or convenience gamepasses at 59-99 Robux; the 79 and 99 Robux passes earned most (pqCXX06vlDw). Sell private servers at 50 Robux for group play, such as racing friends. Make monetisation visible in-game, not subtle.
9. **Price tests and dev products** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: The 75 Robux pass sold most (38) while the 399 Robux pass sold only 6. Lowering a price did not fix low earnings. Add donation boards and skip-stage dev products, which earned about 3,000 Robux in 2024. Use a ClickDetector that fires a short script to prompt a product ID purchase.
10. **Do not lower private-server prices after sale** [[monetisation-other]]: Changing the private server price cancels existing subscriptions, so keep the price stable (he kept 50 Robux since launch, pqCXX06vlDw). Premium payouts add up over time: one day in 2023 paid about 1,000 Robux. Track them on the transaction page, since Creator Hub shows only the past year.
11. **Hold ad spend until retention and monetisation are proven** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Paid ads did not convert: 100k Robux spend yielded 731 Robux revenue (B2vC4-QArWg). Even the 2021 run netted only about 14.4% of spend after Roblox's cut (yG44d_Xnbwg). If you do test ads, split the budget across several days and expect revenue to drop once the paid traffic ends.
12. **Refresh creatives regularly** [[thumbnails-icons]]: A thumbnail built on a trending meme went outdated within a month; the Squid Game thumbnail performed best (6perjdMg3T4). Refresh thumbnails and icons on a schedule rather than leaving one running for months.
13. **Track net earnings with the 0.7 multiplier** [[analytics-metrics]]: Roblox keeps about 30%, so multiply gross Robux by 0.7 before comparing against ad spend. The creator dashboard shows only about two months of developer product history, so export CSV sales and sum them in Excel for older data.
14. **Revive declining games with rebuilds and live ops** [[updates-live-ops]]: When player counts decline, rebuild the medium map, add a themed hard map, and add a teleport level-select menu (y9Ii0VEFaEU). Revenue from a game with ongoing play, such as private servers in 2022 with about 1.7M players at peak, outlasted paid traffic.

## Common mistakes
- Buying ads before retention and monetisation work: 100k Robux bought ~34k net in 2023 and 731 Robux revenue in 2025.
- Spending a large ad budget in one or a few days instead of spreading it out to test performance over time.
- Sending paid traffic to a broken game (queueing, respawn popup, core bugs), which wastes the spend.
- Letting the respawn/reset popup reappear after every death, which can make the game unplayable.
- Leaderboard currency rewards that grow too large and break player scores; cap or rescale them early.
- Hiding monetisation so it is too subtle to notice; he says it must be more prominent in-game.
- Treating a lower gamepass price as a fix for weak earnings; the 2025 price drop from 50 to 25 did not solve it.
- Lowering a private server price after it has sold, which cancels existing subscriptions.
- Testing teleports in Studio, where TeleportService does not work; publish to verify.
- Expecting a trend thumbnail to stay effective; a meme thumbnail was outdated within a month.

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Fix core functionality (queueing, teleports, respawn) and playtest the published game.
- [ ] Confirm the respawn/reset popup does not reappear after each death.
- [ ] Test TeleportService on the published game, not in Studio.
- [ ] Make monetisation visible in-game (clear gamepass and product prompts).
- [ ] Set cosmetic or convenience gamepasses in the 59-99 Robux range.
- [ ] Offer a 50 Robux private server for group play and keep that price stable.
- [ ] Add a donation board and skip-stage dev products.
- [ ] Use a ClickDetector script to prompt product-ID purchases.
- [ ] Cap or rescale leaderboard currency rewards before players accumulate them.
- [ ] Use high-contrast neon hazard colors that stand out against the stage.
- [ ] Display UGC items for sale in-game to earn the 30% commission.
- [ ] Prompt purchases right after a player leaves, since post-exit purchases still pay commission.
- [ ] Set up a CSV export routine for dev product sales before the dashboard history window passes.
- [ ] Record gross Robux and multiply by 0.7 for net earnings.
- [ ] Hold the ad budget until retention and monetisation are proven.
- [ ] If testing ads, plan to split spend across several days.
- [ ] Plan a thumbnail/icon refresh schedule before the first month ends.
- [ ] Track Premium payouts on the Roblox transaction page, not only Creator Hub.

## How the advice changed over time
- 2021 video presents paid ads as a test with a full results breakdown; from 2023 onward the creator frames paid ads as a net loss unless retention and monetisation come first.
- 2021-2023 advice centred on a larger game with paid growth; by 2024 he favours cheap, fast builds (5-minute obby, free models) with private servers and gamepasses as the main income.
- 2025 campaign confirms the 2023 advice: ads without retention returned 731 Robux on 100k spent. This is the most recent evidence and takes precedence over the 2021 results.
- Conflict in notes: the 2022 video states a 30% commission on UGC item sales, while a separate note lists 10% commission on purchases made just after a player leaves. The 10% figure is undated and unverified here; treat 30% as the stated rate and check the current Creator Hub before planning.
