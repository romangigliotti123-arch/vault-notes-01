## Tips
- [0:41](https://youtu.be/XZKHQPObHgw?t=41) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** In a 48-hour jam, pick one simple core concept (a button and wandering enemies) and build it first, then layer on extras.
- [4:09](https://youtu.be/XZKHQPObHgw?t=249) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** If the core loop feels too simple, add a timer, breakable fences, and trapping mechanics to create more decisions.
- [2:05](https://youtu.be/XZKHQPObHgw?t=125) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Make enemy behaviour unpredictable (random wandering, then sudden attacks) so players stay tense rather than learning a pattern.
- [1:24](https://youtu.be/XZKHQPObHgw?t=84) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Switch from third person to first person when third person is too hard to control for the core interaction.
