## Tips
- [1:29](https://youtu.be/jKyzG9HPrRM?t=89) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Prototype pickup systems with temporary parts first; the dev found scripting all-in-one pickup logic caused items to constantly respawn, so test separate approaches.
- [2:09](https://youtu.be/jKyzG9HPrRM?t=129) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Roblox Touched events can be unreliable for pickups or tools; expect to debug them and consider alternative detection methods.
- [2:51](https://youtu.be/jKyzG9HPrRM?t=171) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Welding parts to tween models is awkward on Roblox; use welds/constraints and animate the assembled tool rather than tweening individual models.
- [3:33](https://youtu.be/jKyzG9HPrRM?t=213) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Simplify mechanics where possible, e.g. replant the crop itself instead of adding a separate seed item, to keep the game easy to understand.
