## Tips
- [0:44](https://youtu.be/nQPm7gmRBzs?t=44) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ad credits minimum buy was 10 (2,850 Robux); daily budget minimum is 5. Campaign objective was visits, not awareness.
- [2:06](https://youtu.be/nQPm7gmRBzs?t=126) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Low bids (0.01 per play) push ads to less prominent placements; higher bids get better placement but cost more per play.
- [5:36](https://youtu.be/nQPm7gmRBzs?t=336) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ad traffic brings many joiners but most leave quickly; retention and monetisation of paid-acquired players was poor here, so ads alone did not convert to Robux.
- [8:24](https://youtu.be/nQPm7gmRBzs?t=504) **[[monetisation-other]]** Ads spend cost far exceeded revenue: spent ~2,850 Robux, earned 21 Robux. Validate monetisation before buying traffic.
