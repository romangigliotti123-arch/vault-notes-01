# How to set up Roblox ads PROPERLY!

## Summary
Walkthrough of setting up a Roblox ad campaign on advertise.roblox.com: budget type (daily vs lifetime), schedule, sponsored experience ad set, audience (US/Canada, age, gender), bidding at low Robux bids, and ad creative using the game icon. Low-depth, mostly a UI tour.

## Tips
- [0:41](https://youtu.be/0NK_JBYEILA?t=41) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use daily budget cap if you don't want credits spent quickly; lifetime budget spreads the full credit across the whole campaign duration.
- [1:22](https://youtu.be/0NK_JBYEILA?t=82) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Start audience targeting with US and Canada; the speaker reports these work well for his ads, but test to confirm for your game.
- [1:22](https://youtu.be/0NK_JBYEILA?t=82) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Narrow gender and age targeting to match your spending audience (e.g. 13-17 or 18-24) based on who actually spends Robux in your game.
- [2:04](https://youtu.be/0NK_JBYEILA?t=124) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Bid low per-game (around 0.01 to 0.03 Robux) to avoid overspending; the speaker says Roblox's suggested 0.04-0.25 range is not necessary.
- [2:45](https://youtu.be/0NK_JBYEILA?t=165) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Have a strong game icon before advertising; the icon is what drives clicks in the sponsored tab.
