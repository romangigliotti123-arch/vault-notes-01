## Tips
- [1:23](https://youtu.be/feVPsViqceo?t=83) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Add a tutorial system so new players learn how to play; the creator added one before advertising.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/feVPsViqceo?t=0) **[[common-mistakes]]** Fix obvious economy bugs before spending on ads, e.g. selling price equal to purchase price leaves no profit.
