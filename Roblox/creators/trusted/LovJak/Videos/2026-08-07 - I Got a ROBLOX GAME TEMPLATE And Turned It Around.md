# I Got a ROBLOX GAME TEMPLATE And Turned It Around

[Watch](https://youtu.be/KZ_mxVrvHCI) | LovJak | 61,654 views | 2026-08-07

## Summary
Creator bought a simulator-obby template, reskinned it into a fruit swing-obby (fruits instead of brain rots, new spawns, stealing, tutorial, mute button, daily/playtime rewards), then ran Roblox ads (~$16/day for 6 days). Shares early stats: weak 5-minute retention, mostly 21+ audience, low upvotes, and low reach to all-ages.

## Tips
- [1:20](https://youtu.be/KZ_mxVrvHCI?t=80) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't publish a template unchanged; if the concept already exists (swing obby for brain rots), it won't go viral. Change the core theme first.
- [1:20](https://youtu.be/KZ_mxVrvHCI?t=80) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Differentiate a template by swapping its core collectible/theme, then re-hook items to rarity and income values; he reported this worked first try.
- [2:41](https://youtu.be/KZ_mxVrvHCI?t=161) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add playtime and daily rewards to bring players back; add a progress bar so players can see how far they are from the next goal.
- [3:22](https://youtu.be/KZ_mxVrvHCI?t=202) **[[combat-and-gameplay-systems]]** Make players work for their base by requiring purchase of storage areas; add a stealing system with a timed chase (proximity prompt to recover items) for interaction.
- [3:22](https://youtu.be/KZ_mxVrvHCI?t=202) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use a 30-minute play-time reward tied to joining a group as an incentive, e.g. a free mystery golden fruit, instead of a trading feature once stealing was added.
- [4:03](https://youtu.be/KZ_mxVrvHCI?t=243) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Add many spawn zones so progression lasts; five spawns ended in seconds. Space bases out to make stealing harder.
- [4:03](https://youtu.be/KZ_mxVrvHCI?t=243) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Add a tutorial that runs on first join so new players understand the loop before they leave.
- [4:45](https://youtu.be/KZ_mxVrvHCI?t=285) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Add a mute-background-music button; long testing sessions make looping music a problem for players too.
- [4:45](https://youtu.be/KZ_mxVrvHCI?t=285) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Plan the next update in advance (he scheduled an event calendar and badges) and set the icon and thumbnail before running ads.
- [6:49](https://youtu.be/KZ_mxVrvHCI?t=409) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check 5-minute retention early; he saw only 22% still playing after 5 minutes and suspected the onboarding or age-rating audience mix was the cause.
- [7:31](https://youtu.be/KZ_mxVrvHCI?t=451) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Track reach thresholds: he needed about 500 highly engaged players to unlock all-ages ad targeting, and had 300. Expect ads to reach a narrow audience until then.
- [8:14](https://youtu.be/KZ_mxVrvHCI?t=494) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Watch for recommendation signals: he had zero home recommendations and only 2K visits after ~6 days of ads, so paid traffic alone did not trigger discovery.
