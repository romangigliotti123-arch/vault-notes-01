## Summary
A beginner's log of learning Roblox animation in 24 hours using the free built-in animation editor. Mentions Moon Animator as a paid alternative.

## Tips
- [1:32](https://youtu.be/HBKUn339Frk?t=92) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Space keyframes further apart to slow an animation down; packing them too close makes motion look like a fast smack.
- [2:14](https://youtu.be/HBKUn339Frk?t=134) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** For a walk cycle, use bigger leg and arm swings and spread keyframes out; stiff, tiny, fast swings look like half-walking.
- [1:32](https://youtu.be/HBKUn339Frk?t=92) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Test each animation in-game after adjusting keyframes and iterate; the timing feels different in playback than in the editor.
