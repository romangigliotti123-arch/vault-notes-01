## Tips
- [0:12](https://youtu.be/M-o5hLZbjGY?t=12) **[[testing-debugging-and-qa]]** Use print() to confirm code runs; if the message doesn't show, the problem is above that line. Easiest way to track bugs.
- [0:50](https://youtu.be/M-o5hLZbjGY?t=50) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Break problems into small parts and solve one at a time; add effects, damage or rewards layer by layer after each piece works.
- [1:12](https://youtu.be/M-o5hLZbjGY?t=72) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Use WaitForChild() (e.g. PlayerGui:WaitForChild) for GUIs and parts that may not load in time, to avoid errors.
- [2:05](https://youtu.be/M-o5hLZbjGY?t=125) **[[testing-debugging-and-qa]]** Read the Output window when errors occur; errors like 'attempt to index nil' point to the exact line to fix.
- [2:48](https://youtu.be/M-o5hLZbjGY?t=168) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Practice small scripts daily; even 10 minutes a day builds logic and confidence faster than only watching tutorials.
