# How To Make Roblox UGC Hair [Blender Step-By-Step Tutorial]

## Summary
Blender tutorial for making Roblox UGC hair: imports the R6 block avatar dummy from Studio, models hair strands from circle-to-curve paths, mirrors and merges parts into one mesh, reduces triangles below 4,000, checks face orientation, and sets up the UV/texture in the shader editor.

## Tips
- [0:13](https://youtu.be/P9RpgVY8sN8?t=13) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Export the R6 block avatar dummy from Studio via Avatar > Rig Builder and save-export selection as OBJ to use as a fitting guide in Blender.
- [18:08](https://youtu.be/P9RpgVY8sN8?t=1088) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Convert curves to mesh, join parts, and merge verts at center so the hair has fewer tris and no holes before publishing.
- [24:29](https://youtu.be/P9RpgVY8sN8?t=1469) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Keep UGC hair under 4,000 triangles; check count via Overlays > Statistics and dissolve edges on flat surfaces to reduce it.
- [23:38](https://youtu.be/P9RpgVY8sN8?t=1418) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Disable the mirror modifier while building one half, then apply it after merging to halve the work.
- [29:13](https://youtu.be/P9RpgVY8sN8?t=1753) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Enable Face Orientation overlay to find inverted normals (red faces), then fix with Shift+N and recalculate.
- [30:50](https://youtu.be/P9RpgVY8sN8?t=1850) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** If the hair texture appears flipped on the mesh, rotate all UVs 90 degrees in the UV editor.
