## [[common-mistakes]]
- 2026-05-01 [13:32](https://youtu.be/X9YYjb_mY5w?t=812) Test purchase handling thoroughly: developer products that re-grant previous purchases on each buy (stacking bug) can be exploited and break the economy. ([[2026-05-01 - Can I Make $1M ROBUX in 30 DAYS|Can I Make $1M ROBUX in 30 DAYS?]])
- 2026-05-01 [12:37](https://youtu.be/X9YYjb_mY5w?t=757) Watch for sudden player drops after updates; he saw CCU fall to near zero overnight after an update, so check logs and roll back quickly. ([[2026-05-01 - Can I Make $1M ROBUX in 30 DAYS|Can I Make $1M ROBUX in 30 DAYS?]])
- 2026-05-31 [5:51](https://youtu.be/52srr65Mksc?t=351) Don't rely on tutorial copy-paste; the creator learned nothing from blindly following tutorials and felt no progress. ([[2026-05-31 - I Spent 2,000 DAYS to Make a VIRAL Roblox Game|I Spent 2,000 DAYS to Make a VIRAL Roblox Game]])
- 2026-05-31 [3:38](https://youtu.be/52srr65Mksc?t=218) Avoid copy-pasting repeated code blocks for each mode; it made the early game hard to maintain. ([[2026-05-31 - I Spent 2,000 DAYS to Make a VIRAL Roblox Game|I Spent 2,000 DAYS to Make a VIRAL Roblox Game]])
- 2026-05-31 [2:57](https://youtu.be/52srr65Mksc?t=177) Don't use free models you don't understand as a substitute for writing code; it stalls learning. ([[2026-05-31 - I Spent 2,000 DAYS to Make a VIRAL Roblox Game|I Spent 2,000 DAYS to Make a VIRAL Roblox Game]])
- 2026-05-31 [10:09](https://youtu.be/52srr65Mksc?t=609) Don't spend Robux on ads before the game has basic bugs fixed; bugs wasted an ad spend of 32,000 Robux. ([[2026-05-31 - I Spent 2,000 DAYS to Make a VIRAL Roblox Game|I Spent 2,000 DAYS to Make a VIRAL Roblox Game]])
- 2026-07-04 [19:09](https://youtu.be/eRhFPgj8tc4?t=1149) Avoid visually near-identical boards or models of other games; a similar-looking model led to a DMCA threat, so change distinctive assets early. ([[2026-07-04 - My Viral Roblox Game DIED… So I Made a Better One|My Viral Roblox Game DIED… So I Made a Better One]])
- 2026-08-04 [14:09](https://youtu.be/Cc7HT3uNjeQ?t=849) A single missing word in a script caused hours of debugging; re-read the exact error line and check for typos before rewriting systems. ([[2026-08-04 - I Made a VIRAL Roblox Game Without AI|I Made a VIRAL Roblox Game Without AI]])
- 2026-09-12 [14:12](https://youtu.be/MBRNAqkDEFA?t=852) Check whether an AI tool's 'generated' systems are actually pre-made marketplace templates before trusting its output. ([[2026-09-12 - I Tested Every Roblox AI|I Tested Every Roblox AI]])
- 2026-09-12 [11:30](https://youtu.be/MBRNAqkDEFA?t=690) Don't pay for AI tool subscriptions on free-trial pressure; the paid tiers still failed to finish a working game in the test. ([[2026-09-12 - I Tested Every Roblox AI|I Tested Every Roblox AI]])
