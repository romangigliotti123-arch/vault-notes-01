## Summary
Devlog for a from-scratch classic-style fast food tycoon in Roblox. Covers building a placement system (build mode, delete with refund, flashy effects), a component-based module loader, furniture and a building menu, and a leveling/XP system with cost and refund popups. Mostly entertainment and devlog; little measurable advice.

## Tips
- [1:15](https://youtu.be/6g8ZYtDKPBw?t=75) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Study DevForum technique posts before coding unfamiliar systems like placement; the speaker spent about 10 hours on the placement system.
- [1:55](https://youtu.be/6g8ZYtDKPBw?t=115) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Build placement with build mode, click-to-place, delete toggle, and save progress; add satisfying placement/delete feedback effects.
- [3:15](https://youtu.be/6g8ZYtDKPBw?t=195) **[[code-architecture-and-frameworks]]** Use a component system with a module loader so objects like tables and chairs can find each other and form seating groups.
- [4:10](https://youtu.be/6g8ZYtDKPBw?t=250) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Gather screenshots of other tycoons and make a mood board (Figma) before designing the building menu in Studio.
