# Can I Make $1M ROBUX in 30 DAYS?

[Watch](https://youtu.be/X9YYjb_mY5w) | DuckableDev | 667,097 views | 2026-05-01

## Summary
Developer documents a 30-day challenge to earn 1M Robux with a card-guessing game ("Operation Greed"). Covers rapid prototyping with AI (Claude) assistance, UI design in Figma, monetization via gamepasses/dev products, ads, weekly updates, and bugs. Ends with a poor result; he notes the game died after an update and that purchase stacking bugs hurt the project.

## Tips
- [9:38](https://youtu.be/X9YYjb_mY5w?t=578) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Spend on ads only after the game retains players; he spent $16/day for 10 days on ads, and said you don't need that much budget for the game to blow up.
- [13:32](https://youtu.be/X9YYjb_mY5w?t=812) **[[common-mistakes]]** Test purchase handling thoroughly: developer products that re-grant previous purchases on each buy (stacking bug) can be exploited and break the economy.
- [12:37](https://youtu.be/X9YYjb_mY5w?t=757) **[[common-mistakes]]** Watch for sudden player drops after updates; he saw CCU fall to near zero overnight after an update, so check logs and roll back quickly.
- [4:22](https://youtu.be/X9YYjb_mY5w?t=262) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Design original UI instead of copying genre-standard styles; he prototyped in Figma before implementing to differentiate from similar games.
- [11:15](https://youtu.be/X9YYjb_mY5w?t=675) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Plan regular weekly updates and feed player suggestions (e.g., admin commands gamepass) into them to sustain engagement.
- [0:37](https://youtu.be/X9YYjb_mY5w?t=37) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Look for underserved formats in trending genres; he spotted a card-guessing twist on luck-based clicker games with few competitors.
- [2:17](https://youtu.be/X9YYjb_mY5w?t=137) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use AI for mundane coding tasks but review output; he found it fast for scripts but error-prone for inventory and needed manual fixes.
- [8:18](https://youtu.be/X9YYjb_mY5w?t=498) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Offer gamepasses with clear benefits such as multipliers, exclusive cosmetics and chat tags, plus streak recovery and restock options.
- [11:15](https://youtu.be/X9YYjb_mY5w?t=675) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track concurrent players after each update; he saw CCU rise from 25 to a 225 peak within two days of launch before dropping.
- [10:07](https://youtu.be/X9YYjb_mY5w?t=607) **[[community-and-discord]]** Announce updates to a Discord server to revive player numbers when the game dips.
