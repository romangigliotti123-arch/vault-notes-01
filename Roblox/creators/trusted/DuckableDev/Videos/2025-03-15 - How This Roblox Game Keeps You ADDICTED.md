# How This Roblox Game Keeps You ADDICTED

## Summary
Breakdown of why the Roblox FPS Rivals (by Nosy Games) is addictive: instant matchmaking, progression hooks, social pressure, mastery/ranked, and loss aversion. Mostly entertainment/analysis with some design takeaways.

## Tips
- [1:38](https://youtu.be/I9eyeEG6dpc?t=98) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** For round-based games, use matchmaking that drops players straight into the next match with one click to avoid lobby waiting and keep retention high.
- [2:34](https://youtu.be/I9eyeEG6dpc?t=154) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add progression systems (per-weapon levels, unlockable cosmetics, paid upgrade currency) so players always have a next goal even after losses.
- [3:14](https://youtu.be/I9eyeEG6dpc?t=194) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give XP even to losing players so every match moves them toward the next goal; add persistent daily tasks visible on screen.
- [3:32](https://youtu.be/I9eyeEG6dpc?t=212) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Make the game multiplayer-only (no bots) so friends are pulled in and players invite friends naturally when the game is fun.
- [4:15](https://youtu.be/I9eyeEG6dpc?t=255) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Competitive difficulty that provokes frustration can drive re-engagement; design wins to feel earned and losses to motivate another attempt.
- [5:26](https://youtu.be/I9eyeEG6dpc?t=326) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Require some player skill (learning curve, movement, aiming) rather than idle clicking or RNG rolling so achievements feel earned.
- [5:58](https://youtu.be/I9eyeEG6dpc?t=358) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add ranked mode with leaderboards, medals and rank demotion so players keep playing to maintain status and improve mastery.
- [7:19](https://youtu.be/I9eyeEG6dpc?t=439) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Use loss aversion: show losses on profiles and win-streak resets so players fight for every casual match; limited items add urgency.
