## Tips
- [18:20](https://youtu.be/MBRNAqkDEFA?t=1100) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Ask AI for pointers and directions instead of full code ("follow the breadcrumbs") so you still write the logic yourself.
- [19:02](https://youtu.be/MBRNAqkDEFA?t=1142) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Read every AI-generated chunk until you understand it; don't paste blindly.
- [19:02](https://youtu.be/MBRNAqkDEFA?t=1142) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Type AI-suggested code out by hand instead of pasting it; it keeps you learning how the system works.
- [19:44](https://youtu.be/MBRNAqkDEFA?t=1184) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Use AI sparingly. Vibe-coding without thinking erodes critical thinking and makes you dependent on the tool.
- [16:58](https://youtu.be/MBRNAqkDEFA?t=1018) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Beginners should learn to code without AI first, then add AI once they have experience.
- [16:58](https://youtu.be/MBRNAqkDEFA?t=1018) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Roblox lets you connect Claude or ChatGPT to Studio; this is an alternative to paid standalone Roblox AI generators.
- [14:12](https://youtu.be/MBRNAqkDEFA?t=852) **[[common-mistakes]]** Check whether an AI tool's 'generated' systems are actually pre-made marketplace templates before trusting its output.
- [11:30](https://youtu.be/MBRNAqkDEFA?t=690) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't pay for AI tool subscriptions on free-trial pressure; the paid tiers still failed to finish a working game in the test.
