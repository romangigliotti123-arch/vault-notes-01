## Summary
Tutorial on making Roblox UGC accessories with AI: generate a 3D mask model in Tripo AI (HD model, ≤3,990 triangles, 2K texture, PBR off, FBX export), import into Studio, ungroup, build an Accessory, size/fit it, generate a MeshPart accessory, make a thumbnail with a plugin, and upload as a hat. Speaker claims 30,000 Robux this month.

## Tips
- [0:41](https://youtu.be/KhKOSmdX8sc?t=41) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Use a current image-to-3D model (speaker used Tripo AI) for UGC base meshes; older AI models looked too poor to sell. Refine in Blender if time allows.
- [2:05](https://youtu.be/KhKOSmdX8sc?t=125) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Keep UGC accessory meshes under Roblox's 4,000 triangle limit; the speaker targets 3,990 as a safe buffer.
- [2:48](https://youtu.be/KhKOSmdX8sc?t=168) **[[pricing]]** Turn off PBR in the generator when uploading to Roblox, since PBR costs extra Robux to upload.
- [2:48](https://youtu.be/KhKOSmdX8sc?t=168) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Choose the cheaper HD model setting over Smart Mesh; Smart Mesh gives better results but costs substantially more credits.
- [3:29](https://youtu.be/KhKOSmdX8sc?t=209) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Export textures at 2K and FBX format, the highest texture resolution Roblox supports for meshes.
- [3:42](https://youtu.be/KhKOSmdX8sc?t=222) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** In Studio, ungroup the imported mesh, rename it to Handle, place it inside an Accessory, then fit it using the Avatar accessory tool preview on a character.
- [5:45](https://youtu.be/KhKOSmdX8sc?t=345) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Use a thumbnail maker plugin to preview how the accessory looks in store listings before uploading.
- [6:27](https://youtu.be/KhKOSmdX8sc?t=387) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Upload accessories via the Avatar tab as Hat type under your group, wait for validation, then name and upload.
