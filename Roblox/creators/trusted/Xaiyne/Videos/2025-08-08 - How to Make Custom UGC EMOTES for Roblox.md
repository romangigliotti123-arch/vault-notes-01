# How to Make Custom UGC EMOTES for Roblox

[Watch](https://youtu.be/aqv5bLmqbzc) | Xaiyne | 54,152 views | 2025-08-08

## Summary
Walkthrough of making a custom R15 UGC emote in Roblox Studio using the free Animation Editor: add keyframes per body part, set 10s max length, make a looping curve animation, upload as a 750 Robux animation, then publish as an emote item with a 1,500 Robux publish fee and moderation limits.

## Tips
- [0:51](https://youtu.be/aqv5bLmqbzc?t=51) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use a free R15 dummy from Toolbox that has no scripts to avoid malicious code; check reviews first.
- [2:12](https://youtu.be/aqv5bLmqbzc?t=132) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Use R15 rather than R6 for emotes; R15 has 15 parts and is more versatile and widely used.
- [3:35](https://youtu.be/aqv5bLmqbzc?t=215) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Keyframe each body part; rotating an upper part moves its children (hierarchy), so animate upper limbs first to get natural motion.
- [4:16](https://youtu.be/aqv5bLmqbzc?t=256) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Keep emote length under 10 seconds, the maximum allowed for emotes and animations.
- [7:39](https://youtu.be/aqv5bLmqbzc?t=459) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Make the last keyframe match the first (e.g. same rotation at loop end) so looping emotes don't visibly snap.
- [8:42](https://youtu.be/aqv5bLmqbzc?t=522) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Set the animation to curve type before upload; the marketplace only accepts curve animations.
- [11:29](https://youtu.be/aqv5bLmqbzc?t=689) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Keep every body part within 5 studs of the root part and avoid very fast motion, or the emote fails moderation.
- [10:46](https://youtu.be/aqv5bLmqbzc?t=646) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Select Avatar Item > Emote when publishing; the validator flags issues, and the animation must show 'valid' before submitting.
- [12:34](https://youtu.be/aqv5bLmqbzc?t=754) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Add relevant emote tags at publish time; they help the item surface in marketplace recommendations and cataloging.
- [13:58](https://youtu.be/aqv5bLmqbzc?t=838) **[[monetisation-other]]** Expect creators to receive about 30% of sales since Roblox takes 70%; price accordingly.
- [14:39](https://youtu.be/aqv5bLmqbzc?t=879) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Budget for fees: 750 Robux per animation upload plus 1,500 Robux to publish the item; limited items cost more.
- [14:39](https://youtu.be/aqv5bLmqbzc?t=879) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't assume a pending review means a ban; moderation queues can exceed 5 hours, so submit early and wait.
