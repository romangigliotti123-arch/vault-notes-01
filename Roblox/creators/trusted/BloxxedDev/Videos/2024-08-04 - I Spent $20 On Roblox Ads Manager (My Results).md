## Summary
Creator spends $20 on a Roblox ad (Ads Manager) for their game, records the results over a few minutes. Ad drove a spike to ~92 concurrent players and ~1,920 visits, but only 105 Robux earned, so the creator questions whether the ad was worth it. Runs for 3 days; no further gains after the spike.
