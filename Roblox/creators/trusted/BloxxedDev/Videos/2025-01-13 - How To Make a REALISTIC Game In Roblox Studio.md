# How To Make a REALISTIC Game In Roblox Studio

[Watch](https://youtu.be/xGXCApkkkCE) | BloxxedDev | 25,477 views | 2025-01-13

## Summary
Tutorial on making a realistic-looking Roblox map in Studio: terrain shaping with the Terrain Editor, grass painting, water fill with transparency tweaks, a sunset sky from the Toolbox, the Celestial Dragger plugin for sun placement, and lighting settings.

## Tips
- [0:10](https://youtu.be/xGXCApkkkCE?t=10) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Save the place first and build terrain with the Terrain Editor draw tool; shape a messy oval, add an offset second layer, then smooth it for a natural look.
- [1:33](https://youtu.be/xGXCApkkkCE?t=93) **[[optimization-and-streaming]]** Use Leafy grass instead of normal grass to reduce lag, since it doesn't stick out as much; normal grass looks better under lighting but costs more.
- [2:13](https://youtu.be/xGXCApkkkCE?t=133) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Add water to terrain for realistic lighting and reflections; fill it about halfway up the terrain and scale it to fit so it doesn't stick out.
- [3:03](https://youtu.be/xGXCApkkkCE?t=183) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Set water transparency high and tint it yellow or orange to match the sky; a solid default colour looks cartoonish.
- [3:34](https://youtu.be/xGXCApkkkCE?t=214) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Pick orange or yellow toolbox skies, which look more like sunrise or sunset and read as more realistic.
- [4:15](https://youtu.be/xGXCApkkkCE?t=255) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use the Celestial Dragger plugin to position the sun lower so it lights the scene with shadows without blowing out the view.
- [4:56](https://youtu.be/xGXCApkkkCE?t=296) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Reduce the sun ray intensity after playtesting; the first setting was too bright and blinded the player.

## Numbers
- [0:10](https://youtu.be/xGXCApkkkCE?t=10) Water fill depth: **about halfway up the terrain** (Fill water to roughly mid-height of the terrain)
- [4:15](https://youtu.be/xGXCApkkkCE?t=255) Ambient brightness setting: **0.33** (Lighting tweak to brighten the scene after zeroing values)
