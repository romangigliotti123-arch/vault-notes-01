## Summary
A joke-driven experiment: the creator builds a horror Roblox game using only low-rated, unverified Toolbox free models (skies, beds, maps, enemies, decorations) and tests the chaos that results. Bugs include lag, broken shift lock, enemies spawning far from spawn, camera glitches, and models carrying unwanted scripts. Little transferable strategy.

## Tips
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/od95x-afmBU?t=0) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't import Toolbox models with low ratings or unverified creators without reviewing them; the video's imports broke spawns, shift lock, camera and lag.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/od95x-afmBU?t=0) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Inspect scripts inside free models before importing; the creator says they can carry viruses or hidden scripts, and some changed game behaviour unexpectedly.
- [3:17](https://youtu.be/od95x-afmBU?t=197) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Check imported map and model contents for hidden scripts and oversized geometry before placing them; large models caused lag and blocked paths.
