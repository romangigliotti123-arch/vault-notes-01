# I Made a Successful Roblox Game to Prove It’s Not Luck

[Watch](https://youtu.be/RIWPfMJyVhQ) | ItsZyonic | 372,890 views | 2026-09-08

## Summary
Solo developer's account of building Dodger Dot, a dodge-based Roblox game: prototype mechanics, upgrades/tool crates/gambling loop, Discord playtesting, a 7-day ad campaign with 280 credits that underperformed, turning ads off, turning down a $24,000-for-70% offer, and the game reaching 900 then 7,000 CCU and 20M visits.

## Tips
- [3:47](https://youtu.be/RIWPfMJyVhQ?t=227) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Playtest every development step with Discord fans and other developers, even at the basic unpolished stage.
- [4:23](https://youtu.be/RIWPfMJyVhQ?t=263) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Keep shipping updates daily based on a list of improvements; do not expect a released game to take off on its own.
- [4:23](https://youtu.be/RIWPfMJyVhQ?t=263) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Listen to player feedback constantly while iterating, even when dislike ratio is poor.
- [5:04](https://youtu.be/RIWPfMJyVhQ?t=304) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ads alone may not catch the algorithm; the speaker turned ads off and organic metrics then climbed and CCU followed.
- [6:21](https://youtu.be/RIWPfMJyVhQ?t=381) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not sell early on a mid-metric game; the speaker's game dropped to 300 CCU and then recovered after changes.
- [7:02](https://youtu.be/RIWPfMJyVhQ?t=422) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** After momentum returns, feed it with a battle pass, clans, more tools and cosmetics to keep growth going.
- [7:49](https://youtu.be/RIWPfMJyVhQ?t=469) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Find an idea, prove it works through playtesting, and keep improving it rather than giving up.
- [0:24](https://youtu.be/RIWPfMJyVhQ?t=24) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Build from a mechanic you personally find fun (dodging Align Position balls) and test whether it holds up.
- [1:28](https://youtu.be/RIWPfMJyVhQ?t=88) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Establish a smooth movement system and a round loop first, then add long-term progression like upgrades, crates and tools.
- [2:08](https://youtu.be/RIWPfMJyVhQ?t=128) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Add gamepasses and developer products after the core loop and progression are in place.
