# Making a CASH GRAB game for ROBUX

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Watch](https://youtu.be/Cd6mpD4doGs) | ItsZyonic | 595,905 views | 2024-07-30

## Summary
Creator builds a low-effort "cash grab" RNG obby packed with gamepasses and sponsors it for 3,000 Robux over 2 days. Result: about 28 Robux earned in 72 hours, ~3 minute average session, 33% likes, almost no sales. Concludes cash grabs underperform and polish matters.

## Tips
- [2:14](https://youtu.be/Cd6mpD4doGs?t=134) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't rely on a low-effort cash grab with gamepass spam; the creator's version earned 28 Robux from 3,000 Robux of sponsored ads over 72 hours.
- [6:22](https://youtu.be/Cd6mpD4doGs?t=382) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Put real effort and time into the game; the creator concludes this drives better results than cheap cash-grab design.
- [4:47](https://youtu.be/Cd6mpD4doGs?t=287) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check like-to-dislike ratio and average session time in the analytics overview; a 3-minute session and 33% likes signalled weak engagement.
- [3:01](https://youtu.be/Cd6mpD4doGs?t=181) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Test sponsored experience vs in-game portal ads on small budgets before scaling; the creator planned to compare but did not report the result.
