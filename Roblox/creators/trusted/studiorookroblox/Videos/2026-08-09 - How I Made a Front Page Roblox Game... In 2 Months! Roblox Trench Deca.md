## Summary
Solo developer Timi Tarra recounts making Trench Decay (formerly "Trench") from a Ro Devs Discord 2026 game jam win into a front-page Roblox game in about 2 months. Covers remaking the game, building community on Instagram/TikTok before release, playtesting, fixing bugs, and responding to YouTuber feedback.
