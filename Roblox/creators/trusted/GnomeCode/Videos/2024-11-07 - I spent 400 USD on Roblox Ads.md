# I spent 400 USD on Roblox Ads...

[Watch](https://youtu.be/g9UbIrR0RbU) | GnomeCode | 51,904 views | 2024-11-07

## Summary
Creator spends 400 USD (114,000 Robux) on the new Roblox ad manager (visits campaign, sponsored experience, lifetime budget, two days) to promote a ~1K CCU game. Results: ~19,000 visits, ~2 cents per play, ~5.7 Robux per new player, judged not worth it for a saturated, low-monetised game. Suggests ads mainly for brand-new games.

## Tips
- [11:05](https://youtu.be/g9UbIrR0RbU?t=665) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Don't spend on Roblox ads if your game is already well known to the algorithm; returns were poor (~2 cents per play) for a saturated, low-monetised game.
- [11:46](https://youtu.be/g9UbIrR0RbU?t=706) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use ad spend on brand-new games with zero players: the algorithm doesn't know them yet, so an ad burst gives initial CCU, playtest data and exposure to help recommendations.
- [9:44](https://youtu.be/g9UbIrR0RbU?t=584) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Calculate ad cost per new player and compare it to your actual revenue per player; a game with low monetisation loses money on each ad-acquired player.
- [10:24](https://youtu.be/g9UbIrR0RbU?t=624) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** The game icon and thumbnails drive clicks; test icon changes carefully, since a more orange icon may have hurt CTR. Aim for around 2% CTR.
- [6:50](https://youtu.be/g9UbIrR0RbU?t=410) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Keep icons recognisable and on-brand for existing players; changing the icon too much may reduce clicks.
- [5:26](https://youtu.be/g9UbIrR0RbU?t=326) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Expect most ad-acquired new users to leave quickly; in this test about 50% left by the 5-minute mark, so new-player onboarding matters.
- [3:05](https://youtu.be/g9UbIrR0RbU?t=185) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ad manager lifetime budgets can burn out in hours rather than spreading over the scheduled window; check pacing early.
