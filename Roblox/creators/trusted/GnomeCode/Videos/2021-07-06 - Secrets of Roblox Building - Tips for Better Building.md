# Secrets of Roblox Building - Tips for Better Building

[Watch](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c) | GnomeCode | 240,055 views | 2021-07-06

## Summary
Beginner building tutorial for Roblox Studio: hotkeys (Ctrl+D duplicate, Ctrl+G group, F to focus, Shift to slow camera), axis-locked moving, collisions, physical vs geometric mode, transform tool and snap-to-grid, using Build Rig to size to human scale, variation to avoid cookie-cutter areas, grid increments to avoid z-fighting and gaps, and safely vetting free models.

## Tips
- [1:06](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=66) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Learn hotkeys: Ctrl+D duplicates in place (new copy inside the original spot), Ctrl+G groups parts, Ctrl+1-5 switch tools, F zooms to selection.
- [2:29](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=149) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use Ctrl+D instead of Ctrl+C/Ctrl+V when you want the copy placed beside the original rather than stacked on top.
- [3:50](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=230) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Hold Shift while moving the camera to slow it down for precise detail work.
- [5:32](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=332) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use the move tool locked to a single axis for accurate placement; it stops parts slipping and getting caught on neighbours.
- [6:12](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=372) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Turn on collisions when placing parts so they stop against existing geometry instead of passing through it.
- [9:41](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=581) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use the transform tool's snap-to-grid option aligned to a part so rotations and resizes are relative to that part's orientation.
- [11:46](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=706) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use the Build Rig dummy (Plugins > Build Rig) to check doorways and windows are human-scale before building further.
- [12:27](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=747) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Avoid going too large in scale for third-person games; oversized realistic interiors feel cramped because the camera clips into walls.
- [13:08](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=788) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Avoid oversized empty maps; the speaker's map took ~90 seconds to walk across with nothing to do, which is boring for players.
- [14:05](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=845) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Add variation (random positions, different scales, rotations, multiple models) to avoid repeated grid-like cookie-cutter layouts.
- [16:26](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=986) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Keep grid snapping/increments enabled and build at consistent increments (e.g. 0.5 studs, 1 or 5 studs for big walls) to avoid gaps, overlaps and skewed parts.
- [17:49](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=1069) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Overlapping coplanar faces cause z-fighting (flickering textures); avoid placing two surfaces at exactly the same position.
- [19:52](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=1192) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Size parts to exact dimensions with snapping on so later edits are easy; unsnapped builds end up with decimals like 52.082 that are hard to modify.
- [23:17](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=1397) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Choose increment size per task: 10 studs for big walls, 1 or 0.5 for detail, 0.1 for fine parts; avoid going below ~0.1.
- [26:11](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=1571) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not repeat identical free models everywhere; mix plant pots, colours and sizes so the game has its own identity.
- [26:11](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=1571) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Before using free models, search Explorer for 'script' and delete any scripts you do not understand; hidden malicious scripts are common.
- [26:51](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=1611) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Treat scripts named 'do not delete' or with odd names mimicking anti-virus tools as suspicious and remove them.
- [28:13](https://youtu.be/7vXk_YqxW-c?t=1693) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Prefer Roblox-endorsed (orange badge) free models when worried about safety; they are vetted and usually better quality.
