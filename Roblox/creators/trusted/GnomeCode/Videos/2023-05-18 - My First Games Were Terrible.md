# My First Games Were Terrible...

## Summary
Creator revisits his earliest Roblox projects (2011-2013era: Medieval Quest, Death Island, city of war, Awesome Squad, Be A Secret Agent RPG) and critiques them. Shows dialogue-only games, copy-pasted scripts with no loops, lag from free models, and lost terrain. Mostly a retrospective/devlog with light lessons.

## Tips
- [15:52](https://youtu.be/TJnV2ycnXZc?t=952) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid copy-pasting repeated script blocks; use loops and functions instead. Early repeated if-blocks over 1000 lines were a sign of learning, but refactor early.
- [8:51](https://youtu.be/TJnV2ycnXZc?t=531) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't leave large empty spaces in a map; more stuff does not make a map better. Plan where the player goes before adding content.
- [10:16](https://youtu.be/TJnV2ycnXZc?t=616) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use proper humanoid rigs instead of placeholder parts (like a ball on a character) when you can; it reads as unfinished and hurts immersion.
- [8:18](https://youtu.be/TJnV2ycnXZc?t=498) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Spamming many free models into one scene can cause heavy lag on low-end machines; keep model counts modest for performance.
