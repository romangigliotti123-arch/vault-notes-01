# How to find the RIGHT Roblox game idea

[Watch](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM) | MonarchSilgrit | 261 views | 2026-09-06

## Summary
Method for picking a Roblox game idea: research charts for performing-but-not-huge games, play it as dev and player, copy-with-twist (merge two games or new concept), make the title explain the game, build a bare-bones prototype to test, compare alternatives, then commit instead of overthinking. Illustrated with Clean All the Leaves and its snow clones.

## Tips
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=40) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Target games that are performing well but aren't the biggest; avoid replicating top hits where competition is huge. Look for steady visits and returning players.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=0) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Use Roblox's charts or AI tools to find what's performing, but do the analysis yourself rather than relying on ChatGPT output alone.
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=81) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Play the target game as both developer and player; note what makes you keep playing, since that's what drives others to play it too.
- [2:43](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=163) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Give immediate reward feedback early: a +1 popup on first pickup and a quest completed within ~7 seconds hooked the player fast.
- [4:04](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=244) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't just clone a game; copies can get traffic but usually lose to the original. Add a real twist by merging two games' core concepts or a new concept.
- [5:26](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=326) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Check whether your twist already exists on the platform; a simple reskin (autumn to winter) was tried about six times and none performed.
- [6:08](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=368) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Make the title explain the game, its experience and what players get, e.g. 'Clean the Snow to Survive', so it's understood at a glance.
- [6:49](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=409) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Before full development, build a bare-bones prototype: remove the baseplate and build only the core action to test whether the idea is worth pursuing.
- [7:29](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=449) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** If the prototype fails your test, repeat research with an idea as different as possible; if it's too similar, the problem is likely execution, not the idea.
- [8:10](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=490) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Commit to an idea once filtered instead of endlessly re-deciding; an unmade game never gets played, so you can't learn if it works.

## Games
- **Clean All the Leaves** [2:02](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=122): Immediate rewards (+1 pickup popup, quick quest completion) and a visible progress bar hook players fast.
- **Garden Cleaner Evolution** [4:04](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=244): Higher-quality and better rated, but far fewer players; polish alone doesn't guarantee reach.
- **Fish It** [4:44](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=284): Original outperformed its clone about 2:1 in players despite being released seven months earlier.
- **Clean the Snow / Shovel My Snow / Snow Cleaner Evolution etc.** [5:26](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=326): Simple seasonal reskins of the leaf concept were saturated and underperformed; needs a real added mechanic.
