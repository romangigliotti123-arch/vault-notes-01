# How Much Money I Made With 3 Roblox Games

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.

## Summary
Creator reports results of three small Roblox games (Manifest and Slay, Sort the Sheep, Monarch's Dig Relic) over one summer: ~9,365 visits, ~$130 ad spend, 459 Robux earned (~$1.60), ~0.25% retention. Concludes games failed on discovery and retention, not code. Plans next to remake a popular game's loop with a changed concept.

## Tips
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/LD7LGP10dRc?t=81) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Keep the core idea small: one input, one decision repeated; create difficulty via rule changes rather than raising speed.
- [2:43](https://youtu.be/LD7LGP10dRc?t=163) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Retention matters more than installs: check whether players return next day; the creator's games had ~0.25% retention and nobody came back.
- [2:43](https://youtu.be/LD7LGP10dRc?t=163) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Paid ads alone brought visits but not retained players; ~$130 spend gave 8,800 plays from 641,000 impressions with no lasting players.
- [3:24](https://youtu.be/LD7LGP10dRc?t=204) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Organic discovery was weak: only ~500 players found the games on their own across three titles over a summer.
