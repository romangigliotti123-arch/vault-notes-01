# Stopped Trying to Be Original on Roblox

[Watch](https://youtu.be/MANBsA1MTzI) | MonarchSilgrit | 148 views | 2026-08-18

## Summary
Developer's account of making Dig Relic, a digging game on Roblox, after researching trends. Covers copying a proven UI structure from Grow a Garden and Sell Lemons, a four-objective design framework (satisfying digging, rewards worth finding, active box opening, reason to return), ad spend that yielded nothing, and the kids-audience policy. Recorded before the game was finished; no results shown.

## Tips
- [1:49](https://youtu.be/MANBsA1MTzI?t=109) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Scroll the front page and charts to see what's trending and what has visibly flopped before choosing a concept.
- [2:27](https://youtu.be/MANBsA1MTzI?t=147) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Define the core loop as four objectives: satisfying core action, something worth finding, an active reward-opening step, and a reason to return.
- [3:09](https://youtu.be/MANBsA1MTzI?t=189) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give players a reason to return by spending earnings on bigger tools and displaying collected items on pedestals visible to the whole server.
- [3:41](https://youtu.be/MANBsA1MTzI?t=221) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't over-invest in originality; proven structures from working games can be reused and reskinned, as the speaker did with Grow a Garden and Sell Lemons UI.
- [4:31](https://youtu.be/MANBsA1MTzI?t=271) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** If your target audience is kids, Roblox now requires at least 500 adults who genuinely enjoy and spend in your game before it's cleared as kid-friendly.
- [5:02](https://youtu.be/MANBsA1MTzI?t=302) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Test Roblox ads with small budgets and track results; the speaker lost a couple hundred dollars across two games with no return.
