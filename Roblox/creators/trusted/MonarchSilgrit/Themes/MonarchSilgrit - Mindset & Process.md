## [[development-process-and-speed]]
- 2026-09-06 [6:49](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=409) Before full development, build a bare-bones prototype: remove the baseplate and build only the core action to test whether the idea is worth pursuing. - 2026-10-03 [1:21](https://youtu.be/bbCmt98O9MA?t=81) Split the build into ordered objectives (map, pets, animations, menus, monetization, beta, ads) to avoid a low-effort game. - 2026-10-26 [1:04](https://youtu.be/WA4RzzT39Dw?t=64) Build order: create the place, then core features first, then layer everything else on top. ([[2026-10-26 - STOP Asking Claude to Make Your Whole Roblox Game|STOP Asking Claude to Make Your Whole Roblox Game]])

## [[common-mistakes]]
- 2026-08-18 [3:41](https://youtu.be/MANBsA1MTzI?t=221) Don't over-invest in originality; proven structures from working games can be reused and reskinned, as the speaker did with Grow a Garden and Sell Lemons UI. ([[2026-08-18 - Stopped Trying to Be Original on Roblox|Stopped Trying to Be Original on Roblox]])
- 2026-08-29 [2:56](https://youtu.be/_DwOE49GDdo?t=176) Small developers without a large ad budget struggle to get traction; don't expect ads alone to fix a lack of players. ([[2026-08-29 - Or Maybe My Roblox Game Is Just Bad|Or Maybe My Roblox Game Is Just Bad]])
- 2026-09-06 [4:04](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=244) Don't just clone a game; copies can get traffic but usually lose to the original. Add a real twist by merging two games' core concepts or a new concept. ([[2026-09-06 - How to find the RIGHT Roblox game idea|How to find the RIGHT Roblox game idea]])
- 2026-09-11 [5:27](https://youtu.be/LD7LGP10dRc?t=327) Cloning a successful game's template is not the same as copying its loop; change the core concept while keeping the proven loop. ([[2026-09-11 - How Much Money I Made With 3 Roblox Games|How Much Money I Made With 3 Roblox Games]])
