## [[development-process-and-speed]]
- 2023-04-15 [2:33](https://youtu.be/Pbrvfdn9UN8?t=153) Prototype risky mechanics first (e.g. enemy navigating a multi-floor building) before building the full map, to find blockers early. ([[2023-04-15 - I Accidentally Made My Game Difficult Roblox Shooter Devlog 3|I Accidentally Made My Game Difficult | Roblox Sho]])
- 2023-04-15 [5:38](https://youtu.be/Pbrvfdn9UN8?t=338) Get new enemy behaviour working in-engine with placeholder cylinders before investing in modelling, then model once it plays well. ([[2023-04-15 - I Accidentally Made My Game Difficult Roblox Shooter Devlog 3|I Accidentally Made My Game Difficult | Roblox Sho]])
- 2024-11-01 [3:48](https://youtu.be/LC8AV8Yy6Z4?t=228) Unanchored destruction needs every block welded to its neighbours; welding hundreds of parts manually is too slow, so script it. Keep the algorithm fast or run it offline. ([[2024-11-01 - I Tried Remaking My First Game, 9 Years Later|I Tried Remaking My First Game, 9 Years Later]])

## [[common-mistakes]]
- 2025-09-01 [9:34](https://youtu.be/WNqGLvkf7hQ?t=574) Don't over-simulate: the solution was an enemy-scale problem, not a car simulation. Ask what the object actually needs to do before modelling full systems. ([[2025-09-01 - Adding INSANE Physics To Our Roblox Game|Adding INSANE Physics To Our Roblox Game]])

## [[testing-debugging-and-qa]]
- 2024-11-01 [5:11](https://youtu.be/LC8AV8Yy6Z4?t=311) Making AI bots that simulate players is harder than expected; overpowered bots are easy, realistic aim and movement are not. Playtest with real people where possible. ([[2024-11-01 - I Tried Remaking My First Game, 9 Years Later|I Tried Remaking My First Game, 9 Years Later]])
