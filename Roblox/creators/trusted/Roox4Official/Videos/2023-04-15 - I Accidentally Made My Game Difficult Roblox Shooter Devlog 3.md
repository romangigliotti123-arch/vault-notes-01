## Summary
Devlog #3 of a Roblox shooter prototype. The creator brainstormed game modes with ChatGPT (tower defense, horror, rescue/invasion goals), fixed enemy pathfinding on stairs by adding manual waypoints, built a prototype farm map, and created a minigun enemy, tuning fire rate and modeling it in Blender.

## Tips
- [2:33](https://youtu.be/Pbrvfdn9UN8?t=153) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Prototype risky mechanics first (e.g. enemy navigating a multi-floor building) before building the full map, to find blockers early.
- [3:15](https://youtu.be/Pbrvfdn9UN8?t=195) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** When PathfindingService fails on stairs, add custom waypoints for that section and have enemies follow them instead of default path waypoints.
- [4:39](https://youtu.be/Pbrvfdn9UN8?t=279) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Use crude placeholder blocks for buildings during prototyping and swap in real models later.
- [5:38](https://youtu.be/Pbrvfdn9UN8?t=338) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Get new enemy behaviour working in-engine with placeholder cylinders before investing in modelling, then model once it plays well.
