## Summary
Creator builds an incremental/clicker Roblox streamer game in 3 days: buy streamers with subscribers/views, rebirths, prestige, play buttons, objectives, and gamepasses (2x views, 2x subscribers, 2x rewards, 2x prestige, instant unlocks, Robux-only play button). Reuses a leaderboard script; skipped the upgrade tree. Asks viewers for feedback.

## Tips
- [0:41](https://youtu.be/CbAL1e8NgSU?t=41) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Clone a proven genre (incremental clicker) and theme it with a fresh twist (buy streamers instead of characters); the creator found no big game with that theme yet.
- [1:05](https://youtu.be/CbAL1e8NgSU?t=65) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Add satisfying sound effects and visual pop-ups to the first purchase action; game feel was a key reason the creator found the reference game fun.
- [2:26](https://youtu.be/CbAL1e8NgSU?t=146) **[[code-architecture-and-frameworks]]** Reuse tested modules/scripts from previous projects (e.g. a global leaderboard) instead of rebuilding them from scratch.
- [2:26](https://youtu.be/CbAL1e8NgSU?t=146) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Show costs above buttons before interaction so players know requirements instantly.
