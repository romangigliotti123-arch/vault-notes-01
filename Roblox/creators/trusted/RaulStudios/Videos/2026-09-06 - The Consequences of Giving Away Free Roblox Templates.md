## Summary
A Roblox developer gave away free templates, which grew his Discord to 500+ members and his channel to ~500 subscribers. He recounts collaborating with several developers: one impersonated a verified dev and left him doing most of the work, while a later partner proved legit. He also sold an unprofitable game (Plant RNG) to a front-page developer.
