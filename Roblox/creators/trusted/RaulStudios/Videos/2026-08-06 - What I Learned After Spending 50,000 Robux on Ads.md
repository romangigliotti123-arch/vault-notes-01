# What I Learned After Spending 50,000 Robux on Ads

[Watch](https://youtu.be/7vYv8DA7pqU) | RaulStudios | 31,972 views | 2026-08-06

## Summary
Creator spent ~50,000 Robux on Roblox ads for his plant game. Fixing bugs/polish before ads, then a thumbnail swap raised CTR from ~1% to 1.95%. Campaign returned ~3,000 plays but only 137 Robux, mainly because the game had few gamepasses.

## Tips
- [1:11](https://youtu.be/7vYv8DA7pqU?t=71) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Test thumbnails before running paid ads; swapping in a new thumbnail lifted CTR from ~1% to 1.95%, nearly doubling plays.
- [0:43](https://youtu.be/7vYv8DA7pqU?t=43) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Polish and fix bugs before spending on ads; he updated daily for a week before launch because ads to a rough game waste money.
- [1:42](https://youtu.be/7vYv8DA7pqU?t=102) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Add gamepasses before running ads; a game with almost none could not recoup ad spend, so monetisation must be ready at launch.
- [1:28](https://youtu.be/7vYv8DA7pqU?t=88) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Replace proximity prompts with a simple close-proximity interaction when players feedback says it works better; he found it much better.
- [4:04](https://youtu.be/7vYv8DA7pqU?t=244) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Roblox does not let you swap thumbnails on an active ad; you must cancel and restart, losing its momentum. Get creatives right first.
- [2:12](https://youtu.be/7vYv8DA7pqU?t=132) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Run ad campaigns for 7 days rather than 5; he heard longer runs learn better but cut budget short.
- [3:39](https://youtu.be/7vYv8DA7pqU?t=219) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Study top-performing games' thumbnails and adapt their proven layouts rather than personal taste; popularity implies the thumbnails work.
- [5:04](https://youtu.be/7vYv8DA7pqU?t=304) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Expect benchmarks and dashboard plays to lag by a day after launch; wait for data before judging a campaign.
- [4:20](https://youtu.be/7vYv8DA7pqU?t=260) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Benchmark CTR on Roblox: 1% is bad, about 2% is average; track clicks against actual plays since they can diverge.
- [0:15](https://youtu.be/7vYv8DA7pqU?t=15) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Posting free YouTube Shorts drove over 1,000 views but barely moved visits (max ~20 players); paid reach was needed.
- [2:44](https://youtu.be/7vYv8DA7pqU?t=164) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Check revenue under the group, not personal account, when the game is group-owned.
