# quezdev - How to make successful Roblox games

Quezdev's approach: ride a proven trend for early reach, build fast with plugins (Archimedes for paths, Brush tool for scattering), and monetise mainly through developer-product consumables plus one cheap gamepass. Treat ads as unproven: his Ads Manager test lost money because 4-minute sessions (below the 6-minute benchmark) did not convert cheap clicks. Add progression to lift retention, benchmark ARPDAU against genre peers, and use DevEx for cash-out once eligible. Most evidence is anecdotal, first-hand showcase data rather than controlled tests.

**Style:** Candid, first-hand case studies from a small developer who shares real numbers, including losses. Practical, plugin-focused building tips and transparent revenue breakdowns. Best for solo or small teams copying trends or running small experiments. Payout and promotional segments offer little actionable advice.

## Playbook
1. **Pick a proven trend to ride** [[trend-research-and-validation]]: Quezdev cloned viral Grow a Garden as Grow a Flower, reaching ~2,000 active players and 3M visits. The copy only worked because of the trend's momentum, so treat trend-riding as an early reach boost, not a moat. (WOG3Rttf-V4)
2. **Benchmark the genre before committing** [[analytics-metrics]]: Compare ARPDAU against genre peers before building. Grow a Flower's ARPDAU looked low versus simulator benchmarks, which signalled monetisation headroom. (WOG3Rttf-V4)
3. **Build terrain by stamping scaled models** [[building-art-and-assets]]: Scale a small model up and stamp it out to create hills and terrain variation instead of flat ground. The Central Park-style redesign used a blocky asset pack. (hCW2OhUBPf4)
4. **Generate paths with Archimedes** [[building-art-and-assets]]: Use the Archimedes plugin to generate paths, which the creator says is far faster than building them manually. (hCW2OhUBPf4)
5. **Scatter foliage with Brush tool, then fix placement** [[building-and-level-design]]: Select parts, activate Brush, and click across the map for natural duplicates with variation. Raise the brush radius to spawn in bunches. Check trees for sinking or floating and nudge them by hand. (hCW2OhUBPf4)
6. **Add progression before buying traffic** [[retention-d1-d7]]: A 4-minute average session fell short of the 6-minute 50th-percentile benchmark. Add levels and progression so players keep playing. (QPxXr57M-xM)
7. **Sell consumables as developer products** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: Sell seeds, grow-all-flowers, gear, and starter/super packs as developer products. These drove nearly all Grow a Flower revenue. (WOG3Rttf-V4)
8. **Add one cheap gamepass** [[monetisation-gamepasses]]: A single cheap gamepass, a fence door that prevents theft, earned ~13,000 Robux per week. The creator calls it a modest but useful add-on, not primary revenue. (WOG3Rttf-V4)
9. **Test ads small and judge by retention** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Start with a small test and track cost per play (2.85 Robux) against retention, not CTR. A 5% CTR still lost money because sessions were short. Ads did not pay back. (QPxXr57M-xM)
10. **Playtest during the ad campaign** [[playtesting-and-feedback]]: Collect bugs and player suggestions during the campaign and ship fixes between ad days. (QPxXr57M-xM)
11. **Use leaderboard giveaways as optional promotion** [[social-features-and-virality]]: A 1,000 Robux leaderboard giveaway ran as promotion. The creator flagged it as promotional, so treat it as optional rather than proven growth. (hCW2OhUBPf4)
12. **Watch rank and concurrency spikes** [[analytics-metrics]]: Grow a Flower hovered around the top 1,000 daily revenue after a player spike. Ad campaign day 2 peaked at ~50 concurrent players versus ~28 previously. Use spikes to gauge reach. (WOG3Rttf-V4, QPxXr57M-xM)
13. **Add levels when retention lags** [[updates-live-ops]]: The creator planned more levels to boost retention after the weak ad campaign. Revisit ARPDAU to find monetisation headroom. (QPxXr57M-xM, WOG3Rttf-V4)
14. **Cash out via DevEx once eligible** [[business-and-payouts]]: Requirements: 30,000 Robux minimum, verified email, DevEx portal with banking and tax info, age 13+ in good standing. The current rate is 0.0038 USD per Robux; payouts take 1 to 2 weeks. (i4XHn84ahyc)

## Common mistakes
- Assuming ads pay back: 18 credits (5,130 Robux) returned only 166 Robux (QPxXr57M-xM)
- Judging ads by CTR alone; a 5% CTR still lost money when sessions averaged 4 minutes (QPxXr57M-xM)
- Copying a trend without a distinct hook; the clone only worked because of Grow Garden's momentum (WOG3Rttf-V4)
- Leaving brush-scattered trees sinking into or floating above terrain (hCW2OhUBPf4)
- Using a small brush radius, so foliage spawns one at a time instead of in bunches (hCW2OhUBPf4)
- Relying on gamepasses: one cheap gamepass earned modestly while dev products drove nearly all revenue (WOG3Rttf-V4)
- Skipping ARPDAU benchmarking against genre peers, which hides monetisation headroom (WOG3Rttf-V4)
- Using the old DevEx rate of 0.0035 USD per Robux instead of the current 0.0038 (i4XHn84ahyc)
- Treating a payout showcase as a playbook; the Dive Down video has little actionable advice (i4XHn84ahyc)

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Confirm trend momentum and add a distinct hook beyond the copy (WOG3Rttf-V4)
- [ ] Benchmark ARPDAU and session length against genre peers before launch (QPxXr57M-xM, WOG3Rttf-V4)
- [ ] Stamp scaled models to create terrain variation instead of flat ground (hCW2OhUBPf4)
- [ ] Generate paths with Archimedes and scatter foliage with Brush tool at a larger radius (hCW2OhUBPf4)
- [ ] Nudge sinking or floating trees into the ground by hand (hCW2OhUBPf4)
- [ ] Build enough levels and progression to target ~6-minute sessions (QPxXr57M-xM)
- [ ] Set up consumable developer products: seeds, grow-all, gear, starter/super packs (WOG3Rttf-V4)
- [ ] Add one cheap, useful gamepass such as a theft-prevention fence door (WOG3Rttf-V4)
- [ ] Run a small ad test before scaling; track cost per play against retention (QPxXr57M-xM)
- [ ] Collect bugs and player suggestions during campaigns and ship fixes between days (QPxXr57M-xM)
- [ ] Track daily revenue rank and concurrency peaks to gauge reach (WOG3Rttf-V4, QPxXr57M-xM)
- [ ] Verify email and set up the DevEx portal with banking and tax info (i4XHn84ahyc)
- [ ] Confirm DevEx eligibility: 30,000 Robux minimum and age 13+ (i4XHn84ahyc)
- [ ] Use the current DevEx rate of 0.0038 USD per Robux in revenue estimates (i4XHn84ahyc)
- [ ] Track engagement payouts separately from dev-product revenue (WOG3Rttf-V4)
- [ ] Plan more levels when retention lags after an ad test (QPxXr57M-xM)
