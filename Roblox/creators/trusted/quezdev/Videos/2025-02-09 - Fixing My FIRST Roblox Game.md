# Fixing My FIRST Roblox Game

## Summary
A first-time Roblox dev redesigns the map of his cat-collecting clicker for a Central Park-inspired look. He builds terrain with a blocky asset pack, paths with the Archimedes plugin, and scatters trees, plants, and buildings with the Brush tool plugin. The video ends with a 1,000 Robux leaderboard giveaway, which is promotional.

## Tips
- [0:42](https://youtu.be/hCW2OhUBPf4?t=42) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Scale up a small model and stamp it out to create hills and terrain variation instead of flat ground.
- [2:05](https://youtu.be/hCW2OhUBPf4?t=125) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use the Archimedes plugin to generate paths; the speaker says it is far faster than building paths manually.
- [2:45](https://youtu.be/hCW2OhUBPf4?t=165) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use the Brush tool plugin: select parts, activate brush, click across the map to scatter natural-looking duplicates with variation.
- [3:26](https://youtu.be/hCW2OhUBPf4?t=206) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Increase the Brush radius to spawn foliage in bunches rather than one at a time.
