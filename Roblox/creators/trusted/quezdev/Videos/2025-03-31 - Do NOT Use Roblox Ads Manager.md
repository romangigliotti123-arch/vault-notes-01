# Do NOT Use Roblox Ads Manager

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Watch](https://youtu.be/QPxXr57M-xM) | quezdev | 3,208 views | 2025-03-31

## Summary
Creator and dev partner run a 2-day Roblox Ads Manager sponsored-experience campaign (18 ad credits, ~4,964 Robux net loss) for a backrooms survival game targeting Indonesia/Malaysia. Result: 2,000 plays, 4-min avg session, only 166 Robux earned back. Lessons: ad costs were high, retention was weak, and they plan more levels to boost retention.

## Tips
- [4:33](https://youtu.be/QPxXr57M-xM?t=273) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Average session of 4 minutes was too short (50th percentile benchmark 6 min); add more levels/progression so players keep playing.
- [0:28](https://youtu.be/QPxXr57M-xM?t=28) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Sponsored ads at ~$0.14 CPM-equivalent cost (~5 Robux/1000 impressions) with 5% CTR still lost money; ads didn't pay back. Test small before scaling.
- [1:51](https://youtu.be/QPxXr57M-xM?t=111) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Collect bugs and player suggestions during the campaign and ship fixes between ad days.
- [4:00](https://youtu.be/QPxXr57M-xM?t=240) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Track cost per play (here 2.85 Robux) and compare against retention, not just CTR; cheap clicks don't offset short sessions.
