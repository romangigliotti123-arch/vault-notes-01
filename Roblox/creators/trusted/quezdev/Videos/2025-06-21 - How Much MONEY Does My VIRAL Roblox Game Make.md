# How Much MONEY Does My VIRAL Roblox Game Make?

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Watch](https://youtu.be/WOG3Rttf-V4) | quezdev | 35,136 views | 2025-06-21

## Summary
Creator clones the viral Grow a Garden trend with "Grow a Flower", shares lifetime and weekly revenue, monetisation breakdown (dev products dominate, one gamepass), and notes low ARPDAU versus simulator benchmarks. Growth is attributed mainly to riding Grow Garden's momentum.

## Tips
- [0:34](https://youtu.be/WOG3Rttf-V4?t=34) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Sell consumables via developer products (seeds, growing all flowers, gear, starter/super packs); in this game these drove nearly all revenue.
- [2:13](https://youtu.be/WOG3Rttf-V4?t=133) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** A single cheap gamepass (fence door preventing theft) earned ~13,000 Robux/week, a modest but useful add-on.
- [1:32](https://youtu.be/WOG3Rttf-V4?t=92) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Riding a massive existing trend (Grow Garden) can carry early momentum, though the creator notes the copy only works because of that trend.
- [1:32](https://youtu.be/WOG3Rttf-V4?t=92) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Benchmark ARPDAU against genre peers; this game's was low versus the simulation genre benchmark, signalling monetisation headroom.

## Numbers
