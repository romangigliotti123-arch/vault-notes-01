## Summary
Creator breaks down earnings from Fish Two-Player Obby (2.5M visits, ~1,000 CCU): monetisation mix (lobby skins, infinite revives, potions, troll items, donation board), 72-hour and 30-day Robux totals, and the engagement payout delay. ## Tips
- [1:52](https://youtu.be/a4PQqMC8ruA?t=112) **[[monetisation-other]]** Add monetisation to the lobby (cosmetic skins, donation board) once players arrive; creator says adding these lifted revenue to benchmark ARPDAU.
- [0:32](https://youtu.be/a4PQqMC8ruA?t=32) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Sell paid revives and difficulty potions; these were the main developer-product earners in this game, per the creator.
- [3:13](https://youtu.be/a4PQqMC8ruA?t=193) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Expect a delay on engagement payouts: they only pay out at the end of the payment period, so sales from March 1 may not arrive until April 1.
