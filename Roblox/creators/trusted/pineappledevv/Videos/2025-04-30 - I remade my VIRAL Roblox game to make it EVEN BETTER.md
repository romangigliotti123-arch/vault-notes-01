# I remade my VIRAL Roblox game to make it EVEN BETTER

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Watch](https://youtu.be/S43gUJTZWUk) | pineappledevv | 9,002 views | 2025-04-30

## Summary
Creator remade his viral game Fishbowl as a sequel (seal instead of fish, new maps, new UI, red/blue two-player paths, brain-rot lighting). He cross-promoted it with a portal from Fishbowl into the new game (Celely). The sequel reached about 3,000 recommendations and homepage placement, then stalled, earning 1,200 Robux total and 100 Robux in engagement payouts. He calls it a flop.

## Tips
- [2:13](https://youtu.be/S43gUJTZWUk?t=133) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Cross-promote a new game from an existing popular one with an in-game portal; in this case most of the sequel's plays came from teleports.
- [3:01](https://youtu.be/S43gUJTZWUk?t=181) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** A homepage placement and a ~3,000 recommendation spike did not sustain plays; check analytics after the spike fades, since reach alone does not guarantee retention or revenue.
- [3:32](https://youtu.be/S43gUJTZWUk?t=212) **[[common-mistakes]]** Reskinning a successful game into a sequel with the same mechanics did not carry over the original's success; the creator concluded viral hits are not guaranteed to repeat.

- **Celely** [2:13](https://youtu.be/S43gUJTZWUk?t=133): Destination reached through the Fishbowl portal; the portal was the main traffic source for players reaching it.
