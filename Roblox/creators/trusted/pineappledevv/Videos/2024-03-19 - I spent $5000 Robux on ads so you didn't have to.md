# I spent $5000 Robux on ads so you didn't have to...

[Watch](https://youtu.be/NB8XNNDdSjQ) | pineappledevv | 4,431 views | 2024-03-19

## Summary
Creator spends 5,000 Robux (~20 ad credits) on Roblox's new Ads Manager to promote their game Eat Dirt over 2-3 days. Results: 93k impressions, 1,244 clicks, 460 plays, ~11 Robux per play, DAU rose from under 10 to ~175, avg session 4.8 min, zero revenue recouped.

## Tips
- [2:55](https://youtu.be/NB8XNNDdSjQ?t=175) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Convert ad credits to Robux before judging cost: at 1 credit = 285 Robux, 0.04 credits per play ≈ 11 Robux per play. Compute real cost-per-play in Robux.
- [1:26](https://youtu.be/NB8XNNDdSjQ?t=86) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ads Manager offers a sponsored-experience placement alongside the in-list placement; the sponsored option showed a much larger target audience. Test it rather than leaving defaults.
- [4:18](https://youtu.be/NB8XNNDdSjQ?t=258) **[[monetisation-other]]** Premium payouts need roughly 15+ minute average session time; a 4.8-minute session will earn little, so fix retention before buying traffic.
- [4:58](https://youtu.be/NB8XNNDdSjQ?t=298) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Paid traffic alone may not pay back; check hourly revenue in Overview against ad spend before scaling campaigns.
