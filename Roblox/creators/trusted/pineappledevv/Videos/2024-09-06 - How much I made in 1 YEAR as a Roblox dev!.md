# How much I made in 1 YEAR as a Roblox dev!

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Watch](https://youtu.be/UBs1bebPu0s) | pineappledevv | 3,471 views | 2024-09-06

## Summary
Developer reviews earnings from his first year of Roblox dev across five games (Eat Dirt, Button Simulator Odyssey, Idle Dice, a comment game, plus an unreleased game Halls). Shows Creator Dashboard monetization and engagement payout figures per game, noting donation boards and premium payouts drive most revenue. Total first-year earnings roughly 7-8k Robux.

## Tips
- [2:25](https://youtu.be/UBs1bebPu0s?t=145) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Add a donation board; in Idle Dice it brought 216 Robux from developer products alone, useful for players who won't buy gamepasses.
- [0:42](https://youtu.be/UBs1bebPu0s?t=42) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check earnings in Creator Dashboard monetization overview with custom date range, split by gamepasses, developer products and engagement (premium) payouts.
- [5:14](https://youtu.be/UBs1bebPu0s?t=314) **[[monetisation-other]]** Engagement-based Premium payouts are earned even with zero purchases; Comment Game earned 14 Robux with no purchases, so playtime alone pays something.
- [6:10](https://youtu.be/UBs1bebPu0s?t=370) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect slow growth; a popular Roblox game can take a long time, and not having a hit early is normal.

## Numbers
- [0:42](https://youtu.be/UBs1bebPu0s?t=42) Eat Dirt earnings: **~6,000 Robux total (2,000 gamepasses, 2,000 dev products, 2,000 premium payouts); 46 favorites, 9,000 visits** (Game out ~1 year, most-visited title)
- [2:25](https://youtu.be/UBs1bebPu0s?t=145) Button Simulator Odyssey earnings: **~1,000 Robux total (500 gamepasses/devproducts, 589 premium payouts); 500 visits** (Premium payouts per visit higher than other games)
- [3:52](https://youtu.be/UBs1bebPu0s?t=232) Idle Dice earnings: **216 Robux developer products (donations) + 41 Robux premium payouts** (Unfinished recreation of Cool Math Idle Dice)
- [5:14](https://youtu.be/UBs1bebPu0s?t=314) Comment Game earnings: **14 Robux, all premium payouts; zero purchases; under 500 visits** (Made for YouTube series)

## Games
- **Eat Dirt** [0:13](https://youtu.be/UBs1bebPu0s?t=13): Simple idle-style game with rewards page for staying longer and gamepass shop; best earner, about 6,000 Robux in a year.
- **Button Simulator Odyssey** [1:27](https://youtu.be/UBs1bebPu0s?t=87): Despite more effort, only 500 visits; earned more premium payouts per visit than other games.
- **Idle Dice** [3:41](https://youtu.be/UBs1bebPu0s?t=221): Cool Math recreation with donation board; donations drove 216 Robux, showing value of donation boards.
