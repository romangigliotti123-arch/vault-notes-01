## Summary
Explains Roblox's new Creator Rewards system (from June 24, 2025), which replaces engagement payouts. Covers the daily engagement reward (5 Robux per game for first three games a user plays daily, 10+ minutes), audience expansion rewards (35% revenue share on first $100 of a new user's Roblox spending), and 60-day holding periods.
