## Summary
Creator built a farming/garden sim on Roblox in 10 days (Grass Tycoon and Grow a Garden inspired): plant timers that wither, sell loop, quests, AFK money pad, rebirth/crate/statue system. Spent ~4,000 Robux on ads for ~3,000 visits, earned 931 Robux over 30 days, and concluded the grass niche was saturated.
