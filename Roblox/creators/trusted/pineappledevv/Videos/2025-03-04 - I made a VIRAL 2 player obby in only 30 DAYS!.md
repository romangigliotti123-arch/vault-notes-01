# I made a VIRAL 2 player obby in only 30 DAYS!

[Watch](https://youtu.be/IEAr0I_sUV4) | pineappledevv | 69,334 views | 2025-03-04

## Summary
Creator builds a two-player obby ("carry a fish" with a tray) in ~30 days. Covers team-up booths, milestone checkpoints instead of paid respawns, coin currency, cosmetic rewards for milestones, daily wheel, troll-purchase announcements, and early traction (~167k visits, ~950 peak CCU, ~20k Robux). Mostly devlog/entertainment.

## Tips
- [2:44](https://youtu.be/IEAr0I_sUV4?t=164) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Replace paid respawn with milestone checkpoints: finish a stage section to unlock a checkpoint to the next, reducing frustration from forced Robux spending.
- [4:47](https://youtu.be/IEAr0I_sUV4?t=287) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Reward milestone completion with a cosmetic unlock (new fish/tray skin) plus a dedicated animated milestone screen to make progress feel special.
- [3:25](https://youtu.be/IEAr0I_sUV4?t=205) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Announce who bought a disruptive troll action to all players so victims can retaliate, turning griefing into social interaction.
- [5:28](https://youtu.be/IEAr0I_sUV4?t=328) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add a daily spin wheel for small coin rewards to bring players back each day.
- [6:11](https://youtu.be/IEAr0I_sUV4?t=371) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Sell cheap consumable potions (auto-pickup, freeze, speed, jump) at small Robux prices, while also granting them via the daily wheel so free players are not locked out.
- [4:05](https://youtu.be/IEAr0I_sUV4?t=245) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add a earned in-game currency (e.g. +10 coins per 10 seconds played) as an alternative to Robux for respawns and trolls, letting free players progress.
- [6:37](https://youtu.be/IEAr0I_sUV4?t=397) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Keep the game public during development and playtest live on stream with viewers to get feedback and early players.
- [7:18](https://youtu.be/IEAr0I_sUV4?t=438) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship frequent updates after launch, such as redeemable codes tied to milestones/likes/visits, and add new worlds to sustain interest.
- [1:22](https://youtu.be/IEAr0I_sUV4?t=82) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Take a proven mechanic from a successful game as a starting point and add your own twist (here, carrying a fish instead of an egg).
- [0:17](https://youtu.be/IEAr0I_sUV4?t=17) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect development to take far longer than estimated; the creator assumed 2 days and it took about 30.
