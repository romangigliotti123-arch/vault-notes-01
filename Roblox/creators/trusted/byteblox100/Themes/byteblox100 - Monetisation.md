## [[monetisation-gamepasses]]

- 2024-09-29 [2:37](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=157) Don't clutter the screen with persistent shop/gamepass buttons and pop-ups; they annoy players and mainly push kids to overspend. - 2024-09-29 [7:25](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=445) Don't paywall basic convenience (e.g. auto-collect for 300 Robux) while making the core loop tedious to force purchases; it makes the game feel like a cash grab. ([[2024-09-29 - 7 Things I'll Never Add as a Roblox Developer|7 Things I'll Never Add as a Roblox Developer]])

## [[monetisation-other]]
- 2024-04-06 [1:15](https://youtu.be/7NRp4BEYrsQ?t=75) Set subscription product type to durable if players keep items after cancelling; use consumable if items should be removed when the subscription lapses. ([[2024-04-06 - Roblox Monetization Just Became Serious|Roblox Monetization Just Became Serious]])
- 2024-04-06 [2:05](https://youtu.be/7NRp4BEYrsQ?t=125) Activate the subscription in Creator Hub before prompting; an inactive subscription won't show for purchase. ([[2024-04-06 - Roblox Monetization Just Became Serious|Roblox Monetization Just Became Serious]])
- 2024-04-06 [3:08](https://youtu.be/7NRp4BEYrsQ?t=188) Check GetUserSubscriptionStatusAsync on the server to gate subscriber-only benefits; it returns subscribed and renewing flags. ([[2024-04-06 - Roblox Monetization Just Became Serious|Roblox Monetization Just Became Serious]])
- 2024-04-06 [8:28](https://youtu.be/7NRp4BEYrsQ?t=508) Consider a subscription tied to in-game progress gating, since the speaker suggests this is not currently supported natively. ([[2024-04-06 - Roblox Monetization Just Became Serious|Roblox Monetization Just Became Serious]])
- 2024-11-20 [0:48](https://youtu.be/Jl0SdXksQsI?t=48) Make monetization respectful: let players enjoy the game first, then offer purchases as optional extras. - 2025-02-01 [24:15](https://youtu.be/o-8V99horro?t=1455) When a player has collected every item in a rarity tier, substitute a fallback reward of equal value so duplicates don't waste purchases.
