# 7 Things I'll Never Add as a Roblox Developer

[Watch](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0) | byteblox100 | 180,305 views | 2024-09-29

## Summary
Developer lists seven features he would never add to a Roblox/Steam game: monetized UI interruptions, unnecessary progress bars, exponential difficulty scaling, currency-locked walls, nonrenewable resources without a restart option, upgrade-less features (e.g. lockers), and obbies-for-cash in tycoons. Uses Minecraft, Terraria, Subnautica and Grounded as contrasts. Mostly opinion, few numbers.

## Tips
- [2:37](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=157) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Don't clutter the screen with persistent shop/gamepass buttons and pop-ups; they annoy players and mainly push kids to overspend. Keep monetization out of the player's path.
- [7:25](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=445) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Don't paywall basic convenience (e.g. auto-collect for 300 Robux) while making the core loop tedious to force purchases; it makes the game feel like a cash grab.
- [16:48](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=1008) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Only add a progress bar (durability, hunger, oxygen) if it ties into other systems and creates meaningful decisions; a bar that only punishes with no reward is just annoying.
- [21:43](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=1303) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Don't fake progression with exponentially growing numbers (x2/x3 costs) when you have little content; use flat difficulty increases and add new content for each stage.
- [25:09](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=1509) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give long grinds new things to do at each level (new currency, area, power-ups) so new players who see the grind time don't quit before becoming long-term players.
- [30:08](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=1808) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Replace currency-locked walls with natural gates such as vehicle or equipment limits (Subnautica crush depth, jetpack fuel) so the world feels open and unrestricted.
- [38:35](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=2315) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Avoid soft-locks from nonrenewable items; provide an in-game way to restart or access all content (Grounded-style restart keeping progress) instead of requiring a new save.
- [41:43](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=2503) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give every upgradeable feature real upgrade tiers and playtest them at the relevant progression level so players don't hit a capped, tedious baseline.
- [47:10](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=2830) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** In tycoons, don't attach cash rewards to manual minigames like obbies; it forces players to stay engaged when the point is automation.
- [6:01](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=361) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Monetize, but do it without annoying the player; ad GUIs and sales are fine if placed where they don't block gameplay.

## Games
- **Minecraft** [9:14](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=554): Hunger bar works because it connects to exploration, hunting, and sprinting; a hypothetical thirst bar would only add annoyance.
- **Terraria** [14:01](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=841): No durability bar fits its small finite map and boss-focused progression; the same mechanic would be annoying here.
- **Subnautica** [31:28](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=1888): Depth-based vehicle limits gate progression naturally; the lockers upgrade path is weak as an inventory fix.
- **Grounded** [39:16](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=2356): Nonrenewable resources are fine if the game offers an in-game restart that unlocks content and respawns resources.
- **Terraria (biome swap)** [39:56](https://youtu.be/Nzrtg8SGOY0?t=2396): Machine that converts corruption and crimson items lets players access both without a new save.
