# Should Your Roblox Game Have Ads?

[Watch](https://youtu.be/okGvZjneq60) | byteblox100 | 25,763 views | 2024-02-20

## Summary
Explains how to add ads to a Roblox game using the Ad GUI (SurfaceGui-style ad part with adorn part, face, video toggle, fallback image). Covers how Roblox pays by impressions/clicks, and argues for integrating ads naturally into gameplay (example: tycoon computer screens) rather than giant billboards, once the game has a real player base.

## Tips
- [0:24](https://youtu.be/okGvZjneq60?t=24) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Add an Ad GUI inside a part, scale the part, then pick the face, adornee, and enable video ads. Roblox supplies the ad content and pays you.
- [2:12](https://youtu.be/okGvZjneq60?t=132) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Set a fallback image on the Ad GUI so the slot still shows something when no ad loads (server or client issues).
- [2:54](https://youtu.be/okGvZjneq60?t=174) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Avoid giant billboard ads at spawn; they feel spammy. Weave ads into gameplay surfaces, like in-world screens, where players see them without it hurting the experience.
- [3:36](https://youtu.be/okGvZjneq60?t=216) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Only add ads once the game has a meaningful number of players; ads earn little with no traffic, so monetise after you have an audience.
- [4:18](https://youtu.be/okGvZjneq60?t=258) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Place ads where players will look at them but not feel them as intrusive; if an ad is clickable, avoid accidental clicks that send players to unrelated experiences.
- [5:11](https://youtu.be/okGvZjneq60?t=311) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not spam ads everywhere; irrational ad placement annoys players and hurts retention.
