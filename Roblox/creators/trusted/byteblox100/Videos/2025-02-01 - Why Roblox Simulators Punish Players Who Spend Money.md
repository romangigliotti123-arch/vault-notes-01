# Why Roblox Simulators Punish Players Who Spend Money

[Watch](https://youtu.be/o-8V99horro) | byteblox100 | 361,739 views | 2025-02-01

## Summary
Shows how simulator-style Roblox games manipulate pet/crate drop odds in a scripted demo: rarity roll logic, pity systems, first-time-buyer boosts, dynamic chance reduction as spending rises, end-of-event odds tweaks, and fallback rewards. Frames these as manipulative tactics players should recognise.

## Tips
- [4:06](https://youtu.be/o-8V99horro?t=246) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Roll rarity from the rarest tier down: check legendary first, then epic, rare, common, using one random number from 1 to chance. Checking common first breaks the odds.
- [10:08](https://youtu.be/o-8V99horro?t=608) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Give first-time buyers a boosted chance on their first purchase so the first reward feels exciting and encourages continued opening.
- [13:37](https://youtu.be/o-8V99horro?t=817) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Use a pity counter that resets on a legendary drop to guarantee a top reward after N opens. Players trust visible pity systems.
- [22:15](https://youtu.be/o-8V99horro?t=1335) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Adjust drop chances by outcome: lower legendary odds after rare hits, raise them after bad rolls. This is manipulative; use it deliberately and fairly if at all.
- [24:15](https://youtu.be/o-8V99horro?t=1455) **[[monetisation-other]]** When a player has collected every item in a rarity tier, substitute a fallback reward of equal value so duplicates don't waste purchases. The speaker calls this scummy.
- [25:43](https://youtu.be/o-8V99horro?t=1543) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Keep odds fair and transparent. The speaker says Brawl Stars is fair and simple, while one-in-a-billion pet odds are bad design.
