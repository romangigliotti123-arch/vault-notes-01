# the COOLEST game feature YOU'RE NOT USING! - Roblox Volumetric Audio

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Watch](https://youtu.be/2VZ7DQHYPn8) | crusherfiredev | 61,684 views | 2024-01-20

## Summary
Explains Roblox's undocumented Volumetric Audio property in SoundService (Disabled/Automatic/Enabled, Automatic = Disabled). When enabled, sounds parented to block, cylinder, or ball parts emit from the whole surface, with rolloff distances measured from the surface. Demos ocean ambiance and region-based audio.

## Tips
- [0:21](https://youtu.be/2VZ7DQHYPn8?t=21) **[[sound-and-music]]** Volumetric Audio is set in SoundService; default Automatic currently equals Disabled, so you must set it to Enabled manually.
- [3:52](https://youtu.be/2VZ7DQHYPn8?t=232) **[[sound-and-music]]** Enable volumetric audio for large parts like oceans or waterfalls so sound comes from the whole surface instead of the part center.
- [5:56](https://youtu.be/2VZ7DQHYPn8?t=356) **[[sound-and-music]]** With volumetric audio enabled, a sound inside a part is only heard once the camera is inside it, useful for region-based ambiance.
- [4:34](https://youtu.be/2VZ7DQHYPn8?t=274) **[[sound-and-music]]** Volumetric audio only supports block, cylinder and ball parts per the 2022 Dev Forum release notes; other shapes won't benefit.

## Numbers
