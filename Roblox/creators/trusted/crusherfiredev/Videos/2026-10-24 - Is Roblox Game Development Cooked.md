## Summary
Opinion/devlog on how AI (Claude-era coding tools, skills) is changing Roblox development. Covers AI slop risk for younger audiences, using AI to rapidly test ideas and refactor, UI built with hours of iterative AI refinement, the discipline needed to avoid lazy use, and AI-bubble economics. Sponsor segment skipped.

## Tips
- [3:12](https://youtu.be/4n7thZAAF-E?t=192) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use AI to prototype several variants of a gameplay system quickly, then keep iterating within the one that feels best, instead of committing to the first idea.
- [3:52](https://youtu.be/4n7thZAAF-E?t=232) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Write your architecture and code-style rules as reusable AI skills, then have AI refactor old code to match; speaker says this cut a few weeks of work to a day or two.
- [6:24](https://youtu.be/4n7thZAAF-E?t=384) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Expect AI-built UI to need several hours of back-and-forth refinement: fix consistency, missing elements, and replace animations you dislike before shipping.
- [2:02](https://youtu.be/4n7thZAAF-E?t=122) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid one-prompt generation of whole games; it produces unpolished, low-effort output that older players spot and avoid.

## Numbers
