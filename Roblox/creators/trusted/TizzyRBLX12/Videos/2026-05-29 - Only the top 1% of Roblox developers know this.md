# Only the top 1% of Roblox developers know this...

[Watch](https://youtu.be/0pMW1T0rdUI) | Tizzy | 12,120 views | 2026-05-29

## Summary
Teaches the "games to watch" method: pick rising games from RoTrends, take hypothesis-driven notes on first-time experience, core loop and progression, then recheck CCU a week or two later to build game design intuition. Walks through My Gaming Cafe and Alien Ranch examples.

## Tips
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/0pMW1T0rdUI?t=81) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Find up-and-coming games on RoTrends (sort by momentum or visits) and add each to a Google Sheet for study.
- [2:03](https://youtu.be/0pMW1T0rdUI?t=123) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Before checking data, write a hypothesis on whether the game will grow, with reasons, so you can score your judgment later.
- [4:48](https://youtu.be/0pMW1T0rdUI?t=288) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Recheck tracked games on RoTrends 1 to 2 weeks later and log whether CCU rose or fell versus your hypothesis.
- [8:13](https://youtu.be/0pMW1T0rdUI?t=493) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make the goal, progression path and a curiosity loop clear within the first 60 to 120 seconds of play.
- [8:54](https://youtu.be/0pMW1T0rdUI?t=534) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make other players' progress visible at spawn (social flex element); hidden progress behind walls limits the game.
- [10:57](https://youtu.be/0pMW1T0rdUI?t=657) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give players a reason to return by unlocking something new early; repeating the same item three times reduced curiosity.
- [13:40](https://youtu.be/0pMW1T0rdUI?t=820) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Study games this way for intuition, but still launch your own games; theory alone is not enough.
