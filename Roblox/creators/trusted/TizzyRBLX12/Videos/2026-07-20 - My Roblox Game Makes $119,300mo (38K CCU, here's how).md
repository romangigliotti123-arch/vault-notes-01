# My Roblox Game Makes $119,300/mo (38K CCU, here's how)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus) | Tizzy | 308,427 views | 2026-07-20

## Summary
Producer "Tizzy" explains how his Roblox game Build a Base and Steal reached ~38K CCU and ~$119K/mo. Claims 80% of success is idea plus execution, with talent the bottleneck. Covers UX/accessibility, simple mechanics with depth, clippable moments, a competitor-research-ship-measure loop, and mindset. Largely anecdotal; no methodology for the revenue figures.

## Tips
- [7:56](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=476) **[[team-and-budget]]** Talent is the bottleneck: find good, vetted developers before worrying about ideas. He says ideas are cheap and everywhere.
- [4:33](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=273) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't buy game templates or reskin top games. He bought a $600 template that flopped and reskinned Steal a Cookie into a failed game.
- [5:55](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=355) **[[team-and-budget]]** Vet hires before paying: a $4,000 Upwork hire produced a flopped game. Overpaying an inexperienced team also failed.
- [10:24](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=624) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Play your own game on mobile and console. Many devs never do, and a console onboarding issue was fixed to raise D1 retention.
- [11:45](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=705) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Remove every friction point: broken proximity prompts, unpolished UI and unclear mechanics. He says these separate 1K CCU games from top-100 games.
- [13:09](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=789) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build depth from one simple mechanic. Build a Base and Steal's base-raid loop forces constant layout optimisation, and Sandwich Game uses one RNG roll.
- [14:15](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=855) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make every feature's cause and effect obvious: press this button, this happens. Simple scales, fancy fails.
- [15:37](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=937) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give players a curiosity hook to return tomorrow, such as a new base or pet to unlock, plus a social flex element.
- [16:59](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=1019) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Make the game clippable. Funny moments like ragdolling thieves into the void drew YouTubers who made free content.
- [17:42](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=1062) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Study top games heavily. A creator reportedly played top Roblox games about 12 hours a day to build design intuition.
- [18:22](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=1102) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Loop: competitor research, data, idea, ship, check stats, keep iterating on the metric. Ship volume: nine tutorials and daily patches.
- [19:04](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=1144) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Add a cutscene to the intro. Hack a Business bounce rate reportedly dropped from about 22% to 15-16%.
- [3:03](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=183) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Call games early by reading CCU trajectory. He claims calls at 200 CCU (My Giant Sandwich) and about 50 CCU (airport game) that later grew.
- [7:15](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=435) **[[team-and-budget]]** Find the business behind the business. For Roblox that is talent acquisition, not the idea.
- [9:17](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=557) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Judge ideas on depth, replayability, console and mobile play, mass-market appeal and fantasy demand before building.
- [11:04](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=664) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Apply e-commerce UX and funnel analysis to Roblox game decisions: heatmaps, session recordings and funnel data.
- [20:17](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=1217) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Stay locked in and talk to players in your Discord for feedback and ideas. He says he declines many offers to focus on this game.
- [8:36](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus?t=516) **[[common-mistakes]]** Ignoring how the algorithm works sank some past projects, he says. Account for it when designing and launching.

## Games
- **Build a Base and Steal**: Co-owned hit: base-raid loop, pet and block collection, 38K CCU peak, 30% console players, daily patches.
- **Hack a Business**: Co-owned game making ~1M Robux/72h; cutscene cut bounce rate from ~22% to ~15-16%.
- **My Giant Sandwich**: Creator called it at ~200 CCU; RNG stacking mechanic with depth; simple and easy to understand.
- **Secure the Airport**: Called at ~50 CCU, reached ~30K CCU; airport fantasy and clippable moments.
- **Steal a Brain Rot**: Cited as simple mechanic with depth and clippable pet-stealing moments; example of genius in simplicity.
- **Grow a Garden**: Cited as simple mechanic with depth and social flex element despite being called slop.
- **Spin a Brain Rot**: Made ~$250K in 2 months per Mattia, a dev the creator learned from.
- **Steal Cookies (reskinned to Steal Sigma's Rizz)**: Reskin of a trending game by a $4K Upwork hire flopped; reskinning alone does not work.
- **Destroy the Karens**: Bought template reached 100 CCU then flopped; avoid templates.
- **Farmer Die**: 3.7M visits and 1.8K peak CCU but unprofitable on $12K spend with two full-time hires.
