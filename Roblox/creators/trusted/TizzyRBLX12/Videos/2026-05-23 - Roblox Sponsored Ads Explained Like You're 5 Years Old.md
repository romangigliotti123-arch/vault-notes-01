# Roblox Sponsored Ads Explained Like You're 5 Years Old

[Watch](https://youtu.be/owMnhg3fn_M) | Tizzy | 51,874 views | 2026-05-23

## Summary
Walkthrough of Roblox sponsored ads: defining a strong core fantasy, keyword-researching titles at a third-grade reading level, a thumbnail process, and a simple ad-running rule ($16/day for 3+ days, stop ads once home recommendation impressions exceed ad impressions). Case study takes a game's CTR from 1.54% to 3.27%.

## Tips
- [0:51](https://youtu.be/owMnhg3fn_M?t=51) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Define a clear core fantasy before building or advertising: the role you play, the actions you take, and how it makes you feel.
- [5:20](https://youtu.be/owMnhg3fn_M?t=320) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Keyword-research titles in Creator Xchange by checking visits on games using the keyword; 'hack' showed multi-million-visit games.
- [6:42](https://youtu.be/owMnhg3fn_M?t=402) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Write titles at about a third-grade reading level; check word reading levels (e.g. 'business' over 'corporation').
- [7:23](https://youtu.be/owMnhg3fn_M?t=443) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Make the title give context to the thumbnail, so title plus image together convey a clear core fantasy.
- [9:47](https://youtu.be/owMnhg3fn_M?t=587) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Avoid generic verb-noun templates; use clear fantasy with an implied challenge, e.g. '99 Nights in the Forest'.
- [11:59](https://youtu.be/owMnhg3fn_M?t=719) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Launch at least 10 thumbnails in ads, ideally around 20; more creative variants are better.
- [12:39](https://youtu.be/owMnhg3fn_M?t=759) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Start ads at $16/day on daily budget, run at least 3 days (ideally a week), All Players Recommended, all ages.
- [13:20](https://youtu.be/owMnhg3fn_M?t=800) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Don't panic about stat delays; Roblox data updates roughly every 3 days, so judge ads after several days.
- [13:36](https://youtu.be/owMnhg3fn_M?t=816) **[[monetisation-other]]** Buy ad credits with Robux rather than USD to save money.
- [14:22](https://youtu.be/owMnhg3fn_M?t=862) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Launch updates the same day new stats arrive; watch live player feedback and funnel tracking while ads run.
- [15:03](https://youtu.be/owMnhg3fn_M?t=903) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Keep running ads until home recommendation impressions surpass sponsored ad impressions in Acquisition analytics, then stop.
- [16:24](https://youtu.be/owMnhg3fn_M?t=984) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Good thumbnail, good QPTR and good stats make home recommendation impressions grow, letting you stop ads sooner.
