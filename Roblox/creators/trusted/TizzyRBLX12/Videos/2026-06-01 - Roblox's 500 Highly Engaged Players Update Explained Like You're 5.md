# Roblox's 500 Highly Engaged Players Update Explained Like You're 5

[Watch](https://youtu.be/Rkgbcv6omPg) | Tizzy | 21,566 views | 2026-06-01

## Summary
Explains Roblox's 2026 "500 highly engaged players" gate for reaching under-16 audiences: what counts as a highly engaged player, the bouncer analogy, and the creator's own ad-spend experience (~11 days, ~$180, $16/day) to reach the threshold. Notes ads and home-recommendation traffic as drivers, and that bots inflate ad plays.

## Tips
- [2:39](https://youtu.be/Rkgbcv6omPg?t=159) **[[roblox-platform-changes]]** Since May 19 2026 new games are only shown to 16+ until they reach 500 highly engaged players; plan for that gate at launch.
- [5:06](https://youtu.be/Rkgbcv6omPg?t=306) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ads served to 16+ automatically; you don't need to set targeting to 16+ to hit the threshold.
- [5:46](https://youtu.be/Rkgbcv6omPg?t=346) **[[roblox-platform-changes]]** Highly engaged player = anyone with a minimum platform purchase anywhere on Roblox in the last 60 days; they needn't spend in your game.
- [4:01](https://youtu.be/Rkgbcv6omPg?t=241) **[[roblox-platform-changes]]** Roblox is exploring a paid pre-launch fast-track review (higher upfront fee, recoupable if standards met) for reaching kids before the threshold.
- [8:48](https://youtu.be/Rkgbcv6omPg?t=528) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ads brought most traffic early, but many ad plays were bots that never move; expect low-quality ad traffic.
- [10:11](https://youtu.be/Rkgbcv6omPg?t=611) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Once home recommendation impressions kick in, reaching 500 engaged players accelerates, since the algorithm recommends to players who already play similar games.
- [11:25](https://youtu.be/Rkgbcv6omPg?t=685) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Budget roughly $16/day for ~11 days (~$180 total) to clear the threshold; the speaker says this is only slightly more than prior algo-hitting spend.
- [12:05](https://youtu.be/Rkgbcv6omPg?t=725) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** If ad budget is too tight, earn ad money via commissions rather than skipping ads.
