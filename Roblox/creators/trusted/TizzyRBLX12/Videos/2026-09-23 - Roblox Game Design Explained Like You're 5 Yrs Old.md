# Roblox Game Design Explained Like You're 5 Yrs Old

[Watch](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A) | Tizzy | 61,012 views | 2026-09-23

## Summary
Shows how to break Roblox simulator/tycoon hits into building blocks (fantasy, unique mechanic, core loop, RNG placement, level design, tension, progression) and derive new games by changing ~20% of a reference game in areas that matter. Walks through Ride a Pet, Steal a Brainrot-style games, Escape Tsunami, Kick a Lucky Block, Dig and Clean, and Surf and Rescue.

## Tips
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=40) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Break successful games into building blocks: fantasy, unique mechanic, core loop, RNG, level design, tension, progression. Copy the shared blocks; innovate only on the ones that matter.
- [14:21](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=861) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Pick a reference game in your genre and change at least 20% of it in meaningful areas. Speaker says this is enough for 5-10K CCU.
- [16:22](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=982) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Change more elements for bigger reach: Ride a Pet reportedly changed ~40% versus Steal a Egg, aiming for 100K CCU. Changes must be researched, not random.
- [15:02](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=902) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Innovate on target audience as well as fantasy. A fantasy shift (e.g. saving animals) can reach a different, often female, player base.
- [6:04](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=364) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Use time-based restock (e.g. spawns refresh every 5 minutes) to create FOMO and urgency, as Steal a Egg borrowed from Grow a Garden's stock refresh.
- [4:42](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=282) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Create tension with a timer or a chaser. Ride a Pet uses an egg cracking timer; Steal an Egg and Surf and Rescue add chasing monsters or sharks.
- [7:37](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=457) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Use a pressure-valve level design: players run to grab a reward, decide whether to keep going, then release tension at a safe zone. Escape Tsunami popularised this.
- [5:23](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=323) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add PvP to a PvE chase to intensify tension. Steal an Egg added player-vs-player to the monster-chase loop of Escape Tsunami-style games.
- [3:22](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=202) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Decide where RNG is shown: pre-acquisition (Kick a Lucky Block, rolls visible on kick) or post-acquisition (Ride a Pet, roll when egg returns to base).
- [10:21](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=621) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add a skill check (tap as a bar fills) to a core action to differentiate, as Kick a Lucky Block did with its kick mechanic.
- [10:21](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=621) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Vary progression: Escape Tsunami and Steal an Egg use get faster and collect pets, Kick a Lucky Block uses get stronger, Dig and Clean uses better shovels and collects treasure.
- [2:39](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=159) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Most simulator hits share the core loop: leave base, get a thing, bring it back to base. Build your loop around that proven pattern.
- [4:01](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=241) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Level design is a differentiator. Ride a Pet's open exploration map (inspired by Creatures of Sinaria) was innovative versus the common linear layout.
- [13:02](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=782) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't claim an original idea. Almost every game is derivative; differentiate on the fantasy and unique mechanic instead.
- [17:03](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=1023) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Look beyond the building blocks for retention: first-five-minute progression pacing, retention mechanics, collection expansion and mutations.
- [15:42](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A?t=942) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Set realistic goals: a 20% change to a proven genre targets 1-5K, maybe 10K CCU. 100K CCU needs stacked innovations across multiple blocks.

## Games
- **Ride a Pet**: Exploration map with simulator progression; eggs spawn in the world, roll after returning to base; ~40% changed from Steal an Egg.
- **Steal an Egg**: Took Grow a Garden's 5-minute stock refresh into the world and added PvP while escaping a monster.
- **Escape Tsunami for Brain Rots**: Early linear level design with pressure-valve tension release; PvE tsunami chase; get faster, collect brainrots.
- **Kick a Lucky Block**: Kicking blocks with a skill-check tap; pre-acquisition RNG; progression is get stronger; long-lived via live ops.
- **Dig and Clean**: Cleaning mechanic carried the game; no PvE tension; progression via better shovels; collects treasure.
- **Surf and Rescue**: Same mechanics as Dig and Clean plus clear PvE chase tension (shark, stingray) and a save-animals fantasy aimed at a new audience.
- **Creatures of Sinaria**: Exploration vibe that Ride a Pet borrowed from.
- **Grow a Garden**: Source of the timed stock refresh mechanic used by Steal an Egg.
- **Raisin Anomaly**: Speaker's recent release: egg-restock loop from Steal an Egg plus a feeding mechanic, with fantasy and audience changed.
