# I Made ANOTHER $25,070/mo Roblox Game (13.1k CCU)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I) | Tizzy | 63,487 views | 2026-07-13

## Summary
Creator's post-mortem on Build a Base and Steal (~13.1k CCU, ~$25k/mo, 2 weeks old). Covers why a PvP build-to-survive clone works: PvP depth, building loop, free private servers, sunk-cost retention, tutorial rework (lock base first), removing auto-roll, and roll-tease psychology that doubled PCR.

## Tips
- [2:03](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=123) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Pick mechanics that are simple and intuitive but deep. Prefer depth over complexity so strategy is never a solved strategy.
- [7:42](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=462) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add PvP so every session differs. PvE games like Build to Survive Tsunami get solved within 5 minutes, per the creator.
- [8:24](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=504) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Make private servers free and allow co-op. Players who dislike PvP can still build and invite friends, driving word-of-mouth.
- [10:34](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=634) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Let players invest time and Robux into a persistent base and collection. Sunk cost plus index-filling drives return visits.
- [12:14](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=734) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Lead the tutorial with the most engaging mechanic. Here, building, so players act within 10 seconds. Team tried ~9 tutorials in week one.
- [13:37](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=817) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make the first 30 seconds deliver the game's title promise. Check competitors on an alt account to see what they show first.
- [14:19](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=859) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make the first tutorial step 'lock your base' to signal PvP without text. Avoid text-based tutorials, which the creator says players skip.
- [15:23](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=923) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Don't copy genre mechanics like auto-roll blindly. Auto-roll let kids reach end-game in ~3 hours and wrecked the economy. Remove it.
- [16:44](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=1004) **[[pricing]]** Show a teaser of rare pets on the first roll. Changing roll teases raised PCR from 0.3% to 0.75% per the creator.
- [18:06](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=1086) **[[monetisation-other]]** Gate rolls behind in-game cash, with Robux shown when unaffordable. This turns Robux into a cash sink for luck.
- [18:56](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=1136) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Offer a Robux option for upgrades you can't afford in-game. This gives a clear purchase path at the moment of need.
- [19:36](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=1176) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Use level design to add juking options. A central island with climbable vines expands steal tactics. Plan timed biome changes and events.
- [0:42](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=42) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track D1, average session length and retention cohorts early. Creator watched D1 by traffic source (search ~31%, friends ~27%) to judge intent.
- [9:47](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=587) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Update the game daily in early weeks. Creator credits daily updates and rapid tutorial iteration for early gains.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=0) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Start running ads about two weeks after launch. Creator saw growth after ads began, though no spend figures were given.
- [2:47](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=167) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Scan Creator Exchange and build-genre search for evergreen demand. Spot gaps, e.g. PvE build-to-survive games converted into PvP.
- [5:32](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=332) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Combine proven mechanics from a hit (stealing from Steal a Brain Rot) with a proven evergreen genre (building) to build a foundation.
- [20:16](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I?t=1216) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect economy tuning to be ongoing. The creator nerfed the economy several times and called it only the beginning.

## Games
- **Build a Base and Steal**: Its PvP build-and-steal loop with free private servers and a pet-collection base drove retention. Tutorial starts with locking your base.
- **Build to Defend Loot**: Made the PvE build-to-survive genre PvP with deeper mechanics. Its tutorial delivers the title promise within 30 seconds.
- **Build to Survive Tsunami**: PvE example: strategy becomes solved within 5 minutes, which is why the creator favours PvP.
- **Steal a Brain Rot**: Proved the stealing mechanic and collection fantasy; the creator calls it a market starved for a successor.
- **Build and Steal Brain Rots**: Gage's first version of the loop, which led to Build a Base and Steal v2.
- **Grow a Garden**: Cited as example of leading tutorials with the most engaging mechanic (plant seed, get crop).
- **Rivals**: Cited as the number one earning game in the creator's opinion, due to PvP unique scenarios.
