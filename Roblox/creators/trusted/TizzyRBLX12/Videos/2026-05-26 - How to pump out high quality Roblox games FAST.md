# How to pump out high quality Roblox games FAST

[Watch](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8) | Tizzy | 20,191 views | 2026-05-26

## Summary
Speaker, a former e-commerce agency owner, explains how to hire and manage Roblox devs to ship MVPs fast: hire T-shaped devs via Twitter/Discord reputation, sign SOW contracts with pay-on-completion, write a game design doc, track tasks in Trello, build art before programmer, prototype with AI, and iterate on backups before returning to the programmer.

## Tips
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=81) **[[team-and-budget]]** Hiring is the main bottleneck; find talent through reputation on Twitter, YouTube and Discord mutuals rather than cold job boards.
- [2:03](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=123) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Build in public on Twitter/YouTube to build hiring reputation, even if posts look foolish early on.
- [4:05](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=245) **[[team-and-budget]]** Prefer T-shaped hires (e.g. programmer who also does UI/animation) and run a lean team of about two to four people.
- [6:50](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=410) **[[team-and-budget]]** Hire in the same time zone for fast communication and mutual motivation between programmer and builder.
- [7:31](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=451) **[[team-and-budget]]** Always sign a contract with a clear statement of work; avoid devs who refuse to sign.
- [8:55](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=535) **[[team-and-budget]]** Prefer paying upon completion; paying in full upfront led to delays of about two weeks; 50% upfront is a fallback.
- [8:12](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=492) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Scope an MVP first: just the core loop and basic progression, then playtest to check whether the game is fun.
- [8:55](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=535) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Build a working prototype with AI (e.g. Claude) and free models before hiring a developer to validate the idea quickly.
- [10:58](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=658) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Write a game design document covering game, player actions, leveling, economy and progression; use it as the scope and SOW basis.
- [11:38](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=698) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Break the design doc into Trello columns per discipline (programmer, builder, UI) with explicit task details.
- [12:19](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=739) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Record Loom walkthroughs and give game reference links so developers understand exactly what system to build.
- [13:00](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=780) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Finish models, builds and UI before bringing in the programmer; a pre-built world is more motivating to code into.
- [14:23](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=863) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Launch the MVP and gather live player data quickly rather than overthinking; playtesters can be used before going live.
- [15:45](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=945) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Duplicate the game backup and tweak feel with AI to reduce back-and-forth with the programmer, then batch changes into one request.
- [16:27](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=987) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Stick to the agreed scope; send new ideas as new contracts to avoid resentment and bogged-down developers.
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=40) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Ideas are cheap; execution decides outcomes on Roblox.
- [3:25](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8?t=205) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not think you can buy a hit by throwing money at devs; a reported $4,000 first game was never launched.
