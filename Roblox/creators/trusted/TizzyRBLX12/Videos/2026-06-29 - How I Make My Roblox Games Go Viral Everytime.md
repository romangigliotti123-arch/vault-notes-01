# How I Make My Roblox Games Go Viral Everytime

[Watch](https://youtu.be/4ms4cp2mrtM) | Tizzy | 39,606 views | 2026-06-29

## Summary
Speaker (a Roblox game investor/producer) explains his checklist for predicting which games will go viral: check off-platform demand signals (Steam wishlists, CrazyGames views, Creator Exchange genre gaps), look for a gap in the market and fresh ideas, favor readable mechanics already proven on Roblox, and polish mobile UX and level design by combining proven concepts (Pareto 80/20 tweak).

## Tips
- [1:42](https://youtu.be/4ms4cp2mrtM?t=102) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Check off-platform demand first: Steam wishlists, CrazyGames gameplay views, and YouTube content on the same fantasy show demand before you build.
- [3:46](https://youtu.be/4ms4cp2mrtM?t=226) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Use Creator Exchange, sorted by visits, to see which genres dominate a keyword; a fantasy with only roleplay games is a gap for a different genre.
- [4:27](https://youtu.be/4ms4cp2mrtM?t=267) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Take a fantasy that's popular but under-served and reframe its core loop in a different genre (e.g., roleplay fantasy turned into a checkpoint game).
- [7:00](https://youtu.be/4ms4cp2mrtM?t=420) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Look for a market gap: a fantasy with steady but low CCU for years signals demand that hasn't been innovated on.
- [7:42](https://youtu.be/4ms4cp2mrtM?t=462) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Make the idea fresh: a polished game with generic same-as-everyone mechanics (plain team deathmatch FPS) struggles; add mechanics players haven't seen.
- [8:58](https://youtu.be/4ms4cp2mrtM?t=538) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make each system readable by reusing a mechanic already proven in a top Roblox game so players arrive already knowing how to use it.
- [11:41](https://youtu.be/4ms4cp2mrtM?t=701) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Watch a non-gamer play your game without instruction; points of friction like spamming a buy button stack into a chore.
- [12:19](https://youtu.be/4ms4cp2mrtM?t=739) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Act on live playtest feedback: players asked for max-upgrade buttons, so add buy-max options as popular incremental games do.
- [13:41](https://youtu.be/4ms4cp2mrtM?t=821) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't overlook small friction points; many devs never test on phone even though about 80% of Roblox players are on mobile.
- [15:02](https://youtu.be/4ms4cp2mrtM?t=902) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't ship a complex map with boats if a proven level design works; the speaker simplified his map to a proven layout.
- [17:04](https://youtu.be/4ms4cp2mrtM?t=1024) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Apply the Pareto 80/20 idea: start from a proven loop and change about 20% of it so the game has its own identity without losing familiarity.

## Games
- **Totally Secure Airport**: Pre-release Steam wishlists (200k) and a Roblox version beating the Steam release signal strong demand.
- **Airport Security (CrazyGames)**: CrazyGames gameplay views (6.2M) show the fantasy's off-platform demand.
- **Secure the Airport**: Took the roleplay airport fantasy and made it a checkpoint game; few competitors, strong results.
- **Firefighter games**: Large visits with flat ~1K CCU suggests an innovatable fantasy with little competition.
- **Mecha Chameleon**: Proven Steam hit brought to Roblox got 55k/42k CCU; porting proven games works, but mix mechanics.
- **Strafe / Bonk and Block**: Fresh mechanics (building, mystery-box abilities, destruction physics) and polish drive success over generic FPS.
- **Grow a Garden**: Its HUD buttons and plot layout are widely learned by players; reuse proven systems.
- **Steal a Brainrot**: Base lock mechanic is understood by almost all Roblox players; reuse familiar systems.
- **Cut Grass**: Buy one / buy max upgrade buttons are a proven incremental UX pattern.
- **Build or Get Robbed**: Playtest feedback led to buy-max buttons and a bedwars-plus-grow-a-garden sky layout.
- **Bedwars**: Floating islands and bridge-separated plots are familiar; mixed in for novelty.
