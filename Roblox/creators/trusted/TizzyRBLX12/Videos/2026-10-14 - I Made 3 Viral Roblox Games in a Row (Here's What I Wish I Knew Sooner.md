# I Made 3 Viral Roblox Games in a Row (Here's What I Wish I Knew Sooner)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls) | Tizzy | 61,636 views | 2026-10-14

## Summary
Post-mortem of a simulator game (called Raisin Domly / Reason Anomaly in the transcript) that went viral and then died. Covers onboarding friction, contextual tutorials, progression balancing with funnels and paid tester logs, monetisation (consumables, loss-leader tiers, limiteds, whale sinks), feedback prompts, a dedicated test place, and team management.

## Tips
- [2:10](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=130) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Track every onboarding step as a funnel, from join through steal egg, safe zone, plot, equip, place, hatch. Biggest drop-off shows what to fix first.
- [2:52](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=172) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Auto-equip the key item (e.g. egg) when the player reaches the goal. Forcing a hotbar equip adds friction in the first 90 seconds.
- [3:34](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=214) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Pre-stock starter consumables (e.g. food) in inventory. Teach where to buy them only when the player runs out, via a contextual tutorial.
- [4:57](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=297) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make the first use of the core mechanic a wow moment, e.g. first feed scales the pet ~5x, so players feel the core loop immediately.
- [5:38](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=338) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Teach only the core loop up front, then set players free. Avoid teaching food, treadmills and plot upgrades all at once.
- [6:19](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=379) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Fire contextual tutorials from backend triggers, e.g. at 1K cash teach trail purchase, or on first full-plot placement teach plot upgrade.
- [7:55](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=475) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Balance progression on a fresh alt account, not your own save. Log the timestamp of each unlock (e.g. minute 1 treadmill, minute 5 zone 3) to spot walls.
- [8:37](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=517) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Build progression funnels for milestones like first treadmill and first plot upgrade. Here only ~60% bought treadmill 1 and 26% upgraded plot, signalling price or timing issues.
- [11:49](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=709) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Pay 2-3 testers (1,000-2,000 Robux each) to grind 3 hours and log unlock times and blockers. Feed the logs and reference-game data to an LLM for balancing ideas.
- [12:44](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=764) **[[common-mistakes]]** Balance before launch. A week or two of broken D1 (6-8%) on live hurt scaling and likely cost the game a higher peak.
- [14:36](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=876) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Launch limited/whale products and consumables from the start, not two weeks in. A 1,000 Robux limited with fake scarcity sold well.
- [15:20](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=920) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Don't make top-tier items too OP or sell cash packs early. Both likely fried the in-game economy and shortened the game's life.
- [16:01](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=961) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Make the most powerful food scarce and priced higher. It became the best-selling food because it was limited.
- [16:44](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=1004) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Stack cheap entry-level products (loss-leader style, e.g. 5 and 9 Robux speed tiers) to push players past the first-purchase threshold.
- [18:10](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=1090) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Place an in-game mailbox or feedback prompt. Use AI to summarise feedback into themes like lag, inventory and balance.
- [18:51](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=1131) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Recruit a dedicated tester group before launch and use a separate published test place. Live Roblox shows bugs Studio does not.
- [19:54](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=1194) **[[team-and-budget]]** Own the outcome. Give modelers an art-style guide and AI-generated references from the start, or you get clashing art styles.
- [21:18](https://youtu.be/_JyiKfNNqls?t=1278) **[[team-and-budget]]** Hire fast, fire faster. Replace contractors who miss deadlines; one scrapped map cost $300.
