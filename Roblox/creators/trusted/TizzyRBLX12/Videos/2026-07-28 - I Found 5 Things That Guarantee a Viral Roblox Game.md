# I Found 5 Things That Guarantee a Viral Roblox Game

[Watch](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg) | Tizzy | 40,986 views | 2026-07-28

## Summary
Creator Tizzy (Build a Base and Steal) argues five traits drive virality: clickable core fantasy, onboarding that builds emotional investment one mechanic at a time, social/friend-driven loops, simple mechanics with depth that work on mobile, and clippable community-driven design. Claims a game with all five should reach at least 10K CCU.

## Tips
- [0:43](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=43) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Build a clickable core fantasy a kid would imagine at bedtime (e.g. 'hospital run by animals', 'go to the moon'); the idea should be clear from the thumbnail alone.
- [4:15](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=255) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Explain why the player should care before the action: show the threat or stakes (e.g. pet gets stolen in a cutscene) before the build tutorial.
- [5:37](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=337) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Introduce one mechanic at a time; tutorial completion alone is not enough, check whether players are emotionally invested.
- [6:19](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=379) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Use level design to establish threat immediately on spawn (e.g. Escape Tsunami Brain Rots shows tsunamis in first 5 seconds).
- [7:41](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=461) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Minimize UI during the tutorial; delay HUD, starter packs and paid offers until the player is invested (Sell Lemons example).
- [8:24](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=504) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check Engagement > New user first session retention to diagnose onboarding; a healthy target in his example was ~95% at 30s, ~87% at 1 min, ~65% at 5 min.
- [9:05](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=545) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track every micro-step in Funnels (not one chunked step) and slice by device; high tutorial completion can hide poor console or mobile friction.
- [10:27](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=627) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Play your game on mobile and console to find friction; mobile tap friction and unclear controls kill onboarding.
- [11:09](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=669) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Study top games for why they did something and copy the functionality, not the style or the exact game.
- [12:21](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=741) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Make the game much more fun with friends (aim for 10x) and put interaction with other players inside the core loop so friends invite friends.
- [13:02](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=782) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Use leaderboards and visible fast-moving players (FOMO, envy) to create social pressure; cosmetics also work as social flex.
- [13:46](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=826) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Pick one simple, easy-to-learn core mechanic with depth (e.g. voxel building in Build a Plane) so players create varied content.
- [14:27](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=867) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make every session feel different (new role, new build, new threat) to keep replay fresh; PvP can work well on this basis.
- [15:08](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=908) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make the core mechanic easy on mobile; tap-heavy building cost him a large share of CCU potential in Build a Base and Steal.
- [16:30](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=990) **[[community-and-discord]]** Design for clippability: ask what moments a content creator would film and make those moments happen in the core loop (hiding spots, painting, cool bases).
- [17:10](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=1030) **[[community-and-discord]]** Watch creator videos of your game and note which moments they choose to show, then reinforce those moments in design.
- [17:52](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=1072) **[[community-and-discord]]** Nurture a strong community with a recognized community creator (he credits Blaze in his game's community).
- [18:34](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg?t=1114) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Integrate all five traits; he claims this makes at least 10K CCU likely, though this is an unsupported assertion.

## Games
- **Animal Hospital**: Clear fantasy: 'what if a hospital was run by animals'; recently blew up.
- **Escape Tsunami For Brain Rots**: Level design shows tsunami threat in first 5 seconds, establishing stakes immediately.
- **Sell Lemons**: No UI during tutorial, one mechanic at a time, delays shop and starter packs until players are invested.
- **Build a Base and Steal**: Creator's game; cutscene shows pet stolen before tutorial; voxel building clunky on mobile.
- **Farmer Die**: Frame core mechanic (curing survivors) as a reason to care, not just instructions.
- **Build a Plane**: Simple voxel building with depth; strong TikTok community sharing builds.
- **Brookhaven**: Role-play where each session is different; fresh every login.
- **Build a Boat for Treasure**: Strong community and clippable moments; creator watches videos to see what content creators film.
- **99 Nights in the Forest**: Far more fun with friends; social loop drives word of mouth.
- **Mecha Chameleon**: Clippable hiding and painting moments; spawned clones (Painter Die, Paint n Seek).
- **Steal a Brain Rot**: Base already built, so mobile UX is simple and easy to use.
- **Missiles vs. Cities**: Player interaction built into core loop from initial design.
