# Roblox Game Monetization Explained Like You're 5

[Watch](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets) | Tizzy | 26,253 views | 2026-06-15

## Summary
Creator walks through how his tycoon (inspired by Oil Empire) grew from ~1k CCU and ~$6k/mo to ~2.5k CCU and ~$15k/mo. Method: sort dev products by sales, observe AFK play, read feedback, copy genre mechanics, then sell consumables at high-traffic spots that solve a pain. Check D1, D7, session time after each change.

## Tips
- [0:50](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=50) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Sort your dev products by sales in descending order, then add higher tiers above your best seller (e.g. +30 and +100 speed on top of +10).
- [1:45](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=105) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Play your own game and watch how players actually play; they may not use it as intended (he saw many AFK players).
- [2:25](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=145) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Be careful with gamepasses like auto-collect; they can wreck the game's economy. He chose not to add one.
- [3:06](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=186) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Check the audience feedback section for complaints; repeated 'I'm tired of being stolen from' pointed to a friction point.
- [3:46](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=226) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Borrow proven mechanics from other games in your genre; he added a base-lock feature from Stealing Brains-style games that Oil Empire lacked.
- [4:02](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=242) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Sell repeatable consumables as dev products, not gamepasses. A 24-hour base lock became his top seller within 2 days.
- [4:42](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=282) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track 7-day spend days per user (Acquisition > Home wrecks). Consumables raise it because players can buy again each day.
- [5:24](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=324) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Turn 2x offline earnings from a gamepass into a consumable; one-time passes cap repeat spend, consumables recur daily.
- [6:05](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=365) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Copy competitor monetization patterns you see repeated (e.g. Sell Lemons' 2x offline earnings), then test them in your game.
- [6:52](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=412) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Add multiple purchase pathways to the same product: HUD shop icon, a plus button beside cash, and in-world prompts. He said more pathways is usually better.
- [8:15](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=495) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** When a player can't afford an item, show the error first; on the third attempt open the cash shop directly. Opening it immediately was advised against.
- [8:15](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=495) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Before adding a product, check how it affects other stats and your progression economy so it doesn't break long-term balance.
- [10:20](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=620) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Place dev products at points of interest where players walk most, like the base entrance, not in a rarely visited corner.
- [11:27](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=687) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Make products solve a pain: grinding (speed, cash), theft while AFK (base lock), missing out (offline earnings), or looking casual (cosmetics).
- [12:51](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=771) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use limited stock on cosmetics (e.g. 100 servers, 150 antennas); he says it drives his best-selling antennas and servers.
- [13:31](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=811) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Show the offer at the moment of need, e.g. the 2x offline earnings prompt when the player collects offline earnings.
- [13:50](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=830) **[[pricing]]** Keep impulse items cheap (19 Robux for 2x offline earnings), but avoid very low prices like 9 Robux on cash that undercut your economy.
- [14:12](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=852) **[[analytics-metrics]]** After each change, check D1, D7, session time and first-time experience. Keep it if it helps; revert if it hurts playtime or retention.
- [14:12](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=852) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Run a loop: research, form hypothesis, launch update, check results, keep or revert. Repeat it continuously.

## Games
- **Oil Empire**: Inspiration for this game; its gas-stealing from refineries is a mechanic. It lacks a base lock, so it was borrowed from another game.
- **Sell Lemons**: Uses 2x offline earnings as a consumable dev product, not a gamepass; likely its best-selling dev product.
- **Stealing Brains (as named in transcript)**: Base lock every ~60 seconds protects players from theft; the creator borrowed this mechanic for his game.
