## Tips
- [0:37](https://youtu.be/az7em1IoBhA?t=37) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make emotions clearly visible on faces; the speaker says devs often overlook exaggerated expressions like smug villains or sleeping victims.
- [1:16](https://youtu.be/az7em1IoBhA?t=76) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Tease the unique mechanic in the thumbnail or title, especially in sequels, so players see what makes the game different.
- [2:39](https://youtu.be/az7em1IoBhA?t=159) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Let the thumbnail give context to the title and show the implied challenge and main threat/character clearly.
- [4:04](https://youtu.be/az7em1IoBhA?t=244) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Keep to one or two characters per thumbnail, with a simple background and high color contrast, so it reads instantly.
- [5:33](https://youtu.be/az7em1IoBhA?t=333) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Check thumbnails on mobile before publishing; the common mistake is text that looks fine on desktop but is unreadable on phone. Use qptr.io to preview.
- [6:38](https://youtu.be/az7em1IoBhA?t=398) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Test entirely new concepts (before/after, noob vs pro, first-person, one character plus action) rather than small tweaks.
- [8:01](https://youtu.be/az7em1IoBhA?t=481) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Expect QPTR to decline as impressions grow; refresh creatives when it drops, testing at least two or three thumbnails a week.
- [8:43](https://youtu.be/az7em1IoBhA?t=523) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make the thumbnail communicate the feeling the player will have (power, intensity, dominance), not just the scene.
- [11:17](https://youtu.be/az7em1IoBhA?t=677) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Research by searching the verb keyword in CreatorExchange, sorting by visits, and noting recurring visual themes in top games.
- [13:13](https://youtu.be/az7em1IoBhA?t=793) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Mock up the concept in Canva, then generate with an AI image tool; iterate, and start a new chat when the model keeps repeating errors.
- [14:35](https://youtu.be/az7em1IoBhA?t=875) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Enlarge logos and key text so they stay readable at mobile size; the speaker found the logo was unreadable on phone until he asked for it bigger.
- [17:19](https://youtu.be/az7em1IoBhA?t=1039) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Remove busy background elements like clouds and extra noise; the speaker says visual clutter hurts mobile readability.
