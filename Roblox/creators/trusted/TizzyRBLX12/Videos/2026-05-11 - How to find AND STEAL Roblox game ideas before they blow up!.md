# How to find AND STEAL Roblox game ideas before they blow up!

[Watch](https://youtu.be/BNdHkeKRUdE) | Tizzy | 36,857 views | 2026-05-11

## Summary
Shows how to find small, recently released Roblox games with high CCU relative to visits on RoTrends, then study and adapt their core loop. Uses Farmer Die (copied from Alien Ranch) as the example, stressing playing deeply, checking progression, and targeting neglected games with bugs.

## Tips
- [0:21](https://youtu.be/BNdHkeKRUdE?t=21) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Use RoTrends top-moving games sorted by visits descending; look for high CCU relative to visits and recent creation dates.
- [3:05](https://youtu.be/BNdHkeKRUdE?t=185) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** As a new dev, aim for a first small win that lasts months rather than a viral breakout hit.
- [3:57](https://youtu.be/BNdHkeKRUdE?t=237) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Avoid copying big front-page games; they tend to die within a month. Study smaller games with steady player bases instead.
- [5:19](https://youtu.be/BNdHkeKRUdE?t=319) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Favour genres that are easy to build quickly, such as obbies, simulators and tycoons, especially with AI assistance.
- [6:16](https://youtu.be/BNdHkeKRUdE?t=376) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Check a candidate game's 90-day CCU chart and creation date; target games trending up just before a peak, like buying before a spike.
- [7:38](https://youtu.be/BNdHkeKRUdE?t=458) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Look for games with a good core loop and strong-but-small player base that have many bug complaints and no recent updates; this is an underserved gap.
- [8:35](https://youtu.be/BNdHkeKRUdE?t=515) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Check update cadence on RoTrends to spot games whose developer stopped updating.
- [9:17](https://youtu.be/BNdHkeKRUdE?t=557) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Copy the proven core loop, fix bugs, then add a twist of your own mechanics to differentiate from the original.
- [10:01](https://youtu.be/BNdHkeKRUdE?t=601) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Study the core loop, progression, D1/D7 retention hooks, then monetisation and player feedback before copying a game.
- [10:41](https://youtu.be/BNdHkeKRUdE?t=641) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add a progression or class system as a retention hook; the speaker says survival games commonly use classes for this.
- [10:41](https://youtu.be/BNdHkeKRUdE?t=641) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Play the target game for about 2 hours, not 5 minutes, to see the content and where players drop off.
