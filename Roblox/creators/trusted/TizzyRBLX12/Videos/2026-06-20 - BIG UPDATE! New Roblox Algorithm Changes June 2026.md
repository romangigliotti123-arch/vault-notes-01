# BIG UPDATE! New Roblox Algorithm Changes June 2026

[Watch](https://youtu.be/pu2o80prNV8) | Tizzy | 21,809 views | 2026-06-20

## Summary
Explains Roblox's June 2026 algorithm/metric changes: QPTR replaced by Play Through Rate and First Play Bounce Rate (lower is better), D28 now measured. Frames each game as a funnel and says to fix the biggest bottleneck: packaging for PTR, onboarding for D1, content/progression for D7/D28. Also covers clickbait and clone penalties and A/B testing via Experiments.

## Tips
- [0:52](https://youtu.be/pu2o80prNV8?t=52) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** QPTR was split into Play Through Rate (higher is better) and First Play Bounce Rate (lower is better, measured in first 60s and 61-180s).

- [5:06](https://youtu.be/pu2o80prNV8?t=306) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't ship clickbait titles like 'Mysterious Game' over zero-CCU clones; bounce rate now kills that exploit.

- [8:43](https://youtu.be/pu2o80prNV8?t=523) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Treat the game as a funnel: impression, PTR, bounce, D1, D7, D28; find the biggest leak and fix that stage first.
