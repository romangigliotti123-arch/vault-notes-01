# The Roblox Algorithm Explained Like You're 5 Years Old

[Watch](https://youtu.be/inWzxY26NXo) | Tizzy | 36,943 views | 2026-05-16

## Summary
Explains how Roblox's home recommendation algorithm works: ads supply initial cold-traffic data, Roblox ranks games against their genre and then against "experiences with similar players", and a 7-day rolling window drives ranking. Recommends beta mode during optimisation and improving stats before running more ads.

## Tips
- [1:39](https://youtu.be/inWzxY26NXo?t=99) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Check Analytics > Acquisition > Home Recommendations benchmarks against 'experiences with similar players', not the overview, to see your real competitive position.
- [3:40](https://youtu.be/inWzxY26NXo?t=220) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Turn on Beta mode in Settings to keep your game out of home recommendations while you optimise stats with ad traffic only.
- [12:12](https://youtu.be/inWzxY26NXo?t=732) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Don't run more ads on a dying game without stat improvements; sponsored ad traffic doesn't push you up the home algorithm on its own.
- [16:02](https://youtu.be/inWzxY26NXo?t=962) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Loop: run ads, find your worst stat (e.g. D1), ship a meaningful update fixing it, then run ads again for fresh initial data.
- [17:23](https://youtu.be/inWzxY26NXo?t=1043) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Don't keep restarting ads once on algo; Roblox uses a rolling 7-day data window, so big updates take a week to show fully.
- [4:45](https://youtu.be/inWzxY26NXo?t=285) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Expect low stats from cold ad traffic; ads optimise for the cheapest player, not the most qualified, so don't panic over bot-like lobbies.
- [7:27](https://youtu.be/inWzxY26NXo?t=447) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't judge your game on green overview stats early; they're compared against the whole genre and fall once you're benchmarked against similar-audience games.
