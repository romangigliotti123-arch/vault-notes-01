## Summary
Creator (co-owner of Hack a Business) explains a hypothesis-driven iterative update cycle for improving Roblox stats (avg playtime, D1, D7, payer conversion, ARPPU, QPTR), using funnels, surveys, Discord feedback and competitor research before shipping changes.

## Tips
- [0:42](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=42) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Ship frequent small updates; Hack a Business dev updated daily, sometimes twice a day, and followed the data.
- [0:18](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=18) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add sound and visual effects plus camera shake to every core action; action-and-response feel drives average playtime.
- [2:04](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=124) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Track tutorial step completion with Analytics > Funnels to find where new players drop off.
- [2:45](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=165) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Show, don't tell: let players act and see cause and effect instead of text explanations.
- [3:27](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=207) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Sell a cheap repeatable consumable, e.g. offer to double offline earnings for 19 Robux when collecting; drastically raised payer conversion.
- [4:08](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=248) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Offer three gamepass tiers (cheap starter, mid pro, expensive top tier for whales) to lift average revenue per paying user.
- [3:27](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=207) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Use admin abuses and events plus social flex elements to help D7 retention.
- [4:43](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=283) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Treat every update as a hypothesis; form it as 'if I change X, stat Y will improve' before shipping.
- [9:08](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=548) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Cross-reference qualitative data, quantitative stats and competitor research to strengthen each hypothesis before launching.
- [10:05](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=605) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Read Discord suggestions and watch players live in-game; the speaker calls live observation his favourite data source.
- [12:07](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=727) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Check the New User First Session Retention curve in Analytics > Engagement for the drop-off point, then replay your tutorial with a timer to find the cause.
- [15:33](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=933) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** After each update check the metric; if it did not improve, revert and form a new hypothesis.
- [4:43](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=283) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't launch updates willy-nilly or hotfix without research; each change is an educated guess, not a certainty.
- [3:27](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=207) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Consider a starter pack as the easiest quick win for payer conversion.
- [4:08](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=248) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Packaging (thumbnail and title) is the QPTR lever; the speaker says this is his marketing specialty and has made videos on it.
