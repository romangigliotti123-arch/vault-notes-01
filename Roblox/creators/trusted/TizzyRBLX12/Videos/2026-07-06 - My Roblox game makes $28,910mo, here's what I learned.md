# My Roblox game makes $28,910/mo, here's what I learned...

[Watch](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU) | Tizzy | 60,740 views | 2026-07-06

## Summary
Creator of Hack A Business (Roblox, ~$28.9K/mo Robux earnings, started Sept 2025) shares seven mindset and process lessons: hire via sample tasks, ship fast, playtest, network with value, test many thumbnails, focus on one weak stat, and cross-reference data before updates. Mostly motivational with some concrete tactics.

## Tips
- [3:11](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU?t=191) **[[team-and-budget]]** Before hiring a modeler or dev, give a paid-style sample task; judge quality and speed from the sample before committing.
- [4:21](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU?t=261) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Ship updates fast: in the first week of launch, update daily or twice daily, forming hypotheses from data and iterating quickly.
- [4:46](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU?t=286) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Keep a separate test place and send its link to playtesters; if you lack testers, hire them via the QA Central Discord server.
- [4:46](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU?t=286) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Play your own game heavily; if you're not top of your own game's playtime leaderboard, you aren't testing enough.
- [5:33](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU?t=333) **[[community-and-discord]]** When networking with top devs, offer something of value (paid consultation, bug reports, play their game) rather than asking for free reviews.
- [7:26](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU?t=446) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Test many thumbnails: ads campaigns allow 25 thumbnails each; run multiple campaigns with different thumbnail sets and compare CTR.
- [7:26](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU?t=446) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use AI to produce thumbnail variants quickly, since volume testing beats one-off polished art.
- [9:17](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU?t=557) **[[analytics-metrics]]** When a stat is weak, focus on that single stat until it improves; the speaker's bounce rate drop from ~21% to ~15% roughly doubled impressions and lifted CCU.
- [11:37](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU?t=697) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Expect about 24 hours for most data to update, sometimes up to 3 days early in launch; don't judge an update on minutes of data.
- [12:18](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU?t=738) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Cross-reference multiple data sources (competitor research, onboarding funnel drop-off, engagement, first-session retention) before shipping an update.
- [13:39](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU?t=819) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't spend months polishing before launch; launch and get real data, since the real work starts once players arrive.
- [13:39](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU?t=819) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Do competitor research and market analysis to find a gap before building; a copied core loop with a small map change reached 10K CCU for a competitor.
- [9:17](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU?t=557) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Treat underperformance as your responsibility: set the quality standard and fix training, hiring, or standards rather than blaming the team.
