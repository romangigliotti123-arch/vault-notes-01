# The #1 Reason Nobody Finds Your Roblox Game (How To Get Players)

## Summary
Explains why good Roblox games get no players: market saturation (first-to-market, clones, red ocean), and how to find a "purple ocean" niche with high demand but poor execution. Uses Don't Wake/Don't Steal clones, fishing and airport examples, and Roblox documentation on unique metadata and discovery.

## Tips
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/dUmh6GAvJ-E?t=81) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't use templates or clone existing hits; Roblox docs say near-duplicate experiences with similar metadata/place files rank lower in recommendations and search.
- [1:54](https://youtu.be/dUmh6GAvJ-E?t=114) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Use original titles and imagery; avoid repetitive titles and thumbnails previously published, per Roblox best-practices for discovery.
- [2:35](https://youtu.be/dUmh6GAvJ-E?t=155) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make thumbnails original rather than copying a successful creator's style; the speaker's original thumbnail outperformed the inspired one.
- [5:05](https://youtu.be/dUmh6GAvJ-E?t=305) **[[titles-descriptions]]** When a clone trend saturates, change the title and thumbnail while keeping the core loop; 'Don't Steal the Bobo' kept ~9.5K CCU after clones died.
- [7:29](https://youtu.be/dUmh6GAvJ-E?t=449) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Before building, search the genre keyword on Creator Exchange and check how saturated it is; identify whether you're in a blue, red, or purple ocean.
- [8:09](https://youtu.be/dUmh6GAvJ-E?t=489) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** In saturated genres, add a meaningful twist to the fantasy (ice fishing, cage fishing) rather than a generic version of the same theme.
- [12:18](https://youtu.be/dUmh6GAvJ-E?t=738) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Target a 'purple ocean': high player demand but poor execution in existing games; play top games and make a version far better.
- [12:52](https://youtu.be/dUmh6GAvJ-E?t=772) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Favor ideas that are clickable/relatable or have a passionate existing audience, such as underserved adult audiences that other platforms don't serve.
- [11:36](https://youtu.be/dUmh6GAvJ-E?t=696) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Scan a fantasy's search results for dominant games; if only one game executes well in a high-interest fantasy (e.g. firefighters, airport), that is an opening.
