# Tizzy - How to make successful Roblox games

Tizzy says hits come from execution on proven ideas: find small, recently released games with high CCU relative to visits (RoTrends, Creator Exchange, off-platform demand), copy the proven core loop and change about 20%, and ship an MVP fast with reliable T-shaped devs. Package it with a clear core fantasy, an original thumbnail and a simple title. Run about $16/day ads to seed data, then fix the weakest funnel stat (PTR, bounce, D1, D7, D28) with hypothesis-driven weekly updates. Monetise with consumables and tiers, and keep live ops going with community and competitor research.

**Style:** Systematic, data-driven and framework-heavy. Explains concepts "like you're 5" with named tools such as RoTrends, Creator Exchange and QPTR.io. Draws on his own hits (Hack a Business, Build a Base and Steal). Later videos turn into mindset and accountability talk. Suits producers and team leads more than solo coders.

## Playbook
1. **Find rising small games** [[trend-research-and-validation]]: Use RoTrends top-moving games sorted by visits. Look for high CCU relative to visits, recent creation dates and a 90-day uptrend. Avoid front-page giants, which die within a month. Neglected games with bugs and no updates are gaps.
2. **Validate demand off-platform** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Check whether the idea already worked elsewhere: CrazyGames votes and views, Flash sites, Steam wishlists, TikTok challenges, mobile hits, YouTuber coverage, Creator Exchange genre gaps. Prefer readable mechanics already proven on Roblox. Look for a purple-ocean niche: high demand, poor execution.
3. **Study games to watch** [[playtesting-and-feedback]]: Play a candidate for about 2 hours. Write a hypothesis on first-time experience, core loop and progression before checking data. Recheck CCU 1-2 weeks later to score yourself and build intuition.
4. **Design by changing ~20%** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: Break hits into fantasy, unique mechanic, core loop, progression, tension and level design. Copy the proven loop, fix its bugs, then change roughly 20% in areas that matter. Make it simple with depth, clippable, much better with friends, and mobile-friendly.
5. **Hire and scope an MVP** [[team-and-budget]]: Hire T-shaped devs by Twitter/Discord reputation, and give a sample task first. Use an SOW contract with pay on completion. Write a game design doc and track tasks in Trello. Build art before the programmer. Prototype with AI, and do two or three backup iterations before returning to the programmer.
6. **Package: title and thumbnail** [[thumbnails-icons]]: Define the core fantasy. Write a keyword-researched title at a third-grade reading level. Make thumbnails with readable emotion, a teased mechanic, one or two characters and high contrast. Test 10-20 for ads, then 2-3 new ones per week. The first 60 seconds must match the promise.
7. **Onboarding** [[first-session-onboarding]]: Show the goal, progression and curiosity loop within 60-120 seconds. Lead with the most engaging mechanic so players act within 10 seconds. Use contextual tutorials and show rather than tell. Track funnels, and test on mobile and with non-gamers.
8. **Launch with ads** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Use Beta mode while optimising. Run sponsored ads at $16/day for at least 3 days, ideally a week, with All Players Recommended. Keep them running until home recommendation impressions exceed ad impressions. For the 500 highly engaged player gate, expect about 11 days and about $180.
9. **Read the algorithm and metrics** [[discovery-algorithm]]: Roblox ranks against genre and then against similar players, using a 7-day rolling window. Treat the game as a funnel. PTR is fixed by packaging, bounce rate and D1 by onboarding, and D7/D28 by content and progression. More ads do not fix bad stats.
10. **Monetise with consumables** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: Sort dev products by sales and add tiers above the best seller. Sell repeatable consumables at pain points, such as 2x offline earnings for 19 Robux or a 24-hour base lock. Add three gamepass tiers and a starter pack. Use limited-stock cosmetics and rolls with a teaser. Avoid gamepasses that break the economy.
11. **Hypothesis-driven updates** [[updates-live-ops]]: Find the worst stat. Cross-reference Discord, surveys and live observation (qualitative), funnels and home-rec data (quantitative), and competitors. Write 'if I change X, stat Y improves'. Ship, rerun ads for fresh data, and use Experiments for A/B tests.
12. **Live ops cadence** [[updates-live-ops]]: Ship patches daily as needed. Ship planned updates weekly (Mon-Sun), later biweekly. Playtest internally and with 8-10 core players. Add content when players finish too fast, and use a dedicated test place.
13. **Mindset** [[mindset-and-strategy]]: Ideas are cheap and execution wins. Ship fast, take accountability instead of blaming the algorithm, ads or devs, and focus on one weak stat at a time. Network by offering value.

## Key numbers
- Ads: $16/day, at least 3 days (ideally a week); stop when home-rec impressions exceed ad impressions
- 500 highly engaged players gate: about 11 days and about $180 at $16/day; highly engaged = a purchase anywhere on Roblox in the last 60 days
- Thumbnails: launch 10-20; test 2-3 new per week; 10-15 min each
- Clarity window: goal, progression and curiosity loop within 60-120 seconds
- Roblox ranking uses a 7-day rolling window; stats update about every 3 days
- CTR 1.54% to 3.27% after renaming 'Sell Your Data' to 'Hack a Business'
- Hack a Business: about 1k to 2.5k CCU, about $6k to $15k/mo after monetisation changes
- 2x offline earnings consumable: 19 Robux; limited drops of 100 servers or 150 antennas
- Build a Base and Steal: 13.1k CCU and about $25k/mo at 2 weeks; later 38K CCU and about $119K/mo, then about $131K/mo
- Roll tease raised PCR from 0.3% to 0.75%
- About 80% of Roblox players are on mobile
- First Play Bounce Rate windows: within 60s and 61-180s; Hack a Business about 20-22% (too high)
- Hack a Business time to complete: about 2.5 hours; tutorial completion 77%
- Hack a Business D1 16.09%, D7 1.45%, playtime 20.5 min, payer conversion 0.82%
- Hiring: at least 30 mutual Discord connections for top devs; 2-3 backup iterations before returning to the programmer

## Common mistakes
- Copying big front-page games that die within a month
- Copying clones or templates with near-duplicate metadata, which rank lower
- Clickbait titles or thumbnails that cause high bounce rate
- Running more ads on a game with bad stats and expecting the algorithm to lift it
- Judging green overview stats early, before benchmarking against similar players
- Restarting ads repeatedly instead of waiting out the 7-day window
- Paying devs upfront in full, hiring without a contract or sample task, or thinking money alone buys a hit ($4,000 game never launched)
- Hotfixing or updating without research or a hypothesis
- Never testing on mobile or with non-gamers, and ignoring small friction
- Gamepasses like auto-collect that wreck the economy, prices like 9 Robux that undercut it, and blaming the algorithm, ads or devs instead of fixing the weakest funnel stage

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Idea validated by RoTrends and off-platform demand signals
- [ ] Clear core fantasy, with a unique mechanic that differs about 20% from the reference
- [ ] Game design doc, SOW and Trello board in place
- [ ] MVP with core loop and basic progression, playtested
- [ ] Mobile UX checked, including with a non-gamer
- [ ] Tutorial funnel tracked; goal and curiosity loop clear within 60-120 seconds
- [ ] Consumables, three gamepass tiers and a starter pack ready
- [ ] Keyword-researched title at third-grade reading level
- [ ] 10-20 original thumbnails ready, previewed on mobile (QPTR.io)
- [ ] Beta mode on while optimising
- [ ] Ads set to $16/day, All Players Recommended
- [ ] Plan for the 500 highly engaged player gate
- [ ] Discord set up with a core playtester group
- [ ] Metrics baseline recorded (PTR, bounce, D1, D7, D28, playtime, conversion)
- [ ] Competitor games and monetisation patterns noted
- [ ] Test place set up for updates

## How the advice changed over time
- May 2026 advice centred on spotting ideas and copying loops (RoTrends, off-platform sources). June to July shifted to stat-driven iteration and monetisation. From June 2026, QPTR was replaced by Play Through Rate and First Play Bounce Rate, and D28 was added; prefer this newer framing over QPTR-based advice.
- From May 19 2026, the 500 highly engaged player gate means ads are needed at launch. Earlier advice was about $100-120 of ads to reach the algorithm (about $200 for Farm and Die); the later figure is about $180 over 11 days.
- Later videos (Sept to Oct) lean toward mindset and post-mortems, covering onboarding friction, funnels and whale sinks, with less tactical detail.
