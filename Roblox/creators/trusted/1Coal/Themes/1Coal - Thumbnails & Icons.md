## [[thumbnails-icons]]
- 2023-05-07 [4:02](https://youtu.be/JzB8fpyofnw?t=242) Use blue and green in icons and thumbnails; the speaker claims they tend to get higher click-through rates. ([[2023-05-07 - I made a dead game get 20 million visits|I made a dead game get 20 million visits...]])

- 2023-08-25 [2:36](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=156) Make the title and icon unique and intriguing; avoid generic names like 'Prison Obby' or 'House Tycoon' that copy every other game. ([[2023-08-25 - Your roblox game has 0 players, here's why|Your roblox game has 0 players, here's why...]])
