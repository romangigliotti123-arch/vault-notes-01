# I made a viral roblox game to prove it's not luck…

[Watch](https://youtu.be/gWNOANEPYLY) | 1Coal | 386,212 views | 2024-03-14

## Summary
A Roblox developer's brother built "Baked a Baby" (a chef-vs-babies asymmetric game) after a prototype stalled. The video covers the pivot to a restaurant/oven theme, quick gameplay tweaks, icon CTR testing with ads, and a TikTok push that triggered Roblox recommendations and rapid growth.

## Tips
- [0:52](https://youtu.be/gWNOANEPYLY?t=52) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Get a working prototype out fast, then judge whether it's fun by playtesting; a base plate test felt off and the project was nearly shelved.
- [1:52](https://youtu.be/gWNOANEPYLY?t=112) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Build on your own expertise: the restaurant map was easy because the developer already ran a large roleplay restaurant game.
- [2:25](https://youtu.be/gWNOANEPYLY?t=145) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Add a time-limited starter pack (79 Robux, 15-minute window) to convert players; revenue roughly doubled to tripled after adding it.
- [2:54](https://youtu.be/gWNOANEPYLY?t=174) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Playtest heavily and list many small fixes: visible damage numbers, self-heal ability, teammate/enemy radar, 10-second countdown label, shorter rounds.
- [3:36](https://youtu.be/gWNOANEPYLY?t=216) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make rules obvious: if new players are confused about what to do or what just happened, add small in-game cues that explain it.
- [3:36](https://youtu.be/gWNOANEPYLY?t=216) **[[common-mistakes]]** Cut shop items players won't buy and re-price the rest; a bloated shop dilutes purchases.
- [3:44](https://youtu.be/gWNOANEPYLY?t=224) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give the hunted side a head start (3 seconds) so the asymmetric round feels fair.
- [4:15](https://youtu.be/gWNOANEPYLY?t=255) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Order several icons and A/B test them with about $20 of ads; benchmark CTR: above 3% is good, above 4% is excellent.
- [4:15](https://youtu.be/gWNOANEPYLY?t=255) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Rewrite the description shorter and simpler; keep it to what a player needs to decide to click.
- [4:50](https://youtu.be/gWNOANEPYLY?t=290) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Set the genre category deliberately; Roblox can feature games under that genre's front-page section if they grow.
- [4:50](https://youtu.be/gWNOANEPYLY?t=290) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Paid ads alone plateaued (about $50/day gave the same players); pair them with organic virality.
- [6:10](https://youtu.be/gWNOANEPYLY?t=370) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Pay a TikTok creator (about $400 for the video); one video reached 150k views in an hour and 4M total, driving sustained players.
- [6:58](https://youtu.be/gWNOANEPYLY?t=418) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Early player spikes from external traffic give the Roblox recommendation algorithm enough engagement data to start recommending the game.
