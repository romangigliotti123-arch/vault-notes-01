## Tips
- [0:20](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=20) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Start with a small game like an Obby or tycoon you can finish in a few weeks; ambitious clones of big games take years and large budgets.
- [1:04](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=64) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Apply the 'Xbox test': would a kid's friends keep watching instead of scrolling TikTok? If not, the game doesn't look fun enough.
- [1:04](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=64) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add real stakes such as losing loot or dying, since games without stakes or failure states feel boring to watch and play.
- [1:41](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=101) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Leave room for player choice and self-made stories, like customizable houses or roleplay jobs, rather than a forced linear sequence.
- [2:21](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=141) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Keep the idea simple enough to explain in one or two sentences; overly combined concepts make development hard.
- [2:36](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=156) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make the title and icon unique and intriguing; avoid generic names like 'Prison Obby' or 'House Tycoon' that copy every other game.
- [3:16](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=196) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Use social arbitrage: spot real-life trends (e.g. fidget spinners) and build a game around them quickly.
- [3:16](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=196) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Sort popular gaming YouTube channels' videos by most popular to find proven concepts, then make a similar game.
- [3:53](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=233) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Check App Store trending games and Fortnite's Browse tab; ideas trending there often work on Roblox too.
- [4:08](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=248) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Build games around current popular shows, films or creator uploads (e.g. Wednesday, new MrBeast videos) to tap existing interest.
- [4:49](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=289) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Base ideas on things people love or hate in real life (school, dentist, natural disasters, hospitals, police), as many big Roblox games do.
- [5:06](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=306) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Turn childhood recess games (hide and seek, Simon Says, freeze tag, floor is lava) into games since players already know the rules.
