# I made a dead game get 20 million visits...

[Watch](https://youtu.be/JzB8fpyofnw) | 1Coal | 364,829 views | 2023-05-07

## Summary
Roblox developer reviews a struggling game (wheel drawing obby) sent by a developer, suggests UI cleanup, green/blue icon and thumbnail color, a progress bar, player-progress tracker, and timed rewards. After the changes the game went from a few hundred to ~2,100 CCU with no ad spend, which he attributes to the changes and icon.

## Tips
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/JzB8fpyofnw?t=0) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Look at successful games on other platforms (e.g. iOS Scribble Rider) for proven mechanics to adapt to Roblox.
- [1:20](https://youtu.be/JzB8fpyofnw?t=80) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Prompt a gamepass purchase at the moment the player runs out of a resource (e.g. 2x drawing power when fuel runs out).
- [1:20](https://youtu.be/JzB8fpyofnw?t=80) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** In obbies, offer a skip-stage button; the speaker says this is where most obby money comes from.
- [2:01](https://youtu.be/JzB8fpyofnw?t=121) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make boost items clearly labeled as part of the core system so players know they can use them to improve their run.
- [2:01](https://youtu.be/JzB8fpyofnw?t=121) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Show a live player-progress tracker of other players on the server to add competition, as Tower of Hell does.
- [2:41](https://youtu.be/JzB8fpyofnw?t=161) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Replace text progress with a large progress bar and make side panels collapsible or smaller so gameplay fills the screen.
- [2:41](https://youtu.be/JzB8fpyofnw?t=161) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Give each stage a distinct color instead of uniform gray/sand to reduce boredom.
- [3:21](https://youtu.be/JzB8fpyofnw?t=201) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give timed free rewards (e.g. a boost at 3, 6, 9 minutes) so longer play yields better rewards and boosts feel more valuable than a 30-second timer.
- [4:02](https://youtu.be/JzB8fpyofnw?t=242) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use blue and green in icons and thumbnails; the speaker claims they tend to get higher click-through rates.
- [4:02](https://youtu.be/JzB8fpyofnw?t=242) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make the game icon show the core mechanic in a fun, distinctive way rather than a plain generic shape.
- [4:43](https://youtu.be/JzB8fpyofnw?t=283) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Audit the game against successful front-page games and give the developer a detailed written change list.
