# How and Why I made Lebron Clicker

[Watch](https://youtu.be/USJC5l_wpuY) | callmedogey | 20,682 views | 2025-01-15

## Summary
Creator (MSO5) explains how and why he made LeBron Clicker: a simulator-generator plugin base, free assets, ~$40 ad spend that reinvested Robux into ads, and revenue breakdown (3.2M Robux total, ~$11k USD cashed out). Covers dev product pricing placement and premium payouts.

## Tips
- [3:14](https://youtu.be/USJC5l_wpuY?t=194) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Use a clicker-generator Studio plugin (Simulator Generator) to get a playable base quickly; he built it with no coding.
- [2:34](https://youtu.be/USJC5l_wpuY?t=154) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Free Toolbox assets can carry a map; he built a 99%-free-asset map and still earned money without scripting or building skill.
- [4:37](https://youtu.be/USJC5l_wpuY?t=277) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Clicker core loop: click for money, buy pets, unlock new zones. Keep it simple and playable before polishing.
- [10:14](https://youtu.be/USJC5l_wpuY?t=614) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Place cheap-to-expensive pet developer products right at spawn so players see them immediately; that drove most repeat purchases.
- [10:14](https://youtu.be/USJC5l_wpuY?t=614) **[[pricing]]** Price developer products across spending levels (cheap to ~10,000 Robux) so both low and high spenders have an option.
- [10:14](https://youtu.be/USJC5l_wpuY?t=614) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Developer products out-earned game passes for him (1.9M vs 841k Robux) because players buy pet products multiple times.
- [10:56](https://youtu.be/USJC5l_wpuY?t=656) **[[monetisation-other]]** Premium payouts are significant: players with Premium who never spend still generate income (~1,500 USD cited).
- [1:54](https://youtu.be/USJC5l_wpuY?t=114) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** He spent ~$40 on ads over two months, reached ~9,000 visits, and reinvested earned Robux into ads, which triggered growth.
- [5:58](https://youtu.be/USJC5l_wpuY?t=358) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Update carefully: bugs after updates caused outages; test updates before pushing, since he was up overnight fixing them.
- [5:58](https://youtu.be/USJC5l_wpuY?t=358) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't rely on one thumbnail or upload without checking moderation; a rejected image got the thumbnail removed and a one-day ban.
- [8:50](https://youtu.be/USJC5l_wpuY?t=530) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Cash out only what you need; he kept Robux to fund ads for other games, exchanging ~2.3M Robux for about $8,000 total.
- [8:08](https://youtu.be/USJC5l_wpuY?t=488) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect big swings: revenue came in resurgences, and most of his games earned far less; he says the big success was an outlier.
