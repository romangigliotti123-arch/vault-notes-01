# I Sold My Roblox Game… BIG Mistake

[Watch](https://youtu.be/ZAfE-Row9tU) | callmedogey | 248,945 views | 2025-05-09

## Summary
Creator explains why selling a percentage of his Roblox game Mega Rolls to a studio was a mistake: the studio offered advice, thumbnails and ads in exchange for equity, then played only 2 minutes, pushed pay-to-win suggestions, switched to AI thumbnails, and gave no help when data issues arose. He argues owning games long-term is better than selling.

## Tips
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/ZAfE-Row9tU?t=40) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Never give a percentage of your game to someone in exchange for advertising or advice; advertising is the easy part and studios often don't deliver it.
- [1:20](https://youtu.be/ZAfE-Row9tU?t=80) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Don't make big decisions about your game early in its life; wait until you understand its real potential.
- [2:01](https://youtu.be/ZAfE-Row9tU?t=121) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** If thumbnail click-through is weak, hire an artist or make thumbnails yourself; aim for at least 0.7% CTR as a small dev.
- [2:42](https://youtu.be/ZAfE-Row9tU?t=162) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Be skeptical of feedback from people who play only 2 minutes; ask for feedback from players who spend significant time in the game.
- [3:23](https://youtu.be/ZAfE-Row9tU?t=203) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Don't add pay-to-win monetisation just because a outside party suggests it; it can disappoint players later.
- [5:25](https://youtu.be/ZAfE-Row9tU?t=325) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Consider keeping games instead of selling: his small unupdated games earn about $30-$50/day passively, which can outlast a one-time sale.
