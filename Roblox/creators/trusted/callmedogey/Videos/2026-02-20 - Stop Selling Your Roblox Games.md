# Stop Selling Your Roblox Games...

[Watch](https://youtu.be/IQm6zE2fKUI) | callmedogey | 46,536 views | 2026-02-20

## Summary
Speaker argues small Roblox devs should not sell games or stakes to studios. Says growth comes from improving the six Roblox benchmark stats (playtime, D1/D7 retention, conversion, ARPU, QPTR) below the 75th percentile, which he claims is enough for algorithm promotion. Warns studios take 20-50% and use scouting "goon" systems and group acquisitions to buy games early.

## Tips
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/IQm6zE2fKUI?t=81) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check the six benchmarks in Creator Hub stats; improve any below the 75th percentile, which the speaker says is the priority over advertising.
- [2:43](https://youtu.be/IQm6zE2fKUI?t=163) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** If playtime is low, make the start quick with a fun loop; if D1/D7 retention is low, add progression and daily rewards.
- [2:43](https://youtu.be/IQm6zE2fKUI?t=163) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** If conversion is low, make in-game purchases effective and visible; if ARPU is low, add more expensive, worthwhile purchases.
- [2:43](https://youtu.be/IQm6zE2fKUI?t=163) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** If qualified playthrough rate is low, make new thumbnails; cut object images from reference, remove backgrounds, add stroke in a free editor.
- [3:24](https://youtu.be/IQm6zE2fKUI?t=204) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Studio buyers typically take at least 20% and often 40-50% of revenue; doing the same improvements yourself keeps that money.
- [4:04](https://youtu.be/IQm6zE2fKUI?t=244) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Read Roblox's official Discovery documentation to understand the recommendation algorithm; compare with high-player games in your genre for ideas.
- [6:06](https://youtu.be/IQm6zE2fKUI?t=366) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Adding more purchases is an optimal way to grow revenue stats; the speaker says Roblox's algorithm factors in money made.
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/IQm6zE2fKUI?t=81) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Advertising is one of the least important steps; prioritise benchmark improvement over ads and YouTuber outreach.
