# 4 Ways to Earn REAL MONEY from Roblox Games

[Watch](https://youtu.be/dtRGRx-2WNg) | AlvinBlox | 46,636 views | 2025-03-22

## Summary
Overview of four ways to earn real money from Roblox development: commissions for other developers, building your own games (with Premium payouts, gamepasses, devproducts), building games to sell to other developers (with Robux price ranges), and selling assets for passive income. Compares the methods by risk and pace and recommends combining them over a career. Mostly motivational, with some scam-avoidance advice.

## Tips
- [1:13](https://youtu.be/dtRGRx-2WNg?t=73) **[[team-and-budget]]** Build a concise portfolio using personal projects, join Roblox dev Discord servers and the DevForum to find commission work, and start with small gigs to build credibility.
- [1:54](https://youtu.be/dtRGRx-2WNg?t=114) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Don't try to rival big hit games like Adopt Me or Brookhaven as a new developer; build compact, focused games finishable in days or weeks.
- [2:36](https://youtu.be/dtRGRx-2WNg?t=156) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Simple, well-executed mechanics tend to win on Roblox; avoid overcomplicated designs.
- [3:16](https://youtu.be/dtRGRx-2WNg?t=196) **[[updates-live-ops]]** A successful game is a long-term commitment; you must keep shipping updates and content or players will leave.
- [3:16](https://youtu.be/dtRGRx-2WNg?t=196) **[[team-and-budget]]** Pair with builders or scripters to split workload and raise quality; reach out to respected developers with thoughtful messages.
- [4:25](https://youtu.be/dtRGRx-2WNg?t=265) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Selling a finished game gives upfront cash; ensure well-documented code, modular systems, enough content to show potential, and clear sale terms.
- [5:35](https://youtu.be/dtRGRx-2WNg?t=335) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Sell assets that solve a pain point (environment packs, UI kits) since they can be created once and sold repeatedly.
- [6:16](https://youtu.be/dtRGRx-2WNg?t=376) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Focus on quality over quantity for assets and keep them updated every few months to stay relevant.
- [8:31](https://youtu.be/dtRGRx-2WNg?t=511) **[[common-mistakes]]** Vet clients and buyers, check reputations, and secure clear written terms before committing to avoid scams.
- [8:31](https://youtu.be/dtRGRx-2WNg?t=511) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Combine methods over your career: commissions first, then assets and games to sell, then your own original projects once you have funds.
