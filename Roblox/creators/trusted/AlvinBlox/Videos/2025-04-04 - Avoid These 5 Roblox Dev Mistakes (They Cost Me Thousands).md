# Avoid These 5 Roblox Dev Mistakes (They Cost Me Thousands)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw) | AlvinBlox | 179,601 views | 2025-04-04

## Summary
Five common Roblox dev mistakes: weak thumbnail/title first impressions, not marketing (community, creator promos, modest ads), ignoring player feedback, overcomplicating games, and giving up too soon. Advises simple, focused games and consistent iteration.

## Tips
- [0:30](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw?t=30) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make thumbnails pop: bright colors, Roblox characters, ideally a render, minimal text.
- [1:11](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw?t=71) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Use specific, exciting titles that explain the game in as few words as possible (e.g. Hide or Die).
- [2:00](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw?t=120) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build a community first: create a Discord server or Roblox group and invite friends and testers.
- [2:40](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw?t=160) **[[community-and-discord]]** Share updates and sneak peeks well before release to build excitement rather than hiding the game until launch.
- [3:21](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw?t=201) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Commission small YouTubers, streamers, or TikTok/Shorts creators to get an initial player springboard cheaply.
- [4:02](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw?t=242) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use Roblox ads sparingly; a simple small ad is fine, but don't spend heavily on them.
- [4:02](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw?t=242) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** A fun game should snowball naturally; initial boost from friends' servers and promotion should be enough to reach the algorithm.
- [4:43](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw?t=283) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Add a feedback board or read game page comments and Discord; act on feedback repeated by multiple players.
- [5:24](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw?t=324) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Get feedback early and quickly, before heavy investment creates a sunk cost.
- [5:24](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw?t=324) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Update regularly based on feedback and tell players you are listening to keep them returning.
- [5:55](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw?t=355) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't cram in many systems (battle, clan, pets, story) at once; new players get confused and leave.
- [6:37](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw?t=397) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Start with one core idea and avoid feature creep; add features later as updates based on feedback.
- [6:37](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw?t=397) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Making 10 simple games in a year beats one risky, unproven game taking a full year.
- [7:21](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw?t=441) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Don't quit after a week with no players; stay consistent while the project still feels enjoyable and progressing.
- [8:03](https://youtu.be/f63stwpsaXw?t=483) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Set small milestones (10, then 50, then 100 players) and celebrate each win.
