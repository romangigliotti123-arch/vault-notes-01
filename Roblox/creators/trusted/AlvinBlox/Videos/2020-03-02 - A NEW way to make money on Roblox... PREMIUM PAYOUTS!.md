## Tips
- [1:10](https://youtu.be/oh89BQkBvBM?t=70) **[[monetisation-other]]** Premium Payouts require consistent concurrent players; games with few concurrent players are unlikely to earn meaningful Premium Payouts.
- [1:53](https://youtu.be/oh89BQkBvBM?t=113) **[[monetisation-other]]** Add Premium-only benefits (e.g., exclusive map, level, or item) to encourage players to subscribe, which raises your Premium playtime. Optional, not required.
- [3:22](https://youtu.be/oh89BQkBvBM?t=202) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check the Premium tab in game Developer Stats (game page three-dot menu) to see your Premium score and Robux earned per day; only available for your own games.
- [3:42](https://youtu.be/oh89BQkBvBM?t=222) **[[monetisation-other]]** Do not make Premium benefits pay-to-win, do not gate essential content behind paywalls, and be honest with players, per Roblox's Premium Payouts guidance.
