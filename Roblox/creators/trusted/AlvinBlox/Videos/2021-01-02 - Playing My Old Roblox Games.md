## Summary
Creator Alvin Blocks replays his old Roblox games (Obi Mania 2015, Skydive Paradise 2016, Fight of Dawn 2011) and critiques what went wrong: poor GUI scaling, free models, weak scripting, shallow core loops, and unfinished features. Nostalgic retrospective, not a how-to guide.

## Tips
- [10:56](https://youtu.be/lW3BgRRUEvI?t=656) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Keep shipping new games; each should improve on the last. Reviewing old projects shows how much you have learned since.
- [4:03](https://youtu.be/lW3BgRRUEvI?t=243) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid free models in published games; players can tell from materials and meshes, and they make the game look amateur.
- [2:06](https://youtu.be/lW3BgRRUEvI?t=126) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Test GUI scaling on mobile screens; fixed-size GUIs break on other devices and make the game look broken.
- [7:00](https://youtu.be/lW3BgRRUEvI?t=420) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** A game with one random outcome and little to do leads players to quit after a few rounds. Add depth and reasons to keep playing.
