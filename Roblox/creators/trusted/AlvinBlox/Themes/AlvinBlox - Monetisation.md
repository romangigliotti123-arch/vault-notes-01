## [[monetisation-devproducts]]
- 2018-08-07 [26:40](https://youtu.be/h92s4Xn26oc?t=1600) Use Developer Products (not gamepasses) for consumable currency, since they can be bought repeatedly; gamepasses are one-time purchases. ([[2018-08-07 - Roblox Currency Script System Tutorial - Monetise your Game|Roblox Currency Script System Tutorial - Monetise ]])
- 2024-06-04 [0:36](https://youtu.be/5YNFRCD0pd0?t=36) Create the developer product in game settings Monetization or create.roblox.com, then copy its product ID for scripting; the game must be published first. ([[2024-06-04 - Make MONEY on Roblox with Developer Products - Roblox Scripting Tutori|Make MONEY on Roblox with Developer Products - Rob]])
- 2024-06-04 [1:12](https://youtu.be/5YNFRCD0pd0?t=72) Prompt purchases from a LocalScript using MarketplaceService:PromptProductPurchase with the local player and product ID, e.g. on a button click. ([[2024-06-04 - Make MONEY on Roblox with Developer Products - Roblox Scripting Tutori|Make MONEY on Roblox with Developer Products - Rob]])
- 2024-06-04 [12:03](https://youtu.be/5YNFRCD0pd0?t=723) Developer products are consumable and can be bought repeatedly, unlike gamepasses; use them for currency or temporary boosts. ([[2024-06-04 - Make MONEY on Roblox with Developer Products - Roblox Scripting Tutori|Make MONEY on Roblox with Developer Products - Rob]])

## [[monetisation-other]]
- 2020-03-02 [1:10](https://youtu.be/oh89BQkBvBM?t=70) Premium Payouts require consistent concurrent players; games with few concurrent players are unlikely to earn meaningful Premium Payouts. ([[2020-03-02 - A NEW way to make money on Roblox... PREMIUM PAYOUTS!|A NEW way to make money on Roblox... PREMIUM PAYOU]])
- 2020-03-02 [1:53](https://youtu.be/oh89BQkBvBM?t=113) Add Premium-only benefits (e.g., exclusive map, level, or item) to encourage players to subscribe, which raises your Premium playtime. Optional, not required. ([[2020-03-02 - A NEW way to make money on Roblox... PREMIUM PAYOUTS!|A NEW way to make money on Roblox... PREMIUM PAYOU]])
- 2020-03-02 [3:42](https://youtu.be/oh89BQkBvBM?t=222) Do not make Premium benefits pay-to-win, do not gate essential content behind paywalls, and be honest with players, per Roblox's Premium Payouts guidance.
