# There's so much money to be made from Roblox

## Summary
Speaker (17, claims ~$30k earned from Roblox games) argues the Roblox home page now rewards player retention over short-term hype, and that beginners should build small systems rather than tutorials, avoid saturated genres, and expect many failed projects. Mostly motivational; little hard data.

## Tips
- [2:21](https://youtu.be/hQ1lrFLEF1c?t=141) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Roblox now ranks games on returning players (day-over-day, monthly return), not one-session hype; design for players coming back next week.
- [3:13](https://youtu.be/hQ1lrFLEF1c?t=193) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Players come back for a loop that itches: a small feelable reward, a number going up, a 'one more run' hook.
- [3:53](https://youtu.be/hQ1lrFLEF1c?t=233) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Make a game you'd actually open on a boring afternoon; if you wouldn't return, players won't either.
- [3:53](https://youtu.be/hQ1lrFLEF1c?t=233) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Give players a good feeling within the first 30 seconds (win, reward); confusion in the first moments causes immediate quits.
- [4:35](https://youtu.be/hQ1lrFLEF1c?t=275) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Assume players have zero context; test whether a new player ever wonders 'what do I do now?' and fix any such moment.
- [4:35](https://youtu.be/hQ1lrFLEF1c?t=275) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Build for the second session, not the first.
- [4:53](https://youtu.be/hQ1lrFLEF1c?t=293) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Start by building small standalone pieces (shop button, notifications, leaderboard, inventory, save system) and stack them into a game later.
- [5:47](https://youtu.be/hQ1lrFLEF1c?t=347) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Avoid genres already saturated by tutorial clones (e.g. Grow a Garden-style games); make a game you'd want to play instead.
- [5:47](https://youtu.be/hQ1lrFLEF1c?t=347) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use tutorials only for basics, possibilities and specific resources; try first, break things, then search for your exact error.
- [6:28](https://youtu.be/hQ1lrFLEF1c?t=388) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Pass the change test: if you can re-price an item or add a second currency without breaking things, you understand the system.
- [7:10](https://youtu.be/hQ1lrFLEF1c?t=430) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Use AI as a tutor (explain this error/line), not to generate whole systems you don't understand; read the documentation with code snippets.
- [8:06](https://youtu.be/hQ1lrFLEF1c?t=486) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Expect months to years to learn; keep building and shipping, and accept that most early projects will be scrapped.
- [9:27](https://youtu.be/hQ1lrFLEF1c?t=567) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Treat failed and scrapped projects as tuition; consistent shipping matters more than any single hit.
