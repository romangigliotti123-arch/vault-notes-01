# Watch this before making ROBLOX Games

## Summary
Motivational devlog by a Roblox dev on persisting through early flops. Covers his first games' weak results, losing his main account in 2022, and how Rarity Rumble gained traction through core retention (progression, RNG, leaderboards, enemies). Stresses handling momentum and setbacks by taking responsibility and not tying mood to player counts.

## Tips
- [3:03](https://youtu.be/xnlaYLnCtFc?t=183) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Prioritise core retention over code polish: add meaningful progression, RNG, leaderboards, enemies and something to grind for. Players care about fun, not clean code.
- [3:44](https://youtu.be/xnlaYLnCtFc?t=224) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Treat early player messages and feature requests as fuel; once players give feedback, build with the community rather than for yourself.
- [2:15](https://youtu.be/xnlaYLnCtFc?t=135) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect your first few games to flop; keep making games (second, third, fourth) because a hit often comes after several releases.
- [4:02](https://youtu.be/xnlaYLnCtFc?t=242) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Expect to spend hours debugging issues that work in Studio but break on live servers; test in live server conditions before publishing.
