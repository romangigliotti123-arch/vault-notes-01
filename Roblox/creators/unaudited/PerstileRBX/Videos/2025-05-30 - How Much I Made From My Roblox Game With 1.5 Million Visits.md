## Summary
Creator Perstile breaks down earnings from "Every second you get plus one poop size," a one-week, low-effort Roblox game that reached about 1.4-1.5M visits. Shows 30-day and yearly Robux breakdowns, best-selling products and passes, and DevEx payout (~$824). Mostly entertainment; little actionable advice.

## Tips
- [2:14](https://youtu.be/ET3fRqaFmFc?t=134) **[[pricing]]** Underpriced best seller: creator says the 500-Robux double-size product should have cost more; he estimates roughly 2x revenue at a higher price.
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/ET3fRqaFmFc?t=40) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't assume 1 Robux per visit; creator says that rule is false, with this game earning far more per visit from purchases.
- [2:38](https://youtu.be/ET3fRqaFmFc?t=158) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Test gamepass functionality before launch: the best-selling golden poop pass was broken and did nothing, a visible bug players still paid for.

## Numbers
- [0:22](https://youtu.be/ET3fRqaFmFc?t=22) Visits: **~1.4-1.5 million total visits; 38 concurrent players at time of recording**
- [1:16](https://youtu.be/ET3fRqaFmFc?t=76) Robux earned, 72 hours: **2,500 Robux**
- [1:16](https://youtu.be/ET3fRqaFmFc?t=76) Robux earned, 30 days: **36,000 Robux (19,000 dev products, 16,000 gamepasses)**
- [1:57](https://youtu.be/ET3fRqaFmFc?t=117) Robux earned, past year: **237,000 Robux (130,000 dev products, 100,000 gamepasses)**

## Games
