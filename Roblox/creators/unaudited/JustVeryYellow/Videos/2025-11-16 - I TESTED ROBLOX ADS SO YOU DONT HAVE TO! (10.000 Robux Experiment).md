# I TESTED ROBLOX ADS SO YOU DONT HAVE TO! (10.000 Robux Experiment)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/rzB8CcRzFK8) | JustVeryYellow | 29,167 views | 2025-11-16

## Summary
Creator spends 40 ad credits (10,000 Robux) on Roblox Ads Manager for an obby game over 6 days, targeting mobile/tablet in US, Canada, Europe, Latin America and Asia. Reports impressions, CTR, cost per visit, and concludes 10k Robux is too little to blow the game up.

## Tips
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/rzB8CcRzFK8?t=40) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** In Ads Manager, pick the experience, choose maximize place visits, set a budget (lifetime), duration, and device/region targeting before publishing.
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/rzB8CcRzFK8?t=81) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ads targeting European regions requires accounts aged 13+ due to local laws; plan audience accordingly.
- [10:43](https://youtu.be/rzB8CcRzFK8?t=643) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Measure ad performance by CTR and cost per join; creator saw ~5.69% CTR and ~0.05 ad credits per click-through join.
- [11:23](https://youtu.be/rzB8CcRzFK8?t=683) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** 10k Robux may be too small to make a game blow up; creator estimates 70k–100k Robux is more likely needed.
- [8:11](https://youtu.be/rzB8CcRzFK8?t=491) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check the analytics tab for average session time, DAU and returning vs new players after an ad burst; the spike faded about two days after the ads ended.
- [4:17](https://youtu.be/rzB8CcRzFK8?t=257) **[[monetisation-other]]** Before paying for ads, confirm the game already monetises; this creator earned only ~671 Robux before the campaign.
