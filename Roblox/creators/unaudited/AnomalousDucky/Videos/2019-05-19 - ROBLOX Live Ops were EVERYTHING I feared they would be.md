## Summary
A frustrated creator criticises Roblox's first Live Ops sponsored-event round. He argues the picked games were ones he didn't like or play, rewards were handed out free rather than earned by challenges, and event-driven player spikes rarely return. Mostly opinion; no data beyond rough player counts.

## Tips
- [2:43](https://youtu.be/w_oAK9htLJY?t=163) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Make event rewards earned through a challenge or special play, not handed out to everyone on join. Speaker says free-for-all drops don't feel like prizes.
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/w_oAK9htLJY?t=40) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Event-driven player spikes may not persist. Speaker claims about 90% of players who joined for an event prize never return, so plan retention hooks beyond the event.
- [3:23](https://youtu.be/w_oAK9htLJY?t=203) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Avoid event modes that are instantly available to all players; the speaker argues this removes the reason to participate in the live-ops event.
