## Summary
Commentary rant on Roblox Egg Hunt events (2008 to 2020). Argues the dev-hunt format (2015, then 2019 and 2020, where third-party games hosted eggs) hurt quality versus the dedicated-team events of 2017 and 2018. Notes Egg Hunt 2021 was canceled, and points to fan-made hunts.

## Games

- **Egg Hunt 2017** [4:46](https://youtu.be/79UtWdd4Y9g?t=286): Dedicated team (Elite Builders of Robloxia) produced a well-received event after the 2015-2016 backlash.
- **Egg Hunt 2018** [5:26](https://youtu.be/79UtWdd4Y9g?t=326): Satisfying controls, music, environments and clear egg objectives praised as the best event; a model for quality.
