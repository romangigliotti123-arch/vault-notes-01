# AnomalousDucky - How to make successful Roblox games

This creator's advice is almost entirely commentary on Roblox's platform-wide Egg Hunt and Live Ops events (2019 to 2022), not a general development playbook. The usable points are: event rewards should be earned through a challenge, not handed out on join; event modes should not be instantly open to everyone; dedicated, well-resourced teams produce better event quality; and event spikes fade, so retention must be built separately. Most figures are opinion or unsourced. Live Ops was paused on Nov 21, 2019, so event-specific tactics are historical.

**Style:** Opinion-driven "Let's Talk" rants about Roblox events, with little how-to and mostly unsourced numbers. Useful for understanding what players and creators disliked about platform-wide events and why event spikes faded. Not a current growth plan. Suits developers who want a critical view of event design before planning seasonal content.

## Playbook
1. **Idea: do not build a game around a platform event** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Evidence is thin. Platform events (Egg Hunt, Live Ops) were paused or changed, and the creator argues event-driven spikes rarely return (8ymUUITY3gw, w_oAK9htLJY). Design a core game that holds players without relying on an external event.
2. **Build: make event rewards earned, not free** [[events-and-seasonal-content]]: Rewards should come from a challenge or special play. Free-for-all drops do not feel like prizes (w_oAK9htLJY). Also avoid event modes that are instantly available to all players, since this removes the reason to take part (R5LnkhhJtno, w_oAK9htLJY).
3. **Build: use a dedicated team for event content** [[team-and-budget]]: The creator argues that outsourcing event segments to random third-party developers hurt quality and player trust. Dev-hunt Egg Hunts (2019 with 50 games, 2020) were weaker than dedicated-team events of 2017 and 2018 (79UtWdd4Y9g).
4. **Launch: plan retention hooks beyond the event** [[retention-d1-d7]]: Claimed (unsourced) that about 90% of players who joined for an event prize never return (~10% return). Plan a reason to stay after the event ends, not just a spike at launch (8ymUUITY3gw, w_oAK9htLJY).
5. **Launch: check the current event status first** [[updates-live-ops]]: Roblox paused the Live Ops program on Nov 21, 2019 to find better ways to generate visibility for seasonal updates. Check the Event tab for what is currently active before planning around any event (R5LnkhhJtno).
6. **Growth: do not expect featured visibility to save a small game** [[discovery-algorithm]]: Many featured Live Ops games reached under 100 players, and a sponsored game showed about 400 concurrent players (about 1000 if the event was active). Visibility alone did not help small games (w_oAK9htLJY, R5LnkhhJtno).
7. **Growth: watch like-to-dislike ratios on event-featured games** [[analytics-metrics]]: The creator cites like-to-dislike ratios of 30 to 40% for some featured event games (79UtWdd4Y9g). Treat this as a warning sign that event exposure can attract low-quality players, and track sentiment, not only visits.
8. **Live ops: run seasonal events with quality control** [[events-and-seasonal-content]]: Egg Hunt was annual (March to April) from 2008 and used dedicated games from 2012. Egg Hunt 2021 was canceled. The creator suggests fan-made hunts as a substitute (79UtWdd4Y9g). Evaluate whether Roblox has resumed platform events before committing (8ymUUITY3gw, R5LnkhhJtno).

## Common mistakes
- Handing out event rewards to everyone on join, so they do not feel like prizes (w_oAK9htLJY).
- Making event modes instantly available to all players, which removes the reason to participate (R5LnkhhJtno, w_oAK9htLJY).
- Outsourcing event segments to random developers, which hurt quality and player trust (79UtWdd4Y9g).
- Assuming featured visibility alone will lift a small game; many featured Live Ops games stayed under 100 players (R5LnkhhJtno).
- Relying on event-driven spikes without retention hooks; the creator claims most event joiners do not return (w_oAK9htLJY, 8ymUUITY3gw).
- Ignoring like-to-dislike ratios on event-featured games, which ran 30 to 40% in some cases (79UtWdd4Y9g).
