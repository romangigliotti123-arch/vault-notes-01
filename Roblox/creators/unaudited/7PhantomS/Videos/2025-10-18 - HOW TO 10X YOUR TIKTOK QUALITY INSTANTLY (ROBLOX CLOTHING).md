# HOW TO 10X YOUR TIKTOK QUALITY INSTANTLY (ROBLOX CLOTHING)

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Watch](https://youtu.be/sByQYCWWlwE) | 7PhantomS | 6,832 views | 2025-10-18

## Summary
Short TikTok-slideshow tutorial for improving clothing screenshot quality: render avatar in Catalog Avatar Creator photo studio, AI-upscale the screenshot, remove background in Photopea, and clean up in Paint. Mostly a quality-of-creative workflow, not Roblox game development.

## Tips
- [1:22](https://youtu.be/sByQYCWWlwE?t=82) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Set Catalog Avatar Creator camera distance to 25 and keep camera offset at default for sharper, consistent clothing renders.
- [1:35](https://youtu.be/sByQYCWWlwE?t=95) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Run screenshots through an AI upscaler at 2x resolution (default strength 0.5) to sharpen clothing images before posting.
- [2:29](https://youtu.be/sByQYCWWlwE?t=149) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Remove the background in a free web editor (Photopea quick selection, invert, delete) to get a clean cutout for slideshows.
- [4:28](https://youtu.be/sByQYCWWlwE?t=268) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Image quality alone won't drive taps; the clothing design itself must be good, so prioritise the item over the polish.

## Numbers
- [1:35](https://youtu.be/sByQYCWWlwE?t=95) Upscale factor: **2x (strength 0.5 default)** (AI upscaler settings used for avatar screenshot)
- [3:42](https://youtu.be/sByQYCWWlwE?t=222) Canvas size: **800x1000** (Paint canvas used for final composite)

## Tools
Catalog Avatar Creator (photo studio), Snipping Sketch, AI image upscaler (web), Photopea
