## Summary
Part 1 of a live photobashing series where the creator turns a reference image into a Roblox clothing template (shirt/pants). Covers blending arms, shoulders, neck hole, torso and pants with clone stamp, liquify, color balance and overlay assets. Mostly narrated process with few generalisable rules.

## Tips
- [3:37](https://youtu.be/9Ins2jyZlTc?t=217) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Match color on a flat-tone layer first: add a little magenta via color balance and adjust saturation so pieces share one brightness.
- [4:17](https://youtu.be/9Ins2jyZlTc?t=257) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** For clone stamp blending, set brush opacity around 30-39% and hardness 100 so strokes blend partially instead of hard-copying.
- [3:00](https://youtu.be/9Ins2jyZlTc?t=180) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Pull in textured pieces from the reference to avoid flat-looking areas; the creator says flat shading reads as unrealistic in photobashing.
- [9:19](https://youtu.be/9Ins2jyZlTc?t=559) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Clone stamping degrades image quality; add a little noise afterward to restore texture.
