## Summary
Shows how a Roblox clothing designer advertises his group via TikTok: making catalog avatar outfit screenshots in Photo Studio, editing in Paint.NET, outfit codes, and account warm-up/posting cadence. Stresses patience (about 5 months to blow up).

## Tips
- [0:52](https://youtu.be/xjZXsHxKerE?t=52) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Post consistently, at least every two days minimum; speaker said it took about 5 months of near-daily posting to break out.
- [11:16](https://youtu.be/xjZXsHxKerE?t=676) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't upload on a brand-new TikTok account's first day; spend it engaging (follow, like, favorite) to avoid being shadowbanned as a bot.
- [11:59](https://youtu.be/xjZXsHxKerE?t=719) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Post one video a day when starting; only move to two a day once views grow, and space same-day posts at least 30 minutes apart.
- [12:41](https://youtu.be/xjZXsHxKerE?t=761) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Judge a new TikTok account after about 6 videos; under 20 views early is normal, but if still poor by video 10, consider abandoning it.
- [3:12](https://youtu.be/xjZXsHxKerE?t=192) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use a clean white background, spotlight placed near the end of the stage, and camera distance ~10 with FOV 50 or lower for outfit shots.
- [6:51](https://youtu.be/xjZXsHxKerE?t=411) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use an 800x1000 vertical canvas and keep canvas size when pasting so the image isn't stretched on vertical TikTok video.
- [9:35](https://youtu.be/xjZXsHxKerE?t=575) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Add an outline to overlaid outfit-code text by duplicating the layer, inverting it to white, and blurring it behind the black text.
- [10:16](https://youtu.be/xjZXsHxKerE?t=616) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Sharpen Roblox screenshots lightly (sharpen strength up to about 2.5) to improve perceived quality in the video.
