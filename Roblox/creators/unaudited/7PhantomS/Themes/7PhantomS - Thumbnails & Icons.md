- 2025-07-18 [3:12](https://youtu.be/xjZXsHxKerE?t=192) Use a clean white background, spotlight placed near the end of the stage, and camera distance ~10 with FOV 50 or lower for outfit shots. - 2025-07-18 [6:51](https://youtu.be/xjZXsHxKerE?t=411) Use an 800x1000 vertical canvas and keep canvas size when pasting so the image isn't stretched on vertical TikTok video. ([[2025-07-18 - How To Advertise Your Clothing Group (Roblox)|How To Advertise Your Clothing Group (Roblox)]])

- 2025-07-18 [10:16](https://youtu.be/xjZXsHxKerE?t=616) Sharpen Roblox screenshots lightly (sharpen strength up to about 2.5) to improve perceived quality in the video. ([[2025-07-18 - How To Advertise Your Clothing Group (Roblox)|How To Advertise Your Clothing Group (Roblox)]])
