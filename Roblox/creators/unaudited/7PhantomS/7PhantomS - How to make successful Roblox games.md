# 7PhantomS - How to make successful Roblox games

7PhantomS's five videos cover selling Roblox clothing, not building or launching games. The advice is a clothing-marketing playbook: learn design by making every garment type, take commissions early for income, render clean outfit screenshots, warm up and post steadily on TikTok, judge accounts early, repeat whatever clears 1,000 views, and cross-post slideshows to Pinterest. No video covers game design, retention, monetisation via passes or dev products, or data persistence. 9Ins2jyZlTc and 8Q7xNX-zsG4 add little generalisable guidance. Apply this only to clothing and avatar-item content.

**Style:** Practical, workflow-heavy clothing designer who shows real Photoshop, Paint.NET, and Photopea steps and TikTok slideshow tactics. Suits Roblox clothing and avatar-item sellers. Weak fit for game developers: none of the five videos covers building, launching, or running a game.

## Playbook
1. **Learn clothing design, then practise every garment type** [[building-art-and-assets]]: Learn from channels the creator recommends, then make every clothing piece type. Commissions double as practice. Source: peTkfSG79rM.
2. **Pick a clear group niche** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: His group was archive and Japanese streetwear themed. A narrow niche gave the group an identity. Source: peTkfSG79rM.
3. **Take commissions early for income and capital** [[business-and-payouts]]: About 100-200 Robux per outfit at first. Commissions brought 30,000 Robux in 2.5 months, against about 4,000 from his own group. Source: peTkfSG79rM.
4. **Photobash references into templates with controlled blending** [[building-art-and-assets]]: Match colour on a flat-tone layer first (a little magenta via colour balance, then saturation). Clone stamp at 30-39% opacity, hardness 100. Add noise after cloning. Test overlay texture packs before committing. Source: 9Ins2jyZlTc.
5. **Render and enhance outfit screenshots** [[thumbnails-icons]]: Render in Catalog Avatar Creator at camera distance 25, default offset. AI-upscale at 2x (strength 0.5), then sharpen lightly (up to about 2.5). Remove the background in Photopea and clean up in Paint. Source: sByQYCWWlwE.
6. **Stage outfit shots on a clean background** [[thumbnails-icons]]: Use a white background and a spotlight near the end of the stage. Camera distance about 10, FOV 50 or lower. Outline overlaid outfit-code text by duplicating the layer, inverting it to white, and blurring it behind the black text. Source: sByQYCWWlwE.
7. **Build vertical 800x1000 canvases** [[thumbnails-icons]]: Use an 800x1000 canvas and keep canvas size when pasting, so images are not stretched on vertical TikTok video. Source: sByQYCWWlwE.
8. **Warm up a new TikTok account before posting** [[social-features-and-virality]]: Do not upload on day one. Spend it following, liking, and favouriting to avoid bot-like shadowbans. Source: xjZXsHxKerE.
9. **Set a posting cadence** [[social-features-and-virality]]: Start at one post a day and go to two a day at most once views grow, with same-day posts at least 30 minutes apart. The newest guidance (Dec 2025) is TikTok and Pinterest at least 2-3 times a week. See evolution. Sources: xjZXsHxKerE, peTkfSG79rM.
10. **Judge a new account early and cut losers** [[trend-research-and-validation]]: Under 20 views is normal for the first ~6 videos. If views are still poor by video 10, consider abandoning the account. Source: xjZXsHxKerE.
11. **Find and repeat what works** [[analytics-metrics]]: Track which videos pass 1,000 views and find the common element, such as a specific clothing set. Repeat it. His breakout video reached 3M views after about 5 months of near-daily posting. Sources: xjZXsHxKerE, peTkfSG79rM.
12. **Post best-seller slideshows and cross-post to Pinterest** [[trailers-and-video-marketing]]: A slideshow starting with a best-selling set reached 4M views. Pinterest reached about 1M monthly viewers. Make ads with real effort, not low-effort ones. Source: peTkfSG79rM.
13. **Join design communities for assets and exposure** [[community-and-discord]]: Join clothing design Discord servers for free assets. Showcase work across several servers to get noticed. Source: peTkfSG79rM.
14. **Refresh slideshows around current best sellers** [[updates-live-ops]]: Post a new slideshow every couple of weeks featuring current best-selling items to keep momentum. Source: peTkfSG79rM.

## Common mistakes
- Expecting fast results: breakout took about 5 months of near-daily posting (xjZXsHxKerE).
- Uploading on a brand-new TikTok account's first day, which risks a bot shadowban (xjZXsHxKerE).
- Abandoning an account too early: under 20 views is normal for the first ~6 videos (xjZXsHxKerE).
- Posting same-day videos less than 30 minutes apart (xjZXsHxKerE).
- Leaving flat shading in photobashing; pull textured pieces from the reference so areas don't look unrealistic (9Ins2jyZlTc).
- Hard clone stamping without noise afterwards, which degrades image quality (9Ins2jyZlTc).
- Converting overlay texture packs without testing, which can cause colour and lighting problems (9Ins2jyZlTc).
- Making low-effort ads instead of genuinely good ones (peTkfSG79rM).
- Assuming this covers game development: none of the five videos addresses building, launching, or monetising a Roblox game (all IDs, esp. 8Q7xNX-zsG4).
