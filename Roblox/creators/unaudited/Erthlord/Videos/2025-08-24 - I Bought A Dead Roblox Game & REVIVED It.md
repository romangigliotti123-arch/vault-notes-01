## Summary
A creator buys a dead Roblox obby (Obby but with a Disc) for 23,600 Robux, hires a scripter and builder, rebuilds the map, rebrands with new icon, thumbnail and name, transfers to a new place, and spends 100 ad credits over 5 days to revive it. Mostly a devlog; the Bubix interview gives light advice.

## Tips
- [7:03](https://youtu.be/dB6Us5PaWFw?t=423) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Bubix suggests about 50 ad credits per day as a standard starting budget; the creator spent only 100 total, which he admits was below that.
- [7:03](https://youtu.be/dB6Us5PaWFw?t=423) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Bubix's checklist: the game must be fun, keep players playing a long time, bring them back, be clickable, and be able to make money.
- [3:53](https://youtu.be/dB6Us5PaWFw?t=233) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Creator claims Roblox judges a game on its first rounds of ads, so a badly performing advertised place can hurt later reach; he moved the game to a new place. Unverified.
- [5:51](https://youtu.be/dB6Us5PaWFw?t=351) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Rebrand early with a new icon, thumbnail and name, done by an artist, before advertising.
