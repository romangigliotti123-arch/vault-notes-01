## Summary
A self-described "unk" creator forms a group with four developers (one drops out) to clone trending Roblox brainrot games in a 7-day challenge that stretches to about 9 days. Games are advertised via paid ads after release. After 14 days: ~20,000 visits, 475 Robux plus 822 pending (~1,200 total). The video's main point is that the payoff was small and the fun mattered more.

## Tips
- [1:06](https://youtu.be/fdDscQ6CWIs?t=66) **[[team-and-budget]]** Recruiting multiple devs to build clones in parallel ran into dropouts and delays; plan for at least one developer leaving mid-project.
- [5:25](https://youtu.be/fdDscQ6CWIs?t=325) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Trying to ship a trend-chasing game in 7 days was too tight; the creator needed about 9 days and scope still slipped.
- [3:28](https://youtu.be/fdDscQ6CWIs?t=208) **[[common-mistakes]]** Buying a pre-made game and reskinning it still left bugs and UI problems; the hired scripter had not fixed them by mid-week.
- [2:45](https://youtu.be/fdDscQ6CWIs?t=165) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Roblox trends die quickly, so a clone launched after the trend peaks may miss the audience; speed matters when chasing a meme.
