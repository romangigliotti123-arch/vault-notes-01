# Erthlord - How to make successful Roblox games

Erthlord's six videos are challenge vlogs, not a build course, so most advice is anecdotal and unverified. The consistent lesson: trend-clone and viral-push strategies returned little (about 1,200 Robux after 14 days, 257 peak players, 34 UGC sales). Build something you enjoy, with a simple, polished core loop and a finished game before spending on ads. Expect a 9-day build when you planned 7, plan for a developer dropout, rebrand before advertising, and expect slow traction. Paid ads moved game traffic; they did not move UGC sales.

**Style:** Self-deprecating challenge-vlog creator who documents trend-chasing, UGC selling and reviving dead games, with candid, mostly modest results. Best for developers who want honest cost-and-return reality checks. Offers few systematic build tutorials, so pair his lessons with deeper technical resources.

## Playbook
1. **Pick a fun, personal idea** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Prioritise a game you enjoy building with a simple, clickable core loop. Trend clones in 2025-09 (fdDscQ6CWIs) earned about 1,200 Robux total, so the creator concluded enjoyment and personal projects matter more than chasing trends.
2. **Validate trend timing fast** [[trend-research-and-validation]]: Roblox trends die quickly (fdDscQ6CWIs). A clone launched after a meme peaks misses the audience. If chasing a trend, move within days, and avoid overstimulating clones since simpler designs stood out.
3. **Design a simple buy-feed-sell-upgrade loop** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: Keep the core loop simple and polished; pet-like buy, feed, sell, upgrade cycles were rated best (dB6Us5PaWFw). Bubix's checklist: fun, long sessions, brings players back, clickable, can make money. Add a second map when one map gets repetitive.
4. **Staff with a dropout buffer** [[team-and-budget]]: Four recruited devs on a 7-day deadline lost one mid-project and slipped (fdDscQ6CWIs). Recruit parallel builders only with a backup plan for at least one leaving.
5. **Budget a realistic timeline** [[development-process-and-speed]]: A 7-day trend build needed about 9 days and scope still slipped (fdDscQ6CWIs). Early UGC items took about 2 hours each, later ones 30 min to 1.5 hours (57GCHQtyuZQ). Plan with that padding.
6. **Commission art when modeling is slow** [[building-art-and-assets]]: Hire artists per finished item when your own modeling is the bottleneck. The creator paid per item and uploaded eight at once (57GCHQtyuZQ). Keep briefs tied to trending memes and popular words, which drew early UGC sales.
7. **Audit any bought or reskinned game** [[scripting-and-performance]]: A purchased obby reskinned by a hired scripter still had bugs and UI problems by mid-week (dB6Us5PaWFw). Before paying, test for bugs and check the code and UI, not just visits and favorites.
8. **Rebrand before advertising** [[thumbnails-icons]]: Before spending ads, give the game a new name, icon and thumbnail made by an artist (dB6Us5PaWFw). The thumbnail should show core gameplay, such as the monster chasing the player.
9. **Advertise only finished games** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Bubix suggests about 50 ad credits per day as a starting budget (dB6Us5PaWFw). The 7-day, 50-credit campaign in 69IBB6O4o4c lifted active players to 140 by day 14. Hold credits for launch-ready games, and track CTR and cost per play.
10. **Treat placement and algorithm claims as unproven** [[discovery-algorithm]]: The creator claimed Roblox judges a place on its first ad rounds and moved the game to a new place (dB6Us5PaWFw). This is unverified, and ads alone gave modest organic traction, so don't rely on the algorithm.
11. **Put the game name in short-form captions** [[titles-descriptions]]: Include the game name in Shorts and TikTok captions so viewers can find and play directly. Frame the game as a play-with-friends experience, which the creator saw reach thousands of players (69IBB6O4o4c).
12. **Lead with YouTube over TikTok** [[trailers-and-video-marketing]]: Item promos drove about 108,000 YouTube views against under 5,000 on TikTok (57GCHQtyuZQ). In 69IBB6O4o4c, Shorts-type posts reached 17,000+ TikTok and 6,000 YouTube views, so measure per platform before committing.
13. **Skip low-yield promotions** [[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]: Paid creators charged a few hundred Robux per video and gave only a few replies per day (69IBB6O4o4c). A developer-game booth drew 8 players from 2 hours. Printed posters drew about 600 views, and Twitter meme posts about 100.
14. **Expect slow, delayed traction** [[retention-d1-d7]]: Older UGC items picked up sales days later (57GCHQtyuZQ). Measure retention over the first week rather than judging on day one, and keep updating after launch.

## Common mistakes
- Chasing a trend after it peaks; Roblox trends die fast and clones launched late miss the audience (fdDscQ6CWIs).
- Reskinning a bought game without auditing its bugs and UI; the hired scripter had not fixed them by mid-week (dB6Us5PaWFw).
- Spending ad credits before the game is finished, wasting budget on unready titles (dB6Us5PaWFw).
- Expecting paid UGC ads to produce quick sales; 200 Robux per item on 17 items showed no measurable lift the next day (57GCHQtyuZQ).
- Planning a 7-day build with no slack; the real timeline ran to about 9 days (fdDscQ6CWIs).
- Relying on one developer; a mid-project dropout delayed the clones (fdDscQ6CWIs).
- Spending time on developer-game booths (8 players from 2 hours) or printed posters (~600 views) (69IBB6O4o4c).
- Posting memes on X/Twitter with a small account; almost no engagement (~100 views total) (69IBB6O4o4c).
- Judging a game by day-one numbers; sales and players can pick up days later (57GCHQtyuZQ).
- Using a single map for too long; players got bored after a few plays (core-loops-and-game-design sample; dB6Us5PaWFw).

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Core loop is simple, polished and clickable, with a buy/feed/sell/upgrade style cycle.
- [ ] Game is finished and playable before any ad credits are spent.
- [ ] Second map ready if the first is likely to feel repetitive.
- [ ] New name, icon and thumbnail made by an artist before ads start.
- [ ] Thumbnail shows core gameplay, not generic art.
- [ ] Ad budget around 50 credits/day for a 7-day campaign as a starting point.
- [ ] Track CTR and cost per play before scaling spend.
- [ ] Game name in every short-form caption and description.
- [ ] Frame the game as a play-with-friends experience in promo content.
- [ ] Audit any purchased or reskinned code for bugs before launch.
- [ ] Team plan includes a backup if one developer leaves.
- [ ] Schedule about 9 days for a build originally planned at 7.
- [ ] Prioritise YouTube Shorts for promotion; measure TikTok separately.
- [ ] Plan for slow traction; watch week-one retention, not day-one spikes.
- [ ] Monitor Roblox rule changes that affect item economies, such as the 2023 trade hold (ylBgNBMiyW4).
- [ ] Keep UGC items on trend-led memes and popular words where possible.
- [ ] Avoid paid UGC ads as a quick sales lever.
- [ ] Confirm the place used for ads is a fresh, launch-ready place (claim unverified).
