## Summary
Tutorial on animating Roblox characters in Blender: installing the RBLX animation add-on and starter rigs, keyframing a normal rig, exporting via clipboard script and a Studio import plugin, and rendering a textured, accessorized smooth R6 rig to images or video. Mostly a workflow walkthrough with a community promo.

## Tips
- [1:24](https://youtu.be/WI6adMt2aUY?t=84) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Export animations from Blender with the RBLX add-on's export button, then convert them in Studio with a toolbox plugin's import-from-clipboard option. Smooth R6 rigs don't work in Studio.
- [3:55](https://youtu.be/WI6adMt2aUY?t=235) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Use only R6 textures on the smooth R6 render rig; R15 textures break it.
- [4:17](https://youtu.be/WI6adMt2aUY?t=257) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** When importing an OBJ into Blender, enable 'split by group' so body parts and accessories can be separated and deleted individually.
- [4:36](https://youtu.be/WI6adMt2aUY?t=276) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Attach UGC accessories to rig bones by selecting the item, shift-selecting the bone in pose mode, then Ctrl+P and choose Bone.
