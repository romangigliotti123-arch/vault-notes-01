## [[core-loops-and-game-design]]
- 2023-10-20 [2:44](https://youtu.be/DJsRZ2FfoMk?t=164) Make the game enjoyable: an obby alone is not enough; add a currency (e.g. wins) to buy fun things in-game, since enjoyment drives players and Robux. ([[2023-10-20 - How to become a ROBLOX Developer and make ROBUX!|How to become a ROBLOX Developer and make ROBUX!]])
- 2023-10-25 [2:53](https://youtu.be/OR7xEm4iaO8?t=173) Scale enemy waves so players must add towers over rounds; the speaker found towers were overwhelmed by round nine with no added defenses. ([[2023-10-25 - Making a Tower Defense Game in 1 HOUR in ROBLOX|Making a Tower Defense Game in 1 HOUR in ROBLOX]])
- 2023-12-12 [2:46](https://youtu.be/1jRaSYhrEcU?t=166) Balance pet drop rates and damage values early; the speaker noted the lack of a rarity system and that the game needs balancing. ([[2023-12-12 - Making Pet Simulator 99 in 1 HOUR in ROBLOX|Making Pet Simulator 99 in 1 HOUR in ROBLOX]])
- 2025-12-02 [7:55](https://youtu.be/D6Ej1CgiWOc?t=475) Use invisible hitboxes for hazards so the player dies on contact; spacing obstacles apart matters for playability. ([[2025-12-02 - I Remade Geometry Dash in Roblox|I Remade Geometry Dash in Roblox]])
- 2025-12-02 [9:17](https://youtu.be/D6Ej1CgiWOc?t=557) Switch from tap-to-jump to hold-to-jump; the creator says this made the game feel much better. ([[2025-12-02 - I Remade Geometry Dash in Roblox|I Remade Geometry Dash in Roblox]])
- 2026-01-09 [2:44](https://youtu.be/BYhhm9o3H_E?t=164) Gate new zones behind a purchased rod; verify unlock logic in playtests, since the AI-written gating broke in this test. ([[2026-01-09 - ChatGPT vs Lemonade Remake Fish It|ChatGPT vs Lemonade Remake Fish It]])
- 2026-01-09 [5:59](https://youtu.be/BYhhm9o3H_E?t=359) Give fish rarity tiers with chances and coin values so rarer catches feel rewarding. ([[2026-01-09 - ChatGPT vs Lemonade Remake Fish It|ChatGPT vs Lemonade Remake Fish It]])
- 2026-01-09 [10:09](https://youtu.be/BYhhm9o3H_E?t=609) Add a direct-hit bonus (+50% gold) and milestone popups to reward skilled play. ([[2026-01-09 - ChatGPT vs Lemonade Remake Fish It|ChatGPT vs Lemonade Remake Fish It]])
- 2026-04-19 [11:10](https://youtu.be/Ufr0SVq-o98?t=670) Add simple, visible multipliers like weather (2x growth speed) and pets (money multiplier) to give the core loop variety. ([[2026-04-19 - Gemini vs Lemonade Remake Grow a Garden|Gemini vs Lemonade Remake Grow a Garden]])
- 2026-05-25 [2:45](https://youtu.be/uYuiCJrAcmI?t=165) Add a safe-zone part and have consumables spawn outside it, so the safe zone creates a risk/reward loop. ([[2026-05-25 - I Made a Roblox Game using Cards|I Made a Roblox Game using Cards]])
- 2026-05-25 [3:26](https://youtu.be/uYuiCJrAcmI?t=206) Reward time survived per second and pause the counter inside the safe zone, so players must balance risk against progress. ([[2026-05-25 - I Made a Roblox Game using Cards|I Made a Roblox Game using Cards]])
- 2026-10-06 [0:40](https://youtu.be/eExtO_uF6Jg?t=40) Add a skill element (power bar, perfect hits, combo streak +10% score) so outcomes feel less random. ([[2026-10-06 - I made a Roblox Cash Grab in 7 Days|I made a Roblox Cash Grab in 7 Days]])
- 2026-10-06 [1:22](https://youtu.be/eExtO_uF6Jg?t=82) Give players a progression sink early: upgrades bought with score (multipliers, speed, dartboards) to drive repeat play. ([[2026-10-06 - I made a Roblox Cash Grab in 7 Days|I made a Roblox Cash Grab in 7 Days]])
