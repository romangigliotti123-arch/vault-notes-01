## Summary
Short tutorial on importing low-poly 3D models into Roblox Studio. Covers exporting from Blender as FBX (File > Export), opening the Asset Manager (View > Asset Manager), bulk importing with Import as One Mesh unchecked so parts stay separate, then selecting and inserting the imported meshes in the scene. No game-growth advice.

## Tips
- [0:01](https://youtu.be/WD6xum-dptI?t=1) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Export models as FBX from Blender (File > Export) to bring them into Roblox Studio.
- [1:25](https://youtu.be/WD6xum-dptI?t=85) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** In Asset Manager bulk import, leave Import as One Mesh unchecked if you need the parts separately; it cannot be customized afterward.
