## Summary
Demo of Lemonade's new "cards" feature: the creator builds a survival game (health/hunger, food consumables, safe zone, time-survived system, zombie AI, UI) almost entirely by prompting Lemonade's AI with cards and prompts. Sponsored; little general strategy.

## Tips
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/uYuiCJrAcmI?t=0) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Use prebuilt prompt cards from Lemonade's inventory as starting points, then tweak the prompt; the creator dropped a survival card in and got health/hunger bars immediately.
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/uYuiCJrAcmI?t=81) **[[ai-and-tools]]** When AI-generated code errors (e.g. infinite yield blocking UI), paste the error back into the AI and ask it to fix; this worked on the first try.
- [2:45](https://youtu.be/uYuiCJrAcmI?t=165) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add a safe-zone part and have consumables spawn outside it, so the safe zone creates a risk/reward loop.
- [3:26](https://youtu.be/uYuiCJrAcmI?t=206) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Reward time survived per second and pause the counter inside the safe zone, so players must balance risk against progress.
