## Summary
A friend duo attempts to build a tower defense Roblox game in one hour. Covers map assets, a placement system with rotation and collision, a selection GUI, a cash leaderstat, and a tower module that targets the closest enemy. The result is rough: skins break and towers struggle in later rounds.

## Tips
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/OR7xEm4iaO8?t=0) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Split work between collaborators: one builds map assets and enemies, the other builds placement and GUI systems, to parallelise a short build.
- [1:30](https://youtu.be/OR7xEm4iaO8?t=90) **[[common-mistakes]]** Anchor placed objects and map parts; unanchored towers and props fall or collide unexpectedly during testing.
- [2:12](https://youtu.be/OR7xEm4iaO8?t=132) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Add strokes or outlines to characters and decorations to make a low-effort art style look more cartoonish.
- [2:53](https://youtu.be/OR7xEm4iaO8?t=173) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Scale enemy waves so players must add towers over rounds; the speaker found towers were overwhelmed by round nine with no added defenses.
