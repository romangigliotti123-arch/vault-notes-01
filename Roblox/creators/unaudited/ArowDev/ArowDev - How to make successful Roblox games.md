# ArowDev - How to make successful Roblox games

Build a cheap, cartoon or meme-driven cash-grab with a polished UI, pop-ups, touch effects and about five gamepasses plus developer products. Fund development with Roblox commissions found on developer Discord servers. Copy proven bestsellers with a twist rather than untested trends. Once average playtime nears 11 minutes, test ads (2026 guidance: about 45 ad credits daily for a week, optimized by CTR) and scale only on healthy metrics. Keep shipping new content consistently. Note: the 2025 ad ramp (1,000 to 5,000-10,000 Robux/day) conflicts with the newer 2026 guidance, which takes precedence.

**Style:** Small-developer, devlog-style creator who shares his own revenue numbers. Favours cheap cash-grab games, UGC accessories, and commissions to fund work. Suits solo or budget-limited developers who want copyable formulas. Claims are anecdotal, drawn from a few games, with little controlled evidence.

## Playbook
1. **Pick a proven cash-grab concept** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Choose a cartoon or meme-style cash-grab with clear appeal, such as a cooking game inspired by Grow a Garden (_fVivJ4DtsA) or a meme obby (KsaRtStNO3s). Riding a trending meme can drive spikes, but it can also fade. Keep the concept cheap to build.
2. **Validate demand before copying** [[trend-research-and-validation]]: Do not copy a trend without checking demand: a Crew Kid-style accessory got zero sales in 24 hours (o4YPlpYdToo). For UGC, filter the Creator Store for bestselling accessories over the past day, then copy with your own twist (yM28oinpQIo).
3. **Fund development with commissions** [[team-and-budget]]: While building your own game, take commissions for Blender modelling, music, or scripting. Find them on developer Discord servers rather than Roblox's built-in commission page, which the creator does not recommend. Join communities such as Hidden Devs or RoDevs (JeBDqH7INjY, cqi75Z70Urk).
4. **Set commission rates and build a portfolio** [[pricing]]: Set a rate per job, then raise or lower it until you find the price that maximises total earnings and number of jobs. Ask clarifying questions before starting. Show finished work in a portfolio. Post listings with service, price, DM contact, image and description (JeBDqH7INjY).
5. **Build polished UI and retention hooks** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: Prioritise retention with polished UI, pop-ups and touch effects so players stay engaged longer. The creator links longer average playtime to algorithm reward (cqi75Z70Urk, BxK28jVj1MY).
6. **Add gamepasses and developer products** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: Include about five gamepasses and developer products combined, as ongoing purchase options. Avoid very cheap developer product prices, which drag down average revenue per user. Track which items sell (cqi75Z70Urk, KsaRtStNO3s).
7. **Hit ~11 minute average playtime** [[retention-d1-d7]]: Before running ads, get average playtime to about 11 minutes. The creator reports about 200 CCU with weak playtime versus about 1,000 CCU at 11 minutes with ads (BxK28jVj1MY). Use the Retention tab to check against the average.
8. **Check analytics before spending** [[analytics-metrics]]: In Creator Dashboard, check the Economy, Demographics and Retention tabs. Watch the source breakdown: home recommendations and friends drove most traffic in the meme obby (KsaRtStNO3s). Record Developer Products revenue before ads start so you can compare.
9. **Launch a short ad test** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Set the campaign goal to Plays and audience to All Players, or Recent Players to win back lapsed users. Run about 45 ad credits daily for roughly one week, continuously (BxK28jVj1MY). Upload several creatives and check CTR daily.
10. **Optimise creatives by CTR** [[thumbnails-icons]]: Upload several thumbnails or ad creatives, check CTR daily, and shift the ad toward the higher click-through rate (BxK28jVj1MY). The 2025 video used a different early spend of about 1,000 Robux/day for a week (_fVivJ4DtsA); this is superseded by the 2026 guidance.
11. **Scale ads only on healthy metrics** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Once metrics are green or yellow, scale spend. The 2025 ramp suggested 5,000-10,000 Robux/day (_fVivJ4DtsA). The 2026 video ties strong results to all metrics being green (10,000-100,000 CCU). Verify the units before reusing either figure.
12. **Publish UGC accessories through a group** [[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]: Upload shoulder accessories via Studio as avatar accessories, set the creator to a group, and publish. Price at a 50 Robux minimum. An older group earned about 4k/day from continuous additions (yM28oinpQIo).
13. **Keep uploading and enjoy the project** [[updates-live-ops]]: Keep adding new items and updates consistently. The creator says the older UGC group keeps earning this way, and that lack of enjoyment or discipline is why people fail (yM28oinpQIo, cqi75Z70Urk).
14. **Stay disciplined on the long run** [[mindset-and-strategy]]: Work on the game consistently and keep it enjoyable. Use the growth mechanics you have validated rather than chasing a single viral spike. The meme obby's ~800k visits came mostly from recommendations and friends, not sustained ads (KsaRtStNO3s).

## Common mistakes
- Copying a trending item without checking demand: zero sales in 24 hours (o4YPlpYdToo)
- Pricing developer products very cheaply, which drags down revenue per user (cqi75Z70Urk)
- Running ads before average playtime reaches about 11 minutes (BxK28jVj1MY)
- Pricing UGC accessories below 50 Robux (yM28oinpQIo)
- Starting a commission without asking the client enough clarifying questions (JeBDqH7INjY)
- Relying on the built-in Roblox commission page instead of developer Discord servers (JeBDqH7INjY)
- Setting a commission rate once and never testing other prices (JeBDqH7INjY)
- Treating one viral meme spike as sustainable growth (KsaRtStNO3s)
- Losing motivation or discipline and stopping work on the game (cqi75Z70Urk)
- Applying the 2025 ad ramp without checking the 2026 guidance, which supersedes it (_fVivJ4DtsA, BxK28jVj1MY)
