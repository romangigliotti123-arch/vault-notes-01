## Summary
Short devlog-style video by a small Roblox developer showing metrics for their new cartoon-style cooking game "Cook Food" (inspired by Grow a Garden): ~2.6k CCU, ~700k MAU, ~200k Robux/week after running ads. Ends with a suggested ad-spend ramp.

## Tips
- [2:03](https://youtu.be/_fVivJ4DtsA?t=123) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Start ads at about 1,000 Robux/day for the first week to get the game into the algorithm, then scale to 5,000–10,000 Robux/day once metrics are green/yellow.
- [0:56](https://youtu.be/_fVivJ4DtsA?t=56) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check the Economy, Demographics, and Retention tabs in Creator Dashboard to see in-game currency, where players are, and whether retention beats average.
- [1:15](https://youtu.be/_fVivJ4DtsA?t=75) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Track Developer Products revenue before and after turning on ads; the speaker saw revenue jump once ads started and kept climbing.
