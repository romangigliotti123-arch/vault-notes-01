- [0:30](https://youtu.be/Y5QtYzd95v8?t=30) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Mix player modes so empty servers still work: solo players face AI opponents while friends trigger a race-to-escape.
- [0:52](https://youtu.be/Y5QtYzd95v8?t=52) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Validate layout with a blueprint (interactive HTML mockup) before placing blocks, to check that multiple routes and flow work.

- [1:13](https://youtu.be/Y5QtYzd95v8?t=73) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Cut mechanics that testing shows hurt controls or tempo; the video dropped sliding because it made controls clumsy.
- [1:13](https://youtu.be/Y5QtYzd95v8?t=73) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give players a choice when a challenge is instant-death, such as hack a breaker box or sprint-jump past, so it doesn't feel unfair.
- [1:31](https://youtu.be/Y5QtYzd95v8?t=91) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Replace default Roblox gray buttons with a custom styled HUD; default UI reads as amateur. Optimize touch controls for mobile.
