## Summary
Japanese solo-dev walkthrough of making a Roblox escape game look less cheap: define the core loop with AI, pre-validate the layout with an HTML map, cut tempo-killing mechanics after playtests, polish UI and audio, fix camera and ending issues, then iterate on Reddit feedback. Transcript is heavily garbled, so details are uncertain.

## Tips
- [0:21](https://youtu.be/evyIBvylLdc?t=21) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Agree on a 30-second core loop before touching Studio (here: enter, hack, grab key, escape by helicopter), so the design is fixed first.
- [0:45](https://youtu.be/evyIBvylLdc?t=45) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Design the solo and friends modes differently: solo gets an AI companion, multiplayer becomes an escape race, so one build serves both.
- [0:45](https://youtu.be/evyIBvylLdc?t=45) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Sketch the map as a top-down HTML layout and plan several routes (rooftop parkour, central avenue, garden) before placing parts to avoid tangled flow.
- [1:11](https://youtu.be/evyIBvylLdc?t=71) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Replace default Roblox lighting and skybox with custom moonlight and atmosphere to stop the game looking like a starter place.
- [1:42](https://youtu.be/evyIBvylLdc?t=102) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Playtest and cut mechanics that slow pacing; a slide-under-laser mechanic was dropped because it made controls messy, and focusing on dash and jump improved feel.
- [2:08](https://youtu.be/evyIBvylLdc?t=128) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Replace flat grey default buttons with custom semi-transparent frosted-glass UI and optimise it for mobile touch.
- [2:08](https://youtu.be/evyIBvylLdc?t=128) **[[sound-and-music]]** Layer audio: ambient night wind, crisp sound effects, and a loud climax track at the escape moment to raise perceived quality.
- [2:28](https://youtu.be/evyIBvylLdc?t=148) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Watch playtesters for camera problems: a close camera hid boss attack telegraphs, so pull the camera back to a wider view.
- [2:28](https://youtu.be/evyIBvylLdc?t=148) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make the finale a visible payoff; a helicopter escape that just faded to black was replaced by a dramatic takeoff animation.
- [2:47](https://youtu.be/evyIBvylLdc?t=167) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Break harsh public feedback into specific tasks (lighting, laser circuit, garden stage) instead of reacting emotionally, then ship an update with a second stage.
- [2:47](https://youtu.be/evyIBvylLdc?t=167) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Post the game publicly for feedback early, then ship targeted updates addressing the critiques.
