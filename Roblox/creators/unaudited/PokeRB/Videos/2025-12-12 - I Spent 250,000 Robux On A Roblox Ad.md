# I Spent 250,000 Robux On A Roblox Ad..

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Watch](https://youtu.be/VSVCfNHK2IE) | PokeRB | 230,689 views | 2025-12-12

## Summary
Solo dev spends 1,000 USD (263,000 Robux, ~1/4 of account) on Roblox ads for a week promoting "Ball", a throw-and-steal ball game. Tracks impressions, plays, Robux earned, and player retention collapse after ad ends. Shows bugs, UI scaling pain, and the like/dislike ratio issue.

## Tips
- [2:44](https://youtu.be/VSVCfNHK2IE?t=164) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't rely on ads alone: after the ad ended, player count dropped to zero, so paid traffic must be backed by a game players return to.
- [4:06](https://youtu.be/VSVCfNHK2IE?t=246) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Test UI scaling early across screen sizes; the creator lost about 5 hours fighting UI scale, so plan layouts with scale-based sizing.
- [8:59](https://youtu.be/VSVCfNHK2IE?t=539) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Add an in-game tutorial when core mechanics are non-obvious; players didn't know how to throw and asked for help on day two.
- [9:42](https://youtu.be/VSVCfNHK2IE?t=582) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Watch like-to-dislike ratio early; 7 likes vs 17 dislikes (29% like rate) signalled problems that needed fixing before scaling spend.
