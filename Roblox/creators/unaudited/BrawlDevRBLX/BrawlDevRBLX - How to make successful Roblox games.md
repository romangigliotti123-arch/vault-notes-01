Not suited to developers looking for a practical playbook; use it for entertainment or as a warning about over-aggressive paywalls.

## Playbook
1. **Idea: treat an obby or social game as a satirical vehicle** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: The video's concept is an intentionally pay-to-win obby and social game. It is built for comedy, not as a recommended genre. Use it only as a reference for the joke format, not as a validated design.
2. **Build: gate progress with developer-product paywalls** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: Paywalls in the video include pay-per-jump at 5 Robux per jump, a blindness item, a Newbert chaser, and a nightclub gate at 50 Robux or Premium. This shows how paid gates can be placed, but it is a joke design, not a benchmark for pricing.
3. **Playtest with unannounced sessions** [[playtesting-and-feedback]]: Watch friends who do not know the design play the game. The creator used a friend unaware of the design to gauge frustration. Real reactions show where players get annoyed.
4. **Guard against spammable heavy-spend products** [[common-mistakes]]: A high-cost developer product (a 'nuke') could be spammed and made the game unplayable. The creator had to disable it mid-session. Test spam and server load before launch.
5. **Live ops: disable a broken product mid-session** [[updates-live-ops]]: When a product breaks the server during a live session, turn it off quickly to restore play. The video shows this as the response to a spam-driven break.

## Common mistakes
- Shipping a heavy-spend developer product that players can spam, which can break the server (YGmO7BXSwVg)
- Not testing high-cost products under repeated use before exposing them to players (YGmO7BXSwVg)
- Stacking paywalls on every progression step, which the video presents as frustrating even if it earns money (YGmO7BXSwVg)
