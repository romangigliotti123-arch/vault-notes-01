## Summary
A creator's 100-day devlog building his first Roblox game, starting as hide-and-seek and growing into a parkour game. Covers learning Lua with tutorials and ChatGPT, adding features from viewer comments, opening public testing via Discord, bug-fixing under load, burnout, and a rushed Halloween update. Ends with alpha plans for December.

## Tips
- [1:08](https://youtu.be/3Pa8QmFB_TE?t=68) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Open to public testing early; he got a couple of major bugs and about 67 minor ones, then testers reported faster than he could fix.
- [1:27](https://youtu.be/3Pa8QmFB_TE?t=87) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Taking feature requests from short-form comments led to adding an obby and a race mode, which expanded scope fast; he later said it probably wasn't a good idea.
- [2:30](https://youtu.be/3Pa8QmFB_TE?t=150) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add replayability beyond the core mechanic: round events (low gravity, infected mode), map voting, and collectible power-ups, so players don't just repeat hide and seek.
- [2:50](https://youtu.be/3Pa8QmFB_TE?t=170) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Don't rush seasonal events under pressure; his rushed Halloween update with currency and shop got mixed feedback.
