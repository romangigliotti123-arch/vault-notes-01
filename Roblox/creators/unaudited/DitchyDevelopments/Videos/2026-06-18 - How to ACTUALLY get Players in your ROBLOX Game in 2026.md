# How to ACTUALLY get Players in your ROBLOX Game in 2026

[Watch](https://youtu.be/cfLbW2j2VLs) | DitchyDevelopments | 31,968 views | 2026-06-18

## Summary
Argues that new Roblox games need players before they need ads: a fun, bug-free, retentive game first, then borrowed audiences from dev communities and small reviewers. The strongest method described is posting regular devlog shorts that tell a development story, involving viewers in decisions, and using that audience for playtesting and launch. Claims about 40,000 visits without ads come from the speaker's own game.

## Tips
- [0:41](https://youtu.be/cfLbW2j2VLs?t=41) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Don't buy ads until the game has solid gameplay and retention; the speaker's ad test peaked at ~20 players then fell to near zero within an hour.
- [1:23](https://youtu.be/cfLbW2j2VLs?t=83) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Get your game in front of people already interested in similar Roblox games, e.g. dev Discords, feedback groups, and dev playtest exchanges, rather than spamming links or cold DMing creators.
- [1:23](https://youtu.be/cfLbW2j2VLs?t=83) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Approach small creators who review games in Shorts or Discord communities; quick reviews from good games can bring a surprising number of players fast.
- [2:03](https://youtu.be/cfLbW2j2VLs?t=123) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Post short-form devlog content from day one showing game progress, since this built the audience that drove the speaker's ~40,000 visits.
- [2:44](https://youtu.be/cfLbW2j2VLs?t=164) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Frame content around a personal journey or challenge (e.g. a beginner becoming a Roblox dev, front-page in 30 days) so people follow even if they don't care about the game.
- [2:44](https://youtu.be/cfLbW2j2VLs?t=164) **[[community-and-discord]]** Involve viewers in development: let them name maps, suggest features, or improve mechanics, and implement some of their comment suggestions.
- [3:25](https://youtu.be/cfLbW2j2VLs?t=205) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Use the content audience as the pre-release test group; they will test updates, report bugs and give feedback before public launch.
- [3:25](https://youtu.be/cfLbW2j2VLs?t=205) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build the community before the game is finished so launch goes to people already following the journey, not to strangers.
- [3:25](https://youtu.be/cfLbW2j2VLs?t=205) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Stay consistent and balance building the game with building its community; working with other devs helps but solo is possible.
