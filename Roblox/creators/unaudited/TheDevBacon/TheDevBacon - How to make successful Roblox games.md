## Playbook
1. **Pick a differentiated idea** [[trend-research-and-validation]]: Assume millions of Roblox games exist and deliberately differentiate yours to stand out. Confirm the idea is distinct before building (sample tip, unattributed).
2. **Build a game that deserves players** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: Gameplay quality comes first. Per _XJVDRr2ZuA, the game must deserve players; promotion will not retain players if the gameplay is weak.
3. **Write a distinctive title and description** [[titles-descriptions]]: Avoid generic title patterns that everyone copies, since they make players less likely to click (sample tip, unattributed).
4. **Get friends to play early** [[discovery-algorithm]]: Recruit friends to play at launch to build concurrent players. Constant concurrency with good retention is what makes Roblox push the game into the algorithm (sample tip; _XJVDRr2ZuA mentions friends playing to trigger the algorithm).
5. **Promote on TikTok first** [[trailers-and-video-marketing]]: _XJVDRr2ZuA lists TikTok first among channels and says its algorithm is strong for reaching players. Also cites YouTube as a channel.
6. **Hire creators on SocialSeed carefully** [[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]: Verify your Roblox account, filter by price, and check average views, reviews and past videos. Then send an offer with a due date (sample tip; _XJVDRr2ZuA mentions hiring creators via SocialSeed).
7. **Give YouTubers a concrete reason to play** [[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]: Find YouTuber business emails and offer a concrete benefit such as free admin, since creators play only if it benefits them (sample tip).
8. **Use sponsored ads only as a last option** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: _XJVDRr2ZuA says sponsors work poorly and ads should be the last resort. The two Ad Manager videos (PEEr9ASA3no, x_OV46XrtrM) have no extractable content, so no ad-manager results are known.

## Common mistakes
- Promoting a game before gameplay deserves players; promotion cannot retain weak gameplay (_XJVDRr2ZuA)
- Copying generic title and description patterns that reduce clicks (sample tip)
- Launching without friends or early players, so the game never gets concurrency to enter the algorithm (sample tip)
- Relying on sponsored ads as a first channel; the speaker says they work poorly (_XJVDRr2ZuA)
- Hiring creators without checking average views, reviews, or past videos (sample tip)
- Pitching YouTubers without a concrete benefit such as free admin (sample tip)
