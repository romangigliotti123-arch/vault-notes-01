# BEST Ways To Advertise your ROBLOX Game

[Watch](https://youtu.be/_XJVDRr2ZuA) | TheDevBacon | 617 views | 2024-07-06

## Summary
Short overview of Roblox game promotion channels: TikTok, YouTube, hiring creators via SocialSeed, friends playing to trigger the algorithm, title/description choice, and sponsors (said to work poorly). Stresses that the game must deserve players.

## Tips
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/_XJVDRr2ZuA?t=0) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Assume millions of games exist; differentiate your game on purpose to stand out from the rest.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/_XJVDRr2ZuA?t=0) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Promote on TikTok first; the speaker calls its algorithm extraordinary for reaching players.
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/_XJVDRr2ZuA?t=40) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** Find YouTuber business emails and give them a concrete reason to play, such as free admin, because they play only if it benefits them.
- [1:22](https://youtu.be/_XJVDRr2ZuA?t=82) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** Hire Roblox creators on SocialSeed: verify your Roblox account, filter by price, check average views, reviews and past videos, then send an offer with a due date.
- [2:02](https://youtu.be/_XJVDRr2ZuA?t=122) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Get friends to play early; constant concurrent players with good retention make Roblox push the game into the algorithm.
- [2:02](https://youtu.be/_XJVDRr2ZuA?t=122) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Use a distinctive title and description; generic title patterns everyone copies make players less likely to click.
- [2:51](https://youtu.be/_XJVDRr2ZuA?t=171) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Roblox sponsored ads are the last option; the speaker says they don't work well.
- [2:51](https://youtu.be/_XJVDRr2ZuA?t=171) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build a game that deserves players; promotion will not retain players if the gameplay is weak.
