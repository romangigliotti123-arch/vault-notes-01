## Summary
Explains the two Robux-to-USD rates: the buy price players pay (about 1.25 cents per Robux for the 400 Robux pack) and the DevEx cash-out rate creators get (0.38 cents per Robux). Covers cash-out requirements, the 30% gamepass marketplace cut, and a rough 21% creator take. Promotes a calculator and tracker, which were excluded.

## Tips
- [1:40](https://youtu.be/vbzFbOUGERA?t=100) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Only earned Robux (gamepasses, developer products, Premium payouts) can be cashed out; purchased Robux can never be converted back to USD.
- [5:16](https://youtu.be/vbzFbOUGERA?t=316) **[[business-and-payouts]]** To cash out via DevEx you need at least 30,000 earned Robux, an active Premium subscription, a verified email and good account standing.
- [2:47](https://youtu.be/vbzFbOUGERA?t=167) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Plan gamepass revenue net of Roblox's 30% marketplace cut, which is taken before Robux reach the creator.
- [2:47](https://youtu.be/vbzFbOUGERA?t=167) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Budget around the DevEx rate (about $0.0038 per Robux), not the purchase price; creators keep roughly 21% of what players spend after fees.
