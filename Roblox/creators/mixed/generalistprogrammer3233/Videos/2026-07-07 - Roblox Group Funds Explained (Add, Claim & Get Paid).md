# Roblox Group Funds Explained (Add, Claim & Get Paid)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/XzxoIlpBnJA) | generalistprogrammer3233 | 1,158 views | 2026-07-07

## Summary
Explains how Roblox group funds work: group-owned games/items pool Robux into group funds, only the owner can pay out (one-time or recurring percentage splits), members need ~14 days in group before receiving payouts, and funds must be paid to a person then DevEx'd (two hops). Mostly explainer with a paid-product pitch.

## Tips
- [4:18](https://youtu.be/XzxoIlpBnJA?t=258) **[[team-and-budget]]** Add collaborators to the group well before the first payout; members must be in the group about 14 days before receiving any payout, with no override.
- [3:44](https://youtu.be/XzxoIlpBnJA?t=224) **[[team-and-budget]]** Use a recurring percentage payout to split group revenue with team members; percentages must total 100% or less, owner keeps the remainder.
- [2:34](https://youtu.be/XzxoIlpBnJA?t=154) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Let game earnings collect in group funds rather than buying your own group-owned items to fund the group; buying items loses the marketplace fee.
- [3:44](https://youtu.be/XzxoIlpBnJA?t=224) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Double-check payout amounts before sending; one-time group payouts cannot be undone.
- [5:08](https://youtu.be/XzxoIlpBnJA?t=308) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Group funds cannot be DevEx'd directly; pay group funds out to a person first, then that person DevExes their earned Robux.
- [5:50](https://youtu.be/XzxoIlpBnJA?t=350) **[[business-and-payouts]]** DevEx requires age 13+, verified email, active Premium, and minimum earned Robux balance; rate roughly $0.0035 per Robux (approximate, can change).
- [2:34](https://youtu.be/XzxoIlpBnJA?t=154) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Expect fresh sales to stay pending roughly 3 to 21 days before they become payable.
- [3:03](https://youtu.be/XzxoIlpBnJA?t=183) **[[team-and-budget]]** Only the group owner can issue payouts; treat the owner account as the sole key to group funds, since admins with the spend permission still cannot cash out.
