# How to Make Money on Roblox in 2026 (Every Method Ranked)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/pbt3ui5om8I) | generalistprogrammer3233 | 404 views | 2026-07-07

## Summary
Explains how Roblox earnings flow: Robux spent in your game, ~70% reaches you as earned Robux after Roblox's ~30% fee, then cashed out through DevEx. Compares game passes, developer products, premium payouts and group funds, and ranks them for a solo developer.

## Tips
- [2:28](https://youtu.be/pbt3ui5om8I?t=148) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Sell permanent perks (VIP, permanent boosts) as game passes; sell used-up items (currency, revives, ammo) as developer products.
- [2:28](https://youtu.be/pbt3ui5om8I?t=148) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Developer products have the higher ceiling because hooked players can buy them repeatedly. Sell only a few products and passes, don't overload the store.
- [3:29](https://youtu.be/pbt3ui5om8I?t=209) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make the game sticky: premium members' engaged time generates premium payouts passively, on top of anything you sell.
- [4:17](https://youtu.be/pbt3ui5om8I?t=257) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Do revenue math on roughly 70% of sales after Roblox's ~30% marketplace fee, not the full Robux price.
- [5:15](https://youtu.be/pbt3ui5om8I?t=315) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Only earned Robux count toward DevEx cash-outs; Robux players bought with real money do not.
- [5:15](https://youtu.be/pbt3ui5om8I?t=315) **[[business-and-payouts]]** DevEx requires 30,000 earned Robux minimum, age 13+, verified email, active Premium, and allows one cash-out request per calendar month.
- [5:41](https://youtu.be/pbt3ui5om8I?t=341) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Build a game people return to first, then monetise gently with a few well-placed passes and products that are not pay-to-win.
- [5:41](https://youtu.be/pbt3ui5om8I?t=341) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Reinvest early Robux into better assets, ads and updates rather than cashing out everything immediately.
- [6:42](https://youtu.be/pbt3ui5om8I?t=402) **[[team-and-budget]]** Use a group to collect game revenue and split it among collaborators by payout percentage (e.g. builder 15%, scripter 15%).
