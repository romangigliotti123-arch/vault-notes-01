# How Much Roblox Developers Make (From $0 to Millions)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/CFohgybeybM) | generalistprogrammer3233 | 140 views | 2026-07-03

## Summary
Explains the Roblox earnings pyramid across five tiers by daily players and monthly income, the extreme concentration of payouts, and revenue mix (passes/products ~55%, premium payouts ~30%, UGC ~9%). Walks through DevEx math ($0.0038 per earned Robux, 30% marketplace cut) and the 30,000 earned Robux cashout minimum. Mostly a pitch-adjacent overview with a self-promoted $29 blueprint.

## Tips
- [5:23](https://youtu.be/CFohgybeybM?t=323) **[[pricing]]** Remember players pay Robux but developers keep only ~70% after the marketplace cut; a 1,000 Robux spend yields ~700 earned Robux (~$2.66).
- [6:21](https://youtu.be/CFohgybeybM?t=381) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Only earned Robux count toward DevEx; purchased Robux can never be cashed out. Minimum withdrawal is 30,000 earned Robux (~$114).
- [1:14](https://youtu.be/CFohgybeybM?t=74) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Consistently update a game people want to play week after week; persistence, not talent or tools, is what separates earners early on.
- [3:10](https://youtu.be/CFohgybeybM?t=190) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Prioritise game passes and developer products; they were roughly 55% of income for a small successful game, the largest bucket.
- [3:10](https://youtu.be/CFohgybeybM?t=190) **[[monetisation-other]]** Premium payouts (about 30% of income for small earners) reward time spent by Premium members, so retention and session time directly add revenue.
- [6:04](https://youtu.be/CFohgybeybM?t=364) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Run your projected Robux numbers through the DevEx calculator before committing months to a project as a reality check.
