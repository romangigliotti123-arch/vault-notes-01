## Summary
Podcast interview with Yeucc (Axel Sun, lead dev of Regretevator) on Creators on the Blox. Covers his Myth-community origins (Happy Country, The Sun Experiment, NoCorey), building Regretevator's floor-based updates with a small team, replayability, mixed-style cohesion, awards, hiring friends, translation/AI moderation concerns, and plans to move shorts and merch off-platform.

## Tips
- [4:00](https://youtu.be/XtWTnz56ZpM?t=240) **[[team-and-budget]]** Hire people you already know and trust; Yeucc hires friends for skill and personal fit, and says it keeps the team fun and conflicts resolve well.
- [22:18](https://youtu.be/XtWTnz56ZpM?t=1338) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Weekly floor-based updates with 3-4 people is a heavy load; sustained weekly cadence for months is hard, so scope updates realistically.
- [38:04](https://youtu.be/XtWTnz56ZpM?t=2284) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build each new area as a self-contained floor with its own style and mini-game; contained themes keep mixed styles cohesive and make updates feel fresh.
- [40:50](https://youtu.be/XtWTnz56ZpM?t=2450) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Start from the build or aesthetic, then derive the mini-game from it; reverse-engineering a mini-game from a set piece works for him.

## Numbers
- [2:14:35](https://youtu.be/XtWTnz56ZpM?t=8075) Active team size: **3-4 people working on weekly updates** (Regretevator core dev team, per Yeucc)
- [24:25](https://youtu.be/XtWTnz56ZpM?t=1465) Floor visit time: **about 80 seconds per floor, roughly 1m20s** (Players experience a month of dev work in one floor visit)
- [19:59](https://youtu.be/XtWTnz56ZpM?t=1199) NoCorey peak players: **about 800** (Updated roughly every two weeks, consistent updates kept counts stable)
- [1:19:30](https://youtu.be/XtWTnz56ZpM?t=4770) Jersey Mike's large sub price: **$13 to $16** (Aside, not game-related)

## Games
- **Regretevator** [0:00](https://youtu.be/XtWTnz56ZpM?t=0): Floor-based world where each floor is a themed mini-game; strong characters and cohesive variety drive a devoted fandom, but replayability is a constant challenge.
- **The Sun Experiment** [17:55](https://youtu.be/XtWTnz56ZpM?t=1075): Popular early game abandoned after surge; lack of updates killed players and Yeucc regrets it.
- **NoCorey** [18:37](https://youtu.be/XtWTnz56ZpM?t=1117): Consistent biweekly updates held player counts; not mobile compatible limited TikTok-driven growth.
