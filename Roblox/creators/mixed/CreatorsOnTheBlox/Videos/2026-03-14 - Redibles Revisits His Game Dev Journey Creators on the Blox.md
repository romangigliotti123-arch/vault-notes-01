## Summary
Podcast interview with Redibles (Doors co-creator, 3D modeler, own studio QWiby) covering his path from Roblox hobbyist to Doors success, Floor 2 development lessons, team structure, merch/collabs, and advice to small developers. Mostly anecdote; few concrete growth tactics.

## Tips
- [48:11](https://youtu.be/VyBSvWSIkao?t=2891) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Make sure the game is fun before adding content; Doors' first test was a white hallway with two monsters, and it was fun, so they kept building.
- [48:53](https://youtu.be/VyBSvWSIkao?t=2933) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Keep shipping projects; skill and reputation build over years. Ragdoll Universe pulled about 1,000 players on a good day before Doors hit.
- [48:53](https://youtu.be/VyBSvWSIkao?t=2933) **[[team-and-budget]]** Build a visible portfolio of finished work; developers get hired by showing what they've made, not by cold-messaging teams with no experience.
- [50:14](https://youtu.be/VyBSvWSIkao?t=3014) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add daily runs and seasonal events as reasons for players to return and rejoin; replayability depends on game formula.

## Games
- **Doors** [11:02](https://youtu.be/VyBSvWSIkao?t=662): Built over ~2 years with breaks; launched 2022 and exploded to 100k+ players; Floor 2 jump showed need for upfront planning.
- **Ragdoll Universe** [07:25](https://youtu.be/VyBSvWSIkao?t=445): Passion project that never took off (~1,000 players on good days); shows value of persistence despite low early numbers.
- **Find the Troll Faces** [08:07](https://youtu.be/VyBSvWSIkao?t=487): Joke game that unexpectedly drew players; poor codebase and audio update broke it, forcing takedown.
