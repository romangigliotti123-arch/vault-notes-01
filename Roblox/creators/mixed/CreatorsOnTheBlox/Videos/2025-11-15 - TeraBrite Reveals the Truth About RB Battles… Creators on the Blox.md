## Summary
Podcast interview with TeraBrite (DJ Monopoly and Sabrina Brightite) covering their YouTube origins (intro contests, MySpace), pivot to Roblox via Minecraft and Jailbreak streams, and the history of RB Battles seasons 1-3 (bracket format, custom integrations, managing dev teams, delays, funding problems). Season 4 is unfunded.

## Tips
- [1:04:38](https://youtu.be/W8ghgtipQgI?t=3878) **[[common-mistakes]]** Expect games and creators to drop out late; RB Battles lost multiple integrations, so plan replacements and tell partners a project can take up to a year.
- [0:33:38](https://youtu.be/W8ghgtipQgI?t=2018) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Audience reacted to the big-name guest lineup more than to the core gameplay, so guest-driven hype drove views when plain collab videos stopped performing.
- [0:22:33](https://youtu.be/W8ghgtipQgI?t=1353) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't assume a partner will deliver just because they agreed. Working one-on-one with each game and tracking deadlines was needed; some devs went quiet for months.
- [0:42:26](https://youtu.be/W8ghgtipQgI?t=2546) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Pitch a full competition bracket with prizes to creators instead of single videos; it gave guests a reason to join and turned a dying channel around.
- [0:48:08](https://youtu.be/W8ghgtipQgI?t=2888) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Expect to learn project management fast when hiring devs: use ClickUp, daily calls and detailed specs, since the team underdelivered without close management.
- [0:45:23](https://youtu.be/W8ghgtipQgI?t=2723) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Let viewers compete via custom challenges in participating games, e.g., hidden swords across games, to turn audience requests into engagement.
- [1:26:23](https://youtu.be/W8ghgtipQgI?t=5183) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Fund a multi-game event upfront or from revenue: season 3 cost over $500,000 and was paid out of pocket, causing losses.
- [1:30:37](https://youtu.be/W8ghgtipQgI?t=5437) **[[business-and-payouts]]** A GoFundMe creates refund pressure and risk of underfunding; fund the project through a sustainable model that supports repeated seasons.
- [1:36:57](https://youtu.be/W8ghgtipQgI?t=5817) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Short-form content now outperforms long-form for many Roblox audiences; test shorts, live streams and long-form together to find what works.
- [0:19:08](https://youtu.be/W8ghgtipQgI?t=1148) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't rely on one channel for a niche audience; early growth came from community-driven intros and covers that others adopted.
