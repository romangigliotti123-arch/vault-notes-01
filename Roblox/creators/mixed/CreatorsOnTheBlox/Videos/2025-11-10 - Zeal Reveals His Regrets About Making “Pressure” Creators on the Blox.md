# Zeal Reveals His Regrets About Making “Pressure” | Creators on the Blox

[Watch](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I) | CreatorsOnTheBlox | 236,605 views | 2025-11-10

## Summary
Podcast interview with Zeal, modeler and team lead of Urban Shade (Pressure). Covers his path from modeling contractor to solo-funded developer, team management with 70+ people, lore and writing workflow, Roblox event experiences (Graveyard, Hunted, Takeover), regrets about Pressure's scope and similarity to Doors, the Heartburn and overhaul plans, merch via Makeship, and collaborations by asking nicely.

## Tips
- [12:27](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=747) **[[team-and-budget]]** Pay team members well and pay them per deliverable; Zeal says money is the main incentive for most of his staff, and he hires from friends and open forum posts.
- [15:13](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=913) **[[team-and-budget]]** Hire from a forum post by offering a concrete fixed fee (he cited $5,000) to find talent outside your circle.
- [13:11](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=791) **[[team-and-budget]]** Build leadership gradually: he grew from managing 3 people to 70+ over about two years rather than jumping in at scale.
- [17:33](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=1053) **[[team-and-budget]]** If you lead a project, you need to care visibly; team motivation follows the leader's commitment and vision.
- [20:17](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=1217) **[[team-and-budget]]** Split writing work: the lead gives concept and mechanics, dedicated writers produce documents and dialogue. Saves the lead time.
- [31:07](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=1867) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Make a small game you can beat in one sitting first. Zeal says jumping straight into a huge project made Pressure too big for its own good.
- [54:52](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=3292) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not build content you dislike; Zeal says his burnout came from working on features he did not like, and it grows the more you ship.
- [30:26](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=1826) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid making a game too similar to a hit like Doors; Zeal regrets that Pressure felt too close to it and got boring two years later.
- [22:19](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=1339) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Pick setting by logic and stakes: an underwater facility makes a containment story where threats cannot escape, which shapes the design.
- [24:59](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=1499) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not accept rushed event deadlines blindly; Zeal says the Graveyard event was rushed, too ambitious, and he is still not proud of it.
- [35:10](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=2110) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Roblox events give short deadlines (about one month), so scope to a small event; Zeal says Pressure overdid it and the scale surprised Roblox.
- [52:50](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=3170) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Expect updates to get harder over time: every update must add something new, so plan large reworks as a separate overhaul rather than patching.
- [51:27](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=3087) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Roblox's built-in default avatar and easy model import make content quick to produce, which Zeal cites as a reason to stay on the platform.
- [42:54](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=2574) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Collaborations are usually just asking nicely via DM or Discord; Zeal's examples include Iron Lung art credit and Toontown cosmetics.
- [58:54](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=3534) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Makeship plush merch is low-effort for developers: you supply designs and promote, they produce and sell; proceeds go to paying developers.
- [47:04](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=2824) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Stop caring too much about fan and celebrity attention; Zeal says it can break you mentally.
- [40:29](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=2429) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Use the revenue to fund the team that makes a better next game, instead of optimizing around a game you do not like.
- [34:29](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=2069) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan a rework with reuse in mind: Zeal's overhaul reuses existing enemies and systems with small changes to cut workload.
- [33:49](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=2029) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Cancel an event that is not ready rather than burn out the team; Zeal shelved a 30%-complete Halloween event.
