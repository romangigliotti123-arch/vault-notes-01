## Summary
Podcast interview with Roblox developer/animator Jasper on animation craft, Doors and Untitled Tag Game development, event production lessons, and future plans. Covers character-driven animation, front/back-end split, scoping events, and community-driven design ideas.

## Tips
- [12:26](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=746) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Ship a working 'it works' solution for one-time events; over-engineering the system (Halloween Spotlight) left no time to make the actual content.
- [40:56](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=2456) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Scope events conservatively. Jasper's ambitious Halloween Spotlight scope caused stress and a weaker reception than the Hunt Mega Edition.
- [1:04:13](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=3853) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Make all animation/cutscene pacing first, then have voice actors record to it; dialogue animation done after voice work forces rushing or dragging.
- [1:04:56](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=3896) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build the framework and groundwork first, then polish details on top. Make things exist before making them perfect.
- [1:06:20](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=3980) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Cut scope to something achievable but memorable, and leave slots for future additions rather than promising everything in the first pass.
- [22:22](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=1342) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Give each character a distinct gait and motive; reusing the same idle/walk across characters makes them feel generic. Work closely with designers.
- [24:28](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=1468) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Roblox rigs have no animated face, so convey emotion through body movement and posture.
- [27:10](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=1630) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Learn your character's vision before animating: know how it should move and what performance it needs, then move it from point A to B.
- [36:25](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=2185) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Separate front end (UI, animation, feel) from back end (generation, AI, item logic, state); back end feeds front end via signals.
- [1:09:46](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=4186) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Modern retro style works when polished: retro sounds and retro-style particle/stud assets keep it grounded and avoid the 'retro slop' criticism.
- [47:05](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=2825) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Do not rely on motivation to carry a large project; have clear examples and ready direction for collaborators so they can execute your vision.
- [49:51](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=2991) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build events that are fun for players; Untitled Tag Game gets steady CCU by returning with event content rather than constant updates.
- [52:36](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=3156) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Design for player expression and sandbox freedom: let players find their own routes and optimize playstyle rather than enforcing a closed rule set.
- [1:00:08](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=3608) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Games with user-made maps or content (e.g., Ball and Axe, Super Golf map editors) keep players hooked by giving infinite replayability.
- [1:29:03](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=5343) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Discord bots that pull in-game stats via slash commands are a satisfying, low-maintenance way to engage the community.
- [36:25](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=2185) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Chat-controlled events (Discord bot ingesting YouTube/Twitch chat to trigger in-game chaos) let streamers' audiences directly affect gameplay.
- [1:37:16](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=5836) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Pursue brand and platform deals and networking with partners like Twitch; these opened event and promotion opportunities.

## Games
- **Doors** [8:23](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=503): Jasper joined by redoing animations after seeing weak animation quality; animation quality drives character identity.
- **Untitled Tag Game** [40:15](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=2415): Retro-modern style, player expression (profiles, stickers), and events drove a steady, loyal player base.
- **Nico's Nextbots** [7:42](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=462): Went viral on TikTok in 2022 with a simple chase-away concept; community joined to help.
- **Parkour Reborn** [16:35](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=995): Corporate weekly-meeting workflow did not suit Jasper; he prefers informal calls and chipping away at tasks.
- **Ball and Axe** [59:28](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=3568): User-made map selection keeps players coming back; base game alone would be one-and-done.
- **Boneworks** [55:20](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=3320): Give a direction A to B and let players choose how to get there; sandbox freedom increases replayability.
- **Garry's Mod** [58:06](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=3486): Sold as a tool set; players made endless content, boosting longevity.
- **Something Evil Happens** [1:09:46](https://youtu.be/LWaw0KNwxyM?t=4186): Its polished modern retro style is a reference point for Untitled Tag Game's aesthetic.
