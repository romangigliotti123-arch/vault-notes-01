## Summary
Podcast interview with AyJFried (director/writer at Team I2R, the studio making official Piggy content). Covers Team I2R's history and role with Piggy, Piggy 64 parts one and two, the cross-examination and firewall cut content, and development delays caused by scripter turnover and perfectionism. Mostly lore and entertainment, with some dev-process lessons.

## Tips
- [14:39](https://youtu.be/JsiEVtWLwnA?t=879) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Set public release windows only with buffer; delays happen when unforeseen events (developer leaving, school, idea changes) hit a fixed plan.
- [28:48](https://youtu.be/JsiEVtWLwnA?t=1728) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Avoid overthinking and designing for imagined fan reactions; Part 2 was over-engineered and slower than the simpler, more natural Part 1.
- [30:11](https://youtu.be/JsiEVtWLwnA?t=1811) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Don't depend on one scripter; when scripters leave, the project stalls. Keep backup scripters lined up.
- [43:14](https://youtu.be/JsiEVtWLwnA?t=2594) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Scrap features built in unfamiliar code styles if they bug out in playtest and can't be fixed quickly; rewriting from scratch may be cheaper.
