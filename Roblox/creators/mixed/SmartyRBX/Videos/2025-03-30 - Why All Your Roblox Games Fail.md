# Why All Your Roblox Games Fail

[Watch](https://youtu.be/GPP0DzJGpXc) | SmartyRBX | 12,265 views | 2025-03-30

## Summary
Short motivational video arguing devs fail because they build games for themselves rather than player demand. Recommends reverse-engineering from player fantasy, watching the front page and TikTok, lurking in Discord and game chats, and testing one popular feature (status, skins, upgrades, community, simplicity) in an existing game.

## Tips
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/GPP0DzJGpXc?t=81) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Start from the player fantasy (speed, chaos, power, addiction) and work backwards from demand rather than from your own game idea.
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/GPP0DzJGpXc?t=81) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Watch the Roblox front page, TikTok videos, and comments/game chats to see what is blowing up before committing to a build.
- [2:45](https://youtu.be/GPP0DzJGpXc?t=165) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Ask players in existing games what they grind for, what they rage quit, and what makes them come back.
- [2:45](https://youtu.be/GPP0DzJGpXc?t=165) **[[community-and-discord]]** Lurk in Discord servers of your niche to study what players want.
- [2:45](https://youtu.be/GPP0DzJGpXc?t=165) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add one proven feature (status, skins, upgrades, community, or simplicity) to your current game and measure how players react before building more.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/GPP0DzJGpXc?t=0) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't spend weeks building a game based only on what you find fun; validate demand first, since unplayed polished games are the common failure.
- [2:04](https://youtu.be/GPP0DzJGpXc?t=124) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make sure someone wants a system before investing heavily in it, e.g. before spending 200 hours on it.
