# The Missing Piece to Roblox Development Success

[Watch](https://youtu.be/Lplz7ZFcKlQ) | SmartyRBX | 3,113 views | 2025-06-28

## Summary
Speaker (7-year Roblox dev) argues strategy beats passion: take commissions only as low-leverage income, build simple trend-aligned games with monetization for high-leverage income, judge quality by player fun/retention not dev standards. Uses Sussy Imposter Land and Grow a Garden as case studies; urges charging high and setting revision boundaries.

## Tips
- [1:05](https://youtu.be/Lplz7ZFcKlQ?t=65) **[[pricing]]** Charge what your work is worth and set revision limits and scope in writing before starting any commission; he undercharged and overworked on a 35,000 Robux job.
- [5:11](https://youtu.be/Lplz7ZFcKlQ?t=311) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Seed a new simple game with a small ad budget (he used 1,000 Robux) and keep advertising for weeks until players return without ads.
- [7:14](https://youtu.be/Lplz7ZFcKlQ?t=434) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Prioritise owning monetised games over commissions: a game earns passively 24/7 while commissions require your time.
- [10:03](https://youtu.be/Lplz7ZFcKlQ?t=603) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Make simple games first as a career step; a monetised simple game can earn 10-20x a builder's monthly commission income if it retains players.
- [12:00](https://youtu.be/Lplz7ZFcKlQ?t=720) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Build games players already want; follow proven trends and memes (e.g. Among Us/sus) rather than only your own passion, then add your own twist.
- [14:04](https://youtu.be/Lplz7ZFcKlQ?t=844) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Judge quality by player fun, retention and addictiveness, not by developer graphics standards; players measure quality differently.
- [18:10](https://youtu.be/Lplz7ZFcKlQ?t=1090) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Study successful games objectively and extract what they do right instead of dismissing them as cash-grab or slop.
- [21:35](https://youtu.be/Lplz7ZFcKlQ?t=1295) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Reinvest earnings from simple games into funding your own projects, paying scripters, builders, modelers and UI designers.
- [12:42](https://youtu.be/Lplz7ZFcKlQ?t=762) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Hobbyist assumption that polished, big-open-world games win is wrong; a simple meme game can reach millions of visits.
