# STOP Making Roblox Games Just For Passion

## Summary
Argues Roblox development is a business, not just art. Says hobbyist/passion-first mindset holds devs back and that strategy-first, money-aware, fast-shipping, market-driven thinking wins. Uses Grow a Garden as a case study: learn from its simple loop, retention hooks and stud-style graphics rather than judging it.
