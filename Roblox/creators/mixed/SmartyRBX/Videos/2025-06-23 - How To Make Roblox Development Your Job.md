# How To Make Roblox Development Your Job

[Watch](https://youtu.be/tpVRSnWwZWI) | SmartyRBX | 54,752 views | 2025-06-23

## Summary
Argues that Roblox development can be a career via many routes: learn one core skill, monetize via commissions, dev content (courses, tutoring, assets, affiliate), and UGC, then build simple games first and think long term rather than follow passion alone. Speaker is promotional (courses, Discord, affiliate links), and it is largely opinion, with few hard numbers.
