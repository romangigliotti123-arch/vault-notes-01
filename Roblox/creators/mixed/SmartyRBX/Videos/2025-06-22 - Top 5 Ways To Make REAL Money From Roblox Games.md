# Top 5 Ways To Make REAL Money From Roblox Games

[Watch](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk) | SmartyRBX | 7,992 views | 2025-06-22

## Summary
Career-strategy talk on earning from Roblox dev. Argues for moving from low-leverage skills (building, commissions) to high-leverage ones (scripting, game design, project management, content, info products). Covers monetizing via commissions, dev assets, info products, dev content, and affiliate programs, then reinvesting earnings into owning games, which is where the big money is.

## Tips
- [2:24](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=144) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Plan a career path beyond your first in-studio skill; commissions alone trade time directly for money and cap income.
- [6:13](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=373) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Learn scripting as a high-leverage skill so you can turn a map into a playable game without hiring a scripter, which can be expensive.
- [8:50](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=530) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Make simple, low-effort meme or trend-riding games quickly; Sussy Imposter Land rode the Sus/Among Us meme and found an audience.
- [9:32](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=572) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Advertise early with a paid promotion budget and keep adding guns and gamepasses; the game began drawing players organically afterward.
- [10:13](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=613) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Reinvest game earnings into funding new projects and hiring builders, scripters, and UI designers to scale output.
- [11:52](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=712) **[[team-and-budget]]** Hire specialists for building, modeling, UI, VFX and sound once you have capital; spend your time on design and new projects.
- [12:56](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=776) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Monetize a skill through simple, smaller deliverables (commissions, assets, courses) before attempting a full game.
- [14:19](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=859) **[[monetisation-other]]** Sell dev assets such as scripting systems, models, and music packs; they need less investment than a full game and earn passively.
- [15:00](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=900) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Sell info products like courses or one-on-one tutoring for scripting; many devs want to learn but few good teachers exist.
- [15:35](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=935) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Entertainment dev content draws big views and ad revenue; educational content converts better through paid courses. Choose based on goal.
- [17:39](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=1059) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Use affiliate programs to promote others' courses without creating your own product; the speaker cites roughly 30-50% commission.
- [19:42](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=1182) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Build a portfolio showing your work on Talent Hub and elsewhere; make quick sample builds if you have no examples yet.
- [20:23](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=1223) **[[common-mistakes]]** Set firm commission boundaries and terms of service; cap revisions to avoid clients stretching a job indefinitely.
- [21:05](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=1265) **[[monetisation-other]]** Put modeling work on the UGC catalog and run a UGC store promoted via social media.
- [21:31](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=1291) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Use earnings from monetized skills to fund your own games, since top games earn the Robux that matters most.
