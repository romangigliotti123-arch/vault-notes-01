# Why Good Roblox Games Get 0 Players

[Watch](https://youtu.be/_wqZyaTEsHQ) | SmartyRBX | 20,910 views | 2025-03-23

## Summary
Argues that polished, high-effort Roblox games often get no players while simple, fun, meme-style or easy-to-understand games succeed. Advises building simple games first, thinking like a business, checking trends, and launching an MVP then iterating on player feedback. Includes a self-promotional pitch about Jimmy games' 40M-visit meme games.

## Tips
- [2:10](https://youtu.be/_wqZyaTEsHQ?t=130) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Make simple, easy-to-build, easy-to-understand games first; if you can't get 50-100 players on a simple game, a complex one won't work either.
- [2:53](https://youtu.be/_wqZyaTEsHQ?t=173) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give players quick rewards and progression goals to work toward; simple tycoons, obbies and simulators perform well for this reason.
- [2:53](https://youtu.be/_wqZyaTEsHQ?t=173) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make the game easy to pick up within 30 seconds and avoid long tutorials before players can start playing.
- [3:24](https://youtu.be/_wqZyaTEsHQ?t=204) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Check whether people are searching for your game type; if nobody searches for it, they won't find it.
- [3:24](https://youtu.be/_wqZyaTEsHQ?t=204) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Build in a reason for players to come back every day, not just a one-time fun experience.
- [4:05](https://youtu.be/_wqZyaTEsHQ?t=245) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Study what's trending to learn why those games hold players, not to copy them outright.
- [4:24](https://youtu.be/_wqZyaTEsHQ?t=264) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Launch an MVP: the most barebones version that is already fun, then update based on player feedback.
- [5:06](https://youtu.be/_wqZyaTEsHQ?t=306) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Iterate post-launch using player feedback rather than trying to perfect the game before release.
- [5:06](https://youtu.be/_wqZyaTEsHQ?t=306) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Make the game players will enjoy playing, not the one you think is cool; the market decides success.
- [1:17](https://youtu.be/_wqZyaTEsHQ?t=77) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't spend months building a passion project with custom systems and handcrafted assets before validating demand; it often gets 0-2 players.
- [0:26](https://youtu.be/_wqZyaTEsHQ?t=26) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Think like a business owner, not just an artist; market demand matters more than your personal vision.
