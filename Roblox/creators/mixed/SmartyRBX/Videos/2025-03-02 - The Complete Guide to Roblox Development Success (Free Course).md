# The Complete Guide to Roblox Development Success (Free Course)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU) | SmartyRBX | 47,982 views | 2025-03-02

## Summary
Beginner-oriented Roblox development guide. Argues for mastering one skill first, monetising via commissions, selling assets, and making Dev content (edutainment), then using earnings to fund your own games. Core advice: make simple, quick-to-release games first, not complex dream projects. Mostly motivational, with some concrete tactics.

## Tips
- [1:54](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU?t=114) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Master one dev skill (e.g. scripting) before learning others; splitting focus across many skills slowed the speaker's progress.
- [8:58](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU?t=538) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Build a portfolio of many finished works, then host it on a portfolio site to win commissions.
- [9:40](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU?t=580) **[[community-and-discord]]** Promote commission work on X primarily; include a hire call-to-action in every post and post consistently.
- [11:44](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU?t=704) **[[monetisation-other]]** Sell assets on marketplaces like Clearly Dev for passive sales, or on your own store like Gumroad; promote on social media either way.
- [15:52](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU?t=952) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Dev content niche is less crowded than other YouTube niches; consistent posting can grow channels to hundreds or thousands of subs in months.
- [17:15](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU?t=1035) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Combine education with entertainment (edutainment) to hold viewers; pure entertainment like challenges gets the most views.
- [19:17](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU?t=1157) **[[monetisation-other]]** Sell a paid course on the skill from your tutorials; audiences who trust you as a teacher are more likely to buy.
- [21:42](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU?t=1302) **[[team-and-budget]]** You don't need to master every skill; cover gaps by commissioning or collaborating with other developers.
- [22:24](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU?t=1344) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Start with simple, quick-to-build games such as simulators, Hot Potato, freeze tag, or a quality obby.
- [23:07](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU?t=1387) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Prioritise results (players and Robux) over passion projects; release simple games in days or a week rather than months.
- [23:49](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU?t=1429) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't make your dream or complex game as your first project; long builds often lose motivation and fail to match players' tastes.
- [24:53](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU?t=1493) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Monetise early releases and reinvest earnings to fund bigger projects and hire developers later.
- [26:40](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU?t=1600) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid forming a group and managing a team before you have a playable game; the speaker's group lost years to this.
