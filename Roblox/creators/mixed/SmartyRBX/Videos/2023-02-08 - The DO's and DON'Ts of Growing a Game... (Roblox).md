# The DO's and DON'Ts of Growing a Game... (Roblox)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/rdWyuDEpGyk) | SmartyRBX | 272,404 views | 2023-02-08

## Summary
Generic talk on growing a Roblox game: make it high quality (bug-free, clear objectives, engaging), market it via ads, YouTubers and TikTok, promo codes, listen to players and fix bugs fast, follow Roblox rules, and keep going after failures.

## Tips
- [0:16](https://youtu.be/rdWyuDEpGyk?t=16) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make the game bug-free, good-looking, easy to understand, with clear objectives and always something for players to do, so they have fun and invite friends.
- [0:45](https://youtu.be/rdWyuDEpGyk?t=45) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Market the game actively: make strong ads and use Roblox sponsored ads and normal ads, rather than relying on the game being good alone.
- [0:45](https://youtu.be/rdWyuDEpGyk?t=45) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Reach out to Roblox YouTubers and use TikTok to spread the word about the game.
- [0:45](https://youtu.be/rdWyuDEpGyk?t=45) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Create promo codes and promote them on social channels to drive more people into the game.
- [1:19](https://youtu.be/rdWyuDEpGyk?t=79) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Listen to the player base; fix reported bugs and loading issues fast and tell players you are working on changes, since ignoring them causes declining playtime.
- [1:19](https://youtu.be/rdWyuDEpGyk?t=79) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Develop efficiently and communicate directly with your team so you can re-release updates as fast as possible.
- [1:57](https://youtu.be/rdWyuDEpGyk?t=117) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not break Roblox rules: no copying others' work, no gambling with Roblox items, no hacking, or the game is likely to be removed.
- [2:35](https://youtu.be/rdWyuDEpGyk?t=155) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect many failed projects before a hit; top developers keep making and improving games rather than quitting after one doesn't take off.
