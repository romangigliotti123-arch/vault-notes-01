## Summary
Motivational talk on why Roblox devs fail: avoid tutorial hell by building from scratch, avoid dream-game syndrome by starting with small projects, use discipline over motivation, and adapt to obstacles. Concludes with a three-step plan: start small, focus on progress, build-break-learn. No concrete monetisation, metrics, or platform data.

## Tips
- [1:06](https://youtu.be/_-LugzuCA38?t=66) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Avoid tutorial hell: pick one fundamentals tutorial, follow it, then rebuild the idea yourself without copying code or free models.
- [2:14](https://youtu.be/_-LugzuCA38?t=134) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Avoid dream game syndrome: start with simple games like a tycoon or obby, finish them, then scale up to bigger projects.
- [4:06](https://youtu.be/_-LugzuCA38?t=246) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Build discipline over waiting for motivation; even 5 minutes of work when you don't feel like it builds momentum.
- [5:24](https://youtu.be/_-LugzuCA38?t=324) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** When blocked, adapt: use free assets, learn Blender, Google the problem, watch a video, ask on the DevForum, or rework the feature.
- [7:04](https://youtu.be/_-LugzuCA38?t=424) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Start small and finish the project even if imperfect; every finished project teaches lessons for the next one.
- [7:36](https://youtu.be/_-LugzuCA38?t=456) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Focus on progress over perfection; a first game is a stepping stone, not expected to be amazing.
- [7:36](https://youtu.be/_-LugzuCA38?t=456) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build, break, and learn on every project; small consistent steps compound more than a single giant leap.
