## Summary
Speaker Smarty RBX argues Roblox developers fail by pursuing dream games with idealism and passion before strategy. Advises tempering expectations, starting with simple games (obby, tycoon, simulator), shipping imperfect games, monetizing to fund bigger projects and hiring, and focusing on results, citing Grow a Garden and Big Games as examples.
