# ROBLOX IS A BUSINESS. Adapt or Quit.

[Watch](https://youtu.be/exOJsFcer1Q) | SmartyRBX | 5,482 views | 2025-07-21

## Summary
Argues Roblox devs fail from lack of business skill and victim mindset. Advises strategy-first: take responsibility, analyze failures, market work, build simple games first, match player desires, and treat passion as secondary to business. Mostly motivational, few concrete tactics.

## Tips
- [2:11](https://youtu.be/exOJsFcer1Q?t=131) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Take responsibility for results; when metrics are bad, diagnose what you did wrong and change it rather than blaming players.
- [3:45](https://youtu.be/exOJsFcer1Q?t=225) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Read funnel signals: if players join but don't buy gamepasses, examine why the pass is unappealing and revise or add passes.
- [3:45](https://youtu.be/exOJsFcer1Q?t=225) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't post commission or dev work once and forget it; show actual images and reply to big accounts in the community to build reach.
- [4:26](https://youtu.be/exOJsFcer1Q?t=266) **[[community-and-discord]]** Grow an audience by giving away free resources to followers who engage, as in free GFX templates or build packs.
- [6:14](https://youtu.be/exOJsFcer1Q?t=374) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Follow proven trends like Steal a Brain Rot or Grow a Garden but inject your own unique creative twist.
- [7:22](https://youtu.be/exOJsFcer1Q?t=442) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Make the game better than existing competitors; with heavy supply in 2025, decent games rarely blow up on their own.
- [8:04](https://youtu.be/exOJsFcer1Q?t=484) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Judge your game by what players want, not personal taste; step into the player's shoes.
- [8:38](https://youtu.be/exOJsFcer1Q?t=518) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Prioritise fun over visuals; a realistic-looking but boring game loses players within 20-30 minutes.
- [9:19](https://youtu.be/exOJsFcer1Q?t=559) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Make simple games first, since most Roblox players are young and on mobile; build up to complex projects later.
- [10:40](https://youtu.be/exOJsFcer1Q?t=640) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Avoid starting with a complex game as a new dev; scope down to avoid burnout from abandoning large projects.
