## Summary

Covers cause-effect-meaning mapping of limiting beliefs, reframing (content and context), and reinforcing new beliefs through repetition. - [5:14](https://youtu.be/kGVYnAI5PVY?t=314) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Model the beliefs of people who already succeeded at what you want, temporarily trusting their views over your own, then test them through action.
