# 3 Ways To Promote Your Roblox Game WITHOUT Robux or Ads.

[Watch](https://youtu.be/iD_8dYacd-s) | SmartyRBX | 158,718 views | 2023-08-21

## Summary
Covers three low-cost ways to promote a Roblox game without Robux or ads: targeting in-platform search terms and trends, TikTok clip marketing (using the Tsunami game case study), and approaching smaller YouTubers with unique content or exclusive perks. Claims ads are weaker after recent Roblox updates.

## Tips
- [1:04](https://youtu.be/iD_8dYacd-s?t=64) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Search Roblox's search bar with 'simulators for', 'simulators with', 'simulators about' to find what players actually search; target underserved phrasings.
- [0:22](https://youtu.be/iD_8dYacd-s?t=22) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Use Google Trends and Roblox search autocomplete to spot trending game types and unused keywords; build for queries with few existing games.
- [0:22](https://youtu.be/iD_8dYacd-s?t=22) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Target search with a game matching a niche query that has few competing games; this is described as the easiest way to get visits.
- [1:15](https://youtu.be/iD_8dYacd-s?t=75) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Design your game with clip-worthy funny or surprising moments, then post those moments as short TikTok videos.
- [1:15](https://youtu.be/iD_8dYacd-s?t=75) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Encourage other people to post clips of your game too, not just your own account.
- [2:36](https://youtu.be/iD_8dYacd-s?t=156) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Fix launch bugs before a TikTok push; Tsunami's early bugs stalled players until the viral wave hit.
- [3:30](https://youtu.be/iD_8dYacd-s?t=210) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Approach smaller YouTubers rather than big ones; they are more likely to play and feature your game.
- [3:30](https://youtu.be/iD_8dYacd-s?t=210) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Offer influencers unique value such as exclusive access or a pet themed after them; make the deal beneficial for both sides.
