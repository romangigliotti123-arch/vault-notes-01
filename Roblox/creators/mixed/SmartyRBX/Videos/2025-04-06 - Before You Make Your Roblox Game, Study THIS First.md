# Before You Make Your Roblox Game, Study THIS First

[Watch](https://youtu.be/N2FND74tgGE) | SmartyRBX | 91,601 views | 2025-04-06

## Summary
Argues Roblox games succeed by designing around player psychology rather than polish. Covers five drives: progression, status, FOMO, mastery, and community. Advises studying popular games, reading player comments, and reverse-engineering trigger-action-reward loops before building. Mostly motivational, with few concrete figures.

## Tips
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/N2FND74tgGE?t=0) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Design from player psychology first (desire, then mechanics), not from features, maps, or effects you think are fun.
- [1:27](https://youtu.be/N2FND74tgGE?t=87) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Show visible growth constantly (XP bars, multipliers, prestige) so every action feels like forward movement; the speaker says games feel dead by minute 10 otherwise.
- [2:07](https://youtu.be/N2FND74tgGE?t=127) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give players rare, showable status items (skins, pets, achievements, leaderboard ranks, stats like total kills) to chase and flex.
- [2:51](https://youtu.be/N2FND74tgGE?t=171) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Use daily rewards, limited events, and login streaks; frame them as loss ('you lose this if you don't return tomorrow') rather than a plain reward.
- [3:19](https://youtu.be/N2FND74tgGE?t=199) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Include learnable systems with depth to master, such as crafting combos or hidden strategies, so players feel smart when they figure them out.
- [3:41](https://youtu.be/N2FND74tgGE?t=221) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Build systems that reward interaction (trading, raids, group quests); the speaker claims most singleplayer Roblox games fail.
- [4:16](https://youtu.be/N2FND74tgGE?t=256) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Lurk in popular games and watch where players go, what they click, ignore, and stay for.
- [4:37](https://youtu.be/N2FND74tgGE?t=277) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Read player comments, reviews, TikToks, YouTube comments, and game Discords to learn the language of player pain and desire.
- [4:59](https://youtu.be/N2FND74tgGE?t=299) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Map each dopamine loop as trigger, action, reward; if a game lacks a clear one, it is missing its core design.
