# How I Made $100,000 With Roblox Commissions (And How You Can Do The Same!)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/fEcFzlBQew8) | vuukstudios7635 | 38,552 views | 2023-12-25

## Summary
A Roblox developer's seven-step career roadmap: work free to build a portfolio, move from commissions paid on completion to upfront payment, sell packs in a store, grow an audience, then take equity deals and build games. Mostly promotes his paid Academy; income claims are unverified.

## Tips
- [4:51](https://youtu.be/fEcFzlBQew8?t=291) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Start by working free to build a portfolio of finished work; he calls this sweat equity.
- [5:32](https://youtu.be/fEcFzlBQew8?t=332) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Learn to full-stack (scripting, VFX, animation, building) rather than specialising in one skill, so you can deliver whole projects.
- [6:55](https://youtu.be/fEcFzlBQew8?t=415) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Take commissions paid 100% on completion first, then move to 50/50 splits, then 100% upfront once you have demand.
- [7:35](https://youtu.be/fEcFzlBQew8?t=455) **[[common-mistakes]]** Expect to get scammed early on; he says it is part of the process to reach paid-on-completion work.
- [8:16](https://youtu.be/fEcFzlBQew8?t=496) **[[monetisation-other]]** Once commissions flow, sell pre-made packs in your own store (he cites Payhip or Shopify) at roughly $10-40 versus $100-200 commissions.
- [9:37](https://youtu.be/fEcFzlBQew8?t=577) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Post short-form content daily and long-form weekly on YouTube, TikTok and Twitter to build an audience that sources clients.
- [10:49](https://youtu.be/fEcFzlBQew8?t=649) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not take percentage-of-game deals from unproven developers early; he says these often never pay out.
- [12:52](https://youtu.be/fEcFzlBQew8?t=772) **[[studio-business-funding-and-publishing]]** Percentage deals on front-page-proven teams: he cites 5-8% equity, paid as a share of ongoing revenue from weekly updates.
- [16:24](https://youtu.be/fEcFzlBQew8?t=984) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Only build your own game after you have an established reputation and a trusted team; he says it took him years.
- [17:11](https://youtu.be/fEcFzlBQew8?t=1031) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Find a mentor or accountability structure; he says self-guided learning without one is why most people stall.
