# How My Roblox Game Makes $95,110/mo

[Watch](https://youtu.be/0ENzqNB_EUo) | vuukstudios7635 | 21,687 views | 2026-10-11

## Summary
Creator's Roblox game Ride Storm (~10K CCU, ~$95K/mo Robux) shares lessons: run ads every update, price for your own demographic, listen to Discord feedback, create a new experience each join, use AI as a tool not a replacement, aim higher.

## Tips
- [2:06](https://youtu.be/0ENzqNB_EUo?t=126) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** Run ads (Roblox ads manager) on every major update, especially weekends; a game with 5-10K CCU can be revived by ads, as one of his games went from idle to ~4K CCU.
- [4:20](https://youtu.be/0ENzqNB_EUo?t=260) **[[pricing]]** Check your actual player country breakdown and price for it; he halved dev product and gamepass prices for a mostly Mexico audience and revenue rose about 5x.
- [5:35](https://youtu.be/0ENzqNB_EUo?t=335) **[[community-and-discord]]** Run a Discord and act on feedback each update; his 50K-member server drove content ideas that kept players longer.
- [7:17](https://youtu.be/0ENzqNB_EUo?t=437) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Design for clippable, social moments; TikTok and short clips drove large organic reach, including Spanish-language videos with 200K likes.
- [8:39](https://youtu.be/0ENzqNB_EUo?t=519) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Make every join feel like a new experience through open-ended roleplay and expandable systems so players return rather than grind a single daily reward.
- [10:49](https://youtu.be/0ENzqNB_EUo?t=649) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Use AI to speed up development but build core content like map, UI and scripting yourself; players can often spot AI UI and leave.
- [12:51](https://youtu.be/0ENzqNB_EUo?t=771) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Connect Claude or GPT to Studio via MCP to scan your code and infrastructure for improvements, rather than asking AI to build the whole game.
- [14:14](https://youtu.be/0ENzqNB_EUo?t=854) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Aim for longer-lived games with sustained revenue rather than quick low-quality releases that die within weeks.
- [5:35](https://youtu.be/0ENzqNB_EUo?t=335) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Avoid selling your game to companies unless the offer is exceptional; he argues they often neglect or kill the game.
