# How To Actually Make A Front-Page ROBLOX Game..

[Watch](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ) | vuukstudios7635 | 2,934 views | 2026-06-16

## Summary
Vux Studios explains a checklist for Roblox game ideas (proven outside Roblox, 12+ min playtime, one-sentence pitch, kid-understandable onboarding, grind, social, monetization) and how Roblox's retrieval/ranking pipeline feeds home recommendations, with a 7-14 day window to iterate or move on.

## Tips
- [1:32](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=92) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Base your game on a concept already proven outside Roblox or in another Roblox game; the core loop should be proven even if you add a twist.
- [2:55](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=175) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Don't direct-copy a hit (e.g. swapping tsunami for meteor); change the core loop with a new spin, as Kick a Lucky Block did with escape tsunami.
- [4:15](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=255) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Aim for at least 10-12 minutes of playtime on release; 20+ minutes for a big game, since low playtime stops the algorithm pushing the game.
- [7:00](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=420) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make the game understandable without a tutorial; you should be able to explain it in one sentence. Test with a young kid or relative.
- [8:23](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=503) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build in a social factor (trading, viewing others' bases); avoid solo-only games, which rarely survive long.
- [9:03](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=543) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Build proper monetization from the start; the vendor says better monetization gets the game pushed further by the algorithm.
- [9:44](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=584) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Have a child or relative play for 10-15 minutes; if they call it boring or confusing, simplify the concept.
- [11:20](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=680) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Understand the pipeline: retrieval/candidates filtered by same-genre ads, then ranking by playtime, CTR, PTR, D1/D7 retention and monetization for home recommendations.
- [14:06](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=846) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Give a game 7-14 days running ads to hit home recommendations; if it doesn't, stop investing and move to the next project.
- [14:48](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=888) **[[updates-live-ops]]** During the 7-14 day window, check feedback daily, fix reported bugs and iterate on the loop to improve playtime, as his team did.
- [14:48](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=888) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Don't get stuck on one game; if it never gets home recommendations, abandon it and make another, since time is limited.
- [17:32](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=1052) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Thumbnails matter about half the work for discovery; aim for 3-4% CTR minimum, hire a thumbnail artist or use a thumbnail tool.
- [18:12](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=1092) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check Statistics > Analytics > Acquisition for a Home Recommendations bar; once it appears, turn off ads and let organic recommendations run.
- [18:52](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=1132) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Once a game hits home recommendations, update it weekly; if it doesn't, stop updating and start a new project.
- [18:52](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=1132) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Most revenue comes after home recommendations hit; earlier revenue is minimal, so judge a game by that milestone.
- [19:33](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=1173) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect most hits to last about one to two months; make many games quickly rather than relying on one long-lived title.
