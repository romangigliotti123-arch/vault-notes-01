# How I Develop A 20K CCU Roblox Game

[Watch](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4) | vuukstudios7635 | 11,321 views | 2026-08-06

## Summary
A lead developer of Ride Storm, a 20K+ CCU motorbike roleplay game, explains how the team runs updates and what drove growth. Key points: engaged Discord and feedback monitoring, retention as the main algorithm signal, matching monetisation to audience demographics, building a loyal social niche, and fantasy fulfillment. Mostly anecdotal, with light actionable advice.

## Tips
- [2:41](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=161) **[[community-and-discord]]** Run a Discord server and check the feedback channel daily for bug reports and complaints; your most engaged players give the most useful input.
- [3:23](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=203) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Watch update ratings after each release; a dislike spike after an update signals a problem to fix quickly, as happened after one of their updates.
- [3:23](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=203) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Ship updates almost weekly or biweekly so the game never sits idle; a small team (lead dev plus builders and planners) can sustain that cadence.
- [5:21](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=321) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Prioritise retention above all; high retention is the strongest signal for Roblox's algorithm and drives discovery more than anything else.
- [6:04](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=364) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Pick a mechanic that supports long sessions, such as open-ended driving, so players stay engaged for 30-50 minutes on average.
- [6:41](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=401) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Check your audience's country breakdown in analytics and price gamepasses accordingly; low-spend regions convert poorly at high prices.
- [7:22](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=442) **[[pricing]]** Do not set high Robux prices if your community is mostly from low-spending regions; expensive items cut conversion sharply.
- [8:04](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=484) **[[analytics-metrics]]** When conversion is poor, first check whether pricing matches the demographic you actually attract, before blaming the game.
- [8:45](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=525) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Match social content language to your core audience; their TikTok is mostly Spanish because that is the game's playerbase.
- [9:26](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=566) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Aim for a long-lived niche game with a loyal fanbase rather than a fad cash grab; long-lived games can be sold for significant sums later.
- [11:28](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=688) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not rely on a peak alone; a game with no loyal fanbase dies when its hype ends, as their 5K CCU game fell to about 500 players.
- [11:59](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=719) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Build a social aspect such as racing, roleplay, or police chases so players log in daily to play with friends.
- [13:06](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=786) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Design around a fantasy the audience already has, such as riding motorbikes, and position your game as the accessible way to fulfil it.
- [13:06](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=786) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Frame your game as the solution to a fantasy players cannot afford or safely pursue in real life, then build features around it.
