# How To EASILY Get Roblox's 500 Highly Engaged Players

## Summary
Explains Roblox's new rule that games start 16+ and how to unlock all-ages by reaching 500 "highly engaged" players (age-verified 16+, Robux purchase in last 60 days). Covers two routes: home-recommendation algorithm traction, or a refundable 100k Robux publishing fee. Shares the speaker's own game metrics (playtime, play-through rate, day 1 vs day 7 retention).

- [3:31](https://youtu.be/Oo3sCq-TQXs?t=211) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Ads won't reach 500 highly engaged players. Only two routes work: home-recommendation traction or the publishing fee.

- [8:28](https://youtu.be/Oo3sCq-TQXs?t=508) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Aim for average playtime above 10 minutes; the speaker's game ran 13-15 minutes and that was enough to get recommended.
- [9:10](https://youtu.be/Oo3sCq-TQXs?t=550) **[[monetisation-other]]** Strong payer conversion and revenue per paying user on a small player base improve recommendation odds. Place monetisation where it converts best.
