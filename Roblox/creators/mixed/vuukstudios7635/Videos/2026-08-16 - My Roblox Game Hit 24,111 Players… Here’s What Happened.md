# My Roblox Game Hit 24,111 Players… Here’s What Happened

[Watch](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E) | vuukstudios7635 | 9,672 views | 2026-08-16

## Summary
Creator (VuxVux Studios) on what keeps Roblox games growing: tune the first 30 minutes for easy progression, target D1/D7/D30 retention with rewards and events, price gamepasses by player region, and treat the first 7-14 days after release as make-or-break with active feedback and fixes. Cites his own games as examples.

## Tips
- [2:36](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=156) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make the first 30 minutes as easy as possible: boost luck, cash, or progression for new players to hook them early.
- [4:38](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=278) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Players should understand the full core loop within 2-5 minutes. If they don't, simplify the loop or the onboarding.
- [5:20](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=320) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Keep tutorials short. He saw complaints from players about a 10+ minute tutorial that they wanted to skip.
- [6:01](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=361) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** After the loop is learned, set a goal attainable in 10-15 minutes, then stack another reward after that.
- [6:21](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=381) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Create a problem, then sell the solution: e.g. make collecting manual, then sell a cheap auto-collect gamepass.
- [7:42](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=462) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Offer offline earnings and daily rewards; put an OP item on day 2 to lift day-1 return.
- [8:23](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=503) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** For D7, run community events (e.g. motorbike gatherings) and push updates weekly, ideally Friday or Saturday.
- [8:23](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=503) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give an OP reward on day 7 to keep players who have started to fall for the game.
- [9:04](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=544) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** For D30, the game needs a core mechanic (combat, building, riding) that creates hardcore fans who return after a month.
- [9:42](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=582) **[[pricing]]** Check which regions play most and set gamepass prices accordingly; North American players can support higher prices than Asian or South American markets.
- [11:05](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=665) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Avoid pure pay-to-win; balance ease of access so purchases speed up the grind rather than make players instantly best.
- [11:05](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=665) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Prioritise purchases that speed up grinding; cosmetics work in some genres but depend on the game.
- [13:25](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=805) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track average playtime: aim for 10+ minutes during ads and 20+ once on the algorithm. Low playtime predicts a flop.
- [14:09](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=849) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Once on the algorithm, stats should improve. If they stay flat versus the ad period, change something in the game.
- [14:50](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=890) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Benchmarks he aims for: D1 retention 12-15%, D7 2-3%, pay conversion and QPTR 2-3%.
- [15:30](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=930) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Check whether your game ranks first in search for its genre keywords and whether friend joins drive traffic.
- [15:30](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=930) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Design for friend joins: players who see friends playing join and are more likely to spend. Concrete Simulator gained traction this way.
- [16:27](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=987) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Treat the first 7-14 days after release as make-or-break: collect feedback and ship fixes during this window.
- [17:07](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=1027) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Join your own server while ads run, watch where players leave, and ask them in-game what is good and bad.
- [17:49](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=1069) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** If the game has not picked up the algorithm after 14 days of fixes, consider stopping and moving on.
