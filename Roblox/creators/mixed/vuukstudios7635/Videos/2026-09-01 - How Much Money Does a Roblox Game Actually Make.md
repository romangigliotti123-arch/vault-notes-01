# How Much Money Does a Roblox Game Actually Make?

[Watch](https://youtu.be/2Q4NqQikzsY) | vuukstudios7635 | 29,641 views | 2026-09-01

## Summary
Creator (Vux Studios) breaks down Roblox revenue stages for a ~5K CCU game: ad-driven traffic earns little, home-recommendation traffic earns most, and "hard push" spikes can yield thousands per day. Covers demographics-based pricing, raising prices during pushes, creating problems to sell solutions, and building a playable loop before monetizing.

## Tips
- [1:22](https://youtu.be/2Q4NqQikzsY?t=82) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Market demand must match your game; a good idea on a dying trend (e.g. tsunami escape games) will flop regardless of execution.
- [3:23](https://youtu.be/2Q4NqQikzsY?t=203) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Run ads again on each major update of a long-running game to trigger a new algorithm push; the push itself is largely luck-based.
- [4:04](https://youtu.be/2Q4NqQikzsY?t=244) **[[pricing]]** Choose 18+ DevEx-qualified audience only if your game suits older players; it pays ~42% more per Robux but yields fewer players.
- [4:45](https://youtu.be/2Q4NqQikzsY?t=285) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Check creator dashboard demographics; US spenders justify higher gamepass prices, while low-GDP regions need cheaper offers.
- [5:26](https://youtu.be/2Q4NqQikzsY?t=326) **[[pricing]]** Raise gamepass and dev product prices during a big push, then revert to normal prices when traffic settles.
- [6:06](https://youtu.be/2Q4NqQikzsY?t=366) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Create an in-game problem (slow, tedious, time-consuming, not a hard blocker) and sell the solution as the purchase.
- [6:47](https://youtu.be/2Q4NqQikzsY?t=407) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Offers must feel clearly worth it to players (e.g. 99 Robux for auto skip); players won't buy something that seems just okay.
- [8:50](https://youtu.be/2Q4NqQikzsY?t=530) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build a playable loop first, release it, run ads, and judge via stats whether to continue or cut the game.
- [9:31](https://youtu.be/2Q4NqQikzsY?t=571) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Keep monetisation bare-bones at release; focus on retention, then lock in the full monetisation plan after home recommendations appear.
- [10:12](https://youtu.be/2Q4NqQikzsY?t=612) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Make the loop replayable and engaging within 5 minutes; keep onboarding easy and avoid grind in the first 5-10 minutes where most players leave.
- [10:53](https://youtu.be/2Q4NqQikzsY?t=653) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Give an early shiny reward (e.g. after 5 minutes) to create a dopamine rush that keeps new players playing.
- [10:53](https://youtu.be/2Q4NqQikzsY?t=653) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give players a reason to return the next day: daily rewards, daily missions, or events.
- [11:34](https://youtu.be/2Q4NqQikzsY?t=694) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make the game fulfil a clear player fantasy (raising animals, riding motorcycles, skydiving).
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/2Q4NqQikzsY?t=40) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track hourly spend and peak Robux on the stats page, and use revenue sources and DevEx figures to judge performance.
