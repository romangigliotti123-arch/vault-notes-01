# I Made $300,000 Making Roblox Games, Here's How...

## Summary
Creator (about 7 years on Roblox, about $300k earned) explains Roblox income paths: game creation, commissions, selling games to investors, and YouTube. Covers why commissions are the beginner entry point, how to avoid bad percentage deals, DevEx payout math, and the time needed for hits. Much of the video promotes his paid Academy.

## Tips
- [1:32](https://youtu.be/xBPJnMgLoUE?t=92) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Don't solo-build anime or Battlegrounds-style games as a beginner; well-funded studios dominate those genres.
- [7:18](https://youtu.be/xBPJnMgLoUE?t=438) **[[team-and-budget]]** Beginners should start with paid commissions (building, scripting) to earn money and portfolio while learning.
- [8:42](https://youtu.be/xBPJnMgLoUE?t=522) **[[community-and-discord]]** Find commission work by joining Discord servers, DMing people who post requests, and offering your services directly.
- [9:23](https://youtu.be/xBPJnMgLoUE?t=563) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Don't accept percentage-of-revenue deals from unproven developers with no released games; the scam risk is high.
- [10:04](https://youtu.be/xBPJnMgLoUE?t=604) **[[team-and-budget]]** Only take percentage deals from developers with a track record, e.g. two to three front-page games, before they can pay you in revenue share.
- [12:06](https://youtu.be/xBPJnMgLoUE?t=726) **[[pricing]]** Expect payouts in DevEx rates, far below Robux purchase value; 1,000 Robux is roughly one dollar at DevEx rates, so price work accordingly.
- [16:12](https://youtu.be/xBPJnMgLoUE?t=972) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect years of work and many failures before a hit; consistency and patience are the prerequisites.
- [6:20](https://youtu.be/xBPJnMgLoUE?t=380) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Treat releases like darts: publish more games with good quality to raise odds of one hitting big.
- [3:33](https://youtu.be/xBPJnMgLoUE?t=213) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Selling a percentage of a game to investors is possible; a game earning around $1,000/day is a valuable asset.
- [14:11](https://youtu.be/xBPJnMgLoUE?t=851) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** For YouTube, hire editors and thumbnail makers to offload work; you then only generate ideas and record.
- [14:52](https://youtu.be/xBPJnMgLoUE?t=892) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Batch-record videos and pre-record a week's uploads to run multiple channels with few hours per day.
- [22:16](https://youtu.be/xBPJnMgLoUE?t=1336) **[[business-and-payouts]]** When hiring a team on percentage, expect your cut to be far below the total; a 30-40% share is a weak outcome in his example.
- [23:32](https://youtu.be/xBPJnMgLoUE?t=1412) **[[team-and-budget]]** Services are expensive and the barrier to entry is high; a team or scripting/modelling skill set is needed to start.
