# Here's Why Roblox Developers Are Retiring...

## Summary
A Roblox developer (Vook) explains why many developers are underpaid: accepting tiny Robux commissions, taking percentage deals from unproven teams, and having no USD payment method. He cites earnings estimates for Brookhaven, Bloxburg, and Adopt Me, and promotes his academy. Most of the content is promotional, with limited actionable advice.

## Tips
- [1:08](https://youtu.be/NfXvHlAdIsI?t=68) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Price commissions in USD or DevEx-equivalent hourly rates, not small Robux amounts like 500-1,000 Robux, which can be under $2.
- [2:10](https://youtu.be/NfXvHlAdIsI?t=130) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Convert Robux to USD with a DevEx calculator before quoting; 5,000 Robux was cited as roughly $12 at DevEx rate.
- [2:51](https://youtu.be/NfXvHlAdIsI?t=171) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Account for Robux taxes when pricing work so your quoted rate is still net-profitable.
- [3:06](https://youtu.be/NfXvHlAdIsI?t=186) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not accept percentage-only deals from unproven teams; many never release, leaving you with unpaid work.
- [3:47](https://youtu.be/NfXvHlAdIsI?t=227) **[[team-and-budget]]** If taking a percentage deal, only with a verified or front-page-proven partner, always under a signed contract, plus an upfront payment (e.g. 200k Robux) to cover work costs.
- [4:42](https://youtu.be/NfXvHlAdIsI?t=282) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Get a way to accept USD (bank or PayPal-style account); Robux-only payment limits you to cashing out roughly monthly.
