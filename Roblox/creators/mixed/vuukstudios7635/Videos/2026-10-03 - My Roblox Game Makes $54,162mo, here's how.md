## Summary
Creator V Studios claims a Roblox game earns ~$54K/month (1.4M Robux per 72 hours, ~10K CCU). Key claimed lever: cutting gamepass and dev product prices by half based on player demographics doubled revenue. Also covers selling the fantasy/vision, and perseverance after many failed games.

## Tips
- [3:42](https://youtu.be/4vuXAQt0PuQ?t=222) **[[pricing]]** Check your player country breakdown and set gamepass/devproduct prices for that audience; creator halved all prices and revenue reportedly doubled.
- [5:45](https://youtu.be/4vuXAQt0PuQ?t=345) **[[pricing]]** Don't use US-level prices if most players are from lower-income countries; players there often can't afford 300 Robux passes but can afford ~100.
- [5:59](https://youtu.be/4vuXAQt0PuQ?t=359) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Design gamepasses so buyers either feel 'good deal, buy now' or 'good deal, maybe later'; a third reaction signals bad positioning.
- [6:40](https://youtu.be/4vuXAQt0PuQ?t=400) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Keep paid items on progression, not pay-to-win: e.g. a 2x growth speed-up is acceptable, while items that make paying players dominant break the game.
- [7:23](https://youtu.be/4vuXAQt0PuQ?t=443) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Build the game around a fantasy the player wants to become (police chief, knight, zookeeper), not just the gameplay loop.
- [8:05](https://youtu.be/4vuXAQt0PuQ?t=485) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Sell the final outcome: frame the game as a bridge to a dream state and show players what they will become after weeks of play.
- [9:27](https://youtu.be/4vuXAQt0PuQ?t=567) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Show players a visible progression goal for 3 hours, 10 hours, 7 days and 2 weeks, so they keep returning to reach it.
- [11:33](https://youtu.be/4vuXAQt0PuQ?t=693) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Create an in-game problem (e.g. a slow growth system) and sell the solution (speed-up purchase).
- [12:14](https://youtu.be/4vuXAQt0PuQ?t=734) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect many failures; creator claims 15+ failed games before this hit. Keep shipping rather than quitting after a few failures.
- [14:02](https://youtu.be/4vuXAQt0PuQ?t=842) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Build multiple income streams (e.g. games, commissions, store packs) rather than relying on one product.
- [16:44](https://youtu.be/4vuXAQt0PuQ?t=1004) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Set concrete CCU targets (10K, then 100K, 200K) and track progress against them.
