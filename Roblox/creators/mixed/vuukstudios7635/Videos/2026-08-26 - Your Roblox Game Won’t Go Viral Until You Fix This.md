Roblox creator (Book Studios, claims 24K peak CCU) on why games fail to go viral: run ads for 1-2 weeks, ship a playable base version and fix daily, read feedback and drop-off points, judge pivot/cut by home recommendations growth, and push frequent patches and updates.
