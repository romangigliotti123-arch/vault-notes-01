## Summary
Roblox developer walks through getting players: picking unique or trend-based ideas, 2-4 week builds, Trello organization, commissioning to fund games, thumbnails/icons and titles with emojis, running 1-2 week ad campaigns at 100-200 ad credits, and reading CTR, play-through rate, playtime, D7 retention and home recommendations in analytics. Promotes his academy and community.

## Tips
- [0:45](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=45) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Pick either a unique idea or a trend-based idea; trend games get more CCU but die with the trend, unique games last longer.
- [2:07](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=127) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Check the Roblox trending page to see current genres (e.g. tower, brain rot, sports) and build a twist on a trending concept rather than a copy.
- [3:03](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=183) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Keep early games to about 2-4 weeks of work; release unfinished games and iterate rather than polishing for months.
- [5:03](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=303) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect most releases to miss; success comes from making many games and refining methods, like throwing many darts at a board.
- [5:54](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=354) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Organise each game in Trello with columns for concept, systems, monetisation, building/art, and paid costs.
- [6:35](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=395) **[[team-and-budget]]** Only partner with people you know personally and can trust; split revenue clearly.
- [7:45](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=465) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Prepare two thumbnails plus an icon so you can A/B test them in ads; the icon can double as a thumbnail.
- [7:45](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=465) **[[team-and-budget]]** If you lack funds, learn scripting or building and take commissions from developers to save money for your own game.
- [11:13](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=673) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Use emojis tied to the game in titles and descriptions; kids search emojis on mobile and this helps search discovery.
- [12:14](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=734) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Save roughly 30k Robux for ads and run 100-200 ad credits over 1-2 weeks, spread across days rather than spent at once.
- [12:55](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=775) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Judge ad creatives by CTR; below 1% usually means the thumbnail is weak, so swap thumbnails while ads run.
- [13:36](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=816) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Aim for CTR of 2-3% or higher; he calls over 1.5% very good and around 4% strong.
- [14:16](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=856) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Track play-through rate (plays divided by impressions); aim for 1.5-2% or higher, since CTR alone does not show real plays.
- [15:28](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=928) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Watch average playtime (aim 10-15 min minimum, 25+ min is excellent) and day-7 retention, which Roblox now weights for home page recommendations.
- [16:50](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=1010) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid low-effort template clones of a popular game; some studios reskin one template and get multiple games with thousands of CCU.
- [16:08](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=968) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Reuse a working game source template to release reskinned games quickly once one concept is proven.
- [18:51](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=1131) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Home-page recommendations matter most; Roblox promotes games with decent retention and monetisation and checks whether recommended players actually play.
- [19:33](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=1173) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** After reviewing metrics, add daily rewards and reasons to return to lift playtime and day-7 retention.
