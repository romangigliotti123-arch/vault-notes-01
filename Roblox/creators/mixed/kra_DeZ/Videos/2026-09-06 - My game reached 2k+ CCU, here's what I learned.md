# My game reached 2k+ CCU, here's what I learned

[Watch](https://youtu.be/cJ-YzHHhQs8) | kra_DeZ | 13,848 views | 2026-09-06

## Summary
Creator's Spin or Die (Roblox kill-last-player bottle-spin game) reached ~2k CCU after making a fast AI-assisted build, running paid ads to test thumbnails, and returning to update the game weekly after it flatlined. Covers idea validation, ad testing, dev-product monetisation, and the 'don't give up' stance, with a plug for his paid community.

## Tips
- [2:43](https://youtu.be/cJ-YzHHhQs8?t=163) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Take a viral TikTok trend, check Roblox search for existing games, and fill the gap fast; a similar game launched right as he finished his idea and overtook him.
- [4:41](https://youtu.be/cJ-YzHHhQs8?t=281) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Don't copy a trend exactly; add a unique selling point. His dodgeball clone (Shush Die) got only ~58 visits, while baseball-bat variants got thousands of players.
- [6:01](https://youtu.be/cJ-YzHHhQs8?t=361) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Keep the game simple enough for kids to understand; Roblox audiences skew young, and hard-to-grasp games stall on the algorithm.
- [6:59](https://youtu.be/cJ-YzHHhQs8?t=419) **[[ai-and-tools]]** Use AI to speed up development and thumbnails, but understand the code yourself; he says a months-long build can take days with AI.
- [9:06](https://youtu.be/cJ-YzHHhQs8?t=546) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't assume a quiet game is bad; most failures come from nobody knowing it exists, so promote before judging the design.
- [10:28](https://youtu.be/cJ-YzHHhQs8?t=628) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Before big spend, generate 20+ AI thumbnails and A/B test pairs with a short ad run (about 3 days, lifetime budget) to pick the highest-CTR one.
- [11:08](https://youtu.be/cJ-YzHHhQs8?t=668) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** After the winning thumbnail, run a larger ad burst (~90-100 ad credits over 4-5 days); expect the player spike to drop back to near zero after the ads end.
- [12:30](https://youtu.be/cJ-YzHHhQs8?t=750) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Don't abandon a flatlined game. Returning weeks later and adding small features (a kills leaderboard, end-of-round brawl) restarted growth into the hundreds.
- [13:10](https://youtu.be/cJ-YzHHhQs8?t=790) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Update the game at least weekly; after each update his player count kept growing, while big content dumps ran him out of update ideas.
- [13:51](https://youtu.be/cJ-YzHHhQs8?t=831) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Build many dev products and microtransactions; he argues they earn more Robux than gamepasses since they can be bought repeatedly.
- [15:07](https://youtu.be/cJ-YzHHhQs8?t=907) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Push daily updates in the first week after release to raise the chance of hitting the algorithm; he skipped this and his game flatlined.
