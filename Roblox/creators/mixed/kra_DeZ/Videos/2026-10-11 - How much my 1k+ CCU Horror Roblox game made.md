# How much my 1k+ CCU Horror Roblox game made

## Summary
Creator reviews his 3-year horror game "The Darkrooms [HORROR]", which peaked around 1,000 CCU and failed within about a year. He blames a too-hard, too-scary design for a kids-heavy platform, a bolted-on zombies mode, and pouring earnings into ads to keep it alive. Earnings total roughly 1.56M Robux (about $8K AUD via DevEx). He contrasts it with Spinner Die, built in days.

## Tips
- [9:05](https://youtu.be/dE0njfPNsdY?t=545) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Make games suited to Roblox's mostly-kid audience. Scary horror that players beat once and never return to will underperform.
- [6:47](https://youtu.be/dE0njfPNsdY?t=407) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't bolt a second mode onto an existing game (he added zombies to a horror game). Players want one or the other; build a separate game.
- [5:28](https://youtu.be/dE0njfPNsdY?t=328) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** If a game isn't working, move on rather than pouring years and earnings into it. He reinvested most earnings into ads to keep it alive, with declining results.
- [8:25](https://youtu.be/dE0njfPNsdY?t=505) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Design a gameplay loop that brings players back. A game that is beaten once gives no reason to return.
- [4:48](https://youtu.be/dE0njfPNsdY?t=288) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Time spent is not a proxy for income. A game made in a few days (Spinner Die) outearned a 3-year passion project.
- [11:42](https://youtu.be/dE0njfPNsdY?t=702) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Convert Robux to real money with the DevEx calculator to see true earnings before setting expectations.
- [4:07](https://youtu.be/dE0njfPNsdY?t=247) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** In a horror game, a jump-scare developer product was the top seller, showing that scare-themed purchases can work even if the game as a whole doesn't.
- [12:23](https://youtu.be/dE0njfPNsdY?t=743) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Failed projects still build skills (organisation, scripting). Keep shipping and learning rather than quitting out of frustration.
