# How Much I Spent on Roblox Ads to Hit 2k+ CCU (Proven Strategy)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/lrubtajkaeI) | kra_DeZ | 22,951 views | 2026-10-20

## Summary
Creator's Roblox ad case study on Spin or Die: spent 30 ad credits on a 2-thumbnail test (3 days), then 90 credits on the winning thumbnail, reaching ~2K CCU. Covers CTR benchmarks, campaign setup, and the need for continuous updates; notes organic growth later and a delayed spike after a mechanic update.

## Tips
- [2:13](https://youtu.be/lrubtajkaeI?t=133) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Test many thumbnails (he suggests up to 15 creatives) in the first ad run so you find the highest-CTR one; he tested only two and regrets it.
- [3:31](https://youtu.be/lrubtajkaeI?t=211) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Benchmark ad CTR: under 1–1.5% means drop the thumbnail, ~2.5% is average, 3.5%+ is exceptional; he got about 4%.
- [4:52](https://youtu.be/lrubtajkaeI?t=292) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Run a cheap test campaign first, then a longer main campaign using only the winning thumbnail, lasting about 4–5 days.
- [6:16](https://youtu.be/lrubtajkaeI?t=376) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Set ad campaigns to optimise for Plays, audience All Players, and use a lifetime budget in ad credits.
- [7:39](https://youtu.be/lrubtajkaeI?t=459) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Schedule the next campaign to start right when the previous one ends so no ad time is wasted.
- [8:21](https://youtu.be/lrubtajkaeI?t=501) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Only advertise games whose organic stats (retention, playtime) are already good; he says many of his earlier games failed despite ads.
- [10:12](https://youtu.be/lrubtajkaeI?t=612) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship updates every day during the first ad week and remove anything players dislike; he says letting ads run unattended caused past failures.
- [14:56](https://youtu.be/lrubtajkaeI?t=896) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Don't expect immediate results after a campaign; growth can lag. Spin or Die grew about 4 months after ads via a major mechanic update.
- [16:16](https://youtu.be/lrubtajkaeI?t=976) **[[updates-live-ops]]** A major mechanic addition (a leaderboard and end-of-round 1v1 sudden death) revived a dead game, reaching about 100K new players at one point.
- [1:37](https://youtu.be/lrubtajkaeI?t=97) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't give up on games after the ad run ends; he says his Dark Rooms game fell off and died after he stopped updating it.

## Games
- **Spin or Die**: Ran 30 then 90 ad credits on a winning thumbnail; reached ~2K CCU; grew from later updates, not just ads.
- **The Dark Rooms**: Sudden exponential growth after an ad run faded when the creator stopped updating; the game died.
- **Anime Fighting Tower**: Advertised poorly and faded after about a month; creator didn't update it the first week.
