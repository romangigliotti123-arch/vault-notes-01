# MattTheMethod - How to make successful Roblox games

Roblox revenue is a traffic-and-retention volume game. Pick a simple, shippable tycoon or simulator, decide how players will find it before building, and rely on Roblox's recommendation algorithm plus free short-form promotion (YouTube Shorts) rather than ads. Track average play time and D1 retention, since low playtime starves algorithm traffic. Monetise with developer products first, then gamepasses that speed progression. Revenue fell off a cliff once updates and promotion stopped, so keep shipping. Robux is not 1:1 dollars: DevEx needs 10,000 Robux and pays a fraction of that value.

**Style:** Practical, numbers-first solo developer sharing live analytics from small experiments. Strongest on traffic funnels, retention metrics and DevEx payout math; thin on design depth and production process. Suits solo builders wanting a simple passive-income tycoon or simulator. Treat the 1mYF-cWJPmQ success claims as promotional and unverified.

## Playbook
1. **Find a trend, then validate it** [[trend-research-and-validation]]: Check social platforms for trending topics and engagement data, then look for a trend not yet on Roblox. Verify the trend's cause, since many are just news. If nothing good appears, rerun research on different platforms over several days rather than forcing a weak idea.
2. **Choose a simple tycoon or simulator** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Pick an easy-to-ship tycoon or simulator. Creator's examples: a ~40h initial build plus ~20h since (MPHPumDvMIs) and a 2-week build (TEENlwjuKWI). Prioritise the traffic funnel over design polish. A traditional simulator was quoted at 6 months, which he contrasts unfavourably.
3. **Decide distribution before opening Studio** [[common-mistakes]]: Write down how players will find the game before building. Creator's main mistake to avoid is building first and planning marketing later.
4. **Use AI for repetitive code, keep the decisions** [[ai-and-tools]]: Let AI handle repetitive work. You decide the idea and feed it clear requirements. Periodically paste current scripts, folder structure and Explorer layout and say 'continue from here' to avoid stale, mismatched code.
5. **Hook players and measure play time and D1 retention** [[first-session-onboarding]]: Design the first session to hook players. Track average play time (creator's game ~22 min), the key promotion signal, and day-one retention (~16%) as the returning-player signal. Focus on retention before scaling traffic, since volume only pays once traffic compounds.
6. **Launch with developer products first** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: Sell developer products (currency purchases), described as the most rewarding of the three revenue streams. In TEENlwjuKWI they brought ~31k Robux versus ~23k from gamepasses.
7. **Add gamepasses that speed progression** [[monetisation-gamepasses]]: Sell gamepasses that grant abilities or stat boosts that speed up progression. Gamepasses were the second revenue source (~23k Robux) in TEENlwjuKWI.
8. **Count Creator Rewards for Premium playtime** [[monetisation-other]]: Creator Rewards pay passively for Premium users' time in the game, so non-buying players still generate some revenue. Treat as a supplementary stream, not a core plan.
9. **Promote free with thumbnails and Shorts** [[thumbnails-icons]]: Invest in a strong thumbnail and icon. Post short-form videos (YouTube Shorts, ranting videos) as a free acquisition channel. Creator drove his game's earnings this way with no ad spend.
10. **Earn free traffic from the recommendation algorithm** [[discovery-algorithm]]: Aim for the Roblox recommendation algorithm to surface the game and replace paid marketing. Creator's Dec 2025 argument was that algorithm traffic is the key advantage of building on Roblox.
11. **Optionally seed traffic with ads** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Optionally run ads to get initial traffic until the discovery algorithm picks the game up. Note this softens the earlier 'spend nothing on ads' stance.
12. **Keep updating and promoting** [[updates-live-ops]]: Keep shipping consistent updates and promotion. Creator's game went from ~30 peak CCU to zero after updates stopped, and his two AI-built games flopped partly because he stopped working on them.
13. **Model Robux cash-out honestly** [[business-and-payouts]]: DevEx requires 10,000 Robux minimum. Stated rate ~0.0038 USD per Robux, but 6,500 Robux is given as ~$21 (implies ~0.0032). Verify the current rate and model revenue in dollars, not Robux.
14. **Think in volume and compounding** [[mindset-and-strategy]]: Significant money needs many players. The flywheel is hard to stop once traffic compounds. Winning developers find good ideas and build them fastest rather than being the best programmers.

## Common mistakes
- Building first and planning marketing later; decide how players will find the game before opening Studio.
- Ignoring average play time, even though low playtime kills algorithm traffic.
- Treating Robux as dollars 1:1 instead of modelling the 10,000 Robux DevEx threshold and the lower payout rate.
- Stopping updates and promotion, which collapsed earnings in TEENlwjuKWI.
- Abandoning AI-built games after launch; the speaker's two games flopped partly because he stopped working on them.
- Expecting AI to replace decision-making; you still need to choose ideas and give clear requirements.
- Pasting AI suggestions into stale context; re-share current scripts, folder structure and Explorer layout.
- Forcing a weak trend idea instead of rerunning research over several days.
- Judging feasibility by headline success claims such as the $500k friend story in 1mYF-cWJPmQ.

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Decide the acquisition channel (Shorts, algorithm, optional ads) before opening Studio.
- [ ] Pick a simple tycoon or simulator you can ship in weeks.
- [ ] Build the first session to hook players; benchmark average play time (~22 min) and D1 retention (~16%).
- [ ] Track average play time and D1 retention from day one.
- [ ] Add developer products (currency) before gamepasses.
- [ ] Add gamepasses that give abilities or stat boosts that speed progression.
- [ ] Create a thumbnail and icon with strong click appeal.
- [ ] Prepare several short-form videos for YouTube Shorts before launch.
- [ ] Model revenue in dollars: Robux × verified DevEx rate, with the 10,000 Robux threshold.
- [ ] Set up analytics for play time, D1 retention and revenue per source.
- [ ] Plan a recurring update cadence before launch, not after.
- [ ] Budget a small optional ad spend to seed initial traffic, if used.
- [ ] Keep a current project doc (scripts, folder structure, Explorer layout) for AI sessions.
- [ ] Schedule a post-launch promotion calendar.
- [ ] Re-check trend sources before committing to the idea.
- [ ] Confirm Creator Rewards eligibility for Premium playtime as a supplementary stream.
- [ ] Verify all earnings claims before using them as benchmarks.
- [ ] Plan for a volume game: traffic must compound before revenue becomes significant.
