# MonzterDEV - How to make successful Roblox games

Discovery recommends games that keep players playing and paying: playthrough rate, session length, frequency, stickiness, socialness, payer conversion and revenue per payer. Build a fun, easy first 30 seconds, add reasons to return and to invite friends, and ship updates that move those metrics. Seed an initial audience with a small ads buy, friends and creators so the algorithm gets signal. Viral short-form content is the main route to new users; the 2024 affiliate program adds a payout for those referrals. Source: two videos, MonzterDEV.

**Style:** Short, practical breakdowns of Roblox platform docs and announcements, reading Roblox's own statements closely. Suits developers who want to understand how Discovery and monetisation signals work, and who are deciding between building games and creating social content. Thin on original playtest data; advice mostly relays Roblox's stated principles.

## Playbook
1. **Pick an underserved or growing genre** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Roblox says it wants original games in underserved or growing genres, not more simulators. Horror is cited as high-growth, with Doors as the example. Avoid copying saturated formats.
2. **Make the first 30 seconds fun and easy** [[first-session-onboarding]]: A hard early obstacle makes new players quit, which lowers playthrough rate. Keep the start simple and ramp difficulty only after a few minutes.
3. **Add reasons to return** [[retention-d1-d7]]: Use daily rewards, hourly events, or delayed timers such as eggs that hatch after 6 hours. Add AFK rewards so players earn while idle, which raises session length.
4. **Reward playing with friends** [[social-features-and-virality]]: Give stat boosts that scale with friends in the same server. This raises socialness and gives players a reason to invite others.
5. **Check gamepass purchase stats** [[monetisation-gamepasses]]: Review purchase data for each gamepass. If a pass is never bought, rework its value or give the feature free to all players.
6. **Design eye-catching thumbnails and icons** [[thumbnails-icons]]: Use vibrant colours and attractive subjects. Thumbnail click-through is a discovery signal, so test thumbnails before launch.
7. **Write catchy titles that mention updates** [[titles-descriptions]]: Put new updates in titles (e.g. 'brand new update') to entice returning players and clicks. Keep the description aligned with what the title promises.
8. **Track engagement and monetisation** [[analytics-metrics]]: Use Creator Analytics plus off-platform analytics to track engagement, progression and monetisation trends. Decide what to change from the data, not from guesses.
9. **Seed an initial audience before relying on Discovery** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Start with a small ads buy, friends, influencers and TikTok/YouTube. Life Together got a small ads buy in August 2023, which gave the algorithm enough signal to start ranking it.
10. **Make content that can go viral on short-form video** [[social-features-and-virality]]: Create clips likely to spread on TikTok and Shorts, as Doors did, to bring new users to Roblox. Roblox's affiliate program suits social media creators more than game developers.
11. **Join the affiliate program if eligible** [[roblox-platform-changes]]: Creators earn 50% of a new user's Robux purchases, capped at $100 per user, within 6 months of account creation. Referred users must be brand new with no alternate accounts. Confirm current eligibility; the announced timeline has passed.
12. **Ship updates that move retention, not just frequent patches** [[updates-live-ops]]: Roblox's example is Life Together, where the 11th update was followed by a Christmas TikTok/YouTube breakout and a later 14th update. Judge each update by its effect on engagement and retention metrics.
13. **Introduce products and price points** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: Use developer products, gamepasses and subscriptions to add new price points. Make each purchase worth its price so payer conversion and revenue per payer improve without hurting trust.
14. **Invest in updates and events** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: Roblox says quality correlates with investment in updates and events. It rewards player love, measured by time and money spent, so keep reinvesting in the core loop.

## Common mistakes
- Putting a hard obstacle in the first minutes, which drives new players away and lowers playthrough rate.
- Leaving a gamepass that is never bought unchanged instead of reworking it or making the feature free.
- Using dull thumbnails and icons, which weakens click-through, a discovery signal.
- Titles that never mention updates, missing the chance to draw back returning players.
- Shipping frequent patches that do not move engagement or retention.
- Expecting Discovery to work with no initial audience; the algorithm needs signal first.
- Creating alternate accounts to farm affiliate rewards; referred users must be brand new with no alternates.
- Relying on the affiliate program as a game developer when the video says it suits social media creators more.
- Copying simulator formats when Roblox says it wants original games in underserved or growing genres.

## Launch checklist
- [ ] First 30 seconds are simple and fun, with no hard early obstacle.
- [ ] Difficulty ramps up only after a few minutes of play.
- [ ] Daily reward, hourly event or delayed timer (e.g. 6-hour egg) is in place.
- [ ] AFK rewards are active if the game suits idle play.
- [ ] Friend-scaling rewards exist for playing in the same server.
- [ ] Thumbnail and icon use vibrant colours and an attractive subject.
- [ ] Title and description mention the latest update where relevant.
- [ ] Creator Analytics and off-platform tracking are set up for engagement and monetisation.
- [ ] Each gamepass has a purchase-stat review plan with a rework or free-feature fallback.
- [ ] Developer products, gamepasses and subscriptions have defined price points that match the value delivered.
- [ ] Initial audience plan is ready: small ads buy, friends, influencers, TikTok/YouTube.
- [ ] Short-form clips are planned for TikTok/Shorts.
- [ ] Genre choice is original and in an underserved or growing area, not a saturated simulator.
- [ ] Affiliate eligibility is checked against current Roblox terms before relying on it.
- [ ] Update cadence is planned around retention and engagement goals, not patch frequency.
- [ ] Playtest results are reviewed against playthrough rate, length and frequency.
- [ ] Monetisation is reviewed against payer conversion and revenue per payer.
- [ ] Post-launch plan includes reinvestment in updates and events.
