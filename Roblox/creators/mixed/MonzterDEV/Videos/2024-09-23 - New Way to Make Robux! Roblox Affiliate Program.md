## Summary
Explains Roblox's 2024 creator affiliate program: creators earn 50% of a new user's Robux purchases, capped at $100 per user, within 6 months of signup. Argues it suits social media creators more than game developers, and that viral content and growing genres like horror (Doors) are the main opportunities. Pilot runs through end of 2024, with expansion to eligible creators by mid-2025.
