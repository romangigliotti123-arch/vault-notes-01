# How to Make a Popular Roblox Game

[Watch](https://youtu.be/em15yK5RjBk) | MonzterDEV | 13,661 views | 2024-03-01

## Summary
Breakdown of a Roblox developer forum post on how the Discovery system recommends experiences: the three surfaces (homepage, Discover page, search), the mission and principles, the metrics Roblox measures (playthrough rate, length, frequency, stickiness, socialness, payer conversion, revenue per payer, metadata/media), and a five-step growth journey.

## Tips
- [8:10](https://youtu.be/em15yK5RjBk?t=490) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Make the first 30 seconds fun and easy; a hard early obstacle makes new players quit, lowering playthrough rate. Ramp difficulty after a few minutes.
- [16:59](https://youtu.be/em15yK5RjBk?t=1019) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add AFK rewards so players earn while idle in the game; this raises session length, a discovery signal (common in simulators).
- [17:41](https://youtu.be/em15yK5RjBk?t=1061) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give players a reason to return: daily rewards, hourly events, or delayed-timer systems like eggs that hatch after 6 hours.
- [18:22](https://youtu.be/em15yK5RjBk?t=1102) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Reward players for playing with friends (e.g., stat boosts scaling with friends in server) to increase socialness and invites.
- [19:02](https://youtu.be/em15yK5RjBk?t=1142) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Check per-gamepass purchase stats; if a pass is never bought, rework its value or give the feature free to all players.
- [19:02](https://youtu.be/em15yK5RjBk?t=1142) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use eye-catching thumbnails with vibrant colors and attractive subjects; thumbnail click-through is a discovery signal.
- [19:43](https://youtu.be/em15yK5RjBk?t=1183) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Use catchy titles and mention new updates in them (e.g., 'brand new update') to entice players to return and click.
- [13:10](https://youtu.be/em15yK5RjBk?t=790) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship thoughtful content updates that move engagement and retention metrics, not just frequent patches; Roblox's own example is Life Together's 11th and 14th updates.
- [11:41](https://youtu.be/em15yK5RjBk?t=701) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Grow an initial audience first (small ads buy, friends, influencers, TikTok/YouTube) so the algorithm has signal to start recommending the game.
- [13:44](https://youtu.be/em15yK5RjBk?t=824) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Introduce new products and price points with developer products, gamepasses and subscriptions, and make purchases worth the price.
- [5:30](https://youtu.be/em15yK5RjBk?t=330) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Roblox says quality correlates with investment in updates and events, and rewards player love measured by time and money spent.
- [13:54](https://youtu.be/em15yK5RjBk?t=834) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use Creator Analytics plus off-platform analytics to track engagement, progression and monetization trends and decide what to change.
