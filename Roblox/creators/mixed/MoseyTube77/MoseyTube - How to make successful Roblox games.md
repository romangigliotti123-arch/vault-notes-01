# MoseyTube - How to make successful Roblox games

MoseyTube says to pick a trending, growing-phase concept, reverse-engineer why it works, and build a simple 30-second core loop with fast early progression, layered reveals and return reasons (offline earnings, daily rewards, streaks). Use AI (Claude via MCP in Studio) to build fast. Monetise with many consumable dev products, a few permanent gamepasses and Premium payouts. Launch with $10/day broad ads for ~10 days to seed the algorithm, A/B test thumbnails (>2.5% CTR), watch real players, and stop ads once home recommendations take over. Then update consistently and build many games.

**Style:** Practical, numbers-driven and budget-focused. Treats Roblox as an ads-seeded algorithm game and leans heavily on Claude/AI to build in about 24 hours. Shares real spend and revenue figures and benchmarks. Suits solo or beginner devs wanting a cheap, repeatable launch formula, though many videos end in promos for courses or templates.

## Playbook
1. **Pick an idea from growing charts** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Scroll recommendations and trending lists. Target games in the growing phase, not the already-huge ones. Reverse-engineer why a hit works (e.g. Sell Lemons) and reuse the loop with your own theme. Choose a concept needing no tutorial that works for 9- and 14-year-olds.
2. **Design a 30-second core loop** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: Players should grasp the loop in about 30 seconds with no tutorial, and fully within 2-5 minutes. Keep progression fast (dollars to millions to billions). Reveal content in layers (managers, teleporter, rebirth). Include shared-moment mechanics such as server events or PvP stealing.
3. **Build fast with AI via MCP** [[ai-and-tools]]: Connect Claude (or Codex/ChatGPT) to Studio via MCP. Write long, specific master prompts with concrete numbers, then iterate in chat. Use ultra effort for big builds (~1 hour). Treat output as a foundation and add depth and polish. Claimed ~80% scripting time saved.
4. **Polish the first map and onboarding** [[first-session-onboarding]]: Make the first map look inhabited: NPCs at counters, a vibrant skybox, edge decor. Remove dead UI such as a join-event board with no event. Outsource only your weakest skill (e.g. $50 UI freelancer).
5. **Build retention hooks** [[retention-d1-d7]]: Add offline earnings, daily challenges (3 random tasks per 24h), daily and playtime rewards, streaks, collections with rotating shops, and reward pads such as an hourly wheel spin. Use shared cooldowns to prevent abuse. Aim for multiple reasons to stay, return and spend.
6. **Set up monetisation** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: Favour consumable dev products (20-30 across price points) that solve pain points: speed, 5x cash, auto-collect, 2x offline earnings. Add purchase entry points in the HUD, next to the currency counter and in-world. Sell 2-5 permanent gamepasses (2x cash, VIP). Add intentional friction (e.g. rebirth) and sell the fix. Premium payouts add income.
7. **Make 2-3 thumbnails** [[thumbnails-icons]]: Run thumbnails simultaneously. Make the single main subject much bigger (about half the frame). Check at phone size: the subject should be identifiable in 1 second. Change one element per test.
8. **Launch ads to seed the algorithm** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Run $10/day for 10 days ($100). Use Maximize Plays, all regions, all devices. Raise to $15-20/day for competitive genres. Don't change settings on day one. Watch real players for ~5 minutes to find confusion and drop-off.
9. **Follow the day-by-day decision rules** [[analytics-metrics]]: Day 1 targets: CTR ~2.5%+ and cost per play ~$0.008 or better. Days 2-4: swap weak thumbnails and fix bugs. From day 5: if retention is poor, stop ads and fix gameplay. If home-recommendation impressions appear, keep going. If the algorithm hasn't picked it up by day 14, reconsider that game.
10. **Hit the 5 benchmarks** [[analytics-metrics]]: Onboarding understood in 2-5 minutes. Session time over 10 minutes during ads, then 20+ with home recs. D1 retention 12-15% and D7 2-3%. Conversion rates around 2-3%. Check the acquisition tab for friend joins and search clicks. Hitting enough of them puts a game in the roughly 10% that get players.
11. **Stop ads when organic takes over** [[discovery-algorithm]]: Once home recommendations sustain traffic, stop paid ads. Track favorites as a retention signal. Stay active for the first 7-14 days: fix bugs, watch players and tune monetisation.
12. **Iterate on sales data and live ops** [[updates-live-ops]]: Check that new products don't hurt session time or retention. Remove those that fail and test alternatives. Update consistently. Revive dead games with AI-built updates plus a short $10/day ad test, since the algorithm retains earlier data.
13. **Keep the mindset: many cheap shots** [[mindset-and-strategy]]: Build many games and treat each as a learning step. Test ideas on small budgets (Guess the Job or Die cost $329 and made $12,535 in month one). Execution quality beats idea complexity. Still learn game design.

## Common mistakes
- Players leave too fast because the core loop or first map is unclear or unpolished
- Weak monetisation: too few spend options or everything as one-time game passes
- Building one game and giving up when it doesn't blow up immediately
- Releasing and walking away; the first 7-14 days decide the outcome
- Launching without ads, so the algorithm has no retention data
- Changing or pausing ad settings on day one, or hunting for targeting tricks
- Busy thumbnails with a small subject
- Testing several thumbnail changes at once, so you can't tell what worked
- Dead UI such as a join-event board with no event
- Shipping AI output as-is with no depth or polish
