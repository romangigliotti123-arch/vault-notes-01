# I Got 44% More Visits in a Day by Changing One Thing (Not the Roblox Game)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/tao925_ypVg) | MoseyTube | 9,020 views | 2026-10-14

## Summary
Shows how swapping a thumbnail to a bigger main subject (pilot and money zoomed in) raised CTR from 2.14% to 3.08% (+43.9%) and plays from 5,243 to 7,546 over 7 days at ~$10/day, with the same game and budget. Gives a 6-step test process and a 10-second phone "postage stamp" check.

## Tips
- [4:22](https://youtu.be/tao925_ypVg?t=262) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** View your thumbnail at phone size; if you can't identify the main subject within 1 second, it's too busy.
- [4:22](https://youtu.be/tao925_ypVg?t=262) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make the single main subject much bigger than you think needed; he zoomed the pilot and money to roughly half the thumbnail.
- [4:22](https://youtu.be/tao925_ypVg?t=262) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Change only one element per test so you know what caused the result.
- [4:22](https://youtu.be/tao925_ypVg?t=262) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Run old and new thumbnails with identical budget and ad settings (he used ~$10/day for 7 days each).
- [4:22](https://youtu.be/tao925_ypVg?t=262) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Compare click-through rate between thumbnail variants; CTR is the metric to decide the winner.
- [4:22](https://youtu.be/tao925_ypVg?t=262) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Keep the winner, then test the next element such as text or background, one at a time.
- [2:24](https://youtu.be/tao925_ypVg?t=144) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Keep your recognizable style (title, colors, characters) when redesigning so returning players still recognise the game.
- [1:03](https://youtu.be/tao925_ypVg?t=63) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Remember players see only the small thumbnail in feeds before they ever see your game, so it decides whether they click.
