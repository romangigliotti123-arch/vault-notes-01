## Tips
- [0:19](https://youtu.be/d9UxSCiz3us?t=19) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Run ads at launch: Roblox won't recommend a new game until its retention and return-play data look good, and ads supply that data.
- [1:00](https://youtu.be/d9UxSCiz3us?t=60) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Start with $10/day for 10 days ($100 total); raise to $15–$20/day for competitive genres or games needing more players to be playable.
- [1:53](https://youtu.be/d9UxSCiz3us?t=113) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Keep campaign settings broad: maximize plays, all regions, all devices, and let the algorithm find the audience rather than hunting for targeting tricks.
- [1:53](https://youtu.be/d9UxSCiz3us?t=113) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make 2–3 thumbnails and run them simultaneously to compare click rates, since weak CTR limits reach regardless of game quality.
- [2:41](https://youtu.be/d9UxSCiz3us?t=161) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Target roughly 2.5% CTR or better and about $0.008 cost per play or better on day one.
- [2:41](https://youtu.be/d9UxSCiz3us?t=161) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Don't pause or change settings on day one; let the campaign learn, but watch real players for ~5 minutes to find confusion and drop-off points.
- [3:33](https://youtu.be/d9UxSCiz3us?t=213) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Days 2–4: swap out or remove thumbnails with poor CTR and fix bugs while there is still time before the algorithm decides.
- [3:56](https://youtu.be/d9UxSCiz3us?t=236) **[[analytics-metrics]]** From day 5, if retention is poor, stop ads and fix core gameplay; if stats are decent and home-recommendation impressions appear, keep running.
- [4:34](https://youtu.be/d9UxSCiz3us?t=274) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Stop paid ads once organic players from home recommendations can sustain the game on their own.
