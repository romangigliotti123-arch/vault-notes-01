# My Roblox Game Made $43,292 (Here's Exactly How to Monetize Yours)

## Summary
Breakdown of how the Roblox tycoon "Sell Potatoes" reportedly made $43,292 via monetization. Core lesson: favor consumable developer products over game passes, sell products that solve player pain points, place purchase options in multiple spots, and iterate based on sales data and player observation.

## Tips
- [0:50](https://youtu.be/-PoyiiGhoNo?t=50) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Use developer products (consumables) instead of game passes for most items, since players can rebuy them every session rather than buying once.
- [0:50](https://youtu.be/-PoyiiGhoNo?t=50) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't make everything a game pass; one-time purchases require constant new-player acquisition to keep earning.
- [1:51](https://youtu.be/-PoyiiGhoNo?t=111) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Design products around a specific pain point players already feel, such as slow movement (speed boost), grinding (5x cash), or manual collection (auto collect).
- [2:32](https://youtu.be/-PoyiiGhoNo?t=152) **[[pricing]]** Price consumables high enough to earn but low enough not to break the in-game economy; aim for a no-brainer purchase price.
- [3:02](https://youtu.be/-PoyiiGhoNo?t=182) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Watch real players for 5 minutes each to find frustration points, slowdowns and boring moments; those moments are your product ideas.
- [3:44](https://youtu.be/-PoyiiGhoNo?t=224) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Offer purchase entry points in multiple places: HUD shop button, a plus icon beside the currency counter, and in-world NPCs or buttons where players spend time.
- [4:36](https://youtu.be/-PoyiiGhoNo?t=276) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Reserve game passes for permanent upgrades that change how the game feels, such as a 2x cash multiplier, VIP access or exclusive cosmetics.
- [4:36](https://youtu.be/-PoyiiGhoNo?t=276) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Sell 2x offline earnings as a developer product rather than a game pass, since players will rebuy it every time they log back in.
- [5:11](https://youtu.be/-PoyiiGhoNo?t=311) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check that a new product doesn't hurt session time and retention before keeping it; remove products that don't work and test alternatives.
- [5:11](https://youtu.be/-PoyiiGhoNo?t=311) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Launch with a basic monetization setup and iterate from sales data and player behaviour rather than trying to perfect it on day one.
