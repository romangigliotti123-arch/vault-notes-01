# Why 90% of Roblox Games Never Get Players

[Watch](https://youtu.be/xIBc5He_sy4) | MoseyTube | 15,281 views | 2026-08-25

## Summary
Creator presents a 5-benchmark framework for Roblox game success (onboarding, session time, D1/D7 retention, monetization, acquisition/word of mouth) plus the critical first 7-14 days after launch. Validates it against his game Steal a Superstar, showing its real numbers. Promotes his paid community at the end.

## Tips
- [1:02](https://youtu.be/xIBc5He_sy4?t=62) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make players understand the full core loop (what they do, why, what happens next) within 2-5 minutes; if still confused after 5 minutes, onboarding is broken.
- [1:43](https://youtu.be/xIBc5He_sy4?t=103) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make the next goal reachable within 10-15 minutes so players feel fast progress; avoid grind walls that cause quitting.
- [2:00](https://youtu.be/xIBc5He_sy4?t=120) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Target average session time above 10 minutes while running ads before algorithm pickup; below that, expect a flop.
- [2:00](https://youtu.be/xIBc5He_sy4?t=120) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Once home recommendations kick in, aim for 20+ minute average session time to keep the algorithm pushing the game.
- [2:40](https://youtu.be/xIBc5He_sy4?t=160) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Run ads with a set budget until the game either flops or home recommendations appear; he spent about $148 before home recs on Steal a Superstar.
- [2:44](https://youtu.be/xIBc5He_sy4?t=164) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Benchmark day-1 retention at 12-15% and day-7 retention at 2-3%; D30 has no universal target, higher means more staying power.
- [3:43](https://youtu.be/xIBc5He_sy4?t=223) **[[monetisation-other]]** Build an intentional friction point into the game (e.g. rebirth losing progress) and sell the solution (permanent multipliers, faster speed, extra slots).
- [4:26](https://youtu.be/xIBc5He_sy4?t=266) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Sell fun, social items beyond progression; example: Nuke All developer product that kills all players on the server.
- [4:26](https://youtu.be/xIBc5He_sy4?t=266) **[[pricing]]** Aim for purchase conversion and conversion rates around 2-3%, which he calls healthy for this monetization setup.
- [5:11](https://youtu.be/xIBc5He_sy4?t=311) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check the acquisition tab for friend joins and search clicks; flat numbers signal no word of mouth, which caps algorithm promotion.
- [5:41](https://youtu.be/xIBc5He_sy4?t=341) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Stay active in the game for the first 7-14 days after publishing: watch real players, fix bugs, and adjust monetization.
- [6:21](https://youtu.be/xIBc5He_sy4?t=381) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** If the algorithm has not picked up the game by day 14, seriously reconsider pushing that specific game, though late pop-offs happen.
- [5:41](https://youtu.be/xIBc5He_sy4?t=341) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not release and walk away; the first two weeks decide whether a game gets players.
- [7:02](https://youtu.be/xIBc5He_sy4?t=422) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** You do not need to hit every benchmark; hitting enough of them (he says) puts a game in the roughly 10% that gets players.
