## Summary
Creator breaks down his Roblox game "Guess the Job or Die" (about 2,000 average CCU, 3,000 peak, live about a month). He argues success comes from a simple, instantly understood social-deduction guessing loop layered with retention systems (collections, rewards, streaks), multiple monetisation points, and clippable moments. Video is a promo funnel for a paid course.

## Tips
- [1:12](https://youtu.be/a170E9klbP4?t=72) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Pick a simple, instantly understandable concept (e.g. social-deduction guessing) that needs no tutorial and works for both 9- and 14-year-olds.
- [1:54](https://youtu.be/a170E9klbP4?t=114) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Keep the core mechanic simple, then layer multiple systems on top for depth rather than making the core mechanic complicated.
- [1:54](https://youtu.be/a170E9klbP4?t=114) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add a collection system with a daily rotating shop and limited skins so players return to collect, separate from the core gameplay.
- [2:35](https://youtu.be/a170E9klbP4?t=155) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Stack several independent login reasons: daily rewards, playtime rewards, group rewards, VIP daily rewards, and a streak system.
- [2:35](https://youtu.be/a170E9klbP4?t=155) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Let players pay Robux to recover a broken streak; players will pay to avoid losing progress they already built.
- [2:35](https://youtu.be/a170E9klbP4?t=155) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Build in player-vs-player moments that create chaos and drama (e.g. stealing cash) since clippers and YouTubers gravitate to them.
- [3:16](https://youtu.be/a170E9klbP4?t=196) **[[team-and-budget]]** Bring in outside help only for your bottleneck skill (he hired a Discord contributor for UI polish) instead of being good at everything.
- [3:47](https://youtu.be/a170E9klbP4?t=227) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Sell a VIP pass (1.5x cash, VIP chat, exclusive cosmetics) plus separate double-cash, double-wins and double-streak passes.
- [3:47](https://youtu.be/a170E9klbP4?t=227) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Sell consumable hints (reveal job, reveal one letter) to stuck players who want to avoid losing.
- [3:47](https://youtu.be/a170E9klbP4?t=227) **[[pricing]]** Offer multiple price points within one system, e.g. cosmetic chairs from $100 up to a $150 gaming chair.
- [4:31](https://youtu.be/a170E9klbP4?t=271) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Aim for multiple reasons to stay, multiple reasons to return tomorrow, and multiple reasons to spend, per the speaker's formula.
