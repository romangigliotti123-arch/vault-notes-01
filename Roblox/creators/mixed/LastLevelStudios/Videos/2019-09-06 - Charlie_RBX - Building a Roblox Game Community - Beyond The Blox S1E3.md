# Charlie_RBX - Building a Roblox Game Community - Beyond The Blox S1E3

[Watch](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w) | LastLevelStudios | 7,234 views | 2019-09-06

## Summary
Podcast episode on building a Roblox game community, featuring Charlie_RBX (Stepford County Railway, 4.8M visits, 26k-member group, 6k+ Discord). Covers community types (flat vs hierarchical), benefits, drawbacks, tools (Discord, RoVer, Google Forms, custom bots, suggestion site), moderation balance, creator pressure, leadership selection, and retention via development pathways and regular content.

## Tips
- [1:26](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=86) **[[community-and-discord]]** Choose community depth to fit the genre: flat fan groups for simple games, deep ranked hierarchies for games where ranks unlock gameplay.
- [4:10](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=250) **[[community-and-discord]]** Use community ranks to restrict technically risky modes to trained players so they can't ruin the game for others.
- [5:35](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=335) **[[community-and-discord]]** Budget development time for community management; delegate, then train and check in on delegates regularly.
- [6:17](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=377) **[[common-mistakes]]** Assume community actions reflect on you; spam done in your community's name will be blamed on the owner, so enforce rules.
- [7:01](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=421) **[[community-and-discord]]** Watch for community pressure making you build only what fans want; keep room for your own interests to avoid burnout.
- [7:42](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=462) **[[community-and-discord]]** If you can't commit long term, let fans run an unofficial community so you aren't pressured or guilty when you move on.
- [8:02](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=482) **[[community-and-discord]]** Base the community on Discord with separate voice/text channels per purpose, since Roblox groups lack push notifications, voice, and rich media.
- [8:45](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=525) **[[community-and-discord]]** Use RoVer bot to assign Discord roles from Roblox group rank and link Roblox usernames to Discord accounts.
- [8:45](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=525) **[[community-and-discord]]** Host a community wiki that members can contribute to, so new players can catch up from one central hub.
- [9:28](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=568) **[[community-and-discord]]** Use Google Forms for applications, linked to a spreadsheet you can share with the team to assess applicants collaboratively.
- [10:12](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=612) **[[community-and-discord]]** Replace open suggestion/bug Discord channels, which get flooded with duplicates, with a web tool that merges duplicates and supports voting and comments.
- [10:12](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=612) **[[community-and-discord]]** Use a moderation bot for auto-removal of spam links and invites, plus a warning system so members learn rules before eviction.
- [10:53](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=653) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build a custom customer-service bot with a shared queue so members post once and any staff member can claim the request.
- [12:06](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=726) **[[community-and-discord]]** Delegate moderation with escalation paths; keep potentially damaging permissions close to the creator.
- [14:18](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=858) **[[community-and-discord]]** Give small, frequent sneak peeks so members see progress; avoid the creator personally tracking the hype and reacting to it.
- [14:18](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=858) **[[team-and-budget]]** Hire a people person to run events and listen to the community once it's large, rather than letting logistics consume dev time.
- [14:55](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=895) **[[team-and-budget]]** Do not pick leaders only from friends; choose by personality, maturity and trustworthiness, and keep a path for applicants to reach management.
- [15:38](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=938) **[[community-and-discord]]** Avoid overzealous moderation; keep moderators calm, since heavy-handed enforcement upsets the community.
- [21:52](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=1312) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Publish a clear progression pathway (e.g. Trainee to Signaller) so players see a reachable goal and stay engaged.
- [21:52](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=1312) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Ship content regularly, even small monthly additions like a new train or route, rather than rare large updates.
- [23:48](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=1428) **[[community-and-discord]]** Post non-pushed announcements or sneak peeks every few days, and pushed announcements for major updates.
- [23:48](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=1428) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Also announce on Roblox and Twitter, since many members never join third-party Discord; keep big announcements public.
- [24:31](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=1471) **[[age-ratings-moderation-and-tos]]** Never bypass Roblox moderation to advertise links; use the Social Links feature and your description, and remember most external services are 13+.
