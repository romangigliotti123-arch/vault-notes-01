## Summary
Podcast interview with thebenster (Make a Cake creator) covering his origin on Roblox, building Make a Cake and Back for Seconds, marketing via YouTubers and Roblox-sponsored events, Make a Cake toys, Demolition Crew, a 2021 social-engineering account hack, and his plans to keep games as a side project.

## Tips
- [12:24](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=744) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Roblox approached the creator for sponsored events (Fruity Pebbles, Imagination); being placed in platform events drove large prize-claim numbers.
- [13:47](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=827) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** Reach out to big creators whose videos already feature your game; he DMed ethangamertv on Twitter about his most-viewed video and got a video that drew millions of views.
- [14:29](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=869) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** Cold-DMing creators one by one is tedious; creator outreach is a networking grind with no formal channel.
- [16:41](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1001) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Physical merchandise (toys) is a real-world validation milestone; the Make a Cake toys sold out on Amazon repeatedly.
- [18:33](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1113) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build on a niche you're good at: he focused on comedy chaos games with silly absurdist tone, which kept the game family-friendly.
- [19:14](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1154) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Destruction games with blow-up-and-upgrade loops (like Blow Stuff Up for Fun) are a proven format; Demolition Crew added combos and weapons.
- [20:35](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1235) **[[common-mistakes]]** Tone check: he cut realism for goofiness after about 30 percent of development, saying the gritty version wasn't fun.
- [5:51](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=351) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Sequels vs updates: he kept the old game running as long as it wasn't game-breaking, and launched a new game rather than replacing a still-performing one (about 50k plays/week).
- [8:34](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=514) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Replace a legacy game only if it is game-breaking; otherwise keep it running to preserve nostalgia and reduce maintenance.
- [7:54](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=474) **[[code-architecture-and-frameworks]]** His early code was copy-pasted with no loops, functions, or modules; refactoring for FilteringEnabled was painful. Use functions, loops and modules from the start.
- [22:39](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1359) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Attackers used social engineering to port the phone number and bypass SMS 2FA. Remove phone numbers from dev and key accounts where possible.
- [24:02](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1442) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Use authenticator apps or hardware security keys for 2FA, and add a PIN to your carrier account to block unauthorized number transfers.
- [26:47](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1607) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Protect critical accounts, especially email, with security keys plus 2FA, and use unique emails per service so one breach does not cascade.
- [25:25](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1525) **[[anti-exploit-and-security]]** Developer accounts should support stronger 2FA (authenticator apps or security keys); he said Roblox was working on it, not yet available.
- [29:14](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1754) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Keep the game as a side hobby with about 10-15 hours a week rather than going full time, to avoid burnout and keep the enjoyment.
- [30:36](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1836) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Hire specialist builders and a scripter for a large rebuild of an existing game; he hired three builders and one scripter for a major Make a Cake update.
- [27:52](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1672) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Plan for platform changes: Roblox can depreciate critical features, so keep some ongoing time for maintaining games.
