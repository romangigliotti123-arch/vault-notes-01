# GregTame - Pioneering Roblox VR into the Future (Virtual Reality) - Beyond The Blox S2E6

[Watch](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g) | LastLevelStudios | 2,186 views | 2021-12-09

## Summary
Podcast interview with Greg Tame (GregTame), creator of Tankery, Edgeworks, Cave Explorer, Skywinds, on building Roblox VR experiences. Covers VR market size (~2% of PC), physics-based climbing, single-player-first design, Kinect mocap-to-Blender-to-Roblox animation pipeline, narrative use in VR, and hardware advice.

## Tips
- [2:05](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=125) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Tankery's CSG-injected OBJ tank models kept each tank to ~10 parts, enabling 20v20 battles with low lag.
- [5:22](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=322) **[[team-and-budget]]** Outsource map-making to the community: community-made maps are tested in closed tests before being added, roughly every six months.
- [8:52](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=532) **[[common-mistakes]]** VR is a small niche (~2-3% of Steam PC users, and Roblox has no PlayStation VR); size expectations accordingly before building.
- [22:55](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=1375) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Design VR games to work solo and improve with more players, since you cannot guarantee other players join a small VR server.
- [11:14](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=674) **[[genre-mechanics]]** Physics-based character rigs with grabbable ledges let players climb and solve platforming freely, a major Edgeworks draw inspired by Boneworks.
- [16:19](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=979) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Roblox's default VR GUI behaves oddly; building a custom GUI system was easier than working around it.
- [27:34](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=1654) **[[3d-modeling-rigging-and-animation]]** Mocap pipeline: Kinect via NI Mate into Blender, convert empties to bones, clean up (~2/3 of animation work saved), export FBX to Roblox.
- [28:16](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=1696) **[[optimization-and-streaming]]** 60fps mocap FBX files reach 200-300 KB each; load animations on demand so they don't eat the memory budget, accepting brief pauses on slow connections.
- [31:21](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=1881) **[[brand-deals-and-ip-licensing]]** Use story as a selling point: motion capture, voice acting and narrative differentiate a niche game that players otherwise wouldn't seek out.
- [36:48](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=2208) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Replace QTEs with a physical action in VR (shake hand to break free); players did it instinctively without being told.
- [35:25](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=2125) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Featured-game event submissions are a route to visibility, but niche VR games are unlikely to be picked on that basis alone.
- [12:37](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=757) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** YouTube videos showing VR gameplay drive interest, since viewers cannot easily try the headset themselves; a 2020 VR YouTube trend boosted Edgeworks.
- [41:40](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=2500) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Give VR a month of experimentation first; expect modest earnings and treat it as uncharted territory, not a guaranteed hit.
- [18:44](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=1124) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Stay on the platform you already know when it pays bills; switching engines could cost about a year to reach comparable selling speed.

## Games
- **Tankery** [1:24](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=84): Tank game with 10-part tanks and community-made maps; still ~100 CCU years later with updates every ~6 months.
- **Edgeworks** [9:11](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=551): Physics-based VR climbing sandbox with optional story, inspired by Boneworks; works solo and improves with players.
- **Cave Explorer 1 & 2** [35:25](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=2125): Procedural caves VR experiments; shake-hand escape mechanic replaces QTE; Cave 2 used mocap boss fight.
- **Skywinds** [38:09](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=2289): VR tower defense where you shoot enemies yourself; got YouTube players.
- **Squid Game VR (unnamed)** [40:19](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=2419): Quick commissioned VR prototype for YouTubers; hand-movement-based sprinting and dodging mechanic.
