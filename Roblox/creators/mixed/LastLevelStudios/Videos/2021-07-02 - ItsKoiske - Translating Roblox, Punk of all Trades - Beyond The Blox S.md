Translator ItsKoiske explains why Roblox games need professional localization, the risks of Google Translate and Roblox's forced automatic translations, and how to prioritise languages by player location. Covers translating tycoon terminology, handling bad translations, and switching languages as a player.

## Tips
- [5:32](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=332) **[[localization-and-international]]** Don't ship Google Translate output as your main translation; players and communities notice inappropriate or nonsensical wording and leave.
- [14:28](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=868) **[[localization-and-international]]** If you can't afford translators, enable automatic translations only briefly while human translators correct the strings; don't rely on them long-term.
- [15:09](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=909) **[[localization-and-international]]** Check which countries your players come from, translate the most-played countries first, then add other languages. This saves money and gets more attention.
- [10:24](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=624) **[[localization-and-international]]** You don't need to support every language. Choose only the languages your audience uses, since you control which languages you add.
- [4:54](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=294) **[[localization-and-international]]** Tycoon terms are hard to translate; some words have no natural equivalent in the target language, so use a workaround that conveys the meaning.
- [11:06](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=666) **[[localization-and-international]]** Review auto-generated strings after enabling automatic translations: a large game's string table can balloon, and unwanted strings can be deleted.
- [13:11](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=791) **[[localization-and-international]]** Hire a professional translator for mistakes rather than trusting raw machine output; poor translations hurt developer reputation even when the developer chose them.
- [16:26](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=986) **[[localization-and-international]]** Build a portfolio and join a localization team; the speaker got big jobs through a team vouching for them, not from solo outreach alone.
