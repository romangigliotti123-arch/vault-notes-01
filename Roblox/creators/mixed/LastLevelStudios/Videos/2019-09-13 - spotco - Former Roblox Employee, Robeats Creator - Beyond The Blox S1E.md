## Tips
- [28:14](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=1694) **[[client-server-and-remotes]]** Ship new features behind a server-side flag defaulting off; enable gradually (1%→5%→10%→50%→100%) while watching crash rates, and turn off instantly if they spike.
- [42:40](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=2560) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Join game jams or hack-week style sprints to build a playable prototype fast; it quickly shows whether the core idea is actually fun.
- [43:22](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=2602) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Nail the core gameplay loop first, make it fun, then layer monetisation and extra features on top.
- [45:52](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=2752) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** For free-to-play, design for players returning daily or several times a week, not one-time play; value comes from repeat sessions.
- [46:34](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=2794) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Use daily rotating content (daily shop songs, daily missions), gacha-style rolls, and slow progression grinds so players never see all content in one session.
- [36:33](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=2193) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build a side project you can point to and, ideally, one that makes money; it strengthens job applications more than generic credentials.
- [38:33](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=2313) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Working for yourself beats trading time for money; a game that earns passive income lets you reinvest effort in improving it.
- [37:14](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=2234) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Early monetisation advice from an accelerator case: starter packs and time-limited offers significantly increase revenue on a game.
- [11:12](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=672) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Let your own projects speak in job interviews; side projects that made money help you stand out from recruiters' stacks of resumes.
- [49:27](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=2967) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't limit job hunting to big-name studios; most openings are at smaller companies, and mobile/free-to-play is a realistic entry point.
- [58:38](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=3518) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** To stand out for games roles, show persistence through self-driven projects like learning to code, making games and building in Roblox.
- [16:34](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=994) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Fast-follow proven hits (e.g. Zynga copying Clash of Clans) with a few differentiating features, such as theme, to reduce risk.
