# The SCR story (I quit)

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Watch](https://youtu.be/kQ6JMjjGvow) | LastLevelStudios | 69,498 views | 2023-12-17

## Summary
BanTech (Adam) explains his history with Stepford County Railway (SCR), built from a 2017 signalling-script contribution into a 50M-visit Roblox rail sim, and why he quit the development role. Covers zero-budget growth, motivation decline, and his next projects (a 10-pin bowling game and a workspace-selection plugin).

## Tips
- [5:26](https://youtu.be/kQ6JMjjGvow?t=326) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Aim for a multiplayer experience that satisfies both enthusiasts and casual players; SCR's broad appeal was a deliberate design goal.
- [5:26](https://youtu.be/kQ6JMjjGvow?t=326) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Pick a niche with few competitors on Roblox; SCR had few rivals covering its feature set, so it stood out.
- [6:07](https://youtu.be/kQ6JMjjGvow?t=367) **[[common-mistakes]]** Keep the game's identity focused on the core experience rather than polishing niche details or visuals; SCR kept blocky rails and 2D ballast by choice.
- [6:40](https://youtu.be/kQ6JMjjGvow?t=400) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Watch motivation: if you feel obligation to players rather than enjoyment, it may be time to hand off the project, as a lingering duty can hold the game back.

## Games
- **Stepford County Railway (SCR)** [2:36](https://youtu.be/kQ6JMjjGvow?t=156): Rail network sim with semi-automatic signalling, announcements and platform screens; grew through word of mouth alone to 50M visits.
- **SCR The Big Update 1.0.0** [4:43](https://youtu.be/kQ6JMjjGvow?t=283): Major overhaul (UI, physics, block signalling, XP unlocks, scheduling) that set the game's foundation and drove growth.
- **10 Pin bowling game (rebuild of Lanes)** [7:54](https://youtu.be/kQ6JMjjGvow?t=474): BanTech's next project, being rebuilt with more features beyond bowling.
