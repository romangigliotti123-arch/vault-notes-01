## [[updates-live-ops]]
- 2019-10-11 [3:16](https://youtu.be/vY0om5O-Oh8?t=196) Keep updating games; players leave when content stops, and starting new games rather than updating one loses the existing player base. ([[2019-10-11 - iGottic - Common Roblox Developer Mistakes - Beyond The Blox S1E6|iGottic - Common Roblox Developer Mistakes - Beyon]])
- 2019-10-11 [18:45](https://youtu.be/vY0om5O-Oh8?t=1125) Limit shutdowns; each one drives away players. Use a soft-shutdown script that moves players to a lobby and back via TeleportService. ([[2019-10-11 - iGottic - Common Roblox Developer Mistakes - Beyond The Blox S1E6|iGottic - Common Roblox Developer Mistakes - Beyon]])
- 2021-08-27 [30:36](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1836) Hire specialist builders and a scripter for a large rebuild of an existing game; he hired three builders and one scripter for a major Make a Cake update. ([[2021-08-27 - thebenster - Roblox Security, Make a Cake - Beyond The Blox S2E4|thebenster - Roblox Security, Make a Cake - Beyond]])
