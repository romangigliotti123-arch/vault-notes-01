## [[localization-and-international]]
- 2021-07-02 [5:32](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=332) Don't ship Google Translate output as your main translation; players and communities notice inappropriate or nonsensical wording and leave. ([[2021-07-02 - ItsKoiske - Translating Roblox, Punk of all Trades - Beyond The Blox S|ItsKoiske - Translating Roblox, Punk of all Trades]])
- 2021-07-02 [14:28](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=868) If you can't afford translators, enable automatic translations only briefly while human translators correct the strings; don't rely on them long-term. ([[2021-07-02 - ItsKoiske - Translating Roblox, Punk of all Trades - Beyond The Blox S|ItsKoiske - Translating Roblox, Punk of all Trades]])
- 2021-07-02 [15:09](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=909) Check which countries your players come from, translate the most-played countries first, then add other languages. This saves money and gets more attention. ([[2021-07-02 - ItsKoiske - Translating Roblox, Punk of all Trades - Beyond The Blox S|ItsKoiske - Translating Roblox, Punk of all Trades]])
- 2021-07-02 [10:24](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=624) You don't need to support every language. Choose only the languages your audience uses, since you control which languages you add. ([[2021-07-02 - ItsKoiske - Translating Roblox, Punk of all Trades - Beyond The Blox S|ItsKoiske - Translating Roblox, Punk of all Trades]])
- 2021-07-02 [4:54](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=294) Tycoon terms are hard to translate; some words have no natural equivalent in the target language, so use a workaround that conveys the meaning. ([[2021-07-02 - ItsKoiske - Translating Roblox, Punk of all Trades - Beyond The Blox S|ItsKoiske - Translating Roblox, Punk of all Trades]])
- 2021-07-02 [11:06](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=666) Review auto-generated strings after enabling automatic translations: a large game's string table can balloon, and unwanted strings can be deleted. ([[2021-07-02 - ItsKoiske - Translating Roblox, Punk of all Trades - Beyond The Blox S|ItsKoiske - Translating Roblox, Punk of all Trades]])
- 2021-07-02 [13:11](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=791) Hire a professional translator for mistakes rather than trusting raw machine output; poor translations hurt developer reputation even when the developer chose them. ([[2021-07-02 - ItsKoiske - Translating Roblox, Punk of all Trades - Beyond The Blox S|ItsKoiske - Translating Roblox, Punk of all Trades]])
- 2021-07-02 [16:26](https://youtu.be/qJx27G0N9kE?t=986) Build a portfolio and join a localization team; the speaker got big jobs through a team vouching for them, not from solo outreach alone.
