## [[core-loops-and-game-design]]
- 2019-09-13 [43:22](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=2602) Nail the core gameplay loop first, make it fun, then layer monetisation and extra features on top. ([[2019-09-13 - spotco - Former Roblox Employee, Robeats Creator - Beyond The Blox S1E|spotco - Former Roblox Employee, Robeats Creator -]])
- 2019-10-11 [12:13](https://youtu.be/vY0om5O-Oh8?t=733) Keep art style consistent; mixing realistic and cartoony assets confuses players and loses the style-based player base. ([[2019-10-11 - iGottic - Common Roblox Developer Mistakes - Beyond The Blox S1E6|iGottic - Common Roblox Developer Mistakes - Beyon]])
- 2019-10-11 [14:17](https://youtu.be/vY0om5O-Oh8?t=857) Define a clear target audience (age and demographic) before building; it drives style, marketing channels and platform choices. ([[2019-10-11 - iGottic - Common Roblox Developer Mistakes - Beyond The Blox S1E6|iGottic - Common Roblox Developer Mistakes - Beyon]])
- 2019-10-11 [21:28](https://youtu.be/vY0om5O-Oh8?t=1288) Make campaign modes fully linear or fully open-world; mixing both with hidden required steps frustrates players. ([[2019-10-11 - iGottic - Common Roblox Developer Mistakes - Beyond The Blox S1E6|iGottic - Common Roblox Developer Mistakes - Beyon]])
- 2020-08-24 [17:06](https://youtu.be/uYIE3IVmKY8?t=1026) Compute signal state with a priority order: hard danger from occupancy overrides manual requests, then advanced signals ahead, then manual state with optional roll-back. ([[2020-08-24 - SCR's Signalling System - BTS² 03|SCR's Signalling System - BTS² #03]])
- 2021-08-27 [18:33](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1113) Build on a niche you're good at: he focused on comedy chaos games with silly absurdist tone, which kept the game family-friendly. ([[2021-08-27 - thebenster - Roblox Security, Make a Cake - Beyond The Blox S2E4|thebenster - Roblox Security, Make a Cake - Beyond]])
- 2023-12-17 [5:26](https://youtu.be/kQ6JMjjGvow?t=326) Aim for a multiplayer experience that satisfies both enthusiasts and casual players; SCR's broad appeal was a deliberate design goal. ([[2023-12-17 - The SCR story (I quit)|The SCR story (I quit)]])
