## [[analytics-metrics]]
- 2020-02-14 [46:18](https://youtu.be/_LdpUJ41cso?t=2778) Judge ratings using recent data only; old dislikes from an early buggy version can permanently hurt a game that was later fixed. ([[2020-02-14 - SteadyOn - Clueless Roblox, Game Analytics - Beyond The Blox S1E11|SteadyOn - Clueless Roblox, Game Analytics - Beyon]])
- 2025-11-05 [20:22](https://youtu.be/KNHngRRMPVI?t=1222) Check the new safety analytics in the developer Analytics page: report-abuse breakdowns (exploiting, profanity, bullying) and comparison to the 90th percentile of games. ([[2025-11-05 - Will emissive maps finally put an end to lighting hacks — Beyond The B|Will emissive maps finally put an end to lighting ]])

## [[playtesting-and-feedback]]
- 2020-02-14 [11:47](https://youtu.be/_LdpUJ41cso?t=707) Fix bugs before a big traffic spike; a front-page surge with broken saving or empty plots lost most new players. ([[2020-02-14 - SteadyOn - Clueless Roblox, Game Analytics - Beyond The Blox S1E11|SteadyOn - Clueless Roblox, Game Analytics - Beyon]])
- 2020-02-14 [28:34](https://youtu.be/_LdpUJ41cso?t=1714) Build a tester community or request QA testing from a QA Discord before release. ([[2020-02-14 - SteadyOn - Clueless Roblox, Game Analytics - Beyond The Blox S1E11|SteadyOn - Clueless Roblox, Game Analytics - Beyon]])

## [[team-and-budget]]

([[2019-09-06 - Charlie_RBX - Building a Roblox Game Community - Beyond The Blox S1E3|Charlie_RBX - Building a Roblox Game Community - B]])
- 2019-09-06 [14:55](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=895) Do not pick leaders only from friends; choose by personality, maturity and trustworthiness, and keep a path for applicants to reach management. ([[2019-09-06 - Charlie_RBX - Building a Roblox Game Community - Beyond The Blox S1E3|Charlie_RBX - Building a Roblox Game Community - B]])
- 2020-03-01 [7:57](https://youtu.be/4fz4fpJLUSQ?t=477) Watch for red flags early on a team; mixed experience levels caused repeated mistakes and constant rework fixing them. ([[2021-12-09 - GregTame - Pioneering Roblox VR into the Future (Virtual Reality) - Be|GregTame - Pioneering Roblox VR into the Future (V]])

## [[development-process-and-speed]]
- 2019-09-13 [42:40](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=2560) Join game jams or hack-week style sprints to build a playable prototype fast; it quickly shows whether the core idea is actually fun. ([[2019-09-13 - spotco - Former Roblox Employee, Robeats Creator - Beyond The Blox S1E|spotco - Former Roblox Employee, Robeats Creator -]])
- 2019-09-13 [36:33](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=2193) Build a side project you can point to and, ideally, one that makes money; it strengthens job applications more than generic credentials. ([[2019-09-13 - spotco - Former Roblox Employee, Robeats Creator - Beyond The Blox S1E|spotco - Former Roblox Employee, Robeats Creator -]])
- 2020-02-07 [12:49](https://youtu.be/HU4JJOft4Pw?t=769) Iterate on past scripts, systems and games when you see potential; rebuilding with newer techniques is an effective learning path. ([[2020-02-07 - boatbomber - Lua Learning on Roblox - Beyond The Blox S1E10|boatbomber - Lua Learning on Roblox - Beyond The B]])
- 2020-02-07 [32:10](https://youtu.be/HU4JJOft4Pw?t=1930) Beginners should first build a small project such as a progress-saving obby to learn functions, events, DataStores and movement. ([[2020-02-07 - boatbomber - Lua Learning on Roblox - Beyond The Blox S1E10|boatbomber - Lua Learning on Roblox - Beyond The B]])
- 2020-02-14 [34:41](https://youtu.be/_LdpUJ41cso?t=2081) Spread development out as a marathon; rushing a release reduces polish. ([[2020-02-14 - SteadyOn - Clueless Roblox, Game Analytics - Beyond The Blox S1E11|SteadyOn - Clueless Roblox, Game Analytics - Beyon]])
- 2021-08-27 [5:51](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=351) Sequels vs updates: he kept the old game running as long as it wasn't game-breaking, and launched a new game rather than replacing a still-performing one (about 50k plays/week). ([[2021-08-27 - thebenster - Roblox Security, Make a Cake - Beyond The Blox S2E4|thebenster - Roblox Security, Make a Cake - Beyond]])
- 2021-08-27 [8:34](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=514) Replace a legacy game only if it is game-breaking; otherwise keep it running to preserve nostalgia and reduce maintenance. ([[2021-08-27 - thebenster - Roblox Security, Make a Cake - Beyond The Blox S2E4|thebenster - Roblox Security, Make a Cake - Beyond]])
- 2021-12-09 [2:05](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=125) Tankery's CSG-injected OBJ tank models kept each tank to ~10 parts, enabling 20v20 battles with low lag. ([[2021-12-09 - GregTame - Pioneering Roblox VR into the Future (Virtual Reality) - Be|GregTame - Pioneering Roblox VR into the Future (V]])
- 2021-12-09 [18:44](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=1124) Stay on the platform you already know when it pays bills; switching engines could cost about a year to reach comparable selling speed. ([[2021-12-09 - GregTame - Pioneering Roblox VR into the Future (Virtual Reality) - Be|GregTame - Pioneering Roblox VR into the Future (V]])
- 2022-01-07 [16:57](https://youtu.be/v8S5puPQEw4?t=1017) Extract systems (lighting, permissions, commands, pets) into modular, versioned packages so you can drop them into new games in about 30 seconds. ([[2022-01-07 - Quenty - Whatever Floats Your Boat, Roblox Intern, Open Source Tools -|Quenty - Whatever Floats Your Boat, Roblox Intern,]])
- 2022-01-07 [25:33](https://youtu.be/v8S5puPQEw4?t=1533) Build small games that prove out individual tech pieces while drawing players, then reuse the pieces in bigger projects. ([[2022-01-07 - Quenty - Whatever Floats Your Boat, Roblox Intern, Open Source Tools -|Quenty - Whatever Floats Your Boat, Roblox Intern,]])
- 2022-01-07 [27:06](https://youtu.be/v8S5puPQEw4?t=1626) Expect most accelerator-style projects not to release; the learning still has value, and pieces can be reused in future games. ([[2022-01-07 - Quenty - Whatever Floats Your Boat, Roblox Intern, Open Source Tools -|Quenty - Whatever Floats Your Boat, Roblox Intern,]])
- 2023-12-17 [2:36](https://youtu.be/kQ6JMjjGvow?t=156) Iterate on feedback from the community quickly; SCR's signal prototype grew into a full rebuild within days of a simple fix. ([[2023-12-17 - The SCR story (I quit)|The SCR story (I quit)]])
- 2023-12-17 [3:57](https://youtu.be/kQ6JMjjGvow?t=237) Plan for tooling early: a physical wall-map representation became unmanageable, so a GUI was needed sooner than planned. ([[2023-12-17 - The SCR story (I quit)|The SCR story (I quit)]])

## [[common-mistakes]]
- 2019-09-06 [6:17](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=377) Assume community actions reflect on you; spam done in your community's name will be blamed on the owner, so enforce rules. ([[2019-09-06 - Charlie_RBX - Building a Roblox Game Community - Beyond The Blox S1E3|Charlie_RBX - Building a Roblox Game Community - B]])
- 2019-09-13 [49:27](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=2967) Don't limit job hunting to big-name studios; most openings are at smaller companies, and mobile/free-to-play is a realistic entry point. ([[2019-09-13 - spotco - Former Roblox Employee, Robeats Creator - Beyond The Blox S1E|spotco - Former Roblox Employee, Robeats Creator -]])
- 2019-10-11 [28:45](https://youtu.be/vY0om5O-Oh8?t=1725) Understand an API or script before implementing it; untested features like a camera bob script that tanks frame rate hurt the game. ([[2019-10-11 - iGottic - Common Roblox Developer Mistakes - Beyond The Blox S1E6|iGottic - Common Roblox Developer Mistakes - Beyon]])
- 2019-10-11 [30:48](https://youtu.be/vY0om5O-Oh8?t=1848) Learn from the Roblox Developer Hub and DevForum, and check the Toolbox for scripts, but verify and understand them before use. ([[2019-10-11 - iGottic - Common Roblox Developer Mistakes - Beyond The Blox S1E6|iGottic - Common Roblox Developer Mistakes - Beyon]])
- 2020-02-07 [17:22](https://youtu.be/HU4JJOft4Pw?t=1042) Sandbox user code so errors and infinite loops cannot crash Studio; this lets players experiment freely. ([[2020-02-07 - boatbomber - Lua Learning on Roblox - Beyond The Blox S1E10|boatbomber - Lua Learning on Roblox - Beyond The B]])
- 2020-03-01 [15:46](https://youtu.be/4fz4fpJLUSQ?t=946) Plan infrastructure for scale early: Rover hit ~100k servers and needed 32+ GB RAM, forcing a rewrite and a separate hosted service. ([[2020-03-01 - evaera - Adopt Me!, Roblox Discord Bots - Beyond The Blox S1E12|evaera - Adopt Me!, Roblox Discord Bots - Beyond T]])
- 2020-03-01 [9:21](https://youtu.be/4fz4fpJLUSQ?t=561) Lock down scope with sponsors; mid-project asset rejections forced deleted work and a 15-hour-day crunch at the end. ([[2020-03-01 - evaera - Adopt Me!, Roblox Discord Bots - Beyond The Blox S1E12|evaera - Adopt Me!, Roblox Discord Bots - Beyond T]])
- 2020-03-23 [6:50](https://youtu.be/n0ODJH9EgWc?t=410) Rushed, poorly edited video tutorials are worse than well-written articles; pick a content format you can produce to a decent standard. ([[2020-03-23 - EgoMoose - Bloxy Nominee, Roblox Wiki - Beyond The Blox S1E13|EgoMoose - Bloxy Nominee, Roblox Wiki - Beyond The]])
- 2020-08-24 [31:13](https://youtu.be/uYIE3IVmKY8?t=1873) Lag-skipped sensors can leave a train stuck in a block and hold up the whole network; plan for missed enter/exit events. ([[2020-08-24 - SCR's Signalling System - BTS² 03|SCR's Signalling System - BTS² #03]])
- 2021-08-27 [20:35](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1235) Tone check: he cut realism for goofiness after about 30 percent of development, saying the gritty version wasn't fun. ([[2021-08-27 - thebenster - Roblox Security, Make a Cake - Beyond The Blox S2E4|thebenster - Roblox Security, Make a Cake - Beyond]])
- 2021-12-09 [8:52](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=532) VR is a small niche (~2-3% of Steam PC users, and Roblox has no PlayStation VR); size expectations accordingly before building. ([[2021-12-09 - GregTame - Pioneering Roblox VR into the Future (Virtual Reality) - Be|GregTame - Pioneering Roblox VR into the Future (V]])
- 2022-01-07 [24:10](https://youtu.be/v8S5puPQEw4?t=1450) Don't build tech without designing the game. Windlight Bay had lots of tech (NPCs, building, dialogue) but weak game design, so it didn't find players. ([[2022-01-07 - Quenty - Whatever Floats Your Boat, Roblox Intern, Open Source Tools -|Quenty - Whatever Floats Your Boat, Roblox Intern,]])
- 2023-12-17 [6:07](https://youtu.be/kQ6JMjjGvow?t=367) Keep the game's identity focused on the core experience rather than polishing niche details or visuals; SCR kept blocky rails and 2D ballast by choice. ([[2023-12-17 - The SCR story (I quit)|The SCR story (I quit)]])

## [[mindset-and-strategy]]
- 2019-09-13 [58:38](https://youtu.be/h4W8yHxmxns?t=3518) To stand out for games roles, show persistence through self-driven projects like learning to code, making games and building in Roblox. ([[2019-09-13 - spotco - Former Roblox Employee, Robeats Creator - Beyond The Blox S1E|spotco - Former Roblox Employee, Robeats Creator -]])
- 2020-02-07 [1:25](https://youtu.be/HU4JJOft4Pw?t=85) Start small: edit Roblox template games like Line Runner before attempting a large project; big first projects often demotivate beginners. ([[2020-02-07 - boatbomber - Lua Learning on Roblox - Beyond The Blox S1E10|boatbomber - Lua Learning on Roblox - Beyond The B]])
- 2020-02-07 [5:05](https://youtu.be/HU4JJOft4Pw?t=305) Your first game may be bad; you can't make a 50th game without making a first. Disliking old work is a sign you've improved. ([[2020-02-07 - boatbomber - Lua Learning on Roblox - Beyond The Blox S1E10|boatbomber - Lua Learning on Roblox - Beyond The B]])
- 2020-02-14 [9:43](https://youtu.be/_LdpUJ41cso?t=583) Start with small experimental projects to learn scripting; don't jump straight into a big game, since big projects set you up to fail. ([[2020-02-14 - SteadyOn - Clueless Roblox, Game Analytics - Beyond The Blox S1E11|SteadyOn - Clueless Roblox, Game Analytics - Beyon]])
- 2020-02-14 [25:06](https://youtu.be/_LdpUJ41cso?t=1506) Make games you genuinely care about; copying trending genres (clicker, lifting simulators) flopped and lost money. ([[2020-02-14 - SteadyOn - Clueless Roblox, Game Analytics - Beyond The Blox S1E11|SteadyOn - Clueless Roblox, Game Analytics - Beyon]])
- 2020-02-14 [31:17](https://youtu.be/_LdpUJ41cso?t=1877) Roblox development transfers to real game dev skills: server/client, UX, and monetisation design. ([[2020-02-14 - SteadyOn - Clueless Roblox, Game Analytics - Beyond The Blox S1E11|SteadyOn - Clueless Roblox, Game Analytics - Beyon]])
- 2021-08-27 [29:14](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1754) Keep the game as a side hobby with about 10-15 hours a week rather than going full time, to avoid burnout and keep the enjoyment. ([[2021-08-27 - thebenster - Roblox Security, Make a Cake - Beyond The Blox S2E4|thebenster - Roblox Security, Make a Cake - Beyond]])
- 2021-08-27 [27:52](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=1672) Plan for platform changes: Roblox can depreciate critical features, so keep some ongoing time for maintaining games. ([[2021-08-27 - thebenster - Roblox Security, Make a Cake - Beyond The Blox S2E4|thebenster - Roblox Security, Make a Cake - Beyond]])
- 2021-12-09 [41:40](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=2500) Give VR a month of experimentation first; expect modest earnings and treat it as uncharted territory, not a guaranteed hit. ([[2021-12-09 - GregTame - Pioneering Roblox VR into the Future (Virtual Reality) - Be|GregTame - Pioneering Roblox VR into the Future (V]])
- 2022-01-07 [26:25](https://youtu.be/v8S5puPQEw4?t=1585) Consistently releasing games is a skill in itself; most people never get a project released and popular. ([[2022-01-07 - Quenty - Whatever Floats Your Boat, Roblox Intern, Open Source Tools -|Quenty - Whatever Floats Your Boat, Roblox Intern,]])
- 2022-01-07 [4:17](https://youtu.be/v8S5puPQEw4?t=257) Keep creating things for fun, not just for payoff, to avoid burnout when Roblox becomes a job. ([[2022-01-07 - Quenty - Whatever Floats Your Boat, Roblox Intern, Open Source Tools -|Quenty - Whatever Floats Your Boat, Roblox Intern,]])
- 2023-12-17 [6:40](https://youtu.be/kQ6JMjjGvow?t=400) Watch motivation: if you feel obligation to players rather than enjoyment, it may be time to hand off the project, as a lingering duty can hold the game back. ([[2023-12-17 - The SCR story (I quit)|The SCR story (I quit)]])
