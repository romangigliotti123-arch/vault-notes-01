# The Most Valuable Roblox Algorithm Video You'll Ever Watch

## Summary
Explains how Roblox's recommendation algorithm tests games on the front page using per-player signals (playthrough rate, first-play bounce, play days, play time, Robux per user, spend days). Covers idea selection by stealing the underlying loop, icon/thumbnail clickability, first-minute hooks, retention via progression and daily reasons to return, monetization without pay-to-win, and reading ad data over a 48-hour to 30-day window.

## Tips
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=40) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Roblox tests new games on a small homepage group, then expands if engagement holds; per-player metrics level the field for small games.
- [2:10](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=130) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Copy the reason people play (core loop), not the game itself; change theme and twist mechanics to stay distinct.
- [2:51](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=171) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Check what's growing on the front page, discover page, and clone trackers before choosing an idea; ask what the proven reason to play is and what is uniquely yours.
- [4:18](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=258) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Playthrough rate is the top discovery signal; icons should be simple, colorful, show the core action, and communicate the game instantly.
- [5:39](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=339) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use 16:9 thumbnails for sponsored ads; the speaker cites a 40% playthrough increase versus icons.
- [6:03](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=363) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Get players into fun within seconds: skip loading screens, long tutorials and forced customization; start the core action immediately.
- [6:03](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=363) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** First-play bounce rate directly hurts discovery; fix anything making first-time players leave quickly.
- [7:28](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=448) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give players a reason to return the next day, e.g. progress that changes while offline (crops growing in Grow a Garden), daily rewards, and events.
- [7:28](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=448) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Ship a meaningful update weekly, or at least a quality update every two weeks.
- [7:28](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=448) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build social play into the game; solo games perform worse, so make friends part of the experience.
- [8:54](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=534) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Sell boosts, cosmetics or convenience that players can rebuy; avoid pay-to-win or paywalls that block core gameplay like sprint or scare mechanics.
- [9:36](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=576) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ads can raise player counts but not ranking; judge ad traffic by day-7 retention and play sessions, not impressions or clicks.
- [10:16](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=616) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** A campaign with high impressions but few plays signals a weak game; fix retention before spending more on ads.
- [10:57](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=657) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Qualified-player threshold for Roblox Kids and Select campaigns is now 250 engaged players, down from 500 (changed August of this year per speaker).
- [11:14](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=674) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Ad data takes up to 48 hours and plays are credited up to 30 days later; do not judge a campaign on its first afternoon.
- [11:56](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=716) **[[analytics-metrics]]** When players spike, identify the update or change responsible, then do more of what worked; the speaker suggests using an AI assistant to analyze it.
