# how to pick the BEST niche on Roblox

## Summary
Argues niche choice drives early player acquisition on Roblox. Covers entry-level genres (obbies, tycoons, simulators) needing a twist, fighting and RPG/horror tradeoffs, and a framework: pick genre by competition/demand, MVP scope, and one clear hook. Promotes its own tool Reblox.

## Tips
- [1:35](https://youtu.be/U9RGcuxt9m4?t=95) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Entry genres (obby, tycoon, simulator) work but are saturated; add a distinct twist, e.g. clicky-keyboard or ASMR obby, to get numbers.
- [1:13](https://youtu.be/U9RGcuxt9m4?t=73) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Judge success against your own history, not front-page games; 50 concurrent players from zero is a real win.
- [3:26](https://youtu.be/U9RGcuxt9m4?t=206) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Horror and RPG have less competition at the top but high quality bars; they reward high-risk, high-reward devs with long build time.
- [5:28](https://youtu.be/U9RGcuxt9m4?t=328) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Before picking a genre, honestly ask: can I build it with my team size and time? A solo dev cannot ship a full RPG in 3 months.
- [5:28](https://youtu.be/U9RGcuxt9m4?t=328) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Check market space: saturated niches (simulators, brainrot) need heavy differentiation; look for demand with less competition.
- [6:09](https://youtu.be/U9RGcuxt9m4?t=369) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Build around one singular clear hook rather than five features; the games that blow up have one thing that sets them apart.
- [6:19](https://youtu.be/U9RGcuxt9m4?t=379) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't clone top-earning games (e.g. the 15th pet simulator with a brainrot skin); clones are not a niche and won't get players.
- [4:14](https://youtu.be/U9RGcuxt9m4?t=254) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Scope the MVP to the smallest fully working version with core features, targeting a 2-4 week build for a solo dev.
