## Summary
Breakdown of why the Steal an Egg-style game blew up: a simple, instantly readable idea, a high-contrast icon with one focal point, a verb-a-noun title trend, and broad cross-platform, all-ages appeal. The speaker says to copy the principles, not the game, and to ride trends early while keeping the gameplay good.
