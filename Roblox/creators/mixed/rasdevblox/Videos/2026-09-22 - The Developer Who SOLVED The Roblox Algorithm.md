# The Developer Who SOLVED The Roblox Algorithm

[Watch](https://youtu.be/ILhTEWqz2kU) | rasdevblox | 10,846 views | 2026-09-22

## Summary
Solo developer Muneeb built Catalog Avatar Creator (CAC), a simple outfit try-on game, after a tycoon project stalled. Lessons: solve existing player demand, release small, double down on signals, and let Roblox's growing catalog feed content automatically. Mostly anecdotal, with promotion for the speaker's site.

## Tips
- [2:01](https://youtu.be/ILhTEWqz2kU?t=121) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Find where players already spend time and money, then remove the friction they complain about, rather than inventing something wholly original.
- [3:39](https://youtu.be/ILhTEWqz2kU?t=219) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Release a small version, watch for a real player signal, and double down only if people are engaging. You don't need a finished product first.
- [4:49](https://youtu.be/ILhTEWqz2kU?t=289) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build systems that grow without maintenance. CAC updated automatically as Roblox added catalog items, so the game needed little upkeep.
- [5:30](https://youtu.be/ILhTEWqz2kU?t=330) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Spend time as an active player in the platform's ecosystem; understanding Roblox players beats outspending large brands on marketing.
- [5:48](https://youtu.be/ILhTEWqz2kU?t=348) **[[community-and-discord]]** Run a Discord where players suggest features, then prioritise and build the ones that get traction.
- [5:48](https://youtu.be/ILhTEWqz2kU?t=348) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Follow a repeatable cycle: observe what players care about, find friction, build, release, watch response, then double down or drop.
