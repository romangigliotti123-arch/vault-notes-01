# Stop Blaming the Algorithm (Episode 1)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q) | rasdevblox | 4,871 views | 2026-08-25

## Summary
Episode 1 of a Roblox growth series arguing that games fail on the click, not the algorithm. Covers how Roblox ranks games by retention, playtime and spend, then icon design, title formulas, and trailer structure as the three factors that decide whether players click and join.

## Tips
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=40) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Roblox ranks games against their genre, then against similar-audience games, using retention, playtime and spend. Need traffic first to generate those stats.
- [2:44](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=164) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make the icon readable in about half a second: one clear focal point, high subject-background contrast, minimal clutter, and no raw in-game screenshots.
- [3:24](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=204) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use a face or big eyes, and large text or none. Several top games use a face filling the frame and huge text.
- [4:45](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=285) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Show progression in the icon, such as plus-one or per-second numbers, so players see growth before reading or playing.
- [6:46](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=406) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use negative space to stand out when surrounding icons are busy. Avoid AI-generated slop icons.
- [7:52](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=472) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Keep titles simple and instantly understood, like a half-second comprehension test. Use Verb the Noun format, e.g. Steal an Egg or Clean All the Leaves.
- [9:14](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=554) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Add brackets with freshness tags like [NEW], [BETA], or an emoji to signal an update or urgency and break title staleness.
- [9:54](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=594) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Study top-chart titles and icons, then adapt their patterns rather than copying exactly. Icon and title should reinforce each other.
- [10:10](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=610) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Use in-game clips rather than heavy animated trailers. Trailers with good editing help conversion, but a bad game with a great trailer will still fail.
- [11:31](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=691) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Structure trailers: 5-10s high-action hook, 15-20s core features and map, then combat or features, ending with title and call to action. Aim for 60-90s, with music cut on beat.
- [12:12](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=732) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Start with CapCut if new to editing. DaVinci Resolve is the recommended value option, and Premiere Pro with After Effects if you can pay.
