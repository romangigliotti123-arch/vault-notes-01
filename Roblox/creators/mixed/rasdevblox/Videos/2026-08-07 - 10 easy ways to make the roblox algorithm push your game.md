## Tips
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/Ox5pwUuzF-M?t=0) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make the icon high-contrast with one focal point and a readable emotion; bland icons lower CTR and the algorithm stops pushing the game.
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/Ox5pwUuzF-M?t=40) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Hook players within the first 8-15 seconds with instant fun and rewards; avoid tutorials that make players leave. Slap Battles is the example.
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/Ox5pwUuzF-M?t=81) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build a core loop of repeated actions with visible progress every minute, so the game does not feel the same at 10 minutes as at 1 minute.
- [2:24](https://youtu.be/Ox5pwUuzF-M?t=144) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add retention hooks such as daily rewards, leaderboards, trading of scarce items, and leveling or prestige/rebirth systems.
- [3:07](https://youtu.be/Ox5pwUuzF-M?t=187) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Use limited-time events and items that expire or can never be repurchased to create FOMO and bring players back.
- [3:07](https://youtu.be/Ox5pwUuzF-M?t=187) **[[community-and-discord]]** Prompt players in-game to favorite the game; favorited games in their list can bring them back to play again.
- [3:43](https://youtu.be/Ox5pwUuzF-M?t=223) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Promote the game early through TikToks, friends, and small ads; growth snowballs once the game gets traction, but initial push is needed.
- [4:05](https://youtu.be/Ox5pwUuzF-M?t=245) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Copy what already works but add a creative twist; the speaker says totally original games are harder to blow up.
- [4:40](https://youtu.be/Ox5pwUuzF-M?t=280) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not make monetization scummy: avoid paywalling basics like sprinting or teleporting and cluttering the screen with purchase prompts, since it turns players off.
- [4:59](https://youtu.be/Ox5pwUuzF-M?t=299) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Update consistently, weekly or monthly or every two months, so players do not see the game as dead.
