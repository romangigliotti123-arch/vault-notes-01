# how to keep players addicted to your roblox game

[Watch](https://youtu.be/M-GCMK1Tryk) | rasdevblox | 182 views | 2026-04-27

## Summary
Argues Roblox games succeed by engineering addiction: a core loop (action-reward-repeat), variable rewards, visible progression, FOMO, status/mastery, and social pressure. Promotes a trend-tracking site. Mostly motivational, few concrete numbers.

## Tips
- [0:41](https://youtu.be/M-GCMK1Tryk?t=41) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Define a core loop: a main action plus a reward, then repeat. If you can't identify it in 10 seconds, the game lacks one.
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/M-GCMK1Tryk?t=81) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Randomize reward size, rarity and timing rather than giving the same reward every time, since predictable rewards lead to quitting.
- [2:02](https://youtu.be/M-GCMK1Tryk?t=122) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make every session show forward growth: add level systems, rebirths or prestige so the player feels moved up each session.
- [2:02](https://youtu.be/M-GCMK1Tryk?t=122) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give rewards even when players lose or underperform (e.g. XP on loss, daily tasks visible) so stuck players keep grinding.
- [2:42](https://youtu.be/M-GCMK1Tryk?t=162) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Frame limited items and daily rewards as something players will lose if they don't return, not as something they gain.
- [2:42](https://youtu.be/M-GCMK1Tryk?t=162) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Use limited items, rare spawns and leaderboard resets to create fear of missing out that drives return visits.
- [3:23](https://youtu.be/M-GCMK1Tryk?t=203) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Give players showable status items (rare or most expensive items) and mastery goals worth grinding for.
- [3:23](https://youtu.be/M-GCMK1Tryk?t=203) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add leaderboards and ranked modes so players can compete against each other.
- [4:03](https://youtu.be/M-GCMK1Tryk?t=243) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Use real-player competition and invite mechanics, group quests and shared goals; single-player games struggle on a social platform.
