## Tips
- [1:20](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=80) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Roblox ranks games by retention (day 1, day 7), playtime and spend, first against genre, then against the same audience. Strong stats earn a front-page push.
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=40) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** A brand-new game with zero visits gets no organic lift. Bring the first players yourself via ads or short-form content before expecting the algorithm to help.
- [2:42](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=162) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Icon gets about half a second of attention. It must read instantly without the title, so the subject and game idea should be clear at a glance.
- [3:22](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=202) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use a face with strong emotion and big eyes filling the frame. Many top games (Adopt Me, 99 Nights in the Forest) do this.
- [3:22](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=202) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Give the icon one clear focal point the eye lands on, and make the subject pop from the background with strong contrast.
- [4:45](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=285) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Show progression on the icon with a number overlay (e.g. '+1 per click', '$997M') so players see reward before playing.
- [4:45](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=285) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Avoid raw in-game screenshots and AI-generated icons. The speaker calls them the worst options and says they hurt click-through.
- [4:45](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=285) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Keep the title a half-second comprehension test. Use plain 'verb the noun' names (Steal an Egg, Clean All the Leaves) instead of clever names.
- [5:25](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=325) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Add brackets with an emoji or tag like [NEW], [BETA] to signal freshness and urgency and break up title staleness.
- [6:46](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=406) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make icon and title work together, so each reinforces the other. Study top charts and recent winners and borrow their patterns.
- [10:07](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=607) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Make a trailer of about 60-90 seconds: a high-action hook in the first 5-10s, main features in 15-20s, then a title card with a call to action. Use in-game clips and on-beat music.
- [11:29](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=689) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Use in-game footage rather than a flashy animated trailer. Trailers that over-promise a weak game make players angry; focus on building the game first.
- [12:50](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=770) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Hover-to-preview gameplay on the game tile lets players judge the game before clicking, and the speaker says it strongly influenced his own decisions.
