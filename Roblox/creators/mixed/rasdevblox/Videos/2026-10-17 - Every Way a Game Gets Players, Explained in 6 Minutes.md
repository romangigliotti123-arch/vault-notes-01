# Every Way a Game Gets Players, Explained in 6 Minutes

[Watch](https://youtu.be/DtdGtvALhCA) | rasdevblox | 33,603 views | 2026-10-17

## Summary
Overview of roughly a dozen ways games get players on Roblox, grouped into four buckets: recommendations (home page), search, paid ads, and external traffic (TikTok, YouTube, Discord, friends), plus returning players via updates and events. Stresses that game quality underpins all of them.

## Tips
- [1:04](https://youtu.be/DtdGtvALhCA?t=64) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Get past the 250 highly engaged players threshold first; the home page recommendation depends on clicks, early leave rate, D1/D7 returns and playtime.
- [0:24](https://youtu.be/DtdGtvALhCA?t=24) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Name the game so it describes what it is (e.g. a tycoon or genre term), since search ranks on the game name.
- [1:04](https://youtu.be/DtdGtvALhCA?t=64) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Watch the Up and Coming and Top Earning lists on Roblox to find new games gaining traction.
- [2:17](https://youtu.be/DtdGtvALhCA?t=137) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Only pay for ads once the game already holds players; ads don't rescue a weak game. Roblox tests reported +14% play sessions and +10% week-later retention (median, 21 campaigns).
- [2:42](https://youtu.be/DtdGtvALhCA?t=162) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** TikTok is the biggest external traffic source; even 100k-500k views from small accounts can drive large player counts.
- [3:22](https://youtu.be/DtdGtvALhCA?t=202) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** A large YouTuber playing your game can spread to TikTok and YouTube and grow the game much faster than dev logs.
- [3:22](https://youtu.be/DtdGtvALhCA?t=202) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Dev log videos on YouTube build interest over time but grow slowly.
- [4:04](https://youtu.be/DtdGtvALhCA?t=244) **[[community-and-discord]]** Run a Discord to gather a core group of passionate players who return repeatedly.
- [4:04](https://youtu.be/DtdGtvALhCA?t=244) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Design the game for playing with friends and use friend invites; the co-play signal is a social metric that feeds growth.
- [4:39](https://youtu.be/DtdGtvALhCA?t=279) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship updates and events regularly and use notifications to bring back returning players; don't publish and abandon.
- [5:19](https://youtu.be/DtdGtvALhCA?t=319) **[[analytics-metrics]]** After an update, watch the baseline a week later, not the spike, and try to keep that baseline as high as possible.
- [5:26](https://youtu.be/DtdGtvALhCA?t=326) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Retention decides whether the home page keeps featuring you; a fun game that people return to keeps growing for months.
- [6:06](https://youtu.be/DtdGtvALhCA?t=366) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Growth from any channel fails if the game isn't fun; quality comes first.
