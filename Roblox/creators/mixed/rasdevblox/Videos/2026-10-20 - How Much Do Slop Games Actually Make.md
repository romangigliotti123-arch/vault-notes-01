# How Much Do Slop Games Actually Make?

[Watch](https://youtu.be/53OBGXktIPY) | rasdevblox | 13,028 views | 2026-10-20

## Summary
Breaks down how "slop" Roblox games (fast-made trend clones like Steal a Brain Rot, Grow a Garden, Steal an Egg) earn money: Robux-to-dev conversion, monetization mix of repeatable vs one-time purchases, revenue estimates, launch-and-fade lifecycles, and ten copy-and-monetize tactics. Revenue figures are the speaker's own estimates, not official numbers.

## Tips
- [1:10](https://youtu.be/53OBGXktIPY?t=70) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Devs keep about 70% of Robux spent; a $5 purchase (400 Robux) yields roughly $1.06 to the dev, about 21% of player spend.
- [2:02](https://youtu.be/53OBGXktIPY?t=122) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Sell repeatable consumables (spins, boosts, restocks, eggs) alongside one-time passes, since repeat purchases drive recurring revenue.
- [3:52](https://youtu.be/53OBGXktIPY?t=232) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Find the game blowing up this week and copy its core loop, changing one element (e.g., eggs to a different theme).
- [4:33](https://youtu.be/53OBGXktIPY?t=273) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Use a clear verb-plus-noun title that states the game's content immediately, e.g. 'Run for Eggs' or 'Build to Survive'.
- [5:14](https://youtu.be/53OBGXktIPY?t=314) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Show numbers going up dramatically (huge currency gains) to make progress feel satisfying.
- [5:55](https://youtu.be/53OBGXktIPY?t=355) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Use luck, rarity and timed events to keep players spinning; offer a cheap first purchase to get new players spending.
- [6:35](https://youtu.be/53OBGXktIPY?t=395) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Announce rare unlocks to the whole server in chat so other players want to chase the same reward.
- [6:35](https://youtu.be/53OBGXktIPY?t=395) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Design the game so its moments are clippable for YouTube; exposure from clips brings more players and spenders.
- [7:16](https://youtu.be/53OBGXktIPY?t=436) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan thoroughly before building, then ship quickly to ride a trend rather than spending months on development.
- [7:16](https://youtu.be/53OBGXktIPY?t=436) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Keep updating after launch to extend revenue longer, since trend-driven games spike then fall off within weeks.
- [3:32](https://youtu.be/53OBGXktIPY?t=212) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Expect launch spikes to halve in about 3 weeks; plan updates and new content to counter the decay.
- [2:51](https://youtu.be/53OBGXktIPY?t=171) **[[common-mistakes]]** Budget carefully: one dev spent $12,000 building a game and recouped only $3,000; another spent 15,000 Robux on ads and got back 2,000 Robux.
