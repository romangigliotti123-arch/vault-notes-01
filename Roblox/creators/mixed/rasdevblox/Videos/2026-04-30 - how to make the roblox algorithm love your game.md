# how to make the roblox algorithm love your game

## Summary
Overview of how Roblox's recommendation algorithm works: it rewards engagement (session time, retention, CTR). Covers seeding data with small ad spend, lengthening sessions, day-1/day-7 retention hooks, and non-predatory monetisation. Promotes the speaker's trend-tracking site and Discord.

## Tips
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/G15TVsyC61g?t=40) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Track three metrics: CTR (packaging), session time (target 10-15 min minimum), and day-1/day-7 retention. Fix the lowest one first.
- [1:20](https://youtu.be/G15TVsyC61g?t=80) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Strong CTR with weak retention gets you cut off; high retention with low CTR means nobody sees the game. All three must be healthy.
- [2:01](https://youtu.be/G15TVsyC61g?t=121) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Seed the algorithm with small consistent Robux ad spend, about 10k-30k Robux over 2-4 weeks, to generate initial data rather than one big spend.
- [2:01](https://youtu.be/G15TVsyC61g?t=121) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Seed without Robux by getting friends to play, posting in dev communities and Discord, and making TikToks or YouTube videos. Goal is data, not just players.
- [2:01](https://youtu.be/G15TVsyC61g?t=121) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Lengthen sessions: add stages to obbies, slow progression in simulators, add content to roleplay games, and add AFK mechanics where waiting makes sense.
- [2:01](https://youtu.be/G15TVsyC61g?t=121) **[[matchmaking-and-teleport]]** Make matchmaking instant in shooters or lobby games; every second waiting in a lobby risks the player leaving.
- [2:01](https://youtu.be/G15TVsyC61g?t=121) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make the game feel endless; players leave as soon as it feels like it ends.
- [2:41](https://youtu.be/G15TVsyC61g?t=161) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add daily rewards, login streak bonuses, daily quests and limited-time events as day-1 return hooks.
- [2:41](https://youtu.be/G15TVsyC61g?t=161) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** For day-7 retention, add long-term progression: rebirths, prestige, rare item hunts, and seasonal items that only drop during events.
- [2:41](https://youtu.be/G15TVsyC61g?t=161) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Prompt players in-game to favorite the game, and tell them what they will miss if they don't return (FOMO).
- [3:22](https://youtu.be/G15TVsyC61g?t=202) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Never lock core gameplay behind Robux. Sell cosmetics, speed upgrades and convenience only; pay-to-win tanks like ratio and drives players away.
- [3:22](https://youtu.be/G15TVsyC61g?t=202) **[[monetisation-other]]** Always keep an in-game currency so players don't feel a direct link between spending and real money.
- [3:22](https://youtu.be/G15TVsyC61g?t=202) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Keep purchase prompts small and unobtrusive; a giant purchase button on screen during play hurts retention and the algorithm.
