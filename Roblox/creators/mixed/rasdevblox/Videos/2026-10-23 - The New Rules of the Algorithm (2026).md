- [0:40](https://youtu.be/RC19PKdRt1Y?t=40) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Plan a day-8 hook before launch: promise an update, feature or live event so players return a week later, since the algorithm now judges days 8-28.
- [2:02](https://youtu.be/RC19PKdRt1Y?t=122) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Make the icon promise exactly what the first 30 seconds deliver; mismatched clickbait raises first-play bounce rate, which now hurts ranking.
- [2:43](https://youtu.be/RC19PKdRt1Y?t=163) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Design games that are more fun with friends; intentional co-play days are a ranking signal, and the new cross-game friends chat supports joining together.
- [3:24](https://youtu.be/RC19PKdRt1Y?t=204) **[[trailers-and-video-marketing]]** Add real in-game gameplay video to your game; Roblox says it gave up to 39% more play time from recommendations. Use real current footage, no cinematic trailers, voiceover or fake graphics.
- [4:04](https://youtu.be/RC19PKdRt1Y?t=244) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Get the 16:9 image right, since it is the larger image shown in search results and matters more now than the small icon.
- [4:45](https://youtu.be/RC19PKdRt1Y?t=285) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** To reach under-16 players you need the engaged-player bar (250 now, dropping to 100 in November) and sustain it over 60 days, or pay for expedited review.
- [5:26](https://youtu.be/RC19PKdRt1Y?t=326) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** The new engagement ad goal is in beta and can overspend; pause it manually after reaching the engaged-player target. Ads reportedly don't change organic ranking.
- [6:07](https://youtu.be/RC19PKdRt1Y?t=367) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Nominate your game for Standout Games via the Roblox form; hover-to-preview gameplay videos help it get noticed.
- [6:48](https://youtu.be/RC19PKdRt1Y?t=408) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't copy existing games: copycats decline (Monkey Escape fell to about 6K players while Keyboard Escape holds about 200K), and Roblox is taking down copies.
- [7:28](https://youtu.be/RC19PKdRt1Y?t=448) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give players a reason to come back; stable games with steady CCU outperform one-time spikes under the new rules.
