# rasdevblox - How to make successful Roblox games

Find a proven, trending formula, then ship a simple twist on it as a 2-4 week MVP with a visible core loop and a fun hook within 8-15 seconds. Package it for the click (512x512 icon, verb-the-noun title, 60-90s trailer), seed early data with small Robux ads or outside traffic, and win on retention (10-15 min sessions, D1/D7). Monetise fairly with cosmetics and convenience only, and update on a steady cadence. Most sources are promotional and many figures are unverified.

**Style:** Promo-heavy, beginner-oriented series mixing motivation, trend-chart walkthroughs and algorithm breakdowns. Most videos push the speaker's analytics or game-builder sites. Core packaging and retention advice is sound but thin. Suits total beginners wanting a checklist; verify numbers and treat tool-generated concepts skeptically.

## Playbook
1. **Validate demand before building** [[trend-research-and-validation]]: Check breakout charts and growth curves; favour games that are rising but not yet peaked (Violence District, opportunity score 78, ~60k players). Use opportunity score (demand, competition, momentum, replicability) as a guide, not gut feel. Speaker's claims, unverified: t3i54NTcDi8, N3L63uyLyQ8, q0uePCbBlk0.
2. **Twist a proven idea and scope to your team** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Take one proven loop and add a twist or combine two hits (Illegal Soccer = Rematch + Mario Kart items). Pass the one-sentence test. Do not copy one-to-one; copy principles, not surface features. Check you can ship it solo in 5-15 h/week; avoid full RPGs. Sources: tErJHe9wQn0, Df5ebFGEOXY, 0_dR_081Hq8, U9RGcuxt9m4.
3. **Define the core loop** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: Write it as action, reward, repeat. If it cannot be identified in 10 seconds, the game lacks one. Show forward growth each session (levels, rebirths, prestige), keep progress visible, and reward losing players (XP on loss). Sources: M-GCMK1Tryk, q0lIbkUC2r8, e4o-ECkzDoI.
4. **Build a 2-4 week MVP** [[development-process-and-speed]]: Scope to one map, one core loop, basic stat progression, simple currency and a leaderboard. Prioritise speed over polish; release, then iterate on real player feedback. Avoid waiting for perfection and avoid graphics-first builds. Sources: q0uePCbBlk0, fPT2GCkK1QM.
5. **Deliver fun in the first 8-15 seconds** [[first-session-onboarding]]: Drop players straight into the core action with a short intro: no long tutorial, loading screens or forced customisation. Apply the living-room test: if a friend watching finds it boring, rework the core fun. Sources: fPT2GCkK1QM, q0lIbkUC2r8, Ox5pwUuzF-M.
6. **Build day-1 and day-7 retention hooks** [[retention-d1-d7]]: Add daily rewards, login streaks, daily quests and limited events for day 1. For day 7, add rebirths, prestige and rare-item hunts. Track D7 as a quality signal and add a hook if it is weak. Sources: G15TVsyC61g, Ox5pwUuzF-M, M-GCMK1Tryk.
7. **Monetise without paywalling basics** [[monetisation-gamepasses]]: Sell cosmetics, convenience and rebuyable boosts. Never lock core gameplay (sprint, teleport) or sell pay-to-win. Keep an in-game currency between spend and real money, use small unobtrusive prompts, and add repeatable consumables for recurring revenue. Sources: Ox5pwUuzF-M, e4o-ECkzDoI, 53OBGXktIPY.
8. **Build an audience before launch** [[community-and-discord]]: Pick one platform (TikTok, YouTube or Discord) and post dev logs for about 2 months. Example: 5,000 followers pre-launch. Reach converts poorly but matters: 1% of 200k views is about 2k players. Sources: q0lIbkUC2r8, fPT2GCkK1QM, DtdGtvALhCA.
9. **Design a clickable icon** [[thumbnails-icons]]: Make a 512x512 icon with one high-contrast focal point, a popping colour, 1-2 words max and a slight intrigue. Pass the 2-second test and compare against front-page competitors. Feed view is about 0.5 s, so favour simple over busy. Sources: _Tt-l7EMEHE, MH9TaOMTm9Q, Df5ebFGEOXY.
10. **Write a verb-the-noun title** [[titles-descriptions]]: Use plain titles that pass a half-second comprehension test (Steal an Egg, Clean All the Leaves). Add freshness brackets such as [NEW] or [BETA] for updates. Icon and title should reinforce each other; adapt top-chart patterns rather than copying them. Sources: Df5ebFGEOXY, tErJHe9wQn0, MH9TaOMTm9Q.
11. **Cut a 60-90 second trailer** [[trailers-and-video-marketing]]: Use in-game footage: 5-10 s high-action hook, 15-20 s core features and map, then combat or features, ending with title and call to action. Cut music on beat. Start in CapCut; DaVinci Resolve is the value option. Do not over-promise. Sources: IyEqyPOjEvU, MH9TaOMTm9Q.
12. **Seed data with small ad spend and outside traffic** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Spend about 10k-30k Robux over 2-4 weeks in small consistent bursts, plus friends, Discord and TikTok. For sponsored ads, 16:9 thumbnails (up to 10 per campaign) lifted playthrough 40%. Ads raise player counts, not ranking. Judge by D7 and sessions, not impressions. Sources: G15TVsyC61g, e4o-ECkzDoI, DtdGtvALhCA.
13. **Read the algorithm metrics and fix the weakest** [[discovery-algorithm]]: Track CTR (below 3% means fix packaging), session time (10-15 min target), and D1/D7 retention; fix the lowest first. Current window is 28 days, using playthrough rate and first-play bounce. Playtime per player counts up to 60 minutes. Recommendations need 250 engaged players. Sources: RC19PKdRt1Y, e4o-ECkzDoI, DtdGtvALhCA, q0lIbkUC2r8.
14. **Ship updates and events on a steady cadence** [[updates-live-ops]]: Update weekly to every two months so the game never looks dead. Use limited items, rare spawns and leaderboard resets for FOMO, and send update notifications to bring players back. Trend games spike then fade within weeks, so keep updating after launch. Sources: Ox5pwUuzF-M, fPT2GCkK1QM, 53OBGXktIPY, M-GCMK1Tryk.

## Common mistakes
- Building before validating demand; the speaker blames this for most failed games (t3i54NTcDi8)
- Cloning top earners one-to-one; clones with a skin swap are not a niche (17DGro3FUlM, Df5ebFGEOXY)
- Using ads to mask weak retention; players still leave (G15TVsyC61g)
- Judging an ad campaign on its first afternoon; data lags up to 48 h and plays credit up to 30 days (e4o-ECkzDoI)
- Obsessing over graphics before retention, or hopping between multiple games (fPT2GCkK1QM)
- Using cluttered in-game screenshots or busy icons; a cluttered icon with 4K players was cited (MH9TaOMTm9Q)
- Gating sprint, teleport or core mechanics behind Robux (Ox5pwUuzF-M)
- Long tutorials or loading screens before the fun starts (q0lIbkUC2r8, fPT2GCkK1QM)
- Relying on AI-generated icons; the same source also covers generating them with ChatGPT, so treat that advice as conflicting (_Tt-l7EMEHE)
- Abandoning a game after one flop instead of checking the thumbnail, loop or mechanic (mindset tips)

## Launch checklist
- [ ] One-sentence pitch passes the stranger test
- [ ] Trend and opportunity check done; competitor like ratio checked
- [ ] Core loop identifiable in 10 seconds
- [ ] Fun within 8-15 seconds; no long tutorial or loading screen
- [ ] Rewards given on loss; daily-return hook in place
- [ ] Mobile, tablet, console and desktop supported
- [ ] Icon 512x512, one focal point, 1-2 words, passes 2-second test
- [ ] 16:9 1920x1080 thumbnail ready for sponsored ads
- [ ] Title verb-the-noun, with [NEW] or [BETA] bracket if updated
- [ ] Trailer 60-90 s, in-game footage, 5-10 s hook
- [ ] Two months of dev logs on one platform before launch
- [ ] Discord set up to gather a core returning group
- [ ] In-game favourite prompt added
- [ ] Only cosmetic, convenience or rebuyable monetisation; no core paywalls
- [ ] Ad budget planned at 10k-30k Robux over 2-4 weeks, judged on D7 and sessions
- [ ] Metrics dashboard: CTR (3%+), session time (10-15 min), D1/D7 retention
- [ ] Update schedule chosen (weekly to every two months)
- [ ] Bug-report response plan in place

## How the advice changed over time
- Algorithm evaluation window: earlier advice said 2-4 weeks of data and a 1-2 week wait; the 2026 update extended it from 7 to 28 days (RC19PKdRt1Y, latest wins).
- Engaged-player bar for recommendations: 500 earlier, 250 from August 2026 (RC19PKdRt1Y, DtdGtvALhCA).
- Ranking signals: earlier videos cited retention, playtime and spend; later ones (e4o-ECkzDoI, RC19PKdRt1Y) say playthrough rate and first-play bounce replaced QPTR, with a 60-minute per-player playtime cap.

- Sponsored ads: earlier icon-only campaigns; now 16:9 thumbnails with a 40% playthrough uplift (e4o-ECkzDoI, RC19PKdRt1Y).
