# CORSOBLOX - How to make successful Roblox games

Design so players choose their path instead of gambling for power. Keep every unit or tower near the same strength, so rarity never makes lower tiers pointless. Make upgrade costs reachable and have upgrades add new mechanics, not flat stat boosts. Be explicit about how each paid item is obtained, and offer a free route to premium cosmetics. Playtest stat upgrades before shipping. Keep shipping content regularly, collect feedback into a backlog and design doc, and gate admin tools carefully. The creator is critical of loot boxes and grind-heavy progression, and this is the newest guidance (2025) where it conflicts with 2023 advice.

**Style:** Commentary-driven critic who analyses live games (TDS, ASD, BTD6) with back-of-envelope math and insider anecdotes. Tone is strongly anti-gacha and pro-player. Numbers are often speaker estimates. Best for tower-defense and progression-heavy devs who want to understand player backlash and economy pitfalls, not step-by-step build tutorials.

## Playbook
1. **Pick a core where many units stay viable** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Design towers or units to similar strength so no rarity tier makes the rest useless. The speaker cites BTD6's 23 towers as the model for depth. Avoid designs where top-tier units erase strategy.
2. **Make progression costs reachable** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: Estimate time-to-max before shipping, for example about 1.6M coins at 5,500 coins/hr is about 291 hours, or about 73 days at 4 hrs/day. Exponential prices that take months to max make progression feel unwinnable for most players.
3. **Upgrades add mechanics, not only stats** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: Prefer upgrades that add new mechanics, as BTD6's monkey knowledge tree does. Flat boosts such as +10 placement or +25% starting cash warp strategy and difficulty.
4. **Playtest stat-boosting upgrades** [[playtesting-and-feedback]]: Test placement limits, cash bonuses, and reinforcements with real players before release. The speaker's TDS example: solo placement limit reaches 40 to 50 with max Reinforcements, and that changed the game's balance.
5. **Avoid loot boxes and gacha for power** [[monetisation-other]]: The speaker says gacha can earn big short-term revenue but damages trust and retention. Odds cited: 2.5% for a 7-star unit, about $939 to get the top ASD tower. Treat these as speaker estimates.
6. **Do not push unlucky players to keep paying** [[monetisation-other]]: The speaker argues players who feel their spending is wasted keep spending, and that this pattern is predatory. Prefer transparent purchases with known outcomes.
7. **State exactly how every paid item is obtained** [[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]: The DJ skin backlash came from vague communication that implied a free or deluxe-crate release. Publish the exact acquisition method for each item before announcing it.
8. **Offer a free route to premium cosmetics** [[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]: Pair Robux purchase with a very hard in-game mission. The speaker says players prefer hard-to-get over RNG. Weigh whether cosmetic revenue covers team time, since skins cost music, sound, art, and engineering.
9. **Poll the community before monetisation or mission design** [[community-and-discord]]: Run Discord polls before deciding on monetisation or mission design, to gauge reaction early and avoid backlash.
10. **Price and gate admin or sandbox gamepasses** [[monetisation-gamepasses]]: Price a powerful admin gamepass high, and let owners assign multiple admins so it can be shared. The speaker says the internal research found a large percentage of players quit after gaining admin commands, so gate them carefully.
11. **Keep a steady content cadence after launch** [[updates-live-ops]]: Frequent content updates drive player count. The speaker says TDS slowed to small updates (about 300 days since its last new tower) and lost players, while ASD's new towers boosted count.
12. **Prioritise work with feature analytics** [[analytics-metrics]]: Use feature usage and success data to decide what to build next. The speaker says neglected systems fell behind because the team followed the strongest usage data.
13. **Do not abandon shipped systems** [[updates-live-ops]]: Old features such as consumables and golden perks went unbalanced and were put on a backlog. Keep a backlog, collect community feedback into a design doc, and balance before re-release.
14. **Give constructive feedback channels** [[community-and-discord]]: The speaker advises players to send constructive feedback rather than harass developers, since individual devs are not responsible for each addition. Use this to guide tone of community channels.

## Common mistakes
- Rarity-gated power where top-tier units make lower tiers useless, which kills strategy depth
- Splitting players into casual and tryhard tiers with power upgrades, making balance nearly impossible
- Exponential upgrade costs that take months to max, making progression feel unwinnable
- Flat stat unlocks (+10 placement, +25% starting cash) shipped without playtesting
- Vague wording about paid cosmetics, as in the DJ skin backlash (implied free or deluxe-crate release)
- Loot boxes or RNG that push unlucky players to keep spending (speaker's claim)
- Giving admin or cheat-like tools too cheaply or too early; a large share of players quit after gaining them
- Letting shipped systems like consumables and golden perks go unbalanced on a backlog
- Slowing content updates; TDS went around 300 days without a new tower and lost players (speaker's claim)

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Confirm all towers or units are roughly equal in strength before release
- [ ] Calculate time-to-max and spend-to-max for each progression track at a realistic play time, such as 4 hrs/day
- [ ] Replace flat stat boosts with upgrades that add new mechanics where possible
- [ ] Playtest placement limits, cash bonuses, and reinforcements before shipping
- [ ] Publish exact acquisition methods for every paid item before announcing it
- [ ] Offer a free, hard-to-earn route to each premium cosmetic
- [ ] Avoid loot boxes or gacha for power; if used, disclose odds clearly
- [ ] Run a Discord poll before finalising monetisation or mission design
- [ ] Price any powerful admin or sandbox gamepass high and allow multiple admins
- [ ] Define a content update cadence and keep it after launch
- [ ] Set up feature usage analytics to guide post-launch priorities
- [ ] Create a backlog and design doc for shipped systems and community feedback
- [ ] Set up constructive feedback channels in Discord and social media

## How the advice changed over time
- 2023 advice: pure critic of loot boxes and gacha. Say avoid or do not spend, and warned of addiction risk. Still grants the revenue point (big short-term earnings) but weighs trust and retention more heavily.
- 2024 advice: accepts that cosmetics and admin gamepasses are needed for revenue. Shifted focus to communication (state exact acquisition), free routes (hard missions over RNG), polls, and feedback channels.
- 2025 advice (newest, supersedes earlier tolerance for grind): progression costs must be reachable, and upgrades must add mechanics rather than flat stat boosts. The skill tree critique replaces the 2024 view that expensive progression was acceptable.
- Conflict: the 2023 tip says predatory pricing can earn big revenue short-term; the newer 2024 and 2025 advice favours trust and reachable costs. Follow the newer guidance.
