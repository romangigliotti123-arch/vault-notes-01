# Why the TDS Skill Tree was a MISTAKE...

## Summary
Critique of TDS (Tower Defense Simulator) new coin-funded skill tree. Argues it grinds too hard (~1.6M coins to max, ~73 days at 4 hrs/day), breaks balance (reinforcements +10 placement, bigger budget +25% cash), and pushes TDS from strategy toward grind. Suggests cheaper costs and nerfs; contrasts with BTD6's monkey knowledge tree.

## Tips
- [0:30](https://youtu.be/O2LZcQG5PPQ?t=30) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make progression costs reachable: exponential upgrade prices that take months to max can make a progression system feel unwinnable for most players.
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/O2LZcQG5PPQ?t=81) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Avoid power upgrades that split the player base into casual and tryhard tiers; balancing both sides becomes nearly impossible.
- [3:03](https://youtu.be/O2LZcQG5PPQ?t=183) **[[ab-testing-and-experiments]]** Playtest stat-boosting upgrades before shipping; unlocks like +10 placement limit or +25% starting cash can warp strategy and difficulty.
- [4:28](https://youtu.be/O2LZcQG5PPQ?t=268) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make upgrades add new mechanics rather than only flat stat boosts, as BTD6's monkey knowledge tree does, to keep depth.
