# The Gross Monetization of Roblox's Tower Defense Games (Gambling, Pay-to-Win)

## Summary
Video exposes gambling-style loot box monetisation in Roblox tower defense games (ASD, TDS, a skibidi toilet TD game). Argues 7-star towers are far stronger than 1-star ones, computes average spend to get top tower (~$939 without Premium), and says games promoting this face addiction risk. Ends with player advice: avoid or don't spend.

## Tips
- [1:40](https://youtu.be/H9MoA94zKdc?t=100) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid making rarity-gated power where top-tier units make lower tiers useless; strategy depth suffers. Balance towers to similar strength, as BTD6 does with 23 towers.
- [5:50](https://youtu.be/H9MoA94zKdc?t=350) **[[monetisation-other]]** Predatory gacha/loot box pricing can generate big revenue short-term but speaker argues it damages player trust and retention; weigh this before using it.
- [6:30](https://youtu.be/H9MoA94zKdc?t=390) **[[monetisation-other]]** Players who feel their spending is wasted keep spending; avoid mechanics where unlucky players are pushed to keep paying (speaker's claim).
- [4:26](https://youtu.be/H9MoA94zKdc?t=266) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Frequent content updates (new units/content) drive player count; speaker says TDS slowed to small updates and lost players, while ASD's new towers boosted count.
