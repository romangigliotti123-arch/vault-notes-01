## Summary
Creator recounts a call with the TDS (Tower Defense Simulator) owner. Covers backlash over the DJ skin release and its free-obtain question, a planned expensive admin-commands gamepass (sandbox mode, no stat saving), a Wave Editor for custom game modes, and neglected consumables/golden perks being put on a backlog.

## Tips
- [0:26](https://youtu.be/lFD1wKdwSUs?t=26) **[[monetisation-other]]** Be explicit about experimental paid cosmetics: the DJ skin backlash came from vague communication that implied a free/deluxe-crate release. Say exactly how each item is obtained.
- [5:59](https://youtu.be/lFD1wKdwSUs?t=359) **[[monetisation-other]]** Offer a free route to premium cosmetics (e.g. a very hard mission) alongside Robux purchase; the speaker argued players prefer hard-to-get over RNG, which feels scummy.
- [9:12](https://youtu.be/lFD1wKdwSUs?t=552) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** For a powerful sandbox/admin gamepass, price it high and let owners assign multiple admins so it can be shared; high price guards against players dropping off once they get admin powers.
- [10:33](https://youtu.be/lFD1wKdwSUs?t=633) **[[common-mistakes]]** Admin/cheat-like tools can destroy the core experience; the developer found a large share of players quit right after gaining admin commands, so gate them carefully.
- [12:40](https://youtu.be/lFD1wKdwSUs?t=760) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Don't abandon shipped systems. Old features (consumables, golden perks) went unbalanced; keep a backlog and collect community feedback into a design doc before re-releasing.
- [13:22](https://youtu.be/lFD1wKdwSUs?t=802) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Prioritise updates using feature-success analytics; the team focuses on features with the strongest usage data, which is why neglected systems fall behind.
- [6:40](https://youtu.be/lFD1wKdwSUs?t=400) **[[community-and-discord]]** Run Discord polls before deciding on monetisation or mission design to gauge how the community will react.
- [10:33](https://youtu.be/lFD1wKdwSUs?t=633) **[[ugc-and-avatar-economy]]** Cosmetic skins are costly (music, sound, art, concept, engineering); weigh whether monetisation covers the team time before committing to ultra-rare skins.
