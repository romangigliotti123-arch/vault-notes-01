## Summary
Step-by-step tutorial on creating a Roblox game pass via the Creator Dashboard (Create > Experience > Monetization > Passes > Create Pass, set Sales price), plus an explanation of regional pricing and advice to disable it for donation passes because it can lower the Robux paid in some countries.

## Tips
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/skRRdrpf2F4?t=0) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Create game passes from Creator Hub: pick your experience, open Monetization > Passes, click Create Pass, add name and description, then set the Sales price.

- [3:02](https://youtu.be/skRRdrpf2F4?t=182) **[[pricing]]** Regional pricing adjusts a pass's Robux cost by country purchasing power; a 10 Robux default may cost only 4 Robux in lower-income regions.
