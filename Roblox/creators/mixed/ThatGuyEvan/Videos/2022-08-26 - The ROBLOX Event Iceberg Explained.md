# The ROBLOX Event Iceberg Explained

## Summary
A tiered "iceberg" tour of Roblox event history, from well-known events (sales, Bloxy Awards, Halloween, egg hunts) down to obscure controversies: leaks, rigged awards, cancelled events, doxxing, trolls and lost footage. Mostly entertainment; little actionable game-making advice.

## Tips
- [2:45](https://youtu.be/APeSOO8WRPQ?t=165) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Gate paid seats or premium event access carefully; the 2013-era Bloxy seat sales (3,000 seats at 3,000-50,000 Robux) drew anger over stream quality and value.
- [4:23](https://youtu.be/APeSOO8WRPQ?t=263) **[[monetisation-other]]** Blind-box or gift-style purchases with uncertain outcomes (2012 gift items, 50-40,000 Robux) were seen as a gamble and drew player distrust; disclose contents clearly.
- [14:18](https://youtu.be/APeSOO8WRPQ?t=858) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Removing free currency or free rewards the community relies on was rumoured to push players toward Robux; players react negatively to perceived monetisation-driven changes.
- [22:26](https://youtu.be/APeSOO8WRPQ?t=1346) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Cancelled sponsored events and scrapped near-finished events frustrated players; avoid announcing or leaking content you may not ship.

## Numbers
- [2:45](https://youtu.be/APeSOO8WRPQ?t=165) Bloxy Awards seat price: **3,000 to 50,000 Robux per seat, about 3,000 seats** (Sixth Annual Bloxy Awards stadium seats)
- [4:23](https://youtu.be/APeSOO8WRPQ?t=263) Gift price range (2012): **50 to 40,000 Robux** (Gift items that opened into hats)
- [17:01](https://youtu.be/APeSOO8WRPQ?t=1021) Like-to-dislike ratio: **22%** (Ava Max event, lowest-rated Roblox event cited)
- [30:01](https://youtu.be/APeSOO8WRPQ?t=1801) Fake event hoax duration: **several months** (Be Cardoso 2022 egg hunt troll)

## Games
- **Egg of Destiny (2014 April Fools)** [6:38](https://youtu.be/APeSOO8WRPQ?t=398): A troll game gave a real rare prize; the game is now lost, with no footage left, so archive your events.
- **Ready Player One (2018)** [41:23](https://youtu.be/APeSOO8WRPQ?t=2483): A one-of-one reward with public winner data led to real-world threats; avoid exposing winner identity.
- **Ready Player Two** [27:32](https://youtu.be/APeSOO8WRPQ?t=1652): Publicly listing winners' names led to doxxing; keep personal details private.
