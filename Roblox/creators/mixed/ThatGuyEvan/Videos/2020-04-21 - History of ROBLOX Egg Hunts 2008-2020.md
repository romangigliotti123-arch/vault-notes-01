## Summary
History of Roblox's annual Easter egg hunt events from the 2008 Egg Drop through 2020. Covers each event's format, egg counts, notable mechanics (teleporters, quests, story, pedestal finale, paid passes), and fan reception, with the 2019 and 2020 hunts criticized.

## Tips
- [2:15](https://youtu.be/-4kisZLNwrk?t=135) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Single-game events drove record concurrency: the 2012 hunt in one game peaked near 20,000 players, up from a prior 9,350 record.
- [3:44](https://youtu.be/-4kisZLNwrk?t=224) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** The 2013 hunt, the first true egg hunt with teleporters, reached two million visits in a week; spreading eggs across a connected map worked.
- [8:13](https://youtu.be/-4kisZLNwrk?t=493) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Gating the finale behind a multi-world challenge and a story gave the 2018 hunt the strongest reception; a clear narrative adds replay pull.
- [11:52](https://youtu.be/-4kisZLNwrk?t=712) **[[common-mistakes]]** Splitting the 2020 hunt across 48 games with no hub and paid passes for easier eggs drew heavy backlash; avoid fragmenting progression and paywalling it.

## Games
- **Egg Drop 2008** [0:30](https://youtu.be/-4kisZLNwrk?t=30): Eggs dropped randomly in every game; players built egg funnels to collect them faster, exposing the mechanic's weakness.
- **Egg Drop 2010 (Hunt delayed)** [1:13](https://youtu.be/-4kisZLNwrk?t=73): Scheduled drops overloaded servers and the hunt ended early; plan capacity for launch-day traffic.
- **Stooge Ville / Egg Hunt 2012** [2:15](https://youtu.be/-4kisZLNwrk?t=135): Placing the egg in one game concentrated players and set a concurrency record near 20,000.
