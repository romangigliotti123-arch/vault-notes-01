# why YOU are failing as a Roblox Developer (and how to fix it)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/i3RQXxZeVYc) | 1_Bruce | 13,688 views | 2026-02-20

## Summary
Bruce's short talk on growing a Roblox game without ads: build an audience via Discord, read the overview stats (D1 and D7 retention, payer conversion, qualified playthrough rate), and know your demographics. He argues bad stats mean a weak game, so start a new one. Long-term, a community funnel makes each release do better.

## Tips
- [0:20](https://youtu.be/i3RQXxZeVYc?t=20) **[[community-and-discord]]** Grow an initial audience by advertising to interested players on Discord before paying for Roblox sponsors. He had no budget for ads.
- [0:59](https://youtu.be/i3RQXxZeVYc?t=59) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track the overview page stats: average playtime, D1 retention, payer conversion rate, qualified playthrough rate, and D7 retention. Keep them green relative to similar games.
- [1:39](https://youtu.be/i3RQXxZeVYc?t=99) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Aim for day-1 retention of roughly 10-15%. His community-driven game hit the 20s.
- [2:19](https://youtu.be/i3RQXxZeVYc?t=139) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Benchmark stats against same-genre games, not absolute numbers. QPTR runs lower for adult-targeted games than kids' games.
- [2:59](https://youtu.be/i3RQXxZeVYc?t=179) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Keep D7 retention up by updating the game regularly and building a strong community.
- [3:10](https://youtu.be/i3RQXxZeVYc?t=190) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Study your audience demographics. They shape pricing and conversion, and they can make or break a game.
- [3:50](https://youtu.be/i3RQXxZeVYc?t=230) **[[pricing]]** If your audience is young or low-income, price items very low to keep payer conversion up. Target older, higher-income players where possible.
- [4:35](https://youtu.be/i3RQXxZeVYc?t=275) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't quit because a game underperforms. If stats stay weak, treat it as a dud and apply the lessons to a new game rather than endlessly patching.
- [5:17](https://youtu.be/i3RQXxZeVYc?t=317) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect sustained discipline and sacrifice. Vibe-coding a game does not reliably make it blow up.
- [5:17](https://youtu.be/i3RQXxZeVYc?t=317) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build as many developer connections as possible; they teach you things and help you make better games.
- [5:55](https://youtu.be/i3RQXxZeVYc?t=355) **[[community-and-discord]]** Funnel players from every game into a Discord server to build a community. Each new release then starts from a larger base and performs better.
