# Making Hit Roblox Games | Real Devs Share Secrets to Success

[Watch](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw) | GameMakersDev | 8,858 views | 2021-01-21

## Summary
Panel of four Roblox developers (Andrew Brezza, Josh DeBoer, Ann Or Maesta, Arthur Trusov) discuss whether Roblox can be a career, team size and structure, the 75.5/24.5 revenue split, learning paths, monetisation (game passes, cosmetics, pay-to-skip), discoverability, and Roblox's growth and metaverse direction.

## Tips
- [14:46](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=886) **[[team-and-budget]]** Start with a small team; chemistry matters more than headcount. A small connected team can outperform 20 people.
- [15:27](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=927) **[[team-and-budget]]** Build a strong vision that attracts people into loose orbits; rigid company structure usually forms only after release.
- [16:07](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=967) **[[team-and-budget]]** After release, leave trusted team members in charge of maintaining the game creatively and analytically.
- [36:09](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2169) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Make a novel idea that YouTubers would want to cover; youtuber coverage is a major discovery driver on Roblox.
- [34:49](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2089) **[[common-mistakes]]** Good games can flop at launch and blow up later; discoverability is a real issue, so keep promoting and iterating.
- [44:48](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2688) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Reserve monetisation for game passes; show purchasable options in the main menu so players see what they can unlock.
- [43:16](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2596) **[[monetisation-other]]** Loot boxes were about 1% of one game's revenue; the speaker dropped them for integrity reasons.
- [47:55](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2875) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** In progression or social games, sell pay-to-skip or pay-to-get-ahead rather than only cosmetics that visually split paying and non-paying players.
- [51:22](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=3082) **[[monetisation-other]]** Socially visible items like UGC accessories sell well because players wear them to express identity.
- [52:04](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=3124) **[[monetisation-other]]** Consider paid social boosts that affect nearby players in RPGs; it makes spending more social and fun.
- [25:09](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=1509) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Roblox handles servers, storage, moderation and legal; self-hosting for a large player base was estimated at $10k-$20k/month.
- [32:05](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=1925) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Learn Lua; Luau is backward compatible with Lua and adds strict typing, so Lua knowledge carries over.
- [30:02](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=1802) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Learn by setting incremental goals, such as a part that kills on touch, and understanding why each step works.
- [33:26](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2006) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Learn by dissecting other people's game code line by line, then experimenting and breaking things in Studio.
- [27:14](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=1634) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Try a short trial in each discipline (coding, UI, 3D art, animation) before specialising; animation needs patience.
- [27:54](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=1674) **[[community-and-discord]]** Seek internships through Roblox groups and connect with other developers; asking for help speeds progress.
- [58:38](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=3518) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Do not make money the end goal; a clear shared vision is what makes games good, and money follows.
- [38:55](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2335) **[[common-mistakes]]** Treat account security seriously: change passwords often and protect the account, since breaches are common and can end income.
- [35:29](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2129) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Watch teams from Roblox's accelerator program; their projects tend to be strong bets.

## Games
- **Vehicle Simulator** [1:27](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=87): Dynamic-duo start grew into a 28-person team; 575M play sessions shows scale is reachable with a clear vision.
- **Adopt Me** [34:09](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2049): Named as a game to watch; a major Roblox hit.
- **Bloxburg (Welcome to Bloxburg)** [19:35](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=1175): Built by a solo developer; was the top earning game when the speaker's game hit #2.
- **Among Us (Roblox clone)** [34:49](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2089): Original Among Us flopped at release until a streamer revived it; Roblox copies followed.
- **Piggy** [36:09](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2169): Created a unique social experience that influencers caught on, showing creator-driven discovery.
- **Kinetic Code** [35:29](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2129): Recommended first-person shooter with strong play feel, similar to Battlefield 4.
- **Phantom Forces** [55:11](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=3311): Mature-leaning Call of Duty-inspired shooter showing an older audience exists on Roblox.
- **Miners Haven** [16:07](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=967): Ran for years under handed-off leadership; loot boxes dominated early revenue before membership-style passes.
- **Vesteria** [4:52](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=292): Rigid team structure failed; speaker had more success with leaving people in charge after release.
- **Tycoon Hospital** [45:29](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2729): Game pass unlocks materials and colours; limited free sample shown in main menu to drive purchase.
- **My Droplets** [2:08](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=128): Started solo, then hired six people for a big update to scale the vision.
