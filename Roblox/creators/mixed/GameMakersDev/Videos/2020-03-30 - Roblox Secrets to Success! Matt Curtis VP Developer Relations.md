## Summary
Interview with Roblox VP of Developer Relations Matt Curtis covering Roblox history (founded 2000, Roblox Studio 2006, mobile 2012/2014, Xbox 2016), monetization (cosmetics, pay-to-win left to developers, Premium Payouts), safety/chat filtering for under-13s, tech roadmap (200-player servers, collaborative editing), and competition (Minecraft, Manticore).

- [12:18](https://youtu.be/1q9Q6mRkciA?t=738) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Iterate quickly: Roblox makes publishing, launching and acquiring users fast and cheap, so test many versions.

- [33:45](https://youtu.be/1q9Q6mRkciA?t=2025) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Fast, affordable launch and iteration plus premium payouts make Roblox attractive to new developers targeting older audiences.
