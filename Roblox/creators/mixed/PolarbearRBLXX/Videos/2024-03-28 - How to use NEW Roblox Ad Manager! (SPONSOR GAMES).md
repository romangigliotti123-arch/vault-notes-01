- [6:29](https://youtu.be/sk8HQDgJyE0?t=389) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Schedule sponsored campaigns for weekends when more players are online, per the speaker's reasoning.
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/sk8HQDgJyE0?t=0) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Access sponsoring via Creator Hub: Manage Experiences, Dashboard, scroll down, click Sponsor. Then go to Ads Manager to create a campaign.
