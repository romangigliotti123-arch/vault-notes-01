# PolarbearRBLXX - How to make successful Roblox games

Build a small game around a simple, proven core loop by combining two known genres (e.g., Obby plus speed run), and finish it with real content before spending on monetisation or ads. Add cheap gamepasses and developer products with odd-number pricing, and verify every purchase works. Paid promotion has been tested repeatedly with weak returns: cheap 79-100 Robux ads (Feb 2024) and ~5,000 Robux sponsorships (Mar-Apr 2024). The most recent verdict (Apr 2024, Hh31hjo3J6U) is that sponsoring is not worth it below ~10,000 Robux spend, which supersedes the earlier cheap-ad advice. Judge all promotion by clicks and plays, not impressions.

**Style:** Beginner-level, low-budget, and candid. The creator documents personal experiments with raw numbers, admits failures, and shows the Creator Hub and Canva workflow step by step. Best for new developers who want realistic expectations about gamepasses, developer products, and ad returns. Light on game design and scripting depth, so treat the pricing and shop-wiring tips as basics.

## Playbook
1. **Pick a proven genre mashup** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Combine two proven genres (e.g., Obby plus speed run with rebirths) into one clear core loop, as in Q-Wbi1an-ww. Keep the first game small enough to finish and ship.
2. **Build a finished core experience** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: Get a working game with real content (wins, rebirths, speed upgrades) before adding monetisation or ads. Q-Wbi1an-ww was an unpolished first game, and the creator admits a better game would have helped before sponsoring.
3. **Create gamepasses and wire the shop** [[monetisation-gamepasses]]: Create passes in Creator Hub. Store each pass's asset ID in a folder value and copy IDs from the Creator Hub Passes page into the shop GUI (nkmfdUbj4bQ). Scripting of pass purchases came in a later video.
4. **Price passes at odd numbers with a cheap entry** [[pricing]]: Use odd prices like 199 Robux for a main pass (Story Expansion Pack in nkmfdUbj4bQ). Add a 29 Robux Custom Name pass to lower the barrier to a first purchase.
5. **Make passes deliver ongoing value** [[monetisation-gamepasses]]: Passes should add content with each update (e.g., new story chapters) rather than one-off perks, so they stay worth buying over time (nkmfdUbj4bQ).
6. **Add small developer products** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: Offer a cheap 15 Robux +1 rebirth developer product (Q-Wbi1an-ww). Q-Wbi1an-ww judged +5 rebirth at 50 Robux too high. Note the creator did not verify the +1 rebirth purchase actually worked.
7. **Test purchase flow for lag** [[scripting-and-performance]]: Q-Wbi1an-ww reports developer products as laggy after outsourcing scripting. Test purchase handling in Studio with several players before release, and check the server-side grant logic.
8. **Record a pre-promotion baseline** [[analytics-metrics]]: Before any sponsorship, log plays and revenue. The sk8HQDgJyE0 baseline was ~4,000 plays and 268 Robux daily revenue. Use this to judge whether paid traffic adds anything.
9. **Make simple ad creative** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Use a Canva image with a recognisable figure (background removed via remove.bg) and a play button; the creator says it takes about a minute (SNauF9OES7E). Pick a figure matching the game's audience, as in tNkQSpq2SXg.
10. **Promote only a polished, monetised game** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: The joke game in tNkQSpq2SXg had no real content or passes at launch and drew 3 players from 8.1K impressions. Run paid traffic only after the core loop and purchases are in place.
11. **Set a sponsorship spend threshold** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Latest verdict (Hh31hjo3J6U, Apr 2024): sponsoring was not worth it below ~10,000 Robux spend. ~5,000 Robux gave 140,000 impressions, 1,300 clicks, 0.95% CTR and 376 plays. This supersedes the earlier 70-100 Robux test advice.
12. **Configure an Ads Manager campaign** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Buy ad credits (1 credit = 285 Robux in bwMUqu-wWpw). Create a Visits campaign with a daily budget (8 credits/day in Hh31hjo3J6U), set schedule and targeting, and bid 0.02 credits per play (0.01 won't show, per sk8HQDgJyE0).
13. **Measure payback by clicks and plays** [[analytics-metrics]]: Compare spend to Robux earned in the monetization overview and Creator Dashboard developer product sales. Judge results by clicks and plays, not impressions (tNkQSpq2SXg, bwMUqu-wWpw). Example: ~5,000 Robux spent yielded ~535 Robux earned.
14. **Ship content updates to keep passes valuable** [[updates-live-ops]]: Release regular updates that expand what gamepasses unlock (e.g., new story content for the Story Expansion Pack). Re-check pass and developer product revenue after each update.

## Common mistakes
- Sponsoring or advertising an unfinished game with no passes or real content (tNkQSpq2SXg, Q-Wbi1an-ww).
- Judging ads by impressions instead of clicks and plays (8.1K impressions gave 15 clicks in tNkQSpq2SXg).
- Spending ~5,000 Robux on sponsorship at ~0.95% CTR and expecting it to pay back (Hh31hjo3J6U, bwMUqu-wWpw).
- Trusting the cheap 79-100 Robux 'triple your Robux' ad result from 3 players; the sales figures in SNauF9OES7E do not add up (SNauF9OES7E).
- Counting pending Robux as earned; SNauF9OES7E cites 342 pending Robux that the creator says is not all from that game.
- Shipping developer products without verifying purchases work; the +1 rebirth purchase in Q-Wbi1an-ww was never confirmed.
- Pricing a bigger developer product too high (+5 rebirth at 50 Robux, per Q-Wbi1an-ww).
- Outsourcing scripting and not testing it; developer products were laggy in Q-Wbi1an-ww.
- Selling one-off perks instead of passes that add ongoing content (nkmfdUbj4bQ).
- Using the earlier 70-100 Robux test budget as current advice after the Apr 2024 verdict in Hh31hjo3J6U.

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Finish a playable core loop that combines two proven genres (Q-Wbi1an-ww).
- [ ] Add real content (wins, rebirths, speed) before any monetisation or ads.
- [ ] Create gamepasses in Creator Hub and note each asset ID from the Passes page.
- [ ] Store pass asset IDs in folder values and wire them to the shop GUI (nkmfdUbj4bQ).
- [ ] Price a main pass at an odd number such as 199 Robux.
- [ ] Include a cheap 29 Robux entry pass (Custom Name).
- [ ] Add a small developer product (e.g., 15 Robux +1 rebirth) and verify it grants the reward.
- [ ] Test all purchases in Studio and with multiple players to check for lag.
- [ ] Confirm pass content will be expanded in future updates so passes stay worth buying.
- [ ] Record a baseline of plays and daily revenue before any paid promotion.
- [ ] Check the Creator Dashboard for developer product sales from organic traffic first.
- [ ] Do not run sponsorship until the game is polished and monetised.
- [ ] Plan the sponsorship budget at or above ~10,000 Robux if you proceed, per Hh31hjo3J6U.
- [ ] Create ad creative in Canva with background removed and a play button (SNauF9OES7E).
- [ ] Set Ads Manager bid to 0.02 credits per play and daily budget as needed (sk8HQDgJyE0).
- [ ] Track clicks, CTR and plays, not impressions.
- [ ] Compare ad spend to Robux earned in the monetization overview after each run.
- [ ] Schedule regular content updates tied to pass value.
