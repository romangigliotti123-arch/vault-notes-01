## Summary
Creator documents building a new Roblox pet-collecting simulator ("Robo Simulator" successor) over about a month: modeling pets and eggs in Blender, designing themed areas (spawn, spruce forest, desert, ocean, winter, beach), adding trading and gamepass-based currency, then a paid ad launch that caused server lag and was fixed by optimizing the pet follow script.

## Tips
- [14:35](https://youtu.be/e725ByFD8EI?t=875) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Set a server player count limit before launch; the creator forgot it and servers were overwhelmed at around 300 players.
- [14:35](https://youtu.be/e725ByFD8EI?t=875) **[[optimization-and-streaming]]** Profile lag before a paid ad push; the pet follow script was the main cause of lag, with about 40 players in one place.
- [5:48](https://youtu.be/e725ByFD8EI?t=348) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Model each pet in three variants (normal, golden, darkened) to multiply content from one design.
- [4:18](https://youtu.be/e725ByFD8EI?t=258) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Build areas quickly by reusing an asset pack of trees, flowers and bushes, then add themed assets per area.
