## Summary
Creator makes a Roblox game (copy-style farming/crop-selling loop) with zero Robux budget and promotes it via YouTube Shorts on a new channel. Four shorts over ~3 days gave 6,000 views, 5 subscribers, 11 watch hours, and 382 Robux in 72 hours, mostly attributed to Shorts viewers. Game retention and playtime were poor.
