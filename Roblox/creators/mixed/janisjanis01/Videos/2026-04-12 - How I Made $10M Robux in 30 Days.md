# How I Made $10M Robux in 30 Days

[Watch](https://youtu.be/xqHXDYbuF2w) | janisjanis01 | 853,487 views | 2026-04-12

## Summary
Creator recounts building a tower defense/grow garden hybrid inspired by Bloons, solo in 34 days. Covers ads with rising budget, bugs killing retention, a pre-update notification/follow prompt that tripled players, and the six stats Roblox uses (retention, conversion, ARPPU, QPTR). Reports ~13.5M Robux earned and refusal to sell.

## Tips
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/xqHXDYbuF2w?t=40) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Look at popular Roblox games and find a proven non-Roblox game (e.g. a childhood favourite) with no Roblox equivalent to adapt; creator says zero similar Roblox games existed.
- [2:02](https://youtu.be/xqHXDYbuF2w?t=122) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Roblox pushes games based on six stats: average playtime, D1/D7/D30 retention, player conversion, ARPPU, and qualified play-through rate (plays of 5+ min per click). Aim for green on all.
- [2:42](https://youtu.be/xqHXDYbuF2w?t=162) **[[common-mistakes]]** Ship a stable build before scaling ad spend; bugs drove players away and killed retention on earlier games, and the damage could not be undone by later fixes.
- [3:34](https://youtu.be/xqHXDYbuF2w?t=214) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Add a pre-update notification prompt so players can follow the next update; they get notified on release, which tripled player count for this creator.
- [4:58](https://youtu.be/xqHXDYbuF2w?t=298) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check the six algorithm stats in the dashboard before spending more on ads; low stats mean the game will not be pushed further regardless of budget.
- [6:20](https://youtu.be/xqHXDYbuF2w?t=380) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship big updates regularly; a major update drew the peak of 15.3K CCU and a top-100 spot. Creator kept a weekly update cadence afterwards.
- [7:02](https://youtu.be/xqHXDYbuF2w?t=422) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Ignore critics after release; criticism is constant once a game grows.
- [7:44](https://youtu.be/xqHXDYbuF2w?t=464) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Creator rejected multiple buyout offers and kept 100% ownership; this is a personal choice, not a general rule.
