# I Bought a Failed Roblox Game and Turned it Around (Part 3)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4) | janisjanis01 | 661,722 views | 2026-05-10

## Summary
Creator buys a failed/low-traffic Roblox game (a brain-rot lucky-block game), reworks UI, visuals, scripting, shop, tutorial and thumbnails, then runs ads and launches. Results are weak: ~3,273 Robux total earnings, games near zero players, and the video ends on an update flop.

## Tips
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=0) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Browse the Roblox homepage and Twitter marketplaces to find games for sale; a game's current traction helps predict whether it will do well.
- [1:27](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=87) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Replace cash-grabby UI with a cleaner custom design; the creator remade every frame in Studio after mocking up in Photoshop.
- [3:04](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=184) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use AI 3D tools like Meshy.ai plus Blender to quickly create scenery assets such as hills and leaderboards for a better-looking map.
- [4:49](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=289) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Use ViewportFrames to play animations inside shop item cards; it looks more engaging than static icons.
- [5:30](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=330) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Add a custom speed selector so high-speed players can slow down and control their character more easily.
- [5:56](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=356) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make at least two thumbnails per game and compare them; the creator's ad with a 1.91% CTR was judged weak and replaced.
- [6:37](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=397) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Build a real tutorial with progress-saving and step-tracking scripts plus a spotlight to guide new players; testers found many issues without one.
- [7:18](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=438) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Have testers play before release; they quickly found leaderboard and other bugs that were fixed before launch.
- [7:18](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=438) **[[community-and-discord]]** Create a Discord server early so you can notify players about updates.
- [8:00](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=480) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Use timed in-game events to bring players back, aiming to raise day-7 retention.
- [8:37](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=517) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Offer a cheap boost (e.g. 1.5x cash for 5 Robux) to improve per-conversion rate in early monetisation.
- [8:37](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=517) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Study player stats and make changes based on them rather than guessing.
- [9:19](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=559) **[[common-mistakes]]** Check ad performance early; a low click-through rate (about 1.9%) means the creative needs replacing, not more spend.
- [10:01](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=601) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Leaderboards have a numeric cap (9.22 quadrillion); store values scaled down (divide by 100) and restore on load to bypass it.
- [10:24](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=624) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Keep a dated update schedule; the creator forgot an update day, and the game dropped to zero active players.
- [11:07](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=667) **[[testing-debugging-and-qa]]** Test game loops like wheel spins for repeated calls; spamming the spin caused a wrong rotation bug.
- [11:50](https://youtu.be/BLOToGi2Qt4?t=710) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Track total revenue as Robux earned plus creator rewards, and subtract ad spend to see true profit.
