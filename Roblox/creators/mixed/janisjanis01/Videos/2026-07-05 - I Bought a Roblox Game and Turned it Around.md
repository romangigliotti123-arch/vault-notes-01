# I Bought a Roblox Game and Turned it Around

## Summary
A creator buys three active Roblox games (after failed offers on several), then tries to revive one (a football/penguin-style 1v1 game) with a framework rewrite, limited-time skins, emotes, bots, and thumbnails. An update breaks the game; it fails to recover and the creator loses about 304,494 Robux net.

## Tips
- [0:41](https://youtu.be/OL1FJ17wzZE?t=41) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Buying active games is costly; the creator paid 369,000 Robux for ~100 CCU game and recouped only 64,506 Robux. Check revenue vs. asking price first.
- [2:45](https://youtu.be/OL1FJ17wzZE?t=165) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Before buying, check income per CCU, not just concurrency. A 100-CCU game with horrible income is a poor buy.
- [4:41](https://youtu.be/OL1FJ17wzZE?t=281) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Gamepass-only starter packs can be bought once, limiting sales. Use developer products or limited-time offers for repeat purchases.
- [4:41](https://youtu.be/OL1FJ17wzZE?t=281) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Add purchase feedback (sound, visual effect) so buyers get immediate reward feedback, which supports conversion.
