# I Bought a Failed Roblox Game and Turned it Around (Again)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig) | janisjanis01 | 1,089,054 views | 2025-11-02

## Summary
Creator buys a failed Roblox template for 10,000 Robux, rebuilds it around the trending brain rot theme (brain rods, hydraulic presses), redesigns UI and map, and runs ad campaigns. Launch underperforms: 11 players, about 970 Robux earned, net loss over 400 Robux. Shows many post-launch bugs and weak ad efficiency.

## Tips
- [1:06](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=66) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Buy or build into a trending theme; brain rot content dominated the front page, while older simulator games no longer worked well.
- [9:19](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=559) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Check asset packs before buying: a brain rod pack was a single mesh, so parts could not be split, which broke the game's design.
- [12:46](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=766) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Test with real testers before paid ads; bugs were only found after launching campaigns, which wasted spend.
- [14:23](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=863) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not wait for player data inside PlayerRemoving handlers; a wait there left the plot system stuck and kicked over 700 players.
- [14:23](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=863) **[[common-mistakes]]** Stabilise the game before buying ads; bugs wasted the Robux spent on campaigns.
- [12:04](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=724) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make thumbnails in Roblox Studio rather than Blender; quality was similar and Studio is much faster.
- [16:49](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=1009) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Keep testing thumbnails: a new one raised CTR from 2.26% to 2.81%, and the creator aims for 3% or higher.
- [15:04](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=904) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Monitor campaigns daily: some ads stopped before spending their ad credits, so check spend against budget.
- [15:44](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=944) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Make group rewards prominent; the creator said players did not notice them and reworked the UI to show them clearly.
- [8:37](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=517) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add a reward UI for favorites and likes to lift the like ratio and get favorites.
- [10:16](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=616) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Add a cheap consumable like a cash boost (20% more cash) to lift conversion; it is simple to script.
- [11:42](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=702) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Run timed events such as a golden event with rare mutations to give players reasons to return.
- [16:07](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=967) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship regular updates with new content (new brain rods, events, a diamond tier) to re-engage lapsed players.
- [15:44](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=944) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add an in-game prompt inviting players to the next update, a pattern the creator saw in many popular games.
- [4:45](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=285) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Give the map a clear, detailed theme with small touches like grass, flowers and animated props so it feels alive.
- [6:16](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=376) **[[optimization-and-streaming]]** Spawn visual items on the client rather than the server to reduce lag, as the creator did for brain rods.
- [9:33](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=573) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Anchor spawned models or they keep falling over; forgetting to anchor was a bug the creator hit.
- [12:55](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=775) **[[testing-debugging-and-qa]]** Add safety checks to error-prone code; one check stopped a bug that had produced over 5 million errors.
- [6:16](https://youtu.be/QnAoCsC-4ig?t=376) **[[code-architecture-and-frameworks]]** Avoid hardcoded fixed volumes and limits; the hydraulic press system broke when adding more pads and needed a rescript.
