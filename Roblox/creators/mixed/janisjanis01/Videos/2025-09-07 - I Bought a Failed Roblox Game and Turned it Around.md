# I Bought a Failed Roblox Game and Turned it Around

[Watch](https://youtu.be/YVKHlrlWn-k) | janisjanis01 | 1,570,459 views | 2025-09-07

## Summary
Creator buys a failed Roblox game for 10,000 Robux, reworks it (new theme, UI, framework, crops/crates, mutations, events, group rewards, monetization), then runs ads. Game gains traction after a 10,000 Robux campaign and reaches the algorithm; reports net loss after ~38,000 Robux spent.

## Tips
- [0:17](https://youtu.be/YVKHlrlWn-k?t=17) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Buying a failed game is risky; the creator paid 10,000 Robux plus tax and asked how many copies were sold and whether any remained before buying.
- [5:33](https://youtu.be/YVKHlrlWn-k?t=333) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Keep maps simple so everything is easily accessible; add invisible walls to stop players entering other bases, which would break base gameplay.
- [6:46](https://youtu.be/YVKHlrlWn-k?t=406) **[[optimization-and-streaming]]** Move visual crate/conveyor work to the client and keep only tiny server blocks, to reduce lag from spawning systems.
- [8:44](https://youtu.be/YVKHlrlWn-k?t=524) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Replace the original owner's game passes so profit does not go to the previous seller; add monetization such as rare crates and a starter pack.
- [8:44](https://youtu.be/YVKHlrlWn-k?t=524) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Offer a guaranteed-mutation purchase (gold crate yields golden crop) and a high-tier crate for 499 Robux to drive conversion.
- [9:41](https://youtu.be/YVKHlrlWn-k?t=581) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Add group rewards; the creator considers them essential for any game.
- [10:49](https://youtu.be/YVKHlrlWn-k?t=649) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Build a command to trigger custom events (e.g., Aurora) that spawn exclusive mutations available only while the event is active.
- [10:49](https://youtu.be/YVKHlrlWn-k?t=649) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Invite friends to test before release; this surfaced many bugs that needed a full day of fixes.
- [11:31](https://youtu.be/YVKHlrlWn-k?t=691) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Judge ads by CTR and playthrough rate; the creator aims for over 1% playthrough rate, and a 2.25% CTR was only average.
- [12:26](https://youtu.be/YVKHlrlWn-k?t=746) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Scale a campaign only after the early ad performs; the 10,000 Robux campaign gave a surge to 72 players and algorithm entry.
- [13:05](https://youtu.be/YVKHlrlWn-k?t=785) **[[common-mistakes]]** Bugs such as merged plots can wipe out player traction; check and fix game-breaking bugs quickly after updates.
- [13:47](https://youtu.be/YVKHlrlWn-k?t=827) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Algorithm placement can take days after an ad push; the creator's game entered the algorithm about 5 days after release.
- [0:17](https://youtu.be/YVKHlrlWn-k?t=17) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plugging a third-party framework into an existing game required rewriting most of its scripts; budget time for that rework.
- [3:30](https://youtu.be/YVKHlrlWn-k?t=210) **[[ai-and-tools]]** AI-generated icons and tool art can cover placeholder assets quickly, though quality varies; hire animators for custom animation.
