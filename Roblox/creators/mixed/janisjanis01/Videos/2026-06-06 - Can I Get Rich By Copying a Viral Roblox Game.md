# Can I Get Rich By Copying a Viral Roblox Game?

[Watch](https://youtu.be/CltiB53QTHw) | janisjanis01 | 198,859 views | 2026-06-06

## Summary
Creator clones their own viral tower defense game (Super Tower Defense-style, resource king system) with a new theme (farm/wooden, books instead of balloons), using Blender/Meshie.ai assets, a retention exit popup, and paid ads. Shows early ad-driven CCU climb to ~300 and ~88k Robux over 28 days; concludes the formula works but is hard to repeat.
