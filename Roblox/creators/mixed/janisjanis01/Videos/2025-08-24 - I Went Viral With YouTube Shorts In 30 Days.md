## Summary
A creator runs a 30-day experiment posting YouTube Shorts (for a kids-oriented Roblox-themed channel) to test whether Shorts can reach 10,000 subscribers and earn money. Ends at ~1,821-2,000 subs, 430k views, 1,600 watch hours, not monetized. Covers unlisted-upload mistakes, copying viral concepts, and the sub-to-view pattern.

## Tips
- [0:21](https://youtu.be/5iIIYKztamo?t=21) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Always add the channel description before launch; the creator skipped it and it's a basic setup step.
- [7:53](https://youtu.be/5iIIYKztamo?t=473) **[[common-mistakes]]** Check that each upload is set to Public. Two videos were left Unlisted and got almost no views; verify visibility right after publishing.
- [9:47](https://youtu.be/5iIIYKztamo?t=587) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't rely on a single 'sure-hit' idea. A video the creator was confident in got 8 views; hits are unpredictable, so keep posting.
- [9:06](https://youtu.be/5iIIYKztamo?t=546) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Study a competitor channel with proven Shorts and borrow the formats and topics that perform (e.g. Robux-related videos) rather than inventing from scratch.
