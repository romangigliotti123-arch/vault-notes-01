# I Bought a Roblox Game Template and Turned it Around

[Watch](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk) | janisjanis01 | 511,137 views | 2026-02-08

## Summary
Creator buys a cheap 99-Nights-style tower defense template, reskins it (lighting, custom map, 24 balloon enemies, UI, tutorial, crates), runs Roblox ads, and recovers a broken update. The game reaches about 3,000 players and 42,977 Robux revenue.

## Tips
- [0:27](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=27) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Before buying a template, search for copies of the same concept to check it isn't a stolen or duplicate clone.
- [1:03](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=63) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Check that the group name is available early; the speaker's first choice was taken.
- [1:03](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=63) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make a group banner; the speaker designed one in Photoshop for the group page.
- [1:43](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=103) **[[building-and-level-design]]** Change lighting first; the speaker says it made the template look about 10 times better with minimal effort.
- [1:43](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=103) **[[physics-pathfinding-and-npc-ai]]** Use waypoints to define enemy paths in tower defense; it is the simplest way to script enemy movement.
- [3:08](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=188) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Clean up keybinds and small annoyances; the speaker says small changes can have a big impact on feel.
- [8:41](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=521) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Build the tutorial spotlight from four frames around a hole over the target button, so the player knows exactly what to click.
- [8:41](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=521) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Hide other buttons until the tutorial is completed to reduce confusion for new players.
- [10:45](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=645) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make thumbnails from a Studio scene, upscale the image, then add effects in Photoshop; keep them simple and test several.
- [11:20](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=680) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Run small ad campaigns to test; the speaker's ad CTRs were 2.57% and 2.88%, and the third ad pushed the game into the algorithm.
- [12:37](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=757) **[[events-and-seasonal-content]]** Add a pre-launch sign-up pad for an upcoming update to build a player pool and boost retention at launch.
- [12:37](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=757) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Watch retention: returning players are a strong signal that the game can grow on Roblox.
- [10:27](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=627) **[[testing-debugging-and-qa]]** Have testers play and collect bug reports before publishing; the speaker found many bugs this way.
- [13:49](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=829) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Test updates thoroughly before release; a rushed update broke the game and half the players left.
- [12:07](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=727) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Expect ads to be needed to reach the algorithm; the speaker needed about three ads instead of the usual five.
- [12:07](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=727) **[[common-mistakes]]** Check progression for impossible points; a wave-76 wall was caused by a missing configuration.
- [11:20](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=680) **[[common-mistakes]]** Anchor parts and check placement after building; the speaker had an unanchored plot and chests that fell.
- [4:21](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=261) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Create unique art assets (balloons, blimps, bosses) in Blender and texture them to differentiate from stud-style templates.
- [4:43](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=283) **[[ui-ux-design]]** Add billboard UI for enemies (name and health), and scale UI correctly so it does not render oversized.
- [4:43](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=283) **[[team-and-budget]]** Outsource repetitive or specialist work (animation, modeling, configuration, soundtrack) to freelancers or friends.
- [15:08](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=908) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Keep pushing through setbacks; the speaker said the game went from abandoned to 3,000 players after the fix.
