# Turning a Roblox Game Into a Profitable Business

[Watch](https://youtu.be/Sgs36l6K5EU) | janisjanis01 | 218,464 views | 2025-07-27

## Summary
Creator builds a Grow a Garden-style clone solo, adds plot expansion, merchant, crops, weather/mutations, gifting, starter packs, then runs Roblox ads (~4,500 + ~5,000 + ~4,000 Robux). Game flops initially; ads CTR 1.3–1.9%, ~1,000 Robux lifetime revenue vs. much higher ad spend. Late surge to 140 players.

## Tips
- [0:22](https://youtu.be/Sgs36l6K5EU?t=22) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Build trend-following games fast; the creator argues speed matters because the trend can pass. Late entry to a trend may fail.
- [8:42](https://youtu.be/Sgs36l6K5EU?t=522) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Make several thumbnail variants and A/B test them; creator found a 1.3% CTR too low and redesigned. Arrows and reverse-psychology hooks were used to lift CTR.
- [10:49](https://youtu.be/Sgs36l6K5EU?t=649) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Confirm the game loads and is playable before spending on ads; low play rate was blamed on loading issues.
- [1:05](https://youtu.be/Sgs36l6K5EU?t=65) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make the first session playable and clear; creator noted that a game you can't really play explains poor play rate.
- [3:59](https://youtu.be/Sgs36l6K5EU?t=239) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Test ad spend in small batches and check CTR/play rate after one day before scaling; creator spent ~4,500 Robux on first ad.
- [5:54](https://youtu.be/Sgs36l6K5EU?t=354) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Reuse scripts and systems from previous games to cut build time; creator copied shop, settings and gifting systems.
- [7:18](https://youtu.be/Sgs36l6K5EU?t=438) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Put starter packs prominently; creator considered them the main revenue source.
- [12:14](https://youtu.be/Sgs36l6K5EU?t=734) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track visits, active players and Robux earned against ad spend; a game with ~1 active player and ~1,000 Robux lifetime revenue did not cover ad spend.
- [12:14](https://youtu.be/Sgs36l6K5EU?t=734) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Home-page recommendations indicate the game entered the algorithm; watch for this after ads, as it can bring late organic players.
- [9:23](https://youtu.be/Sgs36l6K5EU?t=563) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** If a contracted modeler quits, creator modeled crops in Blender to save money; a rushed model shipped with exclusive seeds not loading.
