# Making a Viral Roblox RNG Game in 30 Days

[Watch](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY) | janisjanis01 | 285,994 views | 2026-08-30

## Summary
Creator builds an RNG tower-defense balloon game ("Poppa Balloon" + RNG mix) from scratch over 30 days with a scripter friend, releases it, runs Robux ads, and ships a first update. Shows a net loss of about 170k Robux after ads and the 100k publishing fee, with the game reaching roughly 20-100 players.

## Tips
- [0:40](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=40) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Combine a proven viral mechanic from your own hit game with a popular genre (RNG roll + tower defense) rather than inventing from scratch.
- [2:02](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=122) **[[community-and-discord]]** Add group rewards early; the creator says this is how he grew one group past 1 million users.
- [4:40](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=280) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Include a 1.5x coin boost gamepass; creator claims it heavily increases conversion.
- [8:05](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=485) **[[player-psychology-and-game-feel]]** Add rare-roll cutscenes for top rarities; they create dopamine spikes that RNG players expect.
- [10:10](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=610) **[[common-mistakes]]** Playtest and balance early; creator says many of his games failed because of balancing, and found a useless turret and a wave-168 difficulty spike.
- [10:51](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=651) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Build thumbnails in Studio, polish in Photoshop, keep them simple, and A/B test variants when running ads.
- [12:55](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=775) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Overdo playtesting; check progression pacing in both directions (too fast and too slow) and test deep waves, not just early ones.
- [15:41](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=941) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Before release, verify the luck/RNG system and server-lock logic; the launch had broken luck and a non-expiring server lock.
- [15:41](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=941) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Check age-rating/audience settings before paid ads; the creator launched an all-ages ad while the game was 16+ only, cutting eligible players.
- [15:41](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=941) **[[common-mistakes]]** Add invisible boundary walls at launch; players were falling through the map.
- [16:23](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=983) **[[common-mistakes]]** Guard against infinite-stacking exploits: one player stacked hundreds of turrets on one tile, reaching wave 8,679 and breaking the economy.
- [17:04](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=1024) **[[community-and-discord]]** Ask players what they want and ship what the community asks for; the creator added community-requested features in the first update.
- [17:44](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=1064) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Ads spent heavily with little result: 13,000 Robux for ~35 highly engaged players. Test small budgets before scaling.
- [17:44](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=1064) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** A thumbnail showing how a scene started versus ended got about 3% CTR, and the creator says it also worked on YouTube.
- [18:25](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=1105) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Launch content updates to lift player count; a hosted admin-abuse event raised CCU from ~20-30 to over 100.
- [18:25](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=1105) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add a friends boost that rewards inviting friends who invite others, creating a referral chain.
- [19:06](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=1146) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Account for the 100,000 Robux publishing fee and ad spend in your P&L; the creator ended at roughly minus 170,734 Robux net.
