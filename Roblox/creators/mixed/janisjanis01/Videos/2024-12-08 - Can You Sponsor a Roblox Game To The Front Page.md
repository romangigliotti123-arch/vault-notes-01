# Can You Sponsor a Roblox Game To The Front Page?

> Tagged **low-advice**: mostly entertainment/experiment; notes kept short.
[Watch](https://youtu.be/9EXrUN7PUrE) | janisjanis01 | 31,518 views | 2024-12-08

## Summary
Creator spends ~100k Robux on Roblox ads (sponsored experiences) over multiple campaigns to push a simulator game to the front page. Tests icons, device and region targeting, lifetime vs daily budgets. Results: low CTR, fast ad-credit burn, mostly low-engagement new players. Concludes paid ads lose money; creator videos work better.

## Tips
- [4:45](https://youtu.be/9EXrUN7PUrE?t=285) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Watch CTR early; a 0.3% click-through rate and 0.07 play rate signalled a failing ad that wasn't worth continuing.
- [4:45](https://youtu.be/9EXrUN7PUrE?t=285) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Avoid lifetime budgets for sponsored ads in this creator's test; they burned credits quickly and delivered fewer players.
- [7:49](https://youtu.be/9EXrUN7PUrE?t=469) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Expect sponsored traffic to be mostly new Roblox users who barely play games; most left quickly, so retention will be poor.
- [9:29](https://youtu.be/9EXrUN7PUrE?t=569) **[[influencer-and-creator-marketing]]** Creator videos about your game were judged a better player source than paid Roblox ads for this creator.
