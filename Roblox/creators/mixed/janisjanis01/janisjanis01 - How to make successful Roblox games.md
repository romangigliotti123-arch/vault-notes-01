# janisjanis01 - How to make successful Roblox games

Start from a proven trend and make a clearly better version, not a copy. Build a core loop with replayability (rebirths, rounds, uncompletable trading, leaderboards) and a game that lasts longer than about 10 minutes. Polish the lobby, UI and icon, and ship updates regularly. Test with real players before spending. Ads have mostly lost money in this creator's tests, and buying and reviving existing games has produced large net losses in his most recent videos. Track net profit: Robux earned x 0.7, minus ad spend and fees.

**Style:** Candid, numbers-first case-study style. He shows real ad spend, revenue and losses, often on bought or revived games. Useful for solo or small-team devs who want honest failure data. Lighter on concrete mechanics. Some claims are unverified (e.g. 13.5M Robux, 7.8M Robux) and should be checked.

## Playbook
1. **Pick a proven trend and make a clearly better version** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Build on a proven genre (simulator, tower defense, RNG, grow-a-garden style) and add one unique twist. Copying without a twist and entering a trend late were called risky (CltiB53QTHw, pjN_aBDHAfY, fRBNHaMtvFs, NWyK_PbFdxE).
2. **Validate the trend and move fast** [[trend-research-and-validation]]: Speed matters because trends pass. Study successful Shorts and formats, and pivot a stalled game to a current trend such as brain rot or simulators (RECJIE0_CzE, QnAoCsC-4ig, 5LMGVfslAYs).
3. **Design the core loop for replayability** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: Add rebirths, rounds or leaderboards so players keep earning after finishing. Make odds improve with play, and keep the game uncompletable through trading (fRBNHaMtvFs, NWyK_PbFdxE). Be cautious with manipulative streak and near-miss mechanics (Shjq-Xd9Kmw).
4. **Make the first session playable and clear** [[first-session-onboarding]]: Build a real tutorial with progress saving, a spotlight hole over the target button, and hidden buttons until it is done. Playtesters found many issues without one (WJpNCB5J9bg, 5E5pj7CB4mM).
5. **Persist player data** [[data-persistence]]: Use a DataStore so leaderstats and wins survive leaving. Test by giving yourself wins and confirming they persist. Verify equip, leave, rejoin, reset for cosmetics (9aQpeTCZ7yM).
6. **Fix scripts and profile performance** [[scripting-and-performance]]: When a working script breaks, check for single-character typos first. Use the Tag Editor to drive repeated parts with one script. Profile lag before an ad push: a pet follow script caused lag with ~40 players in one place (9aQpeTCZ7yM, e725ByFD8EI).
7. **Polish UI and lobby** [[ui-ux-design]]: Replace default leaderstats with a custom GUI, add animations and click sounds, and use ViewportFrames in shop cards. New players judge quality within minutes, so polish the lobby first (fRBNHaMtvFs, 9aQpeTCZ7yM, WJpNCB5J9bg).
8. **Budget for outside help** [[team-and-budget]]: Hire scripters, builders or icon artists when stuck. Around 70% of revenue went into development on one game. Outsource repetitive config work and expect delivery delays with buffer time (hxjumUFspP0, EJ8qN7EKafM).
9. **Sell gamepasses that help progression or cosmetics** [[monetisation-gamepasses]]: Gamepasses should speed progression, such as a skip button, or sell cosmetics only. Pay-to-win hurts retention. Replacing a previous owner's passes avoids profit leaking to them (NWyK_PbFdxE, Sgs36l6K5EU).
10. **Add developer products with server-side grants** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: Currency needs developer products. A GUI button alone sells nothing, so the server script must grant it. Cheap boosts and starter packs were the main conversion tools (9aQpeTCZ7yM, CltiB53QTHw, Sgs36l6K5EU).
11. **Playtest before any paid push** [[playtesting-and-feedback]]: Invite friends and testers before release. Check pacing both ways (too fast and too slow) and deep waves. Bugs found after ad spend wasted money, and a low play rate was blamed on loading issues (9EXrUN7PUrE, WJpNCB5J9bg).
12. **Launch with icon test and a small ad test** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Set genre and server cap. Test two icons. Run a small daily ad budget (not lifetime) and check CTR and play rate after one day. Stop if CTR is near 0.3% with a 0.07 play rate (5E5pj7CB4mM, 9EXrUN7PUrE, Sgs36l6K5EU).
13. **Grow with consistent Shorts and proper account setup** [[trailers-and-video-marketing]]: Post consistently with hooks, loops and a number in the title. Expect slow starts and single-video spikes. Shorts traffic gave installs but poor retention. New TikTok accounts got near-zero views, while reposting older content helped (5LMGVfslAYs, sM9btPAvb28, IWzAHgHPhhU, 5iIIYKztamo).
14. **Run regular updates and events** [[updates-live-ops]]: Keep weekly updates and events such as an Easter event. A pre-update notification tripled players for one game. Test updates thoroughly, since a rushed one broke a game (xqHXDYbuF2w, Pruto1wqxAU, hxjumUFspP0, OL1FJ17wzZE).

## Common mistakes
- Expecting a 5-day game to go viral or reach the front page (fRBNHaMtvFs)
- Launching with a 10-minute completion, weak icon and no monetisation or replayability (fRBNHaMtvFs)
- Spending on ads before confirming the game loads and is playable (9EXrUN7PUrE, WJpNCB5J9bg)
- Using lifetime budgets for sponsored ads; they burned credits fast with fewer players (9EXrUN7PUrE)
- Launching a paid ad push without profiling lag, which caused server lag (e725ByFD8EI)
- Buying failed or active games without checking sales history; updates broke them and losses were large (YVKHlrlWn-k, OL1FJ17wzZE, WJpNCB5J9bg)
- Relying on Shorts alone; players had poor retention and 7-minute average play (sM9btPAvb28)
- Using a new TikTok account or externally edited videos; low views and possible flagging (IWzAHgHPhhU)
- Pay-to-win gamepasses that drive player loss (NWyK_PbFdxE)
- Copying a trend with no twist or entering it late (CltiB53QTHw, pjN_aBDHAfY)

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Confirm the game loads and is playable on mobile and PC before spending (9EXrUN7PUrE, WJpNCB5J9bg)
- [ ] Set a server player count cap before launch (creator's servers were overwhelmed around 300 players)
- [ ] Set the game genre in settings; this alone raised one game's player count (discovery-algorithm)
- [ ] Playtest with friends and testers; check pacing both ways and deep waves
- [ ] Finish a readable icon and thumbnail; A/B test at least two icons (thumbnails-icons, ab-testing)
- [ ] Build a tutorial with progress saving and a spotlight; hide other buttons until done
- [ ] Verify DataStore persistence: equip, leave, rejoin, reset (9aQpeTCZ7yM)
- [ ] Add developer products with server-side grants and one cheap boost (CltiB53QTHw)
- [ ] Add group rewards and make them visible in the UI
- [ ] Add a pre-update notification / follow prompt (xqHXDYbuF2w)
- [ ] Profile lag with ~40 players in one spot (e725ByFD8EI)
- [ ] Set a daily ad budget, not a lifetime budget; test small first (9EXrUN7PUrE)
- [ ] After one day, check CTR and play rate; stop if CTR ~0.3% with 0.07 play rate
- [ ] Check the six stats on the dashboard before spending more on ads (xqHXDYbuF2w)
- [ ] Compute net: Robux x 0.7 minus ad spend and fees, including the 100k publishing fee if applicable (5E5pj7CB4mM, pjN_aBDHAfY)
- [ ] Set the channel description and check group name availability (titles-descriptions)
- [ ] Prepare Shorts with hooks and loops before launch (5LMGVfslAYs)
- [ ] Schedule the first post-launch update within about a week (hxjumUFspP0)

## How the advice changed over time
- 2023: favoured self-built obbies with replayability, gamepasses and polish; advice centred on fixing visuals and length (fRBNHaMtvFs, 9aQpeTCZ7yM).
- Late 2023 to 2024: paid sponsored ads tested; by 2024-12 the creator concluded paid ads lose money and creator videos work better (5E5pj7CB4mM, 9EXrUN7PUrE). Newer advice supersedes earlier 'ads are needed' suggestions.
- 2025: Shorts became the main promotion channel with strong views but weak retention; 2025-08 showed a Shorts-only channel was not monetized (sM9btPAvb28, 5LMGVfslAYs, 5iIIYKztamo).
- 2025-09 onward: shifted to buying and reviving failed or template games with ads. Results were mixed, and the 2026 videos report large net losses (YVKHlrlWn-k, QnAoCsC-4ig, OL1FJ17wzZE, WJpNCB5J9bg). The most recent advice treats purchase-and-revive as high risk.
- 2026-06 (CltiB53QTHw) reports that cloning a proven own game with ads worked (~88k Robux), which conflicts with the losses in other 2026 videos. The creator's own verdict: the formula works but is hard to repeat.
- 2026: the creator added the six-stat dashboard check, replacing earlier guess-based decisions (xqHXDYbuF2w, analytics-metrics advice).
