# How to Make More Money From Roblox Games (Monetization Guide)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M) | TheDevKing | 89,074 views | 2020-05-12

## Summary
Monetization guide for Roblox games (2020). Covers platform revenue context, game passes vs developer products, premium payouts (judged not worth it yet), subscriptions/battle passes, currency sales and random loot boxes, and a 3-step funnel: convert non-purchasers via milestone starter bundles, drive repeat purchases with in-view items and prompts for currency when short, and turn repeat buyers into whales with content and updates.

## Tips
- [9:57](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=597) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Offer a cheap starter bundle (coins, items, extra level) when a player hits a milestone around 20-30 minutes in; don't prompt purchases in the first 30 seconds.
- [12:36](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=756) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** When a player can't afford an item, show a developer product prompt to buy the exact currency amount needed instead of just saying they can't buy it.
- [12:36](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=756) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Show players the game passes and purchasable items in front of them so they know what exists; many games have too few passes, so add more content.
- [4:02](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=242) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Study top-earning games' game pass and product presentation (shop boards vs GUI, prompt timing) and copy what works for your own game.
- [6:06](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=366) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Sell a spendable in-game currency; developer products generally earn as much or more than game passes for most games.
- [6:47](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=407) **[[monetisation-other]]** Random loot boxes/crates/eggs are a proven mechanic; avoid selling them directly for Robux where it may count as gambling and check local law.
- [14:24](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=864) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Keep adding new content and spend-able items plus new crates or currency uses so whales keep having things to buy.
- [8:33](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=513) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Target the first purchase: a player who has bought once is far more likely to buy again, so the first purchase is the key conversion step.
- [3:21](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=201) **[[monetisation-other]]** Subscriptions/battle passes are hard to implement on Roblox currently; expect easier support later.
- [2:41](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=161) **[[monetisation-other]]** Premium payouts are not yet worth prioritising in his view, though they can be viable for some developers.
