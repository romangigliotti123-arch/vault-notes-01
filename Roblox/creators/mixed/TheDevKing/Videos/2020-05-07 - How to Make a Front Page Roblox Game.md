# How to Make a Front Page Roblox Game

[Watch](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME) | TheDevKing | 79,789 views | 2020-05-07

## Summary
Explains Roblox's "Designing for Engagement" article (five engagement factors: social, variety, convenience, achievement, investment) and advises studying existing front-page games. Includes an interview with rainway gaming, creator of Power Simulator, on how youtuber promotion and a fan-favourite inspired clone drove a front-page run. Some advice is generic.

## Tips
- [1:46](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=106) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Read Roblox's 'Designing for Engagement' article; it lists five engagement drivers: social, variety, convenience, achievement and investment.
- [3:08](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=188) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Build social spaces (lobbies, roleplay areas) and community channels (groups, Twitter, Discord) to create reasons for players to return.
- [4:31](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=271) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Offer both casual and enthusiast paths and multiple ways to win; add progression so players feel they are advancing.
- [6:33](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=393) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add visible progress indicators (XP, coin pickup effects, level-up animations) so players feel rewarded each session.
- [7:15](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=435) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Let players invest time in customisable spaces like homes or builds, and set short- and long-term currency goals so they keep returning.
- [8:37](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=517) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Don't rely only on Roblox sponsored ads; pay YouTubers or influencers to play your game, since viewers copy what they watch.
- [9:26](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=566) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Study current front-page games, note what they share (randomness, events, reward loops), then implement those elements in your own game.
- [10:47](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=647) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Expect your first game may not reach the front page; keep iterating and releasing rather than giving up.
- [12:58](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=778) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Make a game inspired by a popular game whose developers abandoned updates, and target the audience that wanted it.
- [13:39](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=819) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Use a pre-existing audience, such as your own YouTube channel dedicated to the genre, to launch with players before paid ads.
- [14:20](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=860) **[[community-and-discord]]** Contact bigger YouTubers by business email to promote the game; the speaker says it helped the launch.
- [15:42](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=942) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Compete with rivals by making the core experience simpler and faster to understand than theirs, e.g. no slow box-opening loot.
- [16:23](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=983) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Don't copy a game exactly; change key elements and add live events to differentiate from the original.
- [17:44](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=1064) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Split sponsor budget by platform: target mobile-heavy audiences for mobile games and PC for PC; speaker says ~50k Robux can get a good game to the front page.

## Numbers
- [1:46](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=106) Engagement metrics definitions: **DAU = unique players per day; MAU = unique players per month** (Definitions given from Roblox's engagement article)
- [12:58](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=778) Original game player base: **~10,000 players** (Superpower Training still had this many when owners stopped updating it)
- [14:20](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=860) Players before any ads: **5k players** (Power Simulator reached this from YouTube audience alone, before paid ads or sponsorship)
- [17:44](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=1064) Sponsorship budget to front page: **~50k Robux, up to 100k for a stronger push** (Speaker's estimate for a good game; depends on game and platform)
- [18:26](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=1106) Total Robux earned by Power Simulator: **~9 million Robux (before Roblox's cut)** (Speaker's recollection; last checked figure)
- [18:26](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=1106) Front-page duration: **~1 month** (Power Simulator stayed on front page for about a month)

## Games
- **Power Simulator** [12:58](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=778): Inspired by Superpower Training after its owners stopped updating; succeeded via YouTuber promotion, a dedicated audience, and simpler gameplay.
- **Superpower Training** [12:58](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=778): Abandoned by owners for about six months while still earning; a fan-favourite niche that a clone can target.
- **Apocalypse Rising** [15:01](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=901): Competitor in the zombie genre; complicated looting system is a weakness that a simpler rival can exploit.
- **Lumber Tycoon** [5:12](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=312): Example of emergent gameplay, where players find unintended routes like obtaining axes.
- **Fleet of Facility** [17:44](https://youtu.be/AZTDrfuk9ME?t=1064): Example of a heavily mobile-dominated game; target mobile audiences for mobile games.
