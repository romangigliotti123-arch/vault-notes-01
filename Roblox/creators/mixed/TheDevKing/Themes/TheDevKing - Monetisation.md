## [[monetisation-devproducts]]
- 2020-05-12 [9:57](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=597) Offer a cheap starter bundle (coins, items, extra level) when a player hits a milestone around 20-30 minutes in; don't prompt purchases in the first 30 seconds. ([[2020-05-12 - How to Make More Money From Roblox Games (Monetization Guide)|How to Make More Money From Roblox Games (Monetiza]])
- 2020-05-12 [12:36](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=756) When a player can't afford an item, show a developer product prompt to buy the exact currency amount needed instead of just saying they can't buy it. ([[2020-05-12 - How to Make More Money From Roblox Games (Monetization Guide)|How to Make More Money From Roblox Games (Monetiza]])
- 2020-05-12 [6:06](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=366) Sell a spendable in-game currency; developer products generally earn as much or more than game passes for most games. ([[2020-05-12 - How to Make More Money From Roblox Games (Monetization Guide)|How to Make More Money From Roblox Games (Monetiza]])
- 2020-05-12 [8:33](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=513) Target the first purchase: a player who has bought once is far more likely to buy again, so the first purchase is the key conversion step. ([[2020-05-12 - How to Make More Money From Roblox Games (Monetization Guide)|How to Make More Money From Roblox Games (Monetiza]])
