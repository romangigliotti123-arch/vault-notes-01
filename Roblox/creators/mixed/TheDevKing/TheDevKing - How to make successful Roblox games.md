# TheDevKing - How to make successful Roblox games

Study current front-page games and copy what they share (randomness, events, reward loops), but change key elements rather than cloning. Design around Roblox's five engagement drivers (social, variety, convenience, achievement, investment). Launch with a pre-existing audience plus paid YouTuber promotion, not only Roblox ads. Monetise by selling spendable currency, convert first-time buyers with milestone starter bundles, and keep adding content and live events so repeat buyers have things to buy. Platform figures are from 2020 and need re-checking.

**Style:** Practical, numbers-driven playbook from a developer who studied Power Simulator's front-page run. Gives a clear sequence (study, clone-with-changes, promote, monetise, update). Suits beginners who want concrete steps. Some advice is generic, and platform figures are dated 2020.

## Playbook
1. **Idea: study front-page games first** [[trend-research-and-validation]]: Study current front-page games and note what they share, such as randomness, events and reward loops. Take the elements that work and implement them in your own game. Do not copy a game exactly; change key elements and add live events to differentiate.
2. **Idea: design around Roblox's five engagement drivers** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: Read Roblox's 'Designing for Engagement' article. Cover social, variety, convenience, achievement and investment. Offer casual and enthusiast paths and multiple ways to win, and add progression so players feel they are advancing.
3. **Idea: beat rivals on simplicity** [[game-ideas-and-genres]]: Make the core experience simpler and faster to understand than rivals. Example given: avoid slow box-opening loot. Clarity of the core loop is the differentiator.
4. **Idea: target an audience left behind** [[trend-research-and-validation]]: Make a game inspired by a popular game whose developers abandoned updates, and target the players who wanted more. Use this as a validated audience before building.
5. **Build: visible progress feedback** [[retention-d1-d7]]: Add XP, coin pickup effects and level-up animations so players feel rewarded each session. Show progression clearly on screen.
6. **Build: investment and short/long-term goals** [[retention-d1-d7]]: Let players invest time in customisable spaces such as homes or builds. Set short-term and long-term currency goals so players keep returning.
7. **Build: social spaces and community channels** [[social-features-and-virality]]: Build lobbies or roleplay areas as social spaces. Set up community channels (group, Twitter, Discord) to give players reasons to return.
8. **Build: currency and visible passes** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: Sell a spendable in-game currency through developer products, which generally earn as much or more than game passes for most games. Show game passes and purchasable items in front of players. Many games have too few passes, so add more content.
9. **Build: loot boxes with caution** [[monetisation-other]]: Random loot boxes, crates and eggs are a proven mechanic. Avoid selling them directly for Robux, where it may count as gambling, and check local law. Subscriptions and battle passes were hard to implement in 2020; premium payouts were judged not yet worth prioritising.
10. **Launch: build your own audience first** [[trend-research-and-validation]]: Use a pre-existing audience, such as your own YouTube channel dedicated to the genre, to launch with players before paid ads. Start community channels before the push.
11. **Launch: pay creators, split budget by platform** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Do not rely only on Roblox sponsored ads. Pay YouTubers or influencers to play the game, since viewers copy what they watch. Split the budget by platform: mobile-heavy audiences for mobile games, PC for PC. Speaker estimate: ~50k Robux for a front-page push, up to 100k for a stronger one.
12. **Launch: contact bigger YouTubers directly** [[community-and-discord]]: Contact bigger YouTubers by business email to promote the game. The speaker says this helped the launch.
13. **Growth: convert the first purchase** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: Offer a cheap starter bundle (coins, items or an extra level) at a milestone around 20-30 minutes in. Do not prompt purchases in the first 30 seconds. When a player can't afford an item, offer a developer product prompt for the exact currency amount needed. A player who has bought once is far more likely to buy again.
14. **Live ops: keep feeding whales** [[updates-live-ops]]: Keep adding new content, spendable items, new crates or new currency uses so repeat buyers keep having things to buy. Run live events to keep the game fresh. Turn repeat buyers into whales through content and updates.

## Common mistakes
- Copying a popular game exactly instead of changing key elements and adding live events.
- Shipping with a single path to win or no visible progression, so players don't feel they are advancing.
- Prompting purchases in the first 30 seconds of play.
- Showing too few game passes or purchasable items, so players don't know what exists.
- Relying only on Roblox sponsored ads and skipping paid YouTuber or influencer promotion.
- Launching with no pre-existing audience before paid ads.
- Selling loot boxes directly for Robux without checking whether it counts as gambling under local law.
- Showing 'can't afford' with no exact-amount currency purchase prompt.
- Abandoning updates, leaving whales with nothing new to buy.
- Giving up after the first game misses the front page instead of iterating and releasing again.

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Game has at least two play paths (casual and enthusiast) and multiple ways to win.
- [ ] Progress indicators in place: XP, coin pickup effects, level-up animation.
- [ ] Customisable space or build for player investment, with short- and long-term currency goals.
- [ ] Lobby or social area built, plus community channels (group, Twitter, Discord).
- [ ] Pre-existing audience (own YouTube channel or community) ready before any paid push.
- [ ] Promotion budget set aside for YouTubers/influencers, split by mobile vs PC audience.
- [ ] Business emails prepared for bigger YouTubers.
- [ ] Spendable currency developer product live, with exact-amount prompt when a player is short.
- [ ] Game passes and purchasable items visible in the game and shop.
- [ ] No purchase prompts during the first 30 seconds.
- [ ] Starter bundle configured for the ~20-30 minute milestone.
- [ ] Crate or loot mechanic checked for gambling rules; not sold directly for Robux.
- [ ] DAU and MAU tracking in place to measure engagement.
- [ ] Update and live-event calendar planned, with new content and currency sinks.
- [ ] Next game idea ready in case the first one misses the front page.
- [ ] Current Roblox developer share, premium payout and subscription policy re-verified (sourced figures are from 2020).
