## [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]
- 2021-12-01 [25:15](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=1515) Use sponsored ad targeting (gender, age, platform) to learn which audiences engage most, since every engagement also feeds organic ranking. ([[2021-12-01 - Driving Discovery on Roblox RDC 2021|Driving Discovery on Roblox | RDC 2021]])
- 2021-12-01 [25:55](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=1555) Sponsored ads run 1 to 28 days with a daily Robux budget that acts as the bid; the budget largely determines impression share. ([[2021-12-01 - Driving Discovery on Roblox RDC 2021|Driving Discovery on Roblox | RDC 2021]])
- 2021-12-01 [21:33](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=1293) Compared with mobile stores where cost per install is about $2 to $3.50, Roblox sponsored plays cost pennies, making paid discovery cheap to test. ([[2021-12-01 - Driving Discovery on Roblox RDC 2021|Driving Discovery on Roblox | RDC 2021]])
- 2022-05-12 [12:12](https://youtu.be/14RMDByrG1U?t=732) Brand IP lowers user acquisition costs because audiences already have affinity; retention still depends on the game being fun and authentic to the brand. ([[2022-05-12 - Brand Partnerships and IP Games Roundtable|Brand Partnerships and IP Games Roundtable]])
- 2022-05-19 [0:47](https://youtu.be/HxqWoF_wfMQ?t=47) Sponsored tiles lost effectiveness after Roblox put them in one sort instead of scattering them; use them more diligently rather than heavily. ([[2022-05-19 - Clip! Social Media Marketing with KreekCraft, WonderWorksZach & JoshWW|Clip! Social Media Marketing with KreekCraft, Wond]])
- 2023-02-23 [34:23](https://youtu.be/BsyU-SJ9L-c?t=2063) Track whether players actually click key buttons by tying a badge to the click; a hacky but practical analytics method. ([[2023-02-23 - Mobile UIUX for Roblox with Cindering and UndoneBuilder|Mobile UI/UX for Roblox with Cindering and UndoneB]])
- 2023-04-27 [0:05](https://youtu.be/vNKPlYeXj0Q?t=5) Prerelease hype is a cheap way to promote; build it with speculation content rather than paid spend. ([[2023-04-27 - Clip! Marketing New Games on Roblox with KreekCraft & WonderWorksZach|Clip! Marketing New Games on Roblox with KreekCraf]])
- 2024-08-07 [19:32](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=1172) Run a pre-launch countdown: post a daily short teaser across nine social accounts, and pair launch with a UGC item event from a partner. ([[2024-08-07 - (English) What We Wish We Knew When Developing Rift Hunters by Brave T|(English) What We Wish We Knew When Developing Rif]])
- 2025-08-29 [39:04](https://youtu.be/7AtvvUxvCaU?t=2344) Increase prominence of the rewarded prompt; Rivals got roughly 4x impressions almost overnight from a discoverability change alone. ([[2025-08-29 - Learn more about rewarded video ads|Learn more about rewarded video ads]])
- 2025-08-29 [31:14](https://youtu.be/7AtvvUxvCaU?t=1874) Prefer 6-15 second ad length; the platform accepts up to 30 seconds. ([[2025-08-29 - Learn more about rewarded video ads|Learn more about rewarded video ads]])

## [[discovery-algorithm]]
- 2021-11-18 [13:33](https://youtu.be/rVr0IyfYkPg?t=813) Search YouTube for recent uploads of your game (sorted by newest) to see unfiltered new-player behavior and spot UX confusion. ([[2021-11-18 - Finding the Fun with Jandel and Foryxe|Finding the Fun with Jandel and Foryxe]])
- 2021-12-01 [8:54](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=534) Recommendations are personalized by demographics, content preferences, friends' activity and recent engagement; popularity alone no longer guarantees placement. ([[2021-12-01 - Driving Discovery on Roblox RDC 2021|Driving Discovery on Roblox | RDC 2021]])
- 2021-12-01 [10:58](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=658) Optimise for converting impressions into real engagement, not raw clicks; both reaching the right audience and the game's appeal drive ranking. ([[2021-12-01 - Driving Discovery on Roblox RDC 2021|Driving Discovery on Roblox | RDC 2021]])
- 2021-12-01 [5:42](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=342) Concurrent players and ratings were the old popularity levers; under personalization, focus on the appeal and click-to-play conversion of your tile. ([[2021-12-01 - Driving Discovery on Roblox RDC 2021|Driving Discovery on Roblox | RDC 2021]])
- 2022-12-01 [9:23](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=563) Pitch the game to non-Roblox YouTubers and streamers in its genre (e.g. Battlefield/Call of Duty creators); Frontlines got attention this way at its February demo. ([[2022-12-01 - Designing for Older Players Q&A with Bluay, MidnightKrystal, Looty_Hag|Designing for Older Players Q&A with Bluay, Midnig]])
- 2024-08-07 [30:31](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=1831) Improving day-7 retention and playtime should lift monetisation and home-page recommendation, according to the speaker. ([[2024-08-07 - (English) What We Wish We Knew When Developing Rift Hunters by Brave T|(English) What We Wish We Knew When Developing Rif]])
