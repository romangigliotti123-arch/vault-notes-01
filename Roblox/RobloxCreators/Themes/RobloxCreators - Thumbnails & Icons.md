# RobloxCreators: Thumbnails & Icons

All RobloxCreators tips on this theme, grouped by topic, oldest first. Cross-creator synthesis: see [[README]] in roblox-kb.

## [[thumbnails-icons]]
- 2021-07-29 [21:11](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=1271) When the update ships, refresh the thumbnail and landing page with recognizable iconography so players recognize the game. ([[2021-07-29 - Producing Game Expansions with Dspav|Producing Game Expansions with Dspav]])
- 2021-12-01 [13:38](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=818) Pick a thumbnail that represents the game accurately; the title and image set expectations, and a mismatch with the gameplay loop hurts engagement after the click. ([[2021-12-01 - Driving Discovery on Roblox RDC 2021|Driving Discovery on Roblox | RDC 2021]])
- 2022-12-01 [9:23](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=563) Use realistic visuals (Frontlines is moving to realistic rigs) so the game reads as a mature shooter rather than a Roblox kids' game. ([[2022-12-01 - Designing for Older Players Q&A with Bluay, MidnightKrystal, Looty_Hag|Designing for Older Players Q&A with Bluay, Midnig]])
- 2023-04-27 [1:26](https://youtu.be/vNKPlYeXj0Q?t=86) Capture gameplay footage (Shift+P or Ctrl+P) for a trailer; cheap showcase material is enough. ([[2023-04-27 - Clip! Marketing New Games on Roblox with KreekCraft & WonderWorksZach|Clip! Marketing New Games on Roblox with KreekCraf]])
- 2023-05-18 [26:10](https://youtu.be/K1pEI6LVJJA?t=1570) Check UI and text color contrast against WCAG guidelines or a contrast calculator; large text can use lower contrast, but avoid combos like white on yellow. ([[2023-05-18 - Designing for Accessibility with BitwiseAndrea, WooleyWool, and Contro|Designing for Accessibility with BitwiseAndrea, Wo]])
- 2024-08-07 [28:25](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=1705) Match Roblox's usual visual style for icons and thumbnails rather than going too unique; they say platform style fits better. ([[2024-08-07 - (English) What We Wish We Knew When Developing Rift Hunters by Brave T|(English) What We Wish We Knew When Developing Rif]])
- 2025-11-13 [0:50](https://youtu.be/ag569uHM96E?t=50) Upload an authentic in-engine gameplay preview video; it autoplays before the thumbnail on details pages. Avoid cinematic effects, CGI, UI overlays, or off-platform footage. ([[2025-11-13 - Learn more about Video Previews on Roblox|Learn more about Video Previews on Roblox]])
- 2025-11-13 [1:51](https://youtu.be/ag569uHM96E?t=111) Record from the player's vantage point showing real gameplay like exploring or completing a mission. Light post-processing and camera work are fine if they don't misrepresent gameplay. ([[2025-11-13 - Learn more about Video Previews on Roblox|Learn more about Video Previews on Roblox]])
- 2026-04-10 [0:36](https://youtu.be/NAwQr9sTy74?t=36) Add screenshots and a video preview on your asset's detail page, and a custom try-in-Roblox demo experience so buyers can test before purchasing. ([[2026-04-10 - What's the Creator Store|What's the Creator Store?]])
- 2026-04-17 [3:53](https://youtu.be/qNocmxFyfxs?t=233) Copy a Camera into the model, rename it 'thumbnail camera', so the default store thumbnail matches your Studio framing. ([[2026-04-17 - How to sell assets on the Creator Store|How to sell assets on the Creator Store]])

- 2021-11-11 [1:55](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=115) Keep a glossary of standard verbs and nouns (e.g. crop as noun, plant as verb) and apply it across quests, shop and social posts. - 2021-11-11 [15:13](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=913) Use quest titles for task statements (achievements), progress tracking (Part one of three), or flavor and puns, since titles sit a step removed from content.
