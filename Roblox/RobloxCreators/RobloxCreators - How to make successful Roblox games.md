# RobloxCreators - How to make successful Roblox games

Roblox's official Level Up/RDC creator content says: build around a fun, simple core loop, prototype and playtest early (paper/gray-box), onboard players into fun within the first minutes, and give short/medium/long-term goals. Monetise fairly (cosmetics, dev-product currency, varied price points, no pay-to-win). Launch with analytics and community presence, then run a steady content cadence (weekly/biweekly) plus a quarterly expansion built around the core loop. Track D1/D7/D30, session length and funnels, and listen to feedback while keeping design control.

**Style:** Official Roblox Developer Relations educational series: roundtables and workshops with successful studio devs (Piggy, Adopt Me-style, Outlaster, Arsenal, Club Roblox) plus Roblox product talks. Broad, practical, process-oriented; suits teams and solo devs wanting structured frameworks rather than a single growth hack.

## Playbook
1. **Pick audience and validate demand** [[trend-research-and-validation]]: Target one audience per feature, research gaps, benchmark top Roblox games, and don't assume an audience exists (Bitter Sweet ballerina failed). Check Marketplace Most Popular/Past Day (min 15 Robux) for UGC trends.
2. **Define a simple, fun core loop** [[core-loops-and-game-design]]: Core loop = minute-to-minute action, most-repeated actions, and progression engine (explore, fight, upgrade). If it isn't fun nothing saves it; every feature must enhance it, and avoid 'tumor' systems competing with it.
3. **Prototype before production** [[development-process-and-speed]]: Paper prototype in an afternoon for UI/rules; studio prototype in 1-2 weeks for tech risk and reuse. Keep scope small; a flaw found late costs 2-3 months. Kill or pivot fast; soft-launch to find the fun.
4. **Document, scope and plan the team** [[development-process-and-speed]]: Write down every feature detail (Piggy Intercity GDD ~2 months), break tasks by discipline with owners, estimate 6 productive hours per 8-hour day, delay rather than crunch, and gray-box levels before art.
5. **Design onboarding and the first minutes** [[first-session-onboarding]]: Teach essentials by doing, reach fun fast (quick level-ups, starter items), show game range, set short and long goals, end with a call to action. Use trails, contextual just-in-time hints, timed hints for strugglers; D1 retention is the key metric.
6. **Build UI mobile-first and accessible** [[scripting-and-performance]]: Mobile is over 70% of users. Use scale-based UI with aspect ratio constraints, thumb-zone buttons, few buttons, consistent visual language, immediate feedback, good contrast and large tap targets; test on devices and emulator.
7. **Add progression, goals and retention hooks** [[retention-d1-d7]]: Layer complexity with level gates, long-term goal shown early, dailies/quests/achievements to build habit and expose systems. Low D7 means unclear next goal; low D30 means weak endgame.
8. **Monetise fairly** [[monetisation-devproducts]]: Sell cosmetics, avoid pay-to-win, make dev-product currency the main revenue, keep few scalable gamepasses, offer wide price points (even 35 Robux), tag best-value bundles, give purchase feedback, and use event-only currencies and season passes.
9. **Playtest and instrument analytics before launch** [[analytics-metrics]]: Weekly playtests with average, casual and engaged personas; try to break the system. Set funnels and analytics before closed beta; track DAU, MAU, session length, D1/D7/D30, conversion, RPPU, ARPDAU.
10. **Launch with hype and presence** [[discovery-algorithm]]: Build organic hype via influencer early access, puzzles and trailers; avoid overhyping. Join servers on launch day, watch funnels and drop-offs, and ship a bug-fix release within about a week. Optimise title, thumbnail and description for click-to-play conversion.
11. **Grow with discovery tools and ads** [[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]: Recommendations are now personalized; focus on tile appeal and engagement. Sponsored ads (1-28 days, daily budget as bid) cost pennies per play and give targeting insight; rewarded video needs 100K+ DAU. Use video previews with authentic gameplay.
12. **Run content cadence plus expansions** [[updates-live-ops]]: Release at least monthly, ideally weekly/biweekly, mostly art-based reusing systems, at most 2-3 weeks each; plan 3 months out in 1-4 week sprints. Ship at least one expansion per quarter built around the core loop, and keep cadence running during it.
13. **Balance, events and economy protection** [[updates-live-ops]]: Use currency sinks and caps, favour buffs over nerfs, level-gate content, diversify update types to avoid power creep, and join events to re-engage lapsed players (33K CCU peak came from a cross-promo event).
14. **Manage community and feedback** [[community-and-discord]]: Pick feedback channels (Discord, Twitter), post consistently, filter feedback but keep design control, A/B test social features 50/50 for a week, and respect goodwill; communicate when releases miss.

## Key numbers
- Expansion cadence: at least 1 per quarter; build effort 1-3 months
- Cadence releases: at least monthly, ideally weekly/biweekly; max 2-3 weeks each
- Roadmap 3+ months out; sprints 1-4 weeks
- Estimate 6 productive hours per 8-hour day
- Post-launch bug-fix release: dedicated 1 week
- Mobile share of users: over 70%
- Paper prototype: one afternoon; studio prototype: 1-2 weeks; late flaw costs 2-3 months
- Social players retain ~30% better than solo (Block Evolution)
- A/B test: 50/50 split over one week
- Good session length at scale 15-20 min; 12 min pretty good
- Sponsored ads: 1-28 days; mobile CPI $2-3.50 vs Roblox pennies per play
- Rewarded video eligibility: 100K+ avg DAU over 28 days, minimal/mild maturity
- Adopt Me money tree capped $100/day (from $5/10min unlimited)
- UI: 15 px padding, 6 px corner radius (RDC example)
- Creator Store payouts: 30-day escrow, monthly on the 1st

## Common mistakes
- Adding tumor systems or opt-out alternatives that split players from the core loop
- Dropping content cadence during a big expansion, launching to a shrunken base
- Pay-to-win monetisation, especially for older players
- Skipping prototyping and finding design flaws months later
- Over-explaining with text; about 30% of players lost (speaker claim)
- Uncapped economy sources (Adopt Me money tree)
- Quests forcing wasteful spending or inviting griefing (join-leave group quests)
- World too big or empty (2k x 2k, 25 empty plots)
- Ignoring accessibility, color contrast, and mobile (prioritising PC)
- Overhyping before launch, banning critics, or crunching instead of delaying

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Core loop fun and playtested with average, casual and engaged personas
- [ ] Prototype validated; design doc complete
- [ ] Onboarding gets to fun within minutes with clear first goals
- [ ] Analytics and feature funnels live before closed beta
- [ ] UI tested on mobile devices, scaled, accessible contrast
- [ ] Monetisation is cosmetic/fair with varied price points and purchase feedback
- [ ] Economy sinks and caps in place
- [ ] Title, thumbnail and description match gameplay; localized if possible
- [ ] Gameplay trailer or video preview ready
- [ ] Social and Discord channels active with launch-day presence
- [ ] Team on servers on launch day watching DAU and funnels
- [ ] Bug-fix release scheduled for week one
- [ ] Content roadmap 3 months out with the next cadence package in production
- [ ] Playtesters recruited and feedback channel chosen

## How the advice changed over time
- 2021-2022 content emphasised live-ops frameworks (expansions, cadence, quests). 2023 onward shifted to short explainers on core loop, onboarding, engagement metrics. 2024-2026 added referral system, UGC business, Creator Store, rewarded video ads and video previews (newer platform tools supersede older sponsored-tile advice; sponsored tiles weakened after the 2022 sort change).
