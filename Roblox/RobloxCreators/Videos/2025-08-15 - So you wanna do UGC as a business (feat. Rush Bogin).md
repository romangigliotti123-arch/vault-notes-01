# So you wanna do UGC as a business? (feat. Rush Bogin)

[Watch](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A) | RobloxCreators | 69,081 views | 2025-08-15

## Summary
Interview with UGC creator Rush Bogin (Crush streetwear brand) on building a sustainable UGC business: starting by making what you like, getting feedback, building a 25+ item catalog, quality benchmarking against top creators, using Most Popular Past Week for trends, pricing at the floor, free/limited/regular releases, community-first marketing, analytics, and RDC brand partnerships.

## Tips
- [3:21](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=201) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't expect early items to succeed; Rush's first low-quality items drew hate, but the instant feedback showed what worked. Iterate fast.
- [4:11](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=251) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build a catalog of roughly 25 items with variety before treating it as a business; Rush says he has a couple hundred now.
- [5:11](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=311) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't judge items only against your own catalog. Compare against the marketplace quality bar, which keeps rising, and against a hero creator's work.
- [7:12](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=432) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Judge quality on texture and model. Add real-world imperfections like asymmetry, dents and folds, and think about physics.
- [8:22](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=502) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Pick items by combining three inputs: what you love making, consistent trends in Most Popular Past Week, and a hero's quality bar.
- [10:02](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=602) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Replicate the underlying style or trend, not the specific item. Ask what a set of trending items says collectively and build from that.
- [11:52](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=712) **[[pricing]]** Price at the price floor rather than high, since buyers compare prices and undercutting happens often. Luxury items can be priced higher.
- [11:52](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=712) **[[pricing]]** Release one major item free to build hype, and price a recolor of it, which Rush says often sells higher and lifts the rest of the set.
- [12:16](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=736) **[[monetisation-other]]** Free items must be limited quantity, and you pay the upload fee per copy, so a 1,000-copy giveaway costs 1,000 upload fees.
- [12:56](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=776) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use regular (unlimited) items for broad-appeal evergreen designs; use limited items for niche or unusual recolors that not everyone will buy.
- [14:06](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=846) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build a culture and community around your name across Roblox, Discord and Twitter rather than marketing individual items.
- [14:47](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=887) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Don't assume Twitter engagement converts to Roblox sales; it is hard to translate. Treat social as community-building.
- [15:28](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=928) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Show work-in-progress to your community before a full release; they give fast feedback and are often the buyers.
- [16:11](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=971) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build a recognisable signature into every item rather than putting your name on it. Let the style identify you.
- [17:08](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=1028) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use the creator analytics page to dig into trends. Break sales down by gender, age and where they sell, and check the catalog avatar creator share.
- [17:49](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=1069) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Renaming an underperforming item to match a current pop-culture trend (K-pop) revived its sales. Test renames when an item stalls.
- [19:51](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=1191) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Watch for outside pop culture such as movies, TikTok and trends. Sudden item spikes often come from broader environment, so interpret them.
- [21:19](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=1279) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Attend RDC and visit the Brand Partnerships booth with your portfolio; Roblox's team connects creators with brands.
- [21:19](https://youtu.be/KSez8Ecq50A?t=1279) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Have a portfolio link on your social accounts and a community plus store data, which makes brands more willing to partner.
