## Summary
Short clip where the speaker (Erythia, developer of Griffin's Destiny) answers how to increase free-to-paid conversion: accept not all players can pay, offer a wide range of Robux price points including low ones like 35 Robux. Says he doesn't look at conversion rates much.

## Tips
- [0:07](https://youtu.be/Cli3ogW-azk?t=7) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Accept that not all players can pay; offer a wide range of purchasable options so free players can try spending.
- [0:47](https://youtu.be/Cli3ogW-azk?t=47) **[[pricing]]** Include very low Robux price points (e.g. 35 Robux in Griffin's Destiny) so players with little Robux can convert.
- [0:47](https://youtu.be/Cli3ogW-azk?t=47) **[[pricing]]** Offer low-amount currency bundles rather than being ashamed of small numbers; otherwise low-balance players spend Robux elsewhere.
