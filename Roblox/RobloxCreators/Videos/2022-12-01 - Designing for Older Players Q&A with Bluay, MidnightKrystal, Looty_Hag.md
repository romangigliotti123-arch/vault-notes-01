# Designing for Older Players Q&A with Bluay, MidnightKrystal, Looty_Haga, cbmaximillian & 101airsoft

[Watch](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw) | RobloxCreators | 840 views | 2022-12-01

## Summary
Roundtable Q&A with Roblox devs (Arsenal, Frontlines and others) on designing for 13+ players: perceptions of Roblox as kids' platform, discovery via off-platform YouTubers, why build on Roblox vs Unity/Unreal, opt-in tutorials with funnel analytics, and update cadence (seasonal, meaningful thematic updates over frequent ones).

## Tips
- [8:39](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=519) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't mistake it for an audience-only problem: Roblox's kid-focused branding and bright cartoon visuals signal 'kids' games' to older players.
- [9:23](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=563) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Pitch the game to non-Roblox YouTubers and streamers in its genre (e.g. Battlefield/Call of Duty creators); Frontlines got attention this way at its February demo.
- [9:23](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=563) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Use realistic visuals (Frontlines is moving to realistic rigs) so the game reads as a mature shooter rather than a Roblox kids' game.
- [20:31](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=1231) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make tutorials opt-in: show a single prompt on first join asking if they want the tutorial, rather than spamming tooltips and popups.
- [21:52](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=1312) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make tutorials fun and practice-based (e.g. a shooting range with dialogue, replay without dialogue, and a leaderboard) rather than text pop-ups.
- [20:31](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=1231) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track player progress through the tutorial funnel to see where players drop off or struggle; it points you in a direction even if it doesn't explain why.
- [25:33](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=1533) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Set update cadence by genre and game size: a fast shooter like Arsenal ran updates about every two weeks in busy seasons, sometimes as slow as two months.
- [27:38](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=1658) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Use seasons (about every three months) with goals like a season pass to sustain progression, and add smaller content like crates between seasons.
- [29:41](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=1781) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Prioritise meaningful, thematic updates over frequent ones; too much content bloats the game and makes players quit or feel they can't keep up.
- [27:38](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=1658) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Ensure content is replayable and the core gameplay loop is fun on its own; sustainability matters more than update frequency.
- [11:21](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=681) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Roblox lets you deploy and iterate in days: servers, networking and infrastructure are handled, so you can test ideas fast and scrap failing ones.
- [11:21](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=681) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Weigh the revenue share against time and risk; off-platform projects need large budgets and publisher/licensor stakeholders, which Roblox avoids.
- [8:39](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=519) **[[roblox-platform-changes]]** Expect restrictions: Roblox enforces content rules globally, so mature themes (gore, drugs, undead) may be removed, and some regions ban loot boxes.
- [8:39](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=519) **[[roblox-platform-changes]]** Roblox rolled out content ratings so developers can rate their own games' age appropriateness.

## Numbers
- [25:33](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=1533) Update cadence in busy season: **~every 2 weeks (Arsenal); up to 2 months in slower periods** (Arsenal fast-paced shooter team cadence)
