# Clip! Addressing Monetization Mistakes with Shawnyg

[Watch](https://youtu.be/yn4V-fBdUm8) | RobloxCreators | 2,969 views | 2023-06-01

## Summary
Shawnyg discusses monetization mistakes from a Roblox game that went free-to-play. Paying players were compensated with perks (free car, in-game cash, a weapon). When a developer product was misissued, the creator reviewed purchase data and sent in-game notifications to affected buyers explaining the fix and granting compensation.

## Tips
- [0:07](https://youtu.be/yn4V-fBdUm8?t=7) **[[monetisation-other]]** When switching to free-to-play, give everyone who already paid comparable perks (free vehicle, in-game cash, weapon) so paying players feel respected.
- [0:07](https://youtu.be/yn4V-fBdUm8?t=7) **[[community-and-discord]]** Be transparent with paying players after monetization changes; tell them they weren't forgotten and explain what they get.
- [0:48](https://youtu.be/yn4V-fBdUm8?t=48) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** If a developer product is misissued, use purchase data to find affected buyers in the time window and compensate them.
- [0:48](https://youtu.be/yn4V-fBdUm8?t=48) **[[common-mistakes]]** Monitor developer products for failures; broken products between time windows cause compensation issues.
- [0:48](https://youtu.be/yn4V-fBdUm8?t=48) **[[community-and-discord]]** Notify affected players in-game with an apology, the fix, and the compensation when a monetization bug occurs.
