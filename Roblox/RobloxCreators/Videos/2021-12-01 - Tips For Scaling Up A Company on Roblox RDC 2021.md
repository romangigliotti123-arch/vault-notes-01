# Tips For Scaling Up A Company on Roblox | RDC 2021

[Watch](https://youtu.be/TLRakuu4vd0) | RobloxCreators | 2,795 views | 2021-12-01

## Summary
Roblox RDC 2021 talk by UndoneBuilder (Nathan Clemens, Simple Games Inc.) on scaling a Roblox business: tax accountants, forming an LLC/S corp instead of a company partnership, contracts, time tracking, avoiding burnout via purchased assets, hiring outside Roblox, charity giving, and the impact of your work. Mostly business/operations advice, not game-design specifics.

## Tips
- [1:50](https://youtu.be/TLRakuu4vd0?t=110) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Hire a tax accountant early; the speaker's team overpaid ~$3,000 filing themselves, and the accountant found a refund instead.
- [3:12](https://youtu.be/TLRakuu4vd0?t=192) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Form an LLC (under $100 in his US state) for liability protection and tax benefits; consider an S corp to avoid 15.3% self-employment tax.
- [4:34](https://youtu.be/TLRakuu4vd0?t=274) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not form a company partnership. If partnering, each person forms their own LLC/S corp and signs a memorandum of understanding defining ownership and responsibilities.
- [6:36](https://youtu.be/TLRakuu4vd0?t=396) **[[team-and-budget]]** Get every contract and agreement in writing, even if it is informal, to avoid trouble when partners or staff diverge.
- [7:53](https://youtu.be/TLRakuu4vd0?t=473) **[[team-and-budget]]** Track how your team spends time on projects (he uses Toggl-style free tracking); this helps planning, updates and accounting.
- [8:33](https://youtu.be/TLRakuu4vd0?t=513) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use purchased or free marketplace assets to save time and avoid burnout; players did not complain about it in ~10 years.
- [9:13](https://youtu.be/TLRakuu4vd0?t=553) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Useful asset sources: Unity Asset Store (needs conversion), RoDev Market (Roblox-made), game-icons.net for icons, Zapsplat for sound effects. Credit creators.
- [10:35](https://youtu.be/TLRakuu4vd0?t=635) **[[team-and-budget]]** Hire outside Roblox communities: UI, 2D and 3D art skills transfer easily, so search Fiverr and LinkedIn too.
- [11:23](https://youtu.be/TLRakuu4vd0?t=683) **[[community-and-discord]]** Donate 5-10% of income to charity; he says it engages players and players spend more in games that do good (claimed, not measured).
