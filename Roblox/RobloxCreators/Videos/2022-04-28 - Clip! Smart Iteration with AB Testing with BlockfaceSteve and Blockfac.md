## Summary
Short clip on evaluating social features in Roblox games via A/B testing. Split users 50/50 into control (no social feature) and test (with feature), compare week-long retention, spend, session length and sessions. Cites a sprint-button test that cut day-one retention despite seeming useful.

## Tips
- [0:05](https://youtu.be/5N84f1ZuwRc?t=5) **[[analytics-metrics]]** A/B test each core feature: 50% control without the feature, 50% with it, then compare results over one week.
- [0:05](https://youtu.be/5N84f1ZuwRc?t=5) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Measure retention, money spent, time played and session count per group to judge whether a feature helps.
- [0:47](https://youtu.be/5N84f1ZuwRc?t=47) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Don't assume a useful-seeming feature helps; a sprint button cut day-one retention, possibly by reducing social interaction or speeding exploration.

## Numbers
