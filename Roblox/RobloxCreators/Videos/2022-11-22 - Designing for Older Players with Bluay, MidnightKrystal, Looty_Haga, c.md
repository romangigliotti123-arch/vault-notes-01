# Designing for Older Players with Bluay, MidnightKrystal, Looty_Haga, cbmaximillian, & 101airsoft

[Watch](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU) | RobloxCreators | 1,539 views | 2022-11-22

## Summary
Roblox Level Up panel (Nov 2022) on designing games for older players (13+). Panelists from Arsenal/Rolf, Looty (ruin), and Front Lines discuss opt-in tutorials, skill mastery curves, layered complexity, avoiding pay-to-win, balance via consistent weapon order, performance costs of high fidelity, and community feedback filtering.

## Tips
- [9:41](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=581) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make tutorials opt-in for older players; older players skip mandatory tutorials and quit when controls differ from expectations. Offer a toggle to turn off objective tooltips.
- [13:06](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=786) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build direct skill-based competition (aim, precise movement) for older audiences rather than click-to-level mechanics.
- [18:06](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=1086) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Layer a rich meta system with resources to manage on top of simple inputs, so players can optimise reward pacing.
- [18:48](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=1128) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Keep the mastery curve high; if it's too low, players feel they've mastered the game quickly and leave.
- [19:53](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=1193) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Simulators that win on easy pickup often fade; add a next layer of complexity so older players keep progressing.
- [21:14](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=1274) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Balance high skill ceiling against new players; veteran dominance can drive newcomers away, so plan matchmaking or skill separation.
- [21:54](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=1314) **[[common-mistakes]]** Older audiences notice quality issues (blurry textures, clunky movement, low FPS); prioritise polish and performance.
- [25:16](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=1516) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Support emergent, silly play (trick shots, unintended mechanics) and treat them as features if fun, bugs if not.
- [28:41](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=1721) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Pick one target audience rather than trying to appeal to everyone; broad design spreads small-team resources thin.
- [30:58](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=1858) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Building a game you personally want to play is a valid starting point; GTA-style 'forbidden fruit' also draws younger players.
- [35:06](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=2106) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Give players in-game graphics toggles to disable heavy props and lighting, letting them trade visuals for performance.
- [35:49](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=2149) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Write code efficiently; small inefficiencies add up across many complex systems and hurt FPS.
- [38:13](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=2293) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Avoid pay-to-win; sell cosmetics and let players earn competitive items, since paid advantages cause community frustration.
- [39:37](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=2377) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Older players dislike pay-to-win weapons and want them removed; younger players see them as a reason to buy.
- [40:17](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=2417) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Provide simple tools that have hidden depth to discover and master; easy to play, difficult to master.
- [45:06](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=2706) **[[monetisation-other]]** Let players trade time for money (e.g. paying Robux to unlock weapons faster) while still letting free players progress.
- [47:50](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=2870) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make players spend out of enjoyment, not necessity; forced spending makes players feel they cannot compete.
- [50:54](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=3054) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Ensure most players get at least some success; guarantee a floor such as a kill-streak reward to avoid one-sided matches.
- [52:15](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=3135) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give everyone the same random weapon order in RNG modes so bad pulls feel fair; older players value consistency.
- [53:38](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=3218) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Keep early leveling fast (e.g. two kills per level) and avoid multi-day level gaps that bore players.
- [55:00](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=3300) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Tweak weapons to fit playstyles rather than raw power; baseline weapons should compete with later unlocks to prevent power creep.
- [57:18](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=3438) **[[community-and-discord]]** Run a community workshop where players submit weapon and character ideas; hire talented submitters onto the team.
- [58:39](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=3519) **[[community-and-discord]]** Collect feedback from many channels but filter for engaged community members; a vocal minority may not represent the player base.

## Games
- **Arsenal** [1:08](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=68): Fast, easy-to-pick-up shooter with RNG weapon order kept identical across players; high aim skill ceiling keeps veterans engaged.
- **Front Lines** [4:16](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=256): Pivoted from realistic Operation Scorpion to fast arcade shooter after frustration with spawns and slow pacing; FPS and performance are top criticisms.
- **Ruin (Looty)** [8:54](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=534): Diablo-style PvP action RPG; in development. Panel noted it would be too complex for very young players.
- **Team Fortress 2** [25:57](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=1557): Cartoon style and silly emergent mechanics (rocket jumps, market gardening) appeal across ages.
- **Counter-Strike** [41:38](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=2498): Unintended bunny-hop mechanic added a layer of skill that players learned to exploit.
- **Diablo Immortal** [44:25](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=2665): Extreme spend-based progression alienates players; cited as a cautionary example.
- **Call of Duty Warzone** [16:03](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=963): Advanced movement and respawn systems imply older audience; kill-streak floor ensures everyone can contribute.
- **Lego Star Wars** [26:38](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=1598): Multi-layered game with a simultaneous playground experience for young and older players.
