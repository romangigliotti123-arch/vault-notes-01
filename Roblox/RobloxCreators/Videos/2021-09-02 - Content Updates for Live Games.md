# Content Updates for Live Games

[Watch](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os) | RobloxCreators | 2,764 views | 2021-09-02

## Summary
Level Up workshop on content cadence for live Roblox games: what cadence releases are, why they matter, which content types are cheap (art-heavy, reuse existing systems), packaging around the core loop, protecting the economy, and a production pipeline with roadmaps, sprints, and request/string templates, plus promotion and retrospectives.

## Tips
- [1:47](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=107) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Cadence releases should only use existing systems; add new features, currencies or mechanics in major updates first, not in cadence drops.
- [4:38](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=278) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Release content at least monthly; weekly or biweekly if the team can manage it, to keep players' attention.
- [5:22](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=322) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Theme each release; a theme box makes ideation easier and makes marketing and player understanding simpler.
- [6:03](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=363) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Save seasonal ideas for their right time (e.g. no snowmobile in July) so each release has maximum impact.
- [9:20](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=560) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Time-box cadence development and stick to it so releases stay small and major updates still get time.
- [10:00](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=600) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Keep cadence releases primarily art-based (reskins, recolors, re-themed existing assets); art is the easiest part to outsource.
- [10:47](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=647) **[[community-and-discord]]** Source cadence content from fans via contests; recognise or pay creators so they don't feel exploited.
- [14:17](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=857) **[[team-and-budget]]** Small teams can lean on engaged community members to produce cadence content and reduce their workload.
- [12:55](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=775) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Mix permanent cadence content (long tail) with limited-time releases, which energise players and support monetisation.
- [13:35](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=815) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use metrics and player feedback to spot fatigue: falling purchases or quest completion across several releases signals a pullback.
- [17:32](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=1052) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Keep packages small and focused on items players want and will buy multiples of (e.g. cosmetics, prestige items, not one-off cars).
- [18:55](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=1135) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Choose cadence content that supports your core loop (quests, combat, exploration, customization) and identify gaps from it.
- [19:25](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=1165) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Keep tech requests small: prefer tech-light, art-heavy items like furniture and avatar cosmetics; avoid scripted encounters and raids.
- [21:03](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=1263) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Reuse assets: recolor models, reuse rigs and animations for similarly shaped models, or tweak geometry for a new silhouette.
- [22:26](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=1346) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Bring back event or seasonal sale items later; most players will not have owned them yet.
- [25:28](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=1528) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Avoid unlimited resource sources (Adopt Me money tree printed $5/10min, later capped at $100/day); build in limits and track inflation.
- [26:09](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=1569) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Release sink items to pull inflated resources out of inventories; make sure new items differ enough to avoid cannibalising old sales.
- [27:30](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=1650) **[[monetisation-other]]** Decide per cadence item whether it is free, soft-currency, or Robux; a mix covers both paying and non-paying players.
- [28:11](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=1691) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Don't spend more than two to three weeks on a single cadence release; larger impactful releases deserve the time.
- [30:27](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=1827) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Define an asset pipeline with clear owners and handoffs at each stage: design, concept, production, approval, implementation, testing.
- [35:04](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=2104) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan roadmaps at least three months out, using one-to-four-week sprints with built-in iteration and polish time.
- [42:09](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=2529) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use string and asset request templates; use variables for numbers so rebalancing doesn't need re-localisation.
- [46:46](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=2806) **[[community-and-discord]]** Tease releases with images, silhouettes and hints; then post release-day messages and ask players what they want next.
- [49:03](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=2943) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Run a retrospective after each release and compare DAU and payer conversion against previous releases to guide future themes and timing.

## Numbers
- [1:07](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=67) Content drop frequency: **Every few weeks (Adopt Me pets, Bloxburg furniture); at least monthly, ideally weekly or biweekly**
- [28:11](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=1691) Max time per cadence release: **2-3 weeks**
- [25:28](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=1528) Adopt Me money tree cap: **Originally $5 per 10 minutes unlimited; later capped at $100 per day**
- [36:25](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=2185) Sprint length: **1-4 weeks**
- [35:04](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=2104) Roadmap planning horizon: **At least 3 months out**
- [37:41](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=2261) Example cadence timeline: **Asset design to live roughly 3 weeks; next package designed while current is in production**

## Games
- **Adopt Me** [3:05 / 25:28 / 21:03](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=0): New pets added regularly; money tree once gave unlimited cash, causing inflation, so a daily cap was added.
- **Welcome to Bloxburg** [1:07](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=67): Regular furniture drops every few weeks as cadence content.
- **Epic Golf** [3:05](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=185): Regularly released playable course maps as cadence content.
- **Arsenal** [3:46](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=226): Regularly released first-person shooter maps as cadence content.
- **Super Striker League** [3:46](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=226): Stadium releases as cadence content.
- **Piggy** [21:45](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=1305): A skin modified with wings and a halo shows minor geometry changes make a new-feeling item.
