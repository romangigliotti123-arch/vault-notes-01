# What are onboarding techniques on Roblox?

[Watch](https://youtu.be/dXqZC4x2Vn8) | RobloxCreators | 24,895 views | 2023-12-06

## Summary
Explains onboarding funnel (FTUE) and three onboarding techniques for Roblox games: visual elements (subtle directional trails to overt UI highlights), contextual just-in-time tutorials, and timed hints for struggling players. Uses Hello Kitty Cafe and Squishmallows as examples.

## Tips
- [0:00](https://youtu.be/dXqZC4x2Vn8?t=0) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Think of onboarding as a funnel: most players enter, and the goal is to minimise drop-off between starting play and completing the first-time user experience (FTUE).
- [0:44](https://youtu.be/dXqZC4x2Vn8?t=44) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Use visual elements like directional trails to guide players subtly, while still letting them choose their own path; use overt UI highlights where needed.
- [1:23](https://youtu.be/dXqZC4x2Vn8?t=83) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Trigger contextual tutorials during natural play (e.g. entering a new zone, getting a new item) rather than front-loading everything into onboarding; this shortens onboarding and aids recall.
- [1:23](https://youtu.be/dXqZC4x2Vn8?t=83) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Show a feature's tutorial when it is relevant, e.g. when a player is about two-thirds of the way to unlocking it.
- [2:07](https://youtu.be/dXqZC4x2Vn8?t=127) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Add timed hints for players who take longer than most to finish a task, so help reaches struggling players without annoying others.
- [2:07](https://youtu.be/dXqZC4x2Vn8?t=127) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Find struggling players by timing how long most players take each onboarding task and flagging the slow tail.
