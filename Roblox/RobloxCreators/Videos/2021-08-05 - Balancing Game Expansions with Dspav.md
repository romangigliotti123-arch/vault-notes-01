# Balancing Game Expansions with Dspav

[Watch](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs) | RobloxCreators | 2,136 views | 2021-08-05

## Summary
Talk on balancing game expansions/features for live Roblox-style games: plan balance goals with team (engagement frequency, economy impact, content duration), playtest with average, casual and highly engaged player personas, use currency/resource sinks and caps, favour buffs over nerfs, mix intuition with data-driven design, and level-gate content.

## Tips
- [9:09](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=549) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Before balancing, define how often players engage with a feature (daily, per session, weekly) and how long it should last.
- [12:55](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=775) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Playtest as three personas: average player, casual player (is it approachable?), and highly engaged player (is it durable?).
- [13:25](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=805) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Set analytics and feature funnels before closed beta so you can track how far players get into the feature.
- [17:09](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=1029) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add currency or resource sinks (repeatable spend items) to pull inflated economies back toward balance over time.
- [18:32](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=1112) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Cap currencies and resources (e.g. 99 wood) so players spend rather than hoard and you can require more in future content.
- [29:18](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=1758) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Make new content slightly too hard at launch and ease it later; players accept nerfs to difficulty better than sudden increases.
- [30:41](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=1841) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Buff underused items rather than nerfing popular ones; nerfs after heavy investment cause strong player backlash.
- [31:22](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=1882) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Act on economy-threatening features even if players object; remove, fix and re-release if long-term game health is at risk.
- [32:27](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=1947) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Prefer intuitive design over tutorial text; players skip reading, so show what to do instead of telling them.
- [32:27](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=1947) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Level-gate new mechanics and layer complexity over time so players have goals and aren't overwhelmed.
- [35:53](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=2153) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid rebirth systems that trivialise progression; time-based level challenge keeps high-level players' status meaningful.
- [22:38](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=1358) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Target one audience per feature; a feature meant to be hardcore shouldn't be dumbed down to attract new players.
- [21:17](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=1277) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Rotate content across player motivations (killers, explorers, achievers, socializers) so no group feels abandoned.
- [04:21](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=261) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Consider how a feature changes the economy: what resources it creates or sinks and which existing systems it affects.
- [15:07](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=907) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Encourage testers to try to break the system and log bugs and unexpected optimal strategies.
- [25:42](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=1542) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Balance in a mix of intuition and data: build from your vision, tune with average-player data, refine via playtests, then ship with analytics.
- [03:10](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=190) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build sustainable, evergreen content; racing players to create content faster than they consume it is a losing battle.
- [02:29](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=149) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make rewards effortful; exclusive items that take hours to obtain validate effort and advertise the feature to others.
