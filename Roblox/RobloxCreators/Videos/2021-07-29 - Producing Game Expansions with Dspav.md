# Producing Game Expansions with Dspav

[Watch](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc) | RobloxCreators | 3,171 views | 2021-07-29

## Summary
Production overview for Roblox expansions: why production matters (crunch avoidance, identifying design flaws, roadmaps), steps from brainstorming through MVP, task breakdown and costing (assume 6-hour workdays), weekly playtests and meetings, iteration vs feature creep, pre/post-launch hype, launch-day analytics, and a first bug-fix release. Includes Q&A on influencer attention, community feedback, playtester recruitment, and documentation tools.

## Tips
- [1:59](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=119) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Write down every feature detail before building; finding design gaps in week one beats finding them in month three.
- [6:02](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=362) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan two releases ahead and sketch what happens two years out, using a whiteboard or similar, to keep the vision focused.
- [8:07](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=487) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Always bring a solution when raising a problem; critique ideas, never people.
- [10:40](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=640) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Ship a minimum viable version of the expansion first, then iterate on player feedback instead of chasing perfection.
- [12:20](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=740) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Break the feature into tasks (programming, art, UI, design, data) in a tool like Trello or Jira, with owners and dependencies.
- [13:23](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=803) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Estimate task time assuming six productive hours per 8-hour day, and include iteration and testing time in estimates.
- [14:55](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=895) **[[team-and-budget]]** Delay release rather than force crunch when progress lags; team health matters more than the date.
- [15:36](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=936) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Get a playable prototype early and run weekly team playtests with set focus areas and captured bugs.
- [17:49](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=1069) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Reassess the release date when too many changes pile up; prioritize feedback by cost and value.
- [19:12](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=1152) **[[community-and-discord]]** Preview upcoming features to players and track their reactions to guide pivots before launch.
- [20:34](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=1234) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Tease expansions with pre/post-launch events using systems already in the game, such as themed cosmetics or countdown timers; avoid new tech for teasers.
- [21:11](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=1271) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** When the update ships, refresh the thumbnail and landing page with recognizable iconography so players recognize the game.
- [22:08](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=1328) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Implement analytics before launch and watch feature engagement and drop-off points on launch day to catch game-breaking flows.
- [22:48](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=1368) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Join players in-game on launch day to find issues early and build viral moments from developer presence on servers.
- [23:29](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=1409) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Make the first post-launch release a dedicated bug-fix and polish update, ideally a full week, to show players you care.
- [26:04](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=1564) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Release when the feature is ready, not when influencer attention spikes; the first launch impression matters most.
- [27:25](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=1645) **[[community-and-discord]]** Value community feedback but keep final design control; implement the spirit of a player idea in your own way.
- [28:07](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=1687) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Recruit playtesters from passionate Discord members, or from Roblox QA Discord groups when community buzz is low.

## Numbers
- [14:03](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=843) Productive hours per workday for estimation: **6 hours (of an 8-hour day)** (Speaker's advice for costing tasks)
- [24:10](https://youtu.be/HKLRLhHHHVc?t=1450) Post-launch bug-fix release duration: **1 week** (Dedicated week to squash bugs after first release)
