# What are engagement metrics on Roblox?

[Watch](https://youtu.be/N2l0BDgSNtQ) | RobloxCreators | 21,230 views | 2023-09-06

## Summary
Explains Roblox engagement metrics: daily active users, monthly active users, and average session length. Recommends diagnosing engagement drops by checking core loop interaction, friction points, and when declines started. Walks through a hypothetical "Cat Police" game where a fish-head bottleneck caused low engagement and proposes fixes.

## Tips
- [0:08](https://youtu.be/N2l0BDgSNtQ?t=8) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track daily active users (DAU), monthly active users (MAU), and average session length as core engagement health metrics; higher DAU/MAU is better.
- [0:08](https://youtu.be/N2l0BDgSNtQ?t=8) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use average session length to size content so most players can finish a meaningful chunk within a typical play session.
- [0:50](https://youtu.be/N2l0BDgSNtQ?t=50) **[[analytics-metrics]]** When engagement drops, ask: how are players interacting with the core loop, where do they quit (difficulty, missing info, technical issues), and when did the decline start.
- [0:50](https://youtu.be/N2l0BDgSNtQ?t=50) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Recent engagement decline suggests lack of new content; persistently low engagement suggests the core loop or primary features are missing something.
- [1:21](https://youtu.be/N2l0BDgSNtQ?t=81) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Find the bottleneck where players get stuck in the core loop (e.g. resource gathering too hard) and fix it by lowering the cost or increasing the resource supply.

## Games
