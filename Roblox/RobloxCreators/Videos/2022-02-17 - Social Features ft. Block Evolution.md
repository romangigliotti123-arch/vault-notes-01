# Social Features ft. Block Evolution

[Watch](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc) | RobloxCreators | 2,100 views | 2022-02-17

## Summary
Level Up roundtable with Block Evolution (Club Roblox) on social features: player density, party servers, social hooks like typing bubbles, teleport-to-friend, nearby tab, outfit sharing, trading, A/B testing social features, and weekly Friday updates. Mostly design insight with some concrete practices.

## Tips
- [8:15](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=495) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Aggregate players into dense groups (parties/private servers) since a few 'nucleation' players per server spark snowballing social interaction.
- [14:31](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=871) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship a major update on a fixed weekly schedule (they did Fridays for 2+ years) and supplement with midweek fixes or content.
- [15:14](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=914) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Design for social play: they report ~30% better retention for players who play socially versus solo.
- [13:31](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=811) **[[common-mistakes]]** Show social cues like typing dots and an 'in menu' book animation so players know if others are available to talk.
- [18:04](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=1084) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build multi-step, collaborative systems (e.g. pizza production line) where players depend on each other for rewards.
- [21:30](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=1290) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Reward players for initiating social acts, e.g. a task for completing a trade with another player, to spark interaction.
- [21:30](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=1290) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Add a one-tap emote/remote button for mobile and tablet users who avoid text chat.
- [27:36](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=1656) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** A/B test each social feature with 50/50 splits over a week, comparing retention, spend and sessions against a control.
- [27:36](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=1656) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check day-one retention after shipping features; a sprint button cut D1 retention because it reduced social encounters.
- [28:57](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=1737) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Split new and experienced players into separate worlds or protect them, or newcomers get griefed and quit.
- [34:27](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=2067) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add a Friends/Nearby social menu with teleport-to-friend so separated players can reunite quickly in multi-place games.
- [37:30](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=2250) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Add a Nearby tab listing server players with one-click friend requests for people who can't or won't use the website friend flow.
- [40:11](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=2411) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Use bubble chat above avatars in large worlds so messages feel directed and players can see who is talking.
- [46:39](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=2799) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Create bottlenecks and compact hangout spaces (e.g. dome-shaped town hub) so players congregate instead of spreading thin.
- [48:07](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=2887) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Pick social activities by how players interact with them (multi-step, breakable, customizable), not by the theme idea.
- [49:56](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=2996) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Let players save outfits to mannequins they can share, enabling role-play outfit rooms and social customization.
- [50:51](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=3051) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make items tradeable (not currency) to incentivise player-to-player interaction; limit currency trades for economy control.
- [53:32](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=3212) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Get creative with social hooks; surprising abstract activities (pizza factory) beat generic friend menus for engagement.
- [56:35](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=3395) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Keep updates simple with two or three goals; their most complex updates were often least well received.
- [57:16](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=3436) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid overextending with too many interconnected worlds; players got lost, wandered and left.
- [58:50](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=3530) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Benchmark social games at 10+ minute sessions, but prioritise retention over session length for young casual audiences.
- [59:40](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=3580) **[[community-and-discord]]** Use Twitter/Instagram to gather player feedback on updates, bugs and confusion, not just for marketing.
- [26:15](https://youtu.be/BkQL2_DY9Mc?t=1575) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Design environmental and social cues so players can learn without relying on other players to teach them.
