# Writing Quests, Achievements & Dailies with BreakfastCandy

[Watch](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc) | RobloxCreators | 1,500 views | 2021-11-11

## Summary
Level Up workshop part 3 by BreakfastCandy on writing quest text, achievements and dailies. Covers concise, consistent, accurate, precise, immersive text; glossary of verbs/nouns; variety via task wording (buy/find/collect/trade/get/have); building a farming-game quest set with dailies and a themed quest line; Q&A on hard currency rewards and localization idioms.

## Tips
- [0:57](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=57) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Use numerals instead of words for numbers; set character limits matching your UI and stick to them; write basic task text first so key info survives flavor edits.
- [1:37](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=97) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Limit dialogue to three or four taps per sequence as a general rule; adjust to your game's player patience.
- [1:55](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=115) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Keep a glossary of standard verbs and nouns (e.g. crop as noun, plant as verb) and apply it across quests, shop and social posts.
- [3:56](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=236) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Document each quest's intended behavior, including its trigger, so playtests and bug reports can be checked against a record.
- [4:37](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=277) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Use variables in quest text instead of hard-coded values, sourcing numbers from the quest balance spreadsheet to avoid re-editing and relocalizing text.
- [4:57](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=297) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Be precise in task wording (e.g. 'drink freshwater' not 'drink water'); specificity also creates variant tasks with different difficulty.
- [6:20](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=380) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Phrase requirements on the outcome (5 tomatoes) rather than attempts (harvest 5 times) when drops are random, to avoid unfair grind.
- [8:32](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=512) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Avoid quest tasks that require hard currency or Robux spend; prefer soft currency and check per-player wallet analytics before setting amounts.
- [9:53](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=593) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Rate task difficulty by inherent action difficulty and social dependency; trade tasks are harder because they need other players online.
- [10:34](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=634) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Use 'have' or 'get' task wording to credit items players already own, rewarding work already done.
- [11:38](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=698) **[[common-mistakes]]** Do not sacrifice clarity for theme; flavorful text is pointless if players cannot understand the instruction.
- [13:00](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=780) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Define a consistent game voice (excitable vs dry, wacky vs serious) and apply it to all text including UI.
- [14:05](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=845) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Avoid walls of text; use rich text highlights, embedded icons and character limits, and keep minimum font size legible.
- [14:47](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=887) **[[team-and-budget]]** Leave room in UI for localization; German needs significantly more characters than English, so set limits with space for longer strings.
- [15:13](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=913) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Use quest titles for task statements (achievements), progress tracking (Part one of three), or flavor and puns, since titles sit a step removed from content.
- [16:31](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=991) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Deliver story via NPC dialogue pop-ups that bookend a quest line: introduce the story at start and wrap it up on completion.
- [13:47](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=827) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Have quest-giver NPCs deliver quests in their own voice and perspective to make otherwise dull tasks more personal.
- [18:16](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=1096) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build the task list methodically: enumerate every plantable, harvestable, feedable and craftable item so each content piece has a quest task.
- [19:38](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=1178) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Check that harvested items feed your crafting system before writing quests, so you don't paint yourself into a corner without ingredients.
- [23:33](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=1413) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build dailies from easy generic tasks (plant fruit, harvest any crop, throw a party) and medium tasks, avoiding hard ones so players finish within a session.
- [26:08](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=1568) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Choose social tasks like throwing a party that count even on empty servers so solo players are not blocked.
- [34:44](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=2084) **[[monetisation-other]]** Reward small amounts of currency in dailies so it adds up and drives daily engagement; reward larger hard-currency amounts only for long achievements.
- [36:08](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=2168) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Know how long achievements take players to complete, e.g. currency needed for a big purchase divided by earn rate, before setting rewards.
- [37:28](https://youtu.be/iWSZJSE2NEc?t=2248) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid idioms that won't translate; check your player base's country mix before using region-specific language.
