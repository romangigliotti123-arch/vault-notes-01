# Clip! Marketing New Games on Roblox with KreekCraft & WonderWorksZach

[Watch](https://youtu.be/vNKPlYeXj0Q) | RobloxCreators | 2,230 views | 2023-04-27

## Summary
Short clip-style chat between KreekCraft and WonderWorksZach on promoting Roblox games cheaply before release. Covers speculation content (puzzles, hints, wishlist bait), slow-building Twitter hashtags, trailers from gameplay captures, teasing in a few-month window, and leaks as free hype, with a warning about overhyping and raising expectations.

## Tips
- [0:05](https://youtu.be/vNKPlYeXj0Q?t=5) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Prerelease hype is a cheap way to promote; build it with speculation content rather than paid spend.
- [0:46](https://youtu.be/vNKPlYeXj0Q?t=46) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Post cryptic puzzles or secret-account teasers so players speculate and share, generating free buzz.
- [0:46](https://youtu.be/vNKPlYeXj0Q?t=46) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Ask the community for wishlist content, then add those items later; players credit the dev for hitting their wishes.
- [1:26](https://youtu.be/vNKPlYeXj0Q?t=86) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Slow-build a game-specific Twitter hashtag before release so an active community exists to amplify launch.
- [1:26](https://youtu.be/vNKPlYeXj0Q?t=86) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Capture gameplay footage (Shift+P or Ctrl+P) for a trailer; cheap showcase material is enough.
- [2:07](https://youtu.be/vNKPlYeXj0Q?t=127) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid overhyping; high prerelease expectations make an MVP-then-iterate release harder to pull off.
- [2:07](https://youtu.be/vNKPlYeXj0Q?t=127) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Start teasing a few months before release, drip small teasers every few days, escalate toward launch; don't tease 6-8 months out.
- [2:49](https://youtu.be/vNKPlYeXj0Q?t=169) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Leaks from game data drive discussion; devs can accept leaked content as viral marketing rather than fight it.

## Numbers
