## Summary
Brave Turtles founder Kevin Kim explains building and launching Rift Hunters, a Roblox shooter (13+, free-to-play). Covers pre-development research, Dev Relations outreach, a 7-month alpha plus 2-month polish, community-first launch, UGC event exploit and retention lessons, and balancing with target-audience playtests.

## Tips
- [8:00](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=480) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Study top Roblox games and pick a target demographic (they aimed at ages 13-24) before choosing genre and business model.
- [11:38](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=698) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Pick a genre players already play on Roblox but leave for other platforms, then differentiate: they chose shooters with a more imaginative, easier-going style than realistic rivals.
- [10:09](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=609) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Contact the Roblox Dev Relations team early; they gave playtests and feedback that helped the team.
- [10:49](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=649) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Set milestones: about 7 months to an alpha with core gameplay, then about 2 months of polish, bug fixing and balancing before launch.
- [23:34](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=1414) **[[community-and-discord]]** Build a Discord community before launch; initial users are hard to attract without community support. Start about a month before release, not much earlier.
- [24:57](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=1497) **[[common-mistakes]]** Before a UGC item event, prepare for exploiters: rename remote events and place bait coins in unreachable spots so cheaters auto-ban.
- [27:00](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=1620) **[[common-mistakes]]** UGC events bring many users who only collect items and do not spend, lowering long-term monetisation and recommendation metrics afterwards.
- [27:43](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=1663) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Playtest with your actual target audience, not other developers; regular Roblox players play differently and give different feedback.
- [28:25](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=1705) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Match Roblox's usual visual style for icons and thumbnails rather than going too unique; they say platform style fits better.
- [29:06](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=1746) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Focus on day-1 and day-7 retention and make difficulty match expectations; they say the Roblox algorithm drives home recommendation, the key to launch success.
- [30:31](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=1831) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Improving day-7 retention and playtime should lift monetisation and home-page recommendation, according to the speaker.
- [45:38](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=2738) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Budget about a month and a half for balancing a large-scope game; with small teams and budgets, design a smaller scope.
- [47:44](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=2864) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Add a shooting range where new players can try all weapons and masks; it teaches the upgrade system and gives a reason to return.
- [18:45](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=1125) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Sell unique weapons and abilities (masks that boost speed, jump or invisibility) plus a season battle pass with eight-week seasons.
- [19:32](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=1172) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Run a pre-launch countdown: post a daily short teaser across nine social accounts, and pair launch with a UGC item event from a partner.
- [33:53](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=2033) **[[team-and-budget]]** Do not build alone: share your vision with team members and assemble strong people; solo development is long and lonely.
