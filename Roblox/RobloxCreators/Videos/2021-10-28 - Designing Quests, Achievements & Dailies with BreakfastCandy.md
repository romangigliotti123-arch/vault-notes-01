# Designing Quests, Achievements & Dailies with BreakfastCandy

[Watch](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8) | RobloxCreators | 5,903 views | 2021-10-28

## Summary
Part two of a Roblox Developer Relations Level Up workshop on designing quests, achievements and dailies. Uses fictional farming game "Farmin' Fun" to show how to break systems into quest tasks, balance inherent difficulty and modifiers, pace quest chains, avoid social conflict and wasteful tasks, and keep the quest system fresh.

## Tips
- [0:05](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=5) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build quest tasks by listing every system and content item, then asking 'what can the player blank' with both generic and specific terms, so all systems get engagement.
- [4:23](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=263) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Group systems into categories and pick one task per category so no quest set touches the same system twice, giving variety.
- [5:04](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=304) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Respect player time: long tasks suit achievements, not dailies; keep most time on the fun part rather than travel or timers.
- [5:45](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=345) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't force irregular gameplay (e.g. specific decorations, placing or finishing in a way that hurts fun); give adequate rewards when you do ask extra effort.
- [6:26](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=386) **[[monetisation-other]]** Be careful forcing hard-currency spend or wasting resources (powerups, crafting ingredients); players want to spend on their own goals, compensate if you force it.
- [7:07](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=427) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid quests that create social conflict, e.g. 'join a group 5 times' encourages join-leave griefing; design so cooperation is genuine.
- [9:10](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=550) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Tune difficulty via modifiers: quantity, level gates, per-session caps, timers, time limits and cooldowns; generic tasks are easier than specific ones.
- [10:33](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=633) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Pace quests with breathing room between high-challenge periods; overwhelmed players burn out, leave, and take friends with them.
- [11:14](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=674) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Don't spike quest difficulty to drive short-term spend: a speaker's game saw profit rise then casual players quit, leaving hardcore players who then left too.
- [12:05](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=725) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Keep difficulty fair: changes should be within player control (level up, gear, strategy) and rewards commensurate with effort.
- [12:47](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=767) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Target the player: quests simple for veterans (e.g. finding hidden items in unfamiliar maps) frustrate new players; avoid overly clever hiding spots.
- [13:29](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=809) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Compare average quest completion time with your average session length; dailies should not consume a player's whole session.
- [14:09](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=849) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Avoid quests needing literal hours; for collection quests, hide more items than required so the last few aren't tedious.
- [14:49](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=889) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Handle one-time tasks carefully: let prior accomplishments count or allow repeats so players are not stuck on a quest they can't redo.
- [15:30](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=930) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Playtest with others; you are always easier than average players, so use testers to gauge real effort required.
- [16:11](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=971) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Reserve very difficult or long tasks for achievements (Shark Bite example: unlock expensive high-level boats) and give short-term goals early for a sense of progress.
- [17:44](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=1064) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Shape quest chains like a story arc: gradually increase effort to peaks, vary peak frequency and intensity, and end each chain on a satisfying break.
- [18:26](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=1106) **[[updates-live-ops]]** For limited-time quests, ensure players have a realistic chance to finish on time; don't block the end with unreasonable requirements.
- [19:07](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=1147) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Allow min-maxing: let players progress multiple quests at once with one action (e.g. planting wheat seeds that count for both a plant and a harvest quest).
- [19:48](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=1188) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Chain quests so one sets up the next (gather ingredients, then bake a cake), so earlier effort pays off.
- [20:01](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=1201) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Limit active quests per player or let them choose when to start new quests, and space quest events so players can recover.
- [20:42](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=1242) **[[common-mistakes]]** Provide quest management tools: hide all but the current quest or delete unwanted quests when you have many quests.
- [20:52](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=1252) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Refresh dailies regularly so players aren't repeating the same task each day; add quests for new systems, currencies and consumables.
- [21:31](https://youtu.be/1stxDQeHhr8?t=1291) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Tailor quests to player cohorts by player level so players meet quests when they have the skill and knowledge for them.
