# Driving Discovery on Roblox | RDC 2021

[Watch](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU) | RobloxCreators | 764 views | 2021-12-01

## Summary
Roblox RDC 2021 talk by Aman Thakur (search & discovery product) and Richard Sim (commerce). Explains the shift from popularity-based discovery to personalized recommendations (four signal categories), how to improve title/thumbnail/description appeal, planned metadata and analytics, and the sponsored experiences ad system: targeting, daily-budget-as-bid, reporting metrics, and funnel data showing sponsored games perform better.

## Tips
- [12:17](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=737) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Avoid emojis and special characters in titles; users search by typed name, so keep the title clearly readable and spellable for your target age group.
- [13:38](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=818) **[[thumbnails-icons]]** Pick a thumbnail that represents the game accurately; the title and image set expectations, and a mismatch with the gameplay loop hurts engagement after the click.
- [14:20](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=860) **[[titles-descriptions]]** Write informative descriptions that sell the game's unique characteristics; players read them only after clicking the tile, so use them to convert the click into a play session.
- [8:54](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=534) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Recommendations are personalized by demographics, content preferences, friends' activity and recent engagement; popularity alone no longer guarantees placement.
- [10:58](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=658) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Optimise for converting impressions into real engagement, not raw clicks; both reaching the right audience and the game's appeal drive ranking.
- [25:15](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=1515) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Use sponsored ad targeting (gender, age, platform) to learn which audiences engage most, since every engagement also feeds organic ranking.
- [25:55](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=1555) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Sponsored ads run 1 to 28 days with a daily Robux budget that acts as the bid; the budget largely determines impression share.
- [21:33](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=1293) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Compared with mobile stores where cost per install is about $2 to $3.50, Roblox sponsored plays cost pennies, making paid discovery cheap to test.
- [28:00](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=1680) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track impressions, clicks, CTR, cost per click, attributed visits (post-click and post-impression), conversion rate and cost per play in sponsored reporting.
- [5:42](https://youtu.be/DHvfEB8myyU?t=342) **[[discovery-algorithm]]** Concurrent players and ratings were the old popularity levers; under personalization, focus on the appeal and click-to-play conversion of your tile.
