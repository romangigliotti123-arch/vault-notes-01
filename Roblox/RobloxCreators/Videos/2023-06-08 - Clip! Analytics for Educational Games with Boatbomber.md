# Clip! Analytics for Educational Games with Boatbomber

[Watch](https://youtu.be/pWF06iX9_Gg) | RobloxCreators | 1,180 views | 2023-06-08

## Summary
Boatbomber (developer of Clip!, an educational Roblox game) explains the analytics he tracks: time spent per section and device, per-lesson failure rates and attempts, lessons per session, and where players quit. He uses this data to iterate, and gives an example where rewording lessons with real-world metaphors lowered first-try failure rates.

## Tips
- [0:47](https://youtu.be/pWF06iX9_Gg?t=47) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track where time is spent by section and device to decide what to polish or rework, e.g. tutorials vs. a multiplayer section with little time spent.
- [1:29](https://youtu.be/pWF06iX9_Gg?t=89) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track per-lesson failure rates, average tries to pass, lessons completed per session, and where players leave, to find spots that are too hard or demotivating.
- [2:09](https://youtu.be/pWF06iX9_Gg?t=129) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Instrument detailed analytics for almost everything; without knowing what happens in the game you cannot know what to iterate on.
- [2:50](https://youtu.be/pWF06iX9_Gg?t=170) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** When a lesson has a very high first-try failure rate, the concept is likely poorly explained; reword it and re-measure the failure rate.
- [2:50](https://youtu.be/pWF06iX9_Gg?t=170) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Explain hard concepts with real-world metaphors; this lowered failure rates after rewording.
