## Summary
Panel with WonderWorks (Zach) and JoshWWS1 on social media vs Roblox ads in 2022. Sponsored tiles performance declined after the sort change; organic hype via influencer early access (Honey the Unicorn for Overlook) and Twitter puzzles/ciphers built audiences. Advice: build a studio brand that carries audience across experiences.

## Tips
- [0:47](https://youtu.be/HxqWoF_wfMQ?t=47) **[[ads-and-sponsored-experiences]]** Sponsored tiles lost effectiveness after Roblox put them in one sort instead of scattering them; use them more diligently rather than heavily.
- [1:27](https://youtu.be/HxqWoF_wfMQ?t=87) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Give a known creator early access to build organic hype; WonderWorks gave Honey the Unicorn three months early access to Overlook.
- [2:07](https://youtu.be/HxqWoF_wfMQ?t=127) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Grow a stealth Twitter account with hashtags and cool posts before revealing the studio; avoid announcing the brand too early.
- [2:07](https://youtu.be/HxqWoF_wfMQ?t=127) **[[community-and-discord]]** Move Twitter followers into a Discord or Guilded server so you can ping the community directly.
- [2:49](https://youtu.be/HxqWoF_wfMQ?t=169) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Run treasure-hunt style puzzles (ciphered images/codes) on Twitter; players spend hours decoding, which builds excitement and can reveal story or skin content.
- [2:49](https://youtu.be/HxqWoF_wfMQ?t=169) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Do not rely on a single platform because algorithms change; spread the audience across several platforms.
- [3:30](https://youtu.be/HxqWoF_wfMQ?t=210) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Build a studio brand even as a solo developer so the audience carries over from one experience to the next, compounding with each release and update.
