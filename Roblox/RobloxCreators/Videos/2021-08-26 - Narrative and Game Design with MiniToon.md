# Narrative and Game Design with MiniToon

[Watch](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0) | RobloxCreators | 13,494 views | 2021-08-26

## Summary
MiniToon (Piggy developer) Level Up Roundtable with Roblox's Dan Spedenta. Covers writing story-driven games: planning beginning and ending first, level design (gray-box in Studio, varied room looks), playtesting by trying to break the map, community engagement on Twitter/Discord, bi-weekly update cadence, cosmetics over pay-to-win, and analytics use.

## Tips
- [7:24](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=444) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Plan the beginning and ending of a story before writing the middle; MiniToon says this avoids struggling to wrap up later chapters.
- [12:41](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=761) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make sure a new player who jumps in mid-game can still understand the story; confusion leads to quitting immediately.
- [14:35](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=875) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Put essential story in main progression; hidden notes should only be extra lore for superfans, not required to understand the plot.
- [25:46](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=1546) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Give players a reason to replay story games, such as multiple endings, collectibles, or unlockable abilities, since linear stories get no replays.
- [30:55](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=1855) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Block out levels in grey parts in Studio and playtest the layout before adding art, so layout changes stay quick.
- [32:11](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=1931) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give each room a distinct color or props; repeated identical rooms confuse players and make them get lost.
- [33:52](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=2032) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Have developer friends try to break the map or escape it via glitches, then patch out-of-balance exploits before launch.
- [34:34](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=2074) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Run cheat speedrun playthroughs giving yourself every item to test progression and sequence breaks.
- [35:04](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=2104) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Expect to post-launch patch maps for glitches and difficulty spikes; treat it as normal iteration, not failure.
- [36:12](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=2172) **[[community-and-discord]]** Use polls to gauge what players want, but remember the game developer makes the final call.
- [42:39](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=2559) **[[community-and-discord]]** Engage with your community genuinely; supporters who feel heard keep promoting and playing the game.
- [46:13](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=2773) **[[community-and-discord]]** Keep community spaces positive and avoid drama; a safe space keeps players coming back.
- [49:02](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=2942) **[[community-and-discord]]** Run a Discord suggestions channel and upvote feature requests; implement popular ideas and treat reports as free QA.
- [50:27](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=3027) **[[community-and-discord]]** Let fan games and fan characters exist rather than policing them; fan works bring new players to your game.
- [52:54](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=3174) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Set update cadence to what retention can support; MiniToon moved from weekly to bi-weekly to protect his health and quality.
- [53:36](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=3216) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Alternate minor updates (features, items, abilities) with major chapter updates to spread workload.
- [1:10:52](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=4252) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** For younger audiences, avoid pay-to-win; sell cosmetics so monetisation doesn't drive away new players who get wrecked.
- [1:12:51](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=4371) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Switching between projects prevents burnout and builds a library; you also learn new skills from each project.
- [1:13:32](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=4412) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Ship small demos to test ideas before investing in a full game; demos reveal what sticks with less risk and allow more updates.
- [1:17:49](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=4669) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use analytics on specific levels played, levels escaped, and shop items bought; polls alone miss players who never use Discord or Twitter.
- [1:19:18](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=4758) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Do not rely only on default Roblox analytics; track level completion rates to balance difficulty.
- [1:22:14](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=4934) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Step away from the code or story with a walk when stuck; returning fresh resolves typos and writer's block faster.
- [1:08:00](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=4080) **[[team-and-budget]]** Find collaborators by joining communities of the genre you want to build; MiniToon met his core team in horror round-based game communities.
- [59:15](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=3555) **[[common-mistakes]]** Banning critics on a 200-CCU game was a mistake; handle criticism constructively and hear suggestions out.
