# UI/UX Essentials

[Watch](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4) | RobloxCreators | 5,326 views | 2023-08-25

## Summary
Level Up's UI/UX primer for Roblox games. Covers UI priorities (show what matters in the moment, contextual buttons), directing attention via color, size, space and proximity, building a consistent visual language and button conventions, text legibility, UX flows and user stories, known Roblox interaction conventions, and finding friction such as buying consumables one at a time.

## Tips
- [1:56](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=116) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Prioritize what you show: highest-priority UI is frequently used features and decision-driving info; cut everything else to reduce clutter.
- [2:37](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=157) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Use contextual buttons that swap based on game state (e.g. tackle vs. pass in Super Striker League) to reduce on-screen choices.
- [3:18](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=198) **[[common-mistakes]]** Make the most important element the brightest, most eye-catching thing on screen so players look at it first.
- [3:59](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=239) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Give premium pass rewards a bright gold highlight bar so the pass draws attention, as Jailbreak's season pass does.
- [3:59](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=239) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make the primary action (e.g. play button) large and colored; shrink less important buttons like settings to lower their visibility.
- [4:39](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=279) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** In shop UI, use empty space, size and color to make the largest, most valuable bundle stand out; add a best-value banner.
- [5:21](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=321) **[[common-mistakes]]** Group related items with close proximity and size differences so players instantly see which items belong together.
- [5:37](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=337) **[[common-mistakes]]** Limit the UI color palette to a handful of theme-fitting colors; too many colors makes the UI noisy and hard to scan.
- [6:18](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=378) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Follow color conventions: red for close/cancel, green for OK/accept. Ignoring conventions risks confusing players.
- [6:18](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=378) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Assign consistent meaning to colors across the game, e.g. the same green for a stat and its health bar in Arcane Odyssey.
- [7:00](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=420) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Give icons for the same stat a shared color and shape language so players learn meaning and tell them apart at a glance.
- [7:40](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=460) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Style buttons with a container, contrasting background and a highlight for depth so players recognize them as touchable; apply the style consistently.
- [8:21](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=501) **[[common-mistakes]]** Research existing genre and device conventions before designing controls, since players bring habits from other games.
- [8:21](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=501) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make headers larger and bolder than body text and use highly legible fonts with strong contrast; readability beats theme-matching fonts.
- [9:25](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=565) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Design UX around the actions and flow between choices, separate from visual styling; aim for interactions that are intuitive, unobtrusive and convenient.
- [10:42](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=642) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Define your target audience by age, experience, genre familiarity and play style, and let that drive mechanics and input precision demands.
- [11:22](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=682) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Write user stories narrating a hypothetical player's goals, actions, thoughts and emotions for each feature to guide intuitive design.
- [12:37](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=757) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Reuse familiar Roblox interactions, such as the E-to-interact proximity prompt, unless there is a compelling reason to change them.
- [13:18](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=798) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** For unfamiliar mechanics, pick a metaphor players already know (e.g. a deck of cards for spell casting in Spellbound) and prototype it with players.
- [14:40](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=880) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Support two ways to do the same action (e.g. tabs plus search, or two unequip methods) when playtests show players trying different methods.
- [15:20](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=920) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Hunt friction: allow multi-quantity purchases of consumables and cut lengthy animations or extra navigation between inventory and shop.
- [16:01](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=961) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Find friction via user stories, playtests and analytics; small UX fixes can raise engagement, monetization and player happiness.

## Games
- **The Survival Game** [1:56](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=116): Prioritizes map, crafting, health and hunger indicators since they are needed to keep surviving.
- **Super Striker League** [2:37](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=157): Contextual buttons switch between running/tackling and dodging/passing based on ball possession, cutting clutter.
- **Jailbreak** [3:18](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=198): Season pass uses a bright gold bar under premium-exclusive rewards to draw attention to the purchase.
- **Dragon Adventure** [4:39](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=279): Shop uses space, size and color to make the largest currency bundle with a best-value banner stand out.
- **Tower Defense Simulator** [5:21](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=321): Size and proximity visually group related items in the shop.
- **Arcane Odyssey** [6:18](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=378): Stats have distinct colors reused across the UI, e.g. Vitality green matches the health bar.
- **Winds of Fortune** [7:00](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=420): Stat icons share color and shape language so players learn meanings quickly.
- **Backlash** [7:40](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=460): Buttons get a container and a highlight to look touchable.
- **Berry Avenue RP** [9:25](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=565): Avatar customization uses a scrollable item grid, category tabs, search, and a quick-items list; two ways to unequip.
- **Spellbound** [13:18](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=798): Uses a deck-of-cards metaphor for spells and a clock-like spinner for turn order.
