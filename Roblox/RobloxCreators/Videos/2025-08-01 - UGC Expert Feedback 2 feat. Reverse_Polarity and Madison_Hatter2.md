# UGC Expert Feedback 2: feat. Reverse_Polarity and Madison_Hatter2

## Summary
Mentorship session where a UGC beginner (Vogueburnett) builds a pearl-and-gold-chain necklace accessory in Blender with Reverse_Polarity and Madison_Hatter2. Covers array/curve modifiers, fitting to avatar bodies, modifier application before export, UV unwrapping with seams, multiply/overlay texturing, and uploading decals and FBX to Roblox Studio.

## Tips
- [4:34](https://youtu.be/D64IOXAb6SM?t=274) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Manually position array elements along a curve; tinkering is the normal workflow. Use fit-to-curve while positioning, then switch back to fixed count and lower it until gaps close.
- [8:23](https://youtu.be/D64IOXAb6SM?t=503) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Keep a hidden 'backup' collection and save iterations (necklace_101, 102, 103). Duplicate items before applying modifiers so you can recover them later.
- [9:48](https://youtu.be/D64IOXAb6SM?t=588) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Apply array and curve modifiers before joining parts. Unapplied curves cause pieces to fly off into space after joining.
- [10:29](https://youtu.be/D64IOXAb6SM?t=629) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Order modifiers by trial and error; the mirror modifier should almost always sit at the top of the stack.
- [15:02](https://youtu.be/D64IOXAb6SM?t=902) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Position accessories on a reference avatar from Creator Hub (Woman 2.0 or Rthro-style body) rather than a single body; Woman 2.0 has a real neck, blocky has none.
- [17:56](https://youtu.be/D64IOXAb6SM?t=1076) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Export a first test FBX early; scale mismatches are fixable and exporting gives real feedback on how Roblox will import the item.
- [19:18](https://youtu.be/D64IOXAb6SM?t=1158) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Metalness at 100 in Blender does not export to Roblox accessories; approximate metallic looks by hand-painting texture in Photoshop.
- [43:13](https://youtu.be/D64IOXAb6SM?t=2593) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Mark seams on hard edges (about 45-90 degree angles), unwrap by angle-based, and keep UV islands at least one square away from borders.
- [46:51](https://youtu.be/D64IOXAb6SM?t=2811) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Give the most visible UV area to the front of the item; bigger UV islands hold more texture detail.
- [49:04](https://youtu.be/D64IOXAb6SM?t=2944) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Texture in layers: flat base colors first, then multiply for shadows and overlay/soft light for highlights; use 0% hardness brushes and 20-30% smudge strength.
- [1:00:22](https://youtu.be/D64IOXAb6SM?t=3622) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Push shadows and highlights more intensely on the focal piece; keep highlights smaller and sharper than shadows for gold and metal looks.
- [1:03:05](https://youtu.be/D64IOXAb6SM?t=3785) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Test textures in Blender with flat shading and shadows/cavities enabled to approximate how the item will look in Roblox before uploading.
- [1:05:32](https://youtu.be/D64IOXAb6SM?t=3932) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Save an unpublished place file and upload textures as Decals via create.roblox.com, then copy the asset ID into the mesh TextureID. Re-export after UV changes or the texture will not match.
- [1:05:32](https://youtu.be/D64IOXAb6SM?t=3932) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Export FBX with only selected objects, FBX unit scale, and uncheck Upload to Roblox. Apply smooth-by-angle before export if you want smooth shading.
