# Introduction to Quests, Achievements & Dailies with BreakfastCandy

[Watch](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw) | RobloxCreators | 3,970 views | 2021-10-21

## Summary
Level Up workshop by BreakfastCandy (Erin) on content cadence: frequent small, art-heavy releases between major updates. Covers theming, package scoping, economy risks (Adopt Me money tree), monetisation options, asset pipelines, request and quest templates, roadmaps (plan three months out, 1-4 week sprints), and promotion and retrospectives.

## Tips
- [2:33](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=153) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Release cadence content at least monthly; weekly or biweekly if the team can manage it, to keep players' attention.
- [0:29](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=29) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Build cadence content only on existing systems; introduce new features, currencies or mechanics in major updates first.
- [5:18](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=318) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Theme each release; a constrained theme makes ideas easier to generate and helps marketing explain what is coming.
- [9:15](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=555) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Time-box cadence development and keep releases small so the team can repeat them without burning out.
- [9:56](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=596) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Make cadence releases mostly art: reskins, recolors and geometry tweaks of assets already in the game.
- [10:37](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=637) **[[community-and-discord]]** Source cadence art from community contests; credit or pay creators so they do not feel exploited.
- [17:57](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=1077) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Pick items players want to own multiples of (e.g. cars, outfits) and that support the core loop.
- [19:50](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=1190) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Keep tech requests from cadence small; limit them to light hooks like quest triggers so major updates are not delayed.
- [26:02](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=1562) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Watch for inflated currencies; release sink items to pull resources out, and cap faucets (Adopt Me money tree was limited to $100/day).
- [26:43](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=1603) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make each cadence item available via free, soft currency or Robux; a mix covers paying and non-paying players.
- [27:56](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=1676) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Do not spend more than two or three weeks on one cadence release; that time is better spent on larger releases.
- [28:36](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=1716) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Keep effort proportional to reward; if content is too hard to collect, players give up.
- [29:17](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=1757) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track completion, purchases and engagement per release; a downward trend across releases signals fatigue and need for a new system.
- [13:03](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=783) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Mix permanent and limited-time cadence content: permanent gives a long tail, limited time drives urgency and monetisation.
- [15:13](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=913) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Gate content behind progression and add UI search so new players are not overwhelmed on day one.
- [30:18](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=1818) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Define an asset pipeline with a clear owner and handoff for each stage: design, concept, production, approval, implementation, testing.
- [36:08](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=2168) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan releases at least three months out; use 1-4 week sprints with built-in iteration and testing time.
- [38:54](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=2334) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use asset request and quest string templates to record owner, artist, dates, description and trigger; this reduces miscommunication.
- [44:22](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=2662) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** Use embedded variables in quest text (e.g. amount) so rebalancing does not require rewriting or re-localising strings.
- [46:25](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=2785) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Test every release, even furniture-only ones: confirm access, availability and price are as intended.
- [46:25](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=2785) **[[community-and-discord]]** Tease releases on social media early and often with silhouettes or concept art; ask players what they want next on release day.
- [49:07](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=2947) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Run a retrospective after each release focused on process; compare DAU and payer conversion against previous releases.
- [57:57](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=3477) **[[pricing]]** To slow consumption, add player-level gates or raise soft-currency prices instead of blocking players entirely.
- [55:13](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=3313) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Keep a backlog of non-holiday cadence content to release when production slips.

## Games
- **Adopt Me** [2:33 / 26:02](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=0): New pets are cadence content; the original unlimited money tree inflated the economy, so it was capped at $100/day.
- **Welcome to Bloxburg** [1:09](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=69): Regular furniture drops every few weeks are a classic cadence example.
- **Epic Golf** [3:13](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=193): New courses are playable cadence content when course systems already exist.
- **Arsenal** [3:13](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=193): New FPS maps are playable cadence content built on existing systems.
- **Super Striker League** [3:13](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=193): New stadiums are playable cadence content built on existing systems.
- **Piggy** [21:56](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=1316): Adding wings, a halo and a modified weapon to an existing skin makes a cheap new-looking item.

## Quotes

- [4:38](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=278) "Routine is a good thing for both you and for your players. It tells players that this game is alive and will continue to grow."
- [9:56](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=596) "Cadence is all about the art. It should be simple, relatively easy to produce art, mostly things that you already have in the game."
