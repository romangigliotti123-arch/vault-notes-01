# Prototyping Essentials

[Watch](https://youtu.be/iI2t7Yd7Byc) | RobloxCreators | 2,310 views | 2022-02-03

## Summary
Designer's short on why and how to prototype a Roblox game before full production. Covers early flaw detection, finding fun, fine-tuning design, and spotting technical roadblocks. Compares paper prototypes (fast, cheap, good for UI/rules, not reusable) against studio prototypes (reusable, catches technical issues, slower to iterate). Lists what to prototype first and a playtest process.

## Tips
- [0:13](https://youtu.be/iI2t7Yd7Byc?t=13) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Prototype before full development; discovering a flawed design 2-3 months in wastes that whole period.
- [2:14](https://youtu.be/iI2t7Yd7Byc?t=134) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use paper prototypes for UI, controls, and game rules; they can be built in an afternoon and iterated by erasing and redrawing.
- [6:48](https://youtu.be/iI2t7Yd7Byc?t=408) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use studio prototypes to test physics-heavy or technically risky mechanics early so you can pivot sooner if they fail.
- [9:03](https://youtu.be/iI2t7Yd7Byc?t=543) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Keep prototypes narrow: test the core loop, UI/UX, key mechanics, and game rules, not the whole feature.
- [9:03](https://youtu.be/iI2t7Yd7Byc?t=543) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Prototype the core loop first and confirm it is fun before building anything else.
- [10:46](https://youtu.be/iI2t7Yd7Byc?t=646) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Resolve edge cases with your team, run several internal sessions, then playtest with friends, family, and Roblox group or social followers.
- [10:46](https://youtu.be/iI2t7Yd7Byc?t=646) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Iterate prototypes until satisfied, but do not over-polish them; they are not the final feature.
- [5:24](https://youtu.be/iI2t7Yd7Byc?t=324) **[[common-mistakes]]** Beware false positives: paper-fun may not carry over to screens or phones, so retest in the actual platform.

## Numbers
- [0:44](https://youtu.be/iI2t7Yd7Byc?t=44) Time lost if design flaw found late: **2-3 months** (Stated example of cost of discovering a flawed idea mid-development)
- [4:03](https://youtu.be/iI2t7Yd7Byc?t=243) Paper prototype build time: **One afternoon** (Erin built a pizza-making paper prototype in an afternoon)
- [4:43](https://youtu.be/iI2t7Yd7Byc?t=283) Studio prototype setup time: **1-2 weeks** (Typical time to stand up a studio prototype, per speaker)
