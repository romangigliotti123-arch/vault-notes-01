# How to do video ads the right way on Roblox

[Watch](https://youtu.be/R6HbXwyuHzY) | RobloxCreators | 6,602 views | 2026-05-20

## Summary
Roblox's pitch for rewarded video ads: they can be ethical if players opt in for an in-game reward. Covers making ads discoverable, reading the reporting metrics (opt-in rate, frequency, EPM, reward rate), and forecasting earnings with the cost-out tool. Uses Barry's Prison Run as the example.

## Tips
- [2:35](https://youtu.be/R6HbXwyuHzY?t=155) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make rewarded video ads discoverable (front and center in the game), since visibility drives impressions and earnings.
- [3:16](https://youtu.be/R6HbXwyuHzY?t=196) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make ads optional and additive: give a valuable reward (e.g. Barry's Prison Run hat) so players choose to watch, not forced.
- [3:57](https://youtu.be/R6HbXwyuHzY?t=237) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Main reason rewarded video underearns is too few ad impressions, not lack of demand. Check impressions first.
- [5:19](https://youtu.be/R6HbXwyuHzY?t=319) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Run a test: add rewarded video, then watch retention and playtime to confirm it isn't hurting engagement.
- [5:44](https://youtu.be/R6HbXwyuHzY?t=344) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Check opt-in rate; if low or below benchmark, make the ad placement more discoverable.
- [5:44](https://youtu.be/R6HbXwyuHzY?t=344) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Raise frequency toward a couple of ad views per player per day by adding placements or reshowing later in gameplay.
- [6:25](https://youtu.be/R6HbXwyuHzY?t=385) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track EPM (earnings per thousand ad impressions) as a quality signal, though impressions matter more for earnings.
- [7:06](https://youtu.be/R6HbXwyuHzY?t=426) **[[monetisation-other]]** Watch reward rate (share of players finishing the ad). Brands value 90%+; if low, improve the reward or show ads less often.
- [8:20](https://youtu.be/R6HbXwyuHzY?t=500) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use Roblox's cost-out forecast tool with your game stats to model earnings at a given ad load before committing.
