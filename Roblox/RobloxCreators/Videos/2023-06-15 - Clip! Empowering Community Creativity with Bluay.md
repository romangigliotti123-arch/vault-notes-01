## Summary
Short talk on engaging the community of an older-audience Roblox game by letting players create. The speaker runs a Discord "community workshop" where players submit weapon and character ideas. The team usually recreates the idea in its own style instead of using the asset directly. Talented submitters have been onboarded as developers, which the speaker says improved game quality.

## Tips
- [0:48](https://youtu.be/MW-QvpIxO4I?t=48) **[[community-and-discord]]** Run a Discord workshop where players submit ideas (weapons, characters) for the game. Use submissions as concepts to rebuild in-house rather than importing their assets directly.
- [0:48](https://youtu.be/MW-QvpIxO4I?t=48) **[[community-and-discord]]** Recreate community ideas to match your game's style standard, since individual creator styles can break consistency.
- [1:29](https://youtu.be/MW-QvpIxO4I?t=89) **[[team-and-budget]]** Recruit talented community creators as developers. The speaker says this raised game quality and gave fresh input.
