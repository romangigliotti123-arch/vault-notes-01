# Level Design  ft. MiniToon, Erythia, Cullen & Tohb

[Watch](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg) | RobloxCreators | 2,867 views | 2022-03-17

## Summary
Roblox Level Up roundtable on level design with developers MiniToon (Piggy), Erythia/Arie (Sonaria, Dragon Adventures, Tasty Farms), and Tobe and Skelo Donut (Riot Fall FPS), moderated by Roblox's Dan. Covers defining level design, pre-production research, gray boxing, playtesting, landmarking, onboarding spawns, open-world sizing, and mobile considerations.

## Tips
- [9:56](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=596) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Plan level layout around core mechanics first (collection, combat, progression), then add visuals; reposition interactables before decorating.
- [12:52](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=772) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** For fast-paced FPS, prioritise gameplay playability over realism; good level design matters more than detail.
- [20:12](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=1212) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Ask early how to make each level stand out; similar-feeling levels make players lose interest (MiniToon on Piggy's early chapters).
- [23:39](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=1419) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Spend about a week researching real locations and building a mood board before building; take the best parts of several reference photos.
- [29:28](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=1768) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan on paper and gray box before detailing; detailing first forces costly teardown when a passage or tube must move.
- [30:51](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=1851) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Gray boxing: build layouts from simple gray block shapes so you can walk the space and change it cheaply before adding art.
- [36:50](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=2210) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Scrap areas that play badly; watch other people run the map to find issues like unfair vantage points for one team.
- [38:11](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=2291) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use modular building pieces to mix facades; reduces asset count and helps performance when working with many imported assets.
- [40:52](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=2452) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Design for multiple play styles (stealth, roamer, sniper) by giving each a route through the same space.
- [43:40](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=2620) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Run a tester team before big updates; ask testers for immediate improvements, while players and community give the final verdict.
- [45:02](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=2702) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track which items, creatures or features players ignore or over-use to spot weak systems and economy skew; act on what data shows.
- [49:12](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=2952) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Track per-level progress to find where players struggle; for story, send location photos to friends and check if they can reconstruct events.
- [50:10](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=3010) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Keep spawns open and uncluttered, offer only 2-3 initial objectives, and use visual cues like colour coding to guide players to the right building.
- [50:51](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=3051) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Use linear corridors for forced routes and open visual cues (inviting space, obstacles) when players should explore freely.
- [52:13](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=3133) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Use visual cues like particle effects, billboard GUIs and beams to mark interactables for players of all ages and skill levels.
- [52:53](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=3173) **[[common-mistakes]]** Commonly overlooked: social space in lobbies. Too many plots spread out felt empty; consolidating buildings into a town improved it.
- [54:17](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=3257) **[[common-mistakes]]** Keep level art cohesive with core movement mechanics; a great movement system is undermined by an unrelated map layout.
- [54:58](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=3298) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Start open worlds smaller than needed (a 2k x 2k world had players not reaching key locations); expand only after core interactables work.
- [56:50](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=3410) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Focus early progression on immediate gratification and rewards so new players stay; build features around a simple core loop.
- [58:52](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=3532) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Place recognisable landmarks and vistas so new players can orient themselves quickly; pair with an accurate minimap.
- [1:07:04](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=4024) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** In horror, make each unlocked area look visually distinct so progression feels rewarding rather than endless grey corridors.
- [1:21:13](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=4873) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Keep open-world exploration concise for mobile; sprinting and navigation are hard on mobile, so keep regions explorable but not taxing.
- [1:23:17](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=4997) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Hide map edges with distant buildings, mountains or floating islands; use paper-thin tall terrain for distant mountains.
- [1:25:21](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=5121) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Watch new players in an MVP alpha test; touch and break everything and move locations players struggle to reach.

## Numbers

## Games
- **Riot Fall** [0:52](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=52): Fast-paced FPS; level design must support gameplay, cover, vantage points and multiple play styles; iterate with frequent team feedback.
- **Sonaria (Creatures of Sonaria)** [2:55](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=175): Open world; a daily featured-creature boost system helped players discover underused content.
- **Dragon Adventures** [2:55](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=175): Open world: place core interactables first, keep social hub uncluttered, use big assets to encourage exploration.
- **Tasty Farms** [2:55](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=175): Social lobby and plots: consolidate buildings into a town to build user-to-user interaction; early progression matters.
- **Piggy** [4:16](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=256): Make each area visually distinct, use subtle colour-coding for keys and doors, and limit early puzzle choices.
- **Piggy Intercity** [4:16](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=256): Open-world RPG: stand-out landmarks guide players toward major locations while allowing free choice.
- **Winds of Fortune** [1:13:58](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=4438): Uses surface appearance and new materials; stepped away from UV projection toward an artistic style.
