# Roleplay Design and Worldbuilding with Myzta

[Watch](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo) | RobloxCreators | 2,871 views | 2021-09-30

## Summary
Roblox Level Up roundtable with Myzta (Full Flower Studios, Mermaid Life) and Roblox designer Aaron Jennings. Covers world-building, designing for player agency and low friction, art bibles, minimal UI, the avatar customizer, and planning content cadence.

## Tips
- [4:15](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=255) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Let players be whoever they want from the start; avoid over-gamifying. Myzta says My Droplets failed by walling off content in an empty house.
- [19:13](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=1153) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give players lots of content straight out of the gate; don't wall off roleplay behind grinding. Players come to social roleplay games to experience things immediately.
- [14:21](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=861) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Write an art bible covering shapes, colours, UI and character design so every area of the game feels consistent.
- [15:43](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=943) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Build interactive props (lockers, makeup vanity, library books) so players can tell their own stories in the space.
- [16:52](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=1012) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Keep environments low-detail around the avatar so clutter doesn't distract from the roleplay; use chunky, oversized shapes.
- [30:31](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=1831) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Add human-like cues (bridges, stairs) so players understand paths; avoid over-fantasy layouts that feel unnatural.
- [38:08](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=2288) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Treat the avatar customizer as a core system; Myzta redeveloped it about seven times and spent roughly 3 months polishing it.
- [43:12](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=2592) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Define a few design pillars (positive message, social, low friction) and check each new feature against them.
- [45:53](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=2753) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Pillars can change mid-development if players move in a different direction; don't treat them as fixed.
- [46:52](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=2812) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Make tool interactions animated and immediate (click to apply makeup) rather than relying on chat text, to reduce friction.
- [48:15](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=2895) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Keep the HUD minimal: put UI in 3D space where possible, and only show essentials like emotes, map, camera, customizer and backpack.
- [1:06:28](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=3988) **[[community-and-discord]]** Give small feel-good rewards, such as a one-time bonus when a friend joins, to make players happy and encourage social play.
- [1:20:52](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=4852) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Ship something every one to two weeks (outfits, hairs, bug fixes) so players keep returning; going longer lets the game go stale.
- [1:21:32](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=4892) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Plan releases months ahead (they had the rest of the year planned); this reduces team stress and keeps everyone aligned.
- [1:22:54](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=4974) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Tease upcoming content with in-world hints, such as a construction crab hammering at areas under construction.
- [1:24:15](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=5055) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Learn from other Roblox games and identify what they miss, then build on it rather than copying.
- [1:26:18](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=5178) **[[team-and-budget]]** Hire by reviewing portfolio detail; ask candidates to show their work, since vague DMs give no evidence.
- [22:52](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=1372) **[[team-and-budget]]** Recruit via referrals and the DevForum, with a Google form requesting resumes and work samples; be strict about what you need to see.
- [1:07:08](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=4028) **[[community-and-discord]]** Read player feedback in Discord and the group wall, but remove hateful negativity; respond publicly only for broken features.
- [12:16](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=736) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Define world building as providing a unique environment with tools so players can tell their own stories; the world comes alive through player choices.
- [1:18:09](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=4689) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Avoid generic settings; world uniqueness matters because virtual worlds are not limited to real-world constraints.
- [8:58](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=538) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Research the audience you target: Myzta saw a gap for girl-audience fashion and underwater open-world games before building Mermaid Life.
- [26:47](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=1607) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Don't assume an audience exists; Myzta's Bitter Sweet ballerina game failed on Roblox, so validate demand early.

## Games
- **Mermaid Life** [0:06](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=6): Underwater fashion roleplay; low-friction design, customizer focus, cozy warm palette, and many options from the start.
- **My Droplets** [3:33](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=213): Pet/social roleplay game; its empty-house start and over-gamification taught the developer to give players more immediately.
- **Bitter Sweet** [26:47](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=1607): Multiplayer ballerina game that failed to find an audience on Roblox; validate demand first.
- **Royale High** [8:18](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=498): Showed there was a large girl-audience roleplay and fashion market on Roblox.
- **Livetopia** [50:50](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=3050): Open-world playground with lots of content from the start; a reference for furnished, non-empty housing.
- **Sea of Thieves** [49:04](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=2944): Beautiful environment that is not overwhelming or sharp on the eyes; a reference for world design.
- **MeepCity** [52:15](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=3135): Built on mesh terrain; the developer says it causes ongoing collision and map-building delays.
- **Azure** [9:39](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=579): Steam underwater open-world game that felt closed off and empty, a gap Mermaid Life targeted.
