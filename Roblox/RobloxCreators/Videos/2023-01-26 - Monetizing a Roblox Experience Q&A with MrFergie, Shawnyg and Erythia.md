# Monetizing a Roblox Experience Q&A with MrFergie, Shawnyg and Erythia

[Watch](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI) | RobloxCreators | 2,084 views | 2023-01-26

## Summary
Level Up roundtable Q&A with Erythia (Dragon Adventures, Creatures of Sonaria), MrFergie and Shawnyg (Emergency Response: Liberty County) on monetisation: currency naming, dev products vs gamepasses, announcing items, item count, pop-ups, pricing, hiring, limited-time vs limited-stock, and conversion.

## Tips
- [0:31](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=31) **[[monetisation-other]]** Label currency as in-game (e.g. 'in-game cash') and use fantasy names or icons (shooms, trinkets) so it doesn't look like real money.
- [2:54](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=174) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Make dev products (buying currency with Robux) your main revenue source; permanent gamepasses like permanent double cash are risky to balance.
- [2:54](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=174) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Keep gamepasses few and scalable; a handful is fine if they don't break progression.
- [3:38](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=218) **[[monetisation-gamepasses]]** Clearly state in gamepass descriptions what is and isn't included, especially if it only unlocks buying an item with in-game cash.
- [3:38](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=218) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Announce new paid items in update logs, DevForum posts, social media, and an in-game store UI; hype content before release.
- [5:46](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=346) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make every paid item serve a purpose; merge overlapping items (e.g. per-team double XP into one all-teams double XP).
- [5:46](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=346) **[[common-mistakes]]** Watch for loops of barely differentiated content; if players feel overwhelmed or the store is bloated, step back and check if the core loop is still good.
- [6:27](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=387) **[[monetisation-other]]** Split large item lists into stores or categories and add filters (category, price) so players aren't overwhelmed.
- [8:39](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=519) **[[monetisation-other]]** Don't use pop-ups to push purchases; players call it pay-to-win. Use a small, non-intrusive 'need more cash' button instead.
- [9:47](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=587) **[[pricing]]** Don't cut prices just because players ask; respond when many players request the same change, and keep prices fair for your team.
- [11:26](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=686) **[[pricing]]** Start prices by comparing similar items in other games, then adjust for time invested and how much the item adds to the experience.
- [12:10](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=730) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Use testers and analytics to set prices; creators are often off on prices because they don't play their game like players do.
- [13:31](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=811) **[[monetisation-devproducts]]** Selling in-game currency for Robux lets players buy items like tractors with it; this is comparable to selling the item directly.
- [15:18](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=918) **[[team-and-budget]]** When hiring, post on Discord/Guilded dev communities, state pay upfront (Robux or dollars and timeline); avoid vague 'we'll discuss later'.
- [16:13](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=973) **[[team-and-budget]]** Early on, a percentage deal for a new game is hard to fill; freelance on the side to fund paying developers.
- [16:56](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=1016) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Ship a small, finished experience first (a simple roleplay game took under a month) rather than starting with your dream game; it attracts collaborators.
- [16:56](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=1016) **[[team-and-budget]]** Show builds, code and features on Twitter or the DevForum Talent Hub to find developers.
- [19:12](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=1152) **[[monetisation-other]]** Use limited-time items rather than limited-stock; stock caps frustrate players who can't get the item and feel like a lost race.
- [21:23](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=1283) **[[pricing]]** Offer a wide range of Robux price points, including very low ones (Griffin's Destiny has a 35 Robux option), since many players can't pay much.
- [22:04](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=1324) **[[monetisation-other]]** Make the game fun first; players won't pay for something that isn't fun.

## Numbers
- [21:23](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=1283) lowest Robux option: **35 Robux** (Griffin's Destiny offers a 35 Robux purchase option)
- [12:10](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=730) example price comparison: **100 or 1000 Robux** (Speaker's example for an item after ~8 hours of play)
- [6:27](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=387) store size warning: **20 items** (Speaker says a store with ~20 things looks bad; not a firm limit)
- [16:56](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=1016) time to ship simple roleplay game: **under a month** (Solo visual designer's game with finished assets, needing a scripter)
- [16:13](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=973) time spent without a coder: **about 3 years** (Speaker's solo search for a scripter before building a finished game)

## Games
- **Emergency Response: Liberty County** [0:07](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=7): Uses in-game cash with 'in-game' prefix in UI; gamepass-unlocked items prompt purchase; small non-intrusive buy-cash button instead of pop-ups.
- **Creatures of Sonaria** [7:11](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=431): Hundreds of creatures work when each is fresh and distinct; reevaluate if additions become repetitive.
- **Dragon Adventures** [19:12](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=1152): Limited-stock items drew strong negative player reaction; limited-time is safer.
- **Griffin's Destiny** [21:23](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=1283): Offers a 35 Robux option; uses trinkets as currency name.
