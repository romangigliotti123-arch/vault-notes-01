# Onboarding Essentials

[Watch](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI) | RobloxCreators | 5,012 views | 2023-08-02

## Summary
Level Up explains onboarding/first-time user experience (FTUE): the first few minutes that determine whether players return. Covers the player funnel, D1 retention as the key metric, and goals: teach essentials, reach fun fast (quick early level-ups, starter items/currency), show the full game range, set short and long-term goals, and end with a clear call to action and moments of joy.

## Tips
- [1:41](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI?t=101) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Keep onboarding short, just a few minutes, since that is all the time you have to convince players to return the next day.
- [1:28](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI?t=88) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Finish FTUE last before launch, and keep iterating on it after launch; it is arguably the most important content in a free-to-play game.
- [4:10](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI?t=250) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Teach movement and any unusual controls explicitly; surface non-standard controls on the HUD when relevant.
- [4:56](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI?t=296) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Teach the core loop: the repeated actions players do to progress, and why they should spend time doing them.
- [5:26](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI?t=326) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Teach only the basics early; avoid overloading players and layer on complexity and depth after the first session.
- [6:07](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI?t=367) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Tune the level curve so players level up quickly in the first few minutes to create a feeling of competence.
- [6:48](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI?t=408) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Increase difficulty and time required for progression over time, paired with commensurate rewards, to avoid boredom.
- [7:07](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI?t=427) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Make onboarding social where possible; solo or isolated starts conflict with Roblox's play-together motivation and let players learn from each other.
- [7:47](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI?t=467) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Give starter items and a little currency so players can customize early and check the shop without starting at zero.
- [8:27](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI?t=507) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** End the first session aware of the game's full range: show locked features like skill trees, boss fights and trading so players see future value.
- [9:07](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI?t=547) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give goals achievable in the next few sessions plus long-term goals taking weeks or months, and make them clear.
- [9:48](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI?t=588) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Surface goals prominently on the HUD or in the world (hub, quests, achievements, season pass), not buried in menus.
- [10:30](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI?t=630) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Do not end tutorial prompts abruptly; close with a congratulatory note and a call to action, ideally unlocking a new ability.
- [11:11](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI?t=671) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Add moments of joy and delight such as animations for level-ups, rare finds, boss kills and purchases, plus hidden secrets to find.

## Numbers
- [1:41](https://youtu.be/XfxbdKtSbOI?t=101) Onboarding length: **A few minutes** (Time available to convince new players to return next day)
