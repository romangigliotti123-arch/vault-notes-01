# What's a core loop on Roblox?

[Watch](https://youtu.be/gkFKF9A-snY) | RobloxCreators | 37,534 views | 2023-08-07

## Summary
Explains the Roblox "core loop" concept: the central repeated gameplay cycle made of minute-to-minute interaction, most-repeated actions, and a progression engine. Uses an RPG example (explore, fight, upgrade) and Jailbreak (explore, complete objective, power up) to show how both police and criminal teams share one loop.

## Tips
- [0:08](https://youtu.be/gkFKF9A-snY?t=8) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Define your core loop as three parts: minute-to-minute interaction, the most repeated action, and the progression engine that drives the player forward.
- [0:48](https://youtu.be/gkFKF9A-snY?t=48) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Make sure the core loop works for every role or team in the game; Jailbreak's police and criminals share the same progression engine (buying weapons and vehicles).

## Games
- **Jailbreak** [0:48](https://youtu.be/gkFKF9A-snY?t=48): Core loop is explore, complete objective, power up; both police and criminal teams feed the same weapon/vehicle purchase progression engine.
