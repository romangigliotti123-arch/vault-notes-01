# UGC Insights (feat. Reverse_Polarity and Madison_Hatter2)

## Summary
Interview with UGC creators Reverse_Polarity and Madison_Hatter2 on starting in UGC, the UGC upload fee and publishing advance economics, and using the Marketplace bestselling page (sorted by past day, min 15 Robux) to spot trends and decide what to make next.

## Tips
- [2:10](https://youtu.be/Zb1BJow0NV4?t=130) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Budget for the 750 Robux upload fee per item (non-refundable, standard across items) plus the publishing advance before selling UGC.
- [2:53](https://youtu.be/Zb1BJow0NV4?t=173) **[[pricing]]** Publishing advance: until recouped, your item earns 70% of Robux instead of 30%. A 1,500 Robux hat at 95 Robux needs about 22-23 sales to recoup.
- [3:26](https://youtu.be/Zb1BJow0NV4?t=206) **[[business-and-payouts]]** Upload UGC to a group rather than a personal profile when starting out; groups give a storefront and help build community.
- [4:17](https://youtu.be/Zb1BJow0NV4?t=257) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Check Marketplace sorted by Most Popular, Past Day, with min price 15 Robux to filter out free items and see what is currently selling.
- [5:39](https://youtu.be/Zb1BJow0NV4?t=339) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Check favorites on new items as a demand signal; 89,000 favorites is a strong result, and check your own page with the same sort to gauge item health.
- [6:22](https://youtu.be/Zb1BJow0NV4?t=382) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** When one item in a category sells well (e.g., friendly ghost), make related variants (sinister ghost) to ride the trend.
- [7:04](https://youtu.be/Zb1BJow0NV4?t=424) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't assume you can predict sales; no one knows what will sell. Make what you like, and expect some items to sell slowly.
- [7:21](https://youtu.be/Zb1BJow0NV4?t=441) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Don't give up when an item doesn't sell right away; some items sold zero initially and later became bestsellers as players discovered them.
- [8:01](https://youtu.be/Zb1BJow0NV4?t=481) **[[trend-research-and-validation]]** Use last season's sales data (e.g., ghosts vs zombies) to guide next seasonal releases, and keep checking the bestselling page for shifts.
- [8:43](https://youtu.be/Zb1BJow0NV4?t=523) **[[game-ideas-and-genres]]** Combine real-world trends with seasonal themes (e.g., cottagecore mushroom wizard hat turned into a Halloween version).
- [8:43](https://youtu.be/Zb1BJow0NV4?t=523) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Revisit last year's items and remake them with improved skills; a better version of a proven idea can outperform the original.
