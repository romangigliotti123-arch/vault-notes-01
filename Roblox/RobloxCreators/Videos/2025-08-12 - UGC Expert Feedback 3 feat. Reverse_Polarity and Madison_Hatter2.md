## Tips
- [3:14](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=194) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't use the Roblox name or brand in UGC items; Roblox doesn't allow it. Rename the text instead.
- [5:41](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=341) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Name layered clothing meshes exactly: MeshName_InnerCage, MeshName_OuterCage, and armature as 'Armature'. Names like 'tank top.00002' break the cage lookup.
- [6:22](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=382) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** For FBX export of layered clothing, use Selected Objects only and unit scale; Blender defaults are otherwise fine.
- [8:24](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=504) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use Avatar > Accessory Fitting Tool in Studio to convert a mesh into layered clothing, instead of an old plugin's Create Accessory flow.
- [11:08](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=668) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** If the neck looks see-through, turn on Back Face Culling in Blender's Solid mode to find flipped normals, select them, and flip (Shift+N).
- [13:48](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=828) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Keep layered clothing under the triangle limit, stated as 4,000. Remove extra subdivision modifiers; the mesh dropped from 51,136 to 3,196 triangles.
- [15:12](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=912) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Auto smooth the mesh at 180 degrees to fix odd shading on top areas.
- [17:01](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=1021) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Apply the subdivision modifier, then mark edges sharp (Alt+E menu) to get crisp seams and collars.
- [20:18](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=1218) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Unwrap UVs before adding faces or waterproofing, or new faces will be uncolored in the texture; hidden inner faces matter little.
- [22:30](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=1350) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Try to fix problems first; start over only when you can't restore an earlier state to your vision.
- [26:32](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=1592) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** To keep text from warping, keep the UV straight and grid-aligned in the text area; Substance Painter lets you paint text directly.
- [28:30](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=1710) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Add detail with a normal map instead of geometry: make a high-contrast copy of the color map and run it through an online normal map generator.
- [35:23](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=2123) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Make a grayscale metallic map (white = shiny, black = matte) and invert it for roughness; set normal and metallic maps to Non-Color.
- [36:04](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=2164) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Keep texture maps at 1024 or below; Roblox auto-downscales high-res item textures.
- [43:03](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=2583) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use Photoshop's Spot Healing Brush to remove unwanted text or logos from textures.
- [43:18](https://youtu.be/xU3ikvnA5OM?t=2598) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Upload texture maps from a file with Asset Manager access, then insert a Surface Appearance and paste the color, normal, and metallic maps.
