# Clip! Level Design Mistakes on Roblox with Erythia, MiniToon, Skeledonut & Tohb

[Watch](https://youtu.be/CmXyiJw5N_Y) | RobloxCreators | 946 views | 2023-03-16

## Summary
Short clip of level designers discussing overlooked flaws after a first layout pass. Covers spawn and lobby layout for social interaction, keeping the level cohesive with the movement and mechanics, avoiding over-spread world building, and underused lighting and post-processing as a cheap visual upgrade.

## Tips
- [0:05](https://youtu.be/CmXyiJw5N_Y?t=5) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Design spawn and lobby space for user-to-user interaction; players should naturally meet, trade and talk. Tasty Farms cut 25 scattered plots into one clustered town, which felt far more social.
- [0:46](https://youtu.be/CmXyiJw5N_Y?t=46) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Keep the level cohesive with your core mechanics. A strong movement system wasted on a level that doesn't complement it is a common flaw, especially in FPS games.
- [1:27](https://youtu.be/CmXyiJw5N_Y?t=87) **[[common-mistakes]]** Avoid spreading the world too far from where gameplay actually happens; designers get carried away building and drift away from the core mechanics.
- [1:27](https://youtu.be/CmXyiJw5N_Y?t=87) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use lighting and post-processing (dynamic clouds, atmospheres, post-processing effects) to lift the look without physically detailing every object; it is underused and high impact.

## Numbers
- [0:05](https://youtu.be/CmXyiJw5N_Y?t=5) Plots in Tasty Farms spawn area: **25 plots, replaced by one clustered town** (Spread-out plots felt empty; compacting them improved neighborhood feel)

## Games
- **Tasty Farms** [0:05](https://youtu.be/CmXyiJw5N_Y?t=5): Spread 25 plots around an open area felt empty; clustering buildings into a small town fixed the social feel.

## Quotes
- [0:05](https://youtu.be/CmXyiJw5N_Y?t=5) "the social aspect and where users are spawning in that lobby space, it can be so incredibly vital"
- [1:27](https://youtu.be/CmXyiJw5N_Y?t=87) "lighting is very underused in a lot of games and it can make such a big difference"
