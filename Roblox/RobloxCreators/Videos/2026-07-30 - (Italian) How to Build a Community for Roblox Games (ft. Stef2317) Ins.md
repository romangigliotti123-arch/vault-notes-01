# (Italian) How to Build a Community for Roblox Games (ft. Stef2317) | Inspire 2026

[Watch](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk) | RobloxCreators | 13,289 views | 2026-07-30

## Summary
Italian talk by Stefano (Stefan2317, Black Zone Hunters) on building a social-media community around a Roblox game and converting followers into players. Covers platform focus, consistent identity, making followers participants, reducing click friction, giving a reason to play, first-60-seconds promise, and retention as the hardest metric.

## Tips
- [4:15](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=255) **[[community-and-discord]]** Pick one social platform where your target audience already is and do it well, rather than spreading thin across all platforms.
- [8:23](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=503) **[[common-mistakes]]** Adapt content to each platform's language; the same short video often works on one platform and flops on another.
- [10:29](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=629) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Don't chase trends only for views; views without a community don't convert into players.
- [11:53](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=713) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Build a recognizable identity: consistent colors, editing style, voice, characters and format so viewers recognize you while scrolling.
- [14:00](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=840) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Show the game itself as the main hook; the gameplay moment should make viewers want to play with friends.
- [15:25](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=925) **[[community-and-discord]]** Measure community by how many people wait for your next update and give feedback, not total plays.
- [16:07](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=967) **[[community-and-discord]]** Make followers active participants: reply to comments, run contests and Q&As, ask for ideas and credit those who suggest them.
- [18:09](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=1089) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Use algorithm trends to get noticed, but build on owned ground: a community you can reach beats relying on platform recommendations.
- [22:16](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=1336) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't make content only for the algorithm; ask how to keep these viewers and involve them, and keep the tone matching the game.
- [23:00](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=1380) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Reduce friction from video to play: use direct links, repeat the game name, and make the call to action clear.
- [24:23](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=1463) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't put the play call-to-action at the start; first give a reason to watch, then ask people to play at the end.
- [25:04](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=1504) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Give a specific reason to play the game, such as a new update, event or challenge; 'because I made it' rarely works.
- [25:47](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=1547) **[[community-and-discord]]** Offer community-only perks like codes, early beta access or previews to make followers feel special and stay.
- [26:28](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=1588) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Ensure the first 60 seconds deliver what the trailer promised; mismatched first minutes cause immediate quits and bad data.
- [27:11](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=1631) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Give an immediate objective and an early reward loop, like 99 Nights: first task, then fire growth that opens exploration.
- [27:52](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=1672) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Track day-1 and day-7 retention; these are the hardest and most important metrics for Roblox games and feed the algorithm.
- [28:58](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=1738) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track the funnel: impressions, link clicks or game-name searches, game opens, first session, D1 return, D7 return; find where most players drop.
- [31:00](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=1860) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't spam game links everywhere hoping people play; earn their interest first.
- [31:42](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=1902) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't constantly change your identity or genre to follow trends; audiences won't form around an inconsistent creator.
- [33:05](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=1985) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Use community feedback to improve the game before the next promotional push, so the algorithm gets better data.
- [35:10](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=2110) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Put roughly 80% of effort into game development and 20% into marketing; a good game is what converts visibility.
- [35:50](https://youtu.be/yv3GO5AHwJk?t=2150) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Don't assume the problem is only visibility; check the first minutes, core loop, originality and clarity of the game.
