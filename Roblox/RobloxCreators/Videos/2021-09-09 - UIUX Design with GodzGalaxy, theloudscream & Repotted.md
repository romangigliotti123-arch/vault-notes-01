# UI/UX Design with GodzGalaxy, theloudscream & Repotted

[Watch](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI) | RobloxCreators | 4,097 views | 2021-09-09

## Summary
Level Up Roundtable on UI/UX design for Roblox with Peak Precision Studios (Outlaster) developers Dexter (GodzGalaxy), Bella (theloudscream, UI/UX designer) and Logan (Repotted, lead scripter). Covers UX vs UI, accessibility, consistency, mobile-first design, feedback channels, testing and analytics-based measurement.

## Tips
- [10:53](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=653) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Prioritise usability over looks: easy-to-use UI that looks plain beats attractive UI that players can't use. Polish after it functions.
- [11:52](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=712) **[[common-mistakes]]** Check colour contrast on frames and buttons; for audio-based games, show on-screen text for sounds, since accessibility is often skipped.
- [14:35](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=875) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Give immediate feedback on input: hover colour change, click animations, so players know a button is clickable and an action registered.
- [17:48](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=1068) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Create a style sheet (colours, fonts, basic elements, padding) before designing UI to keep frames consistent and reduce rework.
- [19:09](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=1149) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Keep interactions consistent (doors, buttons, menus); players form expectations from first use and get confused when they break.
- [20:17](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=1217) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan UI in a fixed workflow: wireframe, then style sheet, then icons and element styling, before implementing.
- [23:31](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=1411) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Know your audience; for younger players keep UI simple. Use a team member who isn't designing the UI as a stand-in player to flag confusing elements.
- [24:53](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=1493) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Have someone new play with as little help as possible and watch where they get confused; first-time players give the most valuable feedback.
- [28:41](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=1721) **[[first-session-onboarding]]** Put stores in both the world and a menu where possible; a UI button gives constant access and better monetisation.
- [31:14](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=1874) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track which competitions players do and compare with next-day retention to measure each competition's quality more accurately than feedback alone.
- [32:36](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=1956) **[[scripting-and-performance]]** For mobile, cap 3D model triangle counts and brick counts; prioritise smooth performance over fine detail.
- [35:34](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=2134) **[[common-mistakes]]** On mobile, consolidate buttons into one or a few to limit clickable items and avoid covering most of the screen.
- [37:12](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=2232) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Test features before keeping them. Outlaster's quick play mode looked good on paper but players played it and left, so it was removed.
- [41:17](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=2477) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Plan localisation from the start; retrofitting it is painful, especially for custom usernames and dialogue text formatting.
- [43:33](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=2613) **[[community-and-discord]]** Filter player feedback: ask whether it fits the game and won't hurt retention. Players identify problems better than they design solutions.
- [45:15](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=2715) **[[community-and-discord]]** Collect feedback on Twitter and Discord, including a suggestions channel and scheduled Discord Q&As; watch player count and retention after releases.
- [49:27](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=2967) **[[community-and-discord]]** When removing or changing a feature, explain why in update logs or Q&A, and make the change fill a clear gap so players aren't shocked.
- [51:59](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=3119) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Measure UI success with player counts and analytics, since successful UI generates no direct feedback. Beware a vocal minority of dedicated players.
- [1:01:36](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=3696) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Use a clear division of labour: one person does layouts and scripting, the other does icons and mockups, then iterate together heavily.
- [1:09:02](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=4142) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Check accessibility in colour choices; the Outlaster team's early colours ignored colourblind players and a colour-matching competition had to be replaced with symbols.
- [1:10:01](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=4201) **[[building-art-and-assets]]** Start UI on mobile, the most limiting platform, then scale up to PC; mobile-to-PC is easier than PC-to-mobile.
- [1:13:34](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=4414) **[[common-mistakes]]** Don't prioritise PC for a non-combat game; mobile is over 70% of Roblox's user base, so PC should be an afterthought.
- [1:22:37](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=4957) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Test mobile UI with a Discord testing group, Studio's device emulator, and your own phone to check whether you'd actually play on mobile.
- [1:26:00](https://youtu.be/AG-3o_GdqKI?t=5160) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Test features until you're comfortable with them; if it's confusing to you, it will be more confusing to players.
