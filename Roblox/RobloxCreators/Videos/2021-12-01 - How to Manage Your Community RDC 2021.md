# How to Manage Your Community | RDC 2021

## Summary
RDC 2021 talk by MiniToon (Piggy creator) on managing a Roblox game community: why know your consumers, community manager goals, which platforms to use, analytics to watch, a self-check checklist, handling trolls, and examples of well-run communities (Adopt Me, Roblox TikTok, Twitter).

## Tips
- [2:07](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=127) **[[community-and-discord]]** Talk directly to players and listen; predicting what players want without communication is a shot in the dark due to creator bias.
- [7:37](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=457) **[[community-and-discord]]** Use Twitter/X and Instagram for news, polls and fan creations; Discord for community discussion; Roblox group for news on-platform.
- [9:36](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=576) **[[community-and-discord]]** Don't try to be everywhere; focus presence on the platform where your community is most active, often Roblox itself.
- [10:23](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=623) **[[core-loops-and-game-design]]** Use teasers and mysteries (e.g. Piggy's hidden true ending hunt) to make players theorize and retain curiosity.
- [12:34](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=754) **[[community-and-discord]]** Self-check regularly: are you bringing people together without controversy, and does the community admire and respect you?
- [15:44](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=944) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track main demographic, engagement per product (visits for games), and followers/members joining and leaving as community health signals.
- [16:35](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=995) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Watch for quote tweets far exceeding likes/retweets; it signals people discuss you negatively rather than positively.
- [14:50](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=890) **[[community-and-discord]]** Study successful communities: copy what works, avoid what gets them cancelled or backlash.
- [17:54](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=1074) **[[community-and-discord]]** Set clear community rules in advance; expect pushback on change and keep rules sensible, not overly strict.
- [18:58](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=1138) **[[community-and-discord]]** Ignore or block trolls who give no actionable criticism; they want reactions. Engage only with feasible feedback.
- [20:48](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=1248) **[[community-and-discord]]** Thank your community regularly and treat them as an extension of the product, since without them the game is a hollow shell.
- [21:29](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=1289) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Take breaks when needed and protect mental health; you must look after yourself before your community.
- [22:10](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=1330) **[[common-mistakes]]** Assume anything posted, even in DMs, can be shared out of context; be mindful of what you say to avoid future cancellation.
- [23:31](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=1411) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Keep a consistent weekly update log so fans know when news arrives, as Adopt Me does, and request and act on feedback via Discord.
- [4:35](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=275) **[[community-and-discord]]** Establish connections with players so they feel listened to; this builds trust and encourages honest feedback and word of mouth.
- [5:46](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=346) **[[community-and-discord]]** Make players leave support conversations glad they spoke to you; poor support wastes their time and hurts reputation.
