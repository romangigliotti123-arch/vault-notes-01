# Supporting Game Expansions with Dspav

[Watch](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI) | RobloxCreators | 2,658 views | 2021-08-12

## Summary
Dspav talk on supporting a live game feature/expansion: gathering feedback from community, social media, analytics and team retros; explains DAU, MAU, session length, D1/D7/D30 retention, ARPDAU, conversion rate, RPPU, and feature funnels; covers prioritizing feedback, release planning, and themed content rollouts.

## Tips
- [2:43](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=163) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Gather new content ideas from players' posts, but check each request against your vision; meet halfway or decline if it conflicts.
- [4:04](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=244) **[[social-features-and-virality]]** Always ask permission before using fan artwork in your game and credit the creator; run fan-art contests with in-game rewards instead of free work requests.
- [11:21](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=681) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Play on an alt account to see how players actually play; use your dev account to ask for feature feedback, since it's visible and changes behaviour.
- [12:02](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=722) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Test new ideas as limited-time events; if players love it, make it a core-loop feature, if not, iterate and re-release as an event.
- [13:24](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=804) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Treat player requests as problem reports: identify the underlying problem and design your own solution rather than copying the request.
- [15:44](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=944) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Make the first five minutes fun; free-to-play players who aren't hooked quickly are exponentially unlikely to return, unlike paid games.
- [17:47](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=1067) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Low D7 retention usually means players don't know what to work toward next; add short, medium and long-term goals.
- [19:11](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=1151) **[[retention-d1-d7]]** Low D30 suggests weak endgame; build sustainable evergreen long-term goals for advanced players.
- [21:50](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=1310) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Track conversion rate (share of players who ever spend) as the most important monetisation metric; a first purchase predicts repeat spend.
- [23:55](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=1435) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Use RPPU (revenue per paying user) to gauge spend depth; if low, first purchase was bad or first offer was too generous.
- [26:01](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=1561) **[[pricing]]** Prorate prices by progression: scale soft-currency bundle costs and timer-skip prices down as players advance, if your platform allows it.
- [28:00](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=1680) **[[analytics-metrics]]** Build feature funnels by firing an event per step (open store, select, buy, equip, feed) to find drop-off points and fix UX there.
- [30:13](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=1813) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Run team retros with what went well, what didn't, and what to improve; use anonymous input tools like Slido if people hold back.
- [32:06](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=1926) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Prioritise feedback by severity (exploits and crashes first), breadth of sources, and impact versus implementation cost.
- [35:09](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=2109) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Overhaul an existing place rather than launching a 2.0 copy when possible, since a new place strands existing players and rebuilds the base from scratch.
- [37:13](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=2233) **[[community-and-discord]]** Solo devs should pick one feedback channel that yields the most actionable input, ideally one you control, such as your group or Discord.
- [38:36](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=2316) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Theme each release around familiar culture or genre references and tie it into other systems like furniture or cosmetics.
- [39:19](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=2359) **[[updates-live-ops]]** Include feature improvements alongside new content in each release, and collect feedback after every release.
- [42:42](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=2562) **[[mindset-and-strategy]]** Respect the community; player goodwill is your most important currency, and communicate openly when releases miss the mark.
