# What's prototyping on Roblox?

[Watch](https://youtu.be/wN4-AERPU9g) | RobloxCreators | 22,150 views | 2023-08-12

## Summary
Short explainer on prototyping as an experimental design step before building features. Covers two benefits: catching design flaws, oversights and technical constraints early in pre-production, and finding out whether the idea is fun. Stresses keeping prototypes small in scope and low effort.

## Tips
- [0:19](https://youtu.be/wN4-AERPU9g?t=19) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Prototype feature ideas before full build to catch design flaws, oversights and technical limits early instead of months into development.
- [0:19](https://youtu.be/wN4-AERPU9g?t=19) **[[playtesting-and-feedback]]** Play a primitive version of your idea yourself to check whether it is actually fun before investing in it.
- [0:19](https://youtu.be/wN4-AERPU9g?t=19) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Use prototyping to decide whether to pivot if your vision can't be built within your means.
- [0:50](https://youtu.be/wN4-AERPU9g?t=50) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Keep prototypes fast and narrow: test only the aspects you care about, not the full feature.
- [0:50](https://youtu.be/wN4-AERPU9g?t=50) **[[development-process-and-speed]]** Make prototype assets low effort and easy to produce so throwaway work stays cheap.

## Quotes
- [0:19](https://youtu.be/wN4-AERPU9g?t=19) "By prototyping, you ensure that you detect design flaws, oversights and unexpected technical requirements in the early stages of pre-production"
- [0:50](https://youtu.be/wN4-AERPU9g?t=50) "Prototyping should be fast and quick, hitting certain aspects of the feature being tested but not be the entire feature itself."
