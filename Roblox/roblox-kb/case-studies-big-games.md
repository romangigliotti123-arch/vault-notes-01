# Case study: Big Games

**Genre:** Game group / publisher brand

A large Roblox game group (26M members, per PolarBear). PolarBear used its simple icon (curved blue background, white caps text) as a template. SmartyRBX mentions Big Games in a game-buying business context, not confirmed as the same entity.

Also referred to as: Big Games (recreated logo)

## Numbers
- 26M members (PolarBear, G786)

## What creators say about it
- Practice icon and logo design by recreating known game logos in Canva; the exercise doubled as portfolio practice. ([PolarBear 2024-07 4:02](https://youtu.be/yYp23GK9RI4?t=242))
- Study large groups' simple icon systems as templates. PolarBear copied Big Games' curved blue background with white caps text. ([PolarBear 2024-07 3:22](https://youtu.be/tkAis2wTxms?t=202))
- SmartyRBX describes buying games and hiring developers as a high-leverage, strategy-first business. The transcript does not confirm Big Games is that business. ([SmartyRBX 2025-06 16:25](https://youtu.be/MAnvGNgBKgQ?t=985))

## Transferable patterns
- Recreate established logos and icons as low-stakes design practice.
- Use dominant groups' visual identity as a reference template for your own icons.
- Acquisition of existing games is a strategy-first investment path (speaker claim, unverified).
