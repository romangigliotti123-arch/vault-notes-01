# Case study: Violence District

**Genre:** Asymmetrical survival horror (1 vs all)

A Survival 1-vs-all horror game where a killer hunts survivors who complete objectives and use scarce pallets to escape. Reached breakout status and top discovery lists in 2026.

## Numbers
- 1.7B visits and 277.7K peak CCU (RoTrends tracker, third-party) [G2275]
- Demand rated 78 (rasdev, speaker's rating) [G1675]

## What creators say about it
- A breakout-tagged game can keep rising; the speaker rated its demand as near perfect (score 78). ([rasdev 2026-05 0:48](https://youtu.be/N3L63uyLyQ8?t=48))
- Most recent note: the game peaked a couple of days before the video and then stabilised, so breakout momentum can fade quickly. This supersedes the earlier 'still rising' view. ([rasdev 2026-06 2:31](https://youtu.be/q0uePCbBlk0?t=151), [rasdev 2026-05 0:48](https://youtu.be/N3L63uyLyQ8?t=48))
- Weekly limited-time event updates (e.g., Week 2 x Butcher collab) appear on the page and drive discovery; the game appeared on top-trending, top-playing-now and fun-with-friends lists. ([RoTrends (tracker) 2026-10](https://www.rotrends.com/game/6739698191/BUTCHER-x-Violence-District), [Roblox public API 2026-11](https://www.roblox.com/discover#charts-20261101))
- Asymmetric 1 vs 5 design where killers hunt survivors with scarce escape resources creates tension. ([Roblox public API 2026-11](https://www.roblox.com/games/93978595733734/))

## Transferable patterns
- Asymmetric roles with scarce resources create tension
- Use limited-time collab events to re-trigger discovery
- Watch discovery-list placement as a demand signal, and expect stabilisation after a peak
