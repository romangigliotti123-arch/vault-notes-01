# Case study: Pets Go!

**Genre:** Pet-collection simulator with egg RNG

Roblox pet-collection simulator with egg RNG, a growing skill tree and jelly content added in a major update. Sustained mainly by Pet Simulator IP recognition; creators criticise egg odds and monetisation.

Also referred to as: Pets Go

## Numbers
- No CCU or revenue figures given.

## What creators say about it
- A major update adding jelly content made players grind fishing, chests and boosts to unlock huge pets and multipliers. ([Mulch 2024-11 0:00](https://youtu.be/SLrIagp7uHE?t=0))
- A spawn-in flashing arrow to a dice, slot-style reel animation and near-miss zoom build anticipation before a predefined outcome; creators criticise the egg odds. ([janisjanis01 2025-05 4:20](https://youtu.be/Shjq-Xd9Kmw?t=260), [Daiorg 2025-02 6:18](https://youtu.be/nutv8RT5UrQ?t=378))
- Attention is sustained mainly by Pet Simulator IP name recognition. ([Cumin 2025-04 1:23](https://youtu.be/YmAwFbqUA0U?t=83))
- A large, grindier skill tree is flagged as a red flag; CORSO says it resembles TDS's. ([Daiorg 2025-02 6:18](https://youtu.be/nutv8RT5UrQ?t=378), [CORSO 2025-05 3:47](https://youtu.be/O2LZcQG5PPQ?t=227))
- A simulator-map style is one that commissions often imitate. ([SmartyRBX 2025-06 13:37](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=817))

## Transferable patterns
- Update-gated grind to unlock top tiers
- IP recognition as a traffic source
- Reward-reveal animation for anticipation (watch for odds criticism)
- Avoid over-grind and monetisation complaints
