# Case study: Clean All the Leaves

**Genre:** Chore simulator

A plain verb-noun chore simulator where players clean leaves for rewards. Discussed for its title, instant-reward hooks, and a clone that the speaker expects to fade with the trend.

## What creators say about it
- A plain verb-noun title and icon work for a game that already has players, per the speaker. ([rasdev 2026-08 7:27](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=447), [rasdev 2026-09 7:26](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=446))
- Immediate rewards (+1 pickup popup, quick quest completion) and a visible progress bar hook players fast. ([MonarchDev 2026-09 2:02](https://youtu.be/haYW1hXahOM?t=122))
- A leaf-cleaning clone of the same chore-sim concept is expected to fade with the trend. ([kra_DeZ 2026-10 4:48](https://youtu.be/sB9W5BXjQpk?t=288))

## Transferable patterns
- Plain verb-noun titles
- Instant feedback and visible progress bars
- Trend clones of a chore sim tend to fade
