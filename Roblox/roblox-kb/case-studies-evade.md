# Case study: Evade

**Genre:** Nextbot-inspired Roblox game

Roblox game started as a Nextbot-inspired experience, then expanded with cosmetics, emotes and voice chat. Uses consumable temporary boosts and rewarded video placements. Revenue claim is creator-stated and unverified.

## Numbers
- About $80M yearly income cited by VuukStudios (2023-12); speaker says it is unverified (G672).

## What creators say about it
- Started inspired by Nextbots, then added cosmetics, emotes and voice chat for expression and social play. ([Roblox 2025-09 8:01](https://youtu.be/YgyfcMfxQcI?t=481))
- Temporary boosts are the documented example of consumable items. ([Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations))
- Rewarded video placements sit on Second Chance and Streak Saver moments, with a pop-up example. ([Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/ad-placements))
- The ~$80M yearly income claim is a creator-stated, unverified front-page revenue example; use it as a signal, not a benchmark. ([VuukStudios 2023-12 1:26](https://youtu.be/fEcFzlBQew8?t=86))

## Transferable patterns
- Expression systems (cosmetics, emotes, voice) for social play
- Consumable temporary boosts
- Rewarded video at retention moments such as second chances and streaks
- Start from a known mechanic and differentiate
