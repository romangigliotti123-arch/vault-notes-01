# Case study: 3008

**Genre:** Horror

Horror Roblox game (SCP-themed per one mention). Praised for disabling camera shake and a keybind changer. Criticised for toggle crouch without toggle sprint. Camera-shake disabling was confirmed again in 2023.

Also referred to as: 3008 (SCP)

## Numbers
- No CCU or revenue figures given.

## What creators say about it
- Provide a camera-shake toggle and a keybind changer; both were praised. ([Roblox DevForum 2021-07](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919), [Roblox Creators 2023-05 52:23](https://youtu.be/K1pEI6LVJJA?t=3143))
- Toggle options should be consistent: toggle crouch without toggle sprint was a noted gap. ([Roblox DevForum 2021-07](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/making-accessible-games-for-the-disability-aware-era-a-not-fully-comprehensive-guide-by-an-actual-disabled-developer/1375919))

## Transferable patterns
- Ship accessibility options: camera shake off and keybind changes.
- Keep toggle options consistent across actions.
