# Case study: Arsenal

**Genre:** Competitive FPS

Fast, arcade-style first-person shooter with RNG weapon order and quick rounds. Long-running, with cosmetics bought with round earnings, regular map releases and seasons, and a strong anti-cheat stance.

## Numbers
- No specific CCU or revenue figures given in the input.

## What creators say about it
- Earn currency from rounds to buy cosmetics, which feeds back into later rounds; multiplayer drives the core loop (Roblox Creators). ([Roblox Creators 2022-06 42:54](https://youtu.be/Mep4_w8qxeE?t=2574))
- Cadence: ~2-week updates in busy periods, 3-month seasons, and playable new FPS maps built on existing systems (Roblox Creators). ([Roblox Creators 2022-12 25:33](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=1533), [Roblox Creators 2021-09 3:46](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=226), [Roblox Creators 2021-10 3:13](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=193))
- A self-rotating daily shop synced across all servers is the reference pattern (Roblox DevForum). ([Roblox DevForum 2020-06](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-server-synced-daily-shops/632401))
- Anti-cheat must be aggressive: kick or ban exploiters (BloxianCode). Aimbot and ESP scripts make exploiters powerful in shooters (DevForum). ([BloxianCode 2022-02 3:27](https://youtu.be/SN7MyJFOQkU?t=207), [Roblox DevForum 2023-08](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/securing-your-anticheat-common-bad-practices-and-how-powerful-are-exploiters-guide-on-handshakes/2519952))
- Shooters need lag compensation: a valid shot can count for nothing when server delay means the target is dead before it registers (ByteBlox). ([ByteBlox 2025-03 23:25](https://youtu.be/3rxZj5-Qx30?t=1405))
- Make visible stats such as total kills drive status-based grinding among peers (SmartyRBX). Quick-kill weapon progression started a trend of snappy Roblox shooters (Daiorg). ([SmartyRBX 2025-04 2:07](https://youtu.be/N2FND74tgGE?t=127), [Daiorg 2025-08 9:36](https://youtu.be/GkKUU9mCgwc?t=576))

## Transferable patterns
- Earn cosmetic currency from core rounds to close the loop.
- Ship cadence content (maps, seasons) on existing systems.
- Use visible stats (kills, badges) for status.
- Build anti-cheat and lag compensation in from the start for shooters.
