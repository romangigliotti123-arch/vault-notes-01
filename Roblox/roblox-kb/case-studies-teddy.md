# Case study: Teddy

**Genre:** Horror escape game (inferred from scary cutscenes and escape routes)

GnomeCode's scary escape-style game. Features a daycare level with multiple exits per room and drops players straight into rounds. Saturated at ~1K CCU, so ads returned poorly.

Also referred to as: Teddy (referenced game)

## Numbers
- ~1K CCU with strong retention (GnomeCode, self-reported, G922)

## What creators say about it
- A scary cutscene concept served as a model for the creator's own game's tone. ([GnomeCode 2020-06 31:53](https://youtu.be/Z74HM6xcsBE?t=1913))
- Multiple exits per room balance gameplay and give players real escape routes. ([GnomeCode 2023-03 6:17](https://youtu.be/QRwTkEPPChM?t=377))
- Clicking play drops players straight into a round with no waiting for the previous round to end. ([GnomeCode 2024-10 0:49](https://youtu.be/H9CbgCe47Mo?t=49))
- At ~1K CCU the game was saturated: ads gave poor returns and low monetisation turned the spend into a loss. ([GnomeCode 2024-11 0:00](https://youtu.be/g9UbIrR0RbU?t=0))

## Transferable patterns
- Give levels multiple escape or exit routes.
- Remove join waits so players start immediately.
- Stop paid acquisition when the audience is saturated; fix monetisation first.
