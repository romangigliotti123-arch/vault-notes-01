# Case study: Hack a Business

**Genre:** Business/tycoon sim (Tizzy's own game)

Tizzy's business sim, renamed from 'Sell Your Data' for a clearer core fantasy. Growth work centred on title and thumbnail testing, daily updates, and a cutscene to cut high bounce rates. All figures are creator self-reported.

Also referred to as: Hack A Business, Sell Your Data (former name)

## Numbers
- CTR 1.54% to 3.27% after rename (Tizzy, 2026-05, self-reported)
- 1K CCU and 6K USD/month within two weeks (Tizzy, 2026-06, self-reported)
- Bounce rate 21% to 15%, roughly doubling impressions and CCU (Tizzy, 2026-07, self-reported)
- Co-owned game making ~1M Robux/72h; cutscene cut bounce ~22% to ~15-16% (Tizzy, 2026-07, self-reported)

## What creators say about it
- A clearer title and core fantasy lifted CTR from 1.54% to 3.27% (self-reported). ([Tizzy 2026-05 6:42](https://youtu.be/owMnhg3fn_M?t=402))
- Iterate daily or twice daily in the first week using player feedback and data; reached 1K CCU and 6K USD/month in two weeks (self-reported). ([Tizzy 2026-06](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI), [Tizzy 2026-07](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU))
- High bounce rate is the bottleneck: adding content and an index, plus a cutscene, cut bounce from ~21-22% to ~15-16% (self-reported). ([Tizzy 2026-06](https://youtu.be/pu2o80prNV8), [Tizzy 2026-07](https://youtu.be/EZzztgpSaTU), [Tizzy 2026-07](https://youtu.be/LZOohZHsJus))
- Use split before/after thumbnails with large logos, clear blue sky and high contrast, tested on mobile. ([Tizzy 2026-06](https://youtu.be/az7em1IoBhA))

## Transferable patterns
- Title clarity drives click-through rate
- Daily iteration in the first week
- Fix bounce rate before scaling impressions
- High-contrast thumbnails tested on mobile
