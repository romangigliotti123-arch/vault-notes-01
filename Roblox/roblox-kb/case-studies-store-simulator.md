# Case study: Store Simulator

**Genre:** Tycoon / simulator

Store tycoon by Mulch, the creator's first game. Launched with 100K Robux sponsorship that returned ~14.4k Robux net in 3 days. A 2023 ad-driven launch with a rushed release and broken leaderboard scaling died quickly.

## Numbers
- Sponsored for 100K Robux; ~14.4k Robux net back in 3 days (Mulch, self-reported, G287)
- 100K Robux ad spend; ~7x more likes despite fewer visits (Mulch, G1143, comparison baseline unspecified)

## What creators say about it
- Ads rarely pay back quickly: a 100K Robux sponsorship returned ~14.4k Robux net in 3 days (self-reported). ([Mulch 2021-09 0:54](https://youtu.be/yG44d_Xnbwg?t=54))
- Ads cannot fix weak retention: an ad-driven launch with a rushed release and broken leaderboard scaling died quickly. ([Mulch 2023-04 0:00](https://youtu.be/6perjdMg3T4?t=0))
- A later 100K Robux ad-spend game had about 7x more likes despite fewer visits; likes-to-visit ratio can signal engagement. ([Mulch 2025-06 2:45](https://youtu.be/B2vC4-QArWg?t=165))

## Transferable patterns
- Do not rely on ads to rescue a weak retention loop.
- Stress-test leaderboards and data scaling before paid launches.
- Track likes-to-visits ratio as an engagement signal.
