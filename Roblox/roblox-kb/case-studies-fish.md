# Case study: Fish

**Genre:** Fishing and collection

Roblox fishing and collection experience with nets, rarity and mutation trends, and timed events. Inspired by Stardew Valley and fishing games, and described by the speaker as one of the fastest-growing experiences on the platform.

Also referred to as: Fish (Roblox fishing game), Fish game ('It's to Fish', unnamed in transcript)

## Numbers
- rasdev (2026-10): estimated ~$400K/month at ~36K players (creator estimate, self-reported).
- Duckable (2025-03): speaker says it reached billions of players in a few months (speaker claim, unverified).
- janisjanis01 (2026-07): a 450,000 Robux offer for 30% of the game was rejected. The game is unnamed in the transcript, so attribution is uncertain.

## What creators say about it
- Adapt a proven non-Roblox idea with a unique twist. Stardew Valley inspired it, and the creator calls the result unique (janisjanis01, 2026-04 and 2026-08). ([janisjanis01 2026-04 0:40](https://youtu.be/xqHXDYbuF2w?t=40), [janisjanis01 2026-08 1:22](https://youtu.be/fjlOaEALWmg?t=82))
- A simple, high-quality gameplay loop is cheap to advertise (CallMeDogey, 2026-03). ([CallMeDogey 2026-03 4:46](https://youtu.be/7QfaQe4rsq8?t=286))
- Custom timed events with notifications create hype. Exploration-based games need a large explorable map (janisjanis01, 2025-07). ([janisjanis01 2025-07 4:46](https://youtu.be/pD8fKkzF8O8?t=286))
- Net-catching core with rarity and mutation trends borrowed from fishing games (janisjanis01, 2025-09). ([janisjanis01 2025-09 1:04](https://youtu.be/uNLDi_GO2MA?t=64))
- High-value hits are expensive to acquire: a 450,000 Robux offer for 30% was rejected (janisjanis01, 2026-07). ([janisjanis01 2026-07 1:03](https://youtu.be/OL1FJ17wzZE?t=63))
- A non-stud, stylized map look can serve as a visual differentiator (janisjanis01, 2026-02). ([janisjanis01 2026-02 8:03](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=483))

## Transferable patterns
- Adapt proven non-Roblox games with a unique twist
- Timed-event notifications for hype
- Simple loop that is cheap to advertise
- Stylized non-stud art direction
