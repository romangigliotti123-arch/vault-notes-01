# Case study: Sell Lemons

**Genre:** Idle clicker/tycoon

Lemon-stand idle tycoon with a fruit theme: click to sell, upgrade, hire managers, automate and rebirth. Offline income and a 2x offline earnings dev product drive monetization. Readable in about 30 seconds.

## Numbers
- 100K+ CCU and 43M visits (MoseyTube, 2026-06)
- About 100,000 players (MoseyTube, 2026-07, creator statement)

## What creators say about it
- Make the loop instantly readable: a dollar from dad, a lemon stand, click to sell, upgrade. ([MoseyTube 2026-06 0:57](https://youtu.be/Kh_XKYw8ocU?t=57))
- Introduce one mechanic at a time: no UI during the tutorial, and delay shop and starter packs until players are invested. ([Tizzy 2026-07](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg))
- Sell 2x offline earnings as a consumable dev product rather than a gamepass; the creator says it is likely the best-selling dev product. ([Tizzy 2026-06](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets))
- Slow reveals and server events helped drive 100K+ CCU and 43M visits. ([MoseyTube 2026-06](https://youtu.be/2rLYGu0v490))
- A working UI formula was reused by MonarchDev for Dig Relic; participation in an incrementality study (Roblox DevForum, 2026-10) validated ad attribution. ([MonarchDev 2026-08 3:41](https://youtu.be/MANBsA1MTzI?t=221), [Roblox DevForum 2026-10](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-rdc-recap-earnings-roas-reporting-incrementality/4909790))

## Transferable patterns
- 30-second readable loop
- Consumable dev product for an offline-earnings boost
- Delay monetization until players are invested
- Reuse a proven UI formula across projects
