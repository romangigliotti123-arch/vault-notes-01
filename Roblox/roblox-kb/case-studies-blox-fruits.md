# Case study: Blox Fruits

**Genre:** Anime RPG / grinder

Anime-inspired RPG where players collect fruit powers, grind levels and quests, trade items and fight. One of the platform's long-running top earners, with multi-year growth and large update-driven peaks.

Also referred to as: Blox Roots (transcript error)

## Numbers
- ~200K CCU in March 2026 (RoWatcher tracker blog, G1451)
- 27.5B visits; ~11.2B Robux from Premium payouts (speaker estimate, PineappleDev, G689)
- ~$100M in one year (speaker estimate, unverified, VuukStudios, G671)
- ~15k players with pay-to-fast monetisation (Roblox DevForum, G684)

## What creators say about it
- Layered progression with optional depth and forgiving early hours (StudioKrew GGAID analysis, 2026-05). Most recent guidance favours this over pure grind. ([StudioKrew (agency blog, GGAID data) 2026-05](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-may-2026/), [Cumin 2025-05 3:45](https://youtu.be/2auVezIcNAE?t=225))
- Repetitive template quests and heavy level locks make players bored despite high popularity (Cumin, 2025-05). Newer advice treats this as a risk to avoid. ([Cumin 2025-05 3:45](https://youtu.be/2auVezIcNAE?t=225))
- Trading economy and speculation on patch notes sustain play without events (StudioKrew, 2026-08). ([StudioKrew (agency blog, GGAID data) 2026-08](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-august-2026/))
- Monetise via developer products and game passes; top-grossing revenue came from these, not one store page (VuukStudios). The monetisation model was called debatable by Deconstructor of Fun. ([VuukStudios 2024-08 0:59](https://youtu.be/3J7Vp6NgYf4?t=59), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-10 33:07](https://youtu.be/oU3oz6Ddz7g?t=1987))
- Cloning big hits as a first project fails; players dislike worse versions of popular games (1Coal). Ambitious first projects are a warning sign (ArTheDev). ([1Coal 2023-08 0:20](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=20), [ArTheDev 2026-06 0:00](https://youtu.be/wG9XzLnJC5k?t=0))
- Title and icon: big title text, bracketed emoji and a focal character on the icon give visual flair. ([rasdev 2026-08 9:14](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=554), [rasdev 2026-09 5:25](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=325))

## Transferable patterns
- Use layered progression: optional depth, easy early hours.
- Trading and patch-note speculation can sustain engagement between events.
- Monetise through developer products and game passes.
- Use big title text and a single focal character on the icon.
