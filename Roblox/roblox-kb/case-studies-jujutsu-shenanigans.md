# Case study: Jujutsu Shenanigans

**Genre:** Anime battle royale / PvP action

Anime-themed battle royale with regular character and mechanic additions timed to anime seasons, plus non-PvP objectives. Its simple, high-contrast icon stands out on a crowded front page.

Also referred to as: Tze's Shenanigans

## Numbers
- Won Best Action Game (PocketGamer.biz, 2026-09)
- Won Best Action Game (GosuGamers, 2026-09)

## What creators say about it
- Time character and mechanic additions to anime seasons to keep the battle royale growing. ([RoWatcher (tracker blog) 2026-03](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-10-fastest-growing-roblox-games-in-march-2026))
- Layer non-PvP objectives on top of anime PvP to lengthen sessions. ([RoWatcher (tracker blog) 2026-03](https://rowatcher.com/news/top-10-roblox-games-by-players-march-2026-data-breakdown))
- Negative space and a limited palette (yellow and blue on white) make the icon stand out on a loud front page. ([rasdev 2026-08 6:46](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=406), [rasdev 2026-09 5:25](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=325))
- Won Best Action Game awards in September 2026, from two outlets. ([PocketGamer.biz 2026-09](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-reveals-2026-innovation-awards-winners/), [GosuGamers 2026-09](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/79176-full-list-of-winners-at-the-roblox-innovation-awards-2026))

## Transferable patterns
- Season-timed content drops tied to an existing IP
- Non-PvP objectives to extend sessions
- Negative-space icon design for crowded discovery pages
- Awards as credibility signals
