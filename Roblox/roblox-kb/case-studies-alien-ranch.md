# Case study: Alien Ranch

**Genre:** Farming / creature sim

Small Roblox farming game by Tizzy with a strong core loop and deep upgrade paths with friends. Steady player base, but many bugs, no updates and a clunky first-time experience held back CCU. Farmer Die was built to fill its gap.

Also referred to as: Farmer Die (gap reference)

## Numbers
- No CCU or revenue figures given.

## What creators say about it
- A strong, unique core loop and deep upgrade paths with friends drew a steady player base. ([Tizzy 2026-05 1:12](https://youtu.be/BNdHkeKRUdE?t=72), [Tizzy 2026-05](https://youtu.be/0pMW1T0rdUI))
- Many bugs and no updates held it back; the creator built Farmer Die to fill the gap. ([Tizzy 2026-05 1:12](https://youtu.be/BNdHkeKRUdE?t=72))
- A clunky first-time experience and bugs held back CCU; onboarding matters. ([Tizzy 2026-05](https://youtu.be/0pMW1T0rdUI))
- It appeared on the home shelf for a while, indicating some platform visibility. ([Tizzy 2026-05](https://youtu.be/inWzxY26NXo))

## Transferable patterns
- Invest in first-time onboarding to convert new players.
- Ship regular updates to avoid decay.
- Use a competitor's unfilled gap as a new project.
