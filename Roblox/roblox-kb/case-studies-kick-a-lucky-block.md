# Case study: Kick a Lucky Block

**Genre:** Escape-tsunami loop with kick mechanic and social trading

VuukStudios added a kick mechanic to the escape-tsunami loop with a one-sentence pitch and social trading. A later build uses skill-check tap kicks, pre-acquisition RNG and get-stronger progression.

## Numbers
- Rising to top experiences under the updated discovery algorithm (Roblox Investor Relations, G1937). No CCU or revenue figures given.

## What creators say about it
- Copying front-page hits dies within a month (Tizzy, G1654). Superseded: the more recent Tizzy video (2026-09, G2226) reports the game as long-lived via live ops. ([Tizzy 2026-05 2:23](https://youtu.be/BNdHkeKRUdE?t=143), [Tizzy 2026-09](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A))
- Adding a kick mechanic to a proven loop, with a one-sentence pitch and social trading, made the game succeed. ([VuukStudios 2026-06 6:19](https://youtu.be/catPKgFIZbQ?t=379))
- Updated discovery algorithm pushed the game to top experiences quickly, per Roblox Investor Relations. ([Roblox Investor Relations 2026-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm))
- Skill-check tap on kicks, pre-acquisition RNG and get-stronger progression; long-lived via live ops (most recent view). ([Tizzy 2026-09](https://youtu.be/CRCcsYEB_6A))

## Transferable patterns
- Write a one-sentence pitch that carries the whole game.
- Layer one new mechanic onto a proven loop.
- Use live ops to extend lifespan beyond the launch spike.
- Add a skill-check input to make a simple action feel interactive.
