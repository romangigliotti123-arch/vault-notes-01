# Case study: RB Battles

**Genre:** Community mini-game tournament (YouTuber event)

Community-run YouTuber mini-game tournament spanning several Roblox games. Format changed each season, from event page to custom challenges to mocap. Funding was the main obstacle.

Also referred to as: Creators on the Blox tournament

## Numbers
- Championship planning took 12-14 months (Roblox, G617). No attendance or revenue figures given.

## What creators say about it
- Community-run YouTuber events can cross-promote several Roblox games; exploring uncharted territory is the draw. ([Emancyphur 2021-02 15:38](https://youtu.be/0B1Q2IZSlU8?t=938), [Roblox DevForum 2021-02](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/learn-to-script-learn-to-code-in-5-steps/1043259))
- Let community feedback choose which games are featured; competitive events need 12-14 months of planning. ([Roblox 2023-09 18:19](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=1099))
- Format evolved per season: event page (season 1), custom challenges (season 2), mocap (season 3). Most recent source (2025-11) reports funding as the main obstacle. ([Creators on the Blox 2025-11 42:26](https://youtu.be/W8ghgtipQgI?t=2546))

## Transferable patterns
- Use community votes to pick featured games.
- Iterate the event format each season rather than repeating it.
- Plan long lead times and budget early for events.
- Cross-game collaboration as shared promotion.
