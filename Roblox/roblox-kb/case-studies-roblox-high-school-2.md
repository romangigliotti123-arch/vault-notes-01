# Case study: Roblox High School 2

**Genre:** Social roleplay (high school)

High school roleplay game by Cinder Studio, described as their flagship. The house editor was iterated across 2014, 2018 and 2021 versions to be simpler and easier on all platforms. Clickable menu options give a cleaner UI.

## Numbers
- No CCU or revenue figures given.

## What creators say about it
- Iterate the core house editor across versions (2014, 2018, 2021) to make it simpler and cross-platform. ([Roblox Creators 2023-02 1:30](https://youtu.be/BsyU-SJ9L-c?t=90))
- Clickable menu options give a cleaner UI; a 2021 note said this adds complexity but is more elegantly presented. ([Roblox Creators 2021-12 4:48](https://youtu.be/cY1B5_x4RoU?t=288))
- Older copy-paste roleplay games with near-identical looks still drew players in their era. ([rasdev 2026-06 0:00](https://youtu.be/8lWfkv6dmpo?t=0))

## Transferable patterns
- Simplify core editors and iterate them across versions and platforms.
- Clickable menus give a cleaner presentation than complex controls.
- Near-identical looks can retain players in established genres.
