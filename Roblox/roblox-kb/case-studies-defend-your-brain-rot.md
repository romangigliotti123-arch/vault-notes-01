# Case study: Defend Your Brain Rot

**Genre:** Defense game (brainrot meme theme)

Brainrot-themed defense game built by MoseyTube with Claude via iterative prompting. The speaker's own game, which earned Robux (no figure given in one mention). Used a small test ad budget to reach 20k+ visits.

Also referred to as: Defend Your Brainrot, Brain Rot (trend), Brainrot, Brain Rot games (trend), Brain Rot (Roblox game, viral TikTok meme origin)

## Numbers
- 20k visits, ~$177 via DevEx (MoseyTube, self-reported, G1699)
- $10/day for 10 days ad spend reached 20k+ visits, then organic home-recommendation traffic (MoseyTube, G1727)

## What creators say about it
- Built with Claude through iterative prompting; the speaker shipped it as a learning release. ([MoseyTube 2026-05 3:24](https://youtu.be/400F-3qXoHE?t=204))
- Small games earn little: 20k visits earned about $177 via DevEx (self-reported). Treat each release as learning, not income. ([MoseyTube 2026-05 3:04](https://youtu.be/JG37MoDpOTw?t=184))
- A $10/day, 10-day ad test reached 20k+ visits, after which organic home-recommendation traffic arrived (self-reported). ([MoseyTube 2026-06 4:34](https://youtu.be/d9UxSCiz3us?t=274))
- For a trending genre, copy the concept with a unique twist rather than cloning it. ([VuukStudios 2025-11 2:07](https://youtu.be/AbjI7ygMnao?t=127))
- Brainrot is cited as a viral, always-online pay-to-win example that Roblox does not favour, per the framing given. ([Deconstructor of Fun 2026-04 11:37](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=697))
- Off-platform viral meme trends are an idea source; a viral TikTok meme spawned a huge Roblox game. ([rasdev 2026-08 3:03](https://youtu.be/17DGro3FUlM?t=183))

## Transferable patterns
- Use AI-assisted iterative prompting to ship small projects quickly.
- Frame each small release as a learning unit, not an earnings bet.
- Run a small fixed ad test to see whether the algorithm picks up organic home traffic.
- Scan off-platform viral content (e.g., TikTok) for game ideas
- Add a distinct twist to trending concepts instead of cloning
- Avoid always-online pay-to-win mechanics that draw criticism
