# Case study: Spin or Die

**Genre:** Party elimination (bottle-spin, last player standing)

A bottle-spin kill-last-player party game by kra_DeZ, built fast with AI help from two baseplates. Based on a 2023 TikTok trend that had no Roblox version at the time.

Also referred to as: Spin or Die!

## Numbers
- ~1.5K CCU after small updates revived it (kra_DeZ, self-reported) [G2087]
- ~2k CCU after fast build, ad-tested thumbnails and weekly updates (kra_DeZ, self-reported) [G2150]
- 30 then 90 ad credits on a winning thumbnail; ~2K CCU (kra_DeZ, self-reported) [G2397]
- Peak 2.1k CCU (kra_DeZ, self-reported) [G2441]

## What creators say about it
- Built in about 5 days with AI help; the sibling game was abandoned and declined while this one kept updating and grew CCU from events and hype. ([kra_DeZ 2026-08 0:00](https://youtu.be/oMI-GhUcfFE?t=0))
- Adapting a viral TikTok trend with no Roblox version yet filled a gap and peaked at 2.1k CCU (self-reported). ([kra_DeZ 2026-10 0:00](https://youtu.be/sB9W5BXjQpk?t=0))
- Small updates revived a stalled game to ~1.5K CCU (self-reported). ([kra_DeZ 2026-08](https://youtu.be/g6z18oHjbYE))
- Earlier advice credited ad-tested thumbnails and weekly updates after flatlining (~2k CCU). Most recent note (2026-10) supersedes the ad emphasis: growth came from later updates, not just ads. ([kra_DeZ 2026-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/cJ-YzHHhQs8?t=0), [kra_DeZ 2026-10](https://youtu.be/lrubtajkaeI))

## Transferable patterns
- Build quickly from a proven viral trend that has no Roblox version yet
- Ship regular small updates and events to break plateaus
- Ad-test thumbnails, but treat ads as a supplement to content updates
