# Case study: Rivals

**Genre:** Competitive FPS (PvP)

Ranked-capable multiplayer shooter with 1v1 duels scaling to 5v5, per-weapon progression, skins and a cosmetic case economy. Launched weak, was reworked into PvP duels, and won Best Shooter at the 2026 awards.

Also referred to as: RIVALS

## Numbers
- ~291K CCU in March 2026 (RoWatcher tracker blog, G1450 comparison point, not creator-stated)
- Improved rewarded-prompt discoverability gave about 4x impressions in one update (Roblox Creators, G1223)

## What creators say about it
- Rework a failing prototype into a proven competitive loop. Rivals launched with a few hundred players and wasn't fun; the team moved it to PvP duels, a genre rare on Roblox at the time. ([Roblox 2026-04 0:14](https://youtu.be/IuJWzpvpIa8?t=14), [Roblox Newsroom 2026-09](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/2026-roblox-innovation-awards))
- Retention comes from loss aversion and rewards on loss: visible daily tasks, per-weapon progression and XP even on a loss; players grind to protect rank. ([rasdev 2026-04 2:02](https://youtu.be/M-GCMK1Tryk?t=122), [rasdev 2026-04 0:00](https://youtu.be/yFKY2FlwHjE?t=0), [Duckable 2025-03 0:01](https://youtu.be/I9eyeEG6dpc?t=1))
- Use Party API so joined groups are matchmade together and stay together across subplaces (Roblox, 2025-09). Friends-based play is a retention lever. ([Roblox 2025-09 11:00](https://youtu.be/lUc3Xj8Ojd8?t=660))
- Streak-recovery monetisation: build a streak on wins and let players pay to recover it on death (Duckable's referenced product model). ([Duckable 2026-05 8:18](https://youtu.be/X9YYjb_mY5w?t=498))
- Design for touchscreen first: controls, hitbox forgiveness and matchmaking (StudioKrew GGAID analysis). Rewarded-video placement in the world and in weapon trials is a best-in-class example (Roblox Creator Docs). ([StudioKrew (agency blog, GGAID data) 2026-05](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-may-2026/), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/shooter), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/ad-placements))
- A test-build bug can be turned into a new game mode rather than just patched; this helped Rivals win Best Shooter. ([Roblox 2026-09 8:58](https://youtu.be/HXZRH-2zurk?t=538))

## Transferable patterns
- Keep matchmaking instant and multiplayer-only for competitive loops.
- Reward effort on losses so players keep returning while ranked.
- Pair cosmetics (skins, cases) with a competitive genre; a concept-art Discord can feed player-submitted cosmetics.
- Use a high-contrast icon (white background, red character) to pop in feeds.
