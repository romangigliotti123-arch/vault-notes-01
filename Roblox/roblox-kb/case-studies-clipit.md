# Case study: Clipit

**Genre:** Social clip-sharing platform / feed-style game

A clip-sharing platform with strong European and Asian reach, and an earlier feed-style game with in-game clip creation. Precursor to Roblox Moments. Won Best Audio Design at the Roblox Innovation Awards.

Also referred to as: Clip It

## What creators say about it
- Surface clips across languages using local recommendations and automatic translation; funny content crosses language barriers. ([Roblox 2024-09 16:20](https://youtu.be/T7w3Q54JOPE?t=980))
- An early feed-style game with in-game clip creation and commenting was a precursor to Roblox Moments. ([Deconstructor of Fun 2025-09](https://youtu.be/8pruyZlxMI4))
- Audio design won an award, but Yeucc argues it was miscategorized, so awards are not proof of category fit. ([Creators on the Blox 2025-10 58:48](https://youtu.be/XtWTnz56ZpM?t=3528))

## Transferable patterns
- Build in-game clip creation and commenting to drive social retention
- Use local recommendations plus auto-translation for international reach
- Invest in audio design as a standout feature
