# Case study: Pressure

**Genre:** Horror (randomised rooms)

A Roblox horror game with randomised rooms, layered sound, varied threats and death-unlocked lore. Several creators analysed it; one started as a Doors reskin and one overreached in scope.

## What creators say about it
- Randomised room horror with layered sound, varied threats and death-unlocked lore keeps replays fresh. The expendable-prisoner premise reinforces tension. ([Daiorg 2024-08 0:00](https://youtu.be/Yku_51UE_GM?t=0))
- Started as a Doors reskin but evolved into a more unique experience. ([Daiorg 2025-02 4:34](https://youtu.be/nutv8RT5UrQ?t=274))
- Best lobby and storytelling in the set: balanced challenge, strong audio and a fresh eye mechanic. ([GnomeCode 2025-02 12:10](https://youtu.be/-oZIdFe8qHk?t=730))
- Most recent note: a boredom project that grew too big for its scope needed an overhaul; smaller, focused design was the lesson learned. This supersedes earlier praise for scale. ([Creators on the Blox 2025-11 0:00](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=0))
- A recognisable horror cutout from the game was used to draw clicks alongside a Doors reference. ([bubDEV 2024-08 16:31](https://youtu.be/th2DJ7-5MnU?t=991))

## Transferable patterns
- Control scope: smaller, focused designs beat over-ambitious builds
- Evolve a derivative concept into an original one
- Layered audio and varied threats for horror replayability
- Recognisable IP characters can help thumbnails draw clicks
