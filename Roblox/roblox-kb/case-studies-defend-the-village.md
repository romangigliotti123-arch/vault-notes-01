# Case study: Defend the Village

**Genre:** Zombie defense (tower-defense-style with classes)

Zombie defense game by janisjanis01. Players fight zombies for XP and tokens, then buy classes and turrets. Promoted with a Clash of Clans joke Short and used in a 'low quality vs high quality' comparison Short.

Also referred to as: Defend the Village (zombie defense)

## Numbers
- Players rose from 0 to 7 after a TikTok push (janisjanis01, G1493)

## What creators say about it
- A simple fight-earn-upgrade loop (XP, tokens, class and turret purchases) kept players playing. ([janisjanis01 2026-08 2:04](https://youtu.be/fjlOaEALWmg?t=124))
- A Clash of Clans joke Short was used to promote this older game; players rose from 0 to 7 after TikTok. ([janisjanis01 2026-01 4:58](https://youtu.be/IWzAHgHPhhU?t=298))
- Intense gameplay was repurposed as a 'low quality vs high quality' comparison Short. ([janisjanis01 2025-06 2:51](https://youtu.be/5LMGVfslAYs?t=171))

## Transferable patterns
- Use meme or comparison short formats to market old games.
- Repurpose back-catalog games as content.
- Use a fight-earn-upgrade loop with currency and unlocks.
