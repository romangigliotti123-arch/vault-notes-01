# Case study: Pet Simulator 99

**Genre:** Simulator (pets, eggs, rebirths, trading)

Pet and egg simulator with rebirths, trading, playtime rewards, and limited exclusive eggs; described as one of the most popular Roblox games. Frequently cloned, and clones lacked its rarity, balance, and server-side systems.

## Numbers
- RoWatcher tracker blog (2026-03, third-party, not creator-verified): deep reward loop sustains long-term retention at 180k+ CCU.
- StudioKrew agency blog (2026-05, GGAID data, third-party): runs as a product with a release calendar and measured LiveOps cadence (no figure given).

## What creators say about it
- A rebirth loop, weekly updates, and limited exclusive eggs drove retention and spending for a full year (Mulch, 2024-12). ([Mulch 2024-12 0:00](https://youtu.be/tdnhCXx3srA?t=0))
- Reward every egg open and coin break with sound and effects, and show odds. Rarest pets stay hidden-odds and lottery-like (janisjanis01, 2025-05). ([janisjanis01 2025-05 0:25](https://youtu.be/Shjq-Xd9Kmw?t=25))
- Lucky gamepasses give modest odds gains; they are worth buying mainly when spending Robux on high-cost eggs, not for normal progression (Mulch, 2023-12). ([Mulch 2023-12 0:00](https://youtu.be/JwkTY1OqOjg?t=0))
- Clones that copy the surface miss the systems: the speaker's clone lacked rarity, balance, and server-side logic (TheDankDeveloper, 2023-12). ([TheDankDeveloper 2023-12](https://youtu.be/1jRaSYhrEcU))
- Group rewards every 6 hours and trading keep the game uncompletable and drive return visits (janisjanis01, 2024-06). Playtime rewards with small and big goals add pacing (Stewiepfing, 2024-02). ([janisjanis01 2024-06 1:00](https://youtu.be/NWyK_PbFdxE?t=60), [Stewiepfing 2024-02 0:42](https://youtu.be/v8tFfBTr6M8?t=42))
- Teleport and hoverboard convenience purchases sell because players avoid walking the large map (Alex Builds Games, 2026-06). Community felt the long gap since the last major update (Mulch, 2024-11), so keep LiveOps cadence steady. ([Alex Builds Games 2026-06 10:19](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=619), [Mulch 2024-11 0:00](https://youtu.be/axxmwooE-J0?t=0), [StudioKrew (agency blog, GGAID data) 2026-05](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-may-2026/))

## Transferable patterns
- Convenience purchases tied to a large map
- Visible odds plus sound feedback on every open
- Uncompletable trading and group-reward loops
- Steady weekly update and exclusive-egg cadence
