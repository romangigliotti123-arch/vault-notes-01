# Case study: Stepford County Railway

**Genre:** Railway simulation

Railway sim by Last Level Studios with block-based signalling, semi-automatic signalling, announcements and platform screens. Built a ranked, training-based community and grew by word of mouth.

Also referred to as: SCR, Stepford County Railway (SCR)

## Numbers
- 26K group and 6K Discord over 18 months (Last Level Studios, self-reported, G100)
- 50M visits, grown through word of mouth alone (Last Level Studios, self-reported, G667)

## What creators say about it
- Tying community ranks to gameplay unlocks, with training-based progression, sustained a 26K group and 6K Discord over 18 months. ([Last Level Studios 2019-09 0:01](https://youtu.be/hnCm955Du8w?t=1))
- The default Roblox GUI navigation could not handle many buttons; a custom Xbox selection system was required. ([Last Level Studios 2019-10 8:44](https://youtu.be/vY0om5O-Oh8?t=524))
- Block-based sensors with a server-side in-block table scale better and make track editing easier than per-track logic. ([Last Level Studios 2020-08 0:00](https://youtu.be/uYIE3IVmKY8?t=0))
- Depth in a niche sim (semi-automatic signalling, announcements, platform screens) grew the game to 50M visits by word of mouth alone. ([Last Level Studios 2023-12 2:36](https://youtu.be/kQ6JMjjGvow?t=156))

## Transferable patterns
- Link community ranks to in-game progression.
- Build custom input for complex UI, especially on console.
- Use data-driven, server-side systems so content is easy to edit.
- Niche depth can drive word-of-mouth growth without paid promotion.
