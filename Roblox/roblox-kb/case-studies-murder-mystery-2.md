# Case study: Murder Mystery 2

**Genre:** Social deduction (round-based hidden role)

Round-based social deduction game with a rising murderer chance, tradeable knife and gun skins, and seasonal drops. Created 2014; cited as a durable top-10 title for 12+ years.

## Numbers
- OtakuKart (2026-09, third-party): 4 billion visits in Q3 2026, second tier behind Steal an Egg.
- Roblox public API data (2026-11): ~548k concurrent players in 2026.
- RoWatcher tracker blog (2026-03, third-party): top-10 for 12+ years.

## What creators say about it
- Loot boxes monetized high-spending players (Roblox DevForum, 2023-12). Newer advice (janisjanis01, 2024-06) supersedes this: cosmetic-only Godly skins avoid pay-to-win while leaderboards keep players competing. ([Roblox DevForum 2023-12](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921), [janisjanis01 2024-06 0:00](https://youtu.be/NWyK_PbFdxE?t=0))
- The murderer chance rises as you play, creating a never-ending loop (janisjanis01, 2024-06). ([janisjanis01 2024-06 0:00](https://youtu.be/NWyK_PbFdxE?t=0))
- Social deduction plus self-sustaining knife and gun skin trading kept it top-10 for 12+ years (RoWatcher, 2026-03). ([RoWatcher (tracker blog) 2026-03](https://rowatcher.com/news/top-10-roblox-games-by-players-march-2026-data-breakdown))
- Seasonal tradeable items revalue an existing collection. Twelve years of drop history drove an all-time peak (StudioKrew, 2026-08). ([StudioKrew (agency blog, GGAID data) 2026-08](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-august-2026/))
- Remix a proven concept: inspired by Garry's Mod (janisjanis01, 2026-08). ([janisjanis01 2026-08 1:22](https://youtu.be/fjlOaEALWmg?t=82))
- A 12-player round-based hidden-role survival format has lasted over a decade (Roblox public API, 2026-11; see also durable-staple notes). ([Roblox public API 2026-11](https://www.roblox.com/games/142823291/), [VuukStudios 2026-10 11:05](https://youtu.be/DTJeR2gJSZo?t=665), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-10 1:01:01](https://youtu.be/oU3oz6Ddz7g?t=3661))

## Transferable patterns
- Tradeable cosmetic economy with seasonal drops
- Rising-risk loop per play
- Cosmetic-only monetization
- Remixing a proven hidden-role format
