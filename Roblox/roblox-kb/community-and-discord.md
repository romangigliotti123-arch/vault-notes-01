_community building, Discord, groups, social following, player feedback channels._

## Overview
Community advice centres on a Discord hub, fed by Roblox group membership and bots that sync rank to roles. Structure should match game design: flat fan groups for simple games, deep ranked hierarchies where rank unlocks gameplay. Creators stress budgeting time for community management, delegating moderation with escalation paths, and keeping damaging permissions with the creator. Suggestion floods are handled with merging tools, staff-ping controls and warning-based moderation. Fan pressure should not dictate all design, and creators may hand the community to fans if they cannot commit long term. Open-sourcing modules and tools builds reputation and inbound help, mostly from one creator. A 2026 creator advises building a multi-platform audience before release, which conflicts with single-platform advice. Scale figures (2019 to 2023) show large growth but are self-reported.

## Rules and findings
- **Build a Discord/group community before launch** (multiple-creators): Roblox says initial users are very hard to attract without community support; start about a month before release, not much earlier. AlvinBlox: create a Discord or group first and invite friends/testers, then grow slowly (10 to 50 to 100 players) before scaling marketing. Newest source 2025-04-28.
- **Funnel all players and followers into one Discord** (multiple-creators): BruceDevs funnels players from every game into a Discord so each release starts from a larger base. Roblox says move Twitter followers into Discord/Guilded so you can ping them directly. A long-lived Discord (World Zero ~10 years) means you aren't beholden to algorithm swings. Monitor channels actively for patterns, not just create them. ## Where creators agree
- Build a Discord community early and funnel followers into it (Roblox, AlvinBlox, BruceDevs, Tizzy, TheDankDeveloper).
- Listen to feedback but keep final design control (Roblox Creators, Roblox).
- Tease and announce updates publicly (Roblox, AlvinBlox).
- Credit or pay community contributors (Roblox Creators).
- Focus on one platform rather than everywhere (Roblox Creators).
- Offer value when networking (Tizzy, BruceDevs, AlvinBlox).
- Official Roblox docs (T4193, T4244, T4789, Q862) and indie creators (T3837, T3510) agree a community should be built early and engaged continuously.
- Official docs and indie creators agree on involving the community in content and product choices (T3775, T3835, T3650).
- Discord is treated as the main announcement and feedback hub (T3510 indie; T4808 official).
- Funnel players and creators into one Discord hub (T6102, T6371, T6499, T6858, T6800).
- Build audience before launch through consistent public posts (T6012, T6487, T6102).
- Check existing documentation and forums before asking for help (T5193, Q899).
- Official and indie sources agree on team norms: respect, permission before using others' work, calm conflict handling (T7332, T4957, T4966, T6932).
- Act on bug reports and player feedback quickly (T6489, T6135).
- Loyal core players matter more than algorithm swings (T6858, Q1011, T6095).
- Set up a feedback channel before testing or beta (T11622, T15506).
- Use Discord as a feedback and moderation hub (T11364, T13664, T13931).
- Ask for specific input and act on it visibly (T12684, T13606, T13931).
- Free resources or community-building work help grow membership (T13758, T13777, T15506).
- A Discord hub, with bots syncing roles and moderation, is the working community base (Last Level Studios; Lnor Roblox organised Discord via a management bot).
- Pre-launch audience building matters (Last Level Studios advised a Discord before launch; studio rook. built an audience before release).
- Constructive, structured feedback channels beat ad hoc harassment of developers (CORSO; Last Level Studios on routing feedback).
- Polls and early channel feedback help decide direction before building (CORSO polls; Last Level Studios on feedback channels).
- Sharing open-source code builds reputation and helps the platform (Last Level Studios; Emancyphur on answering questions publicly).

## Where creators disagree
- **Where to promote the game**
  - SmartyRBX: promote in Discord promo channels only if allowed (T291), but avoid game-dev servers for player traffic (T292).
  - AlvinBlox: share with dev communities for feedback (T1803).
  - BruceDevs: advertise to interested players on Discord before paying for sponsors (T2415).
