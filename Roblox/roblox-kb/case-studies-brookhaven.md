# Case study: Brookhaven

**Genre:** Kids' roleplay life sim

Long-running kids' roleplay (house, job, family) built on socialising, hidden secrets and creator camera tools. Sold by its solo creator to Voldex in Feb 2025, reportedly priced above Bloxburg's sale.

Also referred to as: Brook Haven, Brookhaven RP

## Numbers
- ~800k daily players and ~$150k/day earnings, speaker benchmark (VuukStudios) G788
- ~$56M earned in 2024 after ~10 years (VuukStudios) G938
- ~1.4M new players/day; top-10 earner despite weak per-player monetisation (Roblox) G2348
- ~400k players, scale comparison (rasdev) G1676
- Avatar editor A/B test: playtime +14%, D0-D7 retention +8.5%; contextual upsells ARPD +10% (Roblox) G1301
- Matchmaking language config: +3% significant playtime (Roblox) G1281
- 41.5B visits (PineappleDev, 2024-01)
- ~17B Robux from Premium payouts, extrapolated from visits (PineappleDev speaker estimate, 2024-01)
- 69.13B visits (RoWatcher tracker blog, 2026-03)
- 621,977 players, #2 top-playing-now (Roblox public API, 2026-11)

## What creators say about it
- Roleplay socialising is the loop: primary players socialise, secondary explorers find hidden secrets; 'meet people, create stories' stays central. ([Roblox Creators 2021-12 25:14](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=1514), [SmartyRBX 2023-06 1:22](https://youtu.be/gc7K98RlOik?t=82), [rasdev 2026-04 0:41](https://youtu.be/M-GCMK1Tryk?t=41), [rasdev 2026-06 0:00](https://youtu.be/8lWfkv6dmpo?t=0))
- A/B test with long windows: an avatar editor test over 28 days lifted playtime 14% and D0-D7 retention 8.5%; contextual upsells lifted ARPD 10%. ([Roblox 2025-09](https://youtu.be/j6OPA95-1O0), [Roblox 2025-09](https://youtu.be/V7MKrGggOOA))
- Player count and earnings diverge: a similar-count game earns far more, showing monetisation depth matters; Brookhaven monetises at a lower rate than Steal a Brainrot. ([Roblox DevForum 2023-12](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-10 59:37](https://youtu.be/oU3oz6Ddz7g?t=3577), [Roblox 2026-10 23:03](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=1383))
- Long life compounds: about 10 years live and ~$56M in 2024 (VuukStudios, creator claim); acquisition by Voldex kept the creator involved for 6 months. ([VuukStudios 2024-12 18:56](https://youtu.be/xBPJnMgLoUE?t=1136), [Roblox 2025-09 31:45](https://youtu.be/Z3T33q4-Y_A?t=1905), [rasdev 2026-10 4:49](https://youtu.be/4GEHEzhvhwI?t=289))
- Built-in creator camera and removing UI made content creation easier; a Creator Cam supports streamers. ([Roblox Creators 2022-05 0:00](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=0), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox))
- Kids' audience brings policy limits: ad targeting limits apply to younger players, and monetisation is kid-focused. ([Roblox DevForum 2023-08](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-successfully-grow-your-game-ft-senseideveloper-vikinglaw-promoting-and-advertising-your-game/2535279), [VuukStudios 2024-03 2:26](https://youtu.be/rbVG7YBwY2k?t=146), [Roblox Creators 2022-12 1:08](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=68))
- Open-ended roleplay with no win condition or fail state lets players create their own stories and supports 45-60+ minute sessions. ([RoWatcher (tracker blog) 2026-03](https://rowatcher.com/news/top-10-roblox-games-by-players-march-2026-data-breakdown), [VuukStudios 2026-07 1:21](https://youtu.be/Bltv__PoNcA?t=81))
- A social hangout layer with voice chat drives long sessions and co-play retention. ([StudioKrew (agency blog, GGAID data) 2026-05](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-may-2026/))
- Steady landmark and bank updates plus a pronounced seasonal shape keep a mature game visible. Note the 2024 visit figure (41.5B) predates the 2026 figure (69.13B); both are as stated by their creators. ([StudioKrew (agency blog, GGAID data) 2026-08](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-august-2026/), [PineappleDev 2024-01 1:41](https://youtu.be/-LXvkK5YkNM?t=101), [RoWatcher (tracker blog) 2026-03](https://rowatcher.com/news/top-10-roblox-games-by-players-march-2026-data-breakdown))
- Established giants have survived repeated audience shifts, so long-running titles remain valid comparison benchmarks. ([Player.One 2026-00](https://www.player.one/robloxs-steal-egg-beating-some-platforms-biggest-games-164086), [GosuGamers 2026-00](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78741-animal-hospital-becomes-roblox-s-latest-horror-sensation-here-s-everything-you-need-to-know))

## Transferable patterns
- A/B test avatar, UI and matchmaking config with fixed windows
- Ship built-in creator tools for content creators
- Plan for kids-audience ad and monetisation limits
- Long-term maintenance and staffed acquisition can compound revenue
- Open-ended sandbox with no fail state to extend session length
- Built-in social hangout (voice chat) for co-play retention
- Seasonal cadence combined with steady small updates
- Revenue extrapolated from visits is a speaker estimate, not a platform figure

## Merged from other notes of the same game
- Brookhaven RP: Open-ended roleplay hangout with no win condition or fail state. Players build their own stories in sessions of 45-60+ minutes. Repeatedly cited as Roblox's largest game by visits.
