# Case study: Phantom Forces

**Genre:** Realistic FPS

Realistic multiplayer shooter with gun customisation, multi-loadout systems and high-quality animations. Went viral early, declined due to long rounds, and is cited as a benchmark that new developers should not start with.

## Numbers
- No CCU or revenue figures given in the input.

## What creators say about it
- Maintain health with frequent small bug-fix and optimisation updates (Last Level Studios). ([Last Level Studios 2019-10 4:38](https://youtu.be/vY0om5O-Oh8?t=278))
- Procedural CFrame-based view model animation is one technique for responsive weapon motion (DevForum). ([Roblox DevForum 2020-05](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/procedural-animation-what-it-is-and-how-to-do-it/564782))
- Multi-loadout customisation needs DataStores for saved loadouts (Emancyphur). ([Emancyphur 2020-08 1:08:24](https://youtu.be/LjETISdUBp8?t=4104))
- Deep gun customisation went viral but declined due to long rounds (Daiorg). Round length matters for retention in shooters. ([Daiorg 2025-08 8:55](https://youtu.be/GkKUU9mCgwc?t=535))
- A big FPS project is not a beginner's first game (Last Level Studios, SmartyRBX). Realistic shooters appeal to older audiences (GameMakers; Roblox Creator Docs). ([Last Level Studios 2020-02 10:24](https://youtu.be/_LdpUJ41cso?t=624), [SmartyRBX 2025-06 1:06](https://youtu.be/MAnvGNgBKgQ?t=66), [GameMakers 2021-01 55:11](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=3311), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox))
- Differentiate from competitors: Brave Turtles chose a more imaginative direction to stand apart from realistic shooters. ([Roblox Creators 2024-08 12:19](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=739))

## Transferable patterns
- Keep shooter rounds short to protect retention.
- Store loadouts in DataStores.
- Ship small patches often to keep a mature game healthy.
- Differentiate when competing in a crowded genre.
