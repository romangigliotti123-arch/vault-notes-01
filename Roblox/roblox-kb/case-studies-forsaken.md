# Case study: Forsaken

**Genre:** Externally promoted Roblox game (genre not stated)

A well-known Roblox game with loyal players and external promotion. Mentioned by Cumin and bubDEV; the sources give limited dev-lesson detail.

## What creators say about it
- Longevity came from who plays the game (loyal players) and external promotion, not only algorithm favour. ([bubDEV 2025-12 10:57](https://youtu.be/7z8rfg_jBuk?t=657))

## Transferable patterns
- Invest in external promotion to build a loyal player base
- Measure longevity by player loyalty, not just recommendation reach
