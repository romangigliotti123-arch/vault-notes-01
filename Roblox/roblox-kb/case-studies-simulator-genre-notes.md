# Case study: Simulator genre notes (generic references)

**Genre:** Simulator (genre-level, not a single game)

Not one game. Generic simulator observations from several creators: a plant-and-harvest garden boost, a one-loop coin-breaking and rebirth design, and a low-budget single-player test.

Also referred to as: Simulator (unnamed), Simulator games (generic reference)

## Numbers
- Single-player simulator ran on $8/day for 7 days (Yann, self-reported, G1712)

## What creators say about it
- Creator believes no simulator has a plant-and-harvest garden boosting power, stamina and gold, so positioned it as unique. This is an unverified claim; check the genre before relying on it. ([janisjanis01 2025-03 2:55](https://youtu.be/Pruto1wqxAU?t=175))
- Core loop of breaking coins, buying upgrades and rebirthing makes the whole game one repeating loop. ([rasdev 2026-04 0:00](https://youtu.be/M-GCMK1Tryk?t=0))
- Single-player genres need less ad budget; an $8/day, 7-day test ran on a small spend. ([Yann 2026-05 4:05](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=245))

## Transferable patterns
- Differentiate with one mechanic the genre lacks, and verify the claim first.
- A single repeating loop makes the pitch easy to explain.
- Test single-player games on small budgets since they need less ad spend.
