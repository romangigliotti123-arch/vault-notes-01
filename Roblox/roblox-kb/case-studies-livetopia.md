# Case study: Livetopia

**Genre:** Open-world life/roleplay playground

An open-world playground with lots of content from launch and furnished housing. Integrated brands organically (Lego car) and is cited for subscriptions in its economy.

## What creators say about it
- Open-world playgrounds with lots of content from the start are a reference for furnished, non-empty housing. ([Roblox Creators 2021-09 50:50](https://youtu.be/UlKAoiFLgUo?t=3050))
- Brand integration done organically (Lego car) was embraced by players and drew new and existing players. ([Roblox 2023-09 5:33](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=333))
- Integrating subscriptions into the economy is an example; it is referenced in the official Creator Implementation Guide. ([Roblox DevForum 2026-04](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/let-your-players-pay-for-subscriptions-within-experiences-in-robux/4552995))

## Transferable patterns
- Fill the world with content at launch
- Integrate brands organically rather than as ads
- Use subscriptions as part of the economy
