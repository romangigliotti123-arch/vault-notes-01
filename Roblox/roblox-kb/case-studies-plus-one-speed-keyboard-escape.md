# Case study: +1 Speed Keyboard Escape

**Genre:** Short-session party / casual

Short-session party and casual Roblox game driven by admin-scheduled chaos events. Won Best Party & Casual Game in 2026 coverage (SecretVerse Studio); Creator Docs cite it as a rewarded video example.

Also referred to as: +1 Speed Keyboard Escape, Keyboard Escape, Keyboard escape game (unnamed)

## Numbers
- 4 billion visits in Q3 2026 (OtakuKart, 2026-09; third-party report, not creator-stated; G2266).
- Scheduled chaos events produce spikes (StudioKrew agency blog citing GGAID data; G2012).
- 4 billion visits in Q3 2026, reported by OtakuKart (G2252). This is a third-party figure, not creator-stated, and is unverified.
- Original retained ~200K players; its copy fell to ~6K (rasdev, G2435)

## Transferable patterns
- Scheduled chaos events to create spikes
- Short-session impulse loops
- Rewarded video in pop-ups
- Party and casual positioning for awards visibility
- Scheduled chaos events to create play spikes
- Short-session loops for impulse play
- Rewarded video ads in pop-ups
- Award coverage to boost visibility
- Use a single escalating mechanic to create felt progression.
- Ride trending game clusters on discovery platforms.
- Do not clone a successful game; differentiate or expect the copy to underperform.
