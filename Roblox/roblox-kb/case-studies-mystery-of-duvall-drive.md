# Case study: The Mystery of Duvall Drive

**Genre:** Narrative horror demo (Roblox official sample)

Roblox Creator Docs' official, uncopylocked haunted-house mission demo with corrupt and normal room swaps, seals, tag-based managers and volumetric audio. Used as a reference for atmosphere, streaming and scripting. No commercial metrics.

Also referred to as: Mystery of Duvall Drive, Mystery of Duvall Drive (expanding pantry), The Mystery of Duvall Drive

- Hide streaming gaps behind terrain and occlusion
- Tag-based managers
- Hide teleports behind cutscenes

- Use tag-based managers to scale interactive props.
- Duck ambience under progression audio cues.
- Study official uncopylocked demos before building.
