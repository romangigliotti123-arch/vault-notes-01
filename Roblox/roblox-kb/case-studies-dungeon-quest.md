# Case study: Dungeon Quest

**Genre:** Co-op dungeon crawler

An early Roblox dungeon crawler known for scale, teleport-based monetisation and a room-creation party model. A later Vexx-era revamp added load fixes and onboarding.

Also referred to as: Dungeon Quest (Vexx)

## Numbers
- 350M visits with tens of thousands of players, using DataStore2 (Roblox DevForum, 2018) [G21]

## What creators say about it
- DataStore2 was used at 350M visits with tens of thousands of players (2018 DevForum report). ([Roblox DevForum 2018-06](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-use-datastore2-data-store-caching-and-data-loss-prevention/136317))
- Teleport-based monetisation was part of the Vex title; one of the original dungeon crawlers. ([Roblox 2024-09](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ))
- Fixed load times, a guided tutorial, teleport-in onboarding, personalised offers and a store revamp produced major D1 retention and conversion gains. ([Roblox 2024-09](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM))
- Room creation with a chosen dungeon is the multiplayer model to copy. ([Cumin 2025-12 19:23](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=1163))

## Transferable patterns
- Fix load times and add guided onboarding to lift retention
- Teleport-in onboarding and teleport-based monetisation
- Room-creation party model for multiplayer
- Plan data-store architecture for very large player counts
