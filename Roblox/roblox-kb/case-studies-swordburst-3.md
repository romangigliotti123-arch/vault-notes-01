# Case study: Swordburst 3

**Genre:** Action RPG / open world

Roblox action RPG (also Sword Burst 3; Twin Atlas named in one mention) by Roblox. Multi-floor open world with a unique flower per floor that rewards exploration. The 2026 verdict says linear, predictable, too-easy content caused failure.

Also referred to as: Swordburst 3 (Twin Atlas), Sword Burst 3

## Numbers
- 75% of DAU at max level shaped content priorities (Roblox, G850)

## What creators say about it
- A unique reward on each floor (a flower) rewards exploration and delights players. ([Roblox DevForum 2024-05](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648))
- Letting players collect Void Tower rewards inside the tower fixed staircase drop-off. ([Roblox 2024-09](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM))
- 75% of DAU at max level shaped content priorities (Roblox). CONFLICT: the 2026 G2361 says linear, too-easy content caused failure; prefer the newer verdict. ([Roblox 2024-09](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM), [Roblox 2026-10](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0))
- Going linear and predictable with too-easy content failed the game; Sword Burst 2's open exploration was the proposed fix. ([Roblox 2026-10](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0))

## Transferable patterns
- Place in-world rewards at drop-off points to keep players progressing.
- Use max-level DAU share to set content priorities.
- Avoid linear, predictable progression; keep exploration open.
