# Monetisation Gamepasses

_gamepasses: which to sell, design, placement._

Evidence base: 382 extracted items from 211 videos by 1Coal, 1_Bruce, Alex Builds Games, AlvinBlox, ArTheDev, Arow, B Ricey, BubuTheDev, ByteBlox, CORSO, CallMeDogey, Crusherfire, Cumin, Daiorg, Deconstructor of Fun, Duckable, Emancyphur, GameMakers, Generalist Programmer, GosuGamers, ItsZyonic, Last Level Studios, LovJak, Matt The Method, MonarchDev, MonzterDEV, MoseyTube, Mulch, Perstile, PineappleDev, Platform Aeronaut (transcript), Poke, PolarBear, Quez, Rapstrike GFX, Raul, RoDev, RoTrends (tracker), RoWatcher (tracker blog), Roblox, Roblox Creator Docs, Roblox Creators, Roblox DevForum, Roblox Investor Relations, Roblox public API, Seibo, SimTek Game Development, SmartyRBX, ThatGuyEvan, The Letus Garden, TheDevKing, ThePurpleGuy, Tizzy, VuukStudios, Yann, janisjanis01, kra_DeZ, rasdev, revolver agz, tef, {EISABOOS} "RBX" (2018-07-24 to 2026-11-01). ## Overview
Gamepass monetisation now has a platform-level change: since 2026-07, Roblox has disabled selling gamepasses across experiences, which Roblox says reduced monetisation. Live-listing snapshots (2026-11) show wide price ladders: cheap entry items from 9 to 99 Robux, mid-tier passes around 99 to 499, and top bundles or multipliers at 999 to 4,999. Competitive shooters monetise through cosmetic skins rather than power. Very high-priced power passes (about 7,500 Robux) drew pay-to-win criticism. Most evidence is listing data and self-reported tracker figures, not per-pass revenue, so pass choices still need your own conversion tests.

## Rules and findings
- **Never gate core progress or the game behind a paywall** (multiple-creators): Passes should be optional extras. Free players must reach all core content; paywalls sell cosmetics or convenience, not progression or required skill performance. Let players earn needed content in play. Sources: [Roblox 2018-07 38:40](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=2320), [Roblox DevForum 2021-05](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207), [Roblox Creators 2023-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0), [Roblox 2023-09 22:19](https://youtu.be/S8ORVEKec_4?t=1339), [Roblox DevForum 2019-03](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902). Newest source 2023-09-19.
- **Don't sell items that remove challenge or fix a loss** (one-creator-repeated): Items that remove challenge disengage players and they eventually leave. Frame purchases with a positive outset, not as a rescue from a negative one. Note the contrast with revive and pay-to-skip advice below. Sources: [Roblox DevForum 2019-03](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902), [Roblox DevForum 2019-03](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902). Newest source 2019-03-01.
- **Prefer cosmetics/convenience over pay-to-win, especially for competitive or young-audience games** (multiple-creators): Officials say pay-to-win causes community frustration, makes new players feel wrecked, and older players dislike it, though younger players see it as a reason to buy. Cosmetics build goodwill; let players earn competitive items. Sources: [Roblox DevForum 2019-03](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902), [Roblox Creators 2021-08 1:10:52](https://youtu.be/dBlP8NdJQM0?t=4252), [Roblox Creators 2021-12 41:01](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=2461), [Roblox Creators 2022-11 0:43](https://youtu.be/2kUFhwfuZ-w?t=43), [Roblox Creators 2022-11 38:13](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=2293). Newest source 2025-06-24.
- **Pay-to-win packs are a counter-view that works in competitive genres (unverified)** (single-mention): Alex Builds Games calls exclusive pay-to-win bundles in combat/tower defense his S-tier method, since kids want superiority. This conflicts with Roblox staff guidance, so weigh the retention and reputation risk. Sources: [Alex Builds Games 2026-06 1:57](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=117), [Alex Builds Games 2026-06 1:57](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=117). Newest source 2026-06-16.
- **Pay-to-skip / pay-to-get-ahead can suit progression or social games** (multiple-creators): GameMakers (2021) says pay-to-skip is the best received by both paying and non-paying players, and cosmetics alone visually split players. Convenience items (speed-ups, timed boosts, teleporter) are favoured by AlvinBlox. Sources: [GameMakers 2021-01 47:55](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2875), [GameMakers 2021-01 47:55](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2875), [AlvinBlox 2025-04 6:46](https://youtu.be/Le4QhC3dDGk?t=406). - **Keep the number of gamepasses small and valuable** (multiple-creators): Suggested counts: about six (1_Bruce), 2-5 permanent (MoseyTube), a handful (Roblox). Many passes overwhelm players and reduce purchases. Hard limit is 15 slots, so don't waste slots on donations unless it's a showcase. Sources: [Roblox Creators 2023-01 2:54](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=174), [1_Bruce 2025-12 4:51](https://youtu.be/wKI3PBO3bB0?t=291), [MoseyTube 2026-06 5:13](https://youtu.be/Kh_XKYw8ocU?t=313), [MoseyTube 2026-06 5:13](https://youtu.be/Kh_XKYw8ocU?t=313), [MoseyTube 2026-06 5:13](https://youtu.be/Kh_XKYw8ocU?t=313). Newest source 2026-06-29.
- **Counter-view: add more passes/content to grow revenue** (multiple-creators): TheDevKing says many games have too few passes; CallMeDogey says adding more purchases grows revenue stats and claims the algorithm factors in money. Roblox says spend is just one signal and fun/retention matter most. Sources: [TheDevKing 2020-05 12:36](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=756), [CallMeDogey 2026-02 6:06](https://youtu.be/IQm6zE2fKUI?t=366), [Roblox 2025-09 30:05](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=1805), [Roblox 2025-09 30:05](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=1805), [SmartyRBX 2024-02 15:38](https://youtu.be/kUNFgrvUkj4?t=938). - **Use passes for permanent one-time benefits; dev products for repeatables** (multiple-creators): Passes can only be bought once: VIP, bundles, battle passes, restricted areas, avatar items, permanent power-ups. Use dev products for potions, temporary boosts, currency. Sell permanent passes at higher prices than dev products, alongside cheaper consumables. Sources: [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/monetization), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/passes), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production), [MoseyTube 2026-07 0:50](https://youtu.be/-PoyiiGhoNo?t=50). Newest source 2026-10-28.
- **Avoid XP/coin/stat boosters as one-off passes that cap spend; avoid economy-breaking passes** (multiple-creators): A very powerful pass (infinite money) leaves buyers never needing to spend again, giving low ARPPU. Tizzy avoided auto-collect because it can wreck the economy. Prefer exclusive items, group ranks, titles and levels. Note the conflict: MoseyTube sells 2x cash multiplier passes. Sources: [Roblox DevForum 2021-05](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-game-monetization-guide/1235207), [Roblox Creators 2022-02 10:00](https://youtu.be/RfpMZZFy8Zw?t=600), [Tizzy 2026-06 2:25](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets?t=145), [MoseyTube 2026-07 4:36](https://youtu.be/-PoyiiGhoNo?t=276), [MoseyTube 2026-07 3:47](https://youtu.be/a170E9klbP4?t=227). Newest source 2026-07-24.
- **Make passes worth buying; bundle VIP with real perks** (multiple-creators): A plain overhead badge VIP at $3.99 won't sell. Bundle exclusive areas, secret levels, multipliers (e.g. 1.5x cash), chat and cosmetics. Check per-pass purchase stats and rework or free up passes that never sell. Sources: [Alex Builds Games 2026-06 8:43](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=523), [MoseyTube 2026-07 3:47](https://youtu.be/a170E9klbP4?t=227), [MonzterDEV 2024-03 19:02](https://youtu.be/em15yK5RjBk?t=1142), [kra_DeZ 2026-09 7:55](https://youtu.be/ZradA-cygP0?t=475). Newest source 2026-09-25.
- **Use tiered pricing and a cheap entry point** (multiple-creators): Offer three tiers (starter, pro, whale) to raise ARPPU; a starter pack is the easiest conversion win. A cheap gateway purchase makes players more likely to spend again (a one-time spender is exponentially more likely to spend again). Raise ARPU with more expensive worthwhile purchases. Sources: [Tizzy 2026-06 4:08](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=248), [Tizzy 2026-06 3:27](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI?t=207), [Roblox DevForum 2022-08](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/the-art-of-robux-getting/1922578), [Roblox Creators 2021-08 23:13](https://youtu.be/4-vZkSrGAxI?t=1393), [CallMeDogey 2026-02 2:43](https://youtu.be/IQm6zE2fKUI?t=163). Newest source 2026-06-10.
- **Match item type to genre** (multiple-creators): Pay-to-express (auras, trails) only sells where players value expression (anime, Adopt Me style). Convenience items (auto collect, trade slots, teleports) should be very cheap and secondary. Revive is a cheap impulse buy (top earner in story/horror/combat per Alex Builds Games, unverified); Daiorg's 99 Nights sells only revive. Sources: [Alex Builds Games 2026-06 7:22](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=442), [Alex Builds Games 2026-06 10:19](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=619), [Alex Builds Games 2026-06 11:20](https://youtu.be/x_SflcuSi1w?t=680), [Daiorg 2025-12 51:39](https://youtu.be/JCMIb008Y6E?t=3099), [Daiorg 2025-12 52:20](https://youtu.be/JCMIb008Y6E?t=3140). - **Make shop and pass buttons visible and uncluttered** (multiple-creators): Show passes in the main menu or on screen. Roblox's Nathan claims monetization roughly quadrupled with an easy-to-find shop and fewer competing buttons; a cluttered game had about a quarter of War Simulator's. Moving a button to where players see it raised battle pass sales. Sources: [TheDevKing 2020-05 12:36](https://youtu.be/Zqz_gclwM0M?t=756), [GameMakers 2021-01 44:48](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=2688), [Roblox Creators 2023-02 33:58](https://youtu.be/BsyU-SJ9L-c?t=2038), [Roblox Creators 2023-02 33:58](https://youtu.be/BsyU-SJ9L-c?t=2038), [Roblox Creators 2023-02 33:58](https://youtu.be/BsyU-SJ9L-c?t=2038). - **Explain what each pass does** (multiple-creators): State clearly in descriptions what is and isn't included (e.g. only unlocks buying with in-game cash). Add an info (i) or ? button next to items. Sources: [Roblox Creators 2023-01 3:38](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=218), [Roblox Creators 2023-03 5:49](https://youtu.be/PjwJb_nSePY?t=349). - **Redesign the store and use contextual upsells** (multiple-creators): Scrolling layout, quick navigation, rotating offers and a daily reward gave Dungeon Quest +20% ARPU. A popup when clicking locked content gave Brookhaven +10% ARPD and +18% payer conversion with no retention loss. Make the top-value option visually dominant. - **Enable the shopping bag icon and free trials** (multiple-creators): Opted-in games had 6x higher Robux spend with the shopping bag menu icon (Roblox experiments). Limited-time game pass free trials gave 4%+ more Robux spend and spenders. Rewarded-ad trials of high-value passes were used in Brookhaven. Older paid perks can later be offered free via ads (Spongebob TD). Sources: [Roblox 2026-10 4:22](https://youtu.be/hoMbv_R8AMU?t=262), [Roblox 2026-10 6:23](https://youtu.be/hoMbv_R8AMU?t=383), [Roblox 2026-10 6:23](https://youtu.be/hoMbv_R8AMU?t=383), [Roblox Creators 2025-08 23:18](https://youtu.be/7AtvvUxvCaU?t=1398), [Roblox 2025-09 17:09](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew?t=1029). - **Design season/battle passes with free and premium tracks** (multiple-creators): Offer both: premium is a superset; free players cannot complete premium missions or earn premium rewards. Grant all completed-tier premium rewards on late purchase. Put a high-visibility reward on the final tiers, highlight premium visually (gold bar), and use 8-week seasons. Premium cost example: about 300 Robux (Jailbreak). A Grades battle pass nearly doubled monetization with flat retention (official). Sources: [Roblox Creators 2021-07 29:57](https://youtu.be/UL4QaSbfyC4?t=1797), [Roblox Creators 2021-09 6:00](https://youtu.be/k2gbQI-ggB4?t=360), [Roblox Creators 2021-09 7:23](https://youtu.be/k2gbQI-ggB4?t=443), [Roblox Creators 2021-09 22:33](https://youtu.be/k2gbQI-ggB4?t=1353), [Roblox Creators 2023-08 3:59](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=239). - **Use passes with a skill-neutral progression in competitive games** (single-mention): Use cosmetic-focused season pass progression, or skill-based matchmaking, where progression must not grant advantage. - **Let players gift passes and earn items** (multiple-creators): Buying passes for friends had a huge impact per a creator. Also let players earn items so non-paying players still get rewards. - **Diagnose revenue by platform and country** (multiple-creators): If revenue drops vs similar games, check monetization by platform/country first. Low conversion: make purchases effective and visible. Top payers should give about half of revenue, else consider a season pass. Build Top Picks with RecommendTopProductsAsync (needs a sale in the last 28 days). - **Don't price private servers prohibitively** (single-mention): Expensive private servers discourage groups of friends playing together. Sources: [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox). Newest source 2026-10-28.
- **Don't add pay-to-win just because outsiders suggest it** (single-mention): It can disappoint players later. Sources: [CallMeDogey 2025-05 3:23](https://youtu.be/ZAfE-Row9tU?t=203). Newest source 2025-05-09.
- **Handle purchase results on the server with the purchased flag** (one-creator-repeated): PromptGamePassPurchaseFinished fires on both cancel and success. Check the purchased flag before granting any benefit. Apply benefits mid-session on purchase so the player sees them immediately, not only at next join. Sources: [Crusherfire 2024-03 3:09](https://youtu.be/kDCLGPMpLl0?t=189), [Crusherfire 2024-03 4:44](https://youtu.be/kDCLGPMpLl0?t=284). Newest source 2024-03-27.
- **Check ownership at join inside pcall** (one-creator-repeated): On PlayerAdded, call MarketplaceService:UserOwnsGamePassAsync(userId, passId) wrapped in pcall. It yields and can error. Use the result to grant the pass's benefits, and make sure the same benefit also applies when the player buys mid-session. Sources: [Crusherfire 2024-03 4:44](https://youtu.be/kDCLGPMpLl0?t=284), [Crusherfire 2024-03 4:44](https://youtu.be/kDCLGPMpLl0?t=284). Newest source 2024-03-27.
- **Make sure each pass grants the item it advertises** (multiple-creators): A pass that shows as owned but gives no item causes refunds and complaints. Verify every pass's grant path against its listed benefit before launch, and rewrite a broken grant script from scratch rather than patching it. Sources: [janisjanis01 2026-09 8:45](https://youtu.be/WJpNCB5J9bg?t=525), [janisjanis01 2023-09 6:50](https://youtu.be/9aQpeTCZ7yM?t=410). Newest source 2026-09-27.
- **Use developer products or limited-time offers for repeatable starter packs** (multiple-creators): A gamepass-only starter pack can be bought once, which caps sales. Sell repeatable bundles as developer products, or as limited-time offers. Two creators reached this view independently. Sources: [janisjanis01 2026-07 4:41](https://youtu.be/OL1FJ17wzZE?t=281), [Yann 2026-05 3:25](https://youtu.be/dbNA3K8lt2w?t=205). Newest source 2026-07-05.
- **Keep first-time experience free of aggressive monetisation** (single-mention): Official guidance: avoid aggressive monetisation in the first-time user experience. Monetisation must not block large portions of content or grant unfair gameplay advantages. Place pass prompts after the player has seen the core loop. Sources: [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/learn-explore-sort). Newest source 2026-10-28.
- **Do not paywall progression to chase Roblox promotion** (one-creator-repeated): One creator claims paywalled progression and addictive loops earn front-page placement. Official guidance says monetisation must not block content, and the same creator advises never locking core gameplay. Follow the official guidance over the placement claim. Sources: [rasdev 2026-08 5:19](https://youtu.be/17DGro3FUlM?t=319), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/learn-explore-sort), [rasdev 2026-04 3:22](https://youtu.be/G15TVsyC61g?t=202). Newest source 2026-10-28.
- **Give each purchase an immediate reward or notification** (single-mention): Pair Robux shop items with an on-screen notification or reward at the moment of purchase. This reinforces the buy and shows the player what they got. Sources: [RoDev 2025-05 6:36](https://youtu.be/Gw2FwndwnOg?t=396). Newest source 2025-05-23.
- **Offer a varied set of pass types** (single-mention): Mix multiplier passes, exclusive cosmetics, chat tags, streak recovery and restock options. Varied benefit types give players several reasons to buy beyond one stat boost. Sources: [Duckable 2026-05 8:18](https://youtu.be/X9YYjb_mY5w?t=498). Newest source 2026-05-01.
- **Make VIP perks meaningful to players** (single-mention): A per-slot storage bag that is not shared across slots makes VIP storage feel pointless and reads as greedy. Check that each VIP perk is actually useful in play before selling it as VIP. - **Decide early whether to use loot boxes** (single-mention): GameMakers frames loot boxes as a values decision because they exploit addictive behaviour. Make the call during planning, not after the economy is built, and pair any use with pity systems and clear odds. Sources: [GameMakers 2022-11 25:44](https://youtu.be/NaR3-VIg5-I?t=1544). Newest source 2022-11-24.
- **Invest in IAP merchandising art when engineering is limited** (single-mention): GameMakers says IAP merchandising is art-heavy and engineering-light, and reports 10-40%+ LTV lifts (self-reported). It is a strong lever for small teams that can produce good shop art but lack programming time. Sources: [GameMakers 2022-08 33:36](https://youtu.be/JVCao-Yhu2M?t=2016), [GameMakers 2022-08 33:36](https://youtu.be/JVCao-Yhu2M?t=2016). Newest source 2022-08-30.
- **Grant pass perks on join and on purchase finished** (single-mention): Apply a pass perk both on PlayerAdded (if already owned) and on PromptGamePassPurchaseFinished (if just bought). Otherwise players must rejoin to receive what they paid for. Pair with an ownership check in pcall. Sources: [TheDevKing 2020-03 13:27](https://youtu.be/RmWgJVjJrqE?t=807). Newest source 2020-03-12.
- **Fire VIP touch-zone prompts from the server** (single-mention): Gamepass prompts triggered by touch zones (for example a VIP area) should be fired through a RemoteEvent from the server. TheDevKing reports touch logic in LocalScripts is unreliable. Sources: [TheDevKing 2019-11 12:05](https://youtu.be/3hMmhmC18RA?t=725). Newest source 2019-11-27.
- **Keep Robux-bought unlocks through rebirth or reset** (single-mention): When a rebirth or prestige resets progress, keep the buttons or unlocks bought with Robux. janisjanis01 says this keeps paying players' value after the reset, which supports continued spending. Sources: [janisjanis01 2025-12 10:16](https://youtu.be/JYvGdKRzwEo?t=616). Newest source 2025-12-13.
- **Avoid paid access (entry fee) to the game** (single-mention): SmartyRBX (low-advice) advises against paid access because it limits how many players can enter. Sell gamepasses and other optional items instead of charging to enter. Sources: [SmartyRBX 2024-11 6:04](https://youtu.be/Pwy9U0B8tJk?t=364). Newest source 2024-11-04.
- **Expect mixed results from unlock-all buttons** (single-mention): janisjanis01 (low-advice) reports that an 'unlock all powers' button drew mixed results, while a double-coins pass helped monetisation. Test a bundle-unlock button on a subset of players before committing to it. Sources: [janisjanis01 2026-10 2:03](https://youtu.be/A2QD-eT3IG0?t=123). Newest source 2026-10-25.
- **Weigh community backlash as a real cost of each paid feature** (multiple-creators): Both janisjanis01 and Deconstructor of Fun treat player and community reaction as a real cost. One creator accepts lower monetisation to keep goodwill, and the other frames the risk as the blast radius when the community reacts. Score this before shipping a paid element. Sources: [janisjanis01 2024-12 18:41](https://youtu.be/Jc7yQTMFexk?t=1121), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-07 44:02](https://youtu.be/jgqmsicPUYE?t=2642), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-07 44:02](https://youtu.be/jgqmsicPUYE?t=2642). Newest source 2026-07-16.
- **Assess the blast radius of each paid element before shipping** (one-creator-repeated): Treat monetisation like a gunpowder barrel. For each paid element, estimate how much risk it adds and how much damage it could do if the community reacts badly. You control what goes in the barrel. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-07 44:02](https://youtu.be/jgqmsicPUYE?t=2642), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-07 44:02](https://youtu.be/jgqmsicPUYE?t=2642). Newest source 2026-07-16.
- **Explain new monetisation to players before launch** (single-mention): Test and explain new paid systems to your audience before launch. This matters most when adding paid systems to a game that previously had none. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-07 48:51](https://youtu.be/jgqmsicPUYE?t=2931). Newest source 2026-07-16.
- **Give every premium currency a defined spend** (single-mention): Do not ship a shop whose currency has no purpose. One creator's rubies had no use, and monetisation was left undefined. Connect each currency to at least one purchasable or unlockable item before launch. Sources: [janisjanis01 2025-10 11:15](https://youtu.be/MML_z-jpmF4?t=675). Newest source 2025-10-11.
- **Offer a free earn path to passes via daily quests** (one-creator-repeated): Let players earn tokens through daily quests to unlock gamepasses for free. Example: collecting 150 chests yields free tokens. One creator accepts lower monetisation in exchange for player goodwill. Trade-off: fewer Robux sales. Sources: [janisjanis01 2024-12 17:59](https://youtu.be/Jc7yQTMFexk?t=1079), [janisjanis01 2024-12 17:59](https://youtu.be/Jc7yQTMFexk?t=1079), [janisjanis01 2024-12 18:41](https://youtu.be/Jc7yQTMFexk?t=1121). Newest source 2024-12-01.
- **Bundle same-type gamepasses in a limited-time sale** (single-mention): During a sale, bundle related passes at a combined price. Example: two 2x Robux gamepasses sold together for 79 Robux. Bundling is a sale tactic, not a permanent price. Sources: [janisjanis01 2024-12 13:53](https://youtu.be/HGobKizBHqQ?t=833), [janisjanis01 2024-12 13:53](https://youtu.be/HGobKizBHqQ?t=833). Newest source 2024-12-15.
- **Price requested automation passes high, but check the rules first** (single-mention): An Auto Rebirth pass was requested for years and is in strong demand. One creator prices it high because it is overpowered. This conflicts with existing guidance to avoid economy-breaking passes and to avoid selling items that remove challenge. Use caution with this pricing. Sources: [janisjanis01 2024-11 12:58](https://youtu.be/yxd5GMEjtDA?t=778), [janisjanis01 2024-11 12:58](https://youtu.be/yxd5GMEjtDA?t=778). Newest source 2024-11-03.
- **Give utility passes a clear benefit and an icon** (single-mention): Design a gamepass with an obvious utility benefit such as auto collect, and create a distinct icon for it so players can see the value at a glance. Sources: [janisjanis01 2025-07 11:10](https://youtu.be/pD8fKkzF8O8?t=670). Newest source 2025-07-13.
- **Reuse proven systems from older games to save build time** (single-mention): Reuse a tested system, such as a rebirth system, from earlier games instead of rebuilding it. This reduces the time before monetisation can ship. Sources: [janisjanis01 2025-09 6:56](https://youtu.be/uNLDi_GO2MA?t=416). Newest source 2025-09-21.

Check both the gamePassId and the purchased flag before granting any perk. This is a specific reason for the existing purchased-flag rule, not a new rule. Sources: [AlvinBlox 2020-03 30:25](https://youtu.be/jOFrwMkl8LI?t=1825). Newest source 2020-03-20.

## Checklist
- [ ] Confirm no pass blocks core progress or the core game
- [ ] List planned passes; keep to about 2-6 (max 15 slots)
- [ ] Make each pass a permanent benefit; use dev products for repeatables
- [ ] Bundle VIP with real perks (areas, multipliers, cosmetics)
- [ ] Offer starter, mid, and top tiers
- [ ] Add a cheap entry-level purchase or starter pack
- [ ] Check each pass for economy-breaking effects (auto-collect, infinite money)
- [ ] Write clear descriptions plus info buttons
- [ ] Put shop in main menu; reduce competing buttons
- [ ] Enable shopping bag menu icon
- [ ] Add contextual upsell popups on locked content
- [ ] Enable game pass free trials / rewarded-ad trials
- [ ] Upload 512x512 icon with key content in the circle
- [ ] Mark passes Listed in Monetization > Shop
- [ ] Check UserOwnsGamePassAsync in PlayerAdded
- [ ] Centralise multipliers in a formula script
- [ ] Create a dedicated Buy Robux promoted pass (49 < price < 801)
- [ ] Decide on Managed Pricing or price optimization
- [ ] Season pass: free and premium tracks, retroactive rewards, final-tier reward, 8-week seasons
- [ ] Review per-pass sales and rework non-sellers
- [ ] Segment revenue by platform and country
- [ ] Allow gifting passes
- [ ] Keep private server prices reasonable
- [ ] Create each pass in Creator Hub under Monetization > Passes (2024 walkthrough) or Creator Dashboard > Passes (2026 official), then mark it 'for sale' in the Sales tab to set a price.
- [ ] Copy the pass asset ID from the URL (roblox.com/game-pass/ID) or the three-dot menu, or use Copy Asset ID. You need it in code to prompt purchases and check ownership.
- [ ] Enable Allow Third Party Sales under Game Settings > Security if purchase prompts fail with 'third party sales disabled'.
- [ ] On the server, connect MarketplaceService.PromptGamePassPurchaseFinished and grant benefits only when the purchased flag is true.
- [ ] On PlayerAdded, call UserOwnsGamePassAsync inside pcall and apply the benefits from the result.
- [ ] Apply pass benefits on both paths: live purchase mid-session and ownership check at join.
- [ ] Before launch, verify each pass grants the advertised item, not just an ownership flag.
- [ ] Add passes that grant items with addItemAsync(id, Enum.InfoType.GamePass). Official docs say pass IDs come from Creator Dashboard > Passes > Copy Asset ID.
- [ ] Sell repeatable starter packs as developer products or time-limited offers, not as one-time gamepasses.
- [ ] Keep the first-time experience free of aggressive pass prompts.
- [ ] Show an on-screen reward or notification the moment a purchase completes.
- [ ] Measure sales of convenience passes such as remote unlock, and cut those that do not sell.
- [ ] Price cheap repeatable boosts low (example: 50 Robux for 2x luck, 12 hours) and consider a price that doubles on each repurchase.
- [ ] Prompt a purchase at the moment the player runs out of a resource, such as fuel for drawing power.
- [ ] Give VIP perks that players actually need, and avoid storage designs that make VIP feel pointless.
- [ ] Mix pass types: multiplier, exclusive cosmetic, chat tag, streak recovery and restock options.
- [ ] When taking over a game, replace the previous owner's passes so revenue goes to you.
- [ ] Keep pass purchases out of the core gameplay path; never block progression behind a Robux prompt.
- [ ] Before building, estimate pass revenue as buyers x price x players, then take 30% off for Roblox's cut.
- [ ] Before creating passes, enable 'Allow third party sales' in game Security settings.
- [ ] Do not pass a pass owner's ID; the marketplace pays the owner automatically.
- [ ] Assume every pass is publicly listed in the Shop and Experience Details page; you cannot hide it.
- [ ] Test each pass in the published client, not Studio, and confirm its effect matches what it advertises.
- [ ] For season passes, set premiumTrack pricing.gamePassId to the gamepass's unique asset ID.
- [ ] Configure in-game purchases and immersive ads in the Creator Dashboard after publishing.
- [ ] Check Data.Owns(player, passName) before showing a pass offer.
- [ ] Choose speed-ups or cosmetics over items that make payers dominant.
- [ ] Make a task slow or tedious but not blocking, then offer a cheap fix; keep the core loop playable.
- [ ] Review each pass: buyers should react 'good deal, buy now' or 'good deal, maybe later'.
- [ ] Show prize wheel odds before any paid spin.
- [ ] Avoid persistent shop buttons and pop-ups; keep ads out of the play path.
- [ ] Have at least a few working passes live before running paid ads.
- [ ] Track the share of players who pay; compare it to 0.6% (weak) and 2% (target) and adjust paywall placement.
- [ ] Check analytics for country and Roblox Plus mix before setting prices.
- [ ] Offer a cheap entry pass or consumable, and grant the same consumables via the daily wheel.
- [ ] Launch with a minimal, working pass set; expand monetisation after retention is stable.
- [ ] Verify the pass effect in the client after each change, since a crown spin speed bug shipped in one example.
- [ ] Create passes in Creator Hub: pick the experience, open Monetization > Passes, click Create Pass, enter name and description, then set the Sales price.
- [ ] Store each pass's asset ID in a folder value and copy the ID from the Creator Hub Passes page when wiring the shop UI.
- [ ] Before changing rewards, confirm no previously free content is moved behind a gamepass.
- [ ] Track gamepass and dev-product revenue separately per game to decide which to lean on.
- [ ] Price cosmetic/convenience passes in the 59–99 Robux range unless testing shows otherwise.
- [ ] Give each pass recurring value (new content per update) if it's meant to keep selling.
- [ ] For powerful admin or sandbox passes, price high and let owners assign multiple admins.
- [ ] Test randomizer or gift-box passes with repeated purchases before launch.
- [ ] For limited shop stock, use per-player decrement logic.
- [ ] Before cutting a pass price, check in-game visibility of the pass.
- [ ] Check whether any revenue plan relies on selling passes across experiences; if so, redesign around in-experience passes (since 2026-07).
- [ ] For competitive shooters, prioritise cosmetic skin passes over any power or advantage item.
- [ ] Before pricing a power pass in the thousands of Robux, test for pay-to-win backlash and offer an earnable route to the same power.
- [ ] Price gameplay tool or weapon convenience passes in the 299 to 799 Robux band, and check each against the snapshot listings before launch.
- [ ] For a multiplier ladder, set an x2 entry near 99 to 149 Robux and scale steeply up to the top tier.
- [ ] Include a sub-50 Robux entry item (listings show 9, 19, 49 and 50) so first purchases are cheap.
- [ ] Benchmark your active pass count against live snapshots, which range from 0 to 13 in most listings (one outlier had 46).
- [ ] Offer a free track beside any paid skip-ahead event pass (example skip price ~3,876 Robux).
- [ ] Keep VIP pricing between 99 and 599 Robux, matched to the perks included.
- [ ] Price cosmetic bundles from 100 to 200 Robux with a top bundle near 700 Robux.
- [ ] Date-stamp every price you record; listings change with game versions and sale windows.
- [ ] Mark RoWatcher and RoTrends figures as self-reported when citing them.
- [ ] Sell capacity or slot upgrades as dev products that repeat as the collection grows (T17871, N7639).
- [ ] Where a paid revive exists, confirm a free fallback still lets players finish the obby or co-op stage (T17930).
- [ ] Before launch, map each pass or product to a point in the core loop where it is offered (T17936), without gating the core loop itself.
- [ ] Check whether any booster also changes leaderboard values before launch, and decide whether that is intended (T17887).
- [ ] Cheap speed or multiplier entry tiers can be priced at roughly 3, 9 and 24 Robux for x2, x4 and x8 (T17870).
- [ ] Create each pass in Creator Hub > Dashboard > Creations > Passes, then copy its asset ID from the three-dot menu for use in scripts.
- [ ] Gate a gameplay bonus (such as 2x clicks) by pass ID, and confirm ownership on the server before applying it.
- [ ] Show a VIP overhead tag only when UserOwnsGamePassAsync returns true for that player's pass ID.
- [ ] Prefer time gates over currency gates when locking content behind a pass or progression step.
- [ ] Create each pass under your experience in the Creator Hub (2025 path: Creation > your experience > Passes > Create Pass), set name and optional description, and copy the numeric ID for scripts.
- [ ] Enable Sales and set a Robux price before testing prompts; an unpriced pass shows 'not currently for sale'.
- [ ] After upload, put the pass on sale; the image may not display until it is reviewed.
- [ ] Grant perks on PlayerAdded if owned and on PromptGamePassPurchaseFinished when bought, so players don't need to rejoin.
- [ ] Fire touch-zone (for example VIP area) gamepass prompts from the server via a RemoteEvent.
- [ ] Before selling a luck pass, test egg or drop odds with and without it over a large sample (about 10,000 opens in Mulch's test).

- [ ] Decide whether loot boxes are in scope during planning; if yes, add pity systems and disclose odds.

- [ ] Test any unlock-all or bundle-unlock button on a subset of players before rolling it out.
- [ ] Write down the role and benefit of each pass, and make an icon for utility passes (T18906).
- [ ] Animate the shop button with a jump or shake to draw attention to Robux items (T18870).
- [ ] Build the shop with placeholder items first, so monetisation is ready when models arrive (T18907).
- [ ] Confirm every premium currency has at least one spend target before launch (T18929).

- [ ] Tell the community about new paid systems before launch, especially in a game that had none (T18958).
- [ ] For sales, bundle two same-type passes at a combined price, for example 2x Robux passes for 79 Robux (T18856, N8093).

## Where creators agree
- Passes must not block core play or progression (Roblox, Roblox DevForum, Roblox Creators).
- Passes are one-time purchases suited to permanent benefits; dev products for repeatables (Roblox Creator Docs, MoseyTube).
- Keep pass count small (1_Bruce, MoseyTube, Roblox Creators).
- Clear, visible, uncluttered shop and descriptions (Roblox Creators, TheDevKing, GameMakers).
- Cosmetics and convenience are safer than pay-to-win (Roblox Creators, DevForum, AlvinBlox, SmartyRBX, CallMeDogey).
- Free and premium season pass tracks with retroactive rewards (Roblox Creators, Roblox Creator Docs).
- Cheap entry purchases drive repeat spend (Roblox Creators, DevForum, Tizzy).
- Pass IDs are required to prompt purchases in code (B Ricey, Crusherfire, Roblox official docs).
- Check ownership on join and handle purchase results on the server, since the prompt fires on cancel too (Crusherfire; janisjanis01 stresses ownership must match the real item).
- Repeatable items belong in developer products, not one-time passes (Yann, janisjanis01).
- Cheap boost passes sell in grind-heavy games (Yann, janisjanis01).
- Never lock core gameplay behind Robux (rasdev, plus official guidance on blocking content).
- Passes are one-time permanent purchases that persist across sessions; grant the benefit on every join.
- Do not gate core progress behind a paywall.
- Plan and test revenue, and test passes before shipping.
- Price with country and Roblox Plus mix in mind; cheaper offers for low-spend regions.
- Keep monetisation out of the play path; ad GUIs are fine if they do not block gameplay.
- Avoid pure pay-to-win; speed-ups beat dominance.
- Cheap cosmetic or convenience passes can be top earners, around 59–99 Robux (Mulch 2024; BubuTheDev 2024).
- Monetisation is visible or not depending on prominence; it must be noticeable to sell (Mulch 2025).
- Players should keep a free path and free existing rewards (Last Level Studios 2019; Cumin 2025).
- Revenue mix between passes and dev products varies per game and should be measured (Mulch 2021; LovJak 2026; Rapstrike 2026).
- Official Roblox listing data and the cheap-entry tier advice agree: ladders starting at 9 to 50 Robux with top bundles near 999 Robux are the common pattern (T17556, T17566, T17568).
- RoWatcher and Roblox both point to cosmetic-first monetisation in shooters, and the existing file already prefers cosmetics in competitive games (T17462, Q1742).
- Platform Aeronaut and Roblox Investor Relations agree the cross-experience pass ban reduced monetisation (T17500, T17510).
- Mulch (T17870) and the existing rule on steep multiplier ladders with a low x2 entry both point to cheap entry boosts that scale up.
- TheDevKing (2019-11, 2020-03) and {EISABOOS} 'RBX' (2025-05) agree on the basic setup flow: create the pass in the Creator Hub or dashboard, set a Sales price, and copy the numeric ID for scripts.
- Both janisjanis01 and Deconstructor of Fun treat player or community reaction as a real cost of monetisation (Q1953, Q1975).

## Where creators disagree
- **Pay-to-win vs cosmetics/convenience**
  - Alex Builds Games: pay-to-win packs are S-tier in competitive games (unverified)
  - Roblox Creators, Roblox DevForum, AlvinBlox, SmartyRBX, CallMeDogey: avoid pay-to-win
- **Cosmetics vs pay-to-skip**
  - Roblox Creators: cosmetics are often best
  - GameMakers: pay-to-skip is best; cosmetics visually split players
  - Alex Builds Games: pay-to-express sells only in expression-heavy games
- **Multiplier/stat boost passes**
  - Roblox DevForum 2021: avoid XP/coin/stat boosters as passes
  - MoseyTube: 2x cash and similar multipliers are core passes
  - Roblox Creators: very powerful passes cause low ARPPU
- **Number of passes**
  - 1_Bruce, MoseyTube: few (2-6)
  - TheDevKing, CallMeDogey: more passes/purchases grow revenue
- **Removing challenge / revive**
  - Roblox DevForum: never sell items that fix a loss or remove challenge
  - Alex Builds Games, Daiorg: revive is a top cheap earner and acceptable
- **Whether cheap boost passes should be sold**
  - Yann and janisjanis01: cheap 2x training-speed and 1.5x coin boost passes sell well (T6661, T6733).
  - Existing knowledge base: avoid XP/coin/stat boosters as one-off passes that cap spend.
  - rasdev (T6845): boosts are fine if players can rebuy them.
- **Paywalled progression for front-page placement**
  - rasdev (T6708): build paywalled progression and addictive loops to earn Roblox front-page placement.
  - rasdev (T6455, Q1024): never lock core gameplay behind Robux.
  - Roblox official (T7483): monetisation must not block large portions of content. Official guidance takes precedence.
- **Starter gear passes that shorten the early grind**
  - tef (T6891): sell a starter gamepass with a better sword and basic armour.
  - Roblox official (T7483): avoid unfair gameplay advantages and aggressive monetisation in the first-time experience.
  - Existing knowledge base: do not sell items that remove challenge.
- **Convenience features (auto-collect) as a paid fix**
  - VuukStudios (T13694, T13768): make collecting manual, then sell a cheap auto-collect pass; create a problem and sell the solution.
  - ByteBlox (T12349): do not paywall basic convenience like auto-collect for 300 Robux while the core loop is tedious; it feels like a cash grab.
- **When to add monetisation at launch**
  - Raul (T13655): add gamepasses before running ads; monetisation must be ready at launch.
  - VuukStudios (T13771): keep monetisation bare-bones at release, focus on retention, lock in the full plan after home recommendations.
  - VuukStudios (T13428): build proper monetisation from the start because the algorithm rewards it.
- **Selling stat, multiplier and booster passes**
  - Existing knowledge rule: avoid XP/coin/stat boosters as one-off passes that cap spend.
  - Matt The Method (T13061): sell gamepasses that give abilities or stat boosts that speed progression.
  - Raul (T13603): sell 2x views/subscribers/rewards/prestige multipliers and instant-unlock passes for premium tiers (low-advice).
- **Shop button visibility**
  - Existing knowledge rule: make shop and pass buttons visible and uncluttered.
  - ByteBlox (T12348): no persistent shop buttons or pop-ups; they push kids to overspend.
- **Pay-to-win through instant unlocks**
  - Raul (T13603, low-advice): sell instant-unlock passes for premium tiers.
  - VuukStudios (T13700): avoid pure pay-to-win; purchases speed up the grind rather than make players instantly best.
- **Pass vs dev product revenue**
  - Mulch (2021) and LovJak (2026-07): gamepasses earned more than dev products in their games.
  - Rapstrike GFX (2026-10): gamepasses earned far less than dev products; don't rely on passes alone.
  - Most recent (2026-10) reports dev products ahead; prefer per-game measurement.
- **One-time vs ongoing pass value**
  - Existing rule: use passes for permanent one-time benefits; dev products for repeatables.
  - PolarBear (2024-02): passes should add ongoing value (e.g., new story content each update) so they stay worth buying.
- **Visibility of monetisation**
  - Existing rule: keep shop and pass buttons uncluttered.
  - Mulch (2025-06): monetisation was too subtle; it needs to be more in-your-face. Lowering price didn't fix sales.
- **Permanent luck boost as a core pass**
  - RoWatcher (T17424): a permanent Advanced Luck pass at 545 Robux is community-favoured and sells as a core pass.
  - Existing file rule: avoid XP/coin/stat boosters as one-off passes that cap spend; it is a stat booster, so caution applies.
- **Pricing high-power admin passes**
  - Existing file rule: price high-power admin or sandbox passes high and allow shared admins.
  - RoWatcher (T17444, N7131): the 7,499 Robux Admin Panel drew pay-to-win criticism, so very high prices carry reputational cost.
- **Cosmetic price band**
  - Existing file rule: price cosmetic and convenience passes in the 59 to 99 Robux band.
  - Roblox public API (N7334, N7364): cosmetic ladders run from 19 to 199 with bundles to 700, so the band is wider than 59 to 99.
- **Multiplier passes that scale to thousands of Robux**
  - Existing file rule: avoid economy-breaking passes.
  - Roblox public API (N7333): an x1000 pass at 4,999 Robux is on sale. A listing shows the pass is sold, not that it performs or is safe.
- **Selling a class's starting item versus keeping paid items cosmetic**
  - GosuGamers (indie, self-reported, T17581): gated a class's starting item behind Robux (Secret Agent buffed with Scanner) and sold original designs as paid classic skins.
  - Existing knowledge file rules: never gate core progress, and keep paid items cosmetic while soft currency unlocks gameplay items.
- **Timing of monetisation relative to the core game**
  - Existing file rule: time monetisation after the core game works.
  - Duckable (T17936, 2026-10): place monetisation throughout the game before launch rather than as an afterthought.
  - Reconciliation: plan hooks during development, but ship a working core loop first. Duckable's advice is more recent but single-source, so treat it as a planning instruction, not permission to gate progress.
- **Selling a revive or fix for a loss**
  - Existing file rule: don't sell items that remove challenge or fix a loss.
  - MonarchDev (T17930, 2026-10): a paid revive option works in co-op obbies alongside a free, less reliable alternative.
  - The newer advice is a narrow exception that depends on the free path remaining viable. The older rule still applies when no free alternative exists.
- **Where gamepass ownership should be enforced**
  - LovJak (T18044, T18047) checks UserOwnsGamePassAsync in a LocalScript or on click to apply a 2x bonus.
  - Existing knowledge file rule 'Handle purchase results on the server with the purchased flag' and 'Check ownership at join inside pcall' favor server-side checks. This addition follows the server-side approach.
- **When to plan monetisation**
  - janisjanis01 (T18704, 2026-08): plan monetisation before building, including a Robux purchase option, a progression starter pack and limited-time items.
