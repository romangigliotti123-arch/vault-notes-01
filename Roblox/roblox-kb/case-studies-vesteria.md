# Case study: Vesteria

**Genre:** Fantasy MMORPG

A fantasy MMORPG on Roblox founded by berezaa, sk3let0n and Polymorphic in the Incubator program. Cited for pay-to-join, mechanics over graphics, and team-structure lessons.

## What creators say about it
- Pay-to-join was cited as a notable exception to Roblox's free-to-play norm. ([Roblox DevForum 2019-03](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902))
- In MMOs, mechanics and world size matter more than graphics (berezaa's example). ([Roblox DevForum 2019-06](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-game/287493))
- A rigid team structure failed; leaving people in charge after release produced better results. ([GameMakers 2021-01 4:52](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=292))
- Role breakdown example for a team: Gameplay Programmers, Animator and Builder roles. ([Roblox DevForum 2020-07](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-becoming-a-leader-for-project-management-and-team-building/690475))

## Transferable patterns
- Prioritise mechanics and world size over graphics in MMOs
- Consider pay-to-join as a deliberate alternative to free-to-play
- Keep post-launch ownership with the people who built the product
