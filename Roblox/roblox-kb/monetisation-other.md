## Overview
Roblox monetisation in 2026 leans toward subscribers and age-verified adult spenders. Official data says Roblox Plus subscribers play about 2x longer and spend about 7x more Robux than non-subscribers, and US over-18 users monetise 50%+ above under-18s. DevEx was raised on June 8, 2026, to $0.0054 per earned Robux for eligible 18+ US spend (standard $0.0038). Paying users are growing, but per-payer bookings are diluted by lower-income international users according to RoWatcher; Roblox's own Q3 2026 data shows bookings per payer up 5%. Trading and seasonal repricing are deliberate economy choices. Indie and tracker figures are self-reported; prefer official Investor Relations data when they conflict.

## Rules and findings
- **Make the game fun and retentive before monetising** (multiple-creators): Players won't pay for something that isn't fun. Design the feature first, then add monetisation only where it fits naturally. Don't degrade a fun feature to force a sale. Monetisation augments a solid base game; without one you earn small amounts. Retention drives most spend. - **Plan monetisation from the start** (multiple-creators): Retrofitting paid offers later is harder and players may call it pay-to-win. Monetise each mechanic at launch so free players never feel something was taken away. Unmonetised games struggle to fund updates and the algorithm favours earning games. 1_Bruce said under-monetising Energy Incremental made it earn little (self-reported). - **Never make paid items pay-to-win, force spending, or use appointment timers** (multiple-creators): Sell things players want but wouldn't be upset not having. Don't gate essential content. Premium perks should be exclusive merch, not tactical advantages. Spend should come from enjoyment, not necessity. Pre-match boosts should affect progression, not damage, health or aim. Avoid hard-currency-required tasks, wasted resources, and timers that can be removed or brought forward. Reward small currency in dailies. - **Offer a tiered price range and a mix of free, soft-currency and Robux paths** (multiple-creators): Give both 15-Robux and 1000-Robux spenders something valuable. 61% of spenders sit in the smallest LTV bucket (1-800 Robux). Make each item free, soft-currency or Robux so payers and non-payers are covered. Give free starter items or soft currency so players use systems early. Balance with discounts, limited-time offers and free items. - **Target veterans and big spenders, but don't ignore tourists** (multiple-creators): Veterans (90+ days) drive most revenue; Brookhaven gets about 10% from the 0-30 day bucket. Concentration claims: 75% from 1-5% of players (Roblox DevForum), ~2% make ~90% (one author), 1-3% make ~70% of mobile revenue (SmartyRBX, claimed), top 15% about 50% (Roblox docs). Tourists prefer immediate, visible items; locals buy long-term value; many locals start as tourists. New players rarely spend in the first 10-30 minutes; a creator saw first buys after 6-7 sessions. - **Use limited-time items and event currency; avoid limited stock (one dissent)** (multiple-creators): Limited-time seasonal items create demand; stock caps frustrate players who lose the race. Keep non-consumable limited-time items permanently owned afterwards. Launch a separate event currency so hoarders can't buy out event content on day one, and track wallets to keep sink-back healthy. Dissent: Tizzy says limited stock (100 servers, 150 antennas) drives his best sellers (self-reported). UGC limiteds are a separate platform product. - **Don't push purchases with pop-ups; trigger offers at moments of need** (multiple-creators): Pop-ups read as pay-to-win; use a small non-intrusive 'need more cash' button or subtle notification, not spammy rainbow buttons. Don't show sales on join; stop modals stacking; show loading-screen offers only after the first session. Instead use 'right item, right time': offer solutions when blocked, dying or waiting (elevator, pre-round shop). In-play prompts need several seconds for Buy Robux; skip them if gameplay can't pause. Jailbreak's garage-UI buying drives ~40% of purchases. - **Design the shop deliberately and give every item a clear purpose** (multiple-creators): Make it easy to find, consistent icon, quick open/close, contextual descriptions, a place to linger. Enable the global Shop button (off by default). Split large lists into categories with filters; ~20 items looks bad, dozens of gamepasses overwhelm. Lobby 3D displays should open the full shop. Currency packs go at the bottom (Alex Builds Games). Shopping bag icon opt-in gave 6x spend. Merge overlapping items, describe items truthfully, sell durables and consumables (enhancement vs expression), match theme, highlight one or two benefits. - **Sell cosmetics and make the first purchase satisfying** (multiple-creators): Cosmetics let devoted fans express themselves; UGC accessories sell because they are worn socially. - **Random paid items: disclose odds, gate via PolicyService, treat as optional and risky** (multiple-creators): Show all outcomes and numeric odds (exactly 100%, or rounding disclaimer) before spend, including indirect purchases (keys, re-rolls, spin tickets) and luck boosts; use a clickable Details button; update odds for one-time outcomes. Check ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted and IsPaidItemTradingAllowed; for restricted users offer free paths, fixed order, direct purchase at expected value, or remove. ## Where creators agree
- Fun and retention come before monetisation; avoid pay-to-win and paywalling core content (Roblox, AlvinBlox, GameMakers, Roblox Creators).
- Premium payouts are additive and earn from non-spenders (DevForum, AlvinBlox, CallMeDogey, MoseyTube).
- Monetise from launch (Roblox, Roblox Creator Docs).
- Avoid intrusive pop-ups (Roblox Creators, waveshine).
- Cosmetics and socially visible items sell (AlvinBlox, GameMakers, Roblox).
- Disclose odds and handle RNG carefully (Roblox DevForum, Roblox Creator Docs, TheDevKing, GameMakers).
- Rewarded video belongs at natural breaks and should be optional (Roblox Creators, Creator Docs, DevForum).
- bubDEV and rasdev both support keeping in-game currency and a minimal starter setup rather than selling direct real-money access.
- Official docs and creator figures agree that creator payouts run through Creator Rewards rather than the deprecated Creator Affiliate program.
- Premium payouts and Creator Rewards reward time spent, not spend; players with zero purchases still generate income.
- Session length and retention are the main lever for passive income.
- Validate monetisation with organic players before paying for traffic.
- Sticky or Premium-retaining design compounds revenue alongside sales.
- Monetisation should be optional extras after the game is fun.
- Hidden-content random or blind-box purchases erode player trust; disclose contents and odds clearly (CORSO, ThatGuyEvan).
- Premium payouts pay for Premium subscribers' playtime, so monetisation can work without gamepasses (ThatGuyEvan, Cumin, Mulch).
- Vague or misleading communication about how cosmetics are obtained causes backlash; state routes explicitly (CORSO).
- Roblox Investor Relations (T17547, T17541) and StudioKrew (T17526) both confirm the June 2026 DevEx rate increase for age-verified 18+ US spend.
- Roblox Investor Relations (T17541) and GamesBeat (T17535) both show Roblox Plus subscribers are a major earnings and spend lever.
- Official Roblox data (T17553, N7262) and RoWatcher (N7119) both show per-payer metrics are sensitive to the mix of new users, though they read the 2026 trend differently.
- GameMakers (T18531) and PineappleDev (T18482) both link spend or playtime to monetisation: cosmetic spend rises with time invested, and Premium payouts scale with Premium players' time in the game.

## Where creators disagree
- **Limited stock vs limited time**
  - Roblox Creators: don't use stock, use time limits
  - Tizzy: limited stock drives best sellers (self-reported)
- **Loot boxes / RNG**
  - GameMakers: dropped them (~1% revenue)
  - TheDevKing: avoid selling directly for Robux
  - Alex Builds Games: use RNG chests in nearly any game
  - Roblox Creator Docs: allowed with odds disclosure and regional restrictions
- **Premium payout priority**
  - TheDevKing (2020): not worth prioritising
  - CallMeDogey, MoseyTube, DevForum: significant or worth designing for
- **How much to monetise**
  - 1_Bruce: restrained monetisation earned little
  - Sensei_Developer and Deconstructor of Fun: avoid predatory feel
  - Roblox survey: ~2 in 3 devs think they are over-monetised
- **Subscription difficulty**
  - TheDevKing (2020) and DevForum (2019): hard / no auto-renew
  - Roblox 2023 onward: native auto-renewing subscriptions
- **Time-pressure offers vs the no-appointment-timers rule**
  - Roblox Creator Docs (T7811): a 15-minute singleUse starter bundle creates urgency for new players.
  - Existing file rule: never use appointment timers and avoid pressure; the 15-minute window must not gate gameplay.
- **Whether badge creation is always free**
  - B Ricey (T5693): badge creation should always be zero Robux, with charges only at very high rates.
  - B Ricey (N2410): recollection of a ~100 per hour threshold is uncertain, so the claim is not firm.
- **Limited stock demand**
  - bubDEV (N2619): template stock of 25 sold out and restocked, showing scarcity can drive sales.
  - Existing file dissent: avoid limited stock; one source only, and this figure is self-reported.
- **Size of Premium payouts in revenue**
  - Generalist Programmer: about 30% of small-earner income (T13504, N5270).
  - Quez: engagement payouts were a small bonus, about 42k Robux per month versus developer products (T12808, N4900).
  - PineappleDev: Eat Dirt about 2,000 of 6,000 Robux from premium payouts (N4666), so a meaningful share for that title.
- **Minimum session for Premium payouts**
  - PineappleDev (T12084): 15+ minute average session needed; a 4.8-minute session earns little.
  - PineappleDev (N4639): possibly about 5 minutes minimum, unconfirmed (same creator, conflicting).
- **Creator Rewards framing**
  - Seibo (T13865): close to free revenue; check whether your game qualifies.
  - PineappleDev (T12913): anecdotal, based on one game; better only for smaller games.
- **Robux per day scale**
  - 1Coal: nearly 100,000 Robux/day at 3,000 players (N4574).
  - PineappleDev: Fishbowl ~100-300 Robux/day from engagement at ~3,000 DAU (N4945).
- **Creator share on Roblox sales (emotes and avatar items)**
  - Existing knowledge rule: 40% commission on avatar items sold in games you own (undated).
  - Xaiyne (2025-08): creators receive about 30% of sales because Roblox takes 70%.
  - Preferred: the more recent 30% figure, pending official confirmation.
- **How much Premium payouts and creator rewards earn**
  - ThatGuyEvan (2020): says you can make 'quite a lot' from Premium payouts without gamepasses (Q1483).
  - Mulch (2024): about 31.69 Robux/day average in 2024, at least 50k total (N6322).
  - ThePurpleGuy (2026): creator rewards only 135 Robux; 303 total including creator rewards (N6497, N6496).
- **Value of commission and emote channels**
  - Mulch (2024): commissions are small unless you sell items directly in-game (T16082).
  - Xaiyne (2026): emotes alone brought over 600k Robux (N6506).
  - These are different revenue lines and sizes; both are self-reported.
- **Whether to allow trading and tradeable rarities**
  - StudioKrew (T17525): tradeable items with community-assigned values keep players returning and seasonal drops reprice inventory.
  - Vindooly (T17531): refused trading because it would hurt the game's economy; may revisit later.
- **Direction of average bookings per paying user**
  - RoWatcher (N7119, Q1738): down 13% YoY in Q4 2025, diluted by international users.
  - Roblox Investor Relations (T17553, N7262): up 5% YoY in Q3 2026 as bookings per MUP rose despite 16% fewer payers.
- **Size of monthly paying users**
  - RoWatcher (N7118): 36.7M in Q4 2025, +94% YoY.
  - Roblox Investor Relations (N7215, T17553): 27M in Q2 2026 (+15% YoY) and 30M in Q3 2026 (-16% YoY).
- **Consumable boosts versus the pay-to-win rule**
  - GosuGamers (T17497): temporary boosts sold in a gear shop gain tactical value during raids.
  - Existing knowledge file rule: never make paid items pay-to-win. Boosts must be designed to respect this.
- **Timeframe of the 300M+ Robux Roblox Plus figure**
  - PocketGamer.biz (T17644, indie, self-reported): 'since launch'
  - Roblox Newsroom (N7537, official): 'first four months', data through 17 Aug 2026
- **Robux-per-visit benchmark**
  - SmartyRBX (T18548): ~1 Robux per visit is a common target for decently monetised games (total monetisation).
  - PineappleDev (T18483): ~0.41 Robux per visit is the average from Premium payouts only in their own games.
  - These measure different things (total monetisation vs Premium payouts) and are not a direct contradiction.
