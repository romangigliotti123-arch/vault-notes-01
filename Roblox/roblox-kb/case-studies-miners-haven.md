# Case study: Miners Haven

**Genre:** Tycoon / mining

A long-running mining tycoon. Known for limited seasonal items, loot-box-driven early revenue before membership-style passes, and deep factory-building.

Also referred to as: Miner's Haven

## What creators say about it
- Seasonal limited-edition items that cannot be bought after the event create strong demand and pride in ownership. ([Roblox 2018-07 18:14](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=1094))
- Ran for years under handed-off leadership; loot boxes dominated early revenue before membership-style passes. ([GameMakers 2021-01 16:07](https://youtu.be/y8H_GRdGaJw?t=967))
- Tycoon depth: players build rare mineral processing factories from machine components. ([Roblox DevForum 2024-10](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/awesome-roblox-a-curated-list-of-awesome-things-related-to-roblox-oss/3215840))

## Transferable patterns
- Use limited-time items to create scarcity and pride
- Evolve monetization from loot boxes toward membership-style passes
- Give tycoon players deep factory-building systems
