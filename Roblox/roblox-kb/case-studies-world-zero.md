# Case study: World Zero

**Genre:** Open-world MMO RPG

Open-world RPG by Twin Atlas with monster-kill loot loops, handcrafted dungeons, a level-40 PvP arena, and a dragon boss event. Launched free with 70k users and later staggered content releases.

Also referred to as: World // Zero, World//Zero, World Zero (Twin Atlas)

## Numbers
- Roblox Creators (2021-07): launched free with 70k users from pre-launch alpha and beta hype.
- Roblox (2026-10): beaten in 5 hours at launch, per Roblox's statement, which prompted staggered releases.

## What creators say about it
- Handcrafted dungeons beat random filler: the tower update's random mazes proved less valuable (Roblox Creators, 2021-07). Later note praises varied dungeons (Cumin, 2025-05). ([Roblox Creators 2021-07 0:50](https://youtu.be/Gv5OVVyWM10?t=50), [Cumin 2025-05 12:47](https://youtu.be/2auVezIcNAE?t=767))
- A simple kill-loot-upgrade-progress loop with boss-fight peaks carries the game (Roblox Creators, 2021-07). ([Roblox Creators 2021-07 9:41](https://youtu.be/UL4QaSbfyC4?t=581))
- Reuse old dungeons as max-level runs to re-engage players who breezed through early content (Roblox Creators, 2021-08). ([Roblox Creators 2021-08 34:29](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=2069))
- Gating previously free tower rewards behind a gamepass caused backlash (Cumin, 2025-05). Newer advice: do not paywall progression that was once free. ([Cumin 2025-05 12:47](https://youtu.be/2auVezIcNAE?t=767))
- Newest note (2026-10): once players beat it in 5 hours, staggered world and dungeon releases plus random cosmetic colors extended play; the Discord community supports updates. ([Roblox 2026-10](https://youtu.be/qLP05zvMsW0))
- Daily quests with one-session objectives and in-game event communication keep players returning (Roblox Creator Docs, 2026-10). ([Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/introduction-to-quest-design), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/liveops-planning))

## Transferable patterns
- Handcrafted content over procedural filler
- Max-level replay runs for veteran players
- Staggered content drops to pace completion
- Avoid gating previously free rewards
