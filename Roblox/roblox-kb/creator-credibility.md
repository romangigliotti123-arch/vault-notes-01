## How to use this

- TRUSTED (score 7.5+): usable as a primary source, still prefer official docs for numbers and limits.
- MIXED (5.0-7.4): do not rely on alone; confirm a recommendation with a second source (docs or another creator).
- WEAK (below 5.0): must not be the sole source of any recommendation.
- INSUFFICIENT (fewer than 4 checkable claims verified): credibility unknown, treat as unverified, not as bad.
- Score = 50% claim accuracy (WRONG weighted 3x, OUTDATED half credit) + 20% evidence/track record + 15% depth + 15% honesty/no hype.
- Claims are judged on AI-extracted tip summaries, so an apparent error can be an extraction slip, not the creator's.

## Method and limits

- Sample: 2 videos per creator (1 if the creator has only one extracted video). This is a small sample; a verdict describes those videos, not the whole channel.
- Selection: video A = most cited by this knowledge base (youtu.be links across roblox-kb); video B = random with seed 20261030 per creator.
- Evidence packet per video: extracted tips and numbers, the best keyword-matched Creator Docs/DevForum notes per tip (often empty or only loosely related), and regex counts for hype, sponsor and deprecated-API wording in the transcript. Blender-research creators had no local Blender docs, so their claims rest on model knowledge only.
- Judge: one model call per creator (sonnet for the 70 most-cited Roblox creators and the Blender four, haiku for the 18 least-cited). Claims marked own-knowledge are not backed by a local doc. Judge output was not hand-audited claim by claim.
- Many claims are basic tutorial steps that are easy to confirm, which pushes scores up; UNVERIFIABLE and OPINION claims are excluded from accuracy.
- Third look (2026-11-01): 19 of the 34 INSUFFICIENT Roblox creators that still had an unused extracted video got one more video each (the next most-cited not yet used), up to 6 more checkable claims, checked against the local Creator Docs / DevForum notes and the official facts in [[ads-stats-monetisation-playbook]] (e.g. DevEx 0.0038 USD per Earned Robux, official cost-per-play benchmarks). Claims were pooled with the earlier ones and the score recomputed with the same formula; evidence/depth/honesty are the mean of the earlier and new judge scores. Rows with `3rd` in the videos column were re-scored this way. Judge: haiku, with sonnet re-checks for the borderline cases (1Coal, CORSOBLOX, CreatorsOnTheBlox, SmartyRBX). Still INSUFFICIENT after the third look: either the new video had too few checkable claims (many are UNVERIFIABLE or opinion), or the creator has no further extracted video.
- Cost: about $2.53 of the $4.00 cap for the first audit plus about $0.26 for the third look. Creators with no unused extracted video, and the two Blender-research creators (no local Blender docs), were not re-checked.
- Hub-note frontmatter (`credibility`, `credibility_score`) was added to each audited creator's `<name> - How to make successful Roblox games` note when one exists; `kb.py` now reads `youtube-learn/supplements/credibility.json` when it regenerates those notes, so the fields survive a rebuild; this table remains the source of truth.
