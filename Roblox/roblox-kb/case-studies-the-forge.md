# Case study: The Forge

**Genre:** Mining and forging / idle-crafting

Roblox mining-and-forging game: mine ore, forge with a hammer minigame, sell for gold, buy upgrades. Adapted from the Flash game Jacksmith and widely cited as fast-growing in 2025-2026.

Also referred to as: Forge

## Numbers
- No CCU or revenue figures. bubDEV (2025-12) cites 'strong stats' and longevity without quantifying them (G1407).

## What creators say about it
- Core loop: mine ore, forge with a hammer minigame, sell for gold, buy upgrades. Each step feeds the next. ([TheDankDeveloper 2026-01](https://youtu.be/3y7EU49_d7s))
- The loop is adapted from the Flash game Jacksmith; creators argue old browser games are a proven source of loops. ([Tizzy 2026-05 1:48](https://youtu.be/8q6kdFVL_fY?t=108), [janisjanis01 2026-04 0:40](https://youtu.be/xqHXDYbuF2w?t=40), [TheDankDeveloper 2026-01](https://youtu.be/3y7EU49_d7s))
- Lead with a strong gameplay video; The Forge was cited as an example for the Home feed (April 2026 post). ([Roblox DevForum 2026-03](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571))
- Conflict: bubDEV (2025-12) cites strong stats and longevity, while the newer Vindooly claim (2026-09) says the game declined after private servers were added. The newer claim is unverified; treat both as unconfirmed. ([bubDEV 2025-12 10:57](https://youtu.be/7z8rfg_jBuk?t=657), [Vindooly 2026-09 9:27](https://youtu.be/GSrXI-biGuA?t=567))

## Transferable patterns
- Remake proven browser-game loops in Roblox
- Minigame-based crafting as skill expression
- Lead with gameplay video for discovery
- Test private-server policy impact before assuming it helps or hurts
