# Case study: Island Royale

**Genre:** Battle royale

Roblox battle royale title from the 2018 era. Notes cover lobby friend invites that place friends on one team before the round, a major-update announcement tweet as an engagement case, and a 2026 Creator Docs mention as a popular battle royale example.

## Numbers
- No CCU, revenue or visit figures in source notes

## What creators say about it
- Let friends join the team from the lobby before the round starts; lobby invites reduce squad-forming friction. ([Roblox 2018-07 10:24](https://youtu.be/4rvzuYMtSlA?t=624))
- Announce major updates publicly (tweet) to lift engagement; the 2018 case shows an update announcement used as an engagement example, with no metrics given. ([Roblox 2018-07 15:36](https://youtu.be/JTV8apW2oBk?t=936))
- Battle royale is a recognised Roblox genre; Roblox Creator Docs cite Island Royale as a popular example of it. ([Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/battle-royale-series/project-setup))

## Transferable patterns
- Pre-round lobby invites that lock friends onto one team
- Publicise major updates through social posts
