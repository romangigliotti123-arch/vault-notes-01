# Case study: Racing game (unnamed, well-known-dev and background examples)

**Genre:** Racing

Unnamed racing entries: a 2018 racing title by a well-known developer that failed despite ~1M visits, a 2026 note on low racing demand on Roblox, and an official Server Authority example place for the networking pattern.

Also referred to as: Racing game (with a well-known dev), Racing game (unnamed, in background footage), Racing

## Numbers
- ~1M visits, did not find success (Roblox, 2018) G44
- Low player demand for racing on Roblox; paid ads needed (Yann, speaker claim, no figures) G1710

## What creators say about it
- Check genre demand before building: Yann says racing has low player demand on Roblox, so paid ads must reach players who do not usually play racing. ([Yann 2026-05 3:25](https://youtu.be/yw32DZtz4mQ?t=205))
- Developer reputation is not enough: a well-known developer's racing game reached ~1M visits and still failed to find success (Roblox, 2018). ([Roblox 2018-07 9:26](https://youtu.be/NmyzbBbrBS4?t=566))
- Learn the networking pattern from official references: Roblox's Server Authority example place (Dec 2025) is the reference for this pattern. ([Roblox DevForum 2025-12](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/studio-beta-build-fair-responsive-games-with-server-authority/4139157))

## Transferable patterns
- Validate genre demand before committing to a build
- Use official example places to learn a networking pattern
- Budget paid acquisition for low-demand genres
