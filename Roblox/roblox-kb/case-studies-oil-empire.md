# Case study: Oil Empire

**Genre:** Tycoon / resource-stealing

A game whose gas-stealing from refineries is a mechanic, and which inspired Hack A Business. Its lack of a base lock led to borrowing that mechanic from another game.

## What creators say about it
- Inspiration for a multi-server collection feature; a competitor check validated the idea, which Hack a Business shipped. ([Tizzy 2026-06](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI))
- Gas-stealing from refineries is a mechanic. Oil Empire lacks a base lock, so that mechanic was borrowed from another game. ([Tizzy 2026-06](https://youtu.be/zoz3PSMqets))
- A clone needs a meaningful core-loop twist; this is the creator's advice from the Hack A Business inspiration. ([Tizzy 2026-06](https://youtu.be/pu2o80prNV8))

## Transferable patterns
- Run a competitor check to validate features before shipping
- Borrow missing mechanics from other games deliberately
- Add a meaningful core-loop twist when cloning a concept
