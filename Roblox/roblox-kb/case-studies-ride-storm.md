# Case study: Ride Storm

**Genre:** Social motorbike roleplay

Verified motorbike roleplay game by VuukStudios. Succeeded via social features, long sessions, a loyal community and fantasy fulfillment. Reported ~10K CCU and ~$95K/month, driven by ads, regional pricing, community feedback and open-ended play.

## Numbers
- ~10K CCU and ~$95K/month (VuukStudios, self-reported, G2306)

## What creators say about it
- Social features, long sessions and a loyal community drove success in this fantasy roleplay game. ([VuukStudios 2026-08 1:16](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=76))
- Revenue drivers cited: ads, regional pricing, community feedback and open-ended play; ~10K CCU and ~$95K/month (self-reported). ([VuukStudios 2026-10 0:00](https://youtu.be/0ENzqNB_EUo?t=0))
- A community motorbike event was suggested as a D7 retention tactic (transcript spelling uncertain). ([VuukStudios 2026-08 8:23](https://youtu.be/Tk0qXybUK0E?t=503))

## Transferable patterns
- Build social features and community so players return.
- Use regional pricing to adjust revenue by market.
- Run community events as a D7 retention lever (unverified in source).
