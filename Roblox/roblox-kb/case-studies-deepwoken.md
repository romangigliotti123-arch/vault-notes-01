# Case study: Deepwoken

**Genre:** Dark-fantasy RPG / skill-based PvP combat

Skill-based RPG combat game where sliding, dashing and sprinting drive combat feel. Features custom matchmaking, large maps, and clan storage compared against shared RPG banks.

## Numbers
- Cut medium ping by 18% in closed beta after raising latency signal weight in custom matchmaking (Roblox, 2025-09)

## What creators say about it
- Raising latency signal weight in custom matchmaking cut medium ping by 18% in closed beta. ([Roblox 2025-09](https://youtu.be/V7MKrGggOOA))
- Movement (slide, dash, sprint) makes combat feel skill-based; in one reference game slide speed decays over time. ([ArTheDev 2025-10 0:00](https://youtu.be/KaCj7Jld35Q?t=0))
- Large maps may need environmental effects that follow the player. ([ArTheDev 2026-04 1:32](https://youtu.be/CnZEnFVAFSw?t=92))
- Onboarding gaps (no tutorial, no quest direction) were criticized; ad-heavy external wikis filled in missing in-game info. ([Cumin 2025-12 7:13](https://youtu.be/hQpC_UHxJDU?t=433), [Cumin 2025-11 0:41](https://youtu.be/cbOZKwPmmko?t=41))

## Transferable patterns
- Latency-weighted matchmaking
- Skill-based movement with decaying slide
- Environmental effects to anchor large maps
- Onboarding and quest direction as retention levers
