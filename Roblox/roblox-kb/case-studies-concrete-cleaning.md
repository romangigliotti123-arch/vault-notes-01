# Case study: Concrete Cleaning

**Genre:** Cleaning simulator

Solo-built cleaning game by VuukStudios. Reached 5K+ CCU but died once hype faded, with no loyal fanbase. Later idled for months and revived to about 4K CCU after an update paired with ads.

## Numbers
- 5K+ CCU and ~$15-20K/month, solo-built (VuukStudios, self-reported, G2060)
- Earlier peak $10-15K/month before dying in 1-2 months (VuukStudios, G2307; newer figure, conflicts with G2060)
- Revived to ~4K CCU after ads on an update (VuukStudios, G2307)

## What creators say about it
- Hype-driven CCU fades fast without a loyal fanbase or a social reason to return; 5K CCU died once hype faded. ([VuukStudios 2026-08 11:28](https://youtu.be/aw0PmBkakb4?t=688))
- Solo-built title reached 5K+ CCU and ~$15-20K/month (self-reported). CONFLICT: the newer G2307 gives the earlier peak as $10-15K/month; prefer the newer figure. ([VuukStudios 2026-08 11:37](https://youtu.be/m7015JSIrpw?t=697), [VuukStudios 2026-10 2:06](https://youtu.be/0ENzqNB_EUo?t=126))
- Ads timed with an update revived an idle game to about 4K CCU (self-reported). The revival was after months idle, so it was not sustained. ([VuukStudios 2026-10 2:06](https://youtu.be/0ENzqNB_EUo?t=126))

## Transferable patterns
- Build retention hooks and social reasons to return before peak hype.
- Time paid ads with an update to re-spike an idle game.
- Treat revenue figures as self-reported and prefer the most recent statement.
