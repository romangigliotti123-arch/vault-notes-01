# Case study: 99 Nights in the Forest

**Genre:** Horror survival

Survival horror where players build a campfire camp, gather resources, craft, survive escalating nights, and rescue kids from a monster. Launched June 2025 and became one of Roblox's biggest hits, winning Best Survival Game in 2026.

Also referred to as: 99 Nights In The Forest

## Numbers
- Peak 14M CCU (Roblox DevForum, G1341)
- Stable ~1-2M concurrent across a week (Last Level Studios, G1352)
- ~1M CCU after mid-2025 soft release (Roblox Creators, G2256)
- Tens of billions of plays (Ventures, G1417)
- Launched June 2025 (Roblox, G1293)

## What creators say about it
- Ads targeted to engaged players, personalised thumbnails and frequent events drove recommendations and D7 retention after launch. ([Roblox 2025-09 10:28](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=628), [Tizzy 2026-05](https://youtu.be/inWzxY26NXo))
- After launch, improve weak regions rather than adding new ones. Biome Refresher is a higher-return update per StudioKrew's GGAID data (agency estimate, not creator-stated). ([StudioKrew (agency blog, GGAID data) 2026-08](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-august-2026/))
- Remix proven mechanics. The sack mechanic came from Dead Rails, and combining proven ideas beats inventing from scratch, per rasdev. ([Daiorg 2025-12 31:47](https://youtu.be/JCMIb008Y6E?t=1907), [rasdev 2026-09 1:27](https://youtu.be/0_dR_081Hq8?t=87), [Roblox Creators 2026-09 29:11](https://youtu.be/hohwBQGQO7c?t=1751))
- Monetise with optional difficulty and revive-only purchases. Daiorg built the first version from a 20-minute plan, then expanded it weekly. ([Daiorg 2025-12 0:00](https://youtu.be/JCMIb008Y6E?t=0))
- Icon and title carry the hook: a one-sentence idea, a readable mascot with a straight-on face and big eyes filling the icon frame, and a title that signals progression. ([rasdev 2026-08 4:05](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=245), [rasdev 2026-09 3:22](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=202), [rasdev 2026-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/0_dR_081Hq8?t=0), [Tizzy 2026-05 10:28](https://youtu.be/owMnhg3fn_M?t=628))
- Check for clones before buying a template; janisjanis01 said a template they bought matched this game's concept closely. ([janisjanis01 2026-02 0:27](https://youtu.be/2gr2TUGdNAk?t=27), [janisjanis01 2025-10 0:23](https://youtu.be/MML_z-jpmF4?t=23))

## Transferable patterns
- Build on a proven survival loop from an earlier hit, then add one distinctive mechanic.
- Make the icon and title explain the game without reading the description.
- Use frequent in-game events and social/friends play to drive word of mouth and retention.
- Offer optional difficulty and revive-style monetisation rather than paywalled progress.
