# Case study: Griffin's Destiny

**Genre:** Animal roleplay (free-to-play)

Free-to-play animal roleplay with trinket currency, guaranteed legendary chests and a regular shop, plus a 35 Robux entry purchase. Known for stylised, mystical habitat environments.

## Numbers
- 35 Robux entry-level purchase option (Roblox Creators, G516, G527). No CCU or revenue figures given.

## What creators say about it
- Guarantee rare rewards (legendary chests) and run a regular shop so players are not frustrated by pure RNG. ([Roblox Creators 2023-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0))
- Offer a 35 Robux option so low-balance players can try paying; a wide price range aids conversion. ([Roblox Creators 2023-01 21:23](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=1283), [Roblox Creators 2023-03 0:47](https://youtu.be/Cli3ogW-azk?t=47))
- Name the soft currency (trinkets) to give the economy its own identity. ([Roblox Creators 2023-01 21:23](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=1283))
- Stylised, mystical habitat environments give the game a distinct look. ([Roblox DevForum 2024-05](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-meet-ahlvie-the-environmental-artist-behind-fantastical-worlds/2964648))

## Transferable patterns
- Guaranteed or pity rewards for rare drops.
- Low-entry price point so first purchases are cheap.
- Named soft currency tied to the theme.
- Distinct stylised art direction as a identity marker.
