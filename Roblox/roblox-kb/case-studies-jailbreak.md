# Case study: Jailbreak

**Genre:** Action roleplay / cops and robbers

Cops-and-robbers open-world roleplay with robberies, contracts, vehicles and a season pass. Built up over years from a prison, bank and cars; billions of plays and film adaptation plans (Badimo).

Also referred to as: Jailbreak (Badimo), jailbreak

## Numbers
- 550k CCU during 2020 Oceans live event (Roblox Creators) G326
- ~7.8B visits (Creators on the Blox, creator) G1735
- Season pass premium ~300 Robux (Roblox Creators) G294
- Multi-year IP with billions of plays; movie adaptation announced (Ventures) G1421
- Animated feature film plans announced 2025 (GamesBeat) G1522
- Launched with a two-developer team (Roblox) G1283

## What creators say about it
- Core loop: explore, complete objective, earn money, power up. Cops and robbers share this loop so both sides earn currency. ([Roblox Creators 2022-01 2:44](https://youtu.be/bROouMt1b3g?t=164), [Roblox Creators 2022-06 6:35](https://youtu.be/Mep4_w8qxeE?t=395), [Roblox Creators 2023-08 0:48](https://youtu.be/gkFKF9A-snY?t=48))
- Season pass with ten tiers, free and premium tracks, daily missions and a visible final reward; gold stripe marks premium icons. ([Roblox Creators 2021-09 1:10](https://youtu.be/k2gbQI-ggB4?t=70), [Roblox Creators 2021-12 1:19](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=79), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/ui-ux-design))
- Live events need a clear countdown and a short length: the 2018 Volcano event failed on an unclear countdown and length; the 2020 Oceans event worked. ([Roblox Creators 2021-12 4:57](https://youtu.be/bKvOPfp3oX0?t=297), [Roblox Creators 2021-12 17:50](https://youtu.be/bKvOPfp3oX0?t=1070), [Roblox Creators 2021-07 32:52](https://youtu.be/UL4QaSbfyC4?t=1972))
- Hints, not tutorials: contextual hints replaced tutorials, and in-world cues such as footprints imply escape routes. ([Roblox 2023-09 3:48](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=228), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding-techniques))
- Build a vertical slice first (one prison, one bank, guns and arrest). Newer 2025 advice says skip a new Jailbreak as a first project. ([Roblox Creators 2022-06 6:35](https://youtu.be/Mep4_w8qxeE?t=395), [SmartyRBX 2025-03 24:53](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU?t=1493), [SmartyRBX 2025-06 1:06](https://youtu.be/MAnvGNgBKgQ?t=66), [rasdev 2026-05 2:37](https://youtu.be/q0lIbkUC2r8?t=157))
- Ad placement respects loyal players: image ads fit the city better than portal ads, and placement is handled carefully. ([Roblox 2023-09 19:50](https://youtu.be/XJLW1G1Z-8A?t=1190))

## Transferable patterns
- Season pass with visible final reward and a gold premium marker
- Live events with a clear countdown and short duration
- In-world hints over text tutorials
- Start with a vertical slice and expand over years
