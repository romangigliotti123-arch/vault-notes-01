# Case study: Work at a Pizza Place

**Genre:** Open-ended roleplay (job simulator)

A long-running open-ended Roblox roleplay job game by Daiorg. Cited as an example of low-polish games with lasting popularity and as proof that solo developers can build top games.

## Numbers
- 17-year longevity and ~50,000 current players (Daiorg, self-reported) [G1427]

## What creators say about it
- Open-ended roleplay with no fixed goal works as a long-lasting time waster. ([Daiorg 2025-08 3:33](https://youtu.be/GkKUU9mCgwc?t=213))
- Longevity matters: 17 years and around 50,000 current players, as stated by the creator. ([Daiorg 2025-12 26:35](https://youtu.be/JCMIb008Y6E?t=1595))
- Early popular, low-polish games set the old quality bar: players valued fun over polish. ([Daiorg 2025-06 2:02](https://youtu.be/tMNQooZtTQo?t=122))
- A solo developer built a top game, cited as evidence that solo devs can succeed. ([RoyalDev 2026-08 5:29](https://youtu.be/6CefFrCFqcI?t=329))

## Transferable patterns
- Open-ended roleplay with no fixed goal for long retention
- Longevity and fun can outweigh polish
- Solo developers can reach top-game scale
