# Case study: Ultimate Football

**Genre:** Sports (American football)

Roblox football game by Roblox, also called Vexx and being renamed NFL Universal Football. Uses a live, context-sensitive controls HUD with large simplified icons, cosmetic customization, and console-first design with subscriptions.

Also referred to as: Ultimate Football (Vexx)

## Numbers
- Subscriptions add 5-7% of monthly revenue even when buried (Roblox, G838)
- Six thumbnails tested; 2.5x conversion gap (Roblox, G851)

## What creators say about it
- Use live, context-sensitive controls with large, simplified icons. ([Roblox 2024-09 28:30](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=1710))
- Cosmetic customization works as a secondary activity and monetization source. ([Roblox 2024-09 28:30](https://youtu.be/DeWXxpnoe4U?t=1710))
- Subscriptions buried in the menu still contributed 5-7% of monthly revenue (Roblox). ([Roblox 2024-09](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ))
- Testing six thumbnails found a 2.5x conversion gap; better thumbnails raised impressions. ([Roblox 2024-09](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM))

## Transferable patterns
- Test multiple thumbnails; conversion gaps can be 2.5x.
- Keep subscriptions discoverable, even when buried, and measure their share.
- Design context-sensitive HUDs with large simplified icons.
- Consider console-first design and renaming for broader reach.
