# Case study: Boxing Stars

**Genre:** Boxing simulator (training sim)

Solo-built boxing simulator by janisjanis01, the creator's own game. Adds snacks, potions, gift boxes, neon gloves and a king-of-the-hill event to drive training progression and friendly competition. Creator cites its clean animations as a quality reference.

Also referred to as: Boxing Stars (creator's game, boxing simulator)

## Numbers
- ~700k visits, early self-built game (janisjanis01, self-reported, G1944)

## What creators say about it
- Layer progression consumables and cosmetics (snacks, potions, gift boxes, neon gloves) onto a training loop to drive progression. ([janisjanis01 2025-03 0:00](https://youtu.be/OAsW5osz46o?t=0))
- A king-of-the-hill event adds friendly competition on top of solo training progression. ([janisjanis01 2025-03 0:00](https://youtu.be/OAsW5osz46o?t=0))
- Clean animations are a quality benchmark; the creator uses this game's animation quality as a reference. ([janisjanis01 2025-11 7:29](https://youtu.be/iEvgIId-8Uw?t=449))
- A solo-built early game reached about 700k visits (self-reported) while the creator learned every part of development alone. ([janisjanis01 2026-08 2:04](https://youtu.be/fjlOaEALWmg?t=124))

## Transferable patterns
- Pair training-loop progression with consumables and cosmetics.
- Add a competitive event (king-of-the-hill) to create social hooks.
- Set animation quality as a benchmark for solo projects.
