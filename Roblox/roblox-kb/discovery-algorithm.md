## Overview
Discovery has shifted from short-term engagement to measured long-term retention. Roblox's Recommended For You (RFY) evaluation window expanded from 7 to 28 days (April 2026), with retention read at day 1, day 2-7 and day 8-28. Roblox says it previously over-weighted short-term clicks and thumbnails. Official signals named in July 2026 are play-through rate (icon to Play), first-play bounce (exit within about 30-90 seconds), spend days and Robux per user. Roblox says the play-through signal is splitting into separate play-through, session-quality and spend signals, so track each one. Co-play, returning players and spend are cited as evidence of a meaningful experience. Roblox also reports the top 10 games' share of hours fell from about 30% to about 20%, and about 40% of the RFY top 100 were created in the last three months, so room for new games is growing. Indie sources add unverified claims about age-based session preferences, under-13 monetisation penalties and growth-based testing.

## Rules and findings
- **Optimise engagement, retention and monetization together** (multiple-creators): Roblox weighs engagement (by far the most weight), retention, monetization, social co-play, variety and relevance. Official advice is to build a high-quality core experience and not game individual signals. Games strong in both retention and monetization get the broadest Home distribution; strength in one area still earns impressions. In June 2026 QPTR was replaced by PTR plus First Play Bounce Rate (0-60s and 61-180s). The lookback went from 7 to 28 days. PTR is one of 20+ signals. - **Build for retention across 28 days, not short-term spikes** (multiple-creators): The algorithm measures Day 1, Days 2-7 and Days 8-28 separately. Roblox said short-term outcomes did not correlate with long-term ones. Returning players make the system send more traffic. New games are not made to wait 28 days: PTR/bounce and Day 1 feed early distribution, and deeper retention earns more later. Ads and editorial features can feed retrieval but do not train ranking. - **Platform edge cases: bots, daily reward, ownership transfers** (single-mention): Known bot engagement is excluded from RFY, and Kids/Select enforcement should not change ranking. ## Checklist
- [ ] Set genre in game settings
- [ ] Upload a custom thumbnail (multiple distinct variants)
- [ ] Confirm Exclude from Recommendations is off
- [ ] Write keyword-rich title/description matching real search phrasings
- [ ] Make the icon/title promise match the first 30-180 seconds
- [ ] Polish tutorial and onboarding to lower first-play bounce
- [ ] Aim for 10-15+ minute sessions; healthy, not addictive
- [ ] Add co-play features: invite prompts, private servers
- [ ] Add non-spammy monetization; consider rewarded video
- [ ] Add gameplay video previews
- [ ] Seed with friends, YouTubers or small spread-out ads (2-4 weeks)
- [ ] Watch Home recommendation impressions daily; do not trust one day
- [ ] Track PTR, first-play bounce, play days, playtime, spend, co-play days
- [ ] Check Audience Reach for Highly Engaged Players vs 250 (100 by Nov)
- [ ] Maintain at least 25 HEP with buffer
- [ ] Stop ads when organic Home traffic sustains lobbies
- [ ] If no Home recs in 7-14 days, reassess or move on
- [ ] Update regularly to extend content life and D28 retention
- [ ] Nominate for Standout Games; apply to Learn & Explore if educational
- [ ] Compare icon with front-page competitors
- [ ] Review Creator Hub Discovery documentation
- [ ] Pull play-through rate (RFY icon impressions to Play presses) weekly; no threshold is published, so track the trend.
- [ ] Measure first-play bounce at 30, 60 and 90 seconds to see where players leave; target the official ~30-90 second window.
- [ ] Split retention into day 1, day 2-7 and day 8-28 cohorts rather than one blended figure.
- [ ] Track spend days and Robux per user alongside session quality.
- [ ] Check Highly Engaged Player status for Kids/Select against the reported 250 threshold, and confirm it with Roblox before relying on it.
- [ ] Compare impressions before and after any monetisation change, especially for US under-13 audiences.
- [ ] Set session-length targets per age band: shorter loops for younger players, deeper loops for older players.
- [ ] Confirm your audience's age-check status when planning age-targeted features (64% of global DAUs have completed it; 80% Australia, 75% US and UK).
- [ ] Treat chart and player-count snapshots as directional context only; log the date and chart type.
- [ ] Plan content updates to sustain the 28-day window rather than relying on one launch spike.
- [ ] Benchmark placements against the 'Most Recommended by Roblox' table (as of Aug 20, 2026) to see which games Roblox is pushing.
- [ ] Confirm whether game-item surfacing on Home is live before counting on it in launch plans.
- [ ] Set launch and monetisation timelines with a buffer beyond announced roadmap dates.
- [ ] Test your game on mobile, console and PC and fix device-specific issues.
- [ ] Check that your first-session experience works for brand-new players, since growth for new players is being measured.
- [ ] Judge discovery performance at the 28-day mark, not after 7 days.
- [ ] Make passes and dev products presentable with clean names, icons and value statements in case they surface before entry.
- [ ] Audit the game profile page (description, thumbnail, media, icon) before launch, since profiles drive discovery (N7710).
- [ ] Before spending on launch traffic, confirm the first session has a hook that holds early players (T17947).
- [ ] Check that D1, D7 and D28 retention are tracked as the signals the speaker says the algorithm weights (T17946), noting this is unverified.
- [ ] Check Home recommendations in the acquisition tab to confirm the game has entered the algorithm.
- [ ] Test title and thumbnail pairs for CTR, comparing clicks per impression.
- [ ] Target roughly 40-50% watch-time retention (self-reported target).
- [ ] Keep titles, thumbnails and updates within one clear niche.
- [ ] Stop ads when the game holds about 50+ players after the ad period ends and Home recommendations are present.
- [ ] Do not use concurrent player count as a ranking input; it is not a signal to optimise.
- [ ] Wait about a week after launch or after a change before judging the algorithm result.
- [ ] Use a rolling 7-day window when comparing stats before and after a fix.
- [ ] If a new game has engaged players but low CCU, check Home recommendation traffic in the acquisition tab before changing the game.
- [ ] Review a week of retention and session-time data before pushing a feature update.
- [ ] Do not judge a new game in its first 72 hours.

## Where creators agree
- Home RFY is the dominant discovery surface (Roblox, VuukStudios, rasdev, MoseyTube, Matt The Method, Tizzy).
- Retention (D1/D7, now to D28), playtime and monetization drive promotion (Roblox, AlvinBlox, rasdev, janisjanis01, Alex Builds Games, Tizzy, waveshine).
- Ads are a data seed and not a substitute for retention (MoseyTube, VuukStudios, Tizzy, bubDEV, Roblox).
- Organic RFY players' behaviour is what ranks (Roblox Creators, DevForum).
- Small games can compete via per-user averages (Roblox, rasdev).
- Non-spammy monetization is favoured (AlvinBlox, rasdev, DevForum).
- Long-term retention, not short-term clicks, is what RFY rewards in 2026: Roblox Newsroom (T17487), Roblox Investor Relations (T17507, T17540), Platform Aeronaut (T17498), StudioKrew (T17519) and GamesBeat (T17532) all say so.
- Short-term engagement and exciting thumbnails were over-weighted before the change: Roblox Newsroom (T17488, Q1749) and Platform Aeronaut (Q1750).
- Friends, returning players and spend mark a meaningful experience: Roblox Newsroom (T17489) and Roblox Investor Relations (Q1752: players who stay longer return more often and bring friends).
- Better service to the audience means better discovery: GamesBeat (Q1756) and Roblox Investor Relations (Q1752).
- Roblox Newsroom (T17680) and PocketGamer.biz (T17642) both say shorter games suit younger players and deeper games suit older players over time.
- Roblox Newsroom (T17678) and the existing 28-day retention rule agree the discovery window extends past the first week.
- CTR (click-through from title and thumbnail) is an important algorithm input (rasdev, EISABOOS).
- Home recommendations are the milestone that marks a game entering the algorithm (janisjanis01).
- Both janisjanis01 and rasdev treat roughly a week as the minimum wait before judging algorithm or fix results.
- Both treat early weak stats as normal for a new game and advise against an early verdict.

## Where creators disagree
- **Do ads help or hurt?**
  - Roblox Creators: ads do not hurt organic discovery and can help cold start.
  - Erthlord (unverified): bad first ad round can hurt a place.
  - Erthlord, LovJak, Raul: ads alone did not trigger pickup.
  - MoseyTube, janisjanis01, Rapstrike GFX: ads got the game into the algorithm.
- **How much playtime matters**
  - Several indies (ItsZyonic, DevForum posters, SmartyRBX): playtime is the key signal.
  - Roblox: playtime benefit capped at 60 min; Deconstructor of Fun: no priority beyond threshold.
  - rasdev, VuukStudios, MoseyTube set 10-20+ min targets.
- **Monetization weighting**
  - SmartyRBX and a DevForum poster: Robux earned is pushed strongly (unverified).
  - Roblox: balanced with retention; sustainable monetization, not spam.
- **Luck vs stats**
  - 1_Bruce: some luck.
  - Erthlord: ticking the boxes works about 9 of 10 times.
  - janisjanis01: low stats mean it goes nowhere.
- **Does changing name/icon harm recommendations**
  - One creator (T1198): no removal.
  - Same item: title change reportedly may.
  - Creators on the Blox: icon change caused a blow-up.
- **Whether the algorithm favors big games**
  - PineappleDev: skewed toward bigger games.
  - Roblox: per-user averages and new-game testing help small games.
- **First-play bounce window**
  - Existing knowledge rule: make the icon match the first 30-180 seconds.
  - Roblox Investor Relations (T17508, July 2026): first-play bounce is users exiting within about 30-90 seconds. Prefer the official, more recent 30-90 second window.
- **Whether ads or monetisation hurt discovery**
  - Existing knowledge rule: ads do not hurt organic discovery; use them as a data seed.
  - Platform Aeronaut (T17498, indie, self-reported): games built around short-term monetisation get fewer impressions, especially with US under-13 audiences. Official sources are silent on ads; treat as unresolved and test.
- **Session length and reach**
  - RoWatcher (T17421, indie): long sessions can create a self-reinforcing recommendation loop.
  - Existing knowledge rule: the playtime benefit is capped at 60 minutes per user per day. Not strictly contradictory; the cap limits the benefit beyond 60 minutes.
- **Ideal session length**
  - Existing knowledge rule: target solid session length (indie heuristics), with no age split.
  - GamesBeat (T17532, indie): younger players favour short games; older players favour deeper games they return to. Refine the rule by age band.
- **What Roblox is optimising for**
  - Roblox Newsroom and Investor Relations (T17487, T17540): optimise for long-term retention and monetisation.
  - GamesBeat (T17533, indie): Roblox is testing discovery optimised for new-player growth. Likely a parallel test; unconfirmed.
- **Whether the algorithm still favours hyper-casual downloads**
  - Deconstructor of Fun speaker: the algorithm weights D1/D7/D28 retention and social gameplay (T17946)
  - Deconstructor of Fun hosts: dispute whether it still favours hyper-casual downloads (T17946)
- **When to stop ads after pickup**
  - janisjanis01 (2026-08): stop ads once player count is above 50 after the ad ends, and Home recommendations appear.
  - Existing file (undated): give a new game 7-14 days with ads, then decide; stop once Home recommendations sustain the game.
- **Time for a new game to reach the algorithm**
  - janisjanis01 (2025-06, T18891, Q1960): about two weeks
  - janisjanis01 (2025-07, T18911, Q1963): about a week; more recent, so preferred
- **When to ship features after a session-time gain**
  - janisjanis01 (2024-11, T18807, low-advice): a 3x session-time jump is a signal to ship features
  - Existing knowledge-base rule: prioritise getting into the algorithm before adding features

## Date context (what may be outdated)
- QPTR (2021-2026 advice) was replaced in June 2026 by PTR plus First Play Bounce Rate; older qPTR advice is outdated.
- 7-day signal windows changed to 28 days (Day 1, 2-7, 8-28), rolled out around Aug 20, 2026.
- 2021 advice on concurrent players and ratings as levers is superseded by personalised ranking.
- 2019 required top-1,000 popularity; now nearly all games are eligible.
- HEP thresholds are changing fast: 500, then 250, then 100 by Nov 2026; maintenance 25.
- Single real-time CCU ranking lasted about 8 years before personalised models.
- Early 2023-2025 advice that playtime or revenue alone drives ranking is simplified; later official sources give the multi-signal view.
- Gameplay video test results are from June-July 2026 and directional.
- 2026-04: RFY evaluation window moved from 7 to 28 days (Roblox Investor Relations, N7216). Any advice that assumes a 7-day RFY window is outdated.

## Instructions for an agent using this file
- If advising on discovery, base mechanics on the official 2026 signals (PTR, first-play bounce, play days, playtime, spend, co-play, 28-day retention), not the older qPTR-only model.
- If a game gets no Home impressions, check genre, custom thumbnail and Exclude from Recommendations first.
- If bounce rate is high, align the icon with the first 30-180 seconds and fix onboarding before spending on ads.
- If using ads, spread small spend over 2-4 weeks, watch for Home impressions, and stop when organic sustains the game.
- If no Home recs after 7-14 days of ads, recommend reassessing or moving on, and label it as indie heuristic.
- If a user wants longer sessions, note the 60 min/day cap and avoid addictive loops.
- If Home impressions fluctuate after a surge or update, advise waiting for the new baseline and checking seasonality and competitors.
- If the user targets under-16 or Select, check HEP counts against the current threshold and the 25 maintenance floor.
- If quoting indie CCU or visit figures, mark them unverified; prefer official statements on conflicts.
- If the game is niche, suggest search-niche targeting and YouTuber outreach as seeds.
- If advice refers to 7-day RFY or week-one retention, rewrite it using the 28-day window and the day 1 / day 2-7 / day 8-28 checkpoints.
- Use 30-90 seconds as the first-play bounce window (official, July 2026), not 30-180 seconds.
- Report play-through rate, first-play bounce, session quality and spend separately; never blend them into one score.
- Label indie-reported figures (HEP 250 threshold, under-13 monetisation penalty, age-based session preferences, growth test) as unverified.
- Do not use chart positions, CCU or player counts as ranking inputs; they are single-day snapshots.
- For US under-13 audiences, flag short-term monetisation as a possible impression risk and recommend a before/after impression check.
- When discussing new games, cite the top-10 share drop and the ~40% recent-content figure as evidence of room, not as a guarantee.
- When an official [OFFICIAL] source conflicts with an indie source, prefer the official source and note the conflict.
- Keep recommendations tied to retention and first-play metrics; do not suggest thumbnail-only fixes.
- If a claim comes from a single indie source and is not confirmed by official Roblox material, mark it as unverified.
- If a feature is dated 'late 2026' or later, check its current status before advising a developer to depend on it.
- If the 7-14 day ad decision rule conflicts with the 28-day window, follow the 28-day window and say so.
- If official Roblox sources and indie sources conflict, prefer the official source and note the conflict.
- Do not treat CCU, visits or active-player spikes as ranking inputs, even when they appear in case studies.
- If asked whether hyper-casual games get favoured by the algorithm, state that this is disputed in the 2026-10 source and that no official confirmation exists (T17946).
- If a game is launching into a crowded market, prioritise an early hook that holds D1 retention over paid launch spend (T17947, N7693).
- If advising on discovery, mention profile pages as a discovery surface alongside Home recommendations (N7710).
- Label all figures from this source (N7693, N7703, N7710) as self-reported indie estimates, not official Roblox data.
- If a game shows Home recommendations in the acquisition tab, treat it as having entered the algorithm and apply the stop-ads check.
- If the game holds above about 50 players after ads end, recommend stopping ads; otherwise keep the 7-14 day window before deciding.
- When advising on discovery, prioritise CTR (title and thumbnail) before feature work, since impressions only feed ranking through clicks.
- Do not present self-reported figures (40-50% retention, 50-player gate) as official Roblox thresholds.
- If a game was launched or changed less than about a week ago, do not conclude the algorithm rejected it. Say the data window is still open.
- If stats are judged before a rolling 7-day window closes, flag the result as premature.
- If a game has good retention but low CCU, check Home recommendation pickup before recommending a gameplay change.
- If the user asks when a new game will be picked up, answer about one week, with two weeks as the older range, and note these are self-reported indie figures.
- Label the one-week and 7-day window claims as creator-reported, not official Roblox guidance.
