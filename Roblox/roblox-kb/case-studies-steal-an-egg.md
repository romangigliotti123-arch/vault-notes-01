# Case study: Steal An Egg

**Genre:** Simulator with PvP stealing

Simulator where players hatch eggs into income-generating pets, upgrade a base and treadmill, and steal eggs from other players. Grew quickly to around 1.3M CCU, then was taken down briefly; many clones followed.

Also referred to as: Steal an Egg, Steal an Egg (as spoken)

## Numbers
- ~1.3M CCU rapid growth (Player.One, G1465)
- ~500K players (rasdev, G2131)
- ~1.1M players with ~$3.3M/month estimate (rasdev, G2391, estimate)
- 6B visits in Q3 2026 (OtakuKart, G2264)
- #1 top-playing-now at 8,412,307 players in an API snapshot (Roblox public API, G2716)
- Reached #1 top-playing within ~3 months of creation (Roblox public API, G2729)

## What creators say about it
- Simple loop: steal eggs, hatch pets, upgrade base. Player-vs-player stealing drives growth. Speed gating (Robux can skip ads) was used to drive engagement (Dexerto). ([Player.One 2026-00](https://www.player.one/robloxs-steal-egg-beating-some-platforms-biggest-games-164086), [Dexerto 2026-00](https://www.dexerto.com/roblox/viral-roblox-game-recreates-black-mirrors-iconic-treadmill-scroll-3401534/))
- Cap servers (seven) so theft feels personal; the consequence layer is a retention driver (StudioKrew GGAID analysis). ([StudioKrew (agency blog, GGAID data) 2026-08](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-august-2026/))
- Use tiered Robux speed upgrades and limited-time eggs removed at updates to create urgency (Mulch). ([Mulch 2026-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/WYODms_JMRA?t=0))
- Live admin-abuse events, global team objectives and rotating biomes bring players back during updates (Mulch; VuukStudios noted daily events keep kids returning). ([Mulch 2026-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/ATiEYhO_Lu4?t=0), [VuukStudios 2026-10 10:02](https://youtu.be/0ENzqNB_EUo?t=602))
- Title and icon explain the concept: verb-noun title and high-contrast icon of a character with a giant egg. Clones of proven loops keep the format alive. ([rasdev 2026-09 2:42](https://youtu.be/IyEqyPOjEvU?t=162), [rasdev 2026-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/Df5ebFGEOXY?t=0), [rasdev 2026-08 2:44](https://youtu.be/MH9TaOMTm9Q?t=164), [rasdev 2026-10 0:44](https://youtu.be/53OBGXktIPY?t=44))
- Conflict: critics call it a cash grab (TheDankDeveloper, Oct 2026) and an AllThingsHow guide says the Aug 2026 takedown followed removal of an AI-video treadmill feature (unconfirmed cause). Treat takedown risk as real and most recent. ([TheDankDeveloper 2026-10](https://youtu.be/eExtO_uF6Jg), [AllThingsHow (guide) 2026-08](https://allthings.how/steal-an-egg-roblox-ban-why-it-happened-and-how-it-returned/), [eGamers 2026-08](https://egamers.io/robloxs-no-1-game-steal-an-egg-was-pulled-hours-after-reaching-the-top/))

## Transferable patterns
- Use a simple, repeatable steal-and-upgrade loop with player-vs-player friction.
- Gate speed or progression with rewarded video or Robux options, but keep the loop readable.
- Plan event cadence and limited-time content to create return visits.
- Expect clones and decline; Deconstructor of Fun said reusing the mechanic is how replacements are made.
