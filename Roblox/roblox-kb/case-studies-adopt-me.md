# Case study: Adopt Me!

**Genre:** Pet collection / life roleplay

Long-running pet-collecting, trading and housing game with coin and house upgrades. Its limited-supply pets drive a player-to-player trading economy, and several sources cite it as a top-grossing title.

Also referred to as: Adopt Me

## Numbers
- ~200k CCU; 23-person distributed team (Last Level Studios) G129
- ~1.4M concurrent, RDC 2020 milestone (TheDevKing) G177
- Surpassed 4B plays with concurrency records (GameMakers) G133
- ~$150M yearly income, unverified speaker claim (VuukStudios) G673
- $30-40M/year estimate; merch/plushies extra (VuukStudios, speaker estimate) G790
- Subscription at $2.99 with Robux-only perk; Price Optimization +3.5% (Roblox) G837
- Trade delay for new accounts cut scam reports 5.5% (Roblox) G1302
- Worm-addition poll: 75% community approval (Roblox) G2355

## What creators say about it
- Protect the economy aggressively: an unlimited money tree caused inflation and was capped at $100/day; an earlier removal was fixed and re-released. ([Roblox Creators 2021-08 31:22](https://youtu.be/pSjFYDEgRxs?t=1882), [Roblox Creators 2021-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=0), [Roblox Creators 2021-10 0:00](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=0))
- Keep a steady cadence: weekly updates, timed events and ads re-engage old users; stagger small and big updates to sustain coverage. ([Roblox Creators 2021-12 23:31](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=1411), [Roblox 2023-09 5:35](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=335), [Roblox 2024-09](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ), [Roblox 2025-09](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew))
- Scarce tradeable items drive demand: limited-supply items and exclusive pet drops sustain trading and pre-drop hype. ([Roblox 2025-09](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew), [RoWatcher (tracker blog) 2026-03](https://rowatcher.com/news/top-10-roblox-games-by-players-march-2026-data-breakdown), [Roblox Creators 2023-04 0:46](https://youtu.be/vNKPlYeXj0Q?t=46), [StudioKrew (agency blog, GGAID data) 2026-05](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-may-2026/))
- Test starter packs and thumbnails: a royal egg starter pack beat a cracked egg, and a dog-walk thumbnail beat an action shot. ([Roblox 2025-09](https://youtu.be/j6OPA95-1O0), [Roblox 2025-09](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew))
- Anchor price on join: show a 900 Robux gamepass immediately to set a high reference price. ([Stewiepfing 2024-02 2:37](https://youtu.be/v8tFfBTr6M8?t=157))
- Combine community feedback with metrics: socials and admin-abuse events feed decisions, and a 75% approval poll backed the worm addition. ([Roblox 2026-10](https://youtu.be/hoMbv_R8AMU), [Roblox 2023-09 5:35](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=335), [Roblox Creators 2021-12 23:31](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=1411))

## Transferable patterns
- Cap any infinite currency source before it inflates
- Limited-supply tradeable items plus a new-account trade delay to cut scams
- Test thumbnails and starter packs with measured outcomes
- Use a cute, high-contrast icon with a character face
