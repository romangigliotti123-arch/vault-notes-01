# Case study: Piggy Intercity

**Genre:** Open-world multiplayer RPG

Roblox open-world multiplayer RPG expanding the Piggy franchise with exploration, base building and player choices. Won Best RPG in 2026 coverage; a 2022 promise of persistent player decisions shipped about four years later.

Also referred to as: Piggy: Intercity

## Numbers
- No CCU, revenue or visit figures in the notes.
- Awards only: Best RPG, reported by Roblox Newsroom, PocketGamer.biz, NetInfluencer and GosuGamers (2026-09; G2097, G2109, G2116, G2129).

## What creators say about it
- Place standout landmarks so they pull players toward major locations, while still letting them choose their own route. ([Roblox Creators 2022-03 4:16](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=256))
- Expanding an existing franchise into an open world with exploration, base building and player choice earned Best RPG recognition in 2026 coverage. ([Roblox Newsroom 2026-09](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/2026-roblox-innovation-awards), [PocketGamer.biz 2026-09](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-reveals-2026-innovation-awards-winners/), [NetInfluencer 2026-09](https://www.netinfluencer.com/roblox-crowns-animal-hospital-sweep-at-2026-innovation-awards/), [GosuGamers 2026-09](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/79176-full-list-of-winners-at-the-roblox-innovation-awards-2026))
- A public 2022 promise of an open-world multiplayer RPG where player decisions persist was kept; it shipped about four years later. ([Roblox 2026-09 1:09:24](https://youtu.be/HXZRH-2zurk?t=4164))

## Transferable patterns
- Landmark-based wayfinding that preserves player freedom
- Franchise reuse: extend a known IP into a bigger genre
- Persistent player choices as a core promise
- Announce long-horizon roadmaps and deliver them publicly
