# Case study: Natural Disaster Survival

**Genre:** Party survival

Classic party survival where players escape environmental disasters with friends. Simple movement and avoidance has stayed popular for about 15 years, spawning a niche of clones and winning Best Classic Game in 2026.

## Numbers
- 436M visits; 15 years of longevity (Roblox, G2204)

## What creators say about it
- Keep the concept simple: escaping environmental hazards with friends stayed popular for years and spawned clones (Daiorg; Roblox). ([Daiorg 2025-08 0:33](https://youtu.be/GkKUU9mCgwc?t=33), [Roblox 2026-09 1:11:32](https://youtu.be/HXZRH-2zurk?t=4292), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/design-for-roblox))
- Simple movement-and-avoid games are one of the few cases where portrait mode is practical (Roblox Creators). ([Roblox Creators 2023-03 2:22](https://youtu.be/PjwJb_nSePY?t=142))
- Friends-meetup use case shown in group invite demos (Roblox). ([Roblox 2023-09 17:03](https://youtu.be/V1uFFUUCLpo?t=1023))
- Pre-round power-up rewarded video placement in the lobby is a documented pattern (Roblox Creator Docs). ([Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/survival))
- Classic formats still recognised as timeless; the speaker calls the random chaos survival with friends the Roblox formula (Daiorg). ([Daiorg 2025-02 0:37](https://youtu.be/nutv8RT5UrQ?t=37), [Roblox Newsroom 2026-09](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/2026-roblox-innovation-awards))

## Transferable patterns
- Keep party survival loops simple enough to read instantly.
- Place rewarded videos in the lobby between rounds.
- Design for friends and group play.
- Expect clones to follow a proven classic format.
