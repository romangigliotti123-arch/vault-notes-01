# Case study: Pet Simulator

**Genre:** Simulator (pet collecting, eggs, currency)

Pet-collecting simulator built around hatching eggs, rarity-tier pets, and collecting currency orbs. Cited across videos as the template for pet and egg mechanics that many Roblox games copy.

## Numbers
- janisjanis01 (2024-08) states Preston's first successful game was 2018, followed by three more successful versions (speaker claim, not verified).

## What creators say about it
- Earn player trust before asking for money; monetization is handled carefully in this genre. ([ByteBlox 2024-11 1:29](https://youtu.be/Jl0SdXksQsI?t=89))
- Constant next milestones with a low difficulty curve sustain engagement. CORSO cites its grindy design as the extreme example of design that earns more money. ([GnomeCode 2024-10 1:31](https://youtu.be/H9CbgCe47Mo?t=91), [CORSO 2025-05 3:47](https://youtu.be/O2LZcQG5PPQ?t=227))
- Build a rarity tier system and give rare pulls a dedicated reaction, such as a cutscene on rare rolls. ([ByteBlox 2024-05 0:00](https://youtu.be/UUgj-hmX2LM?t=0), [janisjanis01 2026-08 8:05](https://youtu.be/pjN_aBDHAfY?t=485))
- Coin-style dev-product purchases are repeatable and act as a recurring revenue source. ([TheDevKing 2019-12 0:00](https://youtu.be/QC2bDZsFiz4?t=0))
- Make currency feedback legible: stack currency pop-ups and use compact number display, which players expect. ([Stewiepfing 2025-08 5:01](https://youtu.be/THpSS73tveg?t=301), [Stewiepfing 2024-04 0:22](https://youtu.be/qMdRT1xf9GM?t=22))
- Players still expect common simulator features even when they are not unique. Speaker notes hoverboards are in many simulators but were requested anyway. ([janisjanis01 2024-11 1:25](https://youtu.be/yxd5GMEjtDA?t=85))

## Transferable patterns
- Egg-and-pet template with rarity tiers and rare-roll spectacle
- Trust-first monetization before paywalls
- Repeatable low-cost dev products
- Stacked, compact currency feedback
