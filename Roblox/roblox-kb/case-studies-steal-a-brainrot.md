# Case study: Steal a Brainrot

**Genre:** Tycoon / steal-and-defend PvP simulator

Viral meme-driven steal-and-defend tycoon: collect brain-rot pets, steal from other bases, defend with conveyors and laser gates. Timed rare spawns and events drove peaks near 25M CCU and spawned many clones.

Also referred to as: Steal a Brain Rot, Steal A Brainrot, Steal a Brainrot (Steal a ... games), Steal a Brainrot (as spoken: steal a brain rock), Stella brain rot (transcribed)

## Numbers
- 25.4M CCU, first game past 25M; shorter average playtime than Grow a Garden (PocketGamer.biz) G1309
- Peak 25.4M CCU (RoWatcher) G1508
- 25.8M CCU, then 99.2% decline after event spikes (RoWatcher) G1513
- 25M concurrent (MoseyTube, creator) G1863
- 25M+ players, still 100K+ daily; revenue unofficial (rasdev) G2389
- ~48B plays, ~300k concurrents (Ventures) G1416
- 15M+ concurrent contributed in Aug 2025 (RoWatcher) G1472
- 1.8M players despite criticism (Cumin) G1236
- Icon shows a $997M figure (rasdev), an icon display, not revenue G2137
- Shop personalisation: over 5% increase in Robux spend per user (Roblox DevForum) G1824
- ~3B visits; fell to fifth in ranking (OtakuKart) G2267

## What creators say about it
- The steal-and-defend loop drives peaks: conveyors, stealing from other bases and laser gates; small bases keep theft visible to everyone (Roblox). ([MoseyTube 2026-07 2:04](https://youtu.be/1Oicl2fFt3o?t=124), [Roblox 2026-10](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc), [RoWatcher (tracker blog) 2026-02](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-brainrot-invasion-how-absurd-meme-games-conquered-roblox-s-charts))
- Timed rare spawns create FOMO bursts that drive spending, especially among kids; event-driven spikes are followed by steep decline (99.2% per RoWatcher). ([Cumin 2025-11 5:01](https://youtu.be/66n0P2NtBoQ?t=301), [RoWatcher (tracker blog) 2026-02](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals))
- Live events can break mobile performance: stacked particle effects caused FPS drops; alerts and microprofiler dumps pinpointed the emitters (Roblox). ([Roblox 2026-10](https://youtu.be/bVU69VImiEc))
- Newer advice supersedes earlier framing: 2025 SmartyRBX advice said learn from it, but the 2026-10 host says it churns, gets copied and is declining. ([Deconstructor of Fun 2026-10 31:03](https://youtu.be/oU3oz6Ddz7g?t=1863), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-10 10:15](https://youtu.be/NrPjXO_7oKk?t=615), [SmartyRBX 2025-06 12:16](https://youtu.be/MAnvGNgBKgQ?t=736), [SmartyRBX 2025-07 0:00](https://youtu.be/vBllyv2fnWw?t=0))
- A personalised shop was cited as lifting Robux spend per user by over 5%, so shop placement is a monetisation lever. ([Roblox DevForum 2026-06](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-shop-a-personalized-in-game-storefront/4701505))
- Cultural timing beats budget: a TikTok-meme origin reached ~48B plays and cultural timing can outperform production budget (StudioKrew). ([Ventures 2025-12 5:46](https://youtu.be/Nb50UhXcpRM?t=346), [StudioKrew (agency blog, GGAID data) 2026-05](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-may-2026/), [Erthlord 2025-09 0:22](https://youtu.be/fdDscQ6CWIs?t=22))

## Transferable patterns
- Pair a steal mechanic with visible shared bases so conflict happens publicly
- Schedule rare timed spawns to create FOMO
- Profile VFX cost during live events with microprofiler
- Prototype quickly and avoid clone-only builds (G2175 advises against cloning)
