# Case study: Mad City

**Genre:** Cop roleplay / prison-escape hybrid

A cop roleplay game built on the appeal of taking down criminals. Early copied Jailbreak's prison layout and E-circle system, later added powers, and did well at launch.

## What creators say about it
- Cop roleplay built on the appeal of taking down criminals; described by 1Coal as one of the biggest games. ([1Coal 2023-08 4:49](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=289))
- Copying Jailbreak's prison layout and E-circle system early drew criticism that it was not original, but it earned respect later. ([Creators on the Blox 2026-06 40:39](https://youtu.be/5vJCBrxPHmY?t=2439))
- Adding powers to a Jailbreak-like game did amazingly well at launch. ([rasdev 2026-07 4:08](https://youtu.be/t3i54NTcDi8?t=248))

## Transferable patterns
- Layer a novel system (powers) onto a proven loop
- Expect originality criticism early; sustained execution can earn respect
- Lean on a clear fantasy (catching criminals)
