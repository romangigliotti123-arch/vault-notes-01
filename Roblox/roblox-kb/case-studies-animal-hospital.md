# Case study: Animal Hospital

**Genre:** Co-op horror / night-shift survival

Cute but eerie night-shift horror where players admit animal patients and spot anomalies. Launched 2026, reached about 737K CCU in two months and swept four awards, including Best New Game and People's Choice.

Also referred to as: Animal Hospital (Anomaly), Anomaly (studio)

## Numbers
- ~737K CCU at two months old (GosuGamers, G1459)
- Nearly doubled to 1.2M CCU after a major update (GosuGamers, G1452)
- 2B+ visits since early June debut (Tubefilter, G1454; solo developer)
- 451 'Most Recommended' placements (StudioKrew, G2011)
- Four awards including Best New Game and People's Choice (Roblox Newsroom G2095; PocketGamer.biz G2100; NetInfluencer G2111)

## What creators say about it
- Clear fantasy title ('what if a hospital was run by animals') and consistent art direction across characters and rooms sold the cute-but-eerie vibe (Tizzy; Roblox). ([Tizzy 2026-07](https://youtu.be/FnsIL4zPCQg), [Roblox 2026-09 54:50](https://youtu.be/HXZRH-2zurk?t=3290))
- Mix proven mechanics: Phasmophobia sanity, 99 Nights streaks and Scary Shawarma Kiosk ideas combined into a fast-growing horror game (GosuGamers). Streak/progression systems like Total Shifts counters are reusable. ([GosuGamers 2026-00](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78741-animal-hospital-becomes-roblox-s-latest-horror-sensation-here-s-everything-you-need-to-know), [GosuGamers 2026-00](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78741-animal-hospital-becomes-roblox-s-latest-horror-sensation-here-s-everything-you-need-to-know))
- Live lore updates almost every other week sustained interest after launch (GosuGamers). ([GosuGamers 2026-09](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/79176-full-list-of-winners-at-the-roblox-innovation-awards-2026))
- Core challenge is pattern recognition: anomalous patients hide among routine cases, so correct reactions to breaks in pattern drive play (AllThingsHow). ([AllThingsHow (guide) 2026-05](https://allthings.how/animal-hospital-anomaly-on-roblox-how-the-night-shift-and-anomalies-work/))
- A major update can nearly double CCU; class visual reworks and a new lore character were part of this (GosuGamers). ([GosuGamers 2026-00](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78773-roblox-animal-hospital-hits-1.2-million-ccu-with-major-update-featuring-new-character-and-items))
- Algorithm support: a fast move from release to top experiences under the updated discovery algorithm (Roblox Investor Relations, 2026-07). ([Roblox Investor Relations 2026-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm), [StudioKrew (agency blog, GGAID data) 2026-08](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-august-2026/))

## Transferable patterns
- Use a fantasy-led title and consistent art direction.
- Combine proven survival/horror mechanics with a fresh twist.
- Ship lore updates on a fixed short cadence.
- Plan major updates to reset interest and lift CCU.
