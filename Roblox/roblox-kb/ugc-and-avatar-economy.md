# Ugc And Avatar Economy

_UGC items, avatar marketplace, Creator Store, limited items, avatar economy, selling assets._

Evidence base: 346 extracted items from 135 videos by AllThingsHow (guide), Arow, CORSO, Cumin, Deconstructor of Fun, Erthlord, GosuGamers, Izzzyzzz, Last Level Studios, Mulch, Roblox, Roblox Creator Docs, Roblox Creators, Roblox DevForum, SimTek Game Development, StudioKrew (agency blog, GGAID data), ThatGuyEvan, ThePurpleGuy, Where The Money Went, Xaiyne, bubDEV, eGamers, janisjanis01, rasdev, {EISABOOS} "RBX" (2019-08-12 to 2026-10-30). Roblox changes quickly: check the date on each source and see Date context below.

## Overview
Indie creators add a second earnings lever beyond selling items: game owners earn a commission when UGC is bought through their experience. A 2022 report cites 30%, while a 2026 source gives a 30% creator / 40% game owner / 30% market fee split for items sold in other people's games. Limited-run items are reported as the biggest earners (one creator's Ice Antlers: 414 sales, 12,082 Robux), and steady releases compound sales. Emote and accessory specs and fees are current as of 2025-2026: 10 s emote cap, 5-stud body limit, 750 Robux animation upload, 1,500 Robux publish fee (Xaiyne, 2025-08). Promised Limited status must be delivered. All revenue and sales figures are self-reported from small samples, so verify platform terms before planning around them.

## Rules and findings
- **Confirm eligibility before planning sales** (multiple-creators): Listing a layered or rigid accessory on the Marketplace requires a Roblox Plus or Premium account, an upload fee, and a publishing advance. Today's Picks nomination requires age 17+, ID verification, Community Standards and Marketplace Policy compliance. Creator Store sellers earn 100% of net proceeds. ## Checklist
- [ ] Confirm account eligibility: Plus/Premium for layered or rigid accessory listing; 17+ and ID-verified for Today's Picks.
- [ ] Build the avatar item through model, texture, import, Accessory Fitting Tool, Avatar Setup, validate, publish.
- [ ] Start from Roblox mannequin/template and reference bodies; run template cleanup before publishing.
- [ ] Never delete cage vertices or faces; keep outer cage tight to clothing.
- [ ] Set asset Category to match Accessory Type; pick the correct rigid slot.
- [ ] Pick Body suit/Full mask only for full-body or full head/face items; otherwise Shorts, Dress/Skirt, or Pants.
- [ ] Categorize honestly; report auto-categorization errors as bugs.
- [ ] Budget the publishing advance for each non-free item; free Limited costs 100 Robux per unit.
- [ ] Publish at most one Limited item per day; non-Limited items have no cap.
- [ ] Decide resellable vs Limited before publishing; Limited is the only resellable or free tier.
- [ ] Restock free Limited from configure page only if never resellable.
- [ ] Set Sale Location (Experience By Place ID) and enable third-party sales if you want items sold.
- [ ] Buy the Avatar Creation Token with Robux before in-experience creation; match token type to asset type.
- [ ] Emote: 80 Robux fee, Avatar Item content type, Emote category, Generate asset ID.
- [ ] Animation pack: original work only, one per day, 80 Robux, validate all seven state groups.
- [ ] Heads: include all 17 FACS base poses; test dynamic heads.
- [ ] Bundle only eyelashes, eyebrows, and hair with a body; parent eyelash/eyebrow accessories under the model.
- [ ] Check IP and polycount; add trademark/branding for distinctiveness.
- [ ] Archive own items only after 180 days; Limited cannot be archived.
- [ ] Enable Distribute on Creator Store to allow copies; keep dependencies under 15,000.
- [ ] Plan for up to 24-hour moderation before sale.
- [ ] Inspect free models for hidden scripts before insert; use RoDefender.
- [ ] Verify skin tones and modesty layer when texturing bodies.
- [ ] Check DevForum before building on alpha features like PromptCreateAvatarAsync.
- [ ] Make the experience Public before expecting outside players or Marketplace sales.
- [ ] Before upload, confirm inner and outer cages are complete on every accessory and layered clothing item; partial cages fail validation.
- [ ] Set MeshPart Material to Plastic, Transparency 0, VertexColor 1,1,1, and strip extra Scripts and Parts from the Model or Accessory.
- [ ] Keep accessories as separate objects from head and body geometry; keep clothing as modular layers.
- [ ] Run Avatar Setup and the Blender validation tool, then verify the body against character specs even if the importer passed.
- [ ] Keep emotes under 10 seconds, keep the root near its start, and target a standard R15 rig.
- [ ] Confirm pilot eligibility (one Marketplace publish in the past 30 days, 12 months clean moderation, clean DMCA) before planning animation or background sales.
- [ ] Confirm the target games use the 'Player Choice' animation setting before promising animation pack reach.
- [ ] Finalize and validate every animation state before publishing a pack; clips cannot be updated afterward.
- [ ] Replace legacy 'Face' and 'Head' AssetType filters with GetHeadShapesAsync, BodyPartDescription.HeadShape, and Roblox-creator Dynamic Head searches.
- [ ] Call PlayerOwnsAsset before saving outfits; request PromptAllowInventoryReadAccess once per session before GetInventory.
- [ ] Grant collaborators Use & View, not Edit, for consumption; add the universe ID under Permissions > Experiences for restricted packages.
- [ ] Name eyebrow and eyelash accessories EyebrowAccessory, EyelashAccessory, or HairAccessory and bundle them with a body or makeup asset.
- [ ] Equip test clothing and accessories in Studio under varied lighting and skin tones; use the Accessory Fitting Tool.
- [ ] Offer customizable skin tones for human-like avatars.
- [ ] Enable Enhanced Protection in Settings > Security if you are a top creator or hold a high Robux balance.
- [ ] Check the current fees page for the upload fee per item type before budgeting catalog size.
- [ ] Archive development items only after confirming no live experience uses them, because archived assets vanish from your own games.
- [ ] Do not count avatar item sales toward the 18+ rate.
- [ ] Use Generated Accessory objects when you need Marketplace publishing, HumanoidDescription equipping, or Toolbox sharing.
- [ ] If building an in-game avatar customizer, use AvatarEditorService as the base.
- [ ] Before publishing an emote, select Avatar Item > Emote and wait until the animation shows 'valid'.
- [ ] Keep emote length at 10 seconds or less.
- [ ] Keep every body part within 5 studs of the root part and avoid very fast motion.
- [ ] Add relevant emote tags at publish time.
- [ ] Select the correct group before publishing; publish as an Avatar Item, not a Developer Item, so it reaches the marketplace.
- [ ] For accessories, upload via the Avatar tab as Hat type, wait for validation, then set the category (face accessory), name it, and add it to a bundle if required.
- [ ] Budget the animation upload (750 Robux) and item publish fee (1,500 Robux, Xaiyne 2025-08); verify the current amount first.
- [ ] Expect marketplace review to take over 5 hours and plan the launch around it.
- [ ] Display UGC items in your game and prompt in-game purchases to earn commission.
- [ ] Plan a try-on experience with an avatar loader to show outfits before purchase.
- [ ] Announce a Limited drop only if you will release it as stated.
- [ ] Track sales per item and keep releasing new items on a schedule.
- [ ] If building a trading-based item economy, keep rare items scarce and avoid frequent re-releases of them (T17481).
- [ ] Before launch, review progression for grind-plus-paid-boost pairings. If the grind feels engineered to sell boosts, rebalance it (T17528).
- [ ] When reworking a cosmetic design, decide whether the old version can be sold as a skin before retiring it.
- [ ] Audit any borrowed script for copied or decompiled source from other games before publishing.
- [ ] Draft private unfinished UGC assets with Asset Iteration once it is live, to avoid moderation delay.
- [ ] Confirm emissive texture support in Studio before planning glow effects for a UGC release.
- [ ] Before publishing, estimate the listing fee (profile-only ~80 Robux vs Marketplace ~1,000 to 1,500 Robux, self-reported) and confirm it in the Creator Hub.
- [ ] Model UGC revenue as a declining curve unless new items are released; do not assume passive income from old uploads.
- [ ] Label any Robux-to-USD conversion as an estimate derived from creator-reported figures, not a platform rate.
- [ ] Before running paid media on UGC, decide whether the content is commissioned from creators or is organic influencer posting, and treat them as separate plans.
- [ ] When repurposing clips as Shorts, trim them to about 10-20 seconds.

## Where creators agree
- Official docs and the indie creators (rasdev, Erthlord) agree that scarcity drives value: Limited items and trading create resale value (N2922, N2624, T7110, N3021).
- Official docs and the creator videos agree that avatar items need validation via Avatar Setup and clean cages before publishing (T9880, T9517, T7850, T9515).
- Roblox Creator Docs and indie creators agree on the Marketplace and resale fee structure (N2923 30% resale; T8708 100% net for Creator Store).
- Official Roblox docs and indie DevForum posts both say moderation and validation gate every catalog listing, so sales cannot start before approval (T14559, T14149, T13678).
- Official and indie sources agree that copies and taken-down items are removed with refunds and earn nothing durable (T11673, T13420).
- Both official and indie sources say animation packs and emotes need specific technical compliance and pilot or program access before publishing (T15475, T13678).
- Official and indie sources agree that Classic heads are being replaced by Dynamic Heads and legacy AssetType filters no longer work (T13167 with N5088).
- Game owners earn a share of UGC sold through their experience (Mulch 2022 and Where The Money Went 2026 both describe a cut on in-game sales).
- Scarcity and release timing matter for sales: Limited-run items are reported as top earners (ThePurpleGuy) and broken Limited promises damage buyer trust (ThatGuyEvan).

## Where creators disagree
- **Robux to USD conversion rate in rasdev's numbers**
  - rasdev (N2920, N2921) implies ~$0.0125 per Robux for Core Blocks and Headless Horseman.
  - rasdev (N2924, N2925) uses DevEx-rate conversion (~$0.0038 per Robux) for larger sales.
  - Official docs give no conversion rate; treat USD figures as unreliable.
- **Trading strategy vs official publishing limits**
  - Erthlord (T6290, N2624) says 100+ small trades per day build inventory value.
  - Official docs (T7110, N3021) cap Limited publishing at 1 per day and charge 100 Robux per free Limited unit (N3020).
  - These cover different actions (trading vs publishing), so they are not a direct contradiction, but Erthlord's trade-volume tactics are unverified and not platform-sanctioned.
- **Label mismatch on Creator Store exponent rule**
  - T8046 is official Roblox Creator Docs but tagged [low-advice video] in the source.
  - Treat the -9 exponent as official; the label is inconsistent.
- **Cheap high-volume UGC versus spec-compliant quality**
  - Arow (T12930, Q1334): skip expensive modelers and make cheap cash-grab emotes and accessories, which the creator says do well.
  - Roblox Creator Docs (T14051, T14149, T15475): items must meet cage, cleanup, validation, and content policy specs; cheap items still need to pass moderation.
  - Resolution: follow the official specs; treat Arow's revenue and strategy claims as unverified and self-reported (N4884, N4964, N4965).
- **Whether Limited or free UGC items are allowed**
  - Roblox DevForum, Aug 2019 (T10549, Q1139): creators cannot release Limited, Limited Unique, or resellable UGC items, and UGC items cannot be free.
  - Existing knowledge file rules and 2026 pilot material treat Limited and resellable availability as an active choice (T13678 context). Resolution: the newer state wins; the 2019 restriction is historical.
- **Upload fee amounts**
  - Roblox DevForum, 2021 (N4220, N4221): 50 R$ partial rollout, then 10 R$ full rollout for shirts, pants, and t-shirts.
  - Roblox Creator Docs, Oct 2026 (N5749, T14180): upload fee required, and fees vary by item type on the fees page; no amount listed. Resolution: use the current fees page; treat 2021 amounts as historical.
- **Which item types can be sold on the Marketplace**
  - Roblox Creator Docs (T14180): only 3D accessories, clothing and character bodies can be sold on the Marketplace.
  - Pilot material (T13415, T13679, T13678) and emote upload (T15469-T15475) show animation packs, emotes, and Avatar Backgrounds going through separate sale or publish paths. Resolution: note that the scope doc may be incomplete; verify current scope per item type before planning.
- **Commission or revenue split on UGC sold in games**
  - Mulch (2022-07): 30% commission to the game owner; Roblox keeps 70% (N6232).
  - Where The Money Went (2026-10): 30% creator / 40% game owner / 30% market fee for items sold in other people's games (N6679). Newer; prefer this for current planning.
  - ThatGuyEvan (2019-08): 70% creator / 30% Roblox, marked as speculation based on the paid-access split, unconfirmed (N6092). Treat as outdated and unverified.
- **Upload and publish fees**
  - ThePurpleGuy (2026-02): 750 Robux upload fee per item (N6501).
  - Xaiyne (2025-08): 750 Robux for animation upload and 1,500 Robux publish fee for an item (N6411, N6412).
  - Xaiyne (2026-03): 300 Robux at upload submit, unclear whether it is a fee (N6508).
- **Whether UGC earnings continue passively after upload or need ongoing releases**
  - Xaiyne says uploaded avatar items keep earning passively after upload (T17988).
  - The existing knowledge-file rule 'Keep releasing items to compound sales' implies that continued new releases drive growth; Xaiyne's own emotes-group figure shows declining earnings (N7729).
