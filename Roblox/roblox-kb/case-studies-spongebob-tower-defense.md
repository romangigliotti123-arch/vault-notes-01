# Case study: Spongebob Tower Defense

**Genre:** Tower defense with gacha and live-ops monetisation (licensed IP)

A licensed tower defense game using a Paramount SpongeBob IP license. Monetised with sand-crate gacha, pay-to-speed tiers, tradable cosmetics, rewarded ads and starter bundles. Mentions come from several creators.

Also referred to as: SpongeBob Tower Defense

## Numbers
- Sand crate exotic odds 1.78% (as stated by Bemmy) [G1147]
- Built in 6 weeks by 4 people; 52 weekly live-op updates; ~70% 18+ audience (as stated by Deconstructor of Fun) [G1584]
- 400 CCU to top-20 earner and 600M+ visits (as stated by Business of Creators; third-party claim) [G2021]

## What creators say about it
- Sand crate gacha with 1.78% exotic odds and bulk-discount prompts drove heavy spending, but low odds triggered visible backlash. ([Bemmy 2025-06 0:00](https://youtu.be/-Z0C5YJtnqY?t=0))
- Monetisation mix: pay-to-speed at 2x/3x/5x/7x, tradable cosmetics, rewarded ads unlocking perks and tiered starter bundles. ([Roblox 2025-09](https://youtu.be/MJ-zYfL10Ew))
- A rewarded ad option was visible in the shop, stacked multiple ad views for different rewards, and was offered during between-level downtime. ([Roblox Creators 2025-08 4:50](https://youtu.be/7AtvvUxvCaU?t=290))
- A fast 6-week build with 4 people was followed by 52 weekly live-op updates, and the audience skews ~70% 18+ despite a kids' IP (as stated). ([Deconstructor of Fun 2026-04 0:00](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=0))
- Licensed IP in a saturated, proven genre, built in weeks, reportedly went from 400 CCU to a top-20 earner with 600M+ visits (third-party claim). ([Business of Creators 2026-08 5:40](https://youtu.be/lpIzMycpiKo?t=340))

## Transferable patterns
- Licensed IP in a proven genre as a fast path to traction
- Place rewarded ads in shop and between-level downtime
- Pay-to-speed tiers and tiered starter bundles
- Publish gacha odds clearly; low odds trigger backlash
