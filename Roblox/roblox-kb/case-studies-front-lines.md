# Case study: Front Lines

**Genre:** Team FPS shooter (realistic vs arcade)

Military FPS. Front Lines pivoted from a realistic Operation Scorpion style to a fast arcade shooter after frustration with spawns and slow pacing. Realistic and high-fidelity shooters have since shown retention limits.

Also referred to as: Frontlines

## Numbers
- 52.5M plays, mostly off-platform 18+ players (Roblox, 2023-09, for Maximal's high-fidelity FPS)

## What creators say about it
- Newer evidence supersedes earlier optimism: a realistic shooter with initial hype fell off after 20-30 minutes (SmartyRBX, 2025-07), and a high-fidelity shooter failed to reach hit-level attraction despite production quality (Deconstructor of Fun, 2026-04). ([SmartyRBX 2025-07 8:38](https://youtu.be/exOJsFcer1Q?t=518), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-04 27:37](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=1657))
- After frustration with spawns and slow pacing, pivot to a fast arcade shooter; FPS and performance are the top criticisms. ([Roblox Creators 2022-11 4:16](https://youtu.be/xXIc-p9hatU?t=256))
- Older advice (2022-12) said realistic visuals and off-platform YouTuber outreach attracted older players and that a fast-paced shooter fits that audience. Treat as dated relative to the 2025-2026 retention notes. ([Roblox Creators 2022-12 9:23](https://youtu.be/0PEBFsguCdw?t=563))
- Earlier notes (2023-2024) cited high-fidelity shooters as proof a market exists; this conflicts with the newer retention data and should be weighed accordingly. ([Roblox 2023-09 2:30](https://youtu.be/UVq0045HNQ4?t=150), [Roblox Creators 2024-08 12:19](https://youtu.be/2t_zhxyLOJ8?t=739))

## Transferable patterns
- Test retention beyond the first 20 minutes, not just launch hype
- Fix spawns and pacing before adding fidelity
- Off-platform creator outreach to reach older players
- Performance and FPS stability as a first-order priority
