# Case study: TTK

**Genre:** Tactical shooter

Tactical shooter from Sable Digital focused on high-fidelity combat: helmet cams, optics and squad gameplay. Won Best Innovation in Tech, as reported by four outlets.

Also referred to as: Sable Digital's title

## Numbers
- Award: Best Innovation in Tech (no revenue or CCU figures given)

## What creators say about it
- High-fidelity combat (helmet cams, optics, squad gameplay) is a differentiator that won Best Innovation in Tech. ([Roblox Newsroom 2026-09](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/2026-roblox-innovation-awards), [PocketGamer.biz 2026-09](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-reveals-2026-innovation-awards-winners/), [NetInfluencer 2026-09](https://www.netinfluencer.com/roblox-crowns-animal-hospital-sweep-at-2026-innovation-awards/), [GosuGamers 2026-09](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/79176-full-list-of-winners-at-the-roblox-innovation-awards-2026))
- Leading with specific tech features (helmet cams, optics) gives press a clear hook. ([Roblox Newsroom 2026-09](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/2026-roblox-innovation-awards))

## Transferable patterns
- Lead with one concrete technical differentiator in press and marketing.
- Use squad-based gameplay to differentiate a shooter.
