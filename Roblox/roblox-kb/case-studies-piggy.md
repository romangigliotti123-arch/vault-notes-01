# Case study: Piggy

**Genre:** Horror escape / chase

Horror escape game where a pig chases players through story chapters with keys, doors and puzzles. Grew through lore-driven community engagement, cosmetic-only shop and teaser-driven launches.

## Numbers
- Official Piggy produced by Team I2R under permission from MiniToon, who provides IP and platform; team self-funds most content (Creators on the Blox, G1639)
- No CCU or revenue figures given in the input; 'huge growth' is unquantified (Emancyphur, G187)

## What creators say about it
- Keep one simple core idea (a pig chases you) so the concept is instantly understood (Roblox Creators). ([Roblox Creators 2021-11 21:26](https://youtu.be/rVr0IyfYkPg?t=1286))
- Story chapters need replay hooks: multiple endings, collectible pages and blueprints serve story and gameplay players (Roblox). ([Roblox 2023-09 7:42](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=462))
- Teasers and mysteries (hunting the true ending) and Easter egg hunts keep players curious; scares attract, lore retains (Roblox Creators). ([Roblox Creators 2021-12 10:23](https://youtu.be/wrf6DRlfoO8?t=623), [Roblox Creators 2022-05 0:00](https://youtu.be/xBlHz30PMKo?t=0))
- Make areas visually distinct, use subtle colour-coding for keys and doors, and limit early puzzle choices (Roblox Creators). ([Roblox Creators 2022-03 4:16](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=256))
- Adding wings, a halo or a modified weapon to an existing skin makes a cheap new-looking cosmetic (Roblox Creators). ([Roblox Creators 2021-09 21:45](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=1305), [Roblox Creators 2021-10 21:56](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=1316))
- Clones often miss assets; a cloned version kept the core loop, cutscenes and sounds but had errors (TheDankDeveloper). Licensing of IP matters for official projects. ([TheDankDeveloper 2023-11 0:47](https://youtu.be/fqMj_b2m-Ik?t=47), [Creators on the Blox 2026-05 0:43](https://youtu.be/JsiEVtWLwnA?t=43))

## Transferable patterns
- Lead with one simple, memorable concept.
- Use teaser-driven launches and lore to retain players between chapters.
- Use colour-coding and distinct zones for readability in horror games.
- Make cosmetics cheap to produce by modifying existing skins.
