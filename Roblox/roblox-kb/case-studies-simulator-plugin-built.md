# Case study: Simulator (plugin-built, 12-hour build)

**Genre:** Simulator

A simulator built in about 12 hours with a plugin. It reached roughly 100 players by riding a trend video, then died after backlash over plugin use.

Also referred to as: Simulator (plugin-built, 12-hour build)

## Numbers
- Hit ~100 players (janisjanis01, self-reported, G912)

## What creators say about it
- Riding a trend video brought a short spike (~100 players) that did not last. ([janisjanis01 2024-10 3:09](https://youtu.be/ZyQ73kfh_b4?t=189))
- Heavy plugin use caused community backlash, and the game died out. Disclose and limit generated content. ([janisjanis01 2024-10 3:09](https://youtu.be/ZyQ73kfh_b4?t=189))

## Transferable patterns
- Trend hijacks are short-lived; plan retention beyond the spike.
- Be transparent about tooling used to build the game.
