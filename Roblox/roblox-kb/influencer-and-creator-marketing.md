# Influencer And Creator Marketing

_YouTuber/influencer outreach, creator programs, sponsored creator content, affiliate/Video Stars, collabs._

Evidence base: 72 extracted items from 27 videos by Cumin, Deconstructor of Fun, Duckable, Erthlord, GosuGamers, Last Level Studios, Lnor Roblox, LovJak, MiniBloxia, Roblox Creator Docs, Roblox Creators, SmartyRBX, TheDevBacon, VuukStudios, bubDEV, janisjanis01, rasdev, {EISABOOS} "RBX" (2021-08-27 to 2026-10-30). Roblox changes quickly: check the date on each source and see Date context below.

## Overview
Creators teach that influencer marketing for Roblox games works best as a permanent, measured channel rather than a one-off test. Indie devs advise making outreach easy and mutually beneficial: cold-DM creators whose videos already feature your game, include screenshots, a trailer and a playable link, offer concrete perks such as free admin, and run a server with assets for creators. Paid creators can be hired on SocialSeed, but slow replies and low views are common. Deconstructor of Fun (mobile-focused, 2026) supplies the most rigorous framework: track attributed installs and organic uplift, plan for long-tail value, gate giveaways behind tracked links, group creators by vertical, use local agencies, and don't cut creators early. Roblox's official docs describe the Creator Affiliate pilot (up to 50% revenue share, eligibility thresholds, FTC disclosure), Creator Events, and the Talent Hub profile. Indie figures are self-reported and unverified.

## Rules and findings
- **Reach out to big creators who already feature your game** (one-creator-repeated): Find large creators whose videos already include your game and DM them (e.g. via Twitter). Last Level Studios DMed ethangamertv about his most-viewed video and got a video with millions of views in the first weekend (self-reported). It is a tedious one-by-one grind with no formal channel. Sources: [Last Level Studios 2021-08 13:47](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=827), [Last Level Studios 2021-08 14:29](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=869), [Last Level Studios 2021-08 13:47](https://youtu.be/3djSOjdGaE8?t=827). Newest source 2021-08-27.
- **Give creators a concrete reason to play** (single-mention): Find YouTubers' business emails and offer a benefit, such as free admin, because they play only if it benefits them. Sources: [TheDevBacon 2024-07 0:40](https://youtu.be/_XJVDRr2ZuA?t=40), [TheDevBacon 2024-07 0:40](https://youtu.be/_XJVDRr2ZuA?t=40). Newest source 2024-07-06.
- **Make the first pitch self-contained** (single-mention): Include screenshots, a trailer and a direct playable link in the first message so the creator sees what the game is and can play immediately. Never send a bare 'play my game' DM. Sources: [Cumin 2026-02 0:41](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=41), [Cumin 2026-02 0:41](https://youtu.be/ROo_Xpms4ok?t=41). Newest source 2026-02-07.
- **Hire creators via SocialSeed with due diligence** (single-mention): Verify your Roblox account, filter by price, check average views, reviews and past videos, then send an offer with a due date. Sources: [TheDevBacon 2024-07 1:22](https://youtu.be/_XJVDRr2ZuA?t=82). Newest source 2024-07-06.
- **Expect slow, low-yield micro-payment outreach** (one-creator-repeated): Paying creators a few hundred Robux per video is slow across time zones; expect only a few responses per day. Erthlord's 4 outsourced videos got over 14,000 views total (self-reported). Sources: [Erthlord 2026-01 10:55](https://youtu.be/69IBB6O4o4c?t=655), [Erthlord 2026-01 10:14](https://youtu.be/69IBB6O4o4c?t=614), [Erthlord 2026-01 14:45](https://youtu.be/69IBB6O4o4c?t=885). Newest source 2026-01-23.
- **Budget realistically for sponsorships** (one-creator-repeated): Small YouTubers told Duckable that sponsor offers were 8x (Forge GUI) and 12x (Super Bullet) below their calculated worth. Lowball offers get declined. Sources: [Duckable 2026-09 9:59](https://youtu.be/MBRNAqkDEFA?t=599), [Duckable 2026-09 9:59](https://youtu.be/MBRNAqkDEFA?t=599). Newest source 2026-09-12.
- **Creator videos can beat paid Roblox ads, but ads still help** (multiple-creators): janisjanis01 judged creator videos a better player source than paid Roblox ads. VuukStudios advises running ads on every major update, especially weekends. Games at 5-10K CCU can be revived by ads (one went idle to ~4K CCU, self-reported). These are not mutually exclusive. Sources: [janisjanis01 2024-12 9:29](https://youtu.be/9EXrUN7PUrE?t=569), [VuukStudios 2026-10 2:06](https://youtu.be/0ENzqNB_EUo?t=126), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 2:49](https://youtu.be/cYTtcwp7FS8?t=169), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 2:49](https://youtu.be/cYTtcwp7FS8?t=169). Newest source 2026-10-11.
- **Do pre-launch marketing and hype** (multiple-creators): Lnor's big update launched with only about three players because no marketing was done. bubDEV says pre-release hype on YouTube, TikTok or Instagram brings a loyal base that gives the algorithm early engagement. Sources: [Lnor Roblox 2025-12 6:46](https://youtu.be/Nywo2NwQPNo?t=406), [bubDEV 2025-12 10:17](https://youtu.be/7z8rfg_jBuk?t=617). Newest source 2025-12-11.
- **A big YouTuber can cascade to other platforms** (multiple-creators): A large YouTuber playing your game can spread to TikTok and YouTube and grow the game much faster than dev logs. LovJak credits inviting creators to play and spend Robux as part of his game's revival. Sources: [rasdev 2026-10 3:22](https://youtu.be/DtdGtvALhCA?t=202), [LovJak 2026-07 3:23](https://youtu.be/Y602HH_KdOc?t=203). Newest source 2026-10-17.
- **Fix the game before building socials** (single-mention): Don't build a Discord or TikTok presence before the game is good; they don't save a weak game. Creator promotion likewise needs a game worth playing. Sources: [VuukStudios 2026-07 5:49](https://youtu.be/Bltv__PoNcA?t=349). Newest source 2026-07-29.
- **Support creators with a server and assets** (single-mention): Run a server for content creators and give them assets, models and thumbnail renders. Mutual benefit drives traffic. Sources: [Roblox Creators 2026-10 39:20](https://youtu.be/gy_fAtIkqfo?t=2360). Newest source 2026-10-30.
- **Treat influencer marketing as a permanent pillar** (one-creator-repeated): Winning strategies keep it permanent. In one case a spend cut raised monthly install decline from ~2.56% to ~2.7x faster (as stated in the source). Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 2:09](https://youtu.be/cYTtcwp7FS8?t=129), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 4:47](https://youtu.be/2BsMLCk8FEU?t=287). Newest source 2026-06-10.
- **Measure attributed and organic uplift** (one-creator-repeated): Use a proxy uplift model comparing organic installs before, during and after the campaign in that market. Store rank rises with country installs, compounding the effect. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 15:46](https://youtu.be/2BsMLCk8FEU?t=946), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 9:36](https://youtu.be/cYTtcwp7FS8?t=576). Newest source 2026-06-10.
- **Judge on long-tail value** (single-mention): One sponsor's installs kept growing to ~50% above the day-30 count by day 365. Plan for lifetime value, not 30 days. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 15:46](https://youtu.be/2BsMLCk8FEU?t=946). Newest source 2026-06-01.
- **Don't cut creators on weak early results** (single-mention): A late whale converted months later, about 15 days after publish, via long-tail tracked links. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 14:23](https://youtu.be/cYTtcwp7FS8?t=863). Newest source 2026-06-10.
- **Gate giveaways behind tracked links and let creators pick prizes** (one-creator-repeated): Only creator-referred players enter, which enables attribution and drives clicks. Niche-tied prizes chosen by the creator feel personal and beat generic gift cards. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 18:27](https://youtu.be/2BsMLCk8FEU?t=1107), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 27:25](https://youtu.be/2BsMLCk8FEU?t=1645). Newest source 2026-06-01.
- **Use local agencies for non-English markets** (one-creator-repeated): They know local creators, often get better rates, cut coordination overhead for small teams, and should have regular strategy meetings. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 31:30](https://youtu.be/2BsMLCk8FEU?t=1890), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 6:11](https://youtu.be/cYTtcwp7FS8?t=371). Newest source 2026-06-10.
- **Group creators by vertical with enough sample** (single-mention): Require at least ~10 videos and ~500k combined views per vertical before deciding; smaller samples are skewed by outliers. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 39:03](https://youtu.be/2BsMLCk8FEU?t=2343). Newest source 2026-06-01.
- **Run as a performance channel alongside paid UA** (one-creator-repeated): Track installs in internal analytics and compare retention against other sources. Influencer spend lifts rankings, reduces CPI, stabilises DAU and feeds UA creatives. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 4:50](https://youtu.be/cYTtcwp7FS8?t=290), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 2:49](https://youtu.be/cYTtcwp7FS8?t=169), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 2:49](https://youtu.be/cYTtcwp7FS8?t=169). Newest source 2026-06-10.
- **Always-on in core countries, blitz-test new markets** (single-mention): Keep always-on campaigns in a few core countries and test new markets with different creators and verticals. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 5:31](https://youtu.be/cYTtcwp7FS8?t=331). Newest source 2026-06-10.
- **Follow official affiliate link practices** (single-mention): Use hyperlinked anchor text (e.g. 'Join Roblox for free') rather than raw links; if bare, use a custom URL shortener. Include an FTC disclosure about commissions. Share on YouTube videos/descriptions/about page, social posts, website, newsletters and blogs. Sources: [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-affiliate), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-affiliate), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-affiliate). Newest source 2026-10-28.
- **Know Creator Affiliate pilot terms** (single-mention): Revenue share up to 50% of new users' Robux spend via affiliate links. Minimum age 13 (parental consent under 18). Eligibility: at least 1 game with 4M+ MAU and 65% like score; proven track record, strong social presence, and reach into growing genres (open world action, sports, driving, social cooperation) and markets (Japan, India, Germany, Brazil, South Korea). Sources: [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-affiliate), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-affiliate), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/brand-developer-directory), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/brand-developer-directory), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-affiliate). Newest source 2026-10-28.
- **Use Creator Events and Talent Hub** (single-mention): Creator Events organizers get possible highlights across the site and Roblox social media, plus 1:1 connections and office hours. Keep a complete, detailed, current Talent Hub profile, which is required for the Brand Developer Directory. Sources: [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/creator-events), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/brand-developer-directory). Newest source 2026-10-28.
- **Use YouTube/Twitter presence and outsourcing for commission clients and channel work** (multiple-creators): Posting regularly on YouTube and Twitter/X attracts commission clients (one student's clients mostly came from outreach). Hire editors and thumbnail makers so you only ideate and record. Short-form is low yield: 6,000 Shorts views gave 5 subscribers. Sources: [VuukStudios 2024-01 2:32](https://youtu.be/a3w0s1fBg5M?t=152), [VuukStudios 2024-12 14:11](https://youtu.be/xBPJnMgLoUE?t=851), [janisjanis01 2025-05 5:41](https://youtu.be/sM9btPAvb28?t=341). Newest source 2025-05-11.
- **Use YouTube gameplay for hardware-gated games** (single-mention): For VR games, YouTube gameplay drives interest because viewers can't easily try the headset; a 2020 VR YouTube trend boosted Edgeworks. Sources: [Last Level Studios 2021-12 12:37](https://youtu.be/v_TGu45AD_g?t=757). Newest source 2021-12-09.
- **Building relationships can start through community engagement** (single-mention): MiniBloxia (600k subscribers) donated 10,000 Robux to Hoppy's stream, which led to a friendship. Low-advice anecdote. Sources: [MiniBloxia 2023-06 0:00](https://youtu.be/JGJU9PebnUg?t=0), [MiniBloxia 2023-06 2:53](https://youtu.be/JGJU9PebnUg?t=173). Newest source 2023-06-09.
- **Negotiate reuse rights for creator cutdowns and short-form clips** (single-mention): When booking a creator activation, ask for cutdowns and short-form clips you can reuse in your own lower-funnel UA ads. Per the source, that reuse, not the single creator video, is the main value. Self-reported indie advice; confirm usage terms in writing. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-10 17:39](https://youtu.be/oU3oz6Ddz7g?t=1059). Newest source 2026-10-22.
- **Judge influencer spend on holistic effects, not direct installs** (single-mention): Credit creator spend for feeding UA creatives, lifting store rankings, stabilising DAU and lowering CPI. Direct install attribution alone undercounts its value. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 1:28](https://youtu.be/cYTtcwp7FS8?t=88). Newest source 2026-06-10.
- **Define the target audience before choosing creators** (single-mention): Set audience by gender, age, country and platform first, then by interests. Only then pick verticals and creators whose audiences match those interests. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 40:25](https://youtu.be/2BsMLCk8FEU?t=2425). Newest source 2026-06-01.
- **Track influencer-acquired users in internal analytics** (single-mention): Tag influencer traffic in your own analytics and compare retention by source. One developer reports influencer-acquired users retained better than other media sources. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 4:50](https://youtu.be/cYTtcwp7FS8?t=290). Newest source 2026-06-10.
- **Reuse creator art and branded events for link-based users only** (single-mention): Put creator-themed art, sales, calendars and daily tasks in-game, but show them only to users who arrived through the creator's tracked link. This ties in-game content to each creator. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 22:37](https://youtu.be/2BsMLCk8FEU?t=1357). Newest source 2026-06-01.
- **Keep a dev channel focused on 1-3 Roblox games** (single-mention): A creator channel that covers a small set of games you enjoy and that have a strong audience builds a recognisable style. Consistent niche helps viewers and the algorithm learn the channel. Sources: [{EISABOOS} "RBX" 2025-12 0:44](https://youtu.be/fy3Z0P9mG3Y?t=44). Newest source 2025-12-03.
- **Post on a fixed cadence and judge after 30-60 days** (single-mention): Post 3-4 videos per week, or daily Shorts plus one long video weekly. Stay consistent for 30-60 days before judging results. Sources: [{EISABOOS} "RBX" 2025-12 1:26](https://youtu.be/fy3Z0P9mG3Y?t=86). Newest source 2025-12-03.
- **Do not stop influencer spend abruptly** (one-creator-repeated): Install decline accelerates sharply once spend is cut. Plan a gradual reduction, not a sudden stop, and expect ongoing installs to erode without creator spend. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 0:05](https://youtu.be/cYTtcwp7FS8?t=5), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-06 1:28](https://youtu.be/cYTtcwp7FS8?t=88). Newest source 2026-06-10.

## Checklist
- [ ] Make sure the game is good before promoting
- [ ] Start pre-launch marketing on YouTube/TikTok/Instagram
- [ ] List big creators whose videos already feature your game and DM them
- [ ] Prepare a pitch pack: screenshots, trailer, direct playable link
- [ ] Offer a concrete perk such as free admin
- [ ] Find YouTuber business emails
- [ ] Use SocialSeed: verify account, filter price, check views/reviews/past videos, send offer with due date
- [ ] Budget fair rates; avoid lowball offers
- [ ] Set up a creator server with assets, models and thumbnail renders
- [ ] Use tracked links for every creator
- [ ] Gate giveaway rewards behind the tracked link
- [ ] Let creators choose giveaway prizes
- [ ] Group creators by vertical; need ~10 videos and ~500k views before judging
- [ ] Measure organic uplift before/during/after campaigns
- [ ] Evaluate on day-365 value, not just day 30
- [ ] Keep creators with slow starts
- [ ] Keep always-on campaigns in core countries; test new markets
- [ ] Use local agencies for non-English markets and hold regular meetings
- [ ] Run Roblox ads on major updates, especially weekends
- [ ] Use hyperlinked anchor text and an FTC disclosure for affiliate links
- [ ] Check Creator Affiliate eligibility (4M+ MAU game, 65% likes, age 13+)
- [ ] Keep your Talent Hub profile complete
- [ ] Outsource editing/thumbnails for your own channel
- [ ] Before signing a creator activation, request in writing the rights to reuse cutdowns and short-form clips in your own paid UA.
- [ ] Ask for raw or multiple cut lengths (e.g., short vertical clips) so the footage can be repurposed across ad placements.
- [ ] Define audience by gender, age, country and platform, then interests, before selecting verticals and creators.
- [ ] Require at least 10 videos and about 500k combined views per vertical before judging a vertical's performance.
- [ ] Tag influencer traffic in internal analytics and compare retention and spend against other media sources.
- [ ] Show creator-themed in-game content (art, sales, calendars, daily tasks) only to users who came through that creator's tracked link.
- [ ] Limit your own dev channel to 1-3 games and post 3-4 videos per week (or daily Shorts plus one long video weekly).
- [ ] Evaluate your own channel's results only after 30-60 days of consistent posting.
- [ ] Hold a weekly strategy meeting with local agencies running non-English campaigns.
- [ ] Judge influencer spend across UA creative performance, store ranking, DAU and CPI, not just direct installs.
- [ ] Plan a gradual reduction of creator spend rather than an abrupt cut, since installs decline faster when spend stops.

## Where creators agree
- Creator videos are a strong player-acquisition channel (Last Level Studios, janisjanis01, rasdev, LovJak, Deconstructor of Fun).
- Make creators' work easy and beneficial: Cumin (pitch pack), TheDevBacon (perks), Roblox Creators (assets and server).
- Pre-release marketing matters (Lnor Roblox, bubDEV).
- Influencer marketing complements paid ads (Deconstructor of Fun, VuukStudios on ads for updates).
- Outreach is slow and effortful (Last Level Studios, Erthlord).

## Where creators disagree
- **Creator videos vs paid ads**
  - janisjanis01: creator videos were a better source than paid Roblox ads.
  - Deconstructor of Fun: influencers should complement paid UA, not compete with it.
  - VuukStudios: ads on major updates can revive a game.
- **Building socials early**
  - bubDEV: pre-release hype on social platforms builds a loyal base.
  - VuukStudios: don't build Discord or TikTok before the game is good.

## Date context (what may be outdated)
- Deconstructor of Fun advice (2026-06) is mobile-game oriented, not Roblox-specific, and is the most recent measurement guidance.
- Last Level Studios' DM tactic dates from 2021; formal channels such as SocialSeed (2024) and the Creator Affiliate pilot (2026-10) are newer.
- Roblox Creator Affiliate pilot details (2026-10) are official but pilot terms may change.
- Erthlord's 2026-01 experience updates the cheap-Robux-per-video approach as slow and low-yield.
- The Laughability community figure (more than 202,000 members) is dated 2026-09 and is self-reported. Treat it as a point-in-time audience size, not a current or verified count.

## Instructions for an agent using this file
- If asked how to get YouTubers to play a game, then recommend a first message with screenshots, a trailer, a direct playable link and a concrete perk.
- If a creator already features the game, then DM them first, starting with the most-viewed video.
- If the budget is a few hundred Robux per video, then warn of slow replies and low views, and suggest fairer rates.
- If hiring creators via a marketplace, then recommend SocialSeed checks: verified account, price filter, average views, reviews, past videos, due date.
- If evaluating a campaign, then compare organic installs before, during and after, and look at 365-day value, not only day 30.
- If early results are weak, then don't cut the creator yet; check long-tail tracked links.
- If running a giveaway, then gate rewards behind the tracked link and let the creator choose prizes.
- If targeting non-English markets, then suggest local agencies with regular strategy meetings.
- If asked about affiliate links, then cite the official rules: anchor text, FTC disclosure, 4M+ MAU, 65% like score, age 13+, up to 50% share, and note pilot status.
- If the game is weak or unreleased, then prioritize game quality and pre-launch hype over Discord or TikTok building; treat indie numbers as unverified.
- When planning creator deals, treat reusable cutdowns and short-form clips as a deliverable to negotiate, not a bonus.
- Label reuse-rights advice as single-source and self-reported (T17950) when presenting it to the user.
- If judging a creator or vertical on early results, wait for at least 10 videos and about 500k combined views per vertical, and 3-4 months for a creator with a weak start.
- If planning a creator budget, define audience demographics first, then pick verticals and creators.
- If reporting influencer performance, include CPI, store ranking, DAU and retention alongside direct installs.
- If a developer asks about cutting creator spend, warn that installs may decline faster once spend stops and suggest a gradual reduction.
- If advising a creator's own channel, recommend 1-3 focused games and a fixed weekly cadence, judged over 30-60 days.
- Label all figures from these sources as indie, self-reported.
