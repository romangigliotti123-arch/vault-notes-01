# Case study: Rainbow Friends

**Genre:** Chapter-based Roblox experience

Large chapter-based Roblox experience with an eye-catching icon. Activity dipped after Chapter 1 and recovered with Chapter 2 (released June 2, 2023). No CCU or revenue figures in the notes.

## Numbers
- Chapter 2 released June 2, 2023; recommendation impressions sharply increased (Roblox DevForum, 2024-02; G712).
- No CCU or revenue figures given.

## What creators say about it
- Creator and player activity dropped after Chapter 1 and recovered with Chapter 2, showing that big updates can revive interest. ([Roblox 2023-09 19:00](https://youtu.be/JjkAaXVULEk?t=1140), [Roblox DevForum 2024-02](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111))
- The Chapter 2 release on June 2, 2023 triggered a sharp rise in recommendation impressions, so major releases can drive discovery. ([Roblox DevForum 2024-02](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/discovery-on-roblox-past-present-and-future-vision/2859111))
- An intriguing title and eye-catching characters make icon and thumbnail creation easy and yield strong click-through rate. ([1Coal 2023-08 2:36](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=156), [Roblox DevForum 2024-08](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-actually-make-a-popular-game/3115791))
- A slightly creepy but colourful icon is clickable; pairing tension with bright colour draws clicks. ([rasdev 2026-05 4:30](https://youtu.be/q0lIbkUC2r8?t=270))

## Transferable patterns
- Big-update reactivation after a decline
- Plan major releases around recommendation exposure
- Character-led icons and thumbnails for CTR
- Memorable names as a thumbnail hook
