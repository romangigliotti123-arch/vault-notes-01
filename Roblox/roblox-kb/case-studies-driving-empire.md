# Case study: Driving Empire

**Genre:** Driving / open-world

Open-world driving game with licensed cars, handling updates, limited-time vehicles and store economy. Sustains a loyal niche audience and is a reference for Roblox rendering and pricing tests.

Also referred to as: Driving Empire (Vexx)

## Numbers
- Sustains 20-50K CCU in a loyal niche (VuukStudios, G1966)
- Rewarded video ads ~7% of revenue (Roblox, G839)
- 29% impressions rise after sustained metrics (Roblox, G2349); standout featuring caused immediate metric declines

## What creators say about it
- Release major changes to the most engaged players first and iterate on thousands of feedback notes (Roblox, car handling). ([Roblox 2023-09 2:05](https://youtu.be/Z-ZKqBPdl44?t=125))
- Store revamp produced strong ARPU uplift; Price Optimization gave no uplift because manual pricing was already at equilibrium (Roblox, 2024-09). ([Roblox 2024-09](https://youtu.be/QC6XWgJRJEM), [Roblox 2024-09](https://youtu.be/ETDit6eO4sQ))
- Limited vehicles that can never be bought again create FOMO (rasdev). ([rasdev 2026-08 3:07](https://youtu.be/Ox5pwUuzF-M?t=187))
- Rendering: mesh streaming, auto LODs and Slim let licensed 200k-triangle cars ship; texture streaming let low-end Android run smoother (Roblox, 2026-09 and 2026-10). ([Roblox 2026-09](https://youtu.be/53dj7AG3foA), [Roblox 2026-10 4:29](https://youtu.be/OToDz3Ghpjo?t=269))
- Standout featuring can cause immediate metric declines; sustained metrics matter more than the spike (Roblox, 2026-10). ([Roblox 2026-10 21:28](https://youtu.be/bkRZeTZAvLw?t=1288))
- Exact name match appears as the enlarged primary tile in search, with search ads shown right after (Roblox Creator Docs). ([Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/search-ads))

## Transferable patterns
- Test changes on engaged players before broad rollout.
- Prefer limited-time or retired items to permanent ones for FOMO.
- Use streaming and LOD tools to ship detail on low-end devices.
- Judge featuring by sustained metrics, not the spike.
