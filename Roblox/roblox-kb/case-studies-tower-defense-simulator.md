# Case study: Tower Defense Simulator

**Genre:** Tower defense

Long-running tower defense game, nearly 5 years old by 2024. Cited for KPI-driven live ops, story events, and retention fixes, and for criticism over consumables and skill-tree balance.

Also referred to as: Tower Defense Simulator (TDS), Tower Defense Simulator (Lunar Overture event)

## Numbers
- Roblox (2025-09): simplified matchmaking raised play time 14% but disconnects rose 40%, so the team kept the control. A hand-drawn thumbnail doubled QPTR.
- Roblox (2024-09): record CCU during the Lunar Overture event (no figure given).

## What creators say about it
- Feedback-driven live ops using KPI funnels, heat maps, and timed events sustained 5+ years of growth (Roblox, 2024-09). ([Roblox 2024-09](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I))
- A tutorial added after about 4 years fixed new-user retention (Roblox, 2024-09). ([Roblox 2024-09](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I))
- Story-driven limited-time events with timed missions and limited UGC rewards drove record CCU and returning users (Roblox, 2024-09). ([Roblox 2024-09](https://youtu.be/DxrLfwsfV1I))
- Simplified matchmaking raised play time 14% but disconnects rose 40%, so the team kept the control. A hand-drawn thumbnail doubled QPTR (Roblox, 2025-09). ([Roblox 2025-09](https://youtu.be/j6OPA95-1O0))
- Criticism to heed: neglected consumables and experimental cosmetics drew backlash over communication (CORSO, 2024-09). A coin-funded skill tree with exponentially rising costs hurts balance (CORSO, 2025-05). ([CORSO 2024-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/lFD1wKdwSUs?t=0), [CORSO 2025-05 0:00](https://youtu.be/O2LZcQG5PPQ?t=0))
- Group shop items by size and proximity, and distinguish skins from crates. Newer 2026 note confirms the 2023 approach. ([Roblox Creators 2023-08 5:21](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=321), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/ui-ux-design))

## Transferable patterns
- KPI funnels and heat maps for live ops
- Tutorials to fix new-user retention
- Thumbnail tests tracked by QPTR
- Shop grouping by size and proximity
