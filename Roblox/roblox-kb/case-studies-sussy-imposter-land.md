# Case study: Sussy Imposter Land

**Genre:** Meme / social-deduction themed

Among Us/Sus meme game by SmartyRBX. Started as a trashy MVP and improved monetisation, gameplay and maps over time to reach about 7.8M visits. Early paid push with a 1,000 Robux ad plus gamepasses and guns seeded players who became organic.

## Numbers
- ~7.8M visits (SmartyRBX, G657)
- 1,000 Robux advertising; grew to millions of Robux monthly (SmartyRBX, G1149, figure not precise)

## What creators say about it
- Ship a trashy MVP and iterate monetisation, gameplay and maps; this reached about 7.8M visits. ([SmartyRBX 2023-10 17:39](https://youtu.be/vemqKJ-5yRE?t=1059))
- Riding the Among Us/Sus meme trend brought early players. ([SmartyRBX 2025-06 8:50](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=530), [SmartyRBX 2025-06 5:11](https://youtu.be/Lplz7ZFcKlQ?t=311))
- A 1,000 Robux ad, plus gamepasses and guns, drove early players who became organic. ([SmartyRBX 2025-06 8:50](https://youtu.be/BMS5iYw_8Vk?t=530), [SmartyRBX 2025-06 5:11](https://youtu.be/Lplz7ZFcKlQ?t=311))

## Transferable patterns
- Ship an MVP early and iterate on monetisation and maps.
- Ride trending meme cycles for initial reach.
- Use a small paid push to seed organic growth.
