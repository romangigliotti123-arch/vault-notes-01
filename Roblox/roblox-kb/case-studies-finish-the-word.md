# Case study: Finish The Word

**Genre:** Word-chain puzzle

Roblox word-chain puzzle: one room, one timer, one word, with no chapters or badges. Won Best Puzzle Game in 2026 coverage (Table Game X) and reached millions of players in four months per Roblox.

Also referred to as: Finish the Word

## Numbers
- Millions of players within 4 months (Roblox, 2026-09; G2198).

## What creators say about it
- Simple word-chain idea with timer pressure; the simplicity itself is the hook. ([Roblox Newsroom 2026-09](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/2026-roblox-innovation-awards))
- One room, one timer, one word, and no chapters or badges reached millions of players in 4 months. ([Roblox 2026-09 26:36](https://youtu.be/HXZRH-2zurk?t=1596))
- Won Best Puzzle Game in 2026 coverage (Table Game X's title). ([Roblox Newsroom 2026-09](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/2026-roblox-innovation-awards), [PocketGamer.biz 2026-09](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-reveals-2026-innovation-awards-winners/), [NetInfluencer 2026-09](https://www.netinfluencer.com/roblox-crowns-animal-hospital-sweep-at-2026-innovation-awards/), [GosuGamers 2026-09](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/79176-full-list-of-winners-at-the-roblox-innovation-awards-2026))

## Transferable patterns
- Minimal scope for fast validation
- Time pressure as the core tension
- Skip progression scaffolding when the core loop is strong
