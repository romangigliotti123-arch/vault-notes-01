# Case study: Catalog Avatar Creator

**Genre:** Avatar try-on utility

Puts the Roblox catalog inside a game so players can try items before buying, and combines multiple items into a full outfit preview. Its simple name and concept are cited as a reason it became a top game.

Also referred to as: Catalog Avatar Creator (AlvinBlox)

## Numbers
- Over 1.8 billion visits in about 2 years (janisjanis01, self-reported, G773)

## What creators say about it
- Adding the Roblox catalog into a game lets players try items before buying. ([janisjanis01 2024-06 4:14](https://youtu.be/NWyK_PbFdxE?t=254))
- A clear name and concept convey the game instantly; the game is cited among the biggest on Roblox. ([AlvinBlox 2025-06 1:15](https://youtu.be/Y93DRfB-Deo?t=75), [AlvinBlox 2025-06 1:15](https://youtu.be/Y93DRfB-Deo?t=75))
- Single-item try-on did not show how items look together; combining multiple items into a full outfit preview solved this. ([rasdev 2026-09 0:00](https://youtu.be/ILhTEWqz2kU?t=0))

## Transferable patterns
- Try-before-buy previews to drive purchases.
- State the concept in one line with the name.
- Preview complete outfits or bundles, not single items.
