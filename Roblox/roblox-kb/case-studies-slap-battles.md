# Case study: Slap Battles

**Genre:** Physics combat / slap arena

Roblox slap game: spawn, slap, repeat, with no tutorial needed. Its slap-to-knock-off-map mechanic was copied for a giant slap concept. No CCU or revenue figures in the notes.

## Numbers
- No figures in the notes.

## What creators say about it
- Spawn, slap, repeat: a simple core loop gives immediate, satisfying feedback and hooks players fast. ([AlvinBlox 2025-06 3:21](https://youtu.be/Y93DRfB-Deo?t=201), [AlvinBlox 2025-06 3:21](https://youtu.be/Y93DRfB-Deo?t=201))
- Players spawn in and slap immediately with no tutorial; instant playability was cited as the model for hooking players. ([rasdev 2026-05 7:05](https://youtu.be/q0lIbkUC2r8?t=425), [rasdev 2026-08 0:40](https://youtu.be/Ox5pwUuzF-M?t=40))
- Reuse a proven mechanic in a new theme: the giant slap concept copies the slap-to-knock-off-map mechanic. ([ItsZyonic 2023-12 8:46](https://youtu.be/C1wSHlu8vVM?t=526))

## Transferable patterns
- Zero-tutorial, instant-play core loop
- Remix a proven mechanic with a new theme
- Immediate feedback on the first action
