# Case study: Ninja Challenge

**Genre:** Obstacle / challenge game

A challenge game that grew without influencer marketing. Notable for monthly updates with new thumbnails, ads to former players, and a second-chance mechanic. Cited in Roblox's 'Science of Game Discovery' RDC session.

## What creators say about it
- Monthly challenge updates with new thumbnails; ads to former players revived home recommendations, and the game grew without influencer marketing. ([Roblox 2025-09 18:15](https://youtu.be/cUxgt4asjFU?t=1095))
- Cited as a case study in Roblox's 'Science of Game Discovery' RDC session. ([Roblox DevForum 2025-10](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-retention-by-acquisition-source-and-select-your-benchmark-set/4010157))
- A post-failure second chance that preserves current progress is an example of good retention design. ([Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/obby))

## Transferable patterns
- Refresh thumbnails and content on a regular cadence
- Re-engage former players with ads
- Offer a second chance after failure that keeps progress
