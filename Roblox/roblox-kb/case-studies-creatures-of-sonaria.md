# Case study: Creatures of Sonaria

**Genre:** Creature survival and battling

Creature battling, survival, and evolution game with hundreds of creatures, developer-creature shop items, and skin sales. Community-designed creatures expanded it after release. A Twin Atlas breakout.

## Numbers
- Tubefilter (2026, month unspecified, third-party press): ~26,000 concurrent players at press time.

## What creators say about it
- Hundreds of creatures work only if each stays fresh and distinct. Reevaluate additions if they become repetitive (Roblox Creators, 2023-01). ([Roblox Creators 2023-01 7:11](https://youtu.be/vQNJztcS2AI?t=431))
- Free in-game currency lets players trial Robux creatures. A rotating shop sinks the economy (Roblox Creators, 2023-01). ([Roblox Creators 2023-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/YdplC2WVkWc?t=0))
- Watch community posts to learn what players want; targeted skin overlay sales performed well (Roblox, 2023-09). ([Roblox 2023-09 25:38](https://youtu.be/9eaev-ypgWo?t=1538))
- Community-designed creatures expanded the game after release (Roblox, 2026-03). ([Roblox 2026-03 0:02](https://youtu.be/eph8HDbEs6U?t=2))
- Strong community drove plushie and sticker demand in the physical merch integration beta (Roblox DevForum, 2025-05). ([Roblox DevForum 2025-05](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/expanding-how-commerce-can-help-you-earn-and-grow-your-communities/3651181))
- Directional arrows and icons guide players in-world (Roblox Creator Docs, 2026-10). ([Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding-techniques))

## Transferable patterns
- Free trial currency for premium items
- Rotating shop as an economy sink
- Community-designed content pipeline
- In-world wayfinding arrows
