## Overview
Business and payouts are shifting from DevEx to Roblox Wallet. Per official September 2026 announcements, Wallet rollout starts December 2026, beginning with invited US 18+ sole proprietors. It pays earnings in USD every business day as holds clear (up to 30 days), with no payout request and no monthly cooldown. DevEx ends in the US in mid-2027 and continues longer in other regions. Groups keep earning Robux until group Wallets launch in 2027. Official totals: DevEx has paid more than $5B since 2013 and about $1.7B in the 12 months to June 2026. About 13,000 creators qualified for DevEx for the first time in that period (+38% YoY). Roblox Game Fund is a past program. Indie macro figures (GDP, jobs) are self-reported and low-advice.

## Rules and findings
- **Meet DevEx eligibility before requesting cash-out** (multiple-creators): You need at least 30,000 Earned Robux, a verified email, a W-9 (US) or W-8 (non-US) on file, and age 13+. The old 100,000 Robux minimum and Premium requirement (2021 guide) are outdated. 30,000 Robux is about $105 at the legacy rate. - **Cash out at most once per month and plan around review times** (multiple-creators): Only one completed DevEx request per calendar month. Requests can't be cancelled; Robux are held during review and refunded only if rejected. Review takes about 10 business days for first-timers and about 5 for returning participants. A first approval may take over a week (CallMeDogey). - **Finish DevEx Portal / Tipalti registration within one week** (multiple-creators): Choose a payment method and tax form within 1 week of the invite or registration link. Otherwise the request is auto-declined and Robux are refunded. Tipalti verification takes 5-8 business days after you submit payment info, so start early. Keep the info accurate, because errors delay payments. After a name-discrepancy rejection, do not resubmit. Contact Roblox Support first, because ID verification may be required. - **Know the current DevEx rates and cash old-rate balances first** (multiple-creators): Legacy rate is 0.0035 USD per Earned Robux, for balances before Sept 5 2025 10am PT. The rate rose about 8.5% in Sept 2025. Eligible US 18+ verified players' purchases earn 0.0054 (about 42% more), launched in June 2026. Spending Robux does not reduce old-rate balances, and those are cashed out first. Group-owned games show the 18+ vs standard split at cash-out. The 100k=$350 figure is the old rate. - **Update tax info before Oct 31, 2026** (multiple-creators): DevEx payments are classified as royalties from Nov 1, 2026. Submit or validate tax info on the Creator Hub Taxes page by Oct 31, 2026 to set the withholding rate. US creators with a valid W-9 face no US withholding; a missing or invalid TIN triggers 24% backup withholding. International creators without valid info may face 24% on the full payout. With valid info they pay 0-30% on the US-sourced portion. A treaty claim needs a valid W-8 with a TIN and a country matching your permanent residence. Roblox recommended requesting payouts by Oct 15, 2026 to avoid the transition. - **Expect US tax forms, keep records, and watch for phishing** (multiple-creators): 1099-NEC is issued by Jan 31 for prior-year cash earnings above $2,000. Store your own per-user pass purchase history, since Roblox doesn't record it. - **Plan cash flow around holds, escrow and subscription payouts** (multiple-creators): Earned Robux have a 60-day holding period and payouts lag engagement. An older 2020 Premium Payouts note says a 28-day lag plus 3 days pending, which is superseded by the 60-day figure. Local-currency earnings escrow at least 60 days and pay monthly. Creator Store sales escrow 30 days and pay on the 1st. Paid access: up to 7 days. Ads pay on the 25th of the following month. Local-currency subscriptions pay 70% in month one and 100% after (30-day hold); Robux subscriptions pay 70% every month (about 5-day hold). Deleting an active local-currency subscription requires full refunds, so take it off sale and cancel renewals instead. Pending Robux clear in 3-5 days (kra_DeZ, unverified). ## Where creators agree
- Reinvesting Robux in ads, teams and new projects beats cashing out early (SmartyRBX, CallMeDogey, DevForum authors, Roblox 2018 talk).
- Treat DevEx as a conversion at a cost; take what you need (CallMeDogey, DevForum authors).
- Diversified income reduces risk (SmartyRBX, Creators on the Blox).
- Get tax and legal help early (Roblox Creators speaker, Roblox docs).
- Keep payment and tax details accurate; official docs repeat this across several pages.
- Most developers earn little (Roblox, Deconstructor of Fun, MoseyTube agree on the median of $1,440).
- Brand outreach: portfolio plus relationship (Roblox Creators and Roblox panels).
- Roblox keeps about 30% of Robux spent, so devs keep ~70% before ad and publishing costs (T5858, T6873, N2462).
- Estimate a game's future earnings or payback before buying or selling it, rather than negotiating blind (T6832, T6622).
- Track net Robux and subtract real costs before calling a game profitable (T6507, T6747, T5858).
- Earned Robux only counts toward DevEx; purchased Robux never does.
- The DevEx rate is $0.0038 per earned Robux for balances earned from Sept 5, 2025, with $0.0035 for older balances.
- The 30% marketplace fee applies to gamepasses and developer products regardless of Premium status.
- DevEx allows one cash-out request per calendar month, with a 30,000 earned Robux minimum in the 2024-2026 sources.
- Sales are subject to holds (up to 30 days) and Creator Rewards carry a 60-day holding period.
- Group payouts and group transfers are irreversible, so verify the target and amounts first.
- Roblox keeps about 30% of Robux spent; creators keep about 70% of gross on standard sales and game passes.
- Only earned Robux count toward DevEx cash-out.
- The DevEx rate is not fixed and can change; plan with a range.
- UGC items commonly cost more than they earn in the first months.
- RoWatcher's two posts both put 2025 creator earnings at about $1.5B (N7120, N7137).
- Official IR and the indie tracker both show creator payouts growing into 2026 (T17554, N7120).

## Where creators disagree
- **Cash out vs reinvest/sell**
  - SmartyRBX says reinvest most profit, but also tells people to convert Robux to cash through DevEx.
  - CallMeDogey cashes out what he needs and keeps the rest for ads.
  - DevForum 2022 author says avoid DevEx altogether.
  - Official docs just describe DevEx; the 18+ rate change makes it somewhat better than the 2022 advice assumed.
- **Sell vs keep games**
  - AlvinBlox: selling gives upfront cash.
  - CallMeDogey: keep games for passive income; studio buyers take 20-50%.
  - 1_Bruce: sold 50% for 3.5M Robux upfront (self-reported).
- **Games vs dev content as income**
  - SmartyRBX says dev content pays thousands a month, while games don't, but also claims a simple game earns 10-20x commission income.
  - Tizzy and MoseyTube show game revenue claims that are unverified and vary widely.
- **Rev share in brand deals**
  - Roblox Creators 2022 says large global brands may not allow rev share and smaller brands may.

## Instructions for an agent using this file
- If asked about DevEx eligibility, cite 30,000 Earned Robux, not 100,000, and mention the W-9/W-8, verified email and age 13+.
- If a user asks about DevEx payout in USD, apply 0.0035 to legacy balances, which are cashed out first, and 0.0054 to eligible US 18+ purchases; otherwise say the current standard rate may have changed and check the docs.
- If the date is near Oct 31 2026, tell the user to submit or validate tax info before that to avoid 24% backup withholding.
- If a user has a name mismatch rejection, tell them not to resubmit and to contact Support.
- If a user asks when money arrives, give the relevant hold: 60 days for earned Robux, 30 days Creator Store, up to 7 days paid access, ads on the 25th of next month.
- If a user plans to sell priced assets, check ID, age, Stripe region, individual ownership, and warn against editing bank info after approval.
- If a user is offered a percentage deal for advertising, advise against it; if a studio buyout is offered, compare a 20-50% cut with keeping the game.
- If a user wants to sell a game, recommend bank escrow, proof of funds, reference checks, shareholder sign-off and disclosure of third-party assets.

Newest source 2025-07-24.
