# Case study: Winds of Fortune

**Genre:** Combat RPG (inferred from mentions)

A Roblox combat and quest game with a stylised art direction, consistent stat icons and combat feedback that teaches play without a tutorial.

## What creators say about it
- Surface appearance and new materials moved the art direction away from UV projection toward a more artistic style. ([Roblox Creators 2022-03 1:13:58](https://youtu.be/czVxazdl0kg?t=4438))
- Stat icons share colour and shape language, so players learn what each stat means quickly. Icons for related stats reuse the palette and shapes; a close-button convention is used across games. ([Roblox Creators 2023-08 7:00](https://youtu.be/lOOvOIJoMi4?t=420), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/ui-ux-design))
- Combat feedback (VFX, damage numbers, health bars, reward coins, quest counters) teaches combat without a tutorial. ([Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding-techniques))

## Transferable patterns
- Use a shared icon language so meanings transfer across UI elements
- Let combat feedback act as the tutorial
- Use standard UI conventions such as a close button
- Move from UV-projected texturing to surface appearance and materials for stylised art
