# Case study: Dead Rails

**Genre:** Train survival

Cart and train survival with lobby matchmaking, harvesting, crafting, enemies and item carrying to fuel the train. Its mechanics (drag/carry, buy/sell, sack) became inspiration for several later survival games.

Also referred to as: Dead Rails (train fuel)

## Numbers
- Over 5B visits (Roblox DevForum, G892)
- Over a million players (Roblox, G1398)

## What creators say about it
- Carry-to-train loop: pick up and throw items, weld and valuable-tag them, and deliver fuel to a train with a Touched-based hitbox (PineappleDev tutorial). ([PineappleDev 2025-03 0:00](https://youtu.be/E6Bev4VCWeA?t=0), [PineappleDev 2025-03 0:00](https://youtu.be/ntHgQBEVERE?t=0), [PineappleDev 2025-03 10:11](https://youtu.be/ntHgQBEVERE?t=611))
- Buy and sell at a station, then buy from shop tables; party-size queue then countdown and teleport into the game (PineappleDev). ([PineappleDev 2025-04 0:00](https://youtu.be/kVtEBqvw5F4?t=0), [PineappleDev 2025-04 0:00](https://youtu.be/KTS-9A9DLvY?t=0))
- Performance: anchor and disable animators for out-of-range NPCs and fix stray awake physics parts (Roblox). ([Roblox 2025-09](https://youtu.be/rwLEnBtXr30))
- Scale data safely: ProfileStore was used by a game with over 5B visits (DevForum). ([Roblox DevForum 2024-10](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/profilestore-save-your-player-data-easy-datastore-module/3190543))
- Leave lore open and hide Easter eggs so players fill the story in (Roblox). Blend personal interests with random, shareable surprises; the game broke out overnight after a quick build. ([Roblox 2025-12 4:48](https://youtu.be/oDIpSDOYfY0?t=288), [Roblox 2026-05 0:04](https://youtu.be/-14vhiLL4xE?t=4))
- Sack mechanic and collaborative survival loop let players understand the game instantly (Roblox Creators; Daiorg). ([Roblox Creators 2026-09 29:11](https://youtu.be/hohwBQGQO7c?t=1751), [Daiorg 2025-12 31:47](https://youtu.be/JCMIb008Y6E?t=1907))

## Transferable patterns
- Build a carry-and-deliver loop that ties item handling to a shared goal.
- Use party/queue flows for group play.
- Disable off-screen NPC animators and clean up physics parts early.
- Leave lore open for player imagination.
