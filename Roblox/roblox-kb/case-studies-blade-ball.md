# Case study: Blade Ball

**Genre:** Reaction-based sword combat

Reaction-based sword combat game. Notes cover between-match activities, mobile-only lobbies, and a blacksmith NPC that generates custom swords from prompts. Newest advice is to pivot to a Hot Potato-style game rather than clone Blade Ball.

## Numbers
- Speaker says a sword that took hours now takes one minute with the blacksmith NPC (Roblox, 2025-09)

## What creators say about it
- Always give players something to do between matches: training, chests, spinning, emotes, server hopping. ([ByteBlox 2024-06 12:38](https://youtu.be/_rksdF5JoHo?t=758))
- Mobile-only lobbies keep mobile players from being stomped by mouse-and-keyboard players in a combat game. ([ByteBlox 2024-11 25:31](https://youtu.be/1KKQ6YrktTk?t=1531))
- Newest advice (2025-03) supersedes earlier remake-focused notes: don't aim for the next Blade Ball, make something like Hot Potato. ([SmartyRBX 2025-03 24:53](https://youtu.be/fz-0n9ed9xU?t=1493), [TheDankDeveloper 2023-10 0:00](https://youtu.be/mwu3k4nXYZQ?t=0), [TheDankDeveloper 2023-10 2:44](https://youtu.be/DJsRZ2FfoMk?t=164))
- Prompt-generated custom weapons let players design their own swords. ([Roblox 2025-09 25:54](https://youtu.be/3h25HUtBHFk?t=1554))

## Transferable patterns
- Between-match activities to hold players
- Platform-split lobbies for fairness
- Avoid chasing clones; pivot to adjacent concepts
- Player-designed content via AI-assisted NPCs
