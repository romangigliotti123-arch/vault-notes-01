# Studio Business Funding And Publishing

_studio structure, hiring, contracts, revenue splits, group funds, funding and investors, publishers, growing a company._

Evidence base: 51 extracted items from 20 videos by Deconstructor of Fun, GameMakers, GamesBeat, Naavik Gaming Podcast, Roblox Creator Docs, Roblox DevForum, The Letus Garden, TheDevKing, Ventures, VuukStudios, janisjanis01, rasdev (2017-11-29 to 2026-10-28). Roblox changes quickly: check the date on each source and see Date context below.

## Overview
Creators cover how Roblox studios get funded and grow. Official Roblox programs (Game Fund with $500,000 minimum and milestone-based payouts, 3-month Accelerator, Community Fund for education, Jumpstart in-kind support) are the ground truth. Indie creators (TheDevKing, The Letus Garden) teach pitching private investors: show proof of work, a roadmap, monetization, a deal structure and a valuation formula (assets plus a weekly projection, with percentage = investment / worth). Newer sources (Ventures 2025, Naavik 2026) show funds taking 10-15% revenue shares, making many small bets and following up with more money on winners. Investors now want an audience and community, not just a demo. Other advice: avoid paid hires early, crowdfund from friends, team up as the game grows, sell only part of a game, and protect group ownership. All indie figures are self-reported and unverified.

## Rules and findings
- **Show proof of work and a return story when pitching investors** (multiple-creators): Pitch on expected return: show existing progress and explain how the investor earns their money back. The goal is to make them believe the game will do well. Investors want games with a future and true potential. Sources: [TheDevKing 2020-04 6:07](https://youtu.be/VOKFE8Qi16c?t=367), [TheDevKing 2020-04 6:07](https://youtu.be/VOKFE8Qi16c?t=367), [The Letus Garden 2020-11 2:07](https://youtu.be/pAfbzNESkYk?t=127). Newest source 2020-11-13.
- **Have four things ready before pitching** (single-mention): Before approaching investors, have a made or nearly finished game, a future plan, a monetization system, and a deal structure. Sources: [The Letus Garden 2020-11 1:06](https://youtu.be/pAfbzNESkYk?t=66). Newest source 2020-11-13.
- **Present a 1-2-3 month roadmap** (single-mention): Show investors a 1, 2 and 3 month roadmap covering how you will improve the game and how you will market it. Sources: [The Letus Garden 2020-11 2:07](https://youtu.be/pAfbzNESkYk?t=127). Newest source 2020-11-13.
- **Write a complete investor application** (single-mention): Include your role, game details, what funds are for (art, hiring, ads), the amount needed, and a timeline to completion. Sources: [TheDevKing 2020-04 8:09](https://youtu.be/VOKFE8Qi16c?t=489). Newest source 2020-04-01.
- **Value your game with a formula before naming a percentage** (single-mention): Total worth = assets (e.g. USD earned converted to Robux) + one-week revenue projection. Investment percentage = investment / total worth. Example (self-reported, hypothetical): 100,000 / 460,714 gives about 22%. Sources: [The Letus Garden 2020-11 3:52](https://youtu.be/pAfbzNESkYk?t=232), [The Letus Garden 2020-11 4:34](https://youtu.be/pAfbzNESkYk?t=274), [The Letus Garden 2020-11 5:57](https://youtu.be/pAfbzNESkYk?t=357), [The Letus Garden 2020-11 5:57](https://youtu.be/pAfbzNESkYk?t=357). Newest source 2020-11-13.
- **Project weekly revenue as day-one revenue x 7, and verify with a test ad** (single-mention): Multiply day-one revenue by 7 for the weekly projection. A small test ad (about 1,000 Robux) helps check the daily revenue figure. Example: 25,000 x 7 = 175,000 Robux. This is a rough, optimistic method. Sources: [The Letus Garden 2020-11 5:16](https://youtu.be/pAfbzNESkYk?t=316), [The Letus Garden 2020-11 5:16](https://youtu.be/pAfbzNESkYk?t=316), [The Letus Garden 2020-11 5:57](https://youtu.be/pAfbzNESkYk?t=357). Newest source 2020-11-13.
- **Do not lowball or accept a bad deal** (single-mention): Prepare accurate numbers before pitching so you do not undersell your game. Sources: [The Letus Garden 2020-11 6:40](https://youtu.be/pAfbzNESkYk?t=400). Newest source 2020-11-13.
- **Crowdfund from several small backers** (single-mention): Ask several friends or devs for small amounts rather than relying on one large investor. Sources: [TheDevKing 2020-04 6:48](https://youtu.be/VOKFE8Qi16c?t=408). Newest source 2020-04-01.
- **Build audience and community before fundraising** (single-mention): Newer advice is that investors expect users and community before funding, not just a playable demo. This updates the older demo-and-proof-of-work pitching advice. Sources: [Naavik Gaming Podcast 2026-03 35:01](https://youtu.be/mykMEsgoi5E?t=2101), [Naavik Gaming Podcast 2026-03 35:01](https://youtu.be/mykMEsgoi5E?t=2101). Newest source 2026-03-17.
- **Apply to Roblox Game Fund with the required package** (single-mention): The application needs team details, a short project description, a pitch deck (high-level strategy, design specs), and a milestone schedule and budget. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Sources: [Roblox DevForum 2021-07](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477), [Roblox DevForum 2021-07](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477). Newest source 2021-07-19.
- **Expect milestone-based funding from Roblox** (single-mention): Roblox agrees deliverables with accepted teams and releases funds as milestones are met. Plan budgets and schedules around milestones. Sources: [Roblox DevForum 2021-07](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477). Newest source 2021-07-19.
- **Prior experience is not required for Roblox funding** (single-mention): New and indie developers can apply. Rejected applicants need not reapply. Sources: [Roblox DevForum 2021-07](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477). Newest source 2021-07-19.
- **Pick Game Fund vs Accelerator by scale** (single-mention): Game Fund suits ambitious, next-gen, large-budget projects, with a $500,000 minimum for approved participants (2021 announcement). Accelerator is a 3-month hands-on program with smaller fixed per-person funding. Sources: [Roblox DevForum 2021-07](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477), [Roblox DevForum 2021-07](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477), [Roblox DevForum 2021-07](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-game-fund/1361477). Newest source 2021-07-19.
- **Use the Community Fund and Education Office Hours for educational games** (single-mention): Educational or curriculum games can apply to the Roblox Community Fund for investments, grants, or advances. Free one-on-one Education Open Office Hours help assess educational value and development issues. Sources: [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/getting-started-for-developers-education), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/resources/getting-started-for-developers-education). Newest source 2026-10-28.
- **Set expectations for Jumpstart support** (single-mention): Jumpstart support is mostly in-kind: guidance, Robux-based operational support and visibility tools. It is not a salary or a traditional publishing deal. Sources: [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-programs/jumpstart). Newest source 2026-10-28.
- **Expect fund terms of a revenue share, with follow-on funding for winners** (single-mention): Ventures describes a fund taking a 10-15% revenue share (LFG). It follows up with more funding for a further percentage of a creation when a game takes off, instead of a one-off investment. Sources: [Ventures 2025-12 17:24](https://youtu.be/Nb50UhXcpRM?t=1044), [Ventures 2025-12 16:44](https://youtu.be/Nb50UhXcpRM?t=1004). Newest source 2025-12-18.
- **Funds make many small bets with power-law returns** (single-mention): Ventures' fund targets about 500 creators and about 200 games in 18-24 months, with an initial $5M. A few games carry the returns and most earn steadily. Top games are cited at $100M+/year (unverified). Sources: [Ventures 2025-12 13:11](https://youtu.be/Nb50UhXcpRM?t=791), [Ventures 2025-12 13:11](https://youtu.be/Nb50UhXcpRM?t=791), [Ventures 2025-12 25:10](https://youtu.be/Nb50UhXcpRM?t=1510), [Ventures 2025-12 16:44](https://youtu.be/Nb50UhXcpRM?t=1004). Newest source 2025-12-18.
- **Percentage deals for proven teams are typically small** (single-mention): VuukStudios cites 5-8% equity for teams proven on the front page, paid as a share of ongoing revenue from weekly updates (self-reported). Sources: [VuukStudios 2023-12 12:52](https://youtu.be/fEcFzlBQew8?t=772). Newest source 2023-12-25.
- **Sell only part of a game when possible** (single-mention): Example: Grow Garden's creator sold to Splitting Point but kept about half ownership. Sources: [rasdev 2026-10 4:08](https://youtu.be/4GEHEzhvhwI?t=248). Newest source 2026-10-07.
- **Avoid paying for builders or scripters early** (single-mention): Do not hire paid builders or scripters unless the concept truly needs professionals. Free resources and friends can cover the basics (2017 advice). Sources: [Roblox DevForum 2017-11](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/game-design-theory-planning-a-game-and-implementing-features/62838). Newest source 2017-11-29.
- **Team up as the game grows** (single-mention): Look for developers to partner with once the game gets bigger rather than building everything alone from the start. Sources: [janisjanis01 2024-06 5:47](https://youtu.be/NWyK_PbFdxE?t=347). Newest source 2024-06-16.
- **Use groups to structure studios, and protect ownership** (multiple-creators): Premium users can be in or create up to 100 groups, so separate studios are possible. One permanent group removal blocks new group ownership for 90 days, and multiple removals permanently remove ownership privileges. Sources: [Roblox DevForum 2019-09](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-premium-is-here/355721), [Roblox DevForum 2019-09](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-premium-is-here/355721), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/group-guidelines). Newest source 2026-10-28.
- **Test clothing in Studio before upload** (single-mention): Uploading to the Avatar Shop now costs Robux per item (2020), so test designs first. This is a minor cost-control tip. Sources: [Roblox DevForum 2020-12](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/upcoming-changes-to-avatar-catalog-upload-requirements/915265). Newest source 2020-12-10.
- **Keep Studio workspace tidy** (single-mention): On first launch, close the left-side windows and keep Explorer and Properties on the right. This is a minor tip with little relevance to business. Sources: [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/education/build-it-play-it-island-of-move/opening-the-template). Newest source 2026-10-28.
- **Do not expect equity or grants from Roblox programs** (single-mention): As of March 2026, Roblox program support is mentorship, promotion, and planned future incentives, not equity or grant money. Plan the budget and runway around that, and treat any Roblox-side cash as a future possibility, not a given. Sources: [GamesBeat 2026-03](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-unveils-jumpstart-and-incubator-programs-to-help-creators-expand-possibilities-exclusive/). Newest source 2026-03-09.
- **Weigh a publisher against self-publishing** (single-mention): Self-publishing means doing both development and distribution (marketing, user acquisition, store presence). The creator argues few studios do both well, so consider a publisher when you lack distribution skill or capacity. Self-reported, low-advice source; check publisher terms and revenue split before signing. Sources: [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-10 45:23](https://youtu.be/oU3oz6Ddz7g?t=2723). Newest source 2026-10-22.
- **Start structured A/B tests once a title is stably earning** (single-mention): GameMakers (indie, self-reported) places the mid-stage at roughly a couple hundred thousand per year in revenue, where a stable title is making money. At that point, shift from ad-hoc changes to structured A/B tests. Treat the threshold as a rough guide, not a fixed rule. Sources: [GameMakers 2025-02 14:21](https://youtu.be/qZRNpN5M8jA?t=861). Newest source 2025-02-04.
- **Build a full experimentation machine only at enterprise scale** (single-mention): GameMakers (indie, self-reported) says enterprise-level games at seven to eight figures per year need a dedicated experimentation machine. Below that stage, keep testing lighter-weight so process does not outpace revenue. Sources: [GameMakers 2025-02 15:01](https://youtu.be/qZRNpN5M8jA?t=901). Newest source 2025-02-04.
- **Benchmark independent targets against 100M+ revenue for major mobile games** (single-mention): A GameMakers speaker (2021-06, self-reported, low-advice) defined 100M+ revenue as the scale threshold for major mobile games. Use it as a yardstick for how big an independent title must get before it competes with large publishers. It is not a Roblox-specific threshold, so check it against current Roblox revenue data before relying on it. Sources: [GameMakers 2021-06 10:46](https://youtu.be/c-LQvmtT-Co?t=646). Newest source 2021-06-28.
- **Bring concrete data to early-stage investors** (single-mention): A GameMakers speaker (2021-06, self-reported, low-advice) said earlier-stage companies lack the history and data investors need to deploy capital with confidence. Expect early-stage investors to scrutinize data. Pair the existing proof-of-work and return-story pitch with concrete metrics and a clear rationale. Sources: [GameMakers 2021-06 29:17](https://youtu.be/c-LQvmtT-Co?t=1757). Newest source 2021-06-28.

## Checklist
- [ ] Finish or nearly finish a playable game
- [ ] Define monetization and deal structure
- [ ] Write a 1-2-3 month roadmap covering improvements and marketing
- [ ] Gather proof of work and progress
- [ ] Build audience and community before pitching
- [ ] Calculate game worth: assets plus day-one revenue x 7
- [ ] Compute investment percentage as investment / total worth
- [ ] Run a small test ad (about 1,000 Robux) to check daily revenue
- [ ] Write an application with role, game details, use of funds, amount and timeline
- [ ] Consider crowdfunding from several friends or devs
- [ ] Do not accept a lowball or bad deal
- [ ] Prepare a Game Fund application: team, description, pitch deck, milestone schedule and budget
- [ ] Choose between Game Fund and Accelerator by project scale
- [ ] If educational, apply to the Community Fund and book Education Open Office Hours
- [ ] Consider selling only part of the game and keeping ownership
- [ ] Avoid paid hires unless needed; use free resources and friends
- [ ] Add partners as the game grows
- [ ] Use groups for separate studios (limit 100 for Premium)
- [ ] Avoid permanent group removals to protect ownership privileges
- [ ] Test clothing in Studio before paying for Avatar Shop upload
- [ ] Before counting on Roblox program money, confirm the current terms: the source says support is mentorship, promotion, and planned incentives, not equity or grants.
- [ ] Build the studio budget without Roblox cash unless the current program terms explicitly include it.
- [ ] Before deciding to self-publish, honestly assess whether you can handle both development and distribution (marketing and player acquisition) well.
- [ ] If distribution is your weak point, compare publisher offers against self-publishing, and review the revenue split and rights terms before signing.
- [ ] Identify your title's revenue stage (stable earner ~couple hundred thousand per year; enterprise seven to eight figures per year) before choosing a testing process.
- [ ] Once a title is stably earning, set up structured A/B tests instead of ad-hoc changes.
- [ ] Below enterprise scale, keep experimentation lightweight; reserve dedicated experimentation infrastructure for seven-to-eight-figure titles.

## Where creators agree
- Investors need confidence in the game's future: TheDevKing and The Letus Garden both say to prove potential with progress.
- TheDevKing and The Letus Garden both say to state clearly what the money is for and how it will be repaid or grow.
- Ventures and VuukStudios both describe revenue- or percentage-based deals rather than flat fees.
- Roblox official sources and Ventures both favor staged, performance-linked funding (milestones and follow-on rounds).
- Keeping partial ownership is encouraged (Grow Garden example via rasdev, and the small percentage deals from VuukStudios and Ventures).

## Where creators disagree
- **What investors need to see**
  - TheDevKing and The Letus Garden (2020): a finished or nearly finished game, proof of work and a roadmap are enough.
  - Naavik Gaming Podcast (2026): users, audience and community matter more than a good product or demo. This is the newer advice.
- **Single large investor vs many small sources**
  - TheDevKing: crowdfund small amounts from several friends or devs.
  - Ventures: funds make many structured bets on creators and follow up with more money, so a single institutional backer is possible.
- **Hiring**
  - Roblox DevForum (2017): avoid paid builders and scripters.
  - TheDevKing: funds can go to art, hiring and ads.
  - VuukStudios and janisjanis01: partner or team up as the game grows.

## Date context (what may be outdated)
- The 2017 advice to avoid paid hires predates later funding programs and is likely outdated for funded teams.
- Game Fund figures ($500,000 minimum) and Accelerator terms come from a July 2021 announcement and may have changed. Verify current terms.
- The 2020 pitching and valuation advice (day-one x 7) is older and unsophisticated. Newer sources (2026) stress audience before funding.
- Avatar Shop upload fees (2020) and Premium group limits (2019) may have changed.
- Ventures fund figures date from December 2025 and are self-reported projections.
- GamesBeat (indie, self-reported, 2026-03) says Roblox programs are not equity or grants 'at this stage'. The 'at this stage' wording and the mention of planned future incentives mean this may change, so re-check before relying on it. Today is 2026-11-01, so this is about eight months old.
- T17959 (2026-10) is the newest item on this point; it is a single self-reported indie view, not official Roblox guidance.
- GameMakers revenue-stage thresholds are from 2025-02 and self-reported; they may have shifted by 2026-11-01.
- Liftoff IPO and AppLovin valuation figures are from 2026-02 and are stale as of 2026-11-01. Market valuations move quickly, so re-check before citing.
- The new items come from a 2021-06 GameMakers talk. Figures such as the ~$5B EA reserves and the 100M+ revenue threshold are over four years old as of 2026-11 and should be verified before use.
- No newer advice in these items overrides existing rules; they only add context to the existing scale and funding guidance.

## Instructions for an agent using this file
- If a user asks about funding, first check whether the project is educational (then Community Fund and Education Open Office Hours), large and ambitious (Game Fund), or small (Accelerator or private investors).
- If a user is pitching private investors, then require game progress, a monetization plan, a 1-2-3 month roadmap, a deal structure and an application listing role, use of funds, amount and timeline.
- If a user needs a valuation, then compute total worth = assets + (day-one revenue x 7) and percentage = investment / worth, and warn that it is a rough, optimistic method from one indie creator.
- If a user has no audience, then recommend building community before fundraising, citing the 2026 Naavik advice over the 2020 advice.
- If quoting revenue, share or fund figures from indie sources, then label them self-reported and unverified; prefer official Roblox statements for program mechanics.
- If a user asks about Game Fund terms, then state the 2021 figures ($500,000 minimum, milestone-based release) and recommend checking current official documentation.
- If a user offers a deal, then advise against lowballing, and note that reference points are 5-8% equity for proven teams and 10-15% fund revenue shares.
- If a user is a solo beginner, then advise using free resources and friends first and adding partners as the game grows.
- If a user manages studio groups, then warn about the 90-day block and permanent loss from group removals, and mention the 100-group Premium limit.
- If a user expects Jumpstart or similar support to be salary or publishing, then correct them: it is in-kind support.
- If asked whether Roblox programs give equity or grants, answer that as of March 2026 they did not, citing T17604, and note that terms may have changed since.
- If asked about Roblox program support, describe it as mentorship, promotion, and possible future incentives, and flag that this is indie-reported (self-reported) rather than official.
- Do not present the 2026-03 characterization as current platform policy without checking newer official sources.
- If a user asks whether to self-publish or get a publisher, present the development-vs-distribution tradeoff from T17959 and say it is a single self-reported view.
- Do not treat this tip as a recommendation to sign with a publisher; it only suggests considering one.
- When a user asks about A/B testing or experimentation, first check their revenue stage against the GameMakers thresholds (N7943, N7944) before recommending heavy process.
- Do not use the Liftoff or AppLovin valuation figures (N7988, N7989) as benchmarks for pricing a studio, game, or deal. They are low-advice, self-reported market context from February 2026.
- Label GameMakers stage thresholds (N7943, N7944) as self-reported indie figures, not platform policy or official guidance.
- If a user asks about the market for Roblox-adjacent companies, present the valuation figures as cited claims that need verification from primary filings.
- If a user asks about the scale at which an independent game competes with large publishers, cite the 100M+ revenue mobile threshold (N8037) as a mobile-market reference, and note it is a 2021 self-reported figure.
- If a user is pitching an early-stage company to investors, assume the investor lacks a track record to deploy capital with confidence (Q1937) and push them to supply concrete data alongside the story.
- Do not present the EA cash-reserve figure (N8036) as guidance for indie funding decisions; it is context only.
