# Case study: Grow a Garden

**Genre:** Farming / idle simulator

Viral cozy farming game: plant, grow, harvest, sell, buy seeds, with offline growth, timed seed stock, pets and weather as multipliers. Began as a 16-year-old creator's farming game (BMWLux) and was later taken over for weekly updates.

Also referred to as: Grow A Garden, Grow A Garden (clone), Grow A Garden 2, Grow Garden, Grow a Garden 2

## Numbers
- Peak ~22.3M CCU (GamesBeat, article) G1198
- Held CCU record ~22.4M before being overtaken; 32.2bn lifetime visits (PocketGamer.biz) G1310
- 21M concurrent (SmartyRBX, creator) G1137
- 22M+ concurrent within months (GosuGamers) G1774
- Peak near 22M CCU, Aug 2025 (RoWatcher, tracker blog) G1471
- ~22M concurrent, reportedly beating Fortnite peak (Cumin, creator claim) G1349
- 21B+ visits (MoseyTube, creator claim) G1698
- 1B visits in 33 days, fastest to 1B (RoWatcher) G1538
- ~30B plays since March 2025 launch (Ventures) G1415; G891 '30B visits' dated 2024-10 predates that launch, treat as unreliable
- Spiked to 20M then fell to 7.6M (rasdev) G2390
- CCU reportedly down over 90% from peak by late 2026 (Deconstructor of Fun host) G2702
- 50-60% of update ideas from players (Roblox) G1395
- Replicated attributes/tags on thousands of parts: 45% crash spike (Roblox) G1306
- Viral early version: 1.5M+ players (JustDucksey) G1076; 8M live active users claimed (janisjanis01) G1106
- Acquired and reshaped by Do Big Studios (RoWatcher) G1512; feature film with Story Kitchen announced Nov 2025 (GamesBeat) G1521
- Five gamepasses priced 299-799 Robux (Roblox public API, 2026-11)
- Rose to top experiences quickly under updated discovery algorithm (Roblox Investor Relations, 2026-07; no figure given)
- Steep decline despite a large event (StudioKrew agency blog, GGAID data, 2026-08; no figure given)
- 'Hugely popular' benchmark; no CCU figures in the notes (CallMeDogey, 2026-03; G1548).
- Rasdev says a game made in a few days by a 16-year-old was bought at a few thousand players by Splitting Point, with the creator keeping half (self-reported; G2294).

## What creators say about it
- Offline growth is the retention core: crops keep growing while players are away, giving a reason to return the next day. Roblox and rasdev credit this. ([Roblox 2025-09 0:03](https://youtu.be/6iB5__RCQDc?t=3), [rasdev 2026-10 7:28](https://youtu.be/e4o-ECkzDoI?t=448), [RoWatcher (tracker blog) 2026-03](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-10-fastest-growing-roblox-games-in-march-2026), [AlvinBlox 2026-01 0:00](https://youtu.be/BjyJlN1npdw?t=0))
- A simple, readable loop beats graphics: SmartyRBX reports simple visuals with easy mechanics retained players better than impressive but boring games. ([SmartyRBX 2025-06 16:06](https://youtu.be/Lplz7ZFcKlQ?t=966), [SmartyRBX 2025-07 8:38](https://youtu.be/exOJsFcer1Q?t=518), [SmartyRBX 2025-07 3:05](https://youtu.be/ddfqOQ7H_h8?t=185))
- Ship weekly updates and let players steer: Roblox says 50-60% of update ideas came from players. ([Roblox 2025-12 0:25](https://youtu.be/oDIpSDOYfY0?t=25), [Roblox 2025-09 0:03](https://youtu.be/6iB5__RCQDc?t=3), [GamesBeat 2025-08](https://gamesbeat.com/?p=305182))
- Show consequences instead of explaining them: a seed turning into a carrot as the first action (Tizzy) teaches the loop; the HUD buttons are widely learned. ([Tizzy 2026-06](https://youtu.be/HFcegpPPvpI), [Tizzy 2026-07](https://youtu.be/nggNvUILQ1I), [RoyalDev 2026-08 9:32](https://youtu.be/RXmiJkaZWV4?t=572), [Tizzy 2026-06](https://youtu.be/4ms4cp2mrtM))
- Expect decay. Newer 2026 advice supersedes the 2025 view of an unbeatable hit: the spike fell from 20M to 7.6M (rasdev) and the host says CCU is down over 90% from peak (G2702). Plan for post-spike retention; G1590 (Apr 2026) framed pay-to-win as disfavoured. ([rasdev 2026-10 2:51](https://youtu.be/53OBGXktIPY?t=171), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-10 10:15](https://youtu.be/NrPjXO_7oKk?t=615), [Deconstructor of Fun 2026-04 11:37](https://youtu.be/PNeLRjewQns?t=697))
- Clones saturate and ship buggy: one clone was buggy and added plushies, mutations and trade before advertising; copy the concept, not the assets. ([LovJak 2026-07 0:00](https://youtu.be/feVPsViqceo?t=0), [waveshine 2026-07 5:47](https://youtu.be/hQ1lrFLEF1c?t=347), [janisjanis01 2025-07 1:06](https://youtu.be/pD8fKkzF8O8?t=66), [janisjanis01 2025-06 0:00](https://youtu.be/B409dMcW-pw?t=0))
- State the sequel's differentiator clearly: night-time crop stealing and smacking adds PvP to the farming loop, and the title teases it ('Steal at Night'). ([Tizzy 2026-06](https://youtu.be/az7em1IoBhA), [JustDucksey 2026-08 0:00](https://youtu.be/kl933X0UCC0?t=0), [GosuGamers 2026-06](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78614-grow-a-garden-2-has-launched-on-roblox-here-s-everything-new-added-in-this-sequel))
- Layout and UI changes: bigger map, shops in the centre, plot purchases instead of fences, and the shop button moved into the top UI. ([LovJak 2026-06 0:45](https://youtu.be/ImcICq5XfL8?t=45))
- Discovery momentum can lift a new sequel fast, but retention decided the outcome; decline followed a large event. ([Roblox Investor Relations 2026-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm), [StudioKrew (agency blog, GGAID data) 2026-08](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-august-2026/))
- Timed seed restocks, offline growth and guild weekly rewards give reasons to return. ([Roblox public API 2026-11](https://www.roblox.com/games/97598239454123/))
- Grow Garden is cited as a hugely popular game people want to replicate, and as the genre example for grow-style concepts. ([CallMeDogey 2026-03 0:00](https://youtu.be/7QfaQe4rsq8?t=0), [MonarchDev 2026-10 0:00](https://youtu.be/bbCmt98O9MA?t=0))
- Recreating the genre with offline progression is a popular variant. ([BloxxedDev 2025-06 0:00](https://youtu.be/Gk-iTN8Ru_Q?t=0))
- Avoid cash-grab grow designs; a prior example was criticised as cash-grab-style. ([TheDankDeveloper 2026-10](https://youtu.be/eExtO_uF6Jg))
- A game made in a few days by a 16-year-old was bought at a few thousand players by Splitting Point; the creator kept half. This mention is grouped under Grow Garden in the notes. ([rasdev 2026-10 3:34](https://youtu.be/4GEHEzhvhwI?t=214))

## Transferable patterns
- Timed offline progress as a return-visit hook
- Make the first action produce a visible result as the tutorial
- Publish weekly updates and gather player-sourced ideas
- Profile replication cost: thousands of replicated attributes or tags caused a 45% crash spike (Roblox)
- Name the sequel's differentiator in title and thumbnail
- Add a night-time PvP layer to a farming loop
- Plan for discovery spikes followed by retention decay
- Tiered gamepads priced in the 299-799 Robux band
- Use genre benchmark games to scope a concept
- Offline progression as a differentiator
- Avoid monetisation that reads as cash-grab
- Rapid prototyping can still attract publisher interest

## Merged from other notes of the same game
- Grow A Garden 2: Sequel to the gardening loop adding guilds, night-time crop stealing and smacking to defend, shop bargaining, and a bigger map with central shops. Adds PvP to farming and sells five gamepasses.
- Grow Garden: Roblox growing game cited as a hugely popular benchmark and genre template; creators recreate it with offline progression. A prior cash-grab-style example is criticised. Grouped notes also include a rasdev deal mention.
