# Case study: Fisch

**Genre:** Fishing game (Roblox)

First-mover Roblox fishing game with skill mini-games and a sell-buy-reward loop, kept alive by seasonal updates. Revived strongly through social features and onboarding changes per Roblox's own account.

Also referred to as: Fish It! (comparison only, not the same game)

## Numbers
- Peaked around 100K CCU vs Fish It!'s 500K+ per RoWatcher tracker article (third-party, not creator-stated, G1449)
- Revived from ~10K to 1.3M+ CCU (Roblox, G2328)

## What creators say about it
- Filling an unserved niche with a simple sell-buy-reward loop and regular seasonal updates gives a fishing game a durable base. ([Duckable 2025-02 0:00](https://youtu.be/OS-k2iOEfhM?t=0))
- Being first with skill mini-games reached ~100K CCU per tracker, but Fish It! reached 500K+. First-mover advantage alone does not win the niche. ([RoWatcher (tracker blog) 2026-00](https://rowatcher.com/news/fish-it-reels-in-270k-players-inside-roblox-s-most-addictive-casual-game-of-2026))
- Revival playbook (per Roblox): grouped map, visible goals, titles, leaderboards, voice chat, invite bonus, and new-player-only servers. Most recent source (2026-10); prefer it for re-engagement. ([Roblox 2026-10](https://youtu.be/LIKiUlkTqZc))
- Quest books with many tasks and rewards need guidance on navigation, bait and progress-speed requirements, or players stall. ([ProjectSupreme 2026-10 0:00](https://youtu.be/6U55tBYn52I?t=0))

## Transferable patterns
- Make goals visible: titles, leaderboards and clear quest targets.
- Add social hooks: voice, invite bonuses and new-player-only servers.
- Explain navigation and progression requirements in any task list.
- Keep a simple core loop fresh with seasonal content.
