# Case study: Blue Lock Rivals

**Genre:** Licensed anime sports PvP

Licensed Kodansha IP game built in about 3 months that reached hundreds of thousands of concurrent players and sold for over $3M to Do Big Studios. Uses balance dashboards and rewarded video placed in the shop.

## Numbers
- Hundreds of thousands CCU; sold for over $3M (rasdev, 2026-10, self-reported)
- Estimated ~$5M/month at peak near 1M players (rasdev speaker estimate, self-reported)

## What creators say about it
- Built in about 3 months, reached hundreds of thousands of CCU, and sold for over $3M (self-reported). ([rasdev 2026-10 3:05](https://youtu.be/4GEHEzhvhwI?t=185))
- Custom dashboards tracking match time and win rate by character style show whether an update shifted balance. ([Roblox 2026-10](https://youtu.be/bVU69VImiEc))
- Licensed IP let millions of fans extend the fandom through community-built games. ([Roblox 2026-10](https://youtu.be/oxiaIhN5Ybo))
- Pair a currency reward with progressive unlock for rewarded video, placed in the shop. ([Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-playbooks/sports), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/ad-placements))

## Transferable patterns
- Balance dashboards for live-ops checks
- Licensed IP to tap existing fandom
- Rewarded video tied to progression unlocks
- Fast prototype-to-sale path (self-reported)
