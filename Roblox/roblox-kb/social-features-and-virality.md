## Overview
Roblox's 2025-07 and 2026-09 updates shift social features toward trust-tiered communication and lower-friction entry. Friends is being renamed Connections, and trusted connections get unfiltered text and voice with people they know in real life. Pre-set Quick Words (late 2026) are pre-reviewed for policy compliance so players of all ages can coordinate. A web player (2026-09) lets people join from search, YouTube, X or email links without installing, so Experience Details Page share links become a new acquisition path. Official framing is that safety and freedom are measured together. Creator-side advice on invites, sharing and promotion is unchanged.

## Rules and findings
- **Make every game social, not just chat-centric** (multiple-creators): Any genre can be social. Add friend lists, join-friend, invites and trading rather than relying on chat. Roblox is the most social platform; social systems (guilds, trading, gifting, housing parties, collaborative building) drive retention. SmartyRBX claims singleplayer games fail (unverified 99%). - **Put a visible invite-friends button/prompt in the game** (multiple-creators): waveshine calls an in-game invite button the most important tip. Roblox says about a third of top 1,000 developers adopted invite prompts in a year, lifting engagement and retention. Build natural in-world invite moments (e.g., step on a square) and offer a bonus (Fisch XP) for friends in server. Make onboarding social, not isolated. ## Where creators agree
- Roblox is inherently social; games should include friend-driven hooks (Roblox Official, RobloxCreators, SmartyRBX, AlvinBlox, Tizzy, waveshine).
- Smaller creators are the best early targets (Roblox Official, SmartyRBX, AlvinBlox, Roblox Creators).
- Clippable, surprising, chaotic moments drive virality (Roblox, Tizzy, MoseyTube, CaberJaber, SmartyRBX).
- Short-form TikTok content works when engaging, not plain ads (SmartyRBX, Zyonic, TheDankDeveloper).
- Teasers, puzzles and leaks generate organic buzz (Roblox Creators, RobloxCreators).
- Early access and creator involvement help launches (Roblox Creators, RobloxCreators, Roblox, SmartyRBX).
- Co-play and friend invites are a core growth and retention lever (Roblox official, Deconstructor of Fun, MonzterDEV, Roblox DevForum, TheDevKing).
- Reward the inviter and invitee; detect referrals with official APIs (Roblox Creator Docs, Deconstructor of Fun).
- Use Share Links for trackable promotion; creator-facing content and short-form clips drive reach (Roblox Creator Docs, DitchyDevelopments, MonzterDEV).
- Give players a reason to return through social spaces and community channels (TheDevKing, Roblox Creator Docs).
- Clips should come from the core gameplay loop and require consent before sharing (Roblox official, Deconstructor of Fun).
- Multiplayer and co-play design beats single-player on Roblox: Duckable, rasdev and Q1021 all point this way.
- Competition with leaderboards and ranked modes creates engagement: janisjanis (T6005) and rasdev (T6443) agree.
- Short-form TikTok can drive some traffic: rasdev and janisjanis both report it, though magnitude differs.
- Official docs and creators agree that global announcements and in-game social signals help engagement (T6879, T7669).
- Friends joining friends drive growth and spending (VuukStudios, Raul).
- Framing games as play-with-friends and building daily social hooks brings players back (Erthlord, VuukStudios).
- Voice and emotes are core engagement features (Roblox official docs and DevForum).
- Short-form vertical video is a main discovery engine. #7Phantom grew a group mainly through TikTok, LovJak recommends vertical gameplay for Shorts and TikTok, and studio rook.'s devlogs reached millions through Instagram.
- Creators and streamers drive discovery, and it works best when the game gives them something to show. Emancyphur and the existing creator-support rules both rely on streamers investigating or covering a game.
- Player-vs-player conflict and stealing generate shareable stories and word-of-mouth (Player.One, RoWatcher).
- Co-play and friend-based social hooks are central to retention and growth (StudioKrew, Roblox Investor Relations; already reflected in existing rules).
- Short-form and social posting is a major zero-budget discovery channel: janisjanis01 (T18712, Q1928) and SmartyRBX (T18478) both recommend regular posting of clips and devlogs to drive players and followers.

## Where creators disagree
- **Paying/commissioning influencers**
  - RobloxOfficial 2018 and AlvinBlox: promo codes, payment in items, commissioning small creators
  - Roblox (2025, Ninja Challenge): skip paying YouTubers/TikTokers early; a fun game attracts creators organically
  - SmartyRBX: needs a very good game and lots of value
- **Competition vs cooperation**
  - RobloxCreators, Roblox, Tizzy: leaderboards and social flex drive competition and retention
  - AlvinBlox: avoid pure leaderboard competition; favor cooperative community
- **Invite-reward pop-ups**
  - waveshine, Roblox 2023, Roblox 2026 (Fisch): invite button/bonus works
  - Roblox 2026 (T3140): prefer social-by-design play-together mechanics over invite-for-reward pop-ups
- **Twitter value**
  - RobloxCreators 2022 and others: Twitter hype, hashtags and puzzles build launch buzz
  - RobloxCreators 2025: Twitter engagement is hard to convert to sales; treat as community building
- **Cold outreach to small creators**
  - Roblox DevForum (T3511): DM YouTubers with 1-10k subscribers to play for free; offer in-game codes as rewards.
  - DitchyDevelopments (T3831): avoid spamming links or cold DMing creators; reach interested players through communities and playtest exchanges.
- **Scaling rewards by friend count**
  - MonzterDEV (T3574): stat boosts that scale with friends in the server to increase invites.
  - Deconstructor of Fun (T3788): design for co-play without punishing solo players; reward invites and group play with bonus currency.
- **Viral short-form as the main route**
  - MonzterDEV (T3625, Q721): viral TikTok/Shorts content is the main route to new users and affiliate earnings. Low-advice.
  - DitchyDevelopments (T3831, T3833): build an interested audience through communities and devlogs; they credit devlog Shorts from day one for their traffic.
- **How much TikTok drives player counts**
  - rasdev (T6855, 2026-10): TikTok is the biggest external traffic source and 100k-500k views can drive large player counts. Preferred as the more recent view.
  - janisjanis (T6396, 2026-01): short-form TikTok alone gave only a slight, temporary CCU increase; bumps fade within a day. Based on early-account experience.
- **Whether to isolate server chat**
  - Existing rule 'Do not isolate friends into separate server instances' conflicts in spirit with official T9828, which allows disabling cross-server chat if the design needs isolated server chat.
  - Official T9827 keeps cross-server chat enabled by default for low-occupancy games. Treat this as context-dependent rather than a true contradiction.
- **Enabling voice chat**
  - Existing rule 'Enable voice chat' is the general default for social games.
  - Official T9363 says disable voice chat in classroom games to create a closed garden. Use the education context to override the general default.
- **Whether to pay TikTok creators early**
  - 1Coal paid about $400 for a TikTok creator video that reached 4M views (T12074).
  - The existing file rule says to skip early paid influencers, but it applies to Twitter. It does not address TikTok paid creators.
- **Which platform to prioritize for item promotion**
  - Erthlord's January 2026 reels favored TikTok (17,000+ views vs 6,000 YouTube) (N5078).
  - Erthlord's July 2026 data favors YouTube (108,000 vs under 5,000 TikTok) for item promos (T13624). The more recent data should be preferred.
- **Which creators to approach first**
  - Emancyphur (T15840): early coverage by popular Roblox YouTubers drove large player growth without paid advertising.
  - Existing rule 'Approach smaller creators first with a playable build' favors smaller creators first.
  - Reconciliation: large channels can drive reach, while small creators give early feedback. Sources do not say which is better for a given budget.
- **Which short-form platform to prioritize**
  - #7Phantom (2025-07): TikTok grew a 40K group, with a 3 million view breakout after about 5 months of near-daily posting.
  - studio rook. (2026-08): TikTok averaged about 2 views per video during development, while Instagram pushed videos to millions.
  - Existing rule 'Post short-form on TikTok/Twitter consistently' does not address Instagram. Both results may be true for different games and timing; test platforms rather than assume one.
- **Naming for the friend list and trusted-contact UI**
  - Roblox [OFFICIAL] (T17573, 2025-07): the platform is renaming Friends to Connections, with trusted connections gating unfiltered chat.
