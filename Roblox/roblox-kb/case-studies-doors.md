# Case study: DOORS

**Genre:** Horror / procedural survival

Procedural hotel-style horror of numbered doors and escalating floors, driven by minimal cues and sound-led threats. Viral since 2024; won Best Use of Audio in Sept 2026 award coverage (LSPLASH).

Also referred to as: Doors

## Numbers
- Launched 2022; exploded to 100k+ players (Creators on the Blox) G1558
- Won Best Use of Audio (Roblox Newsroom) G2091; PocketGamer.biz G2101; NetInfluencer G2113; GosuGamers G2121
- Viral TikTok and Shorts coverage drove new users to Roblox (MonzterDEV) G872; no metric given

## What creators say about it
- Sound is gameplay: players read the world through audio and anticipate threats; a sound cue in a dark room makes players scan and react. ([Roblox Newsroom 2026-09](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/2026-roblox-innovation-awards), [Roblox 2026-09 20:48](https://youtu.be/HXZRH-2zurk?t=1248), [Cumin 2026-08 14:58](https://youtu.be/rjPW4GeeyFg?t=898))
- Teach through minimal cues, not tutorials: press C to crouch, minecart interaction; dying teaches what cues like flickering lights mean. ([Daiorg 2025-12 29:01](https://youtu.be/JCMIb008Y6E?t=1741), [ByteBlox 2024-06 20:12](https://youtu.be/_rksdF5JoHo?t=1212))
- A strong core loop of escalating progression through numbered doors carried a major update despite rough edges. ([Daiorg 2024-10 0:00](https://youtu.be/LXRBAzLaxwQ?t=0))
- Distinct art and soundtrack made it look unlike a typical Roblox game and drove viral growth; a single ominous door icon is a focal point. ([Daiorg 2025-08 17:30](https://youtu.be/GkKUU9mCgwc?t=1050), [rasdev 2026-05 4:30](https://youtu.be/q0lIbkUC2r8?t=270), [1Coal 2023-08 2:36](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=156))
- Avoid near-copies: Pressure's early design was too close to Doors and its creator regrets it; Doors-likes stop feeling fresh. ([Creators on the Blox 2025-11 28:23](https://youtu.be/Qn0uVUSxG8I?t=1703), [Daiorg 2025-02 3:53](https://youtu.be/nutv8RT5UrQ?t=233))
- Shop with clear utility: a Pre-Run Shop sells consumables for Knobs, a flashlight's use is instantly clear, and Revives are a limited core mechanic. Paid items should not block core play. ([Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/contextual-purchases), [Roblox Creator Docs 2026-10](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/monetization-foundations), [ByteBlox 2024-11 1:29](https://youtu.be/Jl0SdXksQsI?t=89))

## Transferable patterns
- Use sound as the primary threat signal
- Teach by minimal in-world cues and let failure teach
- Give the game one distinct art and icon focal point
- Keep shop items clearly useful and limited
