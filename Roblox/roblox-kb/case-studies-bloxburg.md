# Case study: Bloxburg

**Genre:** Life sim / house-building roleplay

Open-ended life simulation where players build houses, take jobs, collaborate on builds and upgrade rooms. A long-running top earner, widely cited as a benchmark for scope and customisation.

Also referred to as: Blocksburg, Welcome to Bloxburg, Blox Burg

## Numbers
- Top earner when a game hit #2 (GameMakers, G204)
- $40M–$100M/yr estimate (VuukStudios, G789, unverified)
- Sold for around $100M (rasdev, G2297, speaker's claim)
- Took years and hundreds of thousands of dollars to build (rasdev, G1634)
- Paid access at 2500 Robux still kept it on the front page of top earners (Roblox, 2018-07; G32).

## What creators say about it
- Scope: it's a massive project beginners overreach on. Treat it as a long-term target, not a first game (SmartyRBX, 2024 and 2025). ([SmartyRBX 2024-09 0:54](https://youtu.be/59AbBgxhfTA?t=54), [SmartyRBX 2025-06 8:49](https://youtu.be/MAnvGNgBKgQ?t=529), [rasdev 2026-05 2:37](https://youtu.be/q0lIbkUC2r8?t=157))
- Open customisation lets players create their own stories (1Coal); building collaboratively is one of its most popular social features (Roblox Creators). ([1Coal 2023-08 1:41](https://youtu.be/YBSsksqfxo0?t=101), [Roblox Creators 2021-07 26:43](https://youtu.be/UL4QaSbfyC4?t=1603))
- Dream house goal split into room upgrades and job promotions, with going to work as the short-term loop (Roblox Creators). ([Roblox Creators 2021-12 11:16](https://youtu.be/hD2Ive5RqtE?t=676))
- Life sims with jobs, housing, fishing and day-night lighting can be prototyped quickly; hair and character style need polish (MonarchDev, 4-hour build). ([MonarchDev 2026-10 7:25](https://youtu.be/QwdZRB6xRKk?t=445))
- Monetisation can be heavy (VuukStudios estimate). Pay-to-join was a notable exception in 2019 (Roblox DevForum). ([VuukStudios 2024-07 7:53](https://youtu.be/NfXvHlAdIsI?t=473), [Roblox DevForum 2019-03](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetisation-101-how-to-effectively-generate-revenue-in-your-roblox-game/246902))
- Solo developers have built top hits (RoyalDev, 2026-08), showing scale is not required to earn top grossing. ([RoyalDev 2026-08 5:29](https://youtu.be/6CefFrCFqcI?t=329), [RoyalDev 2026-09 0:43](https://youtu.be/7FSqZeirnjs?t=43))
- Paid access at 2500 Robux still kept the game on the front page of top earners in 2018. ([Roblox 2018-07 44:54](https://youtu.be/DWUfL8azgIk?t=2694))
- Keep the same icon for years, adding only seasonal hats; changing icons can confuse existing players. ([Roblox Creators 2021-07 1:03:01](https://youtu.be/Gv5OVVyWM10?t=3781))
- Regular furniture drops every few weeks act as cadence content that keeps the game fresh. ([Roblox Creators 2021-09 1:07](https://youtu.be/kQIyqMdh7Os?t=67), [Roblox Creators 2021-10 1:09](https://youtu.be/d4g30UupgKw?t=69))
- Economy progression: house items cost money earned through work, which gates content by time. ([Roblox Creators 2021-12 5:19](https://youtu.be/XTZxQvh1qkc?t=319))
- Balanced economy: Robux buys cash for building, with no gambling or pay-to-win mechanics. ([Roblox DevForum 2023-12](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/understand-the-roblox-algorithm/2772921))

## Transferable patterns
- Set a clear long-term goal with short-term loops (jobs, upgrades).
- Give players open customisation and building as their core creative outlet.
- Treat large-scope life sims as a later project, not a first one.
- Expect heavy monetisation to be a sensitive topic; check the specifics.
- Keep a stable icon across updates
- Fixed-cadence content drops
- Earn-through-play currency that gates progress by time
- Robux-to-currency without pay-to-win
