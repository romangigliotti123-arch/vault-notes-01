# Lighting checks L1-L12 (rbx-lighting)

Every check returns `status` (`pass` | `fail` | `skip`), `measured`, `tolerance`, `issues` (with the number), `fixes` (machine-applicable), `warnings`, `unverified`. The report is produced by `LightingCore.verify` (Luau) and `lighting_ref.py` (Python twin); `lighting_gate.py` always recomputes it from the stored state with the Python twin, so the stored status is never trusted. Numbers below are the generic bands; a genre or time of day changes those marked (G) or (T): see `presets.md` and `bands()` in `lighting_ref.py`; constants in `scripts/presets.json`.

L-checks are judged BEFORE the V-checks of `rbx-visual`: a flat, dark or washed-out image is a lighting defect with a number, not a polish opinion. V5 (lighting and atmosphere) is scored by eye only after the L-gate passed.

| Check | Measures | Fails when | Typical fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L1 Not the default Roblox look** | 9 tracked Lighting properties (Brightness, ClockTime, ExposureCompensation, ShadowSoftness, GeographicLatitude, Ambient, OutdoorAmbient, ColorShift_Top, ColorShift_Bottom) against the Roblox defaults; enabled Atmosphere, Bloom, ColorCorrection, SunRays | fewer than 4 of the 9 differ from the defaults, or none of Atmosphere / Bloom / ColorCorrection / SunRays is enabled. Technology Voxel / Compatibility / Legacy is a warning | `applyPreset` |
| **L2 Atmosphere** | count, Density, Offset, Haze, Glare, Color | no Atmosphere; more than one; Density 0; Offset outside 0-1; Haze outside the band (0-3, G); Glare outside 0-1.5; black Color | `applyPreset`, or set the property |
| **L3 Sky** | count, the six textures, SunAngularSize, StarCount | no Sky; more than one; any of the six textures empty; SunAngularSize outside 1-60. Warnings: all six are the default Roblox textures (pass an asset-library skybox as an override), night scene with StarCount < 500 | `applyPreset` |
| **L4 Post effects** | every enabled Bloom, ColorCorrection, SunRays, DepthOfField, Blur | Bloom: Intensity > 1.0 (G), Threshold < 0.7 (G), Size > 48, or index Intensity x Size/24 / Threshold > 1.2 (G). ColorCorrection: Brightness outside -0.15..0.15, Contrast outside -0.1..0.45 (G), Saturation outside -0.35..0.55 (G), TintColor saturation > 0.4. SunRays: Intensity > 0.35 (G), Spread > 1. DepthOfField: not allowed for the genre (tower defense), FarIntensity > 0.5, NearIntensity > 0.25, InFocusRadius < 20, FocusDistance < 30. Blur Size > 4 | `set` the property to the clamped value |
| **L5 Colour grading cohesion** | hue and saturation of Atmosphere.Color, ColorCorrection.TintColor, FogColor (when legacy fog is on), OutdoorAmbient; the StyleKit Theme hues | two saturated (>= 0.12) grading colours more than 60 degrees apart in hue; or, with a Theme, the mean grading hue is more than 50 degrees from every palette hue and more than 30 degrees from every palette complement. No Theme = `unverified`, the check still runs the internal cohesion | `applyPreset`, or retint to the palette |
| **L6 Scene brightness** | an estimate over >= 8 sampled surfaces (colour, normal, sun, shade, nearby lights, post contrast and brightness) and, when given, the mean/crushed/blown of screenshots | estimated mean luma outside the band (0.28-0.72, G and T), crushed (< 0.06) surfaces above 30% (T), blown (> 0.98) above 10% (G), p90 - p10 < 0.12 (flat image). Screenshots: mean outside the band +-0.04, crushed above the same limit, blown above the limit + 5% (the HUD adds white). `skip` when there are neither samples nor screenshots (the gate fails a skipped L6) | `Lighting.ExposureCompensation` by the computed number of stops, `Lighting.Brightness`, or `applyPreset` |
| **L7 Shadows** | GlobalShadows, Technology, shadow-casting lights, key lights (Range >= 16 and Brightness >= 1), CastShadow on large parts | GlobalShadows off; Technology Compatibility / Legacy; under Future a key light with Shadows off; more shadow-casting lights than the budget (6 PC, 2 mobile); more than 40% of large parts (>= 12 studs) with CastShadow off. Voxel is a warning (blocky shadows) | `setLights`, `setParts`, `set` |
| **L8 Fog and haze density for the genre** | Atmosphere Density and Haze against the genre band | Density or Haze outside the genre band (G): e.g. horror 0.35-0.70, tower defense 0.08-0.32. Warning when the applied preset is another genre. `skip` without an Atmosphere (L2 fails then) | `set` to the band middle |
| **L9 Interiors** | share of sampled surfaces with a roof within 80 studs; mean estimated brightness of those; share of them within range of a light | when indoor share >= 15%: indoor mean below 0.16, or fewer than 30% of the indoor surfaces near a light (flat rooms) | `addInteriorLights` (manual: a warm PointLight, Range 16-24, Shadows on, per room) |
| **L10 Performance budget** | cost points: Technology (Future 4, ShadowMap 2, Voxel 1), Bloom 1 (+0.5 over Size 32), ColorCorrection 0.25, SunRays 1, DepthOfField 2, Blur 1, Atmosphere 0.5, shadow light 1 (1.5 under Future), other light 0.05; also the cost of the mobile-safe variant of the same scene | cost above the budget (12 PC, 7 mobile target); on a mobile target also DepthOfField or SunRays on, or more than 2 shadow-casting lights. On `both` a scene whose mobile variant costs more than 7 gets a warning | `applyPreset` with `mobile = true` |
| **L11 Duplicates and conflicts** | enabled objects per class under Lighting and Camera; legacy fog | more than one Atmosphere, Sky, or enabled Bloom / ColorCorrection / SunRays / DepthOfField / Blur; Lighting.FogEnd < 5000 while an Atmosphere exists | `remove` extras, `set FogEnd 100000` |
| **L12 Time of day and exposure** | sun elevation (from ClockTime and GeographicLatitude, or the engine's sun direction), phase day / dusk / night, OutdoorAmbient luma, ExposureCompensation, Brightness | OutdoorAmbient luma outside the phase band (day 0.18-0.80, dusk 0.08-0.65, night 0.02-0.45; G); ExposureCompensation outside -1.5..1.5; Brightness > 3.5 at night | `applyPreset`, `set` |

## Waivers and unverified items
- `cfg.waive = { L5 = "deliberate neon swamp look, approved in art-direction.md" }`: a failing check with a reason of at least 10 characters is waived; at most 2 waivers pass the gate.
- `unverified` items (no Theme, no genre) are listed in the report and the gate output. A missing genre uses the generic bands; set the genre.

## Why a model and not just rules

L6/L9 brightness is an ESTIMATE from a fixed model, not photometry: screenshots (`--shots-from-visual`) decide when they disagree. [cold:rbx-lighting/why.md] read only to understand how the L6/L9 brightness estimate is computed.
