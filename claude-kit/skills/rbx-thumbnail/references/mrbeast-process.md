# What the MrBeast channel and team say about thumbnails and titles (study notes, own words)

Why this file: the channel is the most heavily tested thumbnail operation on YouTube, and its team has talked about the method in public. The principles transfer to a Roblox icon or thumbnail; the pictures and the face do not (see roblox-mapping.md). Nothing here is Roblox data: grade B/C, and the Roblox qPTR test in `[cold:rbx-thumbnail/protocol.md]` decides what works for a given game.

Source reliability: P = said by the people themselves in an interview or post, L = a leaked internal handbook (unconfirmed, reported through press and blogs), S = a writer's interpretation. Do not quote S as if the team said it.

## The process (what they do)
1. **Packaging before production (L).** The title and thumbnail are decided first and the video is built to deliver them. Reported handbook line, via Simon Willison's summary (2024-09-15): the creative process for every video starts with title and thumbnail. https://simonwillison.net/2024/Sep/15/how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production/ . Roblox use: decide the one hook and the icon concept before building the feature that the update promotes.
2. **CTR picks the concept (L).** "CTR is what dictates what we do for videos" (Creator Handbook summary of the leaked document, 2024-09-18, flagged unconfirmed). Worked example in the handbook: "I Spent 50 Hours In My Front Yard" is unclickable; the same effort as a bathtub full of ketchup is far more interesting. https://www.creatorhandbook.net/leaked-document-allegedly-reveals-mrbeasts-secrets-to-youtube-success-the-key-takeaways/ . Roblox use: if two ideas cost the same to build, build the one whose image is easier to picture in one second.
3. **Many concepts, then many variations (P).** The thumbnail lead Chucky Appleby (YouTube interview, reported by Social Media Today, 2024-03-08) described around 50 title and thumbnail concepts per video before narrowing, and hundreds of variations of small things (shirt colour, suit, house colour). A press figure of about $10,000 per thumbnail comes from a linked article, not from the interview. https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-shares-insights-mrbeasts-team-creates-compelling-thumbnail/709817/ . Roblox use: make 10 or more variants of an icon, not 2; Roblox allows 2-5 active thumbnails, ads allow more (see the KB).
4. **Big differences first, small tweaks later (P).** In his interview with Jon Youshaei (YouTube, 2024-05-28, video IXEewFEDieU) he says the first test uses clearly different formats; the winner then gets small detail tweaks, and text changes are tested last; he also says there are diminishing returns and a point where the next video is the better use of time. He also reports that a thumbnail which looked good as a sketch can look unreal once produced. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IXEewFEDieU . Roblox use: round 1 = three different compositions; round 2 = one change at a time on the winner (colour, expression, text).
5. **Test it, do not guess (P).** He posted (2023-09-06, via Creator Handbook 2023-09-07) that closing his mouth in thumbnails raised watch time on every video in his Test and Compare tests; the team lead later said about 30 videos were tested and all 30 closed-mouth variants had higher watch time, a small but consistent difference. No numbers were published. https://www.creatorhandbook.net/mrbeast-claims-closing-his-mouth-in-thumbnails-is-better-for-views/ . Lesson: an expression rule belongs to that audience; run your own test before copying "open mouth surprise" faces. On Roblox the metric is qualified play-through rate, not clicks.
6. **Outliers and reuse (P).** The team's own tool finds old videos that beat the channel average and re-packages the idea with a new title and thumbnail when the content was good but the packaging was weak (Youshaei interview 2024-05-28). Roblox use: re-package an old update that retained well but was never seen.
7. **A thumbnail library you can search (P).** Same interview: a searchable collection of thumbnails by topic, then similar-looking ones, to find what the winners in a niche look like. Roblox use: before drawing, look at the front page for your genre and write down what 10 icons share.
8. **Brightness and other correlations (S/P).** He told a podcast host that the team checked a thousand thumbnails for a link between brightness and views (thestory.au, citing Joe Rogan's podcast). No result is published. Treat "brighter is better" as a hypothesis, not a rule.
9. **Trust and the next click (P).** The thumbnail lead's line: trust is what makes people come back and click your videos; expectations must be met early because viewers can leave at any time. In the Youshaei interview Jimmy says the click depends heavily on how the last video felt, and to keep the pendulum toward content, never let it swing too far toward clickbait.
10. **Readable in a second (P).** If a viewer needs 10 to 20 seconds to work out what the video is about they scroll on; designs that look big in the editor are small on the feed, so check small. Same social-media-today article.

## The composition rules they use (what the 55 examples show)
Counted from `example-library.md` (our own reading of 55 thumbnails, not an official list; formats are our labels):
- One dominant subject, 1-3 supporting elements. Almost every modern thumbnail is a big face plus one prize, threat or scene.
- The face is large and sits on the left or centre, looking at the camera or straight into the threat. The expression is exaggerated: terror, shock, grin, exhaustion.
- A counter (DAY 6, HOUR 22, #801) or number (100 COPS, $100M) in a top corner in white block capitals with a thick black outline. Two to three words at most.
- Colour pairs with maximum contrast: red on teal, yellow on dark, a single warm figure in a cold field, one bright object on pure white.
- Scale: a tiny person on a huge thing (iceberg, tower, building) or a huge animal or object next to a calm person.
- Repetition leading to a vanishing point (100 police cars, a row of megaphones, a row of busts) says "a lot" without a number.
- Two-panel splits with identical framing (BEFORE/AFTER, $1 vs $500,000) put the whole story into the difference.
- The early thumbnails (2012-2018) are often raw screenshots or cluttered text. The trend over 14 years is fewer elements, bigger subject, more contrast.

## What the study does not show
- Exact CTR numbers per thumbnail: the channel does not publish them and neither do we.
- Whether the current YouTube image is the first one: the listing keeps the winning test variant.
- That the same rules fit a children's game feed. The audience, the metric and the rules differ: see roblox-mapping.md.
