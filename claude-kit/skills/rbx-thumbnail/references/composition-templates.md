# Composition templates (pick three different ones per brief)

Each template is a layout you can build with `scripts/thumb_compose.py` (the `templates` command prints the same names) or hand to an image model as the "composition" sentence of a prompt in `prompts.md`. Roles: hero = your Roblox character or pet, prize = the thing the player wants (or the threat), scene = a background layer, extra = a third element. Study examples are numbered as in `example-library.md`. Fractions are of the canvas; they are starting points, not rules.

| Template | Layout | When it fits | Study examples | Text slot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| face-left-prize-right | hero close-up at the left third (height about 0.9 of the frame, face high), prize or scene filling the right two thirds; hero looks at or toward the prize | most games with a character: tycoon, simulator, obby, survival | 4, 7, 10, 16, 25, 29, 31 | corner top right |
| tiny-hero-huge-scene | hero about 0.2 of the frame height in the lower third, a giant tower, boss or map fills the frame; strong value difference between them | scale, danger, climbing, boss fights | 9, 11, 14, 17 | counter top right |
| versus-duel | two heroes or hero and rival at the left and right edges, equal size, diagonal energy, the prize between them | PvP, rival, race, boss | 1, 12, 13, 15 | none or one word |
| before-after-split | the frame cut in two halves with the same pose and framing, a thin divider; left = start, right = result | progression, pets, bases, skins, updates | 2, 21 | BEFORE / AFTER or 1x / 100x, small, in the top of each half |
| object-only | one giant prize or button in the centre on a flat colour, glow, no character | icon, mystery and unlock games, the strongest icon type for a single reward | 28, 39 | none |
| crowd-in-shape | many small characters packed into one bold shape (ring, box), host or hero outside at the edge | battle royale, last-to-leave, social, events | 16, 25 | number top right |
| pile-and-hero | hero partly buried in or surrounded by a huge pile of the currency, pets or items | collectors, simulators, pet games | 30, 40, 41 | none or one number |
| reach-to-camera | a hand, claw or tool reaching toward the viewer, prize behind | action, scarcity, sports | 19 | counter top right |
| color-isolation | the whole scene in one limited palette (or grey), the hero or prize the single saturated element | horror, mystery, any crowded genre | 37, 9 | none |
| vanishing-line | a row of identical enemies, cars or props converging to a point, hero in the foreground | chase, waves, tower defence, many-enemy games | 6, 8, 48 | number top right |

## Icon variants of the same templates (square 512)
- Hero-fill: the hero's face or whole character fills about 80% of the square, centred, eyes toward the viewer, one background colour with a glow behind the head. No text, or one short word or number.
- Object-only: the prize on a flat colour with an outline and glow. The best performer at 150 px when the object is the game.
- Split: two halves, each one symbol (starter and maxed).
Icons are shown small (about 150 px): drop the scene, keep one subject and one emotion.

## Text slots (see text-colour-cheatsheet.md)
- corner top right (counter, number): the most common slot in the study set (#3, #6, #7, #9, #18, #19, #33, #34).
- Never in the bottom 15%: the player-count bar can cover it.
- Title words stay in the title; the image carries a number, a counter or at most 3 words.

## How to choose three
1. Write the hook as a verb plus an object plus a stake (for example: outclimb rising lava).
2. Pick one template from each family: a face one (face-left-prize-right, hero-fill), a scale one (tiny-hero-huge-scene, vanishing-line) and an object or contrast one (object-only, before-after-split, color-isolation).
3. Build all three; the three must differ in composition, not just in colour. Test them as the first round (mrbeast-process.md item 4).
