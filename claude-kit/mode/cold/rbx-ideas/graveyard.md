# Graveyard: how Roblox and indie games die

Cold file, used as a REJECTION FILTER. For a draft idea, find the closest pattern; if it fits, fix it or drop the idea.
Each entry: pattern - why it dies - the quit moment (the exact moment a player leaves) - tag.
Tags: [K:topic] = Roblox KB (kbq find / kbq case), claims there are creator self-reports unless marked official. UNVERIFIED = general knowledge.

1. **Reskin of a hit** - near-duplicate games rank lower and add nothing the original lacks (a reskin of a top game failed; a bought template flopped). Quit: "I already play the real one." [K:common-mistakes "Don't clone, template or reskin hits"]
2. **Trend-of-the-week chase** - trends fade before the copy ships (cleaning sims). Quit: the trend is over, no reason to stay. [K:common-mistakes]
3. **Hype CCU, no fanbase** - 5K CCU died once hype faded; no social reason to return. Quit: day 2, nothing new to do and no friends there. [K:case-studies-concrete-cleaning]
4. **Rushed launch, broken systems** - a hype-driven launch with a broken leaderboard died quickly. Quit: the first broken thing. [K:case-studies-store-simulator]
5. **Plugin/AI-made backlash** - a game made with a disliked plugin drew player backlash and died after a trend video. Quit: the community says "it is generated." [K:common-mistakes, case-studies-simulator-plugin-built]
6. **Pay-to-win and stacked offers** - free players call P2W and leave, then paying players churn too. Quit: the first pop-up or paywalled advantage. [K:common-mistakes]
7. **Fake urgency / spammy shop** - countdowns, "GET IT NOW", disguised ads; with minors this also breaks the platform rules. Quit: the first pushy prompt. [K:common-mistakes]
8. **Ads to rescue a weak game** - ad spikes without retention burn budget; players fall back to zero (self-reported: 3,000 Robux of ads earned 28). Quit: within 5 minutes. [K:common-mistakes; K:retention-d1-d7 (new ad users about 50% gone by 5 min)]
9. **Huge first project** - large dream games never finish or get 0-2 players; feature creep (one grew into a 200-day build). Quit: the creator's, not the player's. [K:common-mistakes]
10. **Polish for months, no demand check** - polished games nobody asked for. Quit: no one ever arrives. [K:common-mistakes "validate demand and ship"]
11. **Release and walk away** - first two weeks decide; games die when updates stop. Quit: day 8, nothing new. [K:common-mistakes; K:retention-d1-d7 "plan a day-8 hook"]
12. **Empty servers** - solo players who land in an empty server leave. Quit: the first minute alone. [K:social-features-and-virality "Check the solo-player experience in empty servers"]
13. **Social-only loop** - solo-only or friends-required loops struggle in low-population moments. Quit: no one to play with. [K:core-loops-and-game-design "keep solo and empty servers working"]
14. **Long rounds** - a viral gun game declined because rounds were long. Quit: minute 8 of a round you already lost. [K:case-studies-phantom-forces]
15. **Unclear event countdown** - the 2018 Volcano event failed on an unclear countdown and length; the 2020 Oceans event worked. Quit: "when does it end?" [K:case-studies-jailbreak]
16. **Over-long tutorial text** - the speaker claims about 30% lost by explaining too much in text. Quit: the third wall of text. [K:retention-d1-d7 "Fix onboarding by finding the drop-off point", speaker claim]
17. **No moment of joy** - onboarding that ends without reward or tomorrow-hook. Quit: end of the tutorial. [K:retention-d1-d7 "End onboarding with a moment of joy"]
18. **Grind wall** - levels that take days (4-5 days per level at level 70-80 in one official example); nothing to try before buying. Quit: the first wall. [K:retention-d1-d7; K:core-loops "Let players sample content"]
19. **Flattened rarity** - making everything equally obtainable killed one economy's collecting motivation. Quit: "everything is easy to get." [K:core-loops-and-game-design (Cumin)]
20. **Top tier makes the rest useless** - unit tiers not balanced to similar strength. Quit: "nothing but the best matters." [K:common-mistakes "Balance rarity tiers"]
21. **Unanchored novelty** - features not tied to the core loop have a 10-20 minute novelty period. Quit: when the gimmick stops being new. [K:common-mistakes (speaker estimate)]
22. **Update that breaks the game** - CCU can fall to near zero after a bad update. Quit: the broken update; roll back fast. [K:common-mistakes]
23. **Event spikes without a loyal base** - retention decided the outcome; decline followed a large event. Quit: the day after the event. [K:case-studies-grow-a-garden]
24. **Unmoderated social spaces** - parties without moderation caused safety issues and a decline. Quit: the first bad interaction (also a safety problem). [K:case-studies-meep-city]
25. **Realistic high-fidelity shooter** - production quality did not reach hit-level attraction; realistic shooters showed retention limits. Quit: slow pace, slow spawns. [K:case-studies-front-lines]
26. **Challenging a dominant title** - in an uncontested category a leader is hard to displace; a challenger failed. Quit: "why not play the original." [K:case-studies-tower-of-hell]
27. **Cosmetic-only or weekly same-content updates** - burnout and no new reason to play. Quit: the update that changes nothing. [K:common-mistakes "keep updating wisely"]
28. **Mismatched, messy visuals** - free models, repetitive layouts, tool visuals that do not match function, world spread too far. Quit: first impression looks cheap. [K:common-mistakes "Avoid art, level and theme mistakes"]
29. **Pretty with low CCU** - building what the creator likes, not what players want. Quit: no one arrived. [K:common-mistakes "build what players want"]
30. **Known developer, still failed** - a 2018 racing game by a known developer reached about 1M visits and still failed to find success. Quit: not recorded; lesson: visits are not retention. [K:case-studies-racing-game-unnamed]

## Quit-moment test (use on every draft)
- Name the exact minute a bored 12-year-old leaves. If you cannot, the idea is generic.
- If the quit moment is "I already play X", the idea is a clone; change the verb or the constraint, not the theme.
- If the quit moment is "nothing new tomorrow", add a reason to return that is not a streak popup.
