# Design tricks: steal the trick, not the theme

Cold file for the idea skills. Each hit felt fresh because of ONE trick. Use it to find the trick for your own idea; never copy the game's theme, art or name.
Source tags: [V:wiki] fetched and checked 2026-11; [K:topic] Roblox KB (kbq); UNVERIFIED = general knowledge, not fetched this run (do not quote numbers from it).
A [K:...] tag names the KB case-study file to read with `kbq case`; the trick wording itself is this file's reading, not a KB quote.
Format: **Game** - trick. Fresh because. Steal it by.

## Non-Roblox hits

**Getting Over It** - one absurd tool (a hammer) is the whole control scheme; any mistake loses real progress. Fresh because the difficulty IS the joke and the story, and clips sell themselves. Steal it by: one weird verb that is hard on purpose, with a fall that is funny to watch. UNVERIFIED.
**Untitled Goose Game** - you play the pest, with a to-do list of petty mischief. Fresh because the player is the troublemaker, tasks are comic, and the world reacts. Steal it by: invert the role (be the nuisance), give a checklist of small crimes. UNVERIFIED.
**Papers, Please** - a border inspector checks documents against rules that keep changing, while family budget pressures moral choices. Fresh because boring bureaucratic work becomes tense and emotional. Steal it by: take a dull real job and add one rule that changes and one cost to a wrong call. [V:wiki: critics called it an empathy game; indie, one developer, 2013, 5M+ copies by its 10th anniversary]
**Among Us** - everyone has the same job, one secretly sabotages, talk decides. Fresh because the fun is the arguing, not the mechanics. Steal it by: a hidden-role twist where the social bluff is the game. [K:case-studies-sussy-imposter-land: a Roblox meme game rode this; thin trick copying reached about 7.8M visits but little depth]
**Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes** - one player sees the bomb, others hold the manual. Fresh because information is split, so talking fast and clearly is the skill. Steal it by: split the information between players, not the power. UNVERIFIED.
**Portal** - one tool (two linked doors) taught through levels with no text. Fresh because each room is a new use of one idea. Steal it by: one tool, taught by rooms, then twisted three times. UNVERIFIED.
**Return of the Obra Dinn** - freeze-frame deaths; deduce who and how with a book. Fresh because you play detective, not fighter. Steal it by: the player's tool is evidence-gathering and a notebook that pays off when right. UNVERIFIED.
**Superhot** - time moves only when you move. Fresh because one rule turns shooting into puzzle-solving. Steal it by: tie time or danger to the player's own input. UNVERIFIED.
**Overcooked** - tiny kitchens that shift, break or split the team. Fresh because shouting and handoffs are the fun, and the level changes the rules. Steal it by: a chaos map that forces shared shouting and handoffs. UNVERIFIED. (A Roblox KB tools note mentions it as a reference only.)
**Katamari Damacy** - roll up things; everything is a size ladder. Fresh because growth is visible and funny, objects change meaning with size. Steal it by: growth where what you can absorb changes the world. UNVERIFIED.
**Death Stranding (delivery)** - walking with a load across rough ground is the game. Fresh because carrying, balance and route-picking become tension. Steal it by: make transport itself risky and physical (balance, weather, weight). UNVERIFIED.
**Minecraft** - break and place anything, no goal given. Fresh because the player invents the goals. Steal it by: a small set of verbs that combine, plus a world the player edits. UNVERIFIED.
**Fall Guys** - clumsy bean characters in race obstacle courses. Fresh because slapstick lowers the stakes and makes losing funny. Steal it by: clumsy physics so failure is the laugh. UNVERIFIED.
**Jackbox** - phones are the controllers; the room laughs at what people write. Fresh because the content is other people. Steal it by: players generate the content, the game just frames it. UNVERIFIED.
**Phasmophobia** - voice commands to a ghost; sanity drains instead of health. Fresh because speaking aloud to a monster is scary and the sanity meter replaces hit points. Steal it by: use real-world input (voice) as part of the fear. [K:game-ideas-and-genres: Animal Hospital used a Phasmophobia-style sanity meter (single-source, GosuGamers 2026)]
**Hades** - every death moves the story forward. Fresh because failure is rewarded with dialogue and unlocks. Steal it by: make the loss screen the best part. UNVERIFIED.
**Vampire Survivors** - auto-attack, you only steer; builds snowball. Fresh because one input makes a power fantasy simple and fast. Steal it by: delete the attack button and let build choices be the skill. [V:wiki: started 2020 by one developer, about 1,100 GBP spent on assets in early access, 70,000 concurrent Steam players by Feb 2022]
**Undertale** - you can spare enemies; the game remembers. Fresh because the choice is moral and the game talks back. Steal it by: a non-violent way to win that characters notice. UNVERIFIED.
**Stardew Valley** - slow farming with tiny daily goals and relationships. Fresh because it respects short sessions and gives a reason to come back tomorrow. Steal it by: daily micro-goals plus people who change. UNVERIFIED.
**Dark Souls (messages)** - other players leave short notes in your world. Fresh because strangers help or mislead you. Steal it by: asynchronous hints from other players, with trust as a risk. UNVERIFIED.
**Baba Is You** - the rules are blocks you can rewrite. Fresh because breaking the rules is the puzzle. Steal it by: put the rules in the world as objects. UNVERIFIED.
**Hollow Knight (map buying)** - you must find the mapmaker before you see the map. Fresh because being lost is a feeling the design controls. Steal it by: sell or hide information as part of exploration. UNVERIFIED.
**Spelunky** - small random levels, deadly traps, many ways to die funny. Fresh because the same rules produce endless stories. Steal it by: simple systems that interact in surprising ways. UNVERIFIED.
**Lethal Company** - a mining crew with a quota and proximity voice, monsters that react to noise. Fresh because friends' mistakes create the fun and the footage. Steal it by: a work quota plus fear plus proximity chat. UNVERIFIED.

## Roblox hits (trick, not theme)

**Tower of Hell / Obby** - random-ish short climbs with no checkpoints. Fresh because the fail-restart loop is simple and clip-ready. Steal it by: one pure skill challenge with a visible top. [K:case-studies-tower-of-hell, case-studies-obby]
**Doors** - a corridor of rooms with a monster that has simple readable rules. Fresh because each room is a mini puzzle and the danger is learnable. Steal it by: repeated rooms plus a monster with one clear rule. [K:case-studies-doors]
**Murder Mystery 2** - hidden roles on a small map. Fresh because social deduction fits short rounds. Steal it by: a small map, a secret, a short round. [K:case-studies-murder-mystery-2]
**Dress to Impress** - the player's creation is judged by others. Fresh because the fun is being rated. Steal it by: make a creative theme plus a judging moment. [K:case-studies-dress-to-impress]
**Animal Hospital** - cute staff, anomalies hidden among routine patients. Fresh because the pattern-spot game wears a gentle skin. Steal it by: a spot-the-wrong-thing job under a calm theme. [K:case-studies-animal-hospital; the KB marks the blend claim single-source]
**99 Nights in the Forest** - shared survival with a nightly count. Fresh because the streak counter above your head is a social flex. Steal it by: a visible streak that friends can read. [K:case-studies-99-nights-in-the-forest]
**Natural Disaster Survival** - a different disaster every round. Fresh because surprise is built in. Steal it by: a round-based random event with a clear survive condition. [K:case-studies-natural-disaster-survival]
**Please Donate** - players ask each other for money. Fresh because the economy is the social layer. Steal it by: a game where giving is the action. [K:case-studies-please-donate]
**Plane Crazy** - build a vehicle then use it. Fresh because the player's own design is the play. Steal it by: build then test, where failure is funny. [K:case-studies-plane-crazy]
**Work at a Pizza Place** - ordinary jobs as roles with small tasks. Fresh because roleplay jobs give players a purpose without a goal. Steal it by: a mundane job with one fun twist per role. [K:case-studies-work-at-a-pizza-place]

## How to use this file
- Pick the trick that matches the seed set (verb, constraint, feeling), not the genre.
- Write the trick as one sentence about the player's hands or mind, never the theme.
