# social-post-calendar

Players come back when they hear about a date. Vault: tease from about 10 days before an event, run an in-game countdown 1-2 weeks, announce publicly (Roblox and X, not only Discord), recap after, keep a predictable rhythm. The tool lays out slots from your real dates; you write the posts or hand them to `update-announcement-writer` and `short-video-planner`.

## When to use
A release or event date is set and the next four weeks need structure. Not for: a single launch day (`launch-day-planner`), live-ops content design (`event-planner`).

## Inputs (max 3)
1. Release and event dates with time zone. 2. Channels you will actually run (default: Roblox group announcements, Discord, one of TikTok or X). 3. Hours per week available (default: 4).

## Procedure
1. Pick channels honestly: one platform done well beats five (vault); a Roblox group or community page announcement reaches members in-platform.
2. Generate the grid: `python -I .rbx/claude-kit/skillpack/marketing/social-post-calendar/scripts/calendar_gen.py month --start YYYY-MM-DD --release YYYY-MM-DD --event YYYY-MM-DD --short-per-week 3`. Read the FLAG lines: a release not on a Friday or on a Tuesday is called out; decide or change the date.
3. Give every slot a purpose from this set only: tease (image, silhouette, hint), countdown, announce (release-day, with the Details Page URL), recap (clip or thanks to testers), proof (a metric or player clip), ask (poll or what-next question), behind the scenes (one system devlog), community (showcase, see `community-showcase-planner`). A slot with no purpose is deleted.
4. Cadence floor: 2-3 short-form clips per week is a floor from creators, not a target; a calendar the owner cannot keep is worse than a smaller one. Judge results after 30-60 days.
5. Ground each post in a true fact (the build exists, the date is firm). Do not announce what you may not ship (vault: cancelled sponsored events frustrated players).
6. Add a measurement row per week: plays or visits from tracked links, group joins, Discord joins, CTR on thumbnails when changed. Use tracked links per channel (`codes-campaign-planner`).
7. Hand the owner a one-page grid; the owner posts. After four weeks, compare what was planned and what was posted and cut the slots that did not happen.

Not needed.

## Fallback
Same; a table in a document. Nothing is posted by this skill.

## Evidence
`Roblox/roblox-kb/community-and-discord.md` (tease early and often, tweet from about 10 days before an event, release-day message, group announcements with polls; community built around a Discord and group about a month before launch; one platform done well), `Roblox/roblox-kb/updates-live-ops.md` (countdowns 1-2 weeks, cross-channel announcements, Friday release, predictable schedule), `Roblox/roblox-kb/trailers-and-video-marketing.md` (2-3 posts a week floor, game page URL in every promo post, quality over volume; indie numbers), `Roblox/roblox-kb/influencer-and-creator-marketing.md` (cadence judged after 30-60 days). Platform features and reach change: live-check.

## Output
`marketing/calendar-<month>.md`: the generated table with purpose and draft text per slot, the weekly measurement row, flags and decisions made.

## Acceptance checks
- [ ] Generated by `calendar_gen.py` (command quoted) from real dates, not guessed.
- [ ] Each FLAG line has a decision written next to it.
- [ ] Every slot has one of the listed purposes and a true fact.
- [ ] Total weekly posts fit the stated hours per week.
- [ ] No post is scheduled by the skill.

## Pitfalls
Five channels, zero consistency. Teasing a date that slips. Posts without the Details Page URL. A calendar with no measurement. Treating the 2-3 per week floor as a quota to hit at any quality.
